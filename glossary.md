# Glossary

The Hebrew legal terms used in the course, each given in Hebrew, then a transliteration, then its English meaning. Terms are added module by module.

## Terms

- **אבידה** (Aveda) — Lost property, which the finder must return or report to the police (Return of Lost Property Law s. 2).
- **אגודה שיתופית** (Aguda shitufit) — Cooperative society registered under the Cooperative Societies Ordinance.
- **אגרת טיפול** (Agrat tipul) — FOI handling fee, charged per hour from the fourth hour.
- **אדישות** (Adishut) — Indifference: one of the two kinds of recklessness towards a result.
- **אדם מיודע** (adam meyuda) — "Informed person" notified when an enduring power takes effect and entitled to reports (s. 32טז).
- **אדם סביר ונבון** (Adam savir venavon) — The reasonable and prudent person, the standard of care.
- **אומד דעת הצדדים** (omed da'at ha-tzdadim) — the parties' intention, the basis of contract interpretation.
- **אורגן** (Organ) — Organ of a company: general meeting, board, general manager (Companies Law s. 46).
- **אזהרה** (Azhara) — The warning served on a debtor when an execution file opens (Execution Law s. 7).
- **אזכור חוזר** (izkur chozer) — A repeat (short) citation.
- **אחריות מוגבלת** (Ahrayut mugbelet) — Limited liability of shareholders, set in the articles (Companies Law s. 35).
- **אחריות קפידה** (Ahrayut kpida) — Strict liability (s. 22).
- **אחריות שילוחית** (Achrayut shilucheet) — Vicarious liability of an employer or principal.
- **אי־אמון קונסטרוקטיבי** (I-emun konstruktivi) — Constructive no-confidence: 61 members must express confidence in another government (Basic Law: The Government s. 28).
- **אי־התאמה** (i-hat'ama) — non-conformity of goods sold (Sale Law s. 11).
- **אי־התאמה יסודית** (I-hat'ama yesodit) — Fundamental (structural) non-conformity in load-bearing parts of a building.
- **אי־התאמה נסתרת** (I-hat'ama nisteret) — Hidden non-conformity, not discoverable by reasonable inspection; notified on discovery (Sale Law s. 15).
- **אי־כושר להשתכר** (I-kosher le-hishtaker) — Earning incapacity, set at 60%, 65%, 74% or 100%.
- **איומים** (Iyumim) — Threats (s. 192).
- **איחוד עילות** (Ichud ilot) — Joinder of all causes of action from the same facts in one claim (reg. 24).
- **אימוץ הורה שני** (Imutz hore sheni) — Second-parent adoption of a partner's child (Yaros-Hakak).
- **אין להשיב לאשמה** (ein lehashiv la'ashma) — "No case to answer": acquittal at the end of the prosecution case when guilt is not proved even prima facie (CPL s. 158).
- **אין עונשין למפרע** (Ein onshin le-mafre'a) — No retroactive punishment (s. 3).
- **איסור פרסום** (issur pirsum) — Publication ban.
- **אישיות משפטית נפרדת** (Ishiyut mishpatit nifredet) — Separate legal personality of a company (Companies Law s. 4).
- **אכיפה** (akhifa) — enforcement (specific performance), including orders to pay (Remedies Law s. 3).
- **אם נושאת** (Em nos'et) — Gestational surrogate.
- **אמנה** (Amana) — Treaty or convention: a written agreement between States governed by international law, whatever its name.
- **אמרת אגב** (imrat agav) — obiter dictum: a remark not necessary for the decision.
- **אמרת חוץ** (imrat chutz) — Out-of-court statement; in criminal cases a witness's written statement may be admitted under s. 10א.
- **אסדרה** (Asdara) — Regulation as an activity (the Hebrew term for "regulation" in the Regulatory Principles Law 2021).
- **אסיפה כללית** (Asefa klalit) — General meeting of shareholders (annual or special).
- **אפוטרופוס טבעי** (Apotropos tiv'i) — Natural guardian: each parent of a minor (Capacity and Guardianship Law s. 14).
- **אפליה מבנית** (Aflaya mivnit) — Structural (systemic) discrimination, which can operate without intent.
- **אפקט הבעלות** (Efekt ha-ba'alut) — Endowment effect: valuing what one has more than the same thing one does not have.
- **אצילת סמכויות** (Atzilat samchuyot) — Delegation of powers (Basic Law: The Government s. 33).
- **ארגון עובדים יציג** (Irgun ovdim yatzig) — Representative workers' organisation (largest, and at least a third of covered employees).
- **ארכה** (arkha) — extension given before cancelling for a non-fundamental breach (s. 7(ב)).
- **אשם תורם** (Asham torem) — Contributory fault, reducing damages (s. 68).
- **אשרור** (Ishrur) — Ratification: the international act by which a State consents to be bound by a treaty.
- **בג״ץ** (bagatz) — High Court of Justice: the Supreme Court sitting as such.
- **בוררות** (Borerut) — Arbitration under the Arbitration Law 1968.
- **בחינות הלשכה** (bhinot ha-lishka) — the Israel Bar examinations.
- **בטלות יחסית** (Batlut yahasit) — Relative voidness: the doctrine letting a court decline to void a defective act given the circumstances.
- **בידוי נתונים** (Bidui netunim) — Fabrication of data.
- **ביטוח אחריות** (Bituach achrayut) — Liability insurance; the injured third party may claim directly (Insurance Contract Law s. 68).
- **ביטוח חובה** (Bituach chova) — Compulsory motor insurance covering liability for bodily injury.
- **ביטוח חסר** (Bituach chaser) — Underinsurance, reducing proceeds in proportion (s. 60).
- **ביטוי מקדים** (Bitui makdim) — Introductory signal in a footnote (ראו, ראו גם, השוו ל, מנגד, אבל ראו), uniform citation rules r. 38.4.
- **ביטול הרשעה** (bitul harsha'a) — Cancelling a conviction before sentence and ordering probation or community service without conviction (CPL s. 192א).
- **ביטול מחובת הצדק** (Bitul mechovat hatzedek) — Setting aside as of right, typically for defective service.
- **ביטול פסק בוררות** (Bitul psak borerut) — Setting aside an award on the closed grounds in Arbitration Law s. 24.
- **ביטול רישום משטרתי** (bitul rishum mishtarti) — Cancellation of a police-register entry about a closed file (Criminal Information Law s. 33).
- **ביקורת עמיתים** (Bikoret amitim) — Peer review.
- **ביקורת שיפוטית** (Bikoret shiputit) — Judicial review, of statutes or of administrative decisions.
- **בית דין דתי** (beit din dati) — religious court (rabbinical, Sharia, Christian, Druze).
- **בית הדין הבינלאומי לצדק** (Beit ha-din ha-beinleumi le-tzedek) — International Court of Justice: the UN court for disputes between consenting States and advisory opinions.
- **בית הדין הפלילי הבינלאומי** (Beit ha-din ha-plili ha-beinleumi) — International Criminal Court: a treaty court trying individuals for the gravest international crimes.
- **בית הדין הרבני הגדול** (Beit ha-din ha-rabani ha-gadol) — The Great Rabbinical Court in Jerusalem, hearing appeals.
- **בית הדין השרעי** (Beit ha-din ha-shar'i) — Sharia court, with exclusive jurisdiction over Muslims' personal status.
- **בית הדין לחוזים אחידים** (beit ha-din le-hozim ahidim) — the Standard Contracts Tribunal.
- **בית הדין לעבודה** (beit ha-din la-avoda) — labour court.
- **בית המשפט המחוזי** (beit ha-mishpat ha-mehozi) — district court.
- **בית המשפט לענייני משפחה** (Beit ha-mishpat le-inyenei mishpaha) — Family Affairs Court: a magistrates' court designated to hear family matters (Family Court Law 1995).
- **בית משותף** (Bayit meshutaf) — Condominium: a building with two or more flats registered in the condominium register (Land Law s. 52).
- **בית משפט השלום** (beit mishpat ha-shalom) — magistrates' court.
- **בית משפט לתביעות קטנות** (Beit mishpat letvi'ot ktanot) — Small claims court.
- **בעל דין** (ba'al din) — party to legal proceedings, litigant.
- **בעל דין שאינו מיוצג** (ba'al din she-eino meyutzag) — self-represented (unrepresented) party.
- **בעל שיחה** (Ba'al sicha) — Party to a conversation (the speaker or the person it is addressed to).
- **בעל שליטה** (Ba'al shlita) — Controlling shareholder.
- **בעלות** (Ba'alut) — Ownership: the right to possess, use and deal with property (Land Law s. 2; Movable Property Law s. 2).
- **בקשה להישפט** (bakasha lehishafet) — Request to be tried for a fine offence, within 90 days of service (CPL s. 229(א)(2)).
- **בקשה ליישוב סכסוך** (Bakasha le-yishuv sikhsukh) — The bare request that must precede any contested family claim (2014 Law).
- **בקשה לעיון מחדש** (Bakasha le-iyun mehadash) — Request for reconsideration of an administrative decision.
- **ברירת משפט** (breirat mishpat) — Choice of trial: the procedure for fine offences, letting the recipient pay, ask to cancel or ask to be tried (CPL ss. 228–230).
- **ברית הזוגיות** (Brit ha-zugiyut) — Couples Covenant for a man and a woman both registered as having no religion (2010 Law).
- **בשלות** (Bshelut) — Ripeness: the dispute must be concrete enough for decision.
- **גוף דו־מהותי** (Guf du-mahuti) — Dual-nature body: a body bound by some public-law duties though it acts in private law.
- **גוף מבוקר** (Guf mevukar) — Audited body under the State Comptroller Law s. 9.
- **גוף נפרד** (Guf nifrad) — Separate entity: a foreign governmental body with legal personality distinct from the foreign government.
- **גורם זר מתערב** (Gorem zar mitarev) — Intervening cause that may break the causal chain.
- **גוש / חלקה / תת־חלקה** (Gush / helka / tat-helka) — Block / parcel / sub-parcel (a flat in a condominium).
- **גזל** (Gezel) — Conversion of goods (s. 52).
- **גזר דין** (Gzar din) — Sentence.
- **גישור** (Gishur) — Mediation: a mediator helps the parties agree, without power to decide (Courts Law s. 79ג).
- **גלגול שלישי** (Gilgul shlishi) — Third instance; leave given only for issues beyond the parties (Haniyon Haifa).
- **גמול שעות נוספות** (Gmul sha'ot nosafot) — Overtime premium: 125% for the first two daily overtime hours, 150% after.
- **גמירת דעת** (gmirat da'at) — intent to be bound, judged objectively.
- **גמלת הבטחת הכנסה** (Gimlat havtahat hachnasa) — Income support benefit, means-tested.
- **גמר חשבון** (Gmar heshbon) — Final settlement of all payments when employment ends.
- **גניבה ספרותית / פלגיאט** (Gneva sifrutit / plagiat) — Plagiarism: using another's words or ideas as one's own.
- **גרימת מוות ברשלנות** (Grimat mavet be-rashlanut) — Causing death by negligence (s. 304).
- **דבר המלך במועצתו** (dvar ha-melekh be-mo'atzto) — the Palestine Order in Council 1922, the Mandate's constitutional document.
- **דבר מה נוסף** (dvar ma nosaf) — "Something extra": the minimal corroboration needed to convict on a confession.
- **דברי הסבר** (divrei hesber) — Explanatory notes published with a bill.
- **דגל שחור** (Degel shahor) — "Black flag": the image for an order whose illegality is obvious to any conscience.
- **דואר רשום עם אישור מסירה** (do'ar rashum im ishur mesira) — Registered mail with proof of delivery.
- **דוקטרינת הפסילה הפסיקתית** (doktrinat hapsila hapsikatit) — The relative case-law exclusionary doctrine adopted in Yissacharov (CrimA 5121/98).
- **דחייה על הסף** (Dhiya al ha-saf) — Dismissal in limine, without a hearing on the merits.
- **דיון מהיר** (Diyun mahir) — Fast track for smaller money claims and eviction claims (regs 78–82).
- **דיון מוקדם / קריאה טרומית** (Diyun mukdam / kri'a tromit) — Preliminary reading of a private bill, at least 45 days after tabling unless shortened.
- **דיון מקדמי** (Diyun mukdam) — Preliminary discussion between represented parties before the pre-trial (reg. 35).
- **דיון נוסף** (diyun nosaf) — further hearing of a Supreme Court decision by a larger panel.
- **דיות** (dayut) — Sufficiency: whether admitted, credited evidence is enough, including corroboration requirements.
- **דייר מוגן** (Dayar mugan) — Protected tenant under the Tenant Protection Law, evictable only on statutory grounds.
- **דין אישי** (Din ishi) — Personal law: the religious law of a person's community, which governs marriage, divorce and maintenance.
- **דין מצוי / דין רצוי** (Din matzui / din ratzui) — The law as it is / the law as it should be.
- **דין רציפות** (Din retzifut) — Continuity: carrying a bill that passed first reading into the next Knesset under the Continuity Law 1993.
- **דינא דמלכותא דינא** (Dina de-malkhuta dina) — "The law of the kingdom is law": Jewish law's recognition of state law.
- **דיני ממונות / איסור והיתר** (Dinei mamonot / issur ve-heter) — Civil (monetary) law / ritual law in Jewish law.
- **דיני נזיקין** (Dinei nezikin) — Tort law.
- **דיני עונשין** (Dinei onshin) — Criminal law.
- **דיני ראיות** (dinei re'ayot) — Evidence law: the rules on who may testify, what the court may receive, what it proves and who must prove it.
- **דירה ראויה למגורים** (Dira re'uya le-megurim) — Habitable apartment under the First Schedule to the Rental and Borrowing Law.
- **דירקטוריון** (Direktoryon) — Board of directors.
- **דלתיים סגורות** (dlatayim sgurot) — Closed doors (in camera); family matters are heard so by default (Courts Law s. 68(ה)).
- **דמוקרטיה מתגוננת** (Demokratya mitgonenet) — Defensive (militant) democracy, which may bar those who seek to destroy it.
- **דמי אבטלה** (Dmei avtala) — Unemployment benefit.
- **דמי ביטול** (dmei bitul) — cancellation fee (capped by s. 14ה and the Cancellation Regulations).
- **דמי הבראה** (Dmei havra'a) — Recuperation pay, an annual payment set by extension order according to seniority.
- **דמי לידה** (Dmei leida) — Maternity (birth) pay from National Insurance.
- **דמי מחלה** (Dmei mahala) — Sick pay: none for day 1, half for days 2–3, full from day 4.
- **דמי מפתח** (Dmei mafte'ach) — Key money: any payment other than rent for a tenancy or possession under the Tenant Protection Law.
- **דמי פגיעה** (Dmei pgi'a) — Injury allowance after a work accident, up to 13 weeks.
- **דן יחיד** (dan yachid) — Single judge (in the labour court, e.g. for appeals from NII committees under the Third Schedule).
- **דעה נעולה** (De'a ne'ula) — Closed mind; prejudgment that disqualifies a decision-maker.
- **דעת מיעוט** (da'at mi'ut) — Dissenting opinion.
- **דעת רוב** (da'at rov) — Majority opinion.
- **דעת רוב / דעת מיעוט** (da'at rov / da'at mi'ut) — majority opinion / minority (dissenting) opinion.
- **דרישת הכתב** (drishat ha-ktav) — writing requirement (for example Land Law s. 8).
- **דרך מקובלת** (derekh mekubelet) — customary manner, paired with good faith in ss. 12 and 39.
- **האזנת סתר** (Ha'azanat seter) — Secret eavesdropping: listening to a conversation without the consent of any of its parties; a crime (Eavesdropping Law ss. 1–2).
- **הבאת מובאות** (Hava'at muva'ot) — Quotation; one of the fair-use purposes in Copyright Law s. 19(a).
- **הבטחה מינהלית** (Havtaha minhalit) — Administrative promise; binding if given by an authorised official, within power, definite and capable of performance (Sai-Tex).
- **הבשלה (וסטינג)** (Havshala) — Vesting of founders' shares over time.
- **הגנה מן הצדק** (Hagana min hatzedek) — Protection from injustice; a preliminary argument that the proceedings contradict justice and fairness (CPL s. 149(10)).
- **הגנה עצמית** (Hagana atzmit) — Self-defence (s. 34י).
- **הדבר מעיד על עצמו** (Hadavar me'id al atzmo) — Res ipsa loquitur; shifts the burden of proving no negligence to the defendant (s. 41).
- **הודאה בקיום זכות** (Hoda'a bekiyum zchut) — Acknowledgement of a claim in writing or before a court, which restarts limitation (Prescription Law s. 9).
- **הודאת סילוק** (Hoda'at silluk) — Final-settlement waiver; for severance valid only in writing and expressly (s. 29).
- **הודיה חופשית ומרצון** (hodaya chofshit umeratzon) — Free and voluntary confession, the condition of admissibility in s. 12.
- **הודיית בעל דין** (hodayat ba'al din) — Admission of a party: the opposing party's own statement, admissible against him.
- **הודעה לצד שלישי** (Hoda'a letzad shlishi) — Third-party notice by a defendant seeking contribution or indemnity (regs 22–23).
- **הודעה מוקדמת** (Hoda'a mukdemet) — Prior notice of dismissal or resignation; pay in lieu (חלף הודעה מוקדמת) if not given.
- **הודעה נלווית** (Hoda'a nilvet) — Accompanying notice (Form 4) filed with a family claim after the stay.
- **הודעה על תנאי עבודה** (Hoda'a al tna'ei avoda) — Written notice of terms of employment, due within 30 days of starting.
- **הודעת תשלום קנס** (hoda'at tashlum knas) — Fine-payment notice for a fine offence (CPL s. 228(ב)).
- **הוועדה לבחירת שופטים** (Ha-va'ada li-vhirat shoftim) — The Judicial Selection Committee (Basic Law: The Judiciary s. 4).
- **הון מניות רשום / מונפק** (Hon menayot rashum / munpak) — Registered (authorised) / issued share capital.
- **הונאה בעבודה** (Hona'a ba-avoda) — Cheating in a paper: a university disciplinary offence (e.g. TAU rules s. 29.3).
- **הוצאה לפועל** (Hotza'a lapo'al) — Execution, enforcement of judgments and other executable documents.
- **הוראת מינהל** (hora'at minhal) — administrative instruction without legislative effect (permit, licence, notice).
- **הוראת שעה** (hora'at sha'ah) — A temporary provision in force for a set period.
- **הזדהות לאומית** (Hizdahut le'umit) — National digital identification used to log in to government services.
- **הזמנה להציע הצעות** (hazmana le-hatzi'a hatza'ot) — invitation to make offers (to treat).
- **הזמנת נושים** (hazmanat nosim) — Public invitation to the estate's creditors, at least three months (ss. 99, 123).
- **החזרה לוועדה** (hachzara la-va'ada) — Remittal of a matter to an NII committee, often in another composition, with instructions.
- **החזרת גזלה** (Hachzarat gzela) — Restoration of land taken from the possessor by unlawful self-help (Land Law s. 19).
- **החזרת ילד חטוף** (Hachzarat yeled hatuf) — Return of a child wrongfully removed or retained, under the Hague Abduction Convention as incorporated in 1991.
- **החלטה** (hachlata) — Decision: any other ruling of a court, including interim decisions.
- **החלטה לפי צד אחד** (Hachlata lefi tzad echad) — Decision given in a party's absence; may be set aside within 30 days (reg. 131).
- **החלטת הררי** (Hahlatat Harari) — The Knesset's decision of 13 June 1950 to build the constitution chapter by chapter, as Basic Laws.
- **החלק הכללי** (Ha-helek ha-klali) — The general part of the Penal Law (elements, attempt, complicity, defences).
- **החמרת הסיכון** (Hachmarat ha-sikun) — Aggravation of risk during the policy, to be notified to the insurer (ss. 17–19).
- **החצנה** (Hachtsana) — Externality: a cost one person's activity imposes on others who did not agree to it.
- **הטיה אנוכית** (Hataya anokhit) — Self-serving bias: seeing fairness in the light most favourable to oneself.
- **הטיית בחירה** (Hatayat bkhira) — Selection effect: tried cases are not a random sample of disputes, because clear cases settle.
- **הטעיה** (hat'aya) — deceit, including non-disclosure (s. 15).
- **הטרדה מינית** (Hatrada minit) — Sexual harassment as defined in s. 3 of the Prevention of Sexual Harassment Law.
- **היועץ המשפטי לממשלה** (ha-yo'etz ha-mishpati la-memshala) — Attorney General.
- **היזק בזדון** (Hezek be-zadon) — Malicious damage to property (s. 452).
- **היסוד הנפשי** (Ha-yesod ha-nafshi) — The mental element (mens rea).
- **היסוד העובדתי** (Ha-yesod ha-uvdati) — The factual element (actus reus): conduct, circumstances and, in result offences, the result (s. 18).
- **היקש** (hekesh) — analogy, reasoning from a similar rule.
- **היתר פיטורים** (Heter piturim) — Ministerial permit needed to dismiss a pregnant employee or one after maternity leave or in fertility treatment.
- **הכחשה מפורשת ומפורטת** (Hakchasha meforeshet umefuretet) — Express and detailed denial required to avoid deemed admission (reg. 14(ב)).
- **הכנה** (Hakhana) — Preparation, not punishable by itself.
- **הכרה** (Hakara) — Recognition of a State or government by another State; a political act with legal effects.
- **הכרזה על בטלות** (Hakhraza al batlut) — Declaration of invalidity of a statutory provision.
- **הכרעה על יסוד הכתב** (Hachra'a al yesod haktav) — Decision on the written record (small claims reg. 13(ג)).
- **הלימה** (Helima) — Proportionality (desert), the guiding principle of sentencing (s. 40ב).
- **הלכה פסוקה** (halakha psuka) — binding precedent: the legal rule decided by a court.
- **הלכת השיתוף** (Hilkhat ha-shituf) — Presumption of community of property, for marriages before 1974 and, with limits, cohabitants.
- **המג׳לה** (ha-majalla) — the Mejelle, the Ottoman civil code, repealed in full in 1984.
- **המהפכה החוקתית** (Ha-mahapekha ha-hukatit) — The constitutional revolution: the 1992 rights Basic Laws and Bank Mizrahi (1995), which made judicial review of statutes possible.
- **המועצה הישראלית לצרכנות** (Hamo'etza hayisra'elit letzarchanut) — Israel Consumer Council, a statutory body that advises, assists and may represent consumers.
- **הממונה על הגנת הצרכן** (Hamemuneh al haganat hatzarchan) — Consumer Protection Commissioner, head of the Consumer Protection and Fair Trade Authority.
- **הממונה על המרשם** (Ha-memune al ha-mirsham) — Registration commissioner, who hears administrative appeals (ערר) against registrars and superintendents (s. 121).
- **הממונה על חופש המידע** (hamemune al chofesh hameida) — An authority's freedom-of-information officer.

- **שימוש לרעה בהליך** (Shimush lera'a bahalich) — Abuse of process (reg. 4): a step meant to disrupt, delay or harass, or disproportionate to the case.

- **שימוש לרעה בסמכות המכוננת** (Shimush lera'a ba-samkhut ha-mekhonenet) — Abuse of constituent power: using the form of a Basic Law for a norm that lacks stability, generality or fit with the constitutional fabric (Bar-On, Shafir).

- **שיפוט אוניברסלי** (Shiput universali) — Universal jurisdiction over international crimes regardless of where or by whom committed (Eichmann).

- **שכר מולן** (Sachar mulan) — Wage paid late, i.e. unpaid by the determining day (the ninth day after the due date).
- **שכר מינימום** (Schar minimum) — Minimum wage, 47.5% of the average wage, updated each April.
- **שכר ראוי** (Skhar ra'ui) — Reasonable payment for use, owed by a co-owner who used the common property (Land Law s. 33).
- **שלטון החוק הפורמלי / המהותי** (Shilton ha-hok ha-formali / ha-mahuti) — The formal (thin) and substantive (thick) conceptions of the rule of law.

- **שנאת הפסד** (Sin'at hefsed) — Loss aversion: losses weigh roughly twice as much as equal gains.
- **שעבוד** (Shi'bud) — Charge (security interest) registered over a company's assets.
- **שפיטות** (Shfitut) — Justiciability, institutional and normative.
- **שריון / סעיף משוריין** (Shiryun / se'if meshuryan) — Entrenchment / an entrenched section, changeable only by a special majority (for example Basic Law: The Knesset s. 4).
- **תאגיד** (Ta'agid) — Body corporate, legal person.
- **תאונת דרכים** (Te'unat drachim) — Road accident: bodily injury from use of a motor vehicle for transport (1975 Law s. 1).
- **תביעה לפינוי מושכר** (Tvi'a lefinuy muskar) — Claim to evict a tenant, on the fast track with special rules (reg. 81).
- **תביעה משטרתית** (tvi'a mishtartit) — police prosecution.
- **תביעה נגזרת** (Tvi'a nigzeret) — Derivative claim brought for the company by a shareholder or director (Companies Law ss. 194–206).
- **תביעה על סכום קצוב** (Tvi'a al schum katzuv) — Claim for a fixed sum filed directly at the Execution Office (s. 81א1).
- **תביעה שכנגד** (Tvi'a shekeneged) — Counterclaim, filed with the defence (reg. 21).
- **תביעת השתקה** (Tvi'at hashtaka) — SLAPP: a claim meant to silence public criticism; an abuse of process (רע״א 1954/24).
- **תגובה מקדמית** (Tguva mukdemet) — Preliminary response of the respondent to a petition.
- **תגמולי ביטוח** (Tagmulei bituach) — Insurance proceeds.

- **תוחלת** (Tohelet) — Expected value: the probability-weighted average of possible outcomes, net of costs.
- **תום לב** (tom lev) — good faith (Contracts Law ss. 12, 39).
- **תוספת ראשונה** (Tosefet rishona) — First Schedule of the Administrative Affairs Courts Law: subjects of administrative petitions.
- **תורה שבכתב / תורה שבעל פה** (Torah she-bikhtav / torah she-be'al peh) — The Written Law (Pentateuch) / the Oral Law (the interpretive tradition).
- **תזכיר** (Tazkir) — Memorandum of association; exists only for companies formed before the Companies Law 1999.
- **תזכיר חוק** (Tazkir hok) — Bill memorandum: a ministry's draft bill with explanatory notes, published for public comment before it goes to the Ministerial Committee for Legislation.
- **תזכיר משפטי** (tazkir mishpati) — A legal research memo.
- **תחולה נדחית** (Tkhula nidhet) — Deferred application, a remedy used in HCJ 2412/23.
- **תחלוף** (Tachluf) — Subrogation: the paying insurer takes over the insured's claim against the wrongdoer (s. 62).
- **תחליף המצאה** (Tachalif hamtza'a) — Substituted service ordered by the court (reg. 164).

- **תיקון עקיף** (tikkun akif) — An indirect amendment: a change to one law made inside another law.
- **תכלית אובייקטיבית** (tachlit obyektivit) — Objective purpose: the purpose reflecting the legal system's values.
- **תכלית סובייקטיבית** (tachlit subyektivit) — Subjective purpose: what the legislature actually intended.
