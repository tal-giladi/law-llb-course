# Private criminal complaint (קובלנה פלילית)

**What it is for:** prosecuting an offence yourself, as the complainant-prosecutor (קובל), under Criminal Procedure Law [Consolidated] 1982, s. 68, for an offence listed in the **Second Schedule** of that law (for example threats, assault, assault causing actual bodily harm, wounding, trespass without a weapon, malicious damage, insult in a public place, privacy, copyright and trademark offences), except where committed against a family member. The provisions on indictments apply to it (s. 70), so it must contain what s. 85 lists. Used in lesson 05.8.
**Who may file it:** "כל אדם" (s. 68), in practice the victim, in their own name. You may conduct it yourself (s. 71). A complaint against a State employee for an act in the course of duty needs the Attorney General's consent (s. 69). Drafting it for someone else for pay is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).
**Where:** the criminal registry of the court that has jurisdiction, usually the magistrates' court for the area where the offence was committed (⚠️ check the court fee and filing method with the court registry or the Judiciary's information centre before filing). The court sends a copy to the district attorney, who may take over the prosecution within 15 days (ss. 71–72).
**Deadline:** the limitation periods for prosecution in CPL s. 9 apply (misdemeanours, contraventions); see `references/current-numbers.md`.
**Risk:** you carry the burden of proof beyond reasonable doubt, must summon your witnesses and present evidence, and if you do not appear the court may treat it as a withdrawal (s. 133). The court may stay the case until you appoint a lawyer if you cannot manage it or conduct it vexatiously (s. 73). Ask the court registry or a lawyer about cost exposure if you lose (⚠️ not verified for this course). Consider a lawyer before filing.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

**Next steps after filing:** keep the stamped copy; serve the defendant as the registry instructs; prepare your evidence and witness summonses; if the district attorney notifies the court within 15 days that the State will prosecute, the complaint is replaced by an indictment (s. 72) and you become a witness again.
