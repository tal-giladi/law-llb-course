# Trade-mark search and application worksheet (דף עבודה: חיפוש מקדים ובקשה לרישום סימן מסחר)

**What it is for:** checking whether a name or logo is free and registrable, and preparing a trade-mark application in your own name to the Israel Patent Office. Used in lessons 32.5 and 32.9 and the module 32 project.
**Who may file:** you, for your own mark, as an individual (on paper or online with government electronic identification) or for your company (online only, Trade Marks Regulations reg. 6(ב)). Filing for others for pay is reserved to patent attorneys and lawyers (Patents Law s. 154; Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** the Patent Office's trade-mark search and online filing services (check the current addresses on gov.il); WIPO Global Brand Database (branddb.wipo.int); Nice Classification (nclpub.wipo.int).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules and fees before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them.
