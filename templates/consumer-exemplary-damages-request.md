# Consumer written request before exemplary damages (בקשה בכתב לעוסק לפי סעיף 31א(ב) לחוק הגנת הצרכן)

**What it is for:** the written request a consumer must send a dealer before claiming exemplary damages (פיצויים לדוגמה) under s. 31א of the Consumer Protection Law 1981 (s. 31א(ב)). It covers the listed breaches only: refusing a refund under the return policy (s. 4ג), not delivering to the agreed place after misleading about it, charging after a fixed-term deal ended or after cancellation of a continuing deal, not refunding an overcharge (s. 13ד1), a fee for a permitted payment method (s. 13ח), not refunding after cancellation of a door-to-door, distance, timeshare or medical-services deal, refusing the shelf price (s. 17ב(ד)), refusing payment in shekels at the set rate, no warranty certificate, and no warranty repair or spare parts (s. 18א). Used in lessons 29.6 and 29.9 and the module 29 project.
**Who may send it:** the consumer, in his own name. Sending such letters for others for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** to the dealer in writing, including by email (s. 31א(ב)); keep proof of sending (email with the attachment, registered mail receipt, screenshot of the website form).
**Important:** misleading under s. 2 in general is not on the list; claim it as tort damages (s. 31, with proof of reliance) or cancel (s. 32). The ceilings for exemplary damages are fixed in s. 31א(א) and (ג) (see `references/current-numbers.md`). Give a reasonable deadline (14 days is common) before filing a small claim.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before sending or filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them.
