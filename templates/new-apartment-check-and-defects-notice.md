# New-apartment check and defects notice (רכישת דירה מקבלן: בדיקה והודעה על אי־התאמות)

**What it is for:** a buyer's own worksheet for an apartment bought from a developer: checking the contract against the Sale (Apartments) Law 1973 and the Assurance of Investment Law 1974 before and during payments (Part A), keeping a defects file by Schedule item after handover (Part B), and a written notice of non-conformity to the developer (Part C). Used in lessons 20.2 and 20.9 and the module 20 project. It complements the apartment purchase check (registry and second-hand flats, module 13).
**Who may use it:** you, for an apartment you are buying or own (or a later buyer, קונה משנה). Checking or drafting for other buyers as a service is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** the notice goes to the developer (and its service contractor if the contract names one), by email and registered mail or courier with proof of delivery. Complaints about money security: the supervisor under s. 3ד of the Assurance of Investment Law at the Ministry of Construction and Housing (check the current gov.il service page).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them. Periods and amounts: see references/current-numbers.md.
