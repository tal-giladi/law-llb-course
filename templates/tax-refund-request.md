# Tax refund request: cover letter and checklist (בקשה להחזר מס – מכתב נלווה ורשימת מסמכים)

**What it is for:** asking the assessing officer to refund income tax you overpaid in a tax year, as an individual who is exempt from filing an annual return (Exemption from Filing Regulations reg. 2), together with the official refund form (Form 135) or the online refund service. Used in lessons 25.4, 25.9 and 25.10 and the module 25 project. If you must file an annual return (reg. 3, business or foreign income), claim the refund in the return instead.
**Who may file it:** you, in your own name, for your own tax years. Preparing tax filings for others for pay is regulated, and representing others before the Tax Authority is reserved to lawyers, accountants and representing tax advisers (Income Tax Ordinance s. 236; Bar Association Law ss. 20–21).
**Where:** the assessing office (פקיד שומה) for your place of residence, or the Tax Authority's online refund service for employees in your personal area (check the current form and service on gov.il; the site blocks automated access, so confirm them yourself).
**Deadlines:** the report for each year must be filed no later than six years after the end of that year (Income Tax Ordinance s. 160(א)); see `references/current-numbers.md`. The refund carries linkage and interest.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them. Use one letter per tax year.

## Part B: checklist before you file

| Check | Done |
|---|---|
| The year is within six years (s. 160(א)) | ☐ |
| I am exempt from filing for this year (reg. 2) and no reg. 3 exclusion applies | ☐ |
| I have a Form 106 from every employer for the year | ☐ |
| I have the NII certificate for every benefit (unemployment, maternity, reserve pay) | ☐ |
| Every credit point I claim has a section and a document | ☐ |
| Donation receipts are from institutions recognised under s. 46 | ☐ |
| My computation includes all income and all tax withheld | ☐ |
| Bank details are attached | ☐ |
| I kept a copy and the filing receipt | ☐ |
