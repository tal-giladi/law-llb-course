# Appeal against a decision to close a criminal file (ערר על החלטה לסגור תיק)

**What it is for:** a complainant's appeal (ערר) against a decision not to investigate or not to prosecute, under Criminal Procedure Law [Consolidated] 1982, ss. 63–65. It applies where the file was closed because "the circumstances as a whole do not suit an investigation or prosecution" (lack of public interest), for insufficient evidence, or for no guilt. Used in lesson 05.8 and the module 5 project.
**Who may file it:** the complainant (המתלונן), in their own name; where the offence caused a death, the family members listed in the Crime Victims' Rights Law s. 22. Drafting appeals for others as an occupation or for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** "באמצעות המשטרה או התובע" (s. 65): through the police (for files closed by the police or police prosecution) or the prosecutor's office that decided. The appeal is decided by the authority named in s. 64(א): for most police decisions, a district attorney or a senior State Attorney's Office lawyer appointed to head appeals. The closure notice usually states where to send the appeal (⚠️ check the address and email on the notice and on the State Attorney's Office page on gov.il).
**Deadline:** within the period in s. 65 from the day the notice under s. 63 was delivered to you; the decision-maker may extend it (see `references/current-numbers.md`). Ask for an extension in writing **before** the deadline if you need the reasons or materials first.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.
