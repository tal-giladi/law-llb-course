# Execution request checklist (רשימת בדיקה לפתיחת תיק בהוצאה לפועל)

**What it is for:** preparing a request to execute a money judgment (or a settlement with the force of a judgment, or a confirmed arbitral award) at the Execution Office, and planning the steps after the file opens. It is a worksheet that sits next to the official form; the request itself is filed on the Enforcement and Collection Authority's form 206 ("בקשה לביצוע פסק דין (כספי בלבד)"). Used in lessons 08.7 and 08.9 and the module 8 project.
**Who may file:** the creditor (זוכה) in person, for his own judgment. Representing another person before the Execution Office is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20(2)).
**Where:** any Execution Office branch, by prior appointment, or the Authority's online personal area (check on the day whether it accepts filings from unrepresented creditors); call centre *35592.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules and forms before filing.
