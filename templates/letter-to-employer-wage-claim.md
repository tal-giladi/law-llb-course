# Letter to employer: wage and final-settlement demand (מכתב דרישה למעסיק)

**What it is for:** demanding unpaid wages, final-settlement payments and documents from a current or former employer, before going to the labour court. It doubles as evidence that you asked and when. Used in lessons 17.4, 17.5 (adapted for a written discrimination or harassment complaint) and 17.10 and the module 17 project.
**Who may send it:** you, in your own name, about your own employment. Writing such letters for others as an occupation or for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** the employer's registered address and the HR or payroll email; for a cleaning, security or catering contractor, send a copy to the client of the service as well (Enforcement of Labour Laws Law s. 25 notice). Send by email and registered mail and keep proof.
**Deadlines:** severance is late after 15 days from the end of employment (Wage Protection Law s. 20); late-wage compensation must be claimed in court within 60 days of receiving the late wage (s. 17א). Do not wait for an answer if a deadline is close.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.
