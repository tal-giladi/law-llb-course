# Execution debtor response kit (ערכת תגובה לחייב בהוצאה לפועל)

**What it is for:** a debtor's worksheet and drafting outlines for responding to an execution file: reading the warning and building the deadline diary (part A), an "I have paid" claim and an opposition to execution of a bill or fixed-sum claim (part B), a request to release protected money or cancel a lien or restriction (part C), and a household budget with a payment-order or consolidation offer and a settlement letter to a creditor (part D). Used in lessons 28.1–28.4 and 28.8 and the module 28 project.
**Who may file:** the debtor in person, in his own file. Representing another person before the Execution Office is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20). An affidavit must be signed before a lawyer or another person authorised to take affidavits.
**Where:** the Enforcement and Collection Authority's personal area (general request or the dedicated request type), the online general-request form, or any Execution Office branch by appointment; call centre *35592. Oppositions: the branch where the file was opened or the branch next to the competent court (Execution Regulations reg. 106).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules, forms and periods (current numbers) before filing.
