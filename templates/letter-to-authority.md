# Letter to a public authority (פנייה לרשות ציבורית)

**What it is for:** asking a public authority (a ministry, a local authority, or any body with statutory powers) to decide a request in writing, give reasons for a refusal and tell you how to appeal, under the Decisions and Reasons Law 1958; optionally, requesting information under the Freedom of Information Law 1998. Used in lesson 02.7 and the module 2 project.
**Who may send it:** you, in your own name, about your own matter. Drafting such letters for another person as an occupation or for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20(3)).
**Where:** the department's official email or online inquiry form, and for important matters also registered mail with proof of delivery to the authority's address. FOI requests go to the authority's freedom-of-information officer (הממונה על חופש המידע).
**Deadlines and fees:** see `references/current-numbers.md` (45 days to answer; three months' silence is a refusal; FOI periods and fee).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before sending.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them.
