# Small claim for property damage (כתב תביעה – תביעה קטנה בגין נזק לרכוש)

**What it is for:** a statement of claim in the small claims court for damage to your property caused by another person's tort (a car hit in a parking lot, water damage from a neighbour's flat, an object falling from a building site). It follows the contents required by reg. 3 of the Small Claims (Procedure) Regulations 1976 for proceedings opened from 11.4.2025. Used in lesson 04.9 and the module 4 project.
**Who may file it:** an individual (יחיד), in their own name, for a sum up to the small-claims ceiling (Courts Law s. 60; see `references/current-numbers.md`). A company cannot be a plaintiff in small claims. Representation by a lawyer only with the court's leave (Courts Law s. 63).
**Where:** the small claims court in the area of the defendant's home or business, or of the place of the act or omission (reg. 2); online through the Judiciary's services for unrepresented parties, or at the court office. A fee of 1% of the claim with a minimum applies (reg. 6; see `references/current-numbers.md`).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules and the court's current form before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them. The body of the claim must not exceed five pages (reg. 3(ה)). Attach every document you rely on (reg. 3(ד)). Check the court's website for the current electronic form, which may replace this layout.
