# Forum check worksheet (דף בדיקת ערכאה)

**What it is for:** deciding, before you write or file anything, which court or tribunal has jurisdiction over your dispute, which city to file in, and whether you may act there without a lawyer. Used in lessons 01.4 and 01.7 and in the module 1 project.

**Who uses it:** you, for your own dispute only. It is not filed with any court; it is your working note.

**Where:** keep it in your case file. Update it if facts change.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

## How to fill it in

Copy the Hebrew blocks below into a document and fill in the blanks. Part A records the facts; part B records your legal answer, with the section that supports each line. Write the exact Hebrew words of the section you rely on, not a summary. Numbers (ceilings, fees, deadlines) come from the course's current numbers page, with the date you checked.

## Check before you rely on it

- Every line in part B names a section, and you copied its words from the current consolidated text.
- No claim is sent to two forums.
- Ceilings and fees carry the date you checked them.

*This worksheet is for use in your own name only and is not legal advice.*
