# Class action: objection to a settlement and exit notice (התנגדות להסדר פשרה והודעת יציאה מן הקבוצה)

**What it is for:** two documents a class member files in a class action in Israel: (A) a notice of exit from the class (opting out of a certified class under s. 11 of the Class Actions Law 2006, or a request to leave the class covered by a settlement under s. 18(ו)); (B) a reasoned objection to a proposed settlement and to the agreed recommendation on award and fee (s. 18(ד)). Used in lessons 33.3, 33.7 and 33.8 and in the module 33 project.
**Who may file it:** (A) a class member, for himself only; (B) a class member, a person acting for the members' interests, a relevant public authority, an organisation promoting a related public purpose, or the Attorney General (s. 18(ד)). This template is for a member acting in his own name. Filing for other members as an occupation or for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** in the case file at the court named in the published notice (Net HaMishpat, or the court registry), with copies to the lawyers named in the notice, by the date in the notice: 45 days from publication unless the court set a later date (ss. 11(א), 18(ד)).
**Important:** an exit notice under s. 11 follows Form 1 to the Class Actions Regulations 2010 (reg. 7); check the official form on the Judiciary's site, since the form's text is not reproduced in the free consolidations. An objector may not withdraw the objection or receive any benefit in connection with it without the court's approval (s. 18(ד1)). After an exit, your own limitation period does not end before one year from your notice (s. 26(ד)).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules, the notice and the court's decisions in the case before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them. Use one document for each purpose: do not combine an objection and an exit in one filing unless the notice says so.

## Part C: checklist before filing

- [ ] I am inside the class definition and the period.
- [ ] I computed the last day from the publication date (registry or newspaper), not from the day I saw the notice.
- [ ] I read the full settlement and annexes, not only the notice.
- [ ] Each reason in Part B ties to a section of the Law and to a fact I can prove.
- [ ] I propose concrete changes, not only "reject".
- [ ] I filed in the case file and sent copies to all lawyers named in the notice.
- [ ] I kept proof of filing and of sending.
