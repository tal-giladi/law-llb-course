# Foreign judgment enforcement and defence worksheet (דף עבודה: אכיפת פסק־חוץ והתגוננות)

**What it is for:** checking, before anything is filed, whether a foreign court judgment can be declared enforceable in Israel under the Foreign Judgments Enforcement Law 1958, and building the creditor's evidence file or the debtor's defence file. Used in lessons 30.5 and 30.7 and the module 30 project.
**Who uses it:** you, for a judgment in your own favour or against you. It is a working note, not a court document. Drafting applications or defences for others as an occupation is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** keep it in your case file; the application or response is filed in the Israeli civil court (lesson 30.5).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

Fill in every line. Write "יש / אין / לא ידוע" and the document that proves it. Quote the exact words of the section you rely on.
