# Founders' agreement checklist (הסכם מייסדים – רשימת בדיקה)

**What it is for:** agreeing, before or at incorporation, the terms between the founders of a private company, and deciding which terms go into the articles (binding the company and future shareholders, Companies Law s. 17) and which into a founders' agreement (binding only those who sign). It is a checklist of decisions, not a finished agreement. Used in lessons 16.4, 16.7 and 16.9 and the module 16 project.
**Who may use it:** founders for their own company. Each founder may negotiate and write terms for himself; drafting the agreement for other people as a service is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20). With investors, option plans, foreign founders or unequal contributions, use a lawyer.
**Where:** no filing for the founders' agreement itself; amendments of the articles are filed with the Companies Registrar within 14 days (s. 21(ב)).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them. In the last column mark: ת = תקנון (articles), ה = הסכם מייסדים (agreement), ש = שניהם (both).
