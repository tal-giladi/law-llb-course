# Proportionality worksheet (גיליון ניתוח מידתיות)

**What it is for:** testing a law, regulation, by-law or government decision that limits a right, in the order an Israeli court uses: infringement, the four conditions of the limitation clause (Basic Law: Human Dignity and Liberty s. 8; Basic Law: Freedom of Occupation s. 4), and remedy. Used in lessons 06.3 and 06.7 and the module 6 project. The finished worksheet becomes the legal-argument section of a letter to an authority or a petition.
**Who may use it:** you, for an analysis of a measure that affects you, or for study. Preparing such an analysis for another person as an occupation or for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** not filed by itself. Its conclusions go into a prior demand to the authority ([letter to a public authority](letter-to-authority.md)) and, if needed, a petition ([petition to the High Court of Justice](hcj-petition.md)).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them.
