# Proseminar paper outline (מתווה לעבודה פרוסמינריונית)

**What it is for:** planning and building a 15-page academic legal paper, from the research proposal to the source list. Used in lessons 21.1 to 21.6 and the module 21 project.
**Who uses it:** you, for your own academic work. It is not a court document.
**Where:** keep it next to your draft. Parts A and C may be sent to your supervisor; the rest is your working file.
**Disclaimer:** this template is a study aid, not legal advice. Your course's own instructions (length, format, citation style, AI policy) take precedence.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them. Placeholders are marked with ____ or «…».
