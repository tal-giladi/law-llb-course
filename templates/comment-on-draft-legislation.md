# Comment on a draft bill or regulation (הערות לתזכיר חוק או לטיוטת תקנות)

**What it is for:** sending written comments on a government bill memorandum (תזכיר חוק), draft regulations (טיוטת תקנות) or a regulator's draft directive published for public comment. Used in lesson 07.5 and the module 7 project.
**Who may send it:** anyone, in their own name. Paid representation of others' interests is lobbying before the Knesset (Knesset Law ss. 65–66) and drafting legal documents for others as an occupation or for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20); this template is for your own comments.
**Where:** the government legislation site (https://www.tazkirim.gov.il/s/), through the comment form on the memorandum's page, or to the email address named in the memorandum or draft. Regulators that publish their own drafts (for example the Bank of Israel) name their own address.
**Deadline:** the date stated on the memorandum or draft. For government bill memoranda the Attorney General's guideline 2.3005 sets, as a rule, at least 21 days from distribution; see `references/current-numbers.md`.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check the current rules and the deadline on the draft before sending.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them. Keep it to 1–3 pages; the site's form may limit length, in which case paste the summary and attach the full comments as a file.

**Before sending, check:**

- [ ] The deadline on the draft has not passed; you saved the page or a screenshot showing the date.
- [ ] Every comment names a section and proposes wording or a concrete change.
- [ ] Facts and numbers are yours or sourced; nothing personal about third parties.
- [ ] You decided whether your name and comments may be published.
- [ ] You kept a copy and the confirmation of submission.
