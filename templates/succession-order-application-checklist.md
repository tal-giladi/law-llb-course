# Succession or probate order application checklist (בקשה לצו ירושה / לצו קיום צוואה – רשימת בדיקה)

**What it is for:** preparing a complete application to the Registrar of Inheritance for a succession order (intestacy, Form 1) or a probate order (will, Form 2), including the notices to heirs and the documents required by reg. 14 of the Succession Regulations 1998, and planning the steps after the order. Used in lessons 26.1, 26.6 and 26.9 and the module 26 project. It is a checklist and a drafting aid, not the official form: file on the Registrar's current form or online service.
**Who may file it:** any heir or beneficiary, in his or her own name (Succession Law ss. 66–67; Succession Regulations reg. 14). A represented applicant must file online. Filing on behalf of heirs when you are not one yourself, or for pay, is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).
**Where:** the Registrar of Inheritance for the area of the deceased's domicile (reg. 13), online through the Administrator General's site or on paper by email or registered mail to the district office. Service centre *2416 (⚠️ check).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

Notes in English in «guillemets» are guidance; delete them.
