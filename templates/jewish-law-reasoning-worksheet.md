# Jewish-law reasoning in a ruling: worksheet (דף עבודה: המשפט העברי בפסק דין)

**What it is for:** mapping every Jewish-law reference in an Israeli court ruling, tracing it to its sources and judging its weight in the result. Used in lesson 10.5 and the module 10 project.
**Who may use it:** anyone; it is a research tool, nothing is filed.
**Where:** your own notes. Sources: the Cardozo Israeli Supreme Court Project (English), the Judiciary's decision search (Hebrew), Sefaria (Jewish-law texts).
**Deadlines and fees:** none.
**Disclaimer:** this is a study aid, not legal advice. Write as an analyst: describe what the judges did; do not argue which tradition is right.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them.
