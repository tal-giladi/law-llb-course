# Payslip check and final-settlement worksheet (בדיקת תלוש שכר ותחשיב גמר חשבון)

**What it is for:** checking your own payslips against the Wage Protection Law and the protective labour laws (part A), and calculating what your employer owes you when employment ends (part B). Used in lessons 17.2, 17.3, 17.4 and 17.10 and the module 17 project.
**Who may use it:** you, for your own employment. Preparing calculations and claims for others as an occupation or for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where it goes:** attach part B to your demand letter ([letter to employer](letter-to-employer-wage-claim.md)) and, if needed, to a labour-court claim.
**Numbers:** minimum wage, recuperation rate, pension rates and the expedited-hearing ceiling change; take them from `references/current-numbers.md` and write the date you checked them.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them.
