# IP asset inventory (מיפוי נכסי קניין רוחני)

**What it is for:** listing your own creations and business assets, deciding which Israeli regime can protect each one, who owns it, and the next action. Used in lesson 32.1, the practice labs 32.8 and 32.9, and the module 32 project.
**Who may use it:** you, for your own works and your own business. Advising others on their IP as an occupation or for pay is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20) and, before the Patent Office, to patent attorneys (Patents Law s. 154).
**Where:** nothing is filed; keep it with your IP evidence file.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before relying on it.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them.
