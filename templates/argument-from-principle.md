# Argument from principle (טיעון מן העקרונות)

**What it is for:** a short written argument, in your own matter, that a rule should be applied in light of a legal principle (good faith, equality, dignity, free expression, the rule's own purpose). Used in lessons 09.3, 09.5 and 09.6.
**Who uses it:** you, as a party acting for yourself. It can become a section of a letter, a statement of claim or defence, or a written summation (סיכומים) when the court allows one.
**Where:** any court, tribunal or authority in which you are a party. Drafting it for someone else, for pay or as an occupation, is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law, s. 20).
**Disclaimer:** this template is for your own use only and is not legal advice. Check the current law before you rely on it.
