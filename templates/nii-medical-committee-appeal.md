# Appeal to a National Insurance medical appeals committee, and outline of an appeal on law (ערר לוועדה רפואית לעררים / ערעור בשאלה משפטית)

**What it is for:** part A appeals a medical disability decision (authorised doctor or first medical committee) to the NII's medical appeals committee; part B outlines an appeal on a point of law from an appeals committee's decision to the regional labour court. Used in lessons 17.9 and 17.11 and the module 17 project.
**Who may file it:** the insured person in their own name. Before the NII you may be represented by a lawyer, a close friend (ידיד קרוב), a union representative or others listed in NII Law s. 386; before the medical appeals committee in general disability, by your treating doctor or a specialist (Disability Regulations reg. 22). A representative may not sign the claim, waive or settle it (Representation Regulations reg. 3). Drafting for others as an occupation or for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).
**Where:** part A in writing through the local NII branch or online in the NII personal area; part B to the regional labour court for your place of residence. Free Legal Aid is available in NII cases without a means test (NII Law s. 397).
**Deadlines:** part A within 30 days of receiving the decision (general disability reg. 20; work injury reg. 27, with grounds allowed up to 30 more days, reg. 28); a further 30 days only with the NII's consent. Part B within 60 days of receiving the appeals committee's decision (Appeal Time Regulations reg. 2), on law only. Check `references/current-numbers.md`.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them.
