# Enduring power of attorney: preparation worksheet (ייפוי כוח מתמשך – דף הכנה)

**What it is for:** deciding, before you meet the lawyer, what your enduring power of attorney (ייפוי כוח מתמשך) and advance directives (הנחיות מקדימות) should say, under chapter 2-1 of the Capacity and Guardianship Law 1962 (Amendment 18, 2016). Used in lesson 26.7 and the module 26 project. It is not the power of attorney: the official form is prepared online and signed before a lawyer trained by the Administrator General (s. 32יד(א); EPA Regulations 2017 regs. 2, 19).
**Who may use it:** you, an adult who is able to understand the meaning of giving the power (s. 32ב), for your own affairs. A medical-only power may instead be signed before a doctor, social worker, psychologist or registered nurse (s. 32טו). Preparing a power for another person is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).
**Where:** a lawyer from the Administrator General's public database of trained lawyers (EPA Regulations reg. 19(ד)); deposit with the Administrator General, online or in person, as a condition of entry into force (s. 32יז).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

Notes in English in «guillemets» are guidance; delete them.
