# Purchase-tax worksheet for a residential apartment (גיליון חישוב מס רכישה לדירת מגורים)

**What it is for:** preparing the buyer's self-assessment of purchase tax on a residential apartment: who is in the buyer unit, whether the purchase qualifies for the single-apartment scale, the slice-by-slice computation and the deadlines. A short final section checks the seller's side (land appreciation tax exemption). Used in lessons 25.6, 25.7 and 25.11 and the module 25 project.
**Who may use it:** you, for your own purchase or sale. The buyer's and seller's declarations are usually filed by the parties' lawyers; you may file your own. Filing or drafting for the other party is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).
**Where:** the regional Real Estate Taxation office, or the online declaration service (check what is available to unrepresented parties on gov.il).
**Deadlines:** buyer's declaration within 30 days of the purchase (Real Estate Taxation Law s. 73(ג)); payment within 60 days of the sale (s. 90א); objection within 30 days of an assessment (s. 87); appeals committee within 30 days of the decision (s. 88). Bracket amounts change: see `references/current-numbers.md` (the 2025–2027 amounts are frozen by s. 9(ג2)).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.
