# Insurance claim letter (דרישה לתשלום תגמולי ביטוח)

**What it is for:** making a written claim to an insurer, either your own (home contents, comprehensive car, building) or the wrongdoer's liability insurer as an injured third party (Insurance Contract Law 1981, s. 68), so that the insurer's duties to investigate and pay start running. Used in lesson 04.8 and the module 4 project.
**Who may send it:** the insured or the beneficiary, or an injured third party, each in their own name. Handling claims for others for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20); licensed insurance agents act under their own law.
**Where:** the insurer's claims department, by its online claims form or email, and for important claims also by registered mail. Keep the claim number.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before sending.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them. Use paragraph A for your own policy or paragraph B for a third-party claim. Bodily injury in a road accident is claimed from the compulsory insurer under the 1975 Law (lesson 04.6); add a demand for interim payments under s. 5 of that Law.
