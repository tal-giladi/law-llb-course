# Appeal outline (מתווה ערעור אזרחי)

**What it is for:** planning and drafting a civil appeal, or an application for leave to appeal, under the Courts Law ss. 41 and 52 and chapter 18 of the Civil Procedure Regulations 2018: the route check, the dates and money, and the heading and body of reg. 134. Used in lessons 15.8 and 15.11. For leave to appeal a small-claims judgment see lesson 12.7.
**Who may file it:** you, in your own name, against a decision in your own case (reg. 170). Drafting appeals for another person is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** the district court (from a magistrates' court) or the Supreme Court (from a district court); unrepresented appellants file at the appeal court's registry (⚠️ check the Judiciary's appeal service page). Fee (Courts (Fees) Regulations, schedule) and security for costs (Third Schedule to the Regulations): [current numbers](../references/current-numbers.md).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them.

**Checklist before filing**

- [ ] Route checked against s. 52 / s. 41, the 2009 order and reg. 150.
- [ ] Filed within 60 days of service (or of the set-aside decision).
- [ ] Fee paid and security deposited, with notice to the respondent (reg. 135).
- [ ] Every ground is reviewable and has a record reference.
- [ ] Body within the page limit; only permitted attachments.
- [ ] Stay of execution requested if needed (reg. 145).
