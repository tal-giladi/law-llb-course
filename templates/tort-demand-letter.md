# Tort demand letter (מכתב דרישה לפיצוי בגין נזק)

**What it is for:** demanding compensation, in writing, from a person or company that caused you damage outside a contract (a neighbour's leak, a car scratched in a car park, a fall in a shop, an object dropped from a building site), with a dated deadline, and creating a record for the insurer and for the court. Used in lesson 04.8, lesson 04.9 and the module 4 project.
**Who may send it:** you, in your own name, about damage you suffered. Drafting such letters for another person as an occupation or for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20(3)).
**Where:** the wrongdoer's home or business address; for a company, also its registered office; a copy to its liability insurer if known (Insurance Contract Law s. 68). Send by registered mail with proof of delivery and by email.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before sending.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them. Claim only items you can prove; attach every document you rely on. For bodily injury in a road accident, do not use this letter: claim from the compulsory insurer (lesson 04.6).
