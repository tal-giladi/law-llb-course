# Objection to a deposited plan (התנגדות לתכנית שהופקדה)

**What it is for:** objecting to a local outline plan, detailed plan or district plan that has been deposited for public objections under the Planning and Building Law 1965, ss. 100–107. Used in lessons 27.3 and 27.9 and the module 27 project.
**Who may file it:** anyone with an interest in land, a building or "any other planning detail" who is harmed by the plan (s. 100), in their own name; neighbours may object jointly. Drafting objections for others as an occupation or for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** the institution named in the deposit notice (s. 103): for a plan in local power, the local committee with a copy to the district committee; for a plan in district power, the district committee with a copy to the local committee; for a district plan, the National Council with a copy to the district committee. Four copies (Objections Regulations reg. 3), or the Planning Administration's online objection service where offered (check). Keep proof of delivery of the copy (reg. 4).
**Deadline:** two months from the later of the newspaper deposit notices, unless the institution extended it (s. 102). See `references/current-numbers.md`.
**Form:** written, with detailed reasons, and an affidavit verifying the facts (s. 103א); state your interest, the harm and an address for service (reg. 3). At the hearing you may argue only what you wrote (reg. 14).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them.
