# Capstone retrospective and next steps (סקירה לאחור ותוכנית המשך לתיק הגמר)

**What it is for:** closing your capstone matter and learning from it: the closing check, a one-page closing memo, a calibration of your predictions against the outcome, what worked and what went wrong, the lessons to revisit, and a plan for the matters you parked and for a yearly check-up of your own legal affairs. Used in lesson 39.6 and the module 39 project.
**Who may use it:** you, for your own matter. Reviewing other people's matters and advising them on what to do next, as an occupation or for payment, is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** the last pages of your case file (templates/clinic-case-file.md), on top of the archived folder. It is not filed or sent to anyone.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules and periods (references/current-numbers.md) before you enforce, appeal or file anything.

Notes in English in square brackets are guidance; delete them.
