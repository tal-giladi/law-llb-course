# Labour-court statement of claim, expedited track (כתב תביעה לבית הדין האזורי לעבודה – דיון מהיר)

**What it is for:** an employee's own claim against an employer (or former employer) for wages, final-settlement items and severance, in the regional labour court, on the expedited track for claims up to the ceiling (Labour Court Law s. 31). It follows the particulars required by the Labour Court (Procedure) Regulations 1991, reg. 9, and the headings of the official wage and severance claim forms (Forms 2 and 3, reg. 13). Used in lessons 22.2, 22.7 and the module 22 project.
**Who may file it:** you, as the employee, in your own name. Besides a lawyer, only a person appointed by a workers' organisation you are a member of may represent you (Labour Court Law s. 34). Drafting claims for others as an occupation or for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** the regional labour court for the place where you worked or where the work was done (reg. 3(א)(1)). By hand at the court secretariat (reg. 8: three copies for the court and one more per defendant; the Judiciary's service page currently asks for two copies plus one per defendant when filing by hand, ⚠️ check on the day), or online through the Judiciary's labour-court claim service for unrepresented employees, which drafts and files claims up to the expedited ceiling (⚠️ manual check of the current service and login).
**Fee:** half the regular fee, with a minimum, on the expedited track (Labour Court Fees Regulations reg. 6); proceedings under the Wage Protection Law, the Minimum Wage Law and some other statutes are fee-exempt (reg. 4). Amounts: see current numbers.
**Deadlines:** wages and severance follow the ordinary limitation period; late-wage compensation must be claimed within 60 days of receiving the late wage, or one year from the wage becoming late, whichever is earlier (Wage Protection Law s. 17א). Annual-leave redemption and other items have their own rules: check lesson 17.3.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules and the current official form before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them before filing. Keep the body short: facts in numbered paragraphs, one fact per paragraph, each pointing to its annex.

## Filing checklist (delete before filing)

- [ ] ID number at the top of the claim (reg. 8א), my home address as address for service (reg. 10(1)).
- [ ] The employer's exact legal name and number, checked in the Companies Registrar on the day.
- [ ] Every fact paragraph points to an annex; every amount has a basis and a calculation.
- [ ] The total, without late-wage compensation, linkage or interest, is within the expedited ceiling (current numbers); if not, decide whether to waive the excess (reg. 26(א)) or file on the ordinary track.
- [ ] Copies: the court's copies plus one per defendant (reg. 8; check the secretariat's current count).
- [ ] Fee paid or exemption noted; proof of the prior demand attached.
- [ ] Deadline table updated: the employer's defence period, any hearing date, any 60-day late-wage limit.
