# Decision worksheet: should you sue? (גיליון החלטה: האם לתבוע?)

**What it is for:** deciding, with numbers, whether to sue, settle or drop a money dispute of your own, and setting a walk-away figure before you negotiate. Used in lesson 11.5 and in the module 11 project.
**Who uses it:** you, for your own dispute only. It is a working document; it is not filed with any court.
**Where:** keep it in your case file, next to the documents each number relies on.
**Disclaimer:** this template is for your own use only and is not legal advice. Check current fees, ceilings and rules (references/current-numbers.md and the Judiciary's site) before filing.

Fill in the Hebrew headings. Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them. Every number needs a source in the last column: a document, a regulation, the current-numbers page, the Judiciary's annual report, or "assumption".
