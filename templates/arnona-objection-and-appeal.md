# Arnona objection and appeal (השגה למנהל הארנונה וערר לוועדת הערר)

**What it is for:** challenging an arnona (municipal property tax) charge on the grounds the Arnona Appeals Law allows: wrong zone, an error in the property's type, size or use, not being the holder, or (for a business) not being the controlling owner (Local Authorities (Appeal on Arnona) Law 1976, s. 3(א)). Part A is the objection to the arnona director; Part B is the appeal to the arnona appeals committee. Used in lessons 27.8 and 27.10 and the module 27 project.
**Who may file it:** the person charged, in their own name. Discount requests and claims that the arnona order itself is unlawful are not decided in this route (s. 3(ב)): use a discount application or a prior demand and petition. Paid representation by non-lawyers is barred (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** Part A to the arnona director of the local authority (address on the payment notice; many authorities accept online or email filing). Part B to the arnona appeals committee of the same authority, in four copies, delivered or sent by registered mail (Procedure Regulations 1977, reg. 3).
**Deadlines:** objection within 90 days of receiving the payment notice (s. 3(א)); the director answers within 60 days, failing which the objection is deemed accepted unless the appeals committee extended the period by up to 30 days (s. 4); appeal within 30 days of receiving the director's answer (s. 6(א)); administrative appeal to the Administrative Affairs Court from the committee's decision (s. 6(ב)), within 60 days (Administrative Affairs Courts Procedure Regulations reg. 23(ב), as amended 2022). See `references/current-numbers.md`.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them. Keep paying the undisputed part.
