# Seminar research plan (תוכנית מחקר לעבודה סמינריונית)

**What it is for:** planning a 30-page seminar paper that makes an original contribution: the gap, the question, the method, the primary sources, the risks and a 12-week timeline. Used in lessons 36.1, 36.2 and 36.6 and the module 36 project. For the proposal, literature map and source list, keep using the [proseminar paper outline](proseminar-paper-outline.md) (parts A, B and D).
**Who uses it:** you, for your own academic work. It is not a court document.
**Where:** part A goes to your seminar teacher before the first meeting; parts B and C are your working file.
**Disclaimer:** this template is a study aid, not legal advice. Your seminar's own instructions and your faculty's regulations (length, deadlines, presentation, citation style, AI policy) take precedence.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them. Placeholders are marked with ____ or «…».
