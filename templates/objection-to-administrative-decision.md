# Objection or request for reconsideration (השגה / בקשה לעיון מחדש בהחלטה מינהלית)

**What it is for:** asking a public authority to change a decision that affects you: by a statutory objection where a law provides one (for example an arnona objection to the arnona director, or an objection under the law that governs the decision), or otherwise by a request for reconsideration to the decision-maker, which also serves as the prior demand (פנייה מוקדמת) required before an administrative petition (procedure regs, reg. 5(ב)(6)). Used in lessons 14.10 and 14.11 and the module 14 project.
**Who may file it:** you, in your own name, about your own matter. Drafting objections for others as an occupation or for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** the body and official named in the decision's notice of objection rights (Decisions and Reasons Law s. 5) or in the governing law; otherwise the decision-maker, with a copy to the authority's legal adviser. Send by email and registered mail, or through the authority's online form, and keep proof.
**Deadlines:** a statutory objection has its own period (for arnona, see `references/current-numbers.md`); a request for reconsideration should be sent at once, because the administrative petition period runs from the decision, not from the end of correspondence (reg. 3). Statutory objections cover only the grounds their law lists; read them first.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

**After sending:** diary the authority's answer date (Decisions and Reasons Law s. 2: 45 days; three months' silence is a refusal, s. 6(ב)), any statutory answer period (for arnona, see current numbers), and, separately, the last day for an administrative appeal or petition. Do not let the correspondence run past the petition period.
