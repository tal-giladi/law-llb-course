# Demand letter to a company (מכתב דרישה לחברה)

**What it is for:** setting out, formally and in writing, what a company owes you (a refund, a repair, a correction, a service) and by when, and creating a record for a regulator or a small-claims court. Used in lesson 02.7 and the module 2 project; module 8 covers the pre-action letter before a lawsuit.
**Who may send it:** you, in your own name, about your own matter. Drafting such letters for another person as an occupation or for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20(3)).
**Where:** the customer-service address in your contract or invoice, and for important demands also registered mail with proof of delivery to the company's registered office.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before sending.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them. Cite only legal grounds your research memo supports.
