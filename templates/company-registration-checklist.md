# Company registration checklist (רישום חברה – רשימת בדיקה)

**What it is for:** preparing every detail of an application to register a private company with the Companies Registrar (Companies Law 1999, ss. 8–35, 123–123א), the decisions on the articles, and the first 30 days after registration. It is a checklist, not the official form: use the Ministry of Justice's current online registration service. Used in lessons 16.2 and 16.8 and the module 16 project.
**Who may file it:** a first shareholder or a lawyer may file the online application (Companies (Reporting, Registration Details and Forms) Regulations reg. 16ב(א)); with several founders each must identify electronically and approve online (reg. 16ב(ב)); a sole shareholder who is also the sole director approves everything himself (reg. 16ב(ג)). Documents on foreign residents or foreign companies must be filed online by a lawyer (reg. 16א(ד)). Registering companies for others as an occupation is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).
**Where:** the Ministry of Justice, Corporations Authority, online company registration (https://www.gov.il/he/service/company_registration; check the current service and fee).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them.
