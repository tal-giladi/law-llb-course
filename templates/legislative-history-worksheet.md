# Legislative-history worksheet (דף עבודה להיסטוריה חקיקתית)

**What it is for:** rebuilding the legislative history of a law or one section from free sources (Wikisource, the Knesset's open OData database, Reshumot), recording every document with an address and pinpoint, and weighing what it shows about purpose. Used in lessons 36.3 and 36.6 and the module 36 project; also useful for a purposive-interpretation paragraph in your own case.
**Who uses it:** you, for your own research or your own case. It is a working file, not a court document.
**Where:** keep it with your research file. Pages you rely on may be attached to a filing as exhibits if the court's rules allow.
**Disclaimer:** this worksheet is a study aid, not legal advice. Quote the wording from Reshumot, which prevails over every other version.

Placeholders are marked with ____ or «…». Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them.
