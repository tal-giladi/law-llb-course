# Ethics complaint to the Bar (תלונה לוועדת האתיקה של לשכת עורכי הדין)

**What it is for:** preparing a factual complaint about a lawyer's conduct to the ethics committee of the Israel Bar district where the lawyer is registered. Used in lessons 35.6 and 35.8 and the module 35 project.
**Who may file it:** any person, in their own name (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 63). Drafting complaints for others as an occupation or for pay is reserved to lawyers (s. 20).
**Where:** the district ethics committee, on the Bar's official complaint form (Form 1 under rule 2 of the Bar Association Rules (Procedure in Disciplinary Tribunals) 2015). This draft prepares the content; copy it into the official form, which is on the Bar's website and at its offices (check the current form and address before filing).
**Disclaimer:** this is a self-help aid, not legal advice. A complaint is sent to the lawyer for a reply. A baseless or vexatious complaint can lead to a costs order against the complainant (s. 69(א)(5)). A complaint does not stop limitation periods for a civil claim and is not a substitute for one.

### Before you send (English checklist)

- [ ] Facts and dates only; no insults, guesses or conclusions about motives.
- [ ] Every fact that matters has a document in the appendices.
- [ ] You wrote to the lawyer first and attached the letter and any reply.
- [ ] You checked the district where the lawyer is registered (s. 64).
- [ ] You noted your separate deadlines (a fee claim, a malpractice claim, the underlying case) and are not waiting for the complaint to protect them.
- [ ] You copied the content into the Bar's official Form 1 and kept a copy and proof of sending.
