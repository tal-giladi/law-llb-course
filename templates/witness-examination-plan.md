# Witness examination plan (תכנית חקירת עדים)

**What it is for:** planning the evidence hearing in your own civil case: the order of witnesses, short questions for your own witness, cross-examination sheets for the other side's witnesses and experts, the objections you may need to make, re-examination, and a summation table that ties each element to the evidence. Used in lesson 23.10 and the module 23 project; for small claims, use it together with the [hearing-day kit](hearing-day-kit.md).
**Who may use it:** you, as a party in your own case (CPR reg. 170). You may examine and cross-examine witnesses yourself; no friend or relative may do it for you. It is a working paper; you do not file it.
**Where:** the courtroom on the date of the evidence hearing, or by video if the court so orders (CPR reg. 72).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules and the court's decisions in your case before the hearing.

Notes in English in square brackets are guidance; delete them.

## ה. התנגדויות – משפטים מוכנים

[State the ground at once; the other side answers at once; the court rules (CPR reg. 68).]

|---|---|
