# Welfare and housing benefit review request (בקשה לעיון מחדש / ערעור / ערר בענייני הבטחת הכנסה, סיוע בשכר דירה ושירותי רווחה)

**What it is for:** challenging a refusal or reduction of a means-tested benefit, in four parts: (A) a request to the National Insurance Institute to reconsider an income-support decision; (B) an outline of a claim against the NII in the regional labour court about income support; (C) an appeal to the Ministry of Construction and Housing about rent assistance; (D) an appeal (ערר) to the welfare appeals committee against a municipal social services department's decision or failure to decide. Used in lesson 22.6 and the module 22 project.
**Who may file it:** you, in your own name, about your own household's benefit. Before the NII a close friend, a union representative and others may represent you (NII Law s. 386, applied by Income Support Law s. 23); before the welfare appeals committee you may appear yourself or through a representative (Welfare Services (Appeals Committees Procedure) Regulations reg. 4). Drafting such requests for others as an occupation or for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** (A) the NII branch that decided, or the NII personal area online; (B) the regional labour court for your residence, fee-exempt (Labour Court Fees Regulations reg. 4(22)); (C) through the company that handles rent-assistance applications for the Ministry, or the Ministry's online service (⚠️ manual check of the current route and form); (D) in writing to the welfare appeals committee for the area of your local authority (reg. 3(א)); ask the department for its address.
**Deadlines:** (B) within 12 months of notice of the NII decision (Time for Claims Regulations reg. 1, applied by Income Support Law s. 23); do not let a reconsideration request (A) run past that date. (C) the Ministry's appeal period is stated in the decision letter (⚠️ not confirmed; one secondary source says 45 days). (D) the law sets no period; appeal promptly. A public body must answer a written request within 45 days or explain why not (Decisions and Reasons Law s. 2); silence for three months is treated as a refusal without reasons (s. 6(ב)).
**Free help:** Legal Aid represents individuals against the NII in the labour court without a means test (NII Law s. 397); the social services department must help you realise your rights (Welfare Services Law s. 2ב).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them. Attach the decision you are challenging and every document you rely on, and keep proof of sending.
