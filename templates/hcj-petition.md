# Petition to the High Court of Justice (עתירה לבית המשפט הגבוה לצדק)

**What it is for:** asking the Supreme Court, sitting as the High Court of Justice, to issue an order nisi (צו על תנאי) against a public authority whose decision harms you, and if needed an interim order (צו ביניים). Built on the HCJ Procedure Regulations 1984: reg. 2 (content and form, Form 1), reg. 3 (address for service), reg. 4 (supporting affidavit, Form 2), reg. 19 (interim orders). Used in lesson 06.8 and the module 6 project.
**Who may file it:** you, in your own name, about a decision that affects you. A parent may act for a minor child as the child's guardian. Representing another person, or drafting a petition for someone else as an occupation or for pay, is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** the Supreme Court registry in Jerusalem, or by the electronic channel the Judiciary currently offers to unrepresented parties (check before filing). Many decisions of local authorities and some ministries belong instead to the Administrative Affairs Court (Administrative Affairs Courts Law 2000, s. 5 and first schedule): check first.
**Fees and deadlines:** court fee and exemption: see `references/current-numbers.md` and Courts (Fees) Regulations reg. 14. There is no fixed filing deadline, but delay (שיהוי) can defeat a petition; file promptly after the authority's final answer.
**Before filing:** send the authority a written demand and wait a reasonable time for an answer (template: `letter-to-authority.md`). Attach both.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them. Write on one side of the page with margins of at least 5 cm (reg. 2). Number paragraphs and attachments.

## עתירה למתן צו על תנאי [ובקשה למתן צו ביניים]

מוגשת בזאת עתירה למתן צו על תנאי המופנה אל המשיבים ומורה להם לבוא וליתן טעם:

א. מדוע לא תבוטל החלטתם מיום [___] בדבר [___];
ב. [מדוע לא יחליטו מחדש בבקשת העותר תוך ___ ימים, לאחר שימוע / בהתחשב ב־___];
[Ask for specific, achievable relief. Prefer a narrow order: cancel and reconsider.]

[Optional:] **בקשה לצו ביניים:** בית המשפט הנכבד מתבקש להורות למשיבים להימנע מ־[ביצוע ההחלטה] עד להכרעה בעתירה, משום ש־[נזק בלתי הפיך; מאזן הנוחות; סיכויי העתירה – one short paragraph each].
