# Request to cancel a police-register entry and to change a closure ground (בקשה לביטול פרט רישום משטרתי / בקשה לשינוי עילת סגירת תיק)

**What it is for:** clearing a closed criminal file from your police record. Part א asks the police to cancel a police-register entry about a closed file (Criminal Information and Rehabilitation of Offenders Law 2019, s. 33). Part ב asks the prosecutor who closed the file to change the closure ground, typically to lack of guilt, which removes the entry (Criminal Procedure Law [Consolidated] 1982, s. 62(ב)(4)). Used in lessons 24.8 and 24.10 and the module 24 project.
**Who may file it:** the person the entry is about, in his own name. Filing for others as an occupation or for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** part א to the Israel Police Investigations and Intelligence Department (ראש אגף החקירות והמודיעין) by the address or online form on the police website (⚠️ check the current channel and any official form; police.gov.il and gov.il block automated checks). Part ב to the prosecutor named in the closure notice (police prosecution unit or district attorney's office).
**Before you write:** inspect your information at a police station (s. 4) and copy the file number, date of event, offence and closure ground. A non-felony closed file should be cancelled automatically seven years after the event (five for minors) (s. 33(ג)); if it was not, ask why. Convictions and court orders are not cancelled by this request (they lapse by time, ss. 18–23). A stayed file needs the Attorney General's consent (s. 33(א)).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them.
