# Administrative petition and affidavit (עתירה מינהלית ותצהיר)

**What it is for:** a petition to a district court sitting as Administrative Affairs Court against a decision (including an omission) of an authority on a matter listed in the First Schedule of the Administrative Affairs Courts Law 2000, under the Administrative Affairs Courts (Procedure) Regulations 2000. It follows the items of reg. 5(ב); the official Form 1 (petition) and Form 2 (affidavit) are in the regulations' schedule and on the Judiciary's site (⚠️ manual check: Wikisource does not reproduce the forms' text). Used in lessons 14.9 and 14.11 and the module 14 project.
**Who may file it:** you, in your own name: reg. 44א applies chapter 20 of the Civil Procedure Regulations 2018, including reg. 170 (a party may act "בעצמו או בידי עורך דין"), and reg. 5(ב)(1) asks for a lawyer's details only if you are represented. Only a lawyer may represent another person (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20). A company generally needs a lawyer (unverified in a free source).
**Where:** the district court for the district where the decision was made, or where the land is (reg. 2); at the registry, or online where available to unrepresented parties (⚠️ manual check).
**Deadlines and fees:** the petition period (reg. 3, unless another law sets one) runs from the earliest of publication, notice or knowledge; a court may dismiss for delay even within it (reg. 4). Fee: Courts (Fees) Regulations, schedule item 20, or a fee exemption. Service within days of filing (reg. 35). All in `references/current-numbers.md`.
**Before you use it:** check that no statutory objection or appeal applies (lesson 14.10), that the matter is in the First Schedule and not excluded (for example, decisions of the Government or a minister often stay in the HCJ), and that you sent a prior demand.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules and the official forms before filing.

**After filing:** serve the petition on each respondent within the reg. 35(א) period and file proof of service; until then the court will not consider the petition (reg. 7(ב)). Diary: preliminary response or answer as ordered (reg. 10), your further-particulars request (reg. 11), skeleton arguments (reg. 14), a request to cross-examine (reg. 15(ב)), and appeal periods (reg. 33). Periods in `references/current-numbers.md`.
