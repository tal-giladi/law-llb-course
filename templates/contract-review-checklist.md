# Contract review checklist (רשימת בדיקה לחוזה)

**What it is for:** reviewing a residential lease or a service contract before you sign it (or one you signed, as a check), clause by clause, and turning the problems into a short negotiation letter. Used in lessons 03.6 and 03.9 and in the module 3 project.

**Who may use it:** you, for a contract you are a party to. Reviewing contracts for others as an occupation or for pay is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).

**Where:** keep it with the contract in your case file. It is not filed anywhere.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

## How to use it

Go through sections A–E. For every problem write the clause number, the section of law, a classification (V = void / unenforceable, P = presumed unfair, N = negotiable, OK) and a priority (must / try / accept). Then write the letter in section F.

## A. Formation and form

- [ ] Parties: full names, ID or company number; the landlord or supplier is the owner or authorised to contract (land-registry extract or authorisation).
- [ ] Date of the contract, start and end dates; is it a renewal (relevant to s. 25 of the Contracts Law after 7.1.2026)?
- [ ] Writing and signatures; a signed copy for each party (residential lease: Rental and Borrowing Law s. 25ב).
- [ ] Attachments: inventory, condition report with dated photos, plans or specifications.
- [ ] Any interpretation clause ("entire agreement", "read by its language"): note it.

## B. Mandatory rules (cannot be waived against you)

Residential lease (Rental and Borrowing Law, ss. 25א–25טו; most protected by s. 25יד):

- [ ] Flat fit and habitable (ss. 25ה–25ו); no waiver of condition claims.
- [ ] Repairs: tenant pays only for damage from unreasonable use; landlord repairs others within the statutory periods (s. 25ח).
- [ ] Payments: no building insurance, permanent systems or landlord's broker fee charged to the tenant (s. 25ט).
- [ ] Security: total within the statutory cap; uses limited; prior notice; return period (s. 25י).
- [ ] Options to extend: notice periods (s. 25יב).
- [ ] Landlord's cancellation without breach: void unless mutual; notice periods (s. 25יג).
- [ ] Exclusions: hotel, holiday let, dorm, sheltered housing, protected tenancy (s. 25טו).

Any standard contract (Standard Contracts Law s. 5):

- [ ] No term barring access to the courts.
- [ ] No exemption from liability for bodily injury or wilful acts.

Consumer contracts (Consumer Protection Law):

- [ ] Cancellation rights not excluded (distance sale, door-to-door, regulations).
- [ ] Nothing misleading in the contract or the sales pitch (s. 2).

## C. Breach and remedies clauses

- [ ] "Every breach is fundamental": valid only if reasonable (Remedies Law s. 6); ask to list specific breaches.
- [ ] Agreed damages: reasonable relation to foreseeable loss (s. 15); per-day penalties.
- [ ] Deposit forfeiture: counts as agreed damages (s. 15(ג)).
- [ ] Notice method for breach and cancellation (s. 21): realistic addresses for both sides.

## D. Standard terms presumed unfair (Standard Contracts Law s. 4)

- [ ] (1) Exemption or unreasonable limitation of the supplier's liability.
- [ ] (2) Supplier's unreasonable right to cancel, suspend, delay or change its main obligations.
- [ ] (4) Supplier may change price or your main obligations at its sole discretion.
- [ ] (5) Ties you to the supplier or limits your freedom to deal with others unreasonably.
- [ ] (6) Limits your rights or remedies, or sets unreasonable notice requirements.
- [ ] (6א) Unreasonable remedies or agreed damages for the supplier.
- [ ] (7) Shifts the burden of proof to you.
- [ ] (8), (10) Forced arbitration, or arbitration on the supplier's terms.
- [ ] (9) Forum chosen against the law or by the supplier alone.
- [ ] (11) One-way index linkage.
- [ ] (12) "The customer confirms he read the contract" or other declarations.
- [ ] Not covered: a plainly worded price; terms copying a statute (s. 23).

## E. Commercial terms (negotiable)

- [ ] Price, increases, linkage.
- [ ] Term, option, early exit for you.
- [ ] Pets, subletting, renovations, guests (lease); scope, schedule, payment milestones, warranty (services).
- [ ] Who pays which ongoing costs.

## F. Negotiation letter

```text
תאריך: [__.__.____]

לכבוד
[שם המשכיר / הספק]

הנדון: הערות לטיוטת [חוזה השכירות / ההסכם] ל[נכס / שירות]

תודה על הטיוטה. אשמח לחתום לאחר התיקונים הבאים:

1. סעיף [__] – [הבעיה בקצרה]. [הסעיף סותר את סעיף __ לחוק __ / חזקה שהוא מקפח לפי סעיף 4(__) לחוק החוזים האחידים]. נוסח מוצע: "[____]".
2. סעיף [__] – ...
3. ...

[אם רלוונטי:] סעיפים [__] סותרים הוראות של חוק השכירות והשאילה שאין להתנות עליהן לרעת השוכר (סעיף 25יד לחוק), ולכן ממילא אינם ניתנים לאכיפה; מוטב שהחוזה ישקף את הדין.

אשמח לקבל טיוטה מתוקנת.
בברכה,
[שם]   [טלפון]
```
