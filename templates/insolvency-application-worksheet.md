# Individual insolvency application worksheet (דף עבודה לבקשת יחיד לצו לפתיחת הליכים)

**What it is for:** preparing, as a dry run or before filing in your own name, an individual's application to open insolvency proceedings under the Insolvency and Economic Rehabilitation Law 2018: the insolvency test and the choice of door (part A), the creditors list (part B), the two-year report and account of the debts (part C), a forecast of the rehabilitation order (part D) and the conduct check (part E). It sits next to the official forms of the Insolvency Commissioner; the application itself is filed on those forms. Used in lessons 28.5, 28.6 and 28.9 and the module 28 project.
**Who may file:** the individual in his own name (Insolvency Law s. 102(1)); Legal Aid represents eligible applicants. Preparing or filing an application for another person, even a family member, is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** debts above the linked threshold: the Insolvency Commissioner, online or at the district office (s. 104(ד)); at or below it: a notice of inability to pay in an execution file (Execution Law s. 7א2) or an application to a branch with an insolvency track (Insolvency Law ss. 187, 189). Thresholds and fees: current numbers.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules, forms, thresholds and fees before filing.
