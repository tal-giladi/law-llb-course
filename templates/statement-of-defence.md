# Statement of defence (כתב הגנה – בית משפט השלום / המחוזי)

**What it is for:** the defence to a civil claim under the Civil Procedure Regulations 2018: heading (reg. 12), summary in the order of reg. 13 (preliminary arguments, answer to each cause of action, arguments on the remedy) and a detailed statement that denies expressly and in detail every fact you dispute (reg. 14(ב)). Includes optional sections for a counterclaim (reg. 21) and a third-party notice (regs 22–23). Used in lessons 15.2, 15.9, 15.10 and the module 15 project.
**Who may file it:** you, in your own name, if you were sued personally (reg. 170). A company generally files through a lawyer. Drafting a defence for another person is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** the court where the claim was filed, within the defence period from service (reg. 9(ב); fast track reg. 80(א); eviction reg. 81(ב)); periods in [current numbers](../references/current-numbers.md). A counterclaim and third-party notice are filed within the same period.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them. Page limits as for the claim (reg. 9(ד)). **Every fact in the claim you do not deny expressly and in detail in part three is treated as admitted (reg. 14(ב)).** Damages stay in dispute unless you admit them.

**Checklist before filing**

- [ ] Filed within the defence period (counted from service, recess excluded).
- [ ] Heading shows the last filing date (reg. 30).
- [ ] Every preliminary argument is in part two, item 1 (reg. 28); limitation pleaded now or lost (Prescription Law s. 3).
- [ ] Every paragraph of the claim is admitted or denied with your version.
- [ ] Counterclaim and third-party notice decided now.
- [ ] Exhibits attached; no insulting language (reg. 32).
