# Property-agreement checklist (הסכם ממון – רשימת בדיקה)

**What it is for:** reviewing a draft property agreement between spouses or future spouses (Spouses (Property Relations) Law 1973, ss. 1–2), or a cohabitation agreement, before signing, and preparing it for approval or certification. Used in lessons 18.3, 18.4 and 18.10 and the module 18 project.
**Who may use it:** you, for your own agreement. Drafting an agreement for others, or advising both spouses, is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20); each spouse should understand the agreement independently and may take separate advice.
**Where it goes:** approval by the Family Court or the religious court with jurisdiction over the couple's marriage and divorce (s. 2(א)); before or at the wedding, certification by the marriage registrar (s. 2(ג)); before the wedding, certification by a notary (s. 2(ג1)). A cohabitation agreement needs no approval but may be approved by the Family Court to give it the force of a judgment (Family Court Law s. 3(ג)).
**Fees:** see `references/current-numbers.md` (Family Court fee for approving an agreement between spouses with no claim pending).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before signing or filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them.
