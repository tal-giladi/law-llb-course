# Regulator complaint (פנייה לגורם מפקח)

**What it is for:** a complaint about a regulated business to the public body that supervises it: the Consumer Protection and Fair Trade Authority (general consumer matters), the Bank of Israel's Banking Supervision public-enquiries unit (banks and credit-card companies), the Capital Market, Insurance and Savings Authority (insurers, agents, pension and provident funds), or the Ministry of Communications (phone, internet, TV providers). Most regulators now use online forms; use this text to fill the form or attach it. Used in lesson 12.4 and the module 12 project.
**Who may file it:** the person affected, in their own name, free of charge. Drafting complaints for others as an occupation or for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** the regulator's online form (gov.il, or the Bank of Israel site for banking; national digital ID login for some); first complain to the business itself and attach its answer.
**Important:** a regulator's findings do not create new rights in court and do not extend limitation or any statutory deadline (for banking, Banking (Service to Customer) Law s. 16(ד)). The insurance regulator does not normally investigate a matter whose court hearing has begun (Insurance Supervision Law s. 60(ב)).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules and the regulator's current form before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them.
