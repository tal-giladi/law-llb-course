# Insurance rejection response (תשובה לדחיית תביעת ביטוח)

**What it is for:** answering an insurer that rejected, reduced or delayed a claim, point by point under the Insurance Contract Law 1981, before an internal appeal, a regulator complaint or a claim in court. It follows the first claim made on the insurance claim letter template. Used in lessons 20.6 and 20.10 and the module 20 project.
**Who may send it:** the insured or the beneficiary, in their own name. Handling claims for others for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20); licensed insurance agents act under their own law.
**Where:** the insurer's claims department, with a copy to the company's internal public-complaints officer (נציב/ת תלונות הציבור בחברה); later, if needed, the Capital Market, Insurance and Savings Authority public enquiries unit (check the current gov.il page). None of these stops limitation (s. 31).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them. Use only the paragraphs that answer the insurer's actual reasons. Payment periods and limitation: references/current-numbers.md.
