# Fee agreement checklist (בדיקת הסכם שכר טרחה)

**What it is for:** checking a lawyer's fee proposal or engagement agreement before you sign it, or reviewing one you signed when a dispute starts; and writing back to the lawyer with the changes you want. Used in lessons 35.4 and 35.8 and the module 35 project.
**Who may use it:** you, as the client, for your own agreement. Reviewing other people's agreements as an occupation or for pay is legal advice reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** keep it with the agreement; send part ג to the lawyer by email and keep proof of sending.
**Disclaimer:** this is a self-help aid, not legal advice. Statutory caps and tariffs change: check the current text of each law and the course's current-numbers page before relying on a figure.
