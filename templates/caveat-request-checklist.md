# Caveat request checklist (בקשה לרישום הערת אזהרה – רשימת בדיקה)

**What it is for:** preparing a request to register a caveat (הערת אזהרה) under s. 126 of the Land Law 1969 for your own purchase, a promise of a mortgage to you, or an undertaking to you not to sell, and planning its deletion. It is a checklist and a draft of the request's content, not the official form: use the current form or online service of the Land Registry. Used in lessons 13.4 and 13.9 and the module 13 project.
**Who may file it:** the obligor (the registered right-holder who promised) or the beneficiary (you), in your own name (Land Law s. 126(א)). The signature must be verified by a lawyer or by the registrar (Management and Registration Regulations reg. 21(א)). Filing for another person is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20(2)).
**Where:** the Land Registry office where the land is registered (in person, signing before the registrar, by appointment), or through a lawyer online; the Ministry of Justice caveat service at https://www.gov.il/he/service/caveat_filling (check the current rules for private persons).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them.
