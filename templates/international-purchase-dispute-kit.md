# International purchase dispute kit (ערכת מסמכים: סכסוך ברכישה מקוונת מחו"ל)

**What it is for:** getting your money back, or stopping further charges, when a foreign online seller does not deliver, sends something else, or charges you without authority. It contains a timeline, a cancellation and demand letter to the seller, a notice to your card issuer under the Payment Services Law 2019, and a checklist for a small claim with service abroad. Used in lessons 30.6 and 30.7 and the module 30 project.
**Who may send it:** you, in your own name, about your own purchase. Drafting such letters for others as an occupation is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** the seller's support e-mail and contact form; your card issuer's written disputes channel; for a claim, the small claims court of your area.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before sending or filing. The Debit Cards Law 1986 was repealed on 14.10.2020; do not cite it.

## 2. Letter to the seller (English, with the legal basis)

```text
To: ____________ (seller), by e-mail to ____________ and via the contact form
Date: ________
Subject: Cancellation and refund demand – order no. ________

1. On ________ I ordered ____________ for ________ (order confirmation attached).
2. Delivery was promised by ________. [It has not arrived / What arrived is ____________ (photos attached) / I did not authorise the charge of ________ on ________.]
3. I hereby cancel the transaction (Israeli Consumer Protection Law 1981, ss. 14ג and 14ה; חוק הגנת הצרכן).
4. Please refund ________ to my card within 14 days of this notice. If no refund is made, I will pursue my rights with my card issuer, the Israeli Consumer Protection and Fair Trade Authority and the Israeli courts, which have jurisdiction over this consumer contract notwithstanding any foreign-court clause in your terms.

Name: ____________   E-mail: ____________   Phone: ____________
```

## 3. Notice to the card issuer (Hebrew)

Use the template in lesson 30.6 ("הודעה לפי סעיף 17 לחוק שירותי תשלום"). Checklist before sending:

- [ ] The agreed delivery date has passed (or the seller has stopped supplying for good).
- [ ] I sent the seller a cancellation notice (letter 2) and attach it.
- [ ] I name the section: 17 (not supplied), 29 (charge without a document, within 30 days of the statement), or 27 (misuse).
- [ ] I ask separately for a network chargeback for any amount already debited.
- [ ] I ask for a reasoned written notice before any re-charge (s. 17(ב)).

## 5. Small claim with service abroad (checklist)

- [ ] Defendant: the seller (and, if the dispute is about the issuer's duty, the Israeli issuer).
- [ ] Court: small claims court of your area; claim within the ceiling (see current numbers).
- [ ] Statement of claim (template "small claim: contract or consumer"), up to the page limit.
- [ ] Request for service abroad (lesson 30.2 template): reg. 166 ground (usually 166(4)(א) or 166(5)), affidavit with the three items of reg. 167(א), proposed method (e-mail address the seller uses; registered post; Hague Convention).
- [ ] A paragraph on any foreign-court clause: standard-contract term presumed depriving (Standard Contracts Law ss. 4(9), 5(א); רע"א 5860/16 Facebook Inc. נ' בן חמו).
- [ ] Exemplary damages (Consumer Protection Law s. 31א) only if you sent a prior written demand.
- [ ] Exhibits: order, listing, tracking, messages, cancellation, card statement, issuer correspondence.
