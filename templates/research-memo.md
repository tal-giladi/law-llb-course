# Research memo (תזכיר משפטי)

**What it is for:** answering one legal question in your own affairs, with checked sources, before you act. Used in lessons 02.6 and the module 2 project, and before every practice lab in the course.
**Who uses it:** you, for your own matter. Writing memos for other people as an occupation or for payment is legal advice reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20(4)).
**Where:** keep it in the folder of your matter. It is not filed; parts of it become your letter or pleading.
**Disclaimer:** this template is for your own use only and is not legal advice.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them. Aim for two to four pages.
