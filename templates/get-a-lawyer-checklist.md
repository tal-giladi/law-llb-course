# Get-a-lawyer checklist and one-page case summary (מתי לפנות לעורך דין – רשימת בדיקה ותקציר תיק)

**What it is for:** deciding, at each stage of your own civil dispute, whether to continue alone, get one consultation, or hand the case to a lawyer; and preparing a one-page summary for a first meeting with a lawyer, Legal Aid or a university clinic. Used in lesson 12.8 and the module 12 project.
**Who may use it:** you, for your own dispute. Giving legal advice or representing others as an occupation or for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** keep it in your case file; send the summary in advance of a meeting.
**Disclaimer:** this is a self-assessment aid, not legal advice. When in doubt, ask.

Run part א at three points: before the demand letter, before filing, and when the defence or anything unexpected arrives. One **red** answer: get a lawyer. Two or more **amber** answers: get at least one consultation.
