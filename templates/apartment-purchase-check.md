# Apartment purchase check (בדיקת דירה לפני רכישה – רשימת בדיקה)

**What it is for:** a buyer's own checklist before signing a memorandum or contract for a second-hand flat: the Land Registry extract, the condominium file, the Israel Land Authority, the municipality, planning and the house committee, ending in a red-flag table and contract conditions. It does not replace the buyer's lawyer. Used in lessons 13.2 and 13.9 and the module 13 project; module 20 adds the new-apartment (Sale (Apartments) Law) checks.
**Who may use it:** you, for a flat you are buying or own. Checking a property for another person as a service, or advising on it, is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** the Ministry of Justice extract service (https://www.gov.il/he/service/land_registration_extract) or any Land Registry office; the ILA; the local authority; the house committee.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them.
