# Pre-indictment request under CPL s. 60א (פנייה לרשות התביעה לפי סעיף 60א – בקשה להימנע מהגשת כתב אישום)

**What it is for:** a felony suspect's reasoned written request to the prosecution not to file an indictment, or not to file it for a particular offence, after receiving a notice that the investigation material has reached the prosecution (Criminal Procedure Law [Consolidated] 1982, s. 60א). Used in lessons 24.5 and 24.9 and the module 24 project.
**Who may file it:** the suspect, in his own name, or his lawyer. Drafting it for others as an occupation or for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20). In a felony, have a lawyer or the Public Defender write or check it: what you write may be used later.
**Where:** the address in the notice (district attorney's office or police prosecution unit) (s. 60א(ב)). Send by registered mail or email with proof of delivery.
**Deadline:** within the period in s. 60א(ד) from the day you received the notice; the State Attorney, district attorney or head of the prosecution unit may extend it. Ask for an extension in writing before the period ends if you need the material (see `references/current-numbers.md`).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.
