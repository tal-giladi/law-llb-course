# Request to settle a family dispute and requests during the stay (בקשה ליישוב סכסוך ובקשות בתקופת העיכוב)

**What it is for:** drafting, as a dry run or for real, the first filing in a family dispute between spouses, between parents, or between parents and children (Family Dispute Resolution Law 2014, s. 3(א)), and the short requests allowed while claims are frozen: status-quo relief or an exit ban (reg. 10, Form 5), urgent relief (reg. 12, Form 6), and shortening or extending the stay (reg. 16, Form 7). Used in lesson 18.9 and the module 18 project.
**Who may file it:** you, in your own name, about your own family. Representing another person for pay or as an occupation is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20) and, in the rabbinical and Sharia courts, to licensed pleaders.
**Where:** the secretariat of the Family Court under the ordinary venue rules (Family Court Procedure Regulations reg. 6), or of the competent religious court (reg. 2 of the Dispute Resolution Regulations 2016); online where the court offers it to unrepresented parties (check on the day).
**Deadlines and fees:** see `references/current-numbers.md` (fee for the request; 45/60-day stay; 5-day notice; 15-day filing window; days not counted under s. 6א).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. The Regulations prescribe official forms (Forms 1–7); the secretariat may refuse a request not on the form (for example reg. 10(א)). Use the official form from the Judiciary's forms site and treat this template as a drafting aid that follows its structure.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them.

## חלק ד: בקשה לקיצור או להארכה של תקופת עיכוב ההליכים (טופס 7, תקנה 16)

[Concise; only facts needed (reg. 16(ד)). Shortening grounds include: urgent transfer of a child's educational framework; psychological treatment; a new request within a year of an earlier one; urgent maintenance or custody where waiting causes real harm; unknown address or a party abroad; an indictment for violence or sexual offences against the spouse or child; a party in prison or detention; a woman in a shelter; insolvency proceedings; or the unit's recommendation (reg. 16(א)). Extension grounds: bad-faith non-attendance; mourning or illness; a party abroad expected back shortly (reg. 16(ב)). The unit's view is sought in some cases, and a party who did not attend a meeting needs special leave (reg. 16(ג)).]
