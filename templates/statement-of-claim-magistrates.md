# Statement of claim, magistrates' court (כתב תביעה – בית משפט השלום)

**What it is for:** a civil claim in a magistrates' court under the Civil Procedure Regulations 2018, in the three-part form of reg. 9(ג) (heading, summary, detail), with the heading items of reg. 10 in order, the summary items of reg. 11, the detail of reg. 14 and the attachments of reg. 15. Includes the fast-track additions (reg. 79) and an affidavit form (reg. 178). Used in lessons 15.1, 15.2, 15.7, 15.10 and the module 15 project. For small claims use the [small claim template](small-claim-contract-consumer.md) instead.
**Who may file it:** you, in your own name, for your own claim (reg. 170). A company generally files through a lawyer. Drafting a claim for another person is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** the magistrates' court with venue under reg. 7, at the registry or on Net HaMishpat where unrepresented filing is available; the registry serves it (reg. 160). Fee: Courts (Fees) Regulations, schedule item 1 or 4, first half on filing (reg. 6); amounts in [current numbers](../references/current-numbers.md).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules and the Courts Director's format directions (reg. 31) before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them. Page limits in the magistrates' court: part two up to 2 pages, part three up to 9 pages (reg. 9(ד)). The registry refuses a document that breaks regs 9(ג)–(ד), 10 or 15(א)(3)–(4) (reg. 33(ב)).

**Checklist before filing**

- [ ] Three parts in order; part two ≤ 2 pages, part three ≤ 9 pages.
- [ ] Every reg. 10 heading item present, in order; every remedy listed with its value.
- [ ] The summary states when the cause of action arose and the facts giving jurisdiction.
- [ ] Every fact dated and tied to an exhibit; copies, not originals.
- [ ] Fee item cited and first half computed (or exemption request attached).
- [ ] Limitation date checked, with weeks to spare (Prescription Law; reg. 33(ד)).
- [ ] Fast track: Form 5 affidavit, document list and witness list attached.
