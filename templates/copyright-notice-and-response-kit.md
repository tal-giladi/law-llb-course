# Copyright notice and response kit (ערכת הודעה על הפרת זכות יוצרים ומענה לה)

**What it is for:** protecting your own work online and offline: (A) a platform removal request, (B) deciding whether to counter-notify when your content was removed, (C) a demand letter to an infringer, and (D) answering a copyright demand you received. Used in lessons 32.3, 32.4 and 32.8 and the module 32 project.
**Who may use it:** you, for works you created or own (or hold an exclusive licence in), and for content you uploaded. Sending notices or demand letters for other people's works as an occupation or for pay is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** platform forms (YouTube, Meta, TikTok, Google) in their help centres; letters by e-mail and registered mail to the business's address.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before sending. Israel has no statutory notice-and-takedown procedure; platform procedures are the platforms' own rules and change often: check the live form.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them.
