# Complaint to an oversight body (תלונה לנציב תלונות הציבור / לנציב תלונות הציבור על שופטים / למחלקה לחקירות שוטרים)

**What it is for:** a complaint about a public body, a judge or a police officer to the body that oversees it. Part א is a complaint to the Ombudsman (נציב תלונות הציבור, State Comptroller Law 1958, ss. 33–45) about an act, omission or delay of a ministry, local authority or other audited body. Part ב is a complaint to the Judicial Ombudsman (נציב תלונות הציבור על שופטים, Judicial Ombudsman Law 2002, ss. 14–18) about a judge's or registrar's conduct in office, including delay. Part ג is a complaint to the Police Investigations Department (מח״ש, Ministry of Justice; Police Ordinance s. 49ט) about an offence by a police officer. A screening sheet comes first. For complaints about regulated businesses use `regulator-complaint.md`; for misuse of criminal information, the inspector of criminal information (Criminal Information Law 2019, s. 41). Used in lesson 37.6 and the module 37 project.
**Who may file it:** the person harmed, in his own name, signed with name and address (State Comptroller Law s. 34; Judicial Ombudsman Law s. 15). For another person only with his consent or written authorisation (s. 37(1); s. 14(א)). Drafting complaints for others as an occupation or for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** Ombudsman: https://complaint.mevaker.gov.il/ (Hebrew, Arabic, English, Russian), or by mail or in person (https://www.mevaker.gov.il/ombudsman). Judicial Ombudsman: online form in the government personal area, or email (⚠️ check https://www.gov.il/he/service/complaint_against_judge for the current channel and attachment limits). Police Investigations Department: ⚠️ check https://www.gov.il/he/service/filing_a_complaint_against_police.
**Deadlines:** none fatal, but the Ombudsman and the Judicial Ombudsman examine a complaint filed more than a year after the act (or knowledge, or the judgment) only for a special reason (State Comptroller Law s. 39; Judicial Ombudsman Law s. 18(א)). A complaint does **not** extend any objection, appeal or petition period (State Comptroller Law s. 45(א)(2)) and does not stay a court case. See `references/current-numbers.md`.
**Remember:** the Ombudsman cannot investigate judicial acts or matters pending in court (s. 38); the Judicial Ombudsman does not examine the merits of a decision or matters that can be appealed, except conduct (s. 17). If you were arrested or charged in the same event as a complaint against an officer, see a lawyer first.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.
