# Case brief (תקציר פסק דין)

**What it is for:** summarising one Israeli judgment on one page so you can rely on it, quote it and distinguish it. Used in lessons 02.2, 02.3 and 02.6.
**Who uses it:** you, for your own study or your own matter. A brief is a working document; it is not filed.
**Where:** keep it in the folder of your matter, next to the research memo.
**Disclaimer:** this template is for your own use only and is not legal advice.
