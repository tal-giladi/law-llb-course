# Protection-order request (בקשה למתן צו הגנה)

> [!WARNING]
> If you are in danger now, call the police on **100**. The Welfare Ministry's domestic-violence hotline is **118**. A court request does not replace emergency help.

**What it is for:** drafting a request for a protection order under the Prevention of Family Violence Law 1991 (ss. 2–5) for yourself, with an affidavit outline and evidence list. Used in lessons 18.7 and 18.10.
**Who may file it:** the family member who needs protection, in their own name; also the Attorney General or their representative, a police prosecutor, or a social worker appointed under the Youth (Care and Supervision) Law or the Protection of Vulnerable Persons Law (s. 3). Drafting for another person for pay or as an occupation is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).
**Where:** any magistrates' court or Family Court (Family Court Law s. 4(ג)); a religious court only for parties within its jurisdiction. Courts have arrangements for urgent requests outside working hours (check with the court).
**Fees and periods:** no fee (Family Court Fees Regulations reg. 7(1)); no request to settle the dispute is needed (Dispute Resolution Law s. 4א(3)); hearing with both sides within 7 days of an ex parte order (s. 4(א)); duration up to 3 months, extendable (s. 5). See `references/current-numbers.md`.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules and the court's current form before filing. Where the court offers an official form, use it; this template is a drafting aid.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them.
