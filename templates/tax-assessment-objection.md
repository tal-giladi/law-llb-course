# Objection to a tax assessment (השגה על שומה)

**What it is for:** asking the tax official who assessed you to reconsider and change the assessment: an income-tax assessment (Income Tax Ordinance s. 150), a VAT assessment (VAT Law s. 82) or a purchase-tax or land-appreciation-tax assessment (Real Estate Taxation Law s. 87). Used in lessons 25.3, 25.5, 25.8 and 25.11 and the module 25 project. The structure also works, adapted, for a request to waive fines (Income Tax Ordinance s. 192).
**Who may file it:** you, in your own name, about your own assessment. Accountants and representing tax advisers may represent taxpayers before the Tax Authority (Income Tax Ordinance s. 236); drafting or filing objections for others as an occupation is otherwise reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).
**Where:** the office that issued the assessment (assessing office, regional VAT office or regional Real Estate Taxation office), in writing, by registered mail, by hand or through the online service if available; keep proof of filing.
**Deadlines:** 30 days from the day the assessment notice was delivered to you (ITO s. 150(א); VAT s. 82(א); Real Estate Taxation s. 87(א)); see `references/current-numbers.md`. An extension is possible only for a reasonable cause (abroad, illness). If you never filed an income-tax return for the year, the only valid objection is to file the return (ITO s. 150(ב)).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

**After filing (guidance):** diary the decision deadline. Income tax: the later of four years from the end of the tax year in which the return was filed and one year from the objection (ITO s. 152(ג)); VAT: one year from the objection (VAT s. 82(ד)); real-estate tax: eight months, extendable to one year (s. 87(ד)). No decision in time means the objection is accepted. If an order or decision rejects the objection: income tax and VAT, appeal to the District Court (ITO s. 153 and the Appeals Regulations reg. 2: 30 days; VAT s. 83); real-estate tax, appeals committee within 30 days (s. 88).
