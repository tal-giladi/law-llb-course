# Administrative traffic fine: objection and appeal to the traffic tribunal (השגה וערר על החלטה להטיל קנס)

**What it is for:** answering a notice imposing an administrative fine for a traffic violation under the Traffic Administrative Violations Law 2024 (חוק הפרות תעבורה מינהליות, התשפ״ד–2024), in force since 8 February 2026 (in two phases, s. 47(ג)). Part א is an objection (השגה) to the issuing authority (s. 8); part ב is an appeal (ערר) to the traffic tribunal (בית דין לתעבורה) (s. 19; Traffic Administrative Violations (Procedure and Administration in the Traffic Tribunal) Regulations 2026, regs 2–4); part ג is a short affidavit for facts that no document proves (reg. 2(ד)). It does not apply to a fine-payment notice for a criminal fine offence (use `request-to-be-tried.md`) or to a summons and indictment. Used in lessons 37.4 and 37.7 and the module 37 project.
**Who may file it:** the person on whom the decision was served (the driver, or the vehicle owner), in his own name. Filing for others as an occupation or for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** part א to the issuing authority named on the notice (the police for an officer's fine; the local authority for its inspector's fine), by its online form or the address on the notice. Part ב on the traffic tribunal's online system (reg. 2(א)); the respondent is the police or the local authority (reg. 6) (⚠️ check the current system address and any fee on the Ministry of Justice site).
**Deadlines (from service of the notice):** objection within 30 days, or 90 days if you claim someone else drove (s. 8(א)); appeal within 90 days, or within 30 days of the decision on your objection (s. 19(ב)); you need not object before appealing, but after appealing you may not object (s. 8(ד)). Late filing only with reasons and an affidavit (s. 8(ב); s. 19(ג); reg. 4). Without objection or appeal, pay within the period in s. 9. See `references/current-numbers.md`.
**Remember:** the notice states the points to be recorded (s. 4(א)(6)); check them before paying. The tribunal usually decides on the papers (s. 21(א)): put everything in writing, with annexes. Its decision may be appealed to the traffic magistrates' court, or the local-affairs court for a municipal fine (s. 24); an appeal does not suspend payment unless the court orders it.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.
