# Statement of claim to the superintendent of land registration (כתב תביעה למפקח על רישום המקרקעין)

**What it is for:** a condominium dispute between flat owners, or between an owner and the representative committee (נציגות), under Chapter 6 of the Land Law 1969: unpaid maintenance (s. 58), rights and duties under the bylaws, or trespass on another flat or the common property (s. 72(ב), at the claimant's choice). It follows regs 77–81 of the Land (Management and Registration) Regulations 1969. Use the superintendent's official form if the office requires it; this draft gives the content. Used in lesson 13.10 and the module 13 project.
**Who may file it:** a flat owner, another party to the dispute, or the representative committee (Land Law s. 73), in its own name. Representing another person before the superintendent as an occupation or for pay is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20(1)).
**Where:** the superintendent of land registration for the land office where the building is registered or will be registered (reg. 79). Service page: https://www.gov.il/he/service/condominiums-supervisor-claims (check current filing methods). Fee: as for a civil claim in the magistrates' court.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them. Write only facts you can prove; number every exhibit.
