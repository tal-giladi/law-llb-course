# Small claim for a contract or consumer dispute (כתב תביעה – תביעה קטנה בעניין חוזה או צרכנות)

**What it is for:** a statement of claim in the small claims court for a money claim arising from a contract or a consumer transaction: a refund a business refuses, a rental deposit a landlord keeps, unpaid fees for work you did, an overcharge, or replacement, repair or cancellation of a purchase. It follows reg. 3 of the Small Claims (Procedure) Regulations 1976 for proceedings opened from 11.4.2025. Used in lesson 12.5 and the module 12 project. For damage caused by another person's tort use the [small claim for property damage](small-claim-property-damage.md).
**Who may file it:** an individual (יחיד), in their own name, for a sum up to the small-claims ceiling (Courts Law s. 60; see `references/current-numbers.md`); not for a debt assigned to you. A company cannot be a plaintiff in small claims. A lawyer may appear only with the court's leave (s. 63).
**Where:** the small claims court for the defendant's home or business, or the place of the act or omission, whatever a contract says (reg. 2(א)); for internet trade, the defendant's or the plaintiff's area (reg. 2(ב)). Online through the Judiciary's small-claims service (national digital ID, PDFs) or at the court registry. Fee: 1% of the claim with a minimum (reg. 6; see `references/current-numbers.md`).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules and the court's current electronic form before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them. The body must not exceed five pages (reg. 3(ה)). Every fact a document proves gets "(נספח X)".
