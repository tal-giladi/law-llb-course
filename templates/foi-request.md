# FOI request (בקשה לקבלת מידע לפי חוק חופש המידע)

**What it is for:** asking a public authority for information it holds, under the Freedom of Information Law 1998: documents, minutes, data, contracts, criteria, or information about yourself. Used in lessons 14.7 and 14.10 and the module 14 project.
**Who may file it:** any Israeli citizen or resident, including an Israeli company (s. 1); a non-resident only for information about their own rights in Israel (s. 12). File it in your own name. Drafting requests for others as an occupation or for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** to the authority's freedom-of-information officer (הממונה על חופש המידע), by the authority's online form, email or post. Most ministries use the Government FOI unit's online system (⚠️ manual check of the current link: gov.il blocks automated access).
**Deadlines and fees:** see `references/current-numbers.md` (decision period and extensions, delivery period, application fee, handling fee, fee cap, exemptions, fee-appeal period). The periods run from the later of receipt by the officer and payment of the application fee (FOI (Fees) Regulations reg. 9(א)).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

**After sending:** diary the decision date, the possible extension dates and the delivery date (current numbers). If the answer is late, you may not be charged handling or production fees (reg. 6א). A fee decision can be appealed to the magistrates' court where the officer sits (reg. 8). A refusal can be challenged by an administrative petition (FOI Law s. 17; lesson 14.11).
