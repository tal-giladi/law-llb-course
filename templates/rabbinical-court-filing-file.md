# Rabbinical-court filing file (תיק הגשה לבית הדין הרבני)

**What it is for:** preparing, as a dry run or for real, the documents a self-represented party needs in a regional rabbinical court: the request to settle the dispute, a statement of claim that meets reg. כו of the procedure regulations, the documents list, fees, the representation decision and the deadlines. Used in lesson 10.6 and the module 10 project.
**Who may file it:** you, in your own name, about your own matter. Representing another person before the rabbinical courts as an occupation or for pay is reserved to lawyers and licensed rabbinical pleaders (Pleaders Regulations 2001, reg. 1; Bar Association Law s. 20).
**Where:** the secretariat of the competent regional rabbinical court (last joint residence of the spouses, reg. י), or the court's online services where available (check on gov.il on the day).
**Deadlines and fees:** see `references/current-numbers.md` (dispute-resolution periods; fees under the Dayanim (Fees) Regulations; 30/10/30-day periods after judgment).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules and the court's current forms before filing. Where the court has an official form, use it; this template is a drafting aid.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them.

## Checklist before filing

- [ ] I am acting in my own name about my own matter.
- [ ] The request to settle has no facts and no jurisdiction arguments.
- [ ] The claim names the right regional court (last joint residence).
- [ ] Each joined issue has its own facts and precise relief.
- [ ] Every annex is attached and numbered.
- [ ] Fees paid, exemption claimed, or deferral requested.
- [ ] Deadlines diarised.
