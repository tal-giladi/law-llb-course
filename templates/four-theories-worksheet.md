# Four-theories worksheet (ניתוח פסק דין בארבע תיאוריות)

**What it is for:** reading one ruling through natural law, legal positivism, Dworkin's law as integrity, and legal realism or critical theory, opinion by opinion. Used in lessons 09.6 and in the module 9 project.
**Who uses it:** you, for study or to prepare an argument in your own matter. It is a working document; it is not filed.
**Where:** keep it with the case brief of the ruling (template: case-brief.md).
**Disclaimer:** this template is for your own use only and is not legal advice.

Fill in the Hebrew headings. Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them. Quote no more than 15 words at a time and give the paragraph number.
