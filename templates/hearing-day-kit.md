# Hearing-day kit (ערכת יום דיון)

**What it is for:** preparing for and running your own small-claims hearing: the exhibit bundle, a two-minute opening, the disputed facts and their proof, questions for the other side and your witness, the judge's likely questions, and your settlement range. It is your working paper; you do not file it. Used in lesson 12.6 and the module 12 project.
**Who may use it:** you, as plaintiff or defendant in your own case. A lawyer appears in small claims only with the court's leave; a non-lawyer helper only with the court's approval and a power of attorney, and not someone who represents regularly or for pay (Courts Law s. 63).
**Where:** the court on the date in the summons, or by video if the court so decides (Small Claims Regulations reg. 13(ב)).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules and the court's decisions in your case before the hearing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them.
