# Land Registry heirs-registration checklist (רישום זכויות יורשים במקרקעין – רשימת בדיקה)

**What it is for:** taking a succession or probate order (or several, when an earlier death was never dealt with) to the Land Registry and getting the heirs registered as owners in place of the deceased, by the paper route at the registry office or with the online application to the Registrar of Inheritance; planning a later distribution agreement among the heirs. It is a checklist and a drafting aid, not the official form: use the current Land Registry form or online service. Used in lesson 31.4 and the module 31 project; background in lessons 13.2, 26.7 and 26.9.
**Who may file it:** any heir, in his or her own name (Land Management and Registration Regulations reg. 17(ב)); if an administrator of the estate was appointed, the administrator files or consents (reg. 18(ג)). A distribution agreement not approved by a court is registered only on the application of all the heirs (reg. 17(ב)). Filing for another person is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20(2) names the land registry office).
**Where:** the Land Registry office (לשכת רישום המקרקעין) where the land is registered (reg. 18(א)), in person by appointment or by post; or with the online application for the order on the Administrator General's site. Online filing directly at the Land Registry is for lawyers. Israel Land Authority leasehold that is not registered in the Land Registry is handled by the ILA, not by this checklist.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules, forms and fees before filing.

Notes in English in «guillemets» are guidance; delete them.
