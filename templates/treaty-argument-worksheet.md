# Treaty argument worksheet (גיליון עבודה: טיעון על בסיס אמנה בינלאומית)

**What it is for:** preparing the international-law section of a letter, appeal or written argument in your own matter, using a treaty Israel has ratified. Used in lessons 19.2, 19.6, 19.7, the practice lab 19.8 and the module 19 project.
**Who uses it:** you, for your own matter (or your minor child's, as natural guardian). Preparing such arguments for other people as an occupation or for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** it is not filed. Part D becomes a section of your letter, appeal (ערר), statement of claim or petition.
**Disclaimer:** this template is for your own use only and is not legal advice. Check the treaty's status and the Israeli law on the day you write.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them.
