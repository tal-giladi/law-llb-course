# Affidavit and exhibit bundle (תצהיר עדות ראשית ותיק מוצגים)

**What it is for:** preparing your own evidence for a civil trial in the magistrates' or district court: your affidavit in lieu of main testimony, a numbered exhibit bundle with an index that shows how each exhibit will be authenticated, a request to an institution for a records certificate, and a notice of electronic evidence. Used in lessons 23.4 and 23.9 and the module 23 project.
**Who may file it:** you, as a party in your own case (CPR reg. 170). Your affidavit must be signed before a lawyer or another person listed in Evidence Ordinance s. 15, who confirms the warning; that person does not represent you. A witness writes and signs his own affidavit in his own words; you may tell him which facts the case is about, but you may not draft legal documents for other people (Bar Association Law s. 20).
**Where:** filed with the court by the date the court sets (usually before the evidence hearing; check the decision in your case). Electronic evidence: notice at least three days before the hearing (CPR reg. 71).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules and the court's decisions in your case before filing.

Notes in English in square brackets are guidance; delete them before filing.

1. אני צד להליך ת"א [___] בבית משפט השלום ב[___].

3. כמו כן אבקש אישור חתום בידי האחראי על הרשומות אצלכם, ובו פירוט:
   א. שהמוסד נוהג, במהלך ניהולו הרגיל, לרשום אירועים מסוג זה בסמוך להתרחשותם;
   ב. כיצד נאספים הנתונים ונערכת הרשומה;
   ג. כיצד מופק הפלט, ואילו אמצעי הגנה סבירים נוקט המוסד באורח סדיר מפני חדירה לחומר מחשב ומפני שיבוש בעבודת המחשב.
