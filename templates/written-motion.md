# Written motion (בקשה בכתב)

**What it is for:** any written motion in a civil case under the Civil Procedure Regulations 2018 (reg. 50): relief first, facts verified by affidavit, argument with authorities, within the page limits of reg. 50(5). Three ready variants: (A) setting aside a decision given in your absence (reg. 131) with a stay; (B) interim relief (regs 94–110); (C) a disclosure or other motion for the list of motions (regs 49(ג), 59). Used in lessons 15.3, 15.4, 15.5 and 15.11.
**Who may file it:** you, in your own name, in your own case (reg. 170). Drafting motions for another person is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** the court hearing the case (for set-aside: the court that gave the decision). Before filing, write to the other side at least seven days in advance (reg. 49(ד)), except for an ex parte temporary order. Fee for a motion, where payable: Courts (Fees) Regulations; periods and page limits in [current numbers](../references/current-numbers.md).
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

Notes in English in brackets are guidance; delete them.

**Checklist before filing**

- [ ] The relief is in the first paragraph, precise and numbered.
- [ ] Every fact is in the affidavit, with an exhibit.
- [ ] The deadline is met (30 days for set-aside; the reg. 49 periods for listed motions).
- [ ] The seven-day letter was sent, unless the motion is for an ex parte temporary order.
- [ ] Page limits of reg. 50(5) are met.
