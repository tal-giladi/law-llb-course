# Capstone strategy map (מפת אסטרטגיה לתיק הגמר: בחירת העניין, תאוריית התיק ומסלולים)

**What it is for:** choosing your capstone matter with a decision matrix, then writing its case theory and the map of routes, decision points, numbers, timeline and risks that you will run it by. It sits on top of your case file and is revised at every decision point. Used in lessons 39.1, 39.2 and 39.5 and the module 39 project.
**Who may use it:** you, for your own matter. Preparing strategy for another person's matter as an occupation or for payment is legal advice reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20).
**Where:** keep it as the first pages of your case file (templates/clinic-case-file.md). It is a working paper; it is not filed or sent to anyone.
**Disclaimer:** act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules, fees and deadlines (references/current-numbers.md) before you send or file anything.

Notes in English in square brackets are guidance; delete them. Date every revision at the top of the part you change.
