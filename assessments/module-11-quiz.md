# Module 11 quiz: Law, Economics and Society

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on the whole module: efficiency, incentives and the Coase theorem, property and liability rules in Israeli nuisance and contract law, behavioural biases in settlement and consumer contracts, the gap between law in books and law in action and access to justice in Israel, reading court statistics, and deciding whether to sue with expected value, court fees and costs. Each question describes a realistic situation, usually a decision in your own dispute, and asks for the best analysis or next step. The pass mark is 70%.

Take it after the practice lab and, ideally, the module project.

- Anchoring, loss aversion, self-serving bias, s. 13א of the Consumer Protection Law: [11.2 · Behavioural law and economics](../lessons/module-11/lesson-02.md)
- The dispute pyramid, repeat players, the legal-needs survey, Legal Aid: [11.3 · Law and society: how law works in practice](../lessons/module-11/lesson-03.md)
- Clearance rates, base rates, the parole-board study, selection effects: [11.4 · Empirical legal studies: reading data about courts](../lessons/module-11/lesson-04.md)
- Expected value, costs (regs 151–157), court fees, settlement range: [11.5 · Practice lab: should you sue? Expected value, costs, risk and time](../lessons/module-11/lesson-05.md)
