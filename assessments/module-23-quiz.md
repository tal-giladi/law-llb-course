# Module 23 quiz: Evidence

This quiz has 10 scenario questions in the style of the Bar exam's procedural part on evidence: the four layers of evidence law and relaxed forums, competence and privilege, hearsay and its exceptions, documents, institutional records and electronic signatures, illegally obtained evidence, burdens and presumptions, experts, confessions and corroboration, and trial practice. Each question describes a situation and asks what you would do or conclude under the Evidence Ordinance, the Civil Procedure Regulations 2018 and the other statutes taught in the module. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10). You can retake it.
