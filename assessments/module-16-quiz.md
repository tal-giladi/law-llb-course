# Module 16 quiz: Corporate Law

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on business forms and legal personality, incorporation and the articles, company organs and meetings, officers' duties of care and loyalty, lifting the veil and distributions, shareholder remedies (information, oppression, derivative claims), sole traders, VAT registration and partnerships. Several are written in the style of the Bar exam's corporate-law questions. Each question describes a situation and asks what you would do or conclude. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10). You can retake it.
