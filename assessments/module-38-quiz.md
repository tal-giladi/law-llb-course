# Module 38 quiz: Bar-Exam-Style Review

This quiz has 10 Bar-style scenario questions drawn from the whole module: the exam's own rules, jurisdiction and civil procedure, criminal procedure, evidence, execution, insolvency and discipline, contracts, torts and limitation, property, succession, commercial and company law, real-estate taxation, criminal law, the Basic Laws, professional ethics and the writing task. Each question asks you to apply a specific section to facts and choose the one correct answer. The pass mark is 70%.

Take it after the mock exam in the practice lab and, ideally, after the module project.
