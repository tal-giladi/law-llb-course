# Module 32 quiz: Intellectual Property

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on the whole module: the map of Israeli IP regimes, copyright subsistence, ownership and duration, infringement, permitted uses and moral rights, online enforcement, trade marks and passing off, patents and designs, trade secrets and employee inventions, and the two practice labs. Each question puts you in the position of a creator, a small business, an employee or an employer and asks for the best lawful next step. The pass mark is 70%.

Take it after the practice labs and, ideally, the module project.
