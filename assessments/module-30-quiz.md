# Module 30 quiz: Private International Law

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on the whole module: the three questions of private international law, international jurisdiction and service abroad, choice of law in contracts and torts, family and succession across borders, enforcement of foreign judgments, and international online purchases. Each question puts you in the position of a consumer, a creditor, a debtor or a family member and asks for the best lawful next step. The pass mark is 70%.

Take it after the practice lab and, ideally, the module project.

- Presence, consent, reg. 166 grounds, regs 167–169, forum non conveniens, foreign jurisdiction clauses, Hague Service and Apostille: [30.2 · International jurisdiction of Israeli courts and service abroad](../lessons/module-30/lesson-02.md)
- Choice-of-law clauses, the closest connection, the CISG, the Yinon rule in tort: [30.3 · Choice of law in contracts and torts](../lessons/module-30/lesson-03.md)
- Succession Law ch. 7, Spouses Property Relations Law s. 15, divorce jurisdiction and foreign divorces: [30.4 · Family and succession across borders](../lessons/module-30/lesson-04.md)
- The Foreign Judgments Enforcement Law: conditions, reciprocity, five years, defences, recognition: [30.5 · Enforcing foreign judgments](../lessons/module-30/lesson-05.md)
