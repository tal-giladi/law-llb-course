# Module 7 quiz: Legislation and Regulation

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on the whole module: how bills become law and what happens to them at elections, secondary legislation and parliamentary approval, regulators and their tools, the Interpretation Law and validity checks, and taking part in law-making through public comments and Knesset committees. Each question describes a realistic situation and asks for the best decision. The pass mark is 70%.

Take it after you have finished both practice labs and, ideally, the module project.

- Bills, readings, committees, continuity: [07.1 · How a law is made: government bills, private bills, committees](../lessons/module-07/lesson-01.md)
- Regulations, orders, directives, committee approval, emergency regulations: [07.2 · Secondary legislation: regulations, orders, directives, and parliamentary approval](../lessons/module-07/lesson-02.md)
- Regulators, tools and the Regulatory Principles Law: [07.3 · Regulators and regulatory tools](../lessons/module-07/lesson-03.md)
- The Interpretation Law and the validity checklist: [07.4 · Interpreting legislation and checking validity](../lessons/module-07/lesson-04.md)
- Public comments on memoranda and draft regulations: [07.5 · Practice lab: comment on a draft bill or regulation (תזכירי חוק)](../lessons/module-07/lesson-05.md)
- Committees, position papers and following a bill: [07.6 · Practice lab: write to a Knesset committee and follow the bill](../lessons/module-07/lesson-06.md)
