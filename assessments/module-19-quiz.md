# Module 19 quiz: Public International Law

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on the sources of international law, treaties and Israel's treaty practice, customary international law, statehood, jurisdiction and foreign-State immunity, the ICJ and the ICC, human-rights treaties and the UN system, the status of international law in Israeli courts, and using a treaty in an Israeli argument. Each question describes a situation and asks what you would do or conclude. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10). You can retake it.
