# Module 35 quiz: The Legal Profession and Ethics

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on the whole module: admission to the Bar and checking a lawyer, the reserved acts and unauthorised practice, loyalty, conflicts and confidentiality, fees and statutory caps, choosing and supervising a lawyer, complaints, fee disputes and malpractice, and AI in legal work. Several questions are in the style of the ethics part of the Bar exam. Each puts you in the position of a client, a self-represented party or a lawyer and asks for the best lawful step. The pass mark is 70%.

Take it after the practice lab and, ideally, the module project.
