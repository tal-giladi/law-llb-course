# Module 6 quiz: Constitutional Law

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on the whole module: the Basic Laws and the constituent-authority debate, Bank Mizrahi and judicial review of statutes, the limitation clause and proportionality, the protected rights, the institutions, the limits of judicial review and the 2023–2026 dispute, and the practical work of analysing a law and preparing a petition. Each question describes a realistic situation and asks for the best decision. Questions on contested issues test whether you can state the law and each side's argument, not which side you prefer. The pass mark is 70%.

Take it after both practice labs and, ideally, the module project.
