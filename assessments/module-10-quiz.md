# Module 10 quiz: Introduction to Jewish Law and Religious Law in Israel

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on the sources and structure of Jewish law, its place in Israeli civil-court decisions under the Foundations of Law Act, the jurisdiction of the rabbinical courts and the Family Court, the Sharia, Christian and Druze courts, reading Jewish-law reasoning in a ruling, and rabbinical-court procedure for a self-represented party. Each question describes a situation and asks what you would do or conclude. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10). You can retake it.
