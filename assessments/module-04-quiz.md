# Module 4 quiz: Tort Law

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on the structure of the Civil Wrongs Ordinance, negligence, causation and damage, the specific torts, defences and limitation, road accidents, the liability of employers, the state and professionals, insurance claims and small claims. Each question describes a situation and asks what you would do or conclude. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10). You can retake it.
