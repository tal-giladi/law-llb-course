# Module 3 quiz: Contract Law

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on the whole module: the structure of the Contracts Law, formation and preliminary agreements, form requirements, good faith, defects in formation, illegal and standard contracts, interpretation after the 2026 amendment, remedies for breach, and consumer withdrawal. Each question describes a realistic situation and asks for the best decision. The pass mark is 70%.

Take it after you have finished both practice labs and, ideally, the module project.
