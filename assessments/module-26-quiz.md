# Module 26 quiz: Inheritance and Wills

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on the whole module: heirs on intestacy and the spouse's share, the four forms of will and curing defects, validity and interpretation, mutual wills and gifts to witnesses, administering an estate and its debts, succession and probate orders and objections, registering heirs in land, and the enduring power of attorney. Each question describes a realistic family situation and asks for the best lawful decision. The pass mark is 70%.

Take it after both practice labs and, ideally, the module project.
