# Module 27 quiz: Planning, Building and Local Government

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on the whole module: the planning institutions, plans and their hierarchy, objections and planning appeals, building permits and exemptions, enforcement after Amendment 116, betterment levy and compensation, local government and bylaws, and arnona with its discounts, objection and appeal. Each question describes a realistic situation of a resident, owner or buyer and asks for the best lawful decision. The pass mark is 70%.

Take it after both practice labs and, ideally, the module project.
