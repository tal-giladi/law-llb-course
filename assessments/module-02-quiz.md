# Module 2 quiz: Legal Research and Writing

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on finding and checking statutes and rulings, briefing a judgment, interpreting a provision, citing sources, and writing memos and formal letters. Each question describes a situation and asks what you would do or conclude. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10). You can retake it.
