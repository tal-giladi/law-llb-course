# Module 31 quiz: Clinic III: Property, Estates and Money

This quiz has 10 scenario questions that follow real self-represented matters through the clinic: choosing the matter and its money-losing deadlines, your own will and enduring power of attorney, a succession order through two estates, registering heirs in the Land Registry, a tax refund and an objection, defending an execution file and reaching a payment order, an arnona objection, and closing the file. Each question describes a situation and asks what you would do or conclude. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10). You can retake it.
