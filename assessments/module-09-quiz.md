# Module 9 quiz: Legal Theory

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on the whole module: natural law and the places Israeli law refers to justice, legal positivism and the chain of validity, Dworkin's principles and law as integrity, legal realism and the critical theories, rights, justice and the rule of law, and reading a ruling through several theories. Each question describes a realistic situation, often an argument you might make or meet in your own case, and asks for the best analysis or decision. Questions on contested rulings test whether you can state each side's argument, not which side you prefer. The pass mark is 70%.

Take it after the practice lab and, ideally, the module project.
