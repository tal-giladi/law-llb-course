# Module 1 quiz: Israeli Law: Sources, Courts and the Legal System

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on the whole module: who may act for whom, the layers and hierarchy of Israeli law, choosing the court with jurisdiction, precedent, and the people of the legal system. Each question describes a realistic situation and asks for the best decision. The pass mark is 70%.

Take it after you have finished the practice lab and, ideally, the module project.
