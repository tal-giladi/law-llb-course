# Module 14 quiz: Administrative Law

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on the principle of legality, discretion and reasonableness after the 2023 amendment, proportionality, fair procedure, the duty to decide and delegation, tenders and administrative promises, freedom of information, the Ombudsman, and petitions to the Administrative Affairs Court. Each question describes a situation and asks what you would do or conclude. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10). You can retake it.
