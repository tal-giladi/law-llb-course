# Module 21 quiz: Proseminar: Academic Legal Writing

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on choosing and narrowing a research question, finding the literature in free Israeli law journals, structuring a legal paper and choosing its method, footnoting under the uniform citation rules, and academic integrity, plagiarism and the use of AI tools. Each question describes a situation and asks what you would do or conclude. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10). You can retake it.
