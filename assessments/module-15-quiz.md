# Module 15 quiz: Civil Procedure

This quiz has 10 scenario questions in the style of the Bar exam's procedural part: jurisdiction and venue, parties and third-party notices, pleadings and deemed admission, service and default judgments, disclosure, interim relief and security for costs, the pre-trial stage and the fast track, trial evidence, appeals and leave to appeal, and limitation. Each question describes a situation and asks what you would do or conclude under the Civil Procedure Regulations 2018, the Courts Law and the Prescription Law. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10). You can retake it.
