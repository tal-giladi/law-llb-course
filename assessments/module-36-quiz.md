# Module 36 quiz: Seminar Paper

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on what changes from the proseminar to the seminar, original contribution, research methods (doctrinal, comparative, empirical and legal-historical), working with legislative history and court databases at scale, managing and revising a long paper, and presenting and defending it. Each question describes a situation and asks what you would do or conclude. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10). You can retake it.
