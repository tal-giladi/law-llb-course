# Module 39 quiz: Capstone: Your Own Case File, End to End

This quiz has 10 scenario questions that follow a self-represented person's own matter through the whole capstone cycle: choosing the matter with the gates and the decision matrix, writing the case theory and the strategy map, building the evidence and research file, drafting and sending the documents, running the matter with the log and the decision-point routine, and closing it with a retrospective. Each question describes a situation and asks what you would do or conclude. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10). You can retake it.
