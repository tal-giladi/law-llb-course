# Module 18 quiz: Family Law

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on Israeli family law: the dual civil and religious structure and the Family Court, marriage, registration and dissolution, cohabitants and same-sex couples, property between spouses and property agreements, child and spousal maintenance, custody and the best interest of the child, protection orders, parentage, and the request to settle a family dispute. Each question describes a family from one of Israel's communities and asks what the law provides or what step to take, under the statutes and rulings as they stood in October 2026. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10). You can retake it.
