# Module 22 quiz: Clinic II: Work, Family and Home Without a Lawyer

This quiz has 10 scenario questions that follow real self-represented matters through the five tracks of the clinic: choosing the matter and its deadlines, an employment claim on the labour court's expedited track, a National Insurance medical appeal and the appeal on law, the request to settle a family dispute and mediation, a tenancy dispute over repairs and the deposit, welfare and housing benefit reviews, the hearing in the labour court and tribunals, and closing the file. Each question describes a situation and asks what you would do or conclude. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10). You can retake it.
