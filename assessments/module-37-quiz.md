# Module 37 quiz: Clinic IV: Public and Criminal Matters Without a Lawyer

This quiz has 10 scenario questions that follow real self-represented public and criminal matters through the clinic: choosing the matter and its door-closing deadlines, an administrative petition from the decision to the hearing, a police complaint and what to do when the file is closed, a traffic ticket on the administrative or the criminal track, deleting a police record, a complaint to the Ombudsman, the Judicial Ombudsman or the Police Investigations Department, and closing the file. Each question describes a situation and asks what you would do or conclude. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10). You can retake it.
