# Module 5 quiz: Criminal Law

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on the whole module: legality and culpability, the elements of an offence, attempt and complicity, defences, the core offences, sentencing under Amendment 113, and the victim's tools: the police complaint, the Crime Victims' Rights Law, the appeal against closing a file and the private criminal complaint. Each question describes a realistic situation and asks for the best decision. The pass mark is 70%.

Take it after both practice labs and, ideally, the module project.
