# Module 17 quiz: Labour, Welfare and Social Security Law

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on employee status and the employer triangle, minimum wage, hours, leave and sick pay, payslips and late wages, dismissal, notice, severance and pension, equality and sexual harassment, collective labour law and strikes, National Insurance benefits, income support and welfare services, and National Insurance and labour-court procedure. Each question describes a situation and asks what you would do or conclude. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10). You can retake it.
