# Module 25 quiz: Tax Law

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on the whole module: residency and sources of income, credit points and the duty to file, the self-employed and VAT, refunds for employees, assessment, objection and appeal, purchase tax, land appreciation tax and the single-apartment exemption, penalties and voluntary disclosure, and the work grant. Each question describes a realistic situation and asks for the best lawful decision. The pass mark is 70%.

Take it after both practice labs and, ideally, the module project.
