# Module 34 quiz: Environmental Law

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on the whole module: which statute and authority govern a problem, noise and smell standards, water, waste, deposits and hazardous substances, the pollutant release register, environmental information under the FOI Law, financial sanctions, private complaints and the citizens' suit, and the state of climate law. Each question puts you in the position of a resident, an organisation or an official and asks for the best lawful next step. The pass mark is 70%.

Take it after the practice lab and, ideally, the module project.
