# Module 12 quiz: Clinic I: Your Everyday Civil Dispute, Start to Finish

This quiz has 10 scenario questions that follow one everyday civil dispute from intake to enforcement: choosing a dispute and building the facts file, collecting evidence lawfully, the demand letter and settlement, regulators, the small-claims statement, the hearing, judgment, leave to appeal and collection, and when to get a lawyer or free help. Each question describes a situation and asks what you would do or conclude. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10). You can retake it.
