# Module 24 quiz: Criminal Procedure

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on the whole module: the structure of the process and its constitutional frame, police powers of detention, arrest and search, interrogation rights and recording, remand and bail, prosecution decisions and the pre-indictment request, the trial and plea bargains, sentencing and appeals, criminal and police records, fine offences and the request to be tried. Each question describes a realistic situation and asks for the best lawful decision. The pass mark is 70%.

Take it after both practice labs and, ideally, the module project.
