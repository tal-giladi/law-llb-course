# Module 29 quiz: Commercial Law: Payments, Liens and Consumer Protection

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on the whole module: cheques and promissory notes, the holder in due course and the drawer's defences, bounced cheques and restricted accounts, pledges and the registrar, banks and their customers, consumer protection and exemplary damages, and fair-credit and collection rules. Each question puts you in the position of a drawer, a holder, a buyer, a borrower or a consumer and asks for the best lawful next step. Commercial law is a subject of the Bar exam, and several questions follow the Bar's style. The pass mark is 70%.

Take it after both practice labs and, ideally, the module project.
