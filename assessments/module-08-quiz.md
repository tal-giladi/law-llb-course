# Module 8 quiz: Introduction to Procedure

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on the principles of the Civil Procedure Regulations 2018, the stages of a civil case, the choice between small claims and the magistrates' court, criminal and administrative procedure in outline, mediation, arbitration and settlement, and enforcement through the Execution Office. Each question describes a situation and asks what you would do or conclude. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10). You can retake it.
