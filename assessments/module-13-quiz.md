# Module 13 quiz: Property and Land Law

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on property rights in land and movables, the Land Registry and Israel Land Authority leases, land transactions and competing transactions, the caveat, co-ownership and its dissolution, condominiums and the superintendent, possession, neighbours and easements, and good-faith acquisition, lost property and pledges. Several are written in the style of the Bar exam's property questions. Each question describes a situation and asks what you would do or conclude. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10). You can retake it.
