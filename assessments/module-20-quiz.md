# Module 20 quiz: Contracts: Advanced and Special Contracts

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on the special contracts: sale of goods and the duty to inspect and notify, buying a new apartment from a developer and securing the money, residential rental under the fair-rental rules, contracts for work and services, guarantees and the protected guarantor, insurance claims, consumer cancellation and unfair standard terms, and unjust enrichment. Several are written in the style of the Bar exam's contract-law questions. Each question describes a situation and asks what you would do or conclude. The pass mark is 70% (7 of 10). You can retake it.
