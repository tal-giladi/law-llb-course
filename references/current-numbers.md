# Current Numbers: Fees, Ceilings and Deadlines

Every fee, ceiling and deadline the course uses is kept on this page; lessons link here instead of repeating the numbers. Always check the official source before you pay or file. Rows are added module by module.

| Item | Amount | Source (checked) |
|---|---|---|
| Magistrates' court civil ceiling (claims other than land) | ₪2,500,000 on the day of filing | Courts Law s. 51(א)(2), https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט#סעיף_51 (2026-10-16) |
| Small claims ceiling (individual claimant) | Statutory ₪30,000 linked to Aug 2008; Wikisource note: ₪39,900 in 2026 (⚠️ manual check on gov.il / the Judiciary's site) | Courts Law s. 60(א)(1), https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט#סעיף_60 (2026-10-16) |
| General limitation period, civil claim not concerning land | 7 years | Prescription Law s. 5(1), https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההתיישנות#סעיף_5 (2026-10-16) |
| Limitation period, land | 15 years (25 for land registered after settlement of title) | Prescription Law s. 5(2), same URL (2026-10-16) |
| Arnona objection (השגה) to the arnona director | 90 days from receiving the payment notice | Arnona Appeals Law s. 3(א), https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הרשויות_המקומיות_%28ערר_על_קביעת_ארנונה_כללית%29 (2026-10-16) |
