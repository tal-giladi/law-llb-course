# Where to Get Help

Free and low-cost help for people handling their own legal matters in Israel. Check each service's current eligibility rules before relying on it.

| Help | What it offers | Link |
|---|---|---|
| Legal Aid (Ministry of Justice) | Free representation in civil matters if you pass the subject, means and merit tests; some matters (for example National Insurance) have no means test | https://www.gov.il/he/service/legal_aid_application |
| Public Defender | Defence in criminal cases for eligible defendants; ask the judge at the first hearing | https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/ייצוג_על_ידי_סניגור_ציבורי_בהליך_פלילי |
| Assistance units at family and religious courts (Welfare Ministry) | Intake, information and mediation; not representation | https://www.gov.il/he/departments/units/molsa-court-assiatance-units |
| Kol-Zchut | Plain-language guides to rights and procedures | https://www.kolzchut.org.il |
| University law clinics | Free help in specific fields, depending on the clinic's year and capacity | https://en-law.tau.ac.il/clinics |
