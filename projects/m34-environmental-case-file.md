# Project: an environmental case file

Build the file a careful resident keeps on one environmental problem near home or work: the legal map, the evidence log, the public data, a complaint to the body with the duty, a data request, the held 60-day notices with triggers, and a one-page decision on the next route. Finish with a short, neutral submission on one climate-law question. You will reuse the file structure in the year-4 capstone (module 39).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. The complaint and the FOI request may be sent in your own name; the notices are prepared and held unless a trigger you set is met.

## Brief

Use a real problem of your own. If you prefer not to, use the supplied case.

**Supplied case.** Nadia Haddad and her partner Eitan Shapira live in Nesher, 250 metres from an asphalt plant. Since spring 2026 a heavy tar smell enters their flat on many nights, and on two evenings in July thick smoke came from the plant's stack. Their neighbour Moshe Ohana, 78, has a heart condition and stays indoors with the windows shut. Three calls to the municipal hotline and one online complaint to the Ministry's district office got an automatic reply only. A friend says "there's a register online that shows what the plant emits" and another says "you can sue them yourselves".

Time: about 8 hours.

## Steps

1. **Legal map (45 minutes).** Name the statutes and sections that apply (smell, air pollution, licence or permit), the authorities with a duty (municipality: large or small? town association? Ministry district?), and the citizen tools under each (lessons 34.1–34.3).
2. **Evidence log (two weeks, 10 minutes a day; 30 minutes to assemble).** Dates, times, intensity, where in the home, hotline reference numbers, photos or videos with time, neighbours' statements (lesson 34.2).
3. **Data sheet (60 minutes).** Query the PRTR dataset for the town; list the plant's reported releases to air for the last three years with units, methods and any malfunction entries; add the nearest air-monitoring station and what it measures; note what the data does not show (lesson 34.4).
4. **Document 1: complaint (60 minutes).** Template part A to the body with the duty, with exhibits and a request for a specific action and a reasoned answer within 45 days (lesson 34.8; [environmental complaint and notice](../templates/environmental-complaint-and-notice.md)).
5. **Document 2: FOI request (60 minutes).** Three to five named records with periods, the environmental annex, fee and exemption checked, dates diarised (lessons 34.5, 34.8; [FOI request](../templates/foi-request.md)).
6. **Documents 3 and 4: held notices (60 minutes).** Notice under Civil Claims Law s. 5 to the correct authority and the plant; notice to the Minister for a private complaint if you have standing; each with a written trigger (lesson 34.6).
7. **Decision memo (45 minutes).** One page: which route next (wait, notice and suit, private complaint, tort claim, joint action with neighbours or an organisation, petition against non-enforcement), in which order, and the stop signals for getting a lawyer (lesson 34.6).
8. **Climate submission (45 minutes).** A one-page, sourced submission to the Knesset Interior and Environmental Protection Committee on one question about the Climate Bill, with the strongest opposing argument and your answer, the status dated (lesson 34.7; [letter to a Knesset committee](../templates/letter-to-knesset-committee.md)).

## Self-assessment rubric

| Criterion | Excellent (3) | Adequate (2) | Needs work (1) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Legal map | Each problem matched to statute, section, authority with a duty and citizen tool; 20,000-resident rule applied | Statutes right; duties or tools partly missing | General "environmental law" without sections |
| Evidence | Two weeks logged with times, intensity, reference numbers and statements; videos timed | Log present; gaps in times or numbers | No log or only impressions |
| Data | PRTR rows read correctly (units, method, threshold, malfunction); trend over years; limits of data stated | Rows listed; some reading errors | Data copied without reading, or none |
| Complaint | Facts numbered with exhibits; section cited; specific action and 45-day reasoned answer requested | Clear but general request | No section, no request, no exhibits |
| FOI request | Named existing records with periods; annex cites s. 6א, reg. 2, Clean Air s. 21(ד)(2)(ג); dates diarised | Records named; annex or dates missing | "All information"; opinions requested |
| Notices | Right authority by hazard type; both addressees for s. 5; standing for private complaint checked; triggers written | Notices drafted; one addressee or trigger missing | Notices sent at once or to the wrong body |
| Decision memo | Routes compared with costs, time, remedies and risks; order and stop signals clear | Route chosen with some reasons | No comparison |
| Climate submission | Facts dated and sourced; both sides' best arguments; concrete wording proposal | Sourced position; counter-argument thin | Advocacy without facts or dates |

<details><summary>Model answer (supplied case, outline)</summary>

**Legal map.** Smell: Nuisances Law s. 3; smoke: Clean Air Law s. 3 and the plant's emission permit or business-licence conditions (s. 17 if a permit is required). Nesher has more than 20,000 residents, so it must enforce the listed statutes (Local Authorities (Environmental Enforcement) Law s. 2(א)); the Haifa district of the Ministry and the Haifa-area town association also have powers. Citizen tools: complaint and FOI; Civil Claims Law suit (magistrates' court, after notice to the Minister and the plant); private complaint for an offence in their private domain (Nuisances s. 11ה, Clean Air s. 69) after notice to the Minister; tort (Nuisances s. 13; Clean Air s. 70).

**Data.** A data.gov.il query for נשר returns the plants in the town; for the asphalt plant, list releases to air (for example particulate matter and volatile organic compounds) with units and methods for 2023–2025 and any malfunction entries; the nearest monitoring station's readings for the two July evenings. Gap: the malfunction reports and the Ministry's inspection reports for those dates.

**Complaint.** To the Ministry's Haifa district and the municipality's environmental unit: facts in numbered lines (log, videos of the smoke on the two July evenings with times, Moshe Ohana's statement, hotline numbers); Nuisances s. 3, Clean Air s. 3; request for a night inspection, review of permit or licence conditions, an order under Clean Air s. 45 if a breach is found, the inspection results under FOI s. 6א, and a reasoned answer within 45 days.

**FOI request.** To the Ministry's FOI officer: stack monitoring results reported by the plant for March–September 2026; malfunction reports for the July dates; inspection reports, warning letters, orders and financial sanctions in 2025–2026. Annex: s. 6א, reg. 2, Clean Air s. 21(ד)(2)(ג), s. 10 public interest (a resident with a heart condition), s. 11 partial disclosure.

**Notices.** Part B to the Minister (through the Haifa district) and the plant: the hazard, the July events, the remedy (stop night operations of the unit causing the smell until compliance, install controls, report malfunctions to residents). Part C to the Minister under Nuisances s. 11ה and Clean Air s. 69 (smell and smoke in their home). Trigger: no inspection and no written answer by day 45 of the complaint.

**Decision memo.** First: complaint and FOI now. If the trigger is met: send B and C; on day 61, if no real steps, a Civil Claims Law suit in the Haifa magistrates' court with Nadia and Eitan as plaintiffs and a listed organisation invited to join; the private complaint held as a second line because of the criminal burden of proof. Petition against non-enforcement only if the Ministry or municipality refuses in writing to act after a breach is documented (Bremer). Get a lawyer if the plant responds through counsel with expert evidence, or if neighbours want a group suit.

**Climate submission.** Question: should the bill's targets be enforceable in court? Facts with dates (committee text 3.12.2024; status checked on the Knesset database on the date written). Position, the strongest opposite argument, and one wording proposal (for example, a duty to publish an annual progress report and to explain any shortfall to the committee).
