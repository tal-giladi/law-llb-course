# Project: a criminal-procedure self-protection file

Build the file a careful person keeps to protect himself lawfully in the criminal process: a rights card, a stage map of one matter, a pre-indictment request or a fine-notice response, and a record clean-up plan. You will reuse the file in the year-4 clinic (module 37: defending a traffic ticket, a police complaint).

> [!WARNING]
> This project is about lawful self-protection: knowing your rights, asking for a lawyer and the Public Defender, writing to the prosecution, defending yourself only in minor matters and cleaning up records. It never covers avoiding or obstructing an investigation. If you are a suspect in a real, open matter, do not use it as a substitute for a lawyer; use the supplied case.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. The project is a dry run: nothing is filed, except, if you choose, a real request about your own fine notice or your own police record.

## Brief

Choose either:

- **Your own matter**, if it is closed or minor: a fine notice you received, a closed file in your record, or a past questioning; or
- **The supplied case** below. Say which you used.

**Supplied case.** Shlomit Abu, 33, a nurse from Afula, was stopped on 2 June 2026 at 01:30 near a nightclub after a fight between other people. An officer asked to search her bag "just to check"; she agreed without being told she could refuse; he found nothing. Two weeks later she received a summons "under caution" for assault: a woman claims Shlomit slapped her. Shlomit says she separated two women. A friend filmed part of the event. On 10 September 2026 she received a s. 60א notice from the district attorney's office: "assault causing actual bodily harm in aggravated circumstances" (a felony). Separately, on 1 September 2026 she received a camera traffic notice for speeding on 20 August 2026; she was driving. Her record shows one entry: a 2017 file for "theft" (a misdemeanour), closed for insufficient evidence; she was 24 and had been accused of taking a colleague's phone that turned up later.

Time: about 6 hours.

## Steps

1. **Rights card (30 minutes).** Fill the [suspect's rights card](../templates/suspect-rights-card.md) for yourself or for Shlomit, with real phone numbers of a lawyer and the district Public Defender office.
2. **Stage map (45 minutes).** Place every event of the matter on the stage map of lesson 24.1: who decided, the section, the deadline, and the right available at that stage.
3. **Police powers check (30 minutes).** Assess the bag search under Ben Haim and the Search Ordinance; say what follows (lesson 24.2).
4. **Interrogation plan (30 minutes).** Write what Shlomit should do before and during the questioning under caution: lawyer, language, recording, what not to do (lesson 24.3).
5. **Pre-indictment request (90 minutes).** Draft a s. 60א request with the [pre-indictment request](../templates/pre-indictment-hearing-request.md) template: deadline from receipt, facts supported by the video, legal argument (separating others; no intent to injure), personal circumstances (a nurse: a conviction would affect her licence), outcome and alternatives; mark what a lawyer must check (lessons 24.5, 24.9).
6. **Fine notice decision (30 minutes).** Decide pay / cancel / be tried for the speeding notice, with the deadlines and the reasons; if you choose a request, draft it with the [request to be tried](../templates/request-to-be-tried.md) template (lesson 24.9).
7. **Record plan (45 minutes).** Classify the 2017 entry; decide the route (automatic cancellation? s. 33(א) request? change of ground?) and draft the request with the [police record](../templates/police-record-cancellation-request.md) template (lessons 24.8, 24.10).
8. **Stop signals (15 minutes).** List the points at which you would stop and hand the matter to a lawyer, with the reason.

## Self-assessment rubric

Score each criterion 0–2. A total of 13 or more (out of 16) means the project meets the standard.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rights card | Missing or advises resisting | Generic | Personal numbers, correct sections, no obstruction |
| Stage map | Missing | Stages without sources | Every stage with decision-maker, section and deadline |
| Police powers | Not analysed | Conclusion without law | Ben Haim and the Ordinance applied, consequence stated |
| Interrogation plan | Advises lying or "explaining" alone | Partly correct | Lawyer first, language, recording, silence decided with advice |
| s. 60א request | Missing or a long new statement | Some structure | Deadline, evidence-based facts, law, circumstances, outcome and alternatives, lawyer check |
| Fine notice | Pays without thinking | Right route, wrong deadline | Right route, deadline and consequences (s. 229(ח), s. 230) |
| Record plan | Missing | Route without reason | Correct classification, route, and a reasoned request |
| Stop signals | None | Generic | Concrete, tied to stages |

## Model answer

<details><summary>Model answer</summary>

This model uses the supplied case.

**Stage map (excerpt).** 2 June: detention on the street (Arrests Law s. 67) and a consent search; officer; no deadline. Mid-June: questioning under caution (police; Arrests Law s. 28 caution; Interrogation Law; Public Defender Law s. 19 if arrested). 10 September: s. 60א notice (district attorney; s. 60א(ד) period from receipt, see current numbers; extension by a senior prosecutor). Next: decision under s. 62 / 67א; if indicted, s. 74 inspection, preliminary arguments (s. 149), answer (s. 152). Traffic notice: served 1 September; cancellation 30 days (she was the driver, so not relevant), request to be tried 90 days (s. 229(א)). Record: 2017 police-register entry (closed file).

**Police powers.** No suspicion against Shlomit personally was shown and there is no statutory power to search a passer-by's bag; consent was not informed (she was not told she could refuse), so under Ben Haim the search was not lawfully based on consent. Nothing was found, so there is no evidence to exclude; she may complain to the Police Investigations Department if she wishes, and note it in the s. 60א request only if relevant (it is not).

**Interrogation plan.** Call a criminal lawyer before attending; agree a time; bring ID and phone numbers on paper; at the start ask whether she is a suspect (yes, "under caution"); say she wishes to consult a lawyer before answering; ask whether the questioning is video-recorded (assault with aggravated circumstances may be below the ten-year threshold, so any form of recording is lawful, s. 7(3)); read before signing; no contact with the complainant or witnesses; no deleting messages.

**s. 60א request (outline).** Received 10 September; last day per current numbers; request for an extension and for the video and statements. Facts: the video shows two women fighting and Shlomit pulling one away; no slap is visible in the filmed part. Law: no act of assault, or alternatively defence of another (Penal Law s. 34י) with proportionate force; "actual bodily harm" and aggravated circumstances not shown by the medical report. Circumstances: no convictions; a nurse; a conviction would endanger her licence (letter from the Nursing Division or employer). Outcome: close the file; alternatively a lesser, non-aggravated charge; alternatively a conditional arrangement if the offence qualifies (s. 67א(ג)–(ד)); request to present orally (s. 60א(ח)). Lawyer to check paragraphs on the facts before the filmed part.

**Fine notice.** She was the driver and has no evidence of an error; a trial makes sense only with evidence (e.g. calibration doubts, a wrong vehicle). Served 1 September for an offence on 20 August, within the four-month camera limit (s. 225א(א1)). Decision: pay within the period in the notice, knowing it counts as a conviction (s. 229(ח)) and that traffic offences go to the traffic register, not the criminal register (Criminal Information Law s. 8(ב)–(ג)); or, if she has evidence, ask to be tried within 90 days, knowing the fine will not be lower than the notice without special circumstances (s. 230).

**Record plan.** The 2017 entry: closed file, misdemeanour; seven years from the event passed in 2024, so it should have been cancelled automatically (s. 33(ג)). Request: a short letter citing s. 33(ג) and asking for cancellation and written notice; in addition, since the phone was found and she was innocent, a request to the prosecutor to change the ground to lack of guilt (CPL s. 62(ב)(4)). Re-inspect after the answer.

**Stop signals.** The summons under caution (lawyer before questioning); the s. 60א notice in a felony (lawyer checks the request); any offer of a conditional arrangement or plea bargain; any indictment other than a fine offence; a licensing body already weighing her record.
