# Project: a home planning and arnona file

Build the file a careful owner or tenant keeps on their home: the planning institutions and plans that govern it, its permit status and enforcement exposure, the betterment levy and compensation questions, and a fully checked arnona bill, ending in two real-or-dry-run documents: an objection to a nearby plan and an arnona objection. You will reuse the arnona part in the year-3 clinic (module 31, "An arnona objection from start to finish").

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. Both documents are dry runs unless the plan is deposited now, affects you and is within the objection period, or your arnona bill is wrong and you are within 90 days.

## Brief

Use your own home (owned or rented). If you prefer not to use your own address, use the supplied case.

**Supplied case.** Michal and Yossi Avidan, both 44, and their three children live in a 1980s four-storey building in Kiryat Ata, where they own a 98 m² flat on the top floor. A previous owner built a 14 m² roof room with an internal staircase; the land registry extract shows no notes, but the committee's building file has no permit for it. The local committee recently deposited a plan (in its own power under s. 62א) that changes building lines on the next plot to allow a three-storey kindergarten building 3 metres from their building, instead of 6. The 2026 arnona bill charges 118 m² as residential; Yossi's mother, who receives an old-age pension and income support, moved in with them in May 2026.

Time: about 7 hours.

## Steps

1. **Planning map (30 minutes).** The local committee, district, appeals committee and Planning Administration for the address, with links (lesson 27.1).
2. **Plan profile (60 minutes).** All plans covering the parcel; which governs; designation, rights, height, setbacks; any deposited plan nearby with its deadline (lesson 27.2).
3. **Permit status (60 minutes).** Request or review the building file; list every element of the home (rooms, balconies, roof structures, pergolas, storerooms) and classify it: permitted, exempt, needs a permit, needs relief, cannot be legalised without a plan (lesson 27.4).
4. **Enforcement exposure (30 minutes).** For any unpermitted element: which enforcement tools could apply, the s. 221(א) window, likely fines, and the legalisation route (lesson 27.5).
5. **Money (45 minutes).** Any betterment levy due on a future sale or extension, exemptions, and any compensation claim from a harmful plan with its three-year deadline (lesson 27.6).
6. **Municipal check (20 minutes).** One bylaw and one recent council decision that affect the home (lesson 27.7).
7. **Arnona audit (45 minutes).** The six fields, area, classification, holder, discounts the household may claim (lesson 27.8).
8. **Objection to a plan (90 minutes).** A complete objection with an affidavit to the nearby plan, using the [objection template](../templates/plan-objection.md) (lesson 27.9). If no plan is deposited near you, use the supplied case's kindergarten plan.
9. **Arnona objection (45 minutes).** Part A of the [arnona objection and appeal](../templates/arnona-objection-and-appeal.md), plus a separate discount application if relevant (lesson 27.10).
10. **Stop signals (15 minutes).** Write when you would hand each part to a lawyer or an appraiser, and why.

## Self-assessment rubric

| Criterion | Excellent (3) | Adequate (2) | Needs work (1) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Institutions and plans | All bodies and plans identified with sections and links; governing plan justified | Most identified; hierarchy partly explained | Plans listed without status or governing plan |
| Permit status | Each element classified with a legal source; exemptions' conditions checked against the plan | Classification mostly right; some sources missing | Elements assumed lawful or unlawful without analysis |
| Enforcement and legalisation | Tools, windows, fines and a realistic legalisation route | Tools listed; route vague | No exposure analysis |
| Money | Realisation events, exemptions and deadlines (45 days; three years) correct | Levy understood; compensation thin | Levy or compensation misunderstood |
| Plan objection | Standing, evidence-based grounds, remedies, affidavit, right institution and copy, deadline | Complete but one formal element weak | Missing affidavit, deadline or institution |
| Arnona | Correct grounds, evidence, discount separated, deadlines diarised | Grounds right; evidence thin | Discount or illegality claims mixed into the objection |
| Stop signals | Concrete and tied to stakes | General | Missing |

A score of 15 or more out of 21 is a pass; aim for 18.

<details><summary>Model answer</summary>

**1. Institutions.** Kiryat Ata is a city: its council is the local planning committee (s. 18(א)); Haifa district committee and Haifa appeals committee; plans on the Planning Administration's Mavat system (⚠️ manual check).

**2. Plans.** The governing detailed plan for the building and the newly deposited local-committee plan for the next plot. Because the new plan only changes building lines, it is within s. 62א(א)(4); objections go to the local committee with a copy to the district committee (s. 103(3)), within two months of the later newspaper notice (s. 102).

**3. Permit status.** The roof room has no permit: an addition to the building needs a permit (s. 145(א)(2)); it is not an internal change and not exempt. If the plan has no unused rights, relief cannot add area (s. 151(ב)) and legalisation needs a plan change; if the plan allows roof rooms, a permit (with the co-owners' consents or notices, regs. 36–37) may be possible, and the betterment levy and fees will apply.

**4. Enforcement.** The room was built by a previous owner years ago: an administrative demolition order is unavailable (more than six months since completion; occupied long ago; s. 221(א)). A judicial process or an administrative fine remains possible; the current owners and occupiers may be charged as owner or occupier (s. 243(ג)). Realistic course: seek legalisation, and on a future sale disclose it.

**5. Money.** A future sale is a realisation; if any plan added rights since the last levy, the levy (half of the increase) will be due and the Land Registry needs the committee's certificate (Third Schedule ss. 1, 3, 10). Legalising the roof room through a permit using new rights is a realisation too; the own-home exemption covers building up to a total of 140 m² (98 + 14 = 112 m²: within it). The kindergarten plan may reduce value (light, noise): a s. 197 claim within three years of its commencement, against a likely s. 200 defence.

**6. Municipal.** For example the city's bylaw on maintaining buildings' façades, and the council's decision approving kindergarten construction; council minutes on the city's website (s. 248ב).

**7. Arnona.** The bill charges 118 m² against 98 m² + 14 m² = 112 m² (if the roof room is counted) or a different measurement method: ask for the measurement sheet and object on size (s. 3(א)(2)) within 90 days. Note that arnona charges by actual area and use regardless of permits; reporting the roof room for arnona does not legalise it. Yossi's mother's income support does not give the household the senior discount unless she is the holder (reg. 17); check the income-test discount for the household (reg. 2(א)(8)) by application.

**8. Plan objection.** Standing: owners in the adjoining building (s. 100). Grounds: reduced light and privacy from a three-storey building 3 m away; noise and drop-off traffic; legal ground: verify that the changes are only building lines within s. 62א(א)(4) and that the notice to adjoining owners was given (s. 89א(ד) applies to plans up to 3,000 m²). Remedies: building line of at least 5 m; play yard on the far side; drop-off bay on the main street. Affidavit; copy to the district committee with proof.

**9. Arnona objection.** Part A on size with a surveyor's report; separate discount application on Form 1 if the income test fits.

**10. Stop signals.** Legalising the roof room (designer and, if enforcement starts, a lawyer); any enforcement notice; a sale with the unpermitted room; a s. 197 claim (appraiser and lawyer).

</details>
