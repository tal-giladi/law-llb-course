# Project: a household payments, credit and consumer file

Build the file a careful household keeps on its commercial life: every cheque and note it has signed, every pledge on its assets, its bank fees, its loans and credit data, and its open consumer disputes, and end with two documents ready to send or file: a reasoned answer to a bounced-cheque demand (or a s. 10 appeal outline) and a written request plus small claim for exemplary damages. You will reuse the debt and credit parts in the year-3 clinic (module 31, "Defending an execution file and reaching an arrangement").

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. Both final documents are dry runs unless you have a live dispute and are within the relevant period.

## Brief

Use your own household. If you prefer not to, use the supplied case.

**Supplied case.** Avraham and Ruth Mekonen, of Rehovot, run a small catering business from home as sole traders. They gave a hall owner three post-dated cheques of ₪4,500 for a season's rental; the hall closed after one month, and the hall owner endorsed the second cheque to a cheque-discounting company five weeks after its date, when the hall had already closed. Their joint account had seven refused cheques in the last ten months. They are buying a used refrigerated van from a company in Lod for ₪96,000. Their bank charges a monthly "business account" fee although the account is registered as private, and a branch clerk said a ₪50,000 loan would need a deposit plan. Last year they took a ₪20,000 loan through an app (₪1,600 deducted up front), now in arrears; the lender's lawyer demands the whole balance plus ₪2,000 "collection fees". A blender supplier refused to refund a distance purchase cancelled on day 6, and a mobile operator charged them for three months after they cancelled.

Time: about 8 hours.

## Steps

1. **Instruments register (45 minutes).** List every cheque, note and "security cheque" signed in the last two years: holder, purpose, date, completeness, what to ask back or limit (lesson 29.1).
2. **Holder analysis (45 minutes).** For each cheque in dispute, the cheque's journey and the four-question test; decide pay, arrange or defend (lesson 29.2).
3. **Restriction status (30 minutes).** The twelve-month count under s. 1, the warning stage, the emergency-regulation check, and what you would do on the next same-day notice (lesson 29.3).
4. **Pledges (45 minutes).** Searches for every vehicle you own or will buy, by every criterion and register (including the Companies Registrar for a company seller); payoff and release plan (lesson 29.4; [pledge and vehicle purchase check](../templates/pledge-and-vehicle-purchase-check.md)).
5. **Bank audit (45 minutes).** Fee table against the tariff and your track; tying or refusal issues; the letter to the bank's public-enquiries officer (lesson 29.5; [regulator complaint](../templates/regulator-complaint.md)).
6. **Credit check (45 minutes).** For each loan, the real cost on the amount received versus the cap at signing; acceleration and collection-cost check; your free credit-data report and any correction request (lesson 29.7).
7. **Consumer disputes (30 minutes).** Each dispute classified: listed s. 31א breach, non-listed misleading, or neither (lesson 29.6).
8. **Document 1 (75 minutes).** Part A of the [bounced-cheque response kit](../templates/bounced-cheque-response-kit.md) for the disputed cheque, and Part B if you are restricted or close to it (lesson 29.8).
9. **Document 2 (90 minutes).** The [written request](../templates/consumer-exemplary-damages-request.md) and a five-page [small claim](../templates/small-claim-contract-consumer.md) with separate heads (lesson 29.9).
10. **Stop signals and diary (30 minutes).** Every deadline (opposition, appeal, limitation, request deadlines) in one diary, and when you would hand each part to a lawyer.

## Self-assessment rubric

| Criterion | Excellent (3) | Adequate (2) | Needs work (1) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instruments | Every cheque and note listed with holder, purpose and risk; protective steps identified | Most listed; risks partly explained | List incomplete; no protective steps |
| Holder analysis | Four-question test applied with ss. 28–29, 37, Tal Trading and Fair Credit Law s. 11 where relevant | Test applied; one element missing | Defences asserted without addressing the holder |
| Restriction | Correct count under s. 1 with reasons; emergency rules checked; next steps clear | Count right; reasons thin | Stop orders treated as removing cheques from the count |
| Pledges | All registers searched, dated; safe payment sequence | Search done; sequence partly unsafe | No search or seller paid first |
| Bank and credit | Fees matched to tariff and sections; real cost computed on amount received; acceleration and costs tested; credit report reviewed | Mostly right; one computation off | Nominal rate compared with the cap; fees challenged in general |
| Consumer claim | Request sent first; one exemplary head per listed breach; non-listed misleading pleaded with reliance; s. 31א(ה) factors argued | Heads separated; factors thin | Lump sum; no request; non-listed breach claimed as exemplary |
| Diary and stop signals | Every deadline computed from the right trigger; concrete stop signals | Most deadlines; general stop signals | Deadlines missing |

<details><summary>Model answer (supplied case)</summary>

**Instruments.** Three hall cheques (one paid, one with the discount company, one still with the hall owner): stop the third and tell the hall owner in writing why; keep the account covered when stopping. No notes signed except the app loan's note: record it.

**Holder analysis.** The discount company took the second cheque five weeks after its date and after the hall closed: it is arguable that the cheque was overdue (s. 35(ג), an unreasonable time in circulation) and that the company had notice of the defect, so it is not a holder in due course (s. 28(א)); the Mekonens must bring these facts, since status is presumed (s. 29(ב)). If they succeed, after Tal Trading the Mekonens' defence of failure of consideration (eleven months of rental not given) is open against it. At most, they owe the value of the one month used, pro rata. Answer in writing and oppose any execution file within the period.

**Restriction.** Seven refused cheques in ten months: past the warning stage (the warning comes after the fifth). Two more refusals within twelve months, with the days spread, would restrict the joint account and both owners. Stopping the hall cheques must be done with a covered account; on the next same-day notice, deposit before the stated time.

**Pledges.** Search the van by licence number at the Pledges Registrar and the seller company at the Companies Registrar (company charges are registered there). Pay any pledgee directly from the price against a payoff letter, check the cancellation, then pay the balance.

**Bank.** A business-account fee on a private account is a fee not per the tariff or the agreed track (ss. 9י–9י2): demand a refund. Conditioning the loan on a deposit plan is tying unless there is a reasonable business connection (s. 7). Letter to the bank, then the Bank of Israel; diary limitation.

**Credit.** Real cost computed on ₪18,400 received, not ₪20,000, including the ₪1,600 as an addition; compare with the cap at signing. Acceleration lawful only after a warning with the minimum period and itemised arrears (s. 7); ₪2,000 collection fees only if reasonable, incurred and agreed (s. 7א). In execution, ask the registrar to refuse or limit enforcement (s. 10); the note's holder is subject to the Law (s. 11). Order the free credit-data report.

**Consumer.** Blender: distance-sale cancellation within the period; not refunding is listed (s. 31א(א)(4)). Mobile operator: charging after cancellation is listed (s. 31א(א)(2ב)). One written request each, then two small claims, each with refund and one exemplary head, argued with the s. 31א(ה) factors. Note: as sole traders, check that each purchase was a consumer purchase for personal use; business purchases are outside the Law.

**Stop signals.** A lawyer if the discount company sues for a large sum with counsel, if the restriction becomes aggravated, if the van seller's bank refuses to release a floating charge, or if the app lender proves to be unlicensed.
</details>
