# Module 37 project: Clinic IV case file: two public or criminal matters of your own from file to result

Run at least two public or criminal matters of your own through the year-4 clinic: an administrative matter (to the prior demand, and to a petition if the filters support it), a police complaint and its follow-up, a disputed traffic ticket on the right track, a police record you want removed, or a complaint to an oversight body. You open the file with an exposure check, a decision-maker map and an official-records log, order the records, make each decision at its decision point, file in your own name where the step is low-risk and real, follow the answer, prepare for any hearing, and close with a memo and a get-a-lawyer decision. Time: about 12 hours spread over several weeks (most of it waiting for answers).

> [!WARNING]
> Police and criminal matters only in your own name and only as the complainant, the person fined, or the person the record is about. If you are or may become a suspect, or anything you would write may admit an offence, stop and get a lawyer or the Public Defender.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing anything.

## Brief

**Matter 1 (everyone): a low-risk real filing.** Either a record matter (inspection of your own criminal information, and a cancellation or change-of-ground request if an entry exists), or an oversight complaint (Ombudsman, Judicial Ombudsman, or another body from lesson 37.6) about a delay or omission you have documented. If you have neither, inspect your own criminal information anyway and record the result: a clean screen is a result.

**Matter 2 (choose one): a contested matter.** An administrative decision against you (prior demand, then a petition or a reasoned decision not to file), a police complaint about an offence against you (from the complaint to the closure and the right next step), or a traffic ticket you dispute (track, route, document, hearing plan). It must pass the five filters of lesson 37.1.

**Supplied case (if you have no suitable Matter 2).** Supplied case A from the lessons: Ilana Mizrahi, 58, Ashdod, uses a wheelchair and holds a disabled parking card and a vehicle registered for her own use; her building has no parking. She asked the municipality on 10 July 2026 for a reserved space near her home. A letter dated 20 September, received by email on 24 September, refuses "under the municipality's policy not to mark reserved spaces in streets where buildings have private parking". Assume that her prior demand of 26 September is answered on 8 October with "the matter will be re-examined next month", and that after she files, the municipality's answer includes an engineer's affidavit that a free public disabled space exists "within the permitted distance".

## Steps

1. **Intake (60 min).** Five-filter table for your candidates; choose Matters 1 and 2. Open the [clinic case file](../templates/clinic-case-file.md) with the year-2 additions (track sheet, deadline table with proof of each start date, relationship note) and the year-4 additions: exposure check, decision-maker map, official-records log (lesson 37.1).
2. **Records (30 min plus waiting).** Order what each matter needs: the decision and its reasons and an [FOI request](../templates/foi-request.md); the closure notice and the investigation material; the ticket file; an inspection of your criminal information. Log each request.
3. **Matter 1 filing (90 min).** The record request on the [cancellation and change-of-ground template](../templates/police-record-cancellation-request.md) (lesson 37.5), or the complaint on the [oversight-body complaint](../templates/oversight-body-complaint.md) after writing to the body first (lesson 37.6). File it; keep the confirmation.
4. **Matter 2 filing (180 min).** By matter: the [prior demand](../templates/objection-to-administrative-decision.md) and, if the decision point says file, the [administrative petition](../templates/administrative-petition.md) with affidavit, fee or exemption, service and proof of service (lesson 37.2); the [police complaint](../templates/police-complaint-victim.md), the follow-up log, and on closure the [appeal](../templates/appeal-against-closing-file.md) or a request to resume (lesson 37.3); the track decision and the [administrative traffic fine objection or appeal](../templates/traffic-administrative-fine-objection-and-appeal.md) or the [request to be tried](../templates/request-to-be-tried.md) (lesson 37.4). File it if your filters, deadlines and exposure check support it; otherwise record why it stays a dry run.
5. **The answer (60 min, when it arrives).** Record the delivery date; compare with what you asked; decide at the decision point: accept, chase, reconsider, appeal, petition, re-inspect.
6. **Hearing plan (60 min, if a hearing is set or likely).** The [hearing-day kit](../templates/hearing-day-kit.md) with a two-minute opening, the three likely questions, a cross-examination request or plan, skeleton arguments within the period, and the proposal sheet (lesson 37.7).
7. **Closing (45 min).** The closing checklist of lesson 37.8; chase every gap; the six-part closing memo per matter with surviving dates.
8. **Lawyer decision (30 min).** Run the [get-a-lawyer checklist](../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md) with the year-4 red flags at three points (before filing, on the answer, at closing); a one-page summary if needed; the free help that fits.
9. **Reflection (20 min).** Half a page: what you did for real, what stayed a dry run and why, what you would do differently.

## Self-assessment rubric

| Criterion | Weak (1) | Adequate (2) | Strong (3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intake and exposure | Matter not tested; no exposure check | Filters applied; exposure check done | Filters with reasons; exposure check, decision-maker map and records log complete |
| Deadlines | Missing, or counted from the wrong event | Main deadline computed | Every door-closing period with its trigger, proof and reminder; the petition period from the earliest trigger |
| Track and forum | Wrong body or wrong track (for example, a request to be tried on an administrative ticket) | Right body | Right body and track, with the reason written; the alternatives ruled out |
| Documents | Generic or unsupported | Complete on the template | Every fact with an annex or affidavit; a concrete remedy or request; filed with proof (or a reasoned dry run) |
| The answer | Not followed | Logged | Compared line by line; the right response chosen within its period |
| Hearing | Not prepared | Opening written | Skeleton and any cross-examination request within their periods; proposal sheet decided in advance |
| Closing | Not done | Outcome recorded | The result itself checked (the sign, the screen, the points); surviving dates diarised; memo someone else could use |
| Lawyer decision and own-name discipline | Not done; acted for a relative | Checklist run once | Run at three points with year-4 red flags; every document signed by the person whose matter it is |

A strong file scores at least 20 of 24, with no criterion at 1.

<summary>Model answer (supplied case A, key points)</summary>

1. **Intake.** Ilana's own decision; exposure: none. Decision-maker map: the head of the traffic department decided; reviewers: the municipality (reconsideration), the district court sitting in administrative matters for the Southern District (First Schedule item 8(א); reg. 2) (confirm the district with the registry), the Ombudsman for delay only. Track: no statutory objection route, so a petition is the backstop.
2. **Deadline.** Trigger: 24 September (receipt by email; nothing published earlier). The petition period from that date; reminder ten days before the last day ([current numbers](../references/current-numbers.md)). The reconsideration request of 26 September does not stop it.
3. **Records.** FOI and reasons request for the written policy and any inspection report; information about herself, so no application fee.
4. **Prior demand.** Facts with annexes (card, vehicle registration, photographs of the building); Parking for Persons with Disabilities Law s. 4ד(א), the period in s. 4ד(ג) already passed, s. 4ד(ו) inapplicable (no private parking); a policy cannot override a statutory duty; Berman: a request to be heard; an answer within ten days; notice of a petition.
5. **Decision point.** The 8 October answer ("will be re-examined next month") is not a decision and has no date: file.
6. **Petition.** Respondent: the municipality. Grounds in order: breach of statutory duty; a policy contrary to the statute; no hearing. Remedies: an order to allocate a space within 30 days; alternatively, a decision within 30 days after hearing her; costs. Affidavit separating knowledge from belief. Fee or exemption. Served on the municipality's legal department the next day; proof of service filed the same day (reg. 7(ב)).
7. **Answer and hearing.** The engineer's affidavit is the deciding fact: Ilana measures the distance and route, and files a written request to cross-examine within the reg. 15(ב) period. Skeleton within the reg. 14(א) period: two pages, three authorities. Proposal sheet: accept a return to the authority within 30 days with an on-site measurement in her presence and costs; refuse a withdrawal without costs.
8. **Outcome and closing.** The court records the municipality's undertaking to decide again after an on-site measurement; the space is allocated and the sign installed; costs paid. Closing memo with the dates; surviving date: a check after a year that the space is still marked and enforced.
9. **Lawyer decision.** No red flag: no interim order needed, no third party, no expert dispute beyond a measurement. Amber only if the municipality had raised threshold objections she could not answer; then a disability-rights clinic.
10. **Matter 1 (Ilana).** She inspects her own criminal information at a station: no entries. Recorded in the file with the date.
