# Project: your Bar-style review file

Build the file a serious candidate keeps while preparing for the Bar exam: a subject map with your diagnostic scores, a dated revision plan, an error log that names the rule and section behind every mistake, two timed writing tasks, a full mock with a weighted score, and a short retrospective. If you are not heading for the Bar, the same file is a structured self-test of the whole course and shows you which areas of your own legal affairs you understand least.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This project is self-study. It does not qualify you to sit the Bar exam, which requires a recognised LL.B. and a completed 12-month internship (lesson 35.1). Nothing in it is filed or sent.

## Brief

Use your real situation: a law student or intern planning for a sitting, or a learner of this course who wants a final self-test. If you prefer, use the supplied case.

**Supplied case.** Liron Avraham, 27, from Ashdod, finished her LL.B. and her internship ends in five months. She works full time and can study 12 hours a week. In a cold diagnostic she scored: jurisdiction 60%, civil procedure 55%, criminal procedure 40%, evidence 50%, execution and insolvency 35%, discipline 50%; contracts 75%, torts 70%, limitation 45%, property 60%, succession 40%, bills and pledges 45%, companies 55%, real-estate tax 30%, criminal law 65%, Basic Laws 70%, ethics 50%. She has never written a pleading against the clock.

Time: about 12 hours spread over two to three weeks (the mock alone takes about six).

## Steps

1. **The rules of the game (30 minutes).** From lesson [38.1](../lessons/module-38/lesson-01.md), write one page: the three parts and weights, the subjects of reg. 18א, the pass mark and its exceptions, the appeal window, and what you could and could not verify about the next sitting (⚠️ items).
2. **Diagnostic (75 minutes).** Take the lesson quizzes of 38.2–38.6 cold and record a score per reg. 18א subject.
3. **Subject map and plan (45 minutes).** One table: subject, diagnostic score, lessons to revise (from the subject map in 38.1), weeks allocated, drill date. Weakest subjects first; the writing task twice; the mock at the end.
4. **Drills and error log (5 hours).** Do the five 40-minute drills (38.2–38.6). Log every wrong or guessed answer in the error log below, then retest the logged questions after 48 hours.
5. **Writing tasks (2 hours).** Do both tasks of lesson [38.7](../lessons/module-38/lesson-07.md) under time and mark them with the rubric (content, organisation, language).
6. **Mock (6 hours, one day).** Sit the full mock in lesson [38.8](../lessons/module-38/lesson-08.md); compute the weighted score.
7. **Retrospective (30 minutes).** One page: the three subjects that still leak points, the rule types you confuse, your pacing, and the next three actions with dates.

## Self-assessment rubric

| Criterion | Excellent (3) | Adequate (2) | Needs work (1) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rules of the game | Parts, weights, subjects, pass mark and exceptions, appeal window, all with regulation numbers; unverified items flagged | Mostly complete; some numbers missing | General description without sources |
| Plan | Every reg. 18א subject with score, lessons, weeks; weakest first; dated drills and mock | Plan exists but not by weakness | No dates or no subject coverage |
| Error log | Every error has the section, lesson and trap; retests done and recorded | Errors logged by topic only | Few or no entries |
| Writing tasks | Both under time; heading, summary, detail and remedies complete; rubric applied honestly | One task done or over time | Not done or not marked |
| Mock | All parts under time; weighted score; error count by subject | Score computed; no subject breakdown | Not completed |
| Retrospective | Concrete leaks, pacing data and dated next steps | General lessons learned | Missing |
