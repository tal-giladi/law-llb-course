# Module 31 project: Clinic III case file: your estate plan and one property or money matter from file to result

Run your own estate plan and at least one property, estate or money matter of your own through the year-3 clinic: a succession order and the registration of heirs in land, a tax refund with any objection, the defence of an execution file to a payment order, or an arnona objection. You open the file with an asset and title register and a money ledger, order the official records, make each decision at its decision point, file in your own name where the step is low-risk and real, follow the answer, and close with a memo and a get-a-lawyer decision. Time: about 12 hours spread over several weeks (most of it waiting for answers).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing anything.

## Brief

**Part 1 (everyone): your estate plan.** Your own will, written and deposited, or a written decision with reasons why you will make a witnessed will or go to a lawyer; and the completed preparation worksheet for your enduring power of attorney, with a trained lawyer chosen from the Administrator General's database.

**Part 2 (choose one): a property or money matter.** It must pass the five filters of lesson 31.1: it is yours (or an estate in which you are an heir); you can name its office; every fatal deadline is at least two weeks away; you hold or can order the records; the risk is manageable. In an estate, other heirs sign their own documents; use initials for them in anything you share.

**Supplied case (if you have no suitable Part 2 matter).** Supplied case B from the lessons: Liat Sasson, Petah Tikva; mother Esther died in 2019, father Moshe on 3 August 2026, neither with a will; Liat and her brother Eitan are the only children; the flat is registered "משה ששון ½, אסתר ששון ½"; Moshe's account holds ₪96,000; a 2014 car. Assume that both orders are given in December 2026, that the registry first asks for Moshe's order before registering Esther's, and that in February 2027 Eitan offers to sell Liat his half for ₪1,050,000.

## Steps

1. **Intake (60 min).** Five-filter table for your candidates; choose Part 2. Open the [clinic case file](../templates/clinic-case-file.md) with the year-2 additions (track sheet, deadline table with proof of each start date, relationship note) and the year-3 additions (asset and title register; money ledger) (lesson 31.1).
2. **Records (30 min plus waiting).** Order what the matter needs: a current land extract; Form 106 for each open year; the execution-file printout; the arnona measurement sheet; death certificates. Log each request.
3. **Estate plan (150 min).** Decision 1 (form of will), the outside-the-will check of every fund and policy, the will by hand with the [handwritten will template](../templates/handwritten-will.md), deposit and certificate; the [enduring power of attorney worksheet](../templates/enduring-poa-preparation-worksheet.md) parts A to G; the lawyer chosen with a written fee quote (lesson 31.2).
4. **The Part 2 filing (180 min).** By matter: the [succession order checklist](../templates/succession-order-application-checklist.md) with a share table for every estate (lesson 31.3) and the [land-registry heirs-registration checklist](../templates/land-registry-heirs-registration-checklist.md) (lesson 31.4); the refund requests, oldest year first, with the [tax refund request](../templates/tax-refund-request.md) (lesson 31.5); the release, "I have paid" and payment-order requests with the [execution debtor response kit](../templates/execution-debtor-response-kit.md) (lesson 31.6); the objection and s. 325 notice with the [arnona template](../templates/arnona-objection-and-appeal.md) (lesson 31.7). File it if your filters and deadlines support it; otherwise record why it stays a dry run.
5. **The answer (60 min, when it arrives).** Record the delivery date; compare it with what you asked; decide: accept, chase, object (the [tax objection](../templates/tax-assessment-objection.md) within 30 days), appeal (arnona within 30 days), ערר (Land Registry), or the paying-debtor request after three payments.
6. **Closing (60 min).** The closing checklist of lesson 31.8; chase every gap; the six-part closing memo per matter with surviving dates.
7. **Lawyer decision (30 min).** Run the [get-a-lawyer checklist](../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md) with the year-3 red flags at three points (before filing, on the answer, at closing); one-page summary if needed; the free help that fits.
8. **Reflection (20 min).** Half a page: what you did for real, what stayed a dry run and why, what you would do differently.

## Self-assessment rubric

| Criterion | Weak (1) | Adequate (2) | Strong (3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intake and records | Matter not tested; no records ordered | Filters applied; main records held | Filters with reasons; asset and title register and money ledger complete; every record ordered and logged |
| Deadlines | Missing or counted from the wrong event | Main deadline computed | Every money-losing deadline with its start event, proof and reminder; oldest refund year or earliest estate dealt with first |
| Estate plan | No will or plan | Will written; worksheet started | Will meets s. 19 and is deposited; designations aligned; worksheet complete with a qualified attorney, informed person and objective test |
| Part 2 filing | Wrong office or incomplete | Right office and form | Right office, complete documents, correct shares or figures, filed with proof (or a reasoned dry run) |
| The answer | Not followed | Logged | Compared line by line; the right response chosen within its period |
| Closing | Not done | Outcome recorded | Every gap chased; surviving dates diarised; memo someone else could use |
| Lawyer decision | Not done | Checklist run once | Run at three points with year-3 red flags; summary ready; free help identified |
| Own-name discipline | Acted for a relative | Mostly own name | Every document signed by the person whose matter it is; others only helped to understand |

A strong file scores at least 20 of 24, with no criterion at 1.

<summary>Model answer (supplied case B, key points)</summary>

1. **Intake.** Liat's as an heir of both parents; Eitan signs his own documents. Offices: the Registrar of Inheritance for the deceased's domicile (reg. 13), then the Land Registry office for Petah Tikva. Records: both death certificates; current electronic extract; bank letter; car licence. Relationship note: Eitan wants money, not the flat; Liat wants to keep the flat for now.
2. **Shares.** Esther's estate: Moshe ½ (with the household movables, s. 11(א)(1)), Liat ¼, Eitan ¼ of her half, so Moshe ¼, Liat ⅛, Eitan ⅛ of the flat. Moshe's estate: his ½ + ¼ = ¾ to Liat and Eitan equally, ⅜ each. Result: ½ each.
3. **Orders.** Two online applications (children; heirs are a spouse and children); online fee and publication charge for each ([current numbers](../references/current-numbers.md)); Moshe listed as an heir of Esther with his date of death; notice to Eitan for each, sent by registered mail and email, receipt logged; objection period diarised from publication. No renunciation: Eitan wants value, and a renunciation would be a gift.
4. **Land.** Registration requested with Moshe's online application; Esther's order registered on paper at the Petah Tikva office, request signed by Liat before an official, fee per [current numbers](../references/current-numbers.md). The registry first asks for Moshe's order (Esther's order lists him as an heir); supplied; no ערר needed. New extract: Liat ½, Eitan ½. Municipality told of the new holders.
5. **Money.** Bank account released to both heirs on the bank's form, signed by each. Car transferred at the Licensing Office after the order.
6. **The buy-out.** Eitan's ₪1,050,000 offer is a sale between co-owners: a land transaction with purchase and appreciation tax declarations (lessons 25.6, 25.7). Red flag. Liat books one consultation with a real-estate lawyer, bringing the one-page summary and both orders; she signs nothing before it. Eitan is told he should take his own advice.
7. **Closing memo.** Outcome: two orders (numbers, dates); registration (date); money: account divided; documents: orders, extract, bank letter, filed at the front. Surviving dates: the buy-out consultation; arnona notice in both names; no creditors' notice published (decision recorded). "Next time: tick the land-registration request in every online application."
8. **Estate plan (Part 1, Liat).** Default picture: her husband and two children under s. 11; she writes a handwritten will giving her half of the flat to her children, with her husband's right to live in it; deposits it (first deposit free); changes her pension designation from her parents to her husband and children; fills the enduring-power worksheet (husband as attorney, sister as substitute, a friend as informed person, a specialist's opinion as the test).
