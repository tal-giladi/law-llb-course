# Module 13 project: Property file: buying a flat and living in the building

Build the property file a careful buyer and flat owner keeps: a pre-purchase check of one flat, a priority analysis, a caveat plan, a co-ownership plan and a condominium claim. It applies every lesson in module 13 to one property. Use a flat of your own, of your family, or one you are considering; otherwise use the supplied case. It is a dry run: you sign and file nothing unless you decide to, in your own name. Time: about 6 hours.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

## Brief

Choose one flat in a building of several flats. You will (a) check it as if you were buying it, (b) decide who would win in two priority conflicts, (c) plan the caveat, (d) plan how two co-buyers will own it, and (e) prepare one building dispute for the superintendent.

**Supplied case.** Tamar Levi-Nasser and her brother Yoni Nasser, from Kiryat Motzkin, plan to buy together a three-room flat on the third floor of a 12-flat building in Haifa from Boris Kaplan for ₪1.45 million. Tamar will live in it; Yoni invests. The agent says the flat "comes with a parking space and the roof storage room". A current extract (you must imagine it from these facts) shows: sub-parcel 9; owner Boris Kaplan (whole); a first-rank mortgage to a bank (2018); a caveat from February 2026 in favour of "Lior Ben-Ami"; attachments: storage room 9א only. The building has the model bylaws. The committee says flat 9 owes ₪2,400 in fees and that Boris locked the roof door "to protect his storage room". Boris says Lior was a buyer who "disappeared" after a deposit.

## Steps

1. **Rights map (20 min).** What exactly is being sold: the flat, its share in the common property, the attachments; what is not (the parking, the roof). Sections 52, 55, 62 (lessons 13.1, 13.6).
2. **Registry check (60 min).** On the [apartment purchase check (draft)](../templates/apartment-purchase-check.md): every section of the extract, its meaning and the action required (lessons 13.2, 13.9).
3. **Beyond the extract (30 min).** The questions for the municipality, planning, the committee and (if relevant) the Israel Land Authority (lesson 13.9).
4. **Priority (45 min).** Two scenarios: (a) Lior reappears and claims the flat; (b) after Tamar and Yoni sign and pay 30% without a caveat, Boris's creditor attaches the flat. Apply s. 9, the caveat rules and the Ganz and Aharonov lines (lessons 13.3, 13.4).
5. **Caveat plan (30 min).** On the [caveat request checklist (draft)](../templates/caveat-request-checklist.md): applicant, undertaking, attachments, verification, timing, deletion plan (lesson 13.4).
6. **Contract conditions (30 min).** Five clauses in Hebrew tying payments to the cures: the bank payoff, the Lior caveat, the parking, the fees, the roof door.
7. **Co-ownership plan (40 min).** Tamar and Yoni's shares, use and payment for use, expenses, the right to sell a share, first refusal, and what happens if one wants out (ss. 27–37, 99; lesson 13.5). A one-page co-ownership agreement outline.
8. **The building dispute (60 min).** After the purchase, the roof door is still locked by Boris's tenant in the storage room next to it (assume Boris kept another flat in the building). Prepare the committee's claim to the superintendent on the [superintendent claim template (draft)](../templates/superintendent-claim.md): forum and jurisdiction, parties, reg. 80 particulars, facts with exhibits, law, remedies (lessons 13.6, 13.7, 13.10).
9. **Decision (15 min).** One paragraph: sign, sign with conditions, or walk away; and when you would get a lawyer.

## Self-assessment rubric

| Criterion | Weak (1) | Adequate (2) | Strong (3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rights map | "The flat with parking" | Flat and attachments listed | Flat, share, attachments, and what is not included, each with a section |
| Registry check | Ownership only | All sections read | Every entry with meaning, action and who cures it |
| Beyond the extract | None | Some sources | Municipality, planning, committee, ILA questions specific to the flat |
| Priority | Answer without rule | Right rule, thin reasoning | s. 9 and the attachment line applied step by step, with Ganz and the caveat's effect |
| Caveat plan | Not drafted | Applicant and type | Undertaking, attachments, verification, timing and deletion plan |
| Contract conditions | Generic | Some linked to findings | Each payment tied to a specific cure |
| Co-ownership plan | Shares only | Shares and expenses | Shares, use, payment for use, sale, first refusal, exit |
| Superintendent claim | Narrative | Most parts | Jurisdiction justified, reg. 80 particulars, exhibits, precise remedies |

A strong file scores at least 20 of 24, with no criterion at 1.

## Model answer

<details><summary>Model answer</summary>

**Rights map.** Being sold: sub-parcel 9, its share in the common property (s. 55(א)), and the attached storage room 9א (s. 55(ג)). Not being sold: a parking space (no attachment) and the roof (common property, s. 52). "The roof storage room" is 9א only if the plan shows 9א on the roof; check the plan. Attaching parking now needs all owners' consent (s. 62(א)).

**Registry check.** Owner: Boris alone, fine. Mortgage: first rank, 2018: payoff letter from the bank; part of the price paid directly to it against a release undertaking. Caveat for Lior Ben-Ami, February 2026: a registered undertaking for the same flat; blocks any contradicting transfer without Lior's consent or a court order (s. 127(א)); must be deleted (s. 132) before any payment beyond a small deposit held in trust. Attachments: 9א only. No attachment or receiver notes today, but order a fresh extract on each payment day.

**Beyond the extract.** Municipality: permits for anything on the roof (if 9א is a roof room, was it permitted?), arnona debt. Planning: TAMA 38 or urban renewal plans for the building. Committee: confirm the ₪2,400 arrears in writing and any planned levies; pending cases before the superintendent. ILA: check whether the land is ILA land and the lease is registered.

**Priority (a): Lior.** Lior's undertaking (February) precedes Tamar and Yoni's. Under s. 9 they could win only if they acted in good faith, for value, and registered while in good faith; they already know of Lior's caveat, so they cannot be in good faith as to the flat, and in any case his caveat blocks their registration (s. 127(א)). Ganz does not help them: Lior did register a caveat. Result: do not buy until Lior consents to deletion or a court orders it (Boris must sue if Lior's contract was lawfully cancelled).

**Priority (b): attachment after 30% without a caveat.** An attaching creditor is outside s. 9; the Aharonov line generally prefers a buyer whose contract and payment preceded the attachment, but the buyers' failure to register a caveat will be argued against them, and they paid only 30%. Their position is arguable, not safe. Lesson: register a caveat the day the contract is signed (s. 127(ב) would then have protected them).

**Caveat plan.** Joint request by Boris (obligor) and Tamar and Yoni (beneficiaries) signed on the contract day; verification by their lawyer (or before the registrar); attachments: the contract (or the commissioner's waiver for a joint request), IDs, fee receipt; type: "התחייבות למכירת זכויות" on sub-parcel 9 including 9א. Order a new extract three working days later. Deletion: automatically with registration of the transfer; if the contract is cancelled, the buyers sign a deletion request within 14 days (reg. 22), as the contract will say.

**Contract conditions (gist).** (1) No payment beyond 10% (held in trust) until the Lior caveat is deleted and a fresh extract shows it. (2) The bank's payoff amount is paid directly to the bank against its undertaking to delete the mortgage. (3) The buyers' caveat is signed today; deletion mechanism if cancelled. (4) The seller pays the ₪2,400 arrears before handover; the committee's confirmation is a condition of the last payment. (5) The sale does not include parking; the price reflects this, or the seller obtains a registered attachment (unlikely) before closing.

**Co-ownership plan.** Shares by contribution (say Tamar 40%, Yoni 60%), registered; a written co-ownership agreement (registrable under s. 29): Tamar has exclusive use and pays Yoni a monthly sum as payment for use (s. 33) or the parties agree she pays the mortgage instead; expenses by shares unless agreed otherwise (s. 32); each may sell his share (s. 34) but they register a mutual right of first refusal (s. 99); no-dissolution clause for three years at most (s. 37(ב)); an exit formula (valuer, buy-out within 90 days, then agreed sale).

**Superintendent claim.** Plaintiff: the representative committee (s. 73(2)). Defendant: Boris as owner of the other flat (and his tenant, as a party to the dispute, s. 72(ג)). Jurisdiction: blocking access to the roof is trespass on common property by an owner: s. 72(ב), the committee chooses the superintendent. Particulars: address, block and parcel, one building, 12 flats, one entrance (reg. 80). Facts: the roof is common property (concentrated extract: no attachment); the door was locked on a date; demand letter unanswered; photographs. Law: ss. 52, 55; model bylaws; Roker. Remedies: an order to remove the lock and hand keys to the committee within 7 days; an interim order for access for maintenance; costs. Fee as a magistrates' civil claim; appeal period in reg. 83.

**Decision.** Do not sign until the Lior caveat is resolved. If it is deleted, sign with conditions (1)–(5), register the caveat the same day, and use a lawyer for the whole transaction.
