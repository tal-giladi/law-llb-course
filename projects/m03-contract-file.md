# Project: your contract file

Build a complete contract file for one agreement in your own life: how it was made, whether it is valid, what it means, what each side must do, and what you would do on a breach. The result is a short, organised file with a marked-up contract and two ready letters, which you will reuse in module 8 (procedure) and module 12 (clinic).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. This project is a dry run: nothing is sent unless you choose to send a letter in your own matter.

## Brief

Choose one agreement you are a party to: a residential lease, a renovation or service contract, a gym, phone or internet contract, a car purchase, or a private loan. It should have a written text or at least a message thread. If you have no suitable agreement, use the supplied case below and say so.

**Supplied case.** Hadas Mor, 29, from Afula, rented a flat from 1 January 2026 for 12 months at ₪3,800 a month on the landlord's printed form. She paid a ₪15,000 cash deposit. The form says all repairs are the tenant's, the landlord may end the lease on 30 days' notice, and the deposit is returned "after all bills are settled". In March the boiler broke; the landlord has ignored two WhatsApp messages for five weeks. Separately, in February Hadas bought a robot vacuum cleaner online for ₪1,400 that arrived on 20 February and fails to map the flat.

Time: about 4 hours.

## Steps

1. **The facts (20 minutes).** One page: parties, dates, how the contract was made, price, main obligations, documents and messages you hold.
2. **Layers (15 minutes).** List the statutes that govern the agreement from most specific to general (lesson 03.1) with one key section for each.
3. **Formation and form (25 minutes).** Show when and how the contract was made (offer and acceptance, s. 60(ב) timing) and whether any form requirement applies (lessons 03.2–03.3).
4. **Validity (25 minutes).** Test for defects in formation (ss. 14–18) and for illegal or void terms (s. 30, Standard Contracts Law s. 5, mandatory statutes). Write "none found" with reasons if so (lessons 03.5–03.6).
5. **Content (40 minutes).** Mark up the contract with the [contract review checklist](../templates/contract-review-checklist.md). Identify the version of s. 25 that applies and interpret one disputed or unclear clause (lessons 03.7, 03.9).
6. **Good faith (15 minutes).** Note any conduct by either side that raises s. 12 or s. 39 (lesson 03.4).
7. **Breach and remedies (40 minutes).** For a real breach (or a likely one), classify it, choose the remedy path, and build a damages table (lesson 03.8).
8. **Letters (45 minutes).** Draft the right notice with the [notice of breach and cancellation](../templates/notice-of-breach-and-cancellation.md) template and, if you have a consumer purchase, a [consumer withdrawal notice](../templates/consumer-withdrawal-notice.md) or a non-conformity notice (lesson 03.10).
9. **Deadlines and stop signals (15 minutes).** Every running period (withdrawal, repair periods, deposit return, option notice, limitation) with source and check date; the red flags that would send you to a lawyer.

## Self-assessment rubric

Score each criterion 0–2. A total of 14 or more (out of 18) means the project meets the standard.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facts | Vague, undated | Mostly complete | Complete, dated, documents listed |
| Layers | Only the general part | Special law named, no section | Layers ordered with a key section each |
| Formation and form | Not analysed | Conclusion without sections | Formation moment and form requirement shown with sections |
| Validity | Not considered | Grounds listed without elements | Each ground tested element by element |
| Content | No markup | Problems listed without law | Clause-by-clause markup with sections; s. 25 version stated |
| Good faith | Missing or overused | Mentioned generally | Specific conduct tied to s. 12 or s. 39 |
| Remedies | Wrong path (for example immediate cancellation for a minor breach) | Right path, no figures | Right path with a damages table and mitigation |
| Letters | Missing or no operative sentence | Letter present, missing section or date | Correct template, operative sentence, sections, dates, sending method |
| Deadlines | None | Listed without sources | Listed with sources and check dates; most urgent marked |

## Model answer

<details><summary>Model answer</summary>

This model uses the supplied case.

**Layers.** Lease: Rental and Borrowing Law, residential chapter (ss. 25א–25טו, protected by s. 25יד) → rest of that law → Standard Contracts Law (the landlord's printed form, used for his several flats) → Contracts Law → Remedies Law. Vacuum cleaner: Consumer Protection Law (distance sale, s. 14ג; misleading, s. 2) → Sale Law (non-conformity, ss. 11, 14) → Contracts and Remedies Laws.

**Formation and form.** The lease is written and signed (s. 25ב satisfied). It was made in December 2025, before 7.1.2026, so the old s. 25(א) governs; in any case a residential lease between private people is not a business contract. The vacuum purchase was made online: the listing was the offer, the checkout the acceptance (s. 6(א)).

**Validity.** No defect in formation. Several lease terms are void or unenforceable: "all repairs on the tenant" (contradicts s. 25ח), "landlord ends on 30 days" (void unless mutual, s. 25יג), "deposit returned after all bills" (s. 25י(ה) sets the period; the clause cannot extend it against the tenant). The cash deposit of ₪15,000 is above the cap: the lower of one-third of the term's rent (₪15,200) and three months' rent (₪11,400) = ₪11,400 (s. 25י(ב)); the excess ₪3,600 should be returned now.

**Content.** The repair clause is read under s. 25ח regardless of wording; under s. 25(ב1) any ambiguity in the landlord's form is read against him.

**Good faith.** Ignoring repair requests for five weeks breaches the statutory duty and s. 39.

**Remedies.**
- Boiler: a written demand starts the landlord's statutory repair period (s. 25ח(ב)); a broken boiler in winter may be an urgent defect (no reasonable living) with a short period. If he does not repair, s. 25ח(ג) applies the tenant's remedies under s. 9(א)–(ב) of the Rental and Borrowing Law: after reasonable advance notice, repair and claim the reasonable cost back, or reduce the rent in proportion to the loss of value while the defect remains. Damages: cost of alternatives (heaters, showers elsewhere) and distress (Remedies Law ss. 10, 13). Not cancellation at this stage.
- Excess deposit: demand ₪3,600 back.
- Vacuum: received 20 February; the 14-day distance-sale period has passed. But it does not perform its basic function: non-conformity (Sale Law s. 11(3)); notify promptly on discovery (s. 14); ask for repair, replacement or refund; a misleading listing would also be a Consumer Protection Law tort (ss. 2, 31).

**Letters.** (1) Part A notice to the landlord: demand to repair the boiler within the statutory period, return the ₪3,600 excess, and a statement that clauses 6, 9 and 14 contradict the law. Registered mail plus WhatsApp. (2) A non-conformity notice to the online shop with the order number, the defect and the request.

**Deadlines.** Boiler: the statutory urgent-repair period from the written demand. Deposit return: the statutory period after the end of the lease. Landlord's option notice: s. 25יב periods. Limitation: the general civil period (see current numbers). Most urgent: the boiler demand.

**Stop signals.** The landlord threatens eviction in retaliation; damage to health from lack of heating; the online shop is abroad with no Israeli address.

Self-score: facts 2, layers 2, formation 2, validity 2, content 2, good faith 1, remedies 2, letters 2, deadlines 1 = 16.
