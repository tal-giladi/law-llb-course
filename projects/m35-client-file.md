# Project: a client's file for working with a lawyer

Build the file a careful client keeps when hiring and working with a lawyer: a decision on whether you need one, a checked shortlist, a reviewed and corrected fee agreement, an engagement and supervision plan that covers money and AI use, and, for a problem case, a ready-to-send demand letter and ethics complaint. You will reuse the shortlist and the checklists in the capstone (module 39).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. The demand letter and the complaint are dry runs unless you have a real grievance and decide to send them.

## Brief

Use a matter of your own where a lawyer might be needed (a claim, a purchase, a family matter, an injury, a dispute with an authority). If you prefer not to, use the supplied case.

**Supplied case.** Rawan Nasser, 38, a teacher from Shfar'am, slipped on a broken pavement owned by the municipality and fractured her wrist on her way to work in January 2026. She has three potential claims: a tort claim against the municipality and its insurer, a National Insurance work-injury claim (the fall was on the way to work), and a dispute with her own private insurer over a personal-accident policy. A lawyer's office sent her this proposal: "25% of all sums from all sources, including National Insurance, plus ₪2,500 opening fee; expenses included; we may assign the file to a colleague; if you leave us, the full fee on the best offer received." Six months later, in the supplied problem, the office received ₪12,000 from her private insurer and kept it for three months without telling her.

Time: about 8 hours.

## Steps

1. **Do you need a lawyer? (30 minutes).** Run the [get-a-lawyer checklist](../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md) for each claim and decide: alone, consultation, or full representation; note free options (lesson 12.8).
2. **Who may help? (30 minutes).** For each claim, decide whether a non-lawyer may lawfully help (for example a capped NII assistance company) and where only a lawyer may act (lesson 35.2).
3. **Shortlist (60 minutes).** Find three candidates; check each in the Bar's register and for published disciplinary rulings; record the date (lessons 35.1, 35.5).
4. **Conflict check (30 minutes).** List all parties and send (or draft) the conflict-check letter (lesson 35.3; part ב of the [engagement worksheet](../templates/lawyer-engagement-worksheet.md)).
5. **Fee review (90 minutes).** Review the proposal with the [fee agreement checklist](../templates/fee-agreement-checklist.md): identify every cap and unlawful term; calculate the fee at two outcomes; write the corrections letter (lesson 35.4).
6. **Engagement and supervision plan (60 minutes).** Scope, staffing, reporting, deadlines diary, trust account and accounts, AI use and data, the trigger for changing lawyer (lessons 35.5, 35.7).
7. **Problem case (90 minutes).** For the supplied problem (or a real one), sort the issues, draft the demand letter and the complaint, and set the sequence and dates (lessons 35.6, 35.8; the [ethics complaint template](../templates/bar-ethics-complaint.md)).
8. **One-page summary (30 minutes).** What you decided for each claim, and why.

## Self-assessment rubric

| Criterion | Excellent (3) | Adequate (2) | Needs work (1) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Need for a lawyer | Each claim assessed with the checklist; free options named; reasons tied to red and amber items | Assessed; reasons thin | Decision without analysis |
| Who may help | Lawful helpers identified with sections (s. 20, s. 21, NII ss. 315א–315ג); reserved acts kept for lawyers | Mostly right; one section missing | Non-lawyers proposed for reserved acts |
| Shortlist | Three candidates checked in the register and for rulings, dated; reasons for each | Checked; reasons missing | Unchecked list |
| Conflict check | All parties listed; letter cites rules 14–16 and 19–20; walk-away answers stated | Letter sent; some parties missing | No conflict check |
| Fee review | Every cap applied correctly with calculations; unlawful vs unfavourable distinguished; corrections with legal basis | Caps applied; some terms missed | Caps missed or miscalculated |
| Supervision plan | Concrete documents, intervals, diary, money handling and AI terms; change-of-lawyer trigger | Plan general | No plan |
| Problem case | Demand letter first; complaint factual, dated, documented, with rules; refund route and dates; deadlines protected | Complaint adequate; sequence thin | Emotional complaint; no demand letter |

<details><summary>Model answer (supplied case, outline)</summary>

**Need for a lawyer.** Tort claim against the municipality: amber to red (a public defendant with an insurer, medical evidence, a possible dispute about the pavement's condition): a lawyer or at least a consultation. NII work-injury claim: Rawan can file it herself with free help (lesson 17.9); representation at the medical committee is optional. Private insurer: start with a written claim and the Supervisor of Insurance (lesson 20.10); a lawyer if rejected on a significant sum.

**Who may help.** A capped NII assistance company may help with the NII paperwork for the "other assistance" cap (s. 315ב(א)(1)); representation in the labour court is reserved. Only a lawyer may represent her against the municipality and its insurer for pay.

**Shortlist.** Three lawyers with published tort judgments against local authorities; register checked; no disciplinary rulings found (state the date).

**Conflict check.** Parties: the municipality, its insurer, its contractor for pavement maintenance, her private insurer, the NII. Ask whether the office acts for the municipality, its insurer or the contractor in any matter (rules 14(ג), 16); a firm that regularly represents the municipality's insurer should be excluded.

**Fee review.** (1) No statutory cap applies to the tort claim against the municipality (not a road accident): 25% is lawful but negotiable; propose 15–20% on a sliding scale, and state that awarded costs are excluded unless expressly agreed (Orhightech). (2) The NII part is capped by s. 315ב: 25% "of all sums" is unlawful there; if the office represents her at the committee, 15.75% plus file-opening fee (or 17%), on the s. 315ד benefit part. (3) "Expenses included" breaches s. 85. (4) Assignment at will breaches rule 18. (5) Full fee on leaving: replace with payment for work done. (6) The ₪2,500 opening fee should be credited against the success fee. Calculation: a ₪120,000 tort settlement at 25% is ₪30,000; at a proposed 17.5%, ₪21,000; plus VAT in both cases.

**Supervision plan.** Copies of every filing; monthly update; a joint deadline list (limitation against the municipality: seven years from the fall, with any notice requirements under local-authority law checked); trust account, transfer within 14 days, account on request; no client data in public AI tools without specific consent; change lawyer if two deadlines are missed or money is held without notice.

**Problem case.** Breaches: rules 39–41 (notice and transfer), possibly rule 42 (account), and s. 54. First a demand letter: account, receipts, the ₪12,000 less any agreed fee, within 14 days; then a complaint to the district ethics committee if no satisfactory answer, with the insurer's payment letter, her emails and the agreement; a small claim for the money if needed; and a decision whether to end the engagement and move the tort claim to another lawyer before any deadline.
