# Module 22 project: Clinic II case file: one work, benefits, family or housing matter from intake to closing

Run one real matter of your own through the year-2 clinic: an employment claim, a National Insurance appeal, a family request to settle, a tenancy dispute or a welfare or housing benefit review. You build the file and its deadline table, get your own records, send the letter or the internal appeal, draft the court or tribunal document, prepare the hearing, and close the file with a memo and a final get-a-lawyer decision. The default is to act in your own name where the step is low-risk and the deadline is real (a demand letter, a request for your file, an NII medical appeal, a benefit review request); filing in court is your decision, recorded with reasons. Time: about 12–14 hours spread over several weeks.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before sending or filing anything.

## Brief

Choose a matter that passes the five filters of lesson 22.1: it is yours; you can name its track; every fatal deadline is at least two weeks away; you hold or can request the evidence; and the risk is manageable. In a family matter, use initials for the other parent and the children in anything you share.

**Supplied case (if you have no suitable matter).** Supplied case A from the lessons: Rawan Haddad, monthly-paid waitress in Haifa, employed 1 May 2024 to 31 August 2026 at ₪6,800 a month, five-day week; dismissed by WhatsApp "as of today"; pension fund severance contributions of 6% from November 2024 with no s. 14 arrangement; 8 unused leave days; recuperation never paid; ₪824 "חוסר בקופה" deducted from the August payslip; demand letter of 10 September unanswered. Assume that the employer's defence alleges theft and "agreed" deduction; that the expedited hearing ends in a compromise of ₪17,500 payable within 30 days with form 161 and the pension release letter, given the force of a judgment on 15 December 2026 in both parties' presence; and that the money arrives on day 28 but the release letter does not.

## Steps

1. **Intake (60 min).** Five-filter table for up to three matters; choose one. Open the [clinic case file](../templates/clinic-case-file.md) with the three year-2 additions: track sheet, deadline table with the proof of each start date, relationship note (lesson 22.1).
2. **Your records (45 min plus waiting).** Send one written request for the file the other side holds (payslips and attendance records, the NII medical file and minutes, the authority's eligibility file), citing Privacy Protection Law s. 13 where it is personal information. Log the date and the answer.
3. **The first move (90 min).** By track: the [letter to employer](../templates/letter-to-employer-wage-claim.md) with the [final-settlement worksheet](../templates/payslip-and-final-settlement-worksheet.md) (lesson 17.10); the medical appeal, part A of the [NII appeal template](../templates/nii-medical-committee-appeal.md) (lesson 17.11); Form 1, part A of the [request-to-settle template](../templates/request-to-settle-family-dispute.md) (lesson 22.4); part A or B of the [rental letter](../templates/rental-repair-and-deposit-letter.md) (lesson 22.5); part A, C or D of the [welfare review request](../templates/welfare-benefit-review-request.md) (lesson 22.6). Send it with proof of delivery if your filters and deadlines support it; otherwise record why it stays a dry run.
4. **The court or tribunal document (150 min).** By track: the [labour-court statement of claim](../templates/labour-court-statement-of-claim.md) (lesson 22.2); part B of the NII template, the appeal on law, built from a minutes check (lesson 22.3); the meeting preparation sheet, Form 3 and the application to give an agreement the force of a judgment (lesson 22.4); the [small claim](../templates/small-claim-contract-consumer.md) with a remedy table (lesson 22.5); part B of the welfare review request, the labour-court outline (lesson 22.6). Tick the filing checklist and record your filing decision with reasons.
5. **Hearing or meeting kit (120 min).** Adapt the [hearing-day kit](../templates/hearing-day-kit.md): bench and track, disputed facts and their proof, a two-minute opening, up to six closed questions, the bench's likely questions, a written settlement range with non-money conditions; or, for a tribunal or the assistance unit, a one-page summary, documents and the complaints or red lines you will make sure are recorded (lessons 22.4, 22.7).
6. **Closing (60 min).** Compare the outcome (real or supplied) with the money and documents received; chase gaps; plan enforcement; decide on any appeal within its period; write the six-part closing memo with surviving deadlines (lesson 22.8).
7. **Lawyer decision (30 min).** Run the [get-a-lawyer checklist](../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md) with the year-2 red flags at three points (before the first move, before filing, at closing); one-page summary; the free help that fits your track.
8. **Reflection (20 min).** Half a page: what you did for real, what stayed a dry run and why, and what you would do differently.

## Self-assessment rubric

| Criterion | Weak (1) | Adequate (2) | Strong (3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intake and track | Matter not tested; track unclear | Filters applied; forum named | Filters with reasons; forum, internal step and appeal route named; relationship note written |
| Deadlines | Missing or counted from the wrong event | Main deadline computed | Every fatal deadline with its start event, proof of date and calendar reminder |
| Records | No request | Request drafted | Request sent and logged, citing the right right of access |
| First move | Vague or late | Right template, dated | Right template, statutory pre-conditions met, sent with proof, answer logged |
| Court or tribunal document | Argument, missing particulars | Most required particulars | Every required particular (reg. 9; Form 1 bare; points of law from the minutes; remedy table with sources), nothing extra |
| Hearing or meeting kit | None | Bundle and opening | Disputed facts mapped to proof, closed questions, bench's questions, written range with non-money conditions or red lines |
| Closing | Not done | Outcome recorded | Money and documents checked, gaps chased, enforcement planned, appeal decision within the period, surviving deadlines diarised |
| Lawyer decision | Not done | Checklist run once | Run at three points with year-2 red flags; summary ready; free help identified |

A strong file scores at least 20 of 24, with no criterion at 1.
