# Module 15 project: Civil litigation file: from claim to appeal

Build the complete procedural file for one civil case in a magistrates' court on the ordinary track: forum and parties, limitation, the claim, the expected defence and your reply, the disclosure plan, an interim-relief decision, the pre-trial calendar, your trial evidence, and, after an imagined judgment, a motion and an appeal outline. It applies every lesson in module 15 to a single case. A dry run is the default; file in your own name only if you choose. Time: about 8 hours.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

## Brief

Choose a civil money dispute of your own above the fast-track ceiling (see [current numbers](../references/current-numbers.md)) and below the magistrates' court ceiling: unpaid work, a failed renovation, a vehicle or equipment purchase, a loan. Avoid labour, family, land-rights, personal-injury and criminal matters. If you have none, use the supplied case.

**Supplied case.** Amir Jaber, a freelance graphic designer from Isfiya (sole trader), worked for "פרסום הכרמל בע״מ", a Haifa advertising agency, from 2019 to 2025 under a framework agreement signed in January 2019: monthly invoices, payment "שוטף + 60", and a clause "סמכות השיפוט הבלעדית – בתי המשפט בחיפה". He has nine unpaid invoices: three from March–May 2019 (₪18,000 in total) and six from January–June 2025 (₪96,000). In August 2025 the agency's CFO emailed: "אנחנו מכירים בחוב של 2025, נשלם בתשלומים לאחר שנקבל כסף מהלקוח הגדול". Nothing was paid. In September 2026 Amir hears from a former employee that the agency is "moving everything to a new company" with the same owners, and he sees the new company's registration in the Companies Registrar. The agency's lawyer replied to Amir's pre-action letter: the 2019 invoices are time-barred, the 2025 work was "defective and late", and the agency will counterclaim ₪40,000 for a lost client.

## Steps

1. **Forum, venue and parties (30 min).** Court (Courts Law s. 51), track (reg. 78), district (reg. 7, the exclusive clause). Parties: the agency; should the new company be joined (reg. 26)? Write your reasons (lesson 15.1).
2. **Limitation (30 min).** For each group of invoices, compute the limitation date (Prescription Law ss. 5, 6, 9, 15א). Does the CFO's email cover the 2019 invoices? Decide what to claim (lesson 15.9).
3. **Statement of claim (90 min).** On the [statement of claim template](../templates/statement-of-claim-magistrates.md): heading, summary, detail, exhibits, fee; within page limits; every remedy (regs 9–15, 24–25; lessons 15.2, 15.10).
4. **Expected defence and reply (45 min).** Write the agency's likely defence in outline (preliminary arguments, denials, counterclaim) on the [statement of defence template](../templates/statement-of-defence.md), and your reply within reg. 18 (lessons 15.2, 15.10).
5. **Service (15 min).** How will the claim be served on the agency, and when does the defence period end? (regs 160–163; lesson 15.3)
6. **Interim relief decision (45 min).** Decide whether to seek a temporary attachment or injunction against the transfer of assets. Write the reg. 95 analysis and, if you apply, the relief paragraph on the [written motion template](../templates/written-motion.md), with the undertaking and security (lesson 15.5).
7. **Disclosure plan (30 min).** Your own disclosure list; an interrogatory of up to 12 questions; the documents you will ask the agency to disclose (approval emails, client correspondence) (lesson 15.4).
8. **Pre-trial calendar (30 min).** With assumed dates: mediation-intake meeting, list of motions, witness lists, first pre-trial; your settlement range and minimum figure (lesson 15.6).
9. **Trial evidence (45 min).** Your affidavit in lieu of main testimony (ten paragraphs at least); witness list with purposes; ten cross-examination questions for the agency's CFO (lesson 15.7).
10. **After judgment (60 min).** Assume the court gives you ₪96,000 for 2025 but dismisses the 2019 invoices as time-barred and dismisses the counterclaim. Classify the judgment, compute the appeal deadline, fee and security, and write an appeal outline on the [appeal outline template](../templates/appeal-outline.md) on the limitation point; then write how you would respond if the agency appeals (lessons 15.8, 15.11).
11. **Decision memo (20 min).** One page: what you will do now, the dates you diarised, the costs at risk, and when you would get a lawyer ([get a lawyer checklist](../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md)).

## Self-assessment rubric

| Criterion | Weak (1) | Adequate (2) | Strong (3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Forum, venue, parties | Court chosen by habit | Right court and district | Court, track, district and joinder justified with sections; clause applied |
| Limitation | Not computed | One date | Each claim computed with ss. 5, 6, 9, 15א; acknowledgment analysed for scope |
| Claim | Narrative | Most parts present | Every reg. 10 item, summary per reg. 11, dated facts with exhibits, within limits |
| Defence and reply | Not considered | Defence listed | Preliminary arguments, denials and counterclaim anticipated; reply within reg. 18 |
| Interim relief | Not considered | Order named | Reg. 95 test applied to evidence; undertaking, security and deadlines addressed |
| Disclosure and pre-trial | Missing | Some steps | Disclosure list complete, compliant interrogatory, dated calendar, settlement minimum |
| Trial evidence | Argument in the affidavit | Mostly facts | Own-knowledge facts only, warning formula, purposeful witnesses, closed questions |
| Appeal | Wrong route or date | Route and date | Route, date, fee and security correct; grounds reviewable with record references |

A strong file scores at least 20 of 24, with no criterion at 1.

<summary>Model answer</summary>

**1. Forum, venue, parties.** ₪114,000 is above the fast-track ceiling and below the magistrates' ceiling: magistrates' court, ordinary track (Courts Law s. 51(א)(2); reg. 78). The clause gives exclusive venue to Haifa (reg. 7(א), last sentence); file in the Haifa magistrates' court. Sue the agency (contract). Joining the new company needs a cause of action against it; at this stage you have only a rumour and a registration. Keep it in reserve for an amendment (reg. 46) or for piercing the veil later (module 16), but use the evidence now in the interim-relief request.

**2. Limitation.** The 2019 invoices fell due 60 days after month-end (May–July 2019). Seven years (s. 5(1)) run to May–July 2026. The war suspension (s. 15א(א)) adds 7.10.2023–6.4.2024, about six months, because the periods were running on 25.1.2024: new end dates around November 2026 to January 2027. The CFO's email acknowledges "the debt of 2025" only; under s. 9 it restarts limitation for the 2025 invoices, not for 2019, unless the 2019 invoices are part of the same cause (consider *Rubav*: a single framework agreement may be argued to be one cause; the email's wording makes that hard). Conclusion: file by October 2026 and claim all nine invoices; plead the war suspension for 2019 in the summary.

**3. Claim.** Heading per reg. 10 with remedies: ₪18,000 (2019), ₪96,000 (2025), linkage and interest; fee item 1, first half on filing. Summary: the parties; the remedy; the framework agreement, the invoices, the due dates (when the cause arose), the CFO's email; venue by the clause. Detail: one paragraph per invoice group, the work approvals, the email, the pre-action letter. Exhibits: agreement, invoices, approval emails, CFO email, letter.

**4. Defence and reply.** Expect: limitation for 2019 (first preliminary argument), denial of quality and timing with emails of complaints, counterclaim ₪40,000. Reply: the client's approvals of each deliverable; no complaints before the invoices fell due; the CFO's email acknowledging the 2025 debt without reservation. No new cause in the reply. Defence to counterclaim: no causal link; the lost client left for its own reasons (demand disclosure).

**5. Service.** Registered mail or personal delivery to the agency's registered office (regs 161(3)–(4), 162(1), 163(ה)); through the registry if you are unrepresented (reg. 160). The defence period runs from the date on the delivery confirmation.

**6. Interim relief.** A temporary attachment of the agency's receivables or accounts up to the claim (reg. 103) is justified if you can show a reasonable fear that enforcement will be substantially harder: the new company with the same owners, the former employee's statement in an affidavit, and the Registrar extract. Balance: limit it to the sum claimed and to specific accounts; offer a guarantee; attach the Form 7 undertaking. Ex parte is the rule for attachments (reg. 97(ג)); serve personally within the period (reg. 97(ד)).

**7. Disclosure.** Your list includes everything: invoices, approvals, the complaints you received, your WhatsApps with the account manager. Interrogatory: the dates of client approvals; the date the large client paid the agency; the names of those who decided not to pay. Ask for the agency's correspondence with the lost client (counterclaim).

**8. Pre-trial.** Above the threshold: mediation-intake meeting (reg. 37(ב)). List of motions 20 days before the first pre-trial (disclosure disputes). Witness list: you, the account manager, the former employee (if he will testify). Settlement range: best ₪114,000 plus costs; likely ₪96,000–₪105,000; worst ₪96,000 minus a share of the counterclaim; minimum ₪95,000 now.

**9. Trial evidence.** Affidavit: facts of the agreement, each project and approval, the invoices, the email, the non-payment; no adjectives, no hearsay about the "moving everything" story unless the former employee testifies. Cross-examination of the CFO: "You wrote this email on [date]?" "It says 'we acknowledge the debt of 2025'?" "You did not mention defects in it?"

**10. Appeal.** A final judgment: appeal as of right to the Haifa district court within 60 days of service, recess excluded (Courts Law s. 52(א); reg. 137(א)); fee per the schedule; security per the Third Schedule (single judge or panel). Ground: error of law in limitation, failure to apply s. 15א (and, in the alternative, s. 9 if the acknowledgment covered the whole cause). If the agency appeals, consider a cross-appeal within 60 days of the latest trigger (reg. 137(ב)) and, if it asks for a stay, ask that it deposit the judgment sum (reg. 145).

**11. Decision.** File the claim and the attachment request now (limitation), diary the defence period and the 7-day claim deadline if the order comes first; get a lawyer if the attachment is granted and contested, or if the new company becomes a defendant.
