# Module 16 project: Company file: forming, running and protecting a small company

Build the company file that founders and minority shareholders of a small Israeli company actually need: a choice of form, a registration dry run, the articles decisions, a founders' checklist, a conflict-of-interest and dividend check, a creditor check of a counterparty, and a minority shareholder's letter with an escalation plan. It applies every lesson in module 16 to one business. Use a business of your own, one you plan, or the supplied case. It is a dry run: you file and send nothing unless you decide to, in your own name. Time: about 6 hours.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

## Brief

**Supplied case.** Shir Ben-Haim (Tel Aviv) has worked for two years as a self-employed web developer, registered as an exempt dealer, with turnover now around ₪150,000. Her friend Wael Nassar (Haifa), a UX designer and a licensed dealer, wants to join her, and an acquaintance, Boaz Klein, offers ₪200,000 for 20% if they form a company. Shir and Wael will each hold 40% and be the two directors; Shir will be CEO. A year after forming "Pixel Bridge Ltd", the company is offered a large contract by "Coastline Hotels Ltd", whose extract shows "מפרת חוק" status, a bank's floating charge and a change of all directors last month. Meanwhile, Wael signs a hosting contract with his brother's company at a price well above market, and Shir proposes a ₪300,000 dividend although the company's cash is low and a ₪250,000 supplier payment is due in two months. Boaz, the 20% investor, is told nothing.

## Steps

1. **Form and status (30 min).** Is Shir still allowed to be an exempt dealer? Should Shir and Wael work as a partnership or a company? Compare liability, formation and tax registration (lessons 16.1, 16.7).
2. **Registration dry run (60 min).** Fill the [company registration checklist (draft)](../templates/company-registration-checklist.md) for Pixel Bridge Ltd: name search, mandatory clauses, shareholders, directors, office, digital address, the online route and who must approve (lessons 16.2, 16.8).
3. **Founders' terms (60 min).** Fill the [founders' agreement checklist (draft)](../templates/founders-agreement-checklist.md): shares and vesting, Boaz's rights, reserved matters, board, transfers, deadlock, IP; mark what goes into the articles (lesson 16.9).
4. **Wael's contract (30 min).** Personal interest, extraordinary or not, the approval required, consequences of no approval, remedies (lesson 16.4).
5. **The dividend (30 min).** Apply the profit and solvency tests; who decides; consequences if it is prohibited (lesson 16.5).
6. **The Coastline credit check (30 min).** Read the red flags; decide whether and on what terms to sign (lesson 16.8).
7. **Boaz's letter (60 min).** Draft, on the [shareholder information request (draft)](../templates/shareholder-information-request.md), Boaz's request (parts א, ב, ג, ד as applicable) and an outline of a s. 194 demand about Wael's contract; then an escalation table with dates (lessons 16.3, 16.6).
8. **Decision (15 min).** One paragraph for each of Shir, Wael and Boaz: what each should do now, and when each needs a lawyer.

A strong file scores at least 20 of 24, with no criterion at 1.

## Model answer

<details><summary>Model answer</summary>

**Form and status.** Shir's turnover (about ₪150,000) is above the exempt-dealer ceiling for 2026 (VAT Law s. 1; see current numbers), so she must move to licensed-dealer status; web developers are not in reg. 13 but the ceiling decides. Wael, a licensed dealer, may continue. If they work together for profit without a company they are partners (Ordinance s. 1), must register within a month (s. 4) and are jointly and severally liable (s. 20). With an investor coming in and contracts with hotels, a private company is the right form: limited liability (s. 35), easy entry for Boaz, and a company is always a licensed dealer (reg. 13(7)).

**Registration.** Name: "פיקסל ברידג׳ בע״מ" after a registry and trade-mark search (s. 27). Objects: any lawful business (s. 32(1)). Capital: 100,000 ordinary shares without par value (ss. 33–34); issued 40,000 / 40,000 at formation, 20,000 to Boaz on his investment (board allotment within the registered capital, s. 288; report within 14 days, s. 292). Limited liability (s. 35). Online filing by Shir as shareholder; Wael must identify and approve the articles and his declarations online (reg. 16ב(ב)). Registered office and digital address (ss. 123–123א). Then bank, VAT and income-tax files, registers (ss. 124, 127, 224), auditor (s. 154), calendar.

**Founders' terms.** Vesting over three years with a one-year cliff for Shir and Wael; Boaz's shares fully paid, no vesting. Reserved matters (75%, so Boaz with 20% cannot block alone but Shir or Wael can): new issues, sale, interested-party transactions, CEO pay, borrowing above a set sum; in the articles and entrenched (ss. 20(ב), 22). Board: Shir, Wael and, on investment, an observer or seat for Boaz. First refusal, tag-along for Boaz, drag-along at 75%, in the articles. Pre-emption kept (s. 290). IP assignment and non-compete in the founders' agreement. Deadlock: mediation, then BMBY. Disputes: mediation then arbitration.

**Wael's contract.** His brother is a "relative" (s. 1), so Wael has a personal interest (s. 270(1)). Above-market price makes it extraordinary (s. 1). Pixel Bridge has no audit committee: board approval, without Wael voting (s. 278), and, since Wael is a director, the general meeting (s. 272(ב)). Wael had to disclose no later than the first board discussion (s. 269). Without approval the contract has no force against the company and, if the brother knew of Wael's interest and the lack of approval, against him (s. 280). Wael is liable as for breach of contract for the excess (s. 256); no exemption is possible (s. 258(א)). Shir, as director, must have the board convened (s. 257).

**The dividend.** Profit test: only if retained earnings in statements no older than six months cover ₪300,000 (s. 302(ב)). Solvency test: low cash and a ₪250,000 payment due in two months create a reasonable concern; the distribution is prohibited (s. 302(א)). The board decides unless the articles say otherwise (s. 307). If paid, shareholders who knew must repay (s. 310) and directors are deemed in breach unless they prove a s. 311 defence; Boaz, who knew nothing, would not have to repay if he did not and should not have known. A creditor could sue derivatively (s. 204).

**Coastline credit check.** "מפרת חוק" (unpaid fees or missing annual reports, s. 362א), a bank's floating charge (Pixel Bridge would be an unsecured creditor behind the bank), and a recent change of all directors. Decision: sign only with milestone payments in advance, a personal guarantee from the new controlling shareholder, a short payment term, and the right to stop work on non-payment; check the extract again before each milestone.

**Boaz's letter.** (א) Articles, financial statements, general-meeting minutes and register (ss. 184, 187). (ב) Documents on Wael's hosting contract: it is an extraordinary transaction with a director's relative that needs general-meeting approval (ss. 185(א)(1), 270(1), 272(ב)); confidentiality offered. (ג) Boaz holds 20%, above 10%: demand a special meeting on the hosting contract and the proposed dividend, within 21 days (s. 63); if not convened, he may convene within three months at the company's cost (s. 64). (ד) Directors' pay statement (s. 186), since he holds 10% and the company must have an auditor. s. 194 outline to the chairman: the company's loss (the excess over market price), the cause (ss. 254, 280), demand that the company sue Wael and the brother's company; 45 days for an answer, 75 days if it decides to sue. Escalation table: day 0 letter; day 14 answer due; day 21 meeting due; month 3 self-convening deadline; day 45 s. 194 answer; then a motion for approval (s. 198) or oppression (s. 191).

**Decisions.** Shir: register as a licensed dealer now; form the company; stop the dividend until a solvency assessment; get a lawyer before Boaz's investment documents. Wael: disclose and submit the hosting contract for approval or cancel it; get a lawyer if a s. 194 demand arrives. Boaz: send the letter; get a lawyer before any court step (s. 191 or s. 198) and before signing the investment.
