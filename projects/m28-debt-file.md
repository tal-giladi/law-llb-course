# Project: a household debt file, from the warning to a fresh start

Build the complete file a careful person keeps when debts reach the Execution Office: an inventory of every debt and file, a deadline diary, the responses each file needs, a documented household budget, and a reasoned decision between a payment arrangement and insolvency proceedings, with the draft papers for the route you choose. You will reuse this file in the year-3 clinic (module 31, lesson "Defending an execution file and reaching an arrangement").

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. The project is a dry run unless you have your own execution files and decide to act in your own name.

## Brief

Choose either:

- **Your own debts**, if you have open execution files or debts heading there; or
- **The supplied case** below. Say which you used.

**Supplied case.** Miriam Haddad, 47, Acre, divorced, two children (16 and 12) living with her; receives ₪1,800 a month child maintenance under a judgment, paid irregularly. Works as a medical secretary, net ₪7,300. Rent ₪3,000. She has a 2013 car (₪12,000) she uses to reach a clinic in Nahariya.

Execution files (personal area printout, 1 December 2026):

1. Credit-card company, judgment, ₪14,800; warning served by post, signed for 20 November 2026.
2. Private clinic, **promissory note** ₪8,000 she signed for her daughter's orthodontic treatment; the treatment was stopped after two sessions when the dentist left; warning served personally 25 November 2026.
3. Bank, judgment on a personal loan, ₪62,000; warning served two years ago; she did not respond; liens on her account; a notice of a credit-card restriction served 15 November 2026.
4. Electricity company, fixed-sum claim, ₪3,900; she says she paid ₪1,500 in May 2026 by standing order and has the bank statement.

Other debts not yet in execution: a loan from her sister (₪20,000) that she has been repaying at ₪1,000 a month; arnona arrears ₪6,000. In October 2026 she gave her son ₪7,000 from her savings "for his bar mitzvah trip".

Time: about 8 hours.

## Steps

1. **Inventory (45 minutes).** One table for all debts: creditor, document type, file number, amount, service date, measures already taken, security, guarantor (lessons 28.1, 28.2).
2. **Deadline diary (30 minutes).** For each file, every period that runs from service, and the date the restriction takes effect; mark what has passed (lesson 28.1).
3. **Defences (60 minutes).** Decide and outline: the opposition for file 2 (and its deadline); the payment claim for file 4 (is it "I have paid" or opposition, given it is a fixed-sum claim?); a request to release protected money in file 3 (lessons 28.2, 28.3).
4. **Budget (45 minutes).** A documented household budget: income (wages, maintenance), necessary expenses, capacity (lesson 28.4).
5. **The decision (60 minutes).** Test consolidation under s. 74ט and s. 7א1 with numbers. Then test insolvency: s. 2, the 2026 door (execution registrar or Commissioner), and the forecast of a rehabilitation order (lessons 28.4–28.6). Decide, and explain in one page.
6. **Conduct check (30 minutes).** The payments to her sister and the gift to her son: preference or undervalue issues if she files; what to do now (lesson 28.6).
7. **Draft the papers for your route (2 hours).**
   - If arrangement: a payment-order request with form 21 content and a consolidation request through s. 74טו1, plus a settlement letter to the bank (lesson 28.8).
   - If insolvency: the creditors list, the two-year report outline, the account of the debts and the s. 7א2 notice or online-application checklist (lesson 28.9).
8. **Stop signals (15 minutes).** Write when you would go to Legal Aid or a lawyer, and why.

## Self-assessment rubric

Score each criterion 0–2. A total of 13 or more (out of 16) means the project meets the standard.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inventory | Debts missing | All debts, details incomplete | All debts with type, service date, measures, security |
| Diary | Wrong or missing dates | Most dates right | Every period computed from service with its section; passed deadlines identified |
| Defences | Wrong defence for the document | Right defence, weak evidence | Opposition for the note and the fixed-sum claim, release request, each with documents and deadline |
| Budget | Not documented | Documented, unrealistic | Documented, realistic, maintenance treated correctly |
| Decision | No numbers | Numbers for one route | Both s. 74ט and s. 2 tested with numbers; reasoned choice |
| Conduct | Ignored | Identified | Sister's repayments and the gift tested under ss. 219–220 with look-back periods, and advice on what to do now |
| Papers | Missing | Partial | Complete drafts for the chosen route |
| Stop signals | None | Generic | Specific to the facts |

## Model answer

<details><summary>Model answer</summary>

**Inventory.** Execution: credit card ₪14,800 (judgment), clinic ₪8,000 (note), bank ₪62,000 (judgment), electricity ₪3,900 (fixed-sum claim). Others: sister ₪20,000 (being repaid), arnona ₪6,000. Total ≈ ₪114,700.

**Diary.** File 1: payment-order request within the warning period from 20.11 and means inquiry within its period (see current numbers). File 2: opposition within 30 days of 25.11, so by 25.12.2026. File 3: warning periods long passed (deemed evader, s. 67(ד)); the credit-card restriction takes effect 30 days after 15.11, on 15.12, unless she pays, appears and shows she is not evading, or obtains a payment order she complies with (ss. 66ג, 66ד(ג)). File 4: a fixed-sum claim, so opposition within 30 days of service of its warning (s. 81א1(ד)–(ה)); if that period passed, an "I have paid" claim for the ₪1,500 with the bank statement.

**Defences.** File 2: opposition with affidavit: treatment stopped after two sessions, failure of consideration; attach the treatment plan and messages. File 3: release of protected wages (Wage Protection Law s. 8) and of maintenance money received (s. 50(א)(2) protects maintenance due from a third party; maintenance already in her account should be documented by source). File 4: as above.

**Budget.** Net income ₪7,300 + maintenance ₪1,800 (irregular; count what is actually received). Necessary expenses ≈ ₪8,100 (rent 3,000; food 2,400; children 900; utilities and arnona 700; car and fuel 700 needed for work; health 400). Capacity ≈ ₪1,000 at best, less when maintenance is late.

**Decision.** Execution route: ₪1,000 × the longest s. 7א1 period for debts over ₪100,000 (four years) = ₪48,000, even with a three-year extension ₪84,000 over seven years, against ≈ ₪115,000 plus interest: consolidation fails s. 74ט. Insolvency: cash-flow insolvent; balance sheet negative (car ₪12,000, little savings). Total ≈ ₪115,000 is below the 2026 threshold (₪176,923.12) and above the low-debt floor (₪58,794.37): she applies to the execution registrar's insolvency track, most simply by a s. 7א2 notice in the bank's file with the s. 104(ב) documents. The registrar convenes a payment-arrangement meeting; the bank holds most of the execution debt, so its position decides whether an arrangement passes (s. 195); otherwise an opening order (s. 199). Forecast: living allowance for three, payments of a few hundred shekels or none; immediate discharge is possible if her earning capacity does not exceed the allowance (s. 167).

**Conduct.** Repaying her sister ₪1,000 a month while insolvent: payments to a relative within a year before the application can be set aside as preferences (s. 219); she should stop paying her sister now and disclose the payments. The ₪7,000 gift to her son within two years while insolvent is an undervalue transfer (s. 220); disclose it; the trustee may seek its return; it is a possible extension ground (s. 163(ג)(3)).

**Papers.** For insolvency: creditors list (six creditors including the sister and the municipality), two-year report outline, account of the debts (divorce, single income, loan to cover the move), the s. 7א2 notice in the bank's file. Meanwhile, file the opposition in file 2 and the release request in file 3, since they protect her until the order.

**Stop signals.** The avoidance issues (sister, son), the possibility that the bank challenges the order, and the irregular maintenance (a separate maintenance-collection file may help her) make Legal Aid advice worthwhile before filing.
