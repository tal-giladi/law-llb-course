# Module 8 project: Civil case file from letter to enforcement

Build the procedural file for one money dispute of your own, from the pre-action letter through filing, the timeline, a settlement draft and an enforcement plan. It applies every lesson in module 8 to a single case. Send, file and enforce in your own name only if you choose; a dry run is the default. Time: about 6 hours.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

## Brief

Choose a money dispute of your own between a few hundred and a few tens of thousands of shekels: unpaid work, a refund a business refuses, a deposit a landlord keeps, a loan a friend has not repaid. Avoid disputes with your employer (labour court), family disputes (family court), land, personal injury and anything criminal. If you have no suitable matter, use the supplied case.

**Supplied case.** Lina Khoury, a web developer from Haifa (sole trader, עוסק מורשה), built an online shop for "ביתא עיצובים בע״מ", a Tel Aviv furniture company, under a signed quote of ₪18,000 plus VAT, half on signing and half on launch. The company paid the first half. The shop went live on 15 June 2026; the company's manager wrote on WhatsApp "האתר מעולה, נעביר את היתרה בסוף החודש". Nothing arrived. On 1 August the company emailed that "the site has bugs" and it will pay ₪4,000 "as a gesture". Lina has the quote, invoices, the launch email, the WhatsApp message and server logs showing the site has run without errors. The contract says: "כל מחלוקת תידון בבתי המשפט בתל אביב".

## Steps

1. **Forum and code (30 min).** Decide small claims or magistrates' court, and which city (Courts Law ss. 51, 60–61; Small Claims Regulations reg. 2 or Civil Procedure Regulations reg. 7). Identify the procedural code and whether the fast track applies (lessons 08.1–08.3).
2. **Debtor's identity (15 min).** Exact name and number from the Companies Registrar; registered address (lesson 08.8).
3. **Pre-action letter (45 min).** On the [pre-action letter template](../templates/pre-action-letter.md): facts with exhibits, itemised amount, dated deadline, named court, proof of delivery planned (lesson 08.8).
4. **Pleading draft (75 min).** For small claims: a complete claim on the [small claim template](../templates/small-claim-property-damage.md), within five pages. For the magistrates' court: heading (reg. 10), summary (reg. 11), detail (reg. 14), exhibits (reg. 15), and, on the fast track, the affidavit and document list (reg. 79) (lesson 08.2).
5. **The defence you expect (30 min).** Write the defendant's likely defence in five numbered paragraphs, and your reply to each. Mark which of your facts the defence must deny expressly to avoid deemed admission (reg. 14(ב)).
6. **Timeline (30 min).** A dated timeline from the letter to judgment and the appeal period, with every regulation and deadline from [current numbers](../references/current-numbers.md), and what applies to you as an unrepresented party (regs 35, 37, 61, 160).
7. **Filing dry run (30 min).** Prepare the PDFs, compute the fee, and walk through the online service or plan the registry visit up to submission (lesson 08.8).
8. **Settlement (30 min).** Your BATNA and lowest acceptable figure; a ten-clause settlement draft with a default clause and a request for the force of a judgment (lesson 08.6).
9. **Enforcement plan (40 min).** Assume you win. On the [execution request checklist](../templates/execution-request-checklist.md): first filing date, documents, track, fee, the steps after the warning, exempt assets, and the debtor's likely responses (lessons 08.7, 08.9).
10. **Decision (15 min).** One paragraph: what you will do now, the dates you diarised, and when you would get a lawyer.

## Self-assessment rubric

| Criterion | Weak (1) | Adequate (2) | Strong (3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Forum and code | Court chosen by habit | Right court, no reasons | Court, city and track justified with sections; jurisdiction clause considered |
| Debtor's identity | Trade name only | Company name | Registered name, number and address verified |
| Pre-action letter | No amount or date | Amount and date | Itemised amount, dated deadline, named court, exhibits, proof of delivery, no improper threats |
| Pleading | Narrative without structure | Most required parts | Every required item in order, within page limits, every fact with an exhibit |
| Expected defence | Not considered | Listed | Each defence answered; deemed-admission points identified |
| Timeline | Missing steps | Main steps | Every step with regulation and date; unrepresented-party rules applied |
| Settlement | Not drafted | Amount only | BATNA, lowest figure with reason, default clause, force of judgment |
| Enforcement plan | Not drafted | Filing date only | Date, documents, track, fee, steps, exempt assets, debtor responses |

A strong file scores at least 20 of 24, with no criterion at 1.

**Forum and code.** The claim is ₪9,000 plus VAT, so ₪10,620 (if VAT was invoiced). Lina is an individual (a sole trader), so small claims is open (s. 60(א)); the company is the defendant, which is allowed. The magistrates' court is also possible (s. 61), on the fast track (reg. 78). Small claims is cheaper and quicker and the dispute is simple (was the site delivered and working?). Venue: under Small Claims Regulations reg. 2(א) the court of the defendant's business (Tel Aviv) or the place of the act; reg. 2(א) applies "whatever any agreement says", and the clause points to Tel Aviv anyway. If the work was done and the site is used online, reg. 2(ב) (internet trade) may also allow Haifa; the safer choice is Tel Aviv. Code: Courts Law ss. 59–64 and the Small Claims Regulations; Part A of the 2018 Regulations frames the court's discretion.

**Debtor's identity.** "ביתא עיצובים בע״מ", registration number and registered office from the Companies Registrar's public search; letter to both the business address on the quote and the registered office.

**Pre-action letter.** Paragraphs: the quote (A); the first payment and invoice (B); launch on 15.6.2026 (C); the manager's WhatsApp confirming satisfaction (D); the 1.8 email and the offer of ₪4,000 (E); the server logs (F). Amount: ₪9,000 plus VAT as invoiced. Deadline: 14 days, dated. Court: the Tel Aviv small claims court. An offer to discuss. No mention of the police. Email plus registered mail; receipt kept.

**Pleading (small claims).** Heading with Lina's details, the company's name and number; place of the act; remedy ₪10,620 with linkage and interest; grounds: breach of contract (Contracts (Remedies) Law: enforcement of the payment obligation), and the company's own admission that the site was "excellent"; documents A–F; witness: Lina; preference for an in-person hearing; small claims filed this year: none; declaration signed. Two and a half pages.

**Expected defence and reply.**

1. "The site had bugs." Reply: the WhatsApp message after launch and the logs; no bug report before 1 August; the company used the site for six weeks.
2. "Payment depended on a final acceptance test." Reply: the quote says "on launch".
3. "The amount includes extras not agreed." Reply: the amount is exactly the quote's second half.
4. "We offered ₪4,000 in good faith." Reply: an offer below the debt is not payment; good faith (Contracts Law s. 39) cuts against withholding after confirming satisfaction.
5. "Wrong court because of the jurisdiction clause." Reply: the claim is filed in Tel Aviv.

Facts the defence must deny expressly: the quote, the launch date, the WhatsApp message. (Deemed admission applies in full under reg. 14(ב) of the 2018 Regulations in the magistrates' court; in small claims the judge decides on the evidence, but an unanswered fact is still in practice uncontested.)

**Timeline (small claims).** Letter on day 0; deadline day 14; claim filed online about day 20; court serves; Lina checks service at least 60 days before the hearing and serves herself if needed (reg. 7(ב1)); defence period after service (reg. 4(ב)); hearing; judgment at the hearing or within days (reg. 15); leave-to-appeal period (reg. 16(א)). No preliminary discussion or mediation-intake meeting (those are 2018-Regulations steps; small claims has its own rules). All periods from [current numbers](../references/current-numbers.md).

**Settlement.** BATNA: a strong chance of the full amount, the fee recoverable, perhaps modest costs; three to six months. Lowest figure: ₪8,500 plus VAT within 14 days. Draft: parties; the dispute; ₪X paid by date Y to account Z; on default the whole claimed amount falls due with linkage and interest and Lina may execute; no further claims about the online-shop project; each side bears its costs; a request to give the settlement the force of a judgment (Courts Law s. 79א(ב)).

**Enforcement plan.** If judgment is given at a hearing both attended, with no payment date: file after the default period (Execution Law s. 6(ב)); certified judgment copy with the company's number; bank confirmation; form 206; the short track if within its ceiling (s. 20ב), otherwise the regular track; the fee from current numbers. After the warning period: information on the company's bank accounts and a lien on them; liens on money owed to the company by its payment processor if known. Restrictions such as a passport bar apply to individuals, not to a company. Likely response: a payment-order request is unlikely from a company; more likely an "I have paid" claim if it paid part, or a stay request if it appeals. Lina keeps the file active and reports any direct payment (s. 19א).

**Decision.** Send the letter today; diary day 14; if unpaid, file in the Tel Aviv small claims court within the week. Get a lawyer if the company counterclaims for "defects" above the ceiling or the case is transferred to the magistrates' court.
