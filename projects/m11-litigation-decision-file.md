# Project: a litigation decision file

Take one money dispute in your own affairs, or the supplied case, and build the file a careful person prepares before deciding whether to sue: the entitlement and the remedy, the settlement range, a bias audit, the barriers and free help, base rates from official data, a full expected-value worksheet, a settlement offer in Hebrew, and a one-page decision memo. You will reuse this file in Clinic I (module 12), where you take an everyday dispute from start to finish.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. This project is a dry run: you may send the settlement offer in your own matter if you choose, but nothing is filed in court.

## Brief

Choose a dispute in which you are owed money or a refund, or in which someone's activity costs you money: a deposit, an unpaid invoice, a defective product or repair, an insurance payout, a neighbour's damage. If you have nothing suitable, use the supplied case and say so.

**Supplied case (invented; also used in lesson 11.5).** Yousef Haddad, a self-employed graphic designer in Nazareth, agreed by email with "א.ל. אירועים בע״מ", a small events company, to design its branding and a season of event posters for ₪18,500. He delivered everything; the company used the designs in its advertising, paid ₪4,000 in March 2026 and has not answered since. It is still trading and posts new events every week. In September 2026 its manager wrote on WhatsApp: "We can give ₪8,000 to close the matter, final offer." Yousef earns about ₪900 a working day.

Time: about 6 hours.

## Steps

1. **Facts and documents (40 minutes).** A dated, numbered chronology; every document as a numbered attachment; the sum you can prove line by line.
2. **Entitlement and remedy (30 minutes).** State the legal basis of the claim (contract, tort, consumer law), the remedy you want (money, enforcement, an injunction) and the forum, with reasons (lessons 11.1, 08.3). Say whether the remedy is a property rule or a liability rule.
3. **Settlement range (30 minutes).** Estimate the other side's expected cost of defending and your own expected recovery; describe the bargaining range and the transaction costs blocking a deal (lesson 11.1).
4. **Bias audit (20 minutes).** Which biases are likely to affect you and which the other side, with one piece of evidence each (lesson 11.2).
5. **Barriers and help (20 minutes).** Where in the dispute pyramid you are now, what could make you drop out, and the free help that applies (lesson 11.3).
6. **Base rates (30 minutes).** From the Judiciary's latest annual report, the figures for your forum: clearance rate, average case life, your adjusted time estimate (lesson 11.4).
7. **Expected value (60 minutes).** Complete the [decision worksheet](../templates/should-you-sue-worksheet.md), with a source for every number and three sensitivity checks (lesson 11.5).
8. **Settlement offer (45 minutes).** A one-page Hebrew offer to the other side: itemised figure tied to documents, a frame of losses avoided, a deadline, and what happens if it is not accepted (lessons 11.2, 08.8).
9. **Decision memo (30 minutes).** One page: your decision (sue, settle at what figure, drop or wait), your walk-away figure, the non-money considerations, and the signals that would send you to a lawyer.

## Self-assessment rubric

Score each criterion 0–2. A total of 14 or more (out of 18) means the project meets the standard.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facts and documents | Vague, undated | Mostly dated | Dated, numbered, every sum tied to a document |
| Entitlement, remedy, forum | Missing | Named without reasons | Legal basis, remedy type and forum reasoned |
| Settlement range | Missing | One side only | Both sides' expected values and the transaction costs |
| Bias audit | Missing | Generic list | Specific biases with evidence, for both sides |
| Barriers and help | Missing | Help named | Stage of the pyramid, risks of dropping out, specific free help |
| Base rates | None | A figure without source or year | Figures with source and year, adjusted to the case |
| Expected value | Missing or no sources | Calculated, some sources | Every input sourced; three sensitivity checks |
| Settlement offer | Missing or a demand without reasons | Figure and deadline | Itemised anchor, loss framing, deadline, consequence |
| Decision memo | None | A decision without reasons | Reasoned decision, walk-away figure, stop signals |

## Model answer

<details><summary>Model answer</summary>

This model uses the supplied case.

**Facts.** Email agreement of January 2026 for ₪18,500 (attachment 1); delivery emails with the files (2); the company's advertisements using the designs (3); bank record of ₪4,000 paid in March 2026 (4); WhatsApp offer of ₪8,000 (5). Balance claimed: ₪14,500.

**Entitlement, remedy, forum.** Contract claim for the agreed price (Contracts (General Part) Law; Remedies Law). Money only: a liability-rule remedy. Yousef is an individual and the sum is within the small-claims ceiling (current numbers), so small claims: low fee, no lawyers without leave (Courts Law s. 63), judgment at the hearing or within days. The company can be sued there, though it could not sue there itself.

**Settlement range.** Yousef's expected value is about ₪9,250 (below). The company, if it defends, risks about ₪14,500 plus interest and costs, a manager's day in court and a judgment on public record; its expected cost is around ₪12,000–13,000. Range: roughly ₪9,250–12,500. Transaction costs: the company's silence and its manager's "final offer" framing.

**Bias audit.** Yousef: anger and self-serving bias (he may refuse any figure below ₪14,500); sunk-cost thinking about the hours he spent. The company: loss aversion (paying feels like a loss, so it may gamble on Yousef not suing); anchoring with its ₪8,000 "final" figure.

**Barriers and help.** Yousef has named and blamed; he is at the claiming stage. Risk of dropping out: busy work season, dislike of conflict. Help: the court information centre for small-claims procedure; Kol-Zchut on small claims.

**Base rates.** Judiciary report 2023: small claims cleared 94.0% of new cases; average case life 4.8 months. Adjusted for a defended claim: 5–8 months.

**Expected value.** $p = 0.85$ (written agreement, delivery, partial payment and use of the designs); $c = 0.9$ (trading company with an active business; an execution file can attach its bank account); $J = 14{,}500$; $I \approx 350$; $K_w = 300$ (documented expenses); $K_l = 800$ (cautious); $F = 145$ (1% of the claim); $E = 0$; $T = 2{,}100$ (two days at ₪900 and ₪300 for stress).

$$EV = 0.85 \times 0.9 \times 14{,}850 + 0.85 \times 300 - 0.15 \times 800 - 145 - 0 - 2{,}100 \approx 9{,}250$$

Sensitivity: with $c = 0.7$, $EV$ falls to about ₪6,700, below the offer; with $p = 0.95$, it rises to about ₪10,700; halving $T$ adds ₪1,050. The decisive input is $c$, which is why checking that the company is trading matters.

**Settlement offer (summary, in Hebrew in the file).** To the company and its manager: the agreement, the delivery and the use of the designs are documented; the balance is ₪14,500; Yousef will settle for ₪13,000 paid within 14 days; otherwise he will file in the small-claims court for ₪14,500 with interest from filing and costs, and the company will also bear the fee it may be ordered to repay and a day of its manager's time.

**Decision memo.** Decision: send the offer; walk-away figure ₪10,000, slightly above the $EV$, because a certain sum soon is worth more to him than a gamble. If no answer within 14 days, file in small claims and diarise the hearing. Non-money: he wants to keep working in the local events market, so he will keep the tone factual. Stop signals: the company claims the work was defective and produces an expert opinion, files a counterclaim, or turns out to have stopped trading (then reconsider $c$ before spending more).
