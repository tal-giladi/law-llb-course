# Module 39 project: Capstone case file: one matter of your own, end to end

Take one real matter of your own, from any field the course covered, through the whole cycle: choose it with the gates and the decision matrix, write its case theory and strategy map, build the evidence and research file, draft and send the documents in your own name, run it with a log and the decision-point routine to a result or a reasoned stopping point, and close it with a retrospective and a plan for what comes next. Time: about 18 hours of work spread over three to six months (most of the calendar time is waiting for answers).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing anything.

> [!WARNING]
> If your matter touches the police or a criminal proceeding, run it only as the complainant, the person fined or the person a record is about. If you are or may become a suspect, or anything you would write may admit an offence, stop and get a lawyer or the Public Defender (lesson 37.1).

## Brief

**The matter.** One matter of your own that passes the three gates of lesson 39.1 (yours, low exposure, allowed alone) and scores at least 25 of 45 on the decision matrix. If you have no live dispute, choose one of the always-available matters: an employee's tax-refund review across the open years (lesson 31.5), an arnona audit and objection (lesson 31.7), your own will (lesson 31.2), or an information request about yourself to a public authority (lesson 14.10).

**The level.** Real, in your own name, wherever the gates, the deadlines and the get-a-lawyer checklist support it. Any step you do not take for real is drafted in full and marked "dry run" with the reason.

**Supplied case (only to practise before your own matter, or to check your work against the model answer).** Tsega Wondimu, 36, Kiryat Malachi, agreed on 2 July 2026 by WhatsApp with a self-employed contractor on "ריצוף ואיטום מקלחת כולל שיפועים" for ₪28,000 and paid ₪22,000 by bank transfer. From September water leaks into the flat below. A plumber's signed report (25 September) finds no slope to the drain and missing waterproofing in one corner, and that the building's pipes hold pressure. On 14 September she messaged the contractor; he came on 17 September and in a call on 20 September, which she recorded, said "it's the building's pipes". Since then he does not answer. A second contractor quotes ₪16,800; she reimbursed the neighbour ₪2,400 for the ceiling and paid ₪450 for the report.

## Steps

1. **Choose (lesson 39.1, 2 hours).** Inventory of at least five candidates; cross-index row for each; gates with reasons; weighted scores; the choice in three sentences; what you do with the rest. Open the [clinic case file](../templates/clinic-case-file.md) and diarise the first deadline.
2. **Theory and map (lesson 39.2, 2.5 hours).** The case theory (story, rule, remedy, theme) passing the five tests; elements and proof; the other side's best version with your answers and concessions; the strategy map with triggers, deadlines and fallbacks; the [should-you-sue worksheet](../templates/should-you-sue-worksheet.md) for a money matter and your walk-away figure; the backward timeline; a five-item risk register. Use the [capstone strategy map](../templates/capstone-strategy-map.md) (draft).
3. **Evidence and research (lesson 39.3, 3 hours plus waiting).** The proof matrix with a lawful "how obtained" for every exhibit; the records requests, logged; witnesses asked; a [research memo](../templates/research-memo.md) for each contested legal question, citing only texts and rulings you read; the procedure note for your forum; the exhibit index in story order.
4. **Documents (lesson 39.4, 3 hours).** The document ladder for your map; the first document drafted to the standard, self-reviewed and sent with proof of delivery (or a reasoned dry run); the formal filing drafted to the forum's form; your settlement clauses.
5. **Run (lesson 39.5, 2.5 hours plus 15 minutes a week).** The case log and the deadline diary with two reminders per date; the weekly review; the decision-point routine for every incoming document, with the decision in part י of the map; the costs ledger; the [hearing-day kit](../templates/hearing-day-kit.md) or the forum's equivalent with a settlement sheet written in advance.
6. **Close and look back (lesson 39.6, 2 hours).** The closing check against the result itself; enforcement or chasing for every gap; a decision on any further step inside its period; the closing memo with surviving dates; the [capstone retrospective](../templates/capstone-retrospective.md) (draft) with the calibration table; next steps for the parked matters; the annual check-up in your calendar.
7. **Lawyer decision (throughout, 1 hour).** Run part א of the [get-a-lawyer checklist](../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md) at four points: at the choice, before the first document, on the first answer or defence, and before any hearing or settlement. Record each result; for any red answer, the one-page summary and the referral.
8. **Reflection (30 minutes).** Half a page: what you did for real, what stayed a dry run and why, and the one habit from the course you will keep.

## Self-assessment rubric

| Criterion | Weak (1) | Adequate (2) | Strong (3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Choice | Matter not tested, or fails a gate | Gates and scores done | Gates with reasons checked in the reference page; scores with evidence; parked matters decided |
| Case theory | A grievance, not a theory | Story, rule and remedy | Passes the five tests; the other side's best version answered; honest concessions reflected in the remedy |
| Strategy map | A list of steps | Routes and deadlines | Triggers, fallbacks, side routes marked for whether they stop the clock; walk-away figure and backward timeline written before contact |
| Evidence | Documents in a pile | Exhibit index | Proof matrix with lawful sources; gaps closed or the theory changed; records requests logged |
| Research | None, or summaries cited | Current texts cited | Memos on each contested point citing only what was read, with dates and confidence grades |
| Documents | Generic, unsupported | On the right template | To the drafting standard and the forum's form; sent or filed with proof (or a reasoned dry run); enforceable settlement clauses |
| Running the matter | Events not recorded | Log kept | Log with proof and deadlines; weekly reviews; a written decision for every incoming document; costs ledger with receipts |
| Closing and retrospective | Not done | Outcome recorded | Result itself confirmed; surviving dates diarised; calibration with causes; lessons to revisit; next steps and annual check-up |
| Own-name discipline and lawyer decision | Acted for someone else; no check | Checklist run once | Checklist at four points; every document signed by you and about your own matter; any referral made with the summary |

A strong file scores at least 23 of 27, with no criterion at 1.

1. **Choice.** G1: her own contract and payment. G2: no exposure; nothing she writes admits an offence. G3: small claims, an individual within the ceiling ([current numbers](../references/current-numbers.md)); a lawyer only with leave. Scores: stakes 3, merits 3 (written scope, payments, report, recording), forum 3, alone 3, time 3, personal 3, learning 3: 45. First deadline: a formal notice of defect under s. 3 this week.
2. **Theory.** Story: paid for a waterproofed, sloped shower floor; got a floor that leaks; told him; he blamed the pipes and disappeared. Rule: Contract for Services Law ss. 3–4; foreseeable damage under the Remedies Law (lesson 03.8). Remedy, after the honest set-off of the unpaid ₪6,000: ₪16,800 − ₪6,000 + ₪2,400 + ₪450 = ₪13,650. Theme: "paid for a floor that leaks". The other side's best version: the pipes; no chance to repair; the unpaid balance. Answers: the pressure test; the formal notice with two repair dates; the set-off already made.
3. **Map.** Notice with dates (8–9 October) and a deadline (18 October) → pre-action letter with seven days → small claim → hearing → judgment → execution file if unpaid. Side routes: none needed. $EV$ about ₪7,150 with $p = 0.8$, $c = 0.8$, the fee and two days of her time; walk-away ₪9,000 or a proper repair at his expense. Risks: "she refused me entry" (answer: written dates); the court wants an expert (the signed plumber's report with photographs and method).
4. **Evidence.** Exhibits in story order: the WhatsApp quote; the transfers; the plumber's report; her video; the full WhatsApp export showing 14 and 17 September; the recording of 20 September with a transcript (she was a party, so it is not eavesdropping); the second quote; the neighbour's receipt. Witnesses: the plumber, the neighbour. Gap: the contractor's legal name and ID, from his invoice and business page. Memos: sufficiency of notice (made moot by the formal notice); recovery of the neighbour's ceiling (probable, foreseeable damage).
5. **Documents.** The s. 3 notice by WhatsApp, email and registered mail with confirmation; the pre-action letter; a four-page small claim with the reg. 3 lists, the deduction shown openly, and her hearing preference; settlement clauses with a payment date and a default clause.
6. **Running.** Log rows for each step; on the defence ("refused entry"; counterclaim for ₪6,000), the routine: her WhatsApp export answers the first, the set-off answers the second; a two-paragraph reply. Hearing kit: two-minute opening, three photographs, the report, the neighbour, the costs ledger.
7. **Close.** Judgment ₪12,400 plus expenses; not paid within 30 days; execution file on day 31 (Execution Law s. 6(ב)); instalments agreed through the file; file closed after the last payment. Closing memo with the dates. Retrospective: $p$ well calibrated; $c$ and time underestimated (no enforcement stage costed); the open set-off was the decisive good choice; the WhatsApp-only first notice the main mistake. Lessons to revisit: 20.4 (notice), 08.9 (execution). Next steps: the docked-pay question with lesson 17.3; her mother's NII review, which her mother files and signs; the annual check-up in January.
8. **Lawyer decision.** No red answer at any point; amber only if the court had ordered an expert at her cost.
