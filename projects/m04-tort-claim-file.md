# Module 4 project: Tort claim file in your own matter

Build a complete, court-ready file for one tort claim of your own: the legal analysis, the damages table, the defences and limitation, the demand letter, the insurance claim and a small-claims statement of claim. Send and file in your own name only if you choose; a dry run is the default. Time: about 6 hours.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

## Brief

Choose damage you or your household suffered that was not caused by a breach of contract with the defendant: a car damaged in a car park, water from a neighbour's flat, an object dropped from a building, a fall in a shop with minor injury, a dog that damaged your property. Avoid serious bodily injury and road-accident injuries for this project; those call for advice (lessons 04.2, 04.6). If you have no suitable matter, use the supplied case.

**Supplied case.** Hila Weiss, a graphic designer from Modi'in, keeps her electric bicycle in her building's storage room. On 2 August 2026 the building's maintenance company, hired by the residents' committee, sent a worker to clean the room with a pressure washer. He sprayed the bicycle's battery and display directly; the bicycle no longer works. The worker told Hila, in a WhatsApp message, "סליחה, לא שמתי לב שזה חשמלי". A bicycle shop quotes ₪2,900 for a new battery and display; the bicycle cost ₪6,500 in 2024. Hila spent ₪280 on taxis to work for the two weeks until she decided what to do. The maintenance company's website says it is "fully insured for third-party damage". Hila sent nothing yet.

## Steps

1. **Facts and tort map (45 min).** Write the facts in dated sentences. Map each harm to a tort and its section, with elements and evidence (lesson 04.1, 04.4).
2. **Negligence analysis (45 min).** Duty, breach, damage, causation; burden-shifting sections; the defence's best answer (lessons 04.2–04.3).
3. **Defendants (20 min).** Wrongdoer, employer, anyone who hired a contractor, insurer; the legal basis for each (lesson 04.7).
4. **Damages table (30 min).** Every item with an amount, a causation sentence and a document; fair value, not new-for-old (lesson 04.3).
5. **Defences and limitation (30 min).** Contributory fault range, any other defence, and the limitation dates for the tort claim and any insurance claim (lessons 04.5, 04.8).
6. **Demand letter (45 min).** On the [tort demand letter template](../templates/tort-demand-letter.md), with a deadline and a request for the insurer's details (lesson 04.8).
7. **Insurance claim (30 min).** On the [insurance claim letter template](../templates/insurance-claim-letter.md): to the wrongdoer's insurer under s. 68, or to your own insurer if that is better (lesson 04.8).
8. **Small-claims statement of claim (60 min).** On the [small claim template](../templates/small-claim-property-damage.md), complete under reg. 3, within five pages, with exhibits and declaration (lesson 04.9).
9. **Hearing plan (20 min).** A two-minute opening, three expected questions and answers, the defendant's best argument and your reply, and your lowest acceptable compromise.
10. **Diary and decision (15 min).** All dates. Write one paragraph: will you send, wait, file, or stop, and when would you get a lawyer?

## Self-assessment rubric

| Criterion | Weak (1) | Adequate (2) | Strong (3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tort map | "They were unfair"; no sections | Right tort, elements incomplete | Every harm mapped to a tort with section, elements and evidence |
| Negligence analysis | Outcome treated as proof of breach | Four elements stated | Notional and concrete duty, breach by the reasonable-person standard, ss. 38–41 considered, defence answered |
| Defendants | Only the person who acted | Employer added | Wrongdoer, employer or principal, s. 15 checked, insurer and s. 68 |
| Damages | Round figure | Itemised, some documents | Every item documented, fair value, causation sentence, mitigation noted |
| Defences and limitation | Not considered | Limitation date only | Contributory fault range, defences answered, tort and insurance limitation computed and diarised |
| Demand letter | Emotional, no date | Clear request and date | Tort and sections, itemised amount, date, insurer request, reservation, proof of delivery planned |
| Insurance claim | Phone call only | Written, few documents | Written, complete exhibits, s. 23/27/28 timing, request for reasons, s. 68 where relevant |
| Statement of claim | Missing required items | Most of reg. 3 met | Every reg. 3 item, within five pages, exhibits numbered, declaration signed, venue correct |
| Hearing plan | None | Opening only | Opening, questions, defence's best point, compromise figure with reasons |

A strong file scores at least 23 of 27, with no criterion at 1.

## Model answer

<details><summary>Model answer</summary>

**Facts and tort map.** On 2.8.2026 a worker of the maintenance company, cleaning the building's storage room at the committee's request, sprayed Hila's electric bicycle with a pressure washer, destroying its battery and display. Torts: negligence (ss. 35–36) by the worker; employer's liability (s. 13) of the maintenance company. Trespass to chattels (s. 31) is a poor fit: the worker did not take the bicycle or interfere violently with it.

**Negligence.** Duty: a cleaning contractor working in a storage room owes a recognised duty to owners of property stored there; damage to that property is plainly foreseeable. Breach: a reasonable cleaner checks what is in the room and does not spray electrical equipment; the worker's own message admits he did not notice. Damage: the battery and display. Causation: but for the spraying, no water damage; water damage is the very risk of careless pressure-washing. Burden-shifting is not needed: Hila knows the cause. Defence's best answer: "the bicycle was stored in a cleaning area; she should have covered it". Reply: storage rooms are for storage; the company did not warn residents of the cleaning.

**Defendants.** (1) The maintenance company, as employer (s. 13). (2) Its third-party liability insurer, under s. 68 of the Insurance Contract Law, once named. (3) The residents' committee is not a good defendant: it hired an independent contractor (s. 15) and none of the exceptions is evident. The worker personally is liable (s. 16) but adds nothing.

**Damages.**

| Item | Amount | Document | Causation |
|---|---|---|---|
| New battery and display | ₪2,900 | Bicycle shop quote | Direct result of the spraying |
| Taxis for 14 days | ₪280 | Taxi receipts | Loss of use; reasonable for two weeks, but she should now repair to mitigate |
| **Total** | **₪3,180** | | |

No reduction for age is claimed for the parts, because the repair restores a two-year-old bicycle rather than upgrading it; if the company argues betterment, a modest reduction is an acceptable compromise.

**Defences and limitation.** Contributory fault: 0–10% (stored in a shared room, not covered). Limitation: tort claim seven years from 2.8.2026; any claim on Hila's own policy three years from the event (s. 31).

**Demand letter (key paragraphs).**

> **הנדון: דרישה לפיצוי בגין נזק לאופניים חשמליים – אירוע מיום 2.8.2026 במחסן הבניין ברחוב ___, מודיעין**
>
> 1. ביום 2.8.2026, במהלך ניקוי מחסן הבניין על ידי עובדכם, מר ___, תוך כדי עבודתו, ריסס עובדכם במכונת שטיפה בלחץ את האופניים החשמליים שלי, והסוללה והצג יצאו מכלל שימוש (נספחים א–ב).
> 2. עובדכם התרשל כמשמעות הדבר בסעיפים 35–36 לפקודת הנזיקין [נוסח חדש], ואתם אחראים למעשהו לפי סעיף 13 לפקודה. עובדכם אף אישר בכתב כי "לא שם לב שזה חשמלי" (נספח ב).
> 3. נזקיי: סוללה וצג חלופיים – 2,900 ש"ח (נספח ג); נסיעות במונית – 280 ש"ח (נספח ד). סה"כ 3,180 ש"ח.
> 4. אני דורשת לשלם לי 3,180 ש"ח עד ליום 30.8.2026, וכן למסור לי בתוך 7 ימים את שם מבטח אחריות צד ג' שלכם ואת מספר הפוליסה.

**Insurance claim.** Paragraph B of the template to the company's insurer, citing s. 68, attaching the same exhibits, asking for the claim number and written reasons for any refusal.

**Statement of claim.** Venue: the event happened in Modi'in, so the small claims court with jurisdiction over Modi'in is proper (reg. 2(א)(5)); the court for the company's office would also be proper (reg. 2(א)(1)). Defendants: the company (with its registration number) and, if named, its insurer. Grounds: ss. 35–36, 13; s. 68. Damage table as above. Documents: six exhibits. Witnesses: Hila; optionally the neighbour who saw the worker. Hearing: in person; consents to a written decision. Previous small claims: none. Declaration signed. Body: two pages.

**Hearing plan.** Opening: "On 2 August the defendant's worker sprayed my electric bicycle with a pressure washer. He admitted in writing he did not notice it was electric. The repair costs ₪2,900, with ₪280 in taxis. I ask for ₪3,180." Expected questions: why the bicycle was in the room (it is the residents' storage room); whether there was a warning of cleaning (no); whether the quote is reasonable (one quote, shop's letterhead; can bring a second). Lowest compromise: ₪2,700.

**Decision.** Send the demand letter by email and registered mail; if no answer by the deadline, send the s. 68 claim to the insurer; if no payment within 30 days after that, file the small claim. A lawyer is not needed at this amount unless the company counterclaims or the case is transferred.
