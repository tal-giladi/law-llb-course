# Module 12 project: Clinic case file: one dispute from intake to enforcement

Run one everyday civil dispute of your own through the whole clinic: intake and facts file, lawful evidence, the demand letter and negotiation, a regulator where one exists, the small-claims statement, a hearing-day kit, the post-judgment diary and an enforcement plan, and a final decision on whether you need a lawyer. Unlike the module 8 project, which was a procedural dry run, this is the year-1 clinic: the default is to act in your own name where the step is low-risk (sending your letter, a regulator complaint), and to file the claim if your file and the decision checklist support it. Time: about 10–12 hours spread over several weeks, because real answers take time.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before sending or filing anything.

## Brief

Choose a dispute that passes the five filters of lesson 12.1: it is yours; it is a civil money or consumer claim; it is within the small-claims ceiling; it is well inside the limitation period; and you hold or can lawfully get the evidence. Typical clinic disputes: a rental deposit, a refund a shop refuses, a service not delivered, an overcharge by a phone or internet provider, minor damage to your property. Do not use a dispute involving land rights, bodily injury, your employer, a family member, or any criminal exposure.

**Supplied case (if you have no suitable dispute).** The clinic case from the lessons: Tsega Mekonen, Netanya, two-year lease at ₪5,400 a month, cash security of ₪10,800 against a receipt, flat returned on 31 July 2026 with photos sent the same day, the landlord's message of 2 August ("הכל בסדר, אחזיר לך את הפיקדון אחרי שאבדוק את חשבון המים"), his message of 20 September keeping the whole sum "for painting and wear", no prior notice, no quote; water bill of ₪180 actually owed. Assume the landlord ignores the demand letter, files a defence with a painter's quote dated after the claim, and that the hearing ends with judgment for Tsega for ₪9,800 plus ₪400 costs, given in both parties' presence on 2 December 2026, payable within 30 days, and not paid.

## Steps

1. **Intake (60 min).** Five-filter table for up to three disputes; choose one. Open the [clinic case file](../templates/clinic-case-file.md): cover page, parties checked against an official source, chronology, evidence index, remedy calculation, limitation date (lesson 12.1).
2. **Evidence (90 min).** Gap list; traffic-light classification with the section that decides each item; collect the greens; send one written request for information or footage under Privacy Law s. 13 if relevant; numbered PDFs (lesson 12.2).
3. **Letter and negotiation (120 min plus waiting).** Choose and adapt the right template; satisfy any statutory pre-condition (s. 31א(ב), s. 25י(ד), notice of breach); send by two channels with proof; write your walk-away figure and planned concessions; log every exchange; if you settle, use the six-clause settlement (lesson 12.3).
4. **Regulator (45 min).** Name the body for your opponent, or explain why none exists. If one exists and the business refused, file the [regulator complaint](../templates/regulator-complaint.md) in your own name, or dry-run it to the last screen (lesson 12.4).
5. **Statement of claim (150 min).** Draft on the [contract or consumer small-claim template](../templates/small-claim-contract-consumer.md); tick the filing checklist; record your filing decision with reasons (lesson 12.5).
6. **Hearing-day kit (120 min).** Disputed facts and proof, bundle, two-minute opening, five questions, the judge's likely questions, written settlement range; a 30-minute mock hearing with a friend (lesson 12.6; [hearing-day kit](../templates/hearing-day-kit.md)).
7. **After judgment (90 min).** Judgment-day diary with three dates and their sources; payment request; [execution request checklist](../templates/execution-request-checklist.md) and form 206 completed as far as possible; a three-line leave-to-appeal decision (lesson 12.7). If your real case has not reached judgment, do this step on the supplied judgment.
8. **Lawyer decision (45 min).** Run the [get-a-lawyer checklist](../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md) at the three points; one-page summary and five questions; list the two most suitable free-help sources (lesson 12.8).
9. **Reflection (20 min).** Half a page: what you did for real, what remained a dry run and why, what you would do differently.

## Self-assessment rubric

| Criterion | Weak (1) | Adequate (2) | Strong (3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intake | Dispute not tested | Filters applied | Filters applied with reasons; opponent verified; limitation computed with s. 9 considered |
| Facts file | Narrative only | Chronology and exhibits | Every fact dated and tied to a numbered exhibit; remedy table with sources |
| Evidence | Unclassified | Mostly lawful | Every item classified with its section; no red items; recordings transcribed |
| Letter and negotiation | Vague letter | Dated deadline and amount | Right template, pre-conditions met, proof of delivery, written walk-away figure, every offer confirmed |
| Regulator | Not considered | Body named | Right body, business-first step done, specific correction sought, deadlines still protected |
| Statement of claim | Missing reg. 3 items | Most items | Every reg. 3 item, ≤ 5 pages, grounds in three steps with sections, correct interest date |
| Hearing kit | None | Bundle and opening | Disputed facts mapped to proof, closed questions, judge's questions answered, written range |
| After judgment | Dates missing | Some dates | Three dates with sources (reg. 16א counting, recess), payment request, execution file ready |
| Lawyer decision | Not done | Checklist run once | Run at three points, decision recorded, summary and questions ready, free help identified |

A strong file scores at least 23 of 27, with no criterion at 1.
