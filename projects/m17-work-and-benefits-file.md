# Project: an employment and social security file, from payslip to appeal

Build the file a careful employee keeps when a job ends badly and National Insurance becomes involved: the status and employer check, the payslip review, the final-settlement table, the dismissal analysis (hearing, notice, protected grounds), the demand letter, the NII claims with their deadlines, and an appeal from a medical committee. You will reuse this file in the year-2 clinic (module 22, lessons on the employment claim and the National Insurance appeal).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. This project is a dry run, except the demand letter and an NII claim or appeal in your own matter, which you may send if it is low-risk and in time. Nothing is filed in court.

## Brief

Use a real employment that ended in the last year (yours, or a family member's with their consent and their signature on anything sent), or the supplied case, and say which.

**Supplied case.** Nadia Mansour, 44, from Jaffa, worked as a monthly-paid dental assistant at a private clinic in Tel Aviv from 1 January 2021 to 31 August 2026 (5 years and 8 months), last basic wage ₪8,000 for a 42-hour, five-day week. Three days a week she worked until 18:30 instead of 17:00 (1.5 extra hours), which she recorded in a notebook; her payslips show no overtime and the clinic keeps no attendance records. On 30 July 2026 she complained in writing to the clinic owner about repeated sexual remarks by one of the dentists; on 17 August she received a letter dismissing her "for budget reasons" effective 31 August, with no hearing. She has 10 unused leave days; recuperation for earlier years was paid. Her contract contains a s. 14 arrangement and her pension fund shows 6% severance deposits from July 2021. In March 2025 she slipped at the clinic and broke her wrist; the NII recognised a work accident, and on 20 August 2026 she received a medical committee decision of 0% permanent disability that does not mention her hand surgeon's letter describing reduced movement. She wants unemployment benefit from September 2026.

Time: about 7 hours.

## Steps

1. **Facts and documents (30 minutes).** A dated chronology; every document numbered as an annex (contract, payslips, notebook, complaint, dismissal letter, fund statement, NII decision, surgeon's letter).
2. **Status and employer (15 minutes, lesson 17.1).** Employee? Who is the employer? Any manpower or contractor triangle?
3. **Payslip check (40 minutes, lessons 17.2–17.3).** Three payslips against the nine Schedule items; minimum wage; overtime; balances; pension deposits. Note the s. 26ב presumptions.
4. **Dismissal analysis (40 minutes, lessons 17.4–17.5).** Hearing; notice; protected grounds (harassment complaint and retaliation; any permit needed?); the burden of proof.
5. **Final-settlement table (60 minutes, lesson 17.10).** Spreadsheet with inputs and formulas: notice, leave, recuperation, overtime, severance and the fund's share under s. 14; total; which heads are fee-free; expedited-hearing ceiling.
6. **Harassment and retaliation (20 minutes, lesson 17.5).** The elements under s. 3, the employer's s. 7 duties, the remedy without proof of damage, and whether to claim it with the wages or separately.
7. **Demand letter (40 minutes, lesson 17.10).** On the [letter to employer template](../templates/letter-to-employer-wage-claim.md), with the table as an annex.
8. **NII claims (30 minutes, lesson 17.7).** Unemployment: qualifying period and Employment Service registration; any other benefit; the 12-month rule.
9. **Medical appeal (60 minutes, lessons 17.9 and 17.11).** The appeal on the [NII medical committee appeal template](../templates/nii-medical-committee-appeal.md), part A, with the deadline; and a half-page outline of part B in case the appeals committee repeats the error.
10. **Forum, deadlines and stop signals (25 minutes).** A table of every deadline with its source; the forum for each dispute; the red flags that would send you to a lawyer or Legal Aid.

## Model answer

<details><summary>Model answer</summary>

**1. Facts.** 1.1.2021 start; July 2021 first pension deposit; 3.2025 work accident; 30.7.2026 written harassment complaint (annex 5); 17.8.2026 dismissal letter (annex 6); 20.8.2026 NII medical decision received (annex 8); 31.8.2026 end of employment.

**2. Status.** A dental assistant working fixed hours in the clinic's core activity, paid monthly: an employee of the clinic company. No triangle.

**3. Payslip check.** Item 4 (hours actually worked) and item 5 (overtime with quantity and rate) are missing; the clinic keeps no attendance records, so under s. 26ב the clinic must prove she did not work the disputed hours (up to 15 overtime hours a week), and without overtime lines a "global" wage is presumed. Basic ₪8,000 ÷ 182 = ₪43.96 an hour, above the minimum. Pension deposits match the fund statement from July 2021.

**4. Dismissal.** No hearing: a defect giving compensation. Notice: one month after the first year; 14 days given (17–31 August); 16 days owed. The dismissal came 18 days after a written harassment complaint: dismissal because of a complaint is retaliation (Prevention of Sexual Harassment Law s. 3(ב)) and a breach of the EEO Law's protection of complainants. Having shown no fault in her own conduct, the burden shifts to the clinic to prove the budget reason (EEO Law s. 9(א)(2), applied by analogy; the retaliation claim itself rests on timing and the absence of any earlier budget discussion). No ministerial permit is needed (no pregnancy or fertility treatment).

**5. Final-settlement table.** Inputs: wage 8,000; 182 hours; 21.67 working days; 68 months; 10 leave days; overtime 19.5 hours a month for 12 months; recuperation 7 days a year at ₪451.50 (⚠️ extension order; at the old ₪418 rate line 4 is about ₪1,951).

The wage heads (overtime, leave, recuperation as wage under the Wage Protection Law) are fee-free (Fees Regulations reg. 4); notice and severance pay a fee. ₪38,496 is just below the expedited-hearing ceiling (⚠️ ₪39,500 or ₪40,700 depending on the source; check current numbers). Adding the harassment and retaliation claim would take the claim above the ceiling; Nadia could file the wage claim in the expedited track and the harassment claim separately, or one combined ordinary claim. The fund's severance balance is claimed by a release letter, not from the employer.

**6. Harassment and retaliation.** Repeated sexual remarks after she showed she was not interested (s. 3(א)(4)) and possibly demeaning remarks (s. 3(א)(5)); the clinic received a written complaint and, instead of investigating, dismissed her: a failure of its s. 7 duties, making it liable, and retaliation. Remedy: compensation without proof of damage up to the indexed ceiling (s. 6(ב)), in the labour court, possibly behind closed doors. Claim within the limitation period (s. 6(ג)).

**7. Demand letter.** Sent 5 September 2026 by email and registered mail: the table as annex A; ₪38,496; deadline 19 September; release letter to the fund, form 161, a certificate of employment, her attendance records; a statement that she reserves the harassment and retaliation claims and will sign no general waiver. Severance becomes late from 16 September (Wage Protection Law s. 20).

**8. NII claims.** Unemployment: contributions in every month since 2021, so 12 of the last 18 months are met (s. 161); she registers with the Employment Service on 1 September and claims online; five waiting days (s. 172). She was dismissed, so no resignation issue.

**9. Medical appeal.** Work-injury track: appeal to the medical appeals committee within 30 days of 20 August, by 19 September 2026 (Work Disability Regulations reg. 27); a short appeal on 10 September, reasons by 19 October if needed (reg. 28). Ground: the committee ignored the hand surgeon's letter on reduced wrist movement and gave no reasons for 0%; she asks to be examined, to bring a companion with consent (reg. 9) and for the minutes. Part B outline: if the appeals committee again ignores the letter, an appeal on law to the Tel Aviv regional labour court within 60 days of receiving its decision (reg. 2), asking for remand to a different panel.

**10. Deadlines and stop signals.**

| Date | What | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1.9.2026 | Register with the Employment Service; claim unemployment | NII Law s. 161; s. 296 |
| 16.9.2026 | Severance late from this day | Wage Protection Law s. 20 |
| 19.9.2026 | Medical appeal due | Work Disability Regs reg. 27 |

Stop signals: the clinic counterclaims or alleges misconduct; the dentist threatens a defamation suit over the complaint; the clinic becomes insolvent; the NII appeal fails at the regional court. Legal Aid covers the NII appeal without a means test (s. 397); rape crisis centres can advise on the harassment claim.
