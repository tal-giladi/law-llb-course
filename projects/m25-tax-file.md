# Project: a personal tax file

Build the tax file a careful household keeps: a residency and sources check, a credit-points audit, a refund claim for an open year, a compliance calendar, and a real-estate tax analysis for a purchase or sale. You will reuse the file in the year-3 clinic (module 31: a tax refund and an objection).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. The project is a dry run: nothing is filed, except, if you choose, a real refund request for your own tax year.

## Brief

Choose either:

- **Your own household**, using your own Forms 106, certificates and property; or
- **The supplied case** below. Say which you used.

**Supplied case.** Yaakov and Miriam Dahan, from Modi'in. Miriam, 36, is a salaried social worker (₪12,000 a month gross in 2025; she gave only resident and woman points on Form 101). She finished an M.A. (two years) in June 2024. Yaakov, 39, is a self-employed physiotherapist with 2025 turnover of ₪180,000; he paid ₪14,000 in NII contributions and health tax in 2025. They have three children, born 2016, 2020 and 2025. In 2025 Miriam donated ₪2,000 to a recognised charity. They own one apartment in Modi'in (bought 2017, lived in since). On 15 June 2026 they signed a contract to buy a larger apartment for ₪3,100,000 and plan to sell the old one for ₪2,300,000 by the end of 2027. Yaakov also owns, with his three brothers, a quarter of their late mother's apartment in Ashdod (she owned only that apartment).

Time: about 7 hours.

## Steps

1. **Residency and sources (30 minutes).** Confirm residency for 2025 (s. 1) and classify each adult's income by source (s. 2) (lesson 25.1).
2. **Credit-points audit (60 minutes).** For each adult, list the points for 2025 with sections, value them at the year's point value, and mark what payroll missed (lesson 25.2).
3. **Filing duty (20 minutes).** Who must file a 2025 return, who is exempt, and why (s. 131; regs 2–3)?
4. **Refund claim (90 minutes).** For the exempt adult, prepare the refund request for 2025 with the [tax refund request (draft)](../templates/tax-refund-request.md): computation, exhibits, cover letter (lessons 25.4, 25.10).
5. **Self-employed calendar (40 minutes).** For the self-employed adult: VAT status, reporting period, advances, the 52% NII deduction, the annual return date (lessons 25.3, 25.9).
6. **Work grant check (15 minutes).** Is anyone in the household likely to be entitled (lesson 25.9)?
7. **Purchase tax (60 minutes).** With the [purchase-tax worksheet (draft)](../templates/purchase-tax-worksheet.md), decide the scale for the new apartment, compute the tax, and set the deadlines, including the replacement-sale date (lessons 25.6, 25.11).
8. **Appreciation tax on the sale (30 minutes).** Will the sale of the old apartment be exempt? Check s. 49ב(2), s. 49ג and the ceiling (lesson 25.7).
9. **Objection outline (30 minutes).** Assume the director assessed the new purchase at the additional-apartment rate because of the Ashdod share. Outline the objection with the [tax assessment objection (draft)](../templates/tax-assessment-objection.md) (lessons 25.5, 25.11).
10. **Stop signals (15 minutes).** List the points at which you would hand the matter to a lawyer or accountant, with the reason (lesson 25.8).

## Self-assessment rubric

Score each criterion 0–2. A total of 16 or more (out of 20) means the project meets the standard.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Residency and sources | Missing | Conclusion without sections | Each income classified by s. 2 paragraph; residency reasoned |
| Credit points | Missing | Points listed without sections or values | Every point with section and value; missed points identified |
| Filing duty | Wrong | Right conclusion, no reasons | Regs 2–3 applied to each adult |
| Refund claim | Missing | Computation or letter only | Computation, exhibits and letter consistent; within six years |
| Self-employed calendar | Missing | Some dates | VAT status, reports, advances, NII deduction, return date |
| Work grant | Not considered | Mentioned | Reasoned for each adult, with how and when to claim |
| Purchase tax | Missing | Scale chosen without analysis | Buyer unit, disregarded share, slices, deadlines |
| Appreciation tax | Missing | Conclusion only | ss. 49ב(2), 49ג, ceiling and the 24-month timing |
| Objection outline | Missing | Grounds without the undisputed amount | Grounds, undisputed amount, deadline, appeals-committee step |
| Stop signals | None | Generic | Concrete and tied to steps |

## Model answer

This model uses the supplied case. Figures are illustrative; check the year's brackets and values on the current numbers page.

**1. Residency and sources.** Both live, work and raise their children in Israel: residents (s. 1). Miriam: employment income (s. 2(2)). Yaakov: profession (s. 2(1)).

**2. Credit points 2025 (Miriam).** Resident 2 + travel ¼ + woman ½ = 2¾ (ss. 34, 36, 36א). Children (s. 66(ג)(4)): child born 2016 (age 9 in 2025): 2 points per the current text (⚠️ check); child born 2020 (age 5): 2½; child born 2025 (birth year): 2½. M.A.: studies ended 2024, so ½ point a year for 2025 and 2026 (s. 40ג). Total about 10¼ points; payroll gave 2¾ only. Missed about 7½ points × ₪2,904 ≈ ₪21,780 of credit, limited to her actual tax. Donation: 35% × ₪2,000 = ₪700 (s. 46).

**Yaakov.** Resident 2¼; children (s. 66(ג)(5)): age 9: 1; age 5: 2½; birth year: 2½. He claims them in his return.

**3. Filing duty.** Miriam: salary only, below the ceiling, no reg. 3 exclusion: exempt; refund via Form 135 or the online service. Yaakov: business income: must file an online return (ss. 131, 132).

**4. Refund claim (Miriam, 2025).** Income ₪144,000; tax by the 2025 brackets ≈ 10% × 84,120 + 14% × 36,600 + 20% × 23,280 ≈ ₪8,412 + ₪5,124 + ₪4,656 = ₪18,192; less 10¼ points (₪29,766) → tax due 0 (points exceed tax); withheld ≈ tax on ₪144,000 less 2¾ points ≈ ₪18,192 − ₪7,986 = ₪10,206. Refund claim about ₪10,200 plus linkage and interest; exhibits: Form 106, M.A. certificate, children's details, donation receipt, bank confirmation. The year is open until the end of 2031.

**5. Yaakov's calendar.** Physiotherapists are health professionals listed in reg. 13 (lesson 16.7; ⚠️ check the list): licensed dealer regardless of turnover; bimonthly VAT reports by the 15th (turnover below the s. 67(א2) threshold); advances monthly as a percentage of turnover; NII advances; 52% of the ₪14,000 contributions deductible = ₪7,280 (s. 47א); online return by the s. 132 date (often extended).

**6. Work grant.** Miriam's earned income is probably above the band; Yaakov's depends on his profit; run the eligibility check for him.

**7. Purchase tax.** Buyer unit: Yaakov, Miriam and the three children. At signing they own the old Modi'in apartment (to be sold within 24 months: by 15 June 2028; the plan to sell by end-2027 fits) and a quarter of the Ashdod apartment, inherited, within the half disregarded by s. 49ג(3). Single-apartment scale applies (s. 9(ג1ג)(2)(ב)). Tax: 0% on ₪1,978,745; 3.5% on ₪368,295 = ₪12,890; 5% on ₪752,960 (₪3,100,000 − ₪2,347,040) = ₪37,648. Total about ₪50,538. Declaration by 15 July 2026; payment by 14 August 2026. If the old apartment is not sold by 15 June 2028, the difference to the additional-apartment rate is collected.

**8. Appreciation tax.** The old apartment: lived in since 2017 (qualifying), owned more than 18 months, the new one bought as a replacement within 24 months before the sale (s. 49ג(1)), the Ashdod quarter disregarded (s. 49ג(3)), price below the ₪5,008,000 ceiling: exempt under s. 49ב(2) if requested in the seller's declaration and the sale is within 24 months of the June 2026 purchase.

**9. Objection outline.** Assessment delivered on [date]; objection within 30 days (s. 87(א)). Ground: the Ashdod share is a quarter of an inherited apartment, within s. 49ג(3) as applied by s. 9(ג1ג)(4)(א); exhibits: succession order, land-registry extract. Undisputed amount: ₪50,538 with the computation. Request to be heard (s. 87(ז)). If rejected: appeals committee within 30 days (s. 88).

**10. Stop signals.** Yaakov's VAT status or books challenged; any dispute on the price or value of the apartment; failure to sell the old apartment in time; the director's rejection of the objection where about ₪197,000 is at stake (8% × ₪3,100,000 less ₪50,538) (lawyer for the appeals committee).
