# Module 2 project: Research file and letter in your own matter

Build a complete, checked research file on one real legal question in your own affairs, and finish it with a formal letter that you send in your own name or keep as a dry run. Time: about 5 hours.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

## Brief

Choose a matter of your own in which an authority or a company owes you an answer, a decision, information, money or a service: a request an authority has not answered, a refused benefit, a deposit not returned, a charge you dispute. If you have no suitable matter, use the supplied case below.

**Supplied case.** Daniel Avraham, a student from Be'er Sheva, applied in writing to his municipality on 15 January 2026 for an exemption from a municipal fee because of his disability, attaching his National Insurance disability certificate. On 30 March he received an email: "בקשתך נדחתה". No reasons, no mention of appeal. He also wants to know which criteria the municipality uses for such exemptions.

## Steps

1. **Source log (45 min).** List every statute and regulation that bears on the question. For each: the consolidated text link, the amendment check (Wikisource against the Knesset database), the version that applied on your date, and the Reshumot reference for the decisive section (lesson 02.1).
2. **Case-law search (60 min).** Find at least three rulings, at least one from the Supreme Court if any exists. Record citation, court level and status check for each (lesson 02.2).
3. **Two case briefs (60 min).** Brief the two most important rulings on the [case brief template](../templates/case-brief.md) (lesson 02.3).
4. **Interpretation note (20 min).** For the most disputed word or deadline, apply the lesson 02.4 method and compute every period.
5. **Research memo (60 min).** Write the memo on the [research memo template](../templates/research-memo.md) (lesson 02.6), citing every source in the uniform-rules format (lesson 02.5).
6. **Letter (45 min).** Write the letter on the [letter to an authority](../templates/letter-to-authority.md) or [demand letter to a company](../templates/demand-letter-company.md) template (lesson 02.7). Send it in your own name with proof of delivery, or keep it as a dry run.
7. **Follow-up plan (10 min).** One paragraph: the reply deadline, what you will do if the answer is yes, no, or nothing, and when you would get a lawyer.

## Self-assessment rubric

| Criterion | Weak (1) | Adequate (2) | Strong (3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Question | Vague ("my rights") | Specific but mixes several issues | Specific, factual, one question per issue |
| Statutes | Quoted from secondary sources | Consolidated text, no version check | Consolidated text, amendment check, correct version, Reshumot for decisive words |
| Case law | None or only summaries | Rulings found, levels not distinguished | Binding and persuasive rulings distinguished; each checked forward |
| Briefs | Retell the facts | Holding stated, opinions not separated | One page; holding as a rule; majority, concurrence, dissent separated; pinpoints |
| Interpretation and deadlines | Plain reading only; deadlines guessed | Purpose mentioned; deadlines computed without rules | Meanings mapped, purpose sourced; every deadline computed under the Interpretation Law |
| Citations | Missing numbers or years | Mostly correct, inconsistent | Uniform-rules format throughout; pinpoints for every quote |
| Memo | No short answer; no counter-arguments | Answer and analysis, confidence not graded | Answer first; counter-arguments answered; confidence graded; open risks stated |
| Letter | Emotional, no request or date | Clear request, weak record | Specific request and date; brief legal basis; attachments; proof of delivery; diary date |

A strong file scores at least 20 of 24, with no criterion at 1.

**Source log.**
- חוק לתיקון סדרי המינהל (החלטות והנמקות), התשי"ט–1958: ss. 1, 2(א), 2א, 5, 6. Last amended 1995 (Wikisource source box and Knesset list agree: original law and four amendments). Version for 2026: current text.
- חוק הפרשנות, התשמ"א–1981: ss. 3 ("בכתב"), 10 (periods).
- חוק חופש המידע, התשנ"ח–1998: ss. 1, 7; תקנות חופש המידע (אגרות), התשנ"ט–1999, reg. 6(א) (information about oneself exempt from the application fee).
- The municipal by-law or council decision on fee exemptions: to be located on the municipality's website; Daniel has not yet seen it (open item).

**Case law.**
- עע"ם 9135/03 המועצה להשכלה גבוהה נ' הוצאת עיתון הארץ (19.1.2006): Supreme Court, binding; still cited as the starting point for FOI refusals. Use: criteria documents should be disclosed unless a weighed, reasoned exemption applies.
- עע"ם 1758/10 לשכת עורכי הדין בישראל נ' שגיא (אר"ש 15.8.2011): Supreme Court; on administrative evidence and proportionality of decisions harming rights; use limited to the principle that an authority's decision must rest on relevant evidence and a reasoned balance.
- No ruling found specifically on a municipal fee exemption under the Decisions and Reasons Law: open question.

**Briefs.** As in lesson 02.3 for עע"ם 1758/10; for עע"ם 9135/03: Issue: may an authority refuse FOI requests for internal discussions without weighing? Holding: no; s. 9(ב) gives discretion that must be exercised by weighing the interests, with reasons; disclosure is the rule.

**Interpretation and deadlines.** The emailed application was "בכתב" (Interpretation Law s. 3). Received 15.1.2026, not counted; day 45 is 1.3.2026 (a Sunday, so no rest-day extension). The municipality answered on 30.3.2026, after the 45 days, without having given notice of a reason for delay under s. 2(ג): a breach, though the answer has now been given. The answer is a refusal without reasons: breach of s. 2א unless a s. 3 exemption applies, which none apparently does. If the decision is appealable, the municipality also breached s. 5.

**Memo, short answer.** The municipality must give written reasons for refusing the exemption and, if an appeal or objection route exists, tell Daniel how and when to use it (Decisions and Reasons Law ss. 2א, 5). Confidence: clear. Whether the time to object runs before he receives reasons: uncertain; he should act on the assumption that it does. The exemption criteria should be requested under the FOI Law; information about himself is exempt from the application fee. Confidence: likely.

**Letter (Hebrew, to the head of the municipal revenue department, by email and registered mail):**

> הנדון: בקשה לנימוקי החלטה ולפרטי השגה – בקשתי לפטור מאגרה מיום 15.1.2026
>
> 1. ביום 15.1.2026 הגשתי בקשה לפטור מאגרה בצירוף אישור נכות מהמוסד לביטוח לאומי (נספח א).
> 2. ביום 30.3.2026 קיבלתי הודעה "בקשתך נדחתה", ללא נימוקים וללא הודעה על דרכי השגה (נספח ב).
> 3. לפי ס' 2א לחוק לתיקון סדרי המינהל (החלטות והנמקות), התשי"ט–1958, יש להודיע למבקש בכתב את נימוקי הסירוב; לפי ס' 5 לחוק, יש להודיע על זכות ההשגה, דרכיה ומועדיה.
> 4. אבקש לקבל בתוך 14 ימים את נימוקי ההחלטה ואת פרטי הדרך והמועד להשיג עליה.
> 5. בנפרד אני מגיש בקשה לפי חוק חופש המידע, התשנ"ח–1998, לקבלת אמות המידה למתן הפטור.

> בכבוד רב, דניאל אברהם

**Follow-up plan.** Diary: 14 days for the reply; FOI decision within 30 days of receipt (s. 7(ב)). If reasons come and are weak: object within the stated period. If no reply: reminder, then a complaint to the State Comptroller and Ombudsman. Lawyer: if the amount at stake becomes significant or an objection deadline is unclear and close.
