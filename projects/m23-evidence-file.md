# Module 23 project: Evidence file: from evidence map to summation

Build the complete evidence file for one civil case in a magistrates' court: an evidence map by the four layers, a burden table, a screen for privilege, hearsay and unlawfully obtained material, the foundation for every document and digital file, an expert plan, your affidavit and exhibit bundle, a witness examination plan and a summation outline. It applies every lesson of module 23 to a single case. A dry run is the default; nothing is filed. Time: about 8 hours.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

## Brief

Choose a civil dispute of your own where the facts are disputed and documents matter: a renovation, a vehicle purchase, a rental, a service contract. Avoid criminal, family and personal-injury matters for the real-case option. If you have none, use the supplied case.

**Supplied case.** Kamal Halabi, a high-school teacher from Daliyat al-Karmel, bought a used car for ₪62,000 from "רכבי הכרמל בע״מ", a Haifa dealer, on 4.5.2026. The sale form says "הרכב נמכר במצבו, ללא תאונות". The salesman, Eran, told Kamal by phone before the sale that "the car never had an accident"; Kamal recorded the call. On 20.6.2026 a garage found frame repairs consistent with a serious accident; the garage owner told Kamal "this car was totalled". Kamal's insurance company's database shows a 2024 claim on the car. Kamal's brother-in-law, who works at another dealership, sent Kamal a screenshot from that dealership's internal system showing the 2024 accident. The dealer denies everything, says the frame damage happened after the sale, and has filed a photo of Kamal's car parked badly in his own driveway, taken by the dealer's employee through Kamal's garden gate, "to show how he treats the car". Kamal sues for ₪18,000 (loss of value) and ₪2,400 (inspection costs).

## Steps

1. **Evidence map (45 min).** List the elements of a claim for misrepresentation and non-conformity (lessons 03.5, 20.1). For each, the fact, the evidence and the four layers: competence, admissibility, weight, sufficiency (lesson 23.1).
2. **Forum and rules (15 min).** Which court, and does it relax evidence rules? What never relaxes (lesson 23.1)?
3. **Burden table (30 min).** Who bears the burden on each element and on the dealer's defence that the damage happened after the sale; any presumption or shift that helps (lesson 23.6).
4. **Witnesses and privilege (30 min).** Who will testify; who needs a summons; any privilege issue, including the brother-in-law's employer's information and any lawyer Kamal consulted (lesson 23.2).
5. **Hearsay screen (30 min).** For every statement in the file (the salesman, the garage owner, the insurance database, the brother-in-law), decide: hearsay or not, exception, and the lawful route (lesson 23.3).
6. **Documents and digital evidence (45 min).** Foundation for the sale form, the recording, the insurance database record and the garage report; the s. 36 certificate request; the reg. 71 notice (lesson 23.4).
7. **Unlawfully obtained material (30 min).** Decide what to do about the brother-in-law's screenshot, and how to object to the dealer's photo (lesson 23.5).
8. **Expert plan (30 min).** Do you need an expert, which field, party or court expert, deadlines, and what the expert must answer (lesson 23.7).
9. **Affidavit and bundle (90 min).** Kamal's affidavit in lieu of main testimony and the exhibit bundle with index, on the [affidavit and exhibit bundle](../templates/affidavit-and-exhibit-bundle.md) template (lesson 23.9).
10. **Examination plan and summation (60 min).** Cross-examination of Eran and of the dealer's expert, three prepared objections and the summation table, on the [witness examination plan](../templates/witness-examination-plan.md) template (lesson 23.10).
11. **Decision memo (15 min).** One page: the weakest element, what you will do to strengthen it, the dates diarised, and when you would get a lawyer ([get a lawyer checklist](../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md)).

## Self-assessment rubric

| Criterion | Weak (1) | Adequate (2) | Strong (3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence map | List of exhibits | Elements with evidence | Every element with fact, evidence and all four layers; gaps marked with actions |
| Burden | Not addressed | Plaintiff's burden noted | Burden and standard per element and defence; presumptions and shifts considered |
| Hearsay | Gossip in the file | Most hearsay spotted | Each statement classified by purpose; party admissions kept; lawful routes for the rest |
| Documents and digital | Printouts only | Sources listed | Authenticity, integrity and admissibility per exhibit; s. 36 request; reg. 71 notice |
| Unlawful material | Uses the screenshot | Drops it | Drops it with the right section, finds a lawful substitute, objects to the photo under s. 32 |
| Expert | None or wrong field | Field chosen | Field, party or court expert decided with reasons; deadlines; neutral questions |
| Affidavit and bundle | Argument and hearsay | Mostly facts | First-hand facts, dated, exhibit-linked, warning formula; index with sources |
| Examination and summation | Open questions | Some closed questions | Closed, document-tied questions; prepared objections; summation by element and burden |

## Model answer

<details><summary>Model answer</summary>

**1. Map (selected rows).**

| Element | Fact | Evidence | Layers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Representation | "never had an accident" before the sale | Recording of Kamal's call with Eran; sale form "ללא תאונות" | Competent (Kamal, s. 2); admissible: the dealer's own words, a party's admission through its employee, and the form is a party document; lawful recording (Kamal was a party, Eavesdropping Law s. 1); weight high |
| Falsity: accident before sale | 2024 accident | Insurance database record; garage report; expert | Insurance record admissible as an institutional record with a s. 36 certificate; the garage owner's remark "totalled" is hearsay and opinion: he must testify or give an expert opinion |
| Loss | ₪18,000 loss of value | Appraiser's opinion | Needs an expert (lesson 23.7) |
| Costs | ₪2,400 inspection | Invoices | Own documents |

**2. Forum.** Magistrates' court (above the small-claims ceiling; see current numbers), ordinary or fast track by value. Full evidence rules apply; privileges and the exclusion rules in Eavesdropping s. 13 and Privacy s. 32 apply everywhere.

**3. Burden.** Kamal bears the burden on representation, falsity, reliance and loss, on the balance of probabilities. The dealer bears the burden on its defence (damage after sale). If Kamal alleges deliberate deceit, the court will want strong evidence (Zikri); a claim based on non-conformity and misrepresentation is easier. No statutory shift applies; the strongest tool is the dealer's own written statement in the sale form.

**4. Witnesses.** Kamal; the garage owner (summons if reluctant, CPR reg. 83); an appraiser or vehicle engineer (expert); a records officer of the insurer, or a certificate. Eran is the dealer's witness; Kamal may cross-examine him. Any advice Kamal got from a lawyer is privileged (s. 48), and he should not disclose it. The brother-in-law's employer's internal data are not Kamal's to use.

**5. Hearsay.** Eran's words: admission of a party (the dealer, through its salesman), offered to prove the representation was made, admissible. The garage owner's remark: hearsay and opinion; he testifies or gives a written opinion under s. 20. The insurance database: institutional record (ss. 35–36) with a certificate. The brother-in-law's screenshot: hearsay of another dealership's system and unlawfully obtained (below).

**6. Foundation.** Sale form: original in Kamal's hands; the dealer signed it. Recording: original file, unedited, transcript, Kamal's testimony on date and participants, reg. 71 notice three days before the hearing. Insurance record: written request under part ג of the template asking for a certificate covering s. 36(א)(1)–(3); and, for Kamal's own policy data, a Privacy Law s. 13 request. Garage report: the garage owner as witness or expert.

**7. Unlawful material.** The screenshot is copied from a system Kamal had no right to access, and is not addressed to him: a likely privacy infringement (Privacy Law s. 2) and a possible computer offence for the brother-in-law; s. 32 excludes it without consent, and using it would expose both. Drop it; the insurance record proves the same fact lawfully. The dealer's photo was taken into Kamal's private domain through his garden gate (s. 2(3)): object under s. 32; it is also irrelevant to whether the accident preceded the sale.

**8. Expert.** A vehicle assessor or engineer (שמאי רכב) to answer: is the frame repair consistent with the 2024 accident; could it have occurred after 4.5.2026; what is the loss of value. Party expert filed by the reg. 87(ה) period before the evidence hearing, or ask for a court expert if costs are disproportionate (reg. 88; cost-sharing reg. 92). Expect the dealer's expert; plan to cross-examine on whether he inspected the car himself.

**9. Affidavit (extract).**

1. אני התובע בתיק זה, מורה, מתגורר בדאלית אל-כרמל.
2. ביום 1.5.2026 שוחחתי בטלפון עם ערן, איש המכירות של הנתבעת. שאלתי אם הרכב היה מעורב בתאונה. ערן השיב: "הרכב אף פעם לא עשה תאונה". הקלטת השיחה, שבה הייתי צד, ותמליל מצורפים ומסומנים נספח 2.
3. ביום 4.5.2026 חתמתי על טופס מכר שבו נכתב "הרכב נמכר במצבו, ללא תאונות". הטופס מצורף ומסומן נספח 1.
4. ביום 20.6.2026 מסרתי את הרכב למוסך [שם] לבדיקה. הדוח שקיבלתי מצורף ומסומן נספח 4; מר [שם], בעל המוסך, יעיד על ממצאיו.

(No sentence about what the garage owner or the brother-in-law said; no adjectives about the dealer.)

**Index (extract).** נספח 1 – טופס מכר, מקור אצלי, נחתם בידי הנתבעת; נספח 2 – הקלטה ותמליל, קובץ מקור בטלפון, הודעה לפי תקנה 71; נספח 3 – רשומת חברת הביטוח, עם אישור לפי סעיף 36; נספח 4 – דוח המוסך, עורכו יעיד.

**10. Cross-examination of Eran (extract).** "You spoke with Kamal on 1 May?" — "You told him the car never had an accident?" (play the recording if he denies) — "You signed the sale form stating 'ללא תאונות'?" — "Before selling you did not check the insurance claims history?" Stop. Objections prepared: the dealer's photo (s. 32; irrelevance); any hearsay from the dealer's manager about "what the previous owner said"; an employee giving engineering opinions. Summation: element by element with exhibits and transcript pages; the dealer's defence unproved because its expert did not examine the car and no evidence shows a post-sale accident; the remedy.

**11. Decision memo.** Weakest element: timing of the frame damage; strengthen with the insurer's certificate and an assessor's opinion. Dates: s. 36 request sent; expert-opinion filing date; reg. 71 notice date. Get a lawyer if the dealer counterclaims for defamation, or if the expert evidence is disputed by an engineer whose opinion Kamal cannot answer.
