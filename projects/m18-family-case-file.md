# Module 18 project: Family case file, from the request to settle to the first hearing

Build the file a separating spouse or parent needs for the first months of a family dispute in the Family Court: the issue map, the request to settle the dispute and its timeline, the mahut preparation, a property review, a maintenance calculation, a parenting plan, and a safety section. It applies every lesson in module 18. Use the supplied case, or your own situation only with the consent of everyone involved and without identifying details of others. Nothing is filed; if this is your own matter, you decide separately, in your own name, whether to act. Time: about 7 hours.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

> [!WARNING]
> If anyone in the family is in danger, call the police on **100** or the domestic-violence hotline **118** first. Do not use this project to plan around a current threat.

## Brief

**Supplied case.** Adi Mizrahi (a nurse, net ₪13,000 a month) and Karim Saliba (a software developer, net ₪26,000) live in Ramla. Adi is registered as Jewish and Karim as Christian (Roman Catholic). They married in Cyprus in 2015 and are registered as married. They have two children: Maya (5) and Elias (9). The apartment, bought in 2017, is registered to both, with a mortgage. Karim has a pension fund and a study fund; Adi has a pension fund and ₪150,000 she inherited from her aunt in 2021, kept in a separate account. In 2014 they signed a two-line "separation of property" agreement that was never certified or approved. Since July 2026 they have lived apart under one roof. Karim's job offer in Berlin starts in February 2027 and he wants the children to move with him for two years; Adi refuses. Last month, during an argument, Karim threw a phone at the wall and said Adi would "never see the children again" if she blocked the move. He has no weapons. Adi wants: to stay in the apartment with the children, child maintenance, a fair share of pensions, and safety.

## Steps

1. **Issue map (45 min, lessons 18.1, 18.2).** List every issue: dissolution of the marriage (different communities: lesson 10.4 and the 1969 Law), property, pensions, child maintenance, spousal maintenance, residence and parenting time, relocation abroad, the children's passports, safety. For each, the governing law with its section and the forum or forums. Use the [forum check worksheet](../templates/forum-check-worksheet.md).
2. **Safety first (40 min, lesson 18.7).** Decide whether the facts support a protection-order request under s. 3, which ground, and which orders under s. 2. Decide also whether to seek an exit-ban order for the children now (Dispute Resolution Regulations reg. 2א(א)). Draft the exit-ban request on Form 5 structure using the [request-to-settle template](../templates/request-to-settle-family-dispute.md), part B, and, if justified, a protection-order request using the [protection-order template](../templates/protection-order-request.md).
3. **Request to settle and timeline (45 min, lesson 18.9).** Draft Adi's request (part A of the template): no facts. Build the dated timeline from a filing date of today: stay, notices, the 15-day window under s. 4, festival days under s. 6א. Decide which claims Adi would file in the window and where.
4. **Mahut preparation (30 min, lesson 18.9).** One page: interim arrangements Adi could accept for the children and money, what she will not sign without advice (reg. 5(ה)), and her answer to Karim's relocation proposal.
5. **Property (60 min, lessons 18.3, 18.4, 18.10).** Review the 2014 agreement with the [property-agreement checklist](../templates/property-agreement-checklist.md): is it effective (ss. 1–2)? If not, apply the balance of resources. Calculate the balance with your own assumed figures for the apartment, mortgage and pensions, treating the inheritance as excluded (s. 5(א)(1)). State whether s. 5א allows an early balance and when. State what s. 6א means for the apartment.
6. **Child maintenance (45 min, lesson 18.5).** Identify the governing law for each parent (personal law; s. 3א where none applies). Explain how the court might approach it, and calculate an illustrative 919/15-style figure for Elias (9), and separately the approach for Maya (5). Draft a one-page interim-maintenance request in the short form required during the stay (reg. 12(ה)).
7. **Parenting plan and relocation (60 min, lesson 18.6).** Draft a parenting plan for both children; apply s. 25 to Maya and best interest to Elias; write a balanced analysis of the relocation request with Karim's strongest argument and Adi's strongest argument, and the factors a court will weigh.
8. **Decision memo (30 min, lessons 18.1–18.10).** One page: the first three actions with dates, the evidence to collect, the points where Adi needs a lawyer, and free help available.

## Self-assessment rubric

| Criterion | Weak (1) | Adequate (2) | Strong (3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Issue map | Issues listed without law or forum | Forum given for most issues | Every issue with governing law, section and forum; dissolution route for a mixed couple identified |
| Safety | Ignored | Protection order mentioned | Grounds and orders tied to ss. 2–3; exit-ban request drafted on the right form; emergency numbers |
| Request and timeline | Request contains facts | Bare request; partial timeline | Bare request; dated timeline with s. 6א and the s. 4 window; claims and forums chosen |
| Mahut preparation | None | Goals listed | Interim proposals, red lines, reg. 5(ה), relocation answer |
| Property | Agreement assumed valid | Balance attempted | Agreement checked under ss. 1–2; correct exclusions; pensions; s. 5א timing; s. 6א |
| Maintenance | A single figure without basis | Law identified | Personal-law analysis per parent, 919/15 method for Elias, Maya treated separately, interim request in short form |
| Parenting and relocation | One-sided | Plan drafted | Plan complete; s. 25 applied to Maya; balanced relocation analysis with factors |
| Neutrality and accuracy | Takes sides; errors | Mostly balanced | Each side's strongest legal argument; every rule cited; unverified points marked |

A strong file scores at least 20 of 24, with no criterion at 1.

<details><summary>Model answer</summary>

**Issue map.** Dissolution: a Jew and a Christian married abroad; no religious court has exclusive jurisdiction over both, so dissolution goes through the 1969 Law (the President of the Supreme Court decides the forum; lesson 10.4). Property and pensions: Property Relations Law, Family Court. Child maintenance: each parent's personal law (Maintenance Law s. 3), Family Court; s. 3א where no personal law imposes a duty. Spousal maintenance: personal law (s. 2), uncertain for a mixed couple; take advice. Residence, parenting time, relocation, passports: Capacity and Guardianship Law, best interest, Family Court. Safety: Prevention of Family Violence Law, any magistrates' court or the Family Court.

**Safety.** Throwing a phone and threatening that Adi will "never see the children" may support s. 3(2) (reasonable fear of real physical danger) only if there is more; the threats and control may be argued under s. 3(3) (heard with both parties). Many courts would consider an exit-ban order more urgent: the job offer and the threat create a real risk of removal; Form 5 structure, short affidavit, documents (the offer, messages). Emergency: 100 and 118.

**Request and timeline.** Family Court in Ramla's district (last joint residence), Form 1, subjects ticked (property, maintenance, children), no facts. Day 0 filing; mahut within 45 days; five days to notify; stay periods under s. 3(ה); Adi as first filer then has 15 days to file in any competent forum. Claims: property and pensions, child maintenance (treated as filed on day 0, reg. 15(ב)), custody and relocation, all in the Family Court; dissolution separately under the 1969 Law.

**Property.** The 2014 agreement is written but neither approved nor certified (s. 2); it is vulnerable and should not be relied on. Default balance of resources: apartment equity and both pension and study funds accumulated since 2015 are balanceable; the inheritance kept separately is excluded (s. 5(א)(1)). Early balance: separation under one roof since July 2026 reaches nine months within a year in April 2027 (s. 5א(א)(2)), earlier if the court shortens it. The apartment will not be sold while it houses the minor children with Adi unless suitable housing is found (s. 6א). Pension division through a judgment registered under the 2014 Law.

**Maintenance.** For Elias (9), if the Jewish-law approach of 919/15 is applied to the parent whose personal law it is, needs are shared by income (Karim 2/3, Adi 1/3) and parenting time; with the children mostly at Adi's, Karim transfers the larger part. For Karim, Roman Catholic law as applied by the court; where no duty arises, s. 3א (both parents by income). Maya (5): the analysis differs below age 6 for a Jewish parent's duty; a mixed family needs advice. Interim request during the stay: reg. 12(ה), incomes and children's needs only.

**Parenting and relocation.** Maya: tender-years presumption (s. 25; 1858/14). Elias: best interest; he may be heard (reg. 49). Relocation: Karim's strongest argument is the children's benefit from a two-year opportunity and continued involvement of a committed father; Adi's is stability, school, community, the younger child's age and the risk to her relationship with them. Courts weigh the reason for the move, the children's ties, the realistic contact plan, and the risk of non-return (Hague Convention context).

**Decision memo.** (1) Today: exit-ban request; consider a protection order; call 118 for advice. (2) This week: request to settle in the Family Court; collect pay slips, pension statements, mortgage statements, the 2014 agreement, the inheritance trail. (3) Before the first meeting: interim proposal for the children and money. Lawyer: relocation, mixed-couple dissolution, the 2014 agreement. Free help: assistance unit, Legal Aid (means test), Kol-Zchut (⚠️ manual check).
