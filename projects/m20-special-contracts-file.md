# Module 20 project: Special-contracts file: home, money and services

Build the contracts file of one household over one year: a new apartment bought from a developer, the rented flat they live in meanwhile, a renovation, a guarantee for a relative, a home-contents insurance claim, an online purchase and a mistaken payment. It applies every lesson of module 20. Use your own household's documents where you can; otherwise use the supplied case. It is a dry run: you send and file nothing unless you decide to, in your own name. Time: about 6 hours.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

## Brief

Prepare, for one household: (a) a check of a developer's contract and its securities; (b) a defects file and notice after handover; (c) a tenant's repair and security letter; (d) a contractor dispute; (e) a decision on a guarantee; (f) an answer to an insurer's rejection; (g) a consumer cancellation; (h) a restitution demand. Finish with one page: what to send first, to whom, and when to get a lawyer.

**Supplied case.** Nadia Haddad and Eitan Sasson, from Ramla, both 38, two children.

- **New flat.** In March 2025 they signed to buy a four-room flat in a financed project in Lod from "Merkaz Building Ltd" for ₪1.9 million, handover "by 1 March 2026". The contract links "all payments to the construction-inputs index until actual handover" and says "handover protocol constitutes final acceptance". They paid 10% at signing by transfer, the rest by vouchers. Keys came on 15 June 2026. An engineer found a cracked balcony floor, a sliding door that jams and no water pressure in the guest toilet. In September damp appears in the children's room.
- **Rental.** Until July 2026 they rented in Ramla from Shlomo Avraham at ₪5,500 a month, one-year lease from August 2025, cash deposit ₪20,000. The water heater failed in December 2025; Shlomo fixed it after 25 days. Shlomo is withholding ₪8,000 of the deposit "for painting and a broken blind".
- **Renovation.** In the new flat they hired "Yoav Renovations" to build a wardrobe wall from its own boards (₪14,000) and install kitchen fittings they bought (₪6,000). The wardrobe doors sag; the installer left one cabinet unfixed and stopped answering.
- **Guarantee.** Eitan's cousin asks Eitan to guarantee a ₪120,000 business loan from a bank to the cousin's company, in which Eitan has no shares; the bank's form says "all debts of the company, present and future".
- **Insurance.** Their contents insurer rejected a claim for a laptop and jewellery stolen from the rented flat in May 2026, citing (1) "alarm not activated" (a condition on page 22, general conditions, normal type) and (2) "non-disclosure of a prior theft" (the proposal asked about thefts "in the last three years"; a bicycle was stolen from their building's storage in 2024, which they did not mention).
- **Online purchase.** On 1 August 2026 Nadia ordered a sofa online for ₪7,400; it arrived on 20 August; no disclosure document; she wants to cancel on 10 September because it does not fit the room.
- **Mistaken payment.** In July Eitan paid the July rent to Shlomo twice by standing order after the lease ended; Shlomo kept the second ₪5,500 "for damages".

## Steps

1. **Developer contract and money (45 min).** On the [new-apartment check (draft)](../templates/new-apartment-check-and-defects-notice.md), Part A: the linkage clause, the protocol clause, delay compensation for the late handover (s. 5א), and the 10% transferred at signing (lessons 20.2, 20.9).
2. **Defects (45 min).** Part B: the defects file, with Schedule items, periods, burden and notice dates; Part C: the notice, and a second notice for the damp (lesson 20.2).
3. **Rental (40 min).** On the [rental repair and deposit letter (draft)](../templates/rental-repair-and-deposit-letter.md): the deposit cap, the 25-day heater repair (rent reduction or other remedy), and the ₪8,000 withheld (lesson 20.3).
4. **Renovation (30 min).** Classify the wardrobe (sale or service, s. 8(ב)) and the fittings installation; draft notices on the [notice of breach and cancellation](../templates/notice-of-breach-and-cancellation.md) (lessons 20.1, 20.4).
5. **Guarantee (30 min).** On the [guarantee signing checklist (draft)](../templates/guarantee-signing-checklist.md): is Eitan an individual guarantor? What happens to an "all debts" clause? What would you require before signing, or would you refuse (lesson 20.5)?
6. **Insurance (40 min).** Answer both grounds on the [insurance rejection response (draft)](../templates/insurance-rejection-response.md); compute the limitation date (lesson 20.6).
7. **Sofa (20 min).** Cancellation deadline, fee, return and notice route; draft on the [consumer withdrawal notice](../templates/consumer-withdrawal-notice.md) (lesson 20.7).
8. **Mistaken payment (20 min).** A restitution demand to Shlomo; consider set-off against his deposit claims (lesson 20.8).
9. **Plan (30 min).** One page: order of letters, deadlines, forums (small claims or magistrates), and red flags for a lawyer.

## Self-assessment rubric

| Criterion | Weak (1) | Adequate (2) | Strong (3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Developer contract | Clauses listed | Void clauses named | Each clause with section, effect and the money-security action |
| Defects file | List of defects | Periods noted | Schedule item, period, burden, notice date and repair route for each |
| Rental | General complaint | Cap calculated | Cap, repair remedy, lawful and unlawful uses of the security, deadline |
| Renovation | Single complaint | Classification right | Sale vs service, notice and repair chance, deduction or self-repair |
| Guarantee | Sign or not | Status identified | Status, effect of no sum, protections, conditions for signing |
| Insurance | "We disagree" | Both grounds answered | Sections 3, 6–8, 29 applied; reduction computed; limitation date |
| Sofa | Deadline guessed | Deadline right | Deadline from the right start, fee cap, route, return |
| Restitution | Demand only | Elements stated | Elements, set-off, forum and evidence |
| Plan | Missing | Order given | Order, deadlines, forums and lawyer triggers |

A strong file scores at least 22 of 27, with no criterion at 1.

## Model answer

<details><summary>Model answer</summary>

**1. Developer.** The 10% signing payment exceeds the first threshold and was made outside the vouchers: demand at once a guarantee for it (Assurance s. 2) and write to the accompanying bank; complain to the supervisor (s. 3ד) if refused. Linkage "until actual handover" is void beyond s. 5ג: at most half of each payment, never the first 20%, and only to the contractual date (1 March 2026). "Protocol is final acceptance" is void (s. 7א(ב)). Delay: about three and a half months beyond the contractual date; s. 5א gives rent-based compensation from the end of the first month, stepping up after the fourth month; calculate with the rent of a similar four-room flat in Lod.

**2. Defects.** Cracked balcony floor: flooring (Schedule item 2), inspection period, developer's burden; visible at handover, notice within the fixed period from 15 June 2026. Jamming sliding door: aluminium joinery (item 1), same. Low water pressure: piping system (item 6) or a departure from building regulations (s. 4(א)(1)). Damp: sealing (item 7), hidden, notice within a reasonable time from September. Notice offers access (s. 4ב(א)); if the damp recurs after repair within two years of notice, repair and charge (s. 4ב(ב)).

**3. Rental.** Cap: lower of a third of the term's rent (₪22,000) and three months (₪16,500): ₪16,500; ₪3,500 was taken above the cap. Heater: no hot water in winter is arguably urgent (short deadline); 25 days exceeded it; Nadia and Eitan could have repaired and claimed, and may now claim a proportional rent reduction for the excess days (s. 9(א)(2)). The ₪8,000: painting for normal wear is not a lawful use; a broken blind is a lawful use only if they broke it by unreasonable use and failed to repair after notice (s. 25י(ג)–(ד)). Demand return of the balance within the s. 25י(ה) period, with its yield.

**4. Renovation.** Wardrobe from the contractor's own boards: a sale (s. 8(ב); Sale Law s. 2): notify the non-conformity immediately on discovery (s. 14) and demand repair or replacement; price reduction (s. 28) if they keep it. Kitchen installation: a contract for services: notice and a chance to repair (s. 3), then self-repair and recovery or deduction from any unpaid fee (s. 4).

**5. Guarantee.** Eitan is not the debtor's spouse or partner and holds no interest in the company: an individual guarantor of a bank (s. 19). An "all debts" guarantee with no stated sum releases an individual guarantor (s. 21(ב)), so the bank will insist on a sum. ₪120,000 is above the lower protected ceiling and the loan is not for a home: individual but not protected; he would have the fixed-sum, disclosure, notice and late-interest protections, but not s. 27. Advice: sign only for a stated maximum he could pay, for a limited period, after receiving all s. 22 disclosure, or refuse.

**6. Insurance.** (1) The alarm condition in general conditions, normal type, is not next to the theft cover nor highlighted: the insurer may not rely on it (s. 3); alternatively, s. 29 if the alarm would not have prevented the theft. (2) The question asked about thefts; a bicycle stolen from the building's shared storage may not be a theft "from the insured property", and the omission was not fraudulent; at most a proportional reduction (s. 7(ג)), and none if the insurer could have known or the fact did not affect the event (s. 8). Limitation: three years from the May 2026 theft (s. 31).

**7. Sofa.** 10 September is 21 days after delivery, beyond 14 days from delivery; but no disclosure document was received, and the period runs from the later of delivery and the document (s. 14ג(ג)(1)), so it has not yet ended. The missing document is decisive. Cancellation without defect: fee capped (lower of 5% and the shekel cap), including shipping (s. 14ה(ב), (ד)); return to the dealer's place of business; notice by phone, email or the website's cancellation link (s. 14ט).

**8. Restitution.** The second ₪5,500 came from Eitan without a legal basis (no rent owed after the lease): s. 1. Shlomo's "damages" are a separate claim he must prove; he may argue set-off (s. 25 of the Rental Law) only for tenancy debts that exist. Demand return within 14 days; claim in small claims together with the deposit.

**9. Plan.** This week: the guarantee for the 10% payment (most urgent, money at risk); the defects notice; the sofa cancellation (deadline-driven). Within two weeks: the rental letter (deposit and restitution together); the insurance reply with copy to the insurer's ombudsman and the regulator. Then: notices to the renovator. Claims: small claims against Shlomo (deposit, rent reduction, ₪5,500); small claims against the insurer; defects and delay against the developer in small claims if within the ceiling, otherwise the magistrates' court with an expert. Lawyer: if the developer will not secure the 10%, if the defects are structural, before Eitan signs any guarantee above what he can pay.

</details>
