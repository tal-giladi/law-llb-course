# Project: a class-action and public-interest file

Build the file a careful consumer and citizen keeps on the group cases that touch them: every class action in the registry against the businesses and authorities you pay, a decision on your own claim for each, a reasoned reading of one real settlement notice, and a filed-ready objection or exit notice, plus a one-page plan for one public problem you care about. You will reuse the registry search and the decision worksheet in the year-4 capstone (module 39).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. The objection and exit notice are dry runs unless a live notice concerns you and the period is open.

## Brief

Use your own affairs. If you prefer not to, use the supplied case.

**Supplied case.** Hadas and Ibrahim Nasser-Cohen of Lod pay: a mobile operator (with a "content services" line they never ordered), a bank (a monthly fee on a basic account), a health-fund supplementary plan, a car insurer, the Lod municipality (arnona and a "sewage connection levy" the municipality raised in 2024), and they used a scooter-sharing app in 2023. Ibrahim uses a wheelchair; the municipal service centre has no accessible counter. In September 2026 their bank published a settlement notice: ₪6 million to the s. 27א fund for customers charged a fee in breach of the basic-track rules, no payment to customers, release of "all claims arising from fees charged in the period", and an agreed fee and award of ₪1.3 million.

Time: about 9 hours.

## Steps

1. **Map your relationships (30 minutes).** List each business and authority you pay regularly, and for each the Second Schedule item that would apply (lesson 33.1).
2. **Registry search (60 minutes).** Search the registry for each one; record case number, court, class definition, period, stage and status. Note whether you are a member (lesson 33.1).
3. **Certification reading (45 minutes).** For one pending application, test standing and the four conditions of s. 8 and predict the outcome (lesson 33.2).
4. **Your own claims (60 minutes).** For each grievance, complete the decision worksheet: own small claim, regulator complaint, class action, or a combination; limitation under s. 26 (lesson 33.6; [should-you-sue worksheet](../templates/should-you-sue-worksheet.md)).
5. **Settlement reading (60 minutes).** For one real notice (or the supplied bank notice), the fact table, deadline, membership, benefit, release, fee percentages and the s. 19(ג)(2) analysis (lessons 33.3, 33.4, 33.7).
6. **Document 1: objection or exit (120 minutes).** Draft the reasoned objection (or, if your own claim is larger, the exit notice) with the affidavit (lesson 33.8; [exit and objection template](../templates/class-action-objection-and-opt-out.md)).
7. **Public-interest plan (60 minutes).** For one public problem (the supplied: the inaccessible service centre), choose the route (petition, class action by an organisation or the Commission, s. 15 participation, amicus, complaint) and draft a one-page letter to the body best placed to act (lesson 33.5; [letter to an authority](../templates/letter-to-authority.md)).
8. **Diary and stop signals (30 minutes).** Every deadline (objection windows, limitation, administrator challenges) in one diary, and when you would hand each matter to a lawyer.

## Self-assessment rubric

| Criterion | Excellent (3) | Adequate (2) | Needs work (1) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Schedule mapping | Each relationship matched to an item with its exact words; item 11 exclusivity and the s. 3(א) exception applied | Items right; reasons thin | Items guessed or missing |
| Registry | Every relationship searched with the right fields; membership stated with the class definition | Most searched; membership partly stated | No search, or results not recorded |
| Certification | Standing and all four s. 8 conditions tested against the application; s. 8(ב) considered | Conditions tested; one missed | Conclusion without conditions |
| Own claims | Routes compared with numbers; exemplary damages only where a statute allows; s. 26 applied | Comparison made; limitation missing | Route chosen without comparison |
| Settlement reading | Deadline from publication; who gets nothing; release scope; fee on face and real value; s. 19(ג)(2) lines with facts | Most items; fee one way only | Notice summarised without analysis |
| Objection or exit | Short, specific, every point tied to a section and a cure; affidavit limited to own facts | Points tied to sections; cures thin | General complaint; no cures; no affidavit |
| Public-interest plan | Routes eliminated with reasons; right body addressed; facts and evidence attached | Route chosen; letter general | No route analysis |

<details><summary>Model answer (supplied case, outline)</summary>

**Mapping.** Mobile operator, bank, insurer: items 1, 3 and 2. Health-fund supplementary plan: item 2 if run as insurance by the fund's managing company, otherwise item 1 (check). Municipality's levy: item 11 only, administrative affairs court (s. 5(ב)(2)). Scooter app: item 1. Accessibility at the service centre: item 9 if the claim arises under the accessibility chapters; also a petition route.

**Registry.** Search by each party name and by key words ("שירותי תוכן", "היטל ביוב"); record hits. For the levy, a pending item-11 application would make the family members if they paid in the 24 months before filing (s. 21).

**Own claims.** Content services: demand a refund; small claim if refused, with the regulator complaint in parallel; stay in any class action unless the personal claim is pursued. Bank fee: compute 24 months of fees; compare with the settlement below. Levy: personal objection or appeal routes under the municipal law may be faster; the class action caps restitution at 24 months.

**Settlement reading (bank).** Relief for the public under s. 20(ג) is lawful only if paying customers is impractical; the bank holds account records, so individual credit looks practical. Release "all claims arising from fees charged in the period" may be wider than the causes in the application (s. 18(ז)(1)). Fee ₪1.3 million on ₪6 million is about 22% for a settlement with no payment to members; the bill's benchmark (not law) for a pre-certification settlement would be 16% of the first ₪5 million plus 13% of the rest, about ₪930,000.

**Objection.** Three points: (1) identifiable customers should be credited directly (s. 20(ג)(1)); (2) release limited to the causes in the application (s. 18(ז)(1)); (3) fee set on actual benefit and stage (s. 23(ב)), with the balance after a final report (s. 19(ו)). Cures: automatic credit to current and former customers, residue to the fund earmarked for financial education; narrower release; reduced fee. If the family's own fee claim exceeds a few hundred shekels and the evidence is in hand, consider asking to leave (s. 18(ו)) and suing in small claims instead.

**Public-interest plan.** Write to the Commission for Equal Rights of Persons with Disabilities (a listed public authority, s. 4(א)(2)) and to an accessibility organisation, with photos and dates; ask the municipality in writing for an accessibility plan first (prior demand), so that a petition is possible if it refuses.

**Diary.** Objection window (45 days from the notice's publication); limitation for each personal claim; one year after any exit (s. 26(ד)).
