# Project: an estate file, from your own will to a succession order

Build two linked files a careful person keeps: your own estate plan (a will, deposit, named beneficiaries and an enduring-power plan) and an heir's file for one estate (shares, application, notices, land registration and distribution). You will reuse both in the year-3 clinic (module 31: your own will and enduring power of attorney; a succession order from start to finish).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. The heir's file is a dry run unless you are an heir in a real, uncontested estate. Your own will is real if you choose to sign and deposit it.

## Brief

Part A is always your own: your will and plan. For Part B choose either:

- **A real estate** where you are an heir (a parent or grandparent who died), if you have the family's agreement to use the facts; or
- **The supplied case** below. Say which you used.

**Supplied case.** Rafael Ohana died in Haifa on 12 September 2026, aged 79. He married Miriam in 1971; she survives him. Their children: Avi (Haifa), Sigal (Ramat Gan) and Moshe, who died in 2022 leaving two daughters, Noa (19) and Tamar (15), whose mother is Moshe's widow, Orly. Assets: the family flat in Haifa, registered to Rafael and Miriam in equal shares; a savings account in Rafael's name (₪180,000); a car; a pension with Miriam as named beneficiary. Debts: a credit-card balance (₪9,000); the funeral cost ₪22,000, paid by Avi. Rafael had a small carpentry workshop until 2024. In his desk Sigal found a single handwritten page, dated and signed in 2019: "My workshop tools go to my grandson Ido [Avi's son]. Everything else as the law says." The workshop is closed and the tools are in Avi's storeroom. Sigal also thinks that, in 2025, a neighbour helped her father write "a new will" leaving the savings to the neighbour; no one has seen it.

Time: about 7 hours.

## Steps

1. **Your heirs by law (30 minutes).** Draw your own family tree and calculate what each person would take if you died today without a will (lesson 26.1).
2. **Your will (90 minutes).** Plan and handwrite your will with the [handwritten will](../templates/handwritten-will.md) template; run the checklist; decide whether to deposit it (lesson 26.8).
3. **Outside the will (20 minutes).** List your pension, provident and insurance nominations and decide whether to update them (s. 147).
4. **Enduring-power plan (45 minutes).** Fill the [enduring power of attorney worksheet](../templates/enduring-poa-preparation-worksheet.md) for yourself (lesson 26.7). Nothing is signed.
5. **The Ohana shares (45 minutes).** Separate Miriam's property-relations share; calculate the estate; apply s. 11 and representation; deal with the 2019 document and the alleged 2025 will (lessons 26.1–26.4).
6. **The application (60 minutes).** Prepare the application with the [succession or probate order checklist](../templates/succession-order-application-checklist.md): which order, which route, notices, documents, minor heir, deadlines (lessons 26.6, 26.9).
7. **Administration and land (45 minutes).** Debts in order, creditors' notice, Miriam's housing, registration of the flat, a distribution proposal (lessons 26.5, 26.7).
8. **Stop signals (15 minutes).** Write when you would hand Part B to a lawyer, with the reason.

## Model answer

<details><summary>Model answer</summary>

This model covers Part B (the supplied case); Part A is personal.

**Miriam's own share.** Married 1971: the Spouses (Property Relations) Law applies only to marriages from 1974, so the judge-made presumption of community governs (lesson 18.4). The flat is registered half to each: Miriam's half is hers. The savings, built during a long shared life, are presumptively shared: half (₪90,000) is Miriam's. The pension passes to her as named beneficiary outside the estate (s. 147).

**The estate.** Rafael's half of the flat; ₪90,000 of savings; the car; the workshop tools. Debts: funeral ₪22,000 (first, s. 104(א)(1), reimbursed to Avi); order costs; credit card ₪9,000. Cash left about ₪59,000 before order costs.

**The 2019 page.** Wholly handwritten, dated and signed: a valid handwritten will (s. 19). It gives a specific gift (the tools) to Ido and says "everything else as the law says". So the application is for a combined order: probate for the tools gift and succession for the rest (s. 66(ב); Form 6 in the order). Ido is a grandson, not a child, parent or spouse, but the deceased's children are heirs by law in the same file and receive notice anyway.

**The alleged 2025 will.** Anyone holding it must deliver it to the Registrar (s. 75). If it appears and the neighbour helped write it, the gift to him is void (s. 35); if it was in his handwriting it is not a handwritten will of Rafael at all. The family checks the national register for deposited wills before filing. If the neighbour applies for probate, the family objects within the published period (s. 67), and the file moves to the Family Court (s. 67א).

**Shares of the rest (intestacy).** Miriam takes the car (s. 11(א)) and half of the rest. The other half goes to the children: Avi ⅓, Sigal ⅓, and Moshe's ⅓ to Noa and Tamar equally (⅙ each, s. 14). The flat overall: Miriam ½ + ¼ = ¾; Avi 1/12; Sigal 1/12; Noa 1/24; Tamar 1/24.

**Application.** A combined probate-and-succession application by Miriam or a child, online (spouse and children may file online; probate is open online), to the Registrar for Haifa (domicile). Notices: Form 3 to Ido (with a copy of the will, sent to his parents as he may be a minor; check his age), Form 3א to Avi, Sigal, Noa, and Tamar (through Orly as guardian; mark Tamar as a minor). Original 2019 page delivered within seven days of online filing (reg. 14(ד)(1)). Fee and publication charge per current numbers. Deadlines: objection at least 14 days from publication; appeal from the Registrar 15 days.

**Administration.** No administrator needed if the family agrees. Because Rafael had a business until 2024, the heirs publish a creditors' notice with at least three months (s. 123) before dividing the cash; they pay the funeral and the credit card first (s. 104). Miriam stays in the flat as of right: she owns ¾; she may also rely on s. 108 and s. 115 against the others' shares. Avi and Sigal may renounce in Miriam's favour (s. 6(ב)); Tamar, a minor, cannot without court approval (s. 6(ג)), and Noa (adult) may choose.

**Land.** Tick the Land Registry request in the online application, or file the registration request later at the Haifa office, signed by one heir and verified (regs. 17–18). Any later agreement to give Miriam the whole flat must be signed by all heirs, and Tamar's part needs court approval.

**Stop signals.** The alleged 2025 will surfaces and the neighbour seeks probate (litigation with evidence of capacity and influence); creditors of the workshop appear with significant claims; Orly wants Tamar's share sold or renounced; the family disputes the presumption-of-community split of the savings.
