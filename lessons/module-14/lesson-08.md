# 14.8 · Oversight bodies: the Ombudsman, the State Comptroller and sector ombudsmen

By the end of this lesson you will be able to decide whether a complaint to the Ombudsman (נציב תלונות הציבור) is the right tool for a problem with a public body, file one that will be investigated, and use it alongside, not instead of, your legal remedies. Lesson [06.5](../module-06/lesson-05.md) introduced the State Comptroller as an institution and the basic complaint rules; this lesson treats the Ombudsman as a remedy, with the procedure, its limits and the specialised complaint bodies.

## Why it matters

Most problems with the administration are not worth a petition: a benefit delayed for months, a letter nobody answers, a clerk who refuses to accept a form, a fine imposed by mistake. The Ombudsman investigates such complaints for free, can demand documents from any audited body, and often obtains a correction within weeks. It looks at more than legality: "proper administration", "excessive rigidity" and "flagrant injustice" are grounds a court would rarely use.

Its limits are just as important. The Ombudsman cannot order a public body to do anything (except in whistleblower cases), its findings give you no new right in court, and filing a complaint does not stop any deadline. Using it well means knowing when to combine it with an objection or a petition.

## The law

### Who and what

> כל אדם רשאי להגיש תלונה אל נציב תלונות הציבור.

*Gist:* Anyone may complain (s. 33), citizen or not. A written complaint, or an oral one recorded by the office, must be signed and state the complainant's name and address (s. 34); a prisoner's complaint is passed on sealed (s. 35). ([State Comptroller Law](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מבקר_המדינה))

Bodies (s. 36): government ministries, State institutions, local authorities, government companies and other audited bodies in s. 9(1)–(6), and their employees; subsidiaries and State-supported bodies only if designated.

> [...] והמעשה הוא בניגוד לחוק או ללא סמכות חוקית או בניגוד למינהל תקין, או שיש בו משום נוקשות יתירה או אי־צדק בולט; לענין זה, ”מעשה“ – לרבות מחדל ופיגור בעשיה.

*Gist:* The act (including omission and delay) must harm the complainant directly, or another person who consented, and be unlawful, without authority, contrary to proper administration, excessively rigid or flagrantly unjust (s. 37).

### What is not investigated

Section 38 excludes complaints about the President; the Knesset and MKs in their role; the Government and ministers acting as members of the Government (but not as heads of ministries); judicial or quasi-judicial acts; matters pending before or decided on the merits by a court or tribunal; service conditions of soldiers, police and prison officers; and most employment complaints of public employees. Section 39: a complaint where an objection, appeal or appeal-type remedy exists or existed, or filed more than a year after the act or your learning of it, is investigated only for a special reason ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).

### Investigation and result

The Ombudsman may investigate in any way, is not bound by rules of procedure or evidence, must give the body a chance to respond, and may require any person or body to provide information and documents (s. 41). If the complaint is justified, the Ombudsman informs you and the body, may point out the defect and how and when to fix it, and the body must report back; if it does not, the matter may go to the minister or the Knesset State Control Committee (s. 43(א)–(ב)). Suspected crimes go to the Attorney General (s. 43(ד)).

> (א) החלטותיו וממצאיו של נציב תלונות הציבור בענין תלונה – (1) אין בהם כדי להעניק למתלונן או לאדם אחר זכות או סעד בבית משפט או בבית דין שלא היה להם לפני כן; (2) אין בהם כדי למנוע מהמתלונן או מאדם אחר להשתמש בזכות או לבקש סעד שהוא זכאי להם, אולם אם נקבע לכך מועד בחיקוק, לא יוארך המועד על ידי הגשת התלונה או בירורה.
> (ב) שום בית משפט לא ייזקק לבקשת סעד נגד החלטותיו וממצאיו של נציב תלונות הציבור בעניין תלונה.

*Gist:* Findings create no new legal right but do not take away existing ones; a complaint does not extend any statutory deadline; and no court will hear a challenge to the Ombudsman's decisions and findings on a complaint (s. 45).

### Whistleblowers

A public employee who in good faith, through proper channels, reported corruption, a serious breach of law or serious harm to proper administration, and suffered retaliation from a superior, may complain (s. 45א). Here the Ombudsman can issue binding orders, including interim ones: cancelling a dismissal, special compensation, or a transfer (s. 45ג). Bodies must post these provisions at the workplace (s. 45ו).

## Leading cases

### Key text: s. 45(ב), no judicial review of the Ombudsman

Because courts will not review the Ombudsman's decisions on a complaint, there is almost no case law on the Ombudsman's procedure, and you should not expect a court to "enforce" a finding. What you can do is use a justified-complaint finding as evidence in your own proceeding: it is a public body's documented admission or an independent finding that a defect existed. Read s. 45 on [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מבקר_המדינה). Supporters of this design say it keeps the Ombudsman informal, fast and free; critics say findings without enforcement let bodies ignore them, which is why the Law sends unremedied findings to the minister or the Knesset committee.

### Key text: the Comptroller's audit reports

The State Comptroller's audit work (as distinct from complaints) produces public reports on systemic failures, for example the report on protecting the rights of people with disabilities in legal proceedings ([report page](https://library.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Pages/Reports/1427-3.aspx); figures in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)). For a complainant or petitioner, a report on the same body and issue is strong evidence that a problem is systemic. Audit material itself is exempt from FOI (FOI Law s. 2, definition of "public authority", para. (4)), but published reports are free online.

## How it works in practice

### Choosing the right tool

| Problem | Best first tool | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Delay, no answer, lost file, rude or rigid service | Ombudsman (after writing to the body) | Free, informal, can demand documents |
| A decision with a statutory objection or appeal (arnona, planning, NII) | The objection or appeal (lesson 14.10) | The Ombudsman will usually decline (s. 39) and deadlines keep running |
| A decision you need cancelled or changed by a set date | Prior demand, then petition (lesson 14.11) | Only a court can order it |
| Complaint about a judge's conduct | Ombudsman for Judges (law of 2002) | The Ombudsman cannot investigate judicial acts (s. 38(4)) |
| Complaint about State lawyers' conduct in court | Commissioner for complaints about State representatives in the courts (law of 2016) | Specialised body |
| Banks, insurers, health funds, telecom | The regulator's complaint unit (module 12) | Sector expertise and powers |
| Retaliation for reporting corruption at work | Ombudsman, whistleblower route (s. 45א) | Can issue binding orders |

### Filing

- Write to the body first and keep its answer (or its silence): the Ombudsman will ask.
- File online at the Comptroller's complaint system (Hebrew, English, Arabic and Russian) or in writing; sign with your name and address (s. 34).
- Attach the correspondence, the decision, and a one-paragraph timeline.
- Say what outcome you want (an answer, a correction, a refund, an apology).
- If a court deadline exists, file your objection or petition anyway: the complaint does not stop the clock (s. 45(א)(2)).

## Worked example

Olga Petrenko, a pensioner in Ashkelon, applied to her municipality for an arnona discount as an elderly resident on income support. Six months later she has no answer; the arnona department says the file is "with the welfare department", which says it is "with arnona".

1. **Is there an objection route?** The arnona objection (השגה) deals with the amount charged and has strict periods ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)); Olga files one against this year's bill to protect her rights (lesson 14.10).
2. **Decisions and Reasons.** Her application was a written request: over three months' silence is a deemed refusal (lesson 14.4). She writes to the city's legal adviser citing s. 2 and s. 6(ב).
3. **Ombudsman.** The core problem is the run-around between departments, an omission and delay contrary to proper administration (s. 37). She files online in Russian, attaching both departments' emails.
4. **Result.** The Ombudsman asks the municipality to respond within a set time. The discount is approved with retroactive effect. The Ombudsman's letter notes the defect in coordination between the departments.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A problem with a public body that has dragged on (or Olga's case).
- Your correspondence with the body.
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Screen (10 min).** Is the body covered (s. 36)? Is the subject covered (s. 37)? Is it excluded (s. 38)? Is there an objection or appeal, or more than a year since the act (s. 39)? If so, write the special reason.
2. **Deadlines (5 min).** List any statutory deadline for an objection, appeal or petition. Diary it: the complaint will not extend it.
3. **Timeline (10 min).** Five to eight dated lines: what you asked, who answered what, what is still missing.
4. **Draft (15 min).** Use the [regulator complaint template](../../templates/regulator-complaint.md), adapted: addressee נציבות תלונות הציבור, the body, the timeline, the ground (s. 37 wording), the outcome you want, attachments, signature with name and address.
5. **Choose the channel (5 min).** The online system or a signed letter.

### Template

[Complaint to a regulator](../../templates/regulator-complaint.md), adapted for the Ombudsman.

### How you know it worked

- Your screening answers each of ss. 36–39.
- The complaint has your name, address and signature (s. 34).
- A deadline elsewhere is diaried separately.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes; the Ombudsman is designed for the public, there is no fee in the Law, and no lawyer is needed. You may complain on behalf of another person only with their consent (s. 37(1)), and you must not do so as an occupation for payment (Bar Association Law s. 20).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- A statutory deadline is close and the decision has serious consequences: file the objection or petition first.
- You are a whistleblower facing dismissal: the Ombudsman route is strong, but you may also have labour-court claims with their own periods.
- The body's failure caused you significant financial loss you may want to claim in court.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Complaining instead of filing an objection | Objection period expires; complaint declined (s. 39) | File the objection; ask to extend if possible | Check for a statutory route first |
| Expecting the Ombudsman to order a refund | Disappointment; no enforceable order | Use the finding in your objection or claim | Know s. 43 and s. 45 |
| Waiting more than a year | Investigation only for a special reason | Explain the special reason | File within the year |
| Complaining about a court decision | Excluded (s. 38(4)–(5)) | Appeal the decision | Use appeals for judicial acts |
| Unsigned or anonymous complaint | Not investigated (s. 34, s. 40) | Resubmit signed | Sign with name and address |

## Review

- Anyone may complain to the Ombudsman about an act, omission or delay of an audited body that is unlawful, contrary to proper administration, excessively rigid or flagrantly unjust.
- Exclusions (s. 38) and conditional cases (s. 39: existing appeal route, more than a year) decide whether it will investigate.
- The Ombudsman can demand information and report unremedied defects to the minister or the Knesset committee, but findings create no new right, no deadline is extended, and no court reviews them.
- Whistleblowers have a special route with binding orders (ss. 45א–45ג).
- Specialised bodies handle complaints about judges, State representatives in court, and regulated sectors.
