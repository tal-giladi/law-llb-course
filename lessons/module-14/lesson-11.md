# 14.11 · Practice lab: an administrative petition (dry run)

In this lab you write a complete administrative petition and affidavit, assemble the filing package and plan service and deadlines, all as a dry run: nothing is filed. You can use your own matter from lab 14.10 (if the authority refused or ignored your objection) or the supplied case below. Lab [06.8](../module-06/lesson-08.md) did the same for the HCJ; this lab is for the Administrative Affairs Court, where most petitions by individuals are heard.

## Why it matters

A petition is the point where your letters become a court case. Every item you prepared in this module goes in: the authority check (14.1), the discretion grounds (14.2–14.5), the information you obtained (14.7), the objection and prior demand (14.10), and the threshold checklist (14.9). Writing it once, without pressure, shows you where your case is weak while you can still fix it, and gives you a draft you can take to a clinic or a lawyer if you do need one.

## The law

> (ג) כתב עתירה יהא ערוך לפי טופס 1 שבתוספת והעובדות שבו יאומתו בתצהיר אשר יצורף לו ושיהיה ערוך לפי טופס 2 שבתוספת; בתצהיר תהיה הפרדה בין עובדות שהן בידיעתו האישית של המצהיר לבין עובדות הנכונות לפי מיטב ידיעתו ואמונתו, שלגביהן יציין את מקור ידיעתו ואמונתו.

*Gist:* The petition follows Form 1 and its facts are verified by an affidavit in Form 2 that separates what you know personally from what you believe, with the source of the belief ([reg. 5(ג)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_בתי_משפט_לענינים_מינהליים_(סדרי_דין))). The forms are in the regulations' schedule; Wikisource does not reproduce their text (⚠️ manual check of the official forms on the Judiciary's site), so the template follows the items of reg. 5(ב).

> (ב) לא תובא עתירה לעיון בית המשפט כאמור בתקנת משנה (א) אלא אם כן הגיש העותר לבית המשפט אישור בדבר המצאת העתירה כדין לפי תקנה 35.

*Gist:* The court will not look at the petition until you file proof that you served it (reg. 7(ב)); service is due within a few days of filing (reg. 35(א)).

The remaining rules were set out in lesson [14.9](lesson-09.md): venue (reg. 2), period and delay (regs 3–4), contents (reg. 5(ב)), respondents (reg. 6), interim orders (regs 9–9א), and self-representation (reg. 44א with Civil Procedure Regulations reg. 170).

## Leading cases

None new. In the petition's legal section, cite at most four rulings from this module that fit your grounds, each with its number, date and one sentence on the point it supports. A court prefers four apt citations to twenty general ones; attach copies of any that are not officially published (reg. 14(ב), at the skeleton-argument stage).

## How it works in practice

### The filing package

1. The petition (Form 1 structure), signed.
2. The affidavit (Form 2 structure), signed before a lawyer or other person authorised to take affidavits (lesson [06.8](../module-06/lesson-08.md) covered how; ⚠️ check who may verify an affidavit for you).
3. Annexes, numbered: the decision, your prior demand and objection, the authority's answers, FOI answers, supporting documents.
4. The fee (schedule item 20 of the Courts (Fees) Regulations; amount in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)) or a fee-exemption application.
5. Copies for each respondent, a service plan, and a proof-of-service form to file afterwards.

### Diary after filing

| Step | When |
|---|---|
| Serve each respondent | Within the reg. 35(א) period after filing |
| File proof of service | Immediately after service |
| Preliminary response or answer | As ordered (answer: default period in reg. 10) |
| Your request for further particulars | Within the reg. 11 period after the answer |
| Skeleton arguments | Shortly before the hearing (reg. 14) |
| Request to cross-examine | Well before the hearing (reg. 15(ב)) |
| Appeal to the Supreme Court | Reg. 33 periods |

All periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

## Worked example

The supplied case for this lab. Mahmoud Khatib runs a licensed day-care centre (מעון יום) for 30 toddlers in a building the Tamra local council allocated to him for ten years in 2022. On 1 October 2026 the council's property-allocation committee sent him a decision cancelling the allocation, effective 1 November, because of "repeated safety deficiencies reported by the council's safety officer". He was not heard. On 5 October he sent a request for reconsideration, with photographs and an engineer's certificate showing that the deficiencies (a broken gate and a missing fire extinguisher) were fixed on 20 September, and asked for a hearing. On 12 October he filed an FOI request for the safety reports. By 20 October there is no answer.

How the petition is built:

1. **Route first.** Is there a statutory appeal? Not against a council's allocation decision. Note the contrast: had the Ministry's supervisor revoked his day-care licence, the Day-Care Centres Supervision Law 2018 gives an appeal (ערר) to an appeals committee (s. 55), whose final decision is appealed to the Administrative Affairs Court (s. 56(ב)(2); Second Schedule). A petition would then be dismissed for failure to use that route. Always read the governing law before choosing the petition.
2. **Jurisdiction:** First Schedule item 8, decisions of a local authority; the court for the district where the decision was made (reg. 2), here the Northern District (⚠️ confirm the district of the council).
3. **Period:** from 1 October, the earliest date he knew of the decision (reg. 3(ב)); he files well within it, without waiting for the council's answer.
4. **Respondents:** the local council and its allocation committee (reg. 6); if the council has already promised the building to someone else, that person too.
5. **Grounds:** no hearing before cancelling an existing allocation (Berman, lesson 14.4); a decision based on facts no longer true and not checked (lesson 14.5); disproportionate: a warning and re-inspection would achieve safety with far less harm to 30 families and to his livelihood (lesson 14.3).
6. **Remedy:** annul the cancellation; alternatively, return the matter to the committee to decide after a hearing and a new inspection; interim order suspending the cancellation from 1 November.
7. **Interim order:** a separate application verified by affidavit (reg. 9), served on the day of filing (reg. 35(ב)). Because the cancellation is imminent and children's care is affected, this is the step where he should consider a lawyer or a clinic.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

This is a dry run: do not file. If you later decide to file in your own matter, check every period and the current forms first.

### You need

- Your matter from lab 14.10 (decision, objection, the authority's answer or silence, FOI results), or Mahmoud's case.
- The [administrative petition template](../../templates/administrative-petition.md).
- The schedules and the regulations (links above), and [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- About 3 hours over two or three sessions.

### Steps

1. **Threshold memo (25 min).** Fill in lesson 14.9's checklist: schedule item and exceptions, venue, period and last day, delay risk, prior demand, statutory alternative, ripeness, respondents, clean hands. Fix what you can before drafting (for example, send a prior demand if you have none).
2. **Heading and parties (15 min).** Court and district; petitioner with ID, address and contact details (no lawyer: leave that line out); respondents with addresses; each respondent's interest (reg. 5(ב)(1)–(2)).
3. **Opening (15 min).** One paragraph: the decision, its date, the remedy sought, in plain words. Judges read this first.
4. **Jurisdiction and period (15 min).** The enabling provision of the decision, the schedule item, the venue regulation (reg. 5(ב)(3א)); the date of publication, notice or knowledge and why the petition is in time (reg. 5(ב)(3)).
5. **Facts (35 min).** Numbered paragraphs, dated, each pointing to an annex. Only facts you can support; mark which you know personally.
6. **Prior demand (10 min).** The demand, the date, the answer or silence, with annexes; or why there was none (reg. 5(ב)(6)).
7. **Legal argument (40 min).** Two to four grounds, strongest first, each with the rule, one or two rulings, and the application to your facts. Include the authority's best counter-argument and your answer.
8. **Remedy (10 min).** Main remedy and an alternative; costs.
9. **Fee and related proceedings (5 min).** Fee item or exemption; any related proceeding (reg. 5(ב)(8)–(9)).
10. **Affidavit (20 min).** Re-state each fact in the first person, separating personal knowledge from belief and its source (reg. 5(ג)).
11. **Interim order (optional, 15 min).** If the decision is about to take effect, draft a one-page application: the harm if no order, the balance of convenience, your chances, and an offer of security if appropriate.
12. **Service and diary (10 min).** List each respondent's address for service; write your diary of post-filing deadlines.
13. **Self-review (15 min).** Read the petition as the authority's lawyer: write the three threshold arguments they would raise and check that your text answers each.

### How you know it worked

- Every item of reg. 5(ב) appears in your petition, in order.
- Every fact in the petition is verified in the affidavit and supported by an annex.
- Your period calculation starts from the earliest of publication, notice and knowledge.
- The respondent's likely threshold arguments are answered in the text.
- Your remedy is one the court can grant (lesson 14.9).

### Self-check

- [ ] I named the schedule item, the enabling provision and the venue.
- [ ] I joined everyone who may be harmed if I win.
- [ ] I attached the prior demand and the authority's answer.
- [ ] My affidavit separates knowledge from belief.
- [ ] I know I must serve and file proof of service before the court will read the petition.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes, for an individual, in your own name: reg. 44א applies reg. 170 of the Civil Procedure Regulations 2018, and Form 1 asks for a lawyer's details only if you are represented (reg. 5(ב)(1)). A company generally needs a lawyer (unverified in a free source). No one may represent you except a lawyer (Bar Association Law s. 20).

### Where and how

- The district court for the right district, sitting in administrative matters; registry filing, or online where available to unrepresented parties (⚠️ manual check).
- Fee or exemption and all periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Free help before filing: Legal Aid in some fields, university clinics, NGOs (immigration, housing, welfare, disability); see [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You need a temporary or interim order (closure, revocation, demolition, deportation) within days.
- The case turns on expert evidence or cross-examination of officials.
- A third party with lawyers is a respondent, or the authority raises threshold objections you cannot answer.
- You are acting for a company or the stakes are high.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facts in the petition not in the affidavit | Unproven facts; order to amend | File a supplementary affidavit | Draft the affidavit from the petition, paragraph by paragraph |
| Period counted from the authority's last letter | Late or delay argument | Explain the dates; ask to extend (reg. 3(ג)) | Count from the earliest trigger |
| Twenty pages of general law | The judge cannot find your point | Cut to two to four grounds | Opening paragraph plus focused argument |
| Interim order asked inside the petition | Not considered as an application | File a separate application (reg. 9(א)) | Use a separate document with its own affidavit |
| No service or proof of service | Petition sits unread | Serve and file proof | Follow reg. 35 and reg. 7(ב) |

## Review

- A petition follows Form 1, with the items of reg. 5(ב), verified by a Form 2 affidavit separating knowledge from belief.
- Jurisdiction, period, prior demand and respondents are part of the petition itself, not afterthoughts.
- The petitioner serves the petition and files proof of service before the court considers it.
- Interim orders need a separate application with an affidavit, served the same day.
- An individual may file in their own name; write the dry run now, so that a real petition, or a lawyer's brief, starts from a complete file.
