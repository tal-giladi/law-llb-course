# 34.1 · The framework of Israeli environmental law

By the end of this lesson you will be able to take any environmental problem (a noisy generator, a smell from a factory, rubbish dumped in a wadi, a chimney you suspect) and say which statute governs it, who must enforce it, what you as a citizen can demand, and which later lesson of this module shows you how. Israeli environmental law has no single code; it is a set of sector statutes with a shared toolbox, and the skill is knowing which drawer to open.

## Why it matters

Most people who suffer from pollution complain to the wrong body, cite the wrong law, or wait for an authority that has no duty to act. The law gives citizens unusually strong tools here: a private criminal complaint without the police, an injunction suit without proof of negligence, a right to see a factory's own emission reports. They only work if you know which statute you stand on. The same map also explains the policy debates you will meet in lesson 34.7: who should decide, how strict the duties should be, and how much courts should review.

## The law

### The basic prohibitions

> לא יגרום אדם לרעש חזק או בלתי סביר, מכל מקור שהוא, אם הוא מפריע, או עשוי להפריע, לאדם המצוי בקרבת מקום או לעוברים ושבים.

*Gist:* No one may cause strong or unreasonable noise, from any source, that disturbs or may disturb a person nearby or passers-by; s. 3 says the same of smell. [Prevention of Nuisances Law 1961, s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_למניעת_מפגעים)

> לא יגרום אדם לזיהום אוויר חזק או בלתי סביר.

*Gist:* No one may cause strong or unreasonable air pollution; exceeding the ambient values or emitting in breach of the Law is unreasonable by definition. [Clean Air Law 2008, s. 3](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_אוויר_נקי)

These two "open-texture" prohibitions sit beside sector statutes with detailed duties: water (Water Law 1959), waste and littering (Maintenance of Cleanliness Law 1984), packaging and deposits (2011, 1999), hazardous substances (1993), the sea, the coast, radiation, asbestos. Lesson 34.3 covers the main ones.

### Principles written into statute

> [...] בין השאר על ידי קביעת איסורים וחובות בהתאם לעקרון הזהירות המונעת, והכל לשם הגנה על חיי אדם, בריאותם ואיכות חייהם של בני אדם ולשם הגנה על הסביבה [...] למען הציבור ולמען הדורות הבאים

*Gist:* The Clean Air Law's purpose clause adopts the precautionary principle and names future generations. [Clean Air Law s. 1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_אוויר_נקי)

The PRTR Law's purpose clause speaks of transparency and of policy "המבוססת על צדק סביבתי" (based on environmental justice) ([s. 1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_הסביבה_(פליטות_והעברות_לסביבה_–_חובות_דיווח_ומרשם))). Public participation appears as a duty to publish emission-permit applications and drafts and to let "כל אדם" comment within 45 days (Clean Air Law s. 21). "Polluter pays" was the title of a 2008 omnibus law that raised fines and added financial sanctions across the sector statutes (חוק הגנת הסביבה (המזהם משלם) (תיקוני חקיקה), התשס״ח–2008, [Knesset PDF](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/17/law/17_lsr_300127.pdf)).

### Who enforces

> מטרת חוק זה היא [...] באמצעות עיגון והרחבה של סמכויות הפיקוח והאכיפה של עובדי המשרד להגנת הסביבה.

*Gist:* The Enforcement Powers Law 2011 gives Ministry inspectors uniform powers (demand documents, take samples, enter places other than homes without a warrant) for the statutes in its schedules, and investigation and seizure powers for the First Schedule statutes. [ss. 1, 5, 6](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_הסביבה_(סמכויות_פיקוח_ואכיפה))

> רשות מקומית גדולה או איגוד ערים שעיקר עיסוקו שמירה על איכות הסביבה, לפי העניין, יפעלו בתחום שיפוטם לאכיפת חיקוקי הסביבה [...]

*Gist:* A municipality or local council with more than 20,000 residents, and an environmental town association, must enforce the listed environmental statutes in its area; a smaller authority may. [Local Authorities (Environmental Enforcement) Law 2008, ss. 1–2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הרשויות_המקומיות_(אכיפה_סביבתית_–_סמכויות_פקחים))

Note the word "יפעלו" (shall act): for a large authority this is a duty, which matters when you later complain that it did nothing (lesson 34.6).

## Leading cases

### בג״ץ 6971/11 איתנית מוצרי בניה בע״מ נ׳ מדינת ישראל (2.4.2013)

From 1952 to 1997 Eitanit's Nahariya plant spread asbestos waste across the Western Galilee, much of it given to residents for paths and yards. The Asbestos Law 2011 (s. 74) made Eitanit fund half of the clean-up, capped at ₪150 million. The Court (Justice Hendel; Grunis P., Zylbertal J.) held that the section infringed Eitanit's property but passed the limitation clause of Basic Law: Human Dignity and Liberty; it relied on comparative strict and retroactive liability regimes and treated "polluter pays" as at most a secondary purpose. Why it matters: the leading free English text on how Israeli courts weigh polluter-pays liability against property rights, and a reminder that environmental duties are often imposed by targeted legislation. [Versa](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/eitanit-construction-products-v-state)

### Key text: the absence of an express right to a healthy environment

No Basic Law mentions the environment. Petitioners argue that the right to life, body and dignity (Basic Law: Human Dignity and Liberty ss. 2, 4) includes protection from serious pollution and climate harm; the State answers that environmental policy involves budget and economic trade-offs that belong to the Government and the Knesset. The argument is live in the climate petition described in lesson 34.7. For the general debate on judicial review, see [lesson 06.6](../module-06/lesson-06.md).

## How it works in practice

### The enforcement map

| Problem | Main statute | First address | Others |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neighbour's noise, a party at night | Nuisances Law s. 2; Noise Prevention Regs 1992 | Municipal hotline (usually 106); police at night | Private complaint (34.6); tort (04.4) |
| Restaurant, workshop or business noise or smell | Nuisances Law ss. 2–3; Business Licensing Law conditions | Municipal environmental or licensing unit | Ministry district; s. 5 notice (34.6) |
| Factory smoke or smell | Clean Air Law; Nuisances Law s. 3 | Ministry district office; town association | Ministry hotline *6911 for an acute event (⚠️ manual check); PRTR data (34.4) |
| Rubbish or construction waste dumped | Cleanliness Law s. 2 | Municipality; Ministry ("green police") | Report with photos (34.3) |
| Sewage or pollution in a stream | Water Law s. 20ב | Ministry; stream authority; Water Authority | Civil Claims Law (34.6) |
| Chemical spill, fire smoke | Hazardous Substances Law | *6911 and emergency services | Later FOI for the incident report (34.5) |
| New plan or plant near you | Planning and Building Law; Clean Air s. 21 | Planning objection ([lesson 27.3](../module-27/lesson-03.md)); permit comments | Petition (lesson 06.8) |

### Structure of a typical case

1. **Document** (log, photos, measurements, witnesses).
2. **Complain** to the authority with the duty, in writing, citing the section.
3. **Get the data** the authority holds (lesson 34.4, 34.5).
4. **Escalate**: Ministry, Ombudsman of the local authority or State Comptroller ([lesson 14.8](../module-14/lesson-08.md)), notice under the Civil Claims Law, private complaint, petition.

### Reform in progress

The Streamlining Environmental Licensing Law 2024 replaces several separate permits with an integrated emission permit for large plants, modelled on EU law. The consolidated texts show the new provisions taking effect on 5 September 2027, after a 2026 postponement order (⚠️ check the current date before relying on it). Until then, the old permit and business-licence conditions apply.

## Worked example

Lina Khoury lives in Kiryat Ata near the Haifa Bay industrial zone. On many evenings a sharp chemical smell enters her flat; her neighbour Shmuel Peretz's daughter has asthma.

1. **Statute.** A smell from industry is governed by the Nuisances Law s. 3 (strong or unreasonable smell) and, if it is air pollution from a stationary source, the Clean Air Law ss. 3 and 17 (emission permit).
2. **Authority.** The Ministry's Haifa district and the Haifa Bay town association for environmental protection both have enforcement roles; Kiryat Ata, a large authority, must enforce the listed statutes in its area.
3. **Data.** Before complaining she checks the PRTR for plants near her (lesson 34.4) and the air-monitoring station readings (air.sviva.gov.il).
4. **Complaint.** She logs dates and times for two weeks, collects four neighbours' statements, and files a written complaint with the district office and the town association (lab, lesson 34.8).
5. **If nothing happens.** A 60-day notice under the Civil Claims Law s. 5, or a private complaint after notice to the Minister (lesson 34.6).

## Do it yourself

### You need

- One environmental problem near your home or work (or the supplied one: a diesel generator behind a shopping centre in Be'er Sheva runs every night, and you live 40 metres away).
- The table above; your municipality's website; 40 minutes.

### Steps

1. Describe the problem in two lines: what, where, when, how often.
2. Name the statute and section that prohibits it.
3. Find the authority with a duty to act: is your municipality "large" (over 20,000 residents)? Is there a town association for the environment in your area? Which Ministry district?
4. Write down each authority's complaint channel (form, email, hotline) and whether it gives a reference number.
5. Note the citizen tool you would use if they do not act (lesson 34.6).

### Template

None. The complaint template is used in lesson 34.8.

### How you know it worked

- You can name one statute, one section and one authority with a duty, not just "the municipality".
- You have a working complaint channel for at least two bodies.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Not a filing lesson. Every step on the map (complaint, data request, notice, private complaint, objection) is open to an individual in his own name; lessons 34.6 and 34.8 say where a lawyer becomes advisable.

### Where and how

- Ministry of Environmental Protection: district offices and public enquiries on gov.il; the environmental hotline *6911 for acute events (⚠️ manual check: gov.il blocks automated access).
- Local authority: municipal hotline and environmental unit.
- Free help: Kol-Zchut's page on complaining about an environmental hazard (⚠️ manual check), environmental NGOs, university environmental clinics; [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The problem is already in court, or a business threatens to sue you for publicising it.
- You are considering a claim for health damage (this is a tort case, often complex).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Complaining to the police about a factory smell | Referred elsewhere; no record with the competent body | Re-file with the Ministry district or town association | Use the map above |
| Citing "the environmental law" generally | Complaint treated as a request, not a duty | Cite the statute and section | Name the section in the first line |
| Assuming a small council must enforce | No duty exists; frustration | Go to the Ministry district | Check the 20,000-resident threshold |
| Waiting for the authority without documenting | No evidence later | Start a dated log now | Log from day one |

## Review

- Israeli environmental law is a set of sector statutes; the Nuisances Law and the Clean Air Law hold the general prohibitions.
- Precaution, environmental justice, public participation and polluter pays are written into specific statutes, not into a Basic Law.
- Ministry inspectors have uniform powers under the 2011 Law; large local authorities and town associations must enforce listed statutes in their area.
- The usual sequence is: document, complain to the body with the duty, get the data, escalate.
