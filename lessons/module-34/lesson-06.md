# 34.6 · Enforcement: fines, citizens' suits and private complaints

By the end of this lesson you will know the three ways the State enforces environmental law (prosecution, administrative orders, financial sanctions) and the three ways you can enforce it yourself when the State does not: a private criminal complaint, an injunction suit under the 1992 Civil Claims Law, and a damages or class claim. Each citizen route has a 60-day notice that gives the authorities a last chance; used well, the notice alone often solves the problem.

## Why it matters

Environmental enforcement in Israel has always been short of inspectors. The Knesset's answer was to give citizens tools that do not depend on the police or the Ministry acting: the right to prosecute, the right to an injunction without proving negligence, and recognised organisations with standing. They are underused because people do not know the notice rules, sue in the wrong court, or ask for remedies the law does not give. This lesson is the procedural core of the module.

## The law

### State enforcement

**Offences.** Breach of the Nuisances Law is punishable by imprisonment or a fine, doubled for a company and increased for aggravated circumstances, continuing offences and repeat offences; it is strict liability, and the court may add a fine equal to the profit gained (s. 11). Officers of a company are presumed to have breached their supervision duty (s. 11ג). On an indictment or a private complaint, the court may give mandatory and prohibitory orders to prevent or stop the nuisance (s. 10א).

**Administrative orders.** An authorised official may order the person causing a noise or smell nuisance, or the owner of the property, to stop it and restore the previous state; if the order is ignored the official may carry it out and charge double the cost (Nuisances Law s. 11ב). The Clean Air Law has orders to prevent or reduce pollution and to stop using a source (ss. 45–46), published online (s. 46א); the Cleanliness Law has a cleanliness order (s. 13ב). The person ordered may apply to the court to cancel it, but the order stays in force unless the court says otherwise.

**Financial sanctions.** The sector statutes now share one model: a notice of intent stating the breach and the amount; 30 days to argue in writing; a decision and a payment demand; amounts updated by index; additions for continuing and repeat breaches; publication of the sanction and the offender's name; and judicial review (Clean Air Law ss. 50–60: petition to the administrative affairs court, which does not stay payment unless the court orders; PRTR Law ss. 16–27: appeal). The Cleanliness Law gained such a chapter in 2026 (ss. 15ד–15יט).

### The private complaint

> על עבירה לפי חוק זה רשאים המנויים להלן להגיש קובלנה כאמור בסעיף 68 לחוק סדר הדין הפלילי: (1) כל אדם – לגבי עבירה שנעברה ברשות היחיד שלו או שגרמה לו נזק; (2) רשות מקומית – לגבי עבירה שנעברה בתחומה; (3) כל אחד מהגופים הציבוריים והמקצועיים הקבועים בתוספת לחוק למניעת מפגעים סביבתיים (תביעות אזרחיות) [...]

> לא תוגש קובלנה לפי סעיף קטן (א) אלא אם כן מסר הקובל הודעה לשר על כוונתו לעשות כן ולא הוגש, תוך 60 ימים לאחר מכן, כתב אישום מטעם היועץ המשפטי לממשלה.

*Gist:* A person (for an offence in his private domain or that harmed him), a local authority (in its area) or a listed public or professional body may prosecute an offence by private complaint, but only after notifying the Minister and waiting 60 days without an indictment by the Attorney General. [Nuisances Law s. 11ה](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_למניעת_מפגעים) The Clean Air Law (s. 69) and the Cleanliness Law (s. 21א) have the same rule; the Clean Air version omits the local authority.

The complaint itself follows the Criminal Procedure Law (s. 68 onwards): taught in [lesson 05.8](../module-05/lesson-08.md), with the [private criminal complaint template](../../templates/private-criminal-complaint.md).

### The citizens' suit: Civil Claims Law 1992

> לבקשת מי שנפגע או עלול להיפגע ממפגע סביבתי או לבקשת גוף או עמותה כאמור בסעיף 6 רשאי בית משפט לתת צו המורה למי שגורם או עומד לגרום מפגע סביבתי [...] (1) להימנע מהמעשה [...] או להפסיק לעשותו; (2) לתקן את המעוות או להחזיר את המצב לקדמותו [...]; (3) לעשות ככל הדרוש כדי למנוע הישנותו [...]

*Gist:* A person harmed or likely to be harmed by an environmental hazard, or a listed body or qualifying association, may ask the magistrates' court to order the person causing it to stop, restore the previous state, or prevent recurrence. [s. 2, with "court" = magistrates' court in s. 1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_למניעת_מפגעים_סביבתיים_(תביעות_אזרחיות))

> לא יגיש אדם תובענה לפי סעיף 2, אלא לאחר שעברו 60 ימים מהיום שהוא הודיע לרשות ולגורם המפגע על כוונתו להגיש תובענה, והתקיימו שניים אלה: (1) גורם המפגע לא נקט צעדים ממשיים [...]; (2) הרשות לא נקטה צעדים ממשיים על־פי סמכויותיה [...]

*Gist:* Before suing you must notify both the authority and the person causing the hazard, wait 60 days, and then show that neither took real steps to stop, prevent or remedy it. [s. 5](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_למניעת_מפגעים_סביבתיים_(תביעות_אזרחיות))

The rest of the Law:

- **What is a hazard (s. 1):** air pollution, noise, smell, water and sea pollution, waste, hazardous substances, radiation, coastal harm, asbestos, harm to protected areas, trees, forests or protected natural assets, when contrary to a law, order, plan, business licence or permit, or harmful to health or causing real suffering.
- **"The authority" (s. 1):** the Minister of Environmental Protection or his delegate; the Nature and Parks Authority director for protected areas and natural assets; the Minister of Agriculture for trees and forests.
- **No negligence needed (s. 4);** the court balances the harm to the plaintiff or the public against the harm the order would cause (s. 3).
- **Standing (s. 6):** the bodies in the Schedule (among them the Society for the Protection of Nature, Adam Teva V'Din, Life and Environment, the Council for a Beautiful Israel, the KKL-JNF, the Nature and Parks Authority with exceptions, the National Parks Council) and any registered association whose main aim is protecting the environment if at least one of its members could sue.
- **Restoration (s. 8):** the court sets a time and hears the authority first; if it is not done, the court decides how, at the polluter's cost.
- **Recurring hazard (s. 9):** within two years of the date set in the order, a new suit needs no notice, and the court may award compensation for the damage.
- **Group suit (ss. 10–13):** for a group harmed by the same hazard; members join by written consent (s. 12(ב)); settlement or withdrawal needs court approval.
- **Award and fees (s. 14):** when the plaintiff wins, the court orders an award to the plaintiff and sets his lawyer's fee, unless special reasons are recorded.
- **Appeal (s. 15):** to the district court, single judge. **Officers (s. 16)** are liable unless they prove lack of knowledge and reasonable measures.

### Damages and class actions

Every breach of the Nuisances Law is also a private nuisance (s. 13) and every breach of the Clean Air Law a civil wrong (s. 70): see [lesson 04.4](../module-04/lesson-04.md). A class action for an environmental hazard against the person causing it is item 6 of the Second Schedule of the Class Actions Law ([lesson 33.1](../module-33/lesson-01.md)), and the court fee is waived for it (Court Fees Regulations reg. 20(31)).

## Leading cases

### עע״ם 2469/12 ברמר נ׳ עיריית תל אביב-יפו (25.6.2013)

Small shop owners asked the municipality to enforce its by-law against supermarkets that opened on the day of rest; the municipality imposed weekly fines only, which the chains absorbed. The Supreme Court (Naor D.P.; Grunis P., Rubinstein J.) held that an authority with an enforcement duty must consider all the tools the law gives it, not only fines, when fines do not achieve the purpose; failure to consider seeking a closure order was a defect, and the matter was returned for a decision within 60 days. Why it matters here: it is the free English authority for a petition against an authority that "enforces" environmental law only by small fines a polluter treats as a licence fee. The case arose from a contested day-of-rest by-law; the ruling decided only the duty to consider enforcement tools, not the by-law's merits. [Versa](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/bremer-v-tel-aviv-jaffa-municipality)

### Key text: Civil Claims Law s. 5 as a filter

No free full-text ruling on s. 5 was verified. Read the section as a two-sided filter: it protects polluters and authorities from surprise suits, and it lets a plaintiff show the court that both had a fair chance and did nothing "ממשי" (real). Why it matters: the notice is your evidence. Send it to both, by a method you can prove, with facts, sections and the remedy you will ask for.

## How it works in practice

### Choosing the route

| Goal | Route | Court or body | Notice | Who may act |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stop the hazard, fix the damage | Civil Claims Law s. 2 | Magistrates' court | 60 days to the authority and the polluter (s. 5) | Person harmed or at risk; listed body; environmental association |
| Punish and get an order | Private complaint (Nuisances s. 11ה, Clean Air s. 69, Cleanliness s. 21א) | Magistrates' court, criminal | 60 days to the Minister | Person (own domain or harmed); local authority; listed body |
| Your own money | Tort (nuisance, statutory duty) | Small claims or magistrates' | None statutory; demand letter advisable | You |
| Money for many | Class action, item 6 | District or magistrates' (by amount) | None statutory | Representative with class counsel (33.1) |
| Make the authority act | Complaint, then petition | Administrative affairs court or HCJ | Prior written demand | You; organisations |

### The notices

Both notices are short letters. The Civil Claims notice goes to the Minister (or the Nature and Parks Authority or the Agriculture Minister, by type of hazard) and to the polluter. The private-complaint notice goes to the Minister. Send by registered mail and email, keep proof, diary day 61. Template: [environmental complaint and notice](../../templates/environmental-complaint-and-notice.md), parts B and C.

### Cost

An injunction suit under the 1992 Law is not a money claim; it appears to fall under "other claim" in the magistrates' court fee schedule (item 4) (⚠️ check with the registry); amount in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md). An environmental class action is fee-exempt. The court may award costs against a losing plaintiff, and s. 14 gives a winning plaintiff an award and fees.

## Worked example

Kamal and Rim Halabi live in Daliyat al-Karmel. A stone-crushing site 300 metres from their house works at night without the permit conditions it needs; dust covers their yard and their son's asthma has worsened. Complaints to the Ministry district over four months produced one inspection and no order.

1. **Hazard.** Air pollution and noise contrary to the plant's licence conditions and harmful to health: an environmental hazard under s. 1.
2. **Notice (s. 5).** On 1 June they send a notice to the Minister of Environmental Protection (district office copy) and to the operating company: facts, log, photos, the PRTR entry for the site, the remedy sought (stop night work, dust suppression, cleaning the yard), and that they will sue after 60 days.
3. **Private complaint notice.** The same day, a notice to the Minister under Nuisances Law s. 11ה about night noise in their private domain.
4. **Day 61.** The company installed sprinklers but still works at night; the Ministry sent a warning letter. Partial steps: the court will ask whether they were "real" for the night work.
5. **Choice.** They file a Civil Claims Law suit in the Haifa magistrates' court for an order to stop night operations, attaching both notices; they hold the private complaint in reserve. A registered environmental association of which Rim is a member considers joining as co-plaintiff (s. 6(ג)).
6. **If it recurs.** Within two years of the date set in an order, a new suit needs no notice and can include compensation (s. 9).

## Do it yourself

### You need

- The problem, log and data from lessons 34.2 and 34.4, or the supplied one: a dairy farm's manure pile on the edge of a moshav near Kfar Yona smells for weeks; residents' complaints to the regional council have gone unanswered.
- The template; 60 minutes.

### Steps

1. Decide whether the facts are an "environmental hazard" under s. 1 (contrary to a law, licence or permit, or harmful to health or causing real suffering).
2. Identify "the authority" for this hazard type under s. 1.
3. Draft the s. 5 notice: facts, sections, the remedy, the 60-day statement (template part B).
4. Decide whether you can also bring a private complaint (is the offence in your private domain or did it harm you?) and draft the notice to the Minister (part C).
5. Write a decision paragraph: which route, in which order, and what would make you stop.

### How you know it worked

- Your notice goes to both the authority and the polluter and names the remedy.
- You know which body is "the authority" for your hazard and the date of day 61.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Partly. Notices, a Civil Claims Law suit by an individual and a private complaint can be brought in your own name; the private complaint carries the criminal burden of proof and is demanding (lesson 05.8). An association suing needs a lawyer in practice, as a company does in most courts ([can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md)). A class action needs class counsel (lesson 33.1).

### Where and how

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- Notices: registered mail and email to the Minister of Environmental Protection (district office) and the polluter; keep proof.
- Suit: the magistrates' court for the area, through Net HaMishpat or the registry; fee via [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Private complaint: the criminal registry of the magistrates' court (lesson 05.8).
- Free help: environmental NGOs listed in the Schedule may advise or join; university environmental clinics; [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The polluter is a large company represented by counsel and the case needs expert evidence (dust, odour, health).
- You are asked to sign a settlement that ends your suit (group suits need court approval, s. 13).
- You face a counterclaim or a costs application.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Suing under the 1992 Law without the s. 5 notice | Suit struck out | Send the notice; refile after 60 days | Notice first, always |
| Notice to the polluter only | s. 5 not met | Send to the authority; wait 60 days | Two addressees |
| Asking for damages in a first 1992 suit | Remedy not available (only orders; compensation only for recurrence, s. 9) | Add a tort claim | Match remedy to route |
| Filing a private complaint about a hazard that never reached your property | No standing (s. 11ה(א)(1)) | Ask a listed body or the local authority | Check your standing first |
| Filing the 1992 suit in the district court | Wrong court | Transfer or refile in the magistrates' court | s. 1 definition of "court" |

## Review

- The State enforces by prosecution, administrative orders and financial sanctions; sanctions follow a notice, a 30-day hearing and judicial review.
- A private complaint is open to a person for an offence in his domain or that harmed him, a local authority, or a listed body, after 60 days' notice to the Minister.
- The Civil Claims Law gives an injunction in the magistrates' court without proof of negligence, after 60 days' notice to the authority and the polluter, to individuals, listed bodies and environmental associations.
- Damages come through tort or, for many, an item 6 class action; recurrence within two years opens compensation under s. 9.
