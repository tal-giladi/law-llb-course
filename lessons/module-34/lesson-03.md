# 34.3 · Water, waste, hazardous materials and recycling laws

By the end of this lesson you will know the core duty of each sector statute a citizen meets most often (water pollution, littering and dumping, packaging, bottle deposits, poisons) and the one tool each gives you: the presumption that catches the driver who throws rubbish from a car, the private complaint, the statutory compensation when a shop refuses your empty bottles. The aim is breadth with precision: the right section for the right problem.

## Why it matters

Waste is where Israeli citizens can act most directly. Construction waste dumped in open areas, sewage in streams and refused bottle returns are everyday problems, and the statutes were written with citizen enforcement in mind: some pay informers, some let the public prosecute, one gives you compensation without proof of damage. The same statutes also show the policy choice behind modern environmental law: make producers pay for the waste their products create.

## The law

### Water

> מקורות המים שבמדינה הם קנין הציבור, נתונים לשליטתה של המדינה ומיועדים לצרכי תושביה ולפיתוח הארץ.

*Gist:* Water sources are public property under State control. [Water Law s. 1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המים)

> חייב אדם להימנע מכל פעולה המזהמת מים או עלולה לגרום לזיהום מים, במישרין או בעקיפין, מיד או לאחר זמן;

*Gist:* Everyone must avoid any act that pollutes or may pollute water, directly or indirectly, now or later, even if the source was already polluted; no one may throw or discharge material into or near a water source. [s. 20ב](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המים) "Water source" includes streams, groundwater, reservoirs and drainage channels (s. 20א).

### Littering and dumping

> לא ישליך אדם פסולת, פסולת בנין או גרוטות רכב ברשות הרבים או מרשות הרבים לרשות היחיד, ולא ילכלך את רשות הרבים.

*Gist:* No one may throw waste, construction waste or vehicle wrecks into the public domain, or from it into private land, or dirty the public domain. [Cleanliness Law s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_שמירת_הנקיון)

If waste is proved to have been thrown from a vehicle, the owner (and the driver) is presumed to have thrown it unless he proves who did (s. 4). That presumption is what makes a citizen's photo of a car and its plate useful. The Minister or a local authority head may appoint volunteer cleanliness trustees who may require an offender to identify himself (s. 12(ג)). A private person who personally films construction or bulk waste being dumped from a vehicle, so that the vehicle or driver can be identified, and gives the film with a signed report to an authorised Ministry employee, is paid from the Cleanliness Fund if the court finds that the documentation contributed materially to a conviction (s. 15א; amount, index-linked: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)). It does not apply to a case that ended with a fixed fine notice (s. 15א(ה)). Amendment 27 (2026) added a licensing and registration regime for waste businesses and a financial-sanctions chapter (ss. 7א–7לט, 15ד–15יט), parts of it in force at later dates (⚠️ check commencement before citing a specific duty).

### Packaging: producers pay

> [...] הטלת אחריות מורחבת על יצרנים של מוצרים ארוזים, על יצרנים של אריזות שירות ועל יבואנים של מוצרים ואריזות כאמור, בכל הנוגע לסימון האריזות ולביצוע מיחזור מוכר של פסולת האריזות [...]

*Gist:* The Packaging Law imposes extended producer responsibility for marking and recycling packaging waste, with binding targets and recognised bodies acting for producers, duties on local authorities to set separation and collection arrangements, and a ban on landfilling packaging waste. [Packaging Law ss. 1, 9, 12, 23, 27](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_להסדרת_הטיפול_באריזות)

### Bottle deposits

> צרכן שיש בידיו מכל משקה ריק מסומן, רשאי להחזירו לבית עסק שבו מוכרים מכלי משקה מלאים מסוג כלשהו, של היצרן או היבואן [...]

*Gist:* A consumer may return a marked empty container to any shop that sells full containers of that producer or importer, and the shop must accept it and refund the deposit. [Deposit Law s. 6](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הפיקדון_על_מכלי_משקה)

The exceptions (s. 6(ג)): a shop of under 28 m² need accept only containers bought there; a shop need not take more than 50 containers from the same consumer per day; drinks sold for consumption on the premises are excluded unless a deposit was charged. The deposit is 30 agorot unless the Minister sets another amount (s. 2(ב)). Since 1 December 2021 the Law also covers "large" containers of 1.5 to 5 litres (s. 1; s. 7ה and the note to it; ק״ת תשפ״א 752).

> סירב בית עסק לקבל מצרכן מכלי משקה ריקים מסומנים ולשלם לו את דמי הפיקדון בעדם, כאמור בסעיף 6, רשאי בית המשפט לפסוק לצרכן פיצוי שאינו תלוי בנזק [...]

*Gist:* A shop that refuses to take marked empties and refund the deposit can be ordered to pay the consumer exemplary damages without proof of damage, up to a fixed amount per refusal (several refusals to the same consumer on the same business day count as one). [s. 22](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הפיקדון_על_מכלי_משקה) Amount: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### Hazardous substances

> לא יעסוק אדם ברעלים אלא אם כן יש בידו היתר רעלים מאת הממונה;

*Gist:* Dealing in poisons requires a poisons permit from the Ministry's commissioner (with exceptions for pharmacies and over-the-counter products). [Hazardous Substances Law s. 3](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החמרים_המסוכנים) From the reform date (shown as 5 September 2027 in the consolidated text, ⚠️ check) large activities move to an integrated permit (lesson 34.1).

## Leading cases

### בג״ץ 2887/04 אבו מדיגם נ׳ מינהל מקרקעי ישראל (14.4.2007)

The authorities sprayed crops planted by Bedouin on State land in the Negev from the air with a glyphosate herbicide to stop unauthorised cultivation. The petition was granted: Justice Joubran held the spraying ultra vires; Justices Arbel and Naor held that even if authorised it was disproportionate, given the risk to health and dignity, including from accidents, and the existence of other means of enforcement. Why it matters: health risk from a hazardous substance need not be proved to a certainty for a measure to fail proportionality; the Court relied on the product's own warnings and criticised an expert report that copied the manufacturer's material. The case concerned a dispute over land in which the State's position was that the land was State land and the petitioners' position was that the spraying endangered residents; the ruling decided only the method of enforcement. [Versa](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/abu-madigam-v-israel-land-administration)

### Key text: Deposit Law s. 22

The only environmental statute in this module that gives an individual consumer exemplary damages for a refusal. The court weighs enforcement and deterrence, encouraging consumers to claim, and the gravity of the breach, not your actual loss (s. 22(ב)). Compare consumer exemplary damages in [lesson 29.9](../module-29/lesson-09.md).

## How it works in practice

### Which tool for which problem

| Problem | Duty | Your tool |
|---|---|---|
| Driver throws rubbish from a car | Cleanliness s. 2; presumption s. 4 | Report with photo, plate, time, place to the Ministry or municipality (⚠️ manual check of the current reporting form) |
| Truck dumps construction waste in a field | s. 2; s. 15א | Video showing the vehicle and plate, with a signed report to an authorised Ministry employee; payment if it contributes to a conviction |
| Supermarket refuses bottles | Deposit s. 6 | Ask for the manager in writing; small claim for exemplary damages (s. 22); report to the Ministry |
| No separate packaging bins in your city | Packaging s. 23 | Letter to the municipality; complaint to the Ministry |
| Sewage overflow into a stream | Water Law s. 20ב | Ministry, stream authority, the water corporation; *6911 if acute (⚠️ manual check) |
| Pesticide or chemical smell after spraying | Hazardous Substances; plant-protection rules | *6911; later FOI for the incident report (34.5) |

### A small claim for refused deposits

You claim in the small-claims court ([lesson 08.3](../module-08/lesson-03.md)) against the business owner, attaching proof of the refusal (video, a written refusal, a witness), the date and the shop's size if relevant. Ask for the exemplary damages under s. 22 and the deposit itself. Fee and ceiling: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

## Worked example

Yossi Amar collects empty bottles with his teenage children in Afula as a family project. On 3 March 2026 they bring 46 marked bottles, including twelve 1.5-litre bottles, to a 400 m² branch of a supermarket chain. The cashier refuses: "we don't take big bottles, and only from 9 to 11."

1. **Duty.** The branch sells full containers of those producers; it must accept marked empties (s. 6(ב)). Large containers have been covered since December 2021. The 28 m² and 50-per-day exceptions do not apply (400 m², 46 bottles). Section 6 itself sets no hours for returns (⚠️ check the Deposit Regulations before relying on this).
2. **Evidence.** He films the refusal and asks the duty manager to confirm in writing; the manager refuses to sign, which he notes with the time.
3. **Demand.** He emails the chain's customer service the next day, citing ss. 6 and 22, and asks for the deposit and an apology.
4. **Claim.** No answer in 14 days: a small claim for the deposit and exemplary damages under s. 22 for one refusal (one business day), attaching the video, the email and photos of the marked bottles.
5. **Report.** In parallel, a report to the Ministry, which supervises the Deposit Law.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A waste or water problem of your own, or the supplied one: a construction contractor in Sakhnin leaves rubble on the shoulder of a regional road every week; residents have photos of a truck.
- The statutes above; 50 minutes.

### Steps

1. Name the duty and section breached.
2. Check whether a presumption or a citizen tool exists (s. 4 presumption, s. 15א payment, s. 22 damages, private complaint s. 21א).
3. List the evidence that meets it (vehicle, plate, time, place, continuity of the video).
4. Find the reporting channel of the Ministry or municipality and write the report text (8–10 lines).
5. Decide whether a private complaint or a claim is realistic (lesson 34.6).

### Template

[Environmental complaint and notice](../../templates/environmental-complaint-and-notice.md) (part A, complaint to an authority). For a deposit claim: [small claim (contract or consumer)](../../templates/small-claim-contract-consumer.md).

### How you know it worked

- Your report names the section and contains evidence that fits the statutory presumption.
- For a deposit case, you checked the shop's size, the number of containers and the date.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes: reports, complaints, a small claim for deposit refusal, and a private complaint under the Cleanliness Law after notice to the Minister (s. 21א). A company or organisation filing a small claim has special rules ([can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md)).

### Where and how

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- Reports: the Ministry's online forms and *6911 (⚠️ manual check); the municipal hotline.
- Small claim: the magistrates' court sitting as small-claims court; fee and ceiling in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Free help: Kol-Zchut's deposit and littering pages (⚠️ manual check); court information centres.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You want to sue a polluter for damage to your land or business from contamination (expert evidence, large stakes).
- A contractor you reported threatens you; report the threat to the police ([lesson 05.8](../module-05/lesson-08.md)).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Photo without the plate or the moment of throwing | Presumption cannot apply | Add witness statements; report anyway | Film continuously, plate and act |
| Claiming against a 20 m² kiosk for bottles bought elsewhere | Claim dismissed (s. 6(ג)(1)(ב)) | Claim only for bottles bought there | Check size and where bought |
| Counting each bottle as a separate refusal | Inflated claim; credibility lost | One refusal per business day | Read s. 22(א) |
| Calling the police about sewage in a stream | No competent body acts | Report to the Ministry and the stream authority | Use the table above |

## Review

- The Water Law bans polluting any water source, even one already polluted (s. 20ב).
- The Cleanliness Law presumes the vehicle owner threw what came from his vehicle (s. 4) and rewards some documenters of construction waste (s. 15א).
- Packaging and deposits are extended producer responsibility; large bottles have been covered since December 2021.
- A shop's refusal of marked empties can cost it exemplary damages without proof of damage (Deposit Law s. 22).
