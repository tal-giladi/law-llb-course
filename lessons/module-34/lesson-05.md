# 34.5 · Environmental information and freedom of information

By the end of this lesson you will be able to use the special environmental layer of the Freedom of Information Law: the duty of every public authority to publish emission and measurement data without being asked, and the stronger arguments you have when you do ask. The general procedure (who is an authority, periods, fees, petitions) was taught in [lesson 14.7](../module-14/lesson-07.md); this lesson adds only what is different for the environment.

## Why it matters

Environmental disputes are won on data the other side holds: the plant's stack measurements, the municipality's noise readings, the Ministry's inspection report from the night of the smell. Israeli law treats this information differently from ordinary government records. Authorities must publish it on their own initiative, even when the results are within the permitted limits, and the public interest in health and the environment is one of the factors that weighs against refusal. Knowing this turns a polite request into a demand the authority must answer.

## The law

### The proactive duty

> רשות ציבורית תעמיד לעיון הציבור מידע על איכות הסביבה שיש ברשותה, באתר האינטרנט של הרשות הציבורית [...] לענין זה, ”מידע על איכות הסביבה“ – מידע על חומרים שנפלטו, שנשפכו, שסולקו או שהושלכו לסביבה ותוצאות של מדידות רעש, ריח וקרינה, שלא ברשות היחיד.

*Gist:* Every public authority must publish the environmental information it holds on its website: data on substances emitted, spilled, disposed of or thrown into the environment, and results of noise, smell and radiation measurements outside the private domain; the exemptions in s. 9 and the third-party procedure in s. 13 apply with changes. [FOI Law s. 6א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חופש_המידע)

### The 2009 regulations

> רשות ציבורית תעמיד לעיון הציבור מידע שמקורו בדיווח או באיסוף מידע [...] אף אם הפליטה או תוצאות המדידה אינן עולות על המותר על פי כל חיקוק, צו, תכנית, רישיון עסק או כל היתר או רישיון אחר.

*Gist:* The duty covers information that reached the authority in a report or that it measured itself, about substances, noise, smell or radiation of a kind that may cause an environmental hazard, including the 22 categories in the First Schedule, even when the results are within the permitted limits. [Environmental Information Regulations 2009, reg. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_חופש_המידע_(העמדת_מידע_על_איכות_הסביבה_לעיון_הציבור))

The rest of the regulations, briefly:

- **Content (reg. 3):** the source, the substance, quantity and concentration, levels measured, where, when and how measured and by whom, the area and time, the medium, and any exceedance of the permitted limit; processed reports too, marked as processed (reg. 4(ב)). Raw data need not be processed for you (reg. 3(ב)).
- **Where (regs 4–5):** on the internet in a retrievable form, and in the authority's offices at least twice a week; the authority must advertise its website and office hours at least every six months.
- **When (reg. 6):** as soon as possible and no later than three months after receiving the report or making the measurement; the third-party period of s. 13 is not counted.
- **How long (reg. 7):** at least one year online and seven years in the offices.
- **Refusal (reg. 3(ג)):** an authority that withholds information under s. 9 must publish a notice of its decision and the reasons.
- **Schedule (First Schedule):** includes air-quality and stack measurements, hazardous-substance incidents, wastewater and effluent measurements, water-source measurements, pesticide application, landfill biogas and leachate, radiation and radon, noise and smell measurements, soil measurements and industrial effluent leaks.

The regulations took effect 18 months after publication (2009), and for local authorities not listed in the Second Schedule 36 months after (reg. 8).

### Tools against a refusal

When the authority considers refusing under s. 8 or 9, it must weigh your interest (if you stated it) and the public interest in disclosure for public health or safety or environmental protection (s. 10). Exempt parts must be removed and the rest disclosed (s. 11). In a petition, the court may order disclosure of information that would otherwise be withheld if the public interest outweighs the reason for refusal (s. 17(ד)). And under the Clean Air Law, the identity of a source and the types, quantities, concentrations and rates of pollutants are never a trade secret (s. 21(ד)(2)(ג)).

## Leading cases

### עע״ם 9135/03 המועצה להשכלה גבוהה נ׳ הוצאת עיתון הארץ (19.1.2006)

Briefed in [lesson 14.7](../module-14/lesson-07.md): the FOI Law gives every person a right to information as a matter of principle, and the authority bears the burden of justifying a refusal. Why it matters here: s. 6א goes further for environmental information, because the authority must publish without a request; when it has not, your request is also a reminder of a duty it already breached. [Cardozo](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/116)

### Key text: reg. 2 "even if within the limits"

Authorities sometimes refuse monitoring results "because there was no exceedance". Regulation 2 says the duty applies "אף אם הפליטה או תוצאות המדידה אינן עולות על המותר". Why it matters: compliance is not a ground for secrecy; the public is entitled to see the numbers and judge for itself.

## How it works in practice

### First look, then ask

1. Check the authority's website for the s. 6א section. The Ministry's s. 6א repository is also on [data.gov.il](https://data.gov.il/he/datasets/ministry_of_the_environment/freedom-of-info); on 18.10.2026 its files showed a last update in 2017, so ask for current publication.
2. Check the PRTR and the air-monitoring site (lesson 34.4).
3. Then ask for what is missing, by FOI request to the authority's FOI officer ([FOI request template](../../templates/foi-request.md); procedure, fees and periods in lesson 14.7 and [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).

### What to ask for

Ask for records that exist, by type, place and period:

- the results of the plant's continuous or periodic stack monitoring reported to the Ministry under its permit or licence, for named months;
- malfunction or incident reports for named dates;
- the Ministry's or town association's inspection reports and visit summaries, and any warning letters, orders or sanctions;
- the municipality's noise or smell measurement results at a named address;
- the business licence conditions set by the Ministry.

### Pre-empting objections

- **Trade secret:** quote Clean Air s. 21(ד)(2)(ג) for emissions data, and ask for partial disclosure under s. 11.
- **Third party:** the plant may object (s. 13); this pauses the clock but does not decide the request.
- **Workload (s. 8(1)):** narrow by period and record type.
- **"Not our information":** the authority must publish what it holds, whether it measured it or received it in a report (reg. 1, "דיווח", "איסוף מידע").

### The environmental annex

Add this paragraph to the request in the template (Hebrew, as filed):

```text
המידע המבוקש הוא "מידע על איכות הסביבה" כהגדרתו בסעיף 6א(א) לחוק, שהרשות חייבת להעמיד לעיון הציבור
גם ללא בקשה, לפי תקנות חופש המידע (העמדת מידע על איכות הסביבה לעיון הציבור), תשס"ט-2009, ובכלל זה
אף אם תוצאות המדידה אינן עולות על המותר (תקנה 2). לעניין סוד מסחרי, ראו סעיף 21(ד)(2)(ג) לחוק אוויר
נקי, התשס"ח-2008. ככל שתישקל דחייה, אבקש לשקול את העניין הציבורי בבריאות הציבור ובהגנה על הסביבה
(סעיף 10 לחוק) ולמסור את המידע בחלקו (סעיף 11 לחוק).
```

## Worked example

Ofer and Maayan Golan of Kfar Tavor (a local council listed in the regulations' Second Schedule) suspect that a quarry nearby exceeds dust limits. The PRTR shows dust (PM10) releases but only annual totals.

1. **Look first.** The council's website has no s. 6א section; the Ministry's repository is outdated.
2. **Request.** They send one FOI request to the Ministry's FOI officer for: dust monitoring results reported by the quarry for January to June 2026; inspection reports for the same period; and any warning letter or order issued. They add the annex.
3. **Answer.** The Ministry says it notified the quarry as a third party (s. 13). The quarry objects that the measurements are commercially sensitive.
4. **Reply.** They answer in writing: dust concentrations are emissions data, never a trade secret under Clean Air s. 21(ד)(2)(ג); the information should have been published proactively within three months (reg. 6); they ask for partial disclosure at least (s. 11).
5. **Outcome path.** If refused, a petition to the administrative affairs court within the period in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md) (lesson 14.7), arguing s. 17(ד).

## Do it yourself

### You need

- The data sheet you built in lesson 34.4, or the supplied case: residents of Kiryat Tivon want the noise measurements the municipality took near a new highway interchange in 2026.
- The [FOI request template](../../templates/foi-request.md); 60 minutes.

### Steps

1. Check the authority's website and data.gov.il for proactive publication; note the date of the latest data you find.
2. List three to five records you need, each with type, place and period.
3. Draft the request in the template and add the environmental annex.
4. Write two lines anticipating the most likely objection and your answer.
5. Diary the decision date and the delivery date (lesson 14.7).

### How you know it worked

- Each item asks for an existing record, not an opinion or a new analysis.
- The request cites s. 6א, reg. 2 and, for emissions, Clean Air s. 21(ד)(2)(ג).

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Any citizen or resident may request information without giving reasons (FOI Law ss. 1, 7(א)); see [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md). A petition against a refusal goes to the administrative affairs court (s. 17); acting for yourself there is possible (lesson 14.7).

### Where and how

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- The authority's FOI officer; for ministries, the government FOI unit's online system (⚠️ manual check: gov.il blocks bots).
- Fees, exemptions and periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); a request about yourself, or by a registered non-profit with a proper-management certificate, has fee benefits (lesson 14.7).
- Free help: environmental NGOs and the Movement for Freedom of Information (⚠️ manual check of current services); [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The plant joins as a third party with a lawyer in a petition, and the case turns on technical trade-secret evidence.
- You want the data to support a damages claim or a class action (lesson 34.6; [lesson 33.1](../module-33/lesson-01.md)).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Asking "is the plant polluting?" | Refused: not existing information | Ask for named records | List record type, place and period |
| Accepting "no exceedance, so no need to publish" | Lose the data | Cite reg. 2 | Quote reg. 2 in the request |
| Accepting a blanket trade-secret refusal for emissions | Data withheld unlawfully | Cite Clean Air s. 21(ד)(2)(ג) and s. 11 | Pre-empt it in the annex |
| Missing the petition period | Refusal becomes final in practice | File a new, narrower request | Diary every period on receipt |
| Asking only the Ministry | Missing municipal measurements | Send a second request to the municipality | Map who measured what (lesson 34.1) |

## Review

- Every public authority must publish the environmental information it holds, within three months, online and in its offices, even if the results are within the limits (s. 6א; 2009 regulations).
- The duty covers what the authority measured and what was reported to it.
- Against a refusal: the public interest in health and the environment (s. 10), partial disclosure (s. 11), the court's override (s. 17(ד)), and the rule that emissions data is not a trade secret (Clean Air s. 21(ד)(2)(ג)).
- Look first, then ask for named existing records.
