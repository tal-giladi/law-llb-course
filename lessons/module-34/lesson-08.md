# 34.8 · Practice lab: an environmental complaint and a data request

In this lab you build a complete first-stage environmental file: a written complaint to the right authority with a proper log, a data request that asks for the records only the authority holds, and the two 60-day notices prepared and held for the moment you need them. Use a real problem of your own if you have one; the documents are low-risk and may be sent in your own name. Otherwise use the supplied case and keep it a dry run.

## Why it matters

Most environmental problems are solved, or lost, at this stage. An authority that receives a precise, sourced complaint with evidence, and in the same week an FOI request for its own inspection records, knows it is being watched by someone who knows the law. If it still does nothing, you already hold the evidence that the 60-day notices and a court will need.

## The law

The sections you will use were taught in this module; here are the ones each document rests on.

> רשות ציבורית תעמיד לעיון הציבור מידע על איכות הסביבה שיש ברשותה [...]

*Gist:* Environmental information held by a public authority must be published; a request for it is also a reminder of that duty. [FOI Law s. 6א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חופש_המידע)

> לא יגיש אדם תובענה לפי סעיף 2, אלא לאחר שעברו 60 ימים מהיום שהוא הודיע לרשות ולגורם המפגע על כוונתו להגיש תובענה [...]

*Gist:* The 60-day notice to the authority and the polluter precedes a citizens' suit. [Civil Claims Law s. 5](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_למניעת_מפגעים_סביבתיים_(תביעות_אזרחיות))

An authority must answer a written application within 45 days, and must give reasons for a refusal (Decisions and Reasons Law 1958 ss. 2, 2א; [lesson 02.7](../module-02/lesson-07.md)).

| Document | To | Basis | Timing | Answer expected |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Complaint (template part A) | Body with the duty (lesson 34.1) | Sector statute; Decisions and Reasons Law | Week 1, after 2 weeks of log | 45 days |
| FOI request | The same body's FOI officer (and the Ministry if different) | FOI s. 6א; regs; s. 7 | Week 1 | 30 days, extendable ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)) |
| Civil Claims notice (part B) | Authority and polluter | s. 5 | Held; send if no real steps after the complaint | Suit possible after 60 days |
| Private-complaint notice (part C) | Minister | s. 11ה / 69 / 21א | Held | Complaint possible after 60 days |

## Worked example

Supplied case (use it if you have no problem of your own): Tamar and Yaakov Ben-Shimon live in Kiryat Gat, opposite a 24-hour industrial bakery. Since March 2026 the extractor fans on its roof run all night and a burnt-sugar smell enters their bedroom three to four nights a week. Two calls to the municipal hotline brought no visit.

1. **Body with the duty.** Kiryat Gat is a large authority: its environmental unit must enforce (Local Authorities (Environmental Enforcement) Law s. 2(א)). The bakery holds a business licence with Ministry conditions.
2. **Log.** Fourteen nights logged, with times, intensity and the hotline reference numbers; three neighbours' short statements.
3. **Data.** A data.gov.il query for the town lists industrial plants; the bakery is not a PRTR reporter (below the activity thresholds), so the data request goes to the municipality for its measurements, its licensing file and any Ministry conditions.
4. **Complaint.** Template part A to the environmental unit and licensing department, citing Nuisances Law ss. 2 and 3 and asking for a night inspection and measurement.
5. **FOI.** To the municipality's FOI officer: the business licence and its conditions; any noise or smell measurements at the bakery since 2025; inspection reports; warning letters or orders. Environmental annex added.
6. **Held.** Notices B and C drafted, dated blank, in the file. Diary: day 45 for the complaint, the FOI dates, and the decision point.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your problem (or the supplied case), your log from [lesson 34.2](lesson-02.md), your data sheet from [lesson 34.4](lesson-04.md).
- Templates: [environmental complaint and notice](../../templates/environmental-complaint-and-notice.md) and [FOI request](../../templates/foi-request.md).
- 150 minutes (plus two weeks of logging if you start from scratch).

### Steps

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

**Part 1: the complaint (50 minutes)**

1. Confirm the body with the duty (lesson 34.1): municipality (over 20,000 residents?), town association, or Ministry district.
2. Write the facts in numbered lines, one fact per line, each pointing to an exhibit.
3. Cite the statute and section, and the regulation if one fits (lesson 34.2 or 34.3).
4. Ask for specific action: inspection at the relevant hours, measurement, a named enforcement tool, the inspection results, and a reasoned answer in 45 days.
5. Assemble exhibits: log, photos or videos with time, statements, hotline numbers.
6. Send by the authority's form or email, and registered mail if the matter is serious; save the confirmation.

**Part 2: the data request (50 minutes)**

7. Look first: the authority's s. 6א page, data.gov.il (PRTR and the Ministry's repository), air.sviva.gov.il. Note what you found and its date.
8. List three to five named records with period: monitoring results, malfunction or incident reports, inspection reports, licence conditions, warnings, orders and sanctions.
9. Fill in the FOI template; add the environmental annex from [lesson 34.5](lesson-05.md); state your interest briefly (it counts under s. 10).
10. Check the fee and any exemption ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)); send; diary the decision date.

**Part 3: the held notices and the diary (50 minutes)**

11. Draft notice B (Civil Claims Law s. 5) to the correct "authority" and the polluter, with the remedy you would seek.
12. Check your standing for a private complaint; if you have it, draft notice C to the Minister.
13. Do not send B or C yet. Write the trigger for sending: for example, "no inspection within 45 days" or "inspection found breach but no order within 30 days".
14. Build the diary: complaint day 45; FOI day 30 (and extensions); the trigger date; day 61 after any notice.

### Template

[Environmental complaint and notice](../../templates/environmental-complaint-and-notice.md) (parts A, B, C); [FOI request](../../templates/foi-request.md).

### How you know it worked

- An inspector reading your complaint knows what, where, when, which section, and what you want, in under two minutes.
- Each FOI item names an existing record and a period; the annex cites s. 6א, reg. 2 and, for emissions, Clean Air s. 21(ד)(2)(ג).
- Notice B is addressed to both the right authority and the polluter, and states the remedy.
- Your diary has every date.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Complaints, FOI requests and both notices are routinely sent by individuals in their own name ([can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md)). Filing the suit or the private complaint afterwards is also open to you, with the cautions in [lesson 34.6](lesson-06.md).

### Where and how

- The authority's online form, email and registered mail; the FOI officer's form; the Ministry's district office (⚠️ manual check of current addresses: gov.il blocks bots).
- FOI fees, periods and exemptions: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Free help: Kol-Zchut's environmental-complaint page (⚠️ manual check), environmental NGOs, [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The business answers through a lawyer threatening a defamation or harassment claim.
- You are preparing a joint suit for many residents (a group suit or class action).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Complaint without a log | "No evidence of a nuisance" | Log two more weeks and resend | Log first |
| Sending notice B to the polluter only | s. 5 not satisfied | Send to the authority; restart the 60 days | Two addressees, proof of delivery |
| FOI request for "all information" | Refused under s. 8 | Narrow and resend | Named records and periods |
| Sending notices B and C on day 1 | Burns goodwill; looks like a threat | Explain you are open to a solution | Hold them until a trigger |
| No diary | Deadlines missed | Rebuild from receipts | Diary on the day you send |

## Review

- A good first-stage file has three parts: a precise complaint, a targeted data request, and held notices with triggers.
- Complain to the body with the duty; ask for a specific action and a reasoned answer in 45 days.
- Ask for named existing records and add the environmental annex.
- Keep the 60-day notices ready, and send them when a trigger you set in advance is met.
