# 26.5 · Administering an estate: debts, distribution and the spouse's rights

By the end of this lesson you will be able to plan what happens between the order and the money reaching the heirs: who manages the estate, which debts are paid first, how assets that cannot be split are divided, what the widow or widower can stay in, and when a dependant can claim maintenance from the estate even against a will.

## Why it matters

A succession order says who the heirs are and in what shares (lesson [26.6](lesson-06.md)); it does not pay the bank, sell the car or decide who lives in the flat. Most estates are administered informally by the heirs themselves. That works when the family agrees and the estate is solvent. It goes wrong when an heir empties the joint account, when a creditor appears after the money was divided, or when the surviving spouse is told to leave the home within days. Chapter 6 of the Succession Law gives answers to each of these, and knowing them early prevents personal liability for debts you never owed.

The chapter also expresses a policy choice: Israel gives testators very wide freedom, but it is not unlimited. A spouse, children and dependent parents who need support may claim maintenance from the estate "whether by law or by will" (s. 56), and a will cannot exclude it (s. 65(ב)). This is Israel's answer to the forced-share systems of continental Europe and religious law: no fixed share for anyone, but a needs-based claim.

## The law

### Who manages: ss. 77–78, 121–122

Before an administrator is appointed the Family Court may take preservation measures, including freezing dealings, selling perishable assets and appointing a temporary administrator (s. 77). The court appoints an administrator on any interested person's request (s. 78(א)); if all the parties concerned consent, the request goes to the Registrar of Inheritance, who may appoint (s. 78(ב)). A person named in the will to carry it out is appointed unless unable, unwilling or there are special reasons (s. 81). Without an administrator, the heirs manage and divide the estate themselves, acting by agreement; disputes go to the court, and any heir may act alone in an urgent matter (ss. 121–122).

### What an administrator must do and may not do alone: ss. 82–97

The administrator collects the assets, manages them, pays the debts and distributes the rest according to the order or will (s. 82), files an inventory within 60 days of appointment, verified by affidavit and recorded with the Administrator General (s. 84), and reports to the heirs at least once a year (s. 86). Court approval is needed in advance to transfer, charge, divide or liquidate a business unit or an apartment, for a protected tenancy, for any act depending on registration in a statutory register (such as the Land Registry), and for giving a guarantee (s. 97).

### Debts and their order: ss. 99–106

The administrator must publish an invitation to creditors with at least three months to respond (s. 99), and the heirs may do the same (s. 123). Debts are paid first from cash (s. 100(א)); assets to be sold are offered first to the heirs at not less than market price (s. 100(ב)).

> (א) הסכומים הבאים (בחוק זה – חובות העזבון) יסולקו לפי סדר עדיפות זה: (1) ההוצאות הכרוכות בהלווית המוריש, בקבורתו ובהצבת מצבה על קברו [...] (2) ההוצאות של צו ירושה, של צו קיום ושל ניהול העזבון [...] (3) החובות שהמוריש היה חייב ערב מותו [...] לרבות המגיע לאשתו על פי כתובה במידה שסכום הכתובה אינו עולה על סכום סביר; (4) המגיע לבן־זוגו של המוריש על פי עילה הנובעת מקשר האישות [...] והמגיע לבן זוג לפי חוק יחסי ממון בין בני זוג [...]

*Gist:* Debts are paid in this order: funeral, burial and tombstone; the costs of the order and administration; the deceased's debts, including a reasonable ketubah; then the spouse's claims arising from the marriage and under the property-relations law. Debts of the estate come before maintenance from the estate (s. 104(ד)). [s. 104](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הירושה#סעיף_104)

Secured debts are collected from the security (s. 101). If the estate cannot pay all its debts, the administrator must seek an insolvency order for the estate (module 28) unless the court orders otherwise (s. 106).

### Heirs' liability: ss. 126–134

> עד לחלוקת העזבון אין היורשים אחראים לחובות העזבון אלא בנכסי העזבון.

*Gist:* Until distribution, heirs are liable for estate debts only out of the estate's assets. [s. 126](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הירושה#סעיף_126)

After distribution: if creditors were invited and known debts paid, an heir is liable for an unpaid debt only if he knew of it at distribution, and up to the value he received (s. 127). If the estate was divided without inviting creditors and without paying known debts, each heir is liable up to the value of the whole estate, unless he proves he did not know of that debt (s. 128). An heir who hid assets or a debt is liable up to the whole estate (s. 129). A creditor who ignored the invitation without justification can claim from each heir only a proportional part, and only within two years (s. 132(ב)). Among themselves heirs bear debts in proportion to their shares (s. 134).

### Distribution: ss. 107–117

The rest is divided after debts and maintenance (s. 107(א)), not before the creditors' period ends (s. 107(ב)), by agreement or court order (s. 110). Without agreement, assets are divided in kind as far as possible, considering use and sentimental value (s. 112); an indivisible asset goes to the heir offering the highest price, not below market price, and otherwise is sold (s. 113); a farm unit goes to the heir able to run it, the spouse having priority (s. 114).

### The spouse and household: ss. 108, 115

> (א) מי שערב מות המוריש היה גר עמו בדירתו [...] רשאי להוסיף ולגור בה שלושה חדשים, ואם היה יורש – ששה חדשים לאחר מות המוריש [...]

*Gist:* Anyone who lived with the deceased in his home may stay three months after the death, an heir six months, and use the household movables. [s. 108](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הירושה#סעיף_108)

A spouse, children and parents who lived with the deceased in an apartment he owned and lived in may continue to live there as tenants of the heirs who receive it; rent and terms are agreed or set by the court (s. 115(א)). The court may limit this to those with no other home, or to part of the apartment including the kitchen and service rooms (s. 115(ב)).

### Maintenance from the estate: ss. 56–65

> הניח המוריש בן־זוג, ילדים או הורים והם זקוקים למזונות, זכאים הם למזונות מן העזבון לפי הוראות פרק זה, בין בירושה על פי דין ובין בירושה על פי צוואה.

*Gist:* A spouse, children or parents in need of maintenance are entitled to maintenance from the estate, whether the estate passes by law or by will. [s. 56](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הירושה#סעיף_56)

The spouse for as long as he or she remains widowed; children until 18 (disabled children while disabled); adult children up to 23 where the court sees fit; parents the deceased supported, for life (s. 57(א)). A cohabitant is entitled as if married (s. 57(ג)). The court weighs the estate's value, what the claimant receives from it, the standard of living, the claimant's assets and income, and the ketubah (s. 59). The application must be filed before distribution, or within six months after it if circumstances justify (s. 60(א)). Gifts without fair consideration in the two years before death may be added back if the estate is not enough (s. 63). A will clause denying or limiting maintenance is void, and a waiver made during the deceased's life is void (s. 65).

## Leading cases

### Key text: the structure of liability in ss. 126–129

Chapter 6 rewards heirs who follow the procedure. If you invite creditors and pay the known debts before dividing, your exposure is capped at what you received and only for debts you knew of (s. 127). If you skip the invitation, your exposure rises to the value of the whole estate (s. 128). The design pushes families to publish the invitation even when no administrator is appointed (s. 123). Why it matters: the modest cost of a public notice can prevent an exposure equal to the whole estate.

### Key text: maintenance as the only "forced" claim

Israel has no reserved share for children or spouses; s. 27 protects the freedom to make any will. Sections 56–65 are the counterweight, but they depend on need and are measured against the estate. Supporters see this as respecting autonomy while preventing destitution; critics, especially from religious-law and European perspectives, argue that it leaves adult children and spouses who are not needy without protection against arbitrary disinheritance. When you plan your own will, remember that a needy spouse or minor child you exclude may still claim.

## How it works in practice

### A typical informal administration (no administrator)

1. Funeral and tombstone paid (from the estate or reimbursed to whoever paid).
2. Succession or probate order obtained (lesson 26.6).
3. Banks release accounts to heirs according to the order (digital orders are sent to banks directly, Kol-Zchut ⚠️ manual check).
4. Heirs list assets and debts in a shared spreadsheet; publish an invitation to creditors if any doubt exists (s. 123).
5. Heirs sign a distribution agreement (s. 110); land is registered under the order or the agreement (lesson [26.7](lesson-07.md)).
6. Optional: file a distribution inventory (s. 125 makes s. 118 optional for heirs), which is prima facie evidence of what each received (s. 127(ב)).

### When to ask for an administrator

- The heirs cannot agree, one heir controls the assets, or someone is abroad and unreachable.
- There is a business, rental property, a lawsuit or significant debts.
- The estate may be insolvent.

Consent applications go to the Registrar, others to the Family Court; the fee is in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

## Worked example

Nadia Saleh of Nazareth died in 2026 without a will. Heirs: her husband Karim (married 20 years, they lived in her apartment) and three adult children. Estate: the apartment (₪1,200,000), a savings account (₪90,000), a car, and a credit-card debt of ₪25,000; a bank loan secured on the apartment of ₪150,000. The funeral cost ₪18,000, paid by her son Amir. Karim works part-time and has a small pension.

1. **Property relations first.** The apartment was hers before the marriage (excluded from balance); the savings were made during the marriage, so Karim's half (₪45,000) is his.
2. **Debts in order (s. 104).** Funeral: Amir is reimbursed ₪18,000 first. Order costs. The credit card ₪25,000. The secured loan is collected from the apartment's security (s. 101), so it reduces the apartment's net value.
3. **Remaining cash.** ₪45,000 − 18,000 − order costs − 25,000 ≈ ₪1,500. The heirs publish a creditors' notice (s. 123) because Nadia had a small business.
4. **Inheritance.** Karim takes the car (s. 11(א)) and half the rest; the children share the other half (s. 13).
5. **The home.** The children own half the apartment between them. Karim may stay six months free (s. 108) and then as their tenant under s. 115; the court may set the rent, or confine him to part of the flat. If the children want to sell, the co-owner rules of [13.5](../module-13/lesson-05.md) meet s. 115.
6. **Maintenance.** If Karim needs support, he may apply for maintenance from the estate before distribution (ss. 56–57, 60), even though he is also an heir; the court counts what he inherits (s. 59(2)).

## Do it yourself

### You need

- The Saleh case or an estate in your own family (dry run).
- A spreadsheet with columns: asset or debt, value, secured?, priority (s. 104), who holds it.
- 50 minutes.

### Steps

1. Separate the spouse's own property (18.4).
2. List debts and rank them under s. 104; mark secured debts (s. 101).
3. Decide: heirs administer, or an administrator? Write the reason.
4. Draft the text of a creditors' invitation (who, deadline of at least three months, address).
5. Allocate assets in kind under s. 112; apply s. 113 to the indivisible asset.
6. Write the surviving spouse's housing position under ss. 108 and 115.
7. Decide whether anyone has a maintenance claim (ss. 56–59) and the deadline (s. 60).

None.

### How you know it worked

- Debts are paid in the statutory order and before distribution.
- You can state each heir's maximum personal exposure under s. 127 or s. 128.
- The spouse's housing rights are stated with dates.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes, in a simple solvent estate: heirs may administer and divide by agreement (ss. 121–125), publish a creditors' notice, and apply by consent to the Registrar for an administrator. A maintenance claim from the estate is a Family Court claim that a dependant may file in person. Acting for the other heirs as their representative, for pay or as a business, is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).

### Where and how

- Creditors' notice: a public notice (newspaper or another public method), with a period of at least three months (ss. 99, 123).
- Administrator by consent: the Registrar of Inheritance (s. 78(ב); reg. 31); otherwise the Family Court.
- Maintenance from the estate: a claim to the Family Court (reg. 28), before distribution.
- Free help: Legal Aid; the court assistance units (18.9).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- Debts may exceed assets, or the tax authority or a bank claims against the estate.
- One heir controls bank accounts or the business and will not account.
- There is a farm, a business or rental income.
- A dependant claims maintenance against a will.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dividing the money without a creditors' notice | Each heir liable up to the whole estate's value (s. 128) | Pay the debt and seek contribution (s. 134) | Publish a notice (s. 123) and pay known debts first |
| One heir sells the car or empties the account | Breach of s. 122; liability | Demand account; court instructions | Agree in writing who does what |
| Telling the widow to leave immediately | Unlawful under s. 108 | Agree terms or ask the court | Know the 3/6-month and s. 115 rights |
| Missing the maintenance deadline | Claim lost after distribution (six-month grace) | Apply within six months citing circumstances | File before distribution |
| Administrator sells the flat without approval | Act invalid against the estate (s. 97) | Seek retroactive approval | Ask the court first |

## Review

- Heirs administer by agreement unless an administrator is appointed; an administrator needs court approval for major acts (ss. 97, 121–122).
- Debts are paid in the order of s. 104 and before distribution; secured debts are collected from the security.
- Before distribution heirs answer only with estate assets; afterwards their exposure depends on whether creditors were invited (ss. 126–129).
- Assets are divided in kind where possible, indivisible assets go to the highest-bidding heir or are sold (ss. 112–113).
- The spouse and household may stay in the home (ss. 108, 115); needy spouses, children and parents may claim maintenance even against a will (ss. 56–65).
