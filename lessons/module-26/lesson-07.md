# 26.7 · Inheritance of land and the enduring power of attorney

By the end of this lesson you will be able to take a succession or probate order to the Land Registry and get the heirs registered, and to plan the other half of estate planning: who will manage your property and personal affairs if you lose capacity during your life, using an enduring power of attorney (ייפוי כוח מתמשך) and advance directives.

## Why it matters

The order is not the end for land. Until the heirs are registered, the extract still shows the deceased as owner ([13.2](../module-13/lesson-02.md), [13.9](../module-13/lesson-09.md)), and a sale, mortgage or division among heirs gets stuck. Thousands of flats in Israel are registered to people who died decades ago, and the problem grows with every generation.

The second half of the lesson deals with the years before death. A will speaks only on death; it does nothing when a parent with dementia can no longer sign bank forms. Until 2016 the main answer was a court-appointed guardian (אפוטרופוס), often a relative but sometimes a professional, with the person losing control over who decides. Amendment 18 to the Capacity and Guardianship Law (2016, in force 2017) created the enduring power of attorney and advance directives, so people choose in advance who will act and how. The policy aims at autonomy and dignity; critics warn that a private document, activated by the attorney's own declaration, may be abused with less supervision than guardianship. The statute answers with deposit, notification of "informed persons", reporting, and prior approval for major property acts.

## The law

### Registering heirs in land

Inheritance by will is not a "transaction" under the Land Law (s. 6; [13.3](../module-13/lesson-03.md)), so no deed of transfer is needed: the heirs apply to register the inheritance. Registration of inheritance includes registering a succession or probate order, an estate-distribution agreement under s. 110, a distribution order under s. 111, or an order appointing an administrator (Land Management and Registration Regulations reg. 17(א)).

> (ב) רישום ירושה ייערך על פי בקשה של מעוניין בדבר; על אף האמור, רישום הסכם חלוקת עיזבון כאמור בתקנת משנה (א) שאינו מאושר בידי בית משפט ייערך על פי בקשת כל היורשים.

*Gist:* Inheritance is registered on the application of any interested person, but a distribution agreement not approved by a court is registered only on the application of all the heirs. [reg. 17(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_המקרקעין_(ניהול_ורישום))

The application is filed at the registry office where the land is registered, with the order, distribution order or agreement attached if it was given or approved by a court, religious court or the Registrar (reg. 18(א)–(ב)); if an administrator was appointed, the administrator files or consents (reg. 18(ג)). The fee for registering an inheritance is in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md) (Land Fees Regulations, Schedule item 3).

Before distribution, an heir may transfer or charge his share in the estate by a written agreement, but not his right in a specific asset (Succession Law s. 7(א), (ד)).

### The enduring power of attorney: who may grant and who may act

> (א) אדם בגיר שהוא בעל כשירות רשאי, בייפוי כוח מתמשך שנתן לפי הוראות פרק זה [...] למנות אדם אחר, אחד או יותר, שיהיה מוסמך לפעול בשמו [...]

*Gist:* An adult with capacity may appoint one or more persons to act for him, in personal matters, property matters, or both, as specified in the document. [s. 32ב](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הכשרות_המשפטית_והאפוטרופסות#סעיף_32ב)

The attorney must be at least 18, not under guardianship, and for property not bankrupt without discharge or a severely restricted bank customer; not a paid carer, medical or social provider, or paid housing provider (unless a relative); and not the lawyer before whom the document is signed. A non-relative may not be attorney for more than three people (s. 32ג).

### How it is made: ss. 32יג–32יד and the regulations

> (א) ייפוי כוח מתמשך ייחתם בידי הממנה לפני עורך דין שעבר הכשרה לעניין עריכת ייפוי כוח מתמשך, כפי שתיקבע בתקנות, ואין לו עניין אישי בייפוי הכוח.

*Gist:* The document is signed by the grantor before a lawyer who has completed the prescribed training and has no personal interest in it. [s. 32יד(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הכשרות_המשפטית_והאפוטרופסות#סעיף_32יד)

The Administrator General runs the training (at least twelve academic hours), issues a certificate, and keeps a public database of trained lawyers (EPA Regulations reg. 19). The lawyer explains, in plain language and without the attorney present, the legal meaning, the alternatives (guardianship, advance directives under s. 35א), entry into force, reporting and revocation, and certifies free and informed consent (ss. 32יג(ד), 32יד(ג), (ה)). The document is prepared on the prescribed forms online (EPA Regulations reg. 2). The attorney's written consent, signed before a lawyer, is attached (s. 32יד(ד)). A medical-only power may instead be signed before a doctor, social worker, psychologist or registered nurse (s. 32טו).

### Deposit and entry into force: ss. 32יז–32יט

> (א) ייפוי כוח מתמשך יופקד אצל האפוטרופוס הכללי [...] הפקדת ייפוי הכוח היא תנאי מוקדם לכניסתו לתוקף.

*Gist:* The power is deposited with the Administrator General, by the grantor or the lawyer; deposit is a precondition for its entry into force; the last deposited power on a matter prevails. [s. 32יז](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הכשרות_המשפטית_והאפוטרופסות#סעיף_32יז)

It enters into force when the grantor stops being able to understand the matter, once the attorney files a declaration with the Administrator General that the conditions are met and the informed persons were notified (s. 32יט(א)); the grantor may set the test (for example an expert opinion), but not leave it to the attorney's decision alone (s. 32יט(ג)). A property power may be set to take effect at once, operating as an ordinary agency until incapacity (s. 32יט(ב)). Entry into force does not by itself remove the grantor's legal capacity (s. 32כ). The grantor may revoke by written notice while capable (s. 32כג).

### Limits on the attorney: s. 32ו and s. 74א

The attorney may never make a will for the grantor, name insurance beneficiaries, convert religion, vote, consent to adoption, or act under the Dying Patient Law (s. 32ו(ב)). Gifts, donations and loans to named people and larger transactions need express authority in the document (s. 32ו(ג)). Transfers of an apartment or business, most land transactions, renouncing an inheritance share, guarantees, and acts above the statutory thresholds need prior approval (s. 32ו(ד)); since the 2024 amendment that request goes first to the Administrator General, who approves or sends it to the court (s. 74א).

### Advance directives: ss. 32לא and 35א

A grantor may include advance directives in the power, which the attorney must follow unless impossible, illegal or seriously harmful, or unless the grantor, still able to understand, wants otherwise (s. 32לא). A person who prefers a court-appointed guardian may instead sign advance directives naming the desired guardian and stating wishes, before a lawyer, and deposit them; the court then appoints that person unless serious harm would result (s. 35א).

## Leading cases

### Key text: Amendment 18 and the shift from guardianship to choice

Amendment 18 (Capacity and Guardianship Law, 2016) added chapter 2-1 on enduring powers and advance directives and supported decision-making, and reframed guardianship as a last resort (s. 33א). Hebrew Wikipedia's article on the Administrator General describes the amendment as a central reform placing the person's will at the centre ([האפוטרופוס הכללי והממונה על ענייני ירושה](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/האפוטרופוס_הכללי_והממונה_על_ענייני_ירושה), ⚠️ secondary). Why it matters: the law now presumes that a person who planned ahead is governed by his own choices, and courts must respect deposited directives (s. 35א(ד)).

### Key text: s. 74א and the 2024–2025 approval reform

Section 74א, added in 2024 (Hebrew Wikipedia dates the reform's entry into force to August 2025, ⚠️ secondary), routes requests to approve major property acts by guardians and attorneys to the Administrator General first; requests that the grantor opposes, that contradict deposited directives, or that concern a minor's sole apartment go to the court (s. 74א(ד)). Why it matters: an attorney who wants to sell a parent's flat to pay for care files with the Administrator General, expects an answer within 60 days (s. 74א(ו)), and must state the grantor's position.

## How it works in practice

### Registering heirs: the two routes

| Route | Who | How |
|---|---|---|
| With the online application for the order | The applicant | Tick the request to register in the Land Registry and attach the land-registration request form, signed and verified; the fee is paid online (Kol-Zchut, ⚠️ manual check) |
| Separately after the order | Any heir; the administrator if one was appointed | Land-registration request form signed by at least one heir and verified by a lawyer or a registry official, filed by post or in person at the office where the land is registered; if the Registrar gave the order, only the deceased's details are needed (Kol-Zchut, ⚠️ manual check) |

Online filing at the Land Registry itself is limited to lawyers with a smart card. Israel Land Authority leasehold land is handled by the ILA, not the registry. After registration the heirs are co-owners ([13.5](../module-13/lesson-05.md)); a later distribution agreement among them is registered on the application of all heirs (reg. 17(ב)), and a transfer between heirs for consideration may be a taxable sale (module 25).

### Planning an enduring power of attorney

1. Decide the scope: property, personal, medical, or all; one attorney or two jointly; a substitute (s. 32ה).
2. Choose informed persons who will be told when it takes effect and receive reports (ss. 32יג(ז), 32טז); they should not be the attorney's relatives unless the attorney is your relative.
3. Decide how incapacity will be determined and whether the property power starts now.
4. Write advance directives: where you want to live, care preferences, which assets may be sold first, gifts to grandchildren.
5. Find a trained lawyer in the Administrator General's public database; the lawyer prepares the forms online and deposits them, or you deposit them yourself (EPA Regulations reg. 6). Lawyers' fees are not set by statute; ask in advance (⚠️ check whether a deposit fee applies, s. 32יז(ה)).

## Worked example

Part A. Shmuel Katz died in 2025; the Registrar gave a succession order in March 2026: his wife Rina ½, his daughters Michal and Yael ¼ each. The registry extract still shows Shmuel as the sole owner of the flat in Rehovot. Michal files the land-registration request, signed by her and verified by the registry clerk, with the deceased's ID and the fee; because the Registrar gave the order, it is located automatically. The extract now shows Rina ½, Michal ¼, Yael ¼. In 2027 the daughters agree to give Rina the flat in exchange for Shmuel's savings: an estate-distribution agreement registered on the application of all three heirs (reg. 17(ב)).

Part B. Rina, 76, decides to plan ahead. She signs, before a lawyer trained by the Administrator General, an enduring power of attorney for property and personal matters, naming Yael as attorney and Michal as substitute, and her cousin as the informed person. She sets entry into force on a geriatrician's opinion that she can no longer manage her affairs. Advance directives: she wants to stay home with a carer as long as possible; the flat may be rented out, and sold only if her savings run out. The lawyer deposits the document with the Administrator General. Three years later, after a stroke, Yael files the declaration with the geriatrician's opinion; the Administrator General confirms entry into force and notifies Rina and the cousin. When Yael later wants to sell the flat, she must apply under s. 74א; her application must state Rina's position and cannot contradict the directive unless the savings have indeed run out.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- An extract for a property in your family (13.9) or the Katz case.
- A list of your own assets and the people you trust (for a dry-run plan; nothing is signed).
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. From the extract, identify whether a registered owner has died and whether an order exists.
2. Write the registration plan: who applies, which form, who verifies the signature, where, fee (link current numbers).
3. If heirs will divide differently from the order, write who must sign the distribution agreement.
4. Fill a dry-run enduring-power plan: scope, attorney, substitute, informed person, entry-into-force test.
5. Write three advance directives in your own words.
6. Mark which of your planned instructions need express authority (s. 32ו(ג)) or prior approval (s. 32ו(ד)).

### How you know it worked

- Your registration plan names the office and the verifying person.
- Your power-of-attorney plan has no attorney who is disqualified under s. 32ג.
- You know which decisions your attorney could never take (a will, voting).

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Land: partly. An heir may file the registration request in person or by post, signing before a registry official instead of a lawyer; online filing at the registry is for lawyers only ([can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md)).

Enduring power of attorney: no, not alone. The law requires signature before a lawyer trained by the Administrator General (s. 32יד(א)); only a medical-only power may be signed before a doctor, social worker, psychologist or registered nurse (s. 32טו). You can, however, prepare all your decisions in advance so that the meeting is short and the document is yours.

### Where and how

- Land Registry office where the property is registered; appointment needed for in-person service (Kol-Zchut, ⚠️ manual check); fee in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Trained lawyers: the Administrator General's public database (reg. 19(ד)).
- Deposit and information: the Administrator General, online or in person (EPA Regulations regs. 6, 13).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The property is ILA leasehold, unregistered, or in a building not yet registered as a condominium.
- Heirs want to exchange assets (possible tax on the exchange) or one heir refuses to sign.
- There are signs of pressure on an elderly person to sign a power of attorney or a gift.
- You want the power to cover a business, assets abroad, or psychiatric treatment against your objection (s. 32ו(ג)(2)).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Never registering the inheritance | Sale or mortgage blocked; next generation inherits the problem | Register now, possibly through several orders | Register with the order application |
| Registering a distribution agreement signed by some heirs | Refused (reg. 17(ב)) | Get all signatures or a court approval | Sign together |
| An ordinary power of attorney for an elderly parent | It ends when the parent loses capacity (agency law) | Guardianship proceedings | Make an enduring power while capable |
| Attorney decides alone that the grantor is incapable | Not allowed as the sole test (s. 32יט(ג)) | Expert opinion | Set an objective test in the document |
| Attorney sells the flat without approval | Act needs prior approval (s. 32ו(ד)) | Apply under s. 74א | Plan the sale with the Administrator General |

## Review

- Heirs register land by an inheritance-registration request at the registry office; a distribution agreement not approved by a court needs every heir's application (reg. 17).
- An enduring power is granted by a capable adult, signed before a lawyer trained by the Administrator General, and deposited with the Administrator General as a condition of entry into force.
- It comes into force when the grantor can no longer understand the matter, on the attorney's declaration and notice to informed persons; it does not remove capacity by itself.
- Some acts are never allowed (a will, voting), some need express authority, and major property acts need prior approval through the Administrator General (s. 74א).
- Advance directives bind the attorney (s. 32לא) or the court choosing a guardian (s. 35א), within limits.
