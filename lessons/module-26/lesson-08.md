# 26.8 · Practice lab: write your own will and deposit it

In this lab you will plan, write by hand and check your own will, and then, if you choose, deposit it with the Registrar of Inheritance; by the end you will hold either a valid will and a deposit certificate or a complete draft ready to copy out.

## Why it matters

A handwritten will costs nothing, needs no witnesses and can be made today. It is also the form most often challenged, because no one else saw it being made. This lab turns lessons [26.2](lesson-02.md)–[26.4](lesson-04.md) into a document that does what you want and is hard to attack, and closes the gap that defeats many genuine wills: the original cannot be found.

## The law

> צוואה בכתב יד תיכתב כולה ביד המצווה, תישא תאריך כתוב בידו ותיחתם בידו.

*Gist:* Write all of it, date it and sign it, by your own hand. [s. 19](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הירושה#סעיף_19)

> (א) מי שעשה צוואה בכתב יד או בעדים רשאי להפקידה אצל רשם לעניני ירושה; ההפקדה תהיה במסירת הצוואה על ידי המצווה עצמו לרשם לעניני ירושה.

*Gist:* You may deposit your handwritten or witnessed will with the Registrar, in person, by handing it over yourself. [s. 21(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הירושה#סעיף_21)

Also used in the lab: revocation by a later will (s. 36), substitute heirs (s. 41), the executor (s. 81), the drafter-beneficiary rule (s. 35) and interpretation (s. 54). Deposit procedure: in person with scanning and a sealed envelope (Succession Regulations reg. 6); remotely through the Administrator General's site with the original delivered within 45 days (reg. 6ב).

## Leading cases

### Key text: ד״נ 40/80 קניג נ׳ כהן (recap)

A genuine handwritten document failed because it lacked a date and signature ([26.2](lesson-02.md)). Today s. 25 can sometimes save such a will, but only if no one raises a doubt. The lab's checklist exists so your will never needs s. 25.

## How it works in practice

- **Paper and pen:** plain paper, a permanent pen, no correction fluid. If you make a mistake, start again rather than cross out.
- **Language:** any language you write well. A will in a language other than Hebrew, Arabic or English needs a notarised translation when probated (Kol-Zchut, ⚠️ manual check). This lab uses Hebrew.
- **Pages:** number them ("עמוד 1 מתוך 2") and sign each page.
- **Deposit:** book an appointment at a Registrar's office, bring the original and ID with a current photo, pay the fee if it applies, and receive a certificate. The fee is waived for a first deposit or if you have not deposited in the last five years ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)). Only you can deposit and only you can take it back.

## Worked example

Yael Cohen, 41, of Kiryat Shmona, divorced, two children aged 9 and 13, owns half a flat with her sister and has savings. She wants: everything to her children equally; if one dies before her, that child's share to his children or, if none, to the other child; her sister to manage the estate until the younger child turns 25; her grandmother's ring to her niece.

She writes the will by hand from the model below, adding a clause on the ring (a specific gift, s. 40(2)), a substitute clause (s. 41), and an executor clause naming her sister with the sister's agreement (ss. 80–81). She adds a guardian wish for the children (a separate personal letter, since guardianship of minors is decided under the Capacity and Guardianship Law, not by the will alone). She checks: no beneficiary helped write it; the date and signature are in her hand; the pages are numbered and signed. She books an appointment at the nearest Registrar of Inheritance office, deposits it free (first deposit), and photographs the certificate for her sister.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. Write only your own will; do not write a will for anyone else.

### You need

- Plain paper, a pen, a quiet hour alone.
- A list of your assets and debts (the worksheet in the template).
- Your ID card; for remote deposit, a scanner or phone camera and access to the government personal area.
- 150 minutes: planning 45, writing 45, checking 20, deposit 40 (travel not included).

### Steps

**Part A: plan (45 minutes)**

1. List what you own: real estate (with gush/helka from an extract, [13.9](../module-13/lesson-09.md)), accounts, pension and insurance (check named beneficiaries, which pass outside the will, s. 147), vehicles, valuables, digital assets.
2. List your heirs by law and their shares under [26.1](lesson-01.md); decide where you want to differ.
3. Choose beneficiaries and shares; for each, a substitute if they die before you (s. 41).
4. Decide whether to name an executor; ask that person.
5. Check the spouse's rights that the will cannot remove (property relations, maintenance, ss. 108 and 115; [26.5](lesson-05.md)).
6. If you exclude a close relative, give the estate positively to others and consider a short explanation (lesson 26.4).

**Part B: write (45 minutes)**

7. Copy the model text below by hand, adapting it. Write every word yourself, including the date.

```text
צוואה

אני הח״מ, ______________ (שם מלא), ת״ז ______________,
מרחוב ______________, עיר ______________,
בהיותי בדעה צלולה ומבין/ה היטב את טיבה של צוואה, מרצוני החופשי
ובלי שהופעלו עליי לחץ, איום או השפעה כלשהם, כותב/ת בזאת בכתב ידי
את צוואתי זו.

1. אני מבטל/ת בזאת כל צוואה או הוראה שבצוואה שעשיתי לפני היום.

2. מעיזבוני ישולמו תחילה הוצאות הקבורה והמצבה וכל חובותיי.

3. את כל רכושי, מכל מין וסוג, בישראל ומחוצה לה, לרבות זכויותיי
   בדירה הרשומה בגוש ______ חלקה ______ תת־חלקה ______,
   אני מצווה ל: ______________ (ת״ז ______) ול: ______________
   (ת״ז ______), בחלקים שווים ביניהם.

4. אם מי מהזוכים לפי סעיף 3 ילך לעולמו לפניי, יבוא במקומו צאצאיו
   בחלקים שווים, ואם לא הותיר צאצאים – יתווסף חלקו לחלקם של שאר
   הזוכים לפי סעיף 3.

5. את ______________ (נכס מסוים) אני מצווה ל: ______________.

6. אני ממנה את ______________ (ת״ז ______) למנהל/ת עיזבוני
   ולמבצע/ת צוואתי זו.

ולראיה באתי על החתום בכתב ידי,
היום ______ בחודש ______ שנת ______, בעיר ______________.

חתימה: ______________
                                        עמוד ___ מתוך ___
```

8. Do not add witnesses, a lawyer's stamp or anyone else's handwriting. Do not let a beneficiary write or dictate any part.

**Part C: check (20 minutes)**

9. Run the checklist in the template: whole text in your hand; date in your hand; signature at the end and on every page; clear identification of people (ID numbers) and assets; substitutes; executor's consent; no clause benefiting anyone who helped; no condition that is illegal or immoral (s. 34); no gift that depends on someone else's choice (ss. 28–29).
10. Read it as a stranger would (s. 54): could anyone understand it differently? Rewrite if so.

**Part D: deposit (40 minutes, optional but recommended)**

11. In person: book an appointment at a Registrar of Inheritance office, bring the original and your ID; the will is scanned, sealed in a signed envelope and recorded; you receive a deposit certificate (reg. 6).
12. Remotely: log into the government personal area, upload a colour scan, pay any fee, receive the deposit number, and deliver the original within 45 days by post or the drop box at a Registrar's office with a cover letter giving the deposit number (reg. 6ב; Kol-Zchut ⚠️ manual check).
13. Tell your executor where the certificate is (not the content, unless you want to).

### How you know it worked

- Every requirement of s. 19 is met and you can point to it on the page.
- Every beneficiary and asset is identified so that a stranger could apply for a probate order.
- You hold a deposit certificate, or a dated plan for depositing.

### Self-check

- [ ] Whole will, date and signature are in my handwriting.
- [ ] I revoked earlier wills expressly.
- [ ] Each gift has a substitute.
- [ ] No helper or witness benefits.
- [ ] The original is deposited or in a place my executor knows.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. A person may write and deposit his or her own will; that is the purpose of s. 19 and s. 21. Writing a will for someone else is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20) and voids any gift to the writer (s. 35).

### Where and how

- Deposit at any Registrar of Inheritance office by appointment, or remotely through the Administrator General's personal-area service.
- Fee and exemption: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Service centre: \*2416 (⚠️ manual check).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- Your capacity may later be questioned; consider a will before an authority (s. 22) or a lawyer-drafted witnessed will.
- You own a business, foreign assets, a farm or ILA leasehold land with special rules.
- You want mutual wills, successive heirs, trusts or conditions.
- You have a former spouse, a cohabitant or children from different relationships.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Typing the will | Not a will | Rewrite by hand | Handwrite everything |
| Date written by someone else | Needs s. 25 | Rewrite and date | Date it yourself, in full |
| Leaving the only original in a drawer | Lost or destroyed | Prove a copy (s. 68(ב)), uncertain | Deposit it |
| Naming a beneficiary as executor without substitutes | Gaps if they die first | Add a substitute | Substitute clauses for every gift and role |
| Forgetting pension and insurance beneficiaries | The will does not reach them (s. 147) | Update with the fund or insurer | Check named beneficiaries each year |

## Review

- A handwritten will must be wholly written, dated and signed by you (s. 19).
- Revoke earlier wills expressly, name substitutes, identify people and assets clearly.
- No one who helps you write it should benefit (s. 35).
- Deposit gives proof and safekeeping; only you can deposit or withdraw it (s. 21).
- Review the will after every major life event; a new will revokes conflicting old provisions (s. 36(ב)).
