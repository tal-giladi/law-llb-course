# 26.2 · Wills: the four forms and their requirements

By the end of this lesson you will be able to tell a valid handwritten, witnessed, authority or oral will from a document that only looks like one, check each requirement against the statute, and decide whether a defect can be cured by the Registrar or the court under s. 25.

## Why it matters

A will is the only way to change the statutory scheme of lesson [26.1](lesson-01.md), and the Succession Law insists on form. Form protects the testator, who is no longer there to explain, and protects the family against forgery and pressure. But strict form also defeats real wishes: a missing date or a misplaced signature used to void wills that everyone knew were genuine. Since 1985, and especially since Amendment 11 in 2004, the law has tried to hold both values: four fixed forms, and a curing provision (s. 25) that saves a will if its "basic elements" are present and there is no doubt it reflects the testator's free and true wishes.

The debate between formalism and intention runs through the case law. Formalists argue that bright-line rules prevent litigation and protect the vulnerable; intention-based judges argue that the law exists to carry out the dead person's wishes, not to punish clumsy drafting. Knowing where the line sits today tells you how to write a will that never needs s. 25.

## The law

### Four forms: s. 18

> צוואה נעשית בכתב יד, בעדים, בפני רשות או בעל־פה.

*Gist:* A will is made in handwriting, before witnesses, before an authority, or orally. [s. 18](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הירושה#סעיף_18)

### Handwritten: s. 19

> צוואה בכתב יד תיכתב כולה ביד המצווה, תישא תאריך כתוב בידו ותיחתם בידו.

*Gist:* The whole will is written by the testator's own hand, dated in that hand and signed by it. [s. 19](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הירושה#סעיף_19)

No witnesses, no lawyer, no cost. A typed or printed text, or one written by someone else at dictation, is not a handwritten will.

### Witnessed: s. 20

> צוואה בעדים תהיה בכתב, תצויין בתאריך ותיחתם ביד המצווה בפני שני עדים לאחר שהצהיר בפניהם שזו צוואתו; העדים יאשרו באותו מעמד בחתימת ידם על פני הצוואה שהמצווה הצהיר וחתם כאמור.

*Gist:* A written, dated will signed by the testator in front of two witnesses after declaring that it is the will; the witnesses sign on the will, at the same occasion, confirming the declaration and signature. [s. 20](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הירושה#סעיף_20)

The text may be typed. A minor or a person declared legally incompetent may not be a witness (s. 24). A witness and the witness's spouse must not benefit (s. 35, lesson [26.4](lesson-04.md)).

### Before an authority: s. 22

The testator states the will orally before a judge, a court registrar, a Registrar of Inheritance or a member of a religious court, or hands them the written text personally; the text is read to the testator (or read by the testator), who declares that it is the will, and the official confirms this on the will (s. 22(א)–(ב), (ד)). A notary counts as a judge for this purpose (s. 22(ז)). Such a will is prima facie evidence that the named person made it on the stated day and place (s. 22(ו)). The procedure starts with a written request to the authority (Succession Regulations reg. 7). [s. 22](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הירושה#סעיף_22)

### Oral: s. 23

> שכיב מרע וכן מי שרואה עצמו, בנסיבות המצדיקות זאת, מול פני המוות, רשאי לצוות בעל־פה בפני שני עדים השומעים לשונו.

*Gist:* A person on the deathbed, or who reasonably sees himself facing death, may make an oral will before two witnesses who understand his language. [s. 23(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הירושה#סעיף_23)

The witnesses write a memorandum with the date and circumstances, sign it and deposit it with the Registrar as soon as possible (s. 23(ב)). The oral will lapses one month after the circumstances that justified it end if the testator is still alive (s. 23(ג)).

### Deposit: s. 21

A handwritten or witnessed will may be deposited with a Registrar of Inheritance, only by the testator in person (s. 21(א)); a deposited will kept until death is prima facie evidence that the named person made it, at the latest on the deposit date (s. 21(ב)); the testator may take it back at any time (s. 21(ג)). A will made before an authority may also be deposited (s. 22(ה)).

### Curing defects: s. 25

> (א) התקיימו מרכיבי היסוד בצוואה, ולא היה לרשם לענייני ירושה או לבית המשפט, לפי העניין ספק כי היא משקפת את רצונו החופשי והאמיתי של המצווה, רשאי הוא, בהחלטה מנומקת, לקיימה אף אם נפל פגם בפרט מן הפרטים או בהליך מן ההליכים המפורטים בסעיפים 19, 20, 22, או 23 או בכשרות העדים, או בהעדר פרט מן הפרטים או הליך מן ההליכים כאמור.

*Gist:* If the basic elements are present and the Registrar or court has no doubt that the will reflects the testator's free and true wishes, it may uphold the will despite a defect in, or absence of, a detail or procedure of the form, or a witness's incapacity, by a reasoned decision. [s. 25](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הירושה#סעיף_25)

The basic elements (s. 25(ב)): for a handwritten will, the whole will is in the testator's handwriting; for a witnessed will, it is in writing and the testator brought it before two witnesses; for an authority will, it was stated to or handed to the authority by the testator personally; for an oral will, the testator stated it personally before two witnesses who understood him, while on the deathbed or facing death. Since the 2023 amendment the provision names the Registrar as well as the court. A reasoned decision is required when a will is upheld despite a missing signature or date (Succession Regulations reg. 26).

### No probate, no will: s. 39

No rights may be claimed under a will, and it may not be relied on as a will, until a probate order is given (s. 39; lesson [26.6](lesson-06.md)).

## Leading cases

### ד״נ 40/80 קניג נ׳ כהן, פ״ד לו(3)

A young woman took her own life and left a handwritten document excluding her husband from her property. It was in her handwriting but lacked a date and signature. In a further hearing the Supreme Court held, with a majority applying the formal rules as they then stood, that the document could not be upheld as a will, although no one doubted it reflected her wishes. Why it matters: the case became the standard illustration of formalism defeating intention, and is credited with driving the amendments that culminated in the 2004 "basic elements" test of s. 25 (Amendment 11). Summary and citation from [Hebrew Wikipedia: צוואה](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/צוואה) (⚠️ secondary; full text not reached). Today the same document, wholly in her handwriting, has its basic element, and the Registrar or court could uphold it if there is no doubt about free and true wishes.

### Key text: the two-step s. 25 test

The current s. 25 is structured as a gate and a discretion. The gate is the basic element of the chosen form: a typed, unsigned document with no witnesses has no form at all and cannot be cured as a handwritten will. The discretion follows: even with the basic element present, any doubt about free and true wishes defeats curing. Why it matters: the more steps of the form you complete, the less you depend on a judge's certainty.

## How it works in practice

### Choosing the form

| Form | Cost | Strength | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Handwritten | Free | Simple; private | Easiest to attack on capacity and authenticity; handwriting expert needed if disputed |
| Witnessed | Free to make (lawyers charge for drafting) | Two people confirm declaration and signature | Witness or spouse benefiting voids that gift (s. 35); witnesses must sign together with the testator |
| Before an authority | Fee before the Registrar of Inheritance (see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)); notaries charge regulated fees | Prima facie proof of making, date and place (s. 22(ו)) | Needs an appointment and a written request |
| Oral | Free | Only for emergencies | Lapses one month after the danger passes (s. 23(ג)); witnesses must record and deposit it |

### Deposit in practice

You come in person to a Registrar's office with the original and ID; the will is scanned, sealed in an envelope signed by you and the clerk, and recorded in the national register; you receive a deposit certificate (reg. 6). Remote deposit is possible through the Administrator General's personal area with a colour scan, followed by the original within 45 days (reg. 6ב). A will signed with an authority's certified electronic signature may be deposited online (reg. 6א). The deposit fee is waived for a first deposit or if no will was deposited in the previous five years; otherwise a small fee applies (see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)). Only one deposited will is held at a time: to deposit a new one you take the old one back (Kol-Zchut, ⚠️ manual check).

## Worked example

Four documents were found after deaths in 2026.

1. **Leah (Netanya)** wrote three pages by hand, signed each page, but wrote the date only on a typed cover note she attached. Form: handwritten. Defect: the date is not in her hand. Basic element (whole will in her handwriting): present, the cover note is not part of the will. If no one raises doubt about her wishes, the Registrar can uphold it under s. 25 with reasons.
2. **Ahmad (Umm al-Fahm)** had a lawyer type a will; he signed it in front of his neighbour and his nephew, who signed the next day in the lawyer's office. Form: witnessed. Defect: the witnesses did not sign "at the same occasion". Basic element (written, brought before two witnesses): present if he declared it before both. A court can cure it if the evidence leaves no doubt; the probate becomes a court case if anyone objects.
3. **Galina (Ashdod)** typed her will on a computer, printed it and signed it alone. Form: none. It is not handwritten (not in her hand) and there were no witnesses. No basic element: s. 25 cannot help, and intestacy applies.
4. **Yitzhak (Bnei Brak)**, after a heart attack, told two neighbours in the hospital corridor who should receive his savings; they wrote a memorandum the same evening and deposited it with the Registrar the next week. He died ten days later in hospital. Form: oral will, valid if the memorandum records the date and circumstances. Had he recovered and left hospital, it would have lapsed one month after the danger passed.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Four short sample documents you write yourself (see steps) or the four from the worked example.
- The text of ss. 18–25.
- 40 minutes.

### Steps

1. For each document, name the form it claims to be.
2. List every requirement of that form (the section's words) and tick or cross each.
3. For each cross, decide: is the basic element of s. 25(ב) present?
4. If yes, write one sentence on what might raise doubt about free and true wishes (who found it, who benefits, the testator's health).
5. Write the outcome: valid; curable under s. 25 if no doubt; not a will.
6. Choose which form you would use for yourself and why (cost, privacy, risk of challenge).

[Handwritten will: model text and checklist](../../templates/handwritten-will.md) (draft in lesson 26.8).

### How you know it worked

- Each outcome cites the section and the basic element.
- You did not try to cure a typed, unwitnessed document as a handwritten will.
- Your choice of form for yourself names one risk and how you would reduce it.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes, for your own will. Anyone may write a handwritten will or sign a witnessed will they prepared themselves, and may make a will before the Registrar of Inheritance or deposit their own will. Drafting a will for another person is legal work reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20), and a person who drafts a will in which he benefits loses that benefit (s. 35).

### Where and how

- Handwritten: at home; keep the original safe or deposit it.
- Before the Registrar: a written request to the Registrar's office, an appointment, and the fee; before a judge or a religious-court member by request to that court; before a notary by appointment.
- Deposit: in person at a Registrar's office, or remotely through the Administrator General's personal area; the fee and exemption are in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md). Service centre \*2416.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You want to disinherit a close relative, set conditions, or create successive heirs (lesson 26.4).
- Your capacity may be questioned (illness, medication, advanced age) or a beneficiary is helping you.
- You own assets abroad, a business or a farm, or have children from more than one relationship.
- You and your spouse want mutual wills with restrictions on revocation (s. 8א).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Typing a "handwritten" will and signing it | Not a will; no basic element | Make a new will in a valid form | Write every word by hand |
| Date written by another person or omitted | Must rely on s. 25 | Rewrite and date it yourself | Write the full date in your own hand |
| A beneficiary or the beneficiary's spouse as witness | That gift is void (s. 35) | Make a new will with independent witnesses | Use two adult, unrelated, non-benefiting witnesses |
| Witnesses signing on another day | Defect in procedure; dispute | s. 25 if the basic element is present | All three sign together after the declaration |
| Leaving the only original where an heir can destroy it | Will lost; dispute over copies | Seek to prove a copy (s. 68(ב)) | Deposit it with the Registrar |

## Review

- There are four forms: handwritten, witnessed, before an authority (including a notary) and oral (s. 18).
- A handwritten will is wholly written, dated and signed by the testator; a witnessed will is signed before two witnesses who sign at the same occasion.
- Defects can be cured under s. 25 only if the basic element of the form is present and there is no doubt about free and true wishes.
- Deposit with the Registrar is optional, personal and evidentially valuable (ss. 21, 22(ו)).
- A will has no effect until a probate order is given (s. 39).
