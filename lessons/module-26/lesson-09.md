# 26.9 · Practice lab: apply for a succession or probate order

In this lab you will build, step by step, the application an heir files with the Registrar of Inheritance, using a real estate in your family or the supplied case, and stop just before submission unless you are an heir in a real, uncontested estate and choose to file.

## Why it matters

The application is short, but rejections and delays come from small omissions: a missing notice to one heir, an unpaid publication charge, a will that was not delivered within seven days, or choosing the online route when the file must go on paper. A complete first filing usually means an order within weeks and digital delivery to the banks and the Land Registry.

## The law

> הוגשה בקשה לצו ירושה או לצו קיום, יודיע על כך הרשם לעניני ירושה ברבים ויקבע זמן מתאים שלא יפחת משבועיים להגשת התנגדויות [...]

*Gist:* Every application is published with at least two weeks for objections. [s. 67](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הירושה#סעיף_67)

The checklist of what must accompany the application is reg. 14(ב) (lesson [26.6](lesson-06.md)): complete form; death certificate; for probate, the original will; proof of notice to every heir or beneficiary by registered mail or personal delivery; a power of attorney if represented; fee and publication costs paid; no earlier application for the same estate. Applications go to the Registrar for the area of the deceased's domicile (reg. 13). Online filing is open for applications by children, parents and their descendants, or the spouse, and for probate (Second Schedule); the online fee is 85% of the paper fee (Fees Regulations reg. 1).

## Leading cases

### Key text: reg. 14(ב) as a gate

Reg. 14(ב) says an application "will not be accepted" unless each item is met. The Registrar does not fix gaps for you; an incomplete application is returned or held. Why it matters: the lab's checklist mirrors reg. 14(ב) line by line.

## How it works in practice

- **Fees (2026):** the paper application fee, the reduced online fee and the publication charge are in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md). Payment is through the government payments site, at the Registrar's payment kiosks, or at the Postal Bank.
- **Paper filing:** print the form from the Registrar's page; send it with the documents by email to the Registrar's service centre or by registered mail to the district office (Kol-Zchut ⚠️ manual check).
- **Online filing:** the Registrar's online form, with the national ID login; attachments as PDF; you receive an email with a file number, and a second email confirming the application is complete.
- **After filing:** publication on the Administrator General's site and in Reshumot; objection period; the order is issued digitally and sent to the applicant and to banks, insurers, the Tax Authority and the Land Registry (⚠️ manual check).
- **Land:** with the online application you can request registration of the heirs in the Land Registry, attaching the signed and verified request form and paying the registration fee (lesson [26.7](lesson-07.md)).

## Worked example

Supplied case. Ze'ev Amar of Ashkelon died on 3 August 2026 without a will, domiciled in Ashkelon. He leaves his wife Mazal (married 1982), daughters Hadas (Tel Aviv) and Shiri (Brooklyn, New York), and his son Dor's child, Lior, aged 7 (Dor died in 2021; Lior's mother, Keren, is his guardian). Assets: half of the family flat in Ashkelon (registered gush/helka on the extract), a bank account and a car.

1. **Order:** succession order (no will found; Mazal checks the national register of deposited wills, which is free).
2. **Applicant:** Mazal, the spouse, eligible to file online; to the Registrar whose area includes Ashkelon, the deceased's domicile (reg. 13).
3. **Heirs and shares (26.1):** Mazal: car + ½ of the rest; Hadas ⅙, Shiri ⅙, Lior ⅙ (representing Dor). Lior is a minor: the form must say so (reg. 14(א)).
4. **Notices:** Form 3א by registered mail to Hadas, Shiri (international registered mail) and Lior (to Keren as guardian), each stating the heir's share; keep the receipts.
5. **Renunciation:** Hadas wants to renounce in favour of her mother: an affidavit under reg. 16, verified by a lawyer, attached; Lior cannot renounce without court approval (s. 6(ג)).
6. **Fees:** online application fee and publication charge, paid on the payments site.
7. **Land:** tick the Land Registry request and attach the signed request form.
8. **Track:** check the online system; answer any requests for documents.
9. **After the order:** banks release the account by the order's shares; the Land Registry registers the flat (Mazal ½ + her share of Ze'ev's half; the children's shares); the heirs publish a creditors' notice before dividing the bank money (s. 123; [26.5](lesson-05.md)); Lior's share is managed by his guardian, and court approval may be needed for any sale involving his share.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. File only if you are an heir in a real, uncontested estate; otherwise stop before submission.

### You need

- The supplied case, or a real estate where you are an heir.
- The deceased's ID number and date of death; the heirs' names, ID numbers and addresses.
- A land-registry extract if there is real estate (13.9).
- Access to the Registrar's forms (online form opened but not submitted, or Form 1/2 printed).
- 150 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Will search (10 min).** Check whether a will was deposited (the Registrar's free inquiry service), and ask the family whether anyone holds a will; if someone does, it must be delivered (s. 75) and you prepare a probate application instead.
2. **Route (10 min).** Succession, probate or both; online or paper (eligibility); which district.
3. **Family tree and shares (20 min).** Use your 26.1 calculation; mark minors, persons under guardianship or an enduring power in force, and missing heirs.
4. **Fill the form (40 min).** Complete every field of Form 1 or the online form, including the declaration. Do not submit unless step "File" applies to you.
5. **Notices (20 min).** Draft Form 3א (or Form 3 with a copy of the will) for each heir; plan registered mail and keep receipts.
6. **Renunciations (10 min).** If an heir renounces, check s. 6(ב) (spouse, child or sibling only) and prepare the affidavit wording for verification.
7. **Fees (10 min).** Find the current fees for your route in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md) and on the payments site.
8. **Land (10 min).** Prepare the land-registration request for the inherited property.
9. **After the order (20 min).** Write the to-do list: banks, pension funds, the vehicle licensing office, the Land Registry, creditors' notice, distribution agreement.
10. **File (optional).** If you are an heir in a real, uncontested estate, submit, pay and track.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. The Registrar's procedure is used daily by unrepresented heirs, and the online service is designed for them ([can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md)). A renunciation affidavit must be verified before a lawyer (Kol-Zchut ⚠️ manual check), but that is verification, not representation. Filing on behalf of heirs when you are not one yourself, or for pay, is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).

### Where and how

- The Registrar of Inheritance for the deceased's domicile (reg. 13), online or on paper.
- Fees: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Help: the Registrar's service and information centre \*2416; Kol-Zchut's inheritance pages; Legal Aid if eligible.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- Someone objects or the file is transferred to the Family Court.
- An heir is a minor and the family wants a renunciation or a sale involving the minor's share.
- An heir cannot be found, or the deceased had assets or heirs abroad.
- A copy, not the original, is all that remains of the will.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Not checking for a deposited will | Wrong order; later cancellation | Apply under s. 72 | Use the free deposited-will inquiry first |
| Notice not sent to an heir abroad | Application not accepted | Send by international registered mail or personal delivery with an affidavit | Collect all addresses before filing |
| Minor heir not marked | Delay; Attorney General involvement | Correct the application | Mark minors and guardians on the form |
| Original will not delivered after online filing | Application stalls | Deliver it at once | Deliver within seven days (reg. 14(ד)(1)) |
| Dividing the bank money the day the order arrives | Personal exposure to creditors (s. 128) | Pay the debt; seek contribution | Publish a creditors' notice first |

## Review

- Check for a will first; succession and probate orders are different applications.
- Choose the route: online for close family and probate, paper for more distant heirs; file with the Registrar for the deceased's domicile.
- Reg. 14(ב) lists everything the application needs; notices to every heir are mandatory.
- The order is published, objections may be filed for at least two weeks, and the order is then issued digitally.
- After the order: banks, Land Registry, creditors' notice, and a distribution agreement where needed.
