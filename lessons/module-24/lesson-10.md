# 24.10 · Practice lab: a request to delete a police record

In this lab you clean up a record lawfully. You inspect your own criminal information (or use the supplied file), sort each entry by register and status, pick the route that fits it, and write the request that is most often worth writing: a reasoned request to the police to cancel a closed-file entry under s. 33 of the Criminal Information Law, together, where it fits, with a request to the prosecutor to change the closure ground to "lack of guilt".

## Why it matters

> [!WARNING]
> Use only lawful routes and only for your own record. Do not ask anyone to "fix" a record informally, and do not misstate facts in a request: a false statement in an official request may be an offence and will be checked against the file. If an entry concerns a pending case, do not use this lab: talk to a lawyer.

A closed file in the police register can surface in a licensing decision, a security clearance, adoption or foster-care checks, some employment checks by entitled bodies and some visa processes (lesson [24.8](lesson-08.md)). Many people do not know the entry exists until a decision goes against them. The law offers three tools that cost nothing and need no lawyer: inspection (s. 4), cancellation (s. 33) and change of closure ground (CPL s. 62(ב)(4)). They work best when you ask early and give the decision-maker what the criteria require.

## The law

### The powers you use

- **Inspection:** everyone may inspect the information about himself, on screen at a police station of his choice, after identification; notes allowed, photographs not (s. 4(א), (ג); Inspection Regulations regs 2, 6).
- **Correction:** the police correct an error and notify you (s. 7).
- **Change of closure ground:** a reasoned request to the prosecutor who closed the file; a file closed for lack of guilt is removed from the police register (CPL s. 62(ב)(4)).
- **Cancellation:**

> (א) ראש אגף החקירות והמודיעין במשטרה או קצין משטרה בדרגת סגן־ניצב ומעלה שהוא הסמיך לכך רשאים לבטל פרט רישום משטרתי שעניינו תיק סגור, לפי אמות מידה…
> (ב) …(1) הזמן שחלף מיום האירוע…; (2) גיל האדם שהרישום נוגע לו ביום האירוע, נסיבותיו האישיות, קיומו של מידע פלילי נוסף לגביו, מהות העבירות שיוחסו לו וחומרתן.

*Gist:* A senior police officer may cancel a police-register entry about a closed file, under criteria that consider the time since the event, the person's age at the time, his personal circumstances, other criminal information and the nature and seriousness of the offences; a stayed file needs the Attorney General's consent; non-felony closed files are cancelled automatically seven years after the event (five for minors) unless the criteria say otherwise (s. 33). ([Criminal Information Law s. 33](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המידע_הפלילי_ותקנת_השבים#סעיף_33); periods in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

Entries in the criminal register (convictions, orders) are not cancelled by request; they lapse and are deleted by time (ss. 18–23), or the President may shorten the periods (s. 25).

## How it works in practice

### Route finder

| Entry | Route | To whom |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong details (not your file, wrong outcome) | Correction (s. 7) | The police (station or the records unit) |
| Closed for insufficient evidence or "circumstances", and you are innocent | Change of closure ground (CPL s. 62(ב)(4)) | The prosecutor who closed it (police prosecution unit or district attorney) |
| Any closed file, felony or misdemeanour | Cancellation request (s. 33(א)) | The police Investigations and Intelligence Department (address or online form on the police website) |
| Non-felony closed file, 7+ years since the event | Ask why it was not cancelled (s. 33(ג)) | The police |
| Stayed file (עיכוב הליכים) | Cancellation needs the Attorney General's consent | The police, who consult the Attorney General's office |
| Conviction or order | Wait for limitation and deletion; or ask the President (s. 25) | Pardons department |

### What a strong cancellation request shows

1. **Time:** how long since the event, and no new entries since.
2. **Age then:** young age at the time, if relevant.
3. **Personal circumstances:** work, study, family, volunteering, health; and the concrete harm the entry causes (a licence application, a job offer from an entitled employer, foster care).
4. **Other information:** none, or old and minor.
5. **The offence:** its nature and seriousness, and the closure ground; if closed for insufficient evidence, the weakness of the case.
6. **The request:** cancel the entry; alternatively, tell me when I may ask again.

## Worked example

Maya Rosen, 24, from Jerusalem, applies to be a foster parent. At the station she sees one entry: 2021, "possession of a dangerous drug for personal use" (a misdemeanour under the Dangerous Drugs Ordinance, punishable by up to three years), file closed "circumstances as a whole" (she was 19, at a party, with a small amount).

1. **Route.** Not lack of guilt (she does not claim innocence), so changing the ground does not fit. As a non-felony the entry would be cancelled automatically only seven years after the event, in 2028 (s. 33(ג)); the foster-care decision cannot wait, so she writes a s. 33(א) request now.
2. **Content.** Time: five years without any entry. Age: 19. Circumstances: a social-work degree, a job at a youth centre, a letter from her supervisor, the foster-care application. Seriousness: small quantity, personal use, closed without indictment.
3. **Sending.** She sends it to the address on the police website with a copy of her ID, and keeps proof.
4. **Outcome.** Three months later the police notify her that the entry was cancelled. She checks again at a station to confirm.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your ID (biometric ID card, or another listed ID).
- About an hour at a police station during inspection hours (optional; otherwise use the supplied file).
- The template [request to cancel a police record](../../templates/police-record-cancellation-request.md).
- Documents for your circumstances (employer or study letters, the decision that the entry affects).
- 120 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Inspect (45 min, optional).** Go to a station and ask to inspect your criminal information. For each entry note: register, file number, date of the event, offence, outcome, closure ground. Take notes only.
   - Supplied file if you do not inspect: (a) 2018, theft (misdemeanour), closed "insufficient evidence"; (b) 2022, threats (misdemeanour), closed "circumstances as a whole"; (c) 2023, aggravated assault (felony), closed "insufficient evidence", where you were the one attacked.
2. **Classify (15 min).** For each entry, using the route finder, write the route and the reason.
3. **Draft (40 min).** Write one cancellation request under s. 33(א) for the entry that most needs it, addressing every factor in s. 33(ב) with evidence. If one entry fits a change of closure ground, write that request too (the template has both).
4. **Check (20 min).** Read the request as the officer would: is every claim supported by an attached document? Is the harm concrete? Is the request clear?

### How you know it worked

- Every entry has a route; in the supplied file, (a) is a non-felony more than seven years old by 2026, so it should already be cancelled (s. 33(ג)); (b) needs a s. 33(א) request; (c) fits a change of closure ground to lack of guilt, and also a s. 33(א) request.
- Your request addresses time, age, circumstances, other information and seriousness separately.
- You attached proof for each factual claim and kept proof of sending.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Inspection, correction, cancellation and change-of-ground requests are made by the person himself. A lawyer can obtain a printout for representation before an entitled body (s. 4(ד)), which helps if a decision is already pending against you. Requests for others, for pay or as an occupation, are reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).

### Where and how

- Inspection: any police station, published hours.
- Cancellation: the police Investigations and Intelligence Department; form and online channel on the police website (⚠️ manual check on police.gov.il / gov.il, which block bots).
- Change of closure ground: the prosecutor who closed the file (the closure notice names it).
- Periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); free help: the Public Defender does not handle records requests, but some legal clinics do ([where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md)).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The entry is about a pending investigation or trial.
- A licensing body, employer or court is already weighing the entry in a decision about you.
- The request was refused and you want to challenge the refusal (an administrative petition; module 14).
- The entry involves a stayed file or a conviction you want shortened by the President.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Photographing the screen | Forbidden; inspection may end (s. 4(ג)(2)) | Take notes instead | Leave the phone as asked |
| A generic "please delete" letter | Refused for lack of reasons | Resubmit addressing each factor | Use the template's five factors |
| Asking for cancellation of a conviction | Wrong route; no power | Wait for limitation, or ask the President | Check the register first |
| Not following up | You assume it was cancelled | Inspect again after the answer | Diary a re-check |
| Misstating facts | Refusal; possible offence | Correct in writing | Only facts you can prove |

## Review

- Inspect your own information on screen at any police station (s. 4).
- Closed-file entries can be cancelled on request (s. 33(א)) under five statutory factors; non-felonies automatically after seven years (s. 33(ג)).
- A file closed for lack of guilt leaves no entry; ask to change the ground when it fits (CPL s. 62(ב)(4)).
- Convictions and orders are not cancelled by request; they lapse and are deleted by time, or the President may shorten them.
- Write requests with evidence for each factor and check the result afterwards.
