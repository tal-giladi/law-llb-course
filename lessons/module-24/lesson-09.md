# 24.9 · Practice lab: a pre-indictment hearing request and defending yourself in a minor case

In this lab you produce two documents a suspect or defendant writes in his own name. First, a reasoned request to the prosecution not to indict, under CPL s. 60א, for a supplied felony file, prepared as a draft for a lawyer to check. Second, for a fine notice (a traffic ticket or a municipal fine), the decision whether to pay, ask to cancel or ask to be tried, the request itself, and a short plan for the trial if you choose one: the one area of criminal law where self-representation is normal.

## Why it matters

> [!WARNING]
> Part A is a dry run on a supplied case. In a real felony file, have a lawyer or the Public Defender write or check the request: what you write can be used against you. Part B is about fine offences only (parking, most traffic tickets, municipal bylaw fines, littering, noise). If a traffic matter involves injury, alcohol, drugs, driving while disqualified or a summons to trial instead of a fine notice, get a lawyer.

The two parts are the two ends of the scale. A s. 60א request is the suspect's one chance to be heard before a felony indictment (lesson [24.5](lesson-05.md)). The fine notice is the most common criminal document in Israel: paying it is a conviction (s. 229(ח)), ignoring it is a conviction (s. 229(ח2)), and asking to be tried is the only route to a hearing on the facts. Both turn on short deadlines that run from receipt.

## The law

### Choice of trial: ss. 228–229

A fine offence is a "choice of trial" offence (s. 228(א)). Whoever receives a fine notice pays within the period in the notice, unless, within the periods in s. 229(א):

> (1) הגיש לתובע, תוך שלושים ימים מיום ההמצאה, בקשה לביטול… (2) הודיע, תוך תשעים ימים מיום ההמצאה, בדרך שנקבעה בתקנות, שיש ברצונו להישפט על העבירה.

*Gist:* he asked the prosecutor to cancel the notice within 30 days of service (90 days for a vehicle owner who wants to prove someone else drove), or gave notice within 90 days that he wishes to be tried; after a cancellation request is refused, he has 30 days from that decision to ask to be tried. ([CPL s. 229(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_סדר_הדין_הפלילי#סעיף_229); [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)) The prosecutor may cancel if no offence was committed, someone else committed it, or the circumstances as a whole do not justify continuing (s. 229(ג)); his decision on cancellation is final (s. 229(א)(1)). Late requests may be considered if the delay was beyond your control and you acted at once when it ended (s. 229(ה)). Surcharges may be waived for reasonable cause or special personal circumstances, on a request with an affidavit (s. 229(ו)–(ז)).

> (ח) שילם אדם את הקנס רואים אותו כאילו הודה באשמה בפני בית המשפט, הורשע ונשא את עונשו.

*Gist:* payment counts as admission, conviction and service of sentence; and if the periods pass with no payment and no request, you are treated as convicted and sentenced to the fine (s. 229(ח), (ח2)). Fine offences are not entered in the criminal register (Criminal Information Law s. 8(ג)), but traffic offences go to the traffic register.

### The form and the summons: regs 42–43; s. 230

The request to be tried is made on the slip attached to the fine notice (Form 8א) and sent to the address on it within the period; a registered-mail receipt proves the date (reg. 42). A summons and indictment then follows (reg. 43), within a year of your request (s. 230). If convicted, the fine will not be lower than the notice unless the court finds special circumstances (s. 230).

### Time limits for the authority: s. 225א

A fine notice or indictment for a fine offence may not be served more than a year after the offence; for camera-based traffic offences, four months, extended where the owner proves someone else drove (s. 225א(א)–(א2)). This limit does not prevent the trial of someone who asked to be tried (s. 225א(ב)). ([CPL s. 225א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_סדר_הדין_הפלילי#סעיף_225א))

### Special procedure in minor offences: s. 240

For traffic offences without injury, fine offences and administrative offences: a defendant summoned who does not appear is treated as admitting all the facts unless a defender appears for him, and may be tried in his absence if this causes no miscarriage of justice, but without prison (s. 240(א)(2)–(3)). He may send the court written arguments on sentence (s. 240(א)(1)).

### The pre-indictment request: s. 60א

See lesson [24.5](lesson-05.md): a reasoned written request within the period from receipt of the notice; extensions by senior prosecutors; an oral hearing at the prosecution's discretion.

## How it works in practice

### Deciding about a fine notice

| Your position | Route | Deadline (from service) |
|---|---|---|
| The fine is right and you accept it | Pay | Within the period in the notice |
| You were not the driver (owner) | Cancellation request naming the driver, or transfer (הסבה) by the method on the notice | Within 90 days (owner proving another driver) |
| No offence was committed (sign missing, you had a parking permit, a clear error) | Cancellation request with evidence | Within 30 days |
| The facts are disputed and you want a judge | Request to be tried | Within 90 days (or 30 days after a refused cancellation) |
| You paid late or not at all because of illness, army service, being abroad | Surcharge relief request with affidavit (s. 229(ו)) | Any time; explain |

Before choosing a trial, weigh: the fine cannot be lower than the notice without special circumstances (s. 230); a conviction in a traffic offence may also add penalty points and costs; a hearing takes a day off work. A trial makes sense when you have evidence (photos, a permit, a witness, GPS data) and a real legal or factual dispute.

## Worked example

Sapir Levi, 29, from Haifa, receives on 5 May a municipal parking fine for "parking in a disabled bay". She holds a valid disabled-parking card, which was on the dashboard but may have slipped.

1. **Route.** No offence if the card was valid and displayed. She sends a cancellation request on 20 May (within 30 days), with a copy of the card, a photo of the dashboard taken that day, and a short explanation.
2. **Refusal.** On 15 June the municipal prosecutor refuses. She now has 30 days from the refusal to ask to be tried (s. 229(א), closing paragraph).
3. **Request to be tried.** On 1 July she sends the Form 8א slip by registered mail and keeps the receipt (reg. 42).
4. **Trial.** In November she receives a summons to the local-affairs court. She prepares a one-page plan: the card, the photo, the parking inspector's photo (she will ask the prosecutor for it), and a question for the inspector: "Did you check the windscreen from inside the vehicle's line of sight?" She attends: absence would count as admission (s. 240).
5. **Outcome.** The inspector's own photo shows the card's edge on the dashboard. The court acquits.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- CPL ss. 60א, 225א, 228–230, 240 and regs 41–44 (links above).
- For Part A: the supplied case below. For Part B: a real fine notice you received (yours only), or the supplied notice below.
- The templates [pre-indictment request](../../templates/pre-indictment-hearing-request.md) and [request to be tried and cancellation request](../../templates/request-to-be-tried.md).
- 150 minutes.

### Steps

**Part A — s. 60א request (75 minutes).**

Supplied case: Daniel Ohana, 26, from Kiryat Shmona, a cook, is notified on 2 September 2026 that the district attorney's office has received the material in a felony file of "assault causing actual bodily harm in aggravated circumstances" after a fight at a wedding hall. His version: a guest attacked his brother; Daniel pulled the guest away and the guest fell and cut his chin. A video from a guest's phone shows the pulling but not the start. Daniel has no record; he supports his mother; his employer, a hotel, says a conviction would end his employment.

1. **Diary (5 min).** Calculate the last day to write, from receipt, using [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); note who may extend it.
2. **Facts (15 min).** Write the facts you can support with the video and witnesses, in numbered paragraphs. Mark in brackets every fact that needs a lawyer's check.
3. **Legal argument (20 min).** Self-defence of another person (Penal Law s. 34י; lesson [05.4](../module-05/lesson-04.md)); whether the aggravated circumstances and the "actual bodily harm" element are proved; proportionality of the force.
4. **Personal circumstances (10 min).** Age, no record, family, employer letter, the harm a conviction would cause.
5. **Requested outcome (10 min).** Primary: close the file; alternative: a lesser charge; second alternative: a conditional arrangement if the offence is eligible (s. 67א); request to present orally (s. 60א(ח)) and to see the video and witness statements.
6. **Finish (15 min).** Fill the template, attach a list of exhibits, and write a one-line note for the lawyer: "Please check paragraphs [ ] before sending."

**Part B — a fine notice (75 minutes).**

Supplied notice: a police camera traffic notice served on 10 October 2026 to Noa Mansour, the registered owner, for running a red light at 22:14 on 1 October; Noa was abroad that week (passport stamps, boarding passes); her husband had the car.

1. **Route (10 min).** Using the table, choose the route and the deadline. Is the four-month limit for camera notices relevant (s. 225א(א1))?
2. **Draft (30 min).** Write the request on the template: details of the notice, the facts, the evidence attached, the legal basis (s. 229(א)(1), (ג)), the driver's details as required by the notice, and the request.
3. **If refused (15 min).** Write the request to be tried and calculate its deadline from the refusal.
4. **Trial plan (20 min).** One page: what you must prove, the documents, the questions for the prosecution witness, and what happens if you do not attend (s. 240). Use the [hearing-day kit](../../templates/hearing-day-kit.md).

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Part B: yes. Fine-offence cancellation requests, requests to be tried and the trial itself are commonly handled by the person in his own name. Part A: the right is yours, but in a felony a lawyer should write or check it. Acting for someone else (for example, writing requests for friends for pay) is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).

### Where and how

- Cancellation request and request to be tried: the address and online service on the notice (police traffic prosecution, municipal prosecution, other authority); keep proof of sending (reg. 42(ב), 42א(ג)).
- Surcharge relief: a written request with an affidavit to the prosecutor (s. 229(ו)–(ז)).
- Traffic court and local-affairs court: magistrates' court registry and Net HaMishpat.
- Periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); forum rules: [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You received a summons and indictment, not a fine notice, for a traffic offence (the authority chose trial, s. 228(ג)).
- The offence involved injury, alcohol, drugs, or driving while disqualified, or may lead to disqualification.
- You are a professional driver and points or disqualification threaten your work.
- It is a felony file (Part A).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paying "under protest" | Still a conviction (s. 229(ח)) | Usually none | Decide before paying |
| Asking for cancellation after 30 days | Out of time | Ask to be tried within 90 days, or explain delay (s. 229(ה)) | Diary both periods on receipt |
| Waiting for the cancellation decision past 90 days | You still have 30 days after the refusal | Ask to be tried within those 30 days | Read the closing paragraph of s. 229(א) |
| Not attending the trial | Treated as admitting all facts (s. 240) | Ask to set aside a judgment given in absence (s. 130 via s. 240(ב)) | Attend or send a lawyer |
| A s. 60א request full of disputed new facts | Used against you at trial | Through a lawyer, limit to documents and law | Have it checked first |

## Review

- A fine offence gives you a choice: pay (a conviction), ask to cancel within 30 days (90 for a vehicle owner proving another driver), or ask to be tried within 90 days (s. 229).
- Ignoring the notice is treated as a conviction (s. 229(ח2)); late requests need reasons (s. 229(ה)).
- The authority must serve the notice within a year (four months for camera offences) (s. 225א).
- At the trial, attend: absence counts as admission (s. 240).
- A s. 60א request is specific, evidence-based and checked by a lawyer in a felony.
