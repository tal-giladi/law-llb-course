# 03.1 · What a contract is: the Contracts (General Part) Law 1973 and the codification

By the end of this lesson you will know where Israeli contract law lives: a short general statute from 1973, a remedies statute from 1970, and a shelf of special laws for sale, rental, gift, guarantee, services and more. You will be able to take any agreement in your life (a lease, a phone plan, a job offer, a loan to a friend), find the statute that governs it first, and know which of the four classic contract questions your problem raises.

## Why it matters

Almost every legal problem a private person has starts as a contract: the landlord who keeps the deposit, the contractor who walks off the job, the gym that will not let you cancel, the employer who changes your terms. Israeli contract law is unusually accessible to a non-lawyer because it is codified in short, plain Hebrew statutes. The Contracts (General Part) Law has 64 sections; you can read all of it in an evening. Courts quote these sections constantly, and a letter or a small claim that cites the right section is taken seriously.

The catch is structure. The general part is only the base layer. A special law (Sale Law, Rental and Borrowing Law, Consumer Protection Law, Standard Contracts Law) often has a more specific rule that wins. Knowing the layers is what separates a sound argument from a confident mistake.

## The law

### The general part is a default code

> הוראות חוק זה יחולו כשאין בחוק אחר הוראות מיוחדות לענין הנדון.

*Gist:* The Contracts Law applies where no other statute has a special provision on the matter. [Contracts Law s. 61(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28חלק_כללי%29#סעיף_61)

> הוראות חוק זה יחולו, ככל שהדבר מתאים לענין ובשינויים המחוייבים, גם על פעולות משפטיות שאינן בבחינת חוזה ועל חיובים שאינם נובעים מחוזה.

*Gist:* The Contracts Law also applies, as far as it fits, to legal acts that are not contracts and to obligations that do not arise from a contract. [Contracts Law s. 61(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28חלק_כללי%29#סעיף_61)

Section 61(ב) is why rules written for contracts (good faith in s. 39, interpretation in s. 25, notice in s. 60) also reach a resolution of a condominium general meeting, a will-related act, a statutory duty or a unilateral promise.

### Freedom of contract and its limit

> חוזה יכול שייעשה בעל פה, בכתב או בצורה אחרת, זולת אם היתה צורה מסויימת תנאי לתקפו על פי חוק או הסכם בין הצדדים.

*Gist:* A contract may be oral, written or in any other form unless a law or the parties' agreement makes a form a condition of validity. [s. 23](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28חלק_כללי%29#סעיף_23)

> תכנו של חוזה יכול שיהיה ככל אשר הסכימו הצדדים.

*Gist:* The content of a contract may be whatever the parties agreed. [s. 24](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28חלק_כללי%29#סעיף_24)

> חוזה שכריתתו, תכנו או מטרתו הם בלתי חוקיים, בלתי מוסריים או סותרים את תקנת הציבור – בטל.

*Gist:* A contract whose making, content or purpose is illegal, immoral or against public policy is void. [s. 30](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28חלק_כללי%29#סעיף_30) Lesson 03.6 develops this.

### Independence from the Ottoman and English past

> בענינים שחוק זה דן בהם לא יחול סימן 46 לדבר המלך במועצתו לארץ ישראל, 1922–1947.

*Gist:* On matters this law covers, courts no longer import English common law through article 46 of the Mandate Order in Council. [s. 63](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28חלק_כללי%29#סעיף_63) Section 62 repealed the parts of the Ottoman Majalla that dealt with these matters. The Remedies Law has the same clause (s. 24). Lesson 01.2 explained why this mattered: Israeli contract law became a self-standing code, read in Hebrew and developed by Israeli courts.

### The remedies law is the other half

> הופר חוזה, זכאי הנפגע לתבוע את אכיפתו או לבטל את החוזה, וזכאי הוא לפיצויים, בנוסף על אחת התרופות האמורות או במקומן, והכל לפי הוראות חוק זה.

*Gist:* On breach, the injured party may claim enforcement or cancel, and may claim damages in addition to either or instead. [Remedies Law s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28תרופות_בשל_הפרת_חוזה%29#סעיף_2) Lesson 03.8 covers it.

## Leading cases

### ע״א 4628/93 מדינת ישראל נ׳ אפרופים שיכון ויזום (1991) בע״מ, פ״ד מט(2) 265 (1995)

In a dispute over a state housing contract, the majority (Barak, joined by D. Levin; Mazza dissenting) held that a contract is read in light of its purpose and context even where the words seem clear, rejecting a two-stage approach that looks at circumstances only after finding ambiguity.

Why it matters: it shows how far the courts developed the short 1973 code by interpretation, and how the Knesset answered (amendments to s. 25 in 2011 and 2026, lesson 03.7). The code is a living text: always read the current section together with the leading rulings. [English translation](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/360)

### ע״א 6370/00 קל בנין בע״מ נ׳ ע.ר.מ. רעננה לבניה והשכרה בע״מ (2002)

A developer ran a private tender, negotiated with the appellant to full agreement subject to board approval, then gave the work to a contractor who had not bid. In an earlier round the Supreme Court held there was no binding contract but that the developer had negotiated in bad faith. In this round it held that damages under s. 12(ב) can, in suitable cases, cover the profit the bidder would have made (expectation damages), not only its wasted costs. President Barak described ss. 12, 39 and their extension in s. 61(ב) as fundamental provisions of the whole legal system.

Why it matters: it is the clearest statement that the general part is not a technical code but a set of value-laden principles. Lesson 03.4 returns to it. [English translation (portions)](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/219)

## How it works in practice

### The layers, from specific to general

| Layer | Examples | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Mandatory consumer and tenant protection | Consumer Protection Law 1981, Rental and Borrowing Law ch. on residential leases (ss. 25א–25טו) | Rules the parties cannot contract out of |
| Special contract laws | Sale Law 1968, Gift Law 1968, Guarantee Law 1967, Rental and Borrowing Law 1971, Contract for Services Law 1974, Insurance Contract Law 1981, Agency Law 1965 | The default rules for a named contract |
| Standard Contracts Law 1982 | Pre-printed terms of any supplier | Striking out unfair terms |
| Contracts (General Part) Law 1973 | Formation, defects, form, interpretation, performance | Everything not covered above |
| Remedies Law 1970 | Enforcement, cancellation, damages | Breach of any contract, unless a special law says otherwise |

### The four questions

Every contract problem breaks down into four questions, which are also the structure of this module:

1. **Formation:** was a contract made at all? Offer, acceptance, certainty, intent, form (lessons 03.2, 03.3).
2. **Validity:** can it be undone because of how it was made (mistake, deceit, duress, exploitation), or is it void as illegal or unfair (lessons 03.5, 03.6)? Good faith runs through all of it (03.4).
3. **Content:** what does it mean, and what terms are implied (lesson 03.7)?
4. **Breach and remedies:** what can you demand now (lesson 03.8)?

### Finding the text

All the statutes are free on Wikisource's Open Law Book and in the Knesset's legislation database (lesson 02.1). Read the consolidated version and check the date of the latest amendment against your facts: s. 25 was amended in 2026 and the new wording applies only to contracts made or renewed from 7 January 2026.

## Worked example

Yossi Mizrahi, a bus driver from Ashdod, has three agreements on his mind:

- a written lease for his flat, signed in 2025;
- a mobile plan he joined online, with pre-printed terms;
- ₪8,000 he lent his cousin Avi by bank transfer, with a WhatsApp message "תחזיר עד הקיץ".

Step by step:

1. **The lease.** First layer: the residential-lease chapter of the Rental and Borrowing Law (ss. 25א–25טו), much of which cannot be waived against the tenant (s. 25יד). Then the rest of the Rental and Borrowing Law, then the Contracts Law, then the Remedies Law.
2. **The mobile plan.** Consumer Protection Law (cancellation, disclosure), the Communications Law and licence terms (sector rules), the Standard Contracts Law for the pre-printed terms, then the general part.
3. **The loan.** No special loan statute governs a private loan between relatives; the Contracts Law applies directly. An oral or WhatsApp contract is valid (s. 23). The WhatsApp message is evidence of the terms; the time for repayment ("by the summer") will be read under s. 25 and, if vague, s. 41.
4. **Result.** Yossi's file lists, for each agreement, the first statute to read and the general-part sections he will probably need.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- One agreement from your own life: a lease, a service contract, a job offer, a purchase, a loan. Any form: a signed document, an online checkout, an email exchange, an oral promise.
- The statutes linked in this lesson.
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. Write down the agreement's facts in five lines: parties, date, how it was made (signed paper, click, email, oral), price, main obligations.
2. Decide its type: sale, rental, service, gift, guarantee, loan, employment, insurance, or "unnamed".
3. List the layers from the table above that apply to it, most specific first. For each, write the statute name and one section that looks relevant.
4. Mark which of the four questions (formation, validity, content, breach) your situation raises today. Most current problems raise breach; check whether a formation or validity question hides behind it.
5. Save this as page one of your module 3 contract file. Lessons 03.2–03.10 add to it.

### Template

None. Use a one-page table: agreement, type, layers and sections, open questions.

### How you know it worked

- You can name the most specific statute for your agreement and say why it comes before the general part (s. 61(א)).
- You can say which of the four questions your situation raises.
- Every section you listed opens on Wikisource and says what you think it says.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Reading and mapping your own contract is preparation in your own name. Drafting or reviewing contracts for other people as an occupation or for pay is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20, lesson 01.1); this course trains you for your own agreements only.

### Where and how

- Statutes: Wikisource Open Law Book and the Knesset legislation database (free).
- Consumer contracts: the Consumer Protection and Fair Trade Authority publishes guidance; Kol-Zchut has plain-language guides (see [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md)).
- Disputes from your contracts usually go to the small claims court or the magistrates' court (lesson 01.4); see [can I act without a lawyer?](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The agreement concerns land, a business sale, a large loan or a guarantee you are about to sign.
- The other side is a company with a lawyer and the dispute turns on interpretation of a long, negotiated contract.
- You find that a special law with strict deadlines (for example insurance or employment) governs the matter and you do not know the deadlines.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Citing only the Contracts Law when a special law governs | The argument misses the stronger, specific rule (or is defeated by it) | Find the special law and lead with it | Work through the layers from specific to general |
| Assuming an oral agreement "does not count" | You give up a valid claim | Collect evidence of the terms (messages, transfers, witnesses) | Remember s. 23: form is free unless a law requires one |
| Quoting an English or Ottoman rule from an old book | The court disregards it (ss. 62–63) | Re-base the argument on the Israeli statute | Use current Israeli statutes and rulings only |
| Reading an amended section in its new wording for an old contract | Wrong rule applied (for example s. 25 after 2026) | Check the transition provision | Note the contract's date next to every section you cite |

## Review

- Israeli contract law is codified: a general part (1973), a remedies law (1970) and a set of special contract laws.
- A special law prevails where it has a specific rule (s. 61(א)); the general part also reaches non-contractual legal acts and obligations (s. 61(ב)).
- Form is free (s. 23) and content is free (s. 24), within s. 30 and mandatory protective laws.
- Every contract problem is one or more of four questions: formation, validity, content, breach and remedies.
- Kal Binyan and Apropim show the courts developing the code: good faith as a system-wide value, and purposive interpretation that the Knesset later limited.
