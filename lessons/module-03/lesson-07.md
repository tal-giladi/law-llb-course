# 03.7 · Interpretation (s. 25) and implied terms

By the end of this lesson you will be able to take a disputed clause in your own contract and argue its meaning the way an Israeli court would: first by choosing the version of s. 25 that applies, then by using the statutory tools in the right order. You will also know how Israeli law fills gaps the parties left, and where filling a gap ends and rewriting the contract begins.

## Why it matters

Most contract disputes that reach court are not about whether there was a contract but about what it says. "Is the deposit returned within 30 days of leaving or of the final meter readings?" "Does 'renovation' include the bathroom?" Israel has the most dramatic story in contract interpretation of any common-law-influenced system: the Supreme Court's Apropim ruling (1995) made purpose and context central even for clear words; the Knesset responded in 2011; and on 5 January 2026 it passed a third version of s. 25 that, for business contracts made from 7 January 2026, puts the language first. As a private person you will meet both regimes for years, because contracts made before the amendment are still read under the old wording.

## The law

### Two versions of s. 25(א)

> (א) [נוסח לחוזים שנכרתו עד יום 6.1.2026] חוזה יפורש לפי אומד דעתם של הצדדים, כפי שהוא משתמע מתוך החוזה ומנסיבות העניין, ואולם אם אומד דעתם של הצדדים משתמע במפורש מלשון החוזה, יפורש החוזה בהתאם ללשונו.

*Gist:* For contracts made up to 6 January 2026: read the contract by the parties' intention as shown by the contract and the circumstances, but where the intention is explicit in the language, follow the language (the 2011 wording). [s. 25(א), old wording](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28חלק_כללי%29#סעיף_25)

For contracts made or renewed from 7 January 2026 the new s. 25(א) has five paragraphs:

> (1) אופן הפרשנות של חוזה והראיות שיהיו קבילות לפירושו יהיו ככל אשר הסכימו הצדדים; [...]

*Gist:* The parties may agree on the method of interpretation and on the admissible evidence. [s. 25(א)(1), new wording](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/25/law/25_lsr_10622519.pdf)

> (2) חוזה עסקי שלא נקבעו בו הוראות לעניין אופן הפרשנות יפורש בהתאם ללשונו בלבד, אלא אם כן מתקיים אחד מאלה: (א) מלשון החוזה בלבד נובעת תוצאה שאינה מתקבלת על הדעת; (ב) מלשון החוזה בלבד עולה סתירה בין הוראות שונות בו.

*Gist:* A business contract with no agreed method is read by its language alone, unless that gives an unreasonable result or an internal contradiction; then the general rules apply (para. (3)).

> (4) חוזה שאינו חוזה עסקי, חוזה אחיד אף אם הוסכם בו אחרת, וכן חוזה עבודה או הסכם קיבוצי יפורשו לפי אומד דעתם של הצדדים, כפי שהוא משתמע מתוך החוזה ומנסיבות העניין; [...]

*Gist:* Non-business contracts, standard contracts (even if they say otherwise), employment contracts and collective agreements are read by the parties' intention from the text and the circumstances; the weight given to language versus circumstances depends on the parties' relationship (information gaps, special trust), how detailed the contract is, and the parties' experience and legal representation.

Paragraph (5): parties who were not represented by a lawyer in drafting are treated as not having agreed on a method under para. (1), and no agreement may override s. 25(ב1). The amendment does not define "חוזה עסקי"; the courts will have to draw the line. The transition clause: the new wording applies to contracts made after commencement, "לעניין זה יראו חידוש חוזה ככריתתו" (renewal counts as making). [Amendment No. 3, s. 2](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/25/law/25_lsr_10622519.pdf)

### The tools that did not change

> (ב) חוזה הניתן לפירושים שונים, פירוש המקיים אותו עדיף על פירוש שלפיו הוא בטל.
> (ב1) חוזה הניתן לפירושים שונים והיתה לאחד הצדדים לחוזה עדיפות בעיצוב תנאיו, פירוש נגדו עדיף על פירוש לטובתו.
> (ג) ביטויים ותניות בחוזה שנוהגים להשתמש בהם בחוזים מאותו סוג יפורשו לפי המשמעות הנודעת להם באותם חוזים.

*Gist:* Prefer the reading that keeps the contract valid; where one side had the upper hand in drafting, prefer the reading against it; read standard phrases by their usual meaning in that type of contract. [s. 25(ב)–(ג)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28חלק_כללי%29#סעיף_25)

### Gap-filling

> פרטים שלא נקבעו בחוזה או על פיו יהיו לפי הנוהג הקיים בין הצדדים, ובאין נוהג כזה – לפי הנוהג המקובל בחוזים מאותו סוג, ויראו גם פרטים אלה כמוסכמים.

*Gist:* Missing details follow the parties' own practice, else the usual practice in that type of contract, and count as agreed. [s. 26](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28חלק_כללי%29#סעיף_26)

Default rules: time of performance, a reasonable time after the creditor's reasonable notice (s. 41); place, the creditor's place of business or home (s. 44); quality, average (s. 45); price, a reasonable price at the time of contracting (s. 46). Special laws add more (for example the residential-lease chapter's standard contract, s. 25ד of the Rental and Borrowing Law). Courts also complete contracts using good faith (s. 39) where a term is clearly needed to make the contract work.

## Leading cases

### ע״א 4628/93 מדינת ישראל נ׳ אפרופים שיכון ויזום (1991) בע״מ, פ״ד מט(2) 265 (1995)

A dispute over the payment terms of a state housing-construction contract. The majority (Barak, joined by D. Levin; Mazza dissenting) rejected the "two-stage" approach in which a court looks outside the text only after finding it unclear: the purpose of the contract, learned from the text and from external circumstances, guides interpretation from the start, even where the language seems plain.

Why it matters: Apropim defined Israeli interpretation for thirty years and provoked both amendments. For contracts made before 7 January 2026 its approach, as moderated by the 2011 wording and later case law that gave the language a strong presumption, still governs; for business contracts made from that date, the new s. 25(א)(2) points the other way. [English translation](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/360)

### ע״א 9311/99 מנורה חברה לביטוח בע״מ נ׳ נרות ירושלים אילום (2002)

A landlord's insurer paid for fire damage and sued the tenant and the tenant's insurer (subrogation). The Supreme Court read the lease and the insurance policies together and held that the landlord and its insurer had waived the right of subrogation against the tenant.

Why it matters: interpretation looks at the whole web of documents and the commercial arrangement they create. A lease clause about who insures what can decide who bears a fire loss. [English translation](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/255)

## How it works in practice

### The order of work

1. **Date and type.** When was the contract made or last renewed? Before 7 January 2026: old wording. From then: is it a business contract, a standard contract, an employment contract, or a contract between private people? Were the parties represented by lawyers in drafting?
2. **Agreed method.** Does the contract itself say how to interpret it (for example "this agreement reflects the entire agreement and will be read by its language")? From 2026 such a clause matters for business contracts; it cannot override contra proferentem and does not bind unrepresented parties.
3. **Language.** Read the clause, the definitions and the whole contract. Note any internal contradiction.
4. **Circumstances.** Where they are relevant: the negotiations, drafts, emails, the parties' conduct after signing.
5. **Tie-breakers.** Validating reading (s. 25(ב)); against the drafter (s. 25(ב1)); trade usage (s. 25(ג)).
6. **Gap or dispute?** If the contract is silent, fill it (s. 26, ss. 41–46), do not interpret it.

### For private people

Most contracts you sign are either standard contracts or contracts with a landlord, seller or contractor in which you had no lawyer. Under both wordings these are read by intention from text and circumstances, and against the stronger drafter. Keep the advert, the emails and the messages: they are your circumstances.

## Worked example

Daniel Goldberg from Rehovot rented a flat under a lease he signed, without lawyers, in 2024. Clause 12 says: "The deposit will be returned at the end of the tenancy after deducting any debts of the tenant." Daniel left on 31 July 2026. The landlord says she will return the deposit "when the municipality sends the final arnona account", which could take months.

Step by step:

1. **Version.** The lease was made in 2024 and not renewed, so the old s. 25(א) applies. (Even if renewed in 2026, a residential lease between private people is not a business contract and, if the landlord's form is a standard contract, para. (4) would apply anyway.)
2. **Language.** "At the end of the tenancy after deducting debts" does not set a waiting period.
3. **Statute first.** The residential-lease chapter governs deposits: the landlord must return the guarantee or its balance within a fixed period from getting the flat back, or from when the tenant's debts are paid, whichever is later (Rental and Borrowing Law s. 25י(ה)); see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md). This is a special law and it cannot be waived against the tenant (s. 25יד).
4. **Interpretation tools.** Even without the statute: against the drafter (s. 25(ב1)) if the landlord wrote the lease; a reasonable time (s. 41).
5. **Result.** Daniel writes asking for the deposit by the statutory date, offering to pay any arnona balance once it is known, and citing s. 25י and the clause.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- One clause from a contract of yours that is unclear or disputed. Or use Daniel's clause.
- The contract, any emails or messages from the negotiations, and the contract's date.
- 50 minutes.

### Steps

1. Write the contract's date and whether it was renewed after 6 January 2026.
2. Classify it: business, non-business, standard, employment. Note whether each party had a lawyer.
3. Copy the disputed clause in Hebrew. Write the two competing readings.
4. Check for an agreed interpretation clause.
5. Apply the language test, then circumstances, then the tie-breakers. Note which tool helps which reading.
6. Check whether a special law answers the question directly (as s. 25י did for Daniel).
7. Ask: is this really a gap? If so, apply s. 26 or ss. 41–46.
8. Write one paragraph: version of s. 25, your reading, the two strongest tools, the evidence.

### Template

None.

### How you know it worked

- You stated which version of s. 25 applies and why.
- Your paragraph uses at least one statutory tool by number.
- You checked for a special law before relying on interpretation.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes, in your own disputes. Interpretation arguments are central in small claims and magistrates' court cases about leases, services and purchases.

### Where and how

- Put the contract, the clause and the circumstances evidence (messages, adverts) in the claim's appendices.
- In court, explain in two minutes: the version of s. 25, your reading, the evidence.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- A business contract made from 7 January 2026 is in dispute and much depends on whether it is "business": the line is new and unsettled.
- The contract is long and negotiated by lawyers on the other side, with an entire-agreement clause.
- Expert evidence on trade usage (s. 25(ג)) will be needed.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Citing the new 2026 wording for an older contract | Wrong legal test | Re-argue under the old wording | Check the date and renewal first |
| Treating your private lease as a "business contract" | You argue against yourself (language only) | Use para. (4) or the old wording | Classify the contract before arguing |
| Arguing interpretation when a statute answers the question | Longer, weaker case | Lead with the statute | Check special laws first |
| Relying on what you "meant" without evidence | Court relies on the text | Find messages and adverts showing shared understanding | Save negotiation messages |
| Asking the court to add a term that changes the deal | Rejected as rewriting | Frame it as a gap with a customary answer | Use s. 26 only for real gaps |

## Review

- Section 25 now has two versions: contracts made up to 6 January 2026, and contracts made or renewed from 7 January 2026.
- New rule: business contracts are read by their language alone unless the result is unreasonable or contradictory; non-business, standard, employment and collective agreements are still read by intention from text and circumstances.
- The unchanged tools: the validating reading, against the stronger drafter, and trade usage (s. 25(ב)–(ג)).
- Gaps are filled by practice and custom (s. 26) and by default rules (ss. 41–46), not by rewriting the deal.
- Apropim made purpose central; the Knesset narrowed it in 2011 and, for business contracts, reversed its starting point in 2026.
