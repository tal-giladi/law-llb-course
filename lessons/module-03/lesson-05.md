# 03.5 · Defects in formation: mistake, deceit, duress, exploitation

By the end of this lesson you will be able to tell when a contract that was properly made can still be undone because something went wrong in how it was made: a mistake, a lie or a hidden fact, a threat, or the exploitation of someone's distress. You will know who may cancel, how and how fast, and what each side must return.

## Why it matters

Defects in formation are the private person's main exit from a bad deal: the car sold with a turned-back odometer, the "free trial" that hid a 24-month commitment, the elderly parent pressed into signing a loan in a hospital corridor, the online shop that listed a ₪4,000 television for ₪40. Chapter ב of the Contracts Law (ss. 13–22) gives a structured answer. The key practical points are that most defects give a right to cancel by notice, that the notice must be sent within a reasonable time, and that cancellation unwinds the deal both ways.

## The law

### Mistake

> (א) מי שהתקשר בחוזה עקב טעות וניתן להניח שלולא הטעות לא היה מתקשר בחוזה והצד השני ידע או היה עליו לדעת על כך, רשאי לבטל את החוזה.

*Gist:* A party who contracted because of a mistake, and would not have contracted without it, may cancel if the other side knew or should have known of the mistake. [s. 14(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28חלק_כללי%29#סעיף_14)

If the other side did not and should not have known, only the court may cancel, "אם ראה שמן הצדק לעשות זאת", and it may order the mistaken party to compensate the other (s. 14(ב)). No cancellation if the other side offers to perform the contract with the mistake corrected (s. 14(ג)). Mistake covers facts and law, "להוציא טעות שאינה אלא בכדאיות העסקה" (s. 14(ד)): paying too much is not, by itself, a mistake.

### Deceit

> מי שהתקשר בחוזה עקב טעות שהיא תוצאת הטעיה שהטעהו הצד השני או אחר מטעמו, רשאי לבטל את החוזה; לענין זה, "הטעיה" – לרבות אי־גילוין של עובדות אשר לפי דין, לפי נוהג או לפי הנסיבות היה על הצד השני לגלותן.

*Gist:* A party misled by the other side or its agent may cancel; deceit includes failing to disclose facts that law, custom or the circumstances required to be disclosed. [s. 15](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28חלק_כללי%29#סעיף_15) For consumers, the Consumer Protection Law adds a separate ban on misleading a consumer on any material matter (s. 2), enforceable as a tort (s. 31).

### Clerical error

> נפלה בחוזה טעות סופר או טעות כיוצא בה, יתוקן החוזה לפי אומד דעת הצדדים ואין הטעות עילה לביטול החוזה.

*Gist:* A clerical or similar slip is corrected to match the parties' intent and is not a ground to cancel. [s. 16](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28חלק_כללי%29#סעיף_16)

### Duress

> (א) מי שהתקשר בחוזה עקב כפיה שכפה עליו הצד השני או אחר מטעמו, בכוח או באיום, רשאי לבטל את החוזה.
> (ב) אזהרה בתום לב על הפעלתה של זכות אינה בגדר איום לענין סעיף זה.

*Gist:* A party who contracted because of force or threats by the other side may cancel; a good-faith warning that you will use a right (sue, report, cancel) is not a threat. [s. 17](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28חלק_כללי%29#סעיף_17) Courts also recognise economic duress: an improper threat to cause financial harm that leaves no real choice.

### Exploitation

> מי שהתקשר בחוזה עקב ניצול שניצל הצד השני או אחר מטעמו את מצוקת המתקשר, חולשתו השכלית או הגופנית או חוסר נסיונו, ותנאי החוזה גרועים במידה בלתי סבירה מן המקובל, רשאי לבטל את החוזה.

*Gist:* A party may cancel if the other side exploited their distress, mental or physical weakness or inexperience, and the terms are unreasonably worse than usual. Both elements are required. [s. 18](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28חלק_כללי%29#סעיף_18)

### How to cancel and what follows

> ביטול החוזה יהיה בהודעת המתקשר לצד השני תוך זמן סביר לאחר שנודע לו על עילת הביטול, ובמקרה של כפיה – תוך זמן סביר לאחר שנודע לו שפסקה הכפיה.

*Gist:* Cancellation is by notice to the other side within a reasonable time after learning of the ground (for duress, after it ended). [s. 20](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28חלק_כללי%29#סעיף_20)

> משבוטל החוזה, חייב כל צד להשיב לצד השני מה שקיבל על פי החוזה, ואם ההשבה היתה בלתי אפשרית או בלתי סבירה – לשלם לו את שוויו של מה שקיבל.

*Gist:* After cancellation each side returns what it received, or its value if return is impossible or unreasonable. [s. 21](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28חלק_כללי%29#סעיף_21) If only one separable part is affected, only that part may be cancelled, unless you would not have made the contract at all without the defect (s. 19). Other remedies, such as damages for bad-faith negotiation (s. 12) or in tort, are preserved (s. 22). A sham contract is void, but third parties who relied on it in good faith are protected (s. 13).

## Leading cases

### ע״א 118/51 New Zealand Insurance Co. v. Yuval (1953)

A Haifa merchant insured watches already in transit without telling the insurer they had been sent twenty days earlier. Applying the Ottoman Commercial Code then in force, the Supreme Court held that an insurance contract is avoided for non-disclosure of a fact that would have deterred a reasonable insurer, whether or not the insured acted fraudulently; on the facts the case was sent back for further findings.

Why it matters: before 1973, a general duty to disclose existed only in special contracts like insurance. Section 15 now makes non-disclosure a form of deceit in every contract, wherever "law, custom or the circumstances" require disclosure. Comparing this ruling with s. 15 shows how much the codification widened protection. [English translation](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/288)

### Key text: the structure of chapter ב

Most of the doctrine in this lesson comes directly from ss. 13–22, which are short and precise. When you rely on a defect, quote the section's elements one by one and match each to a fact; that is how courts analyse these claims.

## How it works in practice

### Choosing the right ground

| Facts | Ground | You cancel by | Key element to prove |
|---|---|---|---|
| You misunderstood something and the other side noticed | s. 14(א) mistake | Notice | They knew or should have known |
| You misunderstood and they could not know | s. 14(ב) mistake | Court only | Justice requires it; you may pay their damage |
| They lied, or hid a fact they had to disclose | s. 15 deceit | Notice | The misleading statement or silence caused your mistake |
| A typo in price, date or name | s. 16 clerical error | No cancellation; correction | What both parties actually meant |
| Threat of harm (physical, economic, improper) | s. 17 duress | Notice after the duress ends | Improper threat, causal link |
| Your distress or inexperience was exploited, terms very bad | s. 18 exploitation | Notice | Both exploitation and unreasonably bad terms |
| "You overpaid" | None (s. 14(ד)) | — | A bad bargain is not a defect |

### Timing

"Reasonable time" is short in practice: weeks, not months, once you know the facts. Continuing to use the product or the service after you learn of the deceit can be read as a decision to keep the contract. Write your notice as soon as you have the facts.

### Online pricing errors

When a shop lists a product at an obviously wrong price (₪40 instead of ₪4,000) and you order, the shop may cancel for mistake under s. 14(א), because you should have known of the mistake. Where the price was merely attractive, not absurd, courts are less sympathetic to the shop, and consumer law (misleading advertising, s. 2) may protect you.

## Worked example

Malka Avraham, 78, a widow from Dimona, was visited at home by a sales agent for a water purifier. He stayed two hours, told her the city's water was "dangerous for older people", and had her sign a 36-month service contract at ₪189 a month, about three times the market price. Her son discovers it five weeks later.

Step by step:

1. **Consumer law first.** This was a door-to-door sale (רוכלות). Malka is over 65, so the Consumer Protection Law gives her a special extended period to cancel a door-to-door transaction (s. 14ג1(ב)); see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md) and lesson 03.10. That is the simplest exit.
2. **Deceit.** The false claim about dangerous water induced the contract (s. 15). Cancellation by notice is available.
3. **Exploitation.** Her age and the long pressure visit (weakness, inexperience) plus a price far above market (unreasonably worse terms) satisfy both elements of s. 18.
4. **Timing.** Five weeks after signing, but she cancels promptly once her son explains the facts; a reasonable time runs from learning of the ground (s. 20).
5. **Consequences.** Restitution (s. 21): she makes the device available for collection; the company refunds payments. She may also claim damages for misleading conduct under the Consumer Protection Law (s. 31).
6. **Notice.** One letter, by registered mail and email, cancelling under the Consumer Protection Law and, in the alternative, ss. 15 and 18.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A contract you regret, from your own life or a family member's (with their consent; you act only for yourself, so for a relative this stays a dry run). Or use Malka's case.
- 50 minutes.

### Steps

1. Write the facts of how the contract was made: who said what, what was hidden, the pressure, the price compared with the market.
2. Go through the table and test each ground. For each, list its elements and match a fact to each element, or write "missing".
3. Check for a consumer-law cancellation right first (lesson 03.10); it is often simpler.
4. Decide whether cancellation is by notice or needs a court.
5. Note when you learned of the ground and how long ago that was.
6. Write the restitution list: what you received, what you paid, how each will be returned.
7. Draft a cancellation notice of no more than one page: facts, ground and section, "I hereby cancel the contract", restitution proposal, request for refund by a date.

### Template

The notice format in [Notice of breach and cancellation](../../templates/notice-of-breach-and-cancellation.md) (part C covers cancellation for a defect in formation).

### How you know it worked

- Each ground you rely on has every element matched to a fact.
- Your notice states clearly that you cancel, not that you "may consider" cancelling.
- Restitution is addressed both ways.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Cancellation by notice is a step you take in your own name. Claims to enforce restitution go to the small claims court or the magistrates' court, where you may act for yourself.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Where and how

- Send the notice by registered mail and email, keep proof of sending.
- Consumer cases: the Consumer Protection and Fair Trade Authority accepts complaints; consumer organisations can help with letters.
- For an elderly or vulnerable relative, they must sign their own notice (or act through a legal guardian or a lasting power of attorney); you may not act for them as their "representative" (s. 20 of the Bar Association Law).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The contract is a land deal, a loan secured on a home, or a large business deal.
- Cancellation for mistake under s. 14(ב) needs a court and the other side will claim damages.
- Duress involved threats of violence or criminal conduct: report to the police and get advice.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Waiting months after discovering the lie | "Not within a reasonable time"; cancellation fails | Cancel now and explain the delay | Send the notice within days of learning the facts |
| Claiming "mistake" because the price was high | Rejected (s. 14(ד)) | Look for deceit or exploitation facts | Test every ground's elements honestly |
| Relying on exploitation without comparing prices | Second element unproved | Get two or three market quotes | Prove "unreasonably worse than usual" with data |
| Keeping and using the product after cancelling | Seen as affirming the contract; restitution disputes | Offer the item back in writing | Stop using it and make it available |
| Writing "I want to cancel" instead of "I cancel" | Unclear whether cancellation happened | Send a clear second notice | Use operative words: "הנני מבטל/ת בזאת את החוזה" |

## Review

- Chapter ב of the Contracts Law lets a party cancel a contract for mistake, deceit, duress or exploitation.
- Mistake gives cancellation by notice only if the other side knew or should have known; otherwise the court decides (s. 14).
- Deceit includes non-disclosure of facts that had to be disclosed (s. 15); exploitation needs both exploitation and unreasonably bad terms (s. 18).
- Cancel by notice within a reasonable time (s. 20); both sides make restitution (s. 21).
- For consumers, check the Consumer Protection Law first; it often gives a simpler cancellation right.
