# 03.9 · Practice lab: review and negotiate a rental or service contract

In this lab you review a real contract before you sign it (or one you already signed, as a dry run): a flat lease, a renovation agreement, a gym or phone contract. You check every clause against the law you learned in this module, mark what is void, what is presumed unfair and what is just a bad deal, and write a short letter asking for specific changes. The result is a marked-up contract and a negotiation letter you can actually send.

## Why it matters

The cheapest legal advice is the clause you fixed before signing. Residential leases in Israel now sit on a floor of mandatory tenant protections (the 2017 "Fair Rental" amendment to the Rental and Borrowing Law, updated since), and many landlords still use old forms that break it. Service contracts are often standard forms with presumed-unfair terms. A tenant or customer who can say "clause 14 contradicts s. 25י; please replace it with the following wording" usually gets the change.

## The law

The lab applies lessons 03.1–03.8. The main mandatory rules for a residential lease, from the Rental and Borrowing Law (most cannot be waived against the tenant, s. 25יד):

- **Writing and content.** The lease must be written and signed, with a copy to each side (s. 25ב), and include the details in the second schedule (s. 25ג).
- **Condition.** The flat must be fit for living (s. 25ה) and habitable under the first schedule (s. 25ו); delivering an uninhabitable flat is a breach.
- **Repairs.** The landlord must fix defects not caused by the tenant's unreasonable use within a set period after the tenant's demand, and urgent defects (that prevent reasonable living) within a much shorter one (s. 25ח(ב)); see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- **Payments.** The tenant pays rent, occupier taxes such as arnona, utilities and building maintenance; the tenant does not pay for permanent systems, building insurance or a broker who acted for the landlord (s. 25ט).
- **Security.** The total security the landlord may ask for is capped (the lower of two amounts linked to the rent; s. 25י(ב)); it may be used only for listed purposes after reasonable notice (s. 25י(ג)–(ד)), and must be returned within a set period after the flat is returned (s. 25י(ה)).

> חוזה שכירות למגורים ייערך בכתב וייחתם בידי המשכיר והשוכר, ואולם אין באי־קיום הוראה זו כדי לגרוע מתוקפו של החוזה; [...]

*Gist:* A residential lease must be in writing and signed, but is valid without it. [s. 25ב](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_השכירות_והשאילה#סעיף_25ב)

> (א) תניה בחוזה שכירות למגורים המאפשרת למשכיר לבטל את החוזה שלא בשל הפרתו בטלה, אלא אם כן קיימת גם לשוכר זכות ביטול כאמור.

*Gist:* A clause letting the landlord cancel without breach is void unless the tenant has the same right; notice periods apply to both (s. 25יג(ב)). [s. 25יג](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_השכירות_והשאילה#סעיף_25יג)

Options to extend must be exercised by notice within set periods before the end of the term (s. 25יב). The chapter does not apply to hotels, holiday lets, student dorms, sheltered housing or protected tenancy (s. 25טו).

For service contracts, the main tools are the Standard Contracts Law presumptions (lesson 03.6), the remedies rules on fundamental breach clauses and agreed damages (Remedies Law ss. 6, 15) and the consumer cancellation rights (lesson 03.10).

## Leading cases

This lab applies the rulings already studied:

- **Apropim and the 2026 amendment** (lesson 03.7): a lease between private people, or a standard form, is read by intention and circumstances; write down the circumstances (the advert, the viewing, promises made) before signing. [English translation of Apropim](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/360)
- **Levy v. Mabat** (lesson 03.8): if a defect is serious enough that you may later cancel, document the condition at handover. [English translation](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/237)
- **Kal Binyan** (lesson 03.4): negotiating with you in bad faith (for example taking your documents and deposit, then renting to someone else for more) can give rise to damages. [English translation (portions)](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/219)

## How it works in practice

Review in four passes, using the [contract review checklist](../../templates/contract-review-checklist.md):

1. **Formation and form:** who are the parties (is the "landlord" the owner?), dates, signatures, attachments, the inventory.
2. **Mandatory rules:** the residential-lease chapter, consumer law, the Standard Contracts Law's void terms. A clause that contradicts these is unenforceable whatever you sign.
3. **Presumed unfair and one-sided terms:** liability exemptions, unilateral changes, agreed damages, "every breach is fundamental", forum and arbitration.
4. **Commercial terms:** rent, increases, option, pets, subletting, renovation, early exit. These are negotiable, not illegal.

Then write the letter: short, polite, numbered, with proposed wording in Hebrew.

## Do it yourself

> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. Send the letter only for a contract you are a party to.

### You need

- A contract you are about to sign, or one you signed (dry run). If you have none, use the supplied lease below.
- The [contract review checklist](../../templates/contract-review-checklist.md).
- The Rental and Borrowing Law and the Standard Contracts Law open in a browser.
- 2.5 hours: 20 minutes to read, 70 to review, 40 to write the letter, 20 to check.

### The supplied lease (extract)

Landlord: Gil Shapira. Tenant: you. Flat: 3 rooms, Rehov HaPalmach, Jerusalem. Term: 12 months from 1.11.2026, rent ₪6,000 a month.

```text
4. השוכר ימסור למשכיר במעמד החתימה ערובות כדלקמן: פיקדון במזומן בסך 18,000 ₪, ערבות בנקאית בסך 18,000 ₪ ושטר חוב בסך 50,000 ₪ חתום על ידי שני ערבים.
6. כל תיקון בדירה, מכל סוג שהוא, יבוצע על ידי השוכר ועל חשבונו.
7. השוכר יישא בדמי התיווך של המתווך מטעם המשכיר ובפרמיית ביטוח המבנה.
9. המשכיר רשאי לבטל את החוזה בכל עת בהודעה של 30 יום.
11. כל הפרה של סעיף מסעיפי חוזה זה תיחשב כהפרה יסודית.
12. איחור בתשלום דמי השכירות יחייב את השוכר בפיצוי מוסכם בסך 1,000 ₪ לכל יום איחור.
14. הפיקדון יוחזר לשוכר 120 יום לאחר פינוי הדירה ולאחר הצגת כל האישורים.
16. השוכר מצהיר כי ראה את הדירה ומצא אותה מתאימה לצרכיו ומוותר על כל טענת אי-התאמה.
18. מקום השיפוט הבלעדי יהיה בבית המשפט בתל אביב.
```

### Steps

1. **Read once without a pen.** Write three lines: what you are paying, for what, for how long.
2. **Pass 1, formation.** Check the landlord owns the flat (a land-registry extract or the purchase contract), the dates, the attachments (inventory, photos), and who signs.
3. **Pass 2, mandatory rules.** For each clause, ask: does it contradict the residential-lease chapter (ss. 25ב–25יג), the Standard Contracts Law s. 5, or consumer law? Mark these "void / unenforceable".
4. **Pass 3, presumed unfair.** Apply s. 4 of the Standard Contracts Law if the form is a standard contract (a landlord using the same form for many tenants, or a supplier's form). Also check Remedies Law s. 6 (blanket fundamental-breach clauses) and s. 15 (agreed damages).
5. **Pass 4, commercial.** Note terms you want to change for your own reasons (option to extend, pets, early exit).
6. **Rank.** For each problem: must change, try to change, accept.
7. **Write the letter.** Up to one page: thanks, numbered requests, each with the reason (section) and the proposed wording in Hebrew; a closing line that you are ready to sign once the changes are made.
8. **Check.** Re-read as if you were the landlord: is every request reasonable and clearly worded?

**Clause 4 (security).** Total security far above the statutory cap (the lower of one-third of the term's rent and three months' rent: here ₪18,000), counting the cash, the bank guarantee and the promissory note if they involve cost to the tenant; contradicts s. 25י(ב), which cannot be waived against the tenant. Must change. Proposed: "השוכר ימסור ערובה אחת לבחירתו, בסכום שלא יעלה על הסכום המרבי לפי סעיף 25י(ב) לחוק השכירות והשאילה."

**Clause 6 (all repairs on the tenant).** Contradicts s. 25ח: the tenant fixes only damage from unreasonable use; the landlord fixes the rest within the statutory periods. Must change. Proposed: "תיקונים יבוצעו בהתאם לסעיף 25ח לחוק השכירות והשאילה."

**Clause 7 (landlord's broker and building insurance).** Contradicts s. 25ט(ב)(2)–(3). Must delete.

**Clause 9 (landlord cancels at will on 30 days).** Void unless the tenant has the same right, and the notice periods are 90 days for the landlord and 60 for the tenant (s. 25יג). Must change: delete, or make mutual with the statutory periods.

**Clause 11 (every breach fundamental).** A blanket clause has effect only if reasonable at contracting (Remedies Law s. 6). Try to change: list the fundamental breaches (non-payment of rent for more than a set period, causing serious damage).

**Clause 12 (₪1,000 per day for late rent).** Grossly out of proportion to the foreseeable loss (s. 15(א)); in a standard form also presumed unfair (s. 4(6א)). Must change: interest and linkage on late payment, or a modest fixed sum after a grace period.

**Clause 14 (deposit returned 120 days after leaving).** Contradicts s. 25י(ה) (60 days from return of the flat, or when the tenant's debts are paid, whichever is later). Must change.

**Clause 16 (waiver of non-conformity).** Cannot waive the habitability requirement (s. 25ו, s. 25יד(1)); a general waiver is also a declaration presumed unfair in a standard form (s. 4(12)). Must change: attach a signed condition report with photos.

**Clause 18 (exclusive jurisdiction in Tel Aviv).** For a Jerusalem flat and a Jerusalem tenant, a clause overriding the venue rules in a standard form is presumed unfair (s. 4(9)). Try to change: "מקום השיפוט ייקבע לפי הדין."

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes, for your own lease or service contract. Reviewing or drafting contracts for others as an occupation or for pay is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).

### Where and how

- Send the letter by email or WhatsApp and keep a copy; attach the marked-up draft.
- The Ministry of Justice publishes guidance on the residential-lease rules, and Kol-Zchut has plain-language pages on fair rental (see [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md)).
- If you signed already and a void clause is used against you, the small claims court can apply the mandatory rules (lesson 03.10, module 12).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The lease is long-term (several years), commercial, or includes an option to buy.
- You are asked to give a personal guarantee or promissory note for a large sum.
- A renovation or construction contract is above a few tens of thousands of shekels, or involves building permits.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Signing first and planning to "fight later" | Disputes during the tenancy, deposit withheld | Rely on the mandatory rules; they apply anyway | Negotiate before signing |
| Arguing about rent and ignoring the security clause | Excess security tied up for years | Ask for the excess back citing s. 25י(ב) | Check the mandatory rules first |
| Vague requests ("make it fair") | Ignored | Send specific wording | Propose Hebrew text for each change |
| No condition report at handover | Disputes over damage when leaving | Take dated photos immediately | Attach photos and an inventory to the lease |
| Forgetting the option deadline | Option lost | Ask the landlord to agree an extension | Put the s. 25יב deadlines in your calendar on signing |

## Review

- Review in four passes: formation, mandatory rules, presumed unfair terms, commercial terms.
- The residential-lease chapter sets a floor that cannot be waived against the tenant: writing, habitability, repairs, payments, security, cancellation clauses, options.
- In standard forms, use the s. 4 presumptions; check blanket fundamental-breach and agreed-damages clauses under the Remedies Law.
- Ask for specific Hebrew wording and decide your walk-away points before sending.
- Keep the marked-up contract and letter in your contract file.
