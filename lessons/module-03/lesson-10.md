# 03.10 · Practice lab: notice of breach, cancellation, and consumer withdrawal

In this lab you write the three letters a private person sends most often in contract disputes: a notice of breach that gives an extension, a notice of cancellation, and a consumer's notice withdrawing from a transaction. You will work on your own case or on two supplied cases, check every deadline, and finish with letters ready to send and a proof-of-sending record.

## Why it matters

In Israeli contract law, rights are exercised by notice. Cancellation for breach happens when your notice reaches the other side (Remedies Law s. 8); a consumer's withdrawal starts the dealer's refund clock (Consumer Protection Law s. 14ה). A good notice is short, dated, precise and provable. A bad one, sent late, to the wrong address, or with "I would like to consider cancelling", can lose the case before it starts.

## The law

### Notices under the Remedies Law

> (א) הודעה לפי חוק זה תינתן בדרך שקבעו הצדדים, ובאין קביעה כזאת – בדואר רשום או בדרך אחרת המקובלת בנסיבות הענין.
> (ב) נפגע שנתן הודעה כאמור בסעיף קטן (א) ויש יסוד להניח שההודעה הגיעה לתעודתה במועדה, רשאי להסתמך עליה אף אם איחרה להגיע או לא הגיעה כלל.

*Gist:* Give notice in the agreed way, or by registered mail or another customary way; if you sent it properly, you may rely on it even if it arrived late or not at all. [Remedies Law s. 21](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28תרופות_בשל_הפרת_חוזה%29#סעיף_21)

Lesson 03.8 covered the rest: fundamental breach and extension (ss. 6–7), cancellation by notice within a reasonable time (s. 8), restitution (s. 9).

### Non-conformity in a sale

> (א) על הקונה להודיע למוכר על אי־התאמה מיד לאחר מועד הבדיקה [...] או מיד לאחר שגילה אותה, הכל לפי המוקדם יותר.
> (ב) לא הודיע הקונה על אי־ההתאמה כאמור בסעיף קטן (א), אין הוא זכאי להסתמך עליה.

*Gist:* A buyer must notify the seller of a defect promptly after inspection or discovery, or lose the right to rely on it. [Sale Law s. 14](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המכר#סעיף_14) A hidden defect is notified promptly after discovery (s. 15).

### Consumer withdrawal: the three routes

**Distance sales (online, phone, catalogue).**

> (ג) בעסקת מכר מרחוק רשאי הצרכן לבטל את העסקה – (1) בנכס – מיום עשיית העסקה ועד ארבעה עשר ימים מיום קבלת הנכס או מיום קבלת המסמך המכיל את הפרטים האמורים בסעיף קטן (ב), לפי המאוחר מביניהם; [...]

*Gist:* A consumer may cancel a distance purchase of goods within a set period from receiving the goods or the required disclosure document, whichever is later; services have their own rule. [Consumer Protection Law s. 14ג(ג)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_הצרכן#סעיף_14ג) Exceptions include perishable goods, information products, goods made to order and opened recordable media (s. 14ג(ד)). Door-to-door sales have a parallel right (s. 14).

**Longer period for some consumers.** A consumer who is a person with a disability, a senior citizen (65+) or a new immigrant (within five years) has a much longer period for door-to-door sales and for distance sales that involved a conversation with the dealer (s. 14ג1); the dealer may ask to see a certificate, nothing more.

**In-store purchases of listed goods and services.** The Cancellation Regulations 2010 allow cancellation of listed purchases even in a shop: for example furniture, home and garden equipment, and electrical and electronic devices, within a set period from receiving them, provided the goods were not used or damaged; returning them in the original packaging is enough proof (reg. 2). Exclusions include food, medicines, underwear and swimwear, made-to-measure goods, perishables, jewellery above a ceiling, and goods bought with gift vouchers (reg. 6). [Cancellation Regulations](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הגנת_הצרכן_%28ביטול_עסקה%29#סעיף_2)

### Refund and fee

> (1) יחזיר העוסק לצרכן, בתוך 14 ימים מיום קבלת ההודעה על הביטול, את אותו חלק ממחיר העסקה ששולם על ידי הצרכן, [...] ולא יגבה מהצרכן סכום כלשהו, זולת דמי ביטול בשיעור שלא יעלה על 5% ממחיר הנכס [...], או 100 שקלים חדשים, לפי הנמוך מביניהם.

*Gist:* After a distance or door-to-door cancellation the dealer refunds within a fixed period of receiving the notice, and may deduct only a capped cancellation fee; if the cancellation is because of a defect, non-conformity, late delivery or another breach by the dealer, no fee at all. [s. 14ה](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_הצרכן#סעיף_14ה) The regulations have similar refund and fee rules for in-store cancellations (regs. 3–5). The current periods and caps are in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

## Leading cases

This lab applies the rulings already studied. Two points matter most for notices:

- **Levy v. Mabat** (lesson 03.8): when you cancel, restitution runs both ways; offer to return what you received and claim damages for what restitution costs you. [English translation](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/237)
- **Tnuva v. Raabi Estate** (lesson 03.6): misleading a consumer is a tort under the Consumer Protection Law (s. 31); a withdrawal letter can also reserve a claim for misleading conduct. [English translation](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/386)

## How it works in practice

### The anatomy of a good notice

1. **Header:** date, your name and ID number (for the recipient's copy only), the recipient's full name and address, the method of sending.
2. **Subject line:** "הודעה על הפרת חוזה ומתן ארכה" or "הודעת ביטול".
3. **The contract:** date, subject, order or contract number.
4. **The facts:** what was due, when, what happened. Three to five lines.
5. **The law:** the section in one line.
6. **The operative sentence:** "הנני נותן/ת לך ארכה עד ליום..." or "הנני מבטל/ת בזאת את החוזה".
7. **What you ask:** performance by a date, or refund of a sum within the statutory period, with your bank details or the card used.
8. **Reservation:** "אין באמור כדי לגרוע מכל זכות או טענה העומדות לי".
9. **Signature.**

### Sending and proof

- Use the method the contract specifies; otherwise registered mail plus email (and WhatsApp to the number the business uses). For consumers, the dealer must accept cancellation in the ways it used to sell; online cancellation through the website is usually possible.
- Keep: the letter, the registered-mail receipt and tracking printout, the email with its date, any reply.
- Calendar: the end of the extension, the dealer's refund deadline, and a date to file a claim if nothing happens.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- A real breach or a purchase you want to cancel, from your own life. Otherwise, the two supplied cases.
- The [notice of breach and cancellation](../../templates/notice-of-breach-and-cancellation.md) and [consumer withdrawal notice](../../templates/consumer-withdrawal-notice.md) templates.
- The [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md) page for periods and caps.
- 2.5 hours: 40 minutes per case for analysis, 30 for each letter, 20 for checking and the sending record.

### Supplied case 1: the late sofa

Nirit Cohen, Netanya, ordered a made-to-order sofa from a furniture shop on 1 August 2026 for ₪9,600, paid ₪3,000 deposit. The order form says "delivery within 6 weeks". On 30 September there is still no sofa and the shop answers "next month, maybe".

### Supplied case 2: the online heater

Fadi Nasser, 67, Shefa-'Amr, ordered an electric heater for ₪850 from a website after a phone call with a sales rep, on 5 October 2026. It arrived on 9 October. He does not like it, has not used it, and it is in its box. The site's terms say "no returns on sale items".

### Steps

1. **Classify each case.** Breach (Remedies Law) or consumer withdrawal (Consumer Protection Law or regulations)? Could both apply?
2. **Check the period.** For withdrawal: which route (s. 14ג, s. 14ג1, or the regulations), the start date, the last day. For breach: was the breach fundamental? If not, what extension is reasonable?
3. **Check exceptions.** Made-to-order goods, perishables, opened media, and so on.
4. **Decide the remedy.** Extension then cancellation; immediate cancellation; withdrawal with or without fee.
5. **Calculate money.** Refund due; any lawful fee; damages you will reserve.
6. **Fill the template.** One letter per case.
7. **Prepare the sending record:** method, date, proof, follow-up dates.
8. **Plan the follow-up:** what you will do on the day after the refund or extension deadline (a reminder, a complaint to the Consumer Protection and Fair Trade Authority, a small claim).

### Template

- [Notice of breach and cancellation](../../templates/notice-of-breach-and-cancellation.md): part A (breach and extension), part B (cancellation for breach), part C (cancellation for a defect in formation).
- [Consumer withdrawal notice](../../templates/consumer-withdrawal-notice.md).

### How you know it worked

- Each letter has an operative sentence ("הנני נותן/ת ארכה" / "הנני מבטל/ת").
- Every period is calculated with a start date, an end date and its source.
- You have proof of sending and a dated follow-up plan.

<details><summary>Model answer, case 1: the late sofa</summary>

**Classification.** A breach of the delivery term. Consumer withdrawal is doubtful: made-to-order goods are excluded from the regulations (reg. 6(א)(2)) and, if ordered remotely, from s. 14ג (s. 14ג(ד)(4)). The Remedies Law is therefore the main route.

**Fundamental?** Delivery was "within 6 weeks", so by 12 September. A delay of under three weeks for furniture may not be fundamental, and the shop's "maybe next month" is close to anticipatory breach (s. 17). Safer course: an extension.

**Letter 1 (30 September), part A:** facts, the delivery term, "הנני נותנת לכם ארכה לאספקת הספה עד ליום 14.10.2026. אם הספה לא תסופק עד מועד זה, החוזה יבוטל ללא הודעה נוספת ואדרוש את השבת המקדמה בסך 3,000 ₪ ופיצויים."

**If no delivery by 14 October, letter 2 (15 October), part B:** "הנני מבטלת בזאת את החוזה בשל הפרתו (סעיפים 7(ב) ו־8 לחוק החוזים (תרופות בשל הפרת חוזה))", demand refund of ₪3,000 within 7 days, reserve damages (for example the higher price of a replacement sofa, s. 10).

**Sending:** registered mail to the shop's address on the order form, plus email and WhatsApp to the shop's business number. Follow-up: 23 October, small claim in Netanya if no refund.

</details>

<details><summary>Model answer, case 2: the online heater</summary>

**Classification.** A distance sale (website after a phone call). Fadi is 67, a senior citizen, and the sale involved a conversation with the dealer, so s. 14ג1(ג) gives him the extended period; in any case he is within the ordinary period of s. 14ג(ג)(1) from receipt on 9 October. No exception applies (not perishable, not made to order).

**"No returns on sale items".** A dealer cannot contract out of the statutory right; the term is also misleading if presented as the law.

**Money.** Not a defect or breach, so the dealer may deduct a fee: the lower of 5% of the price and ₪100, here 5% of ₪850 = ₪42.50. Refund ₪807.50 within 14 days of receiving the notice, to the card used. Fadi returns the heater to the dealer's place of business (s. 14ה(ב)(2)) or as agreed; many sites arrange courier collection.

**Letter (template, filled):** order number and date, "הנני מבטל בזאת את העסקה בהתאם לסעיפים 14ג ו־14ג1 לחוק הגנת הצרכן", the heater unused in its original box, request for refund within 14 days less a fee of no more than ₪42.50, a copy of his ID showing age only if the dealer asks (s. 14ג1(ד)).

**Sending:** through the site's cancellation form and by email, keep screenshots. Follow-up: if no refund by the 15th day, complaint to the Consumer Protection and Fair Trade Authority and a request to the credit card company to cancel the charge, then small claims.

</details>

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Breach and cancellation notices and consumer withdrawals are exactly what a private person does alone, in their own name. A company you own may send its own notices through its manager; representing other people for a fee is reserved to lawyers.

### Where and how

- Consumer complaints: the Consumer Protection and Fair Trade Authority's online complaint form (gov.il).
- Credit card disputes: your card company's chargeback procedure for an undelivered or cancelled transaction.
- Small claims: individuals may claim up to the ceiling in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); module 8 and module 12 cover filing.
- Free help: Kol-Zchut pages on cancelling transactions; consumer organisations (see [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md)).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You want to cancel an apartment purchase, a large renovation or a business contract.
- The other side answers with its own cancellation notice and claims you breached first.
- An agreed-damages or deposit-forfeiture clause of a large sum is in play.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phoning instead of writing | No proof of notice or of its date | Send a written notice now | Always write; call only to follow up |
| Cancelling without an extension for a doubtful breach | You become the party in breach | Send a correcting notice giving an extension | Use part A first when in doubt |
| Using the item after withdrawing from an in-store purchase | Right lost (reg. 2: unused goods only) | Return it at once; argue minimal handling | Do not use it; keep the original packaging |
| Accepting a credit voucher when a cash refund is due | You lose your money's flexibility | Ask for a refund in the original payment method | Know s. 14ה and reg. 4 before going to the shop |
| Missing the withdrawal period by a day | No statutory right | Ask for goodwill; check for a breach or defect route | Count from the receipt date and diarise it |

## Review

- Notices are the tools of contract law: send them in the agreed way or by registered mail or a customary method, and keep proof (s. 21).
- Breach: extension first unless clearly fundamental; then a clear cancellation notice within a reasonable time.
- Consumer withdrawal has three routes: distance sales (s. 14ג), the extended period for seniors, people with disabilities and new immigrants (s. 14ג1), and the in-store regulations; each has exceptions.
- After withdrawal the dealer refunds within a fixed period and may charge only a capped fee, and none if the cancellation is due to its breach (s. 14ה).
- Plan the follow-up before you send: reminder, regulator complaint, chargeback, small claim.
