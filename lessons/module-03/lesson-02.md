# 03.2 · Offer, acceptance and certainty; preliminary agreements and זיכרון דברים

By the end of this lesson you will be able to look at a chain of messages, a signed "memorandum" or an online checkout and say whether a contract exists, when it was made and on what terms. You will also know how to negotiate without binding yourself too early, which is the single most useful protection a private person has before buying a car, renting a flat or hiring a contractor.

## Why it matters

Many disputes are really about one question: was there a deal? The seller who "changed his mind" after a handshake, the tenant who signed a one-page memorandum and then found a better flat, the shop that refuses to honour a mistaken online price. Israeli law answers with a short set of rules (ss. 1–11 of the Contracts Law) and one demanding idea from the case law: a binding agreement needs both an intent to be bound (גמירת דעת) and enough certainty (מסוימות) to be enforced. Israeli courts are known for finding binding contracts in short documents. If you sign a זיכרון דברים thinking it is a draft, you may already be bound.

## The law

### Offer

> פנייתו של אדם לחברו היא בגדר הצעה, אם היא מעידה על גמירת דעתו של המציע להתקשר עם הניצע בחוזה והיא מסויימת כדי אפשרות לכרות את החוזה בקיבול ההצעה; הפנייה יכול שתהיה לציבור.

*Gist:* A communication is an offer if it shows the sender's intent to be bound and is certain enough that a simple "yes" makes a contract; an offer may be made to the public. [s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28חלק_כללי%29#סעיף_2)

### Withdrawal and lapse

> המציע רשאי לחזור בו מן ההצעה בהודעה לניצע, ובלבד שהודעת החזרה נמסרה לניצע לפני שנתן הודעת קיבול.

*Gist:* The offeror may withdraw the offer if the withdrawal reaches the offeree before the offeree sends acceptance; an offer stated to be irrevocable, or with a fixed acceptance period, cannot be withdrawn once received (s. 3(ב)). [s. 3](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28חלק_כללי%29#סעיף_3)

An offer lapses when rejected or when the acceptance period ends (s. 4), and a late acceptance is only a new offer (s. 9). With no period stated, acceptance must come within a reasonable time (s. 8(א)).

### Acceptance

> הקיבול יהיה בהודעת הניצע שנמסרה למציע והמעידה על גמירת דעתו של הניצע להתקשר עם המציע בחוזה לפי ההצעה.

*Gist:* Acceptance is a notice, delivered to the offeror, showing the offeree's intent to contract on the offer's terms. [s. 5](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28חלק_כללי%29#סעיף_5)

> (ב) קביעת המציע שהעדר תגובה מצד הניצע ייחשב לקיבול, אין לה תוקף.

*Gist:* Acceptance may be by conduct if the offer implies it (s. 6(א)), but an offeror cannot declare that silence counts as acceptance. [s. 6](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28חלק_כללי%29#סעיף_6)

> קיבול שיש בו תוספת, הגבלה או שינוי אחר לעומת ההצעה, כמוהו כהצעה חדשה.

*Gist:* An acceptance that adds, limits or changes anything is a counter-offer, not an acceptance. [s. 11](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28חלק_כללי%29#סעיף_11)

### Filling gaps

> פרטים שלא נקבעו בחוזה או על פיו יהיו לפי הנוהג הקיים בין הצדדים, ובאין נוהג כזה – לפי הנוהג המקובל בחוזים מאותו סוג, ויראו גם פרטים אלה כמוסכמים.

*Gist:* Missing details are filled from the parties' own practice, or else from the usual practice in contracts of that type. [s. 26](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28חלק_כללי%29#סעיף_26) Sections 41 (time), 44 (place), 45 (average quality) and 46 (reasonable price) supply more defaults. Gap-filling is why a contract can be certain enough even if it does not say everything; it cannot replace an essential term the parties never agreed on.

## Leading cases

### ע״א 692/86 בוטקובסקי ושות׳ – חברה לייבוא ושיווק בע״מ נ׳ גת, פ״ד מד(1) 57 (1989)

The court asked whether a preliminary document was a binding contract. It held that intent to be bound is judged objectively, by what a reasonable person would understand from the parties' outward expressions and the circumstances, not by what each party privately thought; a memorandum can be binding even where the parties planned to sign a fuller contract later. Whether it binds depends on the document's completeness and the conduct around it.

Why it matters: it is the starting point courts still cite on זיכרון דברים. "We meant to sign a proper contract later" is not a defence by itself. Free full text: search the case number at [the Judiciary's decision search](https://supremedecisions.court.gov.il) (older rulings may not be online; the holding is widely quoted in later free rulings).

### ע״א 6370/00 קל בנין בע״מ נ׳ ע.ר.מ. רעננה לבניה והשכרה בע״מ (2002)

A developer's private tender led to full agreement on terms "subject to board approval"; the board then chose a contractor who had not bid. The majority held that no binding contract had been formed between the parties, but that the developer had negotiated in bad faith (s. 12). The minority would have found a binding contract within the tender framework.

Why it matters: it shows the line between "agreed but not yet bound" and "bound", and that a party who keeps the other out of a contract unfairly may still pay damages. [English translation (portions)](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/219)

## How it works in practice

### Offer or invitation?

| Communication | Usually | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Goods on a shelf or online with a price and "add to cart" | An offer to the public, accepted at checkout | Intent and certainty are clear (s. 2 allows public offers) |
| A newspaper or Yad2 advert "apartment for sale, ₪1.9m" | Invitation to negotiate | Too many essential terms open (payment schedule, date, condition) |
| A written quote from a contractor with price, scope and dates | Offer | A "yes" is enough to make a contract |
| A tender notice | Invitation to bid; the bids are offers | The tender documents may also create a separate "tender contract" on fair procedure |

### When is a memorandum binding?

Courts look at two things together:

- **Intent to be bound.** Signatures, a deposit paid, the word "agreement" rather than "draft", conduct after signing (taking keys, reporting to the tax authority), whether lawyers were still negotiating.
- **Certainty of essential terms.** For an apartment sale: parties, property, price, payment schedule, delivery date. Missing secondary terms can be filled (s. 26); a missing price usually cannot, unless a mechanism to fix it is agreed.

A clause such as "this memorandum is not binding and no party is bound until a detailed contract is signed" is strong evidence against intent. Its absence, plus a deposit, is strong evidence for it.

### Online contracts

An online checkout usually works as follows: the listed product is the offer; your click on "place order" is acceptance by conduct (s. 6(א)); the contract is made when the acceptance reaches the seller (s. 60(ב): a notice is given when it reaches the addressee or their address). Pricing errors are dealt with under mistake (s. 14), not formation (lesson 03.5).

## Worked example

Lina Haddad, a nurse from Haifa, wants to buy a used car from Moti Ben-Shimon, a private seller in Kiryat Ata. Their messages:

- **Sun 10:00, Moti:** "Mazda 3, 2019, ₪62,000, test valid until March."
- **Sun 12:00, Lina:** "₪58,000 and you replace the tyres?"
- **Sun 15:00, Moti:** "₪60,000, tyres as they are. Offer valid until Tuesday evening."
- **Mon 20:00, Lina:** "Agreed. I'll transfer ₪5,000 deposit tomorrow, rest at the Post Office transfer of ownership on Thursday."
- **Mon 21:00, Moti:** "Someone offered ₪63,000, sorry, I'm withdrawing."

Step by step:

1. **Sunday 10:00.** A price and a car. Possibly an offer, but Lina did not accept it; her reply changed the price and added tyres, so it was a counter-offer (s. 11) that ended Moti's first message (s. 4(1)).
2. **Sunday 15:00.** Moti's message is a certain offer (car, price, condition) with a fixed acceptance period. Under s. 3(ב) he could not withdraw it before Tuesday evening.
3. **Monday 20:00.** Lina's "Agreed" accepts. The deposit and transfer schedule are performance details, not new conditions; a court could still read them as a counter-offer if Moti had said nothing about payment, but they follow the usual practice for car sales (s. 26), so the better view is acceptance.
4. **Monday 21:00.** Too late: a contract was made when Lina's message reached Moti (s. 60(ב)), and the offer was irrevocable anyway.
5. **Result.** Lina has a binding contract. Her remedies (lesson 03.8) include enforcement, or cancellation and damages. She should reply in writing that she holds Moti to the contract and is ready to pay.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- One negotiation from your own life, finished or ongoing: a message thread, emails or a signed memorandum. If you have none, use the Lina and Moti thread above with one change of your own.
- A printout or screenshots with timestamps.
- 50 minutes.

### Steps

1. Number every message in order with date, time and sender.
2. For each message, label it: invitation, offer, counter-offer, acceptance, withdrawal, or none of these. Write the section that supports the label.
3. Find the moment a contract was made, if one was, and say why (s. 5 or s. 6, plus s. 60(ב) for timing).
4. List the essential terms (parties, subject, price, time). Mark any that are missing and say whether s. 26 or ss. 41–46 can fill them.
5. If there is a memorandum, list the signs for and against intent to be bound.
6. Write a two-line conclusion: "A contract was / was not made on [date] on these terms…"
7. Draft one sentence you could have added to keep yourself unbound until a full contract (for example: "הצעה זו אינה מחייבת ואינה מהווה חוזה; הצדדים יהיו מחויבים רק עם חתימת חוזה מפורט").

### How you know it worked

- Every label has a section number next to it.
- You found a single moment of formation, or explained why there is none.
- Your "subject to contract" sentence is clear enough that a stranger would not read it as a commitment.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes, for your own negotiations: offers, acceptances and simple written agreements for a car, a service, a rental. Drafting agreements for others is reserved to lawyers (s. 20 of the Bar Association Law).

### Where and how

- Keep every message: courts accept WhatsApp and email threads as evidence of offer and acceptance.
- If you think a contract was made and the other side backs out, send a short written notice that you hold them to it (lesson 03.10 has the format).
- Disputes over formation are civil claims: small claims for low amounts, magistrates' court above the ceiling (see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- Before signing any זיכרון דברים for land or an apartment. In practice sellers and buyers of apartments are almost always represented, and a memorandum can bind you to a purchase (and to tax reporting deadlines).
- A tender or business deal is involved and the other side argues no contract was formed.
- The amount is near or above the small-claims ceiling and formation is the whole dispute.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Signing a memorandum "just to hold the deal" | You are bound to a purchase on incomplete terms | Get advice quickly; check whether intent and certainty were really present | Write "not binding until a detailed contract is signed" or do not sign |
| Answering "yes, but…" and assuming a deal exists | No contract: your reply was a counter-offer | Ask the other side to confirm the new terms in writing | Accept exactly, or say clearly you are making a new offer |
| Treating silence as agreement | No contract (s. 6(ב)) | Get an express acceptance | Require a written "agreed" |
| Withdrawing a fixed-period offer early | You may be bound anyway | Honour it or negotiate a release | Do not give fixed periods you are not willing to keep |
| Losing the timestamps | You cannot prove the order of messages | Export the chat with dates | Export and save threads as soon as a dispute looks possible |

## Review

- An offer needs intent to be bound and enough certainty that a "yes" makes a contract (s. 2).
- Acceptance must match the offer exactly; any change is a counter-offer (s. 11); silence is not acceptance (s. 6(ב)).
- An offer with a fixed period or stated to be irrevocable cannot be withdrawn once received (s. 3(ב)).
- A memorandum is binding if, judged objectively, the parties intended to be bound and agreed the essential terms (Botkovsky).
- Gap-filling rules (ss. 26, 41–46) complete a contract but cannot invent essential terms never agreed.
