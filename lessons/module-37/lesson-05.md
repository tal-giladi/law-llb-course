# 37.5 · Deleting a police record

Lab [24.10](../module-24/lesson-10.md) taught the routes for cleaning a record and the s. 33 cancellation request. In this clinic lesson you run a real record matter of your own, or Ahmad Darawsha's, from the decision that made the entry matter to the confirmation that it is gone: inspection, a request to change the closure ground where the facts support it, a cancellation request with evidence for each factor, the follow-up, and what to do if the answer is no.

## Why it matters

> [!WARNING]
> Act only on your own record, and only by the lawful routes. Do not ask anyone to "arrange" a record informally, do not offer anything to an official, and do not misstate facts: a request is checked against the file, and a false statement in it may be an offence. If the entry concerns a pending investigation or trial, stop and talk to a lawyer.

Most people discover a closed file when something goes wrong: a licence application, a job with an entitled employer, a foster-care or adoption check, a visa. At that point time matters more than law. The routes are free and need no lawyer, but they work only if you ask early, address what the decision-maker must consider, and attach proof. This lesson adds the timing and the follow-up that lab 24.10 left out.

## The law

### Inspect, correct, cancel

> כל אדם זכאי לעיין במידע הפלילי על עצמו.

*Gist:* Everyone may inspect his own criminal information, on screen at a police station, after identification; notes are allowed, photographs are not. [Criminal Information Law s. 4(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המידע_הפלילי_ותקנת_השבים#סעיף_4); [Inspection Regulations](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_המידע_הפלילי_ותקנת_השבים_(דרכי_עיון_במידע_פלילי))

> הוברר כי נפלה טעות ברישומו של פרט רישום פלילי או של פרט רישום משטרתי, תתקן המשטרה את הטעות [...]

*Gist:* The police correct an error in an entry (s. 7).

> ראש אגף החקירות והמודיעין במשטרה או קצין משטרה בדרגת סגן־ניצב ומעלה שהוא הסמיך לכך רשאים לבטל [...]

*Gist:* A senior police officer may cancel a police-register entry about a closed file, under criteria that weigh the time since the event, your age then, your personal circumstances, other criminal information and the nature and seriousness of the offences; a non-felony closed-file entry is cancelled automatically after a set period from the event (s. 33; periods in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)). [s. 33](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המידע_הפלילי_ותקנת_השבים#סעיף_33)

A file closed for lack of guilt does not stay in the police register; you may ask the prosecutor who closed a file to change its ground (CPL s. 62; lesson 24.10).

### Who sees the entry, and who oversees them

Entitled bodies receive criminal information only for the purposes the Law sets. The licensing authority, for example, receives information about applicants for driving and public-transport licences, in some categories only with the applicant's consent (s. 16; ⚠️ check which register categories reach the authority in your case). The Ministry of Justice inspector of criminal information examines how entitled bodies use and keep the information and how they exercise discretion on it:

> ערך הממונה על הביקורת בירור, בין מיוזמתו ובין על פי תלונה שהוגשה לו, ומצא כי התלונה מוצדקת [...]

*Gist:* The inspector investigates on his own initiative or on a complaint, and if the complaint is justified may order the body to correct the defects; the complaint procedure is published on the Ministry's website. [s. 41](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המידע_הפלילי_ותקנת_השבים)

## Leading cases

### Key text: s. 33 and the missing criteria

There is little published case law on the 2019 Law, and none on s. 33 requests that could be verified on a free source (lesson [24.8](../module-24/lesson-08.md)). The ministerial criteria under s. 33(א) were not found on a free source when this lesson was checked (⚠️ manual check on the police website). Until you find them, write to the statutory factors in s. 33(ב) one by one: they are the minimum the officer must weigh, and a request that addresses each with a document is hard to refuse without reasons.

### Key text: the Decisions and Reasons Law

A cancellation request is a written request to a public official. It must be decided and answered with reasons within the period of Decisions and Reasons Law s. 2, and long silence counts as a refusal without reasons (s. 6; lesson [14.4](../module-14/lesson-04.md)). That gives you a date to chase, and a basis for an Ombudsman complaint about delay (lesson 37.6).

## How it works in practice

### Timing to the decision

| Situation | Do first | Then |
|---|---|---|
| No decision pending; you just want a clean record | Inspect; file the request | Re-inspect after the answer |
| An application is about to be filed (licence, job, foster care) | Inspect now; file the record request before or with the application | Tell the deciding body in writing that a cancellation request is pending, with a copy |
| The deciding body has already raised the entry | Ask it for time and for the specific concern; file the record request at once | Consider a lawyer: you may need to argue the entry before that body too |
| The deciding body refused because of the entry | File the record request; ask the body to reconsider when it is cancelled | Complaint to the inspector if it used information it should not have; lawyer for a petition |

### Two requests, one file

Where the facts support it, send two letters on the same day: to the prosecutor who closed the file, asking to change the ground to lack of guilt (because the evidence shows you did not commit the offence); and to the police, asking to cancel the entry under s. 33 (in any case). The first, if granted, removes the entry; the second does not depend on proving innocence. Tell each addressee about the other request.

### Following up

- Diarise the answer date under the Decisions and Reasons Law; then a reminder letter quoting the file number.
- On a grant: re-inspect at a station after a few weeks and confirm the entry is gone; keep the letter.
- On a refusal: read the reasons. If a factor was ignored or a fact misread, write a short reconsideration request; if time is the reason, diarise when to ask again (and when automatic cancellation will apply); if an entitled body used information it was not entitled to, complain to the inspector (s. 41); if the refusal is arbitrary and the stakes are high, see a lawyer about a petition.

## Worked example

Ahmad Darawsha (supplied case D), 27, from Iksal, applies in October 2026 for a public-transport driver's licence to drive a taxi. He knows of one old file: in March 2020, after a football match in Nazareth, he and another fan were both questioned about a fight. He says the other fan attacked him and he only pushed him away; a bystander's video shows the other man throwing the first punch.

1. **Inspection.** At the station on 5 October he sees one entry: event 14.3.2020, offence "assault" (a misdemeanour), file closed "insufficient evidence". He notes the file number and the closing prosecutor (police prosecution, Northern District). No other entries.
2. **Routes.** A non-felony entry would be cancelled automatically only after the s. 33(ג) period from the event, in 2027 (see current numbers): too late for his application. He chooses two requests: a change of closure ground (the video supports lack of guilt), and a s. 33(א) cancellation.
3. **Change of ground.** To the police prosecution unit named in the file: the file number; the video (he obtained a copy from the bystander, who posted it publicly at the time); the other man's first punch; a request to change the ground to lack of guilt; a request to see the investigation material if the prosecutor doubts the video.
4. **Cancellation.** To the police Investigations and Intelligence Department, on the [cancellation request template](../../templates/police-record-cancellation-request.md), addressing each factor: time (six and a half years without any entry); age (20 at the time); circumstances (eight years of work as a delivery driver, a letter from his employer, the licence application and the harm a refusal would cause); other information (none); seriousness (a misdemeanour, a mutual scuffle, closed without indictment, the video). Copy of his ID; proof of sending.
5. **Application.** He files the licence application and attaches a short letter: a cancellation request is pending, copy enclosed.
6. **Follow-up.** No answer after six weeks; he sends a reminder citing the Decisions and Reasons Law. In January 2027 the police notify him that the entry was cancelled; the prosecution answers that, in view of the cancellation, the ground request is moot. He re-inspects in February: no entries. He sends the letter to the licensing authority.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- Your ID; an hour at a police station during inspection hours.
- The decision the entry affects, if any (application, offer, letter).
- Documents for each factor: employer or study letters, volunteering, family circumstances, the harm the entry causes.
- The [cancellation and change-of-ground template](../../templates/police-record-cancellation-request.md) and your case file.
- About 120 minutes, plus the station visit and waiting.

### Steps

1. **Inspect (at the station).** For each entry: register, file number, event date, offence and class, outcome, closure ground, closing body. Notes only.
2. **Route per entry (15 min).** Use the route finder in lesson 24.10: correction, change of ground, s. 33 cancellation, automatic cancellation date, or wait for limitation. Write the reason.
3. **Timing (10 min).** Using the timing table, write what you must file first and whom you will tell that a request is pending.
4. **Evidence (30 min).** One document per factor; a copy of your ID.
5. **Draft (40 min).** The cancellation request addressing every s. 33(ב) factor separately; the change-of-ground request if the facts support lack of guilt.
6. **Send and log (10 min).** To the addresses or online channel on the police website and to the prosecutor named in the file; proof of sending; answer date diarised.
7. **Close (15 min, after the answer).** Re-inspect; record the result; on a refusal, choose the next step from "Following up".

### Template

[Request to cancel a police-register entry and to change a closure ground](../../templates/police-record-cancellation-request.md) (lessons 24.8, 24.10).

### How you know it worked

- Every entry has a route and, where relevant, an automatic cancellation date.
- Your request has one paragraph and one annex per factor.
- You re-inspected after the answer and recorded what the screen shows.

### Self-check

- [ ] I filed the record request before or with the application the entry affects.
- [ ] I asked for a change of ground only where the evidence supports lack of guilt.
- [ ] I know that convictions are not cancelled by request.
- [ ] I know when to complain to the inspector of criminal information and when to see a lawyer.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Inspection is personal (s. 4); correction, cancellation and change-of-ground requests are made by the person the entry is about; a complaint to the inspector of criminal information is open to the person harmed. A lawyer with a special power of attorney can obtain a printout for representation before an entitled body (s. 4), which helps if a decision is already pending. Requests for others as an occupation are reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20). See [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md).

### Where and how

- Inspection: any police station, published hours.
- Cancellation: the police Investigations and Intelligence Department, form and channel on the police website (⚠️ manual check).
- Change of ground: the prosecutor named in the file.
- Inspector of criminal information: the Ministry of Justice complaint service, <https://www.gov.il/he/service/complaint-criminal-record> (⚠️ manual check).
- Periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); free help: rehabilitation and criminal-law clinics at the universities ([where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md)).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The entry concerns a pending investigation or trial, or a stayed file (the Attorney General's consent is needed).
- An entitled body has already refused you because of the entry and you want to challenge that decision.
- The police refuse without addressing the factors and the stakes justify a petition.
- The entry is a conviction you want shortened (a request to the President, s. 25).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Filing the licence application first and the record request later | The entry is weighed before the request is decided | Ask the deciding body to wait; send a copy of the request | Record request first, or together |
| Asking for "lack of guilt" without evidence | Refused; credibility harmed | Rely on the s. 33 request | Ask for a change of ground only with proof |
| Not re-inspecting | You assume the entry is gone when it is not | Inspect again; write to the police | Diarise a re-inspection |
| A generic letter with no documents | Refused for lack of grounds | Resubmit factor by factor | One paragraph and one annex per factor |
| Treating the inspector as an appeal from the police | The complaint is outside his role | Use reconsideration or a lawyer | The inspector oversees entitled bodies' use of information |

## Review

- Inspect your own information first; every route depends on the exact entry.
- Time the record request to the decision it affects, and tell the deciding body it is pending.
- Where evidence supports it, ask both for a change of ground and for s. 33 cancellation, factor by factor with documents.
- Confirm the result by re-inspection; on a refusal, reconsider, wait for the automatic date, complain to the inspector about misuse, or see a lawyer.
