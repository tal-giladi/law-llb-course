# 37.6 · A complaint to an oversight body

Lesson [14.8](../module-14/lesson-08.md) taught the Ombudsman as a remedy and lesson [01.6](../module-01/lesson-06.md) the limits of the Judicial Ombudsman. In this clinic lesson you file a real complaint of your own (or one of the two supplied ones) with the body that fits: the Ombudsman for a ministry, local authority or other audited body that delays or ignores you; the Judicial Ombudsman for a judge's conduct, including delay in giving judgment; the Police Investigations Department for a suspected offence by a police officer; and the smaller bodies around them. You screen the complaint, write it so that it is investigated, protect your other deadlines, and use the result.

## Why it matters

> [!WARNING]
> A complaint against a police officer about an offence (violence, false arrest, a false report) is a criminal complaint against a named or identifiable person. Report what you saw yourself, keep medical and other evidence, and do not publish accusations. If you were arrested or charged in the same event, talk to a lawyer before you file: your complaint and your own case affect each other.

Oversight bodies are the cheapest remedies in the system: free, informal, run by staff who can demand documents. They fix delay, silence, run-arounds and rudeness that no court would hear. They also have hard edges: none of them changes a judgment; most cannot order a body to act; none extends any deadline; and each declines complaints outside its narrow statutory scope. The skill is to pick the right one, pass its screening on the first try, and keep your legal remedies running beside it.

## The law

### The Ombudsman (State Comptroller Law)

Anyone may complain (s. 33), in writing or orally, signed, with name and address (s. 34), about an act, omission or delay of an audited body that harms him directly and is unlawful, without authority, contrary to proper administration, excessively rigid or flagrantly unjust (s. 37). Judicial acts and matters pending in court are excluded (s. 38); a complaint where an objection or appeal route exists, or more than a year late, is investigated only for a special reason (s. 39). Findings give no new right, a complaint extends no deadline, and no court reviews the findings (s. 45). Details and quotes: lesson 14.8. ([State Comptroller Law](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מבקר_המדינה))

### The Judicial Ombudsman

> התלונה תוגש בכתב תיחתם בידי המתלונן ויצוינו בה שם המתלונן ומענו.

*Gist:* A complaint about a judge is in writing, signed, with the complainant's name and address (s. 15). A person harmed by a judge's conduct in office, including the way the judge runs the trial, may complain (s. 14(א)). "Judge" includes registrars and the judges of the religious courts (s. 1). [Judicial Ombudsman Law](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_נציב_תלונות_הציבור_על_שופטים)

> הנציב לא יברר תלונות כמפורט להלן, אלא אם כן מצא הנציב שקיימת סיבה מיוחדת המצדיקה את בירורן:

*Gist:* Without a special reason, the Ombudsman will not examine a complaint filed more than a year after the act, after you learned of it, or after the judgment (s. 18(א)). Section 17 excludes complaints on substantive legal or evidentiary questions, on matters that can be appealed (except a judge's conduct), recusal requests, and conduct under police investigation or disciplinary charge. If a complaint is justified, the Ombudsman may point out the defect and recommend how to correct it, and may refer the matter to the Minister and the President of the Supreme Court, or recommend disciplinary or tenure steps (ss. 22–23). A complaint does not stay the court case.

### The Police Investigations Department

> על אף האמור בכל דין, חקירה של עבירה כמשמעותה בתוספת הראשונה, אשר בביצועה חשוד שוטר, לא תיערך בידי משטרת ישראל [...]

*Gist:* Offences in which a police officer is a suspect are investigated not by the police but by a department of the Ministry of Justice, which has the powers of the police (Police Ordinance ss. 49ט, 49י). [Police Ordinance](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_המשטרה) The First Schedule covers every offence except, among others, offences punishable by up to one year (unless the State Attorney and the Inspector General decide otherwise), traffic offences, and offences another authority investigates. A complaint about rudeness, delay or a refusal to take a complaint, with no offence, goes instead to the police public-complaints unit or the Ombudsman. If the department closes your complaint, the closure-and-appeal rules of CPL ss. 63–65 apply (lesson 37.3; ⚠️ check the appeal address on the notice).

### The inspector of criminal information

Complaints about how an entitled body used or kept your criminal information go to the Ministry of Justice inspector, who may order corrections (Criminal Information Law s. 41; lesson [37.5](lesson-05.md)).

## Leading cases

### Key text: no judicial review of the Ombudsman (s. 45(ב))

Because no court reviews the Ombudsman's decisions on a complaint, there is almost no case law on its procedure, and the Judicial Ombudsman Law gives its Ombudsman a similar informal role. The rules are statutory. What you can do with a justified finding is use it: as an independent record that a defect existed, attached to a later petition, claim or request for costs (lesson 14.8).

### Key text: the oversight body is not an appeal

The Judicial Ombudsman Law draws the line expressly: conduct, including the management of the trial and delay, is examined; the merits of a decision are not (s. 17). Lesson 01.6 made the same point from the court map. In a complaint, write about what the judge did or failed to do (refused to let you speak, delayed the judgment for months), not about why the judgment is wrong. For the merits, appeal.

## How it works in practice

### Which body?

| Your grievance | Body | Can it order a correction? | Time rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| A ministry, local authority, NII, government company: delay, silence, run-around, rigid refusal | Ombudsman (after writing to the body) | No (findings and follow-up; binding orders only for whistleblowers) | One year, then a special reason |
| A judge or registrar: delay in giving a judgment, rude or one-sided conduct, refusing to hear you | Judicial Ombudsman | No (findings, recommendations, referral) | One year from the act, knowledge or judgment |
| A police officer: violence, false arrest, a false report, a threat | Police Investigations Department | It investigates and decides on prosecution | Criminal limitation; complain at once for evidence |
| A police officer: rudeness, refusal to take a complaint, no answer, no offence | Police public-complaints unit; Ombudsman | No | As for the Ombudsman |
| An entitled body misused your criminal information | Inspector of criminal information | Yes, correction orders (s. 41) | None stated; act promptly |
| State lawyers' conduct in court | Commissioner for complaints about State representatives in the courts | No | See the body's site (⚠️ manual check) |

### What makes a complaint investigated

1. **You first wrote to the body**, and attach its answer or record its silence (for the Ombudsman).
2. **The facts are dated and short**: five to ten lines, each with an annex.
3. **The ground is in the statute's words**: delay, omission, contrary to proper administration (Ombudsman); a judge's conduct in office (Judicial Ombudsman); an offence by an officer (department).
4. **The request is concrete**: decide my file by a date; give judgment; investigate the officer; correct the record.
5. **Your other deadlines are diarised separately**: the complaint stops none of them.
6. **Signed, with name and address**, by you; for someone else, only with his written authorisation (Judicial Ombudsman Law s. 14; State Comptroller Law s. 37(1)).

## Worked example

**Rivka Ohayon (supplied case E, first part).** Rivka, 71, from Kiryat Gat, received rent assistance from the Ministry of Construction and Housing for years. In December 2025 the payments stopped. Her calls were answered "your file is being checked". She wrote on 3 March 2026 and on 2 June 2026, asking for a decision; no answer.

1. **Screening.** The Ministry is an audited body (s. 36). The act is an omission and delay that harms her directly (s. 37). No court case (s. 38). Is there an objection route? The Ministry has an appeals committee for decisions on assistance, but there is no decision to appeal; the complaint is about the absence of one. The first letter was ten months after the stop and the complaint is within a year of her letters: in time.
2. **Deadlines.** Three months of silence on a written request is a refusal without reasons (Decisions and Reasons Law s. 6(ב)); she diarises that a petition is possible and asks a clinic whether to file one, but chooses the Ombudsman first because the problem is the silence.
3. **The complaint.** Online, using the [oversight-body complaint](../../templates/oversight-body-complaint.md): the body, her ID and file number at the Ministry, a seven-line timeline, her two letters as annexes, the ground ("מחדל ופיגור בעשייה... בניגוד למינהל תקין"), and the request: a decision within 30 days and, if she is eligible, payment of the arrears.
4. **Result.** Six weeks later the Ombudsman's office tells her the Ministry found a missing form from her landlord, which nobody had asked her for; she sends it; the arrears are paid. The Ombudsman finds the complaint justified and notes the defect in the Ministry's handling. She files the letter with her case file.

**Lior Ben-Ami (supplied case E, second part).** Lior, 40, sued a contractor in the Tel Aviv small-claims court. The hearing was on 20 November 2025; by October 2026, almost eleven months later, no judgment. Two polite requests in the case file ("בקשה למתן פסק דין") produced nothing.

1. **Screening.** The registrar or judge is a "judge" (s. 1). Delay in giving judgment is conduct in office, not a legal question (s. 17). Not appealable (there is nothing to appeal). Within a year of the act? The delay is continuing; the complaint is filed while it continues.
2. **The complaint.** Online in the personal area: the case number, the hearing date, his two requests with their dates, the request: that judgment be given. He writes nothing about who should win.
3. **Result.** The Ombudsman's office asks the court for a response; judgment is given within a month. The Ombudsman finds the complaint justified on the delay.

**A third kind (for comparison).** Yonatan Tesfaye, 22, from Rehovot, says an officer pushed him to the ground during a street check and he was bruised. He photographs the bruises, gets a medical report the same day, writes down the officer's number and the time, and files with the Police Investigations Department through its service page. He is not under arrest and was not charged; had he been, he would have seen a lawyer first. Nothing in his complaint is about the street check's legality, only what the officer did.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- A grievance of your own against a public body, a court or a police officer, or one of the supplied cases.
- Your letters to the body and its answers (or proof of silence); court case number and dates; medical reports and photographs for a police complaint.
- The [oversight-body complaint](../../templates/oversight-body-complaint.md) draft and your case file.
- About 120 minutes, plus waiting.

### Steps

1. **Choose the body (15 min).** Use the table; write one line on why the others do not fit.
2. **Screen (15 min).** For the Ombudsman: ss. 36, 37, 38, 39. For the Judicial Ombudsman: ss. 14, 17, 18. For the department: is a police officer suspected of an offence in the First Schedule? Write a special reason if you are past a year.
3. **Write to the body first (if not done, 15 min).** For the Ombudsman, a short letter to the body asking for a decision by a date; wait for the answer or the date.
4. **Deadlines (10 min).** List every legal deadline in the same matter (objection, appeal, petition, criminal limitation) and diarise them: the complaint stops none.
5. **Draft (40 min).** On the template: the body, your details, the timeline with annexes, the ground in the statute's words, the request, signature with name and address.
6. **File (10 min).** Online where possible; keep the confirmation and the complaint number.
7. **Use the result (15 min, when it arrives).** Log the finding; decide whether it closes the matter, supports a petition or claim, or needs a follow-up letter to the body quoting the finding.

### Template

[Complaint to an oversight body (תלונה לנציב תלונות הציבור / לנציב תלונות הציבור על שופטים / למח״ש)](../../templates/oversight-body-complaint.md) (new in this module). For a complaint about a regulated business, use the [regulator complaint](../../templates/regulator-complaint.md) instead (lesson 12.4).

### How you know it worked

- The body confirmed receipt and opened an examination (no "not within our authority" answer).
- Your complaint asks for one concrete correction.
- Every legal deadline in the matter is in your diary, separate from the complaint.

### Self-check

- [ ] I can say why I chose this body and not another.
- [ ] I wrote about conduct or delay, not about the merits of a judgment.
- [ ] I attached my letters to the body, or proof of its silence.
- [ ] I know that the complaint does not extend any deadline.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. The Ombudsman and the Judicial Ombudsman are designed for the public, free, and require only a signed complaint with name and address (State Comptroller Law s. 34; Judicial Ombudsman Law s. 15). Anyone may complain to the Police Investigations Department as to the police (CPL s. 58). A complaint for another person needs his consent or written authorisation; complaining for others as an occupation for pay is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20). See [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md).

### Where and how

- Channels: the list above ("Channels"); periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); free help: [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).
- Civil-rights NGOs and university clinics help with complaints against police officers; disability and welfare NGOs with complaints against ministries.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You were arrested or charged in the same event as your complaint against an officer.
- You suffered injury or significant loss and may claim damages from the State (limitation and notice rules apply).
- A legal deadline in the same matter is close and the stakes are high: file the objection, appeal or petition first.
- You are a public employee facing retaliation for reporting corruption (the whistleblower route, lesson 14.8, and labour-court claims).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Complaining to the Judicial Ombudsman that the judgment is wrong | Not examined (s. 17) | Appeal within its period | Write about conduct and delay only |
| Ombudsman complaint without first writing to the body | Sent back, or slower | Write to the body; refile with its answer | Letter first, complaint second |
| Treating the complaint as stopping an appeal period | Appeal or petition lost | File the appeal or petition now | Diarise legal deadlines separately |
| Complaining to the police about an officer's offence | The police may not investigate it (s. 49ט) | Refile with the Police Investigations Department | Use the department for offences by officers |
| A long, angry complaint with no request | Hard to act on; may be dismissed as vexatious | Rewrite: timeline, ground, one request | Five to ten dated lines and one request |

## Review

- Pick the body by the grievance: Ombudsman for public bodies' delay and maladministration; Judicial Ombudsman for a judge's conduct; the Police Investigations Department for offences by officers; the inspector for misuse of criminal information.
- Pass the screening: who, what, exclusions, the one-year rule; write to the body first where required.
- Ask for one concrete correction; attach your letters and dates.
- No oversight body extends a deadline or changes a judgment; run your legal remedies beside it and use its findings afterwards.
