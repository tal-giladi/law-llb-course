# 37.1 · Choosing the matter and building the file

Clinic IV takes real public and criminal matters of your own from the file to the result: an administrative petition against a decision that affects you, a police complaint and its follow-up, a traffic ticket you dispute, a police record you want removed, and a complaint to an oversight body such as the Ombudsman or the Judicial Ombudsman. In this lesson you choose which of them you will run, find the track for each, list the deadlines that close the door, check your criminal exposure before you write a word, and open the file you will carry through lessons 37.2 to 37.8.

## Why it matters

> [!WARNING]
> This module includes police and criminal matters. Run them only in your own name and only on matters where you are the complainant, the person who received a fine, or the person the record is about. If you are a suspect in an open investigation, or anything you would write might admit an offence, stop and get a lawyer or the Public Defender (lesson [24.3](../module-24/lesson-03.md)).

The three earlier clinics (modules [12](../module-12/lesson-01.md), [22](../module-22/lesson-01.md) and [31](../module-31/lesson-01.md)) dealt with private disputes and with registries. Year-4 matters differ in three ways.

First, **the other side holds power over you and holds the file**. The municipality, the police, the prosecution and the court system decide, and they hold the documents. Most of your work is getting the record (the decision, the closure notice, the ticket, the register entry, the court file) and holding the decision-maker to the rules that bind it: authority, relevant considerations, a hearing, reasons, deadlines (module 14).

Second, **the deadlines are short and some are fatal**. An administrative petition must be filed without delay and within a short period from the earliest of publication, notice or knowledge (reg. 3(ב)), and may be dismissed for delay even inside it (reg. 4). An appeal against closing a criminal file runs from delivery of the closure notice (CPL s. 65). A traffic ticket must be answered within periods that differ by track (lesson 37.4). The oversight bodies normally decline complaints that are more than a year old. None of these periods is extended by writing letters, and a complaint to the Ombudsman does not stop any of them (State Comptroller Law s. 45(א)(2)).

Third, **criminal exposure runs both ways**. As a complainant you can be asked about your own conduct; as a driver who disputes a ticket you are a defendant in a criminal or administrative proceeding; as a person asking to delete a record you are describing an old file. Each matter gets an exposure check before any letter.

The theory was taught in modules 5, 6, 14 and 24. This module links back and concentrates on running the file.

## The law

### Your own matter only: ss. 20–21

Only a lawyer may represent another before courts, tribunals and listed offices, draft legal documents for another, or give legal advice as an occupation ([Bar Association Law s. 20](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לשכת_עורכי_הדין)). There is no family exception. In this clinic: your petition, your complaint, your ticket, your record, your oversight complaint. The oversight bodies allow a complaint for someone else with their consent or authorisation (State Comptroller Law s. 37(1); Judicial Ombudsman Law s. 14(א)), but helping a relative that way is not running their case: they sign, and you never do it for pay.

### The deadlines that close the door

> לא נקבע מועד כאמור, תוגש העתירה בלא שיהוי, לפי נסיבות הענין, ולא יאוחר מארבעים וחמישה ימים [...]

*Gist:* Unless a law sets another period, an administrative petition is filed without delay and within the regulation's period from the earliest of publication, notice or knowledge of the decision. [Administrative procedure regs, reg. 3(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_בתי_משפט_לענינים_מינהליים_(סדרי_דין))

> בית המשפט רשאי לדחות עתירה אם ראה כי בנסיבות הענין היה שיהוי בהגשתה [...]

*Gist:* The court may dismiss a petition for delay even when it was filed within the period. [reg. 4](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_בתי_משפט_לענינים_מינהליים_(סדרי_דין))

> מפר רשאי להגיש לגורם מוסמך השגה על החלטה להטיל קנס [...]

*Gist:* Since the Traffic Administrative Violations Law came into force (8 February 2026, in two phases), many traffic tickets are administrative fines: you may object to the authority within a short period from service (longer if you claim someone else drove), and appeal to the new traffic tribunal (s. 19). [Traffic Administrative Violations Law s. 8(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הפרות_תעבורה_מינהליות) Tickets that remain criminal fine offences keep the older route: a cancellation request or a request to be tried within the periods of [CPL s. 229(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_סדר_הדין_הפלילי#סעיף_229) (lesson [24.9](../module-24/lesson-09.md)). Lesson 37.4 shows how to tell the two apart.

The other door-closing periods, all with sources in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md):

- the complainant's appeal (ערר) against closing a criminal file runs from delivery of the s. 63 notice (CPL s. 65; lesson [05.8](../module-05/lesson-08.md));
- the Ombudsman investigates a complaint filed more than a year after the act, or after you learned of it, only for a special reason (State Comptroller Law s. 39; lesson [14.8](../module-14/lesson-08.md));
- the Judicial Ombudsman does the same for complaints more than a year after the act, after you learned of it, or after the judgment (Judicial Ombudsman Law s. 18(א));
- police record entries have no filing deadline, but a pending licensing or employment decision makes the timing urgent (lesson [24.10](../module-24/lesson-10.md)).

### Getting the records

- **Your own criminal information:** "כל אדם זכאי לעיין במידע הפלילי על עצמו" (Criminal Information Law s. 4(א)), on screen at any police station.
- **A closed file:** the closure notice must give the ground (CPL s. 63); you may ask to see the investigation material for an appeal (lesson 05.8).
- **An administrative decision:** a written answer with reasons (Decisions and Reasons Law s. 2; lesson [14.4](../module-14/lesson-04.md)) and the documents through an FOI request (lesson [14.7](../module-14/lesson-07.md)); information about yourself is free of the application fee (see current numbers).
- **A traffic ticket:** under the administrative track you may review the file (s. 36 of the 2024 Law); under the criminal track, ask the prosecutor for the evidence (photo, radar printout) before the trial.

## Leading cases

### בג״ץ 9029/16 אבירם נ׳ שרת המשפטים (1.2.2017)

Self-represented petitioners raised an important question about the judicial-appointments process and lost on delay. The Court applied the three-part test: subjective delay (the petitioner sat on his rights), objective delay (others relied on the decision), and the weight of the rule-of-law interest. Why it matters here: in a clinic file the most common way to lose a good public-law case is to spend the petition period on letters. Your deadline diary is part of the merits. [English translation](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/aviram-v-minister-justice)

### רע״א 8120/08 (no "close friend" in court)

Absent an express statutory provision, only a licensed lawyer may represent a party in court; there is no general right to a relative or "close friend" as representative. Why it matters here: when your father asks you to "deal with" his traffic ticket or your sister asks you to argue her petition, you may help them understand and prepare, but they sign and they appear. [Full text (supremedecisions)](https://supremedecisions.court.gov.il/Home/Download?path=HebrewVerdicts%2F08%2F200%2F081%2Fv04&fileName=08081200_v04.txt&type=2) (⚠️ host unreachable on 18 October 2026; citation from the course research file).

## How it works in practice

### Five matters, five tracks

| Matter | First step and decision-maker | Fatal or near-fatal deadline | Lesson (theory) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Administrative decision against you | Request for reconsideration and prior demand to the decision-maker; then a petition to the district court sitting in administrative matters | Petition period from the earliest of publication, notice or knowledge (reg. 3(ב)); delay (reg. 4) | 37.2 (14.9–14.11) |
| Offence against you | Police complaint (CPL s. 58); then follow-up and, if closed, an appeal (ערר) | Appeal period from delivery of the closure notice (s. 65) | 37.3 (05.7, 05.8) |
| Traffic ticket you dispute | Read the notice: administrative objection (השגה) and tribunal appeal (ערר), or criminal cancellation request and request to be tried | Objection and appeal periods (2024 Law ss. 8, 19); or s. 229(א) periods | 37.4 (24.9) |
| Police record entry | Inspect (s. 4); then correction, change of closure ground or cancellation (s. 33) | None fatal; urgent if a decision is pending | 37.5 (24.8, 24.10) |
| Maladministration, delay, a judge's conduct, a police officer's conduct | Ombudsman; Judicial Ombudsman; the Police Investigations Department for a suspected offence by an officer; other complaint bodies | One year (special reason after that) | 37.6 (14.8, 01.6) |

### Five filters for a Clinic IV matter

| Filter | Pass | Fail: choose another matter or another route |
|---|---|---|
| Yours | A decision addressed to you; an offence against you; a ticket served on you; your own record; your own complaint | Your son's ticket, your neighbour's petition, a complaint "for the street" with no personal harm |
| A known track | You can name the decision-maker and the first document (table above) | You do not know whether the ticket is administrative or criminal: read lesson 37.4 first |
| In time | Every fatal deadline is at least two weeks away | A petition period that ends in five days; a closure notice delivered seven weeks ago |
| Provable | You hold or can order the decision, the closure notice, the ticket and its photo, your register printout | The key document is in the authority's hands and you have not asked for it |
| Low exposure | Nothing you write can be read as an admission of an offence; no investigation of you is open | You were also involved in the fight you complain about; the ticket involves injury, alcohol or disqualification |

### The file

Use the [clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md) from module 12 with the year-2 additions of lesson 22.1 (track sheet, deadline table with proof of each start date, relationship note), and three year-4 additions:

1. **Exposure check.** One line per matter: am I a complainant, a person fined, or a person the record is about? Is any investigation of me open? Does any fact I will write admit an offence? Answer "no" to the last two, or stop and get advice.
2. **Decision-maker map.** For each matter: who decided, under which section, who reviews that decision (the objection body, the appeals authority, the court, the oversight body), and the address and online channel of each.
3. **Official-records log.** Each record you asked for (FOI request, inspection of criminal information, request for investigation material, request for the ticket photo, request for a court protocol), the date, the answer and the next step.

**Dmitri through the five filters.** His: yes, he is the victim (the car is his). Track: police complaint already filed; the next step is the appeal against closing. In time: the closure notice arrived by post on 2 October 2026; he diarises the appeal period from that date, with the envelope as proof. Provable: the dashcam file, the tyre-repair invoice, the neighbour's address. Exposure: low, unless he confronted the neighbour; he did not, and he writes nothing about "what I would have done". The petition period runs from the earliest trigger: she counts from 24 September (receipt), checks that the letter was not published earlier, and diarises the last day with a reminder ten days before. She sends her request for reconsideration on 26 September, knowing it does not stop the clock.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- A list of your dealings with public bodies, the police and the courts in the last year: decisions, refusals, unanswered requests, complaints, tickets, closure notices, anything you saw at a police station or heard in court.
- The documents you hold for each, with envelopes or email headers.
- The [clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md), the [forum check worksheet](../../templates/forum-check-worksheet.md) and the [get-a-lawyer checklist](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md).
- About 90 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Inventory (15 min).** Write down every candidate. Include the quiet ones: a request nobody answered for months, a file you were told was "closed", a ticket in a drawer.
2. **Filters (15 min).** Run each through the five filters; record pass or fail with one reason. Choose at least two matters, one of them an oversight complaint or a record request (low risk, real filing possible).
3. **Track and decision-maker map (15 min).** For each chosen matter: the decision-maker, the section, the reviewer, the first document, the channel.
4. **Deadline diary (15 min).** Every deadline, the event it runs from, the date and the proof. Put each in your calendar with a reminder a week earlier (ten days for a petition).
5. **Exposure check (10 min).** Answer the three questions for each matter in writing.
6. **Records (15 min).** Send what you need today: an FOI or reasons request, a request for the investigation material, an appointment to inspect your criminal information, a request for the ticket photo. Log each.
7. **Lawyer check (5 min).** Run part א of the get-a-lawyer checklist once; note every red answer.

### Template

- [Clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md) (module 12), with the year-2 additions of lesson 22.1 and the exposure check, decision-maker map and official-records log above.
- [Forum check worksheet](../../templates/forum-check-worksheet.md) (lesson 01.7).
- [Get-a-lawyer checklist](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md) (lesson 12.8).

### How you know it worked

- A stranger reading your cover page can say who decided, who reviews, what you will file and by when.
- Every deadline has a start event and a document proving its date.
- Every matter has a written exposure check with no unexplained "yes".

### Self-check

- [ ] Each matter is mine, and I know what any relative must sign himself.
- [ ] I counted the petition period from the earliest trigger, not from the last letter.
- [ ] I know which track my ticket is on, or I have marked it to check in lesson 37.4.
- [ ] I ordered the records before writing to anyone.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes, for every matter in this module, in your own name: an individual may file an administrative petition himself (reg. 44א with Civil Procedure Regulations reg. 170; lesson [14.11](../module-14/lesson-11.md)); anyone may complain to the police (CPL s. 58) and the complainant may appeal a closure (s. 64); the person fined objects, appeals or asks to be tried; the person the record is about inspects it and asks for cancellation (ss. 4, 33); anyone harmed may complain to the Ombudsman and the Judicial Ombudsman. See [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md).

### Where and how

- Channels, forms and fees per matter: lessons 37.2 to 37.6; numbers in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); free help in [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).
- Free help that fits year-4 matters: the Judiciary's information centre (see current numbers); university legal clinics (administrative, criminal and rehabilitation clinics); NGOs in disability, immigration and civil-rights fields; Legal Aid for victims of listed sexual offences and families of homicide victims (lesson 05.7); the Public Defender if you become a defendant facing prison.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You are, or may become, a suspect in the same events.
- A petition needs an interim order within days, or the respondent is represented and raises threshold objections you cannot answer.
- A traffic matter involves injury, alcohol, drugs, driving while disqualified, or a risk of disqualification that threatens your work.
- A record entry is weighed in a decision already pending against you, or concerns a stayed file.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Counting the petition period from the authority's last letter | Late petition, or dismissal for delay | Explain the dates; ask to extend (reg. 3(ג)) | Count from the earliest of publication, notice or knowledge |
| Treating an Ombudsman complaint as a pause | The petition or appeal period passes | File the petition or appeal at once | Remember s. 45(א)(2): no deadline is extended |
| Writing a complaint that admits your own part in the events | Evidence against you | Stop; get advice before any further statement | Do the exposure check first |
| Answering a ticket on the wrong track | Late objection or the wrong body | Re-read the notice; file on the right track, with an extension request if needed | Identify the track on the day the ticket arrives |
| Writing before getting the record | Wrong facts; weaker credibility | Ask for the record, then correct | Records first, letters second |

## Review

- A Clinic IV matter is a dealing with a body that holds power and the file; most work is getting the record and holding the decision-maker to its rules.
- The door-closing periods are the petition period and delay, the appeal against closing a file, the traffic objection and appeal (or the request to be tried), and the one-year rules of the oversight bodies.
- Each matter gets an exposure check before any letter; a complainant or driver can become a defendant.
- The file is module 12's case file with the year-2 additions plus an exposure check, a decision-maker map and an official-records log.
