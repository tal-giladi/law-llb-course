# 37.8 · Closing the file and when to get a lawyer

A public or criminal matter is not over when the letter says "granted". The parking sign must be installed, the reopened file must produce a decision, the cancelled ticket must leave no points, the deleted entry must be gone from the screen, the court costs must be paid, and the Ombudsman's finding must reach the body that ignored you. In this last lesson you close each Clinic IV matter, write the closing memo that keeps the dates that outlive it, and run the get-a-lawyer checklist a final time with the year-4 red flags, including the ones that mean you need the Public Defender.

## Why it matters

> [!WARNING]
> Some year-4 matters can turn from a complaint into exposure: a counter-complaint, a charge after a reopened file, a prosecutor who amends a traffic charge, an entitled body that refuses you because of a record. When that happens the decision is no longer whether a lawyer would help but whether you may lawfully be left without one. A court may not impose actual imprisonment on an unrepresented defendant (CPL s. 15ב), and it must appoint a defender in the cases in s. 15(א).

Authorities often grant on paper and stall in practice; files reopen and close again quietly; points can be recorded even after an acquittal if nobody tells the licensing office; costs orders go unpaid. The closing check catches these. The closing memo also matters for later years: the record you deleted, the policy you defeated, the officer you complained about may come up again, and the memo is your evidence of what happened.

## The law

### When the law requires a defender

The court must appoint a defender in the cases listed in CPL s. 15(א), among them serious charges, defendants with certain disabilities, and cases where the prosecutor has given notice that actual imprisonment may be sought, or where after conviction the court considers prison (s. 15(א)(5)–(6), s. 15א(ג)); and it may not impose actual imprisonment on a defendant who was not represented (s. 15ב). Eligibility for the Public Defender is set by the Public Defender Law (s. 18; lesson [08.4](../module-08/lesson-04.md)). ([CPL](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_סדר_הדין_הפלילי#סעיף_15); [Public Defender Law](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הסנגוריה_הציבורית))

### Free representation for some victims

The Legal Aid Law's schedule gives free representation, without a means test, to victims of listed sexual offences in exercising their rights in the criminal process, and to families of homicide victims (lesson [05.7](../module-05/lesson-07.md)). Other civil and administrative matters are subject to the subject, means and merit tests. ([Legal Aid Law](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הסיוע_המשפטי))

### What outlives the matter

- A non-felony closed-file entry not cancelled on request is cancelled automatically after a set period from the event (Criminal Information Law s. 33(ג)); inspection is available at any time (s. 4).
- After a request to be tried, the summons must come within a set period of your request (CPL s. 230); if it does not, note the date and ask the prosecutor in writing.
- A private complaint, if you keep it as a fallback, is subject to the criminal limitation periods (CPL s. 9).
- All periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

## Leading cases

### Key text: the court's duty to a represented and an unrepresented defendant

The rules in CPL ss. 15, 15א and 15ב are the controlling texts; lesson [24.6](../module-24/lesson-06.md) covers their application. Why they matter here: at the closing stage of a traffic or local-affairs case, and on any reopened file, check whether anything said in the case (a notice that prison may be sought, an amended charge) triggered a duty to appoint a defender. If it did and none was appointed, that is a point for a lawyer on appeal.

### רע״א 8120/08 (no lay representative)

Recap from lesson [37.1](lesson-01.md): only a lawyer may represent another. Why it matters at the close: when you hand a matter over, hand it to a lawyer, a Legal Aid lawyer, the Public Defender or a clinic working under a lawyer's supervision, not to a friend who "knows the system". [Full text (supremedecisions)](https://supremedecisions.court.gov.il/Home/Download?path=HebrewVerdicts%2F08%2F200%2F081%2Fv04&fileName=08081200_v04.txt&type=2) (⚠️ host unreachable on 18 October 2026).

## How it works in practice

### The closing checklist

| Matter | What should have arrived | Check | If not |
|---|---|---|---|
| Administrative petition | The court's decision; the authority's new decision; costs | The new decision was actually carried out (the sign, the permit, the payment); costs paid within the time set | Letter to the authority's lawyer quoting the decision; a request in the case file; for unpaid costs, an execution file (lesson [08.9](../module-08/lesson-09.md)) |
| Police complaint | Notice of the decision in the file | The ground and the delivery date; for an indictment, your victim's rights (s. 8) | Appeal within the s. 65 period; or request to resume; or the fallback decision on a private complaint |
| Traffic ticket | The decision on the objection or appeal, or the judgment | No fine demand and no points recorded for a cancelled ticket | Write to the issuing authority and the licensing office with the decision |
| Police record | The police letter | A new inspection shows no entry | Letter to the police with their letter; the inspector if an entitled body still uses it |
| Oversight complaint | The finding | The body carried out the correction | Follow-up to the Ombudsman: the body has not reported back (s. 43) |

### The closing memo (one page per matter)

1. **Outcome:** what was decided, by whom, on what date; reference numbers (case, file, complaint, ticket).
2. **Money and costs:** fees paid, costs awarded and received, fines paid or cancelled.
3. **Documents:** where the originals are (decision, closure notice, tribunal decision, police letter, finding).
4. **Surviving dates:** automatic-cancellation date; summons deadline; limitation for a private complaint; re-inspection reminder; appeal periods if you are still deciding.
5. **What I would do differently:** three sentences.
6. **Archive:** keep criminal and record files indefinitely; administrative files at least until the decision has been carried out and any appeal period has passed.

### Year-4 red flags

Add these to the [get-a-lawyer checklist](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md):

| Red flag | Matter | Who |
|---|---|---|
| An interim order is needed within days; a represented third party is a respondent; threshold objections you cannot answer | Petition | A lawyer, a clinic, or an NGO in the field |
| You may be a suspect in the same events; a counter-complaint; a serious sexual or violent offence | Police complaint | A lawyer; free Legal Aid for listed victims |
| A summons and indictment instead of a notice; injury, alcohol, drugs, disqualification; points that threaten a professional licence | Traffic | A lawyer; the Public Defender if eligible |
| A notice that prison may be sought; an amended charge | Any criminal trial | Ask the court for a defender (s. 15(א)); the Public Defender |
| A pending decision already weighs the entry; a stayed file; a conviction to shorten | Record | A lawyer; a rehabilitation clinic |
| You were arrested or charged in the event you complain about; injury and a damages claim against the State | Oversight complaint | A lawyer |

Amber signs: the other side is represented and the stakes exceed a month's income; a deadline you cannot meet; facts you cannot prove without documents the other side holds; a matter that has become public.

### Handing over well

Send the lawyer, Legal Aid or the clinic the one-page summary from the get-a-lawyer checklist, the deadline table, the decision-maker map and the key documents, in that order. Ask in writing what the engagement covers and what it costs (lesson [35.5](../module-35/lesson-05.md)). Keep your file: the lawyer acts for you, but the matter remains yours.

## Worked example

Closing three supplied matters.

**Ilana (A).** The court's decision of December 2026 required the municipality to decide again within 30 days after an on-site measurement. On 10 January 2027 it allocated a space 80 metres from her door; the sign was installed on 24 January. Costs were paid on 3 February. Memo: outcome and dates; costs received; originals (decision, allocation letter, photo of the sign) in the file; surviving date: none, except to check after a year that the sign is still there and enforced; what she would do differently: "ask for the measurement on site in the prior demand".

**Dmitri (B).** His appeal against the second closure (insufficient evidence) was accepted in March 2027; the file went to the police prosecution, which filed a charge of damage to property. Dmitri receives notice of the indictment and the hearing dates under s. 8, and prepares a victim statement for sentencing (s. 18; lesson 05.7). His small claim against the neighbour is stayed by agreement until the criminal case ends. Memo: the appeal decision; the indictment; surviving dates: the small claim's limitation period; the next criminal hearing. Red flags: none for him as a victim; he does not need a lawyer to give evidence.

**Ahmad (D).** The police letter of January 2027 says the entry was cancelled; his February inspection shows none. The licensing authority approves his taxi licence in March. Memo: the letter, the inspection date, the licence; surviving date: none; a note to keep the cancellation letter permanently, in case an old printout resurfaces.

**A matter that turned (for comparison).** A learner who asked to be tried on a speeding notice heard the prosecutor say at the first hearing that, because of earlier offences, the State would ask for disqualification and might seek prison. He asked the judge to stop the hearing and to appoint a defender; the court referred him to the Public Defender. His memo records the date and the referral, and hands the file over with the summary.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- Every Clinic IV matter you ran, with its outcome (or the supplied cases).
- The [get-a-lawyer checklist](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md) and your case file.
- About 90 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Closing check (25 min).** For each matter, the row of the checklist: what arrived, what you checked, what is missing.
2. **Chase (20 min).** One letter or request for each gap: to the authority, the police, the licensing office or the oversight body, quoting the decision.
3. **Closing memo (25 min).** Six parts per matter; put every surviving date in your calendar.
4. **Lawyer decision (15 min).** Run the get-a-lawyer checklist with the year-4 red flags for each open matter; write the one-page summary for any red flag.
5. **Archive (5 min).** One folder per matter; the memo on top.

### Template

- [Get-a-lawyer checklist and one-page case summary](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md) (lesson 12.8), with the year-4 red flags above.
- [Clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md), closing sections.

### How you know it worked

- Every "granted" has a matching check that it was carried out.
- Every surviving date is in your calendar.
- For every red flag you have either a referral or a written reason why you continue alone.

### Self-check

- [ ] I confirmed the result itself (the sign, the screen, the points), not only the letter.
- [ ] I know when a court must appoint a defender and may not imprison an unrepresented defendant.
- [ ] I know which free help fits which year-4 matter.
- [ ] My closing memo could be used by someone else to understand the file.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes, the closing steps are yours: follow-up letters, re-inspection, a request in the case file to enforce a decision, an execution file for unpaid costs. Handing over to a lawyer, Legal Aid or the Public Defender is also your decision, and in some criminal situations the court's duty. See [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md).

### Where and how

- Public Defender: ask the court at the first hearing; eligibility under the Public Defender Law ([where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md)).
- Legal Aid: online application on gov.il; free for listed victims of sexual offences and families of homicide victims.
- University clinics: administrative-law, criminal-law, rehabilitation and human-rights clinics take individual matters in term time.
- Periods and fees: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- Any year-4 red flag above is present.
- An authority refuses to carry out a court decision: a contempt or enforcement request is a lawyer's task.
- You were awarded costs against an authority that does not pay and you are unsure how to enforce against a public body.
- A matter you closed reopens against you (a counter-complaint, a charge, a new refusal citing the old record).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Closing on the authority's letter alone | The sign is never installed; the entry is still there | Re-check and write again | Check the result itself |
| Points left on the licence after a cancelled ticket | Licensing steps based on a cancelled violation | Send the decision to the licensing office | Ask for a printout of your points after the decision |
| Not diarising the summons deadline after a request to be tried | You miss that the period passed | Write to the prosecutor; get advice on the effect | Put the s. 230 date in the memo |
| Handing a matter to a friend "who knows the system" | Unlawful representation; documents not his to sign | Take it back; use a lawyer or a clinic | Hand over only to lawyers or supervised clinics |
| Staying alone after a prison warning | Risk of conviction without the protection of s. 15 | Ask the court to appoint a defender at once | Treat a prison warning as an automatic red flag |

## Review

- Close each matter by checking that the result itself happened, not only that a letter said so.
- The closing memo records outcome, money and costs, documents, surviving dates and lessons, and goes on top of the archived file.
- Year-4 red flags include anything that turns you from complainant into suspect or defendant; a notice that prison may be sought means you ask for a defender.
- Hand over only to a lawyer, Legal Aid, the Public Defender or a supervised clinic, with the summary, the deadline table and the decision-maker map.
