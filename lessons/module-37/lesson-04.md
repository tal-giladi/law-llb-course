# 37.4 · Defending a traffic ticket: the request to be tried

Lab [24.9](../module-24/lesson-09.md) taught the classic route for a disputed ticket: the fine-payment notice, the cancellation request, and the request to be tried under CPL s. 229. Since 8 February 2026 that is only half the picture. The Traffic Administrative Violations Law 2024 moved many traffic tickets out of criminal law: they are now administrative fines, answered by an objection (השגה) to the issuing authority and an appeal (ערר) to a new traffic tribunal, with no request to be tried. In this lesson you read your own ticket (or Yossi Friedman's), decide which track it is on, choose your route, and write the document in time.

## Why it matters

> [!WARNING]
> This lesson covers ordinary traffic tickets only: a notice imposing a fine for a listed violation, or a fine-payment notice for a fine offence. If you received a summons and indictment instead of a notice, or the event involved injury, damage to property, alcohol, drugs, driving while disqualified or a risk of disqualification, this is a criminal case: get a lawyer or ask the court for the Public Defender (lesson [24.6](../module-24/lesson-06.md)).

A ticket is the legal document most Israelis receive, and the one most often handled badly. People pay "to get it over with" without checking the points; they write a furious letter to the wrong address; they ask to be tried on a ticket that has no such option; they miss a 30-day period because they waited for an answer. The two tracks now have different addressees, different periods and different consequences. Reading the notice correctly on the day it arrives is most of the defence.

## The law

### The administrative track: the 2024 Law

A "traffic violation" is a breach of a Traffic Ordinance provision listed in the Law's First Schedule, excluding a breach that caused an accident with injury or damage (s. 1). An inspector who has reasonable grounds may impose an administrative fine (s. 2(א)); the breach is then not prosecuted criminally, unless there are aggravating circumstances or a prosecutor decides that criminal proceedings are justified (s. 2(ג)–(ד)). The written notice states the fine, the payment period, the points recorded and how to object or appeal (s. 4). The Law came into force on 8 February 2026 for violations with the lower fines, and six months later for the higher ones (s. 47(ג); ⚠️ check on the notice which track applies).

> מפר רשאי להגיש לגורם מוסמך השגה על החלטה להטיל קנס [...]

*Gist:* You may object to the issuing authority within 30 days of service, or within 90 days if your claim is that someone else drove; the authority decides within a set period, and its silence does not cancel the fine; once you have appealed you may not also object. [s. 8](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הפרות_תעבורה_מינהליות)

> על החלטה להטיל קנס ניתן לערור לבית דין לתעבורה.

> ערר לפי סעיף זה יוגש בתוך 90 ימים מיום ההמצאה [...]

*Gist:* You may appeal to the traffic tribunal within 90 days of service, or within 30 days of the decision on your objection; the tribunal may extend for special reasons. You do not have to object first. [s. 19](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הפרות_תעבורה_מינהליות)

The tribunal is a single-member body set up by the Minister (s. 13); it decides on the written pleadings unless that would harm justice (s. 21(א)). Its final decision may be appealed to the traffic magistrates' court, or for a municipal inspector's fine to the local-affairs court (s. 24). Without an objection or appeal you pay within the period in s. 9 (all periods in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)); unpaid fines carry surcharges (s. 10). You may inspect the file within a set period (s. 36).

The procedure is in the 2026 regulations: the appeal is filed on the tribunal's online system (reg. 2(א)) and states your details, the decision and when you received it, the facts, the grounds and the remedy (reg. 2(ג)); facts are supported by attached documents or an affidavit (reg. 2(ד)); you may ask for a particular form of hearing, with reasons (reg. 2(ו)); the respondent is the police for an officer's fine, or the local authority for its inspector's (reg. 6); documents are delivered through the system (reg. 25). ([Regulations](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הפרות_תעבורה_מינהליות_(סדרי_הדין_והמינהל_בבית_דין_לתעבורה)))

### The criminal track: s. 229

> (2) הודיע, תוך תשעים ימים מיום ההמצאה, בדרך שנקבעה בתקנות, שיש ברצונו להישפט על העבירה.

*Gist:* A fine-payment notice is answered by a cancellation request to the prosecutor within 30 days (90 if the owner proves another driver) or by notice within 90 days that you wish to be tried, or within 30 days after a refused cancellation; paying, or letting the periods pass, counts as a conviction. [CPL s. 229](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_סדר_הדין_הפלילי#סעיף_229) The request goes on the slip attached to the notice, to the address on it, with proof of sending (reg. 42). The summons follows within a year (s. 230); at the trial, absence counts as admission (s. 240). Details in lesson 24.9.

This track still applies to notices served under the old law, to traffic offences outside the 2024 Law's schedule or excluded from it, and to municipal bylaw fines that are not traffic violations.

## Leading cases

### Key texts: a new law, little case law yet

The traffic tribunal began work in 2026 and there is no published appellate case law on the 2024 Law that could be verified on a free source when this lesson was checked. The decisive texts are ss. 2, 8, 19, 21 and 24 of the Law and regs 2–6 of the 2026 regulations (links above). Two points come from secondary sources and should be checked on the tribunal's site before you rely on them (⚠️ manual check): that the tribunal may confirm, cancel or change the violation but may not reduce the fine below the amount set; and the current fee, if any, for an appeal.

### Key text: s. 240, the cost of not attending

For the criminal track, s. 240 remains the rule that decides most lost trials: a defendant summoned in a fine-offence or minor traffic case who does not appear is treated as admitting the facts. Lesson 24.9 explains it; lesson [37.7](lesson-07.md) prepares you for the day.

## How it works in practice

### Step 1: which track? Read the notice

| On the notice | Track | Your options |
|---|---|---|
| "החלטה על הטלת קנס", "הפרת תעבורה", with השגה and ערר options and the traffic tribunal | Administrative (2024 Law) | Pay; object to the issuing authority; appeal to the tribunal |
| "הודעת תשלום קנס", "עבירת קנס", with a "בקשה להישפט" slip | Criminal fine offence (CPL s. 228) | Pay; cancellation request to the prosecutor; request to be tried |
| "הזמנה לדין" and "כתב אישום" | Criminal trial already chosen by the authority | Prepare a defence; consider a lawyer |

If the notice is unclear, write to the issuing authority within the shortest period that could apply (30 days), asking which track it is and stating that you dispute it.

### Step 2: choose the route

| Your position | Administrative track | Criminal track |
|---|---|---|
| The ticket is right | Pay within the period; check the points first | Pay; it is a conviction (s. 229(ח)) |
| Someone else drove (you are the owner) | Objection within 90 days naming the driver, or the transfer method on the notice | Cancellation request within 90 days |
| Clear error (wrong plate, a permit, a missing sign) | Objection within 30 days with the proof; appeal if refused | Cancellation request within 30 days |
| Facts disputed and you want an independent decision | Appeal to the tribunal within 90 days (no objection needed) | Request to be tried within 90 days |
| You missed the period for reasons beyond your control | Ask the tribunal to extend, with an affidavit (s. 19(ג); reg. 4) | Late request with reasons (s. 229(ה)) |

### Step 3: get the evidence

Ask for the file: under the administrative track, inspection of the file (s. 36); under the criminal track, a written request to the prosecutor for the officer's report and any photograph or radar printout. Collect your own: photographs of the place and signs, the phone holder, GPS or app logs, a passenger's statement, parking permits, travel documents.

### Step 4: decide with the whole cost in view

A dispute makes sense when you have evidence and a real factual or legal question. Weigh the points (stated on the notice), the effect on a professional licence, the time, and the surcharges if you lose and paid late. Neither the tribunal (per the secondary source above) nor a criminal court without special circumstances (s. 230) is likely to lower the fine itself.

## Worked example

Yossi Friedman (supplied case C) drives a delivery van in Beit Shemesh. On 6 October 2026 a police officer stopped him and handed him a notice for "holding a phone while driving". Yossi's phone was in a fixed dashboard holder, running navigation; he touched it once to dismiss a message while stopped at a red light. His employer's van has a dashcam facing the cab.

1. **Track.** The notice is headed "החלטה על הטלת קנס" and lists השגה and ערר: administrative track. There is no request to be tried. He diarises 30 days for an objection and 90 days for an appeal, from 6 October (served by hand).
2. **Points.** The notice states the points to be recorded. As a professional driver close to the threshold that would trigger a licensing step, he decides the points matter more than the fine.
3. **Evidence.** He asks his employer for the dashcam file for 6 October and gets it: the phone sits in the holder; his hand touches it once while the van is stationary. He photographs the holder. He asks to inspect the police file (s. 36).
4. **Route.** He could object first, but the police may simply confirm, and the facts (what the officer could see) are disputed. He chooses to appeal straight to the tribunal: no objection is needed (s. 19(ב)), and the tribunal is independent of the officer's unit.
5. **The appeal.** On 20 October he files on the tribunal's online system with the [administrative traffic fine objection and appeal](../../templates/traffic-administrative-fine-objection-and-appeal.md): his details; the decision and the date of service; the facts in numbered paragraphs; the dashcam file and photographs as annexes, with a short affidavit about the clip's source (reg. 2(ד)); the grounds (the phone was in a fixed holder, the touch was while stationary, the officer's view from the patrol car was partial: ⚠️ check the exact wording of the Traffic Regulations provision on the notice before relying on it); the remedy: cancel the decision; and a reasoned request for an oral hearing, because the officer's view is in dispute (reg. 2(ו)).
6. **Next.** He diarises the respondent's answer period if the tribunal orders one (reg. 10), and checks the system weekly. The hearing is in lesson 37.7.

**Contrast.** His wife Chana receives on the same day a municipal fine-payment notice for leaving garbage bins on the pavement, under a bylaw: a criminal fine offence. She uses the old track: a cancellation request within 30 days, and a request to be tried within 30 days of a refusal ([request-to-be-tried template](../../templates/request-to-be-tried.md)).

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- A ticket you received (yours only), or Yossi's case.
- The notice with its envelope or the date of handing over; your own evidence.
- The [administrative traffic fine objection and appeal](../../templates/traffic-administrative-fine-objection-and-appeal.md) and the [request to be tried and cancellation request](../../templates/request-to-be-tried.md).
- About 120 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Track (10 min).** Using the table in Step 1, write which track the notice is on and the words on it that show it.
2. **Deadlines (10 min).** Every period from service, and the proof of the service date. Calendar each with a reminder.
3. **Position (15 min).** Write in two sentences what you say happened and what you can prove. Choose the route from the table in Step 2, with one reason.
4. **Evidence (30 min).** Ask for the file or the officer's material; collect your own; name each annex.
5. **Draft (40 min).** On the right template: for the administrative track, an objection to the issuing authority or an appeal to the tribunal (reg. 2(ג) items, annexes or affidavit, remedy, hearing request with reasons); for the criminal track, a cancellation request, or the request to be tried on the slip.
6. **Send and prove (10 min).** Online system confirmation, registered-mail receipt, or the authority's form confirmation; into the file.
7. **Next dates (5 min).** The authority's decision period on an objection; the 30 days to appeal after it; the answer period at the tribunal; or, on the criminal track, the summons within a year.

### Template

- [Administrative traffic fine: objection and appeal to the traffic tribunal (השגה וערר על החלטה להטלת קנס)](../../templates/traffic-administrative-fine-objection-and-appeal.md) (new in this module).
- [Request to cancel a fine notice and request to be tried](../../templates/request-to-be-tried.md) (lesson 24.9).

### How you know it worked

- You named the track from the words on the notice, not from memory.
- Your document went to the right body (issuing authority, tribunal or prosecutor) within its period, and you hold proof.
- Every fact you state is supported by an annex or an affidavit.

### Self-check

- [ ] I know that an administrative ticket has no request to be tried, and a criminal fine notice has no tribunal appeal.
- [ ] I know the 30/90-day objection periods and the 90-day (or 30 days after an objection) appeal period.
- [ ] I checked the points before deciding to pay.
- [ ] I know when a traffic matter is a criminal case that needs a lawyer.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. The person fined objects and appeals in his own name; the tribunal regulations ask for a lawyer's details only if you have one (reg. 2(ג)(3)); a request to be tried and the fine-offence trial are routinely handled in person (lesson 24.9). Filing for someone else is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20); the police drivers' inquiries service also asks for a lawyer's power of attorney when someone files for another person (⚠️ manual check). See [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md).

### Where and how

- **Administrative track:** objection to the issuing authority shown on the notice (police traffic inquiries for police tickets, the municipality for its inspectors); appeal on the traffic tribunal's online system, reached from the Ministry of Justice site (⚠️ manual check of the address and any fee).
- **Criminal track:** the police National Drivers' Inquiries Centre online service for police notices, <https://www.gov.il/he/service/drivers_inquiries_forms> (⚠️ manual check), or the municipal prosecutor for municipal notices; the request to be tried on the slip, with proof of sending.
- Periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); free help: [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You received a summons and indictment rather than a notice, or the prosecutor chose criminal proceedings for aggravating circumstances (s. 2(ג)–(ד)).
- The event involved injury, damage, alcohol, drugs or driving while disqualified.
- The points threaten a disqualification or a professional licence.
- You want to appeal the tribunal's decision to the court on a point of law.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sending a "request to be tried" on an administrative ticket | No such route; periods run | File an objection or appeal at once; ask to extend if late | Read the heading of the notice |
| Objecting, then waiting past 90 days for an answer | You still have 30 days from the decision; if no decision, the fine is not cancelled | Appeal to the tribunal; you need not wait for the objection | Diary both periods on day 1 |
| Filing an objection after you already appealed | Barred (s. 8(ד)) | Put everything in the appeal | Choose one route at a time |
| Unsupported facts in the appeal | Tribunal decides on the papers against you | Add documents or an affidavit if allowed | Attach proof or an affidavit for each fact (reg. 2(ד)) |
| Paying without checking the points | Points recorded; licensing steps follow | Usually none | Read the points line first |

## Review

- Since 8 February 2026, many traffic tickets are administrative fines: object to the issuing authority (30 days, 90 if another driver) or appeal to the traffic tribunal (90 days, or 30 after an objection).
- Criminal fine notices keep the old route: cancellation request and request to be tried (s. 229), with s. 240 at the trial.
- The heading of the notice tells you the track; the points often matter more than the fine.
- Support every fact with a document or affidavit, ask for the file, and diarise each later step.
