# 02.7 · Practice lab: a formal letter to an authority or company

In this lab you write and, if you choose, send a real formal letter in your own name: to a public authority, invoking its duty to decide and give reasons, or to a company, setting out your demand. It is the most common legal document a self-represented person writes, and often the only one needed.

## Why it matters

Most disputes with authorities and companies end with a letter, not a lawsuit. A clear letter that names the legal duty, asks for something specific and sets a date gets answered far more often than a phone call or an angry email. If it is not answered, the letter becomes evidence: courts and ombudsmen ask first whether you approached the other side and what they said (module 8 covers the pre-action letter before a lawsuit). A letter written now, carefully, is the first page of any case file you will ever open.

## The law

### The duty to answer, give reasons and say where to appeal

> (א) נתבקש עובד הציבור, בכתב, להשתמש בסמכות שניתנה לו על פי דין, יחליט בבקשה וישיב למבקש בכתב בהקדם, אך לא יאוחר מארבעים וחמישה ימים מיום קבלת הבקשה.

*Gist:* A written request to a public official to use a statutory power must be decided and answered in writing within 45 days. ([Decisions and Reasons Law 1958, s. 2(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לתיקון_סדרי_המינהל_%28החלטות_והנמקות%29))

> עובד הציבור שנתבקש כאמור בסעיף 2(א) וסירב לבקשה, יודיע למבקש בכתב את נימוקי סירובו.

*Gist:* A refusal must be explained in writing. (s. 2א; exceptions in s. 3, such as state security or another person's rights)

> היתה החלטה של עובד הציבור נתונה לערר או לערעור על פי חיקוק, יודיע עובד הציבור בכתב לאדם הזכאי להגיש את הערר או את הערעור, על זכות הערר או הערעור ועל דרכי הגשתם ומועדיהם במידה שנקבעו בחיקוק.

*Gist:* If the decision can be appealed, the official must tell you so in writing, with how and when. (s. 5)

> לא השיב עובד הציבור או לא הודיע תוך שלושת החדשים כאמור בסעיף 2(א) או (ג), רואים בכך, לענין כל דין, החלטה לסרב לבקשה, ללא מתן נימוקים.

*Gist:* Silence for three months counts as a refusal without reasons. (s. 6(ב)) Under s. 6(א), in any later proceeding the official bears the burden of showing that an unexplained decision was lawful. The periods are in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

A wrongly addressed request must be passed on to the competent official, who must tell you (s. 2ב), so a reasonable choice of recipient is enough.

### Asking for information instead of action

> (א) בקשה לקבלת מידע תוגש בכתב לממונה או למי שהוסמך לכך על ידו; אין המבקש חייב לציין את הטעם לבקשתו.

*Gist:* A freedom-of-information request is made in writing to the authority's FOI officer, and you need not say why you want the information. ([Freedom of Information Law 1998, s. 7(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חופש_המידע))

The authority must decide within 30 days, extendable by 30 and then, with reasons, by up to 60 more (s. 7(ב), (ג); see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)); a refusal must give reasons and tell you of your right to petition the Administrative Affairs Court (s. 7(ו)). An application fee applies, but information about yourself and your rights is exempt from it ([FOI Fees Regulations, reg. 6(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_חופש_המידע_%28אגרות%29); amounts in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).

### Who may write the letter

> (3) עריכת מסמכים בעלי אופי משפטי בשביל אדם אחר, לרבות ייצוג אדם אחר במשא ומתן משפטי לקראת עריכת מסמך כזה;

*Gist:* Drafting legal documents for another person, as an occupation or for payment, is reserved to lawyers. ([Bar Association Law, s. 20(3)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לשכת_עורכי_הדין)) You write and sign your own letters.

## Leading cases

### עע"ם 9135/03 המועצה להשכלה גבוהה נ' הוצאת עיתון הארץ (19.1.2006)

The Supreme Court held that the exemptions in s. 9(ב) of the Freedom of Information Law give the authority discretion, not a licence to refuse: it must weigh the public interest in disclosure, the requester's interest and the harm disclosure might cause, and explain its decision. Disclosure is the rule. Why it matters for your letter: when an authority refuses with a one-line label ("דיונים פנימיים"), your follow-up letter can ask it to show the weighing that this ruling requires. English text: [Versa](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/council-higher-education-v-haaretz).

## How it works in practice

### Anatomy of a formal Hebrew letter

1. **Top right:** date (Hebrew and Gregorian, or Gregorian only), your full name, ID number only if the authority needs it to identify your file, address, phone, email.
2. **Recipient:** name and role ("לכבוד מר/גב' …, מנהל/ת מחלקת…"), the body, its address or email.
3. **Delivery line:** "בדואר רשום עם אישור מסירה ובדוא"ל" or "באמצעות טופס הפנייה המקוון".
4. **Subject line (הנדון):** short and specific, bold or underlined: "הנדון: בקשה לתיקון תאורת רחוב ברחוב… – פנייה לפי חוק לתיקון סדרי המינהל (החלטות והנמקות)".
5. **Reference line (סימוכין):** earlier letters, file or reference numbers.
6. **Opening:** "שלום רב," or "א.ג.נ.,".
7. **Numbered paragraphs:** facts (dated, with attachments), legal basis (one or two sections, quoted briefly), the request (specific action, by a specific date), and what you will do if there is no answer.
8. **Reservation:** "אין באמור במכתב זה כדי לגרוע מכל טענה או זכות העומדות לי."
9. **Closing:** "בכבוד רב," signature, name.
10. **Attachments list (נספחים)** and **copies (העתק)**, if any.

### Letter to an authority versus a company

| | Authority | Company |
|---|---|---|
| Legal hook | Decisions and Reasons Law ss. 2, 2א, 5, 6; FOI Law s. 7; the specific statute that gives the power | The contract, the Consumer Protection Law or other statute your memo identified |
| Duty to answer | Yes, in writing, within 45 days | Generally no statutory duty to answer; the letter builds the record and sets a deadline before you act |
| Tone | Formal, factual; ask for a decision | Formal, factual; state the demand and the consequence |
| Next step if ignored | Reminder, complaint to the Ombudsman, administrative challenge | Regulator complaint, small claim (modules 8 and 12) |

### Sending it so you can prove it

- **Registered mail with proof of delivery** (דואר רשום עם אישור מסירה): the classic proof. Keep the receipt and the returned card.
- **The authority's online form:** save the confirmation page and reference number as a PDF.
- **Email:** send to an official address; keep the sent message; an automatic receipt helps. Under Interpretation Law s. 3, an email is "בכתב".
- **By hand:** ask for a stamped copy ("העתק עם חותמת נתקבל").
- Use two channels for anything important. Keep everything in one folder, with the memo from lesson 02.6.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

## Worked example

Yonatan Peretz, an IT worker in Sderot, applied to the municipality for a discount on municipal property tax (arnona) and was refused by a short email that gave no reasons and said nothing about appeal. His memo (lesson 02.6) found that the refusal had to be reasoned and, if appealable, had to say how and when to appeal. He writes:

> **הנדון: בקשה למתן החלטה מנומקת בבקשתי להנחה בארנונה – לפי חוק לתיקון סדרי המינהל (החלטות והנמקות), התשי"ט–1958**
>
> 1. ביום 12.4.2026 הגשתי בקשה להנחה בארנונה בגין הנכס ברחוב… (נספח א).
> 2. ביום 20.5.2026 קיבלתי הודעה בדוא"ל כי הבקשה נדחתה, ללא כל נימוק (נספח ב).
> 3. לפי ס' 2א לחוק, עובד הציבור שסירב לבקשה "יודיע למבקש בכתב את נימוקי סירובו". לפי ס' 5 לחוק, מקום שההחלטה נתונה להשגה או לערר, יש להודיע לי על זכות זו, על דרכי הגשתה ועל מועדיה.
> 4. אבקש לקבל בתוך 14 ימים: (א) את נימוקי ההחלטה; (ב) את פירוט הדרך והמועד להשיג עליה.
> 5. אבקש להבהיר כי המועד להגשת השגה, ככל שיש כזה, אינו מתחיל לרוץ לפני שיימסרו לי הנימוקים וההודעה כאמור. ככל שעמדתכם שונה, אבקש לקבלה בכתב.
> 6. אין באמור במכתב זה כדי לגרוע מכל טענה או זכות העומדות לי.
>
> בכבוד רב,
> יונתן פרץ

Notes on the choices:

- Paragraph 3 quotes the statute's words, briefly, with section numbers; no rulings are needed for a request this simple.
- Paragraph 4 asks for two specific things with a date.
- Paragraph 5 is a position, not a statement of law: Yonatan's memo graded "uncertain" whether an objection deadline runs from an unreasoned refusal, so he asks the municipality to state its view in writing rather than relying on his own. Meanwhile, to be safe, he diarises the earliest possible deadline and will file an objection within it (municipal tax objections are covered in year 3).
- He sends by email and registered mail, and files the receipts.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- The memo from lesson 02.6, or another real matter of your own with an authority or a company.
- [The letter to an authority template](../../templates/letter-to-authority.md) or [the demand letter to a company template](../../templates/demand-letter-company.md).
- Your documents (application, replies, receipts, photos).
- Two hours.

### Steps

1. Decide the recipient and the channel. For an authority, find the department's official email or online form; for FOI, the authority's FOI officer.
2. Write the subject line: what you ask and, for an authority, the law you rely on.
3. Write the facts in numbered, dated paragraphs, each pointing to an attachment.
4. Write the legal basis: one or two provisions, quoted briefly, cited as in lesson 02.5.
5. Write the request: exactly what you want, by when. For an authority, ask for "החלטה בכתב" and, if refused, "נימוקים".
6. Write what happens if there is no answer, in neutral words: reminder, complaint, legal steps. No threats you would not carry out.
7. Add the reservation, closing, attachments and copies.
8. Read the letter aloud. Cut anything emotional, anything not needed for the request, and any legal claim your memo does not support.
9. Check it against the self-check below. Have someone read it for clarity (not to draft it for you).
10. **Dry run or real:** you may stop here (dry run) or send it in your own name, using two channels, and diarise the deadline (45 days for a Decisions and Reasons Law request; FOI periods under s. 7; see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).

### Template

- [Letter to a public authority](../../templates/letter-to-authority.md) (Hebrew), with optional FOI paragraph.
- [Demand letter to a company](../../templates/demand-letter-company.md) (Hebrew).

### How you know it worked

- A stranger reading the letter knows in 30 seconds what you want, why you are entitled to it, and by when.
- Every fact has a date and an attachment; every legal statement has a section.
- You can prove when the letter was received.
- If sent: you received a written answer, or you have a dated record showing that none came, and you know your next step.

### Self-check

- [ ] The letter is in my own name, about my own matter.
- [ ] The subject line names what I ask and the legal basis.
- [ ] The request is specific and has a date.
- [ ] The legal basis is quoted briefly and cited correctly.
- [ ] No insults, no threats I would not carry out, no claims my memo does not support.
- [ ] I have proof of delivery and a diary date for the reply.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Anyone may write to an authority or a company in their own name, and the Decisions and Reasons Law and the Freedom of Information Law protect every requester. A company writes through its authorised officers. Writing letters for others, as a service or for payment, is drafting legal documents for another person, reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20(3)).

### Where and how

- **Authorities:** the department's official email or the online inquiry form on its website; registered mail to the address on its letterhead. FOI requests go to the authority's FOI officer; government ministries and many authorities have an online FOI form. The fee and its exemptions are in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- **Companies:** the customer-service address in your contract or on the invoice, plus registered mail to the registered office for important demands.
- **If ignored:** a reminder; a complaint to the State Comptroller and Ombudsman about an authority; a complaint to the relevant regulator about a company; then the court routes in module 8. Free help: [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).
- **Cost:** a letter costs only postage; FOI requests carry a small fee unless exempt.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The authority's decision has a short appeal deadline and you are unsure when it started running.
- The company replies through a law firm and raises legal arguments your memo did not cover.
- The letter would make allegations of fraud, criminal conduct or defamation-sensitive statements about named people.
- The matter involves significant money, your home, your employment or a criminal investigation.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| No specific request or date | The reply is vague or never comes | Send a short follow-up with the exact request and a date | Write paragraph 4 (the request) first |
| Angry or insulting tone | The reader defends instead of deciding; it may harm you later in court | Send a calm, factual follow-up; do not repeat the insults | Read aloud and cut every adjective you would not say to a judge |
| No proof of delivery | The other side says it never received it; deadlines cannot be counted | Resend by registered mail and email; note the new date | Always use two channels and keep receipts |
| Overclaiming the law | Loses credibility; may mislead you about your position | Correct it in the next letter | Cite only what your memo supports |
| Threats you will not carry out | Your next letter is ignored | Replace with a realistic next step | State only next steps you have decided on |
| Missing the authority's reply deadline in your diary | You lose the "deemed refusal" date and its consequences | Recompute under Interpretation Law s. 10 | Diarise day 45 and the three-month date when you send |

## Review

- A formal letter has a specific subject line, dated facts with attachments, a short legal basis, a specific request with a date, and a reservation of rights.
- Authorities must answer written requests within 45 days, give reasons for refusals and say how to appeal; silence for three months is a refusal without reasons (Decisions and Reasons Law).
- An FOI request needs no reasons; refusal must be reasoned and weighed (עע"ם 9135/03).
- Companies generally have no statutory duty to answer, but your letter builds the record for regulators and courts.
- Prove delivery, diarise the deadline, and write only in your own name.
