# 02.5 · The uniform citation rules (כללי האזכור האחיד)

By the end of this lesson you will be able to cite any Israeli law or ruling in the standard academic format, use short forms and "ibid." correctly, and clean up the citations in a letter or a pleading so that a judge or official can find every source in seconds.

## Why it matters

A citation is an address. If yours is wrong, the reader cannot find the source, and a busy judge or official will not try hard. Correct citations also signal care: a self-represented party whose citations look like a lawyer's is read more carefully.

Israel has one widely used standard, the uniform citation rules (כללי האזכור האחיד בכתיבה המשפטית). They were formulated by a joint team of four law journals, משפטים (Hebrew University), משפט וממשל (Haifa), מחקרי משפט (Bar-Ilan) and עיוני משפט (Tel Aviv), and are hosted by Tel Aviv University's law faculty. The **third edition (2021)** is the current one: it was the edition published on the faculty's citation page when this lesson was checked on 2026-10-16, and no later edition could be found. Courts are not bound by the rules and many judgments and pleadings use slightly different conventions, but the core formulas are the same everywhere, and the rules are what law students are taught.

## The law

The citation rules are not law, so this section sets out the binding formulas of the rules themselves, quoted from the [third edition](https://law.tau.ac.il/sites/law.tau.ac.il/files/media_server/law_heb/izkur/izkur2021pdf.pdf).

### Legislation (rule 2.1)

> [הפניה ספציפית] [ל][שם החיקוק], [שנת החיקוק העברית][–][שנת החיקוק הלועזית], [שם הקובץ בקיצור] [שנת הקובץ] [מספר העמוד הראשון][, מספר העמוד הספציפי]

*Gist:* Pinpoint, then the name of the enactment, Hebrew year, en dash, Gregorian year, the Reshumot series and its first page. Examples from the rules: "חוק העונשין, התשל"ז–1977, ס"ח 226"; "תקנות הנמלים (תיקון מס' 3), התשמ"א–1981, ק"ת 956"; "ס' 2 לחוק שכר מינימום, התשמ"ז–1987".

Three details people get wrong:

- The Hebrew year always starts with ה"א and there is no space around the dash: "התשמ"א–1981", not "תשמ"א - 1981" (rules 2.4, 2.5, 1.11).
- When you refer to the **current** text, you need not give the Reshumot reference at all: "חוק הסכמים קיבוציים, התשי"ז–1957." is a complete citation (rule 4.2). Give the Reshumot reference when you mean the original text or a specific amendment.
- Basic Laws have no year in their name: "חוק-יסוד: הכנסת", with a hyphen, except "חוק יסוד: משק המדינה", which has none (rule 4.3).

### Printed rulings (rule 18.1)

> [סוג ההליך] ([פרטי הערכאה]) [מספר התיק] [צד א'] [מפריד בין צדדים] [צד ב'], [הסדרה שבה פורסם פסק הדין] [כרך]\([חלק]\) [עמוד תחילת פסק הדין][, הפניה ספציפית] ([שנת מתן פסק הדין הלועזית])

*Gist:* Proceeding, court (omitted for the Supreme Court), case number, first party, "נ'", second party, report series, volume(part), first page, pinpoint, year of judgment. Example: "ע"א 313/08 נשאשיבי נ' רינאוי, פ"ד סד(1) 398, 424–426 (2010)."

Party names: only the first party on each side, surname only for individuals, full name for companies, no titles, no "ואח'" (rule 18.4). Labour-court rulings separate parties with a dash instead of "נ'" (rule 18.5).

### Rulings from a database (rule 19.1.3)

> [סוג ההליך] ([פרטי הערכאה]) [מספר התיק] [צד א'] [מפריד בין צדדים] [צד ב'][, הפניה ספציפית] ([שם המאגר] [תאריך לועזי מלא של מתן פסק הדין])

*Gist:* Same as above, but instead of a report series give the database and the full date. The rules abbreviate the Judiciary's website and Net HaMishpat as **אר"ש**: "בג"ץ 8300/02 נסר נ' ממשלת ישראל, פס' 35–36 לפסק הדין של הנשיאה (בדימ') ביניש (אר"ש 22.5.2012)." A printed report is preferred where one exists (rule 19.1.1). No database is preferred over another (rule 19.1.2), so a ruling you found free on the Judiciary's site is cited exactly as well as one from Nevo.

### Unpublished rulings (rule 20.1)

Give the full date in brackets: "בג"ץ 470/80 אלגזי נ' שר הבטחון (9.2.1980)."

### Repeat citations (rule 37)

> [שם המקור/כינוי מקוצר], [הפניה לאזכור המלא], [הפניה ספציפית]

*Gist:* Name or short name, a pointer to the footnote with the full citation ("לעיל ה"ש 10"), then the pinpoint ("בעמ' 515"). Use "שם" when the source was the last one cited, in the same or the previous footnote, with nothing in between (rule 37.7). Repeat citations of legislation need no pointer: "ס' 13 לפקודת הראיות." (rule 37.5).

## Leading cases

### ע"א 6821/93 בנק המזרחי המאוחד בע"מ נ' מגדל כפר שיתופי, פ"ד מט(4) 221 (1995)

The judgment that established judicial review of Knesset legislation under the Basic Laws (module 6). It is used here because the rules themselves use it to illustrate short names: after the full citation they add "(להלן: עניין חוק גל)" and later write "עניין חוק גל, לעיל ה"ש 28, בעמ' 470–471". Most Israeli lawyers know it as "פרשת בנק המזרחי"; the short name you choose must be unambiguous and used consistently. English translation: [Versa](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/united-mizrahi-bank-v-migdal-cooperative-village).

### עע"ם 1758/10 לשכת עורכי הדין בישראל נ' שגיא (15.8.2011)

The judgment you briefed in lesson 02.3 shows citation practice in real judicial writing: "בג"ץ 987/94 יורונט קווי זהב (1992) בע"מ נ' שרת התקשורת, פ"ד מח(5) 412, 425-424 (1994) (להלן: עניין יורונט)" and later "(עניין יורונט, בעמוד 425)". It also cites an unpublished Supreme Court ruling the older way, "(לא פורסם, 16.2.2010)", which rule 19.1.2 now discourages when a database version exists. Free text: [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פסק_דין_עע%22ם_1758/10_לשכת_עורכי_הדין_בישראל_נ'_שלום_שגיא).

## How it works in practice

### Common abbreviations

| Abbreviation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ס' / תק' / פס' | Section / regulation / paragraph |
| ס"ח / ק"ת / ה"ח / י"פ | Reshumot series: laws / regulations / bills / notices |
| פ"ד / פ"מ / פד"ע | Supreme Court reports / district (lower) court reports / labour court reports |
| אר"ש | The Judiciary's website and Net HaMishpat (as a database) |
| בעמ' | at page (in repeat citations) |
| נ' | v. (versus) |

The full lists are in appendices א to ז of the rules.

### What practitioners do differently

In pleadings and many judgments you will see "(פורסם בנבו, 28.11.2019)" instead of the rules' "(נבו 28.11.2019)", and "(להלן: "החוק")" with quotation marks. These variations are accepted. What is not accepted is a citation the reader cannot use: a ruling without a case number, a law without a year, a quote without a pinpoint. Choose one style and use it consistently in a document.

### A minimal citation kit for self-represented writing

| Source | Format to use |
|---|---|
| Statute, current text | ס' 2(א) לחוק לתיקון סדרי המינהל (החלטות והנמקות), התשי"ט–1958. |
| Regulation | תק' 9 לתקנות סדר הדין האזרחי, התשע"ט–2018. |
| Supreme Court, printed | ע"א 165/82 קיבוץ חצור נ' פקיד שומה רחובות, פ"ד לט(2) 70, 75 (1985). |
| Supreme Court, from the Judiciary site | עע"ם 1758/10 לשכת עורכי הדין בישראל נ' שגיא, פס' 14 (אר"ש 15.8.2011). |
| Lower court, from Net HaMishpat | ת"ק (תביעות קטנות קר') 23742-11-14 חונדיא נ' אור הקסם בע"מ (אר"ש 15.6.2015). |
| Repeat | עניין שגיא, לעיל ה"ש 3, פס' 20. |

The page number "75" in the Hatzor line is illustrative of a pinpoint; use the actual page of the passage you quote.

## Worked example

Salim Abu-Rabia, a contractor from Rahat, drafted a letter to the Israel Land Authority. His first draft cited:

1. "חוק חופש המידע תשנ"ח 1998 סעיף 7"
2. "בג"צ 987/94 יורונט קווי זהב (1992) בע"מ ואח' נגד שרת התקשורת הגב' שולמית אלוני ואח' פד מח 5 עמ' 412"
3. "פסק דין שגיא מ-2011"
4. "שם" (referring back to item 1, after item 3)

Corrected:

1. "ס' 7 לחוק חופש המידע, התשנ"ח–1998." Pinpoint first, ה"א prefix, en dash.
2. "בג"ץ 987/94 יורונט קווי זהב (1992) בע"מ נ' שרת התקשורת, פ"ד מח(5) 412 (1994)." First party only, no "ואח'", the role not the minister's name (rule 18.4.5), "נ'" not "נגד", volume(part), year of judgment.
3. "עע"ם 1758/10 לשכת עורכי הדין בישראל נ' שגיא (אר"ש 15.8.2011)." A ruling without a case number is unfindable.
4. "ס' 7 לחוק חופש המידע." "שם" can only point to the source cited immediately before; here another source intervened. Legislation needs no "לעיל".

- [ ] Every statute has its Hebrew year with ה"א and its Gregorian year, joined by an en dash.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Not a filing lesson. Citation is a writing skill. It shows up in practice in every letter to an authority, statement of claim, petition and appeal you write for yourself. Courts do not reject a self-represented party's filing for imperfect citation style, but they cannot consider a source they cannot find.

### Where and how

The rules are free at [law.tau.ac.il/izkur](https://law.tau.ac.il/izkur) in PDF and HTML. Questions about the rules can be sent to the address given on that page.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

Citation alone never requires a lawyer. If you cannot find the citation of a ruling the other side relies on, ask the court or the other side for a copy; you are entitled to know what is cited against you.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ruling cited without case number ("פסק דין כהן") | The reader cannot find it; the point is ignored | Add proceeding, number and date | Copy the citation from the header when you first find the ruling |
| Law cited without year | Ambiguity when two laws have similar names; looks careless | Add the Hebrew and Gregorian years | Copy the title line from Wikisource |
| Quote without pinpoint | The reader must search a 40-page judgment | Add the paragraph or page | Record paragraph numbers when you brief (lesson 02.3) |
| "שם" pointing to the wrong source | The reader is misled | Replace with the short name and "לעיל ה"ש" | Use "שם" only for the immediately preceding source |
| Mixing styles in one document | Looks careless | Pick one style and convert | Decide the style before you start writing |

## Review

- The current edition of the uniform citation rules is the third (2021), hosted free by Tel Aviv University.
- Legislation: pinpoint, name, Hebrew year with ה"א, en dash, Gregorian year; Reshumot reference only when you need the original text.
- Rulings: proceeding, court (not for the Supreme Court), number, first parties' short names with "נ'", then the report series or the database and full date (אר"ש for the Judiciary's site).
- Repeat citations: short name, "לעיל ה"ש", pinpoint; "שם" only for the immediately preceding source.
- Practitioner variants are fine if consistent; an unfindable citation never is.
