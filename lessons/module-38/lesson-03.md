# 38.3 · Procedural law review II: criminal procedure, evidence, execution, insolvency and discipline

This lesson reviews the rest of the procedural paper: police powers and the criminal trial, the law of evidence in civil and criminal cases, the Execution Office, individual insolvency, and the Bar's disciplinary procedure. Each block is a compact table of the rules most often examined, with the lesson that teaches it. You then work five Bar-style items and sit a 15-question timed drill.

## Why it matters

Reg. 18א(א)(2), (4) and (6) put these subjects on the procedural paper (lesson [38.1](lesson-01.md)). They share one feature: almost every rule is a number (hours, days, years) or a condition (a ground for arrest, a condition for admissibility, a creditor majority). That makes them easy to examine and easy to confuse. The criminal-procedure periods in particular look alike (24 hours, 48 hours, 15 days, 30 days, 75 days, 9 months), and the exam rewards the candidate who knows which belongs to which stage.

## Leading cases

### ע״פ 5121/98 יששכרוב נ׳ התובע הצבאי הראשי (4.5.2006)

A soldier's confession was obtained without proper notice of his right to consult a lawyer. The Supreme Court adopted a judge-made doctrine of relative exclusion: evidence obtained unlawfully may be excluded if admitting it would substantially harm the right to a fair trial, weighing the nature of the breach, its effect on the evidence and the public interest. The Knesset wrote the doctrine into s. 56א in 2022. Bar point: the test is **relative and discretionary**, limited by s. 56א's words to criminal trials. [English translation](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/411/)

### דנ״פ 1187/03 מדינת ישראל נ׳ פרץ (31.3.2005)

An expanded panel held that the prosecution must, as a rule, defend on appeal the plea bargain it signed, even if the trial court rejected it. Bar point: **the court is not bound by a plea bargain; the prosecution is**. [English translation](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/state-israel-v-peretz)

### ע״פ 4855/02 מדינת ישראל נ׳ בורוביץ (2005)

The Supreme Court set the three-step test for protection from injustice (identify the flaw; ask whether the trial would seriously offend justice and fairness; prefer lesser remedies), later codified as a preliminary argument in CPL s. 149(10). Bar point: cancelling an indictment is the exception. Free source: [Hebrew Wikipedia, הגנה מן הצדק](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/הגנה_מן_הצדק) (⚠️ secondary source).

### ב״ש 298/86 ציטרין נ׳ בית הדין המשמעתי של לשכת עורכי הדין במחוז תל אביב (1987)

Two journalists refused to reveal their sources to the Bar's disciplinary court and were fined. President Shamgar held that journalists enjoy a relative privilege, recognised in case law though no statute grants it: disclosure may be ordered only where the question is relevant, disclosure is necessary to do justice on an essential matter, and the matter is serious. A Bar disciplinary hearing did not justify lifting it, and the fines were cancelled. Bar point: privileges are not a closed statutory list, and they apply before disciplinary tribunals too (compare Evidence Ordinance s. 52). [English translation](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/110/)

## How it works in practice

### Telling the criminal periods apart

Anchor each number to a stage, then learn the stages in order:

| Stage | Number |
|---|---|
| On the street | 3 hours (detention) |
| At the station | 24 hours to a judge |
| Lawyer meeting delays | a few hours / 24 / 48 hours |
| Each remand order | 15 days |
| Remand without the Attorney General | 30 days |
| To indictment | 75 days |
| Pre-indictment request | 30 days from the notice |
| Detention after indictment: start of trial | 30 days |
| Detention after indictment: verdict | 9 months |
| Detention appeal (ערר) | 30 days |
| Criminal appeal | 45 days |

### Evidence questions: the two-question method

For any piece of evidence ask: (1) **Is it admissible?** competence, privilege, hearsay exception, confession rule, illegality; and (2) **Is it enough to convict or decide?** something extra, strengthening, corroboration, reasons for a single witness. Many wrong answers are correct on the first question and wrong on the second.

### Execution and insolvency: follow the clock from the warning

Most execution questions start at the warning. From service: 20 days to comply or ask for a payment order, 21 days to appear for a means inquiry, 30 days to oppose a bill or a fixed-sum claim. Insolvency questions turn on the door (Commissioner, registrar or court), the effect of the opening order, and what discharge does not cover.

## Worked example

**Item 1.** Police arrested Adi at 22:00 on Thursday. The officer in charge decided to keep him in custody. By when must he be brought before a judge, ignoring the Shabbat and holiday rules?

- (A) Within 48 hours.
- (B) Within 3 hours.
- (C) Within 24 hours, by 22:00 on Friday.
- (D) Within 15 days.

*Reasoning:* A person kept in custody must be brought before a judge as soon as possible and within 24 hours (Arrests Law s. 29); s. 29(ב)–(ה) adjust this for Shabbat and holidays, which the question excludes. **Answer: C.**

**Item 2.** Dana is charged with fraud against her employer. The prosecution wants her husband, from whom she has not separated, to testify about her bank transfers. May he?

- (A) No, a spouse is not competent against the accused spouse, and fraud against an employer is not an exception.
- (B) Yes, everyone is competent under s. 2.
- (C) Yes, because the offence is a felony.
- (D) Only with the court's leave for special reasons.

*Reasoning:* In a criminal case a spouse is not competent to testify against the accused spouse (s. 3); the exceptions in s. 5 concern violence, sexual and abuse offences within the family, breach of a protection order and similar. **Answer: A.** He may testify for her (s. 6).

**Item 3.** A witness tells the court she remembers nothing. Her police statement implicates the accused. What may the court do?

- (A) Nothing: a police statement is hearsay.
- (B) Admit the statement and convict on it alone.
- (C) Admit it only if the defence consents.
- (D) Admit the statement under s. 10א, but convict only if something in the evidence strengthens it.

*Reasoning:* Section 10א(א) admits the written statement when the witness testifies and claims not to remember; s. 10א(ד) bars a conviction on it without strengthening. **Answer: D.**

**Item 4.** An execution warning was served on Yaakov on 1 May in a file opened on a promissory note. On 25 May he files an opposition with an affidavit. What happens?

- (A) It is in time; the registrar stays execution and sends the matter to court.
- (B) It is late; 20 days ran out.
- (C) It is in time, but execution continues until the court decides.
- (D) The registrar decides the opposition himself.

*Reasoning:* An opposition to execution of a bill is filed within 30 days of service of the warning (Execution Law s. 81א(ג1)); once filed, the registrar stays execution and transfers the matter to court (s. 81א(ג); reg. 108). **Answer: A.**

**Item 5.** A client complains to the district ethics committee about his lawyer. The committee decides not to file a charge. What must it do?

- (A) Nothing; its decision is internal.
- (B) Give the complainant and the lawyer a reasoned written notice, telling the complainant he may turn to another prosecutor.
- (C) Refer the complaint automatically to the national tribunal.
- (D) Allow the complainant to file the charge himself in the tribunal.

*Reasoning:* Rule 8 of the 2015 procedure rules requires a reasoned written notice and information that the complainant may turn to another prosecutor (the national committee, the Attorney General or the State Attorney under s. 63). A complainant is not a prosecutor. **Answer: B.**

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Partly. You may defend yourself in a criminal case, but the court must appoint a defender in the cases in CPL s. 15, and without one you cannot be sent to prison (s. 15ב); see lesson [24.9](../module-24/lesson-09.md). A debtor may act alone in the Execution Office and in insolvency (lessons [28.8](../module-28/lesson-08.md), [28.9](../module-28/lesson-09.md)). Anyone may complain to the Bar's ethics committee (lesson [35.6](../module-35/lesson-06.md)).

### Where and how

- **Arrest and interrogation:** ask for a lawyer and for the Public Defender if eligible (Public Defender Law s. 19).
- **Execution Office:** the Courts and Execution Authority's online services; [execution debtor response kit](../../templates/execution-debtor-response-kit.md).
- **Ethics complaints:** the [Bar ethics complaint template](../../templates/bar-ethics-complaint.md).

> [!WARNING]
> Criminal exposure is the clearest case for a lawyer. If you are a suspect, use your right to consult a lawyer before you answer questions; this review lesson is not a guide to handling an interrogation.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You are arrested, summoned for interrogation under caution, or receive a s. 60א notice in a felony.
- A creditor asks to sell your home, or a trustee alleges a preference or a transfer to hide assets.
- You are the lawyer under complaint: discipline has professional consequences beyond the case.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Treating 48 hours as the time to a judge | Wrong answer; in real life, a missed release claim | Learn s. 29: 24 hours | Use the "numbers by stage" card |
| Confusing admissibility with sufficiency | Convicting on an unsupported s. 10א statement | Ask the second question: what support is required? | Two-question method |
| Applying s. 56א in a civil case | Wrong rule; the civil routes are s. 32 Privacy Law and s. 13 Eavesdropping Law | Re-read the section's first words | Note "בית המשפט הדן במשפט פלילי" |
| Missing the 30-day opposition window | Execution continues on the bill | Apply to extend with an affidavit (reg. 109) | Diarise 20, 21 and 30 days from the warning |
| Expecting discharge to free a guarantor | The guarantor is still sued | Plan the guarantor's own position | Remember s. 174's "והוא בלבד" |

## Review

- Criminal-procedure periods belong to stages: 3 hours, 24 hours, 15/30 days, 75 days, 30 days and 9 months, 45 days.
- An unrepresented defendant cannot be imprisoned; the prosecution, not the court, is bound by a plea bargain; a defendant's own appeal cannot raise his sentence.
- Evidence: competence first, privilege second, hearsay third, then sufficiency; s. 56א is criminal only.
- Execution runs from the warning (20, 21, 30 days); insolvency turns on the door, the opening order and what discharge leaves untouched.
- Discipline: anyone complains, only listed bodies prosecute; appeal to the Jerusalem District Court within 30 days.
