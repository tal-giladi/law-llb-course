# 38.1 · The Bar exam: structure, rules and strategy

This lesson opens the review module. You will learn how the Israeli Bar exam is built (three written parts, the subjects listed in the regulations, the pass mark and its recent exceptions), where in this course each examined subject is taught, and how to manage time in a 40-question paper. You will finish with a timed drill on the exam's own rules and a personal revision plan that the rest of the module fills in.

## Why it matters

Modules 1–37 taught Israeli law subject by subject. The Bar exam asks the same law in a different way: hundreds of rules, tested as short scenarios with four answers, under time pressure, with the statute open in front of you for part of the exam. Knowing the law and passing a multiple-choice exam on it are different skills. This module trains the second skill and does not re-teach: every review lesson maps the Bar subjects to the lessons that teach them, gives the rules most often examined in compact tables, and then makes you practise.

Be clear about what this module can and cannot do. Nobody can sit the Bar exam on the strength of this course. The Bar Association Law requires a recognised legal education (an LL.B. from a recognised Israeli faculty or college, or a recognised foreign route), a 12-month supervised internship and only then the Bar exams (ss. 24, 25, 35, 38; lesson [35.1](../module-35/lesson-01.md)). This course is not a degree and gives no exemption (lesson [01.1](../module-01/lesson-01.md)). What the module gives you is a realistic self-test: if you can answer Bar-style questions, you can read a statute and a scenario accurately, which is exactly what you need to run your own affairs. If you are, or will be, a law student or an intern, it is also a structured revision course for the real exam.

## The law

### The gate: ss. 24 and 38 of the Bar Association Law

> אדם כשיר להיות עורך דין אם נתקיימו בו אלה: (1) הוא בעל השכלה משפטית גבוהה; (2) עבר תקופת התמחות; (3) עמד בבחינות הלשכה

*Gist:* A person qualifies to be a lawyer only after recognised legal education, an internship and the Bar exams. [s. 24](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לשכת_עורכי_הדין#סעיף_24)

> בתום תקופת ההתמחות על המועמד לחברות בלשכה להיבחן בבחינות הלשכה להסמכה לעריכת דין, שיהיו בין השאר בדין המהותי ובסדרי דין

*Gist:* The exam comes at the end of the internship and covers substantive and procedural law. [s. 38(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לשכת_עורכי_הדין#סעיף_38)

The internship lasts 12 months (s. 35(א)). The written exam is set by a nine-member committee appointed by the Minister of Justice: three judges, four lawyers (two from the public legal service) and two academics (s. 40). The details are in the exam regulations ("the Regulations" below).

### Sittings and applications: regs 15–16

> בחינת הלשכה להסמכה לעריכת דין לפי סעיף 38 לחוק (להלן – בחינת ההסמכה) תיערך פעמיים בשנה

*Gist:* The exam is held twice a year; the Minister may add a sitting for special reasons (reg. 15א). [reg. 15](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_לשכת_עורכי_הדין_(סדרי_בחינות_בדיני_מדינת_ישראל,_באתיקה_מקצועית_החלה_על_עורכי_דין_זרים_ובבחינת_ההסמכה_לעריכת_דין))

In practice the sittings are in June and December, announced at least 90 days ahead (research file; lesson 35.1). The application to sit is filed "לא יאוחר מ־45 ימים לפני מועד הבחינה", no later than 45 days before the exam, with a power to accept late applications in special circumstances (reg. 16).

### Three parts, minimum weights: reg. 18

> בחינת ההסמכה תיערך בכתב ותורכב משלושה חלקים

*Gist:* The exam is written and has three parts: a multiple-choice questionnaire on procedural law and practical subjects, a multiple-choice questionnaire on substantive law, and a writing task. Each questionnaire carries at least 40% of the total grade and the writing task at least 15% (reg. 18(ג)). [reg. 18](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_לשכת_עורכי_הדין_(סדרי_בחינות_בדיני_מדינת_ישראל,_באתיקה_מקצועית_החלה_על_עורכי_דין_זרים_ובבחינת_ההסמכה_לעריכת_דין))

The committee publishes, **no later than three months before the exam day**, the order of the parts, the time for each, each part's weight and the list of statutes (reg. 18(ד)). That publication, not this lesson, is the binding description of any given sitting.

### The subjects: reg. 18א

Procedural paper (reg. 18א(א)), in the regulation's own words:

1. "סמכויות בתי המשפט, לרבות בתי דין דתיים ובתי הדין לעבודה על פי כל חיקוק ודין"
2. criminal procedure, including evidence, investigation, arrest, search, taking identification means and enforcement powers
3. civil procedure, including special procedures for non-criminal branches, evidence, out-of-court dispute resolution, enforcement of foreign judgments and choice-of-law rules
4. "הליכי הוצאה לפועל, חדלות פירעון, פשיטת רגל וכינוס נכסים"
5. "היבטים דיוניים של חוקי היסוד"
6. disciplinary procedure under the Bar Association Law, including complaints, prosecution, the conduct of proceedings and appeals

Substantive paper (reg. 18א(ב)):

1. "דיני החיובים, לרבות דיני החוזים ודיני הנזיקין"
2. "דיני העונשין, לרבות דרכי הענישה"
3. "דיני הקניין, לרבות דיני הירושה"
4. "משפט מסחרי, לרבות דיני התאגידים"
5. "דיני האתיקה המקצועית הנוגעים לעורכי דין"
6. "חוקי יסוד"
7. "מיסוי מקרקעין"
8. "התיישנות"

> לשם מענה על השאלות בבחינה נדרשת היכרות עם הפרשנות שניתנה בפסיקה

*Gist:* Answering the questions requires knowing how the courts have interpreted the statutes, not only their text. [reg. 18א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_לשכת_עורכי_הדין_(סדרי_בחינות_בדיני_מדינת_ישראל,_באתיקה_מקצועית_החלה_על_עורכי_דין_זרים_ובבחינת_ההסמכה_לעריכת_דין))

### Passing, appeals and retakes: regs 18ג–21

> מי שקיבל ציון של 60 אחוזים לפחות מהציון הכולל בבחינה, יראו אותו כמי שעבר את הבחינה.

*Gist:* The pass mark is 60% of the total grade. [reg. 18ג](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_לשכת_עורכי_הדין_(סדרי_בחינות_בדיני_מדינת_ישראל,_באתיקה_מקצועית_החלה_על_עורכי_דין_זרים_ובבחינת_ההסמכה_לעריכת_דין))

Temporary orders have changed the figure for particular sittings: 55% for the exam held on 23.6.2026, and 56% for the December 2024 sitting (Globes; [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)). A temporary order in force from 1.12.2024 to 31.12.2026 lets certain reservists and their spouses who scored 55–59% sit a supplementary oral exam (reg. 18ג(ב)), and gives eligible groups extra time per part and, in some cases, a statute list cut by at least 20% (reg. 18(ה)–(ט)). Candidates with disabilities may ask for accommodations (reg. 18ד).

> ערר לפי פסקה (1) יוגש בכתב, מנומק, בתוך 14 ימים מיום קבלת ההודעה על הציון

*Gist:* An appeal against a grade is written, reasoned and filed within 14 days of notice of the grade; a writing-task appeal is marked by a different examiner, and decisions are due within 30 days of the filing deadline (reg. 18ה). If the committee finds significant flaws in the exam, it may add points to everyone or to those who answered a given part, and must publish its reasons (reg. 18ה1).

> נבחן שנכשל בבחינה רשאי לשוב ולהיבחן במועדים שנקבעו

*Gist:* A candidate who failed may sit again at later sittings; the text sets no maximum number of attempts. [reg. 21(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_לשכת_עורכי_הדין_(סדרי_בחינות_בדיני_מדינת_ישראל,_באתיקה_מקצועית_החלה_על_עורכי_דין_זרים_ובבחינת_ההסמכה_לעריכת_דין))

## Leading cases

### עע״ם 1758/10 לשכת עורכי הדין בישראל נ׳ שלום שגיא (15.8.2011)

A candidate passed the exam, but a civil court had found that he took funds from his employers and a police investigation was pending. The Supreme Court upheld the Bar's decision to defer his admission: passing the exam does not create an absolute right to be admitted, the Bar's admission decisions are administrative and reviewed for reasonableness and proportionality, and "other facts" proved by administrative evidence may justify a refusal under s. 44(א)(2). Why it matters here: the exam is one of several gates, and the ethics material in lesson 38.6 is not only an exam subject but the standard by which the Bar decides who enters. [Full text on Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פסק_דין_עע%22ם_1758/10_לשכת_עורכי_הדין_בישראל_נ%27_שלום_שגיא)

### Key text: the committee's notice for each sitting

There is no court ruling that tells you how a given sitting is built; the committee's notice under reg. 18(ד) does. It fixes the parts, times and weights and, most importantly, the statute lists for the procedural and substantive papers. Prep schools report that the statutes are examined in their version in force one month before the exam, as interpreted in rulings published up to three months before it (Raisman, unofficial). Read the notice first, then build your plan on it.

### Strategy: four rules for a multiple-choice paper

1. **Pace by the clock, not by the question.** On the reported format you have 2.5 minutes per procedural question and 4 minutes per substantive question. Write checkpoint times on the paper: after question 10, 20 and 30.
2. **Three passes.** First pass: answer what you know at once and mark the rest. Second pass: work the marked questions with the statute booklet. Third pass: guess anything still open. The Regulations say nothing about a penalty for a wrong answer; prep schools report none (⚠️ confirm in the committee's notice). If that holds, a blank is never better than a guess.
3. **Read the stem for the trap word.** Bar questions turn on one word: "ללא רשות" versus "ברשות", "מהיום שהומצא" versus "מיום מתן", "פשע" versus "עוון", "בתום לב ובתמורה". Underline it before reading the options.
4. **Eliminate, then choose.** Strike options that state a wrong period, a wrong court or a rule from another statute. Two options often look right; the correct one usually matches the statute's exact condition, and the wrong one adds or drops a condition.

### What the substantive booklet changes

If you get the statutes in Part C, the question is rarely "what does s. X say". It is "apply s. X to these facts", often combined with a ruling that interpreted it (reg. 18א's last sentence). Learn the **location** of each key rule (which law, which section) so you can find it in seconds, and learn the **leading interpretations**, which the booklet does not contain.

## Worked example

Maysa Khoury from Nazareth finished her LL.B. and is in the ninth month of her internship. She wants to sit the next sitting in the procedural and substantive papers and has 12 weeks.

1. **Check the gate.** She will finish the 12-month internship before the sitting (s. 35), and must file her application no later than 45 days before the exam (reg. 16). She diarises the filing date and the 14-day appeal window that will open with her results (reg. 18ה).
2. **Read the notice.** She downloads the committee's notice and statute lists for her sitting (reg. 18(ד)). She does not rely on last year's list.
3. **Diagnose.** She takes the lesson quizzes of this module cold and scores 50% in execution and insolvency, 80% in contracts. She allocates weeks by weakness, not by her favourite subjects.
4. **Plan.** Weeks 1–8: one review lesson (38.2–38.6) per 1–2 weeks, each ending with its 40-minute drill and an error log. Weeks 9–10: the drafting task (38.7) twice a week. Weeks 11–12: the full mock (38.8) twice, a week apart.
5. **Pace.** In her first timed drill she spends 9 minutes on one question about third-party notice. Her rule from now on: after 4 minutes, mark it and move on.

A worked item of the kind she will see:

> A candidate failed the June sitting with 58. Two weeks and three days after receiving her grade she files a reasoned appeal against the marking of the writing task. What is the likely result?

The appeal is out of time: reg. 18ה requires a written, reasoned appeal within 14 days of notice of the grade. She can sit again at the next sitting (reg. 21(א)). Note the trap: if her sitting had been 23.6.2026, her 58 was already a pass, because a temporary order set the pass mark at 55 for that exam (reg. 18ג).

## Do it yourself

### You need

- The committee's current notice and statute lists (if you are a candidate), or this lesson's tables.
- A timer, paper, and 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Rules drill (10 minutes, timed).** Answer the ten questions below without looking back. Then open the answers.
2. **Diagnostic (20 minutes).** Take the lesson quizzes of 38.2–38.6 cold, 5 questions each, without reading those lessons. Record your score per subject.
3. **Plan (15 minutes).** Fill a table with the 14 subjects of reg. 18א, your diagnostic score, the course lessons to revise (from the subject map) and the weeks you will spend. Weakest subjects first.

**Rules drill**

1. How many parts does the written exam have, and which carries the smallest minimum weight?
2. What is the minimum weight of each multiple-choice questionnaire?
3. When must the committee publish the statute lists?
4. What is the general pass mark, and where is it set?
5. Which sitting had a pass mark of 55?
6. Within what period is a grade appeal filed, and in what form?
7. Is there a maximum number of attempts?
8. Which paper examines limitation (התיישנות)?
9. Which paper examines disciplinary procedure, and which paper examines the ethics rules?
10. On the reported format, how many minutes per question do you have in the substantive paper?

1. Three parts: procedural questionnaire, substantive questionnaire, writing task; the writing task has the smallest minimum (15%) (reg. 18(א), (ג)).
2. At least 40% each (reg. 18(ג)).
3. No later than three months before the exam day, together with the order of the parts, times and weights (reg. 18(ד)).
4. 60% of the total grade (reg. 18ג).
5. The exam held on 23.6.2026, by temporary order (reg. 18ג).
6. 14 days from notice of the grade, in writing and reasoned (reg. 18ה).

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Partly. Anyone may study for the Bar and take the practice papers in this module. Only a person with a recognised legal education who completed the internship may sit the exam (ss. 24, 35, 38; lesson [35.1](../module-35/lesson-01.md)), and only a candidate may apply for accommodations or file a grade appeal, in his own name.

### Where and how

- **Exam notices, statute lists, registration, results and appeals:** the Bar's official website, interns' section (⚠️ manual check: open it in your browser; do not follow redirects to unfamiliar domains).
- **The Regulations:** Wikisource (unofficial consolidation) and the Knesset's National Legislation Database for the official version.
- **Free help:** your faculty's career or Bar-preparation unit; the Bar's interns' department for administrative questions.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The Bar refuses to register you as an intern, to let you sit, or to admit you after you passed: these are administrative decisions with short deadlines for a petition (lesson [35.1](../module-35/lesson-01.md)).
- Your grade appeal was rejected and you believe the marking or a question was legally flawed: the next step is an administrative petition, where a lawyer's assessment of the prospects is worth paying for.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Studying from last year's statute list | You learn repealed or unlisted material and miss new statutes | Switch to the current list at once and re-plan | Read the committee's notice first (reg. 18(ד)) |
| Treating ethics and discipline as one subject | You revise the rules but not the procedure, or the reverse | Split them in your plan | Remember: discipline is procedural, ethics substantive (reg. 18א) |
| Leaving blanks | Lost points if wrong answers carry no penalty | Guess on the third pass | Confirm the scoring rule in the notice; then never leave a blank |
| Spending 9 minutes on one question | Ten easy questions at the end go unanswered | Mark and move on after the time limit | Set checkpoints after questions 10, 20 and 30 |
| Missing the 14-day appeal window | The appeal is not heard | Sit the next sitting (reg. 21(א)) | Diarise the window the day results are due |

## Review

- The exam is written and has three parts: procedural multiple choice, substantive multiple choice (each at least 40%) and a writing task (at least 15%) (reg. 18).
- Reg. 18א lists six procedural and eight substantive subjects; knowledge of case-law interpretation is expressly required.
- The pass mark is 60 (reg. 18ג), lowered by temporary order for specific sittings (55 for 23.6.2026); appeals within 14 days (reg. 18ה); no limit on retakes (reg. 21(א)).
- The committee's notice, published at least three months ahead, binds each sitting; the next sitting's date and list are ⚠️ unverified here.
- No one can sit the exam without a recognised LL.B. and the 12-month internship; this module is a self-test and a revision course.
