# 38.7 · The writing task: drafting a legal document under time pressure

This lesson prepares you for the third part of the Bar exam: a short legal document written under a strict time limit. You will learn what the Regulations say about the task, the marking criteria reported for it, a 45-minute method, and the structural rules that every pleading, motion and notice must follow. You will then draft a statement of claim and a notice of cancellation in Hebrew against the clock and compare them with model answers.

## Why it matters

The writing task carries at least 15% of the total grade (reg. 18(ג)), and on the format reported for recent sittings 20% (lesson [38.1](lesson-01.md)). It is the part candidates practise least and lose most cheaply: a missing heading element, a remedy without a sum, a defence that answers nothing. It is also the most practical skill in this course: the same structure carries your own statement of claim, defence, motion or letter in every case you run yourself (lessons [15.10](../module-15/lesson-10.md), [15.11](../module-15/lesson-11.md), [02.7](../module-02/lesson-07.md)).

## The law

### The task in the Regulations

The writing task is the third part of the exam, worth at least 15% (reg. 18(א), (ג)). The committee's notice for each sitting fixes its time and weight (reg. 18(ד)). A writing-task grade may be appealed within 14 days and is re-marked by a different examiner (reg. 18ה). The Regulations do not say which kind of document is set; read the notice and, if available, past papers. A prep school reports that candidates choose one task from those offered, have 45 minutes, and are marked on a general rubric of **language, organisation and content** (Raisman; ⚠️ unofficial).

### What the rules require of the documents you are most likely to draft

| Document | Required structure | Source | Taught in |
|---|---|---|---|

| Summary of a claim | Parties, remedy, the facts founding the cause and when it arose, facts giving jurisdiction | reg. 11 | [15.2](../module-15/lesson-02.md) |
| Detail | Facts and what clarifies the dispute; no case-law essay | reg. 14(א) | [15.2](../module-15/lesson-02.md) |

## Leading cases

### ע״א 9648/16 אורהייטק GIS בע״מ נ׳ חן אביטן – משרד עורכי דין (28.2.2018)

A dispute over whether a fee percentage applied to costs awarded to the client. The Supreme Court held that the drafter bears the burden of clarity and an unclear term is read against him; a percentage fee does not reach awarded costs unless the agreement says so expressly. Bar point for the writing task: **write what you mean in the operative clause, not in your head**; the reader decides what an ambiguous sentence means. [Text hosted by Reichman University](https://www.colman.ac.il/media/4qbiik4c/9648-16_orhightech_v_avitan.doc)

### Key text: regs 9–11 as a marking scheme

There is no ruling on how the writing task is marked. The best proxy is the registry's own gatekeeping: reg. 33 lets the registry refuse a pleading that breaks regs 9(ג)–(ד), 10 or 12 (lesson [15.2](../module-15/lesson-02.md)). If a court registry would refuse your document for its structure, assume an examiner will mark it down for organisation.

## How it works in practice

### A 45-minute method

| Minutes | Step |
|---|---|
| 0–5 | Read the task twice. Underline: who you act for, the document type, the forum, the facts that are legally decisive, the remedy asked for. |
| 5–12 | Plan on scrap paper: the legal basis (sections), the remedy and its sum or wording, the structure required by the rules. |
| 12–38 | Write. Heading first (it earns easy marks), then the summary, then the detail in short numbered paragraphs, one fact per paragraph. |
| 38–43 | Remedy section and signature block; check every sum adds up. |
| 43–45 | Proofread: names, dates, sums, section numbers, the court. |

### What earns marks under each criterion

| Criterion | What examiners look for |
|---|---|
| Content | The right cause and section; every element of the cause pleaded with its facts; the right remedy, with a sum; jurisdiction and venue facts; no invented facts beyond the task |
| Organisation | The structure the rules require; numbered paragraphs; facts before law; one subject per paragraph; headings |
| Language | Clear, formal Hebrew; short sentences; consistent terms (התובעת / הנתבעת throughout); no slang, no abuse (reg. 32) |

### Common task types and their skeleton

The Regulations do not list task types, and no official list of past tasks could be verified. The documents below follow from the subjects on the procedural and substantive papers; the [drafting skeletons template](../../templates/bar-drafting-task-skeletons.md) gives a Hebrew skeleton for each.

| Document | Skeleton |
|---|---|
| Statement of claim | Heading (reg. 10) → summary (reg. 11) → detail → remedies |
| Statement of defence | Heading (reg. 12) → summary: preliminary arguments, answer to each cause, remedy (reg. 13) → detail with express denials |
| Written motion | Heading → the order sought → grounds (facts, then law) → affidavit |
| Demand or cancellation letter | Parties and date → the contract and the breach → the step (extension or cancellation) and its legal basis → demand and deadline → reservation of rights |
| Contract clause | Definitions → operative obligation → condition and time → remedy for breach |
| Short legal opinion or memo | Question → short answer → facts → analysis by element → conclusion (lesson [02.6](../module-02/lesson-06.md)) |

## Worked example

Tomer, an intern, practises with a task: "Write a notice from Yael (landlord) to the tenant, Eli, who has not paid two months' rent under a one-year lease that makes non-payment of rent a fundamental breach."

1. **Read.** Act for the landlord; document: a notice; decisive facts: two months unpaid; the lease makes non-payment fundamental.
2. **Plan.** Basis: an agreed fundamental breach allows cancellation at once (Remedies Law ss. 6, 7(א)); notice within a reasonable time (s. 8); delivery by the agreed method or registered mail (s. 21). A blanket clause making every breach fundamental is valid only if reasonable (s. 6), but a clause naming non-payment of rent is specific. Remedy: cancellation, vacating by a date, payment of arrears; reserve damages.
3. **Write** (excerpt):

> הנדון: הודעה על ביטול הסכם השכירות מיום 1.3.2026 בשל הפרה יסודית
> 1. ביום 1.3.2026 נחתם בינינו הסכם שכירות לדירה ברחוב ...
> 2. לפי סעיף 7 להסכם, אי תשלום דמי שכירות במועדם הוא הפרה יסודית של ההסכם.
> 3. לא שילמת את דמי השכירות לחודשים אוגוסט וספטמבר 2026, בסך כולל של ...
> 4. לפיכך, ובהתאם לסעיפים 6 ו־7(א) לחוק החוזים (תרופות בשל הפרת חוזה), התשל״א–1970, הנני מודיעה על ביטול ההסכם.

4. **Check.** Tomer's first draft also "gave seven days to pay or the lease will be cancelled". That is an extension, needed only for a non-fundamental breach (s. 7(ב)); mixing an extension and an immediate cancellation in one letter makes it unclear which step was taken. He chooses one: immediate cancellation, with a date to vacate.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A timer, paper or a word processor without spell-check suggestions (to simulate the exam), and the Hebrew keyboard.
- 45 minutes for task 1, 20 minutes for task 2, 30 minutes to mark both against the models and the rubric.

### Steps

1. Read task 1, set the timer to 45 minutes and draft. Do not look at the model.
2. Mark your draft with the rubric table above: 0–2 points for each of content, organisation and language, and list what you missed.
3. Open the model answer and compare line by line.
4. Do task 2 in 20 minutes the next day; mark it the same way.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Drafting your own claim, defence, motion or letter is acting for yourself (lesson [01.1](../module-01/lesson-01.md)). Drafting such documents for another person, for pay or as an occupation, is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20(3)).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Where and how

- File claims and motions on Net HaMishpat or at the court registry (lesson [08.8](../module-08/lesson-08.md)); fees and fast-track ceilings in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Court information centres can check form, not content.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The other side is represented and the claim is large or complex.
- You are unsure of the cause of action or the forum, and limitation is close.
- The document is a contract that will govern a long relationship or a large asset.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Writing the facts as one long story | Lost organisation marks; refused filing | Break into numbered paragraphs | One fact per paragraph |
| A remedy without a sum or a legal basis | Lost content marks; vague judgment | Add the sum and section | Plan the remedy before writing |
| Mixing an extension and a cancellation in one letter | Unclear which step was taken | Send a clear follow-up | Decide: fundamental or not (s. 6) |
| Case-law essay in the detail | Breaks reg. 14(א); wastes time | Keep law short and after the facts | Facts first, law in a few lines |
| Insulting language about the other side | Breaks reg. 32; loses language marks | Remove adjectives | Describe acts, not character |

## Review

- The writing task is at least 15% of the grade (reg. 18) and is reported to be 45 minutes, marked on language, organisation and content.
- A pleading has a heading, a summary and a detail, in that order (reg. 9(ג)), with the heading's details in the order of reg. 10.
- Plan before writing: party, document, forum, decisive facts, remedy with a sum and section.
- Notices: extension for a non-fundamental breach, immediate cancellation only for a fundamental one; deliver provably.
- Write clearly: an unclear text is read against its drafter (Orhightech).
