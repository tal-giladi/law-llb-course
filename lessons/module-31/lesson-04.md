# 31.4 · Registering rights in land

An order declares who the heirs are; it does not change the land register. In this lesson you take the order (or orders) from lesson 31.3 to the Land Registry and get the heirs registered in place of the deceased, by the route that fits you, and then plan what the heirs will do with the property: keep it together, divide it by agreement, buy each other out or sell. The registration rules are in lessons 13.2 and 26.7; this lesson runs the file.

## Why it matters

Thousands of Israeli flats are still registered to owners who died decades ago. Nothing forces the heirs to register, so they do not, until a sale, a mortgage or a divorce needs a clean extract. By then a second generation has died, the orders are older, an heir has moved abroad, and the registry wants a chain of orders for every death. Registering right after the order costs one request and one fee; registering twenty years later can cost a lawyer and months.

Registration also protects the heirs. While the extract shows the deceased as owner, an heir cannot register a caveat on a sale of his share, cannot mortgage it, and has a harder time proving his rights against a creditor of another heir.

## The law

### Inheritance is registered on application, not by deed

Inheritance is not a "transaction" in land (Land Law s. 6), so no deed of transfer is signed. Registration of an inheritance covers a succession or probate order, a distribution agreement or order, and an order appointing an administrator (Land Management and Registration Regulations reg. 17(א); lesson [26.7](../module-26/lesson-07.md)).

> (ב) רישום ירושה ייערך על פי בקשה של מעוניין בדבר; על אף האמור, רישום הסכם חלוקת עיזבון כאמור בתקנת משנה (א) שאינו מאושר בידי בית משפט ייערך על פי בקשת כל היורשים.

*Gist:* Any interested person may apply to register an inheritance, but an estate-distribution agreement not approved by a court is registered only on the application of all the heirs. [reg. 17(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_המקרקעין_(ניהול_ורישום))

> הבקשה לרישום ירושה תוגש בלשכה שרשומים בה המקרקעין שלגביהם מבוקש הרישום.

*Gist:* The application is filed at the registry office where the land is registered; an order given or approved by a court, a religious court or the Registrar of Inheritance is attached, and an appointed administrator files or consents. [reg. 18(א)–(ג)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_המקרקעין_(ניהול_ורישום))

### The fee

> בקשה לרישום ירושה על פי דין או על פי צוואה – [...]

*Gist:* The 2026 fee for an application to register an inheritance, by law or by will, is set in the Schedule (item 3(א)); a family member of a person who died in military service is exempt (item 3(ב)); a note of a probate order or of an administrator's appointment carries the same fee (item 4). [Land Fees Regulations, Schedule](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_המקרקעין_(אגרות)), all amounts "updated for 2026", checked 18 October 2026; see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### Before distribution, a share in the estate, not in an asset

An heir may transfer or charge his share in the estate by a written agreement before distribution, but not his right in a specific asset (Succession Law s. 7). After registration the heirs are co-owners of the land in the shares of the order, and each may deal with his registered share (lesson [13.5](../module-13/lesson-05.md)). A distribution agreement among heirs is governed by s. 110.

### Appeal against a registrar's refusal

> התקופה להגשת ערר לממונה על החלטת רשם או מפקח, כאמור בסעיף 121 לחוק, [...]

*Gist:* A registrar's decision may be appealed to the registration commissioner within the period in reg. 74 (see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)), and from there to the District Court. [reg. 74](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_המקרקעין_(ניהול_ורישום))

## Leading cases

### Key text: Land Law s. 6 and regs 17–18

There is no leading appellate ruling on an uncontested inheritance registration; the regulations carry the procedure. Their structure answers the two questions families ask. "Does my brother have to come?" No: any interested person applies for the order's registration (reg. 17(ב), first part). "Then why does he have to sign the division?" Because a distribution agreement is the heirs' own contract, and without a court's approval the registry registers it only on everyone's application (reg. 17(ב), second part). Text: [regs 17–18](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_המקרקעין_(ניהול_ורישום)).

### בג״ץ 6698/95 קעדאן נ׳ מינהל מקרקעי ישראל (8.3.2000)

Briefed in lesson [13.2](../module-13/lesson-02.md): the ILA's allocations and conditions are administrative acts, reviewable for equality and reasonableness. Why it matters here: when the family home is ILA leasehold not registered in the Land Registry, the heirs deal with a public authority, not a private landlord, and its inheritance procedure, fees and conditions must be lawful, reasoned and equal (lesson 13.2 has the source).

## How it works in practice

### Step 1: where are the rights?

Order a current extract (lesson [13.2](../module-13/lesson-02.md)) and read it with the [land-registry heirs-registration checklist](../../templates/land-registry-heirs-registration-checklist.md), part A.

| The extract shows | Where the heirs register | This lesson? |
|---|---|---|
| The deceased as owner, or as lessee of a registered lease | The Land Registry office for the property | Yes |
| ILA as owner and no registered lease for the deceased, or the flat is not on the extract | The ILA's inheritance procedure (⚠️ manual check of the current ILA service) | No: lesson 13.2 |
| The parcel, with caveats for buyers; no sub-parcel for the flat | The developer or housing company's records | No: ask the company in writing |

### Step 2: is every death covered?

Every registered owner who died needs an order. In Liat's case (lesson 31.3) the extract shows "משה ששון ½, אסתר ששון ½": both orders are needed. If a grandparent still appears on the extract, his estate's order is needed too, which may itself be a paper application.

### Step 3: choose the route

| Route | How | Good for | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| With the online application for the order | Tick the request to register in the Land Registry and attach the signed request form; pay the registration fee online | The common case: the Registrar gives the order and passes it to the registry | Press reports from May 2024 describe this as open to unrepresented applicants (⚠️ secondary; check the current form) |
| Paper, at the registry office | Inheritance-registration request signed by at least one heir; signature verified before a registry official or a lawyer; filed by post or in person by appointment | An order you already hold; a court or religious-court order; an old order | Book an appointment; bring ID; a court order is attached as a certified copy (reg. 18(ב)) |
| Online, directly at the Land Registry | Smart-card system | Lawyers only | Not available to private persons |

The verification rule for a private person's signature on the paper request is described by Kol-Zchut as a lawyer or a registry official (⚠️ manual check); for deeds signed at the office the regulations name the registrar (reg. 12).

### Step 4: after registration

Order a new extract and check that each heir appears with the right fraction. Tell the municipality who now holds the property for arnona (lesson 31.7). Then decide, in writing, among the heirs:

| Option | What is registered | Who must sign | Advice? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Keep it together | Nothing more | No one | Write a short co-owners' agreement on use, rent and costs |
| Divide by a distribution agreement (one takes the flat, the other the savings) | The agreement (reg. 17(ב)) | All heirs | Usually; check tax treatment of an exchange |
| One heir buys the other's share for money | A transaction: deed of sale and tax declarations | Both | Yes: purchase and appreciation tax rules apply (lessons 25.6, 25.7) |
| Sell to a third party | Sale by all co-owners | All | As any sale (lesson 13.9) |

## Worked example

**Supplied case B, continued.** Liat filed both succession applications online on 20 October 2026 (lesson 31.3). In her father Moshe's application she ticked the request to register the heirs in the Land Registry, but in her mother Esther's application she did not, because she only learned of the option afterwards.

1. **What will happen.** When Moshe's order is given, the registry receives the request for his half. Esther's half stays registered to her until her order is registered too. Liat diarises: "Esther's order given → file the paper request at the Petah Tikva registry office."
2. **The shares.** The registry will register each order's shares in the deceased's part. The final extract should read Liat ½, Eitan ½ (⅛ + ⅜ each, lesson 31.3). Liat writes the expected extract in her file to compare.
3. **The paper request for Esther's half.** Using the checklist, part C and D: the current form, signed by Liat alone (any interested person may apply); Esther's details (the Registrar's order is located by the registry); the fee in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md). Liat books an appointment at the registry office to sign before an official, rather than paying a lawyer to verify.
4. **Refusal and recovery.** The registry asks for Moshe's order first, because Esther's order lists Moshe as an heir who has since died. Liat supplies it; no ערר is needed. Had the registrar refused on a legal point, she would have had the reg. 74 period for a ערר (see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
5. **Next.** The new extract shows Liat ½, Eitan ½. Eitan wants his share in money. Because a buy-out for money is a sale between co-owners with tax declarations (lessons 25.6, 25.7), Liat logs a red-flag entry: "Before signing any buy-out, get a one-hour consultation on purchase and appreciation tax." Meanwhile she informs the municipality that the heirs now hold the flat.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. File only as an heir in a real, uncontested estate.

### You need

- A current land extract for the property (electronic extract; fee in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- The order or orders from lesson 31.3, or the Sasson case.
- The [land-registry heirs-registration checklist](../../templates/land-registry-heirs-registration-checklist.md) (draft in this module) and the land part of the [succession order application checklist](../../templates/succession-order-application-checklist.md) (part F).
- About two hours, plus an appointment at the registry office if you use the paper route.

### Steps

1. **Read the extract (20 min).** Part A of the checklist: owners, shares, type of right, mortgages, attachments, notes. Decide from the table in step 1 whether the Land Registry is the right register at all.
2. **Chain of orders (15 min).** Part B: one row per registered owner who died; is there an order for each? Compute the expected final shares.
3. **Route (15 min).** Part C: online with the order application, or paper at the office; who signs; who verifies.
4. **Prepare the request (30 min).** Part D: download the current form from the Land Registry's forms page (⚠️ manual check of the form name), fill it, list the attachments, compute the fee for each request.
5. **File or stop (15 min).** If every item is green, file by your chosen route and log it. If part G lists a red flag, stop and record why.
6. **After registration (15 min).** Part E: order the new extract, compare with your expected shares, update the municipality, diarise any ערר period if refused.
7. **Plan the next step (10 min).** Part F: keep together, distribution agreement, buy-out or sale; who must sign; whether advice is needed.

### Template

- [Land-registry heirs-registration checklist](../../templates/land-registry-heirs-registration-checklist.md) (draft in this module): parts A to G.
- [Succession or probate order application checklist](../../templates/succession-order-application-checklist.md), part F.
- [Caveat request checklist](../../templates/caveat-request-checklist.md) (lesson 13.4), if an heir later sells his registered share.

### How you know it worked

- The new extract shows every heir in the order's shares, and no deceased remains registered.
- Every request you filed has a receipt and a log entry.
- You know, in writing, what the heirs plan to do next and who must sign.

### Self-check

- [ ] I checked that the rights are registered in the Land Registry and not only at the ILA or the developer.
- [ ] Every registered owner who died is covered by an order.
- [ ] I know that one heir may apply to register the order, but all must apply for an unapproved distribution agreement.
- [ ] I recognise that a buy-out between heirs is a taxable transaction that needs advice.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Partly, and enough for the clinic. An heir may file the inheritance-registration request on paper, signing before a registry official, or with the online application for the order; online filing directly at the Land Registry is for lawyers ([can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md)). Representing another heir before the Land Registry is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20(2)).

### Where and how

- The Land Registry office where the property is registered (reg. 18(א)), by appointment; or the registration request inside the online application to the Registrar of Inheritance.
- Fees: inheritance registration and extracts in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Help: Kol-Zchut's page on registering heirs' rights (⚠️ manual check); the Land Registry's forms page (⚠️ manual check); [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The property is ILA leasehold not registered in the Land Registry, or in a building not yet registered as a condominium.
- An heir refuses to sign a distribution agreement, or one heir wants to buy out another (tax).
- The extract shows an attachment, a mortgage in default, a caveat for someone else, or a deceased owner from a generation ago with no orders.
- The registrar refuses and the reason is legal rather than a missing document.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Assuming the order registers itself | Extract still shows the deceased years later | File the request now | Tick the registration request online, or file on paper the week the order arrives |
| Registering only the second parent's order | The first parent's share stays in her name | Register the first order too | Map every deceased owner (part B) |
| Filing a distribution agreement signed by some heirs | Refused (reg. 17(ב)) | All heirs sign, or court approval | Sign together |
| Going to the Land Registry for ILA leasehold land | Nothing to register there | Use the ILA's procedure | Read the extract first |
| Treating a buy-out between heirs as "part of the inheritance" | Missed tax declarations and fines | Get advice; file declarations | Ask before signing |

## Review

- An order does not change the register: the heirs must apply to register it at the office where the land is registered.
- Any heir may apply to register an order; an unapproved distribution agreement needs all heirs.
- Check first that the rights are in the Land Registry at all, and that every deceased owner is covered by an order.
- After registration the heirs are co-owners; a buy-out between them is a transaction with tax consequences.
