# 31.6 · Defending an execution file and reaching an arrangement

When an execution file is opened against you, the worst choice is the common one: to do nothing and hope. In this lesson you take your own file (or the supplied one) from the first look at the personal area to a working arrangement: you verify the debt, get protected money released, claim payments the creditor did not report, ask for a payment order you can actually keep, and, after three payments, ask to be recognised as a paying debtor so that the attachments stop. The law is in lessons 28.1 to 28.4 and the full drill in lesson 28.8; this lesson runs the file and its decision points.

## Why it matters

An execution file grows by itself: interest, fees and the creditor's lawyer's fees are added, and once the warning period passes the creditor can ask for bank and salary attachments, a driving or exit ban, and in the end the deemed-evader procedures (lesson [28.2](../module-28/lesson-02.md)). Most debtors in the system owe sums they could pay in instalments; what they lack is the paperwork that turns "I cannot pay it all" into an order the office will enforce against the creditor too.

The law gives that paperwork real force. A payment order caps the period of repayment; a debtor who keeps it is protected from restrictions (s. 66ד(ג)); and since the "paying debtor" chapter, three consecutive payments under a payment order are enough to ask that bank and salary attachments in the file be stayed or cancelled. The clinic case is the ordinary one: a consumer debt, a frozen account and a household that can pay something every month.

## The law

### The payment-order request

> חייב שאין ביכולתו לשלם את החוב הפסוק במלואו או בשיעורים שנקבעו בהוראת תשלום לפי סעיף 69א [...]

*Gist:* A debtor who cannot pay the judgment debt in full, or by the standard payment instruction, applies to the registrar, within the warning period, to pay in instalments that match his means, with a statement of income, assets and expenses and the supporting documents. [Execution Law s. 7א(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_7א) A timely, complete request counts as appearing for the means inquiry (reg. 12ב); a debtor who neither applies nor appears is deemed able to pay and evading (s. 67(ד)). A late request is still worth filing: it opens a means inquiry.

> (א) התקופה לתשלום החוב הפסוק בצו תשלומים… לא תעלה על תקופה כמפורט להלן: (1) שלוש שנים – אם סכום החוב אינו עולה על 100,000 שקלים חדשים; (2) ארבע שנים – אם סכום החוב עולה על 100,000 שקלים חדשים.

*Gist:* A payment order may not run longer than a set period that depends on the size of the debt; a debtor who cannot pay within it is pointed to insolvency (s. 7א2). [s. 7א1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_7א1); figures in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### "I have paid" and arrangements with the creditor

> (א) חייב הטוען שמילא אחר פסק הדין או שאינו חייב עוד למלא אחריו, כולו או מקצתו, עליו הראיה, ורשאי רשם ההוצאה לפועל לקבוע אם ובאיזו מידה מוטל עוד על החייב למלא אחר פסק הדין [...]

*Gist:* A debtor who says he has paid all or part proves it, and the registrar decides how much is still owed. [s. 19](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_19) A creditor must report direct payments (s. 19א), and an instalment arrangement agreed directly with the creditor stops the proceedings while it is kept, once the creditor notifies the office (s. 19ב; lesson [28.4](../module-28/lesson-04.md)).

### The paying debtor: ss. 69ב2–69ב11

The chapter on the "paying debtor" (Wikisource text read on 18 October 2026) defines an instalment direction as one under a payment order or under the Authority's rules (s. 69ב2), and sets the threshold: three consecutive payments under a payment order, or one payment under a rules-based direction (s. 69ב3). A debtor who meets it is recognised as a paying debtor (s. 69ב5), and the registrar then orders that bank-account and salary attachments and attachments of movables other than vehicles not be imposed in the file, or that existing ones be stayed or cancelled (s. 69ב7(2)), subject to exceptions (s. 69ב11). The recognition takes effect 30 days after notice to the creditor (s. 69ב9(ב)). [Execution Law ss. 69ב2–69ב11](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל). The gov.il service for this request describes the same conditions and adds that it does not apply to maintenance files (⚠️ manual check: page read through a search summary).

### Protected wages and benefits

The part of net wages equal to the income-support level for the family, capped at 80% of net wages, cannot be attached and stays protected for a month after it reaches the bank; National Insurance benefits have their own protection ([Wage Protection Law s. 8](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_השכר#סעיף_8); lesson [28.2](../module-28/lesson-02.md)). Banks apply some protections themselves; the rest you ask for.

## Leading cases

### בג״ץ 5304/92 פר״ח 1992 – סיוע משפטי לנפגעי חוק ההוצאה לפועל נ׳ שר המשפטים, פ״ד מז(4) 715 (1993)

Imprisonment orders for debt issued without examining the debtor's means were held to violate liberty and dignity. Why it matters here: the whole system is built around the means inquiry. The debtor who documents his means is treated as a debtor who cannot pay everything; the one who stays silent is treated as one who will not. Briefed in lesson [08.7](../module-08/lesson-07.md) (⚠️ full text not opened; see 08.7 for the secondary sources).

### Key text: s. 66ד(ג) and the paying-debtor chapter

Two provisions reward the debtor who files and pays. A restriction must be cancelled while the debtor keeps a payment order or agreed arrangement (s. 66ד(ג); lesson 28.4), and three consecutive payments under a payment order open the request to stop bank and salary attachments (ss. 69ב3, 69ב7). Why it matters: the clinic's goal is not to win against the creditor but to reach the status in which the file stops hurting while it is paid off.

## How it works in practice

### Decision points

| Decision | Ask | Answer leads to |
|---|---|---|
| 1. What is in the file? | Creditor, document (judgment, bill, fixed-sum claim), principal, interest, fees, service date, measures already taken | The money ledger and the deadline diary (kit, part A) |
| 2. Is the debt itself disputed? | A judgment you were never served? A bill with a defence? | Court route (setting aside a default judgment) or opposition: lesson 28.3; red flag |
| 3. Did you pay anything not credited? | Transfers, receipts, payments to a collection company | "I have paid" request with the payment table (s. 19) |
| 4. Is protected money frozen? | Salary or NII benefits in an attached account | Bank first; then a request to the registrar to release (kit, part C) |
| 5. Can you pay in full or by the standard instruction? | Compare with the household budget | If not: payment-order request with form 21 content and documents |
| 6. Does the offer fit the period? | Balance divided by the offer, against s. 7א1 | If not: insolvency route (lesson 28.5) |
| 7. Arrangement with the creditor? | Would the creditor take a lump sum or instalments directly? | Written arrangement; creditor notifies the office (s. 19ב) |
| 8. Three payments made? | Under the payment order, consecutive | Request to be recognised as a paying debtor |

### Filing

- File each request separately, in your own file, through the Enforcement and Collection Authority's personal area (dedicated request type, or the general request), the online form, or a branch by appointment; call centre \*35592 ([can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md); ⚠️ manual check of current request codes).
- The payment-order request carries the means questionnaire and waiver (form 21) and the documents: payslips, bank statements, rent or mortgage, the family's details, other debts (lesson [28.1](../module-28/lesson-01.md)).
- Kol-Zchut notes that an approved payment-order request does not by itself lift existing restrictions or attachments: ask for that separately in the same filing or right after (⚠️ manual check).

| Line | Creditor's figure | Your figure | Document |
|---|---|---|---|

## Worked example

**Supplied case D.** Yehuda Klein, 44, teaches in a Talmud Torah in Bnei Brak; his wife Rivka is a kindergarten teacher; three children aged 8 to 17 live at home. A credit-card company obtained a default judgment in 2025 for ₪14,800 after he fell behind during a long illness. The warning was served on 9 September 2026; he put it in a drawer. On 12 October his bank account was attached; his October salary and the child allowance were frozen. Today is 18 October 2026. In July 2026 he had paid ₪1,000 to the company's collection agent by bank transfer; the file shows ₪16,350 with no payment credited.

1. **Decision 1, diary.** Warning served 9.9.2026; the warning period for a timely payment-order request has passed (lesson [28.1](../module-28/lesson-01.md); periods in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)). No restriction notice yet. Act today.
2. **Decision 2.** He admits the debt and was served; no court route.
3. **Decision 3.** "I have paid" for ₪1,000, with the transfer confirmation and a request that the creditor be charged costs for not reporting (s. 19, s. 19א).
4. **Decision 4.** He calls the bank: it has released part of the salary as protected but kept the child allowance frozen. He files a request to release the child allowance and the protected wage balance, attaching the account statement marking the NII credit and the payslip.
5. **Decision 5, budget.** Net household income ₪13,700 (his ₪7,200, Rivka's ₪6,500), not counting child allowances. Necessary expenses about ₪13,100: rent ₪4,800, food ₪4,600, utilities and arnona ₪1,200, transport ₪600, school and health ₪1,100, other ₪800. Capacity about ₪600 a month. He files the payment-order request with form 21 content, three payslips for each of them, three months' statements, the lease and the children's details, offering ₪500 a month.
6. **Decision 6.** Balance after the ₪1,000 credit about ₪15,350; ₪500 × 31 months, within the three-year limit for a debt below the s. 7א1 threshold.
7. **Decision 7.** He also writes to the creditor's lawyers proposing ₪8,000 within 60 days (a loan from a free-loan fund) in full settlement, or ₪500 a month, asking them to notify the office under s. 19ב if they agree. Marked "ללא הודאה ומבלי לפגוע בזכויות".
8. **Result.** The registrar accepts the "I have paid" claim, orders release of the child allowance, and grants a payment order of ₪500 a month. The creditor rejects the lump sum.
9. **Decision 8.** After the third consecutive monthly payment (January 2027), Yehuda files the request to be recognised as a paying debtor, attaching the payment confirmations. The attachment on his account is cancelled 30 days after notice to the creditor (s. 69ב9(ב)). Case log: monthly payment dates; the order's end date; a reminder to ask for the file to be closed when the balance reaches zero.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- A printout of your file from the personal area (or the supplied case): warning, service date, debt breakdown, decisions, measures.
- Proof of every payment you made; three recent payslips for each earner; three months' bank statements; lease or mortgage; the family's details; a list of other debts.
- The [execution debtor response kit](../../templates/execution-debtor-response-kit.md): parts A to D.
- About two hours.

### Steps

1. **Read and diary (20 min).** Kit part A: creditor, document type, service date, deadlines, measures taken. Mark which periods have passed.
2. **Verify (20 min).** Money ledger: principal, interest, fees, payments. Mark any payment not credited.
3. **Choose (10 min).** For each decision point in the table, write yes or no with one line of reason.
4. **Release (20 min).** If protected money is frozen: call the bank, then draft the release request (kit part C) with the statements marking each protected credit.
5. **"I have paid" (15 min).** If a payment is missing: the request with a payment table and confirmations (kit part B1).
6. **Budget and offer (30 min).** Kit part D1: one document per line. Compute capacity; set an offer you can keep for the whole period; check it against s. 7א1.
7. **Payment-order request (20 min).** Kit part D2 with the form 21 content; ask in the same request to cancel any restriction while you comply (s. 66ד(ג)).
8. **Creditor letter (10 min).** Kit part D3: a lump-sum and an instalment option, "without prejudice", asking for a s. 19ב notice.
9. **Diary the paying-debtor step (5 min).** The date of the third consecutive payment, and the request you will file then.

### Template

- [Execution debtor response kit](../../templates/execution-debtor-response-kit.md) (lesson 28.8): parts A to D.
- [Clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md): money ledger and payment log.

### How you know it worked

- Every line of the debt is checked, and every payment you made is either credited or claimed.
- Your offer is backed by documents and fits the s. 7א1 period with a margin.
- Your diary shows the date of the paying-debtor request.

### Self-check

- [ ] I filed a separate request for each thing I asked for, in my own file.
- [ ] I asked for protected wages and benefits to be released, with statements.
- [ ] I offered only what I can keep for the whole period.
- [ ] I know that three consecutive payments under a payment order open the paying-debtor request.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes, in your own files. Debtors file requests through the personal area, the online form or a branch ([can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md)). An opposition affidavit must be signed before a lawyer or another authorised person; acting in someone else's file, even a spouse's, is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).

### Where and how

- The Enforcement and Collection Authority's personal area; the online general-request form; any branch by appointment; call centre \*35592 (⚠️ manual check of request types).
- The paying-debtor request: the gov.il service for "חייב משלם" (⚠️ manual check).
- Free help: Legal Aid's execution service; municipal and NGO debt-counselling programmes; [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You were never served with the lawsuit or the bill has a real defence (lesson 28.3): that is a court matter.
- Your files together exceed what you can repay within the s. 7א1 periods, or approach the insolvency thresholds in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md) (lesson 28.5).
- The file is for maintenance, or an arrest or eviction is threatened.
- The creditor refuses any arrangement and the registrar's decision is against you on a point of law (appeal to the magistrates' court, lesson 28.1).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Doing nothing after the warning | Attachments, restrictions, deemed evader (s. 67(ד)) | File a payment-order request now and attend the means inquiry | Diary the warning period on the day of service |
| A letter saying "I cannot pay" with no documents | Not treated as a request | Refile with form 21 content and documents | Use the kit's document list |
| Offering more than the budget allows | Default; measures return | Ask to vary the order (s. 69ב) | Offer from the documented budget, with a margin |
| Missing one monthly payment | The three-payment count restarts | Pay at once and log it | Standing order on a fixed date |
| Settling orally with the creditor's lawyer | Enforcement continues | Get it in writing with a s. 19ב notice | Written arrangement only |

## Review

- Read and verify the file first: every line of the debt and every payment.
- Get protected wages and benefits released; claim unreported payments with "I have paid".
- A documented payment-order request with an offer you can keep for the whole s. 7א1 period turns the file into instalments; restrictions must be cancelled while you comply.
- After three consecutive payments, ask to be recognised as a paying debtor, which stops bank and salary attachments 30 days after notice to the creditor.
