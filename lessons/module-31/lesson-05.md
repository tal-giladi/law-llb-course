# 31.5 · A tax refund and an objection

Most Israeli employees never claim the refund the law gives them for the years in which payroll got their tax wrong. In this lesson you run your own refund file across every open year, file the request for real, and then deal with the answer: an assessment notice that may accept the claim, cut it or refuse it, and, if it is wrong, an objection within 30 days. The rules are in lessons 25.2, 25.4 and 25.5 and the computation drill in lesson 25.10; this lesson is about running the file and its decision points.

## Why it matters

Payroll computes tax month by month for one employer. Anything that does not fit that picture produces overpayment: a second job without tax coordination, a gap between jobs, a degree finished during the year, donations, a disability, a child born, reserve service, a deposit to a private pension. The money comes back with linkage and interest, but only if you ask, and only for six years back (Income Tax Ordinance s. 160(א)). In 2026 the open years are 2020 to 2025, and 2020 closes on 31 December 2026.

The objection is the second half of the file. The assessing officer may accept part of your request and issue an assessment for the rest. An assessment is a decision you can challenge, but the period is short and starts when the notice reaches you. Clinic II taught the habit of counting from delivery (lesson [22.1](../module-22/lesson-01.md)); here it decides whether you can argue at all.

## The law

### The refund window

> הוכח להנחת דעתו של פקיד השומה, כי אדם שילם מס לשנת מס פלונית – בין דרך ניכוי ובין בדרך אחרת – יתר על הסכום שהוא חייב בו, והדו״ח לאותה שנה הוגש לא יאוחר מתום שש שנים אחריה, יהא אותו אדם זכאי [...] שיוחזר לו תשלום היתר בתוספת הפרשי הצמדה וריבית [...]

*Gist:* Overpaid tax is refunded with linkage and interest if the report for that year is filed within six years after the end of the year. [s. 160(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_160)

> לא יוחזר לאדם תשלום לשנת מס שעליה לא מסר דו״ח, או הזניח למסרו, [...] אלא אם הוכיח [...] שאי־המסירה או ההזנחה למסור דו״ח אמיתי ונכון אין מקורן מרמה או מעשה או מחדל שבמזיד [...]

*Gist:* If you were required to file a return for the year and did not, the refund depends on showing the failure was not fraud or wilful. [s. 160(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_160) That is why the first decision is whether you were exempt from filing (Exemption from Filing Regulations; lesson [25.2](../module-25/lesson-02.md)).

The refund is paid within the period in s. 159א(ב) (see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).

### The objection

> היה אדם חולק על השומה, רשאי הוא לבקש מאת פקיד השומה, בהודעת השגה, בכתב, לחזור ולעיין ולשנות את השומה; בקשה כאמור תפרש בדיוק את הנימוקים להשגה על השומה ותוגש תוך שלושים יום מיום המצאת הודעת השומה [...]

*Gist:* The objection is in writing, gives exact grounds, and is filed within 30 days of service of the notice. [s. 150(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_150)

> לא תיעשה שומה לפי מיטב השפיטה על פי סעיף 145 ולא יינתן צו לפי סעיף 152, בלי שניתנה לנישום הזדמנות סבירה להשמיע טענותיו.

*Gist:* No best-judgment assessment or order without a reasonable opportunity to be heard, and the notice must give reasons. [s. 158א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_158א)

If there is no agreement, the officer issues an order (s. 152(ב)); if neither agreement nor order comes within the s. 152(ג) period, the objection is treated as accepted; an order may be appealed to the District Court within 30 days (s. 153; lesson [25.5](../module-25/lesson-05.md)).

## Leading cases

### ע״א 4243/08 פקיד שומה גוש דן נ׳ ורד פרי (30.4.2009)

The Supreme Court held, against the Tax Authority's long practice, that child-care expenses of a working parent could be deductible as an expense in producing income; the Knesset then replaced the deduction with credit points. Briefed in lesson [25.2](../module-25/lesson-02.md); source [Hebrew Wikipedia](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/פסק_דין_ורד_פרי) (⚠️ secondary; official text not opened). Why it matters for your file: the officer's practice is not the law. When an assessment refuses a claim "because we never allow it", the objection asks for the legal basis and the reasons s. 158א requires.

### Key text: s. 152(ג) (deemed acceptance)

If neither an agreement nor an order is made within the later of four years from the end of the year in which the report was filed or one year from the objection, the objection is treated as accepted. Why it matters: diarise that date the day you file the objection. If it passes in silence, write to the office and say so; you do not need to start again. Text: [s. 152(ג)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_152).

## How it works in practice

### Decision points

| Decision | Ask | Answer leads to |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Which years? | 2020–2025 in 2026; is any year about to close? | The oldest open year first |
| 2. Exempt from filing? | Only salary in Israel within the ceiling, no business, no foreign income above the threshold? | Exempt: Form 135 or online. Not exempt: an annual return (and a lesson 25.8 check) |
| 3. Grounds per year | Several employers, a gap year, a degree, donations, reserve service, pension deposits, disability, children | One line per ground, with the document |
| 4. Online or paper? | Do you have the Tax Authority personal-area login and scanned documents? | Online (since tax year 2019, fully online with attachments, per the service page ⚠️); otherwise Form 135 by post or at the office |
| 5. Assessment received | Accepted in full, in part, refused? | Accept, or object within 30 days |
| 6. Objection | Is the refusal factual (missing proof) or legal (the officer's reading)? | New documents; or the legal argument and a request to be heard |
| 7. Order | If the order rejects the objection | Appeal in 30 days, usually with professional help |

### Filing the refund

1. Run the Tax Authority's online simulator for each year as a first estimate (it is a rough check, not an assessment; ⚠️ manual check of the current tool).
2. Collect a Form 106 from every employer for every year; the certificates for each credit you claim (degree, donations, pension, reserve days, disability).
3. File online through the refund service in the personal area, one request per year, attaching the scans and your bank details, or file Form 135 with the [cover letter](../../templates/tax-refund-request.md) at the assessing office for your residence. The service is free (⚠️ manual check).
4. Log the filing date and confirmation. The answer is an assessment notice sent to your registered address and shown in the personal area (⚠️ manual check); the refund is paid to the bank account you gave.

### Objecting

Use the [objection template](../../templates/tax-assessment-objection.md): the assessment and the date you received it; each point disputed with facts, evidence and law; the amount not in dispute; the relief; a request to be heard. Attach copies, not originals. File by registered mail, by hand with a stamped copy, or through the online service if offered (⚠️ manual check).

## Worked example

**Supplied case C.** Hiba Nassar, 38, a nurse in Nazareth, mother of two. Salary only, always below the filing ceiling: exempt from filing.

| Year | Facts | Ground |
|---|---|---|
| 2020 | One employer; maternity leave four months; no other change | Gap months: payroll gave annual credit over 8 months only partly (check) |
| 2021–2022 | Studied for a BA in nursing at night, finished in December 2022 | None for these years; degree points start in the following year |
| 2023 | Main hospital job plus a second clinic job from March, no tax coordination; donations of ₪2,400 to an approved charity | Over-withholding at the second job; degree point; donations credit |
| 2024–2025 | Hospital job only; donations | Degree points; donations credit |

**Decision 1.** 2020 closes on 31 December 2026, so she files 2020 first, then 2023, then 2024 and 2025.

**Decision 4.** She files online, one request per year, attaching each Form 106, the university's confirmation of completion, and the donation receipts. Case log: 25.10.2026, four requests filed; confirmation numbers [___].

**Assessment.** On 2 December 2026 an assessment notice for 2023 arrives (she keeps the envelope; the personal area shows it the same day). It refunds the over-withholding from the second job but refuses two items: the degree point, "studies ended 2023 according to the certificate", and the donations, "receipt does not show s. 46 approval".

**Decision 6.** Both refusals are factual and fixable with documents. The certificate was issued in 2023, but the studies ended in December 2022; the university's academic secretary confirms the date in a letter. The charity appears on the Tax Authority's list of approved institutions; she prints the entry.

**Her objection (key lines, Hebrew, by registered mail and online on 10 December 2026).** "הנדון: השגה על שומה לשנת המס 2023 מיום 2.12.2026, שנמסרה לי ביום 2.12.2026. 1. נקודת זיכוי לתואר אקדמי: לימודיי הסתיימו ביום 28.12.2022 (נספח א – אישור המזכירות האקדמית); התעודה הונפקה ב-2023, אך המועד הקובע הוא סיום הלימודים, ולכן אני זכאית לנקודה החל משנת 2023. 2. זיכוי בשל תרומות: העמותה היא מוסד ציבורי מאושר לפי סעיף 46 (נספח ב – תדפיס ממאגר רשות המסים); מצורפות הקבלות (נספח ג). 3. הסכום שאינו שנוי במחלוקת: ההחזר בשל הניכוי במשרה השנייה. 4. אבקש לתקן את השומה ולהשיב את יתרת ההחזר, ואבקש הזדמנות להשמיע טענותיי אם לא תתקבל ההשגה במלואה." Deadline check: 30 days from 2.12.2026 is 1.1.2027; she files on day 8.

**Result and log.** The officer calls in January, accepts the degree point and the donations, and issues an amended assessment. The 2024 and 2025 requests, which claimed the same items with the same documents, are accepted in full. Case log: amounts received per year, dates, and a note to claim the degree point for the third year in 2026's request next year.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- Form 106 from every employer for every year since 2020 (ask past employers in writing; the personal area may show some).
- The documents behind each ground: degree completion confirmation, donation receipts, pension deposit statements, reserve-days certificate, disability decisions, children's details.
- The Tax Authority personal-area login, or Form 135 and the [tax refund request](../../templates/tax-refund-request.md) cover letter.
- The [tax assessment objection](../../templates/tax-assessment-objection.md) template, for when the assessment arrives.
- About two hours for the requests, and an hour for an objection.

### Steps

1. **Years and exemption (15 min).** List the open years. For each, confirm you were exempt from filing; if not, stop and use lesson 25.8.
2. **Grounds (25 min).** One row per year in your money ledger: grounds, documents held, documents missing.
3. **Estimate (15 min).** Run the simulator for each year; record the estimate (it is not binding).
4. **Missing documents (15 min).** Write to past employers for missing Form 106s; download what you can.
5. **File (30 min).** Oldest year first; one request per year; attach every document; log the confirmations.
6. **Diary (5 min).** For each request, the expected answer and refund period (s. 159א(ב), see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
7. **When the assessment arrives (15 min).** Record the date of delivery; compare it line by line with your request; mark each refused item factual or legal.
8. **Objection (60 min, if needed).** Draft with the template, attach the new evidence, file within 30 days of delivery, diarise the s. 152(ג) date.

### Template

- [Tax refund request: cover letter and checklist](../../templates/tax-refund-request.md) (lesson 25.10): parts A to C.
- [Objection to a tax assessment](../../templates/tax-assessment-objection.md) (lesson 25.5).
- [Clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md): money ledger, one column per year.

### How you know it worked

- Every open year is either filed or marked "no refund" with a reason.
- Every claimed item has a document attached.
- Any objection was filed within 30 days of delivery, with exact grounds and new evidence, and its decision date is diarised.

### Self-check

- [ ] I filed the oldest open year first.
- [ ] I confirmed I was exempt from filing in each year.
- [ ] I counted the objection period from the date the notice reached me.
- [ ] I stated the undisputed amount, so it is not held up by the dispute.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Refund requests and objections are filed by the taxpayer in his own name; accountants and representing tax advisers may also represent you before the Tax Authority (s. 236). The District Court appeal from an order is a full court case; you may appear yourself, but consider professional help (lesson 25.5). Preparing requests for others for pay is not allowed (s. 236; Bar Association Law s. 20).

### Where and how

- Refund: the online refund service for individuals ([gov.il service page](https://www.gov.il/he/service/itc135), ⚠️ manual check) or Form 135 at the assessing office for your residence. No fee (⚠️ manual check). Window and refund period: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Objection: the office that issued the assessment, within 30 days ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- Help: the Tax Authority's call centre and simulator; [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md). Be wary of commercial "refund companies" that take a percentage for a request you can file free.

### Stop and get a lawyer (or accountant) when

- You find a year in which you had to file a return and did not (lesson 25.8).
- The claim involves severance, pension withdrawals, securities, rental income or foreign income.
- The assessment adds a fine or alleges a false statement.
- Your objection is rejected by an order and you are considering a District Court appeal.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starting with the latest year | The oldest year closes unclaimed | None for the closed year | Oldest open year first |
| Claiming a credit with no document | Refused in the assessment | Object with the document within 30 days | Attach a document for every line |
| Counting 30 days from the date printed on the notice | Objection seems late or is filed late | Prove delivery; ask for an extension for reasonable cause | Keep the envelope; note the personal-area date |
| Objecting without new evidence or grounds | Order confirms the assessment | Appeal is harder | State exact grounds and attach evidence (s. 150(א)) |
| Paying a refund company a large share | Losing part of the refund | Cancel if still possible | File yourself; it is free |

## Review

- In 2026 the open refund years are 2020–2025, and 2020 closes on 31 December 2026; file the oldest first.
- Confirm you were exempt from filing; then file one request per year, online or on Form 135, with a document for every claim.
- An assessment that cuts your claim is challenged by a written objection with exact grounds within 30 days of delivery.
- Diarise the s. 152(ג) date; silence past it means the objection is accepted.
