# 31.7 · An arnona objection from start to finish

Arnona is the one tax that almost every adult in Israel pays and almost nobody checks. In this lesson you audit your own arnona account, decide which errors are objection grounds and which belong to another route, file the objection to the arnona director within 90 days, give the notice that ends your liability for a property you no longer hold, and follow the file to the director's answer, a deemed acceptance or an appeal. The law and the appeal drill are in lessons 27.8 and 27.10; this lesson runs the file.

## Why it matters

An arnona error repeats every year. A flat measured 15 m² too large, a storeroom charged as a residential room, a unit charged to a parent after a child moved in: each is a few hundred or thousand shekels a year, and it continues until someone objects. The law makes the objection cheap (a letter, no fee) but strict: 90 days from the payment notice, on a closed list of grounds, to the director, before any appeal.

There is also a trap that is not about objections at all. Liability for a property you stopped holding ends only after written notice to the municipality (Municipalities Ordinance s. 325). An objection cannot undo the months before the notice. Clinic III treats the notice as part of the file, not an afterthought.

## The law

### What the objection can decide

The objection to the director covers only: the wrong zone; an error in the property's type, size or use; not being the holder; and certain business-control claims ([Arnona Appeals Law s. 3(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הרשויות_המקומיות_%28ערר_על_קביעת_ארנונה_כללית%29#סעיף_3)). Claims that the council's imposition or rates are illegal are outside it (s. 3(ב)) and go to an administrative petition; discounts are a separate application to the authority (lesson [27.8](../module-27/lesson-08.md)). The payment notice must state your objection and appeal rights and their periods (s. 7א).

### The periods

The objection is due within 90 days of receiving the payment notice (s. 3(א)). The director answers within 60 days; if he does not, the objection is treated as accepted, unless the appeals committee extended the period by up to 30 days (s. 4). Then:

> הרואה עצמו מקופח בתשובת מנהל הארנונה על השגתו רשאי, תוך שלושים יום מיום שנמסרה לו התשובה, לערור עליה לפני ועדת ערר.

*Gist:* Appeal to the appeals committee within 30 days of receiving the director's answer. [s. 6(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הרשויות_המקומיות_%28ערר_על_קביעת_ארנונה_כללית%29#סעיף_6)

> בכתב הערר יצויינו – שם העורר, משלח ידו, מענו והמען להמצאת מסמכים; פרטי הנכס שבשלו נדרש התשלום; התאריך שבו נמסרה לעורר תשובת המנהל על השגתו; הנימוקים עליהם מבסס העורר את עררו; האם העורר מבקש להתייצב לפני הועדה כדי להשמיע טענותיו.

*Gist:* The notice of appeal states these five items and is filed in four copies. [Procedure Regulations regs. 2–3](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הרשויות_המקומיות_%28ערר_על_קביעת_ארנונה_כללית%29_%28סדרי_דין_בוועדת_ערר%29) All periods are in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### When liability passes

A person who stops being owner or holder must notify the municipality in writing and is not liable after the notice, though still liable for what accrued before ([Municipalities Ordinance s. 325](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_העיריות#סעיף_325)); on a sale or a lease of a year or more, the owner or landlord must notify the new holder (s. 326; lesson 27.8).

### How area is counted

Area is rounded to the nearest whole square metre, a half rounded down (Arrangements Law 1992 s. 8(ב1)); what counts as area (balconies, storerooms, stairwells) is set by your authority's arnona order, so the measurement method in the order is the yardstick, not your feeling about the flat (lesson 27.8).

## Leading cases

### רע״א 187/05 נסייר נ' עיריית נצרת עילית (20.6.2010)

The Supreme Court held that arnona debts are subject to limitation, and that a person may ask the court to stop a local authority from collecting time-barred arnona even through "passive collection", such as refusing the certificate of no debts needed for a sale. Why it matters for the file: when the audit turns up an old debt the authority never pursued, check the dates before paying it to unlock a certificate. Brief in lesson 27.8; source [Hebrew Wikipedia, ארנונה](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/ארנונה) (secondary; the ruling's text on the Judiciary's site could not be reached).

### Key text: s. 3(ב) of the Arnona Appeals Law (the limits of the objection)

The director and the committee may not decide claims that the imposition or rates are illegal beyond the listed grounds. Why it matters: "my flat is 112 m², not 128 m²" and "my son holds the unit" belong in the objection; "the rate for my zone is discriminatory" does not, and putting it there wastes the 90 days. Text: [s. 3](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הרשויות_המקומיות_%28ערר_על_קביעת_ארנונה_כללית%29#סעיף_3).

## How it works in practice

### Decision points

| Decision | Ask | Answer leads to |
|---|---|---|
| 1. When did the notice reach you? | Envelope, email header, app notification | Day 0; objection by day 90 |
| 2. What is wrong? | Area, classification, use, zone, holder, business control | Objection ground under s. 3(א) |
| | A discount you are entitled to | Discount application, not the objection |
| | The order or rate is unlawful | Prior demand and petition; outside the clinic |
| 3. Evidence | Measurement sheet; surveyor; permit plans; photos; lease; s. 325 notice | Attach to the objection |
| 4. Holder changed? | Did you give written notice when it changed? | If not, give it now; object for the period after notice |
| 5. Pay meanwhile? | Discounts lapse if the year is not fully paid by 31 December (lesson 27.8) | Pay the undisputed part, or all under protest, and keep discounts alive |
| 6. Director's answer | Within 60 days? Accepted, partly, refused? | Silence: deemed accepted (s. 4); refusal: appeal within 30 days |
| 7. Committee decision | Against you on a point worth more than a year's tax? | Administrative appeal within 60 days, with advice |

### Filing

Use [Part A of the arnona template](../../templates/arnona-objection-and-appeal.md). File at the address and in the manner stated on the bill (s. 7א), by email or the authority's online form with confirmation, and by registered mail. Log the filing date and compute day 60 for the director's answer.

## Worked example

**Supplied case E.** Salim Halabi, 51, of Shefa-'Amr, owns a two-storey house. On 8 October 2026 he received an amended payment notice (the envelope is postmarked 6 October) after a municipal re-measurement: from 1 January 2026, the upper floor is charged at 128 m² instead of 112 m², and the ground-floor unit, charged separately, is in his name. His son Fadi has lived in the ground-floor unit since 1 March 2026 under a written three-year lease and pays rent.

1. **Decision 1.** Day 0 is 8 October 2026 (delivery). Day 90 is 6 January 2027. He diarises 15 December as his own deadline.
2. **Decision 2.** Size (the upper floor) and holder (the unit): both s. 3(א) grounds. Salim also wonders whether the rate in his zone is unfair compared with a neighbouring zone; that is a s. 3(ב) claim and stays out of the objection.
3. **Decision 3, area.** He asks the municipality for the measurement sheet (it shows the external walls and a 14 m² covered balcony counted as area) and reads the arnona order's measurement method: covered balconies count, but the order excludes the external stairwell, which the sheet included (4 m²). He commissions a licensed surveyor, whose report measures 110 m² within the external walls plus the 14 m² covered balcony: 124 m² under the order's method, the sheet's 128 less the 4 m² stairwell. He will claim 124, not 112: the balcony is counted under the order.
4. **Decision 4, holder.** Salim never notified the municipality when Fadi moved in. Under s. 325 his liability ends only from the notice. On 12 October he files the written notice of change of holder with a copy of the lease; Fadi signs his own request to be registered as holder. In the objection Salim claims "not the holder" from 12 October 2026, and accepts that March to October 2026 stays his (the lease is a matter between him and Fadi).
5. **Filing.** On 20 October 2026, the objection (Part A of the template), by the municipality's online form and by registered mail, with the surveyor's report, the order's measurement clause, the lease and the s. 325 notice.
6. **Decision 5.** He pays the undisputed charge for the upper floor (at 124 m²) and for the unit up to 12 October, keeping his senior discount application separate.
7. **Decision 6.** The director's 60 days end on 19 December 2026. On 1 December the director accepts the stairwell point (124 m²) and the change of holder from 12 October. Nothing is left to appeal. Case log: amended bill received; refund credited to the next instalment; note to diarise next year's notice for any change.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- Your latest arnona payment notice with its envelope or email; previous years' notices.
- The measurement sheet (ask the municipality); your authority's arnona order (on its website); a tape measure, or a surveyor's report if the gap is large.
- Any lease, sale contract or handover protocol, and copies of any s. 325 notices you gave.
- [Arnona objection and appeal template](../../templates/arnona-objection-and-appeal.md); about two hours.

### Steps

1. **Day 0 (5 min).** Record the delivery date and its proof; compute day 90 and set a reminder two weeks earlier.
2. **Audit (40 min).** Compare the notice with the measurement sheet and the order: zone, classification, use, area (by the order's method), holder. List each difference.
3. **Sort (10 min).** Mark each difference: objection ground (s. 3(א)), discount, or legality claim (s. 3(ב)). Keep only the first in the objection.
4. **Holder (15 min).** If someone else now holds all or part of the property, give the s. 325 notice today, and have the new holder ask to be registered.
5. **Evidence (20 min).** Collect the documents for each ground; decide whether a surveyor is worth it (gap × rate × years).
6. **Draft and file (20 min).** Part A of the template, one numbered paragraph per ground, the amount you say is right, the request to be heard; file as the bill says, and by registered mail.
7. **Pay and diarise (10 min).** Pay the undisputed part; diarise day 60 for the director and, if refused, day 30 for the appeal (Part B).

### Template

- [Arnona objection and appeal](../../templates/arnona-objection-and-appeal.md) (lesson 27.10): Part A now, Part B only after the director's answer.
- [Clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md): money ledger, one row per billing year.

### How you know it worked

- Every claim in your objection is a s. 3(א) ground with a document behind it.
- Your s. 325 notice is filed and logged where the holder changed.
- Day 60 and, if needed, the 30-day appeal date are in your calendar.

### Self-check

- [ ] I counted 90 days from delivery, not from the date printed on the bill.
- [ ] I measured by my authority's order, not by my own idea of area.
- [ ] I kept discounts and legality claims out of the objection.
- [ ] I know that silence from the director for 60 days means the objection is accepted.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Holders file their own objections and appeals routinely; the appeals committee does not require a lawyer ([can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md)). Paid non-lawyer "arnona consultants" may not represent you (Bar Association Law s. 20). Your son, spouse or parent files his or her own notice and objection for a property they hold.

### Where and how

- Objection: the arnona director, at the address and in the manner on the bill (s. 7א). No fee.
- Appeal: the authority's arnona appeals committee, in four copies (reg. 3); secretariat details on the authority's site (⚠️ manual check).
- Help: the authority's public-complaints officer; Kol-Zchut's arnona pages (⚠️ manual check); [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The property is a business with large sums over several years, or the authority attaches accounts or sues for arnona.
- You want to challenge the arnona order or the rates themselves (s. 3(ב)).
- The committee decides against you on a point of law worth taking to the Administrative Affairs Court.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Objecting after day 90 | The year's charge stands | Object next year; ask the director to correct by consent | Diarise day 90 on delivery |
| Measuring by your own method | Objection refused | Re-measure by the order's method | Read the order's measurement clause first |
| Claiming "not the holder" for months before any notice | Refused for that period (s. 325) | Give notice now; claim from the notice | Notify the day the holder changes |
| Putting a discount request in the objection | Not decided there; discount lost for the year | File the discount application | Separate the two routes |
| Not paying the undisputed part | Discounts lapse; collection | Pay before 31 December | Pay what is not in dispute |

## Review

- Count 90 days from delivery of the payment notice; object to the director on s. 3(א) grounds only.
- Measure by your authority's arnona order, and attach the evidence for each ground.
- Liability ends only with written notice to the municipality (s. 325): give it the day the holder changes.
- Silence from the director for 60 days is acceptance; a refusal is appealed within 30 days, in four copies.
