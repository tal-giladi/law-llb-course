# 31.2 · Your own will and enduring power of attorney

This is the one Clinic III matter every learner can run for real: your own estate plan. You map what you own and who would inherit without a will, choose the form of will that fits, write and deposit a handwritten will, and prepare every decision for an enduring power of attorney so that the meeting with the trained lawyer who must witness it is short and the document is yours. The law was taught in lessons 26.2, 26.7 and 26.8; this lesson runs the file.

## Why it matters

Most Israelis die without a will, and many lose capacity before they die. Without a will the Succession Law's default shares apply (lesson [26.1](../module-26/lesson-01.md)): a spouse shares with children or parents, a cohabitant's position depends on proof, a stepchild inherits nothing. Without an enduring power of attorney, a family that needs to pay a carer from a parent's account or sell the flat to fund a nursing home must ask the court to appoint a guardian, a process that is slower, more public and less in the person's own hands.

Both documents are cheap. A handwritten will costs paper and an hour, and the first deposit is free; an enduring power costs a lawyer's fee for one meeting. The cost of not making them falls on the people you leave behind. For the clinic, the will is also the cleanest "real, own name" task in the course: nobody else is a party, and nothing is filed against anyone.

## The law

### The handwritten will and its deposit

> צוואה בכתב יד תיכתב כולה ביד המצווה, תישא תאריך כתוב בידו ותיחתם בידו.

*Gist:* Write all of it, date it and sign it, by your own hand. [Succession Law s. 19](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הירושה#סעיף_19)

> (א) מי שעשה צוואה בכתב יד או בעדים רשאי להפקידה אצל רשם לעניני ירושה; ההפקדה תהיה במסירת הצוואה על ידי המצווה עצמו לרשם לעניני ירושה.

*Gist:* You may deposit a handwritten or witnessed will with the Registrar, and only you can do it. [s. 21(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הירושה#סעיף_21)

The 2026 fee schedule lists a deposit fee and, in item 5א, an exemption when no earlier will of yours was deposited in the five years before the deposit, so a first deposit is free (amounts in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); [Fees Regulations, Schedule](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הירושה_(אגרות_הרשם_לעניני_ירושה)), checked 18 October 2026). Remote deposit through the Administrator General's personal area requires the original to follow within the period in reg. 6ב.

### Curing defects, and the limit of the cure

> (א) התקיימו מרכיבי היסוד בצוואה, ולא היה לרשם לענייני ירושה או לבית המשפט, לפי העניין ספק כי היא משקפת את רצונו החופשי והאמיתי של המצווה, רשאי הוא, בהחלטה מנומקת, לקיימה אף אם נפל פגם בפרט מן הפרטים או בהליך מן ההליכים [...]

*Gist:* A will with its basic elements may be upheld despite a defect in a formal detail, if there is no doubt it reflects the testator's free and true wishes. [s. 25](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הירושה#סעיף_25) The cure exists so that small slips do not defeat a will; it is not a plan. Every slip invites an objection and a court case.

### Gifts to the writer, and assets outside the will

A gift in a will to a person who took part in making it, or to a witness, is void (s. 35). Life-insurance and pension benefits paid to a named beneficiary are generally outside the estate (s. 147; lesson [26.1](../module-26/lesson-01.md)), so your will does not govern them: the beneficiary designation at the fund or insurer does.

### The enduring power of attorney

> (א) ייפוי כוח מתמשך ייחתם בידי הממנה לפני עורך דין שעבר הכשרה לעניין עריכת ייפוי כוח מתמשך, כפי שתיקבע בתקנות, ואין לו עניין אישי בייפוי הכוח.

*Gist:* The grantor signs before a lawyer trained by the Administrator General who has no personal interest in it. [Capacity and Guardianship Law s. 32יד(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הכשרות_המשפטית_והאפוטרופסות#סעיף_32יד)

> (א) ייפוי כוח מתמשך יופקד אצל האפוטרופוס הכללי [...] הפקדת ייפוי הכוח היא תנאי מוקדם לכניסתו לתוקף.

*Gist:* It must be deposited with the Administrator General before it can take effect. [s. 32יז](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הכשרות_המשפטית_והאפוטרופסות#סעיף_32יז)

The attorney must qualify under s. 32ג; certain acts are never allowed and larger ones need express authority or prior approval (s. 32ו, s. 74א); entry into force needs a test you set that is not the attorney's own decision alone (s. 32יט(ג)). All of this is in lesson [26.7](../module-26/lesson-07.md).

## Leading cases

### ד״נ 40/80 קניג נ׳ כהן, פ״ד לו(3)

A handwritten document excluding a husband lacked a date and signature; in a further hearing the Supreme Court majority held that it could not be upheld as a will under the formal rules as they then stood. The case is the classic background to the 1985 broadening of s. 25, which now allows defects in details to be cured. Why it matters for your file: even after the amendment, a missing signature or a typed will called "handwritten" is litigation, not a cure. Write it all, date it and sign it. Brief in lesson [26.2](../module-26/lesson-02.md); source [Hebrew Wikipedia: צוואה](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/צוואה) (⚠️ secondary; full text not reached).

### דנ״א 7818/00 אהרון נ׳ אהרוני (2005)

By four to three, the Supreme Court held that a clear intention to revoke a will revokes it even if the revocation did not meet every formal requirement. Why it matters: when you write a new will, say expressly that it revokes all earlier wills, take the deposited old will back, and destroy old copies, so that nobody argues about which document speaks. Brief in lesson [26.3](../module-26/lesson-03.md); source [Hebrew Wikipedia: צוואה](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/צוואה) (⚠️ secondary).

## How it works in practice

### Decision 1: which form of will

| Your situation | Form | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Simple estate, clear wishes, good health, you write Hebrew, Arabic or another language well by hand | Handwritten, deposited | Cheapest, private, hard to forge if deposited |
| Your handwriting is poor or you prefer a typed document | Witnessed (s. 20): two adult, unrelated, non-benefiting witnesses | Typed is allowed; the witnesses confirm |
| Your capacity may later be questioned (age, illness, medication) | Before an authority (Registrar, s. 22) or a lawyer-drafted witnessed will with a doctor's note | Independent record of capacity at the time |
| Business, foreign assets, ILA leasehold farm, mutual wills, trusts, successive heirs, children from several relationships | Lawyer | Drafting choices with tax and dispute consequences |

### Decision 2: what the will must cover

Use [Part A of the handwritten-will template](../../templates/handwritten-will.md): assets, heirs on intestacy, decisions. The clinic adds four checks:

1. **Assets outside the will.** List each pension fund, provident fund and life policy and its named beneficiary. Change the designation at the fund if it contradicts your will.
2. **Substitutes.** For each gift, who takes if the beneficiary dies first (s. 41).
3. **Executor.** Someone you trust, who agreed, who is not the only beneficiary if conflict is likely.
4. **Review triggers.** Marriage, divorce, a new child or grandchild, buying or selling a home, a beneficiary's death, moving abroad. Write them in the case log.

### Decision 3: the enduring power of attorney

Use the [preparation worksheet](../../templates/enduring-poa-preparation-worksheet.md). The decisions are yours; the lawyer's role is to explain, verify free consent, complete the official online forms and (usually) deposit.

| Question | Typical choice | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | Property and personal; medical included or separate | A medical-only power may be signed before a doctor, social worker, psychologist or nurse (s. 32טו) |
| Attorneys | One attorney and one substitute, or two acting jointly on property | A paid carer or the lawyer who signs cannot be your attorney (s. 32ג) |
| Informed persons | One or two people outside the attorney's household | They receive notice and reports; choose someone who will read them |
| Entry into force | On a named specialist's opinion | Not the attorney's own judgment alone (s. 32יט(ג)) |
| Property power now? | Only if you want help with banking today | It then works as an ordinary agency until incapacity (s. 32יט(ב)) |
| Advance directives | Where you want to live; which assets may be sold first; gifts to grandchildren | Large gifts need express authority (s. 32ו(ג)) |

### Costs and offices

- Deposit of the will: any Registrar of Inheritance office by appointment, or remotely; fee and exemption in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Trained lawyers: the Administrator General's public database (EPA Regulations reg. 19). Lawyers' fees are not set by law; ask for a written quote. Whether a deposit fee applies to the power is not confirmed (⚠️ manual check, see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).

## Worked example

**Supplied case A.** Svetlana Orlova, 66, retired engineer in Karmiel, widowed in 2021. She owns her flat (registered in her name alone), savings of about ₪380,000, a pension that pays her monthly and a provident fund. Her son Pavel lives in Haifa with two children; her daughter Marina lives in Berlin and has none. Without a will, Pavel and Marina would inherit equally (lesson 26.1).

**Decision 1.** Simple estate, good health, she writes Russian best but writes Hebrew clearly. She chooses a handwritten will in Hebrew (a will in a language other than Hebrew, Arabic or English would need a translation at probate; lesson [26.8](../module-26/lesson-08.md)).

**Decision 2.** Her wishes: the flat and savings to Pavel and Marina equally; ₪20,000 to each grandchild; her late husband's chess set to Pavel's elder son. Substitute: if a child dies before her, that child's share to his or her children, and if none, to the other child. Executor: Pavel, who agreed. Outside the will: the provident fund names her late husband as beneficiary; she files a new designation naming both children equally. Case-log entry: "Review triggers: a new grandchild; selling the flat; Marina's return to Israel."

**Her will (opening lines, copied by hand from the template).** "אני החתומה מטה, סבטלנה אורלובה, ת״ז [___], מרחוב [___] כרמיאל, בהיותי בדעה צלולה ומרצוני החופשי, מצווה בזה את צוואתי. אני מבטלת כל צוואה קודמת שעשיתי." She numbers the pages, signs each, writes the date in her own hand, and deposits it in person at the Registrar's office in Haifa. It is her first deposit, so no fee. The deposit certificate goes in the file; Pavel gets a photo of it.

**Decision 3.** Enduring power for property and personal matters. Attorney: Pavel; substitute: Marina (she can act from abroad for most banking, but Pavel is local). Informed persons: her friend Olga and Marina. Entry into force: a geriatrician's written opinion that she can no longer understand her financial affairs. Property power not to start now. Directives: stay at home with a carer as long as savings allow; the flat may be rented out but sold only if savings fall below ₪50,000; no gifts above birthday presents. She finds two trained lawyers in Karmiel in the database, asks both for a quote, and books the cheaper one, bringing the completed worksheet.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. Write only your own will; do not write a will for anyone else.

### You need

- Your asset and title register from lesson 31.1, and a list of your pension, provident and insurance policies with their beneficiaries.
- The [handwritten will template](../../templates/handwritten-will.md) and the [enduring power of attorney preparation worksheet](../../templates/enduring-poa-preparation-worksheet.md).
- Plain paper, a permanent pen, ID card; about two hours, plus an appointment at the Registrar.

### Steps

1. **Default picture (10 min).** Write who would inherit and in what shares if you died today without a will (lesson 26.1).
2. **Decision 1 (10 min).** Choose the form from the table; if any "lawyer" row applies, stop the will here, write your wishes as a letter and take it to a lawyer.
3. **Outside-the-will check (20 min).** List each fund and policy with its beneficiary; note any designation to change.
4. **Draft (20 min).** Fill Part A of the will template and draft the text on scrap paper: gifts, substitutes, executor, revocation of earlier wills.
5. **Write (20 min).** Copy by hand onto clean paper; number and sign each page; date it in your own hand; use the Part C checklist.
6. **Deposit (appointment).** Book a Registrar's office or deposit remotely (Part D); file the certificate.
7. **Power-of-attorney decisions (30 min).** Complete Parts A to F of the worksheet: scope, attorneys, informed persons, entry-into-force test, express authority, directives.
8. **Find the lawyer (10 min).** Search the Administrator General's database; ask two lawyers for a written fee quote; book the meeting.
9. **Case log (10 min).** Record the deposit date and number, the beneficiary changes, the lawyer meeting, and the review triggers.

### Template

- [Handwritten will: model text and checklist](../../templates/handwritten-will.md) (lesson 26.8): parts A to D.
- [Enduring power of attorney: preparation worksheet](../../templates/enduring-poa-preparation-worksheet.md) (lesson 26.7): parts A to G.
- [Clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md): log and deadlines.

### How you know it worked

- Your will passes every item of the Part C checklist and is deposited, with the certificate in your file.
- No pension or policy designation contradicts the will.
- The worksheet names an attorney who qualifies under s. 32ג, a substitute, at least one informed person and an objective entry-into-force test.

### Self-check

- [ ] The whole will is in my handwriting, dated and signed by me, and revokes earlier wills.
- [ ] No beneficiary helped write it.
- [ ] I deposited it, or booked the appointment.
- [ ] I know which decisions my attorney could never take and which need approval.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Will: yes, entirely, for your own will (ss. 19, 21). Writing a will for anyone else is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20) and voids gifts to you (s. 35). Enduring power of attorney: not alone; it must be signed before a trained lawyer (s. 32יד(א)), except a medical-only power (s. 32טו). You prepare every decision yourself.

### Where and how

- Will deposit: any Registrar of Inheritance office by appointment, or remotely through the Administrator General's personal area; service centre \*2416 (⚠️ manual check). Fee and exemption in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Enduring power: trained lawyer from the Administrator General's database; deposit by the lawyer or by you (EPA Regulations reg. 6).
- Free information: the Administrator General's pages on gov.il (⚠️ manual check); Kol-Zchut's pages on wills and enduring powers (⚠️ manual check).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- Any "lawyer" row in Decision 1 applies.
- You want to leave a child out, or you expect a challenge from a former spouse, a cohabitant or a sibling.
- You want the power to cover a business, assets abroad, or treatment against your objection (s. 32ו(ג)(2)).
- Someone is pressing you to sign, or offers to "arrange" the will or the power for you.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Typing the will and signing it as "handwritten" | Not a handwritten will; at best a defective witnessed one | Rewrite by hand, or make a witnessed will | Write every word by hand |
| Leaving the old pension beneficiary designation | The fund pays the person named, not the will's heirs | File a new designation with the fund | Check every fund in step 3 |
| Depositing a new will without taking back the old one | Confusion over which will governs | Take the old one back; state revocation | One deposited will at a time |
| Naming as attorney the carer you pay | Disqualified (s. 32ג) | Choose another attorney | Check s. 32ג before the meeting |
| Leaving entry into force to the attorney's judgment | Invalid test (s. 32יט(ג)) | Set a specialist's opinion | Use Part C of the worksheet |

## Review

- Map the default shares first; a will is needed whenever they do not match your wishes.
- Choose the form by your situation; a handwritten will must be written, dated and signed entirely by you, and is best deposited (first deposit free).
- Pension and insurance benefits follow the beneficiary designation, not the will: align them.
- Prepare every decision of the enduring power yourself; the trained lawyer formalises it, and deposit with the Administrator General is a precondition of its effect.
