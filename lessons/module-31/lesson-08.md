# 31.8 · Closing the file and when to get a lawyer

A registry matter is not over when the application is approved. The order must reach the bank and the register, the refund must arrive in the right amount, the payment order must be kept to its last instalment, and the corrected arnona bill must actually be corrected. In this last lesson you close each Clinic III matter, write the closing memo that keeps the dates that outlive it, and run the get-a-lawyer checklist a final time with the year-3 red flags.

## Why it matters

Year-3 matters have long tails. A will is reviewed for the rest of your life; an enduring power of attorney is confirmed every few years until it takes effect; registered heirs still have to decide what to do with a shared flat; the refund file repeats every year until the credit points run out; a payment order lasts up to three or four years, and one missed month undoes the paying-debtor protection; an arnona error corrected this year can return with next year's bill.

The second reason is honesty about limits. The clinic shows how far a person can go alone in estates, land, tax, debt and arnona, and it is far. But each of these areas has a point where the stakes or the law change: an heir who objects, a tax year that should have had a return, debts beyond the payment-order periods. Recognising that point early is a skill, not a failure.

## The law

### The limit that stays: s. 20

The clinic ends where it began: you act for yourself. Representing another, drafting legal documents for another, or advising as an occupation is reserved to lawyers ([Bar Association Law s. 20](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לשכת_עורכי_הדין)). The skills you built (reading an extract, computing shares, filing a refund) will tempt relatives to ask you to "just do mine". You can explain the steps and point them to the templates and the free services; they sign and file.

### Free help: the Legal Aid Law

> לשכת סיוע משפטי תתן שירות משפטי, לרבות ההוצאות הכרוכות בו, בענינים ובהיקף שנקבעו בתקנות, למי שידו אינה משגת לשאת בהם, או למי שנקבע בתוספת בענינים ובהיקף שנקבעו בה…

*Gist:* Legal Aid gives legal services in the matters set by regulations to people who cannot afford them, and to groups in the Schedule, often without a means test. [Legal Aid Law s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הסיוע_המשפטי#סעיף_2) A written application is required; a social-services certificate is prima facie proof of inability to pay (s. 3; lesson [12.8](../module-12/lesson-08.md)).

### Dates that outlive the matter

- **Enduring power of attorney:** until it takes effect, the Administrator General reminds the grantor periodically to confirm or update it (s. 32כא(ב); interval in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- **Succession order:** it may be amended or cancelled on a new fact or claim (Succession Law s. 72); if the heirs publish an invitation to creditors, the period runs from publication (ss. 99, 123; [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- **Payment order:** it ends at its last instalment, within the s. 7א1 period; the paying-debtor recognition depends on continued payment (ss. 69ב3–69ב9).
- **Tax:** each new year opens a new refund year and closes the oldest one (s. 160(א)).
- **Arnona:** each new payment notice restarts the 90 days.

## Leading cases

### רע״א 8120/08 (no "close friend" in court)

Absent an express provision, only a licensed lawyer may represent a party in court. Why it matters at closing: when the matter turns into litigation (an objection to the order moved to the Family Court; a tax appeal in the District Court; an appeal from the arnona committee), the people who can stand in for you are lawyers, and in some forums accountants or tax advisers before the authority itself (Income Tax Ordinance s. 236). Plan the handover with the one-page summary. [Full text (supremedecisions)](https://supremedecisions.court.gov.il/Home/Download?path=HebrewVerdicts%2F08%2F200%2F081%2Fv04&fileName=08081200_v04.txt&type=2) (⚠️ host unreachable on 18 October 2026; citation from the course research file).

### Key text: the get-a-lawyer checklist as a decision rule

There is no ruling on when a layperson should stop. The course's rule, built in lessons 12.8 and 22.8, is the decision rule: one red flag means a lawyer or Legal Aid; two amber signs mean at least one paid consultation, with the one-page summary. Year 3 adds the red flags below. [Get-a-lawyer checklist](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md).

## How it works in practice

### The closing checklist

| Matter | What should have arrived | Check | If not |
|---|---|---|---|
| Will | Deposit certificate | Number and date in the file; executor knows where it is | Ask the Registrar for a copy of the certificate |
| Enduring power | Deposit confirmation from the Administrator General | Informed persons have a copy of the confirmation | Ask the lawyer for the deposit number |
| Succession order | The order, digitally | Shares match your share table; names and IDs correct | Request to amend (s. 72), or appeal within 15 days of a decision |
| Land | An updated extract | Every heir registered in the order's shares; no deceased owner left | Supply missing documents; ערר within the reg. 74 period on a legal refusal |
| Tax refund | Assessment notice and the money | Amount per year, with linkage and interest; bank credit | Objection within 30 days; letter if the refund period passed |
| Execution | Payment order; release; "I have paid" decision; paying-debtor recognition | Amount, period, attachments lifted | Request to the registrar; standing order for payments |
| Arnona | Director's answer and the corrected bill | New area, holder change, refund or credit | Appeal within 30 days; letter quoting deemed acceptance |

### The closing memo (one page per matter)

1. **Outcome:** what was decided, by whom, on what date; reference numbers.
2. **Money:** what was claimed or owed, what was paid or received, when; any balance.
3. **Documents:** what arrived; where the originals are (deposit certificate, order, extract, assessment, payment order, corrected bill).
4. **Surviving dates:** each with its date and a calendar reminder.
5. **What I would do differently:** three sentences.
6. **Archive:** where the file is and for how long (tax files at least until the year can no longer be examined; estate and land files indefinitely).

### Year-3 red flags

Add these to the [get-a-lawyer checklist](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md):

| Red flag | Matter |
|---|---|
| Your capacity may be questioned, or a beneficiary is involved in the will | Will |
| Business, foreign assets, ILA farm, mutual wills, successive heirs, a disinherited child | Will |
| Pressure on an elderly person to sign a power of attorney or a gift | Enduring power |
| An objection; a file transferred to the Family Court; an heir who is a minor, unknown or abroad and uncooperative | Succession |
| ILA leasehold, an unregistered building, an heir who refuses to sign, a buy-out between heirs | Land |
| A year in which you had to file and did not; a fine; an allegation of a false statement; an order you want to appeal | Tax |
| Debts beyond the payment-order periods or near the insolvency thresholds; a judgment you were never served; maintenance files | Execution |
| A business property with large sums; an attachment for arnona; a challenge to the order or rates | Arnona |

Amber signs: the other side or authority is represented and the stakes exceed a month's income; a deadline you cannot meet; facts you cannot prove without documents the other side holds; a second family member with a conflicting interest.

## Worked example

**Closing the supplied docket.**

1. **A, Svetlana (will and power).** Deposit certificate in the file; Pavel has a photo. The enduring power was signed on 3 November 2026 and deposited by the lawyer; the confirmation shows the deposit number. Surviving dates: the Administrator General's periodic reminder; her review triggers (a new grandchild, selling the flat). Memo, "what I would do differently": "Ask the lawyer for the fee in writing before booking; I paid more than the quote for the medical part."
2. **B, Liat (succession and land).** Both orders given in December 2026; shares match her table. The updated extract shows Liat ½, Eitan ½. Gap: the father's bank released the account only after both heirs signed the bank's form; Eitan signed in Eilat. Surviving item: the buy-out. One amber sign (a taxable transaction between siblings) plus a red flag (buy-out between heirs): she books a consultation with a real-estate lawyer and brings the one-page summary.
3. **C, Hiba (tax).** Refunds for 2020, 2023, 2024 and 2025 received with linkage and interest; the 2023 objection accepted. Surviving dates: the 2026 request in 2027, with the third year of the degree point; 31 December 2027, when 2021 closes (no refund expected, noted).
4. **D, Yehuda (execution).** Payment order of ₪500 a month; paying-debtor recognition effective in February 2027; attachments cancelled. Surviving dates: monthly standing order; the order's last instalment; a request to close the file when the balance is zero, and a check that no restriction remains.
5. **E, Salim (arnona).** Corrected bill: 124 m²; the unit registered to Fadi from 12 October. Surviving dates: next year's payment notice (day 90 again); a note to give the s. 325 notice on any future change the same day.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- Your Clinic III case file with every outcome so far (or the supplied docket and its outcomes above).
- The [get-a-lawyer checklist](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md) and your [clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md).
- About 90 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Compare (20 min).** For each matter, put what arrived beside what you asked for, using the closing checklist. List every gap.
2. **Chase (15 min).** For each gap, the request or letter that closes it, with a date. Diarise.
3. **Appeal or not (10 min).** For any decision against you, write the period, the forum and whether you need advice; decide early in the period.
4. **Surviving dates (10 min).** List each date that outlives the matter; put each in your calendar.
5. **Get-a-lawyer run (15 min).** Run the checklist with the year-3 red flags; write the result; if needed, the one-page summary.
6. **Closing memo (20 min).** Six parts, one page per matter, at the front of the file.

### Template

- [Get-a-lawyer checklist and one-page case summary](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md) (module 12), with the year-3 red flags above.
- [Clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md) (module 12): closing memo at the front.

### How you know it worked

- Every matter has either "received" with a date or a chasing step with a date.
- Every surviving date is in your calendar.
- Someone else could pick up each matter from the closing memo alone.

### Self-check

- [ ] I checked the order against my share table and the extract against the order.
- [ ] I checked the refund amount per year, not just that "money arrived".
- [ ] I set up the payments that keep the payment order and the paying-debtor status alive.
- [ ] I know my year-3 red flags and where my free help is.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes, closing your own file needs no one else. The point of this lesson is the decision of when to stop: the checklist gives you a rule, and the one-page summary makes a consultation short and cheap.

### Where and how

- Free help by subject: the Registrar of Inheritance service centre (\*2416, ⚠️ manual check); the Tax Authority's call centre; the Enforcement and Collection Authority (\*35592) and Legal Aid's execution service; the municipality's public-complaints officer; Legal Aid for eligible people ([where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md)).
- Forum rules: [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md); numbers: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- Any year-3 red flag in the table applies.
- Two amber signs apply together.
- A relative asks you to run his matter: point him to the free services and templates instead.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Not reading the order against your share table | A wrong share is registered | Request to amend (s. 72) | Compare on the day it arrives |
| Assuming the refund is right because money arrived | Part of the claim lost | Objection within 30 days of the assessment | Compare per year with the request |
| Stopping the standing order after the attachment is lifted | Paying-debtor status and the order collapse | Resume at once; ask the registrar | Keep paying to the last instalment |
| No calendar for review triggers | Will out of date after a birth or a sale | Write a new will | Diarise triggers in the closing memo |
| Running a relative's file "because you know how now" | s. 20 problem | Hand back; he signs and files | Explain, point, do not act |

## Review

- Close each matter by comparing what arrived with what you asked for, and chase every gap with a dated step.
- The closing memo keeps the dates that outlive the matter: review triggers, reminders, the next refund year, the last instalment, the next arnona notice.
- One red flag means a lawyer or Legal Aid; two amber signs mean at least one consultation with the one-page summary.
- Your skills are for your own affairs; for others, explain and point, but do not act (s. 20).
