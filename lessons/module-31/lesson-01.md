# 31.1 · Choosing the matter and building the file

Clinic III takes real property, estate and money matters of your own from the file to the result: your own will and enduring power of attorney, a succession order for a parent's estate in which you are an heir, registering inherited rights in the Land Registry, a tax refund and an objection, defending an execution file, and an arnona objection. In this lesson you choose which of them you will run, find the office and track for each, list the deadlines that can cost you money, and open the file you will carry through lessons 31.2 to 31.8.

## Why it matters

The year-1 clinic (module 12) was a dispute with a shop or landlord; the year-2 clinic (module 22) was a dispute with an employer, the NII, a former partner or a landlord. Year-3 matters are different again, in three ways.

First, **most of them are not disputes but applications to a registry or an authority**: the Registrar of Inheritance, the Land Registry, the Administrator General, the assessing officer, the arnona director, the execution registrar. The other side is a public body that must act lawfully, give reasons and keep to deadlines. Winning usually means filing a complete, correct application the first time, not out-arguing anyone.

Second, **the money is often larger than in earlier clinics and is lost quietly**. A tax refund for 2020 that is not claimed by 31 December 2026 is gone forever (Income Tax Ordinance s. 160(א)); an arnona overcharge you did not object to within 90 days of the notice stays for that year; an execution warning ignored for 20 days turns an inconvenient debt into restrictions, an exit ban and a deemed-evader finding (lesson [28.1](../module-28/lesson-01.md)). Nobody sends a reminder.

Third, **some matters involve other people's rights even though the step is yours**. An estate belongs to all the heirs; a flat registered to your late father will be registered to you and your siblings together; a will affects your children. You may file in your own name as an heir, but each sibling signs his own renunciation, affidavit or distribution agreement, and you may not act for them (Bar Association Law s. 20).

The theory of each matter was taught in modules 13, 25, 26, 27 and 28. This module links back and concentrates on running the file.

## The law

### Your own matter only: ss. 20–21

Only a lawyer may represent another before courts, tribunals and listed offices (the Land Registry and the Registrar of Inheritance among them), draft legal documents for another, or give legal advice as an occupation ([Bar Association Law s. 20](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לשכת_עורכי_הדין)). Section 21 adds narrow exceptions, including representation that another statute regulates: accountants and representing tax advisers may represent taxpayers before the Tax Authority ([Income Tax Ordinance s. 236](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_236)). There is no family exception. In this clinic that means: your own will, your own refund, your own execution file, your own arnona account, and an estate only in your capacity as one of its heirs.

### The deadlines that cost money

> הוכח להנחת דעתו של פקיד השומה, כי אדם שילם מס לשנת מס פלונית – בין דרך ניכוי ובין בדרך אחרת – יתר על הסכום שהוא חייב בו, והדו״ח לאותה שנה הוגש לא יאוחר מתום שש שנים אחריה, יהא אותו אדם זכאי [...] שיוחזר לו תשלום היתר בתוספת הפרשי הצמדה וריבית [...]

*Gist:* Overpaid tax is refunded with linkage and interest only if the report for that year is filed within six years after the end of the year. [Income Tax Ordinance s. 160(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_160)

> היה אדם חולק על השומה, רשאי הוא לבקש מאת פקיד השומה, בהודעת השגה, בכתב, לחזור ולעיין ולשנות את השומה; בקשה כאמור תפרש בדיוק את הנימוקים להשגה על השומה ותוגש תוך שלושים יום מיום המצאת הודעת השומה [...]

*Gist:* An objection to an income-tax assessment is in writing, gives exact grounds, and is filed within 30 days of service. [s. 150(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_150)

> (א) משהוגשה בקשת ביצוע, ימציא מנהל לשכת ההוצאה לפועל לחייב אזהרה שעליו למלא אחר פסק־הדין, או לשלם את החוב הפסוק בשיעורים שנקבעו בהוראת תשלום לפי סעיף 69א או בצו לפי סעיף 69(ב), או להגיש בקשה לצו תשלומים לפי סעיף 7א, והכל בתוך תקופה של 20 ימים מיום המצאת האזהרה…

*Gist:* An execution warning gives the debtor a fixed period from service to pay, to pay by instalments, or to apply for a payment order. [Execution Law s. 7(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_7)

> הרואה עצמו מקופח בתשובת מנהל הארנונה על השגתו רשאי, תוך שלושים יום מיום שנמסרה לו התשובה, לערור עליה לפני ועדת ערר.

*Gist:* After the arnona director answers your objection, you have 30 days to appeal to the appeals committee; the objection itself is due within 90 days of receiving the payment notice (s. 3(א)). [Arnona Appeals Law s. 6(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הרשויות_המקומיות_%28ערר_על_קביעת_ארנונה_כללית%29#סעיף_6)

Estate and land matters have fewer fatal deadlines but several practical ones: at least two weeks for objections after publication of a succession application ([Succession Law s. 67](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הירושה#סעיף_67)), 15 days to appeal a Registrar's decision, and 15 days for a ערר against a Land Registrar's decision. All periods, with sources, are in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### Getting the records

Anyone may inspect the land register and receive an extract (Land Law s. 124; lesson [13.2](../module-13/lesson-02.md)). Your tax records (employer certificates, the assessing office's file) and your execution file are visible in each authority's personal area; your arnona account and its measurement sheet are available from the municipality. For personal information held about you, the right of inspection under the Privacy Protection Law s. 13 applies (lesson [22.1](../module-22/lesson-01.md)).

## Leading cases

### רע״א 8120/08 (no "close friend" in court)

The Supreme Court held that, absent an express statutory provision, only a licensed lawyer may represent a party in court; there is no general right to be represented by a relative or a "close friend". Why it matters here: when your brother, who lives abroad, asks you to "handle the flat" for both of you, you can file the succession application and the land registration in your own name as an heir, but you cannot sign, appear or argue for him. Each heir signs his own documents. [Full text (supremedecisions)](https://supremedecisions.court.gov.il/Home/Download?path=HebrewVerdicts%2F08%2F200%2F081%2Fv04&fileName=08081200_v04.txt&type=2) (⚠️ host unreachable on 18 October 2026; citation from the course research file).

### Key text: the statutes that send each matter to its office

There is no single leading case for choosing a clinic matter. Put on page 1 of your file the section that gives the office its power, the one that sets the deadline and the one that gives the remedy: Succession Law ss. 66–67 (Registrar of Inheritance); Land Management and Registration Regulations regs 17–18 (registering inheritance); Income Tax Ordinance ss. 150, 160 (objection and refund); Execution Law ss. 7, 7א (warning and payment order); Arnona Appeals Law ss. 3, 4, 6 (objection, deemed acceptance, appeal); Capacity and Guardianship Law ss. 32יד, 32יז (enduring power).

## How it works in practice

### Six matters, six tracks

| Matter | Office and first step | Money-losing or fatal deadline | Lesson (theory) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your own will and enduring power of attorney | Write and deposit the will with the Registrar of Inheritance; plan the power and sign it before a trained lawyer; deposit with the Administrator General | None fatal; the risk is dying or losing capacity first | 31.2 (26.2, 26.7, 26.8) |
| Succession or probate order in a parent's estate | Application to the Registrar of Inheritance, online or on paper | At least 2 weeks for objections after publication; 15 days to appeal a decision | 31.3 (26.1, 26.6, 26.9) |
| Registering inherited land | Inheritance-registration request to the Land Registry office where the land is registered (or with the online succession application) | 15 days for a ערר against a registrar's decision | 31.4 (13.2, 26.7) |
| Tax refund; objection to an assessment | Refund request (Form 135 or online) to the assessing officer; objection in writing | Refund: 6 years after the tax year; objection: 30 days from service | 31.5 (25.4, 25.5, 25.10) |
| Execution file against you | Request in the file: payment order, "I have paid", release of protected money, arrangement | 20 days from service of the warning (21 for the means inquiry) | 31.6 (28.1–28.4, 28.8) |
| Arnona bill | Objection to the arnona director; then appeal | 90 days from the payment notice; appeal 30 days from the answer | 31.7 (27.8, 27.10) |

### Five filters for a Clinic III matter

| Filter | Pass | Fail: choose another matter or another route |
|---|---|---|
| Yours | Your will, your tax year, your execution file, your arnona account, an estate in which you are an heir | Your mother's arnona, your partner's execution file, your aunt's estate when you are not an heir |
| A known office | You can name the office and the first form (table above) | You cannot tell whether the land is registered, ILA leasehold or unregistered: do lesson [13.2](../module-13/lesson-02.md) first |
| In time | Every fatal deadline is at least two weeks away | An execution warning served 18 days ago and no documents; a refund year that ends in a week |
| Provable | You hold or can order the will, death certificate, extract, Form 106, the warning, the bill and the measurement sheet | The original will is lost, the heirs are unknown, the employer has closed down without certificates |
| Manageable risk | The worst outcome is a refused application you can refile, or a modest payment | An heir objects, an heir is a minor or abroad, the assessment alleges fraud, the execution debts exceed the insolvency threshold, the arnona debt is in the tens of thousands |

### The file

Use the [clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md) from module 12 with the three year-2 additions of lesson [22.1](../module-22/lesson-01.md) (track sheet, deadline table with proof of each start date, relationship note), and two year-3 additions:

1. **Asset and title register.** One row per asset: what it is (flat, bank account, pension, car), where it is recorded (Land Registry, ILA, the bank, the pension fund), its identifiers (block, parcel, sub-parcel; account number), the registered owner today, and the document that proves it.
2. **Money ledger.** For each money matter, what you claim or owe, the authority's figure, the difference, and the document behind each line. For a refund, one column per tax year; for execution, one row per file; for arnona, one row per billing year.

### Order the records first

Before writing anything: a current land extract (lesson 13.2; the electronic extract is cheap, see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)); the Form 106 for every employer in every open tax year; a printout of each execution file from the Enforcement and Collection Authority's personal area; the arnona payment notice with its envelope and the municipality's measurement sheet; the death certificate and any will.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- A list of your estate, land, tax, debt and municipal matters from the last six years, or the supplied docket.
- The documents you hold for each: death certificates, wills, extracts, Form 106s, execution warnings, arnona notices with their envelopes.
- The [clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md), the [forum check worksheet](../../templates/forum-check-worksheet.md) and the [get-a-lawyer checklist](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md).
- About 90 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Inventory (15 min).** Write down every candidate: your own will and power of attorney (everyone has this one), any estate in which you are an heir, any land registered to a deceased person in your family, each tax year since 2020 in which you were an employee, any execution file in your name, your arnona account.
2. **Filters (15 min).** Run each through the five filters; record pass or fail with one reason. Choose at least two matters: your will and power of attorney (lesson 31.2) and one money matter.
3. **Office and track (10 min).** For each chosen matter, write the office, the first form or service, and the appeal route from the table.
4. **Deadline diary (15 min).** For each matter, every deadline, the event it runs from, the date and the proof. Put each deadline, and a reminder a week earlier, in your calendar now. Check the 2020 refund year at once: it closes on 31 December 2026.
5. **Asset and title register (15 min).** One row per asset you will deal with; order a current land extract for any land.
6. **Money ledger (10 min).** For each money matter, your figure, the authority's figure and the difference.
7. **Records request (10 min).** Order or download what you do not yet hold (Form 106 from past employers; the execution-file printout; the arnona measurement sheet). Log each request with its date.

- [Clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md) (module 12), with the year-2 additions of lesson 22.1 and the asset and title register and money ledger above.
- [Forum check worksheet](../../templates/forum-check-worksheet.md) (lesson 01.7).
- [Get-a-lawyer checklist](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md) (lesson 12.8): run it once now.

### How you know it worked

- A stranger reading your cover page can say which office you will apply to, with which form, and by when.
- Every asset you will deal with has an identifier and a document proving who is registered today.
- Every money matter has your figure and the authority's figure side by side.

- [ ] Each matter is mine, or an estate in which I am an heir, and I know what the other heirs must sign themselves.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes, for every matter in this module, in your own name: the will is yours by definition; individuals apply to the Registrar of Inheritance online or on paper; an heir may file the inheritance-registration request at the Land Registry, signing before a registry official; taxpayers file their own refund requests and objections; debtors file their own requests in the execution file; holders file their own arnona objections and appeals ([can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md)). Two steps need a professional by law: an enduring power of attorney is signed before a lawyer trained by the Administrator General (lesson 31.2), and some affidavits must be verified before a lawyer. That is verification, not representation.

### Where and how

- Offices, forms and fees per matter: lessons 31.2 to 31.7; numbers in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); free help in [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).
- Free help that fits year-3 matters: the Registrar of Inheritance service centre (\*2416, ⚠️ manual check); the Tax Authority's call centre and its online simulator; the Enforcement and Collection Authority call centre (\*35592) and Legal Aid's execution service; municipal debt-counselling programmes; Legal Aid for eligible people.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- An heir objects, an heir is a minor, cannot be found or lives abroad and will not cooperate, or the estate includes a business, foreign assets or ILA leasehold land with special rules.
- A tax year was one in which you had to file a return and did not, or the assessment alleges fraud (lesson 25.8).
- Your execution debts together exceed what you can repay within the payment-order periods (lesson 28.5).
- The arnona debt is large, the municipality has attached your account, or you want to challenge the arnona order itself.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Waiting until "next year" to claim an old refund year | The year closes and the money is lost (s. 160(א)) | None for the closed year; file the open years now | Check the oldest open year on day 1 |
| Running a sibling's part of the estate "to save time" | s. 20 problem; documents signed for him are not his | Each heir signs his own documents | Write in the relationship note what each heir signs |
| Counting the arnona period from the date on the bill | You object late, or think you are late when you are not | Find the delivery proof and recount | Keep the envelope or the email header |
| Ignoring an execution warning while "collecting documents" | Restrictions and a deemed-evader finding | File a payment-order request at once, documents to follow | File within the warning period, then complete |
| Writing to the authority before ordering your records | Your figures are wrong and your credibility suffers | Order the records, then correct | Records first, letters second |

## Review

- A Clinic III matter is usually an application to a registry or authority, not a dispute; the aim is a complete, correct filing the first time.
- The deadlines that cost money are the 6-year refund window, the 30-day tax objection, the 20-day execution warning and the 90-day arnona objection.
- An estate is yours only as one of the heirs: you may apply, but each heir signs his own documents.
- The file is module 12's case file with the year-2 additions plus an asset and title register and a money ledger; order the official records before writing.
