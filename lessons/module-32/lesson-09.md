# 32.9 · Practice lab: a trademark search and registration application

In this lab you take a name or logo you use or plan to use, search the Israeli and international registers for conflicts, decide whether and how to file, choose the classes, write the specification of goods and services, and prepare a complete application in your own name with the right fee, ready to file through the Israel Patent Office.

## Why it matters

A clearance search costs an evening; a forced rebrand after printing, signage and a website costs months and money. And a registration you file badly (wrong classes, a specification that is too narrow or too vague, a descriptive word) protects much less than you think. This lab gives you a repeatable routine you can use for every new brand, and the judgment to know when the search results mean "stop and get advice".

## The law

> הטוען לבעלות על סימן מסחר שבשימושו או המיועד לשימושו ורוצה לרשום את הסימן, יגיש לרשם בקשה על כך בדרך שנקבעה.

*Gist:* A person claiming ownership of a mark used or intended for use may apply to the Registrar; one application may cover one or several classes of goods. ([Trade Marks Ordinance s. 17](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_סימני_מסחר)) The mark is registered for specific goods or classes, and the Registrar decides classification questions (s. 10).

> בקשה לרישום סימן מסחר, בקשה, הודעה, מודעה או מסמך אחר ... יוגשו בנייר כאמור בתקנה 6א או באתר ההגשה כאמור בתקנות 6ב עד 6ה.

*Gist:* Applications and documents may be filed on paper or on the online filing site; a company or a licensed professional filing in that capacity must file online only (reg. 6(ב)). Online filing requires electronic identification (reg. 6ב(ג)). Anyone may search the Registrar's databases on its website (reg. 78(ב)), or ask the Registrar to search for a fee (reg. 78(א)). ([Trade Marks Regulations 1940](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סימני_המסחר))

The fees are in the First Schedule to the Regulations, updated each year: an application fee for the first class, a lower fee for each additional class in the same application, opposition and renewal fees ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)). After acceptance the application is published and may be opposed within three months (s. 24); registration lasts ten years from the application date and is renewable (ss. 31–32).

## Leading cases

### ע"א 3912/90 Eximin S.A. נ' איטל סטייל פררי טקסטיל ונעליים בע"מ (22.8.1993)

An Israeli manufacturer sold 3,000 pairs of boots with a "V" pocket design to a Belgian buyer for the US market; US customs detained them because the design infringed a registered US trade mark. The Supreme Court (Shamgar P. and Malz J., Goldberg J. dissenting) held that both parties knew a registered mark might exist and neither checked, so both acted without good faith, and it split the loss between them. Why it matters: in a commercial relationship, failing to run a trade-mark search when you know there is a risk can be a breach of the duty of good faith (lesson [03.4](../module-03/lesson-04.md)), not only an IP problem. (⚠️ Hebrew caption rendered from the English translation.) [English translation, Cardozo Versa](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/eximin-sa-v-itel-style-ferarri)

The substantive tests you apply in the search (distinctiveness, the triple test, well-known marks) are in lesson 32.5 (adidas, NRG, Storck, Ilan Car Glazing).

## How it works in practice

### Where to search (checked 2026-10-18)

| Register | What it shows | Address |
|---|---|---|
| Israel Patent Office trade-mark search | Registered and pending Israeli marks, including Madrid marks designating Israel, and file documents | trademarks.justice.gov.il, TradeMarkSearch (per the Patent Office's 2024 report to WIPO; ⚠️ manual check, the site blocks automated access) |
| Trade Marks Journal | Monthly list of accepted applications open to opposition | same site, TradeMarksJournal (⚠️ manual check) |
| Government open data | Downloadable trade-mark data | [data.gov.il, סימני מסחר](https://data.gov.il/he/datasets/ministry_of_justice/simaneymisahr) |
| WIPO Global Brand Database | International registrations and many national registers | [branddb.wipo.int](https://branddb.wipo.int/) |
| Companies Registrar | Company names (Companies Law s. 27) | lesson [16.2](../module-16/lesson-02.md) |
| The market | Unregistered use that may support passing off | search engines, social networks, marketplaces |

### Searching well

- Search the word itself, its phonetic variants, its Hebrew and English (and Arabic or Russian, if relevant) forms, its translation, and its distinctive part alone.
- Filter by the classes you need and by related classes (coffee in 30 and cafés in 43; clothing in 25 and online clothing sales in 35).
- Look at the status: registered, pending, published for opposition, expired or cancelled.
- For a logo, search by its words and by the figurative elements the system offers (⚠️ manual check of the image-search options).

### Classes and the specification

The Patent Office uses the Nice Classification; its 13th edition (NCL 13-2026) has applied internationally since 1 January 2026 ([WIPO](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/madrid-system/w/news/2025/coming-on-january-1-2026-thirteenth-edition-of-the-nice-classification)). Choose the classes for what you actually sell or plan to sell soon; each additional class costs a further fee. Write the specification in clear, standard terms from the Nice list ([NCLPub](https://nclpub.wipo.int/enfr/)); avoid both "all goods in class 30" and an over-narrow "sourdough bread made in Haifa".

### Filing and after

1. File online (with government electronic identification) or, as an individual, on paper on form ר.ס.מ. 1; pay the fee.
2. Examination: the Patent Office reported an average wait of 2–3 months for a first examination at the end of 2024 (WIPO 2024 report). The examiner may object (descriptiveness, prior marks); you answer in writing within the time set, and may ask for a hearing.
3. Acceptance and publication in the Journal; three months for oppositions (s. 24).
4. Registration and certificate; the term runs from the application date (s. 31).
5. Use it, keep evidence of use (s. 41), and renew every ten years (s. 32).
6. Abroad: the Madrid Protocol lets you extend an Israeli application to other countries online; the Patent Office reported joining Madrid e-filing in 2023.

## Worked example

Tamar Avraham, from Kfar Saba, plans to sell handmade soy candles online as "LUMINA".

1. **Search.** In the Patent Office database she finds "LUMINA" registered by a lighting company for lamps (class 11) and a pending "LUMINAH" for cosmetics (class 3). In class 4 (candles) she finds nothing identical, but "LUMEN" for candles. On WIPO's Global Brand Database she finds "LUMINA" designating Israel for candles, filed by a German company, status "refused" two years ago.
2. **Analysis.** Lamps and candles are related goods (lighting the home); "LUMINA" for lamps is a risk under s. 11(9) and in infringement (triple test, lesson 32.5). "LUMEN" is close in sound and meaning. The word also suggests light, so it is weak.
3. **Decision.** She changes to an invented word, "Neroa" (from נר), searches again and finds nothing in classes 3, 4 and 35.
4. **Application.** Mark: the word "NEROA" in standard characters. Class 4: "candles; scented candles; candle wicks". Class 35: "online retail services for candles and home fragrances". Fee: the first-class fee plus one additional class (see current numbers). Applicant: Tamar herself, as an individual (she is not a company).
5. **After filing.** She diarises the examination answer deadline, watches the Journal for similar marks to oppose, and keeps evidence of use from launch.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A name or logo you use or plan to use (or Tamar's "LUMINA").
- A browser; for online filing, your government electronic identification (do not file in this lab unless you have decided to).
- The [trade-mark search and application worksheet](../../templates/trademark-search-and-application-worksheet.md).
- 150 minutes.

### Steps

**Part 1: define the mark (15 minutes)**

1. Write the mark exactly as you will use it: word, logo, or both; colours if you will limit to them (s. 9).
2. Place it on the distinctiveness spectrum (lesson 32.5) and check s. 11 grounds.

**Part 2: search (60 minutes)**

3. Search the Patent Office database for identical and similar words in all classes; record every hit with number, owner, classes, status (worksheet part B).
4. Search WIPO's Global Brand Database for international marks covering Israel.
5. Search the Companies Registrar and the market (search engines, social networks, marketplaces).
6. For each relevant hit, run the triple test and mark the risk: high, medium or low.

**Part 3: decide (15 minutes)**

7. Decide: file as is, change the mark, add a distinctive element, or stop and get advice. Write the reasons.

**Part 4: prepare the application (50 minutes)**

8. Choose classes using NCLPub; write the specification for each (worksheet part D).
9. Fill in the applicant details, the mark representation and any colour limitation.
10. Calculate the fee from the current schedule.
11. Write your post-filing calendar: examination answer, Journal watch, three-month opposition window, renewal in ten years.

**Part 5: review (10 minutes)**

12. Swap with a friend or reread the next day: would an examiner understand exactly what you sell?

### How you know it worked

- Your search table covers identical and similar marks in your classes and related ones, with status and a risk mark.
- Your decision follows from the search, and your mark is distinctive.
- Your specification uses standard terms that cover what you sell, in every class you pay for.
- Your fee and calendar are correct.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. An individual may search, file and prosecute his own application, on paper or online (Regulations regs 6–6ב), and may file or answer an opposition. A company must file online (reg. 6(ב)). Filing for others for pay is reserved to patent attorneys and lawyers (Patents Law s. 154; Bar Association Law s. 20).

### Where and how

- Search: the Patent Office's trade-mark search and Journal (⚠️ manual check of the addresses on gov.il), WIPO's Global Brand Database, data.gov.il.
- File: the Patent Office's online filing system (trademarksonline.justice.gov.il, per the 2024 WIPO report; ⚠️ manual check), with electronic identification, or on paper as an individual.
- Fees: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- Your search shows an identical or very similar mark for the same or related goods that is registered or pending.
- The examiner objects and you want to argue acquired distinctiveness with evidence.
- Someone opposes your application, or you want to oppose theirs with evidence and a hearing.
- You need protection in several countries quickly.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Searching only the exact spelling | Missed "LUMINAH" and "LUMEN" | Repeat with variants | Search sound, meaning and translations |
| Filing in the owner's name when the business is a company (or the reverse) | Wrong owner; transfer needed later | Record an assignment (s. 48) | Decide the owner before filing |
| Paying for classes you will not use | Wasted fees; non-use risk (s. 41) | Drop classes before registration | File for real business plans only |
| A vague specification ("all goods") | Objection and delay | Amend to standard terms | Use NCLPub terms |
| Missing the examination answer deadline | Application treated as abandoned | Ask for an extension (fee) | Diarise every deadline |

## Review

- Search before you use and before you file: Israeli database, Journal, WIPO's Global Brand Database, Companies Registrar and the market (Eximin: not checking can be a breach of good faith).
- Run the triple test on every close hit and decide honestly; a distinctive invented word is the safest choice.
- Choose Nice classes (NCL 13-2026) for real business, with clear standard specifications; each class has a fee.
- Individuals may file on paper or online with electronic identification; companies must file online.
- After filing: examination, acceptance, three months for oppositions, registration for ten years from filing, use and renewal.
