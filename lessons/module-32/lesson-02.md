# 32.2 · Copyright: works, authorship, ownership and duration

By the end of this lesson you will be able to look at a text, photo, drawing, song, piece of software or database and decide whether Israeli copyright protects it, who owns it first (you, your employer, your client or the State), whether a transfer to someone else is valid, and the year in which it falls into the public domain.

## Why it matters

Most copyright disputes that reach Israeli courts are not about whether copying happened but about two earlier questions: is there a protected work at all, and who owns it. A photographer sues a website, and the defence is that the wedding couple, not the photographer, owns the photos. A company sues a former employee over code he wrote at home, and the question is whether it was made "for his work and in its course". A start-up discovers that the founder's freelance designer still owns the logo. Getting these questions right before a dispute starts is the cheapest IP protection there is.

## The law

### What is a work

> ”יצירה ספרותית“ – לרבות יצירה המבוטאת בכתב, הרצאה, טבלה, לקט, וכן תוכנת מחשב;

> ”יצירה אמנותית“ – לרבות רישום, ציור, יצירת פיסול, תחריט, ליטוגרפיה, מפה, תרשים, יצירה אדריכלית, יצירת צילום ויצירת אמנות שימושית, ובכלל זה גופן (Typeface);

*Gist:* "Literary work" includes anything expressed in writing, a lecture, a table, a compilation and computer software; "artistic work" includes drawings, paintings, sculpture, maps, diagrams, architecture, photographs, applied art and typefaces. ([Copyright Law s. 1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_זכות_יוצרים))

> לעניין סעיף קטן (א), מקוריות של לקט היא המקוריות בבחירה ובסידור של היצירות או של הנתונים שבו.

*Gist:* A compilation is original only in the selection and arrangement of its contents, not in the data themselves. (same, s. 4(ב)) Laws, regulations, Knesset proceedings and judicial decisions have no copyright (s. 6), and a design used or intended for industrial production has none either (s. 7).

### A connection to Israel

> (1) היצירה פורסמה לראשונה בישראל; (2) בעת יצירתה של היצירה היה יוצרה אזרח ישראלי, או שמקום מגוריו הרגיל היה בישראל, וזאת בין שהיצירה פורסמה ובין שלא פורסמה.

*Gist:* A work is protected if first published in Israel, or if its author was an Israeli citizen or habitually resident in Israel when it was made. (same, s. 8(א)) Foreign works are protected through treaty orders under s. 9 (Berne, TRIPS and others), so in practice almost any work made anywhere is protected in Israel.

### Who owns it first

> מעביד הוא הבעלים הראשון של זכות היוצרים ביצירה שנוצרה על ידי עובדו לצורך עבודתו ובמהלכה, אלא אם כן הוסכם אחרת.

*Gist:* The employer is the first owner of a work made by an employee for the purpose of the job and in its course, unless agreed otherwise. (same, s. 34)

> (א) ביצירה שנוצרה לפי הזמנה, הבעלים הראשון של זכות היוצרים בה, כולה או חלקה, הוא היוצר, אלא אם כן הוסכם אחרת בין המזמין והיוצר, במפורש או במשתמע. (ב) ביצירה שהיא דיוקן או צילום של אירוע משפחתי או של אירוע פרטי אחר, שנוצרה לפי הזמנה, הבעלים הראשון של זכות היוצרים בה הוא המזמין, אלא אם כן הוסכם אחרת.

*Gist:* In a commissioned work the author keeps the copyright unless the parties agreed otherwise, expressly or by implication; but in a commissioned portrait or photo of a family or other private event, the commissioning party is the first owner unless agreed otherwise. (same, s. 35) Works made or commissioned by the State, or by a State employee in the course of the job, belong to the State (s. 36).

### Transfers and licences

> חוזה להעברת זכות יוצרים או למתן רישיון ייחודי לגביה, טעון מסמך בכתב.

*Gist:* A contract assigning copyright or granting an exclusive licence requires a written document; a non-exclusive licence does not. Transfers can be partial and limited by place, time or type of use. (same, s. 37(ב)–(ג))

### How long it lasts

> זכות יוצרים ביצירה תהא למשך חייו של היוצר וכן שבעים שנים לאחר מותו, בכפוף להוראות פרק זה.

*Gist:* Copyright lasts for the author's life and seventy years after death. (same, s. 38) A joint work: seventy years after the last surviving author dies (s. 39); an anonymous work: seventy years from first publication (s. 40); a sound recording: seventy years, subject to the rules in s. 41; a State work: fifty years from creation (s. 42). Every term ends on 31 December of the year in which it would otherwise expire (s. 43).

### Proving authorship

> מופיע על היצירה בדרך המקובלת שמו של אדם כיוצר היצירה, חזקה היא שאותו אדם הוא יוצר היצירה;

*Gist:* The person named on a work in the usual way is presumed to be its author, in civil and criminal proceedings, unless the contrary is proved. (same, s. 64(1)) This presumption is why a credit line on your photos is worth adding.

### Works made before May 2008

The 2007 Law came into force six months after publication (s. 77). It applies to older works too, but no new copyright arises in a work that had none under the old law (s. 78(ב)), the ownership rules of ss. 33–36 do not apply to works made before commencement (s. 78(ה)), and pre-commencement assignments follow the old law (s. 78(ו)). For an older work, check the 1911 Act and the Copyright Ordinance on ownership.

## Leading cases

### ע"א 360/83 סטרוסקי נ' גלידת ויטמן ומחלבות שטראוס (8.9.1985)

Graphic artists designed signs for an ice-cream maker, combining the maker's own name, a flower it already used, and stripes the artists had used for other clients. The Supreme Court (Netanyahu J., with Shamgar P. and Goldberg J.) held that copyright protects the expression, not the idea; originality means the work originates from its author and is not copied, not that it is novel; a minimal degree of effort and skill suffices, even for simple or commercial works; and a work is judged as a whole, so combining unoriginal elements into a new arrangement can be original. Why it matters: it is the classic Israeli statement that originality is a low bar and that applied, commercial and advertising art is protected; later cases (below) added a creativity requirement. The case was decided under the 1911 Act, including its ownership rule for commissioned engravings, which the 2007 Law replaced with s. 35. (⚠️ Hebrew caption rendered from the English translation.) [English translation, Cardozo Versa](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/struski-v-whitman-ice-cream-and-strauss-dairy-ltd)

### ע"א 8485/08 The FA Premier League Ltd נ' המועצה להסדר ההימורים בספורט (25.1.2010)

Discussed in lesson 32.1 for unjust enrichment. On originality, the Court held that a compilation needs both a minimal investment and a minimal creativity, the author's "imprint"; listing every match in the obvious order met the first but not the second. Why it matters: after Premier League, investment alone ("sweat of the brow") is not enough; databases and lists are protected only in their creative selection or arrangement (s. 4(ב)). [English translation, Cardozo Versa](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/fa-premier-league-v-israel-sports-betting-council)

### ע"א 7996/11 סייפקום בע"מ נ' רביב (18.11.2013)

Lesson [21.5](../module-21/lesson-05.md) covers Safecom's fair-use holding. On subsistence, the Court (Danziger J.) restated originality as three sub-tests, origin, investment and creativity, all of which must be present, and discussed the merger doctrine: where an idea can be expressed in only one way, the expression is not protected, and where there are few ways, protection is thin. It held that the claimant's technical drawings of a functional product were original and protected, and that copying them almost exactly infringed. Why it matters: functional works (drawings, manuals, code) are protected if the author made real choices; the fewer the choices, the closer the copying must be to infringe. [English translation, Cardozo Versa](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/safecom-ltd-v-raviv)

## How it works in practice

### Is it a work? A four-question test

1. **Is it expression rather than an idea, fact, method or news (s. 5)?** A recipe's list of ingredients is a set of facts; the way a food blogger writes the method can be expression.
2. **Is it fixed in some form (s. 4(א)(1))?** An improvised speech that nobody recorded is not; a voice message is.
3. **Is it original: from the author, with some investment and some creativity (Safecom)?** A snapshot is usually enough (choices of angle, timing, framing); a photocopy is not.
4. **Is there a connection to Israel or a treaty (ss. 8–9)?** Almost always yes.

### Ownership in common situations

| Situation | First owner | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| You wrote it in your free time, not for your job | You | Keep dated drafts showing time and place |
| Your employee wrote it for the job, during work | The employer (s. 34) | Employment contract can agree otherwise |
| A freelancer made it for you | The freelancer (s. 35(א)) unless agreed otherwise | Written assignment (s. 37(ג)) |
| A photographer at your wedding or family event | You, the commissioning party (s. 35(ב)), unless agreed otherwise | Read the photographer's contract: many reserve rights |
| A civil servant or soldier, in the course of service | The State (s. 36) | Ask the ministry for permission |
| Two people wrote it together and their parts cannot be separated | Joint authors (s. 1 "יצירה משותפת") | Agree in writing who may license |
| A sound recording | Its producer (s. 33(2)) | Separate from the song's author and the performers |

### Performers are different

Singers, actors and musicians do not get copyright for their performance; their rights are under a separate law, the Performers and Broadcasters Rights Law 1984. When you use a recording you may need permission from the song's author (or the collecting society ACUM), the recording's producer and the performers.

### Duration: worked arithmetic

- An author died on 3 March 1955: 1955 + 70 = 2025; the term ends on 31 December 2025 (s. 43), so the work has been in the public domain since 1 January 2026.
- A government booklet created in 1980: State work, 50 years from creation (s. 42); ends 31 December 2030.
- An anonymous poster first published in 2000: 70 years from publication (s. 40); ends 31 December 2070.

### What about AI-generated material?

No Israeli statute or Supreme Court ruling decides whether output produced by an AI system without human creative choices is a work. Under the originality test above, an author's own creative choices are required, so purely machine-generated output is at best doubtful; where you select, arrange or substantially edit the output, your contribution can be original. Keep a record of what you contributed.

## Worked example

Ori Mizrahi, a graphic designer in Be'er Sheva, works three days a week for an agency and freelances the rest of the week.

1. **A poster he designed for the agency's client, at the office.** Made for his work and in its course: the agency is the first owner (s. 34). The client gets rights only through its contract with the agency.
2. **A logo he designed on a Friday for a café owner he found himself.** A commissioned work: Ori owns the copyright (s. 35(א)) unless they agreed otherwise. The café needs a written assignment to own it (s. 37(ג)); without one it has at most an implied licence to use it as a logo.
3. **Photos he took at his sister's wedding at her request.** A commissioned photo of a family event: his sister is the first owner (s. 35(ב)) unless they agreed otherwise. Ori may not sell them to a stock site without her permission.
4. **A font he drew on weekends.** A typeface is an artistic work (s. 1), and he owns it. He should keep his dated sketch files.
5. **A 1950 photo of his grandfather's moshav, taken by an unknown photographer.** Photographs made before May 2008 follow the old law on authorship and duration (s. 78(ט)); he should not assume it is free, and should check its source.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Five creations of your own or your business (or the five in the worked example).
- Any contracts you signed for them (employment, freelance, photographer).
- The [IP asset inventory](../../templates/ip-asset-inventory.md), parts A and C.
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. For each creation, apply the four-question test and write "work" or "not a work", with the reason (10 minutes).
2. For each work, identify the first owner using ss. 33–36 and the contract terms (10 minutes).
3. Calculate the term for each work, and for one older work in your family or archive (10 minutes).
4. For one work you commissioned or created for someone else, draft a two-paragraph assignment that meets s. 37(ג): parties, the work, the rights assigned (all or part), territory and period, consideration, signatures (15 minutes).

None for this lesson beyond the [IP asset inventory](../../templates/ip-asset-inventory.md). A minimal assignment clause:

```text
אני, [שם היוצר/ת], ת"ז [___], מעביר/ה בזאת ל[שם הנמחה], ת"ז/ח"פ [___], את מלוא זכויות היוצרים
ב[תיאור היצירה] (להלן: "היצירה"), בכל העולם ולכל תקופת זכות היוצרים, בתמורה ל[___].
אין בהעברה כדי לגרוע מזכותי המוסרית ביצירה לפי סעיפים 45–46 לחוק זכות יוצרים, התשס"ח–2007.
תאריך: [___]   חתימת היוצר/ת: [___]   חתימת הנמחה: [___]
```

### How you know it worked

- Each creation has a reasoned answer on protection, ownership and term.
- Your assignment is written, signed by the author, identifies the work and the rights, and does not purport to transfer the moral right (s. 45(ב)).

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. There is no copyright registry in Israel and nothing to file. You may draft and sign an assignment or licence for your own works. Drafting such contracts for other people as an occupation is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).

### Where and how

- Keep an evidence file for each important work: dated drafts, original camera files with metadata, the first publication with its date (lesson 32.8).
- For music, ACUM manages many authors' public performance and broadcasting rights; register with it if you are a songwriter.
- Read the current text of the law on [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_זכות_יוצרים) and check the transitional rules (s. 78) for older works.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- A dispute over ownership involves an employer and real money, for example code you wrote that a start-up now uses.
- You are buying or selling a catalogue of works, or a business whose value is its content.
- The work was made before May 2008 and its ownership or duration matters (old-law rules apply).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paying a freelancer and assuming you own the work | The freelancer remains owner (s. 35(א)) | Ask for a written assignment now | Put an assignment clause in every commission |
| An oral "sale" of copyright | Invalid as an assignment (s. 37(ג)) | Sign a written document confirming it | Always write it down |
| Selling event photos you shot for relatives | Infringes their copyright (s. 35(ב)) | Get their written permission | Agree on rights before the event |
| Treating a database as protected because it was expensive | No copyright without creative selection or arrangement (Premier League) | Use a contract or trade-secret protection | Rely on terms of use and access controls |
| Counting duration to the date of death | Wrong end date | Extend to 31 December (s. 43) | Always use year-end |

## Review

- Copyright protects original expression fixed in any form; ideas, facts, methods, news, laws and judgments are free (ss. 4–6).
- Originality requires origin, some investment and some creativity (Struski, Premier League, Safecom); compilations are protected only in selection and arrangement.
- First owners: the author; the employer for job works; the author for commissions unless agreed; the commissioning party for family-event photos and portraits; the State for State works (ss. 33–36).
- Assignments and exclusive licences need writing (s. 37(ג)); the moral right stays with the author (s. 45(ב)).
- The term is life plus 70 years, ending on 31 December; State works 50 years (ss. 38–43).
