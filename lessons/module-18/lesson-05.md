# 18.5 · Child and spousal maintenance

By the end of this lesson you will be able to say which law fixes a child's and a spouse's maintenance for any family, estimate how a court will split child maintenance between two working parents since 2017, prepare a maintenance claim and an interim request, and know what to do when payments stop.

## Why it matters

Maintenance (מזונות) is the most litigated family issue in Israel and the one that touches daily life soonest: rent, food, school, health. It is also where religious and civil law meet most visibly. Spousal and child maintenance follow each parent's personal law (lesson 18.1), so a Jewish, Muslim, Christian and Druze father may have different obligations on the same income; the civil Maintenance Law fills the gaps and supplies uniform procedural rules.

The 2017 expanded-panel ruling in בע״מ 919/15 reshaped child maintenance for Jewish parents by reading Jewish law to divide the duty for children aged 6 to 15 between both parents according to their means and the time each spends with the children. Fathers' organisations welcomed it as overdue equality; women's organisations warned that it ignores the wage gap and the unequal burden of care; many rabbinical courts have not adopted it. Since February 2025 another dispute has run alongside: which court may decide child maintenance joined to a divorce (HCJ 5988/21 and the November 2025 temporary order; lesson [10.3](../module-10/lesson-03.md)).

## The law

### Personal law first: ss. 2–3

> (א) אדם חייב במזונות הילדים הקטינים שלו והילדים הקטינים של בן־זוגו לפי הוראות הדין האישי החל עליו, והוראות חוק זה לא יחולו על מזונות אלה.

*Gist:* A person owes maintenance to their own minor children and their spouse's minor children under their personal law; the Maintenance Law's substantive rules do not apply to them. [Maintenance Law, s. 3(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לתיקון_דיני_המשפחה_(מזונות)#סעיף_3)

Where no personal law applies or it imposes no duty (s. 3(ב)), the civil rule is:

> (א) אביו ואמו של קטין חייבים במזונותיו. (ב) בלי להתחשב בעובדה בידי מי מוחזק קטין יחולו המזונות על הוריו בשיעור יחסי להכנסותיהם מכל מקור שהוא.

*Gist:* Both parents owe their minor child maintenance in proportion to their incomes from all sources, whoever has custody. [s. 3א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לתיקון_דיני_המשפחה_(מזונות)#סעיף_3א)

Spousal maintenance likewise follows the personal law (s. 2(א)); in fixing it the court may take into account the other spouse's income from work and property (s. 2א), notwithstanding the Women's Equal Rights Law.

### Procedural and civil rules that apply to everyone

- **Scope:** absent agreement, the court fixes scope and manner by the circumstances and, except for s. 3 children, by the recipient's need and the payer's ability (s. 6).
- **Interim maintenance** once a claim is filed (s. 10).
- **Past periods:** a claim filed more than a year after the period claimed may be rejected for that reason alone without reasonable cause; arrears not enforced within two years need the court's leave (s. 11).
- **Agreements:**

> (א) הסכם בדבר מזונות של קטין וויתור על מזונות כאלה אינו קושר את הקטין, כל עוד לא אושר על ידי בית המשפט.

*Gist:* An agreement on a minor's maintenance, or a waiver, does not bind the child until a court approves it. [s. 12(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לתיקון_דיני_המשפחה_(מזונות)#סעיף_12)

- **Variation:** the court may change an agreement, waiver or judgment on circumstances that came to light or changed afterwards, and need not hear a new request within six months of a previous one (s. 13).
- **No assignment, attachment or set-off** of a maintenance right (s. 14).
- **Exemption** for "התנהגות מחפירה" of the recipient towards the payer (s. 9), and wider family duties (adult children, parents, siblings) on the strict ladder of ss. 4–5.

### Procedure in the Family Court

A maintenance claim is filed with a statement of particulars on Form 4; a parent claiming for a minor must add their own income in the 12 months before filing and the estimated value of their assets (reg. 12(ב)(1)). Undisclosed facts within your knowledge can be treated as admitted (reg. 12(ב)(2)). Interim maintenance is requested with an affidavit and documents; the other side answers within 14 days if the court asks (reg. 33). During the stay after a request to settle the dispute, interim maintenance is sought through the urgent-relief route (Dispute Resolution Regulations reg. 12(ה)), and a maintenance claim filed after the stay is treated as filed on the date of the request to settle (reg. 15(ב)), so no months are lost.

### When payments stop: National Insurance

> (א) זוכה שהוא תושב ישראל, זכאי לבקש מאת המוסד תשלום חדשי לפי הוראות חוק זה, ובלבד שהחייב היה תושב ישראל [...]

*Gist:* A resident who holds a maintenance judgment may ask National Insurance to pay monthly, subject to conditions; NII then collects from the debtor. [Maintenance (Assurance of Payment) Law, s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המזונות_(הבטחת_תשלום)#סעיף_2)

The amount is the lower of the judgment and the rate in the regulations (s. 3). Maintenance judgments are also enforced through the Execution Office with special tools, including arrest for non-payment and restrictions (lesson [08.7](../module-08/lesson-07.md)).

## Leading cases

### בע״מ 919/15 פלוני נ׳ פלונית (19.7.2017)

An expanded panel of seven justices, unanimously (Justice Fogelman writing), held that under Jewish law as properly interpreted today, the duty to maintain children aged 6 to 15 rests on *both* parents, according to their relative economic capacity and taking into account parenting time, rather than on the father alone. Below age 6 the father's duty for essential needs remains. The Wikipedia summary describes the method courts now use: (1) each parent's share of the combined income; (2) each pays into a notional "pool" for the children's needs in that proportion; (3) each draws from the pool according to the children's days with them.

Why it matters: it is the leading child-maintenance precedent for Jewish parents in the Family Court. The rabbinical courts, according to the same source, did not change their method. The ruling was criticised from both directions: as insufficient (fathers' groups seeking a civil equal-parenting statute) and as ignoring structural inequality (women's groups). Source: [Hebrew Wikipedia: בע״מ 919/15](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/בע%22מ_919/15_פלוני_נ%27_פלונית) (⚠️ secondary; the judgment on the Judiciary's site was not reachable).

### בר״ע 120/69 שרגאי נ׳ שרגאי (1969)

The child's maintenance claim is the child's own; what a parent joins to a divorce in the rabbinical court is a claim to reimbursement of expenses, which does not bar the child's own claim in the civil court. Why it matters: it protects children from maintenance traded in get negotiations, and it underlies the 2025 HCJ ruling and the debate over Amendment 6 (lesson 10.3). Citation as described in [ה״ח 1851](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/25/law/25_ls1_5852775.pdf).

## How it works in practice

### The age bands for a Jewish father (after 919/15)

| Child's age | Basis | Who pays |
|---|---|---|
| 0–6 | Essential needs (Jewish law) | Father, for essential needs; beyond them, by means |
| 6–15 | Charity-based duty, as read in 919/15 | Both parents by relative income and parenting time |
| 15–18 | Charity-based | Courts generally apply a similar approach (⚠️ check current case law) |
| 18 to end of military or national service | Case-law practice | Commonly a reduced sum (⚠️ check current practice) |

For Muslim, Christian and Druze parents the court applies the community's law to the extent it imposes a duty, and s. 3א where it does not; check the case law of your community's forum (lesson [10.4](../module-10/lesson-04.md)).

### A worked 919/15-style calculation (illustration only)

Two children aged 8 and 11. The court finds their needs (including housing) at ₪6,000 a month. Father earns ₪18,000 net, mother ₪9,000: shares 2/3 and 1/3. Parenting time: mother 60%, father 40%.

1. Each contributes to the pool by income share: father ₪4,000, mother ₪2,000.
2. Each "draws" by parenting time: mother ₪3,600, father ₪2,400.
3. Net transfer: father contributed ₪4,000 and drew ₪2,400, so he pays the mother ₪1,600 a month, plus his share of extraordinary expenses (often in the income ratio).

Real judgments start from a finding of needs and incomes on the evidence; the method shows the logic, not a tariff.

### Spousal maintenance in brief

Under Jewish law the husband's duty to maintain his wife continues while the marriage subsists and ends with the divorce; it may be denied where the rabbinical court finds the wife "rebellious" or where she has sufficient income, and s. 2א lets civil courts consider her income. Under Sharia, maintenance (nafaqa) is the husband's duty during the marriage and a limited period after divorce. Christian communities apply their canon law. After divorce, maintenance between former spouses exists only by agreement or under the civil Maintenance Law where it applies. The wife's own claim in the rabbinical court is protected by s. 4 of the 1953 Law (lesson 10.3).

### Costs and time

A maintenance claim carries a reduced fee and interim requests in it are fee-free (Family Court Fees Regulations, item 5 and reg. 7(2)); rabbinical courts exempt a maintenance claimant (lesson 10.6). Figures: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

## Worked example

Nur and Ahmad Masarwa, Muslims from Taibe, separated in January 2026. Their children are 4 and 9. Ahmad, a contractor, has stopped paying the household bills. Nur works part-time as a teaching assistant.

1. **First step.** Nur files a request to settle the dispute in the Family Court in Kfar Saba (where they last lived together), or in the Sharia court (lessons 18.9, 10.4).
2. **Interim money during the stay.** She files a short urgent-relief request for interim maintenance (Dispute Resolution Regulations reg. 12(ה)): income documents, the children's essential expenses, nothing about the dispute itself.
3. **Governing law.** Ahmad's duty to his children is under his personal law (Muslim law) as applied by the Family Court (s. 3(א), s. 19); where it imposes no duty, s. 3א applies.
4. **The claim.** After the stay she files the maintenance claim with Form 4: her income for 12 months, her assets, each child's needs separately (reg. 11(ב)(3)). It is treated as filed on the date of the request to settle (reg. 15(ב)).
5. **If he does not pay.** She opens a maintenance file in the Execution Office and applies to National Insurance under the 1972 Law.
6. **Ahmad's side.** He files his own Form 4, documents his real income (contractors' income is often disputed) and asks for parenting time; if his income falls later, he may ask to vary the order (s. 13).

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your own figures (with the other parent's consent if real) or the Masarwa case.
- The Form 4 headings (the official form is on the Judiciary's site; check the current version).
- 60 minutes.

### Steps

1. List each child's monthly needs: food, clothing, education, health, leisure, and a housing share. Keep receipts.
2. Fill in both parents' net incomes and assets.
3. Calculate the split by the 919/15 method for children aged 6+, and separately for children under 6.
4. Draft a one-page interim-maintenance request: the parties, the children, the urgent need, the income figures, the sum requested and the documents attached. No arguments about the marriage.
5. Draft the "variation" paragraph you would use if, a year later, the payer lost his job.

### Template

[Written motion](../../templates/written-motion.md), adapted for an interim-maintenance request (Family Court reg. 33, or Dispute Resolution reg. 12 during the stay).

### How you know it worked

- Your needs figure is supported by documents.
- Your calculation shows both parents' contributions and the net transfer.
- Your request contains facts needed for the decision and nothing else.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Maintenance claims, interim requests and variation requests are often filed by parents in person in the Family Court and in the religious courts. A child may sue through a parent or, where their rights may be seriously harmed, alone or through a "ידיד קרוב" (Family Court Law s. 3(ד)).

### Where and how

- Family Court of the last joint residence (reg. 6), or the competent religious court; for a Jewish wife's own maintenance, also the rabbinical court (1953 Law s. 4).
- Execution Office for enforcement; National Insurance for the payment guarantee (check eligibility on the NII site; ⚠️ manual check).
- Free help: Legal Aid in maintenance matters for eligible applicants; the assistance unit; [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The payer is self-employed, has a company or income abroad, or hides income.
- There is a joined divorce claim in the rabbinical court and the forum for child maintenance is disputed (the 2025–2027 temporary order).
- You are asked to sign an agreement waiving child maintenance in exchange for a get or property.
- The other parent lives abroad (international enforcement).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Waiting to file because "we are still talking" | Months of maintenance may be lost (s. 11) | File the request to settle now; reg. 15(ב) protects the date | File early; negotiate in parallel |
| Signing a private agreement on child maintenance | It does not bind the child (s. 12(א)) | Submit it for approval | Have every child-maintenance agreement approved |
| Incomplete Form 4 | Deemed admission of the other side's figures (reg. 12(ב)(2)) | Ask leave to supplement | Attach 12 months of income documents |
| Stopping payment when the other parent blocks contact | Arrears, execution and possibly arrest | Pay and apply separately about contact | Maintenance and contact are separate obligations |
| Asking for variation every few months | The court may refuse within six months (s. 13(ב)) | Wait and document the change | Ask once, with evidence of a lasting change |

## Review

- Child and spousal maintenance follow the personal law (ss. 2–3); s. 3א (both parents in proportion to income) applies where no personal law imposes a duty.
- For Jewish parents, 919/15 (2017) splits maintenance for children aged 6–15 by relative income and parenting time; below 6 the father bears essential needs.
- Child-maintenance agreements bind the child only after court approval; orders can be varied on changed circumstances.
- During the stay, interim maintenance goes through the urgent route; the later claim relates back to the request date.
- Unpaid maintenance: Execution Office with special tools, and National Insurance's payment guarantee.
