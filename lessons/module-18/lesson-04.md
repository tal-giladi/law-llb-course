# 18.4 · Property between spouses: the balance of resources and property agreements

By the end of this lesson you will be able to work out what each spouse is entitled to when a marriage ends under the default regime of the Spouses (Property Relations) Law, protect that right while the dispute is pending, and review a property agreement (הסכם ממון) for validity and approval.

## Why it matters

For most separating couples the home and the pensions are worth more than everything else together. The Spouses (Property Relations) Law 1973 gives a single civil regime for every married couple who married from 1 January 1974, whatever their religion, and it binds religious courts too unless both spouses agree otherwise before them (s. 13; lesson 18.1). Couples who married before 1974 are governed by the judge-made presumption of community (הלכת השיתוף).

The law reflects a policy choice that is still debated. The *deferred community* model keeps each spouse's property separate during the marriage and divides the value only at the end. Supporters see it as respecting autonomy and protecting a spouse who stayed home; critics say that a spouse without assets in their own name is vulnerable during the marriage and in the long months of litigation. The 2008 amendment (Amendment 4) responded by allowing an early balance and widening the court's discretion. Religious courts and some dayanim have questioned how far civil property rules should govern them; the Supreme Court has held that they are bound (Bavli).

## The law

### The property agreement: ss. 1–2

> הסכם בין בני זוג המסדיר יחסי ממון שביניהם (להלן – הסכם ממון); ושינוי של הסכם כזה, יהיו בכתב.

*Gist:* A property agreement between spouses, and any change to it, must be in writing. [s. 1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_יחסי_ממון_בין_בני_זוג#סעיף_1)

> (ב) האישור לא יינתן אלא לאחר שנוכח בית המשפט או בית הדין שבני הזוג עשו את ההסכם או את השינוי בהסכמה חפשית ובהבינם את משמעותו ואת תוצאותיו.

*Gist:* The Family Court or the competent religious court approves an agreement only after it is satisfied that both spouses made it freely and understand its meaning and consequences. [s. 2(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_יחסי_ממון_בין_בני_זוג#סעיף_2)

An agreement made before or at the wedding may instead be certified by the marriage registrar (s. 2(ג)), and one made before the marriage may be certified by a notary who is satisfied of free consent and understanding (s. 2(ג1)). An agreement approved in a divorce judgment of a religious court is treated as an approved property agreement (s. 2(ד)).

### The default: s. 3, s. 4 and s. 5

If there is no agreement, or to the extent it does not provide otherwise, the spouses are deemed to have agreed to the balance of resources (s. 3(א)). During the marriage nothing changes:

> אין בכריתת הנישואין או בקיומם כשלעצמם כדי לפגוע בקנינים של בני הזוג, להקנות לאחד מהם זכויות בנכסי השני או להטיל עליו אחריות לחובות השני.

*Gist:* Marriage by itself neither changes ownership nor makes one spouse liable for the other's debts. [s. 4](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_יחסי_ממון_בין_בני_זוג#סעיף_4)

At the end:

> (א) עם התרת הנישואין או עם פקיעת הנישואין עקב מותו של בן זוג [...] זכאי כל אחד מבני הזוג למחצית שוויים של כלל נכסי בני הזוג, למעט – (1) נכסים שהיו להם ערב הנישואין או שקיבלו במתנה או בירושה בתקופת הנישואין; (2) גימלה המשתלמת [...] על־ידי המוסד לביטוח לאומי, או גימלה או פיצוי [...] בשל נזק גוף, או מוות. (3) נכסים שבני הזוג הסכימו בכתב ששוויים לא יאוזן ביניהם.

*Gist:* On divorce or death, each spouse is entitled to half the value of all the spouses' property, except pre-marriage assets, gifts and inheritances, National Insurance benefits and personal-injury compensation, and assets the spouses agreed in writing to exclude. [s. 5(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_יחסי_ממון_בין_בני_זוג#סעיף_5)

Section 5(ג) adds that "כלל נכסי בני הזוג" includes future pension rights, severance pay, study funds, provident funds and savings.

### Early balance: s. 5א (2008)

The right can be exercised *before* the marriage is dissolved if a request is filed and either (1) a year has passed since divorce proceedings or a property claim began, or (2) the spouses have been estranged or living apart, even under one roof, for at least nine months out of a continuous year. The court may shorten these periods, or bring the balance forward, where a protection order was given against the other spouse after a hearing with both parties, a violence indictment was filed against them, or they were arrested on suspicion of violence against the applicant or a child (s. 5א(ב)). The court may make execution of the balance conditional on depositing consent to a divorce, including a get (s. 5א(ג)), and may stay it if the applicant acts in bad faith regarding the divorce; refusing to waive rights is not bad faith (s. 5א(ד)).

### How the balance is carried out: ss. 6–9

Each spouse's balanceable assets are valued, less their debts (excluding debts relating to excluded assets); the spouse with more pays the other half the difference, in kind or in money (s. 6(א)–(ב)). The court decides disputed points, considering each spouse's economic situation and the children's interests, and avoids, as far as possible, depriving a spouse of a livelihood, harming a business, harming social-rights accumulation or the children's welfare (s. 6(ג)–(ד)).

> ראה בית המשפט או בית הדין כי לצורך ביצוע איזון המשאבים יש למכור דירה המשמשת למגורי בני הזוג או למגורי ילדי בני הזוג הקטינים ובן הזוג המחזיק בהם, לא יורה על ביצוע המכירה והיא תעוכב, כל עוד לא נוכח [...] כי [...] נמצא הסדר מגורים אחר המתאים לצורכיהם [...]

*Gist:* The family home is not sold to carry out the balance until the court is satisfied that the minor children and the parent who keeps them have suitable alternative housing. [s. 6א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_יחסי_ממון_בין_בני_זוג#סעיף_6א)

Assets given away or hidden to defeat the balance are counted as if still owned (s. 7). In special circumstances the court may exclude further assets, divide other than half-and-half (considering future assets and earning capacity), or use an earlier valuation date or an earlier list of assets (s. 8). Registration of an asset in one spouse's name is not enough, alone, to prove it is excluded (s. 9).

### Protective measures: s. 11

> עשה אחד מבני הזוג פעולה שיש בה כדי לסכל זכות או זכות עתידה של בן הזוג השני [...] רשאי בית המשפט [...] לנקוט אמצעים לשמירת הזכות האמורה [...]

*Gist:* If a spouse acts, or is reasonably feared to be about to act, to defeat the other's rights, the court may order disclosure and security, require both spouses' consent for certain acts, or register a note in a public register (for example the Land Registry). [s. 11](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_יחסי_ממון_בין_בני_זוג#סעיף_11)

### Pensions: the 2014 Law

A judgment dividing pension savings can be registered with the pension fund or employer, which then splits the accumulated balance into a separate account for the former spouse, or pays part of the pension when it becomes payable (Pension Savings Division Law 2014, ss. 3–6, 13–14). The law covers a cohabitant too (s. 2). [2014 Law](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לחלוקת_חיסכון_פנסיוני_בין_בני_זוג_שנפרדו)

## Leading cases

### ע״א 1915/91 יעקובי נ׳ יעקובי, פ״ד מט(3) 529

The Supreme Court discussed the nature of the spouses' right to share. Its best-known holding: the right to share exists throughout the marriage but in a general form, and crystallises only when there is a recognised ground for dissolving the partnership, the end of the spouses' economic relationship. Why it matters: it explains why the date of the breakdown (מועד הקרע) is fought over, and why Amendment 4 (s. 5א) was needed to let a spouse realise the right before divorce. Citation and holding as summarised in [Hebrew Wikipedia: הלכת השיתוף](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/הלכת_השיתוף) (⚠️ secondary; full text not reached).

### ע״א 819/94 לוי נ׳ לוי, פ״ד נ(1) 300

For couples under the presumption of community, the Court held that where the asset is the couple's only home, neither the size of each spouse's investment nor even an irregular married life is enough to defeat the presumption of sharing. Why it matters: the family home is treated as the most "shared" asset, which also explains the courts' openness to claims of a *specific partnership* in a home registered in one spouse's name before the marriage. Citation as summarised in the same [Wikipedia article](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/הלכת_השיתוף) (⚠️ secondary).

### Bavli and 4602/13 (recap)

A rabbinical court deciding property must apply the civil rules (Bavli, HCJ 1000/92), but the HCJ will not re-examine its factual findings (HCJ 4602/13; lesson 18.1). Specific-partnership claims in an "external" asset (for example a house one spouse brought into the marriage) are argued in both forums; the outcome turns on evidence of intention to share.

## How it works in practice

### A worked calculation

| | Spouse A | Spouse B |
|---|---|---|
| Apartment bought during the marriage, registered to A | ₪2,000,000 | |
| Mortgage balance on the apartment | −₪800,000 | |
| Pension and study fund accumulated during the marriage | ₪600,000 | ₪200,000 |
| Car | ₪60,000 | ₪40,000 |
| Inheritance received by B (excluded, s. 5(א)(1)) | | (₪500,000 not counted) |
| Credit-card debt for household expenses | | −₪20,000 |
| **Balanceable total** | **₪1,860,000** | **₪220,000** |

Difference: ₪1,640,000. A owes B half: ₪820,000, in money or in kind (for example a transfer of part of the apartment and a pension split under the 2014 Law). Pension values should be the value accumulated during the marriage, calculated by an actuary; the court often appoints one (Family Court Law s. 8(ג)).

### Procedure and costs

1. Request to settle the dispute and mahut meetings first (lesson 18.9).
2. Claim for balance of resources, with the statement of particulars (Form 3) and documents: registry extracts, pension statements, bank statements for the relevant period.
3. Interim protection: s. 11 measures, a caveat-like note in the Land Registry, attachment (lesson [15.5](../module-15/lesson-05.md)).
4. Expert valuations: real estate, business, pensions.
5. Judgment; execution under the Family Court's supervision (Family Court Law s. 7) or the Execution Office.

The fee for a property claim between spouses, and for approval of a spouses' agreement with no claim pending, is set by the Family Court (Fees) Regulations and linked to the CPI; see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### Reviewing a property agreement

Check: (1) in writing and signed by both (s. 1); (2) approved by the Family Court or competent religious court, or certified by the marriage registrar or a notary if made before the wedding (s. 2); (3) evidence of free consent and understanding (each side had time and, ideally, independent advice); (4) a clear list of excluded assets and how new assets, pensions and the home are treated; (5) what happens on death as well as divorce; (6) a clause on changes (which must also be in writing and approved). An unapproved agreement is vulnerable: courts have sometimes given effect to it between the spouses themselves, but you should not rely on that.

## Worked example

Yossi and Michal Peretz from Ashdod, Jewish, married in 2009. Their apartment (bought in 2012) is registered to both; Yossi's business, opened in 2015, is registered to him alone. Michal received ₪300,000 from her late father's estate in 2020 and put it into the joint mortgage. Yossi moved out in March 2025; no proceedings yet.

1. **Default regime.** No agreement, so the balance of resources applies (s. 3).
2. **Early balance.** They have lived apart since March 2025. Nine months of separation within a year had passed by December 2025, so Michal can file for an early balance now (s. 5א(א)(2)).
3. **The inheritance.** Excluded in principle (s. 5(א)(1)), but she mixed it into the joint mortgage. She will argue it should be credited to her; Yossi will argue it became a shared contribution. The outcome depends on evidence of intention.
4. **The business.** Balanceable (acquired during the marriage). The court will try to avoid destroying it (s. 6(ד)(2)) by ordering a payment over time with security.
5. **The home.** With minor children living with Michal, the court will not order a sale until suitable housing is arranged (s. 6א).
6. **Protection.** If Yossi starts moving money out of the business, Michal can ask for s. 11 measures.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes, in law: you may file a balance-of-resources claim, a request for approval of your own property agreement, or a s. 11 request in person. In practice, property cases often require valuations, actuaries and cross-examination of experts, and many self-represented spouses take advice on strategy even if they appear alone. A lawyer may not draft an agreement for both spouses as if representing both; each spouse should get their own advice.

### Where and how

- The Family Court of the spouses' last joint residence (reg. 6), after the request to settle (lesson 18.9).
- Approval of an agreement: a request to the Family Court with both spouses attending (reg. 43: the judge explains the agreement and checks free consent); or the competent religious court.
- Fees and waivers: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Free help: the assistance unit (mediation), Legal Aid for eligible applicants.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- There is a business, real estate abroad, crypto-assets, or suspected hidden assets.
- Pensions are already being paid, or one spouse is a civil servant with a budgetary pension.
- The other spouse has a lawyer and has made an offer with a short deadline.
- A get or religious divorce is being linked to property concessions.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Signing a property agreement and never getting it approved or certified | It may not bind against creditors or in court | Apply for approval jointly | Approve or certify at signing (s. 2) |
| Mixing an inheritance into joint accounts | It may lose its excluded status | Gather evidence of its source and intention | Keep excluded money separate, or record the agreement in writing |
| Forgetting pensions and study funds | The largest asset is left out | Ask for disclosure and an actuarial report | List every fund in the particulars form |
| Waiting years for a divorce to realise the balance | Assets are depleted | File under s. 5א | Check the nine-month and one-year routes |
| Moving assets after separation | The court treats them as still yours (s. 7) and may draw adverse inferences | Restore and disclose | Do not dispose of assets during the dispute |

## Review

- For marriages from 1974, the default is the balance of resources: separate property during the marriage, half of the value of balanceable assets (including pensions) at the end (ss. 3–5).
- Excluded: pre-marriage assets, gifts and inheritances, NI benefits and injury compensation, and assets excluded in writing (s. 5(א)).
- Since 2008 the balance can be brought forward (s. 5א), and the court has discretion to depart from equal division in special circumstances (s. 8); the family home is protected while minor children need it (s. 6א).
- A property agreement must be in writing and approved by a court or, before the wedding, certified by the registrar or a notary (ss. 1–2).
- Religious courts must apply these rules unless both spouses consent otherwise before them (s. 13).
