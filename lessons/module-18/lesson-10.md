# 18.10 · Practice lab: a property-agreement checklist and a protection-order request

In this lab you do two pieces of work that people most often need to do quickly and get right: reviewing a property agreement before signing it and taking it to approval, and preparing a protection-order request. Both are dry runs on a supplied case or, for the agreement, your own situation. The protection-order part uses a supplied case only.

> [!WARNING]
> If you or someone else is in danger now, call the police on **100**. The Welfare Ministry's domestic-violence hotline is **118**. Do not use a classroom exercise to plan your response to a current threat: get help first. The protection-order part of this lab uses an invented case.

## Why it matters

A property agreement is signed once and can decide the division of a home and pensions decades later. Most problems come from what is missing (pensions, the home, death) or from how it was signed (no approval, no independent understanding). A protection-order request, by contrast, is written under pressure; a clear, dated, factual request is what lets a judge act the same day and confirm the order at the hearing within seven days.

## The law

The rules were covered in lessons [18.4](lesson-04.md) and [18.7](lesson-07.md). The provisions you will check against:

> (ב) האישור לא יינתן אלא לאחר שנוכח בית המשפט או בית הדין שבני הזוג עשו את ההסכם או את השינוי בהסכמה חפשית ובהבינם את משמעותו ואת תוצאותיו.

*Gist:* Approval only after the court is satisfied of free consent and understanding. [Property Relations Law, s. 2(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_יחסי_ממון_בין_בני_זוג#סעיף_2)

> (א) [...] יסביר בית המשפט לבעלי הדין, לפני אישור ההסכם, את משמעות הוראות ההסכם, ויברר שהם מבינים את הוראותיו ואת תוצאותיו וערכו אותו בהסכמה חופשית [...]

*Gist:* Before approving a family agreement the judge explains it and checks understanding and free consent; an agreement about a child is approved only if it is in the child's interest. [Family Court Procedure Regulations, reg. 43](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_בית_המשפט_לענייני_משפחה_(סדרי_דין)#תקנה_43)

> (א) בית המשפט רשאי לתת צו הגנה במעמד צד אחד; ניתן צו במעמד צד אחד יתקיים הדיון בנוכחות שני הצדדים בהקדם האפשרי ולא יאוחר משבעה ימים מיום מתן הצו.

*Gist:* Ex parte order possible; hearing with both parties within seven days. [Prevention of Family Violence Law, s. 4(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_למניעת_אלימות_במשפחה#סעיף_4)

## Leading cases

### Key text: the statutes and reg. 43

Both tasks are governed by statute and procedure rather than by a leading case. For the agreement, reread ss. 1–9 and 13 of the Property Relations Law and reg. 43; for the order, ss. 2–5 of the Prevention of Family Violence Law. Leading cases on property (Bavli, Yaakobi) and on cohabitants (2478/14) are in lessons 18.1, 18.3 and 18.4.

## How it works in practice

- **Approval of a property agreement:** a joint request to the Family Court (or the competent religious court) with the agreement attached and the statement of particulars (Form 3, reg. 12(א)); both spouses attend; the judge explains and asks questions; the approval is written on the agreement or given as a judgment (Family Court Law s. 3(ג)). Before the wedding, certification by a notary or the marriage registrar is an alternative (s. 2(ג), (ג1)). Fee: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- **Protection order:** a request to any magistrates' court or Family Court, no fee (Fees Regulations reg. 7(1)), no request to settle (s. 4א of the Dispute Resolution Law); often decided ex parte the same day; hearing within seven days.

## Worked example

**Agreement.** Daniel (a software engineer with stock options) and Maya (a teacher who owns an apartment inherited from her grandmother) plan to marry in the spring. Daniel's mother has offered to help with a deposit on a new home. Their draft agreement, from a template online, says: "כל אחד מבני הזוג יישאר הבעלים הבלעדי של רכושו." Reviewing it:

1. It excludes everything, including pensions and Daniel's options, which neither may have intended. They need a list of excluded assets and a rule for assets acquired during the marriage.
2. It says nothing about the new home: whose name, what share, what if Daniel's mother's money is a gift to her son only.
3. It says nothing about death; under s. 5(א) the balance also applies on death, unless the agreement says otherwise.
4. It is not certified; they should have it certified by a notary or by the marriage registrar before the wedding (s. 2(ג), (ג1)), or approved by the Family Court.
5. Each should understand it independently; if one lawyer drafted it, the other should get separate advice.

**Protection order.** Supplied case: see "Steps", part B.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- The [property-agreement checklist](../../templates/property-agreement-checklist.md) and the [protection-order request](../../templates/protection-order-request.md) (drafts).
- Lessons 18.3, 18.4 and 18.7.
- 150 minutes: 75 for each part.

### Steps

**Part A: the property agreement (75 minutes)**

1. Use your own agreement or draft, the Daniel and Maya draft above, or a cohabitation agreement outline from lesson 18.3.
2. Go through the checklist section by section. For each item, write: covered / missing / unclear, and the section of the law it relates to.
3. Rewrite the three weakest clauses. Use plain Hebrew; define assets by name, registration number or account.
4. Decide the approval route: notary or registrar (before the wedding), Family Court or religious court (any time). Write why.
5. Prepare for the approval hearing: write the five questions the judge is likely to ask under reg. 43 ("Do you understand that you waive half of the pension?") and each spouse's honest answer.

**Part B: the protection-order request (75 minutes)**

Supplied case: Rana, 34, from Lod, lives with her husband Samer and their sons aged 6 and 10 in an apartment registered to both. For a year Samer has controlled her phone and money and threatened to "take the boys to his family and you'll never see them". On Friday he slapped her and broke the bedroom door; the boys saw it. She went to her sister's. Samer works as a security guard and carries a firearm at work. Rana filed a police complaint on Saturday (reference number given).

6. Identify the grounds under s. 3 that apply, with one sentence each.
7. List the orders under s. 2(א) to request, with subsection, places and distances (home, workplace, the boys' school), plus the weapon (s. 2ב).
8. Fill in the request template: parties, relationship (s. 1), the orders, and a numbered, dated chronology in short paragraphs. No adjectives, no conclusions.
9. Write the evidence list: police complaint number, photos of the door, messages, the sister as a witness, any medical record.
10. Address the children: what Rana asks about contact, and what the court must consider (s. 2(ז)–(ח)).
11. Write a one-page plan for the hearing within seven days: the date, who comes, documents, the three questions she expects from the judge and from Samer, and her answers.
12. Write Samer's side: what he might argue (for example, that the dispute is about custody, s. 11), and how the court will test it. This is part of preparing honestly.

### How you know it worked

- Every checklist item has a status and a section; your rewritten clauses name specific assets and cover death as well as divorce.
- Your approval route is lawful for your timing (before or after the wedding).
- The protection-order request ties each order to a subsection of s. 2 and a ground in s. 3, and its chronology is dated and factual.
- Your hearing plan includes the seven-day deadline and the weapon issue.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes, for your own matters. Spouses may write and sign their own property agreement and apply jointly for approval; the judge's explanation under reg. 43 is designed for unrepresented parties. Protection-order requests are routinely filed in person. You may not draft an agreement for both spouses as their adviser, or a request for another person, unless you are a lawyer (Bar Association Law s. 20); a social worker or police prosecutor may apply for a family member under s. 3.

### Where and how

- Approval: the Family Court or the competent religious court; certification before the wedding: a notary or the marriage registrar.
- Protection order: any magistrates' court or Family Court; police 100; hotline 118.
- Fees and periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md). Free help: [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The agreement covers a business, options, real estate abroad or a pension in payment, or one spouse has much more knowledge of the assets than the other.
- One spouse is pressing the other to sign quickly before the wedding.
- In the protection-order case: criminal charges, a firearm used at work, or a parallel custody or property case with a represented respondent.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| A one-line "separation of property" agreement | Unintended loss of pension or home rights; disputes over meaning | Amend in writing and approve again | List assets and rules; use the checklist |
| Signing the agreement a week before the wedding under pressure | Later challenge for lack of free consent | Approve after the wedding with time to reflect | Sign months ahead, with independent advice |
| No clause on death | The balance on death applies by default (s. 5) | Amend and approve | Cover death explicitly, alongside wills |
| A protection-order request written as an essay | The judge cannot see the grounds | Restructure into dated paragraphs | Use the template's chronology |
| Forgetting the weapon | Danger continues | Ask the court to apply s. 2ב at once | Always state whether the respondent has a weapon |

## Review

- A property agreement must be in writing and approved by a court, or certified before the wedding by a notary or the marriage registrar (ss. 1–2); the judge checks understanding and free consent (reg. 43).
- A good agreement lists assets, rules for new assets, debts, the home, pensions and death, and is signed with time and independent advice.
- A protection-order request is fee-free, needs no request to settle, ties each order to s. 2 and each ground to s. 3, and is factual and dated.
- An ex parte order is followed by a hearing within seven days; weapons are banned automatically; the court weighs children's contact separately.
