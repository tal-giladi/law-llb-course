# 16.8 · Practice lab: register a sole-shareholder company and read company filings

In this lab you prepare, field by field, the online registration of a company that you would own and manage alone, and then you read a company's public filings the way a careful supplier, landlord or investor does. Both are dry runs: you will not pay a fee or submit anything unless you decide to, in your own name.

## Why it matters

Lessons 16.2 and 16.5 gave you the law: what the Registrar needs, what the articles say, why creditors check a company before giving credit. In practice it comes down to two short tasks. Founders who prepare the registration properly avoid weeks of corrections and a set of articles they did not mean. Anyone who deals with a company and spends a few shekels on its extract learns who owns and runs it, whether it is up to date with the Registrar, and whether its assets are already charged to a bank.

## The law

- **Online registration.** A shareholder or a lawyer may file online; a sole shareholder who is also the sole director approves the articles and both declarations himself after secure identification (Regulations regs 16א, 16ב(ג)); filing online exempts him from signature verification (reg. 16א(ב1)). [Regulations](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_החברות_(דיווח,_פרטי_רישום_וטפסים))
- **Public registers.** The Registrar's registers are open to the public; anyone may inspect them and get certified copies (Companies Law s. 43(א)). A certified copy of a document held by the Registrar is admissible as an original (s. 41). But the mere registration of a document is not proof that anyone knew its contents (s. 42).
- **What the Registrar holds.** Registration details, the articles and amendments, directors and changes (s. 223), allotments and transfers of shares (ss. 140, 292), the registered office and digital address (ss. 123–123א), annual reports (s. 141), charges (under the surviving parts of the Companies Ordinance, s. 367(א)(1)).
- **"Violating company".** A company that has not paid fees or filed annual reports is marked, after a 30-day warning, as a violating company (s. 362א).
- **Fees.** Registration (lower online), annual fee (reduced if paid by the end of March), extract and scanned-file inspection online, a copy of a specific document, and a reverse search priced as an extract (Fees Regulations, Schedule; amounts on [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).

## How it works in practice

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### What an extract shows and what it means

| Part of the extract | What to check | Red flag |
|---|---|---|
| Name, number, type, date of incorporation | Exact name and number match the contract and invoices | A different number on the invoice; a company formed last month with the same name as an older one |
| Status | Active | "מפרת חוק" (fees or reports unpaid, s. 362א), "בפירוק", "מחוקה" |
| Annual fee and last annual report | Paid and filed for recent years | Several years unpaid |
| Registered office | A real address | Office "c/o" an accountant only, with no business there (common, but ask) |
| Shareholders and shares | Who owns it; recent transfers | All shares transferred last month to an unknown person |
| Directors | Who manages; recent changes | All directors replaced recently; a director who is also director of many struck-off companies (reverse search) |
| Charges | Bank charges over all assets (floating charge) | You are an unsecured creditor behind the bank |
| Capital | Registered and issued capital | Issued capital of ₪100 for a company asking for large credit |

### Beyond the extract

- **Scanned file**: articles (pre-emption, transfer restrictions, exemption clauses), amendments, the charge documents.
- **Court records** (Net HaMishpat search by company name, where available to the public), execution files, published insolvency proceedings.
- **The company itself**: ask for the latest financial statements, a guarantee from the shareholders, or security.

## Worked example

Samir Haddad, a building supplier in Nazareth, is asked for ₪250,000 of credit by "Galil Towers Ltd". He orders the extract online.

1. **Status.** Active, annual fee paid for 2026. Good.
2. **Shareholders.** Two weeks ago all shares moved from Fadi Zoabi to "S.R. Holdings Ltd", formed a month ago. Directors replaced the same day. Samir asks who stands behind S.R. Holdings and orders its extract too.
3. **Charges.** A floating charge to a bank over all assets since 2022 and a fixed charge on equipment. Samir would be an unsecured creditor behind the bank.
4. **Capital.** Issued capital ₪1,000.
5. **Decision.** Samir offers credit of ₪60,000 only, against a personal guarantee from the new controlling shareholder and post-dated cheques, and asks for the latest audited statements before any increase. He records the extract's date in his file, because if things go wrong he may need to show what he knew (lesson 16.5).

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A computer with access to the Corporations Authority's online services (extract ordering) and, for the registration dry run, the [company registration checklist (draft)](../../templates/company-registration-checklist.md).
- A company you deal with or plan to deal with (your employer, landlord, a supplier, a start-up you might join), or the supplied extract below.
- About two and a half hours.

### Part A: dry run of a sole-shareholder registration (75 min)

1. **Facts (10 min).** Decide the business you would put in the company (your freelance work, a small online shop). You will be the only shareholder and director.
2. **Name search (10 min).** Choose a name and two alternatives; search the company registry and the trade-mark database; note any similar names (s. 27).
3. **Fill the checklist (25 min).** Every field: name with בע״מ, objects (s. 32), registered and issued capital and par value (ss. 33–34), limited liability (s. 35), shareholder details and number of shares, director details, registered office, digital address (s. 123א), email for the filer (reg. 16א(ה)).
4. **Articles decisions (15 min).** Mark the clauses you will keep or change: annual meeting (s. 61), exemption, indemnity and insurance (ss. 259–261), share transfer restrictions, donations (s. 11).
5. **Post-registration plan (15 min).** Bank account, VAT as licensed dealer (always, for a company: VAT Registration Regulations reg. 13(7)), income-tax file, registers at the registered office (ss. 124, 127, 224), calendar of annual fee, annual report, financial statements and auditor (ss. 141, 154, 172).

Stop before the payment screen, unless you have decided to register.

### Part B: read a company's filings (60 min)

1. **Order the extract (10 min)** of a real company, or use the supplied one below.
2. **Read it line by line (20 min)** on the table above: for each entry, write what it means and whether it is a red flag.
3. **Scanned file (15 min, optional, small fee).** Read the articles: is there a right of first refusal? an exemption for directors? a non-standard majority?
4. **Questions and protections (15 min).** Write five questions for the company and three protections for the deal (guarantee, security, payment terms).

- Part A: every field in the checklist is filled and every articles decision cites a section.

- **Status "מפרת חוק"** since a warning in 05/2026: three years of unpaid annual fees and no annual report since 2023 (s. 362א). The Registrar may refuse to register changes and charges; it signals poor administration or financial trouble.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. A sole shareholder who is also the sole director may register the company online himself (reg. 16ב(ג)); anyone may order extracts and scanned files (s. 43). Where founders include foreign residents or companies, the online supporting documents must be filed by a lawyer (reg. 16א(ד)).

### Where and how

- Corporations Authority online services: registration, extracts, scanned files, reverse search (⚠️ manual check of the current service names and screens on gov.il).
- Fees: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Free help: the Corporations Authority's information line (⚠️ manual check).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The extract shows recent transfers of control, insolvency or a struck-off status and you are owed significant money.
- You are about to invest in or buy shares of the company.
- You need a charge registered over the company's assets in your favour.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contracting with a company without checking its extract | Credit to a violating or empty company | Ask for a guarantee before more credit | Order the extract before the first delivery |
| Reading only the shareholders, not the charges | Unsecured behind the bank | Take your own security or guarantee | Read every section |
| Filing the registration with a name too close to another company | Refusal or a change order | Change the name (s. 31) | Search the registry and trade marks first |
| Forgetting the post-registration steps | Late VAT registration, missing registers | Register and set up registers now | Use the 30-day plan |
| Ignoring the annual fee | Violating-company status | Pay with arrears | Calendar each January |

## Review

- A sole shareholder-director can register a company online alone; other founders must each approve online.
- The company's public file shows status, shareholders, directors, charges, capital and compliance; anyone may inspect it (s. 43).
- "Violating company" status signals unpaid fees or missing annual reports (s. 362א).
- Red flags call for protections: guarantees, security, payment terms.
