# 16.2 · Incorporation: the Companies Law 1999, the articles and the Companies Registrar

By the end of this lesson you will know exactly what it takes to bring an Israeli company into existence: the application, the articles (תקנון), the declarations of the first shareholders and directors, the fee, and who may file online. You will also know what the articles legally are, how they can be changed, and what happens to contracts signed before the company exists.

## Why it matters

Most people who "open a company" pay a lawyer or accountant for a package and never read the articles they signed. That is fine until the founders fall out, an investor arrives, or one founder wants to sell. Then the articles, which the law treats as a contract between the company and every shareholder, decide who can block what. Since the 2015 and 2021 amendments to the registration regulations, a shareholder can file the registration online without a lawyer verifying the signatures, so understanding the articles is now also a practical skill: if you file yourself, you choose the clauses.

## The law

### The application

> המבקש לרשום חברה יגיש לרשם בקשה בדרך של תקשורת אלקטרונית לפי טופס שקבע השר ולה יצורפו: (1) עותק של התקנון; (2) הצהרה של הדירקטורים הראשונים על נכונותם לכהן כדירקטורים [...]

*Gist:* Registration is applied for electronically, on the Minister's form, with the articles and the first directors' declarations attached. [Companies Law s. 8](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החברות#סעיף_8) A registration fee is paid on filing and an annual fee every year (s. 9). The Registrar registers the company if all requirements are met, gives it a number and a certificate of incorporation, which is conclusive evidence that the registration requirements were met but does not cure a defect in the articles (s. 10). From registration the company must have a registered office in Israel and a digital address, both notified with the application (ss. 123, 123א).

The regulations add the details: the form includes a declaration by each first shareholder; the signatures on the forms are verified by a lawyer who warned the signer (reg. 1(א), (ג)); the articles may be in English (reg. 1(ד)). The online route replaces the lawyer's verification with secure electronic identification:

> בקשה מקוונת לרישום חברה רשאי להגיש אחד מאלה בלבד: (1) בעל מניות; (2) עורך דין.

*Gist:* Only a shareholder or a lawyer may file the online application. [Regulations, reg. 16ב(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_החברות_(דיווח,_פרטי_רישום_וטפסים)) When a shareholder files, each first shareholder (and director) must identify electronically and approve the articles and his declaration online (reg. 16ב(ב)); when the filer is the sole shareholder and also the sole director, he approves everything himself (reg. 16ב(ג)). An online filer is exempt from signing and from verification (reg. 16א(ב1)), but documents about foreign residents or foreign companies can be filed online only by a lawyer (reg. 16א(ד)). The Companies Law itself allows the identity of those signing the articles to be verified by a lawyer *or online as set by regulations* (s. 23(ב)).

> [!NOTE]
> The regulations as consolidated on Wikisource allow a shareholder to file online even when there are several founders, provided each identifies and approves online. Whether the Ministry of Justice's online service currently supports every such case (for example, several founders or a corporate shareholder) is ⚠️ a manual check on gov.il.

### What the articles must contain

> תקנון החברה יכלול את הפרטים הבאים: (1) שם החברה; (2) מטרות החברה; (3) פרטים בדבר הון המניות הרשום [...]; (4) פרטים בדבר הגבלת האחריות [...]

*Gist:* The articles must state the name, objects, registered share capital and limitation of liability. [Companies Law s. 18](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החברות#סעיף_18) Anything else about shareholders' rights, management and the number of directors is optional (s. 19). The objects may be "any lawful business", any lawful business except listed ones, or listed businesses only (s. 32). The articles state the registered capital and the number of shares of each class (s. 33); shares are all with par value or all without (s. 34). The first shareholders sign the articles with their shares, names, addresses and ID numbers (s. 23(א)).

### The name

A company may choose any name subject to the rules (s. 25), must end a limited company's name with "בע״מ" (s. 26), and may not use a name that is the same as or misleadingly similar to a registered body's name or a registered trade mark in a similar field, or that the Registrar thinks misleading (s. 27) or offensive to public policy (s. 28). The Registrar can order a change (s. 29); the court can stop someone using a company's name (s. 30).

### The articles are a contract

> דין התקנון כדין חוזה בין החברה ובין בעלי מניותיה ובינם לבין עצמם.

*Gist:* The articles are a contract between the company and its shareholders and between the shareholders themselves; they are amended only in the ways set by the law. [Companies Law s. 17](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החברות#סעיף_17)

Amendment is by an ordinary majority of the general meeting unless the articles require another majority (s. 20(א)); a clause requiring a special majority can itself be changed only by that majority (s. 20(ב)); a change that harms a class of shares needs the class meeting's approval (s. 20(ג)); and no amendment can force a shareholder to buy more shares or increase his liability without his consent (s. 20(ד)). The company may entrench clauses by a general-meeting resolution (s. 22). An amendment takes effect on the resolution (s. 21(א)) and is reported to the Registrar within fourteen days (s. 21(ב)); changes of name and objects take effect only on registration (s. 40).

### Contracts made before incorporation

> חברה רשאית לאשר פעולה של יזם שנעשתה בשמה או במקומה לפני התאגדותה.

*Gist:* A company may ratify an act its promoter did in its name before it was incorporated; ratification works retroactively but does not harm rights acquired in good faith and for value before it. [Companies Law s. 12](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החברות#סעיף_12) If the other party knew of the promotion, he may treat the promoter as his counterparty or withdraw and claim damages from him if the company did not ratify within a year, or within 30 days of his demand, or it is clear the company will not be formed (s. 13). If he did not know, the promoter is bound; after ratification the company and the promoter are both bound, jointly and severally, and only the company is entitled (s. 14).

## Leading cases

### ע״א 54/96 הולנדר נ׳ המימד החדש תוכנה בע״מ, פ״ד נב(5) 673 (1998)

As summarised by Fiegenbaum and Licht, the Supreme Court refused to give evidentiary weight to the alleged intent behind a shareholders' agreement when interpreting a listed company's articles. Why it matters: articles are a contract (s. 17), but a special one. Investors and creditors rely on the registered text, so courts read the articles of a public company mainly by their wording and less by the founders' private understandings; in a small private company with two founders, the parties' intent carries more weight under the general rule of contract interpretation ([lesson 03.7](../module-03/lesson-07.md)). Free summary: [Fiegenbaum & Licht, n. 14](https://www.ecgi.global/sites/default/files/working_papers/documents/finalfiegenbaumlicht.pdf). The Hebrew full text was not available on a free host we could open (⚠️ unconfirmed Hebrew title).

### Key text: the "default rules" design of the Companies Law

Most of the Companies Law is a set of default rules ("אלא אם כן נקבע אחרת בתקנון") that the articles may change, plus mandatory rules that protect creditors, the public or minority shareholders (for example s. 57 on the general meeting's powers, s. 58 forbids contracting out). The articles you file decide, among other things, whether there is a right of first refusal on share transfers, whether the annual meeting can be skipped (s. 61), and whether officers can be exempted, indemnified or insured (ss. 259–261). Read the default rules first, then decide what to change.

## How it works in practice

### Online registration step by step (dry-run level)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

1. **Choose and check the name.** Search the Corporations Authority registry and the Israel Patent Office trade-mark database for identical or similar names (s. 27).
2. **Prepare the details.** For each first shareholder and director: full name, ID number, address, number and class of shares. The registered office address and the digital address. The objects clause.
3. **Choose the articles.** The online service offers a form of articles to fill (reg. 16ב(ב)(1)); you may attach your own if the Registrar allows. Decide on the four mandatory items (s. 18) and on the optional clauses (lesson 16.9).
4. **Identify and file.** Log in with the national secure identification (הזדהות לאומית) and fill the form (reg. 16א(ב)). If there are other founders, each must log in and approve (reg. 16ב(ב)).
5. **Pay.** The online registration fee is lower than the paper fee ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)). No annual fee is due for the year of registration (Fees Regulations reg. 5(ב)).
6. **Receive the certificate.** The Registrar issues a company number and certificate of incorporation (s. 10). Then open a bank account, register for VAT and income tax as a company (a company is always a licensed dealer, VAT Registration Regulations reg. 13(7)), and set up the shareholders' and directors' registers at the registered office (ss. 124, 127, 224).

### After registration: the reporting calendar

| Event | Report to the Registrar | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Amendment of articles | Within 14 days | s. 21(ב) |
| Change of registered office or digital address | Within 14 days | ss. 123(ב), 123א(ב) |
| Appointment or end of a director | Within 14 days | s. 223 |
| Allotment of shares | Within 14 days | s. 292 |
| Transfer of shares | Within 14 days of the transfer | s. 140(6) |
| Annual report | Once a year, within 14 days after the annual meeting or after sending the financial statements | s. 141 |
| Annual fee | Each year; reduced if paid early | Fees Regulations reg. 5 |

A company that does not pay the annual fee or file its annual report can be registered as a "violating company" (חברה מפרה) after a 30-day warning; the Registrar may then refuse to register changes and charges, and refuse to register new companies controlled by its controlling shareholder (s. 362א).

## Worked example

Rina Abergel, a graphic designer in Be'er Sheva, works as a sole trader. A large client will sign a two-year contract only with a company. She will be the only shareholder and director.

1. **Who may file.** As the sole shareholder and sole director, Rina may file the online application herself and approve the articles and both declarations (reg. 16ב(ג)). No lawyer's verification is needed (reg. 16א(ב1)).
2. **Name.** "רינה עיצובים בע״מ". A search shows "רינה עיצוב גרפי בע״מ" already registered: misleadingly similar (s. 27(א)(1)). She chooses "אברג׳ל סטודיו בע״מ" and checks the trade-mark register.
3. **Articles.** Objects: any lawful business (s. 32(1)). Capital: 10,000 ordinary shares without par value (ss. 33–34). Liability: limited (s. 35). She keeps the default rules but adds a clause allowing the company not to hold an annual meeting (s. 61) and an exemption and indemnity clause (ss. 259–260).
4. **The pre-incorporation contract.** The client sends the contract before the company exists. Rina signs "בשם חברה בייסוד". The client knows of the promotion: if the company does not ratify within a year the client may hold Rina to the contract (s. 13). She ratifies by a director's resolution on the day the company is registered.
5. **After registration.** She opens a company bank account, registers the company for VAT (always a licensed dealer) and income tax, keeps the registers at her studio address, and puts the annual fee and annual report in her calendar. Her sole-trader files will be closed or kept for other work (lesson 16.7).

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A business you run or plan (or the Abergel case).
- The [company registration checklist (draft)](../../templates/company-registration-checklist.md).
- The Companies Law ss. 8–35 open on Wikisource.
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Fill the checklist (15 min).** Name (with two alternatives and a search note), objects, capital and classes, liability, shareholders with shares, directors, registered office, digital address.
2. **Draft the four mandatory clauses in Hebrew (15 min).** One short paragraph each (s. 18).
3. **Mark the defaults you would change (10 min).** Go through ss. 61, 63, 67, 78, 83, 259–261, 290 and write "default" or your change and why.
4. **Plan the first 30 days (5 min).** Bank, VAT, income tax, registers, calendar of reports.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes, for your own company: a shareholder may file the online application (reg. 16ב), and a sole shareholder who is also the sole director approves everything alone. With several founders each must identify and approve online; foreign-resident founders or a foreign corporate shareholder require a lawyer to file the supporting documents (reg. 16א(ד)). Drafting articles or registering companies for others as an occupation is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).

### Where and how

- Ministry of Justice, Corporations Authority, online company registration (⚠️ manual check: https://www.gov.il/he/service/company_registration).
- Fee: online registration fee; annual fee from the next year ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- Free help: the Corporations Authority's call centre and online guides (⚠️ manual check); [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- There are several founders with different contributions, vesting or investor rights (lesson 16.9).
- A founder is a foreign resident or a foreign company, or shares are held in trust.
- The company will be regulated (finance, insurance, health) or needs special share classes.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name too close to an existing company or trade mark | Refusal, a change order (s. 29) or an injunction (s. 30) | Change the name (s. 31) | Search both registries first |
| Signing a contract for a company not yet formed without saying so | You are personally bound (s. 14) | Ratify after incorporation; ask for novation | Sign "חברה בייסוד" and ratify promptly |
| Copying generic articles without reading them | Defaults you did not want (pre-emption, quorum, exemption) | Amend by general-meeting resolution and report in 14 days (ss. 20–21) | Go through the defaults clause by clause |
| Not paying the annual fee | "Violating company" status; changes refused (s. 362א) | Pay with interest and file missing reports | Put fee and report in the calendar |
| Forgetting to report a new director or share transfer | Registry out of date; fines | File the late report | Report every change within 14 days |

## Review

- Registration is online, with the articles and first directors' declarations (s. 8); a shareholder or a lawyer may file online (reg. 16ב).
- The articles must state the name, objects, registered capital and limitation of liability (s. 18); the rest is optional.
- The articles are a contract between company and shareholders and among shareholders (s. 17); amendment by ordinary majority unless the articles say otherwise (s. 20).
- A company may ratify a promoter's pre-incorporation contract; until then the promoter may be bound (ss. 12–14).
- After registration, every change goes to the Registrar within 14 days; unpaid fees lead to "violating company" status (s. 362א).
