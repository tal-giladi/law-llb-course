# 16.7 · The sole trader and partnerships: registration, VAT and liability

By the end of this lesson you will be able to open a business in your own name correctly: decide between exempt and licensed dealer status for VAT, open the files at the Tax Authority and National Insurance, and know the reports that follow. You will also know what changes when two people do business together as a partnership, and you will draft the clauses of a partnership agreement.

## Why it matters

Most Israeli businesses start as a single person: a freelancer, a tutor, a plumber, an online seller. The legal form is simple (there is no separate person; the owner *is* the business), but the registration duties are not, and mistakes are expensive: issuing invoices without being registered, staying an exempt dealer after passing the ceiling, or never opening a National Insurance file and later finding no entitlement to benefits. When a second person joins, the business becomes a partnership whether or not anyone calls it that, with joint and several liability. Knowing the rules lets you make a deliberate choice, and recognise when it is time to move to a company (lesson 16.2).

## The law

### VAT: who is an exempt dealer

> ”עוסק פטור“ – עוסק שמחזור העסקאות שלו בכל עסקיו אינו עולה על 120,000 שקלים חדשים לשנה [...] או על סכום גבוה יותר שקבע שר האוצר;

*Gist:* An exempt dealer is a dealer whose turnover from all his businesses does not exceed the statutory annual ceiling, which is linked to the index every 1 January. [VAT Law s. 1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מס_ערך_מוסף#סעיף_1) The Wikisource consolidation notes the ceiling for 2026 ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)); s. 126(א) adjusts it every 1 January to the consumer price index. An exempt dealer's transactions are exempt from VAT, except sales of land and of equipment for which input tax was deducted (s. 31(3)), so he charges no VAT and deducts none.

Some dealers must be licensed dealers whatever their turnover:

> עוסק הנמנה על אחת מהקבוצות המנויות להלן ירשום אותו המנהל כעוסק מורשה גם אם על פי סכום מחזור עסקותיו [...] הוא היה נחשב כעוסק זעיר: (1) בעל מקצוע חפשי שהוא: אגרונום, אדריכל, הנדסאי, [...] עורך דין, רואה חשבון או שמאי [...]

*Gist:* Listed professionals (among them architects, engineers, technicians, management and tax consultants, economists, surveyors, bookkeepers, translators, insurance agents, lawyers, accountants and appraisers), doctors and other health professionals, driving schools and certain schools, real-estate and car dealers and brokers, and every company and cooperative society are always registered as licensed dealers. [VAT Registration Regulations reg. 13](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_מס_ערך_מוסף_(רישום))

### VAT: registration and reports

> עוסק, מלכ״ר ומוסד כספי חייבים ברישום, במועד ובדרך שנקבעו.

*Gist:* Dealers must register, at the time and in the manner set. [VAT Law s. 52(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מס_ערך_מוסף#סעיף_52) A person who shows he is setting up a business may register before he starts (s. 52(ב)). The regulations require the registration form no later than the day the business starts, in person or through an accountant, lawyer, tax adviser or bookkeeper (reg. 2(א)); a partnership registers with a representative partner resident in Israel (reg. 4); changes (address, partners, closing the business) are notified within 15 days (reg. 8). An exempt dealer declares his previous year's turnover by 31 January each year (reg. 15). A licensed dealer files periodic reports within 15 days after each period, even if there was no activity, and the period is two months for turnover up to the statutory threshold (s. 67(א2), (ב)). A licensed dealer whose turnover stayed below the ceiling for two consecutive years is moved to exempt status (s. 59(א)).

### Income tax and National Insurance

Every Israeli-resident individual aged 18 or over at the start of the tax year must file an annual income-tax return, subject to the exemptions in the Ordinance and regulations (Income Tax Ordinance s. 131(א)(1); [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_131)). In practice a sole trader opens a file with the assessing officer when he starts, receives advance-payment rates, and files a yearly return with his business accounts (⚠️ manual check of the Tax Authority's current combined online "opening a business" service).

> ”עובד עצמאי“ [...] – מי שעסק באותה תקופה במשלח ידו שלא כעובד [...], והתקיים בו אחד מאלה: (1) הוא עסק במשלח ידו, לפחות עשרים שעות בשבוע בממוצע; (2) הכנסתו החודשית הממוצעת ממשלח ידו לא פחתה מסכום השווה ל־50% מהשכר הממוצע; (3) הוא עסק במשלח ידו לפחות שתים עשרה שעות בשבוע בממוצע והכנסתו החודשית [...] לא פחתה מסכום כאמור בלוח א׳ [...]

*Gist:* For National Insurance a self-employed person works in his occupation not as an employee and meets one of three tests of hours or income. [National Insurance Law s. 1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הביטוח_הלאומי#סעיף_1) A self-employed person registers with National Insurance and pays contributions on his business income; one who does not meet the tests may still have duties as a non-working insured person (⚠️ manual check of the NII's current registration service and rates; module 17 covers benefits).

### Partnerships

> כל שותף הוא שלוח של השותפות ושל שאר שותפיו לכל ענין של עסקי השותפות; ופעולותיו של כל שותף, כשהוא עושה בדרך הרגילה עסק מן הסוג שעושה השותפות [...] יחייבו את השותפות ואת שותפיו [...]

*Gist:* Every partner is the agent of the partnership and of the other partners for its business; his acts in the ordinary course bind them, unless he lacked authority and the other side knew it or did not know or believe he was a partner. [Partnerships Ordinance s. 14](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_השותפויות#סעיף_14)

- **Registration** within one month, by a notice signed by the partners with the name, business, main place of business, each partner's details, the managing partners and the term (ss. 4, 7).
- **Liability.** Jointly and severally for all obligations incurred while a partner, including torts in the ordinary course (ss. 18, 20(א)); a retiring partner stays liable for debts incurred before he left unless the creditors agree otherwise (s. 27).
- **Between partners (defaults).** Share capital, profits and losses in proportion to capital; every partner may take part in management; no salary for work; no new partner without everyone's consent; ordinary matters by majority, a change in the nature of the business only unanimously; every partner may inspect the books (s. 34).
- **Duties.** Partners must run the business for the common benefit, be honest and loyal to each other and give each other true accounts and full information (s. 29); a partner who competes without consent must account for his profits (s. 39).
- **Dissolution.** At the end of a fixed term or single venture, or, for a partnership of indefinite duration, on one partner's notice (s. 41); by court order on grounds such as persistent breach, conduct harming the business, losses, or whenever just and equitable (s. 45).

## Leading cases

### ע״א 2026/92 פקיד שומה נ׳ שדות (20.5.2001)

Two partners in the "Sadot" trucking partnership formed a company, transferred all the partnership's assets and obligations to it, and took its shares in the same proportions. The assessing officer said the transfer of the trucks was a taxable sale. The majority (Justice Englard; Justices Levin and Strasberg-Cohen; President Barak and Justice Or dissenting) held that for tax purposes the transfer of a partnership business to a company is a transfer of each partner's interest in the partnership, not of each asset separately, and dismissed the appeal. Why it matters: moving from a partnership or sole trade to a company is a tax event that needs planning, and the question of what a partner owns (a share in the partnership, or a share in each asset) has real consequences. Read it in English at [Versa](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/income-tax-assessor-v-sadot) (Hebrew party names ⚠️ unconfirmed).

### Key text: Partnerships Ordinance s. 1(ב)

The Ordinance keeps English equity and common law on partnerships in force in Israel where they do not contradict it (s. 1(ב)), one of the few surviving Mandate-era references to English law ([lesson 01.2](../module-01/lesson-02.md)). Courts therefore still draw on English partnership doctrine, for example on partners' fiduciary duties, when the Ordinance is silent.

## How it works in practice

### Opening a sole-trader business: the sequence

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

1. **Decide exempt or licensed.** Estimate yearly turnover; check whether your profession is in reg. 13. If you will sell mainly to businesses that deduct VAT, a licensed dealer status may help them and you (you deduct input VAT); if you sell to private customers below the ceiling, exempt status is simpler.
2. **Open the VAT file** before or on the first day of business (s. 52; reg. 2). The Tax Authority offers online opening of a business file (⚠️ manual check of the current service on gov.il).
3. **Open the income-tax file** with the assessing officer (often in the same process) and receive advance-payment instructions.
4. **Register with National Insurance** as self-employed and set advance contributions.
5. **Bookkeeping.** Keep books under the Income Tax (Bookkeeping) Instructions and issue receipts (exempt dealer) or tax invoices (licensed dealer) for every sale.
6. **Calendar.** Exempt: turnover declaration by 31 January. Licensed: VAT report every one or two months by the 15th. Everyone: annual income-tax return; NI advances.
7. **Changes and closing.** Notify VAT within 15 days of moving, adding a partner or closing (reg. 8); close the income-tax and NI files too.

### When to move to a company

When personal liability becomes serious (employees, leases, product liability), income passes the point where company tax plus dividend tax beats personal marginal rates (ask an accountant), or a partner or investor joins. The transfer of the business to the company has tax consequences (Sadot); plan it with an accountant.

## Worked example

Leah Friedman, from Beitar Illit, starts a home business baking cakes for events, expecting ₪70,000 turnover in the first year. Her neighbour Chani Weiss, a translator, wants to work from home translating legal documents for firms, expecting ₪60,000. After a year Leah's sister Esti joins the bakery and they share profits equally.

1. **Leah, VAT.** Bakery is not in reg. 13 and her turnover is below the ceiling ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)): she may be an exempt dealer. She registers before she starts (s. 52(ב)), issues receipts, and declares her turnover by 31 January (reg. 15). If turnover passes the ceiling she must move to licensed status.
2. **Chani, VAT.** A translator (מתורגמן) is in reg. 13(1): she is registered as a licensed dealer regardless of turnover, charges VAT on her invoices and files periodic reports, bimonthly at her turnover (s. 67(א2)).
3. **Income tax and NI.** Both open income-tax files and register with National Insurance. Leah works about 25 hours a week, so she is "self-employed" under the hours test (NI Law s. 1, para. (1)).
4. **The sisters' bakery.** Once Esti joins and they share profits, they are partners (Ordinance s. 1). They must register the partnership within a month (s. 4) and notify VAT of the change within 15 days (reg. 8); a partnership registers with a representative partner (reg. 4). With combined turnover, they check the ceiling again for the partnership as a whole (s. 1 "בכל עסקיו").
5. **Liability.** If a guest is injured by a cake, both sisters are liable jointly and severally (ss. 18, 20). They buy product-liability insurance and write a partnership agreement: capital, profit split, who decides what, salary (the default is none, s. 34(6)), and exit.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your own planned or existing business (or the Friedman–Weiss case).
- The VAT Registration Regulations, reg. 13, and the [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md) page.
- 60 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Status decision (10 min).** Estimated turnover, profession, customers: exempt or licensed? Write your reasoning with sections.
2. **Registration plan (15 min).** The three files, the date you will open each, what you need (ID, bank details, address, description of the business, estimated turnover), and who will help (yourself or an accountant).
3. **Compliance calendar (10 min).** Every recurring deadline for your status, for one full year.
4. **Partnership agreement outline (20 min).** If you will work with someone: in Hebrew, clauses on name and business, capital contributions, profit and loss shares, management and decisions (majority and unanimous matters), salaries or drawings, non-competition (s. 39), books and information (ss. 29, 34(9)), admission of partners, exit, death or incapacity, dissolution and valuation, disputes (mediation then arbitration).
5. **Company test (5 min).** Write the two events that would make you move to a company.

### Template

None specific; for a company structure use the [founders' agreement checklist (draft)](../../templates/founders-agreement-checklist.md).

### How you know it worked

- Your status decision cites the ceiling and checks reg. 13.
- Your calendar has the 31 January declaration (exempt) or the 15th-of-the-month reports (licensed).
- Your partnership outline changes at least two Ordinance defaults deliberately (for example salary and profit shares).

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Registering your own business for VAT, income tax and National Insurance is done in your own name, online or at the offices; reg. 2(א) allows filing in person. Registering a partnership with the Registrar of Partnerships is done by the partners themselves (s. 7). Representing *others* before the tax authorities is reserved to lawyers, accountants and licensed tax advisers (Bar Association Law ss. 20–21; [can I act without a lawyer?](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md)).

### Where and how

- Tax Authority (רשות המסים): VAT and income-tax files; online business-opening service (⚠️ manual check on gov.il).
- National Insurance Institute: registration as self-employed (⚠️ manual check on btl.gov.il).
- Registrar of Partnerships at the Corporations Authority (⚠️ manual check of the online service and fee).
- Free help: Ma'of small-business centres; Tax Authority call centre; [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer (or accountant) when

- You are not sure whether you are an employee or a contractor for your main client (module 17).
- The partnership will hold land, employ staff, or one partner contributes much more than the other.
- You receive a VAT or income-tax assessment you dispute (year 3, Tax Law).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starting to sell before registering | Offence (reg. 17); provisional registration by the director (s. 54) | Register now; report past sales | Register before the first sale (s. 52(ב)) |
| Staying exempt after passing the ceiling | VAT on past sales from your own pocket | Ask to be registered as licensed; report | Track turnover monthly |
| A translator or engineer registering as exempt | Wrong status (reg. 13) | Re-register as licensed | Check reg. 13 first |
| No NI registration | Debts with interest; gaps in benefit rights | Register and settle arrears | Register when you start |
| Two-person business with no agreement | Defaults apply: equal management, no salary, dissolution by notice (ss. 34, 41) | Sign an agreement now | Write it before you start |

## Review

- An exempt dealer has turnover below the indexed ceiling and charges no VAT; listed professions, companies and cooperatives are always licensed dealers (s. 1; reg. 13).
- Register for VAT no later than the first day of business; exempt dealers declare turnover by 31 January; licensed dealers report every one or two months by the 15th.
- Sole traders also open an income-tax file and register with National Insurance as self-employed (ITO s. 131; NI Law s. 1).
- Partners are each other's agents and liable jointly and severally; registration is due within a month but does not create the partnership (Ordinance ss. 4, 6, 14, 20).
- Partners owe each other loyalty and information; defaults on profits, management and salary apply unless agreed otherwise (ss. 29, 34).
