# 07.1 · How a law is made: government bills, private bills, committees

By the end of this lesson you will be able to take any Israeli bill, say what kind of bill it is, where it stands, who decides its final wording and what a member of the public can still do about it. You will also understand the one situation in which the High Court will strike down a law because of the way it was passed, and why that power is contested.

## Why it matters

Most people meet legislation only after it is in force: a new fee, a new duty, a new right. By then the only options are compliance or litigation. Every law, though, passes through a long public process first, with documents published at each stage and real chances to be heard: the ministry's memorandum, the committee hearings, the reservations before the second reading. A citizen who knows the stages can comment while the text is still soft, ask a committee to hear them, and find, years later, the explanatory notes and protocols that a court will use to interpret the law (lesson 02.4).

The process is also a legal question in itself. A law passed in breach of a basic rule of the process may, in rare cases, be void. And since July 2026 the 25th Knesset has been dissolved, with elections on 27 October 2026: knowing what happens to bills when a Knesset ends is a live, practical issue this month.

## The law

### The Knesset sets its own procedure

> הכנסת תקבע סדרי עבודתה; במידה שסדרי העבודה לא נקבעו בחוק תקבעם הכנסת בתקנון; כל עוד לא נקבעו סדרי העבודה כאמור, תנהג הכנסת לפי הנוהג והנוהל המקובלים בה.

*Gist:* The Knesset sets its own working procedure, in a statute or, if none, in its Rules of Procedure, and otherwise by its accepted practice. [Basic Law: The Knesset s. 19](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק-יסוד:_הכנסת#סעיף_19)

The Rules of Procedure (תקנון הכנסת) are a Knesset resolution, not a statute, but they are the binding code of the legislative process. Section 25 of the Basic Law adds the default voting rule: a majority of those voting, abstentions not counted, unless a law requires a special majority.

### Three readings, and a fourth for private bills

> הצעת חוק תידון בכנסת בשלוש קריאות, והצעת חוק פרטית – גם בדיון המוקדם; חוק יתקבל בקריאה השלישית; הוראות חלק זה יחולו גם על חוקי־יסוד.

*Gist:* Every bill passes three readings, a private bill also a preliminary reading; a law is adopted at the third reading, and the same procedure applies to Basic Laws. [Rules s. 74(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנון_הכנסת#סעיף_74)

Section 74(ב) names the three sources of bills: the Government, a Knesset member who is not a minister or deputy minister, and, on a short list of subjects in s. 80 (Basic Laws, the Knesset, elections, parties, the State Comptroller), certain committees.

### The waiting period for private bills

> הדיון המוקדם לא יתקיים לפני שעברו 45 ימים מיום שהונחה הצעת החוק על שולחן הכנסת [...]

*Gist:* A private bill cannot be voted on in the preliminary reading until 45 days after it was tabled, unless the Government agrees, the Knesset Committee shortens the period or the bill is attached to a similar one. [Rules s. 76(ג)–(ד)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנון_הכנסת#סעיף_76) The period is listed in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### The committee shapes the text

> אושרה הצעת חוק בקריאה הראשונה, תכין אותה הוועדה שאליה הועברה לקריאה השנייה ולקריאה השלישית או תציע לכנסת להסירה מסדר היום [...]

*Gist:* After the first reading the committee prepares the bill for the second and third readings, or proposes to remove it. [Rules s. 84(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנון_הכנסת#סעיף_84)

The committee may amend the bill, even sections of the law not mentioned in it, but not beyond its subject; an MK or minister may raise a "new topic" objection, decided by the Knesset Committee (s. 85). A member whose amendment is rejected may ask to attach it as a reservation (הסתייגות) for a plenum vote at the second reading (s. 86). Only MKs and ministers file reservations; the public's channel is the committee hearing (lesson 07.6).

### What happens when a Knesset ends

> הצעת חוק שכנסת יוצאת העבירה לאחר קריאה ראשונה לאחת מועדותיה, רשאית ממשלה שנתכוננה בכנסת הנכנסת, להודיע במליאת הכנסת על רצונה שיחול עליה דין רציפות [...]

*Gist:* A bill that passed first reading in the outgoing Knesset may be carried into the new Knesset if the new Government (for its bills) or the competent committee (for private and committee bills, with the sponsor's consent if re-elected) asks, and the Knesset approves. [Continuity Law ss. 1–6](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_רציפות_הדיון_בהצעות_חוק)

Bills that did not pass first reading lapse and must be tabled again. Separately, Basic Law: The Knesset s. 38 extends enactments that would expire near the end of a Knesset's term until three months into the new term.

## Leading cases

### בג״ץ 4885/03 ארגון מגדלי העופות בישראל נ׳ ממשלת ישראל (2004)

Chapter 11 of the 2003 Arrangements Law reshaped the regulation of poultry farming and was passed in a rushed process with little debate. The Court sharply criticised the use of the omnibus Arrangements Law for structural reforms unrelated to the budget, but held that Israeli law does not recognise a general ground of "legislative due process". The Court will intervene only for a defect that "goes to the heart of the process" (פגם היורד לשורש ההליך): a severe and substantial violation of the basic principles of the legislative process, chiefly the MKs' ability to participate in it. On the facts there was no such defect, and the petitions on the process were dismissed.

Why it matters: it sets the test every later process challenge uses. Rushing, poor drafting or limited debate are not enough. [English translation](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/210)

### בג״ץ 10042/16 קוונטינסקי נ׳ כנסת ישראל (6.8.2017)

The 2016 Economic Efficiency (Arrangements) Law imposed a tax on owners of three or more apartments. The petitioners argued that the arrangement was approved so hastily that members could not form a view on it. The majority (Justice Sohlberg, joined by President Naor and Justices Hayut and Hendel; Justice Mazuz dissenting) refined the participation principle: the question is no longer only whether members could know what they were voting on, but whether the process let them form a substantive position, if only minimally. Relevant factors include the length of debate relative to the bill's complexity, whether a complex arrangement was debated on its own, whether debate affected the text, the factual basis provided and the time members had the text before the vote. Finding a defect going to the heart of the process, the Court annulled the tax arrangement. It was the first time an Israeli law was struck down because of the way it was passed.

Why it matters: the Poultry Farmers test is not theoretical, and the factors give committees (and anyone watching them) a concrete measure of a fair process. [English summary](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/305)

## How it works in practice

### Government bills: the long road

A government bill starts in a ministry. The Attorney General's guideline 2.3005 sets twelve stages: staff work in the ministry; a memorandum (תזכיר חוק) published for public comment; consideration of comments; the Deputy Attorney General's confirmation that there is no legal obstacle; drafting in the Ministry of Justice; the Ministerial Committee for Legislation; publication in Reshumot (הצעות חוק – הממשלה) and tabling; first reading; committee preparation; second and third readings; publication of the law in Reshumot. The memorandum stage is where a member of the public has the most influence, because nothing has been committed yet (lesson 07.5).

### Private bills: many tabled, few passed

Any MK who is not a minister may table a private bill. The Knesset legal department checks it, the Presidium approves tabling, and the bill waits at least 45 days for a preliminary vote within each faction's quota. Before the vote the Ministerial Committee for Legislation decides the Government's position; in practice that decision, more than the debate, predicts the result. If approved, the bill goes to a committee, which prepares it for first reading and publishes it in Reshumot (הצעות חוק – הכנסת). From then on it follows the same path as a government bill.

### Committee bills

Only the Knesset Committee, the Constitution, Law and Justice Committee and the State Control Committee may initiate bills, and only on the subjects listed in s. 80. These skip the preliminary reading.

### The Arrangements Law

Since 1985 most Governments have passed, with each budget, an omnibus law amending dozens of statutes. Supporters say it is the only way to pass coordinated economic reform in a fragmented Knesset. Critics, including the Court in Poultry Farmers and in Quintinsky, say it lets major policy pass without real committee scrutiny. Recent practice splits non-budget items out to the relevant committees, but the tool remains.

### Where to look

| Stage | Document | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Memorandum | תזכיר חוק, with explanatory notes and comment deadline | [Government legislation site](https://www.tazkirim.gov.il/s/) |
| Tabling and first reading | The bill in Reshumot, with דברי הסבר | Knesset legislation database (main.knesset.gov.il) |
| Committee | Agendas, protocols, background papers, versions | The committee's page on the Knesset site |
| Final law | ספר החוקים | Reshumot; free text on [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/ויקיטקסט:ספר_החוקים_הפתוח) |

### The debate on reviewing the process

The strongest argument for reviewing the process is that a law's democratic legitimacy comes from the process: if members could not know what they were voting on, there was no real decision of the elected body. The strongest argument against is that the Knesset's internal procedure is its own business (Basic Law s. 19), that judges cannot measure "enough debate", and that review invites litigation over every late-night vote. The current law, after Quintinsky, is that review exists but only for defects that go to the heart of the process.

## Worked example

Rivka Ben-Haim, a kindergarten owner in Ashdod, hears that "a new law" will require cameras in every private kindergarten. She wants to know whether it is already law and whether she can still say something.

1. **Find it.** She searches the Knesset legislation database for "מצלמות" and "מעונות". She finds two private bills tabled in the 25th Knesset and a government memorandum on the government legislation site.
2. **Classify.** One private bill passed preliminary reading; the other never came to a vote. The government memorandum closed for comments last year.
3. **Stage.** The approved private bill was sent to the Education Committee but never passed first reading.
4. **Elections.** The 25th Knesset dissolved in July 2026. A bill that did not pass first reading cannot be carried over under the Continuity Law; it lapses. If an MK in the 26th Knesset wants it, it must be tabled again and wait the 45 days.
5. **Action.** Rivka writes a dated note in her file: no binding duty now; watch the government site for a revised memorandum; when a bill reaches a committee, ask to be heard (lesson 07.6).

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A topic that affects you (housing, work, transport, a profession, a benefit).
- Access to the Knesset site and the [government legislation site](https://www.tazkirim.gov.il/s/).
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. Search the Knesset legislation database for two or three keywords of your topic. List every bill you find with its number, type (government, private, committee) and date.
2. For each, record the last stage reached: tabled, preliminary reading, first reading, committee preparation, second and third readings.
3. Open the bill text in Reshumot and read the explanatory notes (דברי הסבר). Write one sentence on its purpose.
4. Find the committee that received it and open one protocol of a hearing on it, if any.
5. Search the government legislation site for a memorandum or draft on the same topic and note its comment deadline.
6. Decide what continuity means for each bill after the 27 October 2026 elections: lapses, or may be carried over under the Continuity Law.
7. Write a five-line "bill status note": bill, type, stage, committee, what happens next and the next point where you could be heard.

### Template

None. Lesson 07.6 adds the [letter to a Knesset committee](../../templates/letter-to-knesset-committee.md) template.

### How you know it worked

- Each bill on your list has a type and a last stage you can show with a document.
- You found the explanatory notes and can state the purpose in your own words.
- Your continuity conclusion matches the Continuity Law: only bills past first reading can be carried over.

### Self-check

- [ ] I can list the stages of a government bill from memorandum to Reshumot.
- [ ] I know why a private bill waits before its preliminary reading and who can shorten that wait.
- [ ] I know that the committee, not the plenum, writes the final wording.
- [ ] I can state the Poultry Farmers test and what made Quintinsky different.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Following legislation, commenting on memoranda and asking to be heard by a committee are open to every person in their own name. Representing someone else's interests before the Knesset for pay is regulated: a paid lobbyist (שדלן) needs a Knesset permit (Knesset Law ss. 65–66); acting for yourself, or for your employer as part of your job, is not lobbying under that definition.

### Where and how

- Bills and stages: the Knesset legislation database; a gov.il service page also explains the search (⚠️ manual check: gov.il blocks automated checks).
- Memoranda: the [government legislation site](https://www.tazkirim.gov.il/s/), free registration.
- Committee contact details: each committee's page on the Knesset site.
- Free help: civil-society legislative clinics at the universities and organisations that follow Knesset work; see [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You want to challenge a law in court because of a defect in its process: the Poultry Farmers test is demanding and HCJ practice is technical.
- You represent an organisation or business before the Knesset for pay, or are being paid to do so: check the lobbyist rules first.
- The bill affects you so seriously (licence, livelihood) that you need a full legal analysis of a draft before it passes.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Treating a tabled private bill as "the new law" | You change your conduct, or panic, for nothing | Check the stage in the database | Always record the last stage reached |
| Missing the memorandum stage | Your concerns arrive when the text is fixed | Write to the committee instead (lesson 07.6) | Watch the government legislation site for your topic |
| Assuming a bill survives elections | You prepare for a hearing that will not happen | Check whether it passed first reading and whether continuity was applied | Apply the Continuity Law test after every election |
| Expecting to file a reservation as a citizen | No such right | Ask an MK to adopt your amendment as a reservation | Know who may act at each stage |
| Arguing in court that the debate was "too short" | Petition dismissed under Poultry Farmers | Identify a defect in members' participation, if any | Learn the "heart of the process" test |

## Review

- Every bill passes three readings; private bills also a preliminary reading after a 45-day wait (Rules ss. 74, 76).
- Government bills begin as ministry memoranda published for public comment and pass through the Ministerial Committee for Legislation.
- The committee shapes the text between first and second readings; reservations and "new topic" objections belong to MKs and ministers.
- Bills past first reading may be carried into a new Knesset under the Continuity Law; others lapse.
- The Court reviews the legislative process only for a defect that goes to the heart of the process (Poultry Farmers), and struck down a law on that ground for the first time in Quintinsky.
