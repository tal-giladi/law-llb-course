# 05.8 · Practice lab: appealing a decision to close a file; the private criminal complaint (קובלנה פלילית)

In this lab you take the two steps open to a victim when the State does not prosecute: an appeal (ערר) against the decision to close the file, and, for a limited list of offences, a private criminal complaint in which you become the prosecutor. You finish with a dated appeal ready to send and, if the offence qualifies, a draft private complaint with an honest risk assessment.

## Why it matters

> [!WARNING]
> This lab is for victims who complained to the police. It is not for suspects. A private criminal complaint makes you a prosecutor against a real person: it can escalate a conflict, and a weak or vindictive complaint can be stayed by the court. Never use criminal proceedings to pressure someone in a civil dispute.

Most police files in Israel are closed without an indictment. A closure is not the end: the complainant has a statutory right to appeal, and a well-founded appeal can send the file back for further investigation or lead to an indictment. The appeal is decided by a more senior authority than the one that closed the file, and the most common reason for success is concrete: the appeal shows an investigative step that was not taken (a camera never collected, a witness never questioned) or evidence that was overlooked.

The private criminal complaint (קובלנה) is an old common-law institution kept in Israeli law for minor offences that mostly affect private interests: neighbours' disputes, threats, minor assaults, privacy and intellectual property. It is rare, demanding and risky, but it exists, and in the right case it is the only way to bring the matter before a criminal court.

## The law

### Who may appeal, and against what

> (א) על החלטה שלא לחקור או שלא להעמיד לדין, משום שנסיבות העניין בכללותן אינן מתאימות לפתיחה בחקירה או להעמדה לדין, לפי העניין, שלא נמצאו ראיות מספיקות או שנקבע שאין אשמה, רשאי המתלונן לערור כלהלן: [...]

*Gist:* The complainant may appeal a decision not to investigate or not to prosecute on any of the three grounds (no public interest, insufficient evidence, no guilt), to the authority that s. 64(א) names: for decisions of the police and police prosecutors, a district attorney or a senior State Attorney's Office lawyer appointed to head appeals; for decisions of district attorneys, the State Attorney; above that, the Attorney General. [CPL s. 64](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_סדר_הדין_הפלילי#סעיף_64)

### How and when

> הערר יוגש באמצעות המשטרה או התובע, הכל לפי הענין, בתוך שישים ימים לאחר שנמסרה למתלונן ההודעה לפי סעיף 63, אולם רשאי בעל הסמכות להחליט בערר כאמור בסעיף 64 להאריך את המועד להגשת הערר.

*Gist:* The appeal is filed through the police or the prosecutor within a fixed period from the delivery of the closure notice; the deciding authority may extend it. [CPL s. 65](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_סדר_הדין_הפלילי#סעיף_65) (The period is also in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).)

The notice under s. 63 gives the reason for a decision **not to investigate**; for a decision **not to prosecute** it states that you may learn the reason at any police station or by the ways listed in the notice, except for sexual or violent offences, where the reason is written in the notice (s. 63(ב)). For sexual or violent felonies the appeal decision is due within six months unless special reasons are recorded (s. 65א). If the appeal may lead to an indictment, the suspect is usually told and may respond (s. 65ב).

### The private criminal complaint

> על אף האמור בסעיף 11, רשאי כל אדם להאשים בעבירה מן המנויות בתוספת השניה על ידי הגשת קובלנה לבית המשפט.

*Gist:* Despite the rule that the State prosecutes, any person may prosecute an offence listed in the Second Schedule by filing a private complaint with the court. [CPL s. 68](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_סדר_הדין_הפלילי#סעיף_68)

> הוגשה קובלנה, יעביר בית המשפט העתק ממנה לפרקליט המחוז; התביעה תנוהל, על אף האמור בסעיף 11, בידי הקובל או בא־כוחו, זולת אם פרקליט המחוז הגיש לבית המשפט, תוך חמישה עשר ימים לאחר שקיבל העתק הקובלנה, הודעה שפרקליט מפרקליטות המדינה ינהל את התביעה.

*Gist:* The court sends a copy to the district attorney; the complainant (or his lawyer) conducts the prosecution unless the district attorney announces within 15 days that the State will take it over, in which case an indictment replaces the complaint (ss. 71–72). [CPL s. 71](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_סדר_הדין_הפלילי#סעיף_71)

Other rules:

- No private complaint against a State employee for an act done in the course of duty without the Attorney General's consent (s. 69).
- The rules on indictments apply (s. 70); the complaint must contain the court, the complainant's name and address, the accused's name and address, the facts with place and time, the statutory provisions and the names of the prosecution witnesses (s. 85).
- If the complainant cannot conduct the case or conducts it vexatiously, the court may stay it until he appoints a lawyer (s. 73); a prosecutor who fails to appear may be treated as withdrawing (s. 133).
- The limitation periods for prosecution apply (s. 9).

The Second Schedule, item (1), lists Penal Law ss. 189, 190, 192, 194, 196, 223, 334, 336 (first part), 379, 380, 447 (unless armed), 452, 494 and 496, except where committed against a family member, and other items list offences under the Protection of Privacy Law, the Copyright Law, the Trademarks Ordinance, the Prevention of Nuisances Law, the Animal Welfare Law and others. [Second Schedule](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_סדר_הדין_הפלילי#תוספת_2)

## Leading cases

### בג״ץ 3292/07 עדאלה נ׳ היועץ המשפטי לממשלה (2011)

The petitioners asked the High Court to order criminal investigations into civilian deaths during two military operations in Gaza in 2004; the Military Advocate General refused and the Attorney General agreed. The court denied the petition: a demand for a criminal investigation must rest on a suitable prima facie foundation, opening an investigation is not automatic whenever the outcome is grave, and the long delay in petitioning made relief impractical.

Why it matters for an appeal: the senior lawyer deciding your appeal asks the same question: is there a concrete evidentiary foundation that further investigation could strengthen? An appeal that points to specific evidence and steps meets that test; an appeal that only says "the outcome was terrible" does not. It also shows that delay weakens your position. [English translation](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/24)

### Review of non-prosecution by the High Court

After an appeal is rejected, the decision of the State Attorney or Attorney General can in principle be challenged by a petition to the High Court of Justice, which intervenes only rarely, for extreme unreasonableness or a fundamental flaw in the decision-making. The classic example often cited is בג״ץ 935/89 גנור נ׳ היועץ המשפטי לממשלה, in which the court sent back a decision not to prosecute bank executives after the 1983 bank-shares crisis for reconsideration. A petition is a lawyer's task (module 6 and year 2).

## How it works in practice

### Step 1: read the closure notice

- **Which ground?** Insufficient evidence, no guilt, or no public interest. Each needs a different argument.
- **Who decided?** The police (investigation or police prosecution) or a district attorney. That decides who hears the appeal (s. 64(א)).
- **When did you receive it?** Keep the envelope, the registered-mail slip or the email. Count the appeal period from that date and diarise it.

### Step 2: learn the reasons and, if possible, the material

Ask in writing for the detailed reason if it is not in the notice. Ask also to see the investigation material relevant to your appeal; the prosecution decides what you may see under its internal guidelines (⚠️ manual check of the current guideline on gov.il). If you need time, ask the deciding authority **before the deadline** to extend it until you receive the reasons.

### Step 3: write the appeal

Match each element of the offence to evidence (your elements table, lesson 05.2), then name what is missing and how to get it:

| Ground for closure | Appeal argument that works |
|---|---|
| Insufficient evidence | Specific investigative steps not taken (witness, camera, phone, forensic test); evidence that exists but was not weighed |
| No guilt | Facts showing the suspect did it, or that an exemption claimed does not fit |
| No public interest | Seriousness, repetition, ongoing danger, harm to you, a vulnerable victim, a pattern of similar complaints |

### Step 4: private complaint or not?

Ask four questions: Is the offence in the Second Schedule and not against a family member? Is it within the limitation period? Can you prove every element beyond reasonable doubt with witnesses who will come to court? Is criminal court the best route, or would a civil claim, a protection order or mediation solve the problem better? A private complaint and an appeal can be pursued in sequence; many people appeal first, and file a private complaint only if the appeal fails.

**Supplied case (continues lesson 05.7).** Sapir Ohana's complaint against Eli (threats by WhatsApp, two slashed tyres) was closed. On 20 December 2026 she received, by registered mail, a notice dated 14 December: "התיק נסגר מחמת חוסר ראיות מספיקות". When she asks at the station she learns that the hospital's car-park footage was never requested, and that Eli, questioned once, denied slashing the tyres and said the messages "were jokes". Her colleague was never questioned.

### Steps

1. **Read the notice.** Ground, decision-maker, date of receipt. Calculate the last day for the appeal and write it down with its source.
2. **Decide who hears it** (s. 64(א)) and how to send it (s. 65: through the police or the prosecutor; check the notice).
3. **Map elements to evidence** for each offence; mark gaps.
4. **List the missing investigative steps**, each with why it matters.
5. **Write the appeal** using the template. One page of facts, one of grounds, a precise remedy.
6. **Private complaint check.** Are the offences in the Second Schedule? Is Eli a family member? Within the limitation period? Could you prove each element yourself, without the footage?
7. **Decide and, if relevant, draft** the private complaint (the template), and write three sentences on its risks.
8. **Sending record:** method, date, proof; a follow-up date if you hear nothing.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

- **Appeal (ערר):** yes. The right belongs to the complainant (s. 64), and it is a written administrative step.
- **Private criminal complaint:** yes, by law ("כל אדם", s. 68; the complainant conducts it, s. 71), but it is a full criminal trial in which you carry the burden of proof. The court may stay it until you appoint a lawyer if you cannot manage it (s. 73). Partly, in practice.
- **High Court petition against a rejected appeal:** possible for an individual, but in practice a lawyer's task.

### Where and how

- Appeal: through the police or the prosecution office named in the notice (s. 65), with proof of the sending date.
- Private complaint: the criminal registry of the magistrates' court with jurisdiction; check the fee and filing method with the registry or the Judiciary's information centre (see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- Free help: Legal Aid for victims of sexual offences (including appeals against closure under the 2025 temporary order) and families of homicide victims; [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md); [Kol-Zchut](https://www.kolzchut.org.il).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The offence is sexual, violent within the family, or serious; or the suspect is a public figure or official.
- Your appeal was rejected and you are considering a High Court petition.
- You are about to file a private complaint and the accused has a lawyer, or the case depends on technical evidence.
- The other side threatens a counter-complaint or a defamation claim.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Counting the deadline from the date printed on the notice | Appeal may be treated as late | Ask for an extension under s. 65 at once | Keep proof of the date of receipt |
| Repeating the complaint instead of showing gaps | Appeal rejected | Add specific missing steps and evidence | Use the elements-to-evidence table |
| Filing a private complaint for theft or fraud | Dismissed: not in the Second Schedule | Appeal the closure; file a civil claim | Check the schedule first |
| Private complaint against a family member | Not allowed | Ask for a protection order; appeal the closure | Read the family exception |
| Using a private complaint as leverage in a money dispute | Stay for vexatious conduct; harm to your credibility | Withdraw and pursue the civil route | Use criminal law only for real offences |

## Review

- A complainant may appeal any decision not to investigate or prosecute, on all three grounds, to a more senior authority (s. 64), through the police or prosecutor, within the period in s. 65 from receiving the notice.
- Successful appeals show concrete investigative gaps and link evidence to elements; Adalah shows the prima facie foundation test and the cost of delay.
- A private complaint is available only for Second Schedule offences, not against family members, and not against State employees acting in office without the Attorney General's consent.
- The district attorney may take over within 15 days; otherwise you prosecute, prove guilt beyond reasonable doubt and must appear.
- Appeal first; consider a private complaint only for what you can prove; keep civil and protective routes in view.
