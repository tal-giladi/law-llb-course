# 12.7 · After judgment: payment, enforcement, request for leave to appeal

A judgment in your favour is a piece of paper until the money arrives. In this lesson you read your judgment for its dates, ask for payment, and, if it does not come, open an execution file using what you learned in module 8. You also learn the other road out of a judgment, the request for leave to appeal, with its 30-day period for cases opened from 11 April 2025, and when it is worth taking or answering.

## Why it matters

Many small-claims winners never collect, not because the debtor has no money but because they wait, file at the Execution Office too early or with the wrong documents, or give up when the first warning brings no payment. Others lose a chance to challenge a wrong judgment because they count the appeal period from a website instead of the regulation, or forget that court recess days count in small claims.

Lessons [08.7](../module-08/lesson-07.md) and [08.9](../module-08/lesson-09.md) taught the Execution Office in full. This lesson connects them to your file: dates first, then payment, then enforcement, with leave to appeal as the parallel track.

## The law

### The judgment and its dates: reg. 15

Judgment is given at the end of the hearing or within 7 days, briefly reasoned, and must state how and by when it can be challenged (reg. 15). It usually also states the payment date ("תוך 30 יום"). ([reg. 15](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיפוט_בתביעות_קטנות_(סדרי_דין)))

### Leave to appeal: Courts Law s. 64 and reg. 16

A small-claims judgment can be appealed only with the leave of a district-court judge (Courts Law s. 64). ([s. 64](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט#סעיף_64))

> המועד להגשת בקשה לרשות ערעור על פסק דינו של בית המשפט… הוא 30 ימים מיום מתן פסק הדין או ההחלטה.

*Gist:* For proceedings opened from 11.4.2025 the application must be filed within 30 days; for earlier proceedings the period remains 15 days. If leave is granted, the application is treated as the appeal (reg. 16(ב)); chapter 17 of the Civil Procedure Regulations applies (reg. 16(ג)). ([reg. 16](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיפוט_בתביעות_קטנות_(סדרי_דין)); confirmed against the regulation text on 17 October 2026; see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

### Counting the days: reg. 16א (proceedings from 11.4.2025)

> (א) המועד הקובע לתחילת מניין הימים לפרק הזמן שנקבע הוא יום המצאת המסמך כמפורט בתקנות אלה; אם ניתנה החלטה במעמד בעלי הדין, יימנה המועד ממועד הדיון.
> (ב) תקופת פגרה של בית המשפט תובא במניין הימים שנקבעו בתקנות אלה או שנקבעו בידי בית המשפט, זולת אם בית המשפט הורה אחרת.

*Gist:* Count from the day the judgment was served on you; if it was given at the hearing in the parties' presence, from the hearing day. Court recess days **count** in small claims, unless the court orders otherwise. ([reg. 16א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיפוט_בתביעות_קטנות_(סדרי_דין)))

### What the application contains, and security: CPR regs 134, 135, 149

The application has a heading (the parties, the type of proceeding, the court and case number of the judgment, the date of the judgment and of its service, the competent court and section, the fee and the security) and a body; the reasons for granting leave come first, within the page limit (regs 134, 149(1)). On filing, the applicant deposits security for the respondent's costs in the amount in the Third Schedule, which lists an amount for leave to appeal a small-claims decision (item 71, see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)); the court may exempt or change it (reg. 135). ([regs 134–135, 149](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

### No automatic stay: CPR reg. 145

> (א) הגשת ערעור לא תעכב את ביצוע ההחלטה שעליה מערערים.

*Gist:* Filing an appeal (or an application for leave) does not stop the judgment from being enforced; the losing party must ask the court for a stay (reg. 145(ב)–(ג)). ([reg. 145](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

### When you may go to the Execution Office: Execution Law s. 6(ב)

A creditor may open a file after the payment date set in the judgment or, if none, after the default period from the judgment (from service where the judgment was given in the debtor's absence and the creditor had to serve it). ([s. 6](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_6); periods in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); full treatment in lesson [08.7](../module-08/lesson-07.md))

## Leading cases

### רע״א 9615/05 שמש נ׳ פוקצ׳טה בע״מ (5.7.2006)

The case went from a small claim, through leave to appeal in the district court, to a further application for leave in the Supreme Court, which granted it and increased the award. Why it matters: leave is given where there is a real error of law or a question that matters beyond the parties; Shemesh is the rare example of a small claim reaching the top, not the ordinary course. [English translation (Cardozo)](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/346)

### Key text: reg. 16א(ב)

The rule that recess days count in small claims is the trap of the 2025 amendment. In ordinary civil cases recess is excluded (Civil Procedure Regulations reg. 179(ב)); here it is included unless the court orders otherwise.

## How it works in practice

### The judgment-day diary

| Date | How to compute | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Payment due | As stated in the judgment | The judgment |
| Earliest execution filing | After the payment date, or the default period | Execution Law s. 6(ב) |
| Last day for leave to appeal | 30 days (cases from 11.4.2025) from service, or from the hearing if given in your presence; recess counts | regs 16(א), 16א |
| Set aside an absence judgment (if you were absent) | 30 days from service | reg. 12 |
| Late filing at Execution Office limits interest | More than a year after you could have filed | Execution Law s. 6(ב2) |

### Collecting, step by step

1. **Payment request (on the payment date or the day after).** A short letter: the case number, the sum in the judgment with costs, your bank details, a date 7 days ahead, and that you will open an execution file without further notice. Send by email and registered mail; log it.
2. **Execution file (after the date allowed by s. 6(ב)).** Follow the [execution request checklist](../../templates/execution-request-checklist.md) and lesson [08.9](../module-08/lesson-09.md): form 206, a judgment copy showing the parties' ID numbers, bank details, the fee; choose the track.
3. **After the warning.** Keep the file active: information requests, liens on bank accounts or salary, within the debtor's protections (lesson [08.7](../module-08/lesson-07.md)).
4. **Payment received directly.** Report it to the Execution Office and close the file when paid in full.

### Leave to appeal: should you?

Apply only if at least one is true: the judge decided on a legal rule that is clearly wrong (for example ignoring s. 25י(ד)); the judge refused to hear essential evidence or a witness; or there was a serious procedural failure (no proper notice of the hearing). Do not apply because you disagree with how the judge weighed the evidence: findings of fact by the judge who heard the parties are rarely disturbed on leave. Weigh the fee and the security against the sum (lesson 11.5), and remember the other side's costs if you lose.

### If the other side applies

You will be served with the application. The district court may decide without asking you, or ask for your response by a date. Answer briefly: the judgment applied the law to facts found at the hearing; no question beyond the parties arises. Enforcement may continue unless a stay is ordered (reg. 145); if a stay is requested, you may ask that it be conditioned on depositing the judgment sum.

## Worked example

Huda Abu-Kaf, an accountant from Rahat, sued "הובלות הנגב בע״מ", a moving company that broke her refrigerator and refused to pay. Her case was opened in May 2026. On 3 August 2026, at the hearing, in both parties' presence, the judge gave judgment: ₪6,200 plus ₪500 costs, "to be paid within 30 days".

**Her diary.**

- Payment due: 2 September 2026.
- Last day for leave to appeal (for either side): counted from the hearing (judgment given in the parties' presence, reg. 16א(א)): 30 days, including the summer recess (reg. 16א(ב)) — 2 September 2026.
- Earliest execution filing: after 2 September (s. 6(ב)).

**What happened.** On 1 September the company filed an application for leave to appeal, arguing the refrigerator was already faulty. It did not ask for a stay. On 3 September Huda sent a payment request with a 7-day deadline. No payment. On 14 September she opened an execution file by appointment at the Be'er Sheva branch, on the short track, with form 206 and the judgment downloaded from Net HaMishpat showing both parties' numbers. The company then asked the district court for a stay; the court ordered a stay only if the company deposited the judgment sum in court. The company deposited it. Three weeks later the district court refused leave: the application challenged findings of fact. Huda asked for the deposit to be released to her and closed the execution file once paid.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- Your judgment, or Huda's facts above; or, if your case has not reached judgment, the clinic case with this assumption: judgment for Tsega for ₪9,800 plus ₪400 costs, given in both parties' presence on 2 December 2026, payable within 30 days.
- The [execution request checklist](../../templates/execution-request-checklist.md) and form 206 (lesson 08.9).
- About two hours.

### Steps

1. **Read the judgment (10 min).** Mark: the sum, costs, payment date, whether it was given in your presence, the statement of how to challenge it.
2. **Diary (15 min).** Fill the judgment-day diary for your case, with the regulation for each date. Check whether the case was opened before or from 11.4.2025.
3. **Payment request (20 min).** Write it in Hebrew (half a page); send or keep as a dry run.
4. **Execution file (45 min).** Complete parts A–E and G of the checklist and fill form 206 as in lesson 08.9; stop before filing unless the date has passed and you decide to file.
5. **Leave decision (20 min).** Write three lines: is there an error of law, refused evidence or procedural failure? Would you apply? If the other side applied, write the three-sentence response.
6. **Log (10 min).** Enter every date and action in your case log.

### Template

- [Execution request checklist](../../templates/execution-request-checklist.md) (lesson 08.9), with the Authority's form 206.
- [Clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md), part ז (deadlines) — new in this module.

### How you know it worked

- Your diary has the three dates with their sources, and you know whether recess counts.
- Your payment request names the case, the sum and a date.
- Your execution file is complete or every gap is explained.
- Your leave decision names a ground, or says honestly that there is none.

### Self-check

- [ ] I counted leave to appeal from service, or from the hearing if the judgment was given in my presence.
- [ ] I know that recess days count in small claims unless the court orders otherwise.
- [ ] I know an application for leave does not stop enforcement without a stay.
- [ ] I will not open an execution file before the payment date.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes for collection: a creditor may act for himself at the Execution Office (lesson 08.7). Partly for leave to appeal: you may file it yourself in the district court, but it is a written legal argument under the Civil Procedure Regulations, with a fee and security; consider advice first.

### Where and how

- Payment request: email and registered mail. Execution: the Authority's online personal area or a branch by appointment; call centre (lesson 08.9).
- Leave to appeal: the district court for the area, through Net HaMishpat or the registry; the Judiciary's service page "הגשת ערעור או בקשת רשות ערעור בהליכים אזרחיים" (⚠️ manual check: gov.il blocks automated access). Fee and security: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); fee exemption for inability to pay is possible.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You want to apply for leave on a point of law, or the other side applied and the district court asked for your response on a legal question.
- The debtor files an "I have paid" claim or an objection at the Execution Office.
- The debtor is a company that seems to be closing down or transferring its assets.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Counting 15 days for a case opened in 2026 | Filing earlier than needed, or confusion about the respondent's deadline | Recount under reg. 16(א) | Check the case-opening date |
| Excluding the summer recess | Application late | Apply at once for an extension with reasons | Recess counts (reg. 16א(ב)) |
| Opening the execution file the day after judgment | Request rejected (s. 6(ב)) | Refile after the payment date | Diary the earliest filing day |
| Assuming the other side's leave application stops collection | Months lost | Proceed; oppose any stay or ask for a deposit | Reg. 145(א) |
| Applying for leave to re-argue the facts | Application refused; costs | — | Apply only on a ground of law or procedure |

## Review

- Read the judgment for three dates: payment, earliest execution filing, last day for leave to appeal.
- Leave to appeal: 30 days for cases opened from 11.4.2025 (15 for earlier cases), counted from service or from the hearing if given in the parties' presence; recess counts (regs 16, 16א).
- Leave is for errors of law or serious procedural failures, not for re-weighing facts; it needs a fee and usually security (CPR regs 134–135).
- An application for leave does not stay enforcement (reg. 145); collect with a payment request, then an execution file (lessons 08.7, 08.9).
