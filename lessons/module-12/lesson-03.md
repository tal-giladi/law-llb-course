# 12.3 · The demand letter and negotiating a settlement

Most everyday disputes end with a letter, not a judgment. In this lesson you choose and adapt the right letter from the templates you already have, send it so that delivery and content can be proved, and then negotiate: with a walk-away figure, a plan for concessions, and a settlement written so that it can be enforced if the other side does not pay.

## Why it matters

The letter is your first piece of evidence. It fixes the date on which the other side knew of your claim and the amount, shows the judge that you acted reasonably, and, for some statutory remedies, is a legal condition of suing at all. The Civil Procedure Regulations 2018 make the parties' conduct a factor in costs (reg. 151(א)); a court that sees a clear, polite, dated demand and a reasonable offer will be less sympathetic to a defendant who ignored both.

Negotiation is where most of the value is. A deposit dispute settled at 85% within three weeks is often better than 100% after eight months, a hearing and an execution file. But a settlement is only worth what it says: a vague "we'll sort it out" ends with neither money nor a claim.

The law of the letters (breach and cancellation notices, consumer withdrawal, tort demands, pre-action letters) is already taught in lessons [03.10](../module-03/lesson-10.md), [04.8](../module-04/lesson-08.md) and [08.8](../module-08/lesson-08.md), and settlement and mediation in [08.6](../module-08/lesson-06.md). This lesson is about using them in your own file.

## The law

### When a written demand is a legal condition: Consumer Protection Law s. 31א(ב)

> לא תוגש נגד עוסק תביעה לפיצויים לדוגמה לפי סעיף קטן (א), אלא לאחר שהצרכן שלח או מסר בקשה לפי אותו סעיף קטן, בכתב, לרבות באמצעות תקשורת אלקטרונית…

*Gist:* For the listed consumer breaches (for example a dealer who keeps charging after a valid cancellation of a continuing transaction, or does not refund under its own returns policy), a court may award exemplary damages without proof of loss, up to a capped amount, but only if the consumer first made the request in writing, email included. ([s. 31א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_הצרכן#סעיף_31א); the cap is in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); lesson 12.4 lists the main heads.) If your dispute fits one of the listed breaches, your letter must ask for the specific act (stop charging, refund) in so many words.

### The deposit rules your letter will cite: Rental and Borrowing Law ss. 25ח, 25י

> המשכיר יודיע לשוכר זמן סביר מראש על כוונתו לממש את הערובה ויאפשר לו לתקן את הטעון תיקון בתוך זמן סביר.

*Gist:* Before using the security the landlord must give reasonable advance notice and let the tenant fix what needs fixing; he may use it only for the purposes in s. 25י(ג) and must return the rest within the period in s. 25י(ה). The tenant answers only for defects caused by unreasonable use (s. 25ח(א)). ([s. 25י](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_השכירות_והשאילה#סעיף_25י))

### Costs follow conduct: Civil Procedure Regulations reg. 151(א)

> חיוב בעל דין בתשלום הוצאות נועד לשפות את בעל הדין שכנגד על הוצאותיו בהליך בהתחשב בתוצאותיו, במשאבים שנדרשו לניהולו ובהתנהלות בעלי הדין.

*Gist:* Costs compensate the other party in light of the outcome, the resources the case required and the parties' conduct. The small claims court has the magistrates' court's powers in matters of procedure (Small Claims Regulations reg. 9), so your letter and your reasonable offers are part of the costs picture. ([reg. 151](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

### A settlement the court can enforce: Courts Law s. 79א

> (ב) אין באמור בסעיף קטן (א) כדי לגרוע מסמכותו של בית המשפט להציע לבעלי הדין הסדר פשרה או לתת, לבקשת בעלי הדין, תוקף של פסק דין להסדר פשרה שעשו ביניהם.

*Gist:* Once a case is open, the parties may ask the court to give their settlement the force of a judgment; it can then go straight to the Execution Office (lesson [08.7](../module-08/lesson-07.md)). Before a case is open, a settlement is a contract: if it is broken you sue on it, which is why a default clause matters. ([s. 79א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט#סעיף_79א))

## Leading cases

### Key text: the templates and the statutory pre-conditions

There is no leading ruling on how to write a demand letter. The controlling texts are the statutory pre-conditions (s. 31א(ב) for exemplary consumer damages; s. 25י(ד) for deposits; notice periods in the Remedies Law for cancellation, lesson [03.10](../module-03/lesson-10.md)) and the costs principle in reg. 151. Where a ruling matters, it was taught in its home lesson: good faith in negotiation (s. 12 of the Contracts Law, lesson [03.4](../module-03/lesson-04.md)).

## How it works in practice

### Which letter?

| Your situation | Letter | Template |
|---|---|---|
| You are inside a consumer cancellation period (distance sale, door-to-door, listed goods) | Cancellation notice | [Consumer withdrawal notice](../../templates/consumer-withdrawal-notice.md) |
| The other side breached a contract and you want performance or to cancel | Notice of breach, then cancellation | [Notice of breach and cancellation](../../templates/notice-of-breach-and-cancellation.md) |
| A company owes you a refund, repair or correction, first contact | Demand to a company | [Demand letter to a company](../../templates/demand-letter-company.md) |
| Someone damaged your property or caused you loss | Tort demand | [Tort demand letter](../../templates/tort-demand-letter.md) |
| Earlier contact failed and you are ready to sue | Final letter before claim | [Pre-action letter](../../templates/pre-action-letter.md) |

Often two letters: a first demand, then, if ignored, a short pre-action letter naming the court and a date.

### Sending so you can prove it

- Email to the address in the contract or on the invoice, **and** registered mail with proof of delivery (דואר רשום עם אישור מסירה) to the registered address; for a private person, also WhatsApp with the PDF attached. Keep the receipt and a screenshot of the sent message.
- Log the date of sending and the deadline in your case log.
- Do not threaten a criminal complaint to get paid, and do not publish the dispute online to pressure the other side: both can expose you to liability.

### Negotiation in five moves

1. **Walk-away figure.** Before any call, write down the lowest sum you would accept now and why: your full claim, discounted by the risk of losing parts of it, the time to judgment and the time to collect (lesson 11.5 gives the method). Never reveal it.
2. **Opening.** Your letter's figure is your opening; it must be the full claim with its calculation, not an inflated number you cannot defend.
3. **Concessions.** Plan two or three concessions in advance, each tied to a reason ("I accept the ₪180 water bill"; "I will drop interest if paid within 7 days"). Concede in decreasing steps.
4. **Record.** Confirm every oral offer by a short message the same day: "בהמשך לשיחתנו היום, הצעת לשלם ___ עד ___". Mark offers you make "מבלי לפגוע בזכויותיי" so that the letter itself is not read as a reduced claim.
5. **Close.** When you agree, write it down at once, with a date and method of payment, before thanking anyone.

### The settlement agreement: six clauses

1. The parties (legal names and numbers).
2. The sum, the date and the method of payment (bank details).
3. What each side gives up: on full and timely payment, the payee waives all claims arising from the dispute (ויתור וסילוק).
4. Default: if not paid on time, the full original claim revives, with interest, or the agreed sum becomes immediately enforceable.
5. No admission of liability (often asked for; harmless to you).
6. If a case is already open: the parties ask the court to give the agreement the force of a judgment (s. 79א(ב)).

## Worked example

**The clinic case, continued.** Tsega's first message to Avraham (WhatsApp, 22 September) asked for the deposit; he answered "only the water bill and painting". She then sent a formal demand, built on the [demand letter to a company](../../templates/demand-letter-company.md) adapted for a private landlord.

**The negotiation.** Avraham calls on 8 October: "I'll give you 6,000 and we're done." Tsega's walk-away figure, written in her log before the call, is ₪9,000: she rates her chance of full success high, but values getting the money this month. She says she will consider any offer in writing. That evening she writes: "בהמשך לשיחתנו, מבלי לפגוע בזכויותיי: אסכים לקבל 10,000 ש"ח עד 15.10.2026, בתמורה לוויתור על הפרשי הצמדה וריבית." He offers ₪9,000; she accepts ₪9,500 paid by 15 October, and sends the settlement the same night.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before sending.

### You need

- Your facts file and evidence index (lessons 12.1–12.2).
- The template that fits your dispute (table above).
- About two hours to write and send, then up to three weeks of waiting and negotiating.

### Steps

1. **Pick the letter (10 min).** Use the table. If you are within a consumer cancellation period, send the cancellation notice first; it has its own deadlines (lesson [03.10](../module-03/lesson-10.md)).
2. **Check pre-conditions (10 min).** Does a statute require a written demand or notice before you may sue for the remedy you want (s. 31א(ב), s. 25י(ד), a notice of breach with extension)? Make the letter satisfy it in words.
3. **Draft (45 min).** Facts in numbered paragraphs with exhibit numbers, the section, the itemised amount, a dated deadline (usually 14 days), the named court, the reservation of rights.
4. **Send and prove (15 min).** Email plus registered mail; screenshot and receipt into the file; deadline into the case log.
5. **Walk-away figure (15 min).** Write it with its reasons; list two or three planned concessions.
6. **Negotiate (as it happens).** Confirm every offer in writing the same day.
7. **Settle or escalate (20 min).** If you agree, sign the six-clause settlement. If the deadline passes, go to lesson 12.4 (regulator) or 12.5 (claim).

### Template

- [Consumer withdrawal notice](../../templates/consumer-withdrawal-notice.md), [notice of breach and cancellation](../../templates/notice-of-breach-and-cancellation.md), [demand letter to a company](../../templates/demand-letter-company.md), [tort demand letter](../../templates/tort-demand-letter.md), [pre-action letter](../../templates/pre-action-letter.md).
- Model deposit letter and settlement agreement: in the worked example above.

### How you know it worked

- Your letter states the facts with exhibit numbers, the section, an itemised amount, a dated deadline and a named court.
- You hold proof of delivery and the date is in your log.
- Any settlement states amount, date, method, release, default and parties' legal names.

### Self-check

- [ ] My letter satisfies any statutory pre-condition for the remedy I want.
- [ ] I did not threaten a police complaint or a public shaming campaign.
- [ ] I wrote down my walk-away figure before the first call.
- [ ] Every oral offer was confirmed in writing the same day.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Writing and sending your own demand, negotiating and signing a settlement of your own dispute need no lawyer. Writing such letters for others as an occupation or for pay is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20(3)).

### Where and how

- Registered mail at any post office; keep the proof of delivery. Registered addresses of companies: the Companies Registrar's public search.
- Free help in negotiating: court-annexed mediation once a case is open, and community mediation centres (lesson [08.6](../module-08/lesson-06.md)); [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The other side answers through a lawyer with a counter-demand larger than your claim.
- You are asked to sign a settlement that includes a confidentiality clause with a penalty, a non-disparagement clause or a waiver of rights beyond this dispute.
- The other side alleges you defamed it in posts or reviews.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Claiming exemplary damages without a prior written request | Exemplary damages barred (s. 31א(ב)) | Send the request now; sue after it is ignored | Check pre-conditions before drafting |
| Letter by WhatsApp only to a company | Delivery disputed | Resend by email and registered mail | Two channels, one with proof |
| "Pay me or I will go to the police" | Improper pressure; risk to you | Withdraw the threat in writing | Name a civil court only |
| Accepting an oral offer and waiting | Offer withdrawn; nothing to enforce | Confirm in writing at once | Close in writing the same day |
| Settlement with no date or default clause | Payment never arrives; fight about what was agreed | Sue on the settlement or the original claim | Use the six clauses |

## Review

- Choose the letter by situation; most files need a demand and then a short pre-action letter.
- Some remedies require a prior written request or notice; make the letter satisfy it in words.
- Send by two channels, one with proof of delivery; log the deadline.
- Negotiate from a written walk-away figure; confirm every offer in writing.
- A settlement needs amount, date, method, release and default; once a case is open it can receive the force of a judgment (Courts Law s. 79א).
