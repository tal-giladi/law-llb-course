# 12.6 · The hearing: presenting, questioning, settlement proposals

A small-claims hearing usually lasts between fifteen minutes and an hour, and the judge runs it. In this lesson you build a hearing-day kit, rehearse it, and learn the rules that decide what happens if a party does not come, how the judge questions, what goes into the record, and how to handle the moment almost every hearing reaches: "Have the parties considered a compromise?"

## Why it matters

Self-represented parties lose hearings they should win for three reasons: they cannot find the document when the judge asks for it, they argue instead of answering the question, and they react to a settlement proposal without having decided in advance what they would accept. All three are cured by preparation. The judge has read your claim and the defence; he wants to know which facts are disputed and what proves them. If you have that ready, the hearing is short and usually goes your way.

## The law

### Non-appearance: reg. 11

> נמסרה על הדיון הודעה כראוי ואיש מבעלי הדין לא התייצב – תימחק התביעה; התייצב התובע והנתבע לא התייצב – יתן בית המשפט פסק דין על יסוד כתב התביעה, ובלבד שהתובע הצהיר בפני בית המשפט על אמיתות האמור בכתב תביעתו; התייצב הנתבע והתובע לא התייצב – תידחה התביעה.

*Gist:* If neither party comes, the claim is struck out; if only you come, you get judgment on your claim after declaring it true; if only the defendant comes, your claim is dismissed. ([reg. 11](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיפוט_בתביעות_קטנות_(סדרי_דין))) A defendant who filed no defence may get a judgment on the claim alone (reg. 10). A decision given in a party's absence may be set aside on an application within 30 days of service (reg. 12).

### The judge runs the hearing: reg. 13(א)

> בית המשפט יקבע את סדר הבאת ראיות וטענות הצדדים בדרך שנראית לו ויברר את המחלוקת באופן פעיל.

*Gist:* The judge decides the order of evidence and argument and investigates the dispute actively; you answer his questions and do not run a lawyer-style trial. ([reg. 13](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיפוט_בתביעות_קטנות_(סדרי_דין))) For cases opened from 11.4.2025 the court may hold the hearing by video or decide on the written record in suitable cases, weighing the parties' preferences and the gap between them (reg. 13(ב)–(ג)).

### Requests, the record, judgment: regs 8, 14, 15

You may make a request orally at the hearing (reg. 8(א)). The record contains a summary of what witnesses said and of everything else said at the hearing (reg. 14): if something important is said, ask politely that it be recorded. Judgment is given at the end of the hearing or within 7 days, briefly reasoned, stating how and by when it may be challenged (reg. 15).

### Evidence and representation: Courts Law ss. 62–63

The small claims court may admit evidence not admissible elsewhere (except privilege) and acts in the way most useful for a just and fast decision (s. 62). A lawyer appears only with leave for special reasons; a non-lawyer who does not represent regularly or for pay may represent you with the court's approval and a filed power of attorney (s. 63). ([ss. 62–63](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט#סעיף_62))

### Compromise: Courts Law s. 79א

> (א) בית משפט הדן בענין אזרחי רשאי, בהסכמת בעלי הדין, לפסוק בענין שלפניו, כולו או מקצתו, בדרך של פשרה.

*Gist:* With both parties' consent the court may decide the case, or part of it, by way of compromise; it may also propose a settlement, or give the parties' own settlement the force of a judgment (s. 79א(ב)). ([s. 79א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט#סעיף_79א)) Three different things, each with different consequences (below).

## Leading cases

### רע״א 5234/17 (representation in small claims)

The Supreme Court held that a small-claims judge does not have to tell an unrepresented party that he may ask for permission to be represented. Why it matters on hearing day: the court helps you present your case but does not run it for you; if you need an interpreter, a non-lawyer helper under s. 63(ב), or a postponement, ask for it in writing before the hearing. (⚠️ manual check: decision text on the Judiciary's site; see [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md).)

### Key text: reg. 13(א)

"ויברר את המחלוקת באופן פעיל": the active judge is the defining feature of the forum. Prepare to be interrupted, asked for a document by number, and asked direct questions; prepare short answers.

## How it works in practice

### The hearing-day kit

1. **Exhibit bundle.** Three copies (court, defendant, you) of every exhibit, in number order, with tabs; originals of receipts and photos; the USB with any recording and its transcript; a laptop or phone to play it.
2. **Two-minute opening** (written, read once, then spoken): who you are; what you paid or did; what the other side did; the section; the amount. End with "these are my documents, numbered 1 to 8".
3. **The disputed facts.** Compare your claim with the defence; list each disputed fact and the exhibit or witness that proves it.
4. **Questions for the other side** (if the judge lets you ask): short, closed, one fact each, each tied to a document: "On 2 August you wrote 'הכל בסדר' — you wrote that?" Never ask "why".
5. **Your witness.** A short note of what he saw; tell him to answer only what he is asked, and that he waits outside until called if the judge so orders.
6. **The judge's likely questions** with one-sentence answers: "Why didn't you fix the paint?" "Because I was never asked; s. 25י(ד)."
7. **Settlement range.** Your walk-away figure from lesson 12.3, updated after reading the defence. Write it down; do not say it.

### In the room

- Arrive early; check the cause list; switch off the phone except for exhibits.
- Address the judge as "כבוד השופט" / "כבוד השופטת"; speak to the judge, not to the other side.
- Answer the question asked, then stop. If you do not know, say so.
- If the other side says something new, ask for it to be recorded and say which document answers it.
- If you need time, ask for a short break.

### The three kinds of "compromise"

| What the judge says | What it is | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| "I suggest the defendant pays ₪8,000 and we end here" | A settlement proposal | You may accept, counter, or refuse and get a judgment on the merits |
| "Do the parties agree that I decide by way of compromise?" | Consent to a s. 79א(א) decision | The judge then decides as he sees fit, not strictly on the law; by consenting you accept that, and there is little to appeal |
| "You have agreed; I give the agreement the force of a judgment" | Settlement with force of a judgment (s. 79א(ב)) | Enforceable at the Execution Office if not paid |

Before you answer, compare the proposal with your written range, ask yourself what the judge's proposal suggests about how he sees the evidence, and remember the time and cost of collecting a judgment (lesson 12.7). Insist on a payment date in any agreement.

## Worked example

**The clinic case.** Avraham filed a defence: the walls needed full repainting "because of nails and stains", he had a painter's quote of ₪4,500 (attached, dated 15 October, after the claim), and the remaining ₪6,300 covered "general wear and two months' lost rent while painting".

Tsega's kit:

- **Disputed facts:** condition of the walls (her photos, exhibit 3, and his message of 2 August, exhibit 4); whether he gave notice (his message of 20 September, exhibit 5, is the first mention).
- **Questions for Avraham:** "Your quote is dated 15 October, after you received my claim?" "Before 20 September, did you ask me to repair anything?" "Is there a painter's invoice, or only a quote?"
- **Range:** She would accept ₪9,000 now; less than ₪8,000 only if the judge signals the photos are weak.

At the hearing the judge looks at the photos, asks Avraham whether he told Tsega before keeping the deposit (he says no), and says: "The law requires notice and a chance to repair. I suggest ₪9,800 within 14 days." Tsega accepts on condition of a payment date; the agreement is recorded and given the force of a judgment (s. 79א(ב)). Her case log records the date payment is due.

**An alternative ending.** Avraham does not come. Tsega declares the truth of her claim and receives judgment on it (reg. 11). She diaries that he has 30 days from service to apply to set it aside (reg. 12) and that the Execution Office may be approached only from the date allowed by law (lesson 12.7).

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- Your claim and, if one was filed, the defence; your exhibits.
- The draft [hearing-day kit](../../templates/hearing-day-kit.md) template.
- A friend to play the judge for 20 minutes. About two and a half hours.

### Steps

1. **Disputed facts (20 min).** Compare claim and defence; list each disputed fact and its proof. If no defence was filed, list the points the judge is likely to probe anyway.
2. **Bundle (30 min).** Print three copies, number and tab; prepare the USB and transcript.
3. **Opening (20 min).** Write it, cut it to two minutes, time yourself.
4. **Questions (20 min).** Write up to five closed questions for the other side, each with a document.
5. **Judge's questions (15 min).** Write the five questions you would ask if you were the judge, with one-sentence answers.
6. **Range (10 min).** Update your walk-away figure after reading the defence; write it on a separate card.
7. **Mock hearing (30 min).** Your friend reads the defence aloud, interrupts you with questions, and at the end proposes a compromise. Practise answering, asking for a document to be recorded, and responding to the proposal.
8. **Logistics (5 min).** Diary the hearing date; check service was completed (reg. 7(ב1)); if you need an interpreter or a postponement, write to the court now.

### Template

- [Hearing-day kit (ערכת יום דיון)](../../templates/hearing-day-kit.md) (new in this module).

### How you know it worked

- You can find any exhibit within five seconds of hearing its number.
- Your opening is under two minutes and names the section and the amount.
- In the mock hearing you answered each question in one or two sentences.
- You responded to the compromise proposal from your written range, not on the spot.

### Self-check

- [ ] I know that if I do not come, my claim is dismissed (reg. 11).
- [ ] I know the three kinds of "compromise" and their consequences.
- [ ] I will ask for important statements to be recorded (reg. 14).
- [ ] Any settlement I accept will have a payment date.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes; the hearing is designed for parties without lawyers. A lawyer may appear only with leave; a non-lawyer helper only with the court's approval and a power of attorney, and not someone who represents regularly or for pay (Courts Law s. 63).

### Where and how

- The court building on the date in the summons, or by video if the court so decides (reg. 13(ב)); the case on Net HaMishpat shows decisions and changes.
- Requests (postponement, interpreter, witness summons): in writing through Net HaMishpat or the registry, well before the hearing.
- Judiciary information centre for hearing logistics (lesson 12.8); [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The other side arrives with a lawyer who was given leave, and the case turns on law, not facts.
- The defendant raises a counterclaim or a fraud allegation against you.
- The judge suggests transferring the case to the magistrates' court.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Not attending ("I'm sure he won't come") | Claim dismissed (reg. 11) | Apply to set aside within 30 days of service (reg. 12) with reasons | Diary; ask for a postponement in writing if you cannot come |
| Arguing with the other side | Judge loses patience; points lost | Turn to the judge, answer the question | Speak only to the judge |
| One copy of the documents | Delay; judge works from the other side's copies | Ask for a short break to copy | Three copies, tabbed |
| Consenting to a s. 79א(א) decision without thinking | Bound by a compromise ruling with little to appeal | — | Know the three kinds of compromise |
| Accepting a settlement with no payment date | Payment drags; disputes over terms | Ask the court to add a date before the record closes | Insist on a date |

## Review

- The judge runs the hearing actively (reg. 13); answer questions briefly and point to exhibits by number.
- If you do not attend, your claim is dismissed; if the defendant does not, you may get judgment after declaring your claim true (reg. 11); absence decisions can be set aside within 30 days (reg. 12).
- Prepare a kit: bundle, opening, disputed facts, questions, judge's questions, written settlement range.
- Distinguish a settlement proposal, a decision by way of compromise (s. 79א(א)) and a settlement with the force of a judgment (s. 79א(ב)).
