# 12.1 · Choosing a dispute and the facts file

This clinic takes one real dispute of your own from the first note to the money in your account. In this lesson you choose the dispute, test it against five filters, and build the two documents everything else rests on: the facts file and the case log. By the end you will know whether your dispute is worth running, against whom, for how much, and by when you must act.

## Why it matters

Modules 3, 4 and 8 taught the law of contract remedies, torts, small claims and enforcement. A clinic is different: you are no longer answering a supplied question but running a file, and files are lost less on law than on organisation. Self-represented parties lose because they sued the trade name instead of the company, cannot find the WhatsApp message that proves the promise, count a deadline from the wrong day, or claim a sum they cannot explain. Law clinics at Israeli universities start every case the same way: intake, a facts memo, a chronology and a file. This lesson is your intake.

Choosing well also matters for the law. Only an individual suing in his own right may use the small claims court (Courts Law s. 60(א)); a claim bought or transferred from someone else may not be brought there; and representing someone else is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20). If the dispute is your partner's, your parent's or your company's, it is not your clinic case.

## The law

### Limitation: Prescription Law s. 5 and s. 9

> התקופה שבה מתיישנת תביעה שלא הוגשה עליה תובענה (להלן – תקופת ההתיישנות) היא – (1) בשאינו מקרקעין – שבע שנים…

*Gist:* A civil claim not concerning land must be filed within seven years from the day the cause of action arose; after that, the defendant can have it dismissed. ([Prescription Law s. 5](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההתיישנות#סעיף_5); the period is also in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

> הודה הנתבע, בכתב או בפני בית משפט, בין בתוך תקופת ההתיישנות ובין לאחריה, בקיום זכות התובע, תתחיל תקופת ההתיישנות מיום ההודאה; ומעשה שיש בו משום ביצוע מקצת הזכות, דינו כהודאה לענין סעיף זה.

*Gist:* A written acknowledgement of your right, or a part payment, restarts the period from that day, unless the acknowledgement is coupled with a limitation plea. Keep every "we will pay you" email: it is evidence and it resets the clock. ([s. 9](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההתיישנות#סעיף_9))

Negotiations, a complaint to a regulator or a letter you send do not stop the clock. Most everyday disputes are filed within months, so limitation rarely bites; it matters for the old loan or the "I'll deal with it one day" refund.

### Who may sue in small claims: Courts Law s. 60(א)

The small claims court hears civil claims filed by an individual (יחיד) for money up to a linked ceiling, or for an order to replace, repair or cancel a purchase within that value, but not a claim "על פי זכות שהומחתה" (an assigned right). ([s. 60](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט#סעיף_60); ceiling in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); the full comparison with the magistrates' court is lesson [08.3](../module-08/lesson-03.md).)

### Your own matter only: Bar Association Law s. 20

Only a lawyer may represent another person before courts and the Execution Office, draft legal documents for another, or give legal advice as an occupation. ([s. 20](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לשכת_עורכי_הדין)) You may help a friend understand this course; you may not run their file.

### The supplied clinic case: Rental and Borrowing Law s. 25י

Because many learners will use a rental deposit, the module's supplied case is one. The landlord may use a security only for unpaid rent, repairs the tenant is responsible for, unpaid running charges, or not vacating (s. 25י(ג)); must give reasonable advance notice and a chance to fix (s. 25י(ד)); and must return it, with its fruits, within a set period after the flat is returned (s. 25י(ה)). ([s. 25י](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_השכירות_והשאילה#סעיף_25י); the cap and the return period are in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); the contract side is lesson [03.9](../module-03/lesson-09.md).) The tenant is responsible only for defects caused by unreasonable use (s. 25ח(א)), not for ordinary wear.

## Leading cases

### רע״א 9615/05 שמש נ׳ פוקצ׳טה בע״מ (5.7.2006)

A self-represented mother's small claim against a restaurant that let customers smoke reached the Supreme Court, which increased her compensation and stressed that private claims are a legitimate way to enforce protective statutes. Why it matters for choosing: a small dispute with a clear statutory duty and good evidence is worth running, and the forum takes it seriously. [English translation (Cardozo)](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/346)

### Key text: the statute that gives you the claim

For most clinic cases there is no single leading case; the decisive text is the protective statute (the Rental and Borrowing Law's chapter on residential leases, the Consumer Protection Law, the Remedies Law). Find the section that gives you the right, the one that sets the other side's duty, and the one that sets the remedy, and put all three on page 1 of your facts file.

## How it works in practice

### Five filters for a clinic dispute

| Filter | Pass | Fail: choose another dispute or another route |
|---|---|---|
| Yours | You are the person who paid, rented, was injured or was promised | Your company's debt, your parent's flat, an assigned debt |
| Civil and money-shaped | A refund, a deposit, a repair cost, unpaid fees, replacement of goods | An order to stop a nuisance (magistrates' court), anything criminal, family or employment (labour court) |
| Size | Within the small-claims ceiling, worth your time | Above the ceiling and you do not want to give up the excess; or a sum so small the time costs more (lesson 11.5) |
| Time | Well inside seven years; the facts are fresh | Near the limitation date, or a deadline already missed |
| Evidence | You hold, or can lawfully get, documents for every key fact | Your word against theirs, with nothing in writing |

If no dispute in your life passes, use the supplied case below. Avoid for this clinic: personal injury, land, disputes with your employer, family matters, and anything where you might be a suspect. Lesson 12.8 explains why.

### The facts file

One folder (paper or a cloud folder) with five parts:

1. **Cover page.** Your claim in two sentences; the opponent's exact legal name and number; the remedy and the amount; the limitation date; the forum.
2. **Parties.** For a company: the name and number on the invoice or contract, checked in the Companies Registrar's free public search, and the registered address. For a sole trader: name and ID or business number from the receipt. For a private landlord: name, ID number and address from the lease.
3. **Chronology.** A table, one row per event: date, what happened, who said what, which exhibit proves it. Facts only; no adjectives.
4. **Evidence index.** Numbered exhibits (נספח 1, 2, 3…), each with date, type, source and the fact it proves (lesson 12.2 adds how to collect them lawfully).
5. **Remedy calculation.** Each head of claim with its source: the price paid, the deposit, the repair quote, damages without proof where a statute gives them (lesson 12.4), and the costs you will ask for.

### The case log

A running record, newest entry at the bottom, that you keep until the file is closed: every letter sent and received (with proof of delivery), every call (date, time, name, what was said), every deadline you set or the law sets, and the next action with its date. The log is the file's memory when you return to it after a month; it also becomes your evidence that you acted reasonably (lesson 12.3).

## Worked example

**The clinic case.** Tsega Mekonen, a nurse in Netanya, rented a flat from Avraham Levi, a private landlord, for two years at ₪5,400 a month under a written lease. She paid a cash security of ₪10,800 against a signed receipt. She left on 31 July 2026 and handed over the keys; she photographed every room that day and sent the photos to Avraham on WhatsApp. On 2 August Avraham wrote: "הכל בסדר, אחזיר לך את הפיקדון אחרי שאבדוק את חשבון המים". On 20 September he wrote that he was keeping the whole sum "for painting and wear". He never told her before keeping it, and never sent a painter's quote.

**Five filters.** Hers: yes, she is the tenant who paid. Civil money: yes, return of a deposit. Size: within the ceiling. Time: weeks old. Evidence: lease, receipt, handover photos, two WhatsApp messages.

**Cover page (her draft).** "אבקש להשיב לי את הערובה בסך 10,800 ש"ח ששילמתי למשכיר. המשכיר לא הודיע לי מראש על כוונה לממש אותה, לא נתן לי הזדמנות לתקן, ולא הציג ליקוי שנגרם משימוש בלתי סביר." Opponent: Avraham Levi, ID from the lease, address from the lease. Remedy: ₪10,800 plus linkage and interest, less any water bill she actually owes (she checks: ₪180, which she offers to pay). Limitation: 2033. Forum: small claims, Netanya (where the defendant lives and the flat is).

**What she learns from the law.** Section 25י(ג) lists the only uses of a security; painting after two years is usually ordinary wear unless damage from unreasonable use is shown (s. 25ח(א)); and s. 25י(ד) required advance notice and a chance to fix. Avraham's 2 August message is also an acknowledgement in writing (Prescription Law s. 9). Her case log starts with three entries: the handover, the 2 August message, the 20 September refusal.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A list of the money disputes in your life in the last two years, or the clinic case above.
- Your documents for the dispute you pick: contract, receipts, bank statements, messages.
- The [forum check worksheet](../../templates/forum-check-worksheet.md) and the draft [clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md) template.
- About 90 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Shortlist (15 min).** Write down up to three disputes. Run each through the five filters in the table; record pass or fail with one reason.
2. **Choose (5 min).** Pick the one that passes all five. If none does, use the clinic case.
3. **Identify the opponent (15 min).** Find the exact legal name and number on the invoice, receipt or contract; check a company in the Companies Registrar's public search and copy the registered address.
4. **Chronology (20 min).** Build the table: date, event, exhibit. Read every message again; quote the decisive words exactly.
5. **Evidence index (15 min).** Number your exhibits. Mark the facts that have no exhibit yet: they are your homework for lesson 12.2.
6. **Remedy and limitation (10 min).** Write each head of claim with its source and the total. Compute the limitation date (s. 5) and note any acknowledgement or part payment that restarted it (s. 9).
7. **Open the case log (10 min).** Enter every past step you can document, then the next action with a date.

### How you know it worked

- Someone who has never heard of your dispute can read the cover page and say who owes what to whom, and why.
- Every row in the chronology points to an exhibit or is marked "no exhibit yet".
- The opponent's name and number match an official source.
- The remedy adds up, and every shekel has a source.

### Self-check

- [ ] The dispute is mine, not someone else's.
- [ ] I checked the opponent's legal identity, not the shop sign.
- [ ] I wrote down the limitation date and what restarts it.
- [ ] My case log has a next action with a date.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Building your own file is the core of acting for yourself; you need no permission. You may ask a friend to read it, but no one other than a lawyer may run it for you as an occupation or for pay (Bar Association Law s. 20).

### Where and how

- Companies Registrar public search (free) for company names and numbers; the business receipt for a sole trader's number.
- Forum, ceiling and periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); forum rules: [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md); help: [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The dispute turns out to involve land rights, bodily injury, your employer or a family member.
- The limitation date is close and you are unsure when the cause of action arose.
- The other side says it will sue you for more than you claim (a counterclaim above the ceiling).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Suing the trade name ("Super Deal") instead of the company | Claim against the wrong party; enforcement fails later | Amend the defendant's name before judgment | Copy the name and number from the receipt and check the Registrar |
| Choosing your partner's dispute because "it's the same household" | You are not the party; s. 20 problem if you run it for them | They file in their own name | Apply filter 1 first |
| Chronology full of opinions ("he cheated me") | The judge cannot see the facts | Rewrite: date, act, words, exhibit | One fact per row |
| Claim total with no breakdown | Court awards less, or nothing for the unexplained part | Add the calculation with sources | Build the remedy table before writing anything |
| No record of calls | You cannot prove what was promised | Confirm calls in writing afterwards | Log every call the same day |

## Review

- A clinic dispute must be yours, civil and money-shaped, within the ceiling, in time, and provable.
- The facts file has a cover page, parties, chronology, evidence index and remedy calculation.
- The case log records every step and deadline until enforcement is complete.
- Limitation is seven years for most claims; a written acknowledgement or part payment restarts it (Prescription Law s. 9).
