# 12.8 · When to stop and get a lawyer, and where to get free help

Acting for yourself is a skill, and part of the skill is knowing where it ends. In this lesson you apply a concrete checklist to your own file to decide whether to carry on alone, buy one hour of advice, or hand the case over, and you map the free help that exists for people who cannot afford a lawyer. You finish the clinic with a one-page summary that makes any first meeting with a lawyer, Legal Aid or a clinic short and useful.

## Why it matters

The course's promise is limited by design. You may act for yourself in any Israeli court; only a lawyer may represent others (Bar Association Law s. 20). In small claims, lawyers are the exception (Courts Law s. 63). But some disputes change character: the other side files a counterclaim, a company sends its lawyer, the case is transferred to the magistrates' court, or you realise the real issue is land, employment or family. Carrying on alone at that point can cost more than the dispute is worth. Equally, many people pay for a lawyer they do not need, or give up because they think help costs money, when they are eligible for free Legal Aid or a clinic.

## The law

### The line you may not cross: Bar Association Law s. 20

Only a lawyer may, for another person or for pay, represent before courts, tribunals and listed offices (including the Execution Office), draft legal documents, or give legal advice and opinions; the exceptions in s. 21 (religious courts, patent agents, accountants within their laws, unions in labour matters and others) do not cover helping a friend with his small claim. ([ss. 20–21](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לשכת_עורכי_הדין)) The course trains you to run your own file and no one else's.

### Lawyers in small claims: Courts Law s. 63

A lawyer may represent a party in small claims only with the court's leave, for special reasons recorded; a non-lawyer may represent with the court's approval and a power of attorney, unless he represents regularly, in the course of business or for pay. ([s. 63](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט#סעיף_63)) A lawyer can still advise you outside the courtroom and help you write.

### Legal Aid: Legal Aid Law ss. 2–3 and the Schedule

> לשכת סיוע משפטי תתן שירות משפטי, לרבות ההוצאות הכרוכות בו, בענינים ובהיקף שנקבעו בתקנות, למי שידו אינה משגת לשאת בהם, או למי שנקבע בתוספת בענינים ובהיקף שנקבעו בה…

*Gist:* Legal Aid gives legal services, including expenses, in the matters set by regulations to people who cannot afford them, and to the groups listed in the Schedule in the matters listed there, often without a means test. ([s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הסיוע_המשפטי#סעיף_2)) A written application is required (s. 3(א)); a certificate from the local social-services department is prima facie proof of inability to pay (s. 3(ד)).

A Schedule item added in 5786 (2025–26) gives people aged 65 and over legal aid, without the means test, in proceedings for mistake, deceit, duress or exploitation in a contract, or consumer deceit or unfair influence, based on exploiting their difficulty, weakness, cognitive or health condition or language difficulties (Schedule item 16). ([Schedule](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הסיוע_המשפטי#תוספת))

### What Legal Aid covers, and the means test: Legal Aid Regulations regs 2, 4, 5, 6

Matters include housing rights (eviction, rent, repairs) and money matters including torts (reg. 5(2)–(3)), labour-court matters (5(4)) and benefits for people with disabilities (5(5)); the service includes advice, drafting and representation, including in alternative dispute resolution (reg. 6). The means test in the regulations is an income ceiling set as a percentage of the national average wage, adjusted for family size, with assets taken into account (reg. 2; current figures on the Legal Aid site, ⚠️ manual check). The head of the office may provide a lawyer, or decide that the applicant will appear in person and instruct him in writing how to act (reg. 4(א)(5)), a form of help well suited to small claims. ([Regulations](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הסיוע_המשפטי)) A merit test also applies (the claim must have a reasonable chance); a modest participation fee may be charged (reg. 1א).

### The Israel Consumer Council: Council Law s. 2

The Council may advise consumers, intervene with businesses, and represent consumers before courts (s. 2(א)(1), (4)(ג)). ([s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המועצה_הישראלית_לצרכנות))

## Leading cases

### רע״א 5234/17 (representation in small claims)

A small-claims judge need not tell an unrepresented party that he may ask for permission to be represented (⚠️ manual check: decision text on the Judiciary's site). Why it matters here: the decision to bring help is yours, and you must ask the court for it in time.

### Key text: s. 20 of the Bar Association Law

There is no ruling more relevant to this lesson than the statutory monopoly itself. Lesson [01.6](../module-01/lesson-06.md) set out the profession and its limits; this lesson applies them to the end of a real file.

## How it works in practice

### The decision checklist

Go through it at three points: before you send the demand letter, before you file, and when the defence (or anything unexpected) arrives. One **red** answer means get a lawyer; two or more **amber** answers mean get at least one paid or free consultation.

| # | Question | Red (get a lawyer) | Amber (get advice) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Is the matter outside an individual's money claim (land rights, employment, family, inheritance, bodily injury)? | Yes | — |
| 2 | Could you face criminal exposure (fraud, harassment, privacy offences, a police complaint against you)? | Yes | — |
| 3 | Is the amount at stake, including any counterclaim, above the small-claims ceiling, or more than a month of your income? | Counterclaim above the ceiling | Above a month's income |
| 4 | Has the case been or may it be transferred to the magistrates' court (Courts Law s. 60(ב))? | Transferred, and the other side has a lawyer | Judge raised it |
| 5 | Does the other side have a lawyer with leave, or is it a large company with in-house counsel actively litigating? | — | Yes |
| 6 | Does the case turn on interpreting a complex contract, a statute you cannot find a clear answer to, or expert evidence? | Expert evidence disputed | Interpretation or statute unclear |
| 7 | Is a deadline you cannot meet approaching (limitation, leave to appeal, setting aside an absence judgment)? | Yes, within days | Within weeks |
| 8 | Do you want to appeal, or answer an appeal, on a point of law? | — | Yes |
| 9 | Is there a threat against you of a defamation or privacy suit over what you posted or collected? | Suit filed | Threat made |
| 10 | Are you unable, for health, language or time reasons, to prepare and attend? | — | Yes: ask about Legal Aid or an interpreter |

### Kinds of help, cheapest first

| Help | What you get | Cost | When it fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kol-Zchut and the Judiciary's guides | Plain-language procedure | Free | Always, first |
| Judiciary telephone information centre (077-2703333 or *3852; ⚠️ manual check) and WhatsApp channel | Procedure, case status, hearing logistics; not legal advice | Free | Before filing and before the hearing |
| Volunteer desks at some courts | Help completing forms | Free | Drafting the claim |
| Israel Consumer Council | Advice and intervention with businesses; selective representation | Free | Consumer disputes |
| Legal Aid | Advice, drafting, representation, or instructions to appear yourself | Free or a small participation fee, if eligible | Money and housing matters within the means test; seniors in exploitation cases without a means test |
| University law clinics | Supervised students and lawyers, in the clinic's field | Free | If your issue fits a clinic's field that year |
| One-off paid consultation | A lawyer reviews your file and answers your questions | A fee agreed in advance | Amber answers on the checklist |
| Full representation | A lawyer runs the case | Fee agreement | Red answers |

### Getting the most from one hour

Bring the one-page summary (below), the facts file, and no more than five questions, written. Ask for a written fee agreement before the meeting if you pay. Ask the lawyer to tell you what you can safely do yourself.

## Worked example

**A case that crossed the line.** Yehuda Friedman, a bookbinder in Bnei Brak, sued a renovation contractor in small claims for ₪7,000 for a leaking bathroom. The contractor's defence included a counterclaim for ₪58,000 "for extra works and defamation", citing Yehuda's post in a neighbourhood WhatsApp group naming him. Checklist: item 3 red (counterclaim above the ceiling), item 9 amber-to-red (a defamation claim), item 4 amber (the judge may transfer). Yehuda stops. He books a one-hour consultation, deletes nothing (deleting could look like destroying evidence) but posts nothing more, and on the lawyer's advice asks the court in writing for a short postponement to obtain advice.

**A case that qualified for free help.** Galina Petrova, 74, from Ashdod, signed at her door a ₪12,000 contract for a "health mattress" after a two-hour sales pitch in Hebrew she barely understood. Her cancellation period is long past. She calls the Judiciary's information centre about small claims, and the Israel Consumer Council, which suggests Legal Aid. Under Schedule item 16 of the Legal Aid Law, as a person over 65 alleging consumer deceit or unfair influence that exploited her age and language difficulties, she may get legal aid without a means test. She applies in writing, attaching her contract and a one-page summary her granddaughter helped her type.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your complete clinic file (lessons 12.1–12.7).
- The draft [get-a-lawyer checklist](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md) template.
- About an hour.

### Steps

1. **Run the checklist three times (20 min).** As of before your letter, before filing, and today (or after the defence). Record red and amber answers with one reason each.
2. **Decide (5 min).** Continue alone, consultation, or hand over. Write the decision and the date in your case log.
3. **Find your help (15 min).** For your dispute, list the two most suitable sources from the table, with their current contact route; check whether you may meet the Legal Aid tests (subject, means, merit; or a Schedule group).
4. **One-page summary (15 min).** Parties; the claim in two sentences; amount; three key documents; stage reached and next deadline; what you want from the meeting.
5. **Five questions (5 min).** Write the five questions you would ask a lawyer about your file.

### Template

- [Get-a-lawyer checklist and one-page case summary (מתי לפנות לעורך דין – רשימת בדיקה ותקציר תיק)](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md) (new in this module).

### How you know it worked

- You can say in one sentence why you are continuing alone, or why not.
- Your summary fits on one page and states the next deadline.
- You know which free service you would call first and how.

### Self-check

- [ ] I know I may not run anyone else's case (s. 20).
- [ ] I know a lawyer appears in small claims only with leave (s. 63), but can advise me outside court.
- [ ] I checked whether I may qualify for Legal Aid, including the over-65 consumer item.
- [ ] My decision is recorded in the case log.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Not a filing lesson: this is the decision about whether to continue doing it yourself. Applying for Legal Aid, calling the information centre and booking a consultation are all things you do for yourself.

### Where and how

- Legal Aid: written application, online or at a district office ([gov.il service page](https://www.gov.il/he/service/legal_aid_application), ⚠️ manual check); attach income documents or a social-services certificate (Legal Aid Law s. 3(ד)).
- Judiciary information centre: 077-2703333 or *3852, Sunday–Thursday working hours (as published on gov.il service pages found on 17 October 2026, ⚠️ manual check).
- University clinics: the law faculties' clinic pages (for example [Tel Aviv University's clinics](https://en-law.tau.ac.il/clinics)); fields change yearly.
- Everything else: [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md) and [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- Any red answer on the checklist.
- Two or more amber answers and you cannot get free advice in time.
- You are tempted to help a friend run his case: that is not yours to run (s. 20).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ignoring a counterclaim above the ceiling | Transfer, full procedure, costs exposure | Get advice before the next date | Run the checklist when the defence arrives |
| Paying a lawyer to run a ₪3,000 small claim | Fees exceed the claim; lawyer may not appear anyway (s. 63) | One-off consultation instead | Match the help to the stakes |
| Assuming Legal Aid is only for criminal cases | Free help missed | Apply in writing | Check reg. 5 matters and the Schedule |
| Coming to a consultation with a bag of papers and no questions | Hour wasted | Send the summary in advance | One-page summary and five questions |
| "Helping" a neighbour by writing and filing his claim | Breach of s. 20 if done as an occupation or for pay; responsibility for errors | Let him file in his own name; point him to free help | Teach, do not represent |

## Review

- Run the decision checklist at three points; one red means a lawyer, two ambers mean advice.
- You may act only for yourself (Bar Association Law s. 20); in small claims a lawyer appears only with leave (Courts Law s. 63) but may advise you.
- Legal Aid has subject, means and merit tests; some groups, including seniors exploited in consumer deals (Schedule item 16), need no means test; it can instruct you to appear yourself (reg. 4(א)(5)).
- Free help also comes from the Judiciary's information centre, volunteer desks, the Consumer Council and university clinics.
- A one-page summary and five questions make any consultation worth its hour.
