# 12.5 · Writing the small-claims statement

You have a facts file, evidence and a letter that was ignored or a negotiation that failed. In this lesson you turn them into a statement of claim that a small-claims judge can read in five minutes and decide on: a heading with the right parties, a short story with exhibits, a legal argument in three steps, an itemised remedy, and every item reg. 3 now requires. Lesson [04.9](../module-04/lesson-09.md) drafted a tort claim; this lesson drafts a contract, consumer or deposit claim and ends with a filing decision.

## Why it matters

In small claims the statement of claim is most of your case. There are no written summations, the hearing is short, and the judge prepares by reading your claim and the defence. A claim that tells the story in order, shows each document at the point it matters, and asks for a precise sum is half-way to judgment. A claim that is a long complaint with attachments at the end makes the judge build your case for you, and judges under time pressure often do not.

Since 11 April 2025 the regulations require more: a fixed heading, a body of no more than five pages, lists of documents and witnesses, your hearing preference, the number of small claims you filed in the past year, and a signed declaration with the source of your knowledge. Missing items invite a request to complete the claim, delay, or a weaker first impression.

## The law

### The heading: reg. 3(ב) (proceedings from 11.4.2025)

> כותרת כתב תביעה תכלול את הפרטים האלה: (1) שם התובע ומספר זהותו; (2) מען מגוריו ופרטי התקשרות של התובע, לרבות דואר אלקטרוני ומספר טלפון נייד; (3) שם הנתבע ומספר זהותו אם ניתן לבררו, ואם הנתבע הוא תאגיד – מספר הרישום; (4) מען מגוריו או מען עסקו של הנתבע ופרטי התקשרות עימו…; (5) הסעד המבוקש.

*Gist:* The heading gives both parties' names, ID or registration numbers, addresses and contact details as far as you can find them, and the remedy. ([reg. 3](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיפוט_בתביעות_קטנות_(סדרי_דין)))

### The body: reg. 3(ג) and (ה)

> בגוף כתב התביעה יפרט התובע את כל העניינים האלה: (1) מקום המעשה או המחדל שבשלו תובעים; (2) הסעד המבוקש; (3) נימוקי התביעה; (4) רשימת המסמכים…; (5) רשימת העדים…; (6) אם הוא מעדיף לקיים את הדיון באמצעי טכנולוגי… וכן אם הוא מעדיף הכרעה על יסוד הכתב…; (7) מספר התביעות הקטנות שהגיש במהלך השנה האחרונה.
>
> (ה) גוף כתב התביעה לא יעלה על חמישה עמודים.

*Gist:* The body sets out the place of the act, the remedy, the grounds, the documents, the witnesses, your preference for a remote hearing or a decision on the papers, and how many small claims you filed in the last year, in no more than five pages. ([reg. 3](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיפוט_בתביעות_קטנות_(סדרי_דין)))

### Attachments and declaration: reg. 3(ד)

You attach the documents you rely on that are in your possession, and a declaration that everything in the claim is true or to the best of your knowledge, stating the source of your knowledge; the court may allow a document filed later if it is needed for judgment. ([reg. 3(ד)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיפוט_בתביעות_קטנות_(סדרי_דין)))

### Venue: reg. 2

> תביעה תוגש לבית המשפט שאזור שיפוטו הוא אחד המקומות המנויים להלן, והוא על אף האמור בכל הסכם שבין בעלי הדין…

*Gist:* File where the defendant lives or does business, or where the act or omission occurred, whatever a contract says; for internet publication or trade, only where the defendant or the plaintiff lives or does business (reg. 2(ב)); with several defendants, any court competent for one of them (reg. 2(ג)). ([reg. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיפוט_בתביעות_קטנות_(סדרי_דין)))

### The substantive sections you will plead

- **Deposit:** Rental and Borrowing Law s. 25י(ג)–(ה) and s. 25ח(א) (lesson 12.1). ([s. 25י](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_השכירות_והשאילה#סעיף_25י))
- **Consumer:** the specific duty (cancellation and refund, misleading, overcharge), plus exemplary damages under s. 31א where a listed breach occurred and you sent a written request (lesson 12.3). ([s. 31א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_הצרכן#סעיף_31א))
- **Contract:** breach and the remedy under the Remedies Law: restitution after cancellation, damages, or price reduction (lesson [03.8](../module-03/lesson-08.md)).

## Leading cases

### רע״א 9615/05 שמש נ׳ פוקצ׳טה בע״מ (5.7.2006)

The Supreme Court treated a self-represented small claim based on a protective statute as a serious vehicle for enforcing it. Why it matters for drafting: plead the statute expressly, by section, and connect each element to an exhibit; the court will apply real doctrine to a small claim. [English translation (Cardozo)](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/346)

### Key text: reg. 3 as amended in 2025

There is no ruling yet that matters more to a drafter than the text of reg. 3 itself. Read it before you write, and tick each item on the checklist below.

## How it works in practice

### The shape of a good body (five pages or fewer)

1. **Place** (one line): where the act happened and why this court.
2. **The parties** (two lines): who you are; who the defendant is and its role.
3. **The facts** (one to two pages): numbered paragraphs, chronological, each with "(נספח X)".
4. **The grounds** (half a page to a page): three steps, each with its section: the right ("the tenant is entitled to the security's return"), the breach ("no notice, no listed purpose"), the remedy ("return within the period; linkage and interest").
5. **The amount** (a table): each head, its sum, its source, its exhibit; deductions you accept.
6. **The defendant's expected answer** (two or three lines, optional): what he said in correspondence and why it fails. This often helps the judge.
7. **Documents, witnesses, hearing preference, previous claims, remedy, declaration.**

### Choosing the amount

Claim what you can explain. Add statutory no-proof damages only where a section gives them and its conditions are met. A claim for "distress" (עוגמת נפש) in a contract or consumer case is possible but awarded modestly and only with facts that show real distress; do not let it dominate. Remember the ceiling and do not split one claim in two (lesson [08.3](../module-08/lesson-03.md)).

### Filing checklist

| Item | Source | ✔ |
|---|---|---|
| Right court (defendant's area or place of act) | reg. 2 | |
| Heading: both parties' names, numbers, addresses, contacts; remedy | reg. 3(ב) | |
| Body ≤ 5 pages | reg. 3(ה) | |
| Place, remedy, grounds, documents list, witnesses list | reg. 3(ג)(1)–(5) | |
| Hearing preference (in court / video; decision on the papers or not) | reg. 3(ג)(6), reg. 13 | |
| Number of small claims filed in the last year | reg. 3(ג)(7) | |
| Documents attached as numbered PDFs | reg. 3(ד)(1) | |
| Signed declaration with the source of knowledge | reg. 3(ד)(2) | |

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- Your facts file, evidence index and the letters with proof of delivery (lessons 12.1–12.3).
- The draft [small claim for a contract or consumer dispute](../../templates/small-claim-contract-consumer.md) template, or the [tort template](../../templates/small-claim-property-damage.md) for damage claims.
- Regulations 2, 3 and 13 open in another window. About two and a half hours.

### Steps

1. **Venue and parties (15 min).** Decide the court under reg. 2 and fill the heading from part ב of your file.
2. **Facts (40 min).** Copy your chronology into numbered paragraphs; cut every adjective; put "(נספח X)" in every paragraph that a document proves.
3. **Grounds (30 min).** Write the three steps (right, breach, remedy), each with its section and the decisive exhibit.
4. **Amount (15 min).** Paste the remedy table; check the arithmetic and the interest start date.
5. **The rest (15 min).** Documents and witnesses lists, hearing preference, previous claims, declaration.
6. **Length and read-aloud (15 min).** Check the body is within five pages. Read it aloud in under five minutes; cut what does not help.
7. **Checklist and decision (20 min).** Tick the filing checklist. Decide: file now in your own name (real), file after one more attempt to settle, or keep as a dry run. Write the decision and its reason in your case log. If you file, follow lesson [08.8](../module-08/lesson-08.md) for the online route and diary the service check.

- [Small claim for property damage](../../templates/small-claim-property-damage.md) (lesson 04.9).

### How you know it worked

- Every item in the filing checklist is ticked or deliberately left out with a reason.
- A reader can find the document for each fact by its number without leafing.
- Your grounds name the right, the breach and the remedy with sections.
- The amount table matches the remedy line and the letter.

### Self-check

- [ ] The body is within five pages.
- [ ] I stated my hearing preference and my previous small claims.
- [ ] The declaration names the source of my knowledge.
- [ ] I recorded my filing decision in the case log.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Small claims are designed for individuals acting for themselves; a lawyer appears only with the court's leave (Courts Law s. 63). Only an individual may sue there.

### Where and how

- Online through the Judiciary's small-claims service with the national digital ID, PDFs only; or on paper at the registry of the right court (lesson [08.3](../module-08/lesson-03.md)). Fee or exemption request: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Help with drafting: volunteer desks in some courts; the Judiciary's information centre (lesson 12.8); [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The defendant files a counterclaim above the ceiling, or a third-party notice against someone else.
- The court transfers your claim to the magistrates' court (Courts Law s. 60(ב)).
- Your claim depends on interpreting a complex contract, or on expert evidence the other side disputes.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ten-page narrative | Breaches reg. 3(ה); judge skims | Cut to facts, grounds, amount | Write from the chronology, not from memory |
| Exhibits attached but never referred to | Judge misses the decisive document | Add "(נספח X)" to the paragraphs | One exhibit number per fact |
| Suing the trade name | Wrong defendant; enforcement fails | Ask to amend the defendant's name | Heading from the Registrar check |
| Exemplary damages claimed without the written request | That head dismissed | Drop it or show the request | Check s. 31א(ב) first |
| Interest from "the day I moved out" | Wrong start date | Correct in the hearing | Count from the date payment fell due |
| Declaration unsigned or without source of knowledge | Claim incomplete (reg. 3(ד)(2)) | File a signed declaration | Use the checklist |

## Review

- The heading, body and attachments are fixed by reg. 3 for proceedings opened from 11.4.2025; the body is at most five pages.
- Write facts with exhibit numbers, then grounds in three steps (right, breach, remedy) with sections, then an amount table.
- Choose the court by reg. 2; state your hearing preference and previous claims; sign the declaration.
- Decide deliberately whether to file now, and record why.
