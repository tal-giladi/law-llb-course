# 12.4 · Consumer complaints and regulators before court

Before you sue a business, and sometimes instead of suing it, you can ask a public body to look at what it did. In this lesson you map the regulators that handle everyday consumer, banking, insurance and telecom complaints, learn exactly what their findings can and cannot do for you, and file one complaint that a regulator can act on.

## Why it matters

A complaint to a regulator is free, needs no court fee and no hearing, and a regulated business usually answers the regulator faster and more carefully than it answers you. A bank told by the Supervisor of Banks that a fee was charged against its rules usually refunds it. For a small sum, that is often the cheapest route.

But a regulator is not a court. Its findings give you no new right, it does not award compensation, and filing a complaint does not stop limitation or any court deadline. Some regulators will not look at a matter already before a court. And some of the strongest consumer remedies (exemplary damages without proof of loss) are only available from a court. Lesson [07.3](../module-07/lesson-03.md) explained regulators as institutions; this lesson is about using them in your own dispute.

## The law

### The Consumer Protection Commissioner: Consumer Protection Law s. 20

> (א) תפקידי הממונה יהיו – … (2) לטפל בתלונות שראה בהן ממש על הפרת הוראות חוק זה או על פגיעה אחרת בצרכן;

*Gist:* The Commissioner, who heads the Authority, supervises the Law, investigates offences, imposes administrative sanctions and handles complaints it finds substantial; a complaint in a field another regulator supervises may be referred to that regulator (s. 20(ב)). ([s. 20](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_הצרכן#סעיף_20)) The Authority's power is mainly enforcement against the business, not recovering your money; it may, however, lead the business to settle with you.

### The Israel Consumer Council: Council Law s. 2

> (4) סיוע לצרכנים במימוש זכויותיהם מול גופים פרטיים וציבוריים, בין השאר, על ידי – … (ג) ייצוג צרכנים לפני בית משפט, בית דין או כל ערכאה שיפוטית…

*Gist:* The Council, a government company set up by statute, gives consumers services and assistance, including advice, and may take legal proceedings and represent consumers before courts. ([s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המועצה_הישראלית_לצרכנות)) In practice it intervenes with the business on your behalf; representation in court is selective.

### Banks and credit cards: Banking (Service to Customer) Law s. 16

> (ד) החלטותיו וממצאיו של המפקח בבירור פניה – (1) לא יהיה בהם כדי להעניק לפונה או לאדם אחר זכות או סעד בבית משפט… שלא היו לו לפני כן; (2) … אם נקבע לכך מועד בחיקוק, לא יוארך המועד על ידי הגשת הפניה למפקח או בירורה.

*Gist:* The Supervisor of Banks investigates public complaints about banking corporations that it finds substantial, tells both sides whether the complaint was justified and how to fix the fault (s. 16(א)–(ג)); its findings create no new right in court and do not extend any statutory deadline. ([s. 16](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הבנקאות_(שירות_ללקוח))) The unit asks you to complain to the bank first and attach its answer (Kol-Zchut, ⚠️ manual check).

### Insurance, pensions and provident funds: Supervision of Financial Services (Insurance) Law ss. 60–62

> (ב) הממונה לא יברר תלונה בענין שהחל בו דיון לפני בית משפט או בורר, או שבית משפט או בורר הכריע בו, אלא מטעמים מיוחדים שיירשמו…

*Gist:* The Commissioner (the Capital Market Authority) investigates public complaints about insurers and agents, not bound by rules of procedure or evidence (s. 61(א)); if justified, it may order the insurer to fix the fault in your case or generally (s. 62(א)). It will not normally investigate a matter whose hearing has begun in court or before an arbitrator (s. 60(ב)): complain **before** the hearing starts. ([ss. 60–62](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הפיקוח_על_שירותים_פיננסיים_(ביטוח)))

### Telecom: the Ministry of Communications and the spam rule

The Ministry of Communications' public-complaints unit handles complaints about phone, mobile, internet and TV providers' compliance with their licences and the Communications Law (gov.il, ⚠️ manual check; no statutory complaints section was found in the free text). For unsolicited advertising messages a court, not the Ministry, can award damages without proof of loss for each message sent knowingly in breach (Communications Law s. 30א(י)(1); amount in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)). ([s. 30א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התקשורת_(בזק_ושידורים)#סעיף_30א))

### What only a court gives: exemplary damages, Consumer Protection Law s. 31א

A court may award exemplary damages, unrelated to loss, for listed breaches: not refunding under the dealer's posted returns policy (or posting none), continuing to charge after the end of a fixed-term deal or after a valid cancellation of a continuing transaction, not refunding an overcharge, and others; higher for repeated or aggravated breaches; only after a prior written request (s. 31א(ב), lesson 12.3). ([s. 31א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_הצרכן#סעיף_31א); caps in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

## Leading cases

### Key text: s. 16(ד) of the Banking (Service to Customer) Law

The everyday rule for every regulator's complaint process is set out most clearly here: findings give no new right and do not extend deadlines. Regulators' complaint processes are administrative, not judicial; a finding that your complaint was justified is evidence you can attach to a small claim, but the court decides for itself. If a regulator refuses to act or acts unlawfully, the remedy is administrative law (an administrative petition), which is a lawyer's task (lesson 12.8).

### Key text: s. 60(ב) of the Insurance Supervision Law

The order of steps matters. A regulator that will not investigate once a court hearing has begun forces you to decide early: complain first and wait, or go straight to court.

## How it works in practice

### Which body?

| Your dispute | First | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Shop, online seller, service provider: misleading sale, refund, warranty, cancellation | The business in writing (12.3) | Consumer Protection and Fair Trade Authority complaint; or Israel Consumer Council for help; small claim |
| Bank or credit-card company: fee, charge, refusal, loan terms | The bank's own public-enquiries unit (נציבות פניות הציבור of the bank) | Bank of Israel, Banking Supervision public-enquiries unit (online, national digital ID login, ⚠️ manual check) |
| Insurer, insurance agent, pension or provident fund | The insurer's ombudsman or claims department | Capital Market Authority public-enquiries unit (⚠️ manual check) — before any court hearing starts |
| Mobile, internet, landline, TV provider | The provider's public-enquiries unit | Ministry of Communications complaints unit (⚠️ manual check) |
| Spam texts, calls or emails | Ask the sender to remove you | Small claim for statutory damages per message (s. 30א(י)) |
| Private landlord, private seller, neighbour | — (no regulator) | Demand letter, mediation, small claim |

### What a complaint can achieve

- A finding that the complaint is justified and an instruction to the business to correct it, sometimes including a refund (banking s. 16(ב); insurance s. 62(א)).
- Enforcement against the business (Consumer Protection Authority sanctions), which helps other consumers more than you.
- Pressure that leads to a settlement.

What it cannot do: award damages, give you a judgment you can take to the Execution Office, or stop the clock on limitation.

### A complaint that works

1. Who you are, your account or policy number.
2. The business's legal name and number.
3. The facts in numbered short paragraphs, with dates.
4. Your prior approach to the business and its answer (attach both).
5. The rule you say was broken, if you know it, in one sentence.
6. The specific correction you ask for (refund ₪X; cancel the fee; reinstate the policy).
7. Attachments, numbered as in your facts file.

### Complain first, sue first, or both?

- **Complain first** when the sum is small, the business is regulated, and the rule is clear (a bank fee, a telecom overcharge): it costs nothing.
- **Sue first** when you want exemplary damages, compensation for distress, or the other side is unregulated; or when limitation or a contractual deadline is near.
- **Both**: you may complain and sue in parallel for most regulators; for insurance, complain before the court hearing begins. Tell the court about the complaint and attach any finding.

## Worked example

**Banking.** Salim Halabi, a lab technician from Daliyat al-Karmel, finds that his bank charged him ₪15 a month for six years for a "service package" he never asked for, after it was switched on during a branch visit. He writes to the bank's public-enquiries unit (12.3) and gets a refund of only 12 months.

His complaint to the Banking Supervision unit, filed online with the national digital ID:

- Facts: account number; the date the package started; no signed request; the bank's partial answer (attached).
- Correction sought: refund of the full period with interest, and cancellation of the package.
- Attachments: statements showing the charges, the bank's letter, his own letter.

Four months later the unit finds the complaint justified and instructs the bank to refund the full period. Salim notes in his log that, had the bank refused, the unit's finding would have been evidence in a small claim, but the claim's limitation would have kept running throughout (s. 16(ד)(2)).

**Telecom (Rami from lesson 12.2).** Rami complains to the Ministry of Communications about the price rise against the sales promise, attaching his recording's transcript; in parallel he sends the company a written request to stop overcharging and refund the difference, because if he later sues for exemplary damages he needs that written request (lesson 12.3).

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- Your facts file, your letter to the business and its answer (lesson 12.3).
- National digital ID login (הזדהות לאומית) for online complaint forms.
- The draft [regulator complaint](../../templates/regulator-complaint.md) template.
- About 75 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Map (10 min).** Using the table, write which body handles your opponent, and whether you have done the "first" step.
2. **Decide the order (10 min).** Complain first, sue first, or both, with one reason. Note limitation and any court date.
3. **Draft (30 min).** Fill the template: seven parts, numbered facts, the correction sought.
4. **File or dry-run (15 min).** Find the regulator's current online form (gov.il or the Bank of Israel site), fill it up to submission, and either submit (real, own name) or stop and screenshot the last screen (dry run).
5. **Log (10 min).** Record the complaint number, the date, and a follow-up date; diary any court deadlines that keep running.

If your dispute is with a private person (a landlord, as in the clinic case), there is no regulator: write a one-paragraph note in your file explaining why, and do steps 3–4 on Salim's facts instead.

### Template

- [Regulator complaint (פנייה לגורם מפקח)](../../templates/regulator-complaint.md) (new in this module).

### How you know it worked

- You named the right regulator for your opponent and did the business-first step.
- Your complaint asks for a specific correction and attaches the business's answer.
- Your log shows the complaint number and the deadlines that keep running regardless.

- [ ] I know a regulator's finding is not a judgment and does not stop limitation.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Every regulator listed accepts complaints from the person affected, free of charge. Others may help you write it, but drafting complaints for others as an occupation or for pay is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).

### Where and how

- Consumer Protection and Fair Trade Authority: online complaint form, [gov.il service page](https://www.gov.il/he/service/filing_a_complaint_to_fair_trade_authority) (⚠️ manual check: gov.il blocks automated access).
- Israel Consumer Council: online complaint and phone advice (⚠️ manual check of the current channels).
- Bank of Israel Banking Supervision public-enquiries unit: online form with national digital ID; see Kol-Zchut, [הגשת תלונה ליחידה לפניות הציבור של הפיקוח על הבנקים](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/הגשת_תלונה_ליחידה_לפניות_הציבור_של_הפיקוח_על_הבנקים) (⚠️ manual check).
- Capital Market Authority public-enquiries unit; Ministry of Communications complaints unit: gov.il (⚠️ manual check).
- Elderly consumers: an amendment of 5786 (2025–26, תשפ״ו) added to the Legal Aid Law's schedule legal aid for people aged 65 and over, without a means test, in proceedings for consumer deceit or unfair influence that exploited their difficulty, weakness or health (lesson 12.8).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The regulator's process is finished, the business still refuses, and the sum or the issue is large (for example an insurance claim for a home or a vehicle).
- You think many consumers were harmed the same way: that is class-action territory.
- You want to challenge the regulator's own decision.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Waiting for the regulator while limitation or a contractual claim period runs | Claim time-barred or late | File the claim now; the complaint can continue | Diary deadlines; s. 16(ד)(2) |
| Complaining to the regulator without first going to the business | Complaint returned or delayed | Write to the business, then refile | Do the "first" column |
| Insurance complaint after the court hearing started | Not investigated (s. 60(ב)) | Rely on the court | Complain before the hearing |
| Asking the Authority for exemplary damages | It cannot award them | Sue after a written request (s. 31א) | Know what only a court gives |
| Complaint with no specific correction | Generic answer, nothing changes | Refile with the remedy sought | Part 6 of the complaint |

## Review

- Each regulated sector has a complaints route: Consumer Protection Authority and Consumer Council (general consumer), Banking Supervision (banks and cards), Capital Market Authority (insurance and pensions), Ministry of Communications (telecom).
- Go to the business first, then the regulator; ask for a specific correction.
- A regulator's finding creates no new right and does not extend deadlines; only a court awards damages and exemplary damages.
- For insurance, complain before a court hearing begins.
