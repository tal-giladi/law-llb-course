# 17.2 · Protective legislation: minimum wage, hours, rest, leave and sick pay

By the end of this lesson you will be able to check the five floors that every employment in Israel must meet: the minimum wage, the limits on working hours with their overtime and rest-day premiums, the weekly rest, paid annual leave and sick pay. You will calculate each of them for a real month of work, which is the arithmetic behind the payslip check in lesson 17.10.

## Why it matters

These laws are the core of individual labour law. They are cogent: an employee cannot waive them, and a contract term that gives less is replaced by the statutory floor. They also apply regardless of nationality or the worker's immigration status, a point the Supreme Court made central in Kav LaOved (below). In practice most labour-court claims by individuals are about these floors: unpaid overtime, leave never given or redeemed, sick days paid as unpaid absence, an hourly rate below the minimum.

They are also numbers that change. The minimum wage is updated every April; the figures here were checked on 17 October 2026 against the National Insurance Institute's table and the Wikisource consolidations, and every amount lives in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

## The law

### Minimum wage

> ”שכר מינימום“, לחודש – 47.5 אחוזים מהשכר הממוצע כפי שהוא ב־1 באפריל של כל שנה וכפי שהוא מוגדל על פי חוק זה

> עובד שמלאו לו 18 שנים [...] המועסק במשרה מלאה, כנהוג במקום עבודתו, זכאי לקבל ממעסיקו שכר עבודה שלא יפחת משכר המינימום לחודש, שכר המינימום היומי או שכר המינימום לשעה, הכל לפי הענין.

*Gist:* The monthly minimum wage is 47.5% of the average wage, updated each April; an adult full-time employee must get at least the monthly, daily or hourly minimum, and a part-time employee a proportional amount. ([Minimum Wage Law ss. 1, 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_שכר_מינימום))

> ואולם לא יובאו בחשבון תוספת משפחה, תוספת ותק, תוספת בשל עבודה במשמרות, פרמיה [...] משכורת י״ג, מענקים על בסיס שנתי, והחזר הוצאות לרבות הוצאות כלכלה, אש״ל ונסיעות שמשלם המעסיק.

*Gist:* Only the basic wage, cost-of-living increment and fixed increments count towards the minimum; seniority, shift, family and bonus payments and expense refunds (including travel) do not. (s. 3(ב))

The National Insurance Institute publishes the current monthly, daily and hourly amounts; the hourly figure is published for both a 186-hour and a 182-hour month, because the full-time week in most of the private sector was cut to 42 hours by an extension order from April 2018 (⚠️ the order itself was not opened; see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)). Youth rates are set separately under the Youth Employment Law.

### Hours, overtime and rest

> יום עבודה לא יעלה על שמונה שעות עבודה.

> שבוע עבודה לא יעלה על ארבעים וחמש שעות עבודה.

*Gist:* The statutory day is eight hours (seven at night and before rest days) and the statutory week 45 hours; collective agreements and the 2018 extension order set a shorter week for most employees. ([Hours of Work and Rest Law ss. 2, 3](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_שעות_עבודה_ומנוחה))

> הועסק עובד שעות נוספות, ישלם לו המעסיק בעד שתי השעות הנוספות הראשונות שבאותו יום שכר עבודה לא פחות מ־1¼ מהשכר הרגיל, ובעד כל שעה נוספת שאחריהן לא פחות מ־1½ מהשכר הרגיל.

> הועסק עובד בשעות המנוחה השבועית או בחלק מהן: (1) ישלם לו המעסיק בעד שעות אלה שכר עבודה לא פחות מ־1½ משכרו הרגיל.

*Gist:* The first two overtime hours each day are paid at 125% and later ones at 150%; hours in the weekly rest at 150%. (ss. 16, 17) The weekly rest is at least 36 consecutive hours, including Saturday for Jews and Saturday, Sunday or Friday for others (s. 7). A day of six hours or more includes at least 45 minutes of breaks (s. 20).

> חוק זה אינו חל על העסקתם של: [...] (5) עובדים בתפקידי הנהלה או בתפקידים הדורשים מידה מיוחדת של אמון אישי; (6) עובדים שתנאי עבודתם ונסיבותיה אינם מאפשרים למעסיק כל פיקוח על שעות העבודה והמנוחה שלהם.

*Gist:* Managers, positions of special personal trust and workers whose hours cannot be supervised are excluded; a dispute over exclusion goes to the labour court. (s. 30) Employers must keep a running record of actual hours (s. 25), and if they do not produce it, the burden shifts to them in an overtime claim (Wage Protection Law s. 26ב, lesson 17.3).

### Annual leave

> אורך החופשה לכל שנת עבודה אצל מעסיק אחד או במקום עבודה אחד הוא: (1) בעד כל אחת מ־5 השנים הראשונות – 16 יום; [...] (3) בעד השנה השישית – 18 יום; (4) בעד השנה השביעית – 21 יום; (5) בעד השנה השמינית ואילך – יום נוסף לכל שנת עבודה עד לחופשה של 28 יום;

*Gist:* Leave grows from 16 days a year in the first five years to 28; the count is in calendar days including one weekly rest per seven days, so in a five-day week the first years give 12 working days. ([Annual Leave Law s. 3](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חופשה_שנתית)) A part-year or part-time worker gets a proportional share (s. 3(ב)–(ג)). Leave is paid at the regular wage (s. 10); at least seven days must be taken each year and the rest may be carried over two years with the employer's consent (s. 7); unused leave is redeemed in money when the job ends (s. 13).

### Sick pay

> (1) החל מהיום הרביעי להעדרו כאמור – תשלום על פי חוק זה בעד תקופת מחלתו (להלן – דמי מחלה); (2) בעד הימים השני והשלישי להעדרו כאמור – מחצית דמי מחלה;

*Gist:* No pay for the first day of sickness, half pay for days two and three, full pay from day four ([Sick Pay Law s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_דמי_מחלה); full pay "בגובה שכר העבודה", s. 5). Sick days accrue at a day and a half per full month, up to 90 days (s. 4), and are not redeemed in money when the job ends.

## Leading cases

### בג״ץ 5666/03 עמותת ״קו לעובד״ נ׳ בית הדין הארצי לעבודה (10.10.2007)

Palestinian residents of the West Bank worked for Israeli employers in the Mishor Adumim industrial zone. The National Labour Court had held that Jordanian law, then in force in the area, governed their contracts, so Israeli minimum wage and other protective laws did not apply. An expanded Supreme Court bench (Rivlin J. writing) granted the petition: absent an express choice of law, the "closest connection" test applies; where several legal systems operate in one territory, territorial ties weigh less; and the employment had its closest connection to Israeli law. The Court added that equality requires the same law to govern Israeli and Palestinian workers working side by side for the same employer.

Why it matters: it treats protective labour law as a floor that follows the employment, not the worker's identity, and it is cited whenever an employer argues that a foreign or local law with lower standards applies. [English translation](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/223)

The general principle that protective rights cannot be waived also appears in the statutes themselves: a waiver of collective-agreement rights is void (Collective Agreements Law s. 20, lesson 17.6), and courts read a waiver or "final settlement" signed by an employee narrowly (lesson 17.4).

## How it works in practice

### The minimum-wage check

1. Find the hourly rate: monthly basic wage ÷ the employer's full-time monthly hours (182 in a 42-hour week; check the payslip).
2. Remove what does not count: seniority, shift and family increments, bonuses, travel and meal refunds (s. 3(ב)).
3. Compare with the current hourly minimum ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)). The payslip itself must state the minimum wage for the period (Wage Protection Law, Schedule item 9, lesson 17.3).

### The overtime check

For each day: hours above the daily norm. The first two are paid at 125%, the rest at 150%. Weekly hours above the weekly norm count as overtime even if no single day exceeded eight hours. Hours on the weekly rest day: 150% (plus overtime rules if they also exceed the day's norm, depending on the applicable calculation; ⚠️ the method for combining premiums is set by case law and collective agreements and varies).

### "Global overtime"

Many contracts pay a fixed "global" overtime sum. That is lawful only if it does not pay less than the actual overtime worked; otherwise the employee may claim the difference. A wage described as "including overtime" without a separate component is treated as a regular wage only (Wage Protection Law s. 5).

### Leave and sick days on the payslip

The payslip must show leave and sick-day balances (Wage Protection Law Schedule item 4). Check that each month adds the right accrual and that days taken are deducted correctly.

### Other floors you will meet

Recuperation pay (דמי הבראה), travel-expense refunds and the mandatory pension come from extension orders, not from these statutes; the current rates are in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md) and the pension is covered in lesson 17.4.

## Worked example

Yusuf Haddad, 27, from Nazareth, works full time as a cook in a Haifa restaurant: five days a week, 9.5 hours a day with a 30-minute break, at a monthly wage of ₪6,400 (basic) plus a ₪300 "shift increment". His contract says the wage "includes all overtime". He took 2 sick days in March (a Monday and Tuesday) and has worked there 3 years.

1. **Minimum wage.** Basic ₪6,400 is below the April 2026 monthly minimum of about ₪6,444 (see current numbers); the shift increment does not count (s. 3(ב)). Shortfall ≈ ₪44 a month, from April 2026.
2. **Hours.** 9.5 hours minus the 30-minute break = 9 working hours a day (assuming he may leave during the break, s. 20(ג)). In a 42-hour, five-day week the daily norm is 8.4 hours in practice; at minimum, the hour above 8 each day is overtime under s. 2. Take the conservative statutory figure: 1 overtime hour × 5 days × about 4.3 weeks ≈ 21.5 hours a month at 125%.
3. **Hourly regular wage.** ₪6,400 ÷ 182 ≈ ₪35.16 (below the hourly minimum of ₪35.40, so use ₪35.40). Overtime due ≈ 21.5 × 35.40 × 1.25 ≈ ₪951 a month.
4. **"Includes all overtime".** Under s. 5 the stated wage is treated as regular wage only, so the overtime is unpaid.
5. **Sick days.** Day 1 unpaid, day 2 at 50%. If the payslip shows both days as unpaid absence, the employer owes half a day's pay.
6. **Leave.** Third year: 16 calendar days, 12 working days in a five-day week.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- One month of your own payslip and your actual hours (or Yusuf's case).
- A calculator or spreadsheet; [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Rate (10 min).** Compute the hourly regular wage; remove components that do not count; compare with the hourly minimum.
2. **Hours (15 min).** List each working day's hours from your own record. Mark hours above 8 a day (first two at 125%, the rest at 150%) and rest-day hours (150%).
3. **Premiums (10 min).** Multiply; compare with the overtime paid on the payslip.
4. **Leave and sickness (5 min).** Compute this year's leave days for your seniority and check the balances on the payslip.
5. **Summary (5 min).** A three-line table: component, due, paid, difference.

### Template

None in this lesson; the full [payslip and final-settlement worksheet](../../templates/payslip-and-final-settlement-worksheet.md) is used in lesson 17.10.

### How you know it worked

- Your hourly rate excludes seniority, shift, bonus and refund components.
- Each overtime hour is priced at 125% or 150% according to its position in the day.
- Your leave figure matches the seniority row of s. 3.

- [ ] I know when a worker is excluded from the Hours Law and who decides.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Checking your pay and demanding the difference is your own affair. A labour-court claim for wages can be filed in person, and claims under the Wage Protection Law and the Minimum Wage Law are exempt from court fees (Labour Court Fees Regulations, reg. 4). See lesson 17.1 for who else may represent you.

### Where and how

- First a written demand to the employer (lesson 17.10), then the regional labour court for your workplace.
- A complaint to the Ministry of Labour's enforcement administration can lead to administrative fines on the employer (Enforcement of Labour Laws Law); it does not collect your money for you.
- Free help: Kol-Zchut's minimum wage and overtime pages (⚠️ manual check), workers' rights NGOs, your union.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The employer claims you are excluded from the Hours Law as a manager or "position of trust" and the sums are large.
- Your claim depends on a collective agreement or sector extension order whose terms you cannot find.
- You are still employed and fear retaliation for claiming (the Wage Protection Law protects complainants, s. 28א, but enforcing that protection is harder).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Counting the shift or seniority increment towards the minimum | A shortfall goes unnoticed | Recompute with basic wage only | Read s. 3(ב) before comparing |
| Pricing all overtime at 125% | Under-claiming | Price hours 3 and later at 150% | Count overtime per day, not per month |
| No record of your own hours | The claim rests on the employer's records | Rebuild from messages, entry logs, GPS | Keep a daily note while working |
| Treating "global overtime" as final | Unpaid excess hours | Compare global sum with actual hours | Check monthly, not at the end |
| Expecting sick days to be paid out on leaving | False expectations | Claim leave redemption instead | Know which balances are redeemable |

## Review

- The minimum wage is 47.5% of the average wage, updated each April; only basic and fixed components count.
- Overtime: 125% for the first two daily hours, 150% after; weekly-rest hours 150%; managers and unsupervisable workers are excluded.
- Leave: 16 days a year for the first five years, rising to 28; redeemed in money at the end of employment.
- Sick pay: nothing for day 1, half for days 2–3, full from day 4, accruing 1.5 days a month up to 90.
- Protective laws are cogent and apply to every worker in Israel (HCJ 5666/03).
