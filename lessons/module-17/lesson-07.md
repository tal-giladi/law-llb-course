# 17.7 · National Insurance: contributions and the main benefits

By the end of this lesson you will be able to read your National Insurance record, match a life event to the right benefit, and check the main conditions before you claim: the qualifying period for unemployment and maternity pay, the definition of a work accident, and the medical thresholds for a general disability pension. This is the substantive half of social security law; the procedure (claims, medical committees, appeals) is lesson 17.9.

## Why it matters

The National Insurance Institute (NII, המוסד לביטוח לאומי) pays most of Israel's cash social benefits: old-age and survivors' pensions, children's allowances, maternity pay, unemployment, work injury, general disability, long-term care and income support (on the State's behalf, lesson 17.8). Almost every adult will claim at least one. The rules are detailed, and many entitlements are lost not on the merits but because a claim was late, a form was missing or a qualifying month was not counted. Lesson [16.7](../module-16/lesson-07.md) covered registration and contributions of the self-employed; here the focus is employees and benefits.

## The law

### Contributions

Employees pay contributions through payroll; the employer adds its share. There is a reduced rate on income up to a threshold (about 60% of the average wage) and a full rate above it, up to a ceiling; the NII publishes the rates and the 2026 thresholds ([NII rates page](https://www.btl.gov.il/Insurance/Rates/Pages/%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93%D7%99%D7%9D%20%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D.aspx); figures in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)). Most benefits are calculated from the average wage (שכר ממוצע, s. 1 of the Law) or the basic amount, both updated each January.

### Unemployment: the qualifying period

> לעניין סימן זה, תקופת האכשרה לגבי תקופת אבטלה פלונית היא 12 חודשים קלנדריים שבעדם שולמו דמי ביטוח אבטלה, בעד אחד או יותר מהימים בחודש, בתוך 18 החודשים בתכוף לתאריך הקובע.

*Gist:* To claim unemployment benefit you need 12 calendar months with unemployment contributions (at least one day each) within the 18 months before the claim date; sick days, reserve duty and mourning days count. ([NII Law s. 161](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הביטוח_הלאומי)) The first five days of each four-month period are not paid (s. 172). Registration with the Employment Service is a condition, and a resignation without justified cause delays payment (⚠️ the rule is in s. 166, not quoted). The self-employed are not insured for unemployment except under temporary programmes.

### Maternity pay

> (1) בעד פרק זמן של 15 שבועות – אם שולמו דמי ביטוח בעד 10 חודשים מתוך 14 החודשים שקדמו ליום הקובע או בעד 15 חודשים מתוך 22 החודשים שקדמו ליום הקובע; (2) בעד פרק זמן של 8 שבועות – אם שולמו דמי ביטוח בעד 6 חודשים מתוך 14 החודשים שקדמו ליום הקובע.

*Gist:* An insured employee or self-employed woman gets maternity pay for 15 weeks with 10 of 14 (or 15 of 22) contribution months, or 8 weeks with 6 of 14. (s. 50) Up to half the period may be taken before the birth (s. 52). Paid parental leave can be shared with the other parent under conditions in the Women's Employment Law.

### Work injury

> ”תאונת עבודה“ – תאונה שאירעה תוך כדי עבודתו ועקב עבודתו אצל מעבידו או מטעמו, ובעובד עצמאי – תוך כדי עיסוקו במשלח ידו ועקב עיסוקו במשלח ידו;

*Gist:* A work accident happens during and because of work; occupational diseases listed in regulations are covered too. (s. 79) The Law also treats some accidents on the way to or from work as work accidents (⚠️ s. 80, not quoted). An injured worker unable to work gets injury allowance (דמי פגיעה) for up to 13 weeks (s. 92), at 75% of the regular wage up to a ceiling (s. 97); the employer pays the day of injury, and the first days follow special rules (s. 93). Lasting harm leads to a determination of the degree of work disability by a medical committee and a pension or grant (lesson 17.9).

### General disability

> תנאי לקביעת אי־כושר להשתכר הוא שנקבעה למבוטח נכות רפואית [...] בשיעור של 60% לפחות, ואולם אם נקבעה למבוטח [...] נכות רפואית בשל ליקוי יחיד בשיעור של 25% לפחות, יהיה התנאי [...] קביעת נכות רפואית [...] בשיעור של 40% לפחות.

*Gist:* A general disability pension needs medical disability of at least 60%, or 40% if one impairment is at least 25% (s. 208), and an earning incapacity of at least 50% with little or no income from work (s. 195), set at 60%, 65%, 74% or 100% (s. 209). An authorised doctor sets the medical degree and must give reasons for a figure below the threshold (s. 208(ג)).

### Claim deadline

> כל תביעה לגמלת כסף, תוגש למוסד תוך שנים עשר חודשים מהיום שבו נוצרה עילת התביעה.

*Gist:* Claim within 12 months; a late claim can still succeed, but payment goes back no more than 12 months before the claim (48 months for old-age pension, with limits). (s. 296) Some benefits have their own shorter deadlines; check before filing.

### Other main benefits

- **Old-age pension:** from the retirement age under the Retirement Age Law (67 for men; rising gradually for women, ⚠️ check the current age for your birth year), with an income test until a later age.
- **Children's allowance:** paid automatically for each child under 18.
- **Survivors' pension:** to a widow, widower and orphans of an insured person.
- **Long-term care (סיעוד):** for people over retirement age who need help with daily activities, mostly as services.
- **Income support:** see lesson 17.8.

## Leading cases

### בג״ץ 5492/07 בוארון נ׳ בית הדין הארצי לעבודה (21.7.2009)

The NII stopped the survivors' and dependants' pensions of four widows after finding that they had begun to live with new partners, and the National Labour Court upheld the decisions. The Supreme Court (Hayut J. writing, an expanded panel) denied the petitions: the statute's definition of "wife" includes a common-law wife, so the rules ending a pension on remarriage extend to a common-law relationship, which also involves a shared household; and the rules restoring the pension when a marriage ends apply equally when such a relationship ends.

Why it matters: it shows the NII investigating lifestyle facts and the courts reading benefit statutes by their purpose and internal consistency. It also illustrates the High Court's restraint over the National Labour Court, which is the final word on social-security law except for substantive legal errors. [English translation](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/98)

## How it works in practice

### Your NII personal area

Log in with your national ID credentials on the NII site (⚠️ manual check). It shows reported wages month by month, contribution status, open claims and decisions. Check it before any claim: a missing month (for example, an employer who did not report you) breaks a qualifying period. If months are missing, ask the employer to correct its reports and send the NII payslips as proof.

### Which benefit fits?

| Event | Benefit | First check |
|---|---|---|
| Dismissed or contract ended | Unemployment | 12 of 18 months; Employment Service registration |
| Birth or adoption | Maternity pay; birth grant | 10 of 14 or 15 of 22 months |
| Injured at work or on the way | Injury allowance, then work disability | Employer's form; medical certificate; 12-month claim limit |
| Long illness or disability | General disability; special services; mobility | Medical thresholds (s. 208) and income |
| Retirement age | Old-age pension | Age, residence, income before the conditional age |
| Death of a spouse or parent | Survivors' pension; death grant | Insured status of the deceased |
| Low income | Income support (lesson 17.8) | Age 25+, means test, work test |

## Worked example

Liat Shapiro, 31, from Kiryat Shmona, worked as a kindergarten assistant from February 2025 to June 2026, then was dismissed when the kindergarten closed. She gave birth on 1 September 2026.

1. **Unemployment.** From July 2026 she had about 17 contribution months in the 18 before the claim date: the qualifying period is met. Payment starts after five waiting days; she must register with the Employment Service at once.
2. **Maternity pay.** On her claim date she had at least 10 of the last 14 months with contributions: 15 weeks of maternity pay. Unemployment months count towards the qualifying period (s. 50(ב)(1)).
3. **Overlap.** She cannot receive unemployment and maternity pay for the same days; the NII pays the relevant benefit for each period.
4. **Deadlines.** She files both claims well within 12 months (s. 296), online, with the employer's form ending her employment and the hospital's birth notice (the NII usually receives it directly).

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Access to your NII personal area (or Liat's facts).
- 40 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Record (15 min).** Download or note your reported wages for the last 22 months; mark any gap.
2. **Qualifying periods (10 min).** Count months for unemployment (12 of 18) and maternity (10 of 14 or 15 of 22) at a chosen date.
3. **Benefit map (10 min).** For your household, list which benefits from the table you or a family member could claim in the next two years.
4. **Gaps (5 min).** Draft a two-line request to an employer to correct a missing month.

### Template

None.

### How you know it worked

- Your month count matches the NII's record, gap by gap.
- You can name the deadline for each claim on your list.

### Self-check

- [ ] I know the unemployment qualifying period and waiting days.
- [ ] I know the two maternity qualifying periods.
- [ ] I can define a work accident.
- [ ] I know the 60% and 40%/25% thresholds for general disability.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Every claim is filed by the insured person, mostly online. Before the NII you may also be represented by a lawyer, an accountant, a representing tax adviser, a "close friend" (ידיד קרוב), a union or employers' organisation representative, or a person the NII has approved (NII Law s. 386; Representation Regulations 1970); a representative may not sign the benefit claim itself or waive or settle it for you (reg. 3(ב)).

### Where and how

- Claims online in the NII personal area or at a local branch; deadlines: s. 296 and [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Free help: the NII's rights-realisation services (⚠️ manual check), Legal Aid for NII matters without a means test (NII Law s. 397), Kol-Zchut's benefit pages (⚠️ manual check).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The claim depends on whether an injury is a work accident and the employer disputes it.
- You are asked to repay benefits paid "by mistake" over several years.
- A tort claim against a third party (for example after a road accident) interacts with your NII benefits (NII Law s. 328 gives the NII recourse).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Waiting more than 12 months to claim | Arrears beyond 12 months lost | Claim now; payment goes back 12 months | Claim as soon as the event happens |
| Not registering with the Employment Service on the first day | Unemployment days lost | Register immediately | Register the day after employment ends |
| Missing contribution months | Qualifying period fails | Ask the employer to correct reports; send payslips | Check the personal area quarterly |
| Treating a commute accident as private | Work-injury rights lost | Claim as a work accident and let the NII decide | Report every accident on the way to or from work |
| Assuming a disability pension needs 100% disability | No claim filed | Check the 60% and 40%/25% thresholds | Read s. 208 |

## Review

- Contributions: a reduced rate up to a threshold and a full rate up to a ceiling; benefits follow the average wage and the basic amount.
- Unemployment needs 12 contribution months in 18 (s. 161); maternity 10 of 14 or 15 of 22 for 15 weeks (s. 50).
- A work accident happens during and because of work (s. 79); injury allowance up to 13 weeks at 75% (ss. 92, 97).
- General disability needs medical disability of 60%, or 40% with one impairment of 25% (s. 208), and earning incapacity (s. 195).
- Claim within 12 months; late claims lose arrears beyond 12 months (s. 296).
