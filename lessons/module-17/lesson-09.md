# 17.9 · National Insurance procedure: claims, medical committees, appeals and the labour court

By the end of this lesson you will be able to follow a National Insurance claim from the first form to the National Labour Court, compute every deadline on the way, prepare for a medical committee, and run a simple labour-court case yourself. Most NII disputes are won or lost on procedure: a missed 30-day appeal, a committee that ignored a document, an appeal filed on facts when only law may be argued.

## Why it matters

The NII decides hundreds of thousands of claims a year. Two different tracks lead out of a negative decision. **Medical questions** (degree of disability, its start date, whether it is temporary) go to a medical appeals committee inside the NII, and from there to the labour court on points of law only. **Everything else** (entitlement, income, residence, whether an event was a work accident, the amount) goes straight to the regional labour court as a claim against the NII. Choosing the wrong track, or the right track too late, loses the case. The same labour courts also hear employees' claims against employers, so this lesson ends with the labour-court procedure you will use in lesson 17.10.

## The law

### The claims officer decides, the labour court reviews

> כל תביעה לגמלה שהוגשה למוסד תתברר בידי עובד המוסד שהסמיכה לכך המינהלה (להלן – פקיד תביעות), והוא יחליט אם תינתן הגמלה ובאיזו מידה תינתן.

*Gist:* A claims officer decides every claim. ([NII Law s. 298](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הביטוח_הלאומי))

> לבית דין אזורי [...] תהיה סמכות ייחודית לדון ולפסוק בכל תובענה [...] (1) של הזכאי לגמלה [...] הטוענים שקופחו בזכותם או שלא הוחלט תוך הזמן הקבוע בתקנות בתביעה שהגישו [...]

*Gist:* The regional labour court has exclusive jurisdiction over a claimant who says the NII denied a right or failed to decide in time, and over most other NII disputes. (s. 391)

> (א) תובענה נגד המוסד תוגש לבית הדין לעבודה לכל המוקדם בתום שלושים יום מהיום שבו הוגשה התביעה למוסד עצמו, זולת אם נתן המוסד החלטה בתביעה לפני תום המועד האמור.
> (ב) החליט המוסד בתביעה ונמסרה לתובע הודעה על כך, תוגש תובענה לבית הדין לעבודה תוך שניים עשר חודשים מיום מסירת ההודעה לתובע [...]

*Gist:* You may sue the NII no earlier than 30 days after claiming (unless it has decided), and no later than 12 months after receiving its decision. ([Time for Claims Regulations, reg. 1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הביטוח_הלאומי_(מועדים_להגשת_תובענות)))

### The medical track

> (א) הרואה עצמו נפגע מהחלטת רופא מוסמך, בכך שקבע לו אחוזי נכות רפואית שאינם מזכים בגמלה לפי פרק זה, רשאי לערור עליה לפני ועדה רפואית לעררים.

*Gist:* In general disability, a claimant whose medical degree is below the threshold may appeal to a medical appeals committee; earning-incapacity decisions go to an appeals committee, special-services decisions to a special-services appeals committee. (s. 211)

> (א) תובע רשאי לערור על החלטת רופא מוסמך שקבע לו אחוז נכות רפואית שאינו מזכה בגימלה [...] תוך 30 ימים מהתאריך שבו נמסרה לו הודעה על ההחלטה.
> (ב) לא הוגש ערר במועד הנקוב בתקנת משנה (א), רשאי התובע, בהסכמת המוסד, לערור תוך 30 ימים מהיום האחרון להגשת הערר כאמור.

*Gist:* The appeal is due within 30 days of notice of the decision, with a further 30 days only with the NII's consent; it is filed in writing through the NII with reasons. ([Disability Regulations, regs 20, 21](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הביטוח_הלאומי_(ביטוח_נכות)_(קביעת_אחוזי_נכות_רפואית,_מינוי_ועדות_לעררים_והוראות_שונות))) In work-injury cases the appeal from the medical committee is also due within 30 days, and if filed without reasons the reasons may follow within 30 more days ([Work Disability Regulations, regs 27, 28](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הביטוח_הלאומי_(קביעת_דרגת_נכות_לנפגעי_עבודה))). The NII's own pages say the same: appeal within 30 days, reasons up to 60 days from notice ([NII page](https://www.btl.gov.il/benefits/vaadotRefuiyot/erurVadot/Pages/erurNechutMeavoda.aspx)). Kol-Zchut reports that in practice the NII accepts appeals up to 90 days (⚠️ unconfirmed; do not rely on it).

> העורר רשאי להיות מיוצג בפני הועדה הרפואית לעררים על ידי הרופא שטיפל בו או רופא מומחה.

*Gist:* Before the medical appeals committee in general disability you may be represented by your treating doctor or a specialist. (reg. 22) The doctor or committee examines you alone, but may let another person be present with your consent (reg. 16; work injury reg. 9).

### Appeal to the labour court: law only

> החלטות של ועדה רפואית לעררים, ועדה לעררים וועדה לעררים לשירותים מיוחדים, ניתנות לערעור, בשאלה משפטית בלבד, לפני בית דין אזורי לעבודה [...]

> ערעור על החלטה יוגש לבית הדין האזורי לעבודה תוך שישים ימים מהיום שבו נמסרה ההחלטה למערער [...]

*Gist:* An appeals committee's decision can be appealed to the regional labour court on a point of law only (ss. 123, 213), within 60 days of receiving it ([Appeal Time Regulations, reg. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הביטוח_הלאומי_(מועד_להגשת_ערעור_על_החלטות_מסוימות))); a further appeal to the National Labour Court needs leave. "Law" includes the committee's procedure: failing to examine an impairment, ignoring a medical document, giving no reasons, or applying the wrong item of the impairment list are points of law; disagreeing with a percentage is not.

### Representation and free legal aid

> לא ייוצג אדם בפני המוסד אלא על ידי עורך דין, רואה חשבון, יועץ מס מייצג, ידיד קרוב, נציג ארגון עובדים או מעבידים [...] או אדם אחר שנתקיימו בו התנאים שנקבעו לכך.

*Gist:* Before the NII you may be represented by a lawyer, accountant, representing tax adviser, close friend, union or employers' representative, or a person approved by the NII (s. 386); a representative needs your special authorisation and may not sign the claim itself, waive or settle it (Representation Regulations reg. 3). Legal Aid represents any individual in NII proceedings in the labour court without a means test, and every appealable NII decision must say so in plain language (s. 397). Fees charged by private "rights-realisation" companies are capped by law (ss. 315א–315ט, ⚠️ amounts not checked).

### Labour-court procedure in brief

- **Venue:** for claims against the NII, the regional court for your place of residence; for claims against an employer, the place of work (procedure regs, reg. 3).
- **Forms:** standard forms for wage, severance and benefit claims (reg. 13); otherwise a statement of claim with the particulars in reg. 9.
- **Defence:** 30 days from service (reg. 28); a self-represented defendant states "שהוא טוען לעצמו" and gives a home address (reg. 29(ג)).
- **Evidence:** the court is not bound by the rules of evidence (Labour Court Law s. 32) and may adopt the procedure best suited to justice (s. 33).
- **Expedited hearing:** claims by employees against employers or pension funds up to a ceiling set by regulation are heard in one fast hearing, not bound by procedure; the judgment can be appealed only with leave (s. 31). The ceiling is CPI-linked: Kol-Zchut gives ₪40,700 for 2026, while the courts' fee table effective 1.1.2026 shows ₪39,500 (⚠️ conflict; see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- **Appeals:** a judgment is appealable as of right to the National Labour Court within 30 days (s. 26; reg. 73); leave to appeal an interim decision within 15 days (reg. 74).
- **Fees:** NII claims (s. 391) and income-support claims are exempt (Fees Regulations reg. 4).

## Leading cases

No single free leading ruling governs this procedure; the key texts are ss. 123 and 213 of the NII Law (appeal on law only) and the regulations above. The National Labour Court has developed a large body of case law on what counts as a "point of law" in appeals from medical appeals committees, including the committee's duty to give reasons, to address the claimant's documents and complaints, and to explain a departure from earlier findings; no free full-text ruling was confirmed for this course, so read the doctrine through the NII's and Kol-Zchut's guides and current regional-court judgments.

The High Court of Justice reviews the National Labour Court only for substantive legal errors that require intervention for justice, as Boaron (HCJ 5492/07, lesson 17.7) illustrates; in practice the National Labour Court is the last word on NII law.

## How it works in practice

### The NII dispute map

| Decision | Next step | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Claims officer rejects the claim (non-medical) | Claim in the regional labour court (or ask the NII to reconsider first) | 12 months from notice (reg. 1(ב)) |
| No decision 30 days after claiming | You may sue for failure to decide | From day 30 |
| Authorised doctor / first medical committee sets a low degree | Appeal (ערר) to the medical appeals committee | 30 days (+30 with consent; reasons later in work injury) |
| Medical appeals committee decides | Appeal on law to the regional labour court | 60 days (reg. 2) |
| Regional labour court judgment on such an appeal | Request for leave to appeal to the National Labour Court | Short; check the decision |
| Condition worsened since the last decision | Request for re-assessment (work injury: after six months with a doctor's confirmation, reg. 36) | — |

### Preparing for the committee

1. Get your file: ask the NII for copies of the medical documents and earlier minutes (also possible under the Privacy Protection Law right of access).
2. Bring a one-page summary: diagnoses, treating doctors, limitations in daily life and work, and the impairment-list items you think apply.
3. Bring recent specialist letters, imaging and hospital summaries, with copies to leave.
4. Ask that your complaints be recorded in the minutes, and read the minutes' summary of your complaints before you leave if allowed.
5. You may bring your treating doctor or a specialist to the appeals committee in general disability (reg. 22); ask in advance whether a companion may be present (reg. 16).
6. Remember that an appeals committee can lower as well as raise the percentage; the NII's page says you may withdraw your appeal if it intends to lower it.

## Worked example

Ahmad Khatib, 47, a construction worker from Umm al-Fahm, fell from scaffolding in 2025. The NII recognised a work accident; a medical committee set 15% permanent work disability for his back and did not address his knee, though he had brought an orthopaedic letter. The decision was given to him on 1 August 2026.

1. **Track.** Medical: appeal to the medical appeals committee (work injury), within 30 days, by 31 August 2026 (reg. 27). He files a short appeal on 20 August and sends reasons by 30 September (reg. 28).
2. **Grounds.** The committee ignored the knee injury and the orthopaedic letter; the back percentage does not reflect the MRI findings.
3. **Committee.** The appeals committee raises the back to 20% but again does not discuss the knee; its decision reaches him on 1 November 2026.
4. **Appeal on law.** Ignoring a documented impairment and failing to give reasons are points of law; he appeals to the regional labour court in Haifa by 31 December 2026 (60 days, reg. 2), fee-free, with Legal Aid if he wants it (s. 397).
5. **Likely outcome.** If the court agrees, it returns the matter to the committee (often in a different composition) with instructions to address the knee.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- An NII decision of your own or a family member's (or Ahmad's case), with the date you received it.
- The [NII medical committee appeal template](../../templates/nii-medical-committee-appeal.md).
- 40 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Track (5 min).** Medical or non-medical? Use the dispute map.
2. **Deadline (5 min).** Compute the last day and put it in your diary with a reminder a week earlier.
3. **Grounds (15 min).** For a medical appeal: impairments ignored, documents ignored, wrong list item, insufficient reasons. For a non-medical claim: the legal condition you say you meet and your evidence.
4. **Documents (10 min).** List every document you will attach.
5. **Help (5 min).** Decide whether to ask Legal Aid, a union or a close friend to help, and note the authorisation needed.

### Template

[NII medical committee appeal](../../templates/nii-medical-committee-appeal.md).

### How you know it worked

- Your deadline matches the right regulation for the track.
- Every ground is either a medical disagreement (for the appeals committee) or a point of law (for the labour court), not both mixed.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes, at every stage. NII claims and appeals are filed by the insured person; the labour court is used to self-represented claimants, NII claims are fee-free, and Legal Aid is available without a means test (s. 397).

### Where and how

- Medical appeals: in writing through the local NII branch or online (NII page above).
- Labour court: the regional court for your residence (claims against the NII); see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md) for deadlines and the expedited-hearing ceiling.
- Free help: Legal Aid (NII matters, no means test), the NII's guidance services for claimants (⚠️ manual check), Kol-Zchut (⚠️ manual check), disability-rights NGOs.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The case involves both a work-injury claim and a tort claim against the employer or a third party.
- You lost at the regional labour court on a point of law and want leave to appeal.
- The NII claims a large repayment or alleges fraud.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Missing the 30-day medical appeal | The decision becomes final | Ask the NII to consent to a late appeal within the next 30 days | Diary the date the letter arrived |
| Arguing percentages in the labour court | Appeal dismissed: law only | Recast as a point of law (ignored document, no reasons) | Separate medical and legal arguments |
| Not bringing documents to the committee | Committee decides without them | Appeal on the ground they were not considered, if they were submitted | Bring copies and list them in writing |
| Signing a "rights company" contract without reading the fee | Large share of the benefit lost | Check the statutory cap | Use free Legal Aid first |
| Suing the NII on day 10 after claiming | Premature claim | Wait for the decision or day 30 | Know reg. 1(א) |

## Review

- A claims officer decides; non-medical disputes go to the regional labour court within 12 months of the decision (reg. 1).
- Medical decisions go to a medical appeals committee within 30 days (+30 with consent), then to the labour court on law only within 60 days (ss. 123, 213; reg. 2).
- Before the NII you may be represented by a lawyer, a close friend, a union and others (s. 386); Legal Aid is free of a means test in NII cases (s. 397).
- The labour court is flexible on evidence (s. 32); small employee claims get an expedited hearing appealable only with leave (s. 31).
- Judgments are appealable within 30 days; interim decisions with leave within 15 days (regs 73, 74).
