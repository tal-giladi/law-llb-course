# 17.5 · Equality and sexual harassment at work

By the end of this lesson you will be able to tell whether a hiring decision, a pay gap, a missed promotion or a dismissal is unlawful discrimination, recognise sexual harassment and retaliation as the law defines them, and choose the right route to complain or claim. The law here gives the employee unusual tools: the burden of proof can move to the employer, and compensation can be awarded without proof of any loss.

## Why it matters

Equality at work sits where constitutional rights meet private contracts (lesson [06.4](../module-06/lesson-04.md) on equality and dignity). The Equal Employment Opportunities Law 1988 binds every employer, private or public, and covers the whole life of a job, from the advertisement to the retirement terms. The Prevention of Sexual Harassment Law 1998 is broader still: it is a criminal law, a tort law and an employment law at once, and it applies outside work too. Both laws are enforced mainly by individuals who complain, which is why knowing the procedure matters as much as knowing the definitions.

## The law

### Prohibited discrimination

> לא יפלה מעסיק בין עובדיו או בין דורשי עבודה מחמת מינם, נטייתם המינית, מעמדם האישי, הריון, טיפולי פוריות, טיפולי הפריה חוץ־גופית, היותם הורים, גילם, גזעם, דתם, לאומיותם, ארץ מוצאם, מקום מגוריהם, השקפתם, מפלגתם או שירות במילואים שלהם [...] בכל אחד מאלה: (1) קבלה לעבודה; (2) תנאי עבודה; (3) קידום בעבודה; (4) הכשרה או השתלמות מקצועית; (5) פיטורים או פיצויי פיטורים; (6) הטבות ותשלומים הניתנים לעובד בקשר לפרישה מעבודה;

*Gist:* An employer may not discriminate on any listed ground (including reserve duty of the employee or spouse) in hiring, terms, promotion, training, dismissal or retirement benefits. ([EEO Law s. 2(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_שוויון_ההזדמנויות_בעבודה)) Setting irrelevant conditions is also discrimination (s. 2(ג)); treatment required by the character or nature of the job is not (s. 2(ד)). The actual employer of manpower-agency workers is bound too (s. 2(א1)). Job advertisements must not discriminate (s. 8), and an employer may not demand a candidate's military profile (s. 2א). Disability discrimination is covered by the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law, and equal pay between women and men by the Equal Pay Law 1996.

### The burden of proof

> בתובענה של דורש עבודה או של עובד בשל הפרת הוראות סעיף 2, תהא חובת ההוכחה על המעסיק כי פעל שלא בניגוד להוראות סעיף 2 – (1) לענין קבלה לעבודה, קידום בעבודה, תנאי עבודה [...] – אם קבע המעסיק לגביהם תנאים או כישורים, ודורש העבודה או העובד, לפי הענין, הוכיחו כי נתקיימו בהם התנאים או הכישורים האמורים; (2) לענין פיטורים מהעבודה – אם הוכיח העובד שלא היתה בהתנהגותו או במעשיו סיבה לפיטוריו.

*Gist:* If a candidate or employee proves that they met the stated conditions (for hiring, promotion or terms), or that nothing in their conduct caused the dismissal, the employer must prove it did not discriminate. The burden also shifts if the employer asked for information on a prohibited ground (for example, "are you planning a pregnancy?"). (s. 9)

### Remedies

> (1) לפסוק פיצויים אף אם לא נגרם נזק של ממון בשיעור שייראה לו בנסיבות הענין [...] (2) ליתן צו מניעה או צו עשה, אם ראה שהענקת פיצויים בלבד לא תהא צודקת [...]

*Gist:* The labour court has exclusive jurisdiction and may award compensation without proof of financial loss, and in suitable cases an injunction or order to hire, promote or reinstate. (s. 10) Complaints can also go to the Equal Employment Opportunities Commission in the Ministry of Labour, which may investigate, intervene in proceedings and sue (ss. 18א–18יג).

### Sexual harassment and retaliation

> הטרדה מינית היא כל אחד ממעשים אלה: [...] (3) הצעות חוזרות בעלות אופי מיני, המופנות לאדם אשר הראה למטריד כי אינו מעונין בהצעות האמורות; (4) התייחסויות חוזרות המופנות לאדם, המתמקדות במיניותו, כאשר אותו אדם הראה למטריד כי אינו מעונין בהתייחסויות האמורות; (5) התייחסות מבזה או משפילה המופנית לאדם ביחס למינו או למיניותו, לרבות נטייתו המינית;

*Gist:* Sexual harassment includes sexual extortion, indecent acts, repeated sexual proposals or remarks after the person showed lack of interest, demeaning remarks about sex or sexuality, and publishing intimate images without consent. ([Prevention of Sexual Harassment Law s. 3(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_למניעת_הטרדה_מינית)) Where there is abuse of authority at work, a single proposal or remark is enough even if the person did not show lack of interest (s. 3(א)(6)(ג)). Retaliation (התנכלות) is any harm resulting from harassment or from a complaint or claim about it (s. 3(ב)).

> בית המשפט רשאי לפסוק בשל הטרדה מינית או בשל התנכלות פיצוי שלא יעלה על סך 120,000 שקלים חדשים [...] ללא הוכחת נזק; סכום זה יעודכן ב־1 בינואר של כל שנה [...]

*Gist:* Harassment and retaliation are civil wrongs; the court may award up to ₪120,000 (CPI-linked since August 2013; current figure in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)) without proof of damage, double if motivated by racism or hostility to a group. (s. 6(ב)) The claim must be filed within the general limitation period, without the usual extensions (s. 6(ג)).

### The employer's duties

> מעסיק חייב לנקוט אמצעים סבירים, בנסיבות הענין, כדי למנוע הטרדה מינית או התנכלות במסגרת יחסי עבודה [...] ולשם כך עליו: (1) לקבוע דרך יעילה להגשת תלונה [...] ולבירור התלונה; (2) לטפל ביעילות במקרה של הטרדה מינית או התנכלות שידע אודותיהם [...]

*Gist:* Every employer must take reasonable steps to prevent harassment and retaliation, set up an effective complaint channel and handle complaints effectively; an employer of more than 25 employees must also adopt and publish a code. An employer who fails these duties is itself liable for the harassment committed by its employee or manager. (s. 7)

## Leading cases

### בג״ץ 104/87 נבו נ׳ בית הדין הארצי לעבודה (22.10.1990)

Dr Naomi Nevo was compelled to retire at 60 under Jewish Agency pension rules that retired men at 65. The National Labour Court had rejected her claim. The Supreme Court (Bach J.) held that a different compulsory retirement age for women and men was discrimination: it served no legitimate purpose and harmed women's careers and pensions. An option for women to retire earlier was acceptable; compulsion was not. The discriminatory part of the rule was void as contrary to public policy, even though it was part of a collective arrangement.

Why it matters: the leading early ruling on sex equality at work, and an example of the Supreme Court overriding a collective term on public-policy grounds. [English translation](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/nevo-v-national-labour-court)

### בג״ץ 453/94 שדולת הנשים בישראל נ׳ ממשלת ישראל (1.11.1994)

Ministers appointed men to the boards of government companies without considering the 1993 statutory duty to give women appropriate representation. The majority (Mazza J., Zamir J. concurring) revoked the appointments and sent the ministers back to search for qualified women; Kedmi J. dissented, putting qualifications first. Why it matters: it recognises affirmative action as part of equality rather than an exception to it, the logic behind the EEO Law's permission for measures that correct past discrimination. [English translation](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/212)

### תע״א 3816/09 מלכה ואח׳ נ׳ התעשייה האווירית לישראל בע״מ (Tel Aviv Regional Labour Court, 2013)

The regional labour court held that a candidate had been refused a job because of his Mizrahi origin, the first ruling to recognise discrimination on ethnic grounds in hiring. Why it matters: it shows the s. 9 burden shift working in a hiring case where direct evidence is rare. (⚠️ no free full text confirmed; summary from the Hebrew Wikipedia article "פסק דין מישל מלכה נגד התעשייה האווירית לישראל"; check before citing.)

## How it works in practice

### The discrimination file

1. **The decision.** What happened (not hired, not promoted, paid less, dismissed) and when.
2. **The ground.** Which listed ground, and the evidence linking it: questions asked at interview, remarks, timing (dismissal soon after announcing pregnancy or reserve call-up).
3. **The conditions.** The stated requirements for the job or promotion, and your evidence that you met them (s. 9(א)(1)); for dismissal, your record showing no fault (s. 9(א)(2)).
4. **The comparator.** Who got the job or the pay, and how they differ only in the protected ground.

### The harassment route map

| Route | What it does | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Internal complaint to the employer's appointed officer | Triggers the employer's duty to investigate and act (s. 7) | Write it down; keep a copy |
| Labour court claim against the employer (and the harasser) | Compensation without proof of damage (s. 6); employer liable if it failed s. 7 | Closed-door hearing possible |
| Civil court claim against a harasser outside work | Same tort, in the civil courts | |
| Police complaint | Harassment is also an offence (s. 5) | Opens a criminal file; see module 5 |
| Equal Employment Opportunities Commission | Investigates harassment-related discrimination and retaliation | Free |

A complaint is protected: dismissing or harming the complainant for it is retaliation, a separate wrong.

## Worked example

Fatma Abu-Rabia, 29, from Rahat, applied for a logistics coordinator job in Ashdod. The ad asked for a degree and two years' experience; she has both. At the interview the manager asked whether she "planned more children soon" and whether her husband "agreed to her travelling". She was not hired; a man with one year's experience was.

1. **Ground.** Personal status and parenthood (and possibly sex and residence): listed grounds in s. 2(א).
2. **Burden.** She met the stated conditions (s. 9(א)(1)), and the employer asked for information on a prohibited ground (s. 9(ב)). The employer must prove the decision was not discriminatory.
3. **Remedy.** Compensation without proof of financial loss (s. 10(א)(1)); a request for an order to hire is possible but rarely granted for an outside candidate.
4. **Route.** A written complaint to the Commission and a claim in the regional labour court; she keeps the ad, her CV and a note of the interview questions written the same day.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A hiring, pay, promotion or dismissal decision you experienced or observed (or Fatma's case).
- The ad or job description, your CV or record, and any notes or messages.
- 40 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Facts (10 min).** A dated list of what happened, who said what, and who got the job or benefit.
2. **Ground (5 min).** Match the reason you suspect to a ground in s. 2(א) (or to harassment under s. 3).
3. **Burden (10 min).** Write which s. 9 trigger applies, with the evidence.
4. **Employer's defence (5 min).** The best "nature of the job" or business reason the employer could give, and your answer.
5. **Route (10 min).** Choose internal complaint, Commission, labour court or police, and the first document you would send.

### Template

None in this module; adapt the [letter to employer](../../templates/letter-to-employer-wage-claim.md) for a written discrimination or harassment complaint.

### How you know it worked

- You tied every element to a section number.
- You can explain why the burden of proof shifts, or why it does not.
- You chose a route that fits your goal (stopping the conduct, compensation, punishment).

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Internal complaints, Commission complaints and labour-court claims can be filed in person. The labour court may hear harassment cases behind closed doors, and the EEO Law allows the same (s. 10א). A police complaint is filed in person too (module 5).

### Where and how

- Equal Employment Opportunities Commission: online complaint form on gov.il (⚠️ manual check).
- Regional labour court for your workplace; a harassment claim pays a fee under a specific item (Labour Court Fees Regulations reg. 6א; [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- Free help: rape crisis centres and their legal aid line for harassment, Legal Aid in some matters, NGOs for equality at work, and the Commission itself.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The harasser is your employer or a senior manager and you are still employed.
- You face a defamation threat for complaining.
- The case may also be criminal (indecent act, extortion) and you need to coordinate the police file with the civil claim.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| No same-day note of interview questions | Your account is challenged | Write it now, dated, and say when you wrote it | Note questions immediately after any interview |
| Complaining only orally | The employer denies knowing (s. 7) | Send a written summary of the oral complaint | Complain in writing to the appointed officer |
| Waiting years | Limitation (s. 6(ג)) and fading evidence | File promptly | Diary the limitation date when it happens |
| Treating a single remark as harassment outside authority | Claim fails | Look for repetition or authority | Check s. 3(א)(3)–(6) carefully |
| Ignoring retaliation after complaining | A second wrong goes unclaimed | Add a retaliation head | Record every change after the complaint |

## Review

- The EEO Law bans discrimination on listed grounds in every stage of employment, including irrelevant conditions (s. 2).
- The burden shifts to the employer when you meet the stated conditions, show no fault for dismissal, or were asked a prohibited question (s. 9).
- Sexual harassment includes repeated proposals or remarks after refusal, demeaning remarks and, under abuse of authority, even a single proposal (s. 3).
- Compensation without proof of damage is available in both laws; the employer is liable for harassment if it failed its s. 7 duties.
- Nevo (1990) and the Women's Network case (1994) anchor equality and affirmative action in Israeli labour law.
