# 17.1 · Who is an employee: the tests, contractors and manpower agencies

By the end of this lesson you will be able to look at any working arrangement, your own or one in a case file, and say whether the law treats it as employment, who the employer is, and what follows if the label on the contract is wrong. Every right in this module (minimum wage, leave, severance, pension, protection from discrimination) belongs only to an employee, so this is the first question in any labour dispute.

## Why it matters

This module follows the two Tel Aviv University courses on labour law (דיני עבודה, 1411-9225) and welfare law (דיני רווחה, 1411-9226). Labour law is mostly protective and cogent: the parties cannot contract out of minimum wage, leave or severance. That is exactly why so much litigation is about the threshold question. An employer who calls a worker a "freelancer", pays against an invoice and stops paying when the work ends saves the cost of social rights; the worker who accepts the label may lose them.

The question also decides the forum. The regional labour court has exclusive jurisdiction over claims between employee and employer "including the question whether an employment relationship exists" (s. 24, quoted below), so a dispute over status goes to the labour court, not to the magistrates' court that hears ordinary contract claims (the court map is in lesson [01.4](../module-01/lesson-04.md)). The same status decides tort questions about the employer's vicarious liability (lesson [04.7](../module-04/lesson-07.md)) and National Insurance status as an employee or self-employed person (lesson [16.7](../module-16/lesson-07.md)).

## The law

There is no general statutory definition of "employee" in Israeli law. Each statute uses the word, and the courts give it a common meaning built in the case law. Statute does decide the forum, and it regulates the two common triangles: manpower agencies and service contractors.

### The labour court decides status

> לבית דין אזורי תהא סמכות ייחודית לדון –
> (1) בתובענות בין עובד או חליפו למעסיק או חליפו שעילתן ביחסי עבודה, לרבות השאלה בדבר עצם קיום יחסי עבודה ולמעט תובענה שעילתה בפקודת הנזיקין [נוסח חדש];

*Gist:* Claims between employee and employer arising from employment, including whether an employment relationship exists at all, belong exclusively to the regional labour court (tort claims excepted). ([Labour Court Law s. 24(א)(1)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בית_הדין_לעבודה))

### Manpower agencies

> ”קבלן כוח אדם“ – מי שעיסוקו במתן שירותי כוח אדם של עובדיו לשם עבודה אצל זולתו [...]

> (א) לא יועסק עובד של קבלן כוח אדם אצל מעסיק בפועל תקופה העולה על תשעה חודשים רצופים [...]
> (ג) הועסק עובד כאמור אצל אותו מעסיק בפועל תקופה העולה על תשעה חודשים רצופים [...] ייחשב העובד כעובד המעסיק בפועל, בתום תקופת תשעת החודשים [...]

*Gist:* A manpower agency supplies its own workers to work at someone else's business; after nine consecutive months at the same user, the worker is deemed the user's employee, with seniority carried over (s. 12א(ד)); the Minister may extend to 15 months in exceptional cases, and foreign workers in listed sectors are excluded. ([Manpower Agencies Law ss. 1, 12א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_העסקת_עובדים_על_ידי_קבלני_כוח_אדם))

### Service contractors (cleaning, security, catering)

> חובת מעסיק שהוא קבלן, כלפי עובדו, לפי הוראת חיקוק המנויה בתוספת השלישית [...] תחול גם על מזמין השירות בעד פרק הזמן שהעובד הועסק במתן השירות אצלו, בהתקיים כל אלה: [...]

*Gist:* The client of a cleaning, security or catering contractor becomes liable for the contractor's listed wage duties if at least four workers serve it, for at least six months, and the worker (or a union or workers' rights organisation with consent) gave written notice to the contractor and to the client and 30 days passed without payment. ([Enforcement of Labour Laws Law s. 25](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_להגברת_האכיפה_של_דיני_העבודה)) If the client never set up a way for contractor workers to report violations, it is liable even without the notice (s. 26(ב)).

## Leading cases

The tests come from decades of National Labour Court case law rather than one ruling. In short: the **integration test** asks whether the worker is part of the employer's organisation. Its positive side asks whether the work is an integral part of the business's regular activity rather than an outside service; its negative side asks whether the worker runs a business of his own (own clients, own tools, own staff, profit and loss). Around it, the **mixed test** weighs indicators: control and subordination, personal performance, who supplies tools and premises, method and regularity of pay, exclusivity, duration, and how the parties presented the relationship to the tax and National Insurance authorities. No indicator decides alone, and the label in the contract carries little weight against the facts.

### ע״ע 15868-04-18 כותה נ׳ עיריית רעננה ואח׳ (בית הדין הארצי לעבודה, 7.4.2021)

An expanded bench of seven judges and two public representatives heard four appeals together on the consequences of a finding that a "contractor" was in fact an employee. The majority (Davidov-Motola J.) held that the worker's good faith is not relevant to whether employment existed, though it may affect non-pecuniary damages. Pecuniary compensation is calculated by comparing what the employer would have spent on a salaried employee (including social rights and severance) with the contractor fee actually paid; if the employee cost would have been higher, the difference is owed, and the employer bears the burden of proving the alternative salary. The worker never has to repay the employer even if the contractor fee was higher. President Virt-Livne, dissenting in part, would have allowed a court to deny employee status where the worker acted in clear and extreme bad faith.

Why it matters: it settled years of conflicting rulings and made the "comparison of costs" the standard method, so a worker can estimate the claim before filing. [Full text (gov.il PDF)](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/decision15868-04-18/he/15868-04-18.pdf)

### בג״ץ 4542/02 עמותת ״קו לעובד״ נ׳ ממשלת ישראל (30.3.2006)

Migrant workers' residence permits were tied to a single named employer, so a worker who left the employer lost legal status. The Supreme Court (Levy J., President Barak concurring) held that the binding arrangement violated the workers' dignity and liberty and failed every proportionality subtest, and ordered a new arrangement. Why it matters: it shows the courts reading labour relationships through the employee's dependence on the employer, the same concern that drives the protective tests above, and it is the starting point for the later sector-based permit system. [English translation](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/224)

## How it works in practice

### The status check, in order

1. **The work.** Is it part of the business's core, regular activity (a teacher in a school, a programmer in a software house), or a distinct service bought from outside (an accountant who audits once a year)?
2. **The worker's own business.** Does the worker have other clients, advertise, employ others, bear real financial risk, own the main tools? If not, the negative side of the integration test points to employment.
3. **Control and personal performance.** Fixed hours, a manager who assigns tasks, a requirement to do the work personally, an email address and badge of the business.
4. **Pay.** A fixed monthly amount against an invoice is more like a salary than a project fee.
5. **Duration and exclusivity.** Years of continuous work for one client weigh towards employment.
6. **Who is the employer?** If a manpower agency pays you but you work at a user, check the nine-month rule. If you clean, guard or cook for a contractor at a client's premises, keep the client in the picture (s. 25 notice).

### What to collect

The contract and any invoices; bank records of payments; emails or WhatsApp messages showing who gave instructions and set hours; work schedules, badge or uniform photos; National Insurance and tax registration status. In the labour court the rules of evidence do not bind the court (Labour Court Law s. 32), so messages and screenshots are commonly admitted, but their weight depends on whether they are complete and authentic.

### Timing

A claim for rights under an employment relationship is subject to the general seven-year limitation period (lesson [15.9](../module-15/lesson-09.md)), with shorter periods for some heads (for example late-wage compensation, lesson 17.3). Late filers lose the earliest years first.

## Worked example

Noa Peretz, 34, from Holon, worked four years as a "content writer" for a digital marketing agency. She signed a "services agreement", issued a monthly invoice for ₪9,500 as a licensed sole trader (עוסק פטור), worked Sunday to Thursday from the agency's office, used the agency's laptop and email, and had no other clients. In September 2026 the agency ended the arrangement by WhatsApp.

1. **Integration, positive side.** Writing content is the agency's core business, not an outside service.
2. **Integration, negative side.** Noa had no business of her own: no other clients, no staff, no tools, no financial risk.
3. **Indicators.** Fixed hours, a manager who assigned tasks, office premises, a fixed monthly payment for four years: all point to employment. Only the label and her tax registration point the other way, and the label carries little weight.
4. **Consequence (Kota).** Noa may claim the difference between the agency's cost of employing a writer at her level (salary, pension, severance, leave, recuperation) and the ₪9,500 actually paid. If the agency shows that ₪9,500 exceeded the full employee cost, she gets nothing for those heads but repays nothing either.
5. **Forum.** Regional labour court in the district of her place of work (procedure regs, reg. 3); status is decided there as part of the claim.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your own current or past working arrangement, or that of Noa above.
- The contract, three months of payslips or invoices, and any written instructions.
- 40 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Describe the work (5 min).** Two sentences: what you do and what the business does.
2. **Run the integration test (10 min).** One paragraph for the positive side, one for the negative side.
3. **Score the indicators (10 min).** A table of control, personal performance, tools, pay, duration, exclusivity, registration: for each, "employee", "contractor" or "neutral", with the evidence.
4. **Name the employer (5 min).** Direct employer, manpower agency and user, or service contractor and client. Note dates for the nine-month rule or the s. 25 conditions.
5. **Write the conclusion (10 min).** Three sentences: status, employer, and the evidence you would attach.

### Template

None. Keep the table in your case file; it becomes the "parties and relationship" section of a labour-court claim (lesson 17.10).

### How you know it worked

- Every conclusion in your table is backed by a document or a dated message, not by memory.
- You can say which way the negative side of the integration test points and why.
- You named every potential defendant (agency, user, contractor, client).

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Any individual may sue in the labour court in person; the defence form itself provides for a party who states "שהוא טוען לעצמו" (procedure regs, reg. 29(ג)). Besides a lawyer, a party may be represented by a person appointed by a workers' or employers' organisation of which the party is a member (Labour Court Law s. 34), and a company may act through its manager or an authorised officer (reg. 23). Drafting claims for others as an occupation is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).

### Where and how

- The regional labour court for your place of work; fees and exemptions: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Before suing: a written demand to the employer (and to the client of a service contractor, for the s. 25 clock).
- Free help: the Ministry of Labour's wage enforcement unit, workers' rights NGOs (for example Kav LaOved for migrant and Palestinian workers), the Histadrut or Koach LaOvdim if you are a member, Kol-Zchut's labour pages, and [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The employer says you owe it money back, or files a counterclaim for "unjust enrichment".
- Your status affects a work-permit, residence or professional licence question.
- The claim covers several years and several employers in a chain (agency, user, group company).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accepting the "freelancer" label as final | Social rights never claimed | Run the integration test on the facts | Ask the status question first in every dispute |
| Suing only the manpower agency after it collapsed | Judgment against an empty company | Add the user if the nine-month rule applies | Track months at each user from day one |
| No s. 25 notice to the client of a cleaning contractor | Client not liable; contractor insolvent | Send the written notice now; wait 30 days | Copy the client on every demand to the contractor |
| Filing in the magistrates' court | Transfer and delay | Ask to transfer to the labour court | Check s. 24 before choosing a court |
| Relying on memory for hours and instructions | Weak evidence | Collect messages and schedules | Keep a monthly record while you still work there |

## Review

- Employee status is a question of fact decided by the integration test and supporting indicators, not by the contract's label.
- The regional labour court has exclusive jurisdiction, including over whether employment exists (s. 24(א)(1)).
- A manpower worker becomes the user's employee after nine consecutive months (s. 12א); a client of a cleaning, security or catering contractor can become liable for wages after notice (Enforcement Law s. 25).
- Kota (2021): compensation for misclassification is the difference between the employee cost and the fee paid; the worker never repays.
- Collect documents about control, pay and duration while the relationship still exists.
