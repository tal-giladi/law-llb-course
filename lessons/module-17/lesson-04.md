# 17.4 · Dismissal: hearing, notice, severance pay and pension

By the end of this lesson you will be able to take a dismissal (or a resignation) and work out everything that follows from it: whether the employer had to hold a hearing and whether it was a real one, how much notice was due, whether severance is payable and how much, what the pension fund already holds for you, and whether the dismissal was forbidden altogether. These are the numbers in the final-settlement calculation of lesson 17.10.

## Why it matters

Israeli law has no general statute requiring a "just cause" for dismissal in the private sector. Instead it combines four layers: a duty to hold a fair hearing, developed by the labour courts from good faith and public-law ideas; statutory notice; statutory severance after a year; and specific prohibitions (pregnancy, parental leave, fertility treatment, reserve duty, union activity, discrimination). Collective agreements often add a "just cause" requirement and a joint committee. Most dismissed employees have rights on several layers at once, and the deadlines and documents differ for each.

## The law

### Prior notice

> (ג) הודעה כאמור בסעיף זה תינתן בכתב ותציין את יום הוצאת ההודעה ואת יום הכניסה לתוקף של הפיטורים או ההתפטרות [...]

*Gist:* Notice of dismissal and of resignation must both be in writing, stating the date given and the date it takes effect. ([Prior Notice Law s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הודעה_מוקדמת_לפיטורים_ולהתפטרות))

> עובד במשכורת זכאי להודעה מוקדמת לפיטורים, כמפורט להלן: (1) במהלך ששת חודשי עבודתו הראשונים – של יום אחד בשל כל חודש עבודה; (2) במהלך התקופה החל בחודש השביעי לעבודתו עד תום שנת עבודתו הראשונה – של 6 ימים, בתוספת של יומיים וחצי בשל כל חודש עבודה בתקופה האמורה; (3) לאחר שנת עבודתו הראשונה – של חודש ימים.

*Gist:* A monthly-paid employee gets one day per month in the first six months, then six days plus 2.5 days per month to the end of the first year, then a month. An hourly or daily paid employee reaches a month only after the third year (s. 4). The same periods apply to an employee who resigns (s. 5). The employer may waive the employee's work during notice but must pay for it (s. 6); a party who gives no notice pays the other the wage for the missing period (s. 7).

### Severance pay

> מי שעבד שנה אחת ברציפות [...] אצל מעסיק אחד או במקום עבודה אחד ופוטר, זכאי לקבל ממעסיקו שפיטרו פיצויי פיטורים.

> שיעורם של פיצויי הפיטורים הוא: שכר חודש אחד לכל שנת עבודה בעובד במשכורת [...] חלק של שנה שלאחר שנת עבודה מזכה את העובד בפיצויים יחסיים [...]

*Gist:* After a year of continuous work, a dismissed employee gets one month's wage for each year, with a proportional amount for part of a year ([Severance Pay Law ss. 1, 12](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_פיצויי_פיטורים)); the "wage" for this purpose is basic wage plus seniority, cost-of-living and family increments, not overtime or bonuses ([severance regs, reg. 1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_פיצויי_פיטורים_(חישוב_הפיצויים,_והתפטרות_שרואים_אותה_כפיטורים))), usually the last wage.

> התפטר עובד מחמת הרעה מוחשית בתנאי העבודה, או מחמת נסיבות אחרות שביחסי עבודה לגבי אותו העובד שבהן אין לדרוש ממנו כי ימשיך בעבודתו, רואים את ההתפטרות לענין חוק זה כפיטורים.

*Gist:* A resignation because of a material deterioration in conditions, or circumstances in which the employee cannot be expected to continue, counts as dismissal. (s. 11(א)) The courts generally require the employee first to warn the employer and give it a chance to fix the problem. Other sections treat resignation as dismissal for health reasons (s. 6), by a parent to care for a newborn child (s. 7, conditions in the section), on moving home in listed circumstances (s. 8), and on reaching retirement age (s. 11).

> תשלום לקופת תגמולים, לקרן פנסיה או לקרן כיוצא באלה, לא יבוא במקום פיצויי פיטורים אלא אם נקבע כך בהסכם הקיבוצי [...] או אם תשלום כאמור אושר על ידי שר העבודה ובמידה שאושר.

*Gist:* Pension contributions replace severance only if a collective agreement or a ministerial approval says so. (s. 14) The general approval of 1998 (Official Gazette י״פ 4394, ⚠️ not opened) is the usual source: under a written "s. 14 arrangement" the employer's severance component in the pension fund replaces severance pay, fully or partially according to the rate deposited, and the employee keeps the money whatever the reason for leaving. Severance may be denied in whole or part only under a collective agreement or a labour-court ruling on grounds such as serious misconduct (ss. 16, 17), and any settlement or waiver of severance is valid only if written and expressly about severance (s. 29).

> יראו פיצויי פיטורים כמולנים אם לא שולמו תוך 15 ימים מהמועד לתשלומם [...]

*Gist:* Severance is late if unpaid within 15 days of the end of employment; from day 16 it carries linkage and from day 31 linkage plus 20% a month. ([Wage Protection Law s. 20](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_השכר))

### Dismissals that need a permit

> לא יפטר מעסיק עובדת שהיא בהריון וטרם יצאה לתקופת לידה והורות אלא בהיתר מאת שר העבודה והרווחה, ולא יתיר השר פיטורים כאמור אם הפיטורים הם, לדעתו, בקשר להריון;

*Gist:* A pregnant employee with six months' seniority may not be dismissed without a ministerial permit, which is refused if the dismissal is connected to the pregnancy; no dismissal at all during maternity leave, and none for 60 days after it without a permit; similar protection for fertility treatment. ([Women's Employment Law s. 9](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_עבודת_נשים)) Non-renewal of a fixed-term contract of 12 months or more counts as dismissal for this purpose (s. 9(ה)).

### Pension

Since 2008 an extension order makes pension contributions compulsory for almost all employees. The rates since 2017 are 6% from the employee, 6.5% from the employer for benefits and 6% for severance, on wages up to the average wage, after a waiting period of six months (or three months, with retroactive payment, for someone who already has a pension fund) (⚠️ from secondary sources; the order's consolidated text was not opened). The order includes a s. 14 arrangement for the severance component, so for most employees the fund already holds part of their severance.

## Leading cases

### בג״ץ 3/58 ברמן נ׳ שר הפנים (1958)

The Supreme Court held that a public authority must give a person a fair opportunity to be heard before a decision that harms him, as a rule of natural justice even without statutory words. Why it matters here: this is the public-law root of the hearing duty. Public employers are bound by it directly, and the labour courts extended a parallel duty to private employers through the duty of good faith in employment contracts: the employee must be told the reasons in advance, given time and the documents to prepare, allowed to answer in person (often with a companion), and the decision must be taken with an open mind afterwards. A dismissal without a proper hearing usually leads to compensation rather than reinstatement, except in public bodies or where a collective agreement provides otherwise. [English translation](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/93); see lesson [14.4](../module-14/lesson-04.md).

The private-sector hearing doctrine itself rests on a long line of National Labour Court rulings rather than a single leading case; no free full-text source was confirmed, so this course states the doctrine without citing a particular ruling.

## How it works in practice

### Before a hearing

1. Ask for the invitation in writing, with the reasons and the material relied on.
2. Ask for reasonable time (several days, not hours) and to bring a companion.
3. Prepare a written response and read it out; ask that it be attached to the minutes.
4. After the hearing, ask for the minutes and the written decision.

### After dismissal: the final-settlement items

Last wage; pay in lieu of notice (if work was waived or no notice given); redemption of unused leave (lesson 17.2); recuperation pay due; severance (minus what the fund holds under s. 14, if applicable); form 161 to the tax authority; the release letter to the pension fund; a certificate of employment period (Prior Notice Law s. 8). Many employers ask you to sign a "final settlement" letter; read lesson 17.10 before signing, and remember s. 29.

### Unemployment

A dismissed employee (and a resigning one in some circumstances) may claim unemployment benefit from National Insurance (lesson 17.7). Register with the Employment Service at once; the benefit depends on the qualifying period, and a resignation without justification delays it.

## Worked example

Tamar Ben-David, 41, from Be'er Sheva, worked 6 years and 4 months as a monthly-paid office manager for an import company, last wage ₪11,000 basic. Her pension fund shows a severance component of ₪38,000 under a s. 14 arrangement at 6%. On 1 October 2026 she was called to a "talk" and handed a dismissal letter effective immediately. She had 9 unused leave days.

1. **Hearing.** No advance invitation, no reasons, no chance to respond: no proper hearing. A claim for compensation for the defective process is realistic.
2. **Notice.** After the first year, a month. No notice given, so the employer owes a month's wage in lieu: ₪11,000 (s. 7).
3. **Severance.** 6⅓ years × ₪11,000 ≈ ₪69,667. Under the 6% s. 14 arrangement, the fund's ₪38,000 replaces 72% of severance for the period it covers (⚠️ general approval terms); the employer must top up the rest for that period, plus any period before contributions began. Without the fund statement for each year, the safe claim is: severance in full minus the fund's severance component.
4. **Leave.** 9 days redeemed at her daily wage (₪11,000 ÷ 21.67 ≈ ₪508 a day for a five-day week): ≈ ₪4,570.
5. **Deadlines.** Severance is late after 15 days (from 17 October), with linkage and then 20% a month (s. 20). Tamar writes on 5 October listing every item and asking for the release letter to the fund.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A dismissal or resignation of your own (or Tamar's case), the letter, the last payslip and the pension fund statement.
- 50 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Classify the ending (5 min).** Dismissal, resignation, resignation treated as dismissal (which section?), or end of a fixed term.
2. **Hearing (10 min).** Was there a written invitation with reasons, time, documents and a real chance to respond? List each defect.
3. **Notice (10 min).** Compute the notice days from s. 3 or s. 4 and the pay in lieu.
4. **Severance (15 min).** Seniority in years and months × the severance wage; subtract the fund's severance component only if a s. 14 arrangement is in writing.
5. **Protected status (5 min).** Pregnancy, maternity, fertility treatment, reserve duty, union activity? If yes, was there a permit?
6. **List (5 min).** A table of every item, the amount and the date it becomes late.

### How you know it worked

- Your notice figure matches the right section (s. 3 for monthly, s. 4 for hourly or daily).
- Your severance calculation uses the severance wage, not the gross with overtime.
- You know whether the fund money replaces severance in full, in part or not at all, and why.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes for the hearing (you may bring a companion; a lawyer if the employer agrees or a collective agreement allows), for the demand letter and for a labour-court claim for notice, severance and leave in your own name (lesson 17.1 on representation). A pregnant employee may object to the employer's permit application before the Ministry's official in person.

### Where and how

- The demand letter: lesson 17.10 and the [letter to employer](../../templates/letter-to-employer-wage-claim.md).
- Labour court: severance claims use the standard form (procedure regs, reg. 13; form 3); fees for heads outside the Wage Protection Law: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Permit applications (pregnancy, maternity, fertility treatment) go to the Ministry of Labour's Women's Employment Law unit (⚠️ manual check of the current gov.il service page).
- Free help: Legal Aid in some employment matters, Kol-Zchut's dismissal pages (⚠️ manual check), workers' rights NGOs, your union.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You want to be reinstated (an injunction), especially in a public body or under a collective agreement.
- The employer alleges misconduct that could justify denying severance (ss. 16, 17) or hints at a police complaint.
- You were dismissed while pregnant, on leave or in fertility treatment and the employer says it has a permit.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Resigning in anger without warning first | Severance claim under s. 11 fails | Write at once describing the deterioration and offering to stay if fixed | Warn in writing, give time, then resign citing s. 11 |
| Signing "I have no further claims" on the last day | The employer relies on the waiver | Argue it is not specific (s. 29) and was not informed | Sign only a receipt for sums actually received |
| Treating the fund's severance money as the whole severance | Top-up never claimed | Check whether a written s. 14 arrangement exists and its rate | Ask for the arrangement when you start |
| Missing the release letter to the fund | Money stuck in the fund | Demand the letter in writing | List it in the final-settlement demand |
| Going to the hearing unprepared | Reasons go unanswered | Ask for postponement and the documents | Ask for everything in writing beforehand |

## Review

- A dismissal needs a fair hearing (Berman's principle, extended by the labour courts to private employers).
- Notice: written, by seniority (s. 3 monthly, s. 4 hourly); pay in lieu if not given (s. 7).
- Severance: one month's wage per year after a year; resignation treated as dismissal in s. 11 and other sections; waiver only in writing and expressly (s. 29).
- Pension contributions replace severance only under s. 14 (collective agreement or ministerial approval).
- Pregnancy, maternity and fertility-treatment dismissals need a ministerial permit (Women's Employment Law s. 9).
