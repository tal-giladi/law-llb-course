# 17.11 · Practice lab: a National Insurance claim and an appeal from a medical committee

In this lab you build a National Insurance claim file from scratch, then take it through the two appeal steps that decide most disability cases: the appeal (ערר) to the medical appeals committee and, if the committee errs in law, the appeal to the regional labour court. Use a real claim of your own or of a family member who asks for your help and signs for themselves, or the supplied case.

## Why it matters

Disability and work-injury claims are where the medical committee process matters most, and where people most often give up after a first low percentage. The appeal is free, the deadlines are short, and a well-prepared appeal with documents tied to the impairment list often changes the result. Knowing when the next step is a point of law, not another medical argument, is what separates an appeal that gets a committee to look again from one that is dismissed in the labour court.

## The law

The rules are in lessons 17.7 and 17.9. In short:

> תנאי לקביעת אי־כושר להשתכר הוא שנקבעה למבוטח נכות רפואית [...] בשיעור של 60% לפחות, ואולם אם נקבעה [...] נכות רפואית בשל ליקוי יחיד בשיעור של 25% לפחות, יהיה התנאי [...] 40% לפחות.

*Gist:* The medical threshold for a general disability pension is 60%, or 40% with one impairment of 25% or more; a doctor who sets less must give reasons. ([NII Law s. 208](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הביטוח_הלאומי))

> הערר יוגש בכתב באמצעות המוסד, ויצויינו בו נימוקי הערר.

*Gist:* The appeal to the medical appeals committee is filed in writing through the NII, with grounds, within 30 days of notice (another 30 only with the NII's consent). ([Disability Regulations regs 20, 21](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הביטוח_הלאומי_(ביטוח_נכות)_(קביעת_אחוזי_נכות_רפואית,_מינוי_ועדות_לעררים_והוראות_שונות))) You may be represented before the committee by your treating doctor or a specialist (reg. 22). An earning-incapacity decision is appealed to an appeals committee within 30 days (reg. 27). The appeals committees' decisions are appealable to the regional labour court on a point of law only, within 60 days (NII Law s. 213; [Appeal Time Regulations reg. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הביטוח_הלאומי_(מועד_להגשת_ערעור_על_החלטות_מסוימות))).

## How it works in practice

### The claim file, in order

1. **Benefit and conditions.** Which benefit (lesson 17.7 table) and each statutory condition.
2. **Evidence per condition.** For disability: every diagnosis with specialist letters, test results, hospital summaries, medication list, and a statement of daily limitations; for income conditions, payslips or a statement of no income.
3. **Claim.** Online through the NII personal area or at a branch; keep the submission confirmation. Diary 30 days: if there is no decision, you may sue (lesson 17.9).
4. **Examination.** Bring the documents and a one-page summary; ask that your complaints be recorded.
5. **Decision.** Note the date you received it: every deadline runs from that day.

### The appeal (ערר) to the medical appeals committee

Write it as a list, one impairment per paragraph: the impairment, the evidence, the item of the impairment list you say applies and why, and what the first doctor or committee did wrong (ignored it, used the wrong item, gave no reasons). Attach new documents. Ask to be examined and to bring your doctor.

### From the appeals committee to the labour court

Read the minutes against your appeal. For each ground, ask: did the committee address it, examine you on it, and give reasons? A "no" is a point of law. Disagreement with the percentage alone is not. The appeal goes to the regional labour court for your residence within 60 days; it is fee-free, and Legal Aid can represent you without a means test (s. 397).

## Worked example

Galina Petrova, 54, from Ashdod, a former bookkeeper, stopped working in 2025 because of rheumatoid arthritis in both hands and severe depression. She claimed a general disability pension in January 2026. The authorised doctor set: hands 20%, depression 20%, combined 36%, below the threshold, with a one-line explanation. She received the decision on 10 August 2026.

1. **Deadline.** 30 days: by 9 September 2026 (reg. 20).
2. **Grounds.** (a) Her rheumatologist's letter describes joint deformity and loss of grip in both hands, matching a higher item of the impairment list than the 20% given; (b) her psychiatrist describes a major depressive disorder with marked limitation, supporting a higher psychiatric item; (c) the doctor's reasons for setting less than 40% do not engage with either letter (s. 208(ג)).
3. **Representation.** She asks her psychiatrist to attend the appeals committee (reg. 22).
4. **Committee.** The appeals committee sets the psychiatric impairment at 30% and keeps the hands at 20%; combined about 44%. With one impairment of 30% (at least 25%), the 40% threshold is met.
5. **Next step.** The claims officer now decides her earning incapacity (s. 209). If refused, she appeals to the appeals committee within 30 days (reg. 27). If the medical appeals committee had ignored the rheumatologist's letter, that would have been a point of law for the labour court within 60 days.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- A real NII matter of your own, or of a family member who asks for help and will sign everything themselves; or Galina's case as a dry run.
- The NII decision letter (if any), all medical and income documents.
- The [NII medical committee appeal template](../../templates/nii-medical-committee-appeal.md).
- About 150 minutes, in two sittings.

### Steps

**Part A: the claim file (60 min)**

1. **Benefit and conditions (15 min).** Name the benefit; list each condition with its section.
2. **Evidence table (25 min).** A table: condition, evidence, document date, missing evidence and how to get it.
3. **One-page summary (15 min).** Diagnoses, treating doctors, daily limitations, work history.
4. **Deadlines (5 min).** The 12-month claim rule (s. 296) and any benefit-specific deadline.

**Part B: the appeal to the medical appeals committee (60 min)**

5. **Read the decision (10 min).** List each impairment and percentage given, and the reasons.
6. **Grounds (30 min).** Fill in the template: one paragraph per impairment, with evidence, the list item you rely on, and the defect.
7. **Requests (10 min).** Examination in person, your doctor's attendance, a copy of the minutes.
8. **Deadline and filing (10 min).** Last day under reg. 20 (or reg. 27 for earning incapacity, or work-injury reg. 27); how you will file and keep proof.

**Part C: the point of law (30 min)**

9. **Mock committee decision (10 min).** Write two sentences of a committee decision that addresses one of your grounds and ignores another (or use a real decision).
10. **Point of law (20 min).** Write a half-page appeal to the regional labour court: the decision, the ground ignored, why that is an error of law, the request (remand to the committee, preferably a different panel), and the 60-day deadline.

### Template

[NII medical committee appeal](../../templates/nii-medical-committee-appeal.md) (parts A and B: the appeal to the committee and the outline of the appeal on law).

### How you know it worked

- Every condition of the benefit has at least one document next to it.
- Each paragraph of your appeal names an impairment, a document and a list item.
- Your Part C appeal argues procedure or law (ignored ground, no reasons), not a different percentage.
- Your deadlines are dated from the day the decision was received.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. The insured person files claims and appeals; representation by a close friend, a union or others is possible before the NII (s. 386), and your doctor may represent you before the medical appeals committee (reg. 22). Helping a relative for free is lawful, but they must sign and file in their own name.

### Where and how

- Claims and appeals: NII personal area online or the local branch ([NII medical committees page](https://www.btl.gov.il/benefits/vaadotRefuiyot/erurVadot/Pages/erurNechutMeavoda.aspx)).
- Appeal on law: the regional labour court for your residence; fee-free; Legal Aid without a means test (s. 397).
- Free help: Legal Aid, disability-rights NGOs, the NII's guidance services (⚠️ manual check), Kol-Zchut's medical committee guide (⚠️ manual check).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The case also involves a tort claim (road accident, medical negligence) whose compensation interacts with NII benefits.
- You lost the appeal on law in the regional court and are considering leave to appeal.
- A private "rights company" offers to take a percentage: compare with free Legal Aid first and check the statutory fee cap.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| A general appeal ("the percentage is too low") | The committee confirms the decision | Supplement with specific grounds within the time allowed | One paragraph per impairment, with documents |
| New documents brought only at the labour court | Court cannot weigh new medical evidence | Ask the NII for re-assessment with the new documents | Give the committee everything |
| Not asking for the minutes | No basis for a point of law | Request them in writing | Ask at the end of every committee |
| Counting deadlines from the letter's date, not receipt | Confusion and late filing | File at once and explain the receipt date | Note the date of receipt on the letter |
| Appeal to the labour court on percentages | Dismissed: law only | Reframe as ignored ground or missing reasons | Separate medical and legal arguments |

## Review

- Build the claim file condition by condition, with evidence for each.
- Appeal medical decisions within 30 days, in writing, with grounds per impairment and documents (regs 20–21).
- Your doctor may represent you before the medical appeals committee (reg. 22).
- An appeals committee decision is appealable on law only, within 60 days, to the regional labour court (s. 213; reg. 2).
- Legal Aid represents claimants in NII cases without a means test (s. 397).
