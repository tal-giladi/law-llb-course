# 17.8 · Welfare rights: income support, housing assistance and the welfare services

By the end of this lesson you will be able to test a household against the conditions for income support, explain the constitutional floor the courts have set under it, ask the municipal social services department for help in a way that produces a written, appealable decision, and find the right door for rent assistance and public housing. This is the "welfare law" half of the module (TAU דיני רווחה, 1411-9226).

## Why it matters

Social security (lesson 17.7) insures against risks through contributions. Welfare law is the last safety net, financed from general taxes and tested on means: income support, rent assistance, public housing and the personal social services of the municipalities. It is also where the gap between rights on paper and rights in practice is widest. Studies by the NII and NGOs repeatedly find that many eligible people do not claim (⚠️ no specific figure verified), because the rules are complex, the conditions demanding and the process discouraging. A self-represented claimant who knows the law has real leverage here.

## The law

### Who may receive income support

> תושב ישראל שמלאו לו 25 שנים זכאי לגימלה, בכפוף להוראות חוק זה, כל עוד מתקיים בו אחד מתנאים אלה: (1) אין לו הכושר לעבוד ולהתפרנס כדי מחייתו [...] (2) הוא רשום בלשכת שירות התעסוקה כמחוסר עבודה [...] ולשכת שירות התעסוקה לא הציעה לו עבודה מתאימה; [...] (4) הוא עובד בעבודה [...] שההכנסה מהן פחותה מההכנסה שבשלה אין לשלם לפי הוראות חוק זה גמלה [...]

*Gist:* An Israeli resident aged 25 or over may receive income support while one condition holds: unable to work; registered as unemployed and offered no suitable work; working with low income; at retirement age; a parent of a child under two; caring for a sick spouse or child; staying in a women's shelter; or other listed groups (some from 20, or younger). ([Income Support Law s. 2(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הבטחת_הכנסה)) The claimant must have been resident for 24 consecutive months before claiming (s. 2(ב)). Some groups are excluded, including most full-time students (s. 3).

*Means test:* the benefit, set in the Second Schedule by family size and age, is reduced by income (s. 5(ב)) after statutory disregards (ss. 12–12ב). Income includes NII benefits, maintenance and a notional income from assets (s. 9); cash up to four times the basic amount (six for a couple or parent) is disregarded, and the home one lives in is not an asset.

### The car rule after Hassan

> תובע שהוא בעל שני כלי רכב או יותר או שהוא בעל רכב ששוויו עולה על סכום שקבע השר [...] לא יהיה זכאי לגמלה לפי חוק זה;

*Gist:* Today only a claimant with two or more vehicles, or one worth more than a set amount, is excluded; a cheaper car leads to a deduction, not disqualification, with exceptions for medical and disability needs. (s. 9א, as amended after Hassan; the 2026 figures noted on Wikisource are in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

### The forum

> לבית דין אזורי [...] תהיה סמכות יחודית לדון ולפסוק בכל תובענה לפי חוק זה ובכל הנובע ממנו [...]

*Gist:* Disputes about income support go to the regional labour court (s. 26), fee-free (Labour Court Fees Regulations reg. 4(21)). The NII administers the benefit and decides the claim (s. 27); procedure is in lesson 17.9.

### The welfare services

> נתבקשה מחלקה לשירותים חברתיים של רשות מקומית ליתן טיפול סוציאלי או להגיש סעד, ימסור הממונה על המחלקה למבקש, בכתב, את ההחלטה בבקשה תוך זמן סביר.
> הרואה את עצמו נפגע על ידי אי מתן תשובה תוך זמן סביר או על ידי החלטה רשאי לערור לפני ועדת ערר בכתב.

*Gist:* Every local authority must run a social services department; a request for social care or assistance must be answered in writing within a reasonable time, and a refusal or a delay can be appealed in writing to a three-member welfare appeals committee (a lawyer chair, a public representative and an experienced social worker), whose final decision can be appealed to the Administrative Affairs Court. ([Welfare Services Law s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_שירותי_הסעד)) The department must also help people realise their rights (s. 2ב, ⚠️ text not quoted).

### Housing

Rent assistance for eligible groups (income-support recipients, people with disabilities, new immigrants, single parents and others) and public housing are administered by the Ministry of Construction and Housing under administrative eligibility rules, mostly through the companies Amidar and Amigour and appointed eligibility-checking companies (⚠️ current procedures on gov.il, manual check). Because the rules are criteria, not statute, the tools of lesson [14.4](../module-14/lesson-04.md) and lesson 14.10 (reasons, hearing, FOI request for the criteria, objection) apply.

## Leading cases

### בג״ץ 366/03 עמותת מחויבות לשלום וצדק חברתי נ׳ שר האוצר (12.12.2005)

Petitioners challenged deep cuts in income support in 2002–2003, arguing they breached the constitutional right to dignity. An expanded panel (President Barak writing) recognised that Basic Law: Human Dignity and Liberty protects a right to a dignified minimum existence, beyond mere survival, but the majority held that the petitioners had not shown that the overall system of support (income support plus other benefits and services) fell below that minimum. Levy J. dissented. Why it matters: it established the constitutional floor while leaving the State wide room to design the benefits; claimants must prove the overall effect on real households. [English translation](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/commitment-peace-and-social-justice-society-v-minister-finance)

### בג״ץ 10662/04 חסן נ׳ המוסד לביטוח לאומי (28.2.2012)

The Income Support Law then treated anyone who owned or used a car as having income equal to the benefit, which in effect disqualified them. A seven-justice panel (President Beinisch writing) unanimously declared the provision void: an absolute presumption infringed the right to a dignified minimum existence, and the infringement was disproportionate because an individual assessment of income, or a rebuttable presumption, was available. The ruling took effect after six months, and the Knesset replaced the rule with value-based limits (s. 9א today). Why it matters: the first time the Supreme Court struck down a social-security provision for violating dignity, and a model for arguing that a blanket exclusion should give way to an individual test. [English translation](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/hassan-v-national-insurance-institute)

## How it works in practice

### Income support: the claim

1. Register with the Employment Service if the work test applies (most claimants under retirement age who can work).
2. Claim online or at the NII branch, with ID, bank details, income documents, rent contract and, if relevant, medical documents or maintenance judgments.
3. Expect a home visit or investigation; the NII may ask for bank statements (s. 21).
4. A decision letter states the amount and the right to sue in the labour court (NII Law s. 397(ה) requires notice of free Legal Aid).

### Welfare department: making the request appealable

Ask in writing, describe the need and the help requested, attach documents, and ask for a written decision. If there is no answer in a reasonable time (for most requests, about 45 days is a sensible yardstick, by analogy to the Decisions and Reasons Law, lesson 14.4), send a reminder, then appeal to the appeals committee.

### Related benefits often missed

Arnona discounts from the municipality, public transport discounts, a free or reduced Legal Aid service, electricity and water discounts, and the "maintenance guarantee" (הבטחת תשלום מזונות) when a former spouse does not pay maintenance (Maintenance (Assurance of Payment) Law). Each has its own application.

## Worked example

Miriam Kahana, 38, a single mother of three in Bet Shemesh, works 20 hours a week as a cleaner, earning about ₪3,600 a month. She owns a 2014 car worth about ₪18,000 that she uses to take her son to weekly physiotherapy. Her former husband pays maintenance irregularly.

1. **Condition.** She works with low income (s. 2(א)(4)) and is a parent of three: a possible claimant.
2. **Means.** Her wage and the maintenance actually received count as income, minus the income disregards for claimants under 55 (s. 12א). The benefit is the difference between the schedule amount and her countable income.
3. **Car.** Her car is below the value limit, so it leads at most to a deduction (s. 9א); if her son's therapy follows a set plan or is at least six times a month for over 90 days, the car rule does not apply at all (s. 9א, medical-treatment exception).
4. **Maintenance.** She should apply to the NII for the maintenance-guarantee payment, which pays maintenance regularly and collects from the former husband.
5. **Welfare department.** She asks in writing for help with after-school care for her children; she receives a written refusal "for budget reasons" and appeals to the appeals committee, attaching her income documents.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your household's income and assets for the last three months (or Miriam's facts).
- Wikisource for the Income Support Law; [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- 40 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Condition (10 min).** Which s. 2(א) condition, if any, fits each adult over 20?
2. **Exclusions (5 min).** Check s. 3 (students, institutions) and the 24-month residence rule.
3. **Means (10 min).** List income sources and assets; mark which are disregarded.
4. **Car (5 min).** Apply s. 9א and its exceptions.
5. **Welfare request (10 min).** Draft a three-paragraph written request to the social services department for one concrete service, asking for a written decision.

### Template

[Letter to a public authority](../../templates/letter-to-authority.md), adapted for the welfare request and the request for reasons.

### How you know it worked

- You named the exact s. 2(א) paragraph for each adult, or explained why none fits.
- Your welfare request asks for a written decision, which you can appeal.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Income support is claimed in person or online; disputes go to the labour court with no fee. Before the NII you may be represented by a "close friend" or others under NII Law s. 386 (lesson 17.7). Welfare appeals committees operate under the Administrative Tribunals Law; you may appear yourself (⚠️ representation rules of the committee not checked).

### Where and how

- NII branch or online for income support; the labour court for disputes.
- Social services department of your municipality; the welfare appeals committee for its district (ask the department for the address; ⚠️ manual check).
- Housing: the Ministry of Construction and Housing's eligibility service (⚠️ manual check).
- Free help: Legal Aid for NII matters without a means test (NII Law s. 397), municipal rights-realisation centres, NGOs such as Yedid and Latet (⚠️ manual check), Kol-Zchut (⚠️ manual check).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The NII demands repayment of years of benefits for alleged concealment, or opens a criminal investigation (s. 20ב).
- Eviction from public housing is threatened.
- A child's welfare is involved in a decision by the social services department (protection proceedings belong to module 18).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Not registering with the Employment Service | Benefit stopped | Register and claim again | Keep every Employment Service appointment |
| Not reporting a change in income or household | Overpayment and a repayment demand | Report at once; ask for a payment plan | Report changes in writing within the period required |
| Accepting an oral "no" from the welfare department | Nothing to appeal | Ask for the decision in writing | Always request a written decision |
| Assuming a car always disqualifies | Benefit never claimed | Check s. 9א and its exceptions | Read the current rule, not the pre-2012 one |
| Missing related benefits | Money left on the table | Apply for discounts and maintenance guarantee | Use a rights checklist each year |

## Review

- Income support: resident 25+ (some younger), resident 24 months, meeting a s. 2(א) condition, subject to a means test.
- The constitution protects a dignified minimum existence (HCJ 366/03); blanket exclusions may fail proportionality (Hassan, HCJ 10662/04).
- The car rule now turns on value and use, with medical and disability exceptions (s. 9א).
- Income-support disputes go to the labour court, fee-free (s. 26).
- Welfare department decisions must be written and can be appealed to the welfare appeals committee, then to the Administrative Affairs Court (Welfare Services Law s. 2).
