# 23.9 · Practice lab: an affidavit and a document bundle for your own case

This lab turns the module into a file you can use. You will write your own affidavit in lieu of main testimony, clean it of hearsay, argument and opinion, build a numbered exhibit bundle whose index shows how each exhibit will be proved, write to an institution for a records certificate, and prepare a notice of electronic evidence. Use a real dispute of your own (a dry run, nothing filed) or the supplied case. Plan about two and a half hours.

## Why it matters

The affidavit and the exhibit bundle are your case on paper. In most magistrates' court cases the judge reads them before the hearing and forms a first impression; cross-examination then tests them. A clean affidavit with a bundle that answers "where did this come from?" before the other side asks is the single biggest improvement a self-represented party can make.

## The law

> (א) תצהיר יהיה ערוך בגוף ראשון, מחולק לסעיפים ומכיל רק עובדות שהמצהיר יכול להעיד מתוך ידיעתו הוא…
> (ב) דין מצהיר בתצהיר חלף עדות ראשית כדין עד במשפט, ואולם התצהיר ייחשב כראיה רק אם המצהיר התייצב לחקירה נגדית.

*Gist:* First person, numbered paragraphs, own knowledge only; evidence only if you attend cross-examination. ([CPR reg. 178](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

- The warning must be given and confirmed on the affidavit by a judge, a lawyer or another listed person ([Evidence Ordinance s. 15](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_הראיות#סעיף_15)).
- Copies are filed; originals at the hearing (CPR reg. 70). Electronic evidence: notice at least three days before (reg. 71; lesson [23.4](lesson-04.md)).
- Third-party records prove their content if the s. 36 foundation is laid (lesson [23.4](lesson-04.md)). Your own data held by a business: Privacy Protection Law s. 13 (lesson [12.2](../module-12/lesson-02.md)).

## Leading cases

### Key text: reg. 178 and s. 15 together

No ruling is needed for this lab: the two provisions set the form, the content and the condition of admissibility. The doctrine behind what you remove (hearsay, opinion, privileged or unlawfully obtained material) is in lessons [23.2](lesson-02.md)–[23.5](lesson-05.md) and [23.7](lesson-07.md).

## How it works in practice

1. **Order of work:** evidence map (lesson [23.1](lesson-01.md)) → exhibits → affidavit → clean → index → third-party requests → notice.
2. **Writing the affidavit:** chronological, grouped by topic headings that follow the elements of your claim; each paragraph one fact with a date; each document by exhibit number.
3. **Signing:** after the final edit, before a lawyer or another authorised person. The fee rule in the Evidence (Lawyer's Fee) Regulations 1978 is still written in lirot, so in practice the fee is agreed with the lawyer; ask in advance. Bring ID and the full affidavit with exhibits.

## Worked example

**Supplied case.** Sapir Biton, a graphic designer from Ashdod, hired "שיפוצי הדרום בע״מ" to renovate her bathroom for ₪38,000 under a signed quote (12.1.2026). She paid ₪25,000 by bank transfer. The contractor's foreman stopped coming on 3.3.2026, leaving the room without tiles; in WhatsApp the company's manager wrote on 10.3: "נחזור ביום ראשון, אל תדאגי". Nobody came. She hired another contractor who finished for ₪21,000. Her neighbour, Dalia, saw the foreman remove the toilet bowl and take it to his van. Sapir sues for ₪8,000 (₪25,000 + ₪21,000 − ₪38,000) plus the cost of a missing toilet bowl, ₪1,200.

Sapir's first draft said: "The contractor is a crook who cheats everyone (see Google reviews)" and "Dalia told me she saw the foreman steal my toilet". In the clean version the first sentence is gone (argument, irrelevant, hearsay), and Dalia gives her own affidavit about what she saw. The Google reviews are dropped. The bank transfer is proved by Sapir's own bank statement, and the WhatsApp by a full chat export.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A dispute of your own with documents (or Sapir's case).
- Your evidence map from lesson [23.1](lesson-01.md).
- A computer with a word processor and PDF printer; your phone with the relevant chats and photos.
- The draft template: [affidavit and exhibit bundle](../../templates/affidavit-and-exhibit-bundle.md).
- About 150 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Collect and number (25 min).** Gather every document and file from your map. Put them in the order you will refer to them. Number them נספח 1, 2, 3… and save one PDF per exhibit with a clear name. Keep original files untouched in a separate folder.
2. **Exhibit index (20 min).** Fill in the index in part ב of the template: description, date, source, how you will show authenticity and completeness, basis of admissibility (own document, other party's words, institutional record, public document). Mark any exhibit without a good answer.
3. **Draft the affidavit (40 min).** Using part א, write the heading and opening formula, then topic sections that follow the elements of your claim. One fact per paragraph, with date and exhibit number.
4. **Clean it (20 min).** Read every paragraph and ask: Did I see, hear or do this? Is it a fact, not an argument or opinion? Is the document attached? Is it lawfully obtained and not privileged? Delete or rewrite every paragraph that fails. Move facts that only another person knows into a list of "facts for witness X".
5. **Witnesses (10 min).** For each fact that only someone else knows, write the witness's name and what they perceived. Ask them to write their own affidavit in their own words; do not write it for them.
6. **Third-party records (15 min).** For each exhibit from a bank, phone company, municipality or other institution, complete part ג of the template: a request for the record and a certificate covering the s. 36 conditions. For your own data, add a Privacy Law s. 13 request.
7. **Electronic evidence notice (10 min).** If you will play a recording or show a video or the original chat, complete part ד (reg. 71) and diary the date three days before the hearing.
8. **Final check (10 min).** Run the checklist under part א. Read the affidavit aloud: anything you would be embarrassed to defend in cross-examination comes out.

### How you know it worked

- Every paragraph of your affidavit is a first-hand fact with a date.
- Every document mentioned appears once in the bundle, with a source and an authentication route in the index.
- Every third-party record has a certificate request; every electronic file has a notice date.
- You could hand the bundle to a stranger and they would understand the case in ten minutes.

### Self-check

- [ ] No hearsay, opinion or argument remains in my affidavit.
- [ ] I kept the original files and devices unedited.
- [ ] I know my affidavit counts only if I attend cross-examination.
- [ ] I know who may confirm my affidavit under s. 15.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. You write and sign your own affidavit and assemble your own bundle (CPR reg. 170). A lawyer or other authorised person confirms the warning on your affidavit without representing you (Evidence Ordinance s. 15). You may not draft affidavits for other people as a service (Bar Association Law s. 20); each witness writes their own.

### Where and how

- Filing: by the date in the court's decision, through the court's filing channels for unrepresented parties ([can I act without a lawyer?](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md)).
- Confirmation of the affidavit: a lawyer, or another person authorised by law (s. 15(ב)); agree the fee in advance.
- Free help: court information centre ([where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md)).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- Your key exhibit's authenticity is disputed or you suspect a forgery.
- You cannot prove an essential element without a document the other side or a third party refuses to give.
- The court has ordered strict page limits or formats you cannot meet.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Writing the witness's affidavit for him | Witness falls apart in cross-examination; s. 20 issue | Ask the witness to rewrite it in his words | Give the witness the topics only |
| Exhibit mentioned but not attached | Fact unproved | Ask leave to add it | Cross-check the index against the affidavit |
| Signing before the final edit | Errors on oath | Correct by a supplementary affidavit with leave | Sign last |
| No source column in the index | Authenticity attacked at the hearing | Prepare testimony about each source | Fill the index as you number |
| Forgetting reg. 71 notice | Recording not played | Ask leave, explaining the delay | Diary the notice date |

## Review

- The affidavit is first person, one fact per paragraph, own knowledge, confirmed under s. 15, and counts only if you attend cross-examination.
- Build the bundle before the affidavit, and give every exhibit a source, an authentication route and a basis of admissibility.
- Lay the s. 36 foundation for third-party records in writing, early.
- Give notice of electronic evidence three days before the hearing (reg. 71).
