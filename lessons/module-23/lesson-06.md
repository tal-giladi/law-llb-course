# 23.6 · Burden of proof, standard of proof and presumptions

At the end of a trial the judge may still not know what happened. The law then answers a different question: who loses when the evidence leaves the court in doubt? This lesson covers the burden of proof (who must prove), the standard of proof (how convinced the court must be), and the presumptions and statutory shifts that move the burden from one side to the other. It is a Bar-exam favourite because it decides cases that look evenly balanced. By the end you can mark, element by element, who bears the burden in your own case and use a presumption to put the doubt on the other side.

## Why it matters

Many self-represented litigants believe that if they tell a convincing story the court will find for them. But the court asks: on this element, who had to prove it, and did they prove it to the required standard? If the plaintiff must prove that the defect existed at delivery, and the evidence is evenly split, the plaintiff loses that element, however sympathetic.

Two ideas run through the lesson:

- **The burden of persuasion** (נטל השכנוע) is fixed by substantive law and rarely moves; whoever bears it loses if the scales are level at the end.
- **The evidential burden** (נטל הבאת הראיות) moves back and forth during the trial: once one side brings enough evidence, the other side must answer it or risk losing.

The policy debate is about who should bear the risk of error. In criminal law the answer is clear: it is better to acquit the guilty than to convict the innocent, so the prosecution must prove guilt beyond reasonable doubt. In civil law the default is neutral (whoever claims must prove), but the legislature and the courts shift burdens where one side controls the evidence (an employer's time records, a hospital's files) or creates the risk (dangerous things). Whether courts should go further, for example by awarding damages in proportion to probability when causation is uncertain, divided the Supreme Court in Malul.

## The law

### Civil cases: the default and the standard

The default rule, rooted in the Talmudic maxim and in case law, is that the party who asserts a fact on which its claim or defence depends must prove it: the plaintiff proves the elements of the cause of action, the defendant proves the elements of a defence such as payment, limitation or contributory negligence. The standard is the **balance of probabilities**: the court finds a fact proved if it is more likely than not. If the scales are even, the party bearing the burden of persuasion loses.

The **evidential burden** shifts: once the plaintiff brings evidence that, if believed, proves the element, the defendant must bring evidence to answer it. The burden of persuasion stays where it was.

### Criminal cases: Penal Law s. 34כב

> (א) לא יישא אדם באחריות פלילית לעבירה אלא אם כן היא הוכחה מעבר לספק סביר.
> (ב) התעורר ספק סביר שמא קיים סייג לאחריות פלילית, והספק לא הוסר, יחול הסייג.

*Gist:* No one is criminally liable unless the offence is proved beyond reasonable doubt; and if a reasonable doubt arises that a defence (such as self-defence) applies and is not removed, the defence applies. ([s. 34כב](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_העונשין#סעיף_34כב))

The accused need not prove innocence; he needs only to raise a reasonable doubt. At the end of the prosecution case, if guilt is not proved even prima facie, the court acquits without hearing the defence (Criminal Procedure Law s. 158; module 24).

### Statutory shifts in civil cases

> בתובענה שהוגשה על נזק והוכח בה כי לתובע לא היתה ידיעה או לא היתה לו יכולת לדעת מה היו למעשה הנסיבות שגרמו למקרה… וכי הנזק נגרם על ידי נכס שלנתבע היתה שליטה מלאה עליו, ונראה לבית המשפט שאירוע המקרה שגרם לנזק מתיישב יותר עם המסקנה שהנתבע לא נקט זהירות סבירה… – על הנתבע הראיה שלא היתה… התרשלות שיחוב עליה.

*Gist:* Where the plaintiff could not know how the accident happened, the damage was caused by property under the defendant's full control, and the event fits negligence better than care, the defendant must prove it was not negligent (res ipsa loquitur). ([Civil Wrongs Ordinance s. 41](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_הנזיקין)) Section 38 does the same for damage caused by a dangerous thing (other than fire or an animal) or by something that escaped, against its owner or keeper (lesson [04.2](../module-04/lesson-02.md)).

> בתובענה של עובד לתשלום שכר עבודה, לרבות גמול שעות נוספות… תהא חובת ההוכחה על המעסיק כי העובד לא עמד לרשות העבודה במשך שעות העבודה השנויות במחלוקת, אם המעסיק לא הציג רישומי נוכחות…

*Gist:* In an employee's wage claim with disputed hours, the employer must prove the employee did not work those hours if it did not produce the attendance records it was obliged to keep; for overtime, the shift is limited to 15 hours a week or 60 a month. ([Wage Protection Law s. 26ב](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_השכר#סעיף_26ב); lesson [17.3](../module-17/lesson-03.md))

Under the [Equal Employment Opportunities Law s. 9](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_שוויון_ההזדמנויות_בעבודה#סעיף_9), once a job applicant or employee shows that she met the employer's stated conditions (or, in a dismissal, that her conduct gave no reason for it), the employer must prove it did not discriminate (lesson [17.5](../module-17/lesson-05.md)).

### Presumptions in the Evidence Ordinance

| Section | Presumption | Rebuttable? |
|---|---|---|
| 21 | The signature on an expert opinion or medical certificate made in Israel is genuine | Yes |
| 34 | Official printing, custody, seals and signatures of public documents are genuine | Yes |
| 34א | What was published in Reshumot was duly done | Yes |
| 43 | A 20-year-old document from proper custody was written and signed by its apparent maker | Court's discretion |
| 57א | A name or trademark on goods or packaging is prima facie evidence that its owner made them; a marketer whose name appears without the manufacturer's is treated as the manufacturer unless he discloses who made them | Yes |
| 57א1 | Food without a production date is presumed made within the year before the offence was discovered | Yes |
| 57ג | A letter properly addressed and posted is presumed delivered when it would arrive in the ordinary course of post | Yes, "if the contrary was not proved" |

A presumption saves you proving a fact directly; the other side may rebut it with evidence. Electronic signatures have their own presumptions (Electronic Signature Law ss. 3–4; lesson [23.4](lesson-04.md)).

## Leading cases

### דנ״א 4693/05 בית חולים כרמל חיפה נ׳ מלול (29.8.2010)

A baby was born with severe defects after a hospital negligently delayed a Caesarean section, but it was uncertain whether the delay or the premature birth caused them. The original appeal awarded damages in proportion to the probability that the negligence caused the harm. On further hearing, the majority (Vice-President Rivlin) held that proportional liability is an exception to the balance-of-probabilities standard only where that standard systematically fails: a recurring risk created towards many potential plaintiffs, where "all or nothing" leads to recurring distortion. Justice Procaccia stressed that changing the standard is for the legislature; Justice Naor, dissenting, would have applied proportional liability to inherent uncertainty about causation in bodily-injury cases.

**Why it matters:** the balance of probabilities remains the rule in Israeli civil law; "51% wins everything, 49% wins nothing". If your case on causation is close, invest in evidence that tips it. English summary: [Versa / LARC](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/105/).

### ע״א 475/81 זיקרי נ׳ ״כלל״ חברה לביטוח בע״מ, פ״ד מ(1) 589

In a civil insurance dispute, Justices Bach and Barak disagreed on whether a civil court needs a higher, intermediate standard to find that a party committed a serious crime. Justice Bach supported an intermediate standard for allegations carrying the stigma of a serious offence; Justice Barak held that there are only two standards, but that the more serious the allegation, the stronger the evidence needed to satisfy the balance of probabilities.

**Why it matters:** if you allege fraud or a crime in a civil case (forgery, arson, theft by an employee), expect the court to demand strong evidence, whichever formulation it uses. Source: [Hebrew Wikipedia, נטל ההוכחה](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/נטל_ההוכחה) (⚠️ secondary source; full text not opened).

## How it works in practice

### The burden table

For each element, write who bears the burden of persuasion, what evidence discharges the evidential burden, and whether any presumption or statutory shift helps.

| Element | Burden on | Standard | Helpful presumption or shift |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contract and its terms | Plaintiff | Balance | Signed document; s. 57ג for a posted notice |
| Breach | Plaintiff | Balance | — |
| Payment of the debt | Defendant (it is his defence) | Balance | Bank record (s. 36) |
| Negligence (an escaped dangerous thing) | Shifts to defendant | Balance | Civil Wrongs Ordinance s. 38 |
| Fraud alleged against you | Accuser | Balance with strong evidence (Zikri) | — |
| Unpaid overtime, no time records kept | Shifts to employer (within the cap) | Balance | Wage Protection Law s. 26ב |

### Evidential damage

Where the defendant's own negligence destroyed or failed to create the evidence the plaintiff needed (for example a hospital's missing monitoring records), courts may shift the burden to the defendant on the affected issue; this "evidential damage" doctrine is case law (⚠️ the leading rulings could not be opened on a free host and are not cited). If the other side controls the records, ask for them early (lessons [15.4](../module-15/lesson-04.md), [12.2](../module-12/lesson-02.md)) so that their absence counts against them.

### Not calling a witness

Under case law (⚠️ rulings not cited), courts may infer that evidence a party could have brought but did not (a key employee, an obvious document) would have harmed it. Bring the witnesses who know the facts, or explain why you cannot.

## Worked example

Samir Hasson, a cook from Shfaram, worked for a catering company for two years and sues for unpaid overtime and for wrongful dismissal after he complained about pay.

1. **Overtime.** Samir testifies he worked 55 hours a week. The company produced no attendance records although it had to keep them. Under Wage Protection Law s. 26ב the employer must prove he did not work the disputed hours, but only up to 15 overtime hours a week. Samir claims 12 a week; the burden is on the employer for all of it.
2. **Dismissal.** Samir alleges dismissal because he complained. That is his claim, so the burden is his. He brings the WhatsApp from the manager, "after your complaint we'll see if you stay", dated a week before the dismissal.
3. **The employer's defence** that Samir was dismissed for theft of food. It alleges a crime, so it must prove it with strong evidence (Zikri). It brings only a supervisor's general statement.
4. **Result.** The labour court awards overtime (the employer failed to rebut), finds the theft allegation unproved, and accepts that the complaint motivated the dismissal on the balance of probabilities.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your evidence map from lesson [23.1](lesson-01.md), or Samir's case.
- The statutes cited above open.
- About 40 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Burden column (10 min).** Add to your map a column "burden on" for every element of your claim and every defence you expect.
2. **Standard (5 min).** For each element write the standard; mark any allegation of fraud or crime (yours or theirs) "strong evidence needed".
3. **Shifts and presumptions (15 min).** Search for anything that shifts the burden or creates a presumption: the Civil Wrongs Ordinance (ss. 38, 41), employment statutes, consumer statutes you met in modules 3 and 20, Evidence Ordinance ss. 57א–57ג, the Electronic Signature Law.
4. **The doubt test (10 min).** For each element, imagine the evidence is perfectly balanced. Who wins? Where the answer is "the other side", list the extra evidence that would tip it.

### How you know it worked

- You found at least one presumption or shift you can use, or confirmed there is none.
- You know which elements you would lose on a tie, and have a plan for each.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Analysing burdens and presumptions is part of preparing your own case and summations (CPR reg. 170). In the labour court and small claims, where you are most likely to appear alone, the burden-shifting rules for wages and discrimination are often decisive.

### Where and how

- Use the burden table in your summations: for each element, say who had to prove it and why it is (or is not) proved.
- Statute texts: Wikisource and the Knesset database (lesson [02.1](../module-02/lesson-01.md)).
- Free help: court information centres, labour-court volunteer desks ([where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md)).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The other side accuses you of fraud, forgery or a crime in a civil case.
- Causation depends on statistics or medical probability (medical negligence).
- You are the accused in a criminal case (module 24).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Assuming the defendant must disprove your claim | Element left unproved; claim dismissed | Ask leave to add evidence before the hearing ends | Mark the burden on every element |
| Not asking the employer for time records | Lose the s. 26ב shift if the employer later produces them | Demand them in disclosure | Ask in the pre-action letter |
| Alleging fraud casually in pleadings | Court demands strong evidence; costs if unproved | Withdraw or narrow the allegation | Allege only what you can prove strongly |
| Proving a posted notice by "I sent it" only | Delivery disputed | Rely on s. 57ג with proof of posting | Send by registered mail and keep the receipt |
| Ignoring a presumption that helps you | Waste effort proving the obvious | Cite the section in summations | Check ss. 57א–57ג and special statutes |

## Review

- The burden of persuasion is fixed and decides ties; the evidential burden shifts during the trial.
- Civil standard: balance of probabilities, with strong evidence needed for allegations of crime (Zikri); criminal standard: beyond reasonable doubt, and a reasonable doubt about a defence is enough (Penal Law s. 34כב).
- Malul kept the all-or-nothing balance of probabilities, allowing proportional liability only in narrow recurring-risk cases.
- Statutes shift burdens: res ipsa and dangerous things (Civil Wrongs Ordinance ss. 41, 38), wages without time records (Wage Protection Law s. 26ב), discrimination (Equal Opportunities Law s. 9).
- Presumptions (ss. 21, 34, 34א, 43, 57א–57ג) prove facts until rebutted.
