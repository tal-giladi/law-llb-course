# 35.8 · Practice lab: review a fee agreement and draft an ethics complaint (dry run)

In this lab you put the module together on one supplied case. In part 1 you review a model fee agreement that contains deliberate problems, mark every one with its legal basis and write the corrections. In part 2, the same lawyer mishandles the client's money, and you draft the client's demand letter and ethics complaint as a dry run. Model answers are at the end of each part, hidden until you have done the work.

## Why it matters

Fee agreements are signed in a hurry, often by people who have just been injured or are in a crisis, and complaints are written in anger. Both are better done calmly with a checklist. Doing it once on a supplied case makes it routine when it is your own.

## Leading cases

The two rulings you will use most: ע״א 9648/16 אורהייטק נ׳ אביטן (unclear fee terms are read against the lawyer; awarded costs are not part of a percentage unless expressly agreed), from lesson [35.4](lesson-04.md); and ע״א 2599/13 הרמן נ׳ עלדור (the structure of a malpractice claim), from lesson [35.6](lesson-06.md).

## How it works in practice

Work in this order: read the whole document once without marking; then go clause by clause with the [fee agreement checklist](../../templates/fee-agreement-checklist.md); then write corrections. For the complaint, write the chronology first, then the summary, then the rules, using the [ethics complaint template](../../templates/bar-ethics-complaint.md). Time yourself: real deadlines rarely allow more.

## Worked example

The supplied case. **Yaakov Mizrahi** (invented), 52, a bus mechanic from Holon, was hit by a car on his way to work in March 2026 and broke his leg. The accident is both a road accident (claim against the driver's compulsory insurer) and a work accident (National Insurance work-injury benefits). The police also opened a traffic file against him for crossing on a red light. While he was in hospital, a man introduced himself as "the representative of attorney Dana Lev-Ari (invented name)" and left the agreement below. Yaakov signed it the next day.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- The model agreement and the facts below.
- The [fee agreement checklist](../../templates/fee-agreement-checklist.md) and the [ethics complaint template](../../templates/bar-ethics-complaint.md) (both new in this module).
- About 2.5 hours: 75 minutes for part 1, 75 for part 2.

### Steps

#### Part 1: review the fee agreement (75 minutes)

1. Read the agreement below once.
2. Number every problem you find. For each, write: the clause, what is wrong, the legal basis, and the corrected wording or the action to take.
3. Calculate the lawful maximum fee for the road-accident claim at three outcomes: the insurer pays ₪180,000 without a claim; a filed claim settles for ₪250,000; a judgment for ₪300,000.
4. Write the reply letter to the lawyer (part ד of the checklist) with your five most important corrections.

**The model agreement (deliberately flawed; do not use as a template):**

> **הסכם שכר טרחה**
>
> שנערך ביום 18.3.2026 בבית החולים וולפסון, לאחר פגישת נציגנו עם הלקוח, בין עו"ד דנה לב-ארי (להלן: "עוה"ד") לבין מר יעקב מזרחי (להלן: "הלקוח").
>
> 1. הלקוח מייפה את כוחה של עוה"ד לטפל בכל עניינו הנובע מהתאונה מיום 2.3.2026, לרבות תביעה לפי חוק הפיצויים לנפגעי תאונות דרכים, תביעות מול המוסד לביטוח לאומי, וייצוגו בתיק התעבורה שנפתח נגדו.
> 2. בעד הטיפול בתביעת הפיצויים ישלם הלקוח 22% מכל סכום שיתקבל, בכל שלב.
> 3. בעד הטיפול מול המוסד לביטוח לאומי ישלם הלקוח 20% מכל גמלה שתשולם לו, לכל ימי חייו.
> 4. בעד הייצוג בתיק התעבורה ישלם הלקוח 3,000 ש"ח, ובנוסף 5,000 ש"ח אם יזוכה.
> 5. שכר הטרחה כולל את כל ההוצאות, ולא יימסר פירוט הוצאות.
> 6. עוה"ד רשאית להעביר את הטיפול בתיק, כולו או חלקו, לכל עורך דין אחר לפי שיקול דעתה.
> 7. כל הכספים שיתקבלו עבור הלקוח יועברו לחשבונה הפרטי של עוה"ד ויוחזקו אצלה עד לתשלום מלוא שכר הטרחה, ללא הגבלת זמן.
> 8. הלקוח רשאי לשלם את שכר הטרחה, כולו או חלקו, גם בהעברת רכבו לעוה"ד.
> 9. הפסיק הלקוח את ההתקשרות מכל סיבה שהיא, ישלם את מלוא שכר הטרחה לפי ההצעה האחרונה של המבטחת.
> 10. הלקוח מסכים לשימוש בכל כלי טכנולוגי, לרבות העלאת כל מסמכיו הרפואיים לכלי בינה מלאכותית ציבוריים.
> 11. הלקוח מוותר על זכותו להתלונן ללשכת עורכי הדין ועל כל דרישה לדין וחשבון כספי.
>
> חתימת הלקוח: __________   חתימת עוה"ד: __________

<details><summary>Model answer, part 1</summary>

| # | Clause | Problem | Legal basis | Correction / action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Preamble | The agreement came through an approach at a hospital bed by the lawyer's "representative" | s. 56 (no solicitation, personally or through others) | Disciplinary issue for the complaint; Yaakov may end the engagement and choose freely |
| 2 | 1 | Scope is vague: no mention of appeals, execution, medical committees vs. labour court, or VAT | Fee agreements are read against the lawyer (Orhightech) | Define each service and stage; state VAT treatment |
| 3 | 2 | 22% exceeds the road-accident caps: 8% without a claim, 11% for a filed claim that settles, 13% after judgment, plus VAT | Road Accident Law s. 16; 1977 rules 2–5 | Replace with the statutory scale; any excess paid is refundable |
| 4 | 2 | "Any sum received" is ambiguous on costs and interest awarded | Orhightech: not included unless expressly agreed | Say expressly whether costs are included, within the cap |
| 5 | 3 | 20% of all benefits "for life" exceeds the NII caps and ignores the 60-month benefit part | National Insurance Law ss. 315ב, 315ד | Choose a column (with or without file-opening fee) and the line for the actual handling; cap stated in the agreement |
| 6 | 4 | A ₪5,000 bonus on acquittal is a contingency fee in a criminal (traffic) case | s. 84(א) | Delete the bonus; fixed fee only |
| 7 | 5 | Fee and expenses in one sum, no itemisation | s. 85 (and s. 86) | Separate, itemised expenses with receipts on request (rule 42(ב)) |
| 8 | 6 | Transfer of the mandate at the lawyer's discretion | Rule 18 (separate, express written consent) | Delete; transfer only with Yaakov's separate written consent |
| 9 | 7 | Client money into the lawyer's private account; unlimited holding | Rules 39–41 (trust account, transfer within reasonable time); s. 88 (lien limited, suit within three months of written demand) | Trust account; transfer within a set period; lien only as s. 88 allows |
| 10 | 8 | Payment in kind (the car) | Rule 9(א) (money only; partner rights only in commercial matters, in writing) | Delete |
| 11 | 9 | Full fee on termination "for any reason" | Penalty-like term; deters changing lawyer; reasonable fee for work done is the default | Pay for work done (or a proportional share of the capped fee) |
| 12 | 10 | Blanket consent to upload medical records to public AI tools | Rules 19–21; Bar AI opinions 2024 and 17.9.2026 (a general clause is not informed consent) | Delete; any AI use with restricted data only by specific, informed consent |
| 13 | 11 | Waiver of the right to complain and to an account | s. 63 (anyone may complain); rule 42; s. 87 (refund "despite any agreement"); public policy | Delete; the waiver does not bind Yaakov anyway |
| 14 | — | Not signed by both parties in the version handed over; no copy given | Burden of proof on the lawyer | Ask for a signed copy |

**Calculations (road-accident claim only, before VAT):**

- ₪180,000 without a claim: 8% = ₪14,400.
- Filed claim settling for ₪250,000: 11% = ₪27,500.
- Judgment for ₪300,000: 13% = ₪39,000.

Under clause 2, Yaakov would pay ₪39,600, ₪55,000 and ₪66,000 respectively: overcharges of ₪25,200, ₪27,500 and ₪27,000.

**The five corrections to send first:** the statutory road-accident scale (3); the NII cap with the chosen column (5); delete the acquittal bonus (6); trust account and transfer period (9); separate expenses (7). Then the rest.

</details>

#### Part 2: the ethics complaint (75 minutes)

**What happened next (supplied facts).** In June 2026 the insurer paid an interim ₪40,000 (Road Accident Law s. 5) to the lawyer. Yaakov learned of it only in October 2026, from the insurer. His three emails (12.8, 3.9, 24.9) were not answered. On 5.10 the lawyer transferred him ₪31,200 with a note: "after deduction of our 22% fee". No account was given. Meanwhile his NII work-injury claim was filed late by an intern, without the lawyer's signature, and the medical committee date was postponed twice because no one from the office came.

1. **Chronology (20 min).** Write a dated, numbered chronology of everything from the hospital visit to today, with the document for each entry.
2. **Sort the issues (10 min).** Mark each: ethics, fee, money, negligence (lesson 35.6).
3. **Demand letter first (15 min).** Draft a short letter to the lawyer: demand an account (rule 42), copies of receipts, the excess fee above the statutory cap, and the file (part ד of the [engagement worksheet](../../templates/lawyer-engagement-worksheet.md)). Give 14 days.
4. **The complaint (25 min).** Draft the complaint with the [ethics complaint template](../../templates/bar-ethics-complaint.md): summary in five lines, chronology table, the rules you think were breached, your prior letters, appendices.
5. **Plan the rest (5 min).** Write the order of steps and the date by which you would file a small claim for the excess fee if there is no answer.

<details><summary>Model answer, part 2</summary>

**Sorting.**

- Ethics: solicitation through a representative (s. 56); late notice of money received and no account (rules 40, 42); private account instead of trust (rule 39); fee above the statutory cap deducted (Road Accident Law s. 16; s. 61(1)); unanswered emails and failure to attend the medical committee (s. 54, rule 2); an intern handling the NII claim without the lawyer's supervision (duty of devotion, s. 54; compare the intern's limits in s. 37).
- Fee and money: the excess deducted. On ₪40,000, 22% is ₪8,800. At most 8% applies if no claim was filed (₪3,200 plus VAT); and an interim payment is part of the compensation on which the final fee is calculated, so taking a fee from it at all should be justified in the agreement. Demand at least ₪8,800 minus the lawful fee, and an account.
- Negligence: so far no lasting loss (the committee dates were postponed); keep it under review.

**Demand letter (Hebrew, short).** "בהמשך לפניותיי מיום 12.8, 3.9 ו-24.9.2026 שלא נענו: 1. אבקש דין וחשבון כספי מלא והעתקי קבלות (כלל 42 לכללי לשכת עורכי הדין (אתיקה מקצועית), התשמ"ו-1986). 2. שכר הטרחה שנוכה (22%) עולה על התעריף המרבי לפי סעיף 16 לחוק הפיצויים לנפגעי תאונות דרכים וכללי הלשכה משנת 1977; אבקש להשיב את ההפרש בתוך 14 ימים. 3. אבקש לקבל את כל מסמכי התיק. אין באמור ויתור על טענה."

**Complaint, summary (five lines).** "שכרתי את עו"ד ___ (שם בדוי) לאחר שנציגה פנה אליי בבית החולים. ביוני 2026 קיבלה עורכת הדין ₪40,000 מהמבטחת עבורי, ולא הודיעה לי במשך כארבעה חודשים, למרות שלוש פניות בכתב. ביום 5.10.2026 העבירה לי ₪31,200 לאחר ניכוי 22% שכר טרחה, מעל התקרה בחוק, בלי דוח כספי. תביעתי לביטוח לאומי הוגשה באיחור בידי מתמחה, ואיש מהמשרד לא התייצב לוועדה הרפואית פעמיים."

**Rules cited (as the complainant understands them):** s. 56; s. 54 and rule 2; rules 39, 40, 42; Road Accident Law s. 16 with s. 61(1).

**Appendices:** the agreement; the insurer's letter on the interim payment; the three emails; the bank transfer and note; NII notices of postponement.

**Sequence.** Demand letter now; complaint in parallel or after 14 days without an answer; small claim for the excess fee after 14 days (within the ceiling: lesson [12.5](../module-12/lesson-05.md)); a new lawyer or self-representation for the NII claim, with a fee within s. 315ב; consider ending the engagement in writing and asking for the file. The complaint does not stop any deadline.

</details>

- You found at least ten of the fourteen problems in part 1, each with a correct legal basis.
- Your three calculations match the statutory percentages.
- Your complaint is factual, dated and documented, cites specific rules, and was preceded by a demand letter.

### Self-check

- [ ] I can review any fee agreement against the checklist in under an hour.
- [ ] I know which problems are unlawful (void or refundable) and which are merely unfavourable.
- [ ] My complaint contains no insult, guess or exaggeration.
- [ ] I sequenced the demand letter, the complaint and the refund claim, and protected the client's other deadlines.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes, for your own agreement and your own complaint (lesson [35.6](lesson-06.md)). This lab is a dry run: nothing is sent.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Where and how

- **The complaint:** the district ethics committee where the lawyer is registered, on the Bar's Form 1 (⚠️ manual check of the current form and address on the Bar's website).
- **The refund:** small claims within the ceiling (see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The money held is large, or the lawyer's failures may have cost you a claim (limitation, a lost hearing): get independent advice on a malpractice claim.
- You are still in the middle of an injury claim: put a new lawyer (or yourself) in charge before you escalate.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Treating every bad clause as void | You refuse terms that are lawful but tough | Separate unlawful from unfavourable | Use the legal basis column |
| Sending the complaint before asking for an account | Weaker complaint; the money question unresolved | Send the demand letter now and update the committee | Demand letter first |
| Long, emotional complaint | Hard to act on; risk of costs if baseless | Rewrite as a dated chronology | Five-line summary plus table |
| Forgetting the underlying claims | Deadlines missed while you fight the lawyer | Diary every deadline | Protect the case first |

## Review

- A fee agreement review goes clause by clause: caps (road accidents, NII), the criminal-case ban, expenses, money handling, mandate transfer, payment in money, AI consent, termination and waivers.
- Unlawful terms are refundable or void "despite any agreement"; unfavourable terms need negotiation.
- A complaint is factual, dated, documented and preceded by a written demand; it runs alongside, not instead of, money claims and deadlines.
