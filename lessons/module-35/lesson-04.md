# 35.4 · Fees: fee agreements, contingency fees and statutory caps

By the end of this lesson you will be able to read a lawyer's fee proposal and say whether it is lawful, how it compares with other structures, what the law caps and what it leaves to bargaining, and what to do if you were charged too much. You will calculate real caps for a road-accident claim and a National Insurance claim.

## Why it matters

Most disputes between clients and lawyers are about money, and most of them start with a vague agreement. Israeli law leaves fees largely to contract, but with four kinds of control: a few outright bans (no contingency fee in criminal cases), statutory caps in fields where vulnerable people deal with repeat players (road accidents, National Insurance, disabled soldiers and hostile-action victims, Holocaust survivors), the Bar's powers to review contingency fees and give opinions, and interpretive rules that put the burden of clear drafting on the lawyer. Knowing these controls changes your bargaining position before you sign and your remedies after.

## The law

### Payment in money; percentages allowed: rule 9

> (א) לא יקבל עורך דין שכר טרחה בעד עבודתו המקצועית אלא בכסף. (ב) אין מניעה, כאשר הדין מאפשר זאת, כי שכר הטרחה יחושב כאחוז מנושא הענין.

*Gist:* Fees are paid in money and may be a percentage of the subject matter where the law allows; in commercial matters a lawyer may take partner rights (shares, options) if agreed in writing (rule 9(ג)). [Rule 9](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/כללי_לשכת_עורכי_הדין_(אתיקה_מקצועית))

### Contingency fees: s. 84

> (א) לא יתנה ולא יקבל עורך דין בעד שירותו במשפט פלילי שכר טרחה התלוי בתוצאות המשפט.
> (ב) התנה או קיבל עורך דין שכר טרחה תלוי בתוצאות המשפט במשפט שאינו פלילי, ונראה ללשכה כי השכר מופרז, רשאית היא, על פי בקשת הלקוח, לקבוע את השכר המתאים.

*Gist:* No success fee in criminal cases; in other cases a success fee is lawful, but on the client's request the Bar may set an appropriate fee if it finds the agreed one excessive. A fee within a statutory maximum is never "excessive" for this purpose (s. 84(ג)). [s. 84](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לשכת_עורכי_הדין#סעיף_84)

The criminal ban protects the integrity of the defence: a lawyer paid only for an acquittal has an incentive to gamble with the client's liberty and to avoid a plea bargain that would serve him.

### Fees and expenses separately: s. 85; consequences: ss. 86–87

> לא יתנה ולא יקבל עורך דין מלקוחו תשלום שיכלול שכר טרחתו והוצאות שהוציא בלי להבחין בין שכר לבין הוצאות ובלי לפרש את ההוצאות.

*Gist:* A lawyer may not charge a global sum mixing fee and expenses without separating them and itemising the expenses. [s. 85](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לשכת_עורכי_הדין#סעיף_85)

Breach of s. 84 or s. 85 does not deprive the lawyer of a reasonable fee (s. 86), but whoever paid more than is due under a maximum tariff or ss. 84 and 86 may recover the excess "על אף כל הסכם", despite any agreement (s. 87).

### Tariffs: ss. 81–83 and the recommended minimum

> המועצה הארצית של הלשכה רשאית לקבוע תעריף מינימלי לשכר טרחה בעד שירותי עורכי דין, שיהיה בבחינת המלצה לחברי הלשכה, אך לא יחייבם.

*Gist:* The Bar may publish a minimum tariff, which is a recommendation only and does not bind its members. [s. 81](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לשכת_עורכי_הדין#סעיף_81)

The Bar Association Rules (Recommended Minimum Tariff) 2000 are in force and are indexed to the consumer price index every January; the latest notice (Reshumot, 5786) updated the sums for 2026. They set, for example, percentage scales for money claims and for tort claims with a success fee, and fees for execution files. They matter less to what your lawyer charges you than to two other things: courts and registrars use them as a benchmark when awarding costs, and the Execution Office adds tariff-based fees to a debt (see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md) for the 2026 figures). Binding maximum tariffs exist only where a regulation or statute sets them (s. 82): the main examples are below.

### Road accidents: s. 16 of the Road Accident Victims Compensation Law and the 1977 rules

> (א) המועצה הארצית של לשכת עורכי הדין תקבע, באישור שר המשפטים, תעריף מקסימלי לשכר טרחה של עורך דין בעד הטיפול בתביעות לפי חוק זה, ובלבד ששכר טרחה שנקבע לפי תוצאות הטיפול לא יעלה על 8% מהסכום שהוסכם לשלמו לנפגע, ואם היו הליכים משפטיים – על 13% מהסכום שנפסק; מי ששילם שכר טרחה העולה על התעריף המקסימלי, זכאי להחזר העודף.

*Gist:* A success fee in a road-accident claim may not exceed 8% of an amount agreed without proceedings or 13% of an amount awarded after proceedings, and a client who paid more is entitled to the excess back. [s. 16](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_פיצויים_לנפגעי_תאונות_דרכים#סעיף_16)

The Bar's 1977 rules fill in the middle step: 13% after judgment (r. 2), 11% where a filed claim ends in a settlement (r. 3), 8% where no claim was filed (r. 4), all plus VAT (r. 5). The district committee cannot authorise a higher fee in these cases (s. 16(ב)). [1977 rules](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/כללי_לשכת_עורכי_הדין_(תעריף_מקסימלי_לשכר_טרחה_בטיפול_בתביעות_לפי_חוק_פיצויים_לנפגעי_תאונות_דרכים))

### National Insurance: ss. 315א–315ג

> (א) על אף האמור בכל דין או הסכם, לא יידרש אדם לשלם בעד טיפול בתביעה תשלום העולה על סכום המחושב בהתאם לסוג הטיפול [...]

*Gist:* Whatever the agreement says, a person may not be charged for handling a National Insurance claim more than the statutory cap for that kind of handling, and may recover any excess. [s. 315ב](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הביטוח_הלאומי)

The cap depends on who does what, and the parties choose one of two columns when they contract: a percentage of the benefit **plus** a file-opening fee, or a slightly higher percentage **without** it. The main lines (column B; lower percentages apply to low benefits under column C):

| Kind of handling (s. 315ב(א)) | With file-opening fee | Without file-opening fee |
|---|---|---|
| Assistance without a lawyer's representation (most benefits) | 11.25% | 12.25% |
| Lawyer representing and appearing before a medical or appeals committee, work injury and similar chapters (ה, יג, יג1) | 15.75% (16% of a s. 107 grant) | 17% |
| Same, other benefits | 13.75% | 15% |
| Lawyer in the labour court, having also handled the claim | 24% | 25.5% |
| Lawyer in the labour court only | 12% | 13.5% |

The file-opening fee may not exceed the "basic sum" (₪800 in the statute, indexed under s. 315ט; see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md) for 2026). Some claims carry only the file-opening fee plus the basic sum (benefits in kind, severe disabilities decided without a committee, recognition of a work injury without a benefit claim). A fee-bearing expert opinion paid by results counts as "handling" (s. 315ג(ב)), which closes the loophole of a "doctor's opinion" charged as a percentage.

### Disabled soldiers and hostile-action victims: the 2026 law

The Fee Limitation (IDF and Security Forces Disabled and Hostile-Action Victims) Law, 5786–2026, extends the same approach to claims before the Defence Ministry's Rehabilitation Department and under the Hostile Action Victims Compensation Law. It caps payment for each stage (recognition, medical committees, appeals) by reference to the degree of disability, voids certain terms in engagement agreements (s. 21) and has transitional rules for existing agreements (s. 28). [Text on Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגבלת_שכר_טרחה_(נכי_צבא_הגנה_לישראל_וכוחות_הביטחון_ונפגעי_פעולות_איבה)). The amounts are in the law and its schedule; check the current text before relying on a figure.

### Legal Aid: s. 6א

> עורך דין שנתמנה לתת שירות משפטי לפי חוק זה, לא יקבל שכר טרחה והוצאות אלא לפי תקנות שהותקנו לפי חוק זה.

*Gist:* A lawyer appointed by Legal Aid may take fees and expenses only under the Legal Aid regulations, not from the client. [s. 6א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הסיוע_המשפטי#סעיף_6א)

The client pays only a participation fee (reg. 1א), with an advance before the application is decided (reg. 3א) and exemptions (reg. 3ב); costs awarded to a Legal Aid client go to the State (s. 6). See [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### Money held for the client: rules 39–42 and the lien in s. 88

A lawyer keeps client money in a separate trust account (r. 39), tells the client and passes on money received within a reasonable time (r. 40), and gives an account on request (until three years after the end of the work) and at the end of the work (r. 42). He may hold client money and documents to secure his fee (s. 88), but not money held in trust other than for the client alone or maintenance money for a spouse and children, and only if he sues for the fee within three months after the client's written demand for what was held.

## Leading cases

### ע״א 9648/16 אורהייטק GIS בע״מ נ׳ חן אביטן – משרד עורכי דין (28.2.2018)

A law firm and its client disputed whether a series of emails formed a fee agreement and whether the firm's percentage "of any sum received, awarded or paid" included costs awarded to the client by an arbitrator. The Supreme Court (Elron J., with Shoham and Solberg JJ.) applied the rule that the lawyer bears the burden of proving the agreed terms and of drafting the fee agreement clearly, and that an unclear agreement is read against him. Unless the agreement expressly says so, a percentage fee is not taken from costs awarded to the client.

Why it matters: it is your best argument when a lawyer claims more than the agreement clearly says. Read every clause of a proposal as the court would: what is not written clearly will probably not bind you, but it will also cause a dispute. [Text hosted by Reichman University](https://www.colman.ac.il/media/4qbiik4c/9648-16_orhightech_v_avitan.doc)

### ע״א 136/92 ביניש-עדיאל נ׳ דניה סיבוס חברה לבנין בע״מ, פ״ד מז(5) 114 (as quoted)

Where no fee was agreed, the lawyer is entitled to a reasonable fee under Contracts (General Part) Law s. 46. The Supreme Court listed the methods (percentage, hours, hearings, retainer) and the factors: the size of the transaction, the actual work, its complexity, routine or creative work, a regular or occasional client, the lawyer's experience and standing, and in civil matters the results; and it held that the reasonable fee is a matter of evidence, usually expert evidence, not judicial notice. ⚠️ Cited from the long quotation in the Reichman University ethics newsletter; the judgment text was not opened.

Why it matters: without a written agreement you are exposed to a claim for a "reasonable fee" proved by another lawyer's opinion. A clear agreement is cheaper than litigating what was reasonable. [Newsletter with the quotation](https://www.colman.ac.il/media/farl45p3/gilion_7_dereh_kviat_shahar_hatrha_helek_sheni_lzg.doc)

## How it works in practice

### Fee structures compared

| Structure | Good for | Risks for you | What to insist on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fixed fee | Defined tasks (a will, a contract, a single hearing) | Scope disputes ("that wasn't included") | A precise scope and the price of extras |
| Hourly | Open-ended advice, complex litigation | Open-ended bills | Hourly rate, who bills at what rate, monthly reports, a cap or estimate |
| Percentage of value | Transactions (purchase, estate) | Fee unrelated to actual work | Whether VAT is added; what value counts |
| Success fee (contingency) | Damages claims where you cannot pay upfront | Excessive share of a large award; pressure to settle | Statutory cap if any; whether costs awarded are included (by default no: Orhightech); what happens if you switch lawyer |
| Mixed (base + success) | Most civil claims | Paying twice | Base credited against the success fee |

### Calculation: road accident

Dana was injured in a road accident. The insurer offers ₪240,000 without a claim being filed.

- Maximum fee: 8% × ₪240,000 = ₪19,200 + VAT.
- If she files a claim and it settles for ₪300,000: 11% × ₪300,000 = ₪33,000 + VAT.
- If it ends in a judgment for ₪300,000: 13% = ₪39,000 + VAT.

An agreement for "20% of any compensation" is unlawful to the extent it exceeds these caps; she may pay the cap and recover any excess (s. 16(א)). The National Insurance benefit is not part of the road-accident compensation the lawyer obtained.

### Calculation: National Insurance

Yossi's lawyer represented him before the work-injury medical committee and won a monthly disability benefit. They chose the column with a file-opening fee. The cap is 15.75% of the "benefit part" defined in s. 315ד (in short, the benefit paid under the decision for a period of up to 60 months), plus a file-opening fee not exceeding the basic sum, plus VAT. If the case went on to the labour court with the same lawyer, the cap for the whole handling rises to 24%.

### Before you sign: the fee checklist

1. Is it in writing, signed by both, with the scope of work?
2. Is any part a success fee? Is there a statutory cap for this kind of case? Is the matter criminal?
3. Are expenses separate and itemised (s. 85)?
4. Is VAT included or added?
5. Does the percentage apply to costs awarded to you? (Only if expressly written.)
6. What is payable if the case ends early, you settle yourself, or you change lawyer?
7. Where will money paid to you go (trust account), and how fast will it reach you?

The full checklist is in the draft [fee agreement checklist](../../templates/fee-agreement-checklist.md) (new in this module) and the lab in lesson [35.8](lesson-08.md).

### When the fee is wrong

| Problem | Remedy |
|---|---|
| Above a statutory cap (road accident, NII, the 2026 law, a maximum tariff) | Refund of the excess "despite any agreement" (s. 16(א) Road Accident Law; s. 315ב(ג) NII Law; s. 87 Bar Law); a small claim if within the ceiling |
| Success fee in a civil case that looks excessive | Ask the Bar to set an appropriate fee (s. 84(ב)): its decision is binding in this case |
| No agreement, or unclear agreement | Negotiate; in court the lawyer must prove the terms and a reasonable fee (Orhightech; Beinish-Adiel) |
| Any fee dispute | Agree to arbitration by the Bar's fee-dispute service, or litigate (lesson [35.6](lesson-06.md)) |

## Worked example

Hadas Ben-David, a cleaner from Kiryat Shmona, was hit by a car. An office offers her this agreement: "Fee: 20% plus VAT of all sums received from the insurer and from National Insurance, including costs and interest. Expenses: included in the fee. If the client ends the engagement, the full fee is due on the insurer's last offer."

1. **Road-accident part.** The cap is 8%, 11% or 13% plus VAT depending on the stage (s. 16; 1977 rules). 20% is unlawful above the cap; any excess paid is refundable.
2. **National Insurance part.** If the office also handles her NII work-injury or general disability claim, its fee is capped by s. 315ב by kind of handling; 20% exceeds the cap even for a medical-committee representation (15.75% plus file-opening fee, or 17%).
3. **Costs and interest.** Including costs awarded to her is lawful only if clearly agreed; here it is written, but it cannot lift the total above the statutory caps.
4. **Expenses "included".** A global sum without separating and itemising expenses breaches s. 85; the lawyer still gets a reasonable fee (s. 86), but Hadas may insist on a separate expenses clause.
5. **Termination clause.** A full fee "on the last offer" if she leaves may amount to a penalty or an unfair term; it also deters her from changing lawyer. She should ask for payment for work done, capped proportionally.
6. **Her move.** She returns the draft with corrections: the statutory caps by stage, separate itemised expenses, no fee from costs unless expressly agreed within the cap, a trust-account clause, and payment for work done if she ends the engagement.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A real fee proposal or engagement letter you received, or the worked example.
- A calculator and the tables above.
- 50 minutes.

### Steps

1. Identify the structure (fixed, hourly, percentage, success, mixed) and the type of case (criminal? road accident? NII? Defence Ministry rehabilitation?).
2. Check each statutory control: criminal ban (s. 84(א)), statutory caps (s. 16; s. 315ב; the 2026 law), separate expenses (s. 85), Legal Aid (s. 6א).
3. Calculate the maximum fee at two realistic outcomes (an early settlement and a judgment).
4. Answer the seven checklist questions in writing.
5. Draft a short reply to the lawyer listing the changes you want, each with its legal basis.

- Your two calculations show the fee in shekels at each outcome, with VAT treatment stated.
- Each requested change has a reason a court or the Bar would accept.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Negotiating your own fee agreement, demanding a refund above a statutory cap, asking the Bar to set a contingency fee (s. 84(ב)) and suing for a refund are all done in your own name.

### Where and how

- **Refund above a cap:** a written demand to the lawyer citing the section; then a small claim if within the ceiling (lesson [12.5](../module-12/lesson-05.md)). See [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md) for the ceiling.
- **Excessive success fee (s. 84(ב)):** a written request to the Bar's district committee or its fee committee (⚠️ manual check: the Bar's website did not open for automated checks; ask the district office for the current procedure).
- **Legal Aid:** apply instead of signing a percentage agreement if you may qualify (lesson [12.8](../module-12/lesson-08.md)).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The claim is large (serious injury, permanent disability), and the choice of structure will cost or save you tens of thousands: a second lawyer's opinion on the agreement is worth paying for.
- The lawyer is holding significant money of yours and refuses to account: see lesson 35.6.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Signing a percentage above the statutory cap | You overpay | Demand the excess back; it is recoverable despite the agreement | Check the cap before signing |
| No written agreement | Dispute over a "reasonable fee" proved by expert evidence | Write down what was agreed now and send it to the lawyer | Always sign a written agreement |
| Fee and expenses in one lump sum | You cannot check what was spent | Ask for itemised expenses (s. 85) | Require a separate expenses clause |
| Assuming the fee includes costs awarded to you, or not | Dispute at the end of the case | Rely on Orhightech: not included unless explicit | Make the agreement say it either way |
| Agreeing to a success fee in a criminal case | Unlawful arrangement; disciplinary offence by the lawyer | Renegotiate to a fixed or hourly fee | Never accept it |

## Review

- Fees are contractual but controlled: no success fee in criminal cases (s. 84(א)), separate expenses (s. 85), and refunds of any excess over a lawful cap despite the agreement (s. 87).
- Statutory caps: road accidents 8% / 11% / 13% plus VAT by stage; National Insurance by kind of handling; the 2026 law for disabled soldiers and hostile-action victims; Legal Aid lawyers paid only under the regulations.
- The Bar's minimum tariff is a non-binding recommendation, indexed yearly, used mainly for costs and execution fees.
- The lawyer must draft the fee agreement clearly and bears the risk of ambiguity; a percentage does not reach awarded costs unless expressly agreed (Orhightech).
- Without an agreement, the lawyer gets a reasonable fee that must be proved (Beinish-Adiel).
