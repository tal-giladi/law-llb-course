# 35.5 · Choosing and supervising a lawyer

Lesson [12.8](../module-12/lesson-08.md) taught you when to stop and get a lawyer. This lesson is about what happens next: how to find a lawyer lawfully and sensibly, what to agree before you sign, and how to stay in charge of your own case once someone else is running it. By the end you will have a shortlist, a comparison, an engagement checklist and a supervision routine for a real matter.

## Why it matters

Hiring a lawyer does not hand over your case; it appoints an agent. You keep the decisions that matter (whether to sue, settle, appeal, plead guilty), and the lawyer owes you loyalty, information and an account. In practice, many clients lose control: they do not know who handles the file, do not see what is filed, cannot reach the lawyer before a deadline, and only learn what they owe at the end. The Bar's rules give you tools against all of this, but only if you use them from the first meeting.

## The law

### What a lawyer may tell you in advertising: s. 55 and the Advertising Rules

> עורך דין לא יפרסם פרסומת אף אם היא עומדת בתנאים שנקבעו בכללים אלה, אם יש בה כדי להטעות את הציבור או לפגוע בו, או שהיא פוגעת בכבוד המקצוע.

*Gist:* No lawyer's advertisement may mislead or harm the public or offend the dignity of the profession, even if it meets the technical rules. [Advertising Rules, r. 2א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/כללי_לשכת_עורכי_הדין_(פרסומת))

Advertising is allowed only through listed media (print, directories, websites, social networks and lawyer apps, the office sign and stationery) and may contain only listed details: name, title, academic degrees, areas of practice, languages, year of admission, publications, positions, office details, and free text offering a legal service "that does not include any reference to the fee" (r. 3(ב)(14)). Since 2018 a lawyer may publish recommendations, thank-you letters and ratings, provided they do not create a misleading impression (r. 3(יא)), and may name clients or show photos with them only with their written informed consent (r. 3(ה), (ט)). Paid articles and interviews must be marked as advertising (r. 3(י)(2)). The lawyer must keep copies of ads for three years (r. 3א).

> לא ישדל עורך דין, בעצמו או על ידי אחר, כל אדם למסור לידיו עבודה מקצועית.

*Gist:* A lawyer may not solicit work, personally or through others. [s. 56](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לשכת_עורכי_הדין#סעיף_56)

A lawyer who approaches you at a hospital bed after an accident, or a "representative" who calls you on a lawyer's behalf, is doing what s. 56 forbids. Rule 5 lets a lawyer give written information about the office and its experience to people who ask.

### Starting and ending the relationship: rules 12, 13, 27

A lawyer may decline a case at his discretion but must tell you within a reasonable time (r. 12); once he accepts, he may stop only for the reasons in rule 13 and in a way that does not harm you (lesson [35.3](lesson-03.md)). A new lawyer who takes over must notify the previous lawyer without delay and make sure the notice arrived (r. 27). You may end the relationship at any time; what you owe is a matter of the agreement and of a reasonable fee for work done (lesson [35.4](lesson-04.md)).

### Control of the mandate: rule 18

> על אף הוראה כללית שביפוי כוח המרשה להעביר את הטיפול לעורך דין אחר, לא יעביר עורך דין את יפוי הכוח לעורך דין אחר, אלא אם כן נתן לכך הלקוח הסכמה נפרדת ומפורשת בכתב [...]

*Gist:* Even if the power of attorney allows transfer, the lawyer may not transfer it to another lawyer without your separate, express written consent, though he may delegate tasks to another lawyer or use one to help. [Rule 18](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/כללי_לשכת_עורכי_הדין_(אתיקה_מקצועית))

### Money and accounts: rules 39–42 and s. 88

Your money goes into a separate trust account (r. 39); you must be told of sums received and get them within a reasonable time (r. 40); you may demand an account at any time until three years after the work ends, and receive one in any case at the end (r. 42(א)); you may ask for copies of receipts for expenses (r. 42(ב)). The lawyer's lien (s. 88) lets him hold money and documents to secure his fee, but not maintenance money or money held in trust for others, and only if he sues within three months of your written demand.

### Your right to a lawyer before authorities: s. 22

> אדם שייפה כוחו של עורך דין זכאי להיות מיוצג על ידיו בפני כל רשויות המדינה, רשויות מקומיות וגופים ואנשים אחרים הממלאים תפקידים ציבוריים על פי דין [...]

*Gist:* A person who appoints a lawyer may be represented by him before any state or local authority or public body, subject to powers to require personal attendance and to enactments that regulate representation. [s. 22](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לשכת_עורכי_הדין#סעיף_22)

## Leading cases

### על״ע 3174/93 פלוני נ׳ ועד מחוז תל אביב (unpublished, as reported)

According to the Reichman University ethics newsletter, the Supreme Court held that a lawyer who has accepted a case cannot adopt a "sit and do nothing" stance because the fee agreement was breached: unless and until he withdraws properly, he must handle the matter loyally and to the best of his ability. The National Ethics Committee has added that a lawyer may not delay service for non-payment where this could cause the client irreversible harm, and that a clause making service conditional on payment is improper. ⚠️ The decision is unpublished and was not opened; cited from the newsletter.

Why it matters: if your lawyer goes silent before a deadline because of a fee dispute, he is in breach. Write to him immediately, demand that he act or withdraw properly so that you can act yourself or appoint someone else, and protect the deadline. [Newsletter text](https://www.colman.ac.il/media/yanlrhr0/gilion_13avthat_tashlom_shar_hatrha_hafsakat_yizog_pnia_l.doc)

### Key text: rule 42 and the three-year window

You do not need to suspect wrongdoing to ask for an account. Rule 42 gives you the right on demand. A lawyer who does not answer within a reasonable time breaches a specific rule, which makes a later complaint simple and concrete (lesson [35.6](lesson-06.md)).

## How it works in practice

### Finding candidates

| Source | What it tells you | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Recommendations from people with the same kind of case | Real experience of service | One person's luck |
| Lawyers' websites and directories | Areas of practice, experience, languages (Advertising Rules) | Ratings and "success rates" can mislead; ads may not mention fees |
| Published judgments | Whether the lawyer actually litigates this kind of case and how the court saw it | A search by name returns all results, not only wins |
| The Bar's register and disciplinary rulings | Licence and public discipline (lesson [35.1](lesson-01.md)) | No record does not mean no complaints |
| Legal Aid, clinics, NGOs in the field | Free or low-cost options (lesson 12.8) | Eligibility tests, waiting time |

### The first meeting

Bring the one-page summary from the [get-a-lawyer checklist](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md) and the questions in part א of the [engagement worksheet](../../templates/lawyer-engagement-worksheet.md). Ask for a written fee proposal before the meeting if the meeting itself is paid. Ask what you can do yourself to keep costs down (collect documents, draft the chronology, attend the medical committee).

### Comparing proposals

Put two or three proposals side by side: scope, who does the work, fee at two outcomes (lesson 35.4), expenses, reporting, response time, AI use and data handling (lesson [35.7](lesson-07.md)). The cheapest proposal with an unclear scope is often the most expensive.

### Supervising

1. **Copies of everything filed and received.** Ask for them as a standing instruction; in court cases you can also see the file on Net HaMishpat with your national ID login (lesson [08.8](../module-08/lesson-08.md)).
2. **A diary of deadlines.** Keep your own; ask the lawyer to confirm each one.
3. **Decisions stay with you.** Settlement offers must be passed on to you; you decide.
4. **Accounts.** Ask for a statement after every payment received for you (rules 40, 42).
5. **Changing lawyer.** Write a termination notice, ask for the file and money (s. 88 limits), sign a new power of attorney, and make sure the new lawyer notifies the court and the old lawyer (r. 27). Do it well before a deadline.

## Worked example

Nadia Khoury, a pharmacist from Haifa, needs a lawyer for a dispute with a contractor over defects in her new apartment (lesson [20.9](../module-20/lesson-09.md)).

1. **Shortlist.** A colleague's lawyer (real-estate generalist), a firm whose website lists "construction defects" and shows client ratings, and a lawyer whose name appears in three published judgments against contractors.
2. **Checks.** All three are in the Bar's register; none has a published disciplinary ruling. The firm's site shows a "98% success rate". Nadia treats it as advertising that may mislead (r. 2א) and asks what it measures.
3. **Proposals.** Generalist: ₪350 per hour, no estimate. Firm: 15% of any recovery plus ₪5,000 base, expenses included. Litigator: ₪12,000 fixed until the end of the preliminary hearing, then 10% of any recovery, expenses separate.
4. **Analysis.** The firm's "expenses included" breaches s. 85; its percentage on top of a base is not credited. The generalist's open hourly rate is risky for a case needing an engineer's opinion. The litigator's mixed structure caps the early cost and separates expenses; Nadia asks to add that the percentage does not apply to costs awarded to her (Orhightech) and that her money goes to a trust account.
5. **Engagement.** She signs with the litigator after receiving written answers to the conflict questions (the contractor's company was never a client) and a clause that her data will not go into public AI tools.
6. **Supervision.** She keeps her own diary, asks for copies of each filing, and asks for an account when the contractor pays an interim sum.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A matter of your own where you would consider a lawyer (or Nadia's case).
- The [engagement worksheet](../../templates/lawyer-engagement-worksheet.md) and the [get-a-lawyer checklist](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md).
- About an hour.

### Steps

1. Run the get-a-lawyer checklist (lesson 12.8) and decide what you need: a one-off consultation, limited help, or full representation (10 min).
2. Find three candidates from at least two different sources; record why each is on the list (10 min).
3. Check each in the Bar's register and for published disciplinary rulings (10 min; lesson 35.1).
4. Write the ten questions of part א, adapted to your matter (10 min).
5. If you have proposals, compare them in a table with the fee at two outcomes (10 min). If not, write what proposal you would accept.
6. Write a one-page supervision plan: what you want copies of, how often you want updates, your deadline diary, and what would make you change lawyer (10 min).

### Template

- [Choosing and supervising a lawyer worksheet](../../templates/lawyer-engagement-worksheet.md) (new in this module).
- [Get-a-lawyer checklist and one-page case summary](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md) (from module 12).

### How you know it worked

- You have three checked candidates and a reason for each.
- Your comparison shows the cost at two outcomes and flags any s. 85 or cap problem.
- Your supervision plan names the documents you will receive and a trigger for changing lawyer.

### Self-check

- [ ] I know what a lawyer's ad may and may not say, including about fees and ratings.
- [ ] I know that my lawyer may not transfer my mandate without my separate written consent.
- [ ] I can ask for an account at any time up to three years after the work ends.
- [ ] I know the steps to change lawyer without losing my file or a deadline.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Choosing, instructing, supervising and dismissing your own lawyer are your rights as a client. You may also take over your case yourself at any stage by filing a notice that you are acting in person (regs 170 and following of the Civil Procedure Regulations; lesson [01.1](../module-01/lesson-01.md)).

### Where and how

- **The Bar's register and published rulings:** the Bar's website (⚠️ manual check).
- **Your court file:** Net HaMishpat, with your personal login (lesson 08.8).
- **Free or low-cost alternatives:** Legal Aid, clinics, NGOs (lesson 12.8; [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md)).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You are changing lawyer close to a hearing or deadline in a complex case: get the new lawyer in place first, then end the old engagement.
- The previous lawyer refuses to release your file or money and the amounts are significant (lesson 35.6).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Choosing by ad rating or "success rate" | Misleading picture of the lawyer | Ask what the figure means; check judgments | Use several sources |
| No written scope | "That was not included" bills | Agree the scope in writing now | Part ג of the worksheet before signing |
| Not keeping copies or a deadline diary | You discover problems too late | Ask for the full file today | Standing instruction for copies |
| Changing lawyer days before a hearing | Postponement, costs, lost hearing | Ask the court for time, explaining the change | Change early; new lawyer first |
| Letting the mandate pass to an unknown lawyer | Someone you never chose runs your case | Object in writing (r. 18) | Refuse general transfer clauses |

## Review

- Find lawyers through several sources; ads must not mislead, may not mention fees in free text, and may show ratings and recommendations (Advertising Rules r. 2א, 3).
- Solicitation is banned (s. 56): a lawyer who approaches you uninvited is breaching the law.
- Fix scope, staffing, reporting, fees, money handling and AI use in writing before you sign.
- You control the mandate (r. 18) and the money (rules 39–42); you may demand an account for three years after the work ends.
- A lawyer who accepted a case must act or withdraw properly; he may not go silent over a fee dispute (על״ע 3174/93, as reported).
