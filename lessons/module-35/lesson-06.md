# 35.6 · When things go wrong: ethics complaints, fee disputes and malpractice claims

By the end of this lesson you will be able to sort a grievance against a lawyer into the right channel, know what each channel can and cannot give you, and avoid the common mistake of using the wrong one: complaining to the Bar when you want your money back, or suing for negligence when the real problem is a fee. Lesson [04.7](../module-04/lesson-07.md) introduced professional liability in tort; here you apply it to lawyers, alongside the Bar's disciplinary system.

## Why it matters

There are three different systems, with different purposes:

- **Discipline** protects the public and the profession. It punishes the lawyer (up to expulsion), but it is not designed to compensate you, though it can order modest compensation and the return of a fee.
- **Fee disputes** are about what you owe or should get back.
- **Malpractice claims** compensate you for loss caused by the lawyer's negligence or breach of contract.

The same facts often belong in more than one system. A lawyer who missed a limitation period, hid it, and then billed you in full may face a complaint (misleading a client), a fee refund (no fee for worthless work) and a negligence claim (the value of the lost claim). Choosing and sequencing them well is a skill.

## The law

### What is a disciplinary offence: s. 61

> אלה עבירות משמעת: (1) הפרת הוראה מהוראות סעיף 53 או של דין אחר המטיל חיוב או איסור על עורך דין בקשר למקצועו; (2) הפרת כללי האתיקה המקצועית [...]; (3) כל מעשה או מחדל אחר שאינו הולם את מקצוע עריכת הדין.

*Gist:* A disciplinary offence is a breach of the duty to uphold the profession's dignity (s. 53) or of any law imposing duties on lawyers, a breach of the ethics rules, or any other act or omission unbecoming the profession. [s. 61](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לשכת_עורכי_הדין#סעיף_61)

Paragraph (3) is deliberately open: conduct need not breach a specific rule to be punished. Discipline covers conduct abroad (s. 62) and continues alongside criminal proceedings (s. 80). A criminal conviction involving moral turpitude can itself lead to a penalty (s. 75) and to temporary suspension (s. 78).

### Who files the charge, and on whose complaint: s. 63

> ועדת האתיקה הארצית, ועדת אתיקה מחוזית וכן היועץ המשפטי לממשלה ופרקליט המדינה (להלן – קובל) רשאים להגיש קובלנה לבית דין משמעתי בשל עבירת משמעת, בין ביזמתם ובין על פי תלונת אדם אחר [...]

*Gist:* Only the National and district ethics committees, the Attorney General and the State Attorney may file a disciplinary charge, on their own initiative or on anyone's complaint. [s. 63](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לשכת_עורכי_הדין#סעיף_63)

So **anyone** may complain, including a client, an opposing party, a judge or a member of the public; but the complainant is not a party who prosecutes. The 2015 procedure rules set the path: a complaint on Form 1 (available on the Bar's website and offices; the committee may accept another format in special cases) (r. 2); the committee sends it to the lawyer, warning that the reply may be used as evidence (r. 4); the lawyer replies within 30 days (r. 5); the committee may ask the complainant for more details, an affidavit and evidence (r. 6); it decides within 90 days whether to file a charge (r. 7(א)); facts older than ten years need specific reasons (r. 7(ב)); if it decides not to file, it must tell the complainant and the lawyer in a reasoned written notice and tell the complainant that he may turn to another prosecutor (r. 8); if it files, it tells the complainant of the right to attend (r. 10). [2015 rules](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/כללי_לשכת_עורכי_הדין_(סדרי_הדין_בבתי_הדין_המשמעתיים))

### The hearing and the penalties: ss. 64–69

The case is heard by the district tribunal where the lawyer is registered (s. 64), in public unless one of the listed grounds justifies closed doors; even then the complainant may attend with a companion (s. 65א(ד)). The tribunal may admit evidence a court would exclude, for special reasons (s. 67).

> אלה הענשים [...]: (1) אזהרה; (2) נזיפה; (3) קנס בסכום שלא יעלה על 25,000 שקלים חדשים לכל עבירה [...]; (4) השעיה לתקופה קצובה שלא תעלה על עשר שנים; (5) הוצאה מן הלשכה.

*Gist:* Penalties range from a warning and a reprimand to a fine of up to ₪25,000 per offence, suspension for up to ten years, and expulsion. [s. 68](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לשכת_עורכי_הדין#סעיף_68)

Section 69 adds orders that matter to clients: the convicted lawyer may be ordered to return a fee obtained in circumstances amounting to an offence (s. 69(א)(3)), and to pay compensation of up to ₪25,000 per offence to the complainant or another injured person, without affecting civil liability (s. 69(א)(4); see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)). The risk for complainants: if the lawyer is acquitted and the complaint was frivolous, vexatious or baseless, the tribunal may order the complainant to pay costs (s. 69(א)(5)), but only after giving him a chance to be heard (r. 45), and he may appeal like a civil judgment (s. 73). Suspensions and expulsions are published with the lawyer's name (s. 69(ב)–(ג)), and every judgment is available to the public (s. 69ב).

### Appeals: ss. 70–72

The lawyer and the prosecutor may appeal to the National Disciplinary Tribunal, and from there to the Jerusalem District Court within 30 days (ss. 70–71); the National Ethics Committee and the Attorney General may appeal even if they did not prosecute. A judgment is not stayed merely because an appeal is possible (s. 72).

### Fee disputes: ss. 84(ב), 89 and rule 31

On a non-criminal success fee, the client may ask the Bar to set an appropriate fee (s. 84(ב), lesson [35.4](lesson-04.md)); a court, tribunal or arbitrator may ask the Bar for its opinion on a fee (s. 89), which does not bind the court. Many fee disputes are settled by agreed arbitration through the Bar's district fee committees (⚠️ manual check: the procedure and forms are on the Bar's website, which could not be opened for this lesson). Before suing another lawyer for a client, a lawyer must first write to him and try to settle (rule 31): if you hire lawyer B to sue lawyer A, expect B to write to A first.

### Malpractice: the Civil Wrongs Ordinance and the Prescription Law

> [...] או שבמשלח יד פלוני לא השתמש במיומנות, או לא נקט מידת זהירות, שאדם סביר ונבון וכשיר לפעול באותו משלח יד היה משתמש או נוקט באותן נסיבות – הרי זו התרשלות

*Gist:* A professional is negligent if he did not use the skill or care that a reasonable, prudent and qualified member of that profession would have used in the circumstances. [s. 35](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_הנזיקין#סעיף_35)

The client also has a contract claim: the duty of loyalty and care is an implied term of the engagement. The limitation period is seven years (Prescription Law s. 5(1)) from the day the cause of action arose (s. 6), but if the facts were hidden from you for reasons outside your control and you could not have discovered them with reasonable care, it runs from discovery (s. 8); concealment by the defendant suspends it (s. 7). See [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

## Leading cases

### ע״א 2599/13 הרמן נ׳ עלדור (3.9.2015)

A lawyer, acting also as notary, certified an elderly man's signature on a general power of attorney without making sure he understood it allowed the sale of his property; he then represented the man's son, the attorney-in-fact, in selling the father's building to buyers who were not his clients. The sale was void for lack of authority. The Supreme Court (Danziger J., with Hendel and Zylbertal JJ.) held the lawyer liable in negligence to the buyers: a lawyer's duty of care toward non-clients is recognised case by case, and here his double role created the necessary proximity. The test of breach is the "reasonable lawyer", who is not a detective but must act to dispel suspicion when warning signs appear (quoting Penthouse Rahmani, 2012). Several warning signs were ignored. The buyers' contributory fault was set at 25%.

Why it matters: it gives you the structure of any malpractice claim: duty (to clients always; to non-clients where the lawyer's role creates reliance), breach measured by the reasonable lawyer and the warning signs he should have acted on, causation and damage, and contributory fault. [Text hosted by Reichman University](https://www.colman.ac.il/media/3x4nweif/2599-13_herman_v_aldor.doc)

### רע״א 9930/17 אל מדאמין בע״מ נ׳ חנא (recap from lesson 35.3)

The natural forum for ethics questions is the Bar's institutions; a court will decide an ethics issue only where it affects a party's legitimate interests in a case before it. Why it matters here: a civil court is not a substitute for a complaint, and a complaint is not a substitute for a damages claim. [Text](https://www.colman.ac.il/media/f3yhrn00/9930-17_al_madamin_ltd_v_hana_1.doc)

## How it works in practice

### Which route?

| What you want | Route | Who decides | What you can get |
|---|---|---|---|
| The lawyer held to account; protect others | Complaint to the district ethics committee | Ethics committee, then disciplinary tribunal | Penalty; up to ₪25,000 compensation per offence; return of a fee obtained by an offence |
| A fee above a statutory cap back | Written demand, then a small claim or civil claim | Court | The excess, despite any agreement |
| A success fee reduced | Request under s. 84(ב) | The Bar | A binding fee determination |
| A fee dispute settled | Agreed arbitration through the Bar, or court | Arbitrator / court | A binding award or judgment |
| Your money or file released | Written demand (s. 88 limits; rule 40, 42); complaint; court | Lawyer / committee / court | Release; an account |
| Compensation for a lost case or a bad transaction | Malpractice claim in contract and negligence | Court | Damages (the value of what was lost, minus contributory fault) |

Sequencing: first a written demand to the lawyer (it often solves money and file issues, and it is evidence); then the fee route or the claim; a complaint can run in parallel, but the tribunal's findings are not binding in your civil case, and a civil judgment can support a later complaint.

### Writing the complaint

Keep it factual and short: who you are, the lawyer's name and number, the engagement, a numbered chronology with dates, the specific acts or omissions, the rule you think was breached (if you know it), what you asked the lawyer and what he answered, and the documents. Do not exaggerate or insult: the committee will send your complaint to the lawyer, and a baseless or vexatious complaint can cost you (s. 69(א)(5)). You will draft one in the lab (lesson [35.8](lesson-08.md)) with the draft [ethics complaint template](../../templates/bar-ethics-complaint.md).

### Building a malpractice claim

1. **What did the lawyer undertake?** The engagement letter and emails (lesson 35.5).
2. **What would a reasonable lawyer have done?** Usually an expert opinion from another lawyer in the field.
3. **What did you lose because of it?** In a lost lawsuit, the value of the claim multiplied by its realistic chance ("a case within a case").
4. **Contributory fault and mitigation.** Did you ignore advice or delay?
5. **Limitation.** Seven years from the cause of action or from discovery (ss. 5, 8).

Expect a counterclaim for unpaid fees (and vice versa: a fee claim often triggers a negligence claim).

## Worked example

Tomer Levi, a delivery courier from Ashkelon, hired attorney Ronit Barak in 2021 to sue his former employer for unpaid overtime. He paid ₪6,000. In 2026, after unanswered calls, he checked Net HaMishpat and found that no claim was ever filed; part of his claim is now time-barred. Ronit had written to him in 2023 that "the case is progressing".

1. **Discipline.** Failing to act, misleading the client about progress and not replying are breaches of loyalty and devotion (s. 54, rule 2) and conduct unbecoming (s. 61(3)). Tomer files a complaint with the district ethics committee where she is registered, attaching his payment receipt, the 2023 message and a Net HaMishpat search printout.
2. **Fee.** He writes demanding the ₪6,000 back and an account (rule 42). If she refuses, a small claim for the fee is simple; he can also ask the tribunal, if she is convicted, to order the fee returned (s. 69(א)(3)).
3. **Malpractice.** The lost part of his overtime claim (wages more than seven years old at the time a claim would now be filed) is the loss. Limitation for his claim against her: seven years, and since her message hid the facts, arguably from 2026 when he discovered them (s. 8). Because the amount and the "case within a case" analysis are complex, he books a consultation with another lawyer, who will first write to Ronit (rule 31).
4. **The rest of his wage claim.** He still files the timely part in the labour court himself (lesson [22.2](../module-22/lesson-02.md)) without waiting for the outcome of the complaint.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A grievance with a lawyer (yours, a family member's described to you, or Tomer's case).
- The engagement documents, payments and messages.
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. Write a numbered chronology of the relationship, with dates and documents (15 min).
2. Mark each problem as: ethics, fee, money or file held, negligence (several may apply) (5 min).
3. For each problem, choose the route from the table and write what you would get from it (10 min).
4. Calculate the limitation date for any negligence claim, with the section that fixes the start (5 min).
5. Write the order in which you would act, and the first letter you would send (10 min).

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes for a complaint, a fee demand, a request under s. 84(ב), and a small claim for a fee refund. Partly for a malpractice claim: you may sue in your own name, but the expert evidence and the "case within a case" make professional help advisable when the stakes are significant.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Where and how

- **Complaint:** to the ethics committee of the district where the lawyer is registered, on the Bar's Form 1 (r. 2 of the 2015 rules), with documents. The form and addresses are on the Bar's website (⚠️ manual check). No fee is set for a complaint in the law or the 2015 rules.
- **Fee refund:** small claims within the ceiling (lesson [12.5](../module-12/lesson-05.md); [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- **Malpractice:** a civil claim in the magistrates' or district court by amount (lesson [15.1](../module-15/lesson-01.md)).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The loss is large or the "case within a case" is complex: you will need an expert opinion and careful pleading.
- The lawyer threatens a defamation claim over your complaint or posts: complaints to the competent body are generally protected, public posts are riskier (lesson [12.8](../module-12/lesson-08.md)).
- A deadline in your underlying case is still open: protect it first.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Complaining to the Bar to get money back | Months of process; at most limited compensation | Add a fee demand and, if needed, a claim | Use the table: money goes to the fee or claim route |
| Insulting or exaggerated complaint | Credibility lost; costs risk if baseless (s. 69(א)(5)) | Withdraw and refile a factual version | Facts, dates, documents only |
| Waiting for the disciplinary outcome before suing | Limitation may run | Calculate the date now | Run the routes in parallel where needed |
| Posting accusations online | Defamation exposure | Remove; complain to the committee instead | Use the competent body |
| Suing without an expert opinion | Claim fails on breach | Get an opinion from a lawyer in the field | Plan the evidence first |

## Review

- Discipline, fee disputes and malpractice claims are separate systems with different remedies; many cases need more than one.
- Anyone may complain; only ethics committees, the AG and the State Attorney may prosecute (s. 63); the committee must decide within set times and give reasons if it does not prosecute (2015 rules).
- Penalties run from a warning to expulsion (s. 68); the tribunal may order a fee returned and up to ₪25,000 compensation per offence (s. 69).
- Fee issues: statutory refunds, the Bar's determination of excessive success fees (s. 84(ב)), Bar opinions for courts (s. 89), arbitration or court.
- Malpractice is measured by the reasonable lawyer acting on warning signs (Herman v. Eldor), within seven years of the cause or its discovery.
