# 28.5 · Individual insolvency under the 2018 law: the process

When debts can no longer be repaid within the Execution Office's limits, the law offers a different deal: collective proceedings in which all creditors are stopped, the debtor's assets and part of his income go to them for a limited period, and what is left is discharged. This lesson covers the entry into that process for a private person: the test, the three doors (execution registrar, Insolvency Commissioner, court), the application, and what changes on the day the opening order is given.

## Why it matters

The Insolvency and Economic Rehabilitation Law 2018 (חוק חדלות פירעון ושיקום כלכלי) replaced the Bankruptcy Ordinance of 1980 for proceedings opened from 15 September 2019 (s. 373(א)). Its first sentence changes the moral frame of the old law:

> חוק זה נועד להסדיר את פירעון חובותיו של חייב… במטרה – (1) להביא ככל האפשר לשיקומו הכלכלי של החייב; (2) להשיא את שיעור החוב שייפרע לנושים; (3) לקדם את שילובו מחדש של חייב שהוא יחיד במרקם החיים הכלכליים.

*Gist:* The law has three purposes of equal rank: the debtor's economic rehabilitation, the highest possible recovery for creditors, and the reintegration of an individual into economic life. ([s. 1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חדלות_פירעון_ושיקום_כלכלי#סעיף_1))

Under the Ordinance, "bankruptcy" was a status that could last a decade, ran through the courts and the Official Receiver, and carried stigma. The new law moved most individual cases out of the courts to an administrative official, the Insolvency Commissioner (הממונה על הליכי חדלות פירעון ושיקום כלכלי), and, for low debts, to a dedicated registrar in the Execution Office; it fixed a default three-year payment period followed by discharge (lesson 28.6). The academic argument is about balance: critics of the old system pointed to endless proceedings and debtors who never recovered; critics of the new one worry about strategic filers and creditors who receive little. The law answers with the creditors' right to object, the trustee's investigation and longer periods for bad faith.

## The law

### The test: s. 2

> חדלות פירעון היא מצב כלכלי שבו חייב אינו יכול לשלם את חובותיו במועדם, בין אם מועד פירעונם הגיע ובין אם לאו, או שהתחייבויות החייב, לרבות התחייבויות עתידיות ומותנות שלו, עולות על שווי נכסיו.

*Gist:* Insolvency is either a cash-flow test (cannot pay debts when due, including future ones) or a balance-sheet test (liabilities, including future and contingent ones, exceed the value of assets). An individual may also apply if the order will help prevent insolvency (s. 104(א)(1)). ([s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חדלות_פירעון_ושיקום_כלכלי#סעיף_2))

### Three doors: s. 103

> (א) בקשת היחיד לצו לפתיחת הליכים תוגש לממונה…: צו לפתיחת הליכים לבקשת יחיד שסך חובותיו עולה על 150,000 שקלים חדשים (מתואם לשנת 2019; בשנת 2026, 176,923.12 ש״ח).
> (ב) …בקשה לצו לפתיחת הליכים של יחיד שסך חובותיו אינו עולה על 150,000 שקלים חדשים… תוגש לרשם ההוצאה לפועל…

*Gist:* An individual whose total debts exceed the threshold applies to the Insolvency Commissioner; one whose debts do not exceed it applies to the execution registrar under the low-debt chapter; a creditor's or the Attorney General's application goes to the court (the magistrates' court for individuals, s. 4). The threshold is linked and updated each year. ([s. 103](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חדלות_פירעון_ושיקום_כלכלי#סעיף_103); figures in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

### The application to the Commissioner: ss. 104–108

- **Contents (s. 104(ב)).** An affidavit on the official form that the conditions are met; a report on the official form, verified by affidavit and supported by documents, covering the **two years before the application**: assets, income, expenses, debts and obligations including bank accounts and guarantees given, identities of creditors and debtors; lawsuits and collection proceedings; profession and education; what the applicant knows about the spouse's, minor children's and dependent adult children's finances; and a waiver of confidentiality for the listed bodies. Sensitive personal details may be marked so creditors do not see them (s. 104(ג)).
- **Where.** To the Commissioner in the district where the applicant lives or has his business or assets, "and it may also be filed online" (s. 104(ד)).
- **Decision.** Within 30 days if the conditions are met, or 30 days after further information or a hearing (s. 105). Notice is published and sent to known creditors (s. 106); creditors may inspect the application (s. 107).
- **Challenge.** Anyone harmed may apply to the court to cancel the order within 45 days of publication (s. 108).

### The low-debt track: ss. 186–208

A debtor whose debts do not exceed the threshold may open proceedings in one of two ways (s. 187):

1. a **notice of inability to pay** in an execution file under Execution Law s. 7א2, which is treated as an application (lesson 28.1); or
2. an **application to the execution registrar**, provided debts exceed a lower floor (linked; see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)), or below it for special reasons, for example tax-collection proceedings (s. 187(ב)).

The case is run by a **dedicated execution registrar** trained for insolvency, with the Commissioner's and court's powers (s. 188), at a branch with an insolvency track in the district where most of the debtor's files are (s. 189). First comes an attempt at agreement:

- Within 30 days the registrar convenes a **payment-arrangement meeting** run by the registrar's representative (s. 190), within a further 30 days (s. 191); the debtor must attend and cooperate and is told his rights, including the living allowance he would get under an opening order (s. 191(ה)).
- If the debtor and all creditors party to it agree, the arrangement binds them and the registrar stays or closes the execution files (s. 192).
- If not all agree, the registrar may convene a creditors' meeting; the arrangement passes if a majority of those voting among creditors in execution debts, holding **at least three-quarters** of those debts, and every non-execution creditor party to it, support it (s. 195), and the registrar approves it as fair, possibly with financial-conduct training, but never if a dissenting creditor gets less than he would in a rehabilitation order (s. 196).
- Without an arrangement the registrar gives an **opening order** (s. 199), and the ordinary rules apply with the registrar in the Commissioner's place (ss. 201–204). Complex cases may move to the magistrates' court (s. 207), and cases that turn out much larger than the threshold, or involve avoidance claims, move to the Commissioner (s. 208).

### A creditor's petition: ss. 109–110

A creditor may ask the court to open proceedings against an insolvent individual. Insolvency is presumed, for example, after a formal demand for a debt above a linked amount unpaid within 30 days, an unpaid execution warning or tax demand above that amount, or an unpaid judgment; a labour-court judgment has a lower amount (s. 110). The debtor may rebut the presumption by showing non-payment is not due to insolvency (s. 110(ג)). (Amounts: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).)

### What the opening order does: s. 121

> (א) עם מתן צו לפתיחת הליכים – (1) נכסי קופת הנשייה יעמדו לפירעון חובות העבר של היחיד והוצאות הליכי חדלות הפירעון, בלבד; … (3) יוקפאו ההליכים נגד היחיד… ואם הוטלו הגבלות על היחיד במסגרת הליכי גבייה – ההגבלות בטלות;

*Gist:* The estate is reserved for past debts and the costs of the proceedings; past debts are paid only under the law; proceedings against the debtor are stayed and restrictions imposed in collection proceedings fall away; no imprisonment in lieu of a fine; a trustee is appointed; the interim period begins with statutory restrictions and the trustee's investigation. Amendment No. 9 (2026) added s. 121(ב): the order does not exempt the debtor from any other legal duty, including administrative decisions, except paying past debts. ([s. 121](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חדלות_פירעון_ושיקום_כלכלי#סעיף_121); [Amendment 9](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/25/law/25_lsr_10981603.pdf))

### The interim period: ss. 123–124, 142, 156

- **Credit** only with the Commissioner's approval, except for basic living needs within amounts he sets (s. 123); the debtor must say he is in insolvency proceedings when taking credit (s. 124).
- **Restrictions by law** (s. 142): passport, exit from Israel, cheques, credit cards (debit cards against a credit balance excepted), and founding a company if the trustee sees a risk; the Commissioner may lift them, fully or partly, after hearing creditors (s. 143).
- **Living allowance** (s. 156): the Commissioner sets an amount the debtor keeps from his income; the rest goes to the trustee in periodic payments; it can be changed when circumstances change.

### Fees

An individual's application has a fee, of which an individual not eligible for Legal Aid pays a fixed part up front and the balance comes out of the estate; an individual eligible for Legal Aid pays nothing up front (Fee Regulations, reg. 4); half is refunded if the application is withdrawn before the order (reg. 7). Amounts are CPI-linked each January ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).

## Leading cases

### Key text: s. 1 and s. 2

The new law is young and its leading appellate cases on individuals are still forming; none could be opened from a free source and checked for this lesson. For an exam, argue from the text: s. 1 makes rehabilitation and reintegration purposes of the same rank as creditor recovery, so a reading that keeps an honest debtor in proceedings indefinitely runs against the statute; s. 2 makes insolvency an economic test, not a moral one.

## How it works in practice

### Which door?

| Total debts (2026 figures, linked) | Who applies | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Up to the low-debt floor | Individual | Execution registrar, only for special reasons | Or a s. 7א2 notice in an execution file |
| Floor to threshold | Individual | Dedicated execution registrar (insolvency track) | Payment-arrangement meeting first |
| Above threshold | Individual | Insolvency Commissioner, online | Order within 30 days if conditions met |
| Any (presumption amounts apply) | Creditor or Attorney General | Magistrates' court | Debtor can rebut |

Figures: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### The online application to the Commissioner

The Commissioner's online system lets an individual file before an order is given ("בקשות ופניות טרם מתן צו פתיחת הליכים"), with personal details, the official forms and supporting PDFs, and pay the fee (Kol-Zchut, ⚠️ manual check; the Commissioner's call centre number given there should be confirmed on gov.il). Prepare first:

1. Two years of bank statements for every account (yours and, as known, your spouse's).
2. Payslips, pension and benefit statements, tax assessments.
3. A list of every creditor with address, amount and file number (the Execution Office personal area exports your files).
4. Guarantees you gave; lawsuits; vehicles, land, savings, provident funds.
5. A budget of household expenses with evidence.
6. A short honest account of how the debts arose.

## Worked example

Natalia Ivanova, 52, a former shop owner in Haifa, closed her business in 2024. Her debts: bank loans and overdraft ₪260,000, a supplier's judgment ₪48,000, VAT ₪31,000, National Insurance ₪12,000 and a credit card ₪9,000. She now earns ₪7,400 net as an employee and rents a flat with her 17-year-old son.

1. **Insolvent?** Yes, on both tests: she cannot pay as debts fall due, and her liabilities far exceed her assets (an old car).
2. **Which door?** Total ₪360,000, above the threshold: the Insolvency Commissioner, online.
3. **Preparation.** She downloads her execution files, two years of bank statements, her payslips, tax and VAT correspondence, the business closure documents and the lease; she writes how the business failed after a road-works closure and her illness.
4. **Fee.** She is not eligible for Legal Aid on means; she pays the up-front part of the fee.
5. **Order.** Three weeks later the Commissioner gives an opening order. The bank's execution files are stayed; the exit ban and credit-card restriction from the supplier's file fall away, but the statutory interim restrictions of s. 142 now apply; a trustee is appointed; the Commissioner sets a living allowance and a monthly payment to the trustee.
6. **What she must not do.** Take new credit without approval, hide income, or pay one favoured creditor.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. An individual may apply in his own name to the Commissioner or the execution registrar, and the forms are built for that. Legal Aid represents eligible individuals in insolvency proceedings (and its clients pay no up-front fee, Fee Regulations reg. 4(1)). Representing another person is reserved to lawyers.

### Where and how

- Above the threshold: the Insolvency Commissioner's online system (gov.il, ⚠️ manual check), or the district office.
- At or below it: a s. 7א2 notice in your execution file, or an application at a branch with an insolvency track (s. 189).
- Fees and thresholds: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md). Help: Legal Aid, debt-counselling NGOs, [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You own a home, a business that still operates, or significant assets.
- You recently transferred assets to relatives or paid one creditor in full (avoidance risk, lesson 28.6).
- A creditor has petitioned the court against you, or a creditor applies to cancel your order.
- Your debts include fines, maintenance or debts from fraud (not dischargeable, lesson 28.6).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Applying to the Commissioner with debts below the threshold | Wrong forum; delay | File with the execution registrar | Total all debts with interest first |
| Leaving out a bank account or a guarantee | Credibility lost; risk of cancellation or a longer payment period (ss. 163(ג), 183) | Disclose at once to the trustee | Two years of statements for every account |
| Taking a new loan after the order | Breach of s. 123; possible cancellation | Disclose; ask approval | Ask the Commissioner first |
| Paying a favoured creditor (a relative) before filing | Payment may be set aside (s. 219) | Disclose | Stop paying selectively once insolvent |
| Assuming the order frees you from all duties | Fines, administrative orders and new debts continue (s. 121(ב)) | Comply | Read s. 121(ב) |

## Review

- The 2018 law aims at rehabilitation, creditor recovery and reintegration, equally.
- Insolvency is a cash-flow or balance-sheet test (s. 2).
- Individuals with low debts go to a dedicated execution registrar (with a payment-arrangement meeting first); higher debts go to the Commissioner; creditors go to the magistrates' court.
- The opening order stays proceedings and cancels collection restrictions, but brings interim restrictions, a living allowance, monthly payments and a trustee.
