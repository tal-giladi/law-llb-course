# 28.9 · Practice lab: assess and prepare an individual insolvency application (dry run)

In this lab you take a household drowning in debt and do what an honest applicant must do before filing: test whether insolvency is the right route, pick the door, assemble the two-year report and the creditors list, write the account of how the debts arose, and forecast the rehabilitation order. Nothing is filed. The point is to know, before anyone presses "submit", what the process will ask and what it will lead to.

## Why it matters

Insolvency applications fail or turn harsh for predictable reasons: incomplete disclosure, transfers to relatives before filing, new debts taken in the run-up, or a debtor who could in fact pay within the Execution Office's limits. Doing the assessment first protects the applicant from those traps, and also tells him honestly whether a consolidation or a negotiated arrangement (lessons 28.4, 28.8) would serve him better.

## The law

The rules are in lessons 28.5 and 28.6: the insolvency test (s. 2); the doors and thresholds (s. 103, ch. 12, Execution Law s. 7א2); the application's contents (s. 104(ב)–(ד)); the opening order's effects (s. 121) and interim restrictions (s. 142); the living allowance (s. 156); payment = earning capacity − living allowance (s. 162); the three-year default and its extensions (s. 163); immediate discharge (s. 167); non-dischargeable debts (s. 175); exempt assets and the home (ss. 217, 229); avoidance (ss. 219–221); the application fee and its up-front part (Fee Regulations reg. 4). Full text: [Insolvency Law](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חדלות_פירעון_ושיקום_כלכלי). Thresholds and fees: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

## Leading cases

### Key text: s. 104(ב) — the application is a disclosure document

There is no ruling to brief in this lab. Read s. 104(ב) as the specification of your work product: affidavit, two-year report on the official form verified by affidavit with documents, and the waiver. Everything later in the case, including the length of the payment period (s. 163(ג)(1)), depends on how complete and honest this disclosure was.

## How it works in practice

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- Above the threshold: the Insolvency Commissioner's online system (gov.il), where an individual files before an order with the official forms and PDF attachments and pays the fee (⚠️ manual check of the current screens and forms).
- At or below it: a s. 7א2 notice in an execution file, or an application to a branch with an insolvency track.
- Withdrawing before the order refunds half the fee (reg. 7).

## Do it yourself

### You need

- The supplied case, or your own finances (bank statements, payslips, the Execution Office personal area, credit-data report).
- The [individual insolvency application worksheet](../../templates/insolvency-application-worksheet.md) (draft template for this module).
- Insolvency Law and Fee Regulations as above; [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- About two and a half hours.

### The supplied case

Shlomit and Eyal Peretz, Kiryat Gat, married, three children (14, 10, 6). Eyal (44) ran a small moving company as a sole trader until it collapsed in 2025 after a key client went bankrupt; he now works as a salaried driver (net ₪7,600). Shlomit (41) is a school secretary (net ₪6,200). They rent (₪4,100).

**Eyal's debts:** bank business loan ₪180,000 (Shlomit is **not** a guarantor; Eyal's mother is, for ₪50,000); VAT ₪46,000; income tax ₪28,000; National Insurance ₪14,000; leasing company for a returned truck ₪35,000 (judgment); fuel supplier ₪12,000 (note in execution); credit cards ₪16,000; a court fine for a traffic offence ₪1,500. Total ≈ ₪332,500.

**Assets:** a 2015 private car worth ₪22,000 used to get to work; ₪9,000 in a savings account; a provident fund (קרן השתלמות) with ₪31,000; household goods.

**Events:** in March 2026 Eyal repaid his brother ₪15,000 "so he would not be hurt"; in June 2026 he sold a trailer to a friend for ₪3,000 (worth about ₪12,000). He has six execution files; an exit ban in one of them.

### Steps

**Part A — is insolvency the route? (20 min)**

1. Apply both limbs of s. 2 with numbers.
2. Run the Execution Office alternative: household capacity × the longest s. 7א1 period versus total debt. Would consolidation pass s. 74ט?
3. Write a one-paragraph recommendation.

**Part B — the door and the fee (10 min)**

4. Total debts against the 2026 thresholds: Commissioner or execution registrar? Online or by notice?
5. The fee: full amount, the up-front part, and whether Legal Aid eligibility would change it (reg. 4).

**Part C — the application content (60 min)**

6. **Creditors list:** creditor, address, basis, amount, file number, security, guarantor.
7. **Two-year report outline:** income (both spouses), expenses, assets including the provident fund and savings, debts, bank accounts, guarantees, lawsuits and collection proceedings, profession and education; what Shlomit's finances are and how far they must be disclosed.
8. **Account of the debts:** half a page, factual, including the client's bankruptcy and the steps Eyal took.
9. **Disclosure of the two transactions:** how and where you will disclose the payment to the brother and the trailer sale.

**Part D — forecast (40 min)**

10. **Interim period:** what stops (execution files, the exit ban from collection) and what starts (s. 142 restrictions, living allowance, monthly payment).
11. **Avoidance:** test the brother's repayment (s. 219: relative, one year) and the trailer (s. 220: undervalue, two years; s. 221). Effect on the period (s. 163(ג)(3))?
12. **Payment:** estimate earning capacity and living allowance (state your assumptions) and the monthly payment (s. 162).
13. **Assets:** car (s. 217, Second Schedule item 4 if needed for work and within the cap), savings, provident fund (s. 228).
14. **Surviving debts:** which of Eyal's debts survive discharge (s. 175)? What happens to his mother as guarantor (s. 174(א))?

**Part E — red flags (20 min)**

15. List what Eyal must stop doing today (new credit, paying favoured creditors, moving assets) and what he must gather.
16. Decide whether to apply alone or get Legal Aid or a lawyer, and why.

- Your recommendation follows from both the s. 2 test and the failed consolidation test, with numbers.

- Your forecast states payment, period, assets and surviving debts with reasons.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes, the application is designed for individuals in their own name; Legal Aid represents eligible applicants. You may never prepare or file an application for someone else (Bar Association Law s. 20), even a spouse or parent.

### Where and how

- The Commissioner's online system; the execution registrar's insolvency track for low debts; fees and thresholds in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Help: Legal Aid, debt-counselling NGOs, [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- There are transfers to relatives or friends in the last years, a home, a running business, or a spouse whose assets may be drawn in.
- A creditor has already petitioned the court.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hiding the payment to a relative | Longer period; possible cancellation (ss. 163(ג), 183) | Disclose in the report | Disclose everything from the past two years and beyond if relevant |
| Filing while able to pay within the execution periods | Application weak; possible abuse finding | Use consolidation or a payment order | Run the s. 74ט numbers first |
| Taking a "last" loan to pay a lawyer or a creditor before filing | New debt, possible bad-faith ground | Disclose | Stop borrowing once you plan to file |
| Expecting the guarantor to be freed | Family conflict | Plan with the guarantor | Read s. 174(א) |

## Review

- Test insolvency with numbers and compare it honestly with the Execution Office routes.
- The application is a disclosure document: two years, every account, every creditor, every transfer.
- Forecast the order: payment from earning capacity minus allowance, three years by default, surviving debts, guarantor still liable.
- Stop new credit, preferences and transfers the day you decide to file.
