# 28.4 · Consolidating files and payment arrangements

A debtor with one file needs a payment order; a debtor with five files needs a plan. This lesson compares the instruments the Execution Law offers for paying over time, from the automatic payment instruction to the consolidation of all files under one order, tests each against the statutory limits, and shows where the Execution Office route ends and insolvency begins. You finish by building a consolidation request.

## Why it matters

Several files against one debtor compete: each creditor attaches what it can, each adds fees and lawyer's fees, and the debtor pays whoever moved first. Consolidation (איחוד תיקים) replaces the race with one monthly payment divided among all the creditors. It is powerful, but the law gives it only to a debtor who can actually pay everything within the statutory periods; a debtor who cannot belongs in insolvency proceedings (lessons 28.5–28.6). Choosing between the two is the most important decision an over-indebted person makes, and the law has changed it in recent years: the "limited means debtor" status, for decades the Execution Office's answer for chronic debtors, can no longer be granted to new debtors.

## The law

### Payment orders and their periods: ss. 69, 7א1

The registrar may order payment by instalments after a means inquiry, on an affidavit, or on a written agreement between the parties (s. 69(א)); a debtor may ask to vary an order whenever his ability changes (s. 69ב).

> (א) התקופה לתשלום החוב הפסוק בצו תשלומים… לא תעלה על תקופה כמפורט להלן: (1) שלוש שנים – אם סכום החוב אינו עולה על 100,000 שקלים חדשים; (2) ארבע שנים – אם סכום החוב עולה על 100,000 שקלים חדשים.

*Gist:* A payment order may not run longer than a set period that depends on the size of the debt; the registrar may extend it by a further limited period with reasons and must review the extension, and longer periods are possible only with both parties' consent (s. 7א1(ב)–(ד)). If the debtor cannot pay even within the extended period, s. 7א2 sends him to insolvency. ([s. 7א1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_7א1); figures in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

### Agreed arrangements: ss. 19ב, 68א(ח), 7א(ה)

A creditor and debtor may agree on instalments paid directly; the creditor notifies the office and proceedings stop while the debtor keeps to it (s. 19ב). An agreement reached before an execution investigator, or a creditor's consent to a payment-order request, is approved by the registrar and then has the force of a payment order (ss. 68א(ח), 7א(ה)). A restriction must be cancelled while the debtor keeps a payment order or agreement (s. 66ד(ג)).

### Consolidation of files: ss. 74א–74טו

- **Who and what.** The debtor may ask to consolidate all pending files against him, except maintenance files, non-money files and bill files transferred to court (ss. 74א, 74ב). The request goes to the branch where most of his files were opened, on form 7 with the form 21 questionnaire and waiver and supporting documents, with enough copies for all creditors (regs 29, 29א). One request only, unless circumstances fundamentally change (reg. 29(ב)).
- **On filing.** Proceedings continue unless the registrar stops them for a limited period for special reasons (s. 74ג); the registrar must order an **exit ban** (s. 74ד) and may attach the debtor's assets (s. 74ה). The debtor must offer a monthly sum not below the payment-order minimum, pay it on filing and every month until the decision (s. 74ו).
- **Disclosure.** Full information about assets over the past five years and all debts, even disputed ones (s. 74ז).
- **The test.**

> (א) לא יינתן צו איחוד אלא אם כן נוכח רשם ההוצאה לפועל, לאחר חקירת היכולת של החייב ובהתחשב בנכסיו העתידיים, לרבות בכושר השתכרותו, כי החייב יוכל לפרוע את חובותיו בכל התיקים התלויים ועומדים נגדו תוך פרקי הזמן הקבועים בסעיף 7א1 או אם הסכימו לכך כל הזוכים…

*Gist:* Consolidation is granted only if, after a means inquiry in which every creditor may cross-examine (s. 74ח), the registrar is satisfied that the debtor can repay all files within the s. 7א1 periods, or all creditors agree. ([s. 74ט](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_74ט))

- **Refusal and cancellation.** False or hidden details, incomplete disclosure, new non-essential spending after filing, or an undisclosed new file justify refusal (s. 74י) and later cancellation, as do missed payments, non-cooperation with the administrator or a change of circumstances (s. 74טו).
- **The order.** One payment schedule for all files; the registrar may stop proceedings in the files, appoint an administrator who collects and distributes (s. 74יד), and join later files (s. 74יב).

### Consolidation through a payment order: s. 74טו1

When giving any payment order, the registrar must consider consolidating all the debtor's files under it, after explaining the consequences, if the debtor agrees. This makes consolidation a by-product of an ordinary payment-order request, without a separate form 7 procedure. ([s. 74טו1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_74טו1))

### The closed door: the limited means debtor, ss. 69ג–69י1

From 1994 the registrar could declare a debtor who needed a long repayment period a "limited means debtor" (חייב מוגבל באמצעים), consolidate his files, freeze most enforcement and impose restrictions (ss. 69ג–69ו); a temporary 2015–2019 track even let such debtors obtain a discharge in the Execution Office (ss. 69י2 and following, now expired). Then:

> מיום התחילה של חוק חדלות פירעון ושיקום כלכלי, לא יוכרז חייב כחייב מוגבל באמצעים לפי סימן זה.

*Gist:* Since the Insolvency Law came into force (15 September 2019), no new declarations are made; existing ones continue. Debtors who cannot pay within the s. 7א1 periods are now referred to insolvency proceedings (ss. 7א2, 77א). ([s. 69י1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_69י1))

## Leading cases

### Key text: s. 74ט and s. 7א2 read together

No ruling is briefed: the free hosts available did not provide an appellate decision on consolidation that could be checked. The doctrine is in the text. Section 74ט lets the Execution Office help only a debtor who can pay in full within a bounded time; s. 7א2 sends everyone else to insolvency, where partial payment and discharge are possible. Read with s. 69י1, which closed the limited-means route, the design is a move from managing chronic debt indefinitely in the Execution Office to a time-limited rehabilitation with a fresh start, which is how the Insolvency Law states its own purposes: the debtor's economic rehabilitation and his reintegration into economic life (s. 1(1), (3)).

## How it works in practice

### Choosing an instrument

| Debtor's position | Best fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| One file, can pay within the s. 7א1 period | Payment order (s. 7א) or agreed arrangement (s. 19ב) | Simple; restrictions cancelled while complied with |
| Several files, can pay all within the periods | Consolidation (form 7), or consolidation through a payment order (s. 74טו1) | One payment, divided among the files under the order; no race between creditors |
| Several files, cannot pay all within the periods | Insolvency (s. 7א2 notice below the threshold, or application to the Commissioner above it) | Consolidation will be refused under s. 74ט |
| Creditor willing to accept a discount | Negotiated settlement, approved or reported to the office | Fastest; record it in writing with the closing of the file |

### Building the numbers

1. Total of all files (principal, interest, fees, lawyer's fees) from the personal area.
2. Net monthly income of the household and fixed necessary expenses (housing, food, children, health, transport to work).
3. Monthly capacity = income − necessary expenses. Multiply by the maximum months under s. 7א1 (plus a possible extension).
4. If the product is less than the total debt, consolidation fails the s. 74ט test; consider insolvency.

## Worked example

Daniel Mizrahi, a bus driver in Netivot, has four files: a bank overdraft (₪41,000), a mobile phone company (₪6,500), a gym (₪3,200) and a furniture shop (₪19,300): ₪70,000 in total. He nets ₪8,900; his wife nets ₪5,400; rent, food, three children and transport leave about ₪2,300 a month.

1. **Period.** Total ₪70,000 is within the shorter tier in s. 7א1; maximum 36 months (and a possible reasoned extension).
2. **Capacity.** ₪2,300 × 36 = ₪82,800. More than the debt, so the s. 74ט test can be met, even with interest accruing.
3. **Route.** Instead of a separate form 7 request, Daniel files a payment-order request in the bank's file, asks for ₪2,200 a month and agrees to consolidation under s. 74טו1.
4. **Disclosure.** Form 21 with the household's income, the waiver, payslips, the lease and the children's details; he lists a disputed parking-fine debt too.
5. **Exit ban.** He knows a consolidation request triggers one (s. 74ד) and postpones a planned family trip; he could ask for an exception with security.
6. **Result.** The registrar consolidates the four files under one order of ₪2,250 a month, divided among the files as the order provides; restrictions in the bank's file are cancelled while he complies (s. 66ד(ג)).

Contrast: if Daniel's capacity were ₪900 a month, ₪900 × 36 = ₪32,400, far below ₪70,000. Consolidation would fail; the honest route is a s. 7א2 notice, which below the threshold opens insolvency proceedings before a dedicated execution registrar.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your own list of debts, or the supplied case: Ruth Gabay, a widowed pensioner in Safed, has three files (₪22,000 credit card, ₪9,000 dental clinic note, ₪14,000 electricity company) and a monthly pension of ₪6,100 with ₪4,700 of necessary expenses.
- Execution Law ss. 7א1, 7א2, 74א–74טו1; regs 29–29א; [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- 60 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Debt table (10 min).** One row per file: creditor, file number, type of document, principal, current balance, measures taken.
2. **Budget (15 min).** Household income and necessary expenses, with the document for each line.
3. **Test (10 min).** Capacity × maximum months versus total debt. Does the s. 74ט test pass?
4. **Choose (5 min).** Payment order, consolidation, consolidation through a payment order, or insolvency. Write why.
5. **Draft (20 min).** If consolidation: fill in the content of form 7 (debts, offer, disclosure of five years of assets) as a Hebrew draft; if insolvency: write the s. 7א2 notice in three sentences and list the s. 104(ב) documents you will need (lesson 28.5).

### Template

- Draft for this module: [execution debtor response kit](../../templates/execution-debtor-response-kit.md), part D (budget and consolidation or arrangement proposal).

### How you know it worked

- Your choice follows from the numbers, not from preference.
- The draft offers a monthly sum you can actually pay, supported by documents.
- You disclosed every debt, including disputed ones.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Payment-order and consolidation requests are designed to be filed by the debtor. Agreements with creditors are made in your own name; a lawyer for the creditor will often draft them, so read every term before signing.

### Where and how

- Consolidation: form 7 and form 21, at the branch where most files are, or through the personal area if the request type is offered there (⚠️ manual check); copies for every creditor (reg. 29א(ב)).
- Payment-order requests: personal area, online form or branch.
- Periods, minimum payments and thresholds: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md). Free budget help: municipal and NGO debt-counselling services, Legal Aid ([where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md)).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The numbers show you cannot pay within the periods: get advice on insolvency before signing any arrangement.
- A creditor's lawyer asks you to sign a new promissory note, a guarantee by a relative, or a waiver of protections as part of an arrangement.
- You own a home and creditors are pressing for a sale.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Offering an amount you cannot keep | Order cancelled; restrictions return (s. 66ד(ג), s. 74טו) | Ask to vary the order at once (s. 69ב) | Base the offer on a documented budget |
| Hiding a debt or an asset | Request refused or order cancelled (ss. 74י, 74טו) | Disclose now | Full disclosure under s. 74ז |
| Filing consolidation just before a trip abroad | Exit ban on filing (s. 74ד) | Ask for an exception with security | Plan the timing |
| Choosing consolidation when the numbers do not work | Months lost, then refusal | Move to insolvency | Run the s. 74ט test first |
| Paying a creditor directly without notifying the office | Payments not credited | Report under s. 19ב(ד) | Use the office or report every payment |

## Review

- Payment orders, agreed arrangements and consolidation all spread payment over time, within the s. 7א1 periods unless creditors consent.
- Consolidation unites all files under one order but requires full disclosure, a monthly payment from filing and proof of full repayment within the periods; it brings an exit ban.
- The registrar must consider consolidation whenever he gives a payment order (s. 74טו1).
- No new limited-means declarations since 2019: a debtor who cannot pay within the periods goes to insolvency.
