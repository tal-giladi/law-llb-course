# 28.8 · Practice lab: respond to an execution file and propose an arrangement

In this lab you are the debtor. You take a realistic file with three creditors, read every document, find what is wrong, decide what to dispute and what to pay, and produce the papers: the deadline diary, a payment-order request, a request to release protected money, an opposition outline, and a settlement proposal to one creditor. It is a dry run unless you have a file of your own and choose to act in your own name.

## Why it matters

Debtors lose most in execution through delay and disorder, not through the law. A complete, documented response filed in the warning period turns a frightening file into a manageable payment plan, keeps restrictions away, and often persuades a creditor to settle. The skills here also make you a better creditor (lesson 08.9) and prepare the insolvency assessment in lesson 28.9.

## The law

All rules come from lessons 28.1–28.4: the warning and its periods (s. 7), the payment-order request and its documents (s. 7א; form 21, reg. 14א), the period limits (s. 7א1), the means inquiry and deemed evasion (s. 67), the opposition to a bill (s. 81א; reg. 106), "I have paid" (s. 19), exempt and protected property (ss. 22, 50; Wage Protection Law s. 8), restrictions and their cancellation (ss. 66ב–66ד), arrangements with a creditor (s. 19ב) and consolidation through a payment order (s. 74טו1). Full text: [Execution Law](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל). Periods and thresholds: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

## Leading cases

### Key text: the warning, form 21 and s. 19ב

This lab works from documents, not rulings: the warning's content (s. 7(א1)), the form 21 questionnaire and waiver that every payment-order request needs (reg. 14א), and s. 19ב, which turns a private agreement with a creditor into a stop on enforcement. The constitutional background (בג״ץ 5304/92) is in [lesson 08.7](../module-08/lesson-07.md#leading-cases).

## How it works in practice

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- File in the Authority's personal area (general request, or the dedicated request type), through the online general-request form, or at a branch by appointment; call centre *35592 (⚠️ manual check of current request codes).
- Keep a copy of everything filed and the confirmation of filing.
- A settlement with a creditor is signed by both, and the creditor notifies the office; you may report payments yourself (s. 19ב(ד)).

## Worked example

## Do it yourself

### You need

- The supplied case, or your own file (personal area printout, warnings, notices).
- The [execution debtor response kit](../../templates/execution-debtor-response-kit.md) (draft template for this module).
- Execution Law ss. 7–7א2, 19, 19ב, 22, 50, 66ב–66ד, 67, 81א; Wage Protection Law s. 8; [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- About two and a half hours.

### The supplied case

Yonatan Levi, 34, lives in Be'er Sheva with his partner and their one-year-old daughter. He is a salaried warehouse worker (net ₪7,900); his partner is on maternity leave and then part-time (net ₪3,000). Rent ₪3,400; a car loan ₪900 a month (the car takes him to night shifts, no public transport at that hour).

His mailbox and personal area show:

1. **File A**, a mobile phone company, small-claims judgment of 3 March 2026, ₪7,200 principal; warning served by registered mail, signed for on **4 November 2026**. He paid ₪2,000 by bank transfer to the company on 20 April 2026 after a phone call, and has the transfer confirmation. The file shows the full ₪7,200 plus interest and fees.
2. **File B**, a furniture shop, execution of a **promissory note** for ₪9,500 he signed when buying a sofa; the sofa arrived broken and the shop refused to replace it; he has photos and emails. Warning served personally on **28 October 2026**.
3. **File C**, a bank, judgment for an overdraft, ₪26,000; warning served a year ago; he did nothing; his account was attached last week and his October salary is frozen; a 30-day notice of an **exit ban** arrived on **1 November 2026**.

Today is **10 November 2026**.

### Steps

**Part A — read and diary (25 min)**

1. For each file write: creditor, document type, amount claimed, service date, what is already happening (liens, restrictions).
2. Build the deadline diary: for each file, the last day to pay or request a payment order, to appear for a means inquiry, to oppose (B), and the date the exit ban in C would take effect. Mark which deadlines have already passed.

**Part B — decide (20 min)**

3. For each file choose the response: pay / payment order / "I have paid" / opposition / request to release / settlement. Explain in two lines why.
4. Decide whether to ask for consolidation through a payment order (s. 74טו1) and whether the numbers pass the s. 7א1 period limits.

**Part C — draft (80 min)**

5. **File A:** an "I have paid" request for ₪2,000 (s. 19), with the payment table and the transfer confirmation, asking to reduce the debt and to charge the creditor costs for failing to report (s. 19א).
6. **File B:** an opposition outline with the affidavit's main facts (failure of consideration), the attachments and the request to stay and transfer to court; check the deadline.
7. **File C:** (i) a request to release protected wages from the frozen account (Wage Protection Law s. 8), with payslips and statements; (ii) a payment-order request with the form 21 content (household income, expenses, dependants, other debts) and a monthly offer, asking for consolidation of A and C under s. 74טו1 and cancellation of the exit-ban notice while he complies (s. 66ד(ג)).

**Part D — settlement proposal (25 min)**

8. Write a one-page letter (Hebrew) to the bank (file C) proposing either a discounted lump sum funded by a relative's loan, or ₪X a month for Y months, explaining why this is better for the bank than enforcement, and asking it to notify the office of the arrangement under s. 19ב. Mark it "without prejudice" (ללא הודאה בחבות ומבלי לפגוע בזכויות).

### How you know it worked

- Your diary is right (see the model answer) and no deadline is left without an action.
- Each draft asks for a specific order and lists its attachments.
- Your monthly offer is supported by the household budget and fits the period limits.
- Your settlement letter gives the creditor a reason to agree.

**Numbers.** Household net ₪10,900. He offers ₪1,000 a month, leaving a margin for B if he loses.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes, in your own files. The opposition's affidavit must be signed before a lawyer or another authorised person; the court hearing on the note can be handled in your own name.

### Where and how

- Personal area, online general request, or a branch; call centre *35592.
- Free help: Legal Aid execution service; debt-counselling NGOs and municipal programmes; [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md); forum rules: [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The note case in court turns on bills-of-exchange law (the shop assigned the note to a finance company claiming to be a holder in due course).
- The bank refuses any arrangement and you cannot pay within the period limits: move to lesson 28.9.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| One letter covering all three files | Requests not processed | One request per file (and a consolidation request) | Work file by file |
| Missing the opposition deadline in B | Execution of the note continues | Extension application with affidavit (reg. 109) | Diary from personal service |
| Offering more than the budget allows | Default; restrictions return | Ask to vary (s. 69ב) | Base the offer on documents |
| Settling orally with the bank | Bank keeps enforcing | Get it in writing; s. 19ב notice | Written arrangement only |

## Review

- Start with the diary: service dates drive every right.
- Match each file to its defence: judgments → payment order or "I have paid"; notes → opposition.
- Release protected money and ask for restrictions to be cancelled while you comply.
- A reasoned, documented arrangement offer often ends enforcement faster than any request.
