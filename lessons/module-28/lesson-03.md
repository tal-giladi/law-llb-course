# 28.3 · The debtor's defences: opposition, "I paid" claims and requests to the registrar

A debtor in an execution file has more than "pay or ask for instalments". If the file rests on a cheque or note he does not owe, he can oppose it and move the dispute to court. If he already paid, he can make an "I have paid" claim. If the office or the creditor got something wrong, he can ask the registrar to fix it, and if the judgment itself is defective he goes back to the court. This lesson sorts these defences, their deadlines and their proof, and ends with you drafting one.

## Why it matters

Choosing the wrong defence costs time the debtor does not have. Arguing "I never owed this" against a judgment in the Execution Office fails, because the registrar cannot reopen a judgment; the same argument against a cheque is exactly what the opposition procedure is for, but only within its period. An "I have paid" claim with no documents earns the debtor special costs. The doctrine is about the boundary between execution and adjudication: the Execution Office enforces, the courts decide liability, and the defences are the gates between them.

## The law

### Opposition to execution of a bill: s. 81א

> (ג) החייב רשאי להגיש התנגדות לביצוע הבקשה שתהא נתמכת בתצהיר ובו יפורטו העובדות ונימוקי ההתנגדות, ומשהוגשה ההתנגדות יעכב רשם ההוצאה לפועל את הביצוע ויעביר את הענין לבית המשפט.
> (ג1) על אף האמור בסעיף 7(א), המועד להגשת התנגדות לביצוע הבקשה יהיה שלושים ימים מיום המצאת האזהרה.

*Gist:* A cheque, note or bill of exchange can be executed like a judgment, but the debtor may oppose with an affidavit setting out the facts and grounds; the registrar then stays execution and sends the matter to court; the period runs from service of the warning. ([s. 81א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_81א); period in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

The regulations fill in the procedure: the opposition is filed at the branch where the file was opened or the branch next to the court with local jurisdiction over a claim on the bill, with one copy, an affidavit, copies of every document relied on and copies for service (reg. 106). The registrar stays execution against the opponent and transfers the file, and in court **the request to execute becomes a statement of claim in summary procedure** (reg. 108(ב)); steps already taken, such as liens, stay in place unless the court orders otherwise (reg. 108(ג)). If the court dismisses the opposition, the creditor returns the decision to the office within 14 days and execution resumes (reg. 108(ד)). A late opposition needs an application to extend time with an affidavit and the main grounds, which the court decides (reg. 109). ([regs 106–109](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_106))

### Opposition to a fixed-sum claim: s. 81א1

A fixed-sum claim (contractual debt with written evidence, or a statutory debt, up to a ceiling) may be filed in the Execution Office only after a registered-mail notice to the debtor and a waiting period (s. 81א1(ב1), (ג)). The debtor may oppose within the same period from service of the warning, including on the ground that the claim does not qualify; the registrar stays it and sends it to court (s. 81א1(ד)–(ה)). ([s. 81א1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_81א1); ceiling in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

A debtor who files an opposition in time, or an "I have paid" claim within the means-inquiry period, does not have to appear for a means inquiry while it is pending; if it is dismissed, the clock restarts from the dismissal (s. 67(א1)(2); reg. 12א(ה)).

### "I have paid": s. 19

> (א) חייב הטוען שמילא אחר פסק הדין או שאינו חייב עוד למלא אחריו, כולו או מקצתו, עליו הראיה, ורשאי רשם ההוצאה לפועל לקבוע אם ובאיזו מידה מוטל עוד על החייב למלא אחר פסק הדין; עד לקביעתו כאמור, רשאי רשם ההוצאה לפועל להורות על עיכוב ביצועו… מטעמים מיוחדים שיירשמו.

*Gist:* A debtor who says he has complied, or no longer owes all or part, bears the burden of proof; the registrar decides how much is still owed and may stay execution meanwhile only for special recorded reasons, normally against security (s. 19(ב)); a baseless claim earns special costs (s. 19(ג)). A decision under s. 19 is appealable to the magistrates' court as of right (s. 80(ב)). ([s. 19](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_19))

"I have paid" is about what happened after the judgment (payment, a release, a later settlement, an agreed set-off). A payment made before the judgment was a defence for the trial; if the judgment ignored it, the remedy lies with the court that gave it. The claim is not a back door to reargue liability.

### The creditor's duties: ss. 19א, 19ב, 20

The creditor must report to the office every payment received outside it, within a short period (s. 19א(א)); if he agreed an arrangement with the debtor, he notifies the office, proceedings in the file stop while it is kept, and he may renew them only after a registered-mail notice to the debtor and a waiting period (s. 19ב). The debtor may report payments under the arrangement himself; if the creditor does not respond in time, the principal is reduced accordingly (s. 19ב(ד)). Anything collected above the debt is returned, by an order enforceable against the creditor (s. 20). ([ss. 19א–20](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_19א))

### Stays and the court that gave the judgment: ss. 17–18

Execution of a judgment is stayed only under the Execution Law or by a court order (s. 17). If the judgment is set aside (for example a default judgment, see [lesson 15.3](../module-15/lesson-03.md)), the registrar may order restoration of the previous position (s. 18).

### Requests and objections to the registrar

Anything else goes to the registrar as a request (e.g., to correct the debt calculation, to release an exempt item, to cancel a restriction, to change a payment order) or, against an act of the office, as an objection under s. 80(א) and reg. 118. Decisions are appealable to the magistrates' court, with leave except for the list in s. 80(ב) (lesson 28.1).

## Leading cases

### Key text: regs 106–109 of the Execution Regulations

No appellate ruling on the opposition procedure could be opened from a free source for this lesson. The regulations carry the procedure, and their key move is reg. 108(ב): once an opposition is filed, the dispute is a summary-procedure claim in court, where the debtor asks for leave to defend on the strength of the affidavit he filed with the opposition. Court practice treats the affidavit as the debtor's main evidence at that stage, so a thin affidavit is the most common reason oppositions fail. ([regs 106–109](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_106))

### Key text: s. 19(א) — the burden is on the debtor

The words "עליו הראיה" decide most "I have paid" claims. Bank transfers, signed receipts, written settlements and correspondence win; "I paid him cash" without a receipt usually loses.

## How it works in practice

### Which defence?

| Situation | Defence | Where | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| File on a cheque you gave for a sofa never delivered | Opposition to execution of a bill (s. 81א) | Execution Office branch → magistrates' court | 30 days from service of the warning |
| File on a "fixed-sum claim" for a gym membership you cancelled in writing | Opposition (s. 81א1(ד)) | Branch → court | 30 days from service |
| File on a judgment; you paid the creditor directly after the judgment | "I have paid" (s. 19) | Registrar | As soon as possible; ideally within the means-inquiry period |
| File on a default judgment in a case you never knew about | Application to set aside in the court that gave it, plus a stay request (s. 17) | That court | Court rules; act at once |
| Interest or fees miscalculated | Request to the registrar to correct the debt | Registrar | Any time |
| The creditor agreed an arrangement but keeps enforcing | Report the arrangement and payments (s. 19ב(ד)); request to stop proceedings | Registrar | At once |

### Drafting an opposition (outline)

1. Heading: the branch, file number, the parties as in the file, "התנגדות לביצוע שטר".
2. Facts: the transaction, why the cheque was given, what went wrong (no consideration, failed consideration, fraud, payment, forgery, the cheque was a guarantee, the holder is not a holder in due course), with dates.
3. Legal grounds: the defence under the Bills of Exchange Ordinance, briefly.
4. Request: stay execution and transfer to court; if relevant, transfer to the court with local jurisdiction (reg. 106(ה)).
5. Affidavit verifying the facts, signed before a lawyer; attachments: the cheque copy, the contract, correspondence.

## Worked example

Michal Ben-Ami, a teacher in Kfar Saba, gave a renovation contractor a post-dated cheque for ₪18,000 as the final payment. He abandoned the job halfway; she stopped the cheque. Three months later the contractor opened an execution file on the cheque. The warning was served on her on 2 December 2026.

1. **Which document?** A cheque: opposition is the right defence; the merits (failure of consideration) are open between the immediate parties.
2. **Deadline.** 30 days from 2 December. She diaries the last day and aims to file a week earlier.
3. **No means inquiry.** While the opposition is pending she does not have to appear (s. 67(א1)(2)).
4. **Affidavit.** She sets out the contract, the payment schedule, photos of the unfinished work, the WhatsApp messages in which the contractor said he would not return, the cost of completing the work with another contractor, and the stop-payment instruction.
5. **Filing.** At the Kfar Saba branch, with copies for the contractor. The registrar stays the file and transfers it to the Kfar Saba magistrates' court.
6. **In court.** The request becomes a summary-procedure claim; the judge gives her leave to defend on her affidavit; the parties later settle for ₪4,000.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your own file on a bill, a fixed-sum claim or a judgment you have paid; or the supplied case: Yusuf Halabi of Daliyat al-Karmel paid a ₪6,300 small-claims judgment by bank transfer to the creditor's account a week after the judgment, but the creditor opened an execution file anyway two months later and the warning arrived today.
- Execution Law ss. 19–20, 67(א1)(2), 81א; regs 106–109.
- 60 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Classify (10 min).** What document is the file based on? Which defence(s) are open, and which are closed?
2. **Deadline (5 min).** Compute the last day, from the service date.
3. **Evidence (15 min).** List every document that proves your defence and where it is. Note what is missing and how you will get it (bank confirmation, receipts).
4. **Draft (25 min).** In Hebrew: either a one-page opposition with an affidavit outline, or an "I have paid" request with a payment table (date, amount, method, proof) and the order you ask for (a finding that the debt is paid, closing the file, costs against the creditor under ss. 19א and 20).
5. **Creditor's view (5 min).** What will the creditor answer, and what is your reply?

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. You may file an opposition, an "I have paid" claim and requests in your own file, and defend yourself in the magistrates' court when the opposition is transferred. The affidavit must be signed before a lawyer (or another person authorised to take affidavits); having a lawyer verify your signature is not the same as being represented.

### Where and how

- Opposition: at the branch where the file was opened or next to the competent court (reg. 106(א)); check whether the personal area accepts oppositions online in your case (⚠️ manual check).
- "I have paid" and other requests: personal area, online general request, or a branch; call centre *35592.
- Periods and ceilings: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md). Free help: Legal Aid, court information centres, [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The amount is large, the creditor is a bank or finance company with a lawyer, and the dispute is about the law of bills (holder in due course, guarantees).
- You missed the opposition period: an extension application must explain the delay convincingly.
- Your defence depends on fraud or forgery.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Filing an opposition without an affidavit | Not a valid opposition; no stay | Refile at once with an affidavit, or apply to extend time | Use the reg. 106 checklist |
| Missing the 30 days | Execution continues; extension needed | Extension application with affidavit and grounds (reg. 109) | Diary from the service date |
| "I have paid" based on cash without receipts | Claim rejected, special costs (s. 19(ג)) | Find corroboration (messages, witnesses) | Always pay by traceable means and keep receipts |
| Using "I have paid" to argue the judgment was wrong | Rejected | Apply to the court that gave the judgment | Separate liability from payment |
| Not reporting payments under an arrangement | Debt in the file stays too high | Report under s. 19ב(ד) with proof | Report every payment |

## Review

- Against a bill or fixed-sum claim: opposition with an affidavit within the period; the dispute moves to court as a summary-procedure claim.
- Against a judgment you have paid: "I have paid" under s. 19, with the burden on you and a stay only for special reasons.
- Against a defective judgment: the court that gave it, not the Execution Office.
- The creditor must report payments and arrangements and return over-collection.
