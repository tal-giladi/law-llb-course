# 28.7 · Corporate insolvency in brief

Most people meet corporate insolvency not as owners but as creditors: an employee whose wages stopped, a supplier holding unpaid invoices, a customer who paid a deposit. This lesson sketches the corporate side of the 2018 law at the depth a Bar candidate and a creditor need: how proceedings open, what the stay does, the trustee's choice between rehabilitation and liquidation, the order of payment, and the personal liability of directors. You finish by drafting an employee's debt claim.

## Why it matters

Corporate insolvency decides who bears a company's losses. The law's purposes apply here too (s. 1): rehabilitate the business where possible, maximise recovery, and, for individuals, reintegration. Between creditors the law is not neutral: secured lenders come first in their collateral, then employees, withheld taxes and maintenance in a fixed priority, then everyone else. Within the company, the 2018 law added a direct duty on directors and the CEO to act when the company is insolvent (s. 288), the modern partner of the veil-lifting and distribution rules in [lesson 16.5](../module-16/lesson-05.md). Insolvency is also a Bar-exam subject, mostly at the level of priorities, the stay and the trustee's powers.

## The law

### Who may apply: ss. 3, 8–11

Proceedings start at the initiative of the company, a creditor or the Attorney General (s. 3(ב)), in the district court (s. 4). A company may ask to be operated for its rehabilitation, with an initial outline and funding (s. 8). A creditor may apply if the company is insolvent, proving it directly or through a presumption (s. 9):

> (א) …חזקה היא כי התאגיד נמצא בחדלות פירעון בהתקיים אחד מאלה: (1) הנושה מסר לתאגיד דרישה לתשלום חוב שסכומו עולה על 75,000 שקלים חדשים (מתואם לשנת 2019; בשנת 2026, 88,461.56 ש״ח)… והחוב לא שולם בתוך 30 ימים…

*Gist:* Insolvency is presumed after an undisputed formal demand above a linked amount unpaid within 30 days (application within three months), a receiver for all or most assets, an unpaid execution warning or tax demand above that amount, an unpaid court judgment above it, or an unpaid labour-court judgment above a much lower amount; the creditor must say what collection steps he took and why they are not enough. ([s. 10](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חדלות_פירעון_ושיקום_כלכלי#סעיף_10); amounts in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

### The stay: ss. 29–31

> הקפאת ההליכים נגד התאגיד משמעה כי – (1) לא יהיה ניתן לפתוח בהליכי גבייה של חובות עבר נגד התאגיד או להמשיך בהליכי גבייה שטרם הושלמו…

*Gist:* After the opening order: no collection of past debts, no realisation of fixed charges or crystallisation of floating charges without the court, no new charges, no liens (existing ones fall away, with tax exceptions), no legal proceedings without the court's permission for special reasons, no late-payment additions to penal debts (s. 29). The stay protects only the company, except in exceptional cases (s. 30), and does not cover criminal or administrative proceedings (s. 31). ([s. 29](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חדלות_פירעון_ושיקום_כלכלי#סעיף_29))

### The trustee, rehabilitation or liquidation: ss. 35–43, 94

The court appoints a trustee, who takes over the powers of the company's organs and officers (s. 43). If rehabilitation is ordered, the trustee runs the business, may sell it as a going concern, or prepares a rehabilitation plan voted on by classes of creditors; if rehabilitation fails, the court moves the company to liquidation (s. 94). Outside proceedings, a company may agree a debt arrangement with creditors under Part 10, and since Amendment No. 9 (2026) may ask the court for a **temporary stay** of up to 90 days to negotiate one, with conditions on how it operates in the meantime and a court-appointed arrangement administrator (ss. 319ג–319טז).

### Who gets paid, in what order: ss. 232–242

1. **Secured creditors** from their collateral (subject to the special rules in Part D chapter 6).
2. **Costs of the proceedings.**
3. **Priority debts** (s. 234), in this order: employees' wages and severance for the period before the application, net of the National Insurance benefit under chapter ח of the National Insurance Law, up to linked caps; tax and National Insurance withheld at source and not transferred; maintenance arrears under a judgment; VAT principal from the 12 months before the order; and principal of compulsory payments under a spread arrangement signed before the order, within limits. If unsecured assets do not suffice, priority debts are paid from assets subject to a floating charge (s. 234(ב)).
4. **General debts** (s. 235), with linkage and basic interest to the order, without late-payment additions.
5. **Deferred debts.**

> (א)(1) חוב בשל שכר עבודה… ובשל פיצויי פיטורין… בניכוי הגמלה המשולמת לפי פרק ח׳ לחוק הביטוח הלאומי, בסכום שלא יעלה על 25,630 שקלים חדשים (נכון לשנת 2019; בשנת 2026, 33,250 ש״ח) לעניין שכר עבודה או על 39,945 שקלים חדשים (נכון לשנת 2019; בשנת 2026, 51,820 ש״ח) לעניין פיצויי פיטורין…

*Gist:* Employees rank first among priority creditors, up to caps updated each January by the average wage; the National Insurance Institute pays employees a benefit for unpaid wages and severance and then takes their place against the company. ([s. 234](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חדלות_פירעון_ושיקום_כלכלי#סעיף_234); caps in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

### Undoing transactions

The individual rules (lesson 28.6) apply to companies: preferences within three months (a year for a related party), undervalue transfers within two years (four for a related party), concealment within seven years (ss. 219–221).

### Directors and the CEO: s. 288

> (א) ידע דירקטור או מנהל כללי או היה עליו לדעת כי התאגיד נמצא בחדלות פירעון ולא נקט אמצעים סבירים לצמצום היקפה, רשאי בית המשפט… להורות כי הדירקטור או המנהל הכללי יישא באחריות…

*Gist:* A director or CEO who knew or should have known the company was insolvent and did not take reasonable steps to reduce the scope of the insolvency may be held liable for the resulting harm to creditors. He is presumed to have acted reasonably if he assessed the company's position and acted to obtain turnaround help, negotiate a debt arrangement, or open insolvency proceedings; the company may not exempt or indemnify him; good-faith reasonable reliance on information that the company was solvent is a defence. ([s. 288](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חדלות_פירעון_ושיקום_כלכלי#סעיף_288))

## Leading cases

### רע״א 7708/23 גוליבר אנרג׳י בע״מ נ׳ עו״ד ליהי בלומנפלד, מנהלת ההסדר לחברות (22.9.2024)

Under a creditors' arrangement for two insolvent companies, their shares were sold to a buyer; the price went to the arrangement funds, which paid dividends to creditors and the administrator's fees. A VAT refund later arose from those payments, and the companies (now in the buyer's hands) claimed it. The Supreme Court (Grosskopf J., Mintz and Kabub JJ. agreeing) heard the application as an appeal because of doubt whether appeals under the law are by leave or as of right, and dismissed it: given the close link between the arrangement funds and the payments that generated the refund, the funds had a quasi-proprietary right to it, filling a gap the parties had left in the arrangement. Why it matters: it shows the court reading a creditors' arrangement purposively to keep value with creditors, and it flags an open procedural question about routes of appeal under the 2018 law. [Full text (free host)](https://capitax.co.il/Attachments/7708-23.pdf).

### Key text: s. 288 with Companies Law s. 6

For directors' exposure, read s. 288 next to veil-lifting and prohibited distributions ([lesson 16.5](../module-16/lesson-05.md)). Section 288 does not need fraud; it needs inaction while insolvent.

## How it works in practice

### For an employee of a company in trouble

1. Keep payslips, contract, attendance records and messages about late wages.
2. Send a written demand; a labour-court judgment above the lower linked amount creates a presumption for a creditor's application (s. 10(א)(5)).
3. Once proceedings open, file a **debt claim** with the trustee within the period (s. 210), on the official form; wage and severance claims carry no fee (Fee Regulations reg. 6(ב)).
4. Ask the trustee to confirm your claim to the National Insurance Institute, which pays the insolvency benefit for wages and severance up to its limits, then claims from the company.
5. Pension contributions that were deducted and not transferred are handled through the pension fund and the trustee (⚠️ manual check of the current National Insurance and Kol-Zchut guidance).

### For a supplier

- Before proceedings: secure yourself (retention of title, guarantees from shareholders, lesson 16.5).
- After: stop collection steps (they are stayed), file a debt claim with evidence of each invoice, and watch the trustee's notices on a rehabilitation plan or sale.
- Payments received from the company in the three months before the application may be challenged as preferences, unless in the ordinary course of business (s. 219(ב)(2)).

## Worked example

"אור־טק מערכות בע״מ", a 40-employee firm in Petah Tikva, stops paying wages in July 2026. Engineer Shira Golan is owed two months' wages (₪34,000) and severance (₪60,000). A bank holds a floating charge; the company owes VAT and a supplier ₪120,000.

1. **Opening.** The supplier has an unpaid court judgment above the linked amount; it applies to the district court relying on the presumption in s. 10(א)(4). The court opens proceedings and appoints a trustee.
2. **Stay.** Shira's lawsuit in the labour court is stayed; she cannot enforce without the court's permission (s. 29(5)).
3. **Debt claim.** She files a claim for wages and severance with the trustee, with payslips and her contract, without a fee.
4. **National Insurance.** On the trustee's confirmation the Institute pays her benefit up to its limits.
5. **Priority.** For the balance (after the benefit), Shira ranks first among priority creditors up to the 2026 caps for wages and for severance; VAT principal of the last 12 months ranks lower in the priority list; the supplier is a general creditor.
6. **Directors.** The trustee finds the board kept taking orders it could not fulfil for six months while insolvent and did nothing else; he applies against the CEO and directors under s. 288.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A real company that owes you money (check the Companies Registrar for insolvency status), or the supplied case: you are Shira above.
- Insolvency Law ss. 29, 210–211, 234, 288; Fee Regulations reg. 6(ב).
- 40 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Status (5 min).** How would you find out whether proceedings opened, and who the trustee is? (Published notice; the Companies Registrar; the court file.)
2. **Claim (20 min).** Draft, in Hebrew, the content of a debt claim: creditor details, the period worked, wages owed by month, severance calculation basis, documents attached, statement that the claim is for wages and severance (no fee).
3. **Ranking (10 min).** Split your claim into the part the National Insurance benefit covers, the priority part up to the caps, and any general balance.
4. **Officers (5 min).** List the facts that would support a s. 288 application and who would bring it.

### Template

- None (debt claims are filed on the trustee's or Commissioner's official form).

### How you know it worked

- Your claim states amounts by component with documents for each.
- Your ranking names s. 234(א)(1) and the 2026 caps from current numbers.

### Self-check

- [ ] I know collection and lawsuits against the company stop after the order.
- [ ] I know employees rank first among priority creditors, up to caps, after secured creditors.
- [ ] I know wage claims carry no debt-claim fee.
- [ ] I know s. 288 can make directors personally liable for inaction while insolvent.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes, as an individual creditor: you may file your own debt claim and object to the trustee's decision in your own name. A company that is itself a creditor, and any company applying for proceedings, generally acts through a lawyer in the district court (the corporate-representation rule; check per forum in [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md)).

### Where and how

- Debt claims: to the trustee, within the period after publication; forms on the Commissioner's gov.il pages (⚠️ manual check).
- Employees: the National Insurance Institute's insolvency benefit (Kol-Zchut "גמלה לעובדים בפשיטת רגל או בפירוק", ⚠️ manual check); the labour-court judgment you already have is your best evidence.
- Linked amounts and caps: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); help: unions, Legal Aid for employees, [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You are a director or shareholder of the insolvent company.
- The trustee rejects your claim or treats a payment you received as a preference.
- You are a secured creditor or a landlord of the company.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Employee keeps pursuing an execution file after the order | Stayed; wasted fees | File a debt claim | Check for an opening order first |
| Missing the debt-claim period | No share in distributions | Ask the trustee to extend (s. 210(ג)) | Diary from publication |
| Supplier accepts a large repayment just before the application | Set aside as a preference (s. 219) | Negotiate with the trustee | Ordinary-course terms; security up front |
| Directors keep trading while insolvent "to save the company" without advice | Personal liability (s. 288) | Get turnaround advice; consider proceedings | Document the board's assessment and steps |

## Review

- Corporate proceedings open on the company's, a creditor's (often by presumption) or the Attorney General's application in the district court.
- The stay freezes collection and lawsuits against the company; a trustee takes over and chooses rehabilitation, sale or liquidation.
- Secured creditors, costs, priority debts (employees first, up to caps), general debts, deferred debts.
- Directors and the CEO who do nothing while the company is insolvent may be personally liable (s. 288).
