# 28.2 · Execution measures: attachments, restrictions and protected assets

Once the warning period has passed, the creditor can ask the registrar for measures against the debtor's money, property and freedom of movement. This lesson goes through the measures in the order a creditor usually asks for them, the conditions the law sets for each, and the protections that keep the debtor and family above a minimum: exempt movables, protected wages and benefits, the home and its alternative housing. By the end you can tell whether a lien or restriction in your file is lawful and ask to cancel one that is not.

## Why it matters

The Execution Law's measures are strong because the Execution Office acts without a new trial: a lien order reaches the bank the same day. The protections are equally concrete, but they are not always applied automatically. Banks and employers apply some on their own (protected wages, some benefits), while others (tools of the trade, a disabled person's car, the home) depend on the debtor asking. Knowing both sides is what lets a creditor collect without costs being awarded against him, and a debtor keep what the law says he keeps.

The debate behind the rules is old and still live: how far a creditor's property right may reach into the debtor's livelihood and home. Israeli law answers with lists (s. 22), formulas (Wage Protection Law s. 8) and a standard (s. 38's "reasonable" housing), and gives the registrar a residual discretion to spare property whose sale would hurt more than it yields (s. 22(ה)).

## The law

### Information first: s. 7ב

The registrar may order public bodies, banks and others listed in the Second Schedule to give information about the debtor's address, assets, income and expenses, on the basis of the debtor's waiver (s. 7ב(א)) or, from the bodies in the Second Schedule, under s. 7ב(א2)–(א3) after full service of the warning or the debtor's appearance (s. 7(ג)) and a written notice with a short waiting period (s. 7ב(א4)), and only so far as needed to enforce and to check means (s. 7ב(ב)). ([s. 7ב](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_7ב))

### Liens

- **Movables in the debtor's hands (s. 21).** Seized and sold; a car parked in public is seized only near the debtor's premises, or after registration of the lien at the licensing office and notice, and never if it is registered as a disabled person's car (s. 21א).
- **Property held by third parties (ss. 44–52).** A lien on "all the debtor's assets" held by a bank or employer catches what it holds on the day of service and what comes in within three months; the third party reports within ten days (s. 44). A lien served on a bank's head office reaches all its branches (s. 45א).
- **Land (ss. 34–38).** Registered at the Land Registry, then sale; the home has its own regime (below).

### What cannot be attached: s. 22 and s. 50

> (א) ואלה מיטלטלין שאין מעקלים אותם: (1) צרכי אוכל כדי מחיית החייב ובני משפחתו הגרים עמו, לתקופה של שלושים יום; (2) ריהוט, מערכות בגדים, מיטות… ציוד רפואי, תרופות…

*Gist:* Exempt movables include a month's food, basic furniture, clothes, medical equipment and household goods; religious articles; tools of the trade including a vehicle up to a set value; a disabled person's aids and car; pets; personal phones and computers of an individual debtor and family (not business equipment); one item of each type in the Fifth Schedule up to a set value; and low-value items (s. 22(א)). The exemption for tools, disability aids and Fifth-Schedule items does not protect against the seller who financed the item (s. 22(ב)), and the registrar may spare other items whose sale would cause disproportionate harm (s. 22(ה)). ([s. 22](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_22))

Section 50 adds money in third parties' hands that cannot be attached: maintenance due to the debtor, a protected income amount for a debtor who is not an employee, rent grants, listed welfare payments, health-basket and long-term-care insurance payments, and others; several are protected for a set period after reaching the bank (s. 50(א)). Most of these protections do not apply against a maintenance creditor (s. 50(ב)). ([s. 50](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_50))

### Wages: Wage Protection Law s. 8

> (א) משכר העבודה החדשי לא יהיה ניתן לעיקול, להעברה או לשעבוד סכום השווה לגימלה בשיעור הנקוב בטור ג׳ בתוספת השניה לחוק הבטחת הכנסה…

*Gist:* The non-attachable part of monthly net wages equals the income-support benefit the employee would get for his family composition, capped at 80% of net wages; the protection follows wages into the bank for a month; it does not apply to maintenance debts; occupational pensions count as wages. ([s. 8](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_השכר#סעיף_8)) National Insurance benefits have their own protection in the National Insurance Law (ss. 303, 311). Amounts: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### The home: s. 38

> (א) היו המקרקעין שעוקלו משמשים, כולם או מקצתם, דירת מגורים לחייב, לא יהיה רשם ההוצאה לפועל רשאי להורות על מכירת המקרקעין ועל פינוי החייב ובני משפחתו… אלא לאחר שהוכח, להנחת דעתו, שיהיה לחייב ולבני משפחתו הגרים עמו מקום מגורים סביר…

*Gist:* The home may be sold only after the registrar is satisfied that the debtor and family will have reasonable housing, can afford it, or that an alternative arrangement has been provided; only after a hearing with full service; the unrepresented debtor may ask for time to get a lawyer or Legal Aid (s. 38(א)–(א1), (ג1)). The insolvency law's home-protection rule, s. 229, applies to what counts as reasonable housing and alternative arrangement (s. 38(ב)). For mortgages, the deed may instead provide, with a clear explanation to the debtor, an alternative arrangement worth renting a suitable flat in the area for a limited period (s. 38(ג)(2)). ([s. 38](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_38); period in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

### Restrictions: ss. 66א–66ד

The registrar may restrict a passport, exit from Israel (not if travel is needed for the health of the debtor or a dependant), cheques ("special restricted customer"), credit cards (not prepaid or immediate-debit cards against a credit balance, s. 66א1) and founding or holding interests in companies (s. 66א), taking into account the harm and the other measures already tried. Conditions, one of which must exist (s. 66ב):

> (1) …הוכח לרשם ההוצאה לפועל כי הוא בעל יכולת המשתמט מתשלום החוב… (2) החוב הפסוק נובע ממזונות… (3) החייב הוא בעל יכולת המשתמט… כאמור בסעיפים 7ג, 67(ד)… ובלבד ש… חלפו שישה חודשים…

*Gist:* Restrictions need either a finding that the debtor has means and evades, a maintenance debt, or deemed-evader status plus a waiting period and a minimum debt; a restriction takes effect only 30 days after a written notice, during which the debtor can pay or appear and show he is not evading (s. 66ג); it must be cancelled when he complies with a payment order or an agreement (s. 66ד(ג)). ([ss. 66א–66ד](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_66א); thresholds in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

### The evaders' register, bringing and arrest

A debtor with means who evades, with judgment debts above a threshold, may be entered in a public register of evading debtors when other measures have failed (s. 66ה). The registrar may order a debtor brought before him (צו הבאה, s. 69יב). Imprisonment for ordinary civil debt is no longer part of the law: arrest orders survive for maintenance debts (ss. 70, 74), only against a debtor with means, after other measures, and with notice; never against a minor or a person without legal capacity (s. 71).

## Leading cases

### Key text: s. 38 and its link to s. 229 of the Insolvency Law

The leading appellate decisions on s. 38 could not be opened from a free source for this lesson, so none is briefed. The statute carries the doctrine. Since 2018, s. 38(ב) imports from s. 229 of the Insolvency Law the rules on what counts as reasonable housing and an alternative arrangement (housing in the family's area that fits its needs, or a court-set alternative for a period). In insolvency proceedings s. 229 goes further: the court may approve the sale of the home only if the benefit to creditors outweighs the harm to the family, considering age and health, and there is no less harmful way to pay. Section 38 does not copy those two tests in terms, but they show the proportionality the legislature expects when a home is at stake. Read both sections together ([s. 38](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_38); [s. 229](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חדלות_פירעון_ושיקום_כלכלי#סעיף_229)) and Kol-Zchut's explanation of alternative housing (⚠️ manual check).

### בג״ץ 5304/92 פר״ח נ׳ שר המשפטים (1993)

See [lesson 08.7](../module-08/lesson-07.md#leading-cases). Its influence shows in today's design: arrest only for maintenance, only against a debtor shown to have means, and restrictions as the main tool against evasion.

## How it works in practice

### The creditor's usual sequence

1. Information requests (bank accounts, employer, vehicles, land).
2. Bank-account and wage liens (cheap, fast, effective).
3. Car lien by registration, then seizure.
4. After the conditions in s. 66ב: restrictions, starting with exit from Israel and credit cards.
5. Land, and only exceptionally the home, through a hearing under s. 38.

### The debtor's checks

| You see | Check | If wrong |
|---|---|---|
| Account frozen, salary paid in | Has the bank left the protected wage amount (s. 8) and protected benefits? | Ask the bank first; then a request to the registrar to release protected sums |
| Work van seized | Tools of the trade within the value cap (s. 22(א)(4))? | Request to cancel the lien, with proof of use for livelihood |
| Car seized, you have a disabled badge | Registered as a disabled person's car (s. 21א)? | Request immediate release |
| Restriction notice | Which s. 66ב condition does it rely on? Was the warning fully served (s. 7(ג))? | Within the 30-day notice: pay, appear for means inquiry, or request cancellation |
| Notice of hearing on sale of home | Reasonable housing or alternative arrangement (ss. 38, 229), time to get a lawyer | Ask for postponement to get Legal Aid (s. 38(ג1)); prepare housing evidence |

## Worked example

Igor Petrenko, a self-employed electrician in Ashdod, owes ₪38,000 in three execution files. He did not appear for a means inquiry after the warning in the largest file, served on him personally eight months ago. One creditor asks for: a lien on his business account, seizure of his van, an exit ban, and a lien on the flat he owns with his wife, where they live with two children.

1. **Business account.** Allowed. But Igor is not an employee: money due to him is protected up to the amount of a protected wage (s. 50(א)(3)), and he can ask the registrar to apply that.
2. **Van.** It carries his tools and is his only way to work. If its value is within the cap set in regulations, it is exempt (s. 22(א)(4)). He files a request with photos, invoices and his business registration.
3. **Exit ban.** Igor is deemed an evader (s. 67(ד)); more than six months have passed since full service of the warning and the debt exceeds the threshold (s. 66ב(א)(3)(א)). The ban is lawful. It takes effect 30 days after the notice (s. 66ג). If Igor appears and files a payment-order request he can comply with, the registrar must cancel it while he complies (s. 66ד(ג)).
4. **The flat.** A lien on his share may be registered; sale and eviction need a hearing and proof of reasonable housing (s. 38). For a ₪38,000 debt with wage, account and instalment routes still open, a registrar is unlikely to find a sale of the family home justified, and the creditor would first have to show reasonable housing for the family.
5. **What Igor does.** Within the 30 days: appears, files a payment-order request with documents, asks for consolidation of the three files (lesson 28.4) and for release of the van.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your own execution file, or the supplied case: Hila, a single mother of two in Tiberias, salaried, owes ₪21,000 in one file; her account has been frozen and contains her salary and child allowance; the creditor has asked for an exit ban; she has a 2014 car worth about ₪15,000 that she uses to take her son to physiotherapy.
- Execution Law ss. 21א, 22, 38, 50, 66א–66ד; Wage Protection Law s. 8; [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. **List measures (10 min).** For each measure requested or taken in the file, write the section that allows it and the condition it needs.
2. **Exemptions (15 min).** For each frozen or seized item, decide: exempt, partly exempt, or attachable. Cite the section.
3. **Restrictions (10 min).** Check service of the warning (s. 7(ג)), the s. 66ב condition, and the 30-day notice. Is there a payment order or agreement that requires cancellation (s. 66ד(ג))?
4. **Draft (10 min).** Write, in Hebrew, a short request to the registrar to release a protected sum or item: facts, the exemption with its section, the documents you attach, the order you ask for.

### Template

- Draft for this module: [execution debtor response kit](../../templates/execution-debtor-response-kit.md), part C (request to cancel a lien or restriction).

### How you know it worked

- Each measure is matched to its section and condition; each exemption claim is tied to a section and a document.
- Your request asks for a specific order (release ₪X from account Y; cancel the lien on vehicle Z).

### Self-check

- [ ] I know that protected wages and many benefits stay protected for a period after reaching the bank.
- [ ] I know that restrictions need a s. 66ב condition and a 30-day notice.
- [ ] I know that the home cannot be sold without a hearing and reasonable housing.
- [ ] I know that arrest is now limited to maintenance debts.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes, as debtor or creditor, in your own file. A request to the registrar is an ordinary filing through the personal area, the online general-request form or a branch.

### Where and how

- The Authority's personal area (general request, or the dedicated request type where one exists); any branch by appointment; call centre *35592 (⚠️ manual check of the current request codes).
- For frozen salary or benefits, speak to the bank first: it applies some protections itself.
- Amounts and periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md). Help: Legal Aid execution service, debtor-support NGOs, [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- A hearing on selling your home is set (you are entitled to time to obtain representation, s. 38(א1), (ג1)).
- A third party (spouse, parent, company) says the attached property is theirs (s. 40, s. 65): this becomes a dispute with evidence.
- You face an arrest order in a maintenance file.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Creditor asks to seize a work van that is the debtor's livelihood | Lien cancelled; possible costs | Target accounts and wages | Check s. 22 before asking |
| Debtor assumes the bank will release protected child allowance automatically | Money stays frozen for weeks | Request release, with bank statements showing the source | Keep benefits in a separate account and document the source |
| Ignoring a restriction notice | Exit ban takes effect after 30 days | Appear, pay or request cancellation; travel only after cancellation | Act within the notice period |
| Asking to sell the home for a small debt | Request refused after a costly hearing | Use other measures | Exhaust wage, account and instalment routes first; prepare proof of reasonable housing |
| Debtor transfers assets to a relative | Possible criminal and civil exposure; transfer may be set aside | Undo and disclose | Never move assets once a file is open |

## Review

- Measures run from information and bank/wage liens to restrictions and, rarely, the home.
- Exempt movables (s. 22), protected third-party money (s. 50), protected wages (Wage Protection Law s. 8) and benefits limit what can be taken.
- Restrictions need a s. 66ב condition and a 30-day notice and must be cancelled when the debtor complies with a payment order.
- The home may be sold only after a hearing and proof of reasonable housing (ss. 38, and Insolvency Law s. 229).
