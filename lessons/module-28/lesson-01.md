# 28.1 · The Execution Law in depth: the file, the warning and the debtor's rights

Lesson 08.7 showed a creditor how to open an execution file. This lesson looks at the same file from the inside and mostly from the debtor's chair: who runs it, how the warning is served and what it must say, the clock it starts, and the four things a debtor can do before enforcement begins. By the end you will be able to read a real warning, diary its deadlines and pick the right response.

## Why it matters

Many people who meet the Execution Office (לשכת ההוצאה לפועל) meet it as debtors, and the debtor's position is decided in the first three weeks. A debtor who acts inside the warning period can ask for instalments, be examined fairly, or stop a bill that he does not owe. A debtor who ignores the warning is treated by law as one "with means who evades payment" (s. 67(ד)) and becomes exposed to information orders, restrictions on travel, cheques, credit cards and company activity, and in the end to arrest proceedings.

The academic interest is the design of the system. The Execution Law of 1967 began as a creditor's statute. Since the 1990s, and decisively since the broad reform of 2008–2009 (the many sections marked "תשס״ט" in the consolidated text), it has become a two-track law: generous with the debtor who cannot pay (payment orders, consolidation of files, referral to insolvency proceedings) and tough with the one who can but will not. The constitutional starting point, בג״ץ 5304/92 on imprisonment for debt, is set out in [lesson 08.7](../module-08/lesson-07.md#leading-cases); this lesson builds on it. The Execution Law is also a Bar-exam procedural subject, and examiners like the deadlines.

## The law

### Who decides: s. 3א and s. 80

The execution registrar (רשם ההוצאה לפועל) has the powers of a judicial officer in the file and decides requests, payment orders, restrictions and the like (s. 3א). The director of the office and its staff carry out acts: serving, attaching, issuing orders the registrar made. That split decides how you challenge something:

> (א) הרואה עצמו נפגע על ידי פעולה של מנהל לשכת הוצאה לפועל, או החלטה שלו או של עובד מערכת ההוצאה לפועל, רשאי להגיש ערר לפני רשם ההוצאה לפועל.

*Gist:* Against an act of the office or its staff you file an objection (ערר) to the registrar; against the registrar's decisions you appeal to the magistrates' court, with leave, except for a closed list that is appealable as of right (s. 80(ב)); an objection or appeal does not stop the file unless ordered (s. 80(ג)). ([s. 80](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_80)) The objection is filed in writing within a very short period of learning of the act (reg. 118; see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)). ([reg. 118](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_118))

### What opens a file

A court judgment in a civil matter (s. 1), and documents that a law says are enforced like one: a registered mortgage, a confirmed arbitral award, a settlement with the force of a judgment (s. 81); a bill of exchange, promissory note or cheque (s. 81א); and a "claim for a fixed sum" up to a ceiling based on written evidence (s. 81א1). The kind of document matters to the debtor: against a judgment he cannot argue the merits in the office, but against a bill or a fixed-sum claim he can oppose, and the dispute moves to court (lesson 28.3). ([s. 81א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_81א))

### The warning: s. 7(א)–(א1)

> (א) משהוגשה בקשת ביצוע, ימציא מנהל לשכת ההוצאה לפועל לחייב אזהרה שעליו למלא אחר פסק־הדין, או לשלם את החוב הפסוק בשיעורים שנקבעו בהוראת תשלום לפי סעיף 69א או בצו לפי סעיף 69(ב), או להגיש בקשה לצו תשלומים לפי סעיף 7א, והכל בתוך תקופה של 20 ימים מיום המצאת האזהרה…

*Gist:* The warning gives the debtor a fixed period from service to comply with the judgment, to pay in the instalments set by a standard payment instruction or registrar's order, or to apply for a payment order; eviction warnings may have different periods. ([s. 7](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_7); periods in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

> (א1) האזהרה תכלול… הודעה לחייב בשפה פשוטה וברורה כי אם אין ביכולתו לעמוד בתשלום החוב הפסוק במלואו או בתשלומים שנקבעו לו, עליו להתייצב לחקירת יכולת בלשכת ההוצאה לפועל, בתוך 21 ימים מיום המצאת האזהרה…

*Gist:* The warning must tell the debtor in plain language that if he cannot pay he must appear for a means inquiry within a slightly longer period with listed documents, and must spell out the consequences of doing nothing: information orders without consent, restrictions, the register of evading debtors, arrest proceedings. ([s. 7(א1)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_7))

### Service, and why "full service" matters: s. 7(ב)–(ה)

The warning is served like a court document, or digitally under the Digital Communication with Public Bodies Law (s. 7(ב)(1)). But the heaviest tools (information orders under s. 7ב(א2)–(א3), restrictions, the evaders' register, arrest) may be used only if the debtor appeared before the registrar or was served with **full service** (המצאה מלאה) (s. 7(ג)). A debtor who files any request in the file is treated as fully served on that day unless the registrar orders otherwise (s. 7(ג1)); a refusal to accept counts as service (s. 7(ד)). No enforcement before service and the end of the period, unless the registrar finds that the circumstances justify an earlier step (s. 7(ה); reg. 14 requires a reasoned request with an affidavit).

### The debtor's four answers

1. **Pay**, or pay by the instalments in the standard payment instruction (הוראת תשלום) under the Authority's rules for individual debtors (s. 69א; the rules do not apply to companies or maintenance).
2. **Ask for a payment order (s. 7א).** Within the warning period, with a reasoned statement of means supported by documents, covering the debtor, spouse, minor children and adult children living with him, and a signed waiver of confidentiality; the office will not accept a request without them (s. 7א(א1)). While it is pending the debtor pays what he offered, never below the regulatory minimum (s. 7א(ד)). The order must fit the maximum repayment periods in s. 7א1 (with a reasoned extension possible), unless creditor and debtor agree otherwise. ([s. 7א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_7א); periods in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))
3. **Say he cannot pay at all (s. 7א2).** If even the longest permitted instalment plan is beyond him, the debtor notifies the registrar. Below the statutory debt threshold the notice is treated as an application to open insolvency proceedings before a dedicated execution registrar; above it, the office gives information about applying to the Insolvency Commissioner (lessons 28.5–28.6).
4. **Appear for a means inquiry (s. 67(א1)).** At any office, during reception hours, answering the questionnaire on form 21 and signing the waiver (reg. 12א). A payment-order request that meets s. 7א and is filed in time counts as appearing (reg. 12ב).

> (ד) לא התייצב חייב לחקירת יכולת במועד… יראו אותו כבעל יכולת המשתמט מתשלום חובותיו…

*Gist:* A debtor who does not appear for the means inquiry is deemed a debtor with means who evades payment; one who appears without the required documents may be so deemed. ([s. 67(ד)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_67))

The debtor's other defences (opposition to a bill, "I have paid", requests to the registrar) are in lesson 28.3.

## Leading cases

### בג״ץ 5304/92 פר״ח 1992 – סיוע משפטי לנפגעי חוק ההוצאה לפועל נ׳ שר המשפטים, פ״ד מז(4) 715 (1993)

Briefed in [lesson 08.7](../module-08/lesson-07.md#leading-cases): imprisonment orders for debt issued without examining the debtor's means were held to violate liberty and dignity. Why it matters here: it explains the place of the means inquiry in the warning (s. 7(א1)) and why the law's harshest consequences attach only to a debtor found, or deemed, able to pay. (⚠️ full text not opened; see 08.7 for the secondary sources.)

### Key text: the warning itself, s. 7(א1)

No ruling is needed to read a warning; the statute prescribes its content. Treat s. 7(א1) as a checklist: if the warning you received does not state the means-inquiry period, the documents to bring and the consequences of not appearing, note it. A defective warning is a ground for an objection or a request to the registrar, though it rarely ends the file.

## How it works in practice

### The life of a file, from the debtor's side

| Day | What happens | Your move |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Creditor files; the system issues the warning | Nothing yet |
| Service | Warning reaches you (post, messenger, digital service, or you file something and are deemed served, s. 7(ג1)) | Write the service date down; it starts every clock |
| Service + warning period | Last day to pay, follow the payment instruction, or file a payment-order request | File with documents, not just a letter |
| Service + means-inquiry period | Last day to appear for a means inquiry | Appear at any branch with the documents listed |
| Service + 30 days (bills, fixed-sum claims) | Last day to oppose | See lesson 28.3 |
| After that | Creditor may ask for liens and information; restrictions only on the conditions in s. 66ב | Lesson 28.2 |

All periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### Where to see and file

- **The Authority's personal area** (אזור אישי) on the Enforcement and Collection Authority site shows the file, the debt and the decisions, and both creditors and debtors can file requests there with a national digital ID login; a "general request" uses request code 988 when no dedicated form exists (⚠️ manual check: gov.il service page "הגשת בקשה כללית להוצאה לפועל"; the module 8 author could not confirm this, and a search of the service pages on 18 October 2026 shows creditors and debtors listed as filers).
- **Without the personal area:** an online general-request form, or in person at any branch by appointment ("פנייה מקוונת ללשכות ההוצאה לפועל"); call centre *35592.
- **Decisions** appear in the personal area; the stated response time for some requests is weeks, so file early (⚠️ manual check).

### Fees

Debtors do not pay to file most requests; creditors pay fees that are added to the debt (s. 9). Interest and linkage on the judgment debt run until payment, which is why a quick, realistic payment order is usually cheaper than delay.

## Worked example

Fatma Abu-Rabia, a care worker in Rahat, finds a registered letter from the Execution Office on 3 November 2026: a warning in a file opened by a furniture shop on a small-claims judgment of ₪9,800 she did not pay after her hours were cut. She earns about ₪6,200 net, has three children and pays a ₪1,100 loan instalment.

1. **Service date.** She signed the post-office receipt on 3 November. That is full service, so the clocks start on 3 November.
2. **Which document?** A judgment. She cannot reopen the merits in the office (and she agrees she owes the money).
3. **Options.** Paying in full: no. The standard payment instruction attached to the warning sets a monthly amount she cannot meet. Cannot pay at all: no, she can pay something. So: a payment-order request (s. 7א) within the warning period.
4. **Documents.** Three payslips, the loan statement, bank statements for her account, the children's details, rent contract, and the waiver of confidentiality on form 21.
5. **Amount.** She offers ₪350 a month, explaining her budget. The request goes to the creditor for comment (s. 7א(ב)); she starts paying ₪350 immediately (s. 7א(ד)).
6. **Result.** The registrar fixes ₪400 a month. Because she filed in time, she is treated as having appeared for the means inquiry (reg. 12ב) and is not exposed to restrictions while she complies.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A warning you received, or the supplied case: a warning served by post on 10 November 2026 in a file for a ₪14,500 judgment held by a phone company against you.
- Execution Law ss. 7, 7א, 7א1, 7א2, 67, 80 and regs 12א, 12ב open; [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Read the warning (10 min).** Identify: file number, branch, creditor, type of document, the debt breakdown (principal, interest, fees, lawyer's fees), the service date, and whether s. 7(א1) content is there.
2. **Diary (10 min).** Compute the last day to pay or ask for a payment order, the last day for a means inquiry, and (if the file is a bill or fixed-sum claim) the last day to oppose.
3. **Choose (10 min).** Write two lines for each of the four answers (pay, payment order, cannot pay at all, means inquiry) saying whether it fits and why.
4. **Paper list (10 min).** List the documents your chosen route needs and where you get each.
5. **Challenge (5 min).** Is there an act of the office you object to (for example, a lien before the warning period ended)? Decide: objection to the registrar or appeal, and by when.

### How you know it worked

- Every deadline in your diary is tied to the service date and a section number.
- You chose one route and can explain in one sentence why the others do not fit.
- Your document list matches s. 7א(א) and form 21.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Debtors and creditors may act for themselves before the Execution Office; representing another person there is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20). Forum table: [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md).

### Where and how

- The Authority's personal area, the online general-request form, or any branch by appointment; call centre *35592.
- Means inquiry: any branch during reception hours, without an appointment requirement in the regulation (reg. 12א(א)); check current reception arrangements.
- Free help: Legal Aid has an execution-office service for debtors who meet its tests; NGOs that help debtors; the Kol-Zchut execution portal. See [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You did not know of the file and enforcement has already started (a lien on your account or salary): you may need an urgent request to stay proceedings or to set aside the underlying judgment.
- The file is on a bill or fixed-sum claim you dispute: the opposition becomes a court case.
- Your total debts are large or growing faster than you can pay: get advice on insolvency before you agree to a payment order you cannot keep.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Counting from the date on the letter instead of the service date | Late request; or panic before the period starts | Recount from service; file immediately | Write the service date on the warning the day it arrives |
| Sending a letter "I cannot pay" with no documents | Request not accepted (s. 7א(א1)) | Refile with the statement, documents and waiver | Use form 21 and the document list |
| Ignoring the warning | Deemed an evader (s. 67(ד)); restrictions possible | Appear now for a means inquiry or file a payment-order request | Act in the warning period |
| Arguing in the office that the judgment was wrong | Rejected: no jurisdiction over the merits | Apply to the court that gave it (set aside, appeal) | Separate "I don't owe it" from "I can't pay it" |
| Appealing an office act directly to court | Wrong forum | File an objection to the registrar | Act of office → objection; registrar's decision → appeal |

## Review

- The registrar decides; the office acts. Acts are challenged by objection, decisions by appeal (s. 80).
- The warning starts the debtor's clocks from the date of service; filing anything counts as full service.
- Four answers: pay, payment order with documents and waiver, notice of inability (insolvency route), means inquiry.
- Doing nothing makes you a deemed evader and opens the way to restrictions.
