# 29.6 · Consumer protection: misleading, unfair practices and enforcement

By the end of this lesson you will be able to match what a business did to the right section of the Consumer Protection Law, choose between a complaint to the regulator, your own claim and a class action, and know which of your claims can carry statutory damages without proof of loss. [Lesson 20.7](../module-20/lesson-07.md) covered distance sales, cancellation and standard terms; [lesson 12.4](../module-12/lesson-04.md) the practical complaint route. This lesson is about the Law's structure, its enforcement machinery and the remedies that make a consumer claim worth bringing.

## Why it matters

The Consumer Protection Law 1981 works on three levels at once. It is a code of conduct for dealers (no misleading, no unfair influence, disclosure duties, price display, cancellation rights). It is a regulatory statute: an Authority with inspectors, financial sanctions and criminal prosecution. And it is a source of private rights: every breach of its conduct chapters is a tort (s. 31), a misleading transaction may be cancelled (s. 32), and a list of common breaches carries exemplary damages without proof of loss (s. 31א). The levels interact badly for the unprepared consumer: the regulator does not get your money back, a tort claim for misleading needs proof that you were misled and harmed (Barazani), and exemplary damages are available only for listed breaches and only after a written request. Using the right lever is most of the work.

## The law

### Misleading (s. 2)

Section 2(א) forbids any act or omission, before or after the deal, liable to mislead a consumer on a material matter, and lists matters deemed material: quality, quantity, kind, origin, maker, price, delivery date and place, use, risks, warranty and more ([lesson 20.7](../module-20/lesson-07.md) quotes it). Three points matter in practice. The test is "liable to mislead": no actual victim is needed for the prohibition (Barazani). Omission counts, so a silent fine-print condition that changes the deal can mislead. And s. 2(ב1)–(ב2) forbid signs that disclaim liability for bodily harm or announce "no refunds" without the required qualification.

### Unfair influence (s. 3)

> לא יעשה עוסק, במעשה או במחדל [...] דבר העלול לפגוע ביכולתו של צרכן לקבל החלטה אם להתקשר בעסקה עמו, באופן שיש בו שלילה של חופש ההתקשרות של הצרכן או פגיעה מהותית בחופש ההתקשרות שלו [...]

*Gist:* A dealer may not do anything liable to impair a consumer's ability to decide whether to contract, in a way that negates or materially harms his freedom to contract. [s. 3(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_הצרכן#סעיף_3) Deemed unfair influence (s. 3(ב)): restricting a consumer's ability to leave; preventing him from consulting; repeated home visits or calls after he asked to stop; exploiting a mental, intellectual or physical disability or ignorance of the language; threats; supplying goods or services for payment without an explicit request; a false impression of a prize; and, in most places other than restaurants, forbidding the consumer to bring food or drink of the kind sold there. In a declared emergency the Minister may also treat an exceptional price rise on essential goods as unfair influence (s. 3א).

### Disclosure (s. 4)

A dealer must disclose any defect, inferior quality or other feature known to him that significantly reduces the value of the goods, any feature requiring special handling to prevent harm, and details set by regulations (s. 4(א)); for example, the Used Car Trade Regulations 1978 set what a used-car dealer must disclose.

### Enforcement by the Authority

The Government appoints a Commissioner for Consumer Protection and Fair Trade who heads the Authority (ss. 19–19א). The Commissioner supervises the Law, investigates offences and prosecutes, takes administrative enforcement action, and handles complaints that have substance (s. 20). Inspectors may demand documents, take samples and enter business premises (s. 21). For a list of breaches the Commissioner may impose a financial sanction, a fixed sum for a corporation and a lower one for an individual dealer, indexed and increased for continuing and repeated breaches; the dealer may make representations first and appeal to the magistrates' court within a set period (ss. 22ג–22כ). Serious breaches are offences (s. 23). Amounts and periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

Two limits. A complaint to the Authority does not get you a refund: the Authority enforces the law; it does not adjudicate your claim. And it does not stop limitation ([lesson 07.3](../module-07/lesson-03.md)).

### Private remedies

> דין מעשה או מחדל בניגוד לפרקים ב׳, ג׳, ד׳ או ד׳1 כדין עוולה לפי פקודת הנזיקין [...]

*Gist:* Breach of the conduct chapters is a tort; the right belongs to the injured consumer, and to a dealer injured in its business by misleading. Where the conduct harmed or may harm the public and a consumer organisation helped the claimant, the court may add compensation to that organisation, up to four times the claimant's damage or a fixed sum, whichever is higher. [s. 31(א)–(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_הצרכן#סעיף_31)

> נקשרה עסקה בין עוסק לצרכן והעוסק הפר, בקשר לאותה עסקה, הוראה מההוראות המפורטות להלן, רשאי בית המשפט לפסוק, בשל אותה הפרה, פיצויים שאינם תלויים בנזק [...] בסכום שלא יעלה על 10,000 שקלים חדשים:

*Gist:* For a listed breach in a concluded transaction the court may award exemplary damages not dependent on damage up to a ceiling, and up to a higher ceiling for a repeated breach, a continuing breach (after a regulator's warning or a court order) or aggravated circumstances. [s. 31א(א), (ג)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_הצרכן#סעיף_31א) The amounts are written in the section itself, with no indexation clause (unlike s. 31(ב)); see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

The list is closed. It covers: refusing to refund under the posted (or missing) return policy (s. 4ג); misleading about the delivery place and not delivering there; continuing to charge after a fixed-term deal ended or after a continuing deal was cancelled; not refunding an overcharge (s. 13ד1); charging a fee for paying by a permitted method (s. 13ח); not refunding after cancellation of a door-to-door, distance, timeshare or medical-services deal; refusing to sell at the shelf price (s. 17ב(ד)); refusing to accept shekels at the set rate; not giving a warranty certificate; and not repairing or supplying parts under warranty in time (s. 18א). Misleading under s. 2 in general is **not** on the list: for it, the remedies are tort damages (with causation) and cancellation (s. 32). A written request to the dealer must come first (s. 31א(ב)). In setting the amount the court considers enforcement and deterrence, encouraging consumers to claim, the gravity, scope and circumstances, the value of the deal, the dealer's size and the criminal fine, and not the consumer's actual damage (s. 31א(ה)).

### Cancellation for misleading (s. 32)

A consumer may cancel within a reasonable time of discovering material misleading or exploitation, even if someone other than the dealer misled him; the dealer refunds in the way the consumer paid within a short period, and the consumer returns the goods where he received them; the dealer may still claim for a significant deterioration in their value (s. 32(ב1)).

## Leading cases

### ע״א 10085/08 תנובה – מרכז שיתופי נ׳ עיזבון ראבי (Tnuva Central Cooperative v. Raabi Estate, 4.12.2011)

Tnuva added silicone to about 13 million litres of long-life milk to solve a frothing problem, without listing it on the label, in breach of a binding standard. A consumer's class action was certified and the District Court awarded ₪55 million. The Supreme Court (Hayut J., Amit and Vogelman JJ. concurring) held that the omission was misleading, that in a class action causation may be established at the group level without proving each member's reliance, and that harm to a consumer's autonomy to choose what he eats is compensable non-pecuniary damage even without bodily harm; it reduced the award to about ₪38.5 million (₪250 per member for roughly 154,000 members who were upset, excluding those indifferent), paid as benefits to the group and the public. Why it matters: for individual claims Barazani still requires personal causation, but Tnuva shows how consumer harm is valued and why class actions are the main private enforcement tool for small, widespread misleading. [Versa (Cardozo)](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/tnuva-central-cooperative-v-raabi-estate)

### דנ״א 5712/01 ברזני נ׳ בזק חברה ישראלית לתקשורת בע״מ, פ״ד נז(6) 385 (2003)

Briefed in [lesson 20.7](../module-20/lesson-07.md): misleading is prohibited even if no one was misled, but a personal tort claim under s. 31 requires that the claimant was misled and harmed. Read it with Tnuva to see the gap that class actions and s. 31א fill. [Versa (Cardozo)](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/barazani-v-bezeq)

## How it works in practice

### Choosing the lever

| Situation | Best lever | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A refund refused after a lawful cancellation, charging after cancellation, shelf price refused, warranty repair refused | Written request, then small claim with exemplary damages (s. 31א) | Listed breach; no need to prove loss |
| You bought because of a false ad and lost money | Cancellation (s. 32) and/or tort damages (s. 31) | Not on the s. 31א list; prove reliance and loss |
| A misleading practice that cost you little but affects many | Complaint to the Authority; consumer organisation; class action | Small individual loss; public harm |
| High-pressure sale at home, repeated calls after "stop" | Cancellation; complaint to the Authority (s. 3) | Unfair influence; regulator can sanction |
| Spam texts or emails advertising without consent | Small claim under the Communications Law s. 30א | Statutory damages per message, separate regime |

### What the Authority does with your complaint

It checks whether the complaint has substance, may ask the dealer for a response, and uses the pattern of complaints for inspections, sanctions and prosecutions. Expect a general reply, not a refund order. File online through gov.il (⚠️ manual check of the current service), and keep the reference number: a pattern of complaints and any warning to the dealer may later make a breach "continuing" for s. 31א(ג).

## Worked example

Noa Peretz and Itai Ben-Shimon, a couple on a kibbutz in the Jezreel Valley, ordered a sofa online for ₪7,800 after an ad promising "delivery to your door within 14 days, free". The dealer delivered after five weeks, only to the kibbutz gate, and asked ₪350 to carry it in. When they cancelled the order of a matching armchair the next day, the dealer kept charging their card for the armchair's instalments for three months.

1. **Delivery place.** The ad misled about the place of delivery (s. 2(א)); they asked in writing for delivery to the door and the dealer did not deliver there. That is on the list (s. 31א(א)(1א)).
2. **Charging after cancellation.** The armchair cancellation was a distance-sale cancellation (s. 14ג); not refunding or cancelling the charge is listed (s. 31א(א)(4)).
3. **Delivery date.** The late delivery is misleading about the delivery date, but not a listed breach: for it they claim actual damage (the ₪350, the time off work) under s. 31, showing they relied on the ad.
4. **Request first.** They send one written request covering both listed breaches (s. 31א(ב)), with a 14-day deadline.
5. **Claim.** In small claims: refund of the armchair charges, ₪350 and proven damage, and exemplary damages for the two listed breaches. They argue the s. 31א(ה) factors: a large online dealer, a common practice, the ratio of the charge to the deal.
6. **Regulator.** They also complain to the Authority, attaching the ad, as the practice affects other buyers.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A consumer problem of your own (a refund refused, a charge after cancellation, a misleading ad you relied on), or the supplied case: Ibrahim Saleh of Shefa-'Amr was refused the shelf price of ₪89 at the till, where the price was ₪129, and the cashier said "the system decides".
- The Law, ss. 2–4, 31–32, and the list in s. 31א(א).
- 40 minutes.

### Steps

1. Write the facts as dated events, each with the evidence (photo, screenshot, receipt, card statement).
2. For each event, name the section breached and whether it is on the s. 31א list.
3. For listed breaches, draft the written request on the [consumer written request for exemplary damages](../../templates/consumer-exemplary-damages-request.md).
4. For non-listed misleading, write two lines on reliance and actual loss.
5. Decide whether to complain to the Authority and write the three facts you would give it.
6. Write a one-paragraph argument on the amount of exemplary damages using the s. 31א(ה) factors.

### Template

[Consumer written request for exemplary damages](../../templates/consumer-exemplary-damages-request.md); [regulator complaint](../../templates/regulator-complaint.md); [small claim for a contract or consumer dispute](../../templates/small-claim-contract-consumer.md).

### How you know it worked

- Every claimed head of exemplary damages points to a paragraph of s. 31א(א).
- The request was sent in writing before the claim, and you kept proof.
- Misleading that is not listed is claimed as damage with causation, not as exemplary damages.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. The written request, the complaint to the Authority and a small claim are designed for consumers acting in their own name; a dealer that is a company may be sued in small claims. A class action is a lawyer's case in practice (module 33). Only bodies listed in the Standard Contracts Law may apply to the Tribunal.

### Where and how

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- Written request: email or registered mail to the dealer; keep proof of sending.
- Authority complaint: online on gov.il (⚠️ manual check).
- Small claim: in the court for the dealer's area or, for internet trade, your area; ceiling and fee in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Free help: Israel Consumer Council (may assist and appear for consumers), Legal Aid (no means test for people 65+ in exploitation-based consumer cases), court information centres.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The same practice harms many people and you want a collective remedy.
- The dealer sues you for defamation over a review or complaint.
- The transaction is large (a car, a renovation, a timeshare) and the dealer has a lawyer.
- The product injured someone: a product-liability or tort claim, not a consumer claim.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Claiming exemplary damages for a misleading ad | Head dismissed: not listed | Claim cancellation and proven damage | Check the s. 31א list first |
| No written request before the claim | Exemplary damages barred (s. 31א(ב)) | Send the request, then amend | Request first, with proof |
| Expecting the Authority to refund you | Months lost | Send the request and sue | Use the Authority for public harm |
| Claiming for an ad you never saw | No causation (Barazani) | Rely on what you saw, or a listed breach | Keep screenshots of what you relied on |
| Not asking for the higher ceiling where the dealer was warned | Lower award | Show the regulator's warning or order (s. 31א(ג)) | Ask the Authority whether it warned the dealer |

## Review

- Misleading (s. 2), unfair influence (s. 3) and disclosure duties (s. 4) are the core conduct rules; breach of them is a tort (s. 31).
- The Authority supervises, sanctions and prosecutes but does not adjudicate individual refunds, and a complaint does not stop limitation.
- Exemplary damages cover a closed list of breaches, require a prior written request, have fixed ceilings and are set by deterrence factors, not by loss.
- Misleading in general gives cancellation (s. 32) and damages with causation (Barazani); Tnuva shows group causation and autonomy harm in class actions.
- Spam advertising is a separate regime with its own statutory damages (Communications Law s. 30א).
