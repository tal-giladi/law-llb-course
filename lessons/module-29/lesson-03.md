# 29.3 · Bounced cheques and the restricted-account rules

By the end of this lesson you will be able to tell from your bank's letters how close you are to restriction, act in the hours that can still save a cheque, appeal a wrong restriction to the magistrates' court within the short period, and, on the other side, turn a bounced cheque you received into money. The Cheques Without Cover Law is administrative, not criminal: it does not punish bouncing a cheque, but it can shut you out of cheque banking for a year or more, and that matters to anyone who rents, runs a small business or pays by post-dated cheques.

## Why it matters

A restriction (הגבלה) follows automatically from a count of refused cheques. The bank does not weigh your reasons; it counts. A restricted customer cannot open an account or draw cheques for a year, and an aggravated-restricted customer is cut off from cheques on all his accounts for two years, with his name possibly published by the Bank of Israel. Restriction also follows from execution proceedings and insolvency ([lesson 28.2](../module-28/lesson-02.md)). The Law gives the customer two lifelines: a notice on the day a cheque cannot be covered, and an appeal on limited grounds. Both are lost by delay measured in hours and days.

## The law

### What counts as a refused cheque

> ”שיק שסורב“ – שיק שהוצג לפרעון בתאריך הנקוב בו או אחריו, והבנק סירב לפרעו מחמת שלא היתה יתרה מספקת בחשבון והוא לא היה חייב לפרעו מכוח הסכם עם המושך, ואין נפקא מינה אם היתה סיבה נוספת לסירוב או אם ניתנה הוראת ביטול;

*Gist:* A cheque counts as refused when it was presented on or after its date and the bank refused it for lack of sufficient balance (and had no agreement obliging it to pay), even if there was another reason as well, including a stop order. [s. 1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_שיקים_ללא_כיסוי#סעיף_1) A stopped cheque in a covered account does not count; a stopped cheque in an overdrawn account does. A cheque refused because of an attachment on the account does not count if presented within a set period of the bank receiving the attachment notice (s. 2א).

### The count

> חשבון יהיה מוגבל [...] ובעליו יהיה מוגבל [...] אם סורבו במשך שנים עשר חדשים עשרה שיקים או יותר שנמשכו על החשבון, ובלבד שעברו לפחות חמישה עשר ימים בין הסירוב הראשון לסירוב האחרון.

*Gist:* An account and its owner become restricted when a set number of cheques drawn on it are refused within twelve months, provided a minimum number of days separates the first refusal from the last. [s. 2(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_שיקים_ללא_כיסוי#סעיף_2) The minimum-days rule means that a single bad day, with many cheques refused together, does not restrict on its own. Count and days: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

- **Warning.** After the fifth refused cheque the bank sends a warning to every owner, attorney and authorised signatory within a few business days (s. 2(א1); regs 6–7). A missing or defective warning is **not** a ground of appeal if the bank sent it (s. 10(ב)).
- **Same-day notice.** When a cheque is presented and the balance cannot cover it, the bank must notify the account owner as early as possible so he can deposit money until two and a half hours before the end of the business day (the "last time"); if he does not, the cheque counts as refused (s. 7א).
- **Restriction notice and start.** The bank sends a restriction notice within a few business days of the last refused cheque, and the restriction starts on the fifteenth day after the notice was sent (s. 3ב(ג); regs 8–9). The bank may still pay cheques dated before the start for a short period (s. 6(א)).

### Kinds and periods of restriction

| Status | When | Period | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Restricted account / customer | The s. 2(א) count | 1 year (s. 3ב(א)) | No cheques on that account; may not open an account (s. 4(א)–(ב)) |
| Aggravated-restricted (מוגבל חמור) | A restricted customer whose further account is restricted, or a second restriction within 3 years after the first ended (s. 3) | 2 years (s. 3ב(ב)) | All his accounts restricted, including joint ones; may not draw any cheque (ss. 3(ב), 4(ג)) |
| Special-restricted (מוגבל מיוחד) | Restriction imposed by the execution registrar, the Fines Collection Center, a bankruptcy court or a rabbinical court (s. 3ג) | As set by that authority, up to 5 years; default 1 year | As aggravated, except publication of name |

Drawing a cheque on a restricted account while restricted is an offence (s. 16(א)). The holder's rights on the cheque are not affected by non-payment under this Law (s. 6(ב)).

### Appeal to the magistrates' court

> לקוח מוגבל או לקוח מוגבל חמור, רשאים לבקש מבית משפט השלום שיבטל הבאת שיק במנין השיקים שסורבו בהתקיים אחת מאלה: (1) הבנק סירב לפרוע את השיק מחמת טעות; [...] (3) ללקוח היה יסוד סביר להניח שהיתה חובה על הבנק לפרוע את השיק [...]

*Gist:* A restricted customer may ask the magistrates' court to remove a cheque from the count when the bank refused it by mistake; when it was refused because of an attachment and was drawn before the attachment notice; when the customer reasonably believed the bank had to pay (sufficient balance or a credit agreement); when hostile action against him or his property prevented him from handling his affairs; when he lives in an area under a declared emergency and his income fell significantly between drawing and presentment; or when the bank failed to send the same-day notice of s. 7א, sent it to wrong contact details, or the last time had not passed. [s. 10(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_שיקים_ללא_כיסוי#סעיף_10) For the s. 7א ground the court must be persuaded that the customer could have deposited the money (s. 10(א1)).

> הבקשה תוגש תוך עשרים ימים מיום ההמצאה של הודעה לפי סעיף 3א(א) לחוק [...]

*Gist:* The appeal must be filed within a short period from service of the bank's restriction notice; if the customer also received the Supervisor's notice of aggravated restriction, he may file within that period or within a shorter period from service of the Supervisor's notice, whichever ends later. [Procedure Regulations reg. 5](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיקים_ללא_כיסוי_%28סדרי_דין%29#סעיף_5) It is filed on form 1 in the magistrates' court nearest the branch (reg. 1), against the bank (reg. 3), with a detailed affidavit, copies of the evidence, and a copy of your prior request to the bank to remove the cheque from the count and the bank's answer (reg. 4), and with a deposit for costs (reg. 17). The court may order that the restriction not start meanwhile (s. 10א; reg. 8). Periods and the deposit: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### Emergencies

The Minister may exempt cheques from the count in emergencies (s. 21(ב)), and the Supervisor of Banks may suspend restrictions for up to 90 days (s. 2(א2)). Under the Swords of Iron regulations of 2023, cheques refused from 7 to 31 October 2023 were removed from the count for everyone, and cheques refused in a second period until 21 January 2024 were removed for residents of listed communities, people missing, abducted or held captive, reservists in emergency service, people hospitalised from hostile action, and their relatives, on a declaration to the bank. Restrictions based on such cheques are void. [Swords of Iron regulations](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיקים_ללא_כיסוי_%28סייגים_לתחולת_החוק%29_%28חרבות_ברזל%29_%28הוראת_שעה%29) Check the current regulations whenever a new emergency is declared.

### The holder's side

> אוחז בשיק שלא נפרע מכל סיבה שהיא, ימסור לו הבנק הנמשך, לפי בקשתו, פרטי זיהוי של המושך או של בעל החשבון כפי שקבע השר בתקנות.

*Gist:* A holder of an unpaid cheque, whatever the reason, is entitled on request to the identity details of the drawer or account owner from the drawee bank. [s. 12](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_שיקים_ללא_כיסוי#סעיף_12) The branch gives name, ID number and registered address of the owner, and of the attorney or signatory who signed (reg. 5).

## Leading cases

### Key text: the automatic count and its narrow exceptions (ss. 1, 2, 10)

The Law is designed to be mechanical: the definition in s. 1 counts a cheque even if a stop order was given, and the closed list of grounds in s. 10(א) leaves no general "fairness" exception. The 2020 amendment added the same-day notice (s. 7א) and the matching ground in s. 10(א)(6), shifting part of the risk to banks that fail to warn. Magistrates' courts decide most appeals, and few rulings reach the Supreme Court; we could not verify a free leading appellate ruling, so the statute carries the doctrine. Free text: [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_שיקים_ללא_כיסוי).

### Key text: how the defences on the cheque relate (פקודת השטרות s. 37; חוק שיקים ללא כיסוי s. 6(ב))

Restriction and liability on the cheque are separate. Getting a cheque removed from the count does not discharge the cheque, and a valid defence on the cheque (lesson 29.2) does not remove it from the count. A customer who stops a cheque for a good reason should make sure the account is covered so that the refusal is for the stop order only.

## How it works in practice

### Drawer: the day a cheque cannot be covered

1. Your bank's app or SMS gives the same-day notice (s. 7א). Deposit enough before the last time stated, or transfer from another account.
2. If you cannot, call the payee and ask him not to re-present until a set date; a cheque presented again and refused counts again.
3. Keep every bank message with its time stamp: the appeal ground in s. 10(א)(6) depends on what the bank sent and when.

### Drawer: after the fifth refused cheque

Ask the branch, in writing, for the list of refused cheques and dates, check each against the definition, and challenge any mistake with the bank at once (the bank must correct an erroneous restriction and tell the Bank of Israel, s. 9). The Bank of Israel publishes restricted account numbers (s. 14(א)); you can check an account online (⚠️ manual check of the current Bank of Israel service).

### Appeal checklist

- Bank's restriction notice and the date you received it; compute the last day.
- Your written request to the bank to remove the cheque and its answer (or proof that you asked).
- Form 1, an affidavit covering every fact, the evidence (statements showing the balance or credit line, the bank's messages, proof of hostile action or reserve service), the deposit and the court fee.
- If the account is joint, serve the co-owners with form 2 (reg. 10).

### Holder: a cheque bounced on you

1. Ask the presenting bank for the printout of the refused scanned cheque (Cheque Clearing Law s. 2(ג)) and the drawee bank for the drawer's details (s. 12).
2. Send the drawer a short demand with a deadline; if he says the deal failed, you are his immediate party and the merits will be argued (lesson 29.2).
3. Choose: open an execution file on the cheque (Execution Law s. 81א; [lesson 28.1](../module-28/lesson-01.md)), or sue in small claims if you are an individual and expect a dispute. Interest on a bill in execution runs under s. 81א3.

## Worked example

Moshe Friedman runs a small print shop in Beitar Illit. In March and April eight of his cheques were refused. On 2 May his supplier presented a ₪4,000 cheque; Moshe's credit line had been cut a week earlier without notice, and the bank's same-day SMS went to a phone number he had replaced in January after notifying the branch in writing. The cheque was refused, as was a ₪900 cheque on 20 May. On 24 May he received a restriction notice.

1. **Count.** Ten refused cheques within twelve months, with more than the minimum days between the first and the last: the conditions of s. 2(א) are met.
2. **Grounds.** The 2 May cheque: the bank's notice went to contact details that did not match what Moshe gave (s. 10(א)(6)); and he had reasonable grounds to think the credit line covered it, since it was cut without notice (s. 10(א)(3)). For the s. 7א ground he must show he could have deposited ₪4,000 that day (s. 10(א1)): his customer's transfer was in his other account.
3. **Effect.** Removing one cheque leaves nine: no restriction.
4. **Steps.** On 25 May he writes to the branch asking to remove the cheque from the count, attaching his January letter. On 28 May the bank refuses. He files form 1 in the magistrates' court nearest the branch before the reg. 5 deadline, with an affidavit, the January letter, the credit-line documents, the other account's statement, the bank's refusal, and the deposit, and asks for a s. 10א order that the restriction not start.
5. **Meanwhile.** He pays the supplier by bank transfer and asks it in writing not to re-present the cheque.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your bank's tariff and the last 12 months of account statements, or the supplied case: Leila Nassar of Umm al-Fahm, a reservist's spouse, whose seven cheques were refused from November 2023 to June 2024, three of them in October and November 2023.
- The Law, ss. 1–3ב, 7א, 10; the Procedure Regulations, regs 1–5, 17; the Swords of Iron regulations.
- 50 minutes.

### Steps

1. List every refused cheque in the last twelve months: date, sum, reason given by the bank.
2. Strike out any cheque that does not meet the s. 1 definition (refused for a stop order with sufficient balance; refused for attachment within the s. 2א window) and any removed by emergency regulations.
3. Count the rest and check the days between the first and last refusal. Write whether you are restricted, at warning stage or clear.
4. For each remaining cheque, test the six grounds of s. 10(א) and note the evidence you would need.
5. Write the letter to the bank asking it to remove a cheque from the count (required as an attachment, reg. 4(3)).
6. Fill in the [bounced-cheque response kit](../../templates/bounced-cheque-response-kit.md), part B (appeal outline), with your facts and compute the last filing day.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. The appeal is filed by the restricted customer himself in the magistrates' court; an individual may appear in person. A company account holder normally needs a lawyer in court. Asking the bank to correct the count, and complaining to the Bank of Israel, are free and need no lawyer.

### Where and how

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- Request to the bank: in writing to the branch and to the bank's public-enquiries officer.
- Appeal: form 1, the magistrates' court nearest the branch (reg. 1), through Net HaMishpat or the court registry; deposit and court fee in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); the period runs from service of the notice.
- Complaint: Bank of Israel Banking Supervision public-enquiries unit ([lesson 29.5](lesson-05.md)); it does not extend the appeal period (Banking (Service to Customer) Law s. 16(ד)).
- Free help: court information centres, Kol-Zchut on restricted accounts (⚠️ manual check), Legal Aid where eligible.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You are aggravated-restricted and your business depends on cheques.
- The restriction comes from an execution or insolvency authority: the remedy is there, not in a s. 10 appeal ([lesson 28.3](../module-28/lesson-03.md)).
- You are accused of drawing cheques on a restricted account (s. 16) or of fraud.
- The bank cut your credit line and you want damages, not only removal from the count.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stopping a cheque in an overdrawn account | It counts as refused | Cover the account and ask the payee not to re-present | Cover the account whenever you stop a cheque |
| Ignoring the same-day SMS | Cheque refused, count rises | Appeal only if the bank's notice was defective | Keep contact details current; act before the last time |
| Appealing on "the warning never arrived" | Not a ground (s. 10(ב)) | Look for a real s. 10(א) ground | Read the closed list |
| Filing after the period | Appeal dismissed | Ask for an extension with reasons at once | Diary the period on receipt of the notice |
| No prior request to the bank attached | Defective filing (reg. 4(3)) | Ask the bank now and attach its answer | Ask the bank first, in writing |

## Review

- A cheque counts if refused for lack of balance, even with a stop order; restriction follows a set count over twelve months with a minimum spread of days.
- The bank must warn after the fifth refusal and notify on the day a cheque cannot be covered, giving time to deposit.
- Restriction lasts one year; aggravated two years on all accounts; special restriction comes from execution, fines, insolvency or rabbinical courts.
- The s. 10 appeal has a closed list of grounds, a short period from the bank's notice, and requires a prior request to the bank, an affidavit and a deposit.
- The holder may get the drawer's details and a printout of the refused cheque, then execute or sue.
