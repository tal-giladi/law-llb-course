# 29.4 · Pledges and liens: the Pledges Law and the registrar

By the end of this lesson you will be able to run a pledges search before you buy a car or equipment, read what it shows, get a pledge removed after you pay off a loan, and explain to a lender or an execution registrar how a pledge ranks and how it may be realised. [Lesson 13.8](../module-13/lesson-08.md) introduced the pledge and the lien as property rights; this lesson takes the creditor's and the registrar's view and adds the procedure.

## Why it matters

Most cars bought on credit in Israel carry a pledge in favour of a bank, a credit-card company or a car-finance company, registered at the Registrar of Pledges (רשם המשכונות). A buyer who pays the seller and drives away may find the lender realising the car through the Execution Office, because a registered pledge binds everyone. Small businesses pledge equipment, deposits and receivables; individuals pledge savings plans and securities to banks. The Pledges Law is short, but its rules on perfection, priority and realisation decide who is paid when the debtor fails, and its registration system is the only public record of security over movables.

## The law

### Substance over form

> הוראות חוק זה יחולו על כל עסקה שכוונתה שעבוד נכס כערובה לחיוב, יהא כינויה של העסקה אשר יהא.

*Gist:* The Law applies to every transaction whose purpose is to charge an asset as security for a debt, whatever it is called. [s. 2(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המשכון#סעיף_2) A "sale" of a car to the lender with a right to buy it back, or a transfer of rights "in trust" to secure a loan, may be a pledge in disguise and then needs registration to bind other creditors. The security may cover a present or future, renewing, conditional, fixed or unfixed debt (s. 1(ב)), and extends to interest, costs and realisation expenses unless agreed otherwise (s. 7(א)); but if the debt is later increased by agreement, the pledge does not cover the increase without a new pledge (s. 7(ב)).

### Perfection: effective against other creditors

> כלפי נושים אחרים של החייב יהיה כוחו של מישכון יפה – [...] (3) בנכסים נדים ובניירות ערך שלא הופקדו כאמור ובכל מקרה אחר – עם רישום המישכון בהתאם לתקנות שהותקנו לפי חוק זה, אולם כלפי נושה שידע או היה עליו לדעת על המישכון יהיה כוחו של המישכון יפה אף ללא רישום.

*Gist:* Against other creditors (including a trustee or liquidator, s. 24) a pledge of movables or securities is effective from deposit with the creditor, or from registration, and without registration only against a creditor who knew or should have known of it. [s. 4](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המשכון#סעיף_4) Assets with their own register follow it (s. 4(1)): land (mortgage at the Land Registry), company charges (Companies Registrar), ships and aircraft.

### Priority and further pledges

> החייב רשאי לשעבד את הנכס הממושכן במישכון נוסף בלי נטילת רשות מאת הנושה; אולם נושה נוסף לא יוכל להיפרע מן המשכון אלא לאחר שסולק החיוב שהובטח במישכון שלפניו; והכל באין קביעה אחרת בהסכם המישכון הקודם.

*Gist:* A second pledge needs no consent of the first pledgee, but ranks after it unless the first pledge agreement provides otherwise; with the first pledgee's consent, a later pledge may rank equal or ahead. [s. 6](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המשכון#סעיף_6) In practice most bank pledge agreements forbid further pledges without consent; a breach is a breach of contract, but the Law does not void the second pledge. A bank may not refuse a further pledge unreasonably (Banking (Service to Customer) Law s. 7ח).

### Redemption, expiry and release

- The debtor, and anyone whose rights may be harmed, may redeem after the due date by paying, whatever the agreement says (s. 13(א)); before the due date too, but on an interest-bearing debt he pays the interest until the due date or for a limited period after payment, whichever is shorter, unless the agreement says otherwise (s. 13(ב)).
- When the debt ends, the pledge ends, and the debtor may demand the return of deposited goods or the deletion of the registration (s. 15).
- A bank that has been fully paid by the customer himself must cancel its charges and, for a registered pledge or mortgage, file the deletion notice within a set period and at its own cost ([Banking (Service to Customer) Law s. 9א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הבנקאות_%28שירות_ללקוח%29#סעיף_9א)). For a renewing obligation (a credit line) the customer must ask for cancellation.

### Realisation

> מימוש המשכון יהיה על פי צו בית המשפט, אולם – [...] (2) במשכון שהופקד [...] או שנרשם [...] יכול שהמימוש יהיה על פי צו ראש ההוצאה לפועל; (3) במשכון שהופקד [...] והוא משמש ערובה לחיוב המגיע לגוף מוסדי, וכן במשכון ניירות ערך, יכול שהמימוש יהיה על ידי הגוף המוסדי עצמו ללא צו כאמור;

*Gist:* A pledge is realised by court order; a registered or deposited pledge may be realised through the Execution Office; and a deposited pledge held by an institutional body (bank, insurer, provident-fund manager, stock-exchange member), or a pledge of listed securities, may be realised by that body itself without an order. [s. 17](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המשכון#סעיף_17) Self-realisation must be by a commercially reasonable sale after reasonable advance notice to the debtor and others affected, and the body is liable for damage from improper realisation (s. 19). The parties may not agree on the method of realisation before the debt is due (s. 16(ב)): a clause signed with the loan that lets the lender "take the car and sell it as it sees fit" is ineffective. Execution realisation follows the rules for attached assets (s. 18). For a mortgage or pledge on a residential apartment of an individual, the creditor must wait six months from the first missed payment and the debtor has further protections ([Execution Law s. 81ב1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_81ב1); [lesson 28.2](../module-28/lesson-02.md)). The creditor may also sue for the debt instead of realising, and for any shortfall after realisation (s. 23).

### The register: what you can see

> אדם רשאי לעיין במאגר באמצעות האינטרנט ולהפיק דוח עיון או נסח עיון; העיון ייעשה לפי חתך חיפוש שבחר מבקש העיון [...] (ב) נסח עיון או דוח עיון שהופק באמצעות האינטרנט אינו מהווה דוח רישום או נסח רישום [...]

*Gist:* Anyone may search the pledges database online, by licence number, the debtor's ID number (with or without name) or the pledge registration number, and print a search report (current pledges) or a search extract (cancelled pledges); an online report is not an official registration report, which you get at a registration office. [regs 13–13א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_המשכון_%28סדרי_רישום_ועיון%29#סעיף_13א) Each search criterion is a separate search and a separate fee (reg. 13(א), Second Schedule); fees are indexed every January ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).

### Reform: still pending

The Ministry of Justice published a memorandum for a new Pledges Law in 2010, and a government bill passed a first reading in 2012 and again in July 2015; among other things it would have let individuals pledge future assets, which drew criticism. As of October 2026 the Wikisource consolidated text shows no new law; the 1967 Law and the 1994 regulations (last amended 2023, with online filing) apply. Treat any "new pledges law" you read about as a proposal until you see it in Reshumot. (⚠️ We found no 2024–2026 enactment; check the Knesset database before relying on this.)

### Liens

A lien is created by law, not agreement: a garage keeping a car it repaired, a contractor keeping goods it worked on. Its source and limits are in [lesson 13.8](../module-13/lesson-08.md) (Movable Property Law s. 11). A lien is lost when the holder gives up possession voluntarily; it is not registered, so a pledges search will not show it.

## Leading cases

### ע״א 2833/04 רגיס בע״מ נ׳ טרבלסי, נאמן לנכסי דן רולידר בע״מ (3.8.2009)

A seller of heavy engineering equipment took a charge from the buyer company but did not register it with the Companies Registrar within the statutory period. After a liquidation application was filed, the seller obtained an extension from the Registrar and registered. The Supreme Court (Naor J., Arbel and Rubinstein JJ. concurring) held that a charge registered late, after the liquidation process began, cannot be validated against the liquidator and other creditors, because their rights crystallise at that point; the seller's extension application, which did not mention the pending liquidation, also undermined its good faith. Why it matters: registration is a race; the register protects creditors who rely on it, and a secured creditor who sleeps becomes an unsecured one when insolvency arrives. [Versa (Cardozo)](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/regis-ltd-v-trabelsi)

### Key text: the prohibition on agreeing realisation in advance (חוק המשכון s. 16(ב))

Section 16(ב) is mandatory: it protects the debtor at the moment he is weakest, when he signs the loan. Together with s. 13(א) (redemption cannot be excluded after the due date) and s. 19 (commercially reasonable sale, notice and liability), it is the Law's debtor-protection core. On its plain words, a clause signed with the loan that lets a lender seize and sell without an order, outside the cases in s. 17(3), cannot bind the debtor. We could not verify a free appellate ruling applying s. 16(ב), so none is briefed. [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המשכון#סעיף_16)

## How it works in practice

### Searching before you buy a car

1. Get the licence number and the seller's ID number from the vehicle licence and the seller's ID card.
2. Search the pledges database online by licence number and, separately, by the seller's ID (the Ministry of Justice service on gov.il; ⚠️ manual check of the current address). The online search costs a small indexed fee per criterion ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
3. Read the report: pledge number, creditor, debtor, asset, registration date and period.
4. Check the vehicle licence and the Transport Ministry's vehicle record for other restrictions such as execution attachments (⚠️ manual check of the current service); attachments are not pledges and may not appear in the pledges database.
5. If a pledge appears: do not pay the seller. Ask the lender for a payoff letter (מכתב סילוק), pay the lender directly from the purchase price, and get the cancellation confirmation; then pay the seller the balance.
6. Repeat the search on the day of transfer; a pledge registered in between binds you.

### Getting your own pledge removed

After the last payment, ask the lender in writing for written confirmation and deletion. A bank must file the deletion itself within the period in s. 9א; a non-bank lender must do so on your demand (s. 15(ב)). If it refuses, you may apply to the registrar with proof of payment or to court.

### Registering a pledge (as a private lender)

An individual lending money against a car or equipment may register a pledge himself: a pledge notice on form 1 signed by both parties, filed online or at a registration office, with a fee per year of registration for up to five years ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)). Registration has a stated period; check whether you must extend it before it lapses (⚠️ manual check of the gov.il extension service).

## Worked example

Olga Kuznetsov of Ashdod agrees to buy a 2021 hatchback for ₪62,000 from a private seller she found online. The seller says the car is "clean".

1. **Search.** She searches by licence number: one current pledge, registered in 2022 in favour of a car-finance company, debtor: the seller. She also searches by the seller's ID: a second pledge on a motorcycle, irrelevant to her.
2. **Effect.** The pledge is registered, so it binds her as a later buyer (s. 4(3)); the market-overt rule protects a pledgee, not a buyer from a pledgor ([lesson 13.8](../module-13/lesson-08.md)).
3. **Payoff.** The seller asks the finance company for a payoff letter: ₪21,400 valid for seven days. Olga and the seller sign a short agreement: Olga pays ₪21,400 directly to the finance company and the balance to the seller only after the cancellation appears in the database.
4. **Release.** The finance company files the cancellation; Olga runs the search again (cancelled pledges appear in a search extract, not the current report), then pays the balance and transfers ownership.
5. **What if she had skipped the search?** The finance company could ask the execution registrar to realise the car (s. 17(2)); Olga's remedy would be against the seller.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A car, motorcycle or equipment you own or plan to buy, with its licence number; or the supplied case: Yosef Amsalem of Kiryat Shmona is buying a used tractor from a farm in the Galilee and has its registration number and the seller's company number.
- A payment card for the online search fee (optional: you can stop before paying).
- 40 minutes.

### Steps

1. Open the Ministry of Justice pledges search service and list the search criteria it offers. Note the fee for each.
2. Run (or simulate) a search by licence number. Record what each field of the report means.
3. Write which further search you would run (seller's ID, company number) and why.
4. For a car with a pledge, write the payoff and release sequence as numbered steps, including who pays whom and when.
5. Draft a two-clause addition to a car purchase agreement that protects you against an undisclosed pledge.
6. Fill in the [pledge and vehicle purchase check](../../templates/pledge-and-vehicle-purchase-check.md).

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Searching, registering a pledge in your own loan, demanding release and objecting in the Execution Office to an improper realisation are things an individual does himself. Disputes over priority in an insolvency, and realisation of a company's charges, normally need a lawyer.

### Where and how

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- Search and registration: the Registrar of Pledges at the Israel Corporations Authority, Ministry of Justice; online search and filing on gov.il (⚠️ manual check), or at a registration office; fees in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Release from a bank: written demand to the branch; complaint to the Bank of Israel if it is late ([lesson 29.5](lesson-05.md)).
- Objection to realisation: request to the execution registrar in the file ([lesson 28.3](../module-28/lesson-03.md)).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- A lender has started realising an asset you bought or own.
- The pledgor is in insolvency and several creditors claim the same asset.
- The "sale" you made to a lender is really security and you want it treated as a pledge.
- The asset is a company's, or the pledge is a floating charge.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buying without a pledges search | The car is realised for the seller's debt | Claim from the seller; negotiate with the lender | Search by licence number and seller's ID |
| Paying the seller and trusting him to pay the lender | Pledge stays; seller disappears | Pay the lender directly; demand release | Payoff letter and direct payment |
| Treating the online report as an official certificate | Dispute over what the register showed | Order an official registration report | Print and keep the dated online report; get an official one for large deals |
| Not asking a non-bank lender to delete the pledge after payment | You cannot sell the asset | Written demand under s. 15(ב), then the registrar or court | Ask for deletion with the last payment |
| Signing a clause allowing seizure without an order | The lender tries self-help | Rely on s. 16(ב) and s. 17; report to the regulator | Strike such clauses before signing |

## Review

- Any transaction meant as security is a pledge (s. 2(ב)); registration or deposit makes it effective against other creditors (s. 4).
- A second pledge ranks after the first unless the first pledgee agrees otherwise (s. 6).
- Realisation is by court or Execution Office order, or by an institutional body for deposited pledges and listed securities, with notice and a commercially reasonable sale; the method may not be agreed before the due date.
- The debtor may redeem; on payment the pledge ends and must be deleted, by a bank within a set period at its own cost.
- Search the register online by licence number, ID or pledge number; the online report is not an official certificate. The reform bill is still pending.
