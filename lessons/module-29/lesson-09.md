# 29.9 · Practice lab: a consumer claim with exemplary damages

In this lab you prepare, from written request to hearing notes, a small claim against a business that breached the Consumer Protection Law, with a separate head of exemplary damages for each listed breach. You apply lesson 29.6 and the small-claims technique of [lesson 12.5](../module-12/lesson-05.md); you do not need to file, but everything you write should be ready to file.

## Why it matters

Exemplary damages turn a ₪200 overcharge into a claim worth bringing, and they are why many businesses settle once they receive a well-built request. But courts award them only for listed breaches, only after a written request, and in amounts they set by deterrence factors. A claim that lumps everything into "compensation for aggravation" usually gets the refund and little else. A claim that pleads each breach, each request and each factor gets a real award.

## The law

No new law. You apply Consumer Protection Law ss. 31א(א)–(ה), 31, 32 and the specific duty breached (ss. 13ד, 14ה, 17ב, 18א and others) from [lesson 29.6](lesson-06.md) and [lesson 20.7](../module-20/lesson-07.md); the small-claims ceiling and the individual-plaintiff rule in the Courts Law s. 60 and the bar on lawyers without leave in s. 63 ([lesson 08.3](../module-08/lesson-03.md)); and, if the business is a bank, the Banking (Service to Customer) Law ([lesson 29.5](lesson-05.md)).

## Leading cases

Barazani ([lesson 20.7](../module-20/lesson-07.md)) governs any head of damage for misleading: you must show you saw and relied on what misled you. Tnuva ([lesson 29.6](lesson-06.md)) is a class-action case; do not cite its group causation in an individual small claim, but its reasoning on autonomy harm may support a modest non-pecuniary head.

## How it works in practice

Filing is online through the Judiciary's small-claims service or at the registry, with the fee in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); the claim is limited to the ceiling and to five pages. Only an individual may sue in small claims; the business may be a company. Exemplary damages count toward the ceiling, so price your heads realistically. Expect a hearing within months; settlement offers often come after service.

## Worked example

Hila Goren of Tel Aviv cancelled her home-internet contract by email on 3 March. The provider kept charging ₪119 a month for April, May and June. On 10 June she bought headphones online for ₪399 from a large retailer and cancelled within the period by the website link; the retailer refunded only ₪299, citing "handling", which is more than the lawful fee.

1. **Listed breaches.** (a) Continuing to charge after cancellation of a continuing deal (s. 31א(א)(2ב)); (b) not refunding after cancellation of a distance sale, by keeping more than the lawful fee (s. 31א(א)(4)).
2. **Requests.** For (a) her cancellation notice by email suffices under s. 31א(ב), but she also sends a written request to stop and refund. For (b) she sends a written request on 20 June for the balance. She keeps both emails.
3. **Heads.** Provider: refund ₪357; exemplary damages for one breach. Retailer: refund of the unlawful part of the deduction; exemplary damages for one breach. Two separate claims, one per defendant (or one claim if the court allows joinder; keep them separate for simplicity).
4. **Amount.** She argues the s. 31א(ה) factors: deterrence (continuing charges are a known practice), the ratio of the breach to the deal, the size of the businesses, and that she had to chase them for months; she asks for a sum within the ordinary ceiling and explains why.
5. **Exhibits.** Cancellation email, card statements, the website's cancellation confirmation, the refund record, both requests and any answers.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A consumer dispute of your own with at least one listed breach, or the supplied case: Yasmin Darwish of Jaffa had a washing machine still under warranty; the importer's technician came twice, did not repair it and refused to supply a part "for the next six weeks". Separately, a supermarket refused the shelf price of ₪24.90 on detergent marked at the till as ₪34.90.
- The [consumer written request for exemplary damages](../../templates/consumer-exemplary-damages-request.md), the [small claim for a contract or consumer dispute](../../templates/small-claim-contract-consumer.md), and the [hearing-day kit](../../templates/hearing-day-kit.md).
- About 2½ hours.

### Steps

1. **Facts (20 min).** Write a dated chronology: each event, each payment, each contact, each document.
2. **Classify (15 min).** For each event, name the duty breached (section) and whether it is a listed breach under s. 31א(א), and which paragraph.
3. **Request (20 min).** Write the written request on the template, covering every listed breach; set a 14-day deadline. Note how you will prove you sent it.
4. **Heads (20 min).** Make a table: head, sum, legal basis, evidence. Keep refund, proven damage, non-pecuniary damage and exemplary damages separate; one exemplary head per listed breach.
5. **Amount (15 min).** Write one paragraph per exemplary head using the s. 31א(ה) factors. If the business was warned by the Authority or the breach repeats, explain why the higher ceiling applies (s. 31א(ג)).
6. **Statement of claim (40 min).** Fill the small-claim template, within five pages, every fact with an exhibit number.
7. **Hearing notes (20 min).** On the hearing-day kit, write a two-minute opening, three questions for the business's representative, and your answer to "we offered a voucher".

### Template

[Consumer written request for exemplary damages](../../templates/consumer-exemplary-damages-request.md); [small claim for a contract or consumer dispute](../../templates/small-claim-contract-consumer.md); [hearing-day kit](../../templates/hearing-day-kit.md).

### How you know it worked

- Every exemplary head cites a paragraph of s. 31א(א) and a dated written request.
- Misleading that is not listed appears, if at all, as proven damage with reliance.
- The total is within the small-claims ceiling and each sum is explained.
- Your claim fits in five pages and every fact has an exhibit.

### Self-check

- [ ] My request went out before the claim, and I can prove it.
- [ ] I did not claim exemplary damages for a breach that is not listed.
- [ ] I argued the amount with the statutory factors, not with my aggravation.
- [ ] I know what I will say if the business offers a partial refund at the hearing.

<details><summary>Model answer (Yasmin)</summary>

Warranty: the importer did not repair the machine within the warranty and did not supply a part within the period set under s. 18א: a listed breach (s. 31א(א)(8)). Shelf price: refusing to charge the displayed price is a listed breach (s. 31א(א)(5)). Two written requests: to the importer (repair or replace within a set time, citing s. 18א) and to the supermarket (refund the ₪10 difference, citing s. 17ב(ד)). Two claims, one per defendant. Importer: proven damage (laundromat costs with receipts), a modest non-pecuniary sum, and exemplary damages for the warranty breach, argued from the importer's size, the essential nature of a washing machine and the length of the failure. Supermarket: ₪10 refund and exemplary damages for the shelf-price breach; the amount is argued from deterrence and the chain's size rather than the ₪10, and if the Authority has warned the chain about shelf prices, the higher ceiling for a continuing breach. Exhibits: warranty certificate, technician visit reports, messages, photos of the shelf label and the receipt, both requests.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Small claims exist for this; lawyers appear only with leave (Courts Law s. 63), and a company may be sued but may not sue there.

### Where and how

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- Written request first (email with proof), then the small-claims court for the business's area or, for internet trade, your own area; fee and ceiling in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Free help: Israel Consumer Council, court information centres, Legal Aid (no means test for people 65+ in exploitation-based consumer cases).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The business counter-claims or threatens defamation proceedings.
- The breach affects many consumers and a class action is realistic.
- Your losses exceed the small-claims ceiling.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| One lump sum for "compensation" | Court awards only the refund | Split into heads with bases | Use the heads table |
| No proof the request was sent | Exemplary head dismissed | Find the sent email or receipt | Send by email and keep it |
| Claiming exemplary damages for a non-listed breach | Head dismissed | Plead it as proven damage | Check the s. 31א list |
| Exceeding five pages | The court may require a shorter claim | Cut to facts and exhibits | Write from the chronology |
| Accepting a voucher at the hearing without thinking | Losing the exemplary head | Ask for a short break; weigh the offer | Decide your minimum before the hearing |

## Review

- Each listed breach gets its own written request, head and argument; non-listed misleading is proven damage with reliance.
- The amount of exemplary damages is argued from deterrence, gravity, the deal and the business's size, not from your loss.
- Small claims fit these cases: individual plaintiff, company defendant, short claim, exhibits for every fact.
