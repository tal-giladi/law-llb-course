# 29.7 · Credit and debt: fair-credit rules and collection

By the end of this lesson you will be able to check whether a loan you took (or guaranteed) breaks the Fair Credit Law, read your own credit-data report and fix an error in it, and answer a collection letter in a way that uses every protection the law gives a consumer debtor. [Module 28](../module-28/lesson-01.md) covers what happens once a debt reaches the Execution Office; this lesson is about the credit contract and the stage before execution.

## Why it matters

Israel's consumer-credit market includes banks, credit-card companies, insurers, licensed non-bank lenders, peer-to-peer platforms and private lenders. The Fair Credit Law, first enacted in 1993 as the Non-Bank Loans Law and rewritten by Amendment No. 5 in 2017, now applies to almost all of them: it requires a written contract and full disclosure, caps the real cost of credit and default interest, regulates acceleration and collection costs, and gives the court and the execution registrar power to rewrite unlawful loans. The Credit Data Law 2016 created a central credit-data system at the Bank of Israel from which credit bureaus build reports that lenders use; an error there can block a mortgage. And the Consumer Protection Law adds a simple rule many collectors break: no collection step before a written debt notice and a waiting period.

## The law

### Fair Credit Law: form and disclosure

> חוזה הלוואה בין מלווה ללווה טעון מסמך בכתב; הסכמת הלקוח לכריתת חוזה ההלוואה תינתן במפורש ותתועד בידי המלווה [...]

*Gist:* A loan contract must be in writing and the borrower's consent explicit and documented. [s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_אשראי_הוגן#סעיף_2) Before signing, the lender must give a copy and a reasonable chance to read it, and afterwards a copy of the signed contract (s. 3(א)). The contract must disclose, among other things: the parties and the guarantor; the loan amount and the amount actually received; the annual interest rate including compounding; for variable or linked loans, the base and its components; every other addition, itemised; the real cost of credit, the maximum cost of credit and the maximum default interest that apply; the schedule of payments; the steps the lender may take on default and the conditions for acceleration; the default interest rate; early-repayment terms; and the lender's regulator (s. 3(ב)). Breach of disclosure lets the borrower use the Contracts Law remedies for bad faith in negotiation and mistake (s. 4).

### The caps

> לא יכרות מלווה חוזה הלוואה שבו שיעור העלות הממשית של האשראי הידוע במועד כריתת החוזה עולה על שיעור העלות המרבית של האשראי הידוע באותו מועד, ולא יקבל תשלום לפי חוזה כאמור.

*Gist:* No lender may make, or take payment under, a loan whose real cost of credit at signing exceeds the maximum cost of credit known at that time. [s. 5(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_אשראי_הוגן#סעיף_5) The real cost of credit is the annual ratio of all additions (interest, fees, any other charge) to the amount the borrower actually received (s. 1). The maximum, for a shekel loan, is the Bank of Israel interest rate plus a fixed number of percentage points (First Schedule item 1; a lower cap applies to very short institutional loans, item 3); the maximum default interest is the cap multiplied by a fixed factor (s. 6; Second Schedule). Making a loan above a much higher rate (Bank of Israel rate plus a larger margin) is an offence punishable by imprisonment (s. 15ד; Third Schedule). Margins and the current Bank of Israel rate: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

If the contract is not in writing, omits the key disclosures or uses a base the lender controls, the real cost is computed as if the highest rate actually charged had been fixed for the whole loan (s. 5(ג)): a lender cannot escape the cap by hiding the price.

The caps do not apply to loans above a large indexed amount (s. 15(ב)). The form and disclosure duties, and the acceleration rules, do not apply to an individual who lends not in the course of business; but a loan to someone who is not a relative is presumed to be in the course of business unless the lender proves otherwise (s. 15(א)).

### Acceleration and collection costs

> ביקש המלווה להקדים את מועד הפרעון של יתרת ההלוואה, ישלח ללווה התראה בדבר הקדמת מועד הפירעון, שבה יפרט, בין השאר, פעולות שהלווה יכול לנקוט כדי למנוע את הקדמת מועד הפירעון, ככל שישנן, תוך תקופה שלא תפחת מ־21 ימי עסקים מיום משלוח ההתראה [...]

*Gist:* A lender may accelerate only on real risk of non-payment, a material increase in risk, a fundamental breach or similar grounds, and must first send a warning listing what the borrower can do to prevent acceleration, with a minimum period in business days, and itemising the arrears and the default interest. [s. 7](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_אשראי_הוגן#סעיף_7) The lender may charge only reasonable collection costs it actually incurred, and only if the contract allowed them (s. 7א).

### In court and in execution

A lender suing or opening an execution file on a loan (a judgment, a bill or a fixed-sum claim) must attach the contract and state how the sum was computed: the disclosures, payments made, the balance, arrears split into principal, linkage, interest and default interest, accelerated sums and collection costs (s. 8). The court, on its own initiative, must cancel or change a contract or term that does not meet the Law, may reduce the rates to the caps or lower, and may order repayment of excess (s. 9). The execution registrar may refuse to open or may close a file on a loan that appears unlawful, and may lower default interest for a lender's unreasonable delay (s. 10); his decision under the Law is appealable as of right to the magistrates' court (s. 14). The protections follow the bill to a holder who knew it came from a loan (s. 11; [lesson 29.2](lesson-02.md)) and are open to a guarantor (s. 12).

### Credit data

> דוח ריכוז נתונים יימסר ללקוח בלא תשלום, לפי בקשתו, אחת לשנה [...]

*Gist:* Every customer is entitled once a year, free, to a summary report of the credit data held about him in the Bank of Israel's database; further reports in the same year cost a fee. [s. 38(ד)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_נתוני_אשראי#סעיף_38) The report shows the data, which credit bureaus asked for it, and any data showing a clear failure to pay (s. 38(ב)); it can be in Hebrew or Arabic (s. 38(ג)).

- **Correction** (s. 56): a customer who thinks data is wrong, incomplete or inaccurate may ask the Bank of Israel to delete, complete or correct it; the Bank marks the item as under review and asks the source, which must reply within a set short period (extended to a set number of business days from 26 January 2027). If the dispute remains, the Bank may add a note or delete the item (s. 57).
- **Opt-out** (s. 22): a customer may ask that his data not be kept in the database; from 26 January 2027 the data is first withheld from users and deleted after a year.
- **Retention** (s. 23): data is kept ten years, but after three years only for documentation.

A 2026 amendment (from 26 January 2027) extends the system to companies and changes several rules; check the version in force when you act.

### The consumer debt notice

> עוסק הטוען כי צרכן לא שילם חיוב במועד שנקבע לכך, ישלח אליו הודעת חוב [...] בתוך ארבעה חודשים מהמועד האמור [...] (ב) עוסק לא ינקוט פעולה שמטרתה גביית חוב מהצרכן, לרבות שליחת התראה לפני נקיטת הליכים משפטיים [...] בטרם חלפו 14 ימים מיום שליחת הודעת חוב.

*Gist:* A dealer who claims a consumer missed a payment must send a written debt notice within four months, itemising the deal, the debt and its calculation and how to contact it; it may take no collection step, including a pre-action warning, and may charge no collection costs, until a waiting period has passed after the notice; the notice is sent by the dealer itself, not by a collector, and the dealer must prove it was sent. If it was not sent, the court or the execution registrar may cut the collection costs. [CPL s. 13ב2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_הצרכן#סעיף_13ב2) The consumer may ask for documents proving the deal, which must be sent within 14 days (s. 13ב2(ה)).

### Collection conduct

We found no Israeli statute that regulates debt collectors' conduct as a whole. The protections are scattered: the debt notice above; the Fair Credit Law's limits on collection costs; the Execution Law's power to reduce accumulated interest and default charges in a file (s. 81א4, except base interest and statutory judgment interest save for special reasons); the Penal Law's offences of threats and extortion by threats, and the Prevention of Stalking Law 2001, for collectors who cross the line; and banking regulation: the Bank of Israel has imposed financial sanctions on banks for improper conduct in collecting debts from customers (⚠️ press release title seen; details not opened).

## Leading cases

### Key text: the court's duty to rewrite an unlawful loan (חוק אשראי הוגן ss. 9–10)

Section 9 does not wait for the borrower to plead: the court must, "on its own initiative or on application", cancel or change a non-compliant contract or term, may reduce the rates to the caps or lower and may order repayment. Section 10 gives the execution registrar the same gatekeeping role at the moment a lender tries to enforce. Together with s. 5(ג) they make the Law enforceable by small borrowers who never read it. We could not open a free full text of an appellate ruling interpreting the 2017 amendment, so none is briefed. [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_אשראי_הוגן#סעיף_9)

### Key text: the consumer's own credit report (חוק נתוני אשראי s. 38)

The free annual summary report is the consumer's window into the system that decides whether he gets a mortgage. It also lists every bureau that asked for his data, so he can see who checked him. Errors are corrected through s. 56, not by arguing with the bank that refused the loan. [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_נתוני_אשראי#סעיף_38)

## How it works in practice

### Checking a loan in five minutes

1. Is there a written contract and did you get a copy? (s. 2–3)
2. Compute the real cost: total of everything you pay beyond the sum you received, as an annual rate on that sum. Use the lender's stated real cost and recompute.
3. Compare with the cap at signing: Bank of Israel rate at that date plus the margin ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); the Bank of Israel publishes its rate history).
4. Default interest: is it within the cap times the factor?
5. If the loan was accelerated: did you get a warning with the minimum period and itemised arrears? (s. 7)

### Your credit report

Request the free annual report from the Bank of Israel's credit-data system (online, with identification; ⚠️ manual check of the current service on boi.org.il). Read every item: lender, type, balance, missed payments. For an error, write first to the source (the bank or lender) and in parallel file a correction request with the Bank of Israel under s. 56, attaching proof (payment confirmations, closing letter).

### Answering a collection letter

Ask: was a debt notice sent by the dealer itself, with the details, at least the waiting period before this letter? Ask in writing for the documents proving the deal (s. 13ב2(ה)). If the debt arises from a loan, ask for the contract and the s. 8 breakdown. Dispute collection costs not allowed by the contract or charged before the waiting period. Offer an arrangement only for what you actually owe.

## Worked example

Tamar Levi, a reservist from Modi'in, took a ₪15,000 loan from a licensed non-bank lender through an app in 2024. The contract shows ₪15,000, but ₪1,200 was deducted as an "opening fee", so ₪13,800 reached her account. Monthly payments of ₪780 for 24 months. After two missed payments during her reserve service, the lender demanded the whole balance at once, added default interest and ₪1,500 "lawyer's collection fees", and opened an execution file on a promissory note she had signed.

1. **Real cost.** She pays ₪18,720 in total for ₪13,800 actually received; the real cost is computed on ₪13,800, including the opening fee as an addition (s. 1). If the result exceeds the cap at signing, the lender may not take payment above it (s. 5(א)).
2. **Acceleration.** Did she receive a warning with at least the minimum business days and the arrears itemised (s. 7(ג)–(ד))? If not, acceleration was premature.
3. **Collection costs.** ₪1,500 is allowed only if reasonable, actually incurred and provided in the contract (s. 7א).
4. **Execution.** The lender had to attach the contract and the full breakdown (s. 8). Tamar asks the registrar to refuse or close the file, or to reduce the sums, under s. 10, and opposes the note within the period, raising the Fair Credit Law against any holder (s. 11). A decision against her may be appealed as of right (s. 14).
5. **Reserve service.** She also checks emergency rules for reservists (deferral laws, execution protections) in force at the time (⚠️ check current rules), and asks the registrar to reduce accumulated default interest (s. 81א4).
6. **Credit report.** She orders her free annual report to see how the default was reported, and asks for a note under s. 57 if she disputes it.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- One loan or credit line of yours (bank, card, app, car finance), its contract and payment history; or Tamar's facts.
- The Bank of Israel interest rate on the signing date (Bank of Israel website) and the margins in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. Fill a table: loan amount, amount actually received, every addition, payments and dates.
2. Compute the real cost of credit as an annual rate (an online loan calculator is fine) and compare with the cap at signing.
3. Check s. 3(ב): list any missing disclosure.
4. Request your free annual credit-data report (or write down exactly how you would) and list any item you would dispute.
5. Draft a two-paragraph reply to a collection letter asking for the debt notice, the contract, the s. 8 breakdown and the proof of the deal.
6. Draft a correction request under s. 56 for one item, using the [regulator complaint](../../templates/regulator-complaint.md) structure addressed to the Bank of Israel's credit-data system.

### Template

[Regulator complaint](../../templates/regulator-complaint.md) (adapt for a credit-data correction); for an execution file, the [execution debtor response kit](../../templates/execution-debtor-response-kit.md).

### How you know it worked

- Your real-cost figure is computed on the amount you actually received.
- Your reply names ss. 7, 7א and 8 of the Fair Credit Law and s. 13ב2 of the Consumer Protection Law where they apply.
- You know when you are next entitled to a free report.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. A borrower may raise the Fair Credit Law in his own name in the Execution Office, in small claims and in the magistrates' court, and may request and correct his credit data himself. A guarantor may raise the same defences (s. 12).

### Where and how

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- Credit report and corrections: the Bank of Israel credit-data system, online (⚠️ manual check).
- Complaints: about a bank or card company, the Bank of Israel public-enquiries unit; about a non-bank lender, the Capital Market, Insurance and Savings Authority, which supervises licensed credit providers (⚠️ manual check of the current complaint route).
- Execution files: requests to the registrar under ss. 10, 14 of the Fair Credit Law and s. 81א4 of the Execution Law ([lesson 28.3](../module-28/lesson-03.md)).
- Free help: Paamonim and other debt-counselling bodies (⚠️ manual check), Legal Aid where eligible, court information centres.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The lender is unlicensed or the loan looks like loan-sharking with threats: also go to the police.
- The debt is large, secured on your home, or you are considering insolvency ([lesson 28.5](../module-28/lesson-05.md)).
- Many borrowers of the same lender are affected (a possible class action).
- A credit-data error is costing you a mortgage and the source refuses to correct it.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Comparing the nominal rate with the cap | A loan above the cap looks lawful | Compute the real cost on the amount received | Include every fee |
| Paying "collection fees" on demand | Paying sums the law does not allow | Ask for the contract clause and proof of the cost | Dispute before paying |
| Ignoring a lender's acceleration warning | The whole balance falls due with default interest | Act within the warning period | Read the warning: it must say how to prevent acceleration |
| Arguing with the bank about a credit-report error | Error stays | File a correction under s. 56 | Correct at the source and the Bank of Israel |
| Assuming a private loan from a friend is outside the Law | Losing protections | Remember the presumption of lending in business for non-relatives (s. 15(א)) | Write the loan down anyway |

## Review

- Loans need a written contract and full disclosure; the real cost of credit, computed on the amount received, may not exceed the cap at signing, and default interest has its own cap.
- Acceleration needs grounds and a warning with a minimum period; collection costs must be reasonable, incurred and agreed.
- Courts must rewrite unlawful loans; the execution registrar can refuse to enforce them; protections bind holders and help guarantors.
- Every person may get a free annual credit-data report from the Bank of Israel and request corrections.
- Dealers must send a debt notice and wait before any collection step; collection conduct is otherwise governed by scattered rules.
