# 29.5 · Banks and customers: duties, fees and complaints

By the end of this lesson you will be able to read your bank's tariff against your statement, spot a fee or a refusal the law does not allow, and get it reversed through the cheapest route that works. Banks are the commercial counterparty almost every adult deals with every month, and Israeli law treats them differently from ordinary contracting parties: a special statute, a regulator who investigates individual complaints, and a standard-form contract that a court can rewrite.

## Why it matters

The bank-customer relationship is long, one-sided in information, and governed by standard forms. Three bodies of law give the customer leverage: the Banking (Service to Customer) Law 1981, which lists conduct banks may not engage in and controls fees; the Standard Contracts Law 1982, which applies to the account agreement and loan forms ([lesson 20.7](../module-20/lesson-07.md)); and the general duty of good faith in contracts ([lesson 03.4](../module-03/lesson-04.md)), which Israeli courts apply to banks with particular strictness because of their position. The regulator, the Supervisor of Banks at the Bank of Israel, investigates individual complaints for free and can order a refund. Most banking disputes of an individual are small, document-heavy and well suited to a complaint followed, if needed, by a small claim.

## The law

### Services a bank may not unreasonably refuse

> לא יסרב תאגיד בנקאי סירוב בלתי סביר לתת שירותים מהסוגים הבאים: (1) קבלת פקדון כספי במטבע ישראלי או במטבע חוץ; (2) פתיחת חשבון עובר ושב במטבע ישראלי, וניהולו כל עוד מתקיימת אחת מאלה: (א) החשבון ביתרת זכות לטובת הלקוח; (ב) הלקוח עומד בתנאי ההסכם [...] אולם אין חובה לתת שירות שיש בו משום מתן אשראי ללקוח.

*Gist:* A bank may not unreasonably refuse to take a deposit, to open and run a current account in credit (or while the customer meets the account terms), or to sell bank cheques; it is not obliged to give credit, and imposing unreasonable conditions counts as refusal. [s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הבנקאות_%28שירות_ללקוח%29#סעיף_2) Small and very small banks are excepted (s. 2(ו)). A restricted customer under the Cheques Without Cover Law may still have an account; he may not draw cheques on it ([lesson 29.3](lesson-03.md)).

### Misleading and exploitation

> לא יעשה תאגיד בנקאי – במעשה או במחדל, בכתב או בעל־פה או בכל דרך אחרת – דבר העלול להטעות לקוח בכל ענין מהותי למתן שירות ללקוח [...]

*Gist:* A bank may not do anything liable to mislead a customer on a material matter, including the nature of the service, its timing, its return, the identity of the provider, the usual price, a professional opinion, the warranty terms and the period and renewal of the service. [s. 3](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הבנקאות_%28שירות_ללקוח%29#סעיף_3)

> לא יעשה תאגיד בנקאי [...] דבר שיש בו משום ניצול מצוקתו של לקוח, חולשתו השכלית או הגופנית, בורותו, אי־ידיעתו את השפה או חוסר נסיונו, או הפעלת השפעה בלתי הוגנת עליו, הכל כדי לקשור עסקה של מתן שירות בתנאים בלתי סבירים [...]

*Gist:* A bank may not exploit a customer's distress, mental or physical weakness, ignorance, lack of the language or inexperience, or exert unfair influence, to make him take a service on unreasonable terms or for a price unreasonably different from the usual one. [s. 4](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הבנקאות_%28שירות_ללקוח%29#סעיף_4) Compare the general rule on exploitation in contracts ([lesson 03.5](../module-03/lesson-05.md)).

### Disclosure, tying, switching

- **Disclosure** (s. 5): the Governor sets rules on what banks must disclose and when they must sign a written agreement and give a copy; every advertisement encouraging a loan must carry a statutory warning about default interest and execution (s. 5(ג)).
- **Tying** (s. 7): a bank may not make a service conditional on buying another service or asset unless there is a reasonable business connection (a mortgage may require property insurance; a loan may not require a new savings plan).
- **Human response** (s. 5א3) and, from 22 March 2027, **recording and delivery of telemarketing calls**: a bank that markets by outgoing calls must record them, keep them for at least two years and give the customer a recording within ten business days of a request; if it does not, the court treats it as admitting the customer's version (s. 5א5).
- **Switching** (ss. 5ב, 5ב1): a customer may move his account, standing orders and direct debits to another bank through a regulated process.

### Fees

> תאגיד בנקאי לא יגבה מלקוח עמלה אלא בעד שירות שניתן בפועל ללקוח.

*Gist:* A bank may charge a fee only for a service actually given (s. 9י3), only for a service in its full or reduced tariff and according to it (s. 9י), not above its published tariff (s. 9י1), and not above a lower amount agreed with the customer (s. 9י2). [ss. 9י–9י3](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הבנקאות_%28שירות_ללקוח%29#סעיף_9י) The Governor may declare services supervised and cap their fees (ss. 9יא–9יב). Individuals and small businesses are entitled to the reduced tariff and to fee "tracks" set by the Supervisor (⚠️ manual check of the current tracks).

### Remedies and the regulator

> דין נזק שנגרם לאדם על ידי עבירה על הוראות חוק זה, או על כל תקנה שניתנה לפיו, כדין נזק שמותר לתבוע עליו פיצויים על פי פקודת הנזיקין [...]

*Gist:* Damage caused by a breach of the Law is a tort; it makes no difference whether it was direct or indirect, an actual loss or lost profit, whether the bank was prosecuted, or whether it intended or could foresee the damage. [s. 15](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הבנקאות_%28שירות_ללקוח%29#סעיף_15)

> המפקח יברר פניות הציבור בדבר עסקיהם עם תאגידים בנקאיים שראה בהן ממש [...] (ד) החלטותיו וממצאיו של המפקח בבירור פניה – (1) לא יהיה בהם כדי להעניק לפונה או לאדם אחר זכות או סעד בבית משפט [...] שלא היו לו לפני כן; (2) [...] אם נקבע לכך מועד בחיקוק, לא יוארך המועד על ידי הגשת הפניה למפקח או בירורה.

*Gist:* The Supervisor investigates public complaints that have substance; if a complaint is justified he tells the complainant and the bank his findings and how to fix the defect; but his findings create no new right in court and filing a complaint does not extend any statutory deadline. [s. 16](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הבנקאות_%28שירות_ללקוח%29#סעיף_16) The Law cannot be waived by contract (s. 17) and protects guarantors of the customer too (s. 17א).

## Leading cases

### Key text: the regulator's complaint power and its limits (חוק הבנקאות (שירות ללקוח) s. 16)

Section 16 makes the public-enquiries unit an investigator, not a court: it can find a complaint justified and require the bank to correct the defect, and banks usually comply, but its findings bind no court and it does not stop the clock on limitation or on the s. 10 cheque appeal. Why it matters: complain early, and in parallel diary every court deadline. [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הבנקאות_%28שירות_ללקוח%29#סעיף_16)

### Key text: the bank's heightened duty of good faith

Israeli courts describe the bank as owing its customer duties beyond those of an ordinary contracting party, because banking services are essential, the bank holds information the customer lacks, and the relationship rests on trust; the duties of care and disclosure in particular cases are derived from the Contracts Law (ss. 12, 39), the Civil Wrongs Ordinance and this Law. We could not open a free full text of the leading Supreme Court rulings on this duty, so none is briefed here; build your argument from the statutory duties above and confirm any ruling you cite in a free source first. The Bank of Israel has also used the Standard Contracts Law against bank forms, for example a claim by the Supervisor against depriving terms in a mortgage bank's standard guarantee form (⚠️ Bank of Israel press release title seen; its date and text were not opened).

## How it works in practice

### The three-step route

1. **Complaint to the bank.** Write to the branch and to the bank's public-enquiries officer (each bank has one). State the fee or refusal, the section, the tariff line, and the refund or action you want, with a deadline of 14 days.
2. **Complaint to the Bank of Israel.** If the bank refuses or does not answer, file online with the Banking Supervision public-enquiries unit (free; national digital login or online form; ⚠️ manual check of the current address on boi.org.il). Attach the bank's answer. The unit asks the bank for a response and decides.
3. **Small claim.** For a refund or damages within the ceiling, sue in small claims in your own name; the bank, a company, may be sued there. A justified finding by the Supervisor is useful evidence, even though it does not bind the court.

### Reading your fees

Download the bank's current tariff for individuals and your last twelve months of statements. For each fee line: which service, which tariff item, which track you are on, whether the service was actually given (s. 9י3), and whether you agreed a lower rate (s. 9י2). Typical errors: a fee for a branch transaction you did online, a fee above your track, a fee for a standing-order failure caused by the bank, interest and fees after you closed the account.

## Worked example

Ahmad Abu-Rabia, a teacher in Rahat, notices over a year: a monthly account-management fee though he is on a basic fee track; a ₪25 "special handling" charge each time a direct debit was rejected, including two months when his salary arrived late because the bank delayed crediting it; and a branch clerk who told him he must open a savings plan to get a ₪20,000 car loan.

1. **Fee track.** If his track includes account management, the monthly fee exceeds what he agreed (s. 9י2) and must be refunded.
2. **Special handling.** A fee only for a service in the tariff (s. 9י) and actually given (s. 9י3). Where the rejection was caused by the bank's own delay, the fee is unjustified, and the delay itself may be a tort (s. 15).
3. **Savings plan.** Conditioning a loan on a savings plan has no reasonable business connection: tying, s. 7(א).
4. **Route.** He writes to the bank's public-enquiries officer with a table of the fees, the tariff items and the sections, asking for a refund within 14 days and written confirmation that the loan will not be conditioned. The bank refunds the track fee but not the handling fees. He files with the Bank of Israel, attaching the table and the bank's answer.
5. **Outcome planning.** If the Supervisor finds the complaint justified, the bank usually refunds; if not, he considers a small claim within the limitation period (complaining did not stop it).

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your bank's current tariff for individuals, your fee track, and twelve months of statements; or the supplied case: Rina Ohayon of Netivot, 72, was told by phone that a "premium package" would cost ₪10 a month "forever", and is now charged ₪38.
- The Law, ss. 2–7, 9י–9י3, 15–17.
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. Build a table: date, fee, tariff item, your track, service given? (yes or no), section breached.
2. Choose the three strongest items and write one sentence for each linking the facts to a section.
3. For any phone promise, note the date and time of the call; from 22 March 2027 you may ask for the recording (s. 5א5).
4. Draft a letter to the bank's public-enquiries officer on the [regulator complaint](../../templates/regulator-complaint.md) structure, addressed to the bank first.
5. Draft the follow-up complaint to the Bank of Israel, adding the bank's answer.
6. Compute the limitation period for a small claim on the oldest fee and note it in your diary.

### Template

[Regulator complaint](../../templates/regulator-complaint.md) (use section 4 for the prior complaint to the bank); for court, [small claim for a contract or consumer dispute](../../templates/small-claim-contract-consumer.md).

### How you know it worked

- Each fee you challenge has a tariff item and a section next to it.
- Your letter asks for a specific sum and a date.
- You know which deadlines the complaint does not extend.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Complaints to the bank and the Bank of Israel are free and personal; small claims are designed for individuals, and a bank may be sued there. A customer that is a company cannot sue in small claims and normally needs a lawyer in the magistrates' court.

### Where and how

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- Bank's public-enquiries officer: contact details on each bank's website.
- Bank of Israel Banking Supervision public-enquiries unit: online form on boi.org.il (⚠️ manual check: the site blocks automated access).
- Small claims court: online filing, fee in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Free help: Kol-Zchut on bank complaints (⚠️ manual check), Legal Aid's consumer service where eligible, the Israel Consumer Council.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The dispute concerns a mortgage, a business loan or a guarantee of a large sum.
- The bank has started realising a pledge or a mortgage ([lesson 29.4](lesson-04.md)).
- Many customers are affected in the same way (a possible class action).
- You suffered a large loss from investment advice or a misleading product.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Complaining "the fees are too high" in general | No action | Match each fee to a tariff item and a section | Build the table first |
| Waiting for the Supervisor and missing a court deadline | Claim or appeal barred (s. 16(ד)(2)) | File in court in parallel | Diary every deadline on day one |
| Accepting a loan with a tied product | Paying for something you did not want | Complain under s. 7 and cancel the tied product | Ask: is there a business connection? |
| Relying on an oral promise with no record | Your word against the bank's | From 2027, ask for the recording; write a confirmation email the same day | Ask the bank to confirm in writing |
| Signing away rights in the account agreement | The clause is void anyway (s. 17), but you may not know it | Rely on s. 17 and the Standard Contracts Law | Read the agreement against the Law |

## Review

- Banks may not unreasonably refuse deposits, an account in credit or bank cheques; they need not give credit.
- Misleading (s. 3), exploitation (s. 4) and tying (s. 7) are prohibited; breaches are torts (s. 15) and the Law cannot be waived (s. 17).
- Fees only for a service given, in the tariff, not above it, and not above what was agreed (ss. 9י–9י3).
- Complain to the bank, then to the Bank of Israel; the Supervisor's findings help but bind no court and stop no deadline.
- From 22 March 2027 banks must record telemarketing calls and give them to the customer on request.
