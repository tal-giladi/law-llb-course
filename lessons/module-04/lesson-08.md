# 04.8 · Practice lab: a tort demand letter and the insurance claim

In this lab you write the two documents that settle most tort disputes before court: a demand letter to the person or company that caused your damage, and a written claim to the insurer that will actually pay. You finish with a dated file and a diary of every deadline.

## Why it matters

Most tort claims are paid by insurers: the neighbour's home policy, the contractor's third-party policy, the driver's property insurance, your own comprehensive cover. Insurers move when a written claim with documents arrives, because the Insurance Contract Law then sets their duties and their clock. A clear demand letter to the wrongdoer does the rest: it states the tort, the amount and the date, it asks for the insurer's details, and it becomes exhibit 4 in your small claim if nothing happens (lesson 04.9).

## The law

### Notice, investigation, payment

> קרה מקרה הביטוח, על המבוטח להודיע על כך למבטח מיד לאחר שנודע לו…

*Gist:* When the insured event happens, the insured must notify the insurer as soon as they learn of it. ([Insurance Contract Law 1981, s. 22](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חוזה_הביטוח))

> (א) משנמסרו למבטח הודעה על קרות מקרה הביטוח ותביעה בכתב לתשלום תגמולי הביטוח, על המבטח לעשות מיד את הדרוש לבירור חבותו.

*Gist:* Once the insurer receives notice and a written claim, it must immediately do what is needed to establish its liability. (s. 23(א)) You must provide, within a reasonable time after being asked, the information and documents needed (s. 23(ב)).

> תגמולי הביטוח ישולמו תוך 30 ימים מהיום שהיו בידי המבטח המידע והמסמכים הדרושים לבירור חבותו, אולם תגמולי ביטוח שאינם שנויים במחלוקת בתום לב ישולמו תוך 30 ימים מהיום שנמסרה למבטח תביעה…

*Gist:* Payment is due within 30 days after the insurer has the information it needs, and undisputed amounts within 30 days of the claim. (s. 27) Linkage and interest are added (s. 28).

### A third party can claim from the wrongdoer's insurer

In liability insurance the insurer must, at the injured third party's demand, pay that party what it owes the insured, after 30 days' notice to the insured (s. 68, lesson 04.7).

### Limitation is shorter for insurance

A claim for insurance proceeds is time-barred three years after the insured event; for life, health and long-term-care policies made or renewed from 25.11.2020, five years (s. 31; see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)). A tort claim against the wrongdoer still has the general period (lesson 04.5). Do not let the insurer's process run past the three years.

## Leading cases

### Key text rather than a ruling

This lab applies the rules from lessons 04.2–04.7 rather than a new ruling: negligence and its burden-shifting sections, causation and itemised damage under s. 76(2) of the Ordinance, employers' liability and s. 68 of the Insurance Contract Law, and the interim-payment duty under s. 5 of the road-accidents Law. The Insurance Contract Law itself is the key text: read ss. 22–31 and 68 in full on [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חוזה_הביטוח) before you send anything.

## How it works in practice

### Which documents, to whom, in what order

| Situation | First document | To | Then |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neighbour's leak damaged your flat | Demand letter, asking for their home-insurance details | Neighbour | Claim to their insurer (s. 68); or to your own home insurer |
| Car damaged by another driver | Claim with the accident-report form to the other driver's property insurer | Their insurer | Demand letter to the driver if the insurer refuses |
| Object fell from a building site | Demand letter, asking for the contractor's third-party insurer | Contractor and subcontractor | Claim to the insurer (s. 68) |
| Fall in a shop | Demand letter asking the shop to refer it to its insurer | Shop chain's head office | Claim to the insurer |
| Bodily injury in a road accident | Claim and demand for interim payments | Compulsory insurer of your vehicle (or the one that hit you if you were outside) | Diary day 60 (s. 5) |

### Delivery and diary

Send by email and registered mail, or the insurer's online claims form plus email; save every confirmation. Diary: the reply date in your letter, day 30 after a complete claim to an insurer (s. 27), day 60 for road-accident interim payments, the three-year insurance limitation date, and the seven-year tort limitation date.

### If the insurer refuses or stalls

Ask for written reasons. Insurers are supervised by the Capital Market, Insurance and Savings Authority, which runs a public-complaints unit (⚠️ check the current complaint route on gov.il). A complaint does not stop limitation; a lawsuit does.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

## Worked example

Yael and Itamar Ben-Ami, from Kibbutz Ein HaShofet, park outside a hardware store in Yokneam. A store employee reversing a forklift into the loading bay hits their car's rear door. The employee admits it and photographs the forklift's position. Repair quote: ₪5,800. They also rented a car for two days: ₪420.

They write to the store's head office on the [tort demand letter template](../../templates/tort-demand-letter.md):

> **הנדון: דרישה לפיצוי בגין נזק לרכב מס' 12-345-67 – אירוע מיום 3.9.2026 בחצר החנות ביקנעם**
>
> 1. ביום 3.9.2026 בשעה 10:15 לערך, בעת שרכבנו חנה בחניית הלקוחות של חנותכם, פגע בו מלגזה שנהג בה עובדכם, מר ___, תוך כדי עבודתו (נספחים א–ב).
> 2. עובדכם התרשל כמשמעות הדבר בסעיפים 35–36 לפקודת הנזיקין [נוסח חדש], ואתם אחראים למעשהו לפי סעיף 13 לפקודה.
> 3. נזקינו: תיקון הדלת האחורית – 5,800 ש"ח (נספח ג); רכב חלופי ליומיים – 420 ש"ח (נספח ד). סה"כ 6,220 ש"ח.
> 4. אנו דורשים לשלם לנו 6,220 ש"ח עד ליום 24.9.2026, וכן לקבל בתוך 7 ימים את פרטי מבטח האחריות שלכם.

Their own comprehensive insurer would pay the repair, less the deductible, and then claim from the store; but a claim on their own policy may affect their premium. They choose to claim directly from the store and its insurer first, and diary 24.9.2026 and 3.9.2029 (three years for any insurance claim).

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A tort incident of your own with a property or small personal loss, or the supplied case.
- The damages table from lesson 04.3 and the defences table from lesson 04.5.
- The [tort demand letter template](../../templates/tort-demand-letter.md) and the [insurance claim letter template](../../templates/insurance-claim-letter.md).
- About two and a half hours.

**Supplied case.** Sarit Biton, a hairdresser in Kiryat Shmona, parks on the street. A roofer working on the building above drops a bucket of bitumen onto her car's bonnet and windscreen. The roofer works for "גגות הצפון בע"מ", whose name is on the van. Two quotes: ₪7,400 and ₪8,100. Sarit missed a day's work taking the car to the garage: ₪650 (she has a client-cancellation list). She has comprehensive insurance with a ₪1,500 deductible.

### Steps

1. **Choose the target (10 min).** Decide whom to demand from (the wrongdoer, the employer, both) and whether to claim from your own insurer, the wrongdoer's insurer, or both. Write one sentence on why.
2. **Finalise the damages table (20 min).** Every item with an amount and a document; drop items you cannot prove.
3. **Draft the demand letter (40 min).** Use the template. Paragraph by paragraph: facts with dates and exhibits; who is responsible and why (section numbers); the tort; the itemised table; the amount and date (14 days is usual); request for the insurer's details; reservation of rights.
4. **Cut (10 min).** Read it aloud. Remove adjectives, guesses and any legal claim your analysis does not support.
5. **Draft the insurance claim (30 min).** Use the template, paragraph A for your own policy or B for a third-party claim under s. 68. Attach the same exhibits. Ask for the claim number and for written reasons for any refusal.
6. **Road-accident checklist (20 min).** If your matter involves bodily injury in a road accident, write the checklist from lesson 04.6 for it (vehicle, insurer, police report, medical file, income proof, receipts, s. 5 demand). Otherwise write it for the supplied case of lesson 04.6.
7. **Diary (10 min).** Enter: reply date; day 30 after a complete insurance claim; day 60 for interim payments if relevant; insurance limitation (three years); tort limitation (seven years).
8. **Send or keep as a dry run (10 min).** If you send, use two channels and save the proofs in your file.

### How you know it worked

- A stranger can tell from the subject line and first paragraph who did what, when, and what you want.
- Every amount in the table has an exhibit; the total is a sum, not a round number.
- The letter names the employer and asks for the insurer.
- Your diary has at least four dated entries.
- If sent: you have proof of delivery and, within the deadline, a reply or a record that none came.

### Self-check

- [ ] Both documents are in my own name, about my own damage.
- [ ] I named the tort and the sections (35–36, 13, 63 or 41 as relevant).
- [ ] I asked for the liability insurer's details or claimed under s. 68.
- [ ] I did not threaten steps I will not take.
- [ ] I diarised the three-year insurance limitation separately from the tort limitation.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Writing to the wrongdoer and claiming from an insurer in your own name is the normal way these disputes start. Writing such letters or handling claims for others for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20); licensed insurance agents work under their own law.

### Where and how

- The wrongdoer: home or business address and registered office; companies' registered addresses are in the Companies Registrar's public data.
- Insurers: online claims forms and claims-department emails on their websites.
- Supervision of insurers: the Capital Market, Insurance and Savings Authority's public-complaints unit (⚠️ manual check of the current route on gov.il).
- Deadlines and limitation periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md). Free help: [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The claim involves bodily injury beyond a few weeks' recovery, or any road-accident injury the insurer disputes.
- The insurer offers a settlement that includes a full waiver of future claims.
- The insurer says the policy does not cover the event because of a disclosure issue when the policy was made.
- The three-year insurance limitation date is close and the insurer is still "investigating".

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Only calling the insurer by phone | No written claim, so the s. 23 and s. 27 clocks never start | Send a written claim with documents now | Always claim in writing and keep the number |
| Waiting for the insurer past three years | The insurance claim is barred; only the tort claim remains | Sue the wrongdoer within the tort period | Diary the s. 31 date on day one |
| Claiming from your own insurer without checking the deductible and premium effect | You lose more than you gain | Claim from the wrongdoer's insurer instead | Compare both routes before choosing |
| Demand letter to an employee only | No response; no insurer | Write to the employer's head office | Name the employer and cite s. 13 |
| Round-number demand with no exhibits | Insurer asks for documents; months lost | Send the itemised table and exhibits | Finish the damages table first |

## Review

- Most tort claims are paid by insurers; a written claim with documents starts the insurer's duties (ss. 23, 27).
- An injured third party may demand payment directly from the wrongdoer's liability insurer (s. 68).
- A demand letter names the tort, the responsible employer, an itemised amount and a date, and asks for the insurer.
- Insurance claims have a three-year limitation (five for some health and life policies); the tort claim against the wrongdoer has its own period.
- Diary every date and keep proof of delivery: the file you build here is the court file of lesson 04.9.
