# 04.9 · Practice lab: small claim for property damage (dry run)

In this lab you turn the file from lesson 04.8 into a complete small-claims statement of claim for property damage, check it against the regulations in force since April 2025, and rehearse the hearing. It is a dry run: you file only if you decide to, in your own name.

## Why it matters

The small claims court (בית משפט לתביעות קטנות) is where most self-represented tort claims end: car damage in a car park, a neighbour's leak, a dropped bucket of bitumen. It was designed for people without lawyers: the judge runs the hearing actively, the forms are short, the fee is low, and a lawyer may appear only with the court's leave. But the 2025 amendments added firm requirements: a five-page limit, a list of documents and witnesses, a declaration of truth, and the number of small claims you filed in the last year. A claim that ignores them invites delay or worse.

## The law

### Jurisdiction and who may sue

> בית משפט לתביעות קטנות ידון בתביעה אזרחית שהגיש יחיד –
> (1) לתשלום סכום שאינו עולה על 30,000 שקלים חדשים…

*Gist:* The small claims court hears civil claims filed by an individual for payment up to a linked ceiling (and certain consumer orders). ([Courts Law, s. 60(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט#סעיף_60); the current linked amount is in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

The court may transfer a claim that does not suit it, or a claim by someone who already filed more than five small claims in that court that year (s. 60(ב)). Companies cannot sue there, but can be sued. A party may be represented by a lawyer only with the court's leave (s. 63).

### Venue and contents

The claim goes to the court for the defendant's home or business, or the place of the act or omission, whatever any agreement says (reg. 2(א)).

> (ג) בגוף כתב התביעה יפרט התובע את כל העניינים האלה:
> (1) מקום המעשה או המחדל שבשלו תובעים;
> (2) הסעד המבוקש;
> (3) נימוקי התביעה;
> (4) רשימת המסמכים שעליהם הוא מבקש להסתמך בכתב התביעה;
> (5) רשימת העדים שבכוונתו להעיד לתמיכה בתביעה;

*Gist:* The body of the claim must set out the place of the act, the remedy, the grounds, the list of documents and the list of witnesses; it must also state your preference for a video hearing or a written decision and the number of small claims you filed in the last year. ([Small Claims (Procedure) Regulations, reg. 3(ג)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיפוט_בתביעות_קטנות_(סדרי_דין)), for proceedings opened from 11.4.2025)

The heading must include your name, ID number, address, email and mobile, and the defendant's details as far as you can find them, including a company number (reg. 3(ב) as amended in 2025). You must attach the documents you rely on and a declaration that the contents are true or to the best of your knowledge, with the source of that knowledge (reg. 3(ד)). The body may not exceed five pages (reg. 3(ה)).

### Fee, defence, hearing, judgment

- Fee: 1% of the claim with a minimum (reg. 6; see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- The defendant has 30 days from service to file a defence (reg. 4(ב)); it may add a counterclaim or a third-party notice (reg. 5).
- If the defendant files no defence, the court may give judgment on the claim alone (reg. 10). If you do not appear, your claim is dismissed; if the defendant does not appear, judgment may be given on your claim after you declare its truth (reg. 11).
- The judge sets the order of the hearing and investigates the dispute actively (reg. 13(א)); the hearing may be by video, or decided on the written record, in suitable cases (reg. 13(ב)–(ג)).
- Judgment at the end of the hearing or within 7 days, briefly reasoned, stating how and when it can be challenged (reg. 15).
- Leave to appeal to the district court: 30 days from judgment for proceedings opened from 11.4.2025 (reg. 16(א); see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).

## Leading cases

### רע"א 9615/05 שמש נ' פוקצ'טה בע"מ (5.7.2006)

The smoking-in-a-restaurant claim from lesson 04.4 began as a small claim by a self-represented mother. Justice Rubinstein, on a further appeal, stressed that civil claims, including small claims, are a legitimate and important way for individuals to enforce protective statutes, and increased her compensation. Why it matters: small claims court is not a lesser forum; a well-built small claim can apply real tort doctrine and reach the Supreme Court. English text: [Versa](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/shemesh-v-focaccetta-ltd).

### רע"א 5234/17 (representation in small claims)

The Supreme Court held that a small-claims judge is not required to tell an unrepresented party that they may ask for permission to be represented (⚠️ manual check: decision text on the Judiciary's site, linked in [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md)). Why it matters: the court will help you present your case, but it will not run it for you; you must ask for what you need.

## How it works in practice

### Before filing

1. The demand letter has gone and its deadline has passed (lesson 04.8).
2. Your claim is within the ceiling. Do not split one claim into two to fit under it.
3. You know the defendant's exact name and, for a company, its registration number (Companies Registrar's public search).
4. You chose the court: the defendant's area or the place of the accident.
5. You decided whether to add the liability insurer as a second defendant (s. 68 of the Insurance Contract Law).

### Filing

- Check the Judiciary's website for the current way for unrepresented parties to open a small claim (online service or the court office) and the current form (⚠️ manual check).
- Pay the fee. Keep the case number.
- Follow service instructions: if the court did not serve the claim 60 days before the hearing, you must serve it at least 40 days before (reg. 7(ב1)).

### The hearing, in 15 minutes

Bring three copies of everything, originals of photos and receipts, and your witness. Prepare a two-minute opening: what happened, why the defendant is responsible, how much and why. Expect the judge to ask questions and to propose a compromise; decide in advance the lowest amount you would accept and why.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

## Worked example

Lior Azoulay, a tour guide from Eilat, finds his parked car's side mirror and door smashed in a hotel car park. CCTV shows a delivery van of "הובלות הדרום בע"מ" reversing into it; the driver left without leaving details. The company ignored his demand letter. Damage: ₪3,950 (garage invoice) plus ₪350 for the assessor's report.

His statement of claim, on the [small claim template](../../templates/small-claim-property-damage.md):

- **Heading:** Lior's details; defendant 1 the company with its registration number from the Registrar; defendant 2 the van's property insurer, if he has learned its name (otherwise he sues the company alone).
- **Place:** the hotel car park in Eilat, so the Eilat small claims court.
- **Facts:** four numbered paragraphs, each with an exhibit (photos, CCTV stills, demand letter, no reply).
- **Liability:** negligence of the driver (ss. 35–36), employer's liability (s. 13), the insurer's duty under s. 68.
- **Damage:** a two-line table, total ₪4,300.
- **Documents and witnesses:** six exhibits; witness: the hotel's security officer, who extracted the CCTV.
- **Hearing:** prefers an in-person hearing; agrees to a decision on the written record if the defendant agrees.
- **Previous small claims this year:** none.
- **Declaration:** signed, source: personal knowledge and documents.

The body runs to two pages. Fee: 1% of the claim with the minimum. He diaries the 30-day defence period and checks service before the 40-day mark.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your file from lesson 04.8 (demand letter, damages table, exhibits), or the supplied case from lesson 04.8 (Sarit Biton's car).
- The [small claim for property damage template](../../templates/small-claim-property-damage.md).
- The text of reg. 2, 3, 6, 13 and 16 of the Small Claims (Procedure) Regulations.
- About two and a half hours.

### Steps

1. **Check jurisdiction (10 min).** Are you an individual? Is the amount within the ceiling in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)? Is the remedy money? Write yes or no for each with the section.
2. **Choose the court (10 min).** Under reg. 2(א), list the possible courts and pick one; write why.
3. **Identify the defendants (15 min).** Exact names, ID or company numbers, addresses. Decide whether to add the insurer.
4. **Write the facts (25 min).** Numbered, dated, first person, one exhibit per fact. No adjectives.
5. **Write the grounds (20 min).** The tort and its sections, the employer's liability, s. 41 if the cause was in the defendant's control, s. 68 for the insurer. One short paragraph each.
6. **Write the damage table (10 min).** From lesson 04.3; every item with an exhibit number (s. 76(2)).
7. **Complete the required items (15 min).** Documents list, witnesses list, hearing preference, number of small claims in the last year, the remedy with linkage and interest, the declaration.
8. **Check the length and the attachments (10 min).** Body at most five pages; every exhibit attached and numbered.
9. **Rehearse (25 min).** Write your two-minute opening. Write the three questions you expect the judge to ask and your answers. Write the defendant's best argument and your reply. Write your lowest acceptable compromise.
10. **Dry run or real (10 min).** Stop here, or file in your own name, pay the fee and diary: defence period, service deadline, hearing date, 30 days for leave to appeal after judgment.

### How you know it worked

- The claim contains every item in reg. 3(ב)–(ה), and the body is within five pages.
- Every fact has an exhibit and every shekel has a document.
- The grounds name the tort and the sections that make each defendant liable.
- You can deliver your opening in two minutes without notes.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes; this is the court designed for it. An individual sues in their own name; a lawyer may appear only with the court's leave, and the court may also allow a non-lawyer who does not represent people regularly or for pay (Courts Law s. 63). A company cannot sue in small claims but defends through an authorised person.

### Where and how

- The small claims court for the defendant's area or the place of the event (reg. 2).
- Filing route and current form: the Judiciary's website and information centre (⚠️ manual check of the online service for unrepresented parties; the centre's numbers are in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- Fee, ceiling and deadlines: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md). Free help: [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md); forum rules: [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The defendant files a counterclaim above the ceiling or the court transfers the case to the magistrates' court.
- The damage includes a bodily injury, or the claim is really about land rights or a continuing nuisance needing an injunction.
- You want to appeal: leave to appeal has its own rules and a fee.
- The defendant is a large company that brings a lawyer with the court's leave and raises legal defences you cannot answer.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Filing in your home town when the event and defendant are elsewhere | Transfer or dismissal; delay | Ask to transfer to the right court | Apply reg. 2(א) before filing |
| Body of the claim over five pages, no witness list | Court asks you to amend; hearing delayed | File an amended claim quickly | Use the template's sections and count pages |
| Missing exhibits | You cannot rely on the document at the hearing without leave | Ask leave to submit it, with reasons | Attach everything at filing (reg. 3(ד)) |
| Not appearing at the hearing | Claim dismissed (reg. 11) | Apply to set aside promptly with reasons | Diary the hearing; ask in advance for a video hearing if needed |
| Missing the 30 days for leave to appeal | Judgment final | Rarely fixable | Diary the date the day judgment is given |

## Review

- Small claims court hears an individual's money claims up to a linked ceiling; companies can be sued but cannot sue there.
- Venue: the defendant's area or the place of the act (reg. 2); fee 1% with a minimum (reg. 6).
- Since April 2025 the claim must include documents and witness lists, hearing preferences, the number of recent small claims, a signed declaration, and stay within five pages.
- The judge runs the hearing actively and often proposes a compromise; know your lowest figure.
- Judgment comes within days; leave to appeal must be sought within 30 days.
