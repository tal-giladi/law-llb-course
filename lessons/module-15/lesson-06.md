# 15.6 · The preliminary hearing, settlement and the expedited procedure

Between the last pleading and the trial lies the stage where most civil cases actually end. The 2018 Regulations fill it with structured steps: a discussion between represented parties, a free mediation-intake meeting, a single list of motions, witness lists, and at most two or three pre-trial hearings in which the judge shapes the trial or proposes a compromise. Claims of modest value skip most of this on the fast track. By the end of this lesson you can build your pre-trial calendar, prepare for the hearing and know what the judge may do there.

## Why it matters

The pre-trial hearing is where the judge forms a first impression of the case and of you. It is also where the judge may propose a settlement, decide which witnesses will be heard and for how long, appoint a court expert, or even give judgment if the defence reveals no defence at all (reg. 63(ב)(16)). A party who arrives without having read the other side's documents, without a list of motions, or without a settlement range, loses ground it cannot recover.

The design of this stage reflects a policy choice: **managerial judging**. Under reg. 3 the court manages the case, and the pre-trial stage is where it does so. Critics worry that pressure to settle can push weaker parties into bad compromises; supporters point out that most parties prefer a fair settlement now to a slightly better judgment in two years. Your job is to know your numbers well enough to decide for yourself (lesson [11.5](../module-11/lesson-05.md)).

## The law

### The preliminary discussion: regs 34–36

> (א) בתוך שלושים ימים ממועד המצאת כתב הטענות האחרון ינהלו בעלי הדין, זולת בעל דין שאיננו מיוצג בידי עורך דין, דיון מקדמי…

*Gist:* Within a set period after the last pleading, represented parties must meet to define and narrow the issues, consider ADR and agree on steps to make the case efficient, showing each other documents and answering questions transparently; an unrepresented party is exempt. ([reg. 35](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

A report on Form 4 is filed no later than 20 days before the first pre-trial hearing, jointly or separately (reg. 36). A party who breaches these duties may be ordered to comply, may pay costs at once, and a plaintiff's case may be suspended or, for special reasons, struck out (reg. 38).

### The mediation-intake meeting: reg. 37

In claims above a set value (except personal-injury and road-accident claims), the court's mediation unit appoints a mediator within a set period after the last pleading, "whether or not the party is represented" (reg. 37(ב)). The meeting is free and held within a set period of the notice; an invitation counts as a court summons (reg. 37(ד)). The parties attend in person, a company through an officer who knows the dispute and has authority to agree to mediation (reg. 37(ט)). A party who does not attend pays the mediator's and the other parties' costs (reg. 37(י)). Mediation itself remains voluntary (Courts Law s. 79ג). The chapter does not apply to eviction claims, vehicle claims, and a few others (reg. 39). Threshold and periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### The list of motions: reg. 49

Between the last pleading and the first pre-trial hearing, no motions are filed except a closed list: interim relief, early testimony, stay for arbitration or a foreign clause, a defendant's medical examination, joinder of a party, consolidation, amendment, recusal, exemption from attending, approval of a settlement, and objections to a court expert (reg. 49(א)). Stay, examination, joinder, consolidation and amendment must be filed within 60 days of the last pleading (reg. 49(ב)). Every other motion goes on a list filed no later than 20 days before the first pre-trial hearing, one page per motion, after writing to the other side at least seven days earlier (reg. 49(ג)–(ה)). A motion not on the list needs leave later, given only if it could not have been listed or there is a reasonable excuse (reg. 49(ז)).

### Witness lists: reg. 62

> התובע יגיש עד עשרים ימים לפני ישיבת קדם־המשפט הראשונה והנתבע יגיש עד ארבעה עשר ימים לאחר מכן, את רשימת העדים שבכוונתו להזמין למתן עדות… הרשימה תכלול בצד כל עד פירוט תמציתי של הצורך והמטרה של העדתו…

*Gist:* The plaintiff files its witness list before the first pre-trial and the defendant after it, each with a short explanation of why each witness is needed; a witness may be listed without a name where there is a real fear of pressure on the witness. ([reg. 62](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

### The pre-trial hearing: regs 61 and 63

The pre-trial is set after the defence is filed and the time for the last pleading has passed (reg. 61(א)). The court may order the parties to attend in person (reg. 61(ב)); a hearing by video with an unrepresented party needs that party's consent (reg. 61(ג)); not attending is treated like not attending a trial (reg. 61(ד); lesson 15.7). As a rule the pre-trial stage should not exceed two sessions in the magistrates' court and three in the district court (reg. 63(א)). The judge's powers include (reg. 63(ב)):

- setting the stages and dates of the case, and how third-party notices and counterclaims are heard;
- deciding whether main evidence is oral or by affidavit (reg. 67);
- deciding the motions on the list;
- splitting the trial, limiting time for proof, fixing the order of witnesses and the length of examinations, and ruling that a witness is unnecessary;
- appointing a court expert;
- ordering amendments, defining the issues, giving interim relief;
- hearing on the spot anyone present in court and getting answers to questions about the dispute;
- striking out or dismissing the claim, or giving judgment, if the defence reveals no defence at all (63(ב)(16));
- proposing a compromise, or proposing that the court decide by way of compromise, or ADR (63(ב)(17)).

At the final pre-trial, the court sets the trial dates and times for each witness and for summations (reg. 63(ד)).

### Postponing a hearing: reg. 175א

> (ג) טעמים כגון שיקולי נוחות, הסכמת בעלי הדין, עומס עבודה, קיום משא ומתן לפשרה או הליך גישור שנמשך למעלה משישה חודשים… לא יהוו, ככלל, עילה מספקת לשינוי מועד הדיון.

*Gist:* Hearings are held on the set date; a request to postpone uses Form 10 with supporting documents, and convenience, the parties' consent, workload or long negotiations are not, as a rule, enough. ([reg. 175א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

### Settlement and compromise: Courts Law ss. 79א, 79ג

The court may, with the parties' consent, decide the case by way of compromise (s. 79א), and may with consent refer it to mediation (s. 79ג). A settlement may be given the force of a judgment. Lesson [08.6](../module-08/lesson-06.md) explains both; here the point is timing: the pre-trial is where the judge raises them (reg. 63(ב)(17)).

### The fast track: regs 78–82

> (1) תביעה שסכומה או שווי נושאה אינו עולה על 75,000 שקלים חדשים, זולת כל אלה: (א) תביעה שהוגשה בקשה לאשרה כתובענה ייצוגית; (ב) תביעה שעילתה בחוק פיצויים לנפגעי תאונות דרכים…; (ג) תובענה לפיצויים בשל נזק גוף, ובכלל זה תביעות לפי פוליסת ביטוח;

*Gist:* Claims up to the ceiling (other than class actions, road-accident and personal-injury claims) and claims to evict a tenant run on the fast track; a temporary order from 7.10.2026 to 1.10.2031 adds declaratory claims under s. 28 of the new law limiting fees for IDF and security-forces disabled veterans and victims of hostile action. ([reg. 78(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

The court may move a magistrates' court claim onto the fast track, or move a fast-track claim off it (reg. 78(ב)–(ג)). The special rules:

- **Pleadings:** each party attaches an affidavit on Form 5 verifying its pleading, a list of relevant documents with copies, expert opinions if needed, and its witness list (reg. 79(א)); the disclosure chapter does not apply (reg. 79(ב)).
- **Times:** a shorter defence period (reg. 80(א)); third-party notice only with leave (reg. 80(ב)); one pre-trial hearing, within a set period of the last defence (reg. 80(ג)); trial within a set period of the last defence (reg. 80(ד)); decisions on motions within seven days (reg. 80(ה)).
- **Trial:** one day, with oral summations on that day; written authorities up to five pages in total, 14 days before (reg. 80(ו)–(ז)).
- **Counterclaim:** only if it is itself fast-track, or arises from the same subject or circumstances (reg. 80(ח)).
- **Judgment:** at the end of the hearing or within 14 days, briefly reasoned (reg. 82).
- **Eviction claims:** no other remedies in the same claim (they may be sued for separately without leave), a short defence period, no counterclaim or third-party notice, no pre-trial, and a hearing within a set period of the defence (reg. 81).
- **Vehicle claims:** evidence is brought at the pre-trial hearing unless the court orders otherwise (reg. 81א).

Ceilings and periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

## Leading cases

### Key text: regs 49, 63 and 78–82

The pre-trial and fast-track rules are new, and their application lives mostly in thousands of short magistrates' and district-court decisions rather than in leading Supreme Court rulings. Read regs 49 and 63 in full before your first pre-trial hearing, and check the Courts Law ss. 79א–79ג for settlement. When you research, search for "תקנה 63(ב)(16)" or "תקנה 49(ז)" in free decision databases and read the most recent district-court decisions on appeal (lesson [02.2](../module-02/lesson-02.md)).

## How it works in practice

### Your pre-trial calendar (ordinary track)

| When | What | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 30 days after the last pleading | Preliminary discussion (represented parties only) | 35 |
| 30 days after the last pleading | Interrogatories, disclosure affidavits | 56–57 |
| 45 days after the last pleading | Mediation-intake mediator appointed (above threshold) | 37(ב) |
| 60 days after the last pleading | Last day for stay, joinder, consolidation, amendment motions | 49(ב) |
| 20 days before the first pre-trial | Plaintiff's witness list; list of motions; Form 4 report | 62, 49(ג), 36 |
| 14 days after the plaintiff's list | Defendant's witness list | 62 |
| First pre-trial | Motions decided; trial plan; settlement proposal | 63 |

Check every period against [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); count from service (reg. 179).

### Preparing for the hearing

1. A one-page note for yourself: the issues in dispute, what is admitted, what each witness will prove.
2. Your list of motions, already filed.
3. Your settlement range: your best realistic outcome, your worst, costs and time to trial (lesson [11.5](../module-11/lesson-05.md)). Decide your minimum before you enter the room.
4. Bring copies of every exhibit and the other side's key documents, tabbed.

### When the judge proposes a compromise

Ask for a short break, or a few days, to consider. Ask whether the proposal is a settlement you sign (s. 79א(ב)) or consent to let the judge decide "by way of compromise" (s. 79א(א)). In the second case you hand the judge a free hand on the outcome, without knowing the figure; having agreed, you cannot later complain that the result departs from strict law. Accept only if you understand both.

## Worked example

Daniel Gold, a Haredi bookbinder from Bnei Brak, sues a printing house for ₪62,000 for defective binding machines. Fast track (reg. 78(א)(1)).

1. **Pleading.** He attaches his Form 5 affidavit, a list of documents (invoices, emails, a technician's report), copies, and a witness list naming the technician (reg. 79(א)).
2. **Defence.** Due within the fast-track period (reg. 80(א)); the printer attaches its own affidavit and documents, and asks leave to bring in the machine's importer as a third party (reg. 80(ב)). The court refuses: the importer's role is marginal and would delay a fast-track case.
3. **Mediation intake.** The claim is above the threshold; a mediator invites them to a free meeting (reg. 37(ב)). They do not agree to mediate.
4. **One pre-trial.** Within the period of reg. 80(ג), at a single session, the judge narrows the dispute to whether the defect existed at delivery, appoints a court expert with costs shared, and sets a one-day trial (reg. 80(ד), (ו)).
5. **Settlement.** After the expert's report finds a manufacturing defect, the judge proposes ₪45,000. Daniel, who computed that a judgment of about ₪55,000 in four months, minus his time and the expert's fee, was worth about ₪47,000 to him, accepts ₪46,000 after a short negotiation. The settlement is given the force of a judgment.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A pending or planned case of your own (or Daniel's).
- Regs 35–39, 49, 61–63, 78–82 and 175א open.
- About 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Track (5 min).** Ordinary, fast track or eviction? Write the rule.
2. **Calendar (15 min).** Assume the last pleading was served on a date you choose. Fill in the calendar table with actual dates, including recess.
3. **Witness list (10 min).** Write a reg. 62 list of up to four witnesses, each with one sentence on why the witness is needed.
4. **Motions (5 min).** List any motion you would need. Mark which must be filed within 60 days and which go on the list.
5. **Settlement range (10 min).** Write your best, likely and worst outcomes, costs and time to trial, and your minimum acceptable figure.

### How you know it worked

- Your calendar shows a date for every step that applies to you, and omits reg. 35 if you are unrepresented.
- Each witness has a stated purpose.
- You know which of your motions are time-limited.
- You have a minimum figure written down before any hearing.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. As an unrepresented party you are exempt from the preliminary discussion (reg. 35(א)) but must attend the mediation-intake meeting (reg. 37(ב)) and the pre-trial hearing, and a video pre-trial needs your consent (reg. 61(ג)).

### Where and how

- Lists and requests are filed with the registry or on Net HaMishpat (lesson [08.8](../module-08/lesson-08.md)); the postponement request uses Form 10 (reg. 175א(ב)).
- Thresholds, ceilings and periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Free help: court information centre, volunteer desks ([where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md)).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The judge proposes deciding "by way of compromise" and you do not understand the consequences.
- The other side asks for judgment at the pre-trial because your defence is "empty".
- A court expert is appointed and the case turns on the report.
- The case is moved off the fast track into the ordinary track.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Not attending the mediation-intake meeting | Costs (reg. 37(י)) | Pay; ask for a new date if there was a reason | Treat the invitation as a court summons |
| No list of motions | Motions need leave later (reg. 49(ז)) | Ask leave with a reasonable excuse | Diary 20 days before the first pre-trial |
| Witness list without reasons | Witness may be refused at the pre-trial | Supplement the list at once | Write one line of purpose per witness |
| Asking to postpone because "both sides agree" | Refused (reg. 175א(ג)) | Attend | Postpone only for a real, documented reason |
| Accepting a compromise without numbers | Regret; limited appeal | None, once it is a judgment | Set your minimum beforehand |

## Review

- Represented parties hold a preliminary discussion; everyone above the threshold attends a free mediation-intake meeting (regs 35–37).
- Motions before the first pre-trial are a closed list; others go on the list of motions 20 days before, after a seven-day letter (reg. 49).
- The pre-trial judge manages the trial, decides motions, may appoint experts, propose compromise, and give judgment where there is no real defence (reg. 63).
- Postponements are the exception (reg. 175א).
- The fast track front-loads affidavits and documents, limits third parties and counterclaims, and aims at one pre-trial, a one-day trial and quick judgment (regs 78–82).
