# 15.2 · Pleadings under the 2018 Regulations: claim, defence, reply and amendment

Pleadings are where a civil case is won or lost on paper: they fix what is in dispute, what is admitted and what each side may prove. Lesson 08.2 gave you the three-part structure and the page limits. This lesson goes further: what each part must and may not contain, how the registry screens a pleading before it is even filed, how deemed admission really works, what a reply may and may not do, how to amend, and how the courts deal with abusive pleadings. By the end you can draft a claim and a defence that survive both the registry and the other side.

## Why it matters

Under the 1984 rules a pleading was a long narrative and the real dispute surfaced only at trial. The 2018 Regulations reverse this. A pleading is now short and structured, the summary comes first because the judge reads it first, documents are attached at once, and facts not denied in detail are admitted. The registry can refuse to accept a document that breaks the form rules (reg. 33(ב)), and a refused document is treated as never filed. For a self-represented party this is the most dangerous stage: there is no warning before deemed admission takes effect, and a claim refused on the last day of the limitation period may be barred.

Two debates frame the rules. **Fact pleading versus notice pleading:** Israeli law requires the material facts, not just the legal conclusion, so the other side can answer; the reg. 9(ד) page limits push towards brevity without abandoning fact pleading. **Access versus abuse:** the right of access to court protects even weak claims, but reg. 4 and the strike-out powers protect defendants and the system from claims meant to harass. The Supreme Court's 2025 SLAPP decision (below) shows the balance moving.

## The law

### Structure and limits: reg. 9

> (ג) כתב תביעה וכתב הגנה יכללו שלושה חלקים לפי הסדר המפורט להלן: (1) כותרת; (2) תמצית הטענות; (3) פירוט הטענות.

*Gist:* Every claim and defence has a heading, a summary and a detailed statement, in that order. ([reg. 9(ג)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

Page limits differ by court and type: in the magistrates' court two pages for the summary and nine for the detail; longer in the district court and longer still in large money claims and personal-injury claims there (reg. 9(ד); figures in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).

### What each part contains: regs 10–14

The heading of a claim contains "these details only, in this order" (reg. 10): court, plaintiff's name and ID, lawyer if any, plaintiff's address and contacts (withheld in claims for sexual offences, violence or harassment), defendant's name and ID if obtainable, defendant's address, whether a party is a minor, lacks capacity or is a corporation, the type and subject of the claim from the Courts Director's list, every remedy and the value of the subject matter, the fee and the fee item, any related proceeding, and the summons (Form 2). The defence heading adds the case number and the last date for filing the defence (reg. 12, reg. 30).

The claim's summary states, briefly: the parties, the remedy, the facts that found the cause of action and when it arose, and the facts that give the court jurisdiction (reg. 11). The defence's summary states preliminary arguments, then the answer to each cause of action in order, then arguments on the remedy (reg. 13).

> (א) חלקו השלישי של כתב הטענות יכלול את פירוט העובדות המשמשות יסוד לכתב הטענות וכל מידע נוסף, שתכליתו לסייע בהבהרת המחלוקת ובמיקוד הפלוגתות שבין בעלי הדין.
> (ב) הנתבע ייחשב מודה בכל העובדות הכלולות בכתב התביעה, זולת אלה שהכחיש באופן מפורש ומפורט בחלקו השלישי של כתב ההגנה, ואולם לעניין שיעור דמי נזק יראו אותו כשנוי במחלוקת זולת אם הנתבע הודה בו במפורש.

*Gist:* The detail sets out the facts and anything that clarifies the dispute; a defendant admits every fact not denied expressly and in detail, except the amount of damages, which stays disputed unless expressly admitted. ([reg. 14](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

### Attachments: reg. 15

Attach "these documents only": a copy of every material document the pleading rests on (or say where it is), any medical expert opinion you will rely on (or a request for a court expert), and in personal-injury claims a medical-privacy waiver (Forms 1–1ב) and the questionnaire affidavit (reg. 15(א), reg. 17). A non-medical expert opinion may be attached (reg. 15(ב)). Originals are not attached, only copies (reg. 70(א)).

### The reply: reg. 18

> (א) תובע רשאי להגיש כתב תשובה לכתב ההגנה בתוך ארבעה עשר ימים מיום שהומצא לו כתב ההגנה; כתב תשובה לא יכלול נימוק תביעה חדש או כל טענה שאינה מתיישבת עם טענותיו הקודמות של בעל דין; לא הגיש התובע כתב תשובה יראו אותו כמכחיש את כל הטענות שנטענו בכתב ההגנה, ובלבד שהתובע לא הודה בהן במפורש.

*Gist:* A reply is optional, short, and may not add a new cause of action or contradict earlier pleadings; without one, the plaintiff is treated as denying the defence. ([reg. 18](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

Note the asymmetry with reg. 14(ב): a defendant's silence admits, a plaintiff's silence denies. A reply is still useful when the defence raises new facts (a payment, a release, limitation) that you want to answer with your own facts, for example an acknowledgment that restarts limitation (lesson 15.9).

### The registry as gatekeeper: reg. 33

> (ג) לא התקבל המסמך לרישום, יראו אותו כאילו לא הוגש מלכתחילה, ואולם יהיה ניתן לתקן את הפגם עד היום האחרון להגשתו או בתוך שבעה ימים מהמועד שלא קובל, לפי המאוחר…

*Gist:* A document refused for breaking the listed form rules (regs 9(ג)–(ד), 10, 12, 15(א)(3)–(4), 18(ב), the appeal-form rules and the Courts Director's technical directions) is treated as not filed, but may be cured within a short period. ([reg. 33(ב)–(ג)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

If the refused document is a statement of claim and refusal could bar the claim by limitation, it is treated as accepted provided the defect is cured within seven days (reg. 33(ד)). A party who thinks the refusal is wrong may ask, within seven days, for a judge to decide (reg. 33(ה)). Reg. 33 has been amended repeatedly, including four times in 2024–2025, so check the current list of rules it enforces before filing.

### Form and tone: regs 27, 30–32

No contradictory or alternative facts without an affidavit that you do not know the truth (reg. 27); state the last filing date in the margin of the heading (reg. 30); follow the Courts Director's technical directions on format (reg. 31); and draft "in a way that is not insulting, shaming or abusive" (reg. 32).

### Amendment: regs 46–47

> (א) בית המשפט רשאי בכל עת להורות כי יתוקן כל עניין בכתב טענות… לשם קיומו של הליך שיפוטי ראוי והוגן, תוך התחשבות, בין השאר, בהתנהלותו של מבקש התיקון, השלב הדיוני שבו מוגשת הבקשה, והמטרה שהתיקון המבוקש צפוי להשיג.

*Gist:* The court may allow any amendment at any time for a fair process, weighing the applicant's conduct, the stage and the purpose; the amended pleading is filed within 20 days, and the court decides whether the other side may amend in reply. ([regs 46–47](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

A request to amend is one of the motions allowed before the first pre-trial hearing, within 60 days of the last pleading (reg. 49(א)(7), (ב)); after that it goes on the list of motions or needs leave (lesson 15.6).

### Ending a claim early: regs 41–45

| Power | Grounds | Effect on a new claim |
|---|---|---|
| Striking out (מחיקה), reg. 41 | No cause of action; vexatious; persistent non-compliance; any other reason | Not res judicata (reg. 41(ב)); a new claim continues from the same stage unless the court orders otherwise |
| Strike-out for abuse, reg. 42 | Abuse of process | All or part of the pleading |
| Dismissal (דחייה), reg. 43 | Res judicata, limitation, or any other reason | Final: bars a new claim on the same cause |
| Discontinuance, reg. 44 | Plaintiff's request | By striking out, so reg. 41(ב)–(ג) applies |
| Administrative strike-out, reg. 45 | Six months of inactivity, after a 20-day warning | Can be reversed on request within 14 days for special reasons |

### Fees: two halves

In a claim for a fixed sum in the magistrates' or district court the fee is paid in two halves: the first on filing, the second, linked, up to 20 days before the first hearing; part is refunded if the case ends early by settlement, withdrawal, mediation or arbitration, and the second half is not due after a default judgment ([Fees Regulations reg. 6](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_בתי_המשפט_(אגרות)); amounts in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).

## Leading cases

### רע״א 1954/24 וקנין נ׳ קיבוץ ניר דוד (7.1.2025)

A kibbutz sued an activist for defamation over Facebook posts in the public campaign over access to the Asi stream. The magistrates' and district courts refused summary dismissal. On leave in a third instance, the Supreme Court (Sohlberg J., Stein J. concurring, Acting President Amit dissenting) held that the claim was a SLAPP (תביעת השתקה), that such claims are an abuse of process, and that the court may dismiss them at the threshold or award costs closely correlated with the sum claimed. The majority listed indicators: a power gap between the parties, a baseless or borderline cause of action, an inflated damages claim, a selective choice of defendants, statements made in a debate of public interest, and the plaintiff's overall conduct.

**Why it matters:** it is the leading authority on using regs 4, 41 and 42 against a claim whose purpose is to silence, and a rare grant of leave in a third instance (lesson 15.8). Free summary: [Hebrew Wikipedia, תביעת השתקה](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/תביעת_השתקה) (⚠️ secondary source; the full text is on the Judiciary's site, which could not be reached for this course).

### בש״א 6479/06 בנק דיסקונט לישראל בע״מ נ׳ שנפ (15.1.2007)

Decided under the old rules, this decision states the standard the 2018 Regulations now codify in regs 3–4: a litigant must act as a reasonable and decent litigant would, protecting its own interest while respecting the other parties' justified procedural expectations and its duties to the court. Free summary and quotation: [Hebrew Wikipedia, שימוש לרעה בהליכי משפט](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/שימוש_לרעה_בהליכי_משפט) (⚠️ secondary source).

**Why it matters:** procedural good faith is not a slogan. It is the yardstick for costs (reg. 151(ג)), for strike-out for abuse (reg. 42), and for whether an amendment is allowed (reg. 46(א), "the applicant's conduct").

## How it works in practice

### Drafting order

1. Write the **detail** first, as numbered paragraphs, one fact each, with the date and the exhibit number.
2. Then write the **summary** from the detail: the parties, the remedy, the facts that found each cause and when it arose, jurisdiction. Count the pages.
3. Write the **heading** last, from the reg. 10 list, in order. Compute the fee.
4. Number the exhibits and attach copies only.
5. Read the whole pleading as the defendant: which paragraph would you deny, and how?

### Denials that work

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| "The defendant denies paragraphs 3–9." | "Paragraph 4 is denied. The defendant did not receive the goods on 3.3.2026; the delivery note (exhibit 2 to the claim) is signed by a person who is not its employee. The goods arrived on 17.3.2026 (exhibit B)." |
| "The defendant has no knowledge of paragraph 6." | "Paragraph 6 is not admitted and is denied: the defendant was not party to the conversation described; it was conducted with an independent contractor, Mr. X." |

A bare "no knowledge" is risky: it is not an express and detailed denial of a fact within your own sphere.

### Amendment in practice

Ask early, explain why the new fact or remedy was not pleaded, attach the amended pleading with changes marked, and offer to pay the other side's costs of the amendment. The later the stage, the harder the court will look at your conduct.

## Worked example

Michal Ben-Ami, a dental hygienist from Ashdod, lent her cousin Tomer ₪48,000 by bank transfer in 2024 to open a café. He repaid ₪8,000. She sues for ₪40,000 in the Ashdod magistrates' court (fast track, lesson 15.6).

1. **Detail first.** Twelve paragraphs: the request on WhatsApp (exhibit 1), the transfer (exhibit 2), the ₪8,000 repayment with the bank reference "החזר הלוואה" (exhibit 3), her demand letter (exhibit 4), his reply "אשלם כשהעסק יתאושש" (exhibit 5).
2. **Summary.** One page: parties, remedy ₪40,000 plus interest, the loan and its date, the demand date as the date the cause arose, venue (defendant lives in Ashdod).
3. **Heading.** In reg. 10 order, with the fee item for a fixed-sum claim and the first half of the fee.
4. **Registry.** The first filing is refused: her summary ran to three pages. She cuts it to two and refiles the next day, within the cure period (reg. 33(ג)); the claim keeps its place.
5. **The defence.** Tomer's lawyer writes: "The transfer was a gift to the business. Paragraphs 1–12 are denied." Michal notes that the summary-level denial does not deny, in detail, the ₪8,000 repayment marked "החזר הלוואה" or his written reply; those facts are deemed admitted (reg. 14(ב)) and point strongly to a loan.
6. **Reply.** Within 14 days she files a two-page reply: no new cause, only facts answering the "gift" story (the bank reference, his message). She does not add a new claim for the café equipment she later remembers lending; that would need an amendment (reg. 46), not a reply (reg. 18(א)).

## Do it yourself

### You need

- The facts and documents of a money dispute of your own (or Michal's case).
- Regs 9–18 and 33 open; the [statement of claim template](../../templates/statement-of-claim-magistrates.md) and the [statement of defence template](../../templates/statement-of-defence.md).
- About an hour.

### Steps

1. **Detail (20 min).** Write at least eight numbered factual paragraphs with dates and exhibit numbers.
2. **Summary (10 min).** Reduce it to the four reg. 11 items. Check: is the date the cause arose stated?
3. **Heading (10 min).** Fill in the reg. 10 items in order; mark "[to check]" where unknown.
4. **Registry check (5 min).** Run the reg. 33(ב) list: three parts in order, page limits, heading items, required forms.
5. **The defence (15 min).** Write three denials of your own paragraphs that satisfy reg. 14(ב), and one that would fail.

- [Statement of claim, magistrates' court](../../templates/statement-of-claim-magistrates.md)
- [Statement of defence](../../templates/statement-of-defence.md)

### How you know it worked

- Your summary fits the page limit and contains only the reg. 11 items.
- Every factual paragraph has a date and, where possible, an exhibit.
- Your strong denials each give your own version of the fact.
- You can name what the registry would refuse and why.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. You may draft and file your own claim, defence and reply (reg. 170); the registry serves them for you (reg. 160). The form rules apply to you exactly as to a lawyer, and the registry will refuse a non-compliant document.

### Where and how

- The registry of the court, or Net HaMishpat where unrepresented filing is available (lesson [08.8](../module-08/lesson-08.md); ⚠️ check on the day).
- Fee: first half on filing; a fee exemption for inability to pay is requested with the first filing ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- The Courts Director's technical directions on document format are published on the Judiciary's site (reg. 31; ⚠️ manual check).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The defence raises limitation, res judicata or a release you signed.
- The other side moves to strike out or dismiss your claim at the threshold.
- You need to amend late in the case or the amendment adds a new party.
- You suspect the claim against you is a SLAPP and the sum claimed is large.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Summary longer than the limit | Registry refuses; treated as not filed (reg. 33) | Cure within the period in reg. 33(ג) | Count pages before uploading |
| Blanket denial | Facts deemed admitted (reg. 14(ב)) | Ask leave to amend quickly (reg. 46) | Deny paragraph by paragraph, with your version |
| New cause of action in the reply | Struck from the reply (reg. 18(א)) | Apply to amend the claim | Use the reply only to answer new facts |
| Filing on the last day of limitation and being refused | Claim may be barred | Cure within seven days to keep the filing date (reg. 33(ד)) | File well before the deadline |
| Insulting language about the other side | Strike-out of passages, costs (regs 32, 42, 151(ג)) | Withdraw and apologise in an amended pleading | Write facts, not adjectives |

## Review

- A pleading has three parts in fixed order, with page limits and a closed list of heading items (regs 9–13).
- A defendant admits what it does not deny expressly and in detail; damages stay disputed; a plaintiff without a reply is treated as denying (regs 14(ב), 18(א)).
- The registry refuses non-compliant documents; a refused claim keeps its date only if cured within seven days (reg. 33).
- Amendment is discretionary and depends on conduct, stage and purpose (reg. 46); striking out is not res judicata, dismissal is (regs 41, 43).
- Abusive claims can be struck out or met with heavy costs; the 2025 SLAPP decision applies this to claims meant to silence (regs 4, 42; רע״א 1954/24).
