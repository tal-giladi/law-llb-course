# 15.4 · Disclosure, inspection of documents and interrogatories

Before the trial, each side may make the other show its documents and answer questions on oath. Justice Haim Cohn once compared civil litigation to poker; modern procedure insists that the cards go face up before the main hand. This lesson covers the three disclosure tools of the 2018 Regulations (interrogatories, disclosure of documents and inspection), privilege, and the sanctions for a party who hides documents. By the end you can send a proper interrogatory, prepare your own disclosure affidavit, and decide what to do when the other side stonewalls.

## Why it matters

Most civil cases turn on documents: contracts, emails, invoices, bank statements, medical files. The party with the documents (often the company, the bank, the insurer) has an advantage that disclosure is meant to cancel. Disclosure also drives settlement: once each side sees the other's file, the range of likely outcomes narrows. For a self-represented litigant disclosure is both an opportunity (you can make the company produce its internal records) and a risk (you must disclose your own documents, including the unhelpful ones, and a document you hid may be excluded or may cost you the case).

The policy debate is about **cost and proportionality**. Broad disclosure finds the truth but can be used to bury a weaker opponent in work. The 2018 Regulations answer by deadlines, a cap of 25 questions, page limits, the duty to approach the other side before going to court, and by abolishing separate disclosure on the fast track, where documents are attached to the pleadings instead.

## The law

### Interrogatories: reg. 56

> (א) לא יאוחר משלושים ימים לאחר שהוגש כתב הטענות האחרון או בתוך מועד אחר שיורה בית המשפט, בעל דין רשאי לשלוח לבעל דין אחר שאלון שיכלול שאלות ובלבד שהן נוגעות לעניין הנדון ולא די שיהיו קבילות בחקירה שכנגד של עד בעל פה ורשאי בעל דין להציג מסמך הנוגע לדברים הנדונים חלף מענה לשאלון; התשובה לשאלון תינתן בתצהיר בתוך שלושים ימים מיום מסירת השאלון…

*Gist:* Within a set period after the last pleading a party may send another party written questions; they must be relevant, and it is not enough that they would be allowed in cross-examination; the answer is given by affidavit within a set period, and a relevant document may be produced instead of an answer. ([reg. 56(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

The interrogatory is capped at 25 questions including sub-questions, or 50 in large district-court money claims and district-court personal-injury and road-accident claims (reg. 56(ב)). At trial you may use some answers without the rest, unless the court finds them inseparable (reg. 56(ד)).

### Disclosure and inspection: regs 57–58

> לא יאוחר משלושים ימים לאחר שהוגש כתב הטענות האחרון, או במועד אחר שיורה בית המשפט, יחליפו בעלי הדין תצהירי גילוי מסמכים המאמתים את רשימת כל המסמכים הנוגעים לעניינים השנויים במחלוקת, המצויים או שהיו מצויים ברשותו או בשליטתו של בעל הדין ושאיתר בעל הדין לאחר חקירה ודרישה…

*Gist:* Disclosure is now automatic and mutual: within a set period after the last pleading, each party gives an affidavit listing every document relevant to the issues that is or was in its possession or control and that it found after reasonable inquiry, explaining what happened to documents no longer held. ([reg. 57](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

Inspection follows within a set period after the exchange: each side lets the other copy or scan, at the cost of the party asking (reg. 58). All periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### Disputes go on the list of motions: reg. 59 and reg. 49

Any request about disclosure, inspection or answers to an interrogatory goes on the list of motions filed no later than 20 days before the first pre-trial hearing (reg. 59(א), reg. 49(ג)). Before listing a motion you must write to the other side at least seven days in advance (reg. 49(ד)). If privilege is claimed, the court may inspect the document itself to decide (reg. 59(ב)).

### Sanctions: reg. 60

> (א) הליכי גילוי ועיון נאותים מהווים תנאי בסיסי לקיומו של הליך שיפוטי ראוי והוגן; סבר בית המשפט שבעל דין לא קיים כראוי את חובתו לפי פרק זה, רשאי הוא לחייבו באופן מיידי בהוצאות ובמקרים מיוחדים אף למחוק את כתב טענותיו.

*Gist:* Proper disclosure is a basic condition of a fair process; a party who breaches its duties can be ordered to pay costs at once and, in special cases, have its pleading struck out. ([reg. 60(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

A party who does not disclose a document, or refuses inspection, may not use it as its own evidence without leave, given only for a reasonable excuse; and if the hidden document harms its case, the court may strike out its claim or defence, or at least order costs (reg. 60(ג)).

### Privilege: Evidence Ordinance ss. 44–51

> (א) דברים ומסמכים שהוחלפו בין עורך דין לבין לקוחו או לבין אדם אחר מטעם הלקוח ויש להם קשר ענייני לשירות המקצועי שניתן על ידי עורך הדין ללקוח, אין עורך הדין חייב למסרם כראיה, אלא אם ויתר הלקוח על החסיון…

*Gist:* Communications between lawyer and client about the professional service are privileged unless the client waives. ([Evidence Ordinance s. 48](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_הראיות))

Other statutory privileges cover state security and public interest (ss. 44–46), self-incrimination (s. 47), doctors and psychologists (ss. 49–50, which the court may lift where the need for justice outweighs privacy), and clergy (s. 51). Privilege must be claimed specifically, document by document, not as a blanket.

### Fast track and medical claims: regs 79 and 87

On the fast track the disclosure chapter does not apply (except the court's power to inspect privileged documents); instead each party attaches to its pleading an affidavit and a list of relevant documents with copies of those it holds, and reg. 60 applies to a document it failed to attach (reg. 79). In medical matters the defendant may demand that the plaintiff be examined by the defendant's expert (reg. 87(ב)).

### Can you appeal a disclosure decision at once?

Item 10 of the 2009 no-leave order bars leave to appeal against decisions under the disclosure chapter of the **1984** Regulations, except decisions on disclosure of a particular document, inspection and privilege. Whether and how it applies to the 2018 Regulations has not been amended in the order's text ([no-leave order](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/צו_בתי_המשפט_(סוגי_החלטות_שלא_תינתן_בהן_רשות_ערעור)); ⚠️ check current case law). In any case, leave on interim decisions is limited by Courts Law s. 52(ב) (lesson 15.8).

## Leading cases

### ע״א 127/95 מועצת הפירות נ׳ מהדרין, פ״ד נא(4) 334

As a rule a party need not create documents or prepare accounts to answer an interrogatory. The Supreme Court recognised an exception where the requesting party has a strong cause of action or defence and the relationship between the parties is special, such as a fiduciary relationship, that would normally require giving such information.

**Why it matters:** an employee, partner, beneficiary or principal can sometimes make the other side prepare an account, not just hand over existing papers. Decided under the 1984 rules. Free summary: [Hebrew Wikipedia, גילוי מוקדם](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/גילוי_מוקדם_(משפטים)) (⚠️ secondary source; full text not opened).

### רע״א 4217/96 כל אל ערב נ׳ אלסינארה, פ״ד נ(1) 437

Under the old rules the Supreme Court held that the party harmed by non-disclosure may choose between the two sanctions: excluding the hidden document, or striking out the defaulting party's pleading. Reg. 60(ג) now writes both options into the text and links the choice to whether the document helps or harms the party who hid it.

**Why it matters:** if the other side hides a document that helps it, ask for exclusion; if it hides one that harms it, ask for strike-out or costs. Free summary: [Hebrew Wikipedia, גילוי מוקדם](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/גילוי_מוקדם_(משפטים)) (⚠️ secondary source; full text not opened).

## How it works in practice

### The disclosure calendar (ordinary track)

| Day (from the last pleading) | Step | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Up to the reg. 56 period | Send the interrogatory | 56(א) |
| Up to the reg. 57 period | Exchange disclosure affidavits | 57 |
| Answer period from receipt | Answer interrogatory by affidavit | 56(א) |
| Up to the reg. 58 period after the exchange | Inspection and copies | 58 |
| 7 days before listing | Write to the other side about any dispute | 49(ד) |
| 20 days before the first pre-trial | File the list of motions, including disclosure disputes | 49(ג), 59(א) |

Exact periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### A good interrogatory

- One fact per question, about the issues in dispute: "On what date did your company receive the returned goods? Attach the warehouse record."
- No argument, no law, no questions you could ask only to embarrass ("Do you admit you are a liar?").
- Count sub-questions: "(a) when, (b) by whom, (c) where" is three.
- Ask for documents by description and date range, not "all documents".

### Your own disclosure affidavit

List every relevant document in your possession or control (emails, WhatsApp, bank statements, photos), grouped and dated; mark privileged ones with the ground; and say what happened to relevant documents you no longer have (a phone that was replaced). Do not leave out the unhelpful ones: you may lose the use of what you hide, and a harmful hidden document can sink your case (reg. 60(ג)).

## Worked example

Hadas Cohen, a pharmacist from Modi'in, sues her former landlord, Yigal Asulin, for the return of a ₪15,000 deposit and ₪3,000 for a broken boiler she replaced. He defends: the flat was left damaged, and he spent ₪20,000 on repairs. The case is on the fast track (₪18,000).

1. **Fast track: no interrogatory.** Hadas cannot send a reg. 56 interrogatory; on the fast track each side attaches documents to its pleading (reg. 79). She attached the lease, the bank transfer of the deposit, the boiler invoice and photos from the day she left.
2. **The landlord's list.** His defence lists "repair invoices" but attaches only a summary sheet. Hadas writes to him at least seven days before any motion, as reg. 49(ד) requires, asking for the invoices themselves.
3. **No answer.** At the single pre-trial hearing she asks the court to apply reg. 60 by way of reg. 79(ב): he cannot rely on invoices he did not attach unless he shows a reasonable excuse.
4. **Outcome.** The court gives him seven days to produce the invoices, with costs. Two of them turn out to be dated before Hadas moved in. The case settles at ₪14,000 to Hadas.

On the ordinary track the same dispute would have run through a disclosure affidavit (reg. 57), inspection (reg. 58) and an interrogatory of up to 25 questions (reg. 56).

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A dispute of your own (or Hadas's case moved to the ordinary track).
- Regs 56–60 and Evidence Ordinance ss. 44–51 open.
- About 50 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Your documents (15 min).** List every document relevant to the dispute in your possession or control. Mark each helpful, neutral or harmful. Mark any privilege, with the section.
2. **Disclosure affidavit (10 min).** Turn the list into the body of a reg. 57 affidavit, in the first person, with a paragraph on documents you no longer have.
3. **Interrogatory (15 min).** Write an interrogatory of no more than 12 questions to the other side. Count sub-questions. Strike out any question that is argument rather than fact.
4. **The stonewall (10 min).** Assume the other side answers six questions with "irrelevant". Write the seven-day letter required before listing a motion (reg. 49(ד)), and one line for the list of motions.

### Template

- [Written motion (בקשה בכתב)](../../templates/written-motion.md) for a disclosure motion on the list of motions.

### How you know it worked

- Your list includes the harmful documents.
- Each question asks for one relevant fact or document.
- You know the date by which your list of motions must be filed.
- You can name the sanction you would ask for, and why.

### Self-check

- [ ] I know disclosure of documents is now mutual and automatic on the ordinary track.
- [ ] I know the 25-question cap counts sub-questions.
- [ ] I know privilege must be claimed specifically and the court may look at the document.
- [ ] I know the fast track replaces disclosure with documents attached to the pleadings.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. You may send and answer interrogatories and exchange disclosure affidavits yourself (reg. 170). Answers and disclosure are given by affidavit, which must be signed before a lawyer or another person authorised to take affidavits (Evidence Ordinance s. 15; lesson 15.7). Making a false statement in an affidavit is a criminal offence.

### Where and how

- Interrogatories and disclosure affidavits go directly to the other side (through the registry if you are unrepresented, reg. 160); disputes go on the list of motions (reg. 49(ג)).
- Periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Free help: court information centre; Legal Aid if eligible ([where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md)).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The other side claims privilege over key documents or seeks your medical or psychological file.
- You are asked for documents that might incriminate you (Evidence Ordinance s. 47).
- The other side moves to strike out your pleading for non-disclosure.
- Disclosure involves large volumes of electronic data or a company's internal systems.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leaving a harmful document off your list | Strike-out or costs (reg. 60(ג)) | Disclose it now with an explanation | Disclose everything relevant |
| Not disclosing a helpful document | Cannot use it without leave (reg. 60(ג)) | Ask leave, showing a reasonable excuse | Build the list from your whole file |
| 40-question interrogatory | Objection; questions over the cap struck (reg. 56(ב)) | Send a compliant one if still in time | Count sub-questions |
| Going to court without writing first | Motion refused (reg. 49(ד)) | Write the seven-day letter and re-list | Always write first |
| Blanket "privileged" claim | Overruled; court may inspect (reg. 59(ב)) | Claim document by document | Name the section for each item |

## Review

- On the ordinary track: interrogatory (up to 25 questions), mutual disclosure affidavits and inspection, each within a set period after the last pleading (regs 56–58).
- Disputes go on the list of motions after a seven-day letter (regs 49, 59).
- Hide a helpful document and you cannot use it; hide a harmful one and you may lose your pleading (reg. 60).
- Privilege comes from the Evidence Ordinance and is claimed item by item; the court may inspect.
- On the fast track documents are attached to the pleadings instead (reg. 79).
