# 15.3 · Service, default judgment and setting it aside

Every deadline in civil procedure runs from service, so a party who does not understand service does not know their own deadlines. This lesson covers the rules of service under the 2018 Regulations (electronic, mail, personal, substituted, abroad), what happens when a defendant does not answer, and how a defendant who missed the defence period can ask to set aside the judgment. By the end you can compute when you were served, and write a set-aside request that a judge can grant.

## Why it matters

Default judgments are among the most common judgments in the magistrates' courts. Many are correct: the debtor had no defence. But some are given against people who never learned of the claim, because it went to an old address, or who read an SMS and did not open the link, or who thought "I'll deal with it after the holiday". A default judgment is enforceable at the Execution Office like any other (lesson [08.7](../module-08/lesson-07.md)), and it may later prevent you from disputing facts it decided.

The doctrine balances two values. **Finality:** a plaintiff who did everything right is entitled to a quick end, and defendants should not be rewarded for ignoring the court. **The right to be heard:** a judgment without a hearing is the exception, and courts are reluctant to let a person lose a good defence over a procedural slip. The law resolves this with two routes to set aside, one almost automatic and one discretionary.

## The law

### The purpose of service: reg. 158

> (א) מטרת ההמצאה היא להביא לידיעת הנמען את תוכנו של מסמך שנדרש שיהיה בידיעתו, ולעניין כתב הטענות הראשון המוגש – גם להחיל על הנתבע את מרות בית המשפט.
> (ב) הנמען ייחשב כמי שיודע את תוכנו של מסמך אם הומצא לו לפי תקנות אלה.

*Gist:* Service brings a document to the addressee's knowledge and, for the first pleading, subjects the defendant to the court; lawful service counts as knowledge whether or not you read it. ([reg. 158](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

### Who serves: reg. 160

A represented party's lawyer serves copies on the other side within three days of filing; an unrepresented party files with the registry, "which will act to serve it" (reg. 160).

### The methods and when they take effect: reg. 161

- **Electronic:** from the court, by an electronic notice with a link to Net HaMishpat sent to the email in your pleading, and for an unrepresented party also by SMS to the mobile number in the pleading. For an unrepresented recipient of a court notice, service takes effect when the document is **opened**, as shown by the system clock; a document sent or opened after 17:00 on Sunday to Thursday, or on Friday or a rest day, counts as served on the next working day (reg. 161(1)(ד)). An affidavit that it was not received or received later displaces the presumption (reg. 161(1)(ה)).
- **Fax:** documents of up to 15 pages, not the first document or a claim or defence; after 15:00 counts as next working day (reg. 161(2)).
- **Registered mail with delivery confirmation:** to the address in the pleading, or the known home or business address; the date on the confirmation is the date of service; a letter to the address you gave that was not collected is treated as served unless the court orders otherwise (reg. 161(3)).
- **Personal delivery:** by a lawyer or an authorised process server, who signs a delivery-verification notice; if the recipient refuses, or no one is found despite reasonable diligence, leaving it visibly by the door counts (reg. 161(4)).

### Exceptions to electronic service: reg. 162

The first document in a case and a statement of claim are served by mail or personal delivery unless the recipient agreed otherwise in writing; and a party who is not the court may not serve an unrepresented party electronically without that party's written consent (reg. 162(1)–(2)). The Judiciary publishes a consent form for unrepresented parties who want to receive documents by email (⚠️ manual check on the Judiciary's site).

### On whom: reg. 163

Serve the person if possible, or their lawyer if represented. If the person cannot be found, an adult family member living with them at home suffices (reg. 163(ב)). The State is served at the district attorney's office; a company at its office or registered address; a local authority at the head's office or its legal adviser; a prisoner through the Prison Service; a foreign state under the Foreign States Immunity Law (reg. 163(ד)–(ט)).

### Substituted service: reg. 164

> (א) נוכח בית המשפט שאי־אפשר להמציא מסמך לנמען בדרך שנקבעה, רשאי הוא להורות על המצאתו בכל דרך אחרת הנראית לו מתאימה בנסיבות העניין.

*Gist:* If service in the prescribed way is impossible, the court may order any suitable method, on a written request with an affidavit and a Population Registry printout showing the efforts made. ([reg. 164](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

### Service abroad: regs 166–168

A pleading may be served out of Israel only on one of the grounds in reg. 166: among them a defendant domiciled in Israel, land in Israel, a contract made, breached or governed by Israeli law, an act or omission in Israel, damage in Israel from a product or service of an internationally active defendant, an injunction about acts in Israel, enforcement of a foreign judgment or award, and a necessary or proper party to a claim properly brought against someone else. The plaintiff applies in writing with an affidavit (reg. 167). A defendant served abroad may contest jurisdiction or plead forum non conveniens by a written application no later than the defence date; the defence period then runs from the decision (reg. 168).

### Counting days: reg. 179

Periods run from the day of service, or from the hearing if the decision was given in the parties' presence; court recess days are not counted unless the court orders otherwise (reg. 179). Some rules override this: recess days do count for curing a refused filing (reg. 33(ג)) and for hearings on interim relief (reg. 97(ה)–(ו)).

### No defence: reg. 130

> נתבע שלא הגיש כתב הגנה בתוך המועד שנקבע לכך או שכתב ההגנה נמחק, רשאי בית המשפט לדרוש מהתובע הוכחה מספקת של התביעה, כולה או מקצתה, או לתת פסק דין על יסוד כתב התביעה בלבד.

*Gist:* If no defence is filed in time, or the defence is struck out, the court may ask the plaintiff for proof or give judgment on the claim alone. ([reg. 130](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

The court is not obliged to give judgment automatically; for unliquidated damages it often asks for proof. Costs in a default judgment follow the Bar's minimum recommended tariff unless the court orders otherwise (reg. 154).

### Setting aside: reg. 131

> נתן בית המשפט החלטה לפי צד אחד והגיש בעל הדין שנגדו ניתנה ההחלטה בקשת ביטול בתוך שלושים ימים מיום שהומצאה לו ההחלטה, רשאי בית המשפט לבטלה, בתנאים שייראו לו…

*Gist:* A party against whom a decision was given in its absence may apply within 30 days of service of the decision; the court may set it aside on conditions it sees fit. ([reg. 131](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

The appeal period against such a decision runs from service of the decision on the set-aside request (reg. 137(ג)), so applying to set aside does not cost you the appeal. A decision granting a set-aside request cannot be challenged by leave to appeal ([no-leave order, item 7](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/צו_בתי_המשפט_(סוגי_החלטות_שלא_תינתן_בהן_רשות_ערעור))); only a refusal can.

### The two routes in the case law

The courts distinguish (summary on [Kol-Zchut](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/בקשה_לביטול_פסק_דין_שניתן_בהיעדר_הגנה_במעמד_צד_אחד), ⚠️ manual check):

1. **As of right (מחובת הצדק):** if the judgment is defective, typically because the claim was never lawfully served, it is set aside without weighing the merits.
2. **By discretion:** if service was lawful, the court weighs the **reason for the default** and, more heavily, the **prospects of the defence**. The request must therefore set out the defence in substance, supported by an affidavit (reg. 50(1)), and ideally attach the draft defence. A deliberate disregard of the proceedings weighs heavily against the applicant; mere negligence can often be cured by costs.

## Leading cases

### ע״א 202/70 אליהו נ׳ יחזקאל, and its critics (רע״א 2237/06 בנק הפועלים נ׳ וינשטיין, 8.3.2009; רע״א 1170/08 עיריית חולון נ׳ הדר חברה לביטוח, 9.6.2010)

In *Eliyahu* the Supreme Court (Agranat and Etzioni JJ.) held that a judgment given without a hearing on the merits, such as a default judgment, does not create issue estoppel: the issue was never actually litigated. In *Weinstein*, Naor and Rubinstein JJ. said the rule should not be followed, because it lets a defendant skip a case on purpose and keep the right to litigate the same issue later; Melcer J. preferred to keep it. In *Holon Municipality*, Rivlin J. relied on that view and held that a judgment given in a party's absence, even without a defence, can create issue estoppel, including one from a small-claims judgment.

**Why it matters:** "it is only ₪8,000, let them take a default judgment" is a dangerous calculation. The facts found against you may follow you into a bigger case. Free summary: [Hebrew Wikipedia, מעשה בית דין](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/מעשה_בית_דין) (⚠️ secondary source; full texts not opened).

### Key text: reg. 131 and the no-leave order

There is no single leading Supreme Court decision under reg. 131 that this course could open in full. Read reg. 131 with reg. 137(ג) and item 7 of the no-leave order, and when you research, search Net HaMishpat decisions for "תקנה 131" with "סיכויי הגנה" (lesson [02.2](../module-02/lesson-02.md)).

## How it works in practice

### Diarise service, not reading

Write down the date of service as the rule defines it: the date on the postal confirmation, the date in the delivery-verification notice, or for an unrepresented party served electronically by the court, the date you opened the document. Then add the period and check for recess and the 17:00 rule.

### If you find a default judgment against you

1. Get the judgment and the proof of service from the case file (Net HaMishpat or the registry).
2. Check service: right address? right method (claims cannot be served by email without your consent)? an adult family member who actually lives with you?
3. Count 30 days from service of the judgment on you (reg. 131). If you are late, ask in the same request to extend time, with reasons.
4. Write the request: (a) the facts of service and why it was defective, or why you did not file; (b) your defence on the merits, paragraph by paragraph; (c) the draft defence attached; (d) an affidavit verifying the facts (reg. 50(1)); (e) a request to stay execution meanwhile (lesson 15.5 and reg. 145).
5. If the execution file is already open, tell the Execution Office registrar that a set-aside request is pending and attach the court's decision on a stay.

### If you are the plaintiff

Ask for judgment once the defence period has passed, attaching the proof of service. For a liquidated sum (a loan, an invoice) judgment is often given on the claim alone; for damages, prepare an affidavit of proof.

## Worked example

Oleg Kuznetsov, a bus driver from Karmiel, finds a ₪26,000 attachment on his bank account. The Execution Office file rests on a default judgment from the Krayot magistrates' court in favour of a gym chain, for a 36-month membership he cancelled in writing in 2023.

1. **The file.** Proof of service: registered mail to "רחוב הגפן 12, קריית ביאליק", his address until 2022. The postal confirmation says "לא נדרש" (not collected).
2. **Service analysis.** Reg. 161(3) treats an uncollected letter as served only when sent to the address the recipient gave in his own pleading; Oleg had filed nothing. His current address was in the Population Registry. This points to defective service and a set-aside as of right.
3. **Timing.** He was never served with the judgment either; he learned of it from the bank. He files at once, asking in the alternative for an extension.
4. **Request.** Facts of service; his 2023 cancellation email and the gym's confirmation; the draft defence; an affidavit; a request to stay the execution file.
5. **Result.** The court sets aside the judgment with no costs against him and orders him to file the defence within 30 days. The gym cannot seek leave to appeal the set-aside (no-leave order, item 7). Had service been lawful, he would still have a strong case on discretion, because his written cancellation gives the defence real prospects.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A document you received from a court, an authority or a company (or the Oleg case).
- Regs 158–164, 130–131 and 179 open; the [written motion template](../../templates/written-motion.md).
- About 50 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Service date (10 min).** For three documents (an email notice, a registered letter, one handed to a family member), write the date service took effect and the rule.
2. **Deadline (5 min).** Add a 60-day defence period to one of them, with recess and the 17:00 rule.
3. **Diagnosis (10 min).** In Oleg's case or your own, write whether service was lawful, and why, citing the rule.
4. **Draft (25 min).** On the written motion template, draft a reg. 131 request: facts of service, reason for the default, the defence on the merits in five paragraphs, the relief asked (set aside, stay, time for defence), and the list of exhibits.

### Template

- [Written motion (בקשה בכתב)](../../templates/written-motion.md), with the set-aside variant.

### How you know it worked

- Each service date cites the sub-rule that fixes it.
- Your request deals with both routes: defective service and, in the alternative, discretion.
- The defence on the merits is specific enough that a judge could see its prospects.
- You asked for a stay of execution.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. You may apply to set aside a decision against you and ask for a stay (reg. 170). As an unrepresented party the registry serves your documents (reg. 160), and you may consent in writing to receive documents by email (reg. 162(2)).

### Where and how

- File the request in the court that gave the judgment; tell the Execution Office registrar it is pending (lesson [08.7](../module-08/lesson-07.md)).
- Periods and fees for requests: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Free help: court information centre; Legal Aid if eligible ([where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md)).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The judgment is large, or the execution file includes a lien on your home or salary.
- More than 30 days have passed and you need an extension.
- You were served abroad, or you want to contest Israeli jurisdiction (reg. 168).
- The plaintiff claims you knew of the case and ignored it on purpose.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Counting from the day you read the letter, not the postal date | Defence late; default judgment | Apply under reg. 131 with an extension request | Diary the date the rule fixes |
| Ignoring an SMS notice from Net HaMishpat | Service may take effect on opening, or be presumed | Act at once; file an affidavit if not received (reg. 161(1)(ה)) | Open and save every court notice |
| Set-aside request with no defence on the merits | Refused on discretion | File a new, complete request if still in time | Attach a draft defence and an affidavit |
| Moving house without updating the address given to the court | Mail to the old address is treated as served (reg. 161(3)) | Ask to set aside; explain | Update your address in every open case |
| Letting a small default judgment stand | Possible issue estoppel later (*Holon*) | Seek to set aside while in time | Defend or settle; do not ignore |

## Review

- Service is what starts the clock; lawful service counts as knowledge (reg. 158).
- Claims go by mail or personal delivery; electronic service to an unrepresented party by the other side needs consent; court notices to the unrepresented take effect on opening (regs 161–162).
- Without a defence the court may demand proof or give judgment on the claim (reg. 130).
- Set-aside: within 30 days; as of right for defective service, otherwise on the reason for the default and the defence's prospects (reg. 131); appeal time waits (reg. 137(ג)).
- A default judgment may create issue estoppel in later litigation.
