# 15.9 · Limitation of actions

Every civil claim has an expiry date. Under the Prescription Law 1958 most claims expire seven years after the cause of action arose, but the date moves: it starts later if you did not know the facts, it pauses for minority, marriage, mental disability, and since 2024 for the first months of the war, and it restarts when the debtor acknowledges the debt. This lesson teaches the law in depth (module 4 covered the tort-specific rule in lesson 04.5) and gives you a method to compute a limitation date and to raise or answer a limitation defence. Limitation is a favourite Bar-exam topic because it combines statute, calculation and procedure.

## Why it matters

Limitation decides more cases than most doctrines, often without any trial: the court dismisses the claim at the threshold (reg. 43). For a plaintiff the cost of a mistake is total; for a defendant, a limitation defence not raised at the first opportunity is lost (s. 3). And because the law looks at the date the claim is **filed**, a claim refused by the registry on the last day can be fatal unless cured within seven days (reg. 33(ד); lesson 15.2).

The doctrine reflects competing values. Limitation protects defendants from stale claims when evidence has been lost, gives certainty, and pushes plaintiffs to act. Against this stands the right of access to court and the sense that a debtor should not profit merely from time. Israeli law resolves the tension by making limitation **procedural**: it bars the court from hearing the claim, but the right itself survives (s. 2). Courts therefore read limitation narrowly in doubtful cases, and use the suspension rules generously.

## The law

### Limitation is procedural: ss. 2–4

> תביעה לקיים זכות כל שהיא נתונה להתיישנות, ואם הוגשה תובענה על תביעה שהתיישנה וטען הנתבע טענת התיישנות, לא יזדקק בית המשפט לתובענה, אך אין בהתיישנות בלבד כדי לבטל את הזכות גופה.

*Gist:* Every claim is subject to limitation; if the defendant pleads it, the court will not hear a time-barred claim, but limitation alone does not extinguish the right. ([s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההתיישנות))

The defendant must plead limitation at the first opportunity after the claim is filed, or the court will not consider it (s. 3). Under the Regulations that means the defence, as a preliminary argument, first in its summary (reg. 13(1), reg. 28). Limitation cannot be raised against a set-off or counterclaim arising from the same subject or circumstances, if the main claim was not time-barred or limitation was not pleaded against it (s. 4). The court may dismiss a time-barred claim at any time (reg. 43). Because the right survives, a creditor holding a pledge or mortgage may still realise it (s. 20).

### The periods: s. 5

> (1) בשאינו מקרקעין – שבע שנים;
> (2) במקרקעין – חמש עשרה שנה; ואם נרשמו בספרי האחוזה לאחר סידור זכות קנין לפי פקודת הקרקעות (סידור זכות הקנין) – עשרים וחמש שנה.

*Gist:* Seven years for claims not concerning land; fifteen years for land, and twenty-five for land registered after settlement of title. ([s. 5](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההתיישנות); [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

Other laws set their own periods, and the Prescription Law does not override them (s. 27): for example the insurance-claim period in the Insurance Contract Law, the torts rule for damage discovered late (Civil Wrongs Ordinance s. 89; lesson [04.5](../module-04/lesson-05.md)), and short periods for claims against the State over damage caused by the security forces (lesson 04.7). The Law applies to the State too (s. 28).

### When the clock starts: ss. 6 and 8

> תקופת ההתיישנות מתחילה ביום שבו נולדה עילת התובענה.

*Gist:* The period starts on the day the cause of action arose. ([s. 6](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההתיישנות))

> נעלמו מן התובע העובדות המהוות את עילת התובענה, מסיבות שלא היו תלויות בו ושאף בזהירות סבירה לא יכול היה למנוע אותן, תתחיל תקופת ההתיישנות ביום שבו נודעו לתובע עובדות אלה.

*Gist:* If the plaintiff did not know the facts constituting the cause of action, for reasons beyond his control that reasonable care could not have prevented, the period starts when he learned them. ([s. 8](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההתיישנות))

Note what s. 8 covers: facts, not law. Not knowing that you had a legal claim does not delay the start.

### Suspensions: ss. 7, 10–18ב

The following periods are not counted:

| Section | Ground |
|---|---|
| 7 | While the plaintiff refrained from suing because the defendant knowingly misled him (including knowingly hiding a fact of the cause), used force, threatened him or exploited his distress |
| 10 | While the plaintiff was under 18 |
| 11 | While the plaintiff could not manage his affairs because of mental or intellectual disability and had no guardian; with a guardian, until the guardian learned the facts |
| 12 | While the plaintiff was the defendant's guardian or ward |
| 13 | While the parties were married to each other |
| 14 | While a party was in a state where, because of conditions or relations with Israel, litigation was impossible |
| 15 | Between filing and dismissal of an earlier claim on the same cause that was dismissed in a way that allows a new claim |
| 15א | The period from 7 October 2023 to 6 April 2024 (Swords of Iron), for periods still running on 25.1.2024, and with special rules for land and for periods that had just expired |
| 18א | In claims for sexual assault or abuse of a minor (and some sexual assaults on young adults), the time before the victim turns 28 |

After a suspension under ss. 10 or 12–15, the period does not end less than a year after the suspension stopped; under s. 11, less than two years (s. 16). If the competent court was closed in the last year of the period, it does not end less than a year after it reopened (s. 17). If a right passed by inheritance, the period does not end less than a year after it passed (s. 18). In a claim against a person indicted for listed sexual or abuse offences, limitation does not end less than a year after the criminal judgment became final (s. 18ב).

The war suspension in s. 15א applies also to periods set in other laws, except a closed list (for example the Air Transport Law and the State Liability Law's security-forces period) (s. 15א(ה)). A court may refuse to revive a claim that had already expired before the 2024 amendment, for special reasons, considering whether the defendant changed position (s. 15א(ד)).

### Acknowledgment: s. 9

> הודה הנתבע, בכתב או בפני בית משפט, בין בתוך תקופת ההתיישנות ובין לאחריה, בקיום זכות התובע, תתחיל תקופת ההתיישנות מיום ההודאה; ומעשה שיש בו משום ביצוע מקצת הזכות, דינו כהודאה לענין סעיף זה.

*Gist:* A written acknowledgment, or one made in court, of the plaintiff's right, even after the period expired, starts the period afresh; part performance (a part payment) counts as acknowledgment; but not an acknowledgment coupled with a limitation plea. ([s. 9](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההתיישנות))

### Agreed periods and judgments: ss. 19 and 21

Parties may agree, in a separate written contract, on a longer period, and for non-land claims also on a shorter one, but not less than six months (s. 19). A judgment may be met with a limitation plea only after 25 years in which the creditor took no step to enforce it; a judgment that needs no enforcement does not expire (s. 21).

### Limitation and laches: s. 27

The Law does not affect any power to dismiss a claim for delay (s. 27). In civil claims, however, a claim filed within the limitation period is rarely dismissed for laches: the defendant must show something close to bad faith or a change of position caused by the delay (see below).

## Leading cases

### ע״א 8438/09 רובאב חברה לנכסים בע״מ נ׳ אחים דוניץ בע״מ, פ״ד סה(2) 635 (19.4.2012)

The Supreme Court discussed the breadth of a "cause of action" for limitation. Courts usually read a cause narrowly for limitation, because that favours plaintiffs (a later head of damage gets its own period). In *Rubav* the Court read it broadly where that favoured the plaintiff: an acknowledgment under s. 9 of one part of the right restarts the period for the whole cause. It distinguished *Yupiter* (below), where for s. 6 a breach and a later damage were treated as separate causes.

**Why it matters:** "cause of action" has no fixed size; it is shaped by the purpose of the rule being applied. When you analyse an acknowledgment, ask whether it covers the whole cause. Free summary: [Hebrew Wikipedia, התיישנות](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/התיישנות) (⚠️ secondary source; full text not opened).

### ע״א 3599/94 יופיטר נ׳ בנק לאומי לישראל בע״מ, פ״ד נ(5) 423 (1997)

The Court held that, for the start of the period under s. 6, a claim based on a breach and a claim based on damage that appeared later may be different causes, so a later damage can start its own period. Free summary: [Hebrew Wikipedia, התיישנות](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/התיישנות) (⚠️ secondary source).

### ע״א 6805/99 תלמוד תורה הכללי והישיבה הגדולה עץ חיים בירושלים נ׳ הוועדה המקומית לתכנון ולבניה ירושלים, פ״ד נז(5) 433 (2.7.2003)

A leading case on laches in civil law: within the limitation period, the right of access to court is not defeated by a laches plea unless the defendant carries a heavy burden, showing delay amounting to bad faith that worsened its position. Free summary: [Hebrew Wikipedia, שיהוי](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/שיהוי_(משפט)) (⚠️ secondary source).

**Why it matters:** as a plaintiff, file within the period and laches will rarely hurt you; as a defendant, do not rely on laches when limitation fails.

## How it works in practice

### The limitation worksheet

1. **Cause:** write each cause of action separately (contract, tort, unjust enrichment).
2. **Period:** s. 5 or a special law (s. 27). Land or not?
3. **Start:** date the cause arose (s. 6) or the date you learned the facts (s. 8). Why did you not know earlier?
4. **Suspensions:** minority, marriage, disability, misleading by the defendant, an earlier claim, the war period. For each, the dates.
5. **Restart:** any written acknowledgment or part payment (s. 9). Date and scope.
6. **Floors:** s. 16 (one or two years after a suspension), s. 17, s. 18.
7. **Agreement:** a contractual period (s. 19): separate written contract? at least six months?
8. **Result:** the last day to **file**, with a safety margin of weeks, not days.

### Using s. 9 in practice

A debtor's message "אני יודע שאני חייב לך, אשלם כשאוכל" is a written acknowledgment; so is a part payment marked "on account". Keep them. A letter saying "I owe nothing, and anyway your claim is time-barred" is not.

### As a defendant

Put limitation in the defence, first among preliminary arguments, with the dates (regs 13, 28; s. 3). You may ask the court to decide it first (reg. 76) or to dismiss (reg. 43).

## Worked example

Rania Haddad, a nurse from Shefa-'Amr, lent ₪30,000 to a colleague, Igor, in March 2017, repayable in March 2018. Igor did not pay. In June 2021 he sent her a WhatsApp: "סליחה על העיכוב, אני מחזיר לך את ה-30 אלף, תני לי עוד קצת זמן". In January 2025 he paid ₪2,000 by bank transfer marked "על חשבון ההלוואה". Rania wants to sue in October 2026.

1. **Cause and period.** Contract (loan), not land: seven years (s. 5(1)).
2. **Start.** The cause arose when repayment was due, March 2018 (s. 6). Seven years would end in March 2025.
3. **Acknowledgment 1.** The June 2021 message is a written acknowledgment of the right: the period starts again from June 2021 (s. 9), ending June 2028.
4. **War suspension.** The period was running on 25.1.2024, so 7.10.2023 to 6.4.2024 is not counted (s. 15א(א)), adding about six months.
5. **Acknowledgment 2.** The January 2025 part payment "on account of the loan" is part performance and restarts the period again (s. 9): seven years from January 2025, ending in January 2032.
6. **Result.** Her claim in October 2026 is well within time. In her claim she pleads the dates of both acknowledgments in the summary, anticipating the limitation defence (reg. 11(3): when the cause arose).

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A possible claim of your own, ideally an old one (or Rania's case with different dates).
- The Prescription Law open; a calendar.
- About 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Worksheet (20 min).** Fill in the eight-step worksheet for your claim with actual dates.
2. **Variation (10 min).** Change one fact (no WhatsApp; Rania and Igor married in 2019 and divorced in 2023; Rania was 16 when the cause arose) and recompute.
3. **Defence paragraph (10 min).** Write the limitation paragraph a defendant would put in the summary of the defence, with dates.
4. **Clause (5 min).** Read a contract you signed (insurance, gym, services) for a limitation clause. Is it a separate written contract? Is the period at least six months?

- [Statement of defence](../../templates/statement-of-defence.md): the preliminary-arguments section.

### How you know it worked

- Your last-day-to-file date accounts for every suspension and restart.
- You can say which section each adjustment comes from.
- Your defence paragraph puts limitation first and gives dates.
- You know whether a contractual clause would bind.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes: computing limitation and pleading it are part of conducting your own case (reg. 170). The stakes are high, because an error is final.

### Where and how

- The limitation date governs filing in any civil forum, including small claims (lesson [08.3](../module-08/lesson-03.md)) and arbitration (s. 1 defines "court" to include an arbitrator).
- Periods and special periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Free help: Legal Aid where eligible ([where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md)).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The limitation date is within a few months, or may already have passed.
- The claim is for personal injury, medical negligence, sexual abuse, or against the State or the security forces (special periods).
- The claim concerns land and the registration history is unclear.
- You rely on s. 7 (misleading by the defendant) or s. 8 (late knowledge).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Counting from the contract date instead of the date of breach or due date | Wrong limitation date | Recompute with s. 6 | Identify when the cause arose |
| Ignoring a special period in another law | Claim barred earlier than you thought | None, once barred | Check s. 27 and the specific law |
| Defendant forgets to plead limitation | Defence lost (s. 3) | Ask leave to amend at once (regs 28, 46) | Put it first in the defence |
| Relying on a verbal acknowledgment | No restart (s. 9 requires writing or court) | Look for a written message or part payment | Get acknowledgments in writing |
| Filing on the last day and being refused | Barred unless cured in seven days (reg. 33(ד)) | Cure within seven days | File weeks early |

## Review

- Limitation is procedural: the court will not hear a time-barred claim if the defendant pleads it at the first opportunity, but the right survives (ss. 2–3, 20).
- Seven years generally, fifteen or twenty-five for land; special laws prevail (ss. 5, 27).
- The clock starts when the cause arose, or when the plaintiff learned the facts (ss. 6, 8), and pauses for the listed grounds, including the war period 7.10.2023–6.4.2024 (ss. 7, 10–18ב).
- A written acknowledgment or part payment restarts it (s. 9); agreed periods must be in a separate written contract and not under six months (s. 19).
- Within the period, laches rarely defeats a civil claim.
