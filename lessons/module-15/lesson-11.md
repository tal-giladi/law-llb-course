# 15.11 · Practice lab: a motion and an appeal outline

This lab closes the module with the two documents a litigant writes most after the pleadings: a written motion, and an appeal. In part A you draft a motion to set aside a decision given in your absence, with a request to stay execution. In part B you read a short magistrates' court judgment, classify it, compute the dates and money, and write an appeal outline. Use your own matter or the supplied case. Nothing is filed.

## Why it matters

Motions decide the path of a case and appeals decide whether a wrong result stands. Both are judged on paper, within page limits, by judges who read hundreds of them. A motion that states the relief in the first line and proves its facts by affidavit gets a decision; an appeal that identifies a reviewable error gets a hearing. Lessons 15.3, 15.5 and 15.8 gave you the rules; here you apply them under time pressure.

## The law

> (1) המבקש יפרט את טיעוניו כולל אסמכתאות, ויצרף לבקשה תצהיר לשם אימות העובדות שביסודה;

*Gist:* A written motion sets out the arguments with authorities and attaches an affidavit verifying its facts; the court may dismiss it without a response, ask for a response, decide on the papers or after cross-examining the deponents; motion and response are page-limited. ([reg. 50](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

Also open: reg. 49(ד) (writing to the other side seven days before a motion, except an ex parte temporary order), reg. 131 (set-aside within 30 days), reg. 145 (stay), regs 134–137 (appeal form, security, deadlines), reg. 144 (new evidence), Courts Law s. 52 ([Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט)), and Prescription Law ss. 8 and 15א ([Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההתיישנות)).

## Leading cases

Not a case lab. Apply *Haniyon Haifa* and *Menora* from lesson [15.8](lesson-08.md) to decide whether your grounds are reviewable, and *Rubav* from lesson [15.9](lesson-09.md) if your appeal involves limitation.

## How it works in practice

A motion is written in this order: the relief (one sentence, numbered if several), the facts (numbered, each verified in the affidavit), the argument (rule, application, authority), and the request for costs. An appeal outline is written in this order: what the lower court decided and on what basis, the relief sought on appeal, and the grounds, each one a separate heading with the record reference (exhibit, transcript page) and the authority.

## Worked example

The supplied case for this lab:

**Maria Nassar**, a pharmacist from Jaffa, bought a used car from **"רכבי השפלה בע״מ"** in January 2018 for ₪78,000; the dealer's ad and the sale contract said 62,000 km. In June 2024 a garage's computer printout from the manufacturer's service system showed the car had 141,000 km in 2017. She sued in the Tel Aviv magistrates' court in March 2026 for ₪32,000 (loss of value, per an appraiser's opinion attached to the claim) for deceit and breach of contract.

**Part A facts.** The dealer's defence was filed late; Maria did not notice. Neither did the court, which set a pre-trial hearing for 6 July 2026. On that day Maria was in hospital after a car accident (she has a discharge letter). The court struck out her claim for non-attendance (reg. 75(1)) and awarded the dealer ₪2,500 costs. The decision was opened by Maria on Net HaMishpat on 20 July 2026 at 18:40. The dealer opened an execution file for the costs.

**Part B facts.** Assume instead that the case went to trial. The magistrates' court dismissed her claim in a judgment served on 1 September 2026. It held: (1) the claim was time-barred, because seven years from January 2018 ended in January 2025; (2) in any event the printout was "not sufficient proof", without mentioning that the dealer's own manager confirmed in cross-examination that the dealer had not checked the service history (transcript, p. 14). Costs ₪6,000.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- Your own decision or judgment, or the Nassar case.
- The [written motion template](../../templates/written-motion.md) and the [appeal outline template](../../templates/appeal-outline.md).
- Regs 49–50, 75, 131, 134–149 and the Prescription Law open.
- About two and a half hours.

### Steps

**Part A: the motion (70 min)**

1. **Diagnose (10 min).** What kind of decision was given (reg. 75(1))? What is the route (reg. 131, not appeal)? When was it served (reg. 161(1)(ד): opened after 17:00)? Last day to file?
2. **Relief (5 min).** Write the relief paragraph: set aside the strike-out; reinstate the claim; stay the execution of the costs; costs of the motion.
3. **Facts (20 min).** Numbered facts: the hearing date, the accident, the hospital stay, when she learned of the decision. Each with an exhibit.
4. **Argument (20 min).** Reason for the absence (documented, not deliberate); prospects of the claim (the printout, the appraiser); no prejudice to the dealer that costs cannot cure. Add: the dealer's defence was itself late, which she may raise if the case is reinstated.
5. **Affidavit (10 min).** A short affidavit verifying the facts (reg. 50(1), reg. 178), ready for the warning before a lawyer or other authorised person.
6. **Pre-motion letter (5 min).** Is a seven-day letter to the dealer needed first (reg. 49(ד))? Decide and write one line of reasons.

**Part B: the appeal outline (80 min)**

7. **Classify (5 min).** Judgment or other decision? Appeal as of right or leave (Courts Law s. 52)? Which court?
8. **Dates and money (10 min).** Last day to appeal (reg. 137(א), from 1 September 2026, recess excluded); the fee item and the security amount from [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
9. **What the court decided (10 min).** Summarise the judgment's two holdings in four lines.
10. **Ground 1, error of law (20 min).** Limitation: the court ignored s. 8 (late knowledge: the facts were hidden until June 2024) and s. 15א (the war period). Recompute the date and show the claim was in time. Authority: the sections; *Rubav* on cause of action if relevant.
11. **Ground 2, ignored evidence (20 min).** The judgment does not address the manager's admission (p. 14). Explain why that is a reviewable error (*Menora*: failure to address material evidence), not a mere credibility dispute.
12. **Relief and stay (10 min).** Relief: allow the appeal, give judgment for ₪32,000 or return the case for decision on the merits (reg. 146). Do you need a stay of the ₪6,000 costs? Write one paragraph.
13. **Page check (5 min).** Is the body within the district-court limit (reg. 134(א)(3))?

### How you know it worked

- The motion's first paragraph states every relief requested.
- Every fact in the motion is in the affidavit and has an exhibit.
- The motion is filed within 30 days of service as the rule defines it.
- Each appeal ground is a reviewable error with a record reference and authority.
- You computed the appeal deadline, fee and security.

<summary>Model points for the Nassar case</summary>

**Part A.** The decision was opened on 20 July 2026 at 18:40, after 17:00, so it counts as served on 21 July (reg. 161(1)(ד)); 30 days run from then (reg. 131), excluding court recess (reg. 179(ב)) if it falls in the period. Route: set-aside under reg. 131, because the strike-out was given in her absence; an appeal would run only from the decision on the set-aside request (reg. 137(ג)). The reason is strong (hospitalisation, documented); the claim has prospects (manufacturer's printout, appraiser). Ask also to stay the costs execution pending the decision. Reg. 49(ד) requires writing to the other side at least seven days before a motion (the only exception is an ex parte temporary order); send the letter at once and file on day 8, still well within the 30 days.

**Part B.** A final judgment: appeal as of right to the Tel Aviv district court (s. 52(א)) within 60 days of service (reg. 137(א)). Ground 1: the cause of deceit concerns facts hidden from her; under s. 8 the period started in June 2024, and even counting from January 2018, s. 15א excludes 7.10.2023 to 6.4.2024 from a period still running on 25.1.2024, so the seven years end about July 2025; with s. 8 the claim is clearly in time. Note also that s. 7 (knowing deceit by the defendant) may apply. Ground 2: the judgment does not address the manager's admission, material evidence going to the dealer's knowledge; this is the *Menora* exception. Relief: judgment for ₪32,000, or return to the magistrates' court to assess (reg. 146(א)(3)–(4)).

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes: motions and appeals in your own case (reg. 170). For appeals, unrepresented parties file at the appeal court's registry (see [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md)).

### Where and how

- Motion: in the court that gave the decision; appeal: the district court of the same district.
- Fees, security and periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); free help: [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The appeal is to the Supreme Court or needs leave.
- The motion involves interim relief with a large undertaking.
- You are out of time and need an extension.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Appealing a decision given in your absence instead of applying to set aside | Wrong route; time lost | Apply under reg. 131 if still in time | Check whether the decision was given in absence |
| Motion without an affidavit | Facts unproven; motion dismissed (reg. 50) | Refile with an affidavit | Attach the affidavit every time |
| Appeal grounds that re-argue credibility | Dismissed (*Menora*) | Reframe as law or ignored evidence | Look for reviewable errors first |
| Forgetting the appeal security | Appeal may be struck out (reg. 135(ג)) | Deposit promptly or ask exemption | Deposit with the filing |

## Review

- A motion leads with the relief, proves its facts by affidavit, and argues rule, application and authority (reg. 50).
- Decisions given in your absence go to set-aside first (reg. 131); the appeal clock waits (reg. 137(ג)).
- An appeal outline states what was decided, what you want, and reviewable grounds with record references.
- Compute deadlines from service, and count fee and security before you decide.
