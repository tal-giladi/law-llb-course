# 20.6 · Insurance contracts: disclosure, claims and limitation

By the end of this lesson you will be able to read an insurer's rejection letter and test each of its reasons against the Insurance Contract Law 1981: was the question you "failed to disclose" ever asked in writing, does the exclusion appear where the law requires, is a full refusal allowed or only a proportional reduction, and when does your claim become time-barred. Lesson [04.8](../module-04/lesson-08.md) taught you to file the claim; this lesson teaches you to fight the answer.

## Why it matters

Insurance is a standard contract written by one side, sold through agents, and tested only after a loss, when the insured is weakest. The 1981 law reversed the old common-law approach that let an insurer avoid a policy for any material fact the insured did not volunteer. Today the insured must answer the questions the insurer asked in writing, fully and honestly; an innocent wrong answer usually leads only to a proportional reduction; fraud and a few defined cases lead to full exemption; and most rules may be varied only in the insured's favour (s. 39). Rejection letters often cite these sections loosely. A precise reply, with the right section, frequently changes the outcome before any court.

## The law

### Disclosure: answer what you are asked

> (א) הציג המבטח למבוטח לפני כריתת החוזה, אם בטופס של הצעת ביטוח ואם בדרך אחרת שבכתב, שאלה בענין שיש בו כדי להשפיע על נכונותו של מבטח סביר לכרות את החוזה בכלל או לכרותו בתנאים שבו (להלן – ענין מהותי), על המבוטח להשיב עליה בכתב תשובה מלאה וכנה.
> (ב) שאלה גורפת הכורכת ענינים שונים, ללא אבחנה ביניהם, אינה מחייבת תשובה כאמור אלא אם היתה סבירה בעת כריתת החוזה.
> (ג) הסתרה בכוונת מרמה מצד המבוטח של ענין שהוא ידע כי הוא ענין מהותי, דינה כדין מתן תשובה שאינה מלאה וכנה.

*Gist:* The insured must give full and honest written answers to written questions on material matters; a sweeping catch-all question binds only if reasonable; and only fraudulent concealment of a known material matter counts when no question was asked. [s. 6](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חוזה_הביטוח#סעיף_6)

> (א) ניתנה לשאלה בענין מהותי תשובה שלא היתה מלאה וכנה, רשאי המבטח, תוך שלושים ימים מהיום שנודע לו על כך וכל עוד לא קרה מקרה הביטוח, לבטל את החוזה בהודעה בכתב למבוטח. [...]
> (ג) קרה מקרה הביטוח לפני שנתבטל החוזה מכוח סעיף זה, אין המבטח חייב אלא בתגמולי ביטוח מופחתים בשיעור יחסי, שהוא כיחס שבין דמי הביטוח שהיו משתלמים כמקובל אצלו לפי המצב לאמיתו לבין דמי הביטוח המוסכמים, והוא פטור כליל בכל אחת מאלה: (1) התשובה ניתנה בכוונת מרמה; (2) מבטח סביר לא היה מתקשר באותו חוזה, אף בדמי ביטוח מרובים יותר, אילו ידע את המצב לאמיתו [...]

*Gist:* Before a loss, the insurer may cancel within a short period of learning of a wrong answer; after a loss, it pays proceeds reduced in the ratio of the agreed premium to the premium it would have charged on the true facts, and is fully exempt only for fraud or if a reasonable insurer would not have insured at all. [s. 7](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חוזה_הביטוח#סעיף_7)

> המבטח אינו זכאי לתרופות האמורות בסעיף 7 בכל אחת מאלה, אלא אם התשובה שלא היתה מלאה וכנה ניתנה בכוונת מרמה: (1) הוא ידע או היה עליו לדעת את המצב לאמיתו בשעת כריתת החוזה או שהוא גרם לכך שהתשובה לא היתה מלאה וכנה; (2) העובדה שעליה ניתנה תשובה שלא היתה מלאה וכנה חדלה להתקיים לפני שקרה מקרה הביטוח, או שלא השפיעה על מקרהו, על חבות המבטח או על היקפה.

*Gist:* Unless the answer was fraudulent, the insurer has no remedy if it knew or should have known the truth, caused the wrong answer (for example its agent filled in the form), or if the wrong fact ceased or did not affect the event or the liability. [s. 8](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חוזה_הביטוח#סעיף_8)

Aggravation of risk during the policy (ss. 17–19) works the same way: notify a "material change" in writing; the insurer may cancel before a loss, or reduce proportionally after it; full exemption only for fraud or if no reasonable insurer would continue; no remedy if the change ceased, did not affect the event, or was a step to prevent serious harm notified at once.

### The policy and its exclusions

> תנאי או סייג לחבות המבטח או להיקפה יפורטו בפוליסה בסמוך לנושא שהם נוגעים לו, או יצויינו בה בהבלטה מיוחדת; תנאי או סייג שלא נתקיימה בהם הוראה זו, אין המבטח זכאי להסתמך עליהם.

*Gist:* A condition or exclusion must appear next to the subject it limits or be specially highlighted; otherwise the insurer may not rely on it. [s. 3](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חוזה_הביטוח#סעיף_3) An insured's written answer is binding only if a copy is attached to the policy (s. 4); until the policy is delivered, the insurer's standard terms filed with the Capital Market Authority apply (s. 2(ב)). As a standard contract, the policy is also open to the unfair-terms test (Standard Contracts Law s. 3; lesson 20.7), except for terms that follow a statute or approved terms (s. 23).

### The claim

- **Notice and investigation.** Notify the insurer at once after learning of the event (s. 22); once notice and a written claim arrive, the insurer must investigate at once, and the insured must supply information and documents within a reasonable time (s. 23).
- **Obstruction.** Late notice or late documents reduce the proceeds only to the extent they let the insurer reduce its liability, unless justified or harmless (s. 24(א)); deliberate obstruction limits the insurer to what it would have paid anyway (s. 24(ב)).
- **Fraud.** False facts or concealment about the event or the liability, with intent to defraud, exempt the insurer entirely (s. 25). An event caused deliberately by the insured: exemption (s. 26).
- **Payment.** Within a fixed period after the insurer has what it needs; undisputed amounts within the same period after the claim, claimable separately (s. 27). Linkage from the event and interest after a set period (s. 28); in personal lines (life, accident, health, car, home and contents) a court must add special interest on undisputed amounts not paid on time (s. 28א). Periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

> היתה חבות המבטח או היקפה מותנים על פי חוק או על פי החוזה [...] במעשה או במחדל של המבוטח או של המוטב, שלא השפיעו השפעה של ממש על סיכון המבטח, רשאי בית המשפט, אם נראה לו צודק לעשות כן בנסיבות הענין, לחייב את המבטח בתגמולי הביטוח, כולם או מקצתם, אף אם התנאי לא קויים או הופר.

*Gist:* If a condition was breached but the breach did not substantially affect the insurer's risk, the court may order payment anyway. [s. 29](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חוזה_הביטוח#סעיף_29) Classic use: an alarm that was not switched on during a burglary that would have happened anyway.

### Property insurance points

Proceeds put the insured, as far as possible, where he would have been (s. 56(ג)); underinsurance reduces proceeds in proportion (s. 60); the insured must take reasonable steps to limit the loss and the insurer pays for them, even above the sum insured (s. 61); after paying, the insurer takes over the insured's claim against the wrongdoer, without harming the insured's claim for the rest (s. 62).

### Limitation

> תקופת ההתיישנות של תביעה לתגמולי ביטוח היא שלוש שנים לאחר שקרה מקרה הביטוח ולעניין ביטוח חיים, ביטוח מפני מחלות ואשפוז וביטוח סיעודי – חמש שנים לאחר שקרה מקרה הביטוח; היתה עילת התביעה נכות שנגרמה למבוטח ממחלה או מתאונה, תימנה תקופת ההתיישנות מיום שקמה למבוטח זכות לתבוע תגמולי ביטוח לפי תנאי חוזה הביטוח [...]

*Gist:* An insurance claim is time-barred a fixed number of years after the event (longer for life, health and long-term-care policies made or renewed from 25 November 2020); for disability, from when the right to claim arose under the policy. [s. 31](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חוזה_הביטוח#סעיף_31) Filing the claim with the insurer does not stop the clock; for policies from that date the insurer must say so, and warn a year and three months before the end (s. 31א). Periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

## Leading cases

### ע״א 118/51 New Zealand Insurance Co. נ׳ יובל (1953)

A Haifa merchant insured a consignment of watches already twenty days in transit from Paris, without telling the underwriters; the watches were stolen in France, and no one knew whether before or after the policy. The Supreme Court held that the Ottoman Mercantile Code, read with its French source, governed, and that a non-disclosed fact voids the policy if it would have stopped an ordinary reasonable underwriter from accepting the terms, whether or not the insured acted fraudulently; it remitted the case to decide whether twenty days in transit was such a fact, given the "lost or not lost" clause. Why it matters: it shows the strict pre-1981 regime (avoidance for innocent non-disclosure, a duty to volunteer facts) that the Insurance Contract Law replaced with a duty to answer written questions and proportional reduction. Lesson [03.5](../module-03/lesson-05.md) used it for mistake. [Cardozo ISCP](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/288)

### Key text: the 2020 limitation amendment

[Amendment No. 11](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/23/law/23_lsr_591228.pdf) (התשפ״א) lengthened limitation for life, health and long-term-care insurance and added the insurer's duty to tell claimants that negotiation does not stop the clock (s. 31א), for policies made or renewed from 25 November 2020. Its premise: claimants lost good claims while waiting for an insurer's answer. Practical lesson: a pending claim is not a reason to wait; calendar the limitation date on day one.

## How it works in practice

### Reading a rejection letter

| Insurer's reason | Test | Your answer if it fails |
|---|---|---|
| "You did not disclose X" | Was there a written question on X? Was it specific (s. 6(ב))? Is the answer attached to the policy (s. 4)? Did the agent fill it in (s. 8(1))? | No question, no duty, unless fraud (s. 6(ג)) |
| "Your answer was wrong" | Fraud? Would a reasonable insurer have refused outright? | Proportional reduction only (s. 7(ג)) |
| "Exclusion clause Y" | Is Y next to the subject or highlighted (s. 3)? | Insurer may not rely on it |
| "Condition Z breached" (alarm, locks) | Did the breach substantially affect the risk? | Ask the court for s. 29 relief |
| "Late notice / missing documents" | Did the delay actually reduce the insurer's ability to investigate (s. 24(א))? | No reduction if harmless |
| "Fraud" | Specific false statement with intent (s. 25)? | Demand particulars; deny |

### Where to go

1. Written appeal to the insurer's internal ombudsman (נציב תלונות הציבור of the company); every insurer has one (⚠️ check the company's site).
2. Complaint to the Capital Market, Insurance and Savings Authority's public enquiries unit (⚠️ manual check of the gov.il page). Neither stops limitation.
3. Claim: small claims court within the ceiling, or magistrates' court; the claim for proceeds must be filed before the s. 31 date.

## Worked example

Yusuf Mansour of Nazareth has a home-contents policy. The proposal form asked "Has the property been burgled in the last three years?"; he answered "no", forgetting a 2023 break-in in which only a bicycle was taken from the balcony. In June 2026 his flat is burgled and jewellery and a laptop worth ₪28,000 are stolen. The policy has a condition, printed on page 14 among general terms in the same font, that "the alarm must be activated whenever the flat is empty"; the alarm was off. The insurer rejects the claim for "non-disclosure and breach of the alarm condition", and says it is "still examining" four months later.

1. **Non-disclosure.** There was a written, specific question; the answer was wrong. Fraud? He forgot a minor balcony theft; intent is unlikely. A reasonable insurer would still have insured, perhaps at a higher premium. So: proportional reduction at most, the ratio of premiums (s. 7(ג)). He asks the insurer to state the premium it would have charged.
2. **Alarm condition.** It is a condition limiting liability buried among general terms, not next to the theft cover nor highlighted: the insurer may not rely on it (s. 3). Alternatively s. 29: the burglars broke a window grille, so the alarm would have changed little.
3. **Payment and interest.** He supplied all documents in July; the payment period has passed (s. 27). Linkage and interest run (s. 28), and contents insurance is a personal line for special interest on undisputed amounts (s. 28א).
4. **Limitation.** The event was June 2026; he calendars the s. 31 date and files suit well before it if there is no settlement.
5. **Letter.** He replies point by point on the [insurance rejection response (draft)](../../templates/insurance-rejection-response.md), copies the company's ombudsman, and files a complaint with the Capital Market Authority.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- One of your own policies (home, car, health) and its proposal form, or Yusuf's facts.
- The [insurance rejection response (draft)](../../templates/insurance-rejection-response.md) and the [insurance claim letter](../../templates/insurance-claim-letter.md).
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. Find the proposal questions and your answers; check whether a copy is attached to the policy (s. 4).
2. Find two exclusions in your policy and check whether they meet s. 3 (placement or highlighting).
3. Write the limitation date for an event that happened today, for this policy type (s. 31; note whether it was made or renewed after November 2020).
4. Take one rejection reason (supplied or imagined) and answer it on the template, citing the section and the facts.
5. Calculate a proportional reduction with assumed premiums (agreed ₪1,200, would have been ₪1,500): proceeds × 1,200/1,500.

### How you know it worked

- Each rejection ground is met with a section and a fact.
- You calculated, not guessed, any reduction.
- The limitation date is in your calendar, with a reminder months before.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes, in your own name: claims to your insurer, internal appeals, regulator complaints and small or magistrates' claims. Licensed insurance agents act under their own law; handling claims for others for pay is otherwise reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20). Public "claim recovery" firms that are not lawyers may not represent you in court.

### Where and how

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- Internal ombudsman of the insurer; then the Capital Market Authority public enquiries (⚠️ manual check on gov.il).
- Court: small claims court for individuals within the ceiling; magistrates' or district court above; fees and ceilings in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Free help: Kol-Zchut insurance pages (⚠️ manual check); Legal Aid where eligible.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The insurer alleges fraud (s. 25) or a deliberate event (s. 26): criminal exposure.
- The claim is for disability, life or long-term care, with medical committees and large sums.
- The limitation date is close and the case is not ready.
- The insurer offers a final settlement with a waiver.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Waiting for the insurer's answer past the limitation date | Claim time-barred (s. 31) | Sue at once if there is any time left | Calendar the date on day one |
| Accepting "non-disclosure" as a full refusal | Losing proceeds that were only reducible | Demand the premium comparison (s. 7(ג)) | Check the question and fraud first |
| Not keeping a copy of the proposal | Cannot test s. 4 and s. 8 | Demand it from the insurer (s. 5) | Keep a copy when buying |
| Exaggerating the loss | Full exemption for fraud (s. 25) | Correct immediately in writing | Claim only documented items |
| Ignoring the agent's role | Insurer avoids responsibility | Argue s. 8(1): the insurer caused the answer | Write down who filled the form |

## Review

- The insured must answer the insurer's written questions fully and honestly; no question, no duty, except fraudulent concealment.
- A wrong innocent answer means a proportional reduction after a loss, not a refusal, unless no reasonable insurer would have insured.
- Exclusions must be placed next to their subject or highlighted (s. 3); conditions that did not substantially affect the risk can be excused (s. 29).
- The insurer must pay within fixed periods, with linkage and interest, and special interest in personal lines.
- Limitation runs from the event and is not stopped by the claim; longer for life, health and long-term care under newer policies.
