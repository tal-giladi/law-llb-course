# 20.4 · Services and contracting work

By the end of this lesson you will be able to handle the most common private contract after sale and rental: you hire someone to do work, a renovation, a car repair, a wedding photographer, a web designer, and the result is late, defective or held hostage. You will know which short statute governs, what you must do before you may fix the problem yourself, and when the contractor may keep your property until paid.

## Why it matters

The Contract for Services Law 1974 has eleven sections and most people have never heard of it, yet it governs every renovation, repair and professional job done by someone who is not your employee. Its logic mirrors the Sale Law (lesson 20.1) with one key change: because work, unlike goods, can usually be fixed, the law gives the contractor a chance to repair before the customer may repair at the contractor's expense or cut the fee (ss. 3–4). The law also answers questions that come up the moment a dispute begins: may the garage keep my car? who pays if the workshop burns down? may I hire someone else and deduct? On top of it sit the consumer rules for continuing services and technician visits, and the Remedies Law's refusal to force anyone to do personal work.

## The law

### What a contract for services is

> חוזה קבלנות הוא חוזה לעשיית מלאכה או למתן שירות בשכר כשהקבלן אינו עובדו של המזמין.

*Gist:* A contract for services is a contract to do work or give a service for pay, where the contractor is not the customer's employee. [s. 1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חוזה_קבלנות#סעיף_1) Whether someone is a contractor or an employee is decided by substance, not labels ([lesson 17.1](../module-17/lesson-01.md)).

> על חוזה להספקת נכס שיש להפיקו או לייצרו והקבלן קיבל עליו לתת את עיקר החמרים הדרושים לכך, יחולו הוראות חוק המכר, תשכ״ח–1968.

*Gist:* If the contractor supplies most of the materials for a thing to be made, the contract is a sale. [s. 8(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חוזה_קבלנות#סעיף_8) A carpenter who builds a kitchen from his own wood is a seller (Sale Law s. 2); one who installs a kitchen you bought is a contractor. Like the other special-contract laws, it applies only where no other law has special provisions and the parties did not agree otherwise (s. 8(א)).

### Defects: notice and the chance to repair

> (א) לא היו המלאכה או השירות בהתאם למוסכם (להלן – פגם), על המזמין להודיע לקבלן על הפגם זמן סביר לאחר שגילה אותו או שהיה עליו לגלותו, ואם הפגם ניתן לתיקון – לתת לקבלן הזדמנות נאותה לתקנו.
> (ב) אין המזמין זכאי להסתמך על הפגם – (1) אם לא קיים את המוטל עליו לפי סעיף קטן (א) זולת אם ידע הקבלן על הפגם; (2) אם בא הפגם מסיבה שהמזמין אחראי לה.

*Gist:* The customer must notify within a reasonable time after he discovered, or should have discovered, the defect, and give the contractor a proper chance to repair it; otherwise (unless the contractor knew of it) he may not rely on it, nor on a defect he caused. [s. 3](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חוזה_קבלנות#סעיף_3) "Reasonable time" is softer than the Sale Law's "immediately".

> (א) לא תיקן הקבלן את הפגם תוך זמן סביר לאחר שהמזמין עשה כאמור בסעיף 3(א), רשאי המזמין – (1) לתקן את הפגם ולדרוש מהקבלן החזרת הוצאותיו הסבירות; (2) לנכות מן השכר, כל עוד לא תוקן הפגם, את הסכום שבו פחת שווי המלאכה או השירות עקב הפגם [...]
> (ב) פגם שתיקונו דחוף עד שאין לדרוש מן המזמין שימתין לתיקונו בידי הקבלן, רשאי המזמין לתקנו [...] גם בלי שעשה כאמור בסעיף 3(א).
> (ג) לא ניתן הפגם לתיקון, זכאי המזמין לניכוי מן השכר [...]

*Gist:* If the contractor does not repair within a reasonable time after notice, the customer may repair and recover reasonable costs, or deduct the loss of value from the fee; an urgent defect may be repaired without first giving the contractor a chance; a defect that cannot be repaired justifies a deduction. [s. 4](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חוזה_קבלנות#סעיף_4) These are added to the general remedies (s. 7): cancellation for fundamental breach and damages ([lesson 03.8](../module-03/lesson-08.md)).

### The customer's property in the contractor's hands

> לקבלן תהא זכות עכבון על נכס שמסר לו המזמין לביצוע מלאכתו או למתן שירותו, כדי תשלום הסכומים המגיעים לו מן המזמין עקב עיסקת הקבלנות.

*Gist:* The contractor may hold the customer's property that was given to him for the job until he is paid what is due for that job. [s. 5](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חוזה_קבלנות#סעיף_5) The lien covers sums from that transaction, not old debts from other jobs.

> (ב) אבד או ניזק הנכס בעודו בידי הקבלן – (1) אחראי הקבלן, לענין חוק השומרים, תשכ״ז–1967, כשומר שכר ששמירת הנכס אינה טפלה למטרה העיקרית של החזקתו; (2) פטור המזמין מתשלום השכר, זולת אם אבד או ניזק הנכס אחרי שהמזמין היה חייב לקבלו ומסיבה שהקבלן אינו אחראי לה.

*Gist:* While the contractor holds the property he is liable as a full paid bailee, and if it is lost or damaged the customer need not pay, unless it happened after the customer should have collected it and was not the contractor's fault. [s. 6(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חוזה_קבלנות#סעיף_6) A paid bailee is liable for loss or damage unless caused by circumstances he could not foresee and whose results he could not prevent ([Bailees Law s. 2(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_השומרים#סעיף_2)). The customer must collect the work on the agreed date or within a reasonable time after notice of completion (s. 6(א)).

### No forced personal work, and agreed damages

> הנפגע זכאי לאכיפת החוזה, זולת אם נתקיימה אחת מאלה: [...] (2) אכיפת החוזה היא כפיה לעשות, או לקבל, עבודה אישית או שירות אישי; [...]

*Gist:* A court will not order a contractor to perform personal work, or a customer to accept it. [Remedies Law s. 3(2)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28תרופות_בשל_הפרת_חוזה%29#סעיף_3) The practical remedy against a contractor who walks off the job is cancellation, hiring someone else, and damages for the extra cost. Agreed damages for delay are enforced without proof of damage, but the court may reduce them if they bear no reasonable relation to the damage foreseeable at contracting (s. 15(א)).

### The consumer layer for services

- **Continuing services** (gym, internet, cleaning, maintenance contracts): the consumer may cancel by any of the routes in s. 14ט and the contract ends within a few business days of the notice (s. 13ד(ג)); continued charging after cancellation exposes the business to exemplary damages (s. 31א(א)(2ב)).
- **Services bought at a distance:** cancellation within 14 days, and for a one-off service only if at least two non-rest days remain before it (s. 14ג(ג)(2); [lesson 03.10](../module-03/lesson-10.md)).
- **Technician visits** must be within set hours and waiting times, with fixed compensation for lateness (s. 18א(ג)–(ד)). Amounts: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

## Leading cases

### Key text: the eleven sections and their place in the codification

The Contract for Services Law was one of the special-contract laws (with the Sale Law, the Rental and Borrowing Law, the Bailees Law, the Guarantee Law and others) that replaced the Ottoman Majalla's second book (s. 9) and cut the link to English common law (s. 10). Its value lies in the defaults it supplies when the parties wrote nothing: what counts as a defect, who may repair, who bears the risk. Most reported disputes turn on facts (was the defect notified, was the repair chance real) rather than on the text, which is why a well-documented file usually decides the case.

### ע״א 1846/92 לוי נ׳ מבט בנייה בע״מ (1993)

Buyers of a new apartment suffered years of water penetration that the builder failed to repair effectively; they cancelled and the court allowed cancellation. On appeal, the Supreme Court held that, although restitution required them to pay for their years of use, damages put them where they would have been had the contract been performed, so they recovered the same sum and the deduction was cancelled. Why it matters here: repeated unsuccessful repairs after notice are the classic path from "give the contractor a chance" (s. 3(א)) to cancellation for fundamental breach, and cancellation need not leave the customer paying for the contractor's failure. Lesson [03.8](../module-03/lesson-08.md) used it for the remedies arithmetic. [Cardozo ISCP](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/237)

## How it works in practice

### Before the job

- Write the scope: drawings, materials (brand, model), dates, price per stage, who supplies what (this decides sale or service, s. 8(ב)).
- Pay by stages against completed work, not a large advance.
- Agree a delay clause with reasonable agreed damages.
- Photograph the starting condition; keep receipts for materials you supply.

### When something goes wrong

1. Write the defect list with photos, and give a date for repair (s. 3(א)). For a contractor who stopped working, also give an extension to resume, then cancel (Remedies Law s. 7(ב)).
2. Allow access and log every visit.
3. After the reasonable time: get two quotes, hire another contractor, keep invoices (s. 4(א)(1)); or keep the work and deduct the loss of value from the unpaid fee (s. 4(א)(2)).
4. If he holds your property (car, laptop, dress), pay the undisputed amount, or the full amount under protest, to get it back; then claim. His lien covers only the current job.

## Worked example

The Friedman family in Beit Shemesh hire Avraham Cohen, a renovator, to redo their bathroom for ₪38,000: ₪15,000 on signing, ₪15,000 at "tiling done", ₪8,000 at the end, finish by 1 May. The Friedmans buy the tiles and sanitary ware themselves. Cohen tiles the room, takes the second payment and disappears on 20 April with the toilet uninstalled and a shower slope that floods the floor. He does not answer calls. The Friedmans also left their old washing machine at his storeroom to be disposed of; meanwhile their son's laptop is held by a repair lab that demands ₪600 for a new job plus ₪400 left unpaid from an older repair.

1. **Classification.** The Friedmans supplied the main materials, so this is a contract for services, not a sale (s. 8(ב)).
2. **Defect and abandonment.** The flooding slope is a defect (s. 3(א)); the stoppage is a breach of the duty to do the work (s. 2). Write to Cohen by WhatsApp and registered mail: list the defects, demand return to work and repair by a date (say 10 days).
3. **Urgency.** The uninstalled toilet in a family home may justify an urgent repair without waiting (s. 4(ב)); keep the plumber's invoice.
4. **After the date passes.** Cancel the rest of the contract, hire another renovator, and claim the extra cost over the ₪8,000 still unpaid and the cost of redoing the slope (s. 4(א)(1); damages). They cannot get an order forcing Cohen to finish (Remedies Law s. 3(2)).
5. **The laptop.** The lab's lien covers the ₪600 for this job only (s. 5); the ₪400 from the older repair is not covered. Offer ₪600 and collect.
6. **Forum.** Small claims court for the cost of completion and repair, within the ceiling.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A service contract or quotation of yours (renovation, repair, event supplier), or the Friedman facts.
- The [notice of breach and cancellation template](../../templates/notice-of-breach-and-cancellation.md) (Parts A and B).
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. Classify the contract: service, sale (s. 8(ב)) or employment. Write one line of reasons.
2. List the contractor's obligations: scope, materials, dates, price stages.
3. Identify each defect or delay and whether it is urgent (s. 4(ב)).
4. Draft a notice on Part A: defects, a reasonable date to repair or resume, access offered, the self-repair and deduction warning (s. 4).
5. Draft the follow-up on Part B: cancellation after the date, and the list of damages with quotes.
6. If property is held, decide what amount the lien covers (s. 5).

### Template

[Notice of breach and cancellation](../../templates/notice-of-breach-and-cancellation.md), Parts A and B; for the claim, the [small claim template](../../templates/small-claim-contract-consumer.md).

### How you know it worked

- The notice gives a concrete repair date and offers access.
- The cancellation comes only after the date passed (or for an urgent defect, after a documented emergency).
- Your damages are supported by quotes or invoices, and you deducted what you did not pay.

### Self-check

- [ ] I can tell a contract for services from a sale under s. 8(ב).
- [ ] I know why I must normally let the contractor repair first.
- [ ] I know the limits of the contractor's lien.
- [ ] I know why a court will not order a contractor to finish the job.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. You may notify, negotiate, cancel and sue in your own name; disputes of this size usually go to the small claims court, which an individual may use (Courts Law s. 60) and where the contractor, even if a company, will be the defendant. Acting for other customers is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).

### Where and how

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- Notices: WhatsApp for speed, followed by email or registered mail for proof.
- Claims: small claims for amounts within the ceiling; magistrates' court above it; fees and ceiling in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Evidence: photos, the quotation, payment records, an independent professional's written assessment of the defect and the repair cost.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The job is large (structural work, a full renovation, building an extension) and the contractor disputes the defects.
- The contractor files a claim or a lien on your property for more than the job.
- You suffered bodily injury or there is a safety hazard.
- Employment status is in question (a "contractor" who was in substance your employee).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hiring a second contractor without notice to the first | Recovery refused (s. 3(ב)(1)) | Show urgency (s. 4(ב)) or the first contractor's refusal | Notice, a date, then act |
| Large advance payment | Money lost if the contractor disappears | Claim; consider criminal complaint only for fraud | Pay by stages against completed work |
| Withholding all payment for a small defect | You become the party in breach | Pay, deduct only the loss of value (s. 4(א)(2)) | Deduct proportionately and say why |
| Arguing with a garage over the car | Car stays held under s. 5 | Pay the job's sum under protest, then claim | Get a written quote before work |
| Vague scope ("renovate the bathroom") | Dispute over what was agreed | Rely on messages and practice (Contracts Law s. 26) | Detailed scope and materials list |

## Review

- A contract for services is work for pay by a non-employee; if the contractor supplies most materials for a thing to be made, it is a sale.
- Notify defects within a reasonable time and give a proper chance to repair; then self-repair or deduct (ss. 3–4); urgent defects may be repaired at once.
- The contractor has a lien on your property only for that job (s. 5) and holds it as a paid bailee (s. 6).
- No court will force personal work; cancel, replace and claim damages.
- Consumer law adds cancellation of continuing services and technician-visit compensation.
