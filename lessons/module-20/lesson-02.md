# 20.2 · Buying a new apartment: the Sale (Apartments) Law, defects and buyer guarantees

By the end of this lesson you will be able to read a developer's contract the way the law reads it, check that your money is protected at each payment, and turn a list of defects found after handover into a notice that keeps every right the Sale (Apartments) Law gives you. Lesson [13.9](../module-13/lesson-09.md) covered a second-hand flat and the land registry; this lesson covers what is special about buying new from a developer.

## Why it matters

A new apartment is usually the largest purchase of an Israeli family, paid in instalments to a company for a building that does not yet exist. Two risks dominate. The first is losing the money if the developer collapses before handover: the Assurance of Investment Law 1974 answers it with mandatory security. The second is a flat delivered late, different from the plans or full of defects: the Sale (Apartments) Law 1973 answers it with a mandatory specification, statutory inspection and warranty periods with shifting burdens of proof, fixed delay compensation, and a ban on contracting out "except in the buyer's favour" (s. 7א). Both laws are consumer statutes in substance; developers' standard contracts routinely contain clauses that these laws make void. Knowing which clauses are void is worth more than negotiating them.

## The law

### Who is protected, and against whom

A "מוכר" is someone who sells an apartment he built or will build, himself or through others, in order to sell it, including someone who bought it from the builder in order to resell ([s. 1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המכר_(דירות)#סעיף_1)). A private owner selling his home is not a "seller" under this law; the general Sale Law (lesson 20.1) applies. "Sale" includes a lease of 25 years or more, which covers most Israel Land Authority land. The law protects the buyer and later buyers (קונה משנה).

### The specification

> המוכר דירה חייב לצרף לחוזה המכר מפרט והוראות תחזוקה ושימוש.

*Gist:* The seller must attach a specification and maintenance instructions to the contract; any change in the specification needs both signatures. [s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המכר_(דירות)#סעיף_2) The specification is in the minister's statutory form and must be filled in fully and precisely (s. 3(א), (ג)); it includes a price list of items the buyer may waive, move or add (s. 3(א1), (ה)–(ו)).

> מוכר שלא מסר לקונה מפרט בהתאם לסעיף 2, יראוהו, על אף האמור בחוזה המכר, כמי שהתחייב למסור את הדירה לקונה כשכל דבר שצריך היה לפרט במפרט הוא באיכות גבוהה בהתחשב בנסיבות העניין.

*Gist:* No specification, or a defective one, means the seller is treated as having promised high quality in every item it should have specified, and the burden of proving that quality is his. [s. 5](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המכר_(דירות)#סעיף_5)

### Non-conformity and the periods

> המוכר לא קיים את חיוביו כלפי הקונה או קונה המשנה אם התקיים אחד מאלה:
> (1) הדירה או כל דבר שבה [...] שונים מן האמור במפרט, בתקן רשמי או בתקנות הבניה;
> (2) תוך תקופת הבדק התגלתה אי־התאמה בדירה, זולת אם הוכיח המוכר שאי־ההתאמה נגרמה בשל מעשה או מחדל של הקונה או קונה המשנה;
> (3) תוך תקופת האחריות התגלתה בדירה אי־התאמה והקונה או קונה המשנה הוכיח שמקורה בתכנון, בעבודה או בחומרים;
> (4) בתוך תקופה של 20 שנים [...] התגלתה אי־התאמה יסודית, זולת אם הוכיח המוכר שאי־ההתאמה היסודית נגרמה בשל מעשה או מחדל של הקונה [...];
> (5) התגלתה אי־התאמה יסודית, אף לאחר תקופת הבדק לאי־התאמה יסודית, והקונה הוכיח שמקורה בתכנון, בעבודה או בחומרים;
> (6) המוכר לא מסר לקונה הוראות תחזוקה ושימוש.

*Gist:* Any departure from the specification, an official standard or the building regulations is a breach; a defect found in the inspection period is presumed the seller's unless he proves the buyer caused it; in the following warranty period the buyer must prove it comes from design, work or materials; structural defects carry a long presumption period and then the same buyer's burden. [s. 4(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המכר_(דירות)#סעיף_4) These breaches count as non-conformity under the Sale Law (s. 4(ב)), so its remedies apply.

The **inspection period** depends on the item and is set in the [Schedule](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המכר_(דירות)#תוספת): from one year for "any other non-conformity" up to longer periods for joinery, flooring, machinery, yard works, insulation, piping, sealing, cracks in non-bearing walls and exterior cladding. The **warranty period** is three years from the end of the inspection period (s. 4(ג)). All periods run from handover. Exact periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### Notice and the right to repair

> (א) הקונה או קונה המשנה יהיה זכאי להסתמך על אי־התאמה –
> (1) שניתן היה לגלותה בעת העמדת הדירה לרשות הקונה, אם הודיע עליה [...] בתוך שנה ממועד העמדת הדירה לרשות הקונה;
> (2) שלא ניתן היה לגלותה בבדיקה סבירה בעת העמדת הדירה לרשותו אם הודיע עליה למוכר תוך זמן סביר לאחר שגילה אותה.

*Gist:* Visible defects must be notified within a fixed period from handover; hidden defects within a reasonable time after discovery. [s. 4א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המכר_(דירות)#סעיף_4א) This is more generous than s. 14 of the Sale Law ("immediately").

> (א) התגלתה אי־התאמה או אי התאמה יסודית הניתנות לתיקון, על הקונה או קונה המשנה לתת למוכר הזדמנות נאותה לתקנה, ועל המוכר לתקנה תוך זמן סביר.
> (ב) חזרה והתגלתה אי־ההתאמה [...] לאחר שהמוכר תיקן אותה פעם אחת או יותר במשך שנתיים [...] או שתיקונה [...] דחוף והמוכר לא תיקן אותה בתוך זמן סביר [...], רשאי הקונה או קונה המשנה לתקנה והמוכר יישא בהוצאות התיקון.

*Gist:* The buyer must give the developer a fair chance to repair; only a recurring defect within two years of notice, or an urgent one left unrepaired, lets the buyer repair at the developer's cost. [s. 4ב](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המכר_(דירות)#סעיף_4ב) This is the developer's "right to repair" (זכות התיקון), and courts often enforce it before awarding money.

### Delay, linkage, registration, legal fees

- **Delay compensation (s. 5א).** For contracts made from 7 July 2022, once a set period after the contractual handover date has passed, the buyer is entitled, without proving damage, to monthly compensation equal to the rent of a similar flat, rising by multipliers the longer the delay, or the contractual compensation if higher; no compensation if the buyer alone caused the delay or for force majeure under s. 18 of the Remedies Law. Older contracts have a different formula. Multipliers and periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md). [s. 5א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המכר_(דירות)#סעיף_5א)
- **Late-payment interest** is capped by the minister and starts only after a grace period (s. 5ב).
- **Linkage (s. 5ג, contracts from July 2022).** Payments are not linked to any index, except that the parties may link up to half of each payment to the construction-inputs index, never the first 20% paid at signing, and only until the contractual handover date.
- **Registration (s. 6ב).** The developer must register the condominium and then the buyer's rights within set periods ([lesson 13.6](../module-13/lesson-06.md)).
- **Legal fees (s. 6ג).** The developer may charge the buyer for its lawyer's registration work only up to a capped amount, paid directly to the lawyer.

> (א) אין להתנות על הוראות חוק זה, אלא לטובת הקונה או קונה המשנה.
> (ב) [...] ויתור, לרבות בכתב, על זכויות מצד הקונה [...] בקשר לאיחור במסירה לפי סעיף 5א או לאי־התאמה [...], המהווה תנאי להעברת זכויות מקונה לקונה משנה, לביצוע תיקונים בדירה או להעמדת הדירה לרשות הקונה – בטל.

*Gist:* The law cannot be contracted out of against the buyer, and a waiver demanded as the price of handover, repairs or consent to resale is void. [s. 7א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המכר_(דירות)#סעיף_7א)

The 2022 commencement: the amendment came into force seven days after publication (s. 5 of [Amendment 9](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/24/law/24_lsr_628987.pdf), passed 30 June 2022). The Wikisource consolidation gives 7 July 2022 in the section notes but 7.7.2023 in s. 11(ד); the Knesset text supports 2022.

### Securing the buyer's money

> לא יקבל מוכר מקונה, על חשבון מחיר הדירה, סכום העולה על שבעה אחוזים מהמחיר, אלא אם עשה אחת מאלה, והכל על אף האמור בחוזה המכר: (1) מסר לקונה ערבות בנקאית או ערבות מנותן ערבות אחר [...]; (2) ביטח את עצמו אצל מבטח [...]; (3) שעבד את הדירה [...] במשכנתה ראשונה לטובת הקונה [...]; (4) רשם [...] הערת אזהרה על מכירת הדירה [...] ובלבד שלא נרשמו לגביהם שעבוד, עיקול או זכות של צד שלישי שיש להם עדיפות על ההערה; (5) העביר על שם הקונה את הבעלות [...].

*Gist:* Beyond a small first payment, the developer may take money only against one of five securities: a bank or other licensed guarantee, an insurance policy, a first mortgage, a clean first-ranking caveat, or registered transfer. [Assurance of Investment Law s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המכר_(דירות)_(הבטחת_השקעות_של_רוכשי_דירות)#סעיף_2) The guarantee covers the money paid excluding the VAT component, which a state fund repays if the guarantee is called (ss. 3ג1–3ג2). The guarantee is triggered by an attachment, insolvency order or receivership against the seller or landowner, or an "absolute impediment" to handover; cancellation of the contract alone is not such an impediment.

Other duties: without guarantee or insurance, payments may not exceed the stages set in the [1975 Regulations](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_המכר_(דירות)_(הבטחת_השקעות_של_רוכשי_דירות)_(סייג_לתשלומים_על_חשבון_מחיר_דירה)) (s. 3); securing costs are the seller's (s. 3א); the buyer must be told in writing of these rights and of the absence of project financing (s. 2א1); in a financed project, payments go only through the bank's voucher booklet, and each payment is an irrevocable instruction to issue a guarantee within a fixed number of business days (s. 3ב); a mortgage lender may release loan money only after checking that security was given (s. 3ג); and a supervisor at the Ministry of Construction and Housing keeps a register and examines public complaints (s. 3ד).

## Leading cases

### Key text: the two-track structure and the 2008 and 2022 reforms

We could not open a Supreme Court ruling on these laws on a free official host on 17 October 2026 (supremedecisions.court.gov.il was unreachable), so this lesson relies on the statutory text and its amendment history, which the consolidation records section by section.

- **The 1990 and 2011 amendments (Nos. 3 and 5)**, as the consolidation's tags on ss. 4–4ב and the Schedule show, built the item-by-item inspection periods, the warranty period, the 20-year presumption for structural defects and the developer's right to repair before the buyer may repair at its cost. The policy choice is a sliding burden: the newer the defect, the more the law presumes it is the builder's.
- **The 2008 Assurance amendment** (tagged תשס״ח in ss. 3ב–3ח) created the voucher booklet, the project account and the supervisor. Its premise: buyers do not read guarantees, so the law makes the payment channel itself produce the guarantee.
- **The 2022 amendment (No. 9)** rewrote delay compensation in steps tied to market rent, limited index linkage of the price and voided waivers extracted as a condition of handover or repairs.

Why it matters: these are consumer-protection rules dressed as contract law. In a dispute, start from the statute and treat the developer's clause as valid only if it is more favourable to you (s. 7א). The Pamesa case (lesson 20.1) shows the other end of the chain: the developer who repairs a defective product can claim from its supplier, so a developer's "the supplier is to blame" is not your problem.

## How it works in practice

### At signing

1. Read the specification against the plans and the sales brochure; anything promised orally goes into the specification, signed (s. 2(ב)).
2. Check who the seller is, whether there is project financing (a voucher booklet) and the written notice under s. 2א1.
3. Check the payment schedule: first payment within the threshold; every later payment through vouchers or against one of the s. 2 securities.
4. Strike or note void clauses: blanket waivers, "delay compensation only after six months", full linkage, legal fees above the cap.
5. Register a caveat if that is the chosen security, and check the extract for prior charges ([lesson 13.4](../module-13/lesson-04.md)).

### At handover and after

1. Do a handover inspection with an independent engineer (בדק בית); photograph everything.
2. List defects in the handover protocol; sign "subject to reservations", never "accepted without claims".
3. Send a written defects notice within the period for visible defects; later notices for hidden defects as they appear (s. 4א).
4. Give access for repairs (s. 4ב(א)); log every visit and every recurrence.
5. If a defect recurs within two years of notice, or an urgent defect is left, repair and claim the cost (s. 4ב(ב)).
6. Keep a defects file by item, with the Schedule period for each.

## Worked example

Tsega and Avi Mengistu sign in May 2023 to buy a four-room flat off-plan in Netanya from a developer for ₪2.2 million, handover "by 1 June 2025". The project has bank financing and a voucher booklet. The contract says: "delay compensation will be paid only after a delay of six months"; "the buyer waives any claim upon receiving the keys". They receive the keys on 1 December 2025. In January 2026 they find a leak under the kitchen sink and cracked floor tiles; in September 2026 damp appears on the bedroom wall below the balcony.

1. **Payments.** Each payment through a voucher must produce a bank guarantee (s. 3ב(ה)); they check that they hold guarantees covering all payments except VAT. One payment was made by bank transfer at the sales office's request: they ask the bank to confirm a guarantee for it, or demand the developer secure it (s. 2).
2. **Delay.** Contract made after July 2022, so the new s. 5א applies. The six-month clause is void as less favourable than the law (s. 7א). They claim rent-based compensation per month from the date set in s. 5א, at the stepped rate, unless the developer proves force majeure.
3. **Waiver at handover.** Void under s. 7א(ב), because it was a condition of receiving the keys.
4. **Leak and tiles.** Within the inspection period (piping, flooring, Schedule items): presumed the developer's; notify now; give access to repair (s. 4ב(א)).
5. **Damp.** Possibly a sealing failure (Schedule item 7, a long inspection period): presumed the developer's. Hidden, so notice "within a reasonable time after discovery" (s. 4א(א)(2)).
6. **If repairs fail.** If the leak recurs after a repair within two years of notice, they may repair and claim the cost (s. 4ב(ב)); otherwise they claim in court with an engineer's opinion.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A developer's contract and specification (yours, a relative's, or the sample many developers publish online), or the Mengistu facts above.
- The [new-apartment check and defects notice (draft)](../../templates/new-apartment-check-and-defects-notice.md).
- 50 minutes.

### Steps

1. Fill Part A of the template: seller, project financing, securities, payment schedule against the s. 2 threshold and the voucher rule.
2. Mark every clause about delay, linkage, waivers and legal fees: valid, void (s. 7א) or valid only because it favours the buyer.
3. For three defects (supplied or yours), fill the defects table in Part B: item, Schedule category, period, burden of proof, date of discovery, notice deadline.
4. Draft the defects notice in Part C.
5. Write two lines: when you would let the developer repair, and when you would repair and claim.

### How you know it worked

- Every payment is matched with a security or is within the first threshold.
- Each defect has a Schedule category and a burden of proof.
- Void clauses are named with the section that voids them.
- The notice offers access to repair and reserves the s. 4ב(ב) right.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Partly. You may check your contract, send defects notices, deal with the developer's service department, complain to the supervisor under s. 3ד or to the bank supervisor, and sue for defects in your own name. In practice the purchase itself is done with a lawyer (and the developer's lawyer registers the rights); a defects claim usually needs an engineer's expert opinion, which you may commission yourself. Advising or drafting for other buyers in the building is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20); the house committee may hire one for common-property defects.

### Where and how

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- Defects notice: in writing to the developer and, if named in the contract, its service contractor; keep proof of delivery.
- Complaints on money security: the supervisor at the Ministry of Construction and Housing (s. 3ד; ⚠️ check the current gov.il service page); bank matters go to the Supervisor of Banks (s. 3ד(ד)). A complaint does not extend any deadline (s. 3ד(ג)(4)(ב)).
- Claims: small claims court for a modest defects or delay claim by an individual within the ceiling; magistrates' court above it ([lesson 15.2](../module-15/lesson-02.md)); fees and ceilings in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The developer is in financial trouble, a receiver is appointed or the bank refuses a guarantee.
- Structural defects or defects in the common property affect the whole building.
- The delay compensation claim is large or the developer pleads force majeure.
- You are asked to sign any waiver or "final settlement".

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paying by transfer outside the voucher booklet | Payment may be unsecured | Demand a guarantee for it at once | Pay only by voucher |
| Signing "received without reservations" | Developer argues waiver | Rely on s. 7א(ב); send a defects notice | Write reservations in the protocol |
| Notifying visible defects after the fixed period | Loss of reliance on them (s. 4א(א)(1)) | Argue the defect was hidden | Defects notice soon after handover |
| Repairing yourself without giving access | Claim reduced or refused (s. 4ב(א)) | Show the developer refused or the repair was urgent | Offer access in writing first |
| Accepting the contract's delay formula | Lower compensation than the statute | Claim under s. 5א; clause is void | Compare every clause with s. 5א |

## Review

- The developer must deliver a full statutory specification; a missing or defective one means "high quality" with the burden on the developer.
- Defects in the inspection period are presumed the developer's; in the warranty period the buyer must prove origin; structural defects carry a long presumption.
- Visible defects need notice within the fixed period from handover; hidden defects within a reasonable time of discovery; the developer has a right to repair first.
- Delay compensation is statutory, rent-based and cannot be reduced by contract; waivers demanded for handover are void.
- Beyond the first payment, every shekel must be secured, usually by the voucher booklet and a bank guarantee.
