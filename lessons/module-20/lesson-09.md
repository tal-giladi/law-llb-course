# 20.9 · Practice lab: an apartment-purchase checklist and a defects claim to the contractor

In this lab you take one new apartment from the developer's draft contract to a defects claim: you mark up the contract, match every payment with a security, build the defects file after handover, send the notice, and outline the claim. It applies lesson 20.2 and the registry checks of lesson [13.9](../module-13/lesson-09.md).

## Why it matters

Buyers of new flats usually discover their rights only after the problems appear, when the contract is signed, the money paid and the defects period running. Doing the whole cycle once, on paper, shows where the decisive moments are: the payment channel at signing, the protocol at handover, the first written notice, and the decision whether to let the developer repair or to claim.

## The law

The lab applies lesson 20.2. The rules you will use most:

- Specification and maintenance instructions attached, full and signed (Sale (Apartments) Law ss. 2–3); a missing or defective specification means "high quality" with the burden on the developer (s. 5).
- Non-conformity heads and periods, and burden of proof (s. 4 and the Schedule); notice rules (s. 4א); the developer's right to repair and the buyer's right to repair at its cost after a recurrence or for an urgent defect (s. 4ב).
- Delay compensation (s. 5א), interest and linkage limits (ss. 5ב–5ג), registration duties (s. 6ב), no waiver against the buyer (s. 7א).
- Securities for every payment beyond the first threshold (Assurance of Investment Law s. 2), the voucher system (s. 3ב), written notice of rights (s. 2א1). Full texts: [Sale (Apartments) Law](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המכר_(דירות)), [Assurance of Investment Law](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המכר_(דירות)_(הבטחת_השקעות_של_רוכשי_דירות)). Periods and amounts: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

## Leading cases

### Key text: lesson 20.2, and Levy v. Mabat on failed repairs

Lesson 20.2 explains why this area is governed mostly by statute. One ruling matters for the claim stage: in **ע״א 1846/92 לוי נ׳ מבט בנייה בע״מ (1993)** the buyers cancelled after years of unsuccessful repairs of water penetration, and the Supreme Court let them recover, as damages, the sum they had to pay as restitution for their use of the flat ([Cardozo ISCP](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/237)). The usual claim is not cancellation but the cost of repair plus delay and distress; Levy v. Mabat shows how far persistent failure can go.

## How it works in practice

The cycle in four moments:

1. **Signing.** Specification read against the plans; payment schedule matched to securities; void clauses noted; caveat or voucher system confirmed.
2. **Handover.** Independent inspection; protocol signed with reservations; photographs; meter readings.
3. **Notice.** One consolidated written notice soon after handover; separate notices for hidden defects as they appear; access offered.
4. **Claim.** If repairs fail or are refused: an expert opinion with repair costs, delay compensation calculated under s. 5א, and a claim in the right court.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. Send notices only for a flat you bought.

### You need

- The supplied case below, or your own developer contract and defects.
- The [new-apartment check and defects notice (draft)](../../templates/new-apartment-check-and-defects-notice.md), the [apartment purchase check](../../templates/apartment-purchase-check.md) and the [small claim template](../../templates/small-claim-contract-consumer.md).
- About two and a half hours.

**Supplied case.** Maayan and Dor Elkayam sign on 15 January 2024 to buy a three-room flat on the fifth floor of a new building in Be'er Sheva from "Negev Heights Building Ltd" for ₪1.48 million, handover "by 30 June 2025, subject to a grace period of 90 days". Extracts from the contract and events:

- Clause 7: "The buyer shall pay 15% on signing, 25% on completion of the frame, 40% on completion of plaster, 20% on handover." A voucher booklet from a bank is attached; the sales manager asked for the signing payment by transfer to the company's account.
- Clause 11: "Delay compensation shall be 1% of the paid sum for each full month of delay beyond the grace period."
- Clause 12: "All payments are linked to the construction-inputs index until actual handover."
- Clause 18: "Upon receipt of the keys the buyer confirms the apartment conforms to the specification and waives any claim."
- Clause 21: "Legal fees for registration: ₪9,500 plus VAT, payable to the seller."
- The specification lists "aluminium windows" with no brand or profile, and the line for kitchen cabinet length is blank.
- Keys are delivered on 1 December 2025. The buyers sign clause 18's protocol. An engineer they hire on 10 December finds: no slope in the service balcony floor (water pools), a cracked tile in the living room, a window that does not lock, a missing safety rail on the service balcony.
- In February 2026 damp appears under the window in the children's room. The developer's technician seals the outside in March; in October 2026 the damp returns.

### Steps

1. **Contract mark-up (30 min).** On Part A3 of the new-apartment template, classify clauses 7, 11, 12, 18 and 21: valid, void (with section), or valid only as more favourable.
2. **Money (20 min).** On Part A2, match each payment stage to a security. What do you do about the signing payment by transfer?
3. **Specification (10 min).** What follows from the vague window line and the blank kitchen line (ss. 3, 5)?
4. **Defects file (30 min).** On Part B, list each defect, its Schedule item and period, burden of proof, visible or hidden, and the notice deadline.
5. **Notice (25 min).** Draft the Part C notice for the December defects; then a second notice for the recurring damp, invoking s. 4ב(ב).
6. **Delay (15 min).** Compute months of delay under s. 5א as compared with clause 11 (assume similar rent ₪4,000 a month; use the multipliers in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
7. **Claim outline (20 min).** Forum, parties, heads of claim with amounts, evidence list, and what you ask the court to do first.

### How you know it worked

- Every clause has a section and a verdict.
- Every payment has a security or a demand to secure it.
- Each defect has a Schedule item, a period, a burden of proof and a notice date.
- The damp notice relies on recurrence within two years of the first notice.
- The delay figure is computed under s. 5א, not clause 11.

**1. Clauses.** Clause 7: payments by stage are lawful only if each payment beyond the first threshold is secured; in a financed project, every payment goes by voucher (Assurance s. 3ב). Clause 11: void to the extent it gives less than s. 5א; the contract was made after July 2022, so the statutory rent-based formula applies from the point set in s. 5א, without the 90-day "grace period" beyond what the statute allows (s. 7א). Clause 12: void beyond s. 5ג: at most half of each payment may be linked, never the 20% paid at signing, and only up to the contractual handover date, not actual handover. Clause 18: void under s. 7א(ב) as a waiver required as a condition of handover. Clause 21: valid only up to the statutory maximum under s. 6ג and the regulations, paid directly to the lawyer against a tax invoice; ₪9,500 plus VAT to the seller is suspect on both counts.

**2. Money.** Signing payment of 15% (₪222,000): above the first threshold, so it must be secured; it was paid by transfer, outside the voucher system. Demand immediately in writing that the developer secure it under s. 2 (bank guarantee or insurance through the accompanying bank) and write to the bank; if refused, complain to the supervisor (s. 3ד) and get a lawyer. Later stages: pay only by voucher and check a guarantee arrives within the business-days period in s. 3ב(ה).

**3. Specification.** The window line lacks required detail and the kitchen line is blank: for those items the developer is treated as having promised high quality in the circumstances, and must prove it delivered it (s. 5).

**4. Defects file.**

| Defect | Category | Burden | Notice |
|---|---|---|---|
| No slope on service balcony | Departure from building regulations and standards (s. 4(א)(1)); also flooring/sealing items | Developer (breach on its face; inspection period) | Visible at handover: within the fixed period from 1 Dec 2025 |
| Cracked tile | Flooring and cladding (Schedule item 2) | Developer, inspection period | Visible: same |
| Window does not lock | Joinery/aluminium (item 1); also habitability-style safety | Developer | Visible: same |
| Missing safety rail | Building regulations (s. 4(א)(1)); urgent safety | Developer | Visible; urgent: s. 4ב(ב) if not fixed promptly |
| Damp under window | Sealing (item 7) | Developer, inspection period | Hidden: reasonable time after discovery (Feb 2026); recurrence Oct 2026 |

**5. Notices.** First notice in December 2025 lists the four defects with the engineer's report, offers access, demands repair in reasonable time, flags the safety rail as urgent, and states that the clause 18 waiver is void. Second notice in October 2026: the damp recurred after a repair within two years of the February notice; the buyers will repair (with the engineer's specification) and claim the cost (s. 4ב(ב)).

**6. Delay.** Contractual date 30 June 2025, keys 1 December 2025: five months. Under the post-2022 s. 5א, no compensation for the first month after the contractual date; then rent from the end of month one to the end of month four, then 1.25 times rent from month five. With ₪4,000 rent: August to October, three months at ₪4,000 = ₪12,000; November at ₪5,000 if month five is counted (the text's step boundaries leave a one-month gap on a literal reading; argue the purposive reading): about ₪17,000. Clause 11 (1% of paid sums after a 90-day grace) is lower and cannot reduce the statutory figure.

**7. Claim outline.** Plaintiffs: both buyers. Defendant: the developer company. Heads: repair costs per expert (balcony slope, tile, window, rail, damp), delay compensation under s. 5א, linkage overcharged under clause 12, legal fees above the cap, distress. If the total is within the small claims ceiling, the small claims court; otherwise the magistrates' court with a court expert likely to be appointed. First request: an order that the developer carry out repairs under the expert's supervision within a fixed time, or pay the repair cost; plus the money heads. Evidence: contract, specification, vouchers and guarantees, protocol, engineer's report and photographs, notices with delivery proof, the technician's March 2026 visit record.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Partly. Checking the contract, sending notices, complaining to the supervisor and a modest small claim are within reach in your own name. A claim with an expert opinion against a developer in the magistrates' court, or anything involving an unsecured payment or the developer's insolvency, is usually handled with a lawyer. You may not advise or act for other buyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).

### Where and how

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- Notices to the developer by email and registered mail.
- Money security: the supervisor under s. 3ד (Ministry of Construction and Housing, ⚠️ manual check); the accompanying bank.
- Court: small claims or magistrates' court ([lesson 15.2](../module-15/lesson-02.md)); fees and ceiling in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- A payment is unsecured and the developer stalls.
- The defects affect the structure or the common property of the whole building.
- The developer sues you for linkage or late-payment interest.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paying outside vouchers | Unsecured money | Demand security at once | Vouchers only |
| One notice and then silence | Hidden defects missed | New notice per defect | Keep the defects file live |
| Claiming without an expert opinion | Defects unproven | Commission one before filing | Engineer at handover and before suing |
| Using the contract's delay formula | Lower compensation | Claim under s. 5א | Compare with the statute |

## Review

- The decisive moments are signing (securities), handover (protocol), first notice, and the repair-or-claim decision.
- Void clauses cost nothing to ignore but must be named.
- A defects file by Schedule item makes notices and claims straightforward.
- Recurrence within two years of notice turns the developer's right to repair into the buyer's right to repair at its cost.
