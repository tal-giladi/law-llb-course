# 20.5 · Guarantees and the protected guarantor

By the end of this lesson you will be able to read a guarantee the way a court will, before you sign it for a relative or friend and after the debtor stops paying. You will know when the guarantor is a mere back-up the creditor must approach second, when he is protected by special rules that banks must follow, and which of those rules release him altogether.

## Why it matters

Guarantees are how Israeli families finance one another: parents guarantee a child's mortgage, siblings a business loan, friends a lease. When the debtor defaults, the guarantor often learns for the first time how much he owes, because interest, fees and penalties accrued while nobody told him. The Guarantee Law 1967 sets general rules for every guarantee; since the 1990s it adds a chapter for individual guarantors facing professional lenders, rewritten in the 1997 amendment, that requires a fixed sum, full disclosure before signing, timely notice of default and, for the protected guarantor, that the lender first exhaust its remedies against the debtor. Lesson [03.3](../module-03/lesson-03.md) introduced the fixed-sum rule as a form requirement; here you learn the whole structure and its limits, especially the definition of "creditor" that excludes most private creditors.

## The law

### The general part: every guarantee

> ערבות היא התחייבותו של אדם לקיים חיובו של אדם אחר כלפי אדם שלישי.

*Gist:* A guarantee is a promise to perform another person's obligation to a third person; it may cover all or part of an obligation, present or future, fixed or not. [s. 1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הערבות#סעיף_1) There is no guarantee without a valid obligation (s. 2); it arises by agreement with the creditor or a notified undertaking (s. 3).

The guarantor's general defences:

- **Accessory liability.** The guarantor owes no more, and nothing more onerous, than the debtor (s. 4(א)); every defence of the debtor is his (s. 7(א)); if the claim against the debtor is time-barred, so is the claim against him (s. 7(ב)).
- **Changes.** A reduction of the debt releases the guarantor to the same extent; an increase agreed by creditor and debtor does not bind him; another change binds him, but he may keep the old terms or cancel if the change is fundamental and harms him (s. 5).
- **Creditor's fault.** If the creditor caused the non-performance, the guarantor is released; if the creditor let another security lapse, he is released to the extent of the loss (s. 6).
- **Demand first.** Guarantor and debtor are jointly and severally liable, but the creditor must first demand payment from the debtor, unless the guarantor waived that or the debtor is insolvent, dead, abroad or hard to reach (s. 8). Most bank forms include the waiver.
- **Future debts.** A guarantee of future debts may be cancelled by written notice to the creditor while the debt has not yet arisen, compensating the creditor for loss caused by the cancellation (s. 15).
- **Recourse.** A guarantor who paid may recover from the debtor with reasonable costs and interest (s. 9), and takes over the creditor's securities over the debtor's property (s. 12).

### Chapter ב: the individual guarantor and a professional lender

> ”נושה“ – מי שמתן הלוואות הוא במהלך עסקיו הרגיל, אף אם אינו עיסוקו העיקרי;
> ”ערב יחיד“ – מי שאינו תאגיד, ולמעט בן זוג של החייב או שותפו של החייב [...]; היה החייב תאגיד, לא ייחשב כערב יחיד גם מי שהוא בעל ענין בתאגיד [...]

*Gist:* The chapter protects an individual who is not the debtor's spouse or partner, nor an interested party in a debtor company, and only against a creditor who lends money in the ordinary course of business. [s. 19](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הערבות#סעיף_19) A private landlord, a seller on credit or a friend who lent money is not a "נושה" here, so their guarantors have only the general part.

> ”ערב מוגן“ – (1) ערב יחיד, שבחוזה הערבות [...] נקוב סכום שאינו עולה על 60,000 שקלים חדשים [...]; (2) ערב יחיד, שבחוזה הערבות [...] נקוב סכום שאינו עולה על 500,000 שקלים חדשים [...] ובלבד שהחיוב הנערב נועד לרכישת זכויות בדירה המיועדת למגורי החייב, ילדיו הבגירים או הוריו.

*Gist:* A protected guarantor is an individual guarantor whose guarantee is for a stated sum up to a lower ceiling, or up to a higher ceiling for a loan to buy a home for the debtor, his adult children or parents; the ceilings are index-linked and updated twice a year (s. 20). [s. 19](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הערבות#סעיף_19) Current figures: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

> (א) ערב יחיד ערב רק לסכום הנקוב בחוזה הערבות ולתוספות כמפורט בסעיף 25.
> (ב) לא נקוב בחוזה הערבות סכום קצוב – פטור ערב יחיד מערבותו.

*Gist:* An individual guarantor is liable only for the stated sum plus the permitted additions; with no stated sum he is released. [s. 21](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הערבות#סעיף_21) The permitted additions are agreed linkage and interest, late interest capped a few percentage points above the contract rate (running only after notice of default), and court or execution costs (s. 25).

**Disclosure (ss. 22–24).** Before signing the lender must disclose the principal, the annual effective rate, the term and repayment schedule, variable-rate mechanics, linkage, additions, the late-interest rate, whether the guarantor is individual or protected, the number of guarantors and his share, whether it is a new or replacement debt, and the late-interest additions. The consequences differ by item: release if the principal was not disclosed and there was a significant gap between guarantee and loan, or if his status, the replacement character or the late rate was not disclosed; the minimum customary rate if interest was not disclosed; the guarantor's own understanding at signing for other items (s. 23). He must get a copy and a reasonable chance to read it before signing (s. 24).

> (א) לא קיים החייב את חיובו, יודיע על כך הנושה לערב יחיד תוך 90 ימים מיום שהיה על החייב לקיים את החיוב; לא הודיע כאמור, יופטר הערב כדי הנזק שנגרם לו בשל כך.

*Gist:* The lender must notify the individual guarantor of the debtor's default within a fixed period, or the guarantor is released to the extent of his loss; before accelerating an instalment loan it must give advance notice, and the guarantor may then choose to continue the original instalments. [s. 26](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הערבות#סעיף_26) Notices go by registered mail to the address the guarantor gave (s. 26(ה)). Periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

> (א) לא תוגש תובענה נגד ערב מוגן אלא לאחר שנתקיימו שניים אלה: (1) ניתן פסק דין נגד החייב; (2) רשם ההוצאה לפועל אישר שעל מנת להיפרע מהחייב, ננקטו כל הליכי ההוצאה לפועל, לרבות הליכים למימוש משכנתה על דירת מגורים [...] והכל כשהם סבירים בנסיבות הענין.

*Gist:* A protected guarantor may be sued only after judgment against the debtor and confirmation by the execution registrar that all reasonable enforcement steps were taken, including on a mortgaged home; exceptions: the debtor died, became legally incompetent, entered insolvency or left Israel permanently, or a debtor company is in liquidation or receivership. [s. 27](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הערבות#סעיף_27) "Suing" includes a demand to pay, set-off and enforcing a security against him (s. 27(ד)). Protected guarantors share the debt equally, counted among all guarantors (s. 29).

> על אף האמור בכל דין, בערבות לפי פרק זה, התניה על הוראות הפרק ועל סעיפים 5, 6, 7, 11 ו־12 שאינה לטובת ערב יחיד בטלה.

*Gist:* No clause can take these protections away, nor the general defences in ss. 5–7, 11–12, from an individual guarantor. [s. 32](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הערבות#סעיף_32) Note what is missing from the list: s. 8 (demand from the debtor first), which a bank form may waive.

### Guarantees outside chapter ב

A lease guarantor, the guarantor of a car bought on credit from a private seller, or a guarantor of a supplier's debt has only the general part: no fixed-sum rule, no disclosure rule, no 90-day notice. For leases, a personal guarantor is outside the residential security cap (Rental and Borrowing Law s. 25י(ב), lesson 20.3). An **autonomous bank guarantee** (such as a Sale Law guarantee, lesson 20.2) is a different instrument: the bank pays on demand, independently of the underlying dispute, except in fraud.

## Leading cases

### Key text: the 1997 amendment and the policy of the chapter

We could not open a Supreme Court ruling on chapter ב on a free official host on 17 October 2026 (supremedecisions.court.gov.il was unreachable), so the key text is the [1997 amendment](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/14/law/14_lsr_211444.pdf) (Amendment No. 2, התשנ״ח), which replaced chapters ב and ג with the present individual-guarantor chapter. Its premises:

- **Information asymmetry.** The lender knows the debtor's risk; the guarantor signs out of family loyalty. Hence disclosure before signing and notice on default.
- **Bounded exposure.** A guarantor must know his maximum (s. 21), and late interest is capped and runs only after notice (s. 25).
- **Guarantee as last resort.** For the protected guarantor, the lender must exhaust the debtor first (s. 27), including selling a mortgaged home.
- **Limits of protection.** The chapter excludes spouses, partners and controlling holders of a debtor company, and creditors who are not professional lenders, because those guarantors are seen as part of the debtor's economic unit or as dealing with parties who are not institutional lenders.

The debate: lenders argue that protections raise the cost of credit and push them to demand guarantees from spouses or company controllers, who are unprotected; consumer advocates argue the exclusions are too broad. The text is the current compromise.

## How it works in practice

### Before you sign

1. Who is the creditor? A bank, credit company or other professional lender (chapter ב), or a private person (general part only)?
2. Are you an individual guarantor (not a spouse, partner or interested party in a debtor company)?
3. Is a fixed sum stated? Is it within a protected ceiling? Is it a home-purchase loan?
4. Did you receive the disclosure items and a copy in advance (ss. 22, 24)?
5. Read the waivers: demand from the debtor first (s. 8), joint and several liability, consent to changes.
6. Agree with the debtor in writing how he will keep you informed, and keep your address updated with the lender (s. 26(ה)).

### When the debtor defaults

1. Collect: the guarantee, the disclosure documents, every notice and its date.
2. Check the 90-day notice (s. 26(א)) and the acceleration notice (s. 26(ד)).
3. If protected, check whether s. 27's conditions are met before paying anything.
4. If you pay, keep receipts; claim recourse from the debtor (s. 9) and take over the securities (s. 12).

## Worked example

Wafa Halabi, a teacher in Daliyat al-Karmel, signed two guarantees for her brother Karim at a bank in 2024: (a) a ₪70,000 car loan to Karim personally, and (b) "all debts present and future" of Karim's carpentry company, in which Wafa holds no shares, no sum stated. In March 2026 Karim stops paying both. The bank writes to Wafa in September demanding ₪70,000 plus five months of late interest on the car loan, and ₪210,000 for the company's overdraft.

1. **Creditor.** A bank: chapter ב applies.
2. **Individual guarantor.** Sister, not spouse or partner; no interest in the company: individual guarantor for both.
3. **Company guarantee.** No stated sum: Wafa is released (s. 21(ב)). Nothing in the bank's form can change this (s. 32).
4. **Car loan.** ₪70,000 stated, within the protected ceiling (check the current figure): she is a protected guarantor. The bank did not sue Karim or take execution steps: it may not even demand payment from her yet (s. 27(א), (ד)).
5. **Late notice.** Notice came about six months after the default, well beyond the s. 26(א) period: she is released to the extent of her loss, at least the late interest that accrued meanwhile (and late interest runs only after notice in any case, s. 25(ב)).
6. **Answer.** Wafa writes to the bank: the company guarantee is void for lack of a sum; she is a protected guarantor and the s. 27 conditions are not met; late interest before notice is not owed. She asks for the disclosure documents (s. 24(ב)).

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A guarantee you signed or were asked to sign (a loan, a lease, a supplier credit line), or Wafa's facts.
- The [guarantee signing checklist (draft)](../../templates/guarantee-signing-checklist.md).
- 40 minutes.

### Steps

1. Identify the creditor and decide whether chapter ב applies (s. 19, "נושה").
2. Decide whether you are an individual guarantor and a protected guarantor; note the current ceilings from [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
3. Fill Part A of the checklist: sum, disclosure items received, waivers, number of guarantors.
4. Fill Part B for a default scenario: notice dates, acceleration notice, s. 27 status, defences under ss. 5–7.
5. Write your decision: sign as is, sign with changes (a lower sum, a time limit, a right to information), or refuse.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes, in your own name: you may refuse to sign, negotiate terms, write to the lender, and defend a claim or an execution file brought against you. In the execution office, a guarantor sued on a judgment or a promissory note may file an objection ([lesson 08.7](../module-08/lesson-07.md)). Defending other guarantors as a service is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).

### Where and how

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- Letters to the lender's collections department; complaints about a bank to the Bank of Israel's Banking Supervision Department public enquiries unit (⚠️ manual check of the current gov.il or boi.org.il page).
- Defence: in the court where you are sued, or by objection in the execution office; time limits for objections are short ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- Free help: Legal Aid where the means test is met; debt-counselling non-profits for budgeting (not legal representation).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You are sued for a large sum or the lender is enforcing against your home.
- The debtor is in insolvency proceedings and you are asked to sign a settlement.
- You guaranteed a company's debts and may be an "interested party".
- A promissory note (שטר חוב) you signed as guarantor is being enforced.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Signing "all debts present and future" for a private creditor | Unlimited exposure (chapter ב does not apply) | Cancel for future debts by written notice (s. 15) | Insist on a stated maximum and end date |
| Not updating your address with the lender | Notices "received" at an old address (s. 26(ה)) | Argue actual non-receipt; check the record | Send address changes by registered mail |
| Paying at the first demand | Protections under ss. 26–27 lost in practice | Recovery from the lender is hard; seek advice | Check ss. 21, 26, 27 first |
| Assuming a spouse is protected | Spouses are not individual guarantors (s. 19) | General defences only | Know the exclusion before signing |
| Signing a blank promissory note as "guarantee" | Fast execution against you | Object in the execution office | Sign only a guarantee with a stated sum |

## Review

- Every guarantee is accessory: the guarantor has the debtor's defences, is released by the creditor's fault, and may cancel for future debts.
- Chapter ב protects individual guarantors only against professional lenders, and excludes spouses, partners and interested parties in a debtor company.
- An individual guarantor owes only a stated sum plus limited additions; with no sum he is released.
- Lenders must disclose before signing and notify default in time; the protected guarantor may be pursued only after the debtor is exhausted.
- These protections cannot be waived (s. 32), but demand-first under s. 8 can.
