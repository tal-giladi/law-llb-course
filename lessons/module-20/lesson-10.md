# 20.10 · Practice lab: a rental dispute and a rejected insurance claim

In this lab one event, a slow leak in a rented flat, produces two disputes: with the landlord over repairs, rent and the deposit, and with your own insurer over damaged belongings. You write the letters, answer the rejection, and outline both claims. It applies lessons 20.3 and 20.6.

## Why it matters

Real problems rarely respect the chapter headings of a law book. A tenant whose belongings were ruined needs to know whom to claim from, in what order, and how a payment from one affects the claim against the other. Damages for breach are not reduced by insurance the injured party receives (Remedies Law s. 16), but an insurer that pays takes over the insured's claim against the wrongdoer (Insurance Contract Law s. 62). Sequencing matters, and so do the deadlines in both laws.

## The law

- **Rental.** Repairs: s. 7, s. 25ח (deadlines); remedies: s. 9 (self-repair, rent reduction), s. 15 (no rent when use is impossible), s. 25 (set-off); security: s. 25י (cap, use, return); payments: s. 25ט. [Rental and Borrowing Law](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_השכירות_והשאילה).
- **Insurance.** Disclosure: ss. 6–8; exclusions: s. 3; conditions: s. 29; claim duties and obstruction: ss. 22–24; payment and interest: ss. 27–28א; limitation: ss. 31–31א; subrogation: s. 62. [Insurance Contract Law](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חוזה_הביטוח).
- **Damages and insurance:**

> בקביעת שיעור הפיצויים לא יובא בחשבון סכום שהנפגע קיבל או זכאי לקבל בשל הפרת החוזה לפי חוזה ביטוח.

*Gist:* Damages for breach are not reduced by insurance money the injured party received. [Remedies Law s. 16](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_%28תרופות_בשל_הפרת_חוזה%29#סעיף_16) Read with s. 62 of the Insurance Contract Law: the insurer that paid steps into your claim to the extent of its payment; you keep the claim for the rest.

Periods and amounts: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

## How it works in practice

Order of work after a leak in a rented flat:

1. Stop the damage (s. 61 of the Insurance Contract Law: reasonable mitigation is covered); photograph everything.
2. Same day: notify the landlord in writing (repair demand, urgent) and your insurer (s. 22).
3. Within days: claim to the insurer with an inventory and receipts (s. 23); diary the limitation date.
4. If the landlord does not repair: s. 9 remedies; if the flat is unusable, s. 15.
5. If the insurer rejects: point-by-point reply, internal ombudsman, regulator.
6. At the end of the lease: security return demand (s. 25י(ה)).
7. Claims: against the landlord for breach (repairs, rent, damaged goods not paid by the insurer, security); against the insurer for proceeds.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. Send letters only in your own matters.

### You need

- The supplied case, or a rental or insurance problem of your own.
- The [rental repair and deposit letter (draft)](../../templates/rental-repair-and-deposit-letter.md), the [insurance claim letter](../../templates/insurance-claim-letter.md), the [insurance rejection response (draft)](../../templates/insurance-rejection-response.md), the [regulator complaint](../../templates/regulator-complaint.md) and the [small claim template](../../templates/small-claim-contract-consumer.md).
- About two and a half hours.

**Supplied case.** Lior Amsalem rents a two-room flat in Jerusalem from Esther Pinto: one-year lease from 1 September 2025 at ₪5,200 a month; cash security of ₪15,600 plus a ₪30,000 promissory note. Lior has home-contents insurance. The proposal form asked: "Has there been water damage in the flat in the last three years?" Lior answered "don't know".

- 3 January 2026: Lior notices damp on the bedroom wall behind the wardrobe and messages Esther with photos. Esther replies "it's condensation, open a window".
- 20 January: the wall is wet and the wardrobe's back panel is swollen. Lior messages again. No answer.
- 2 February: a plumber Lior calls finds a leaking pipe inside the wall. Lior pays ₪1,400 for the urgent repair and ₪2,100 for drying. The wardrobe (₪3,500), a mattress (₪2,800) and clothes (₪1,900) are ruined. The bedroom was unusable from 20 January to 15 February.
- 5 February: Lior claims ₪8,200 from the insurer. On 30 April the insurer rejects: (a) "gradual leak exclusion" (clause 9.4(ג), in the general exclusions chapter, normal type); (b) "non-disclosure: the building's records show a 2024 water-damage claim for the flat below"; (c) "late notice: the damage began in early January".
- Esther refuses to pay the plumber, says Lior "damaged the wall", and at the end of the lease (31 August 2026) keeps the whole cash security "for the wall and repainting".

### Steps

1. **Repair demand and rent (25 min).** Was the defect urgent? Which deadline applied (s. 25ח(ב))? Was Lior entitled to repair (s. 9(א), 9(ג)) and to reduce rent or claim exemption for the bedroom (s. 9(א)(2), s. 15)? Draft Part A of the rental letter as Lior should have sent it on 20 January.
2. **Set-off (10 min).** Could Lior have deducted the plumber's bill from the March rent (s. 25)? What are the risks?
3. **Security (20 min).** Was the cash security lawful (s. 25י(ב))? Was the promissory note? Did Esther use the security lawfully (s. 25י(ג)–(ד))? Draft Part B of the rental letter.
4. **Insurance reply (40 min).** Answer each of the insurer's three grounds on the rejection-response template, with sections. Calculate the limitation date.
5. **Regulator (10 min).** Draft a short complaint on the regulator complaint template.
6. **Who pays what (20 min).** Allocate each loss item between the landlord and the insurer; explain s. 62 and Remedies Law s. 16.
7. **Claims outline (25 min).** One small claim against Esther and one against the insurer, or one claim with both defendants? Heads, amounts, evidence.

<details><summary>Model answer</summary>

**1. Repairs and rent.** A hidden leak inside the wall is a defect for the landlord, not caused by unreasonable use (s. 25ח(א)–(ב)). By 20 January it was spreading and threatening the bedroom: arguably urgent ("not allowing reasonable living" in that room), with the short deadline; at the latest, the general outer limit from the 3 January demand. Esther ignored both. Lior could repair without further notice for an urgent defect (s. 9(ג)) and recover reasonable costs (₪1,400 + ₪2,100). For the bedroom, a proportional rent reduction for 20 January to 15 February (s. 9(א)(2)), say one-third of the rent for the period, or rent exemption for the part whose use was impossible (s. 15). The 20 January letter: describe the leak, state it is urgent, demand repair within the short statutory period, offer access, warn of self-repair and rent reduction.

**2. Set-off.** Debts from the tenancy may be set off (s. 25). Deducting ₪3,500 from March rent is lawful if the debt is clear; the risk is that Esther calls it non-payment. Send a written notice of set-off with invoices before deducting.

**3. Security.** Cap: lower of rent for a third of 12 months (₪20,800) and three months (₪15,600): ₪15,600; the cash security is exactly at the cap. The promissory note costs nothing up front and is outside the cap. Esther may use the security only for listed purposes (unpaid rent, tenant-caused damage the tenant failed to repair, unpaid running bills, not vacating), after reasonable advance notice and a chance to cure (s. 25י(ג)–(ד)). The wall was damaged by the leak, not by Lior; repainting for ordinary wear is not tenant damage. Demand return of ₪15,600 with its yield within the s. 25י(ה) period after 31 August, and the return of the promissory note.

**4. Insurance.** (a) Gradual-leak exclusion in the general exclusions chapter in normal type: not next to the water-damage cover and not highlighted, so the insurer may not rely on it (s. 3). (b) Non-disclosure: the question asked about water damage "in the flat"; the 2024 claim was for the flat below, a different property. Lior answered "don't know", honestly; no fraud. Even if the answer were incomplete, after a loss the insurer could only reduce proportionally (s. 7(ג)), and it must show the premium difference; and if the insurer could have checked the building's records, s. 8(1) may apply. (c) Late notice: the leak was hidden; the damage became apparent around 20 January and was diagnosed on 2 February; notice on 5 February was prompt. In any case, delay reduces proceeds only if it actually hurt the investigation (s. 24(א)); the plumber's report and photos were available. Payment period has passed (s. 27); add linkage and interest (s. 28), and contents insurance is a personal line for special interest on undisputed sums (s. 28א). Limitation: three years from the event (early 2026) for contents insurance (s. 31); diary it with a reminder.

**5. Regulator.** A one-page complaint to the Capital Market Authority's public enquiries: policy and claim numbers, the three grounds, why each fails (ss. 3, 6–8, 24), the documents, and the request. Note that the complaint does not stop limitation.

**6. Allocation.** Landlord (breach of ss. 7, 25ח): plumber and drying ₪3,500, rent reduction, and the damaged goods to the extent foreseeable from failure to repair after 3 January. Insurer: the damaged contents (₪8,200 claimed, including the plumber if the policy covers search and repair). Lior may claim the goods from both, but cannot recover twice in fact: if the insurer pays the contents, it takes over Lior's claim against Esther to that extent (s. 62); Esther cannot argue that Lior's insurance reduces her damages (Remedies Law s. 16).

**7. Claims.** Two separate small claims are simpler (different causes and defendants): against Esther for ₪3,500 + rent reduction + ₪15,600 security; against the insurer for ₪8,200 + interest. Both within the small claims ceiling. Evidence: lease, messages with dates, photos, plumber's report and invoices, handover protocol, the proposal form and policy pages, the rejection letter, the reply and its proof of delivery.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes: both disputes are the small claims court's daily work, and an individual brings them himself; the insurer and the landlord can be sued there. Claims for others for pay are reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).

### Where and how

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- Letters by email and registered mail; the insurer's internal ombudsman; the Capital Market Authority public enquiries (⚠️ manual check).
- Small claims court for each claim within the ceiling; fees in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The landlord enforces the promissory note in the execution office.
- The insurer alleges fraud.
- Mould or the leak has caused health problems (bodily injury claims).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Notifying the insurer only after the landlord refuses | Insurer argues late notice (s. 24) | Show the delay did not harm the investigation | Notify both at once |
| Settling with the landlord for "everything" | Prejudices the insurer's subrogated claim (s. 62(ג)) | Tell the insurer; exclude its share | Settle only your uninsured part |
| Withholding the last month's rent "against the deposit" | You breach the lease | Pay; demand the deposit | Keep the two separate |
| Letting the insurer's "still examining" run on | Limitation runs regardless (s. 31א) | Sue in time | Diary the date |

## Review

- One event can create claims against the landlord (breach) and the insurer (proceeds); run them in parallel.
- Urgent defects justify immediate self-repair; rent reduction and set-off are lawful tools if used with written notice.
- The security is capped and may be used only for listed purposes after notice.
- Exclusions must be prominent; non-disclosure needs a question and leads to reduction, not refusal, without fraud.
- Insurance money does not reduce the landlord's damages, but the insurer that pays takes over that part of your claim.
