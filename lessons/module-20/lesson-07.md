# 20.7 · Consumer contracts: distance sales, cancellation and unfair terms

By the end of this lesson you will be able to cancel a consumer purchase with the right notice on the right day, tell a depriving standard-contract term from a merely harsh one, and decide when a consumer claim can include compensation without proof of damage. Lessons [03.6](../module-03/lesson-06.md) and [03.10](../module-03/lesson-10.md) introduced the Standard Contracts Law and the three cancellation routes; this lesson covers the full distance-sale regime, the structure of the Standard Contracts Law, and the remedies that make consumer claims worth bringing.

## Why it matters

Most contracts an Israeli adult signs are standard forms: phone and internet, gyms, banks, insurers, online shops, airlines, developers. Two statutes govern them. The Consumer Protection Law 1981 regulates conduct (no misleading, disclosure duties) and gives specific cancellation rights; the Standard Contracts Law 1982 lets courts and a specialist Tribunal strike out depriving terms. Each has a trap. Cancellation rights are lost by a day or by sending the notice the wrong way; a misleading-advertising claim fails without proof that the claimant relied on the advertisement; a depriving term is struck out only for you in your court case unless a body with standing goes to the Tribunal. Knowing which tool fits saves months.

## The law

### Distance sales: disclosure, document, cancellation

A **distance sale** is a contract made after the dealer's distance marketing (post, phone, TV, any electronic means, catalogues, ads) without the parties being together ([s. 14ג(ו)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_הצרכן#סעיף_14ג)). The dealer must disclose before the deal its identity and address, the main features, the full price (including delivery), the delivery date and method, the offer's validity, the warranty and the right to cancel (s. 14ג(א)), and must deliver a written document with these details, in Hebrew or the language of the marketing, no later than delivery (s. 14ג(ב)).

> בעסקת מכר מרחוק רשאי הצרכן לבטל את העסקה – (1) בנכס – מיום עשיית העסקה ועד ארבעה עשר ימים מיום קבלת הנכס או מיום קבלת המסמך [...], לפי המאוחר מביניהם; (2) בשירות – בתוך ארבעה עשר ימים מיום עשיית העסקה או מיום קבלת המסמך [...], לפי המאוחר [...]: בעסקה מתמשכת – בין אם הוחל במתן השירות ובין אם לאו, ובעסקה שאינה עסקה מתמשכת – בתנאי שביטול כאמור ייעשה לפחות שני ימים, שאינם ימי מנוחה, קודם למועד שבו אמור השירות להינתן.

*Gist:* Goods may be cancelled from the deal until a set number of days after receiving the goods or the disclosure document, whichever is later; services within the same period from the deal or the document; a one-off service only if at least two non-rest days remain before it. [s. 14ג(ג)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_הצרכן#סעיף_14ג) Because the period runs from the later of goods and document, a dealer who never sent the document has kept the window open.

Exceptions (s. 14ג(ד)): perishable goods; accommodation, travel, holiday or leisure services if cancellation falls within a few non-rest days before the service; information (software, digital content); goods made specially for the consumer; recordable goods whose original packaging was opened. For tourism services given wholly abroad, the dealer may offer the foreign provider's cancellation policy instead, if he disclosed it in advance (s. 14ג2).

> בעסקת מכר מרחוק שנערכה עם צרכן שהוא אדם עם מוגבלות, אזרח ותיק או עולה חדש, רשאי הצרכן לבטל את העסקה בתוך ארבעה חודשים [...], ובלבד שההתקשרות בעסקה כללה שיחה בין העוסק לצרכן, ובכלל זה שיחה באמצעות תקשורת אלקטרונית.

*Gist:* A person with a disability, a senior citizen (65+) or a new immigrant (within five years) has a much longer period to cancel a door-to-door sale, or a distance sale that included a conversation with the dealer. [s. 14ג1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_הצרכן#סעיף_14ג1) The dealer may ask only for the listed proof of status (s. 14ג1(ד)).

### Refund and fee

> ביטל צרכן חוזה [...] עקב פגם בנכס [...], עקב אי התאמה [...], עקב אי־אספקת הנכס או השירות במועד שנקבע לכך בחוזה או בשל כל הפרה אחרת של החוזה בידי העוסק – (1) יחזיר העוסק לצרכן בתוך 14 ימים מיום קבלת ההודעה על הביטול, את אותו חלק ממחיר העסקה ששולם [...] ולא יגבה מהצרכן דמי ביטול כלשהם;

*Gist:* When the consumer cancels because of the dealer's breach, the dealer refunds within a set period and charges nothing; when the consumer cancels for no reason, the dealer may keep only a capped cancellation fee (the lower of a percentage and a shekel sum), and "fee" includes shipping and packing costs. [s. 14ה](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_הצרכן#סעיף_14ה) The consumer returns the goods: at the place of delivery after a breach-based cancellation, to the dealer's premises otherwise. Amounts and periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### How to cancel

> היתה לצרכן זכות לבטל עסקה לפי חוק זה או לפי חוזה, יאפשר לו העוסק לבטל את העסקה בהודעת ביטול שימסור לו הצרכן בכל אחת מהדרכים המפורטות להלן [...]: (1) בעל פה – בטלפון או בהודעה בעל פה במקום העסק [...]; (2) בדואר רשום; (3) בדואר אלקטרוני; (4) בפקסימיליה, אם יש לעוסק; (5) באינטרנט [...]

*Gist:* Whenever a consumer has a right to cancel, under the law or the contract, the dealer must accept the notice by phone or in person, registered mail, email, fax or, for online deals, a prominent cancellation link on the home page; the notice states the consumer's name and ID number. [s. 14ט](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_הצרכן#סעיף_14ט) A continuing transaction ends within a few business days of the notice (s. 13ד(ג)).

### Misleading and its remedies

> לא יעשה עוסק דבר – במעשה או במחדל, בכתב או בעל פה או בכל דרך אחרת לרבות לאחר מועד ההתקשרות בעסקה – העלול להטעות צרכן בכל ענין מהותי בעסקה [...]

*Gist:* A dealer may not do anything, by act or omission, before or after the deal, that is liable to mislead a consumer on any material matter; the section lists matters deemed material (quality, quantity, delivery date, use, risks, maker, origin, dates and more). [s. 2(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_הצרכן#סעיף_2)

- **Tort (s. 31).** Breach of the misleading and disclosure chapters is a tort under the Civil Wrongs Ordinance.
- **Cancellation (s. 32).** A consumer may cancel a transaction tainted by material misleading or exploitation within a reasonable time of learning of it, even if someone other than the dealer misled him; the dealer refunds within a short period.
- **Exemplary damages (s. 31א).** For listed breaches, mostly refusals to refund after a lawful cancellation, continued charging after cancellation, ignoring the shelf price or refusing warranty service, the court may award damages not dependent on damage up to a ceiling, higher for repeated or aggravated breaches; the consumer must first send a written request (s. 31א(ב)). Ceilings: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### Standard Contracts Law: the structure

> ”תנאי“ – תניה בחוזה אחיד [...] ולמעט תניה שספק ולקוח הסכימו עליה במיוחד לצורך חוזה מסויים;

*Gist:* A "term" is any clause of the standard contract, including incorporated clauses, but not a clause specially agreed for this contract; "supplier" and "customer" are defined by who proposed the form, whichever side gives or receives the goods. [s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_החוזים_האחידים#סעיף_2)

The test is s. 3 (deprivation of customers, or an unfair advantage of the supplier liable to deprive them, considering the whole contract). Section 4 lists presumptively depriving terms; beyond module 3's list, note:

- **(6)** limiting the customer's legal rights or remedies, or conditioning them on unreasonable notice requirements (for example "claims must be filed within 30 days");
- **(6א)** unreasonable remedies for the supplier, including unreasonable agreed damages in its favour;
- **(8)–(10)** limiting the customer's right to raise claims in court, sending all disputes to arbitration, a supplier's choice of forum, or arbitration terms the supplier controls;
- **(11)** one-way indexation (prices rise with an index but never fall);
- **(12)** a declaration that the customer read the contract or knows certain facts, except information the customer himself supplied.

Section 5 voids outright any term barring access to the courts, and any exemption from liability for bodily injury or wilful acts. The law does not apply to a price term in plain and clear language, to terms that follow a statute, or to collective agreements (s. 23).

**Two forums.** In a case between a supplier and a customer, any court may strike out or change a depriving term, for that case only (s. 19), and must notify the Attorney General (s. 20). The Standard Contracts Tribunal acts on an application by the Attorney General, the Consumer Protection Commissioner, approved consumer organisations and public bodies (s. 16); its decision applies to every contract made on that form and on similar forms of the same supplier, even before the decision, though not to obligations already performed (s. 18), and it may order the supplier to use a corrected form (s. 17). Appeal lies to the Supreme Court (s. 10).

## Leading cases

### דנ״א 5712/01 ברזני נ׳ בזק חברה ישראלית לתקשורת בע״מ, פ״ד נז(6) 385 (2003)

Bezeq advertised that international calls would be charged by exact time used, but its method overcharged. A customer who had never seen the advertisement sued, and asked for a class action. On further hearing the Supreme Court held that s. 2(א) prohibits conduct "liable to mislead" whether or not anyone was misled, but that a claim for compensation under s. 31 is a tort claim governed by the Ordinance's causation and damage rules; a consumer who did not see or rely on the advertisement has no personal cause of action for compensation. Why it matters: misleading is a regulatory wrong even without victims (enforcement, fines, Commissioner action), but your own damages claim must show you were misled and harmed; the later Class Actions Law 2006 and s. 31א exemplary damages partly answer the gap. [Cardozo ISCP](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/77)

### ע״א 294/91 חברה קדישא גחש״א ״קהילת ירושלים״ נ׳ קסטנבאום, פ״ד מו(2) 464 (1992)

A burial society's standard terms required tombstone inscriptions in Hebrew letters and dates only; a widower wanted his wife's dates in Latin characters too. The Supreme Court ordered the society to allow it: the majority treated the restriction as against public policy (Contracts Law s. 30), with Barak J. reasoning that basic values such as human dignity enter private law through such open-ended provisions; Elon J. dissented. Why it matters: a standard term may fall not only under the Standard Contracts Law test but also as contrary to public policy, especially where the supplier is a monopoly and the term touches dignity. ⚠️ Free text: search the case number on [the Judiciary's decision search](https://supremedecisions.court.gov.il); we could not open it on 17 October 2026.

## How it works in practice

### Cancelling a distance purchase in five lines

1. Note three dates: the deal, receipt of the goods, receipt of the disclosure document. The period runs from the later of the last two (goods) or of the deal and document (services).
2. Check the exceptions in s. 14ג(ד) and your extended right under s. 14ג1.
3. Send the notice by one of the s. 14ט routes, with name and ID; keep the proof (screenshot of the site's cancellation form, email, registered mail).
4. Return the goods as s. 14ה requires; photograph them.
5. If no refund within the period: send a written request (needed for exemplary damages, s. 31א(ב)), then sue in small claims.

### Attacking a term

1. Is it a "term" (not specially negotiated)? Is it excluded by s. 23 (plain price term, statutory term)?
2. Is it void (s. 5)? Is it presumed depriving (s. 4, which paragraph)? Can the supplier rebut the presumption given the whole contract?
3. In your own case: ask the court to strike or change it (s. 19). For the market: write to the Consumer Protection and Fair Trade Authority or a consumer organisation, which can go to the Tribunal (s. 16).

## Worked example

Rivka Goldshmidt, 71, of Bnei Brak, gets a phone call from a mattress company and orders a "therapeutic" mattress for ₪6,900, delivered on 2 June; no written document arrives. In late August she wants to cancel: the mattress hurts her back, and the "orthopaedic certificate" the caller described turns out not to exist. She also signed up online for a gym near home: twelve months, auto-renewal, "I declare that I read and understood all terms", "every dispute will be decided by an arbitrator appointed by the gym", and "the member may cancel only by registered letter".

1. **Mattress, period.** She is a senior citizen and the deal included a phone conversation: the four-month period applies (s. 14ג1(ג)); in any case the ordinary period never started because she received no document (s. 14ג(ג)(1)). She may still cancel in late August.
2. **Mattress, reason and fee.** The non-existent certificate is a misleading statement on a material matter (s. 2(א)(4)); cancelling for it is a breach-based cancellation: full refund, no fee, and she makes the mattress available at her home (s. 14ה(א)). She may also cite s. 32.
3. **Gym, cancellation route.** The registered-letter-only clause contradicts s. 14ט, which lets her cancel by phone, email or the site's cancellation link; the contract ends within a few business days (s. 13ד(ג)).
4. **Gym, terms.** The "I read and understood" clause is presumed depriving (s. 4(12)); the gym-appointed arbitrator is presumed depriving (s. 4(8), (10)). In any claim she asks the court to strike them (s. 19).
5. **If charged after cancellation.** She sends a written request to stop and refund; continued charging is a listed ground for exemplary damages (s. 31א(א)(2ב)).
6. **Forum.** Small claims court, in her own name.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- One consumer contract of yours (internet, phone, gym, an online order) and its confirmation emails, or Rivka's facts.
- The [consumer withdrawal notice](../../templates/consumer-withdrawal-notice.md) and the [contract review checklist](../../templates/contract-review-checklist.md) (section D).
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. For one distance purchase, write the three dates and compute your cancellation deadline (s. 14ג(ג); s. 14ג1 if it applies).
2. List the cancellation routes the dealer must accept for that purchase (s. 14ט); check its website for the cancellation link.
3. In the standard contract, mark three terms and classify each: void (s. 5), presumed depriving (s. 4, give the paragraph), or valid.
4. Draft a cancellation notice on the template, stating whether it is breach-based (no fee) or not.
5. Write the written request that would precede an exemplary-damages claim (s. 31א(ב)).

[Consumer withdrawal notice](../../templates/consumer-withdrawal-notice.md); [contract review checklist](../../templates/contract-review-checklist.md), section D; for court, the [small claim template](../../templates/small-claim-contract-consumer.md).

### How you know it worked

- Your deadline calculation names the start date and the rule that sets it.
- Each marked term cites a paragraph of s. 4 or s. 5.
- The notice says whether a fee may be deducted and why.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Consumer disputes are the core of the small claims court, where an individual acts for himself; the dealer may be sued there even if it is a company. You cannot apply to the Standard Contracts Tribunal yourself (s. 16), but you can raise a depriving term in your own case (s. 19) and complain to the Authority. A class action needs a lawyer in practice (module 33 in year 4).

### Where and how

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- Cancellation notice by a s. 14ט route; written request before claiming exemplary damages.
- Complaint: the Consumer Protection and Fair Trade Authority (⚠️ manual check of the gov.il complaint page); it does not stop limitation ([lesson 07.3](../module-07/lesson-03.md)).
- Claim: small claims court within the ceiling; fees in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); free help from the Israel Consumer Council and Legal Aid's consumer service (⚠️ manual check).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The same term or practice harms many customers (consider a consumer organisation or a class action).
- The dealer counter-claims or threatens a defamation suit over your review.
- The contract is a large financial product (loan, insurance, investment).
- You are a business, not a consumer: the Consumer Protection Law will mostly not help.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Counting days from the order, not from receipt | Cancelling too early or giving up too soon | Recount from the later date | Note all three dates |
| Accepting a "registered mail only" demand | Delay and extra cost | Cancel by phone, email or website link (s. 14ט) | Know the statutory routes |
| Claiming for an ad you never saw | No causation (Barazani) | Rely on what you did see or on s. 31א | Keep screenshots of what misled you |
| Suing for exemplary damages without a prior written request | Claim dismissed for that head (s. 31א(ב)) | Send the request now, then amend | Request in writing first |
| Arguing "the contract is unfair" in general | Court leaves the term in place | Point to s. 4's paragraph or s. 5 | Classify each term precisely |

## Review

- Distance sales: disclosure before, a document by delivery, and a cancellation period that runs from the later of goods and document; seniors, people with disabilities and new immigrants get much longer where a conversation took place.
- After a breach-based cancellation the dealer refunds in full; otherwise only a capped fee; notices may be given by phone, email, mail or a website link.
- Misleading is prohibited as conduct, but compensation requires causation and damage (Barazani); exemplary damages cover listed breaches after a written request.
- Standard terms: void (s. 5), presumed depriving (s. 4), or tested under s. 3; courts act case by case (s. 19), the Tribunal for everyone (s. 18).
- Public policy (s. 30) is a further tool against a standard term, as in Kestenbaum.
