# 20.3 · Rental: the Rental and Borrowing Law and the fair-rental rules

By the end of this lesson you will be able to answer the questions that fill most Israeli tenancy disputes: who must fix what and by when, what the tenant may do if the landlord does not, how much security the landlord may hold and when it must come back, and how either side may end the lease. Lesson [03.9](../module-03/lesson-09.md) used these rules to review a lease before signing; here you learn them as a system and apply them once the tenancy has gone wrong.

## Why it matters

Many Israeli families rent, typically from private landlords on short leases. For decades the Rental and Borrowing Law 1971 was almost entirely default law: landlords' forms could exclude repairs, demand six months' security and shift building insurance to the tenant. The 2017 amendment (known as the fair-rental law, שכירות הוגנת) added a residential chapter, ss. 25א–25טו, most of which cannot be waived against the tenant (s. 25יד). It did not create a rent tribunal or rent control; it set minimum terms. Knowing where the mandatory floor lies, and where the old default rules still govern, is the whole skill. At the other end, a small and shrinking group of tenancies is still governed by the Tenant Protection Law 1972 and key money, with strong security of tenure.

## The law

### The general rules (ss. 1–25): still the base

The tenant's right to possess and use is good against everyone (s. 21(א)), and a buyer of the property steps into the landlord's shoes even without knowing of the lease (s. 21(ב)); the tenant keeps his claims against the new owner (s. 21(ג)).

> המשכיר חייב, תוך זמן סביר לאחר שקיבל דרישה לכך מאת השוכר, לתקן במושכר [...] כל דבר הגורם הפרעה של ממש לשימוש הרגיל במושכר או לשימוש בו על פי המוסכם [...] (להלן – פגם), בין שהפגם היה בזמן מסירת המושכר לשוכר ובין שהתהווה לאחר מכן [...]

*Gist:* The landlord must repair, within a reasonable time after the tenant's demand, any defect that substantially disturbs ordinary or agreed use, whether it existed at handover or arose later. [s. 7(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_השכירות_והשאילה#סעיף_7)

> דרש השוכר מהמשכיר לתקן את הפגם או את אי־ההתאמה ולא תיקן המשכיר [...] בתוך זמן סביר [...], רשאי השוכר – (1) לתקן את הפגם או את אי־ההתאמה ולדרוש מהמשכיר החזרת הוצאותיו הסבירות; (2) להפחית את דמי השכירות, כל עוד לא תוקן הפגם [...], לפי היחס שבו פחת שווי השכירות [...]

*Gist:* If the landlord does not repair within a reasonable time, the tenant may repair and recover reasonable costs, or reduce the rent in proportion to the loss of value while the defect lasts; he must announce a self-repair in advance (s. 9(ב)), except for an urgent defect (s. 9(ג)). [s. 9](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_השכירות_והשאילה#סעיף_9)

Other base rules:

- Conformity at delivery follows Sale Law s. 11 (lesson 20.1), including defects in the surroundings (s. 6(ב)); the tenant must notify within a reasonable time and allow inspection and repair (s. 6(ג)), but failure to do so costs him only to the extent it harmed the landlord (s. 6(ד)).
- A clause exempting the landlord from defects he knew of and did not disclose is void (s. 8).
- If use is impossible for reasons connected with the property, the tenant who does not cancel is exempt from rent for that time (s. 15).
- The tenant may not make changes without consent (s. 16א) and must let the landlord inspect and repair at reasonable times with notice (s. 17).
- Assignment and sublease need consent, but an unreasonable refusal in a land lease lets the tenant go ahead (s. 22(א)); consent must be in writing and in advance (s. 22(ב)).
- A lease with no fixed term, or one that continued after its end, ends by reasonable notice (s. 19).
- The tenant is liable as a paid bailee (s. 24, Bailees Law).
- Debts arising from the tenancy may be set off (s. 25).

### The residential chapter (ss. 25א–25טו)

> המשכיר ימסור את הדירה המושכרת כשהיא ראויה למגורים; יראו דירה שמתקיים בה אחד התנאים המנויים בתוספת הראשונה כדירה שאינה ראויה למגורים [...] (ב) מסר המשכיר את הדירה כשהיא אינה ראויה למגורים, יראו במסירה כאמור הפרת חוזה.

*Gist:* The flat must be habitable; a flat without drainage, electricity, natural light and ventilation with lockable openings, drinking water, a separated toilet, or with an unreasonable risk to safety or health is not, and delivering it is a breach. [s. 25ו](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_השכירות_והשאילה#סעיף_25ו) This cannot be contracted out of at all, even in the tenant's favour (s. 25יד(1)). Separately, the flat must be "fit for living" (מתאימה למגורים) under s. 25ה, enforced through the non-conformity rules.

> על אף האמור בסעיף 7, המשכיר יתקן על חשבונו כל ליקוי או פגם אחר בדירה המושכרת שאינו כאמור בסעיף קטן (א) ואינו קל ערך, בתוך זמן סביר ולא יאוחר מ־30 ימים מיום שקיבל דרישה לכך מאת השוכר, ואם היה פגם שתיקונו דחוף – יתקן המשכיר את הפגם בתוך זמן סביר ולא יאוחר משלושה ימים [...]; לעניין זה, ”פגם שתיקונו דחוף“ – פגם שאינו מאפשר מגורים בדירה באופן סביר [...]

*Gist:* The landlord pays for and repairs every non-trivial defect not caused by the tenant's unreasonable use, within a fixed outer limit from the tenant's demand, and much faster for a defect that prevents reasonable living; if he does not, the s. 9 remedies apply. [s. 25ח(ב)–(ג)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_השכירות_והשאילה#סעיף_25ח) The tenant pays for damage from his own unreasonable use (s. 25ח(א)). Periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

**Payments (s. 25ט).** The tenant pays rent, occupier's taxes (arnona), utilities and routine building maintenance; he does not pay for buying or upgrading permanent systems, building insurance, or a broker who acted for the landlord.

> ביקש המשכיר מהשוכר ערובה, אחת או יותר, הכרוכה בהוצאה כספית לשוכר, ובכלל זה ערבות בנקאית, ערבות מנותן ערבות אחר או מזומן, לא יעלה גובהן המצטבר של הערובות על הנמוך מבין סכומים אלה: (1) סכום דמי השכירות [...] בעד שליש מתקופת השכירות; (2) סכום השווה לפי שלושה מדמי השכירות לחודש [...]

*Gist:* Securities that cost the tenant money (cash, bank guarantee, guarantee from another licensed provider) are capped in total at the lower of two rent-based amounts. [s. 25י(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_השכירות_והשאילה#סעיף_25י) A personal guarantor or a promissory note costs the tenant nothing up front and is outside the cap, a point landlords use and tenants should notice. A 2026 amendment (the Economic Plan law for the 2026 budget) defines the "other guarantee provider" (נותן ערבות אחר) as a licensed credit or deposit-and-credit provider, a licensed stable-payment provider or an insurer.

The landlord may use the security only for unpaid rent, unrepaired tenant damage, unpaid running payments, or failure to vacate, each up to the sum due (s. 25י(ג)); he must warn the tenant a reasonable time in advance and allow a cure (s. 25י(ד)); and he must return the balance, with its yield, within a fixed period after getting the flat back or after the tenant's debts are paid, whichever is later (s. 25י(ה)).

**Other residential rules.** A landlord who sells must tell the tenant in writing who the buyer is (s. 25יא). The landlord must say a reasonable time before the end whether he wants to extend and on what terms; options must be exercised by notice within fixed periods, and a landlord's option is valid only if its terms were set in advance (s. 25יב). A no-cause cancellation clause for the landlord is void unless the tenant has one too, with minimum notice periods for each side (s. 25יג). Lease form and content: s. 25ב–25ג; [lesson 03.9](../module-03/lesson-09.md).

### Who is outside the chapter

> הוראות סימן זה לא יחולו על חוזה שכירות למגורים כמפורט להלן: (1) [...] דירה בבית מלון [...] או דירה אחרת המושכרת למטרת נופש; [...] מעונות לסטודנטים [...]; דירה שחוק הגנת הדייר [...] חל עליה; (2) חוזה [...] שתקופת השכירות לפיו אינה עולה על שלושה חודשים, ואין לצדדים ברירה להאריכה; (3) [...] עולה על עשר שנים [...]; (4) [...] דמי השכירות לפיו, לחודש, עולים על 20,000 שקלים חדשים [...] הסכום [...] יתעדכן ב־1 בינואר של כל שנה [...]

*Gist:* Holiday lets, hotels, dorms, sheltered housing, protected tenancies, leases of up to three months without an option, very long leases, and high-rent leases above an index-linked ceiling are outside the chapter; between close relatives the non-waiver rule does not apply. [s. 25טו](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_השכירות_והשאילה#סעיף_25טו) In a sublease of up to ten years, repairs remain the head landlord's duty (s. 25טו(ג)(2)). Current ceiling: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### The Tenant Protection Law in brief

> ”דמי מפתח“ – כל תמורה שאינה דמי שכירות, הניתנת בקשר לשכירות של מושכר, או בקשר לקבלת החזקה או להחזרת החזקה במושכר לפי חוק זה – ואין נפקא מינה מתי שולמו;

*Gist:* Key money is any payment other than rent connected with the tenancy or with getting or returning possession. [Tenant Protection Law s. 1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_הדייר#סעיף_1)

The law protects tenancies that existed in older buildings, or that were let for key money; a property vacant in August 1968, or vacated after it, or in a building completed after it, is outside the law unless let for key money (ss. 9, 10, 14). A protected tenant may be evicted only on a closed list of grounds such as non-payment, breach of a term giving a right to evict, malicious damage, nuisance or the landlord's own need with alternative housing (s. 131), and even then the court may refuse if eviction would be unjust (s. 132). On leaving, the outgoing tenant shares in the key money paid by the incoming tenant, by fixed percentages that depend on how long he held the property (ss. 74–78). New protected tenancies are rare; you meet the law in old city-centre shops and flats and in family estates.

## Leading cases

### Key text: the 2017 amendment and its design

We did not find a free official text of a Supreme Court ruling interpreting the 2017 residential chapter on 17 October 2026 (supremedecisions.court.gov.il was unreachable), and lower-court rulings vary. The key text is the [amending law itself](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/20/law/20_lsr_389390.pdf), read with three design choices visible in the consolidated text:

1. **Floor, not ceiling.** Most sections may be varied only in the tenant's favour (s. 25יד(2)); habitability may not be varied at all (s. 25יד(1)). The rent itself is free.
2. **Defaults kept.** The 1971 general rules still govern everything the chapter does not address, which is why s. 9 (self-repair, rent reduction) remains the tenant's main tool after a missed deadline in s. 25ח.
3. **Excluded markets.** Short lets, dorms and very expensive flats were left out (s. 25טו), on the view that those tenants can bargain or are protected elsewhere.

The academic debate is whether a minimum-terms law without rent regulation helps tenants or simply raises rents and shifts costs; supporters point to the end of six-month deposits and of tenants paying for boilers and building insurance; critics note that guarantor and promissory-note requirements and short leases remain. Contract doctrine from module 3 still applies: good faith in negotiation (Kal Binyan, [lesson 03.4](../module-03/lesson-04.md)) and interpretation against the drafter of a standard lease ([lesson 03.7](../module-03/lesson-07.md)).

## How it works in practice

### A repair dispute, step by step

1. Write to the landlord, describe the defect, attach photos, and set a deadline within s. 25ח(ב) (urgent or not). WhatsApp is common; follow up by email so you have a clean record.
2. Allow access (s. 17). Log visits.
3. If the deadline passes: for self-repair, give advance notice (s. 9(ב)), get two quotes, repair, and send the invoice; or reduce the rent in proportion while the defect lasts (s. 9(א)(2)). Do not simply stop paying.
4. If the flat is unusable (no water, no electricity for days), rent exemption for that time (s. 15) or cancellation for fundamental breach.
5. Claim in small claims if the landlord refuses to reimburse.

### The security at the end

1. Hand back the flat in writing, with photos and a handover protocol signed by both sides if possible; return keys against a receipt.
2. Pay final bills and arnona and send proof (s. 25ט(א)).
3. If the landlord wants to use the security, he must give notice and a chance to cure (s. 25י(ד)).
4. Demand return of the balance with its yield within the s. 25י(ה) period. Then claim.

## Worked example

Olga Kuznetsova, a new immigrant, rents a two-room flat in Ashdod from Moshe Dahan for one year at ₪4,200 a month. The lease asks for a cash deposit of ₪16,800 (four months), a personal guarantor, and says "the tenant bears all repairs and the building insurance". In January the boiler breaks and there is no hot water; she messages Moshe on 5 January. In February black mould spreads in the bedroom from a roof leak. In April Moshe tells her he has sold the flat and "the new owner wants you out in 30 days".

1. **Deposit.** The cash cap is the lower of four months (a third of a 12-month term) and three months: three months, ₪12,600 (s. 25י(ב)). The extra ₪4,200 was taken contrary to a non-waivable rule; she may demand it back now. The personal guarantor is outside the cap.
2. **Clauses.** "Tenant bears all repairs" is void as to defects not caused by her unreasonable use (s. 25ח(ב), non-waivable); "tenant pays building insurance" is void (s. 25ט(ב)(2)).
3. **Boiler.** No hot water in winter arguably prevents reasonable living: an urgent defect with the short deadline. If Moshe does not act, Olga may repair without further notice (s. 9(ג)) and claim the cost, or deduct it by set-off (s. 25).
4. **Mould and roof.** A health risk may make the flat "not habitable" (First Schedule, item 6) and is in any case a defect for the landlord. Demand repair within the outer limit; if it is not fixed, rent reduction under s. 9(א)(2) for the bedroom.
5. **Sale.** The buyer steps into Moshe's shoes (s. 21(ב)) and is bound by the lease to its end; Moshe had to notify her in writing of the buyer's details (s. 25יא). "Out in 30 days" has no basis unless the lease has a mutual no-cause cancellation clause, and then the landlord's minimum notice applies (s. 25יג(ב)).
6. **Letter.** Olga sends one letter to Moshe and the buyer: refund of the excess deposit, the boiler cost, the roof repair deadline, and a statement that the lease continues.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your own lease (current or past) or the Olga case; messages and photos.
- The [rental repair and deposit letter (draft)](../../templates/rental-repair-and-deposit-letter.md).
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. Check s. 25טו: is your lease inside the residential chapter? Is it a protected tenancy?
2. List each clause on repairs, payments, security, options and cancellation, and mark it valid, void (s. 25יד), or default (ss. 1–25).
3. Calculate the security cap for your lease.
4. Pick one live issue (a defect, the deposit, the sale) and write a timeline.
5. Draft the letter on the template, citing the section and the deadline, and the remedy you will use if it passes.

### Template

[Rental repair and deposit letter (draft)](../../templates/rental-repair-and-deposit-letter.md); for a lease review before signing, the [contract review checklist](../../templates/contract-review-checklist.md).

### How you know it worked

- The letter names one section for each demand and a concrete date.
- You chose a remedy the law allows (repair and recover, reduce rent, set off), not "I will stop paying".
- The security calculation shows both amounts in s. 25י(ב) and picks the lower.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Tenants and landlords handle their own lease disputes; most are decided in the small claims court, where an individual acts for himself and a lawyer appears only with leave (Courts Law s. 63). A company landlord can be sued there but cannot sue there. Eviction claims are different: they go to the magistrates' court under the special eviction-claim procedure ([lesson 15.6](../module-15/lesson-06.md)); a tenant facing eviction should get advice. Protected-tenancy disputes are legally complex.

### Where and how

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- Letter first (template); then a small claim on the [small claim template](../../templates/small-claim-contract-consumer.md) for money (deposit, repair costs, rent reduction) within the ceiling ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- Free help: Kol-Zchut's rental pages (⚠️ manual check), municipal tenant-rights desks in some cities, Legal Aid where the means test is met.
- Module 22 (Clinic II) takes a tenancy dispute from the letter to judgment.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You receive an eviction claim or a demand under a promissory note.
- The property may be a protected tenancy or key money is involved.
- The landlord's lender or a receiver is selling the property.
- Large sums for damage are claimed against you or your guarantor.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stopping rent because of a defect | Tenant in breach; security used; eviction risk | Pay, then use s. 9 remedies | Demand repair, wait the deadline, then repair or reduce |
| Repairing without telling the landlord | Reimbursement refused (s. 9(ב)) | Show urgency (s. 9(ג)) or that he refused | Give written notice of intended repair |
| No handover protocol at the end | Dispute over damage; security withheld | Rely on photos and witnesses | Protocol and photos on the last day |
| Assuming the 2017 rules apply to a holiday let or dorm | Wrong deadlines and rights | Use the general rules (ss. 1–25) | Check s. 25טו first |
| Signing a blank promissory note as "security" | Exposure beyond the cash cap | Demand its return at the end | Insist on a capped bank guarantee instead |

## Review

- The general 1971 rules still govern: repair on demand (s. 7), self-repair or rent reduction after a reasonable time (s. 9), rent exemption when use is impossible (s. 15), a buyer is bound by the lease (s. 21).
- The 2017 chapter adds a non-waivable floor: habitability, repair deadlines, who pays what, a security cap and limits on its use and return, option and cancellation rules.
- Check the exclusions in s. 25טו before applying the chapter.
- The Tenant Protection Law gives security of tenure and key-money shares to a small group of old or key-money tenancies.
- Write first, use the statutory remedies, and sue in small claims for money.
