# 33.7 · Practice lab: read a class-action settlement and decide whether to opt out or object

In this lab you will work through a real settlement notice published in August 2026, in a case about how a shared-scooter company charged for partial minutes, and reach a reasoned decision: stay in, ask to leave, or object. You will practise on the notice as a member would receive it, check it against the registry, and test every term against the Law. The same method works for any notice from your bank, insurer, phone company or supermarket.

## Why it matters

The notice is the only moment the Law speaks directly to you as a member. It gives you 45 days. After that, an approved settlement binds you (s. 24), whatever you would have said. Reading a notice well takes about an hour once you know what to look for, and the decision is usually simple; this lab makes the hour routine.

## The law

No new law. You apply the settlement rules of ss. 18–19 ([lesson 33.3](lesson-03.md)), the remedies and fee rules of ss. 20, 22–23 ([lesson 33.4](lesson-04.md)), the exits and limitation of ss. 11, 18(ו), 19(ד) and 26(ד), and the choice of route from [lesson 33.6](lesson-06.md). Keep [the Law](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות) open.

## Leading cases

None new. Use Reichert and Schwartzman (lesson 33.4) for the fee and the s. 19(ג)(2) checklist (lesson 33.3) for the settlement.

## How it works in practice

Where to find the full papers: the notice names the lawyers' offices where the settlement can be read; the registry holds the settlement application and its annexes (search the registry by the party name "ליים" or the case number, lesson 33.1). The notice itself says the full settlement prevails over the notice.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- The notice: `https://www.li.me/he-il/class-action-notice` (Hebrew). If the page has changed, use the summary in step 1.
- Net HaMishpat registry (lesson 33.1), sections 18–26 of the Law.
- Your own scooter-app history if you used Lime; otherwise act for the supplied member: Amir Saleh, a student in Tel Aviv who rode Lime about twice a week from 2022 to 2024 and deleted the app in March 2025.
- The [exit and objection template](../../templates/class-action-objection-and-opt-out.md), part A and part C.
- 120 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Extract the facts (20 min).** Fill a table with: court, case number (ת״צ 55398-11-23), judge, filing date of the settlement application (30.7.2026), date of the court's decision approving the notice (2.8.2026), publication date (find it), the claim (rounding partial minutes up to a full minute), and the parties. If the page is gone, the notice said: Lime will give benefits worth ₪1,600,000 within 60 days of the effective date; each user with an account at the time of performance who rode at least once in the 18 months before the effective date gets ₪3 in scooter credit, and users with 3 or more rides get an extra credit allocated randomly so that the total reaches ₪1.6 million; credits are automatic and valid for two years; Lime will disclose in the app and terms that time is rounded up to the next minute; members release all claims arising from the facts of the case; agreed recommendation: award ₪32,143 and fee ₪305,357, each plus VAT, half the fee within 30 days of the effective date and the rest after Lime reports performance; exit by written notice to the Tel Aviv district court registry citing the case number, with copies to all counsel, by hand or registered mail.
2. **Deadline (10 min).** Find the publication date in the registry (open the case and look for the notice or the decision ordering publication). Add 45 days. Write whether the window is still open today. If it has closed, continue as a dry run and note that a late filing needs the court's leave (s. 18(ד) "or a later date").
3. **Am I in the class? (10 min).** Quote the class definition. Apply it to yourself or to Amir. Note: the class runs "until the judgment approving the settlement becomes final".
4. **What do I get? (15 min).** Apply the benefit formula. Amir has no account at the time of performance: what does he get? What does he release?
5. **Test the settlement against s. 19(ג)(2) (25 min).** For each consideration write one line: (א) gap between ₪3 credits and the overcharge claimed; (ב) objections (unknown yet); (ג) stage: before certification, after an answer?; (ד) examiner: appointed?; (ה) risks for the class if litigated; (ו) scope of the release.
6. **Test the class and the release against s. 18(ז)(1) (10 min).** Does the settlement's class (to final judgment) or its release ("arising from the facts") go beyond the application? You need the application's class definition from the registry to answer.
7. **Test the fee (10 min).** Award plus fee as a percentage of ₪1.6 million; then of an estimated real value if half the credits are never used. Compare with the Reichert ceiling as read in Schwartzman (stage: settlement before certification) and the bill's 16% benchmark (not law).
8. **Decide (20 min).** Choose: stay in, ask to leave (s. 18(ו)), or object (s. 18(ד)), and write five lines of reasons. If you leave, draft part A of the template; if you object, list the points for lesson 33.8.

### How you know it worked

- Your fact table has every item in step 1, with the publication date and the computed last day.
- You applied the class definition and benefit formula to a specific person and reached a number.
- Each s. 19(ג)(2) line has a fact from the notice or the registry.
- Your decision is reasoned in money and principle, not in irritation.

**Facts.** Tel Aviv district court, ת״צ 55398-11-23, Judge A. Dafdi; settlement application filed 30.7.2026; notice approved 2.8.2026. Publication date to be confirmed in the registry; if it was published in early August, the 45 days ended in mid-to-late September 2026, so the window has most likely closed by 18 October 2026: this is a dry run.

**Membership.** Amir rented Lime scooters and, like every rider of a per-minute service that rounds up, was charged for partial minutes as full minutes: he is in the class.

**Benefit.** He has no account at the time of performance and no ride in the 18 months before the effective date (he stopped in 2024): he receives nothing. Yet the release binds him (s. 24). Even active users receive ₪3 of credit (plus about ₪1 for frequent riders), usable only by riding again.

**Future change.** Lime will disclose rounding; it will not stop rounding. Disclosure answers the misleading claim but leaves the practice.

**s. 19(ג)(2).** (א) The application's claimed loss per rider is not in the notice; if frequent riders lost several shekels a month, ₪3 is a small fraction. (ג) Before certification; the notice mentions no answer stage, so the case is early. (ד) No examiner is mentioned; the court must appoint one unless it gives special reasons (s. 19(ב)(1)). (ה) The legal issue (rounding contrary to the agreement) seems simple, which reduces the class's risk. (ו) The release covers all claims "arising from the facts", which may be wider than the causes in the application.

**s. 18(ז)(1).** If the application defined the class up to its filing date, a settlement class running to final judgment adds members not in the application; this must be checked in the registry and is a proper point for objection.

**Fee.** ₪337,500 plus VAT on ₪1.6 million of face value is about 21%; if half the credits are never used, about 42% of real value. For a settlement before certification, the bill's benchmark (not law) would be 16% of the first ₪5 million, about ₪256,000. Points: base the fee on credits actually used, and pay the balance only after a final report (s. 19(ו)).

**Decision for Amir.** Leaving gains him nothing: his own claim is a few shekels. Staying gives him nothing either. Objecting is the only act with value, for him and for riders like him: ask that former users receive a refund or a credit not tied to riding again, that the release be limited to the causes in the application, and that the fee be tied to credits actually used. If the window has closed, he can only ask the court for leave to file late, with reasons.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Reading the notice, checking the registry, leaving and objecting are acts of a member in his own name (s. 18(ד), (ו)).

### Where and how

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- Exit: written notice to the court registry named in the notice, citing the case number, with copies to all counsel, by the method the notice requires (here: by hand or registered mail with confirmation).
- Objection: in the case file, with an affidavit (lesson 33.8).
- Deadline: 45 days from publication ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- Your own loss under the release is large (not the case here).
- Someone offers you a benefit to stay silent or withdraw an objection.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Relying on the website notice only | Missing terms in the annexes | Read the settlement in the registry | Treat the notice as a summary |
| Counting 45 days from the day you read it | Late filing | Ask the court for leave with reasons | Find the publication date |
| Leaving to "protest" | You lose your share and change nothing | Object instead | Leave only to sue yourself |
| Objecting to everything | The court discounts the objection | Pick the two or three strongest points | Use s. 19(ג)(2) as the filter |

## Review

- A notice gives the class, the benefit, the release, the fee and the deadline; the full settlement in the registry prevails.
- Compute the deadline from publication; check who gets nothing but still releases claims.
- Leave only when you will pursue a meaningful claim yourself; object when the settlement has a defect you can name and prove.
