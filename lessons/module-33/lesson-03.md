# 33.3 · Settlements, opting out and objections

By the end of this lesson you will know exactly what happens between "the parties have reached a settlement" and "the court approved it", where in that process a class member can speak, and what each of the three ways out of a class does to your own claim. Most Israeli class actions that end with anything for the class end in a settlement, and a settlement binds every member who did not leave. This is the part of the Law written for you as a member.

## Why it matters

A settlement is negotiated by the representative, class counsel and the defendant. The members are absent. Each negotiator has interests that may differ from yours: counsel's fee, the defendant's wish for a broad release at the lowest cost. The Law answers this "agency problem" with four safeguards: full disclosure on oath, public notice, a right to object, and an independent examiner, all under the court's duty to find the settlement "appropriate, fair and reasonable". The safeguards work only if someone reads the notice.

## The law

### No settlement without the court

> לא ייעשה הסכם ליישוב הסכסוך שבענינו הוגשה בקשה לאישור או שבענינו אושרה תובענה ייצוגית (בחוק זה – הסדר פשרה), אלא באישור בית המשפט.

*Gist:* No agreement settling a dispute that is the subject of an application or a certified class action may be made without the court's approval. [s. 18(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_18) The application is supported by affidavits of the parties' lawyers disclosing every material detail (s. 18(ב)), and by the parties' affidavits declaring any benefit received (reg. 12(ב)). It must explain why the settlement is appropriate, fair and reasonable and set out, among other things, the gap between what it gives and what the class might win, the stage of the case, the risks, how members will be found and paid, and which causes and remedies will become res judicata (reg. 12(א)).

What a settlement may not contain:

> הסדר פשרה לא יכלול – (1) עילות תביעה, בעלי דין או חברי קבוצה, אשר לא נכללו בבקשה לאישור או בהחלטה על אישור התובענה הייצוגית [...]; (2) הוראה בדבר תשלום גמול למבקש או לתובע המייצג, או שכר טרחה לבא כוח מייצג, ואולם הצדדים רשאים להגיש לבית המשפט המלצה מוסכמת לענין תשלום גמול ושכר טרחה [...]

*Gist:* A settlement may not cover causes, parties or class members not in the application or certification decision, and may not itself fix the award and fee; the parties may only submit an agreed recommendation, which must appear in the notice. [s. 18(ז)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_18)

### Publication and who receives notice

Unless the court dismisses the application on its face, it orders publication of a notice under s. 25 and sends the notice, the application, the settlement and the claim to the Attorney General, the Director of Courts (for the registry) and any body the Minister sets (s. 18(ג)). By Schedule item, the regulations add the relevant regulator: the Consumer Protection Commissioner (item 1), the Insurance Supervisor (item 2), the Banking Supervisor (item 3), the Competition Commissioner (item 4), the Securities Authority (item 5), the Ministry of Environmental Protection (item 6), the employment-equality and disability commissions (items 8, 9) and the Chief Labour Relations Officer (item 10) (reg. 12(ד)). The Attorney General is served at the District Attorney's office for the court's area (reg. 12(ה)); the Courts Administration's directive 233/14 tells registries to send these files to the relevant District Attorney's office (directive s. 11).

The notice's text, form and placement are approved by the court; it must state the main terms and refer to the registry and other places where the full text and annexes can be read (s. 25(ד)). The court chooses the medium (newspapers, the company's website, personal email, SMS) considering cost, effectiveness, the size of each member's stake, the number of members and their language (s. 25(ה)).

### The objection

> אדם הנמנה עם הקבוצה שבשמה הוגשה בקשה לאישור הסדר פשרה, אדם הפועל לטובת עניינם של חברי הקבוצה, רשות ציבורית הפועלת לקידום מטרה ציבורית בקשר לענין שבו עוסקת הבקשה לאישור או התובענה הייצוגית, ארגון הפועל לקידום מטרה ציבורית כאמור, וכן היועץ המשפטי לממשלה, רשאים להגיש לבית המשפט, בכתב, בתוך 45 ימים מיום פרסום ההודעה כאמור בסעיף קטן (ג) או בתוך מועד מאוחר יותר שיקבע בית המשפט, התנגדות מנומקת להסדר הפשרה וכן להמלצה המוסכמת בענין גמול ושכר טרחה [...]

*Gist:* A class member, a person acting for the members' interests, a relevant public authority or organisation, and the Attorney General may file a reasoned written objection to the settlement and to the agreed fee-and-award recommendation within 45 days of publication or a later date set by the court. [s. 18(ד)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_18) Period: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

An objector may not withdraw the objection, or take any benefit in connection with it, without the court's approval, and must disclose everything in an affidavit; if the court accepts the objection in whole or part it may award the objector an award, weighing the benefit the objection brought the class (s. 18(ד1)). The parties may respond within the time the court sets (s. 18(ה)). Lessons 33.7 and 33.8 are the practical side.

### The three ways out

| Route | When | How | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opt out of a certified class (s. 11) | After a certification decision is published | Notice to the court on Form 1 (reg. 7) within 45 days of publication or a later date; the court may extend for a special reason (s. 11(ב)) | You are not a member; the judgment does not bind you |
| Ask to leave a settlement (s. 18(ו)) | After a settlement-approval application is published | Ask the court, within the objection period, to allow you to leave the class covered by the settlement | If allowed, the settlement is not res judicata for you |
| Opt-out condition in an approved settlement (s. 19(ד)(2)–(3)) | When the court makes approval conditional on an exit option | Notice to the court at the time and in the way the court sets | Approval is not res judicata for you |

> חבר קבוצה אשר אינו מעוניין כי יחול עליו הסדר הפשרה, רשאי לבקש מבית המשפט, בתוך המועד שנקבע להגשת התנגדויות לפי סעיף קטן (ד), להתיר לו לצאת מן הקבוצה שעליה יחול ההסדר.

*Gist:* A member who does not want the settlement to apply to him may ask the court, within the objection period, to allow him to leave the class covered by it. [s. 18(ו)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_18) Note the words "ask ... to allow": in practice notices tell members to give notice on the Form 1 model, and courts grant such requests routinely, but the text gives the court the last word.

After any exit, limitation for your own claim does not end before one year from your notice, provided it had not expired when the application was filed (s. 26(ד)). In an opt-in case under s. 12 (rare: large individual claims, not competition or securities claims), only those who give notice on Form 2 within 45 days are in the class (reg. 8).

### The court's decision

> בית המשפט לא יאשר הסדר פשרה אלא אם כן מצא, כי ההסדר ראוי, הוגן וסביר בהתחשב בענינם של חברי הקבוצה, ואם הבקשה לאישור הסדר הפשרה הוגשה לפני שאושרה התובענה הייצוגית – גם כי התובענה שהוגשה עומדת, לכאורה, בתנאים לאישור תובענה ייצוגית [...]

*Gist:* The court approves only a settlement it finds appropriate, fair and reasonable for the members; if the case has not been certified, it must also find that the claim prima facie meets the certification conditions and that ending it by settlement is the efficient and fair way. [s. 19(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_19)

- **The examiner (בודק).** The court appoints an expert in the field, not recommended by a party or an objector and with no earlier role, unless it finds for special reasons that no opinion is needed (s. 19(ב)(1)–(2)). The examiner receives the application and the objections, may hear the parties and propose changes, and reports within sixty days on the settlement's advantages and disadvantages for the class; the parties may respond within thirty days (s. 19(ב)(3)–(5)).
- **Reasons.** The decision states the class, the causes, common questions and relief, and the main terms, and addresses: the gap between the settlement and what the class might have won; the objections and how they were decided; the stage of the case; the examiner's opinion; the risks against the settlement's merits; and which causes and remedies become res judicata (s. 19(ג)).
- **Conditions.** The court may make approval conditional on terms needed to protect members, enforce the law or supervise performance, including an exit option (s. 19(ד)(1)), and may order a final report on performance (s. 19(ד1)).
- **Fees.** On approval the court sets the award and fee itself, may consider the agreed recommendation, and may make the fee conditional on performance (s. 19(ו); lesson 33.4).
- **Failure.** If approval is refused or later cancelled, what was said in the approval proceedings is not evidence in any civil case (s. 19(ז)).
- The approval and the settlement with annexes go to the registry (s. 19(ה)).

### The binding effect

> פסק דין בתובענה ייצוגית יהווה מעשה בית דין לגבי כל חברי הקבוצה שבשמם נוהלה התובענה הייצוגית, אלא אם כן נקבע במפורש אחרת בחוק זה.

*Gist:* A judgment in a class action, including one approving a settlement, binds every member of the class on whose behalf it was conducted, unless the Law expressly provides otherwise. [s. 24](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_24)

## Leading cases

### Key text: the s. 19(ג)(2) considerations as the court's checklist

The free Supreme Court rulings on settlement approval could not be opened for this lesson (supremedecisions.court.gov.il was unreachable when checked), so the statute carries the doctrine. The six considerations in s. 19(ג)(2) are the court's own checklist and the best structure for any objection: (א) the gap between the settlement and the likely judgment; (ב) the objections; (ג) the stage; (ד) the examiner's opinion; (ה) risks and chances against the settlement's merits; (ו) the scope of the release. An objection that follows these headings answers the questions the judge must answer in writing anyway. [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_19)

### Key text: a real 2026 notice, ת״צ 22690-06-20 (Tel Aviv district court, filed 27.5.2026)

A notice under s. 25(א)(3) in an application against Bank Hapoalim about cash deposits by business customers. After mediation with a court-appointed expert, the bank agreed to pay ₪10 million, not to members, but as a public-benefit remedy under s. 20(ג) to the fund under s. 27א; no further changes for the future, because the bank had already fixed the service after the applicants' demand. The release covers all claims about gaps between deposited and credited sums; objections and exit notices on the reg. 7 form were due within 45 days of publication. Why it matters: it shows a settlement in which members receive nothing personally, which is lawful only if the court finds individual payment impractical (s. 20(ג)(1)), the first question an objector should test. [Notice PDF](https://www.bankhapoalim.co.il/sites/default/files/media/PDFS/hesder_pshara_22690-06-20.pdf)

## How it works in practice

### Where notices appear

Newspapers (Hebrew and, where the court orders, Arabic or Russian), the defendant's website and app, personal messages to customers, and the registry, where the settlement application and its annexes are filed. Search the registry by the defendant's name and open documents titled בקשה לאישור הסדר פשרה.

### Timeline after a settlement is filed

1. Application with affidavits (s. 18(ב); reg. 12).
2. Court reviews; orders publication and service on the AG, the Director of Courts and the regulator (s. 18(ג); reg. 12(ד)).
3. Publication; the 45-day window for objections and exit requests opens (s. 18(ד), (ו)).
4. Examiner appointed; report within sixty days of receiving the papers; responses (s. 19(ב)).
5. Parties respond to objections (s. 18(ה)); AG files its position if it chooses.
6. Hearing; reasoned decision (s. 19(ג)); publication of the approval (s. 25(א)(4)).
7. Performance; final report (s. 19(ד1)); balance to the fund (lesson 33.4).

### The Attorney General's role

The AG receives every settlement application and may object (s. 18(ד)); the State Attorney's civil department has a class-actions unit, and the District Attorney's office files the AG's position in the cases it chooses. The AG's positions commonly address the scope of the release, the real value of benefits in kind and the fee (the State Attorney's page on class actions could not be read when checked: `https://www.justice.gov.il/Units/StateAttorney/Civil/Pages/ClassActions.aspx` now redirects to gov.il). From 1 January 2027 the Attorney General (Opinions, Representation and Supervision) Law 2026 changes how the Government's position is presented in court; its chapter on representation does not apply to powers given to the AG by statute (s. 12(א)(2) of that Law), which on its face includes the s. 18(ד) power to object. How this will work is untested and the Law is under challenge in the High Court ([lesson 33.5](lesson-05.md)).

## Worked example

Eli Dahan of Ashdod receives an SMS from his insurer on 3 March: "Notice of a settlement application in ת״צ ___ about interest on late payouts; full text on our site." He opens the notice.

1. **Is he in the class?** Class: "policyholders who received a payout more than thirty days after the claim, in the seven years before filing". Eli had a car-theft payout that came after ninety days: yes.
2. **What does he get?** Automatic credit of the missing interest to those identified from the insurer's records; ₪1.2 million to the fund for those not identified.
3. **The deadline.** Forty-five days from publication; the notice is dated 1 March, so the window closes about mid-April. He diaries the date.
4. **His choices.** Stay in and receive the credit; object if the interest rate used is lower than the Insurance Contract Law requires; or ask to leave and sue alone, which is worth it only if his own interest claim is large (it is ₪640, so no).
5. **Decision.** He stays in, but sends the examiner nothing and files nothing. He checks the registry after the hearing for the approval decision.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- The Bank Hapoalim notice (link above) or any settlement notice from the registry or a newspaper.
- Sections 18, 19, 24–26 open.
- 50 minutes.

### Steps

1. Identify: case number, court, judge, filing date, publication date and who published.
2. Quote the class definition. Write who is in and who is out.
3. List what members get: money, credit, benefit in kind, a change for the future, a payment to the fund.
4. Copy the release paragraph. Is it limited to the causes in the application (s. 18(ז)(1)), or does it reach "any claim connected with" the facts?
5. Find the agreed fee and award, or note that none is stated.
6. Compute the last day for an objection or exit request (45 days from publication; if publication date is missing, find it in the registry).
7. List who else received the notice by law (AG, Director of Courts, which regulator under reg. 12(ד)).
8. Write a one-paragraph reading: the strongest point in favour of the settlement and the strongest point against it, each tied to an s. 19(ג)(2) consideration.

### Template

None for this lesson; the objection and exit templates come in [lesson 33.8](lesson-08.md).

### How you know it worked

- Your timeline has a concrete last day and you know which document gives the publication date.
- You can say whether the release is wider than the claim.
- Each point for or against cites an s. 19(ג)(2) consideration.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. A class member may object and ask to leave in his own name (s. 18(ד), (ו)); the Law does not require a lawyer for either, and many objections are filed by members personally. Objecting on behalf of other members is representing others (Bar Association Law s. 20); an organisation objects through its own officers or a lawyer.

### Where and how

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- File in the case file at the court named in the notice: through Net HaMishpat (personal login for unrepresented parties, where available) or at the court registry, with copies to the lawyers listed in the notice.
- Exit request: on the model of Form 1 to the Class Actions Regulations (reg. 7), by the date in the notice.
- Fee: objections and exit notices in a class action are filed in an existing case; check with the registry whether a motion fee is charged ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- Free help: court information centres; the Israel Consumer Council; Kol-Zchut on class actions (⚠️ manual check).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- Your personal loss is large and you are deciding whether to leave the class and sue alone.
- The defendant offers you something to withdraw your objection (s. 18(ד1) requires court approval).
- You want to object on behalf of an organisation, or to appeal an approval.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Missing the 45 days | Objection not heard; you are bound | Ask the court to extend, with reasons (s. 18(ד) allows a later date) | Diary the date on the day you see the notice |
| Objecting without reasons | Objection given little weight | File a supplement before the hearing | Tie every point to s. 19(ג)(2) |
| Sending the objection only to the company | It is not before the court | File in the case file | Read the notice's filing instructions |
| Leaving the class with a ₪50 claim | You lose the settlement benefit for nothing | Ask to withdraw the exit request before approval | Leave only if your own claim is worth suing for |
| Assuming the settlement fixes the fee | You ignore your right to object to it | Object to the recommendation too | Section 18(ז)(2) bars fees in the settlement itself |

## Review

- Every settlement needs court approval after disclosure affidavits, publication, service on the AG and the relevant regulator, and usually an examiner's report.
- Members, organisations, public authorities and the AG may file a reasoned objection within 45 days of publication; objectors cannot be bought off without court approval.
- There are three exits: s. 11 after certification, s. 18(ו) from a pending settlement, and a court-imposed exit condition under s. 19(ד); each extends limitation by at least a year (s. 26(ד)).
- The court approves only a settlement that is appropriate, fair and reasonable, with written reasons on six statutory considerations; approval binds all who did not leave (s. 24).
