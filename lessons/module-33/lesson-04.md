# 33.4 · Remedies, the class-actions fund, fees and awards

By the end of this lesson you will be able to look at any class-action judgment or settlement and ask the questions that decide whether it is good for the class: who actually receives money, what happens to money no one claims, how much the lawyers and the representative take, and whether that is in proportion to what the class got. These are the questions the court must answer, and the ones an objector should press.

## Why it matters

A headline sum ("₪10 million settlement") says little. If the money is paid as discount coupons most members never use, the real value is lower than it looks. If no member can be identified, the money goes to a public fund and members get nothing personally. If the fee is fixed as a percentage of the headline sum, class counsel profits from inflating it. The Law gives courts tools for each problem, and the case law and a pending bill show how the balance is being struck.

## The law

### The menu of remedies

> הכריע בית המשפט בתובענה הייצוגית [...] לטובת הקבוצה [...] רשאי הוא [...] להורות [...]: (1) על תשלום פיצוי כספי או על מתן סעד אחר, בשיעור ובאופן שיקבע, לכל אחד מחברי הקבוצה שהוכחה זכאותו [...]; (2) על כך שכל חבר קבוצה יוכיח את זכאותו [...]; (3) על תשלום פיצוי כספי בסכום כולל ועל אופן חישוב חלקו של כל חבר קבוצה, ובלבד שסכום הפיצוי הכולל ניתן לחישוב מדויק על יסוד הראיות [...]

*Gist:* When the class wins, the court may order payment or other relief to each member whose entitlement was proved, require each member to prove entitlement, or order an aggregate sum calculable precisely from the evidence with a formula for each share; any unclaimed balance is first redistributed among members in proportion to their loss (never above full compensation), and what remains goes to the s. 27א fund, earmarked for a field close to the subject of the case. [s. 20(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_20) Where members must prove entitlement, the court may appoint an administrator (ממונה), whose decisions can be challenged before the court (s. 20(ב)(1); reg. 15), and may order the defendant to pay members' costs of proving (s. 20(ב)(2)). The court weighs the expected relief against the cost of proof (reg. 13(א)); a defendant that must pay members personally but lacks current addresses checks them against the Population Registry (reg. 13(ב)).

### Relief for the public

> מצא בית המשפט כי פיצוי כספי לחברי הקבוצה, כולם או חלקם, אינו מעשי בנסיבות הענין, בין משום שלא ניתן לזהותם ולבצע את התשלום בעלות סבירה ובין מסיבה אחרת, רשאי הוא להורות על מתן כל סעד אחר לטובת הקבוצה, כולה או חלקה, או לטובת הציבור, כפי שימצא לנכון בנסיבות הענין.

*Gist:* If paying members is impractical, for example because they cannot be identified and paid at reasonable cost, the court may order any other relief for the class or for the public; money for the public goes to the s. 27א fund, earmarked as close as possible to the subject of the case. [s. 20(ג)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_20) The same rules apply to settlements (s. 19(ד2)).

### Limits

- **No exemplary damages, and no damages without proof of damage**, in a class action, except for accessibility claims under item 9; non-pecuniary damage may be awarded (s. 20(ה)). So the statutory and exemplary damages of the Consumer Protection Law ([lesson 29.9](../module-29/lesson-09.md)) are available to you in your own claim, not in a class action.
- **Public bodies and financial stability:** in fixing the amount and manner of payment the court may weigh harm to the public or, for private defendants, harm from damage to the defendant's financial stability (s. 20(ד)).
- **Restitution against an authority** is limited to the 24 months before filing; each member may still sue personally for earlier periods (s. 21).
- The court may supervise performance and require a final report (s. 20(ו)).

### Award and fee

> הכריע בית המשפט בתובענה הייצוגית [...] לטובת הקבוצה [...] לרבות בדרך של אישור הסדר פשרה, יורה על תשלום גמול לתובע המייצג [...]

*Gist:* When the class wins, including by an approved settlement, the court orders an award to the representative unless special reasons make it unjustified, considering his effort and risk (especially where the relief sought is declaratory), the benefit to the class and the public importance of the case; in special cases it may make an award even without certification or success, or to an organisation that took part in the hearing. [s. 22](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_22)

> בית המשפט יקבע את שכר הטרחה של בא הכוח המייצג בעד הטיפול בתובענה הייצוגית, לרבות בבקשה לאישור; בא הכוח המייצג לא יקבל שכר טרחה בסכום העולה על הסכום שקבע בית המשפט כאמור.

*Gist:* The court fixes class counsel's fee, and counsel may not receive more than the court fixed. [s. 23(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_23) The considerations are: the benefit to the class; the complexity, effort, risk and expenses; the public importance; how counsel conducted the case; and the gap between the relief claimed and the relief awarded (s. 23(ב)). The court may pay part of the fee on account (s. 23(ג)) and make payment conditional on the judgment being carried out (s. 23(ד)); in settlements, likewise (s. 19(ו)).

### Court fees

For class actions other than item 11, the court fee is paid in two parts: the applicant pays the first part on filing; if the claim is dismissed the applicant pays the rest; if the class wins or a settlement is approved the defendant pays the rest and reimburses the first part; after an approved withdrawal the applicant pays the rest unless the court orders otherwise (Court Fees Regulations reg. 7א(א)). For item 11 a single, lower fee applies, reimbursed by the authority if the class wins (reg. 7א(ב)). Amounts: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### The two funds

> מוקמת בזה קרן למימון תובענות ייצוגיות [...] שתפקידה לסייע לתובעים מייצגים במימון בקשות לאישור ותובענות ייצוגיות, אשר יש חשיבות ציבורית וחברתית בהגשתן ובבירורן.

*Gist:* The fund for financing class actions helps representative plaintiffs finance applications and class actions of public and social importance; it is run by a nine-member board appointed by the Minister of Justice. [s. 27](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_27) Private residents and qualifying organisations may apply; the board weighs public and social importance, good faith, chances, duplicate cases, the cost and difficulty of proof, and other funding sources (Funding Regulations regs 2–3). The Wikisource note records that the fund's operation was extended until 15.6.2027; it does not fund derivative or securities-holder claims under Companies Law s. 209(א) (s. 27(ז)).

> מוקמת בזה קרן לניהול ולחלוקת כספים הנפסקים כסעד מכוח סעיף 20(א)(3) ו־(ג)(2). [...] מטרת הקרן לחלק את הכספים [...] לטובת מטרות ציבוריות [...]

*Gist:* A second fund, managed by the Administrator General, receives money awarded as residual or public relief and distributes it for public purposes through a five-member committee chaired by a person qualified to be a district judge. [s. 27א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_27א) Under the committee's published procedure (2022), each award opens a file at the Administrator General's office; the committee decides the kind of project, issues a public call or allocates directly, selects recipients, reports to the court and pays against reports on performance. [Procedure](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/נוהל_עבודת_הוועדה_לחלוקת_כספים_שנפסקו_כסעד)

## Leading cases

### ע״א 2046/10 עזבון המנוח משה שמש נ׳ דן רייכרט (23.5.2012)

The Supreme Court tied class counsel's fee to the benefit actually delivered to the class, as a percentage that falls as the recovery grows and that is higher when the case ends in a judgment than in a settlement, so that counsel's fate follows the class's fate and counsel has an incentive to see relief through to payment. Why it matters: it is the leading method for fixing fees under s. 23, and the government's 2024 bill proposes to codify it. Holding as described in the explanatory notes to the 2024 bill; ruling text not opened. [Bill PDF](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/25/law/25_ls1_4708827.pdf)

### רע״א 2957/17 סופרגז חברה ישראלית להפצת גז נ׳ שוורצמן (17.3.2024)

The Supreme Court held that the percentages set in Reichert are the maximum ceiling for award and fee, not a starting point, and the government relies on it for setting the award and the fee together as one combined rate. Why it matters: in 2026 a court asked to approve a fee will measure it against the Reichert ceilings as read in Schwartzman. As described in the 2024 bill notes; ruling text not opened. [Bill PDF](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/25/law/25_ls1_4708827.pdf)

### ע״א 10085/08 תנובה נ׳ עזבון ראבי (4.12.2011)

Briefed in lesson 29.6. Here: because the members who bought the milk could not be identified, the Court used s. 20(ג) to fix ₪250 per affected member and direct the total to public purposes (research grants and free milk for people in need), dropping the discount arrangement, and reduced the fees. Why it matters: the model for relief for the public. [Versa (English)](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/tnuva-central-cooperative-v-raabi-estate)

## How it works in practice

### Benefits in kind and coupons

Settlements often give credits, discounts or extra service instead of cash. The government's 2024 notes report a concern that such benefits are valued above what members actually use, while fees are calculated on the inflated value, and that courts approve the parties' agreed fee recommendation in most cases. Ask: is the credit automatic or must members claim it? Who no longer uses the service and gets nothing? Does the fee rest on the face value or on the expected take-up?

### The pending reform

Amendment 16 (Government Bill, July 2024) proposes a Sixth Schedule of maximum combined award-and-fee rates by tranche of compensation and by stage (for the first ₪5 million: 25% after a judgment, 20% for a settlement after certification, 16% for a settlement before certification; lower rates above), calculated on the net sum paid to the class or the fund, an extra up to 2% only in exceptional cases, and abolishing the agreed recommendation. These fee parts were **not** among the parts approved in committee in October 2026; they are not law. Use them only as a benchmark of the government's view.

## Worked example

The Bank Hapoalim settlement (ת״צ 22690-06-20, lesson 33.3) proposes ₪10 million to the s. 27א fund as relief for the public, and no future change because the bank already fixed the service.

1. **Remedy type.** Section 20(ג): legitimate only if paying the business customers who lost money is impractical. The bank holds deposit records; an objector would ask why customers whose credited sum fell short cannot be identified and paid directly.
2. **Earmarking.** The court must earmark the money for a field close to the subject (s. 20(ג)(2)), for example financial education for small businesses.
3. **Fee.** The notice states no agreed recommendation; the court will fix award and fee under ss. 22–23. Under Reichert as read in Schwartzman, the percentage must reflect a settlement before certification, the lower stage.
4. **Proposed benchmark only.** Under the bill's table for a settlement before certification, the ceiling would be 16% of the first ₪5 million (₪800,000) plus 13% of the next ₪5 million (₪650,000), ₪1.45 million in all for award and fee together; the bill is not law, but it shows what the government considers proportionate.
5. **Conclusion for a member.** If your business lost money in deposits, the settlement gives you nothing personally: consider asking to leave and claim your own loss if it is significant (s. 18(ו)), or object that direct payment is practical.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- The Lime settlement notice: `https://www.li.me/he-il/class-action-notice` (filed 30.7.2026, Tel Aviv district court, ת״צ 55398-11-23), or any notice with an agreed fee recommendation.
- Sections 20, 22, 23 open; a calculator.
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. Classify each benefit under s. 20: individual payment, aggregate sum, benefit in kind, public relief, future change.
2. Write down the headline value (₪1.6 million in credits), the award (₪32,143 plus VAT) and the fee (₪305,357 plus VAT).
3. Compute award plus fee as a percentage of the headline value. Then estimate take-up: if only half of the credits are used before they expire, what is the percentage of the real value?
4. Identify who gets nothing: members with no account, or no ride in the 18 months before the final judgment.
5. List the s. 23(ב) considerations and mark each "supports the fee", "neutral" or "against".
6. Compare with the bill's 16% benchmark for settlements before certification (a benchmark, not law).
7. Write three sentences an objector could file about the fee.

### Template

None. Lesson 33.8 turns this into an objection.

### How you know it worked

- You have two percentages: on the headline value and on your estimated real value.
- You named at least one group of members who receive nothing.
- Your three sentences cite s. 23(ב) and the stage of the case.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Not a filing lesson as such: remedies and fees are decided by the court. A member influences them in two ways open to anyone in their own name: an objection to the settlement and the agreed fee recommendation (s. 18(ד)), and, after judgment, a challenge to an administrator's decision on his own entitlement (s. 20(ב)(1); reg. 15).

### Where and how

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- Challenge to an administrator's decision: to the court that appointed him, within the period in reg. 15(א) from service or publication, supported by an affidavit; the administrator is the respondent ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- Fund information: the Administrator General publishes the distribution committee's calls (⚠️ manual check: gov.il); fund decisions are collected on odata.org.il.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- An organisation you run wants to apply to the s. 27 fund to finance a case, or to the s. 27א fund for a grant.
- You are owed a large sum and the settlement gives you a coupon.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Valuing credits at face value | Fee looks proportionate when it is not | Object with a take-up estimate | Ask how many members will use the benefit |
| Accepting public relief when members are identifiable | Members lose personal compensation | Object under s. 20(ג)(1): payment is practical | Check what records the defendant holds |
| Expecting exemplary damages in a class action | Wrong expectations; wrong forum choice | Sue personally where the statute allows | Read s. 20(ה) |
| Treating the bill's fee table as law | A wrong argument in an objection | Cite Reichert and Schwartzman | Check the Knesset status first |
| Ignoring the administrator's decision on your claim | It becomes final | Challenge it within the reg. 15 period | Diary the period from service |

## Review

- Section 20 offers individual relief, member-by-member proof with an administrator, an aggregate sum with redistribution, and, where payment is impractical, relief for the public through the s. 27א fund.
- No exemplary damages and no damages without proof of damage, except for accessibility claims; restitution from authorities is capped at 24 months.
- The court sets the award (s. 22) and fee (s. 23); Reichert ties the fee to the benefit as a falling percentage, and Schwartzman treats those percentages as a ceiling.
- The s. 27 fund finances important cases; the s. 27א fund, run by the Administrator General, distributes residual and public relief.
