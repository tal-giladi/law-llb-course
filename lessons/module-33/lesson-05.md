# 33.5 · Public-interest litigation: public petitioners, NGOs and amicus briefs

By the end of this lesson you will be able to map a public problem, one that harms many people or no one in particular, to the legal route that can reach it, and to know which routes are open to you as an individual and which belong to organisations. You met standing and the public petitioner in [lesson 06.6](../module-06/lesson-06.md) and [lesson 14.9](../module-14/lesson-09.md); this lesson does not repeat them. It adds the class action as a public-interest tool, the "friend of the court", and the Attorney General's voice for the public interest, which changed by statute in 2026.

## Why it matters

Some wrongs are diffuse: a regulation no one enforces, a public service closed to people with disabilities, a charge every resident pays. Private litigation reaches them badly, because no one person's stake justifies the cost. Israeli law offers three answers: relaxed standing in public law (the public petitioner), the class action brought or joined by an organisation, and participation in someone else's case as amicus curiae or under s. 15. Which one fits depends on whether you want money for a group, a change in government action, or a voice in a case already running.

## The law

### Organisations in class actions

> ”ארגון“ – תאגיד, למעט תאגיד שהוקם על פי דין, או הקדש, הקיים ופועל באופן סדיר וממשי במשך שנה לפחות לקידום מטרה ציבורית, אחת או יותר, ושנכסיו והכנסותיו משמשים להשגת המטרות הציבוריות בלבד, ובלבד שפעילותו אינה מטעם מפלגה או גוף פוליטי אחר או בזיקה למפלגה או לגוף כאמור או לשם קידום מטרותיהם;

*Gist:* An organisation is a corporation (not a statutory one) or endowment that has operated regularly and genuinely for at least a year to promote a public purpose, uses its assets and income only for that purpose, and does not act for or in connection with a party or other political body. [s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_2)

Such an organisation may apply for certification within its purposes if the court is persuaded that it would be hard for an individual to apply; the Israel Consumer Council may apply without that showing (s. 4(א)(3)); it need only show a reasonable possibility that the class was harmed (s. 4(ב)(2)). The Government's 2024 bill proposes to require registration with the Registrar of Non-Profits or Endowments and to exclude organisations whose main activity is filing class actions; those parts were not advanced in October 2026.

> בית המשפט רשאי להתיר לחבר קבוצה [...] וכן לרשות ציבורית הפועלת לקידום מטרה ציבורית בקשר לענין שבו עוסקת התובענה הייצוגית או לארגון הפועל לקידום מטרה כאמור, אשר אינם התובע המייצג, להשתתף בדיונים בתובענה הייצוגית, אם מצא כי הדבר דרוש לשם ניהולה היעיל וההוגן של התובענה הייצוגית [...]

*Gist:* The court may allow a class member, a relevant public authority or an organisation to take part in the hearing of a class action, or of an application for certification, where needed for an efficient and fair hearing and to protect the members' interests. [s. 15](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_15) An organisation that took part may receive an award for its effort and contribution (s. 22(ג)(2)); organisations may also object to settlements (s. 18(ד)) and receive an award if the objection succeeds (s. 18(ד1)).

### The Attorney General's appearance

> ראה היועץ המשפטי לממשלה, כי זכות של מדינת ישראל או זכות ציבורית או ענין ציבורי מושפעים או כרוכים, או עלולים להיות מושפעים או כרוכים, בהליך פלוני שלפני בית משפט [...] רשאי הוא, לפי ראות עיניו, להתייצב באותו הליך ולהשמיע דברו, או להסמיך במיוחד את נציגו לעשות זאת מטעמו.

*Gist:* Where the Attorney General considers that a right of the State, a public right or a public interest is or may be affected in a court proceeding, he may appear in it and be heard, himself or through a specially authorised representative. [Ordinance s. 1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_סדרי_הדין_(התייצבות_היועץ_המשפטי_לממשלה)) Section 2 lets the AG represent public servants and statutory bodies that are parties by virtue of their office. In class actions the AG has a separate statutory power to object to settlements (Class Actions Law s. 18(ד)).

### The 2026 Attorney General Law

> הסמכות לקבוע את עמדת הרשות המבצעת בכל הליך המתנהל בפני בית משפט, דרך כלל או להליך מסוים, נתונה בידי הממשלה, והיא תיקבע בידי היועץ המשפטי לממשלה, אלא אם כן קבעה הממשלה עמדה אחרת.

*Gist:* The Government holds the power to set the executive's position in any court proceeding; the AG sets it unless the Government has set a different one. [s. 7](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_היועץ_המשפטי_לממשלה_(חוות_דעת,_ייצוג_ופיקוח)) If the AG says he cannot present the Government's position, or a minister thinks it will not be presented properly, the Government may appoint another lawyer (ss. 8–9); in that case the AG may not appear under the Ordinance without the approval of the Government, a ministerial committee or the Minister (s. 10(א)). The representation chapter does not apply to criminal proceedings, to powers given to the AG by statute, or to representation of judicial and quasi-judicial bodies (s. 12). The Law was passed on 15 July 2026 and takes effect on 1 January 2027 (s. 18).

## Leading cases

### בג״ץ 910/86 רסלר נ׳ שר הביטחון (1988)

Briefed in [lesson 06.6](../module-06/lesson-06.md): a public petitioner may be heard where the issue concerns the rule of law. Here the point is the choice it opens: when an issue affects the public at large and the remedy is a change in government action, a public petition, not a class action, is the natural route. [English translation](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/312)

### עע״מ 3782/12 מפקד מחוז תל אביב־יפו במשטרת ישראל נ׳ איגוד האינטרנט הישראלי (24.3.2013)

Briefed in [lesson 14.9](../module-14/lesson-09.md): an organisation promoting Internet users' interests had standing as a public petitioner in the administrative affairs court. Here: the same organisation could not have brought a class action on that issue, because blocking orders give no Second Schedule cause; the public petition was the only route. [English translation](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/381)

### מ״ח 7929/96 כוזלי נ׳ מדינת ישראל, פ״ד נג(1) 529 (1999)

In a retrial application arising from a well-known murder case, the Supreme Court first adopted the amicus curiae concept into Israeli law, allowing a body without a personal interest to be heard to assist the court. An empirical study published in 2015 found that applications to appear as amicus grew steadily after Kozli, that most came from NGOs supporting individuals against the State or corporations, and that courts approved most of them. Why it matters: it is the gateway for organisations to add expertise to cases they did not bring. Citation and holding as reported in the Israel Law Review article; ruling text not opened (⚠️). [Abstract](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/israel-law-review/article/abs/an-american-friend-in-an-israeli-court-an-empirical-perspective/6BB24130445102E367E3DD5D38383605)

## How it works in practice

### Choosing the route

| Goal | Route | Who | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stop or change a government decision or rule | Petition (HCJ or administrative affairs court) | Affected person or public petitioner | Prior demand to the authority; delay rules (lesson 14.9) |
| Money back for many people from a business or authority | Class action | Individual with a cause; organisation if hard for individuals; listed public authority | Only Second Schedule causes |
| A voice in a class action already running | Participation under s. 15; objection to a settlement under s. 18(ד) | Member, organisation, public authority | Award possible if it contributed |
| Expertise in any case of public importance | Amicus curiae application | Organisation or expert body | Court's discretion; join, do not take over |
| A regulator to act | Complaint to the regulator; FOI request | Anyone | Lessons 12.4, 14.7 |

### The AG dispute, stated neutrally

The 2026 AG Law is one of the most contested statutes of the 2023–2026 period ([lesson 06.6](../module-06/lesson-06.md)). It passed by 65 to 51.

- **Critics** (for example the Israel Democracy Institute) argue that it removes the two tools the AG has used to keep the Government within the law, binding legal opinions and control of the State's position in court. Petitions to annul it were filed, including by the Movement for Quality Government.
- **Supporters** (the bill's sponsors and commentators who backed it) argue that the elected Government should decide what the State argues in its own name, that the Law keeps the AG independent in criminal matters (s. 14(ב)) and keeps his opinions authoritative unless the Government decides otherwise and reports to a Knesset committee (s. 5).
- **Current law.** Until 1 January 2027 the previous case law governs, under which the AG represents the Government and only in rare cases may the Government be represented separately. The status of the petitions at the date of checking was not verified (⚠️). For class actions, note that the AG's power to object to settlements is a statutory power, which the representation chapter on its face excludes (s. 12(א)(2)).

## Worked example

Noa Levi-Mekonen of Rehovot is deaf. Her municipality's website has no captions on its video guides, and the city's arnona appeals hotline is voice-only.

1. **Is there a class action?** Item 9(1) covers claims under the accessibility chapters of the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law. Damage need not be proved (s. 20(ה) exception) and no court fee is charged (fees reg. 20(31)). But is the municipality a "business" or covered by the accessibility duties? Noa's own claim under the Equal Rights Law is the anchor.
2. **Who should bring it?** An organisation of deaf people that has operated for over a year may apply if it is hard for an individual (s. 4(א)(3)); the Commission for Equal Rights of Persons with Disabilities, a listed public authority, may apply itself (s. 4(א)(2)).
3. **A public petition instead?** If the goal is to compel the municipality to adopt an accessibility plan, an administrative petition with a prior demand may be faster than a class action.
4. **Noa's role.** She writes to the organisation and the Commission with her documents, and files a complaint with the Commission. If a class action is already pending, she can ask to take part (s. 15).

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A public problem you know first-hand (or the supplied one: Yusuf Masarwa of Taibe, whose neighbourhood's bus stops have no shelter or accessible boarding, in breach of accessibility rules).
- Tables above; lessons 06.6 and 14.9 at hand.
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. Write the problem in two sentences: who is harmed, by whom, and what change you want.
2. Use the route table to list every possible route; strike out those that do not fit (no Schedule item, no government decision, nothing pending).
3. For the best route, name who would bring it and why you, an organisation or a public authority is best placed.
4. Search the registry (lesson 33.1) and, for petitions, the court's case search by the authority's name, for a pending case.
5. Draft a one-page letter to a relevant organisation or public authority: the facts, the evidence you hold, the legal route you see, and an offer to give an affidavit. Do not ask them to act for you as a lawyer; offer information.
6. If a case is pending, write two sentences explaining what your participation (s. 15) or an amicus brief would add.

[Letter to an authority](../../templates/letter-to-authority.md) (adapt the addressee to an organisation or the Commission).

### How you know it worked

- You eliminated routes with reasons, not by preference.
- Your letter states facts with dates and attaches evidence, and asks for a specific action.
- You know whether a case already exists.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Partly. An individual may file a petition or a request to take part in a class action (s. 15) in his own name, and may write to organisations and public authorities. Representing an organisation in court is generally a lawyer's job (a corporation usually needs a lawyer in the civil courts; see [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md)). An amicus application is made by the organisation, usually through its lawyer.

### Where and how

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- Petitions: lessons 06.8 and 14.11 for the procedure and templates.
- Request to take part in a class action: a written motion in the case file (s. 15).
- Public authorities that can act: the Commission for Equal Rights of Persons with Disabilities, the Nature and Parks Authority, the Equal Employment Opportunities Commission (First Schedule); the Israel Consumer Council (s. 4(א)(3)).
- Free help: legal clinics at universities often run public-interest cases; Legal Aid does not handle class actions.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You are asked to be the named petitioner or applicant in a case with costs risk.
- The case is against the State and involves a security or budget issue where courts are cautious (s. 8(ב)).
- An organisation you run wants to file an amicus brief or apply for certification.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bringing a class action to change a government policy | No Schedule item; dismissed | Refile as a petition | Match route to goal |
| An organisation formed last month applies | Not an "organisation" under s. 2 | Find an individual with a cause or a qualifying body | Check the one-year rule |
| Amicus who tries to run the case | Application refused or limited | Limit the brief to expertise | Offer a perspective, not a party's case |
| Treating the 2026 AG Law as already in force | Wrong analysis of who speaks for the State | Check the 1.1.2027 commencement and the petitions | Read s. 18 of that Law |
| Writing to an NGO asking it to "represent" you | Confusion over roles and s. 20 | Offer facts and evidence | Ask the NGO to consider its own action |

## Review

- Public petitioners reach government action; class actions reach money for groups on listed causes; s. 15 participation and amicus briefs add voices to cases already running.
- Organisations qualify under s. 2 and may apply when it is hard for an individual; the Consumer Council and three listed public authorities have special standing.
- The AG may appear where a public interest is involved (Ordinance s. 1) and may object to class settlements (s. 18(ד)); the 2026 AG Law, effective 1.1.2027 and under challenge, shifts control of the State's litigation position to the Government.
