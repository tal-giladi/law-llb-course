# 33.1 · The Class Actions Law: purpose and the Second Schedule

By the end of this lesson you will be able to tell whether a grievance you share with many other people can become a class action at all, find out in the public registry whether someone has already filed one, and explain the debate about whether Israel's class-action system mainly protects consumers or mainly feeds lawyers. You will not file a class action in this module: in practice that is a lawyer's case. You will learn to read, use and, when needed, challenge one, because every adult in Israel is a member of dozens of classes and is bound by what is done in their name.

## Why it matters

You pay a bank, an insurer, a phone company, a supermarket and a local authority. When one of them overcharges ₪3 a month, no one sues for ₪3. The class action (תובענה ייצוגית) lets one person with a claim sue on behalf of everyone in the same position, without their authorisation, so that many small harms become one case worth bringing. [Lesson 11.1](../module-11/lesson-01.md) showed the economics: without aggregation, the expected cost of breaking a rule that hurts each person a little is close to zero.

The same tool binds you. If a settlement is approved in a case about your bank account, it ends your own claim on those facts unless you opted out (s. 24). Notices about these cases appear in newspapers, on company websites and in the registry, and most people ignore them. This module teaches you to read them and act.

Class actions are also a contested policy area in 2026: a government bill to reshape the Law has been in the Knesset since 2024, and in October 2026 a joint Knesset committee approved part of it for second and third reading (see "How it works in practice").

## The law

### Purpose

> מטרתו של חוק זה לקבוע כללים אחידים לענין הגשה וניהול של תובענות ייצוגיות, לשם שיפור ההגנה על זכויות, ובכך לקדם בפרט את אלה: (1) מימוש זכות הגישה לבית המשפט, לרבות לסוגי אוכלוסיה המתקשים לפנות לבית המשפט כיחידים; (2) אכיפת הדין והרתעה מפני הפרתו; (3) מתן סעד הולם לנפגעים מהפרת הדין; (4) ניהול יעיל, הוגן וממצה של תביעות.

*Gist:* The Law sets uniform rules for class actions in order to protect rights, in particular access to court (including for groups who find it hard to sue alone), enforcement and deterrence, proper relief for those harmed, and efficient, fair and complete handling of claims. [s. 1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_1)

Courts use these four purposes to interpret every other section. Notice that only one of them (relief) is about the members getting money; two (enforcement and efficiency) are about the system.

### What a class action is

> ”תובענה ייצוגית“ – תובענה המנוהלת בשם קבוצת בני אדם, שלא ייפו את כוחו של התובע המייצג לכך, ואשר מעוררת שאלות מהותיות של עובדה או משפט המשותפות לכלל חברי הקבוצה;

*Gist:* A class action is a claim conducted on behalf of a group of people who did not authorise the representative plaintiff, and which raises substantial questions of fact or law common to all members. [s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_2) "Class counsel" (בא כוח מייצג) is defined in the same section as a lawyer.

### Only listed claims

> לא תוגש תובענה ייצוגית אלא בתביעה כמפורט בתוספת השניה או בענין שנקבע בהוראת חוק מפורשת כי ניתן להגיש בו תובענה ייצוגית; על אף האמור, לא תוגש נגד רשות תובענה ייצוגית לפיצויים בגין נזק שנגרם על ידי צד שלישי, שעילתה הפעלה או אי הפעלה של סמכויות פיקוח, הסדרה או אכיפה של הרשות ביחס לאותו צד שלישי [...]

*Gist:* A class action may be filed only for a claim listed in the Second Schedule or where another statute expressly allows it; and no class action for damages lies against an authority for harm caused by a third party on the ground that the authority did or did not use its supervisory, regulatory or enforcement powers over that third party. [s. 3(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_3) Filing needs the court's certification (s. 3(ב)); [lesson 33.2](lesson-02.md) covers it.

This is a **closed list**. If your grievance does not fit an item, there is no class action, however many people share it.

### The Second Schedule in 2026

| Item | Claims that may be brought | Typical case |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Against a business (עוסק under the Consumer Protection Law) about a matter between it and a customer, whether or not they made a deal | Misleading label, hidden fee, unlawful price |
| 2 | Against an insurer, insurance agent or managing company (pension, provident fund) and a customer, member or insured | Unpaid interest on insurance payouts |
| 3, 3א–3ד | Against a bank; a regulated financial-services provider; a payment company; a financial-information licensee; a gmach (free-loan fund) | Unlawful bank fee |
| 4 | Under the Economic Competition Law | Cartel overcharge |
| 4א, 5 | Trading platforms; claims from holding, buying or selling a security or a fund unit | Misleading prospectus |
| 6 | Environmental nuisance against the person causing it | Factory emissions |
| 7 | Prohibition of Discrimination in Products, Services and Entry to Public Places Law 2000 | A venue refusing entry by origin |
| 8 | Discrimination at work (Equal Employment Opportunities Law; Equal Pay Law) | Systematic pay gap |
| 9 | Accessibility: Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law chapters ד, ה, ה1; accessibility provisions under the Planning and Building Law; TV subtitles and sign language | Inaccessible website or building |
| 10 | Labour-court claims under s. 24(א)(1), (1א) or (3) of the Labour Court Law, minimum wage, the right to sit at work, manpower contractors (not workers covered by a collective agreement to which their employer is party) | Unpaid overtime across a chain |
| 11 | Against an authority to restore sums unlawfully collected as tax, fee or other compulsory payment, and against the Israel Land Authority for sums unlawfully collected in its activity; such claims may be brought only under this item | Unlawful arnona or water charge |
| 12 | Against an advertiser under s. 30א of the Communications Law (spam) | Unsolicited marketing SMS |
| 13, 13א | Pension clearing system; pension advice and marketing licensees | Delayed transfer of pension money |
| 14 | Against a credit-rating company | Negligent rating |

Source: [Second Schedule](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#תוספת_2), checked 18 October 2026. Read the exact wording of the item before relying on it.

> ”רשות ציבורית“ – גוף המנוי בתוספת הראשונה;

*Gist:* The First Schedule lists the public bodies that may themselves apply for certification: the Commission for Equal Rights of Persons with Disabilities, the Nature and Parks Authority, and the Equal Employment Opportunities Commission. [s. 2 and First Schedule](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#תוספת_1)

### The registry

> מנהל בתי המשפט ינהל פנקס תובענות ייצוגיות [...] הפנקס וכן המסמכים שנמסרו למנהל בתי המשפט יחד עם ההודעות כאמור, יהיו פתוחים לעיון הציבור באתר האינטרנט של הנהלת בתי המשפט.

*Gist:* The Director of Courts keeps a Class Actions Registry in which applications, certification decisions, withdrawals, settlement applications, settlement approvals and published notices are recorded, and the registry and the documents are open to the public online. [s. 28(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_28) Before filing, an applicant must check the registry for a pending case raising the same or similar questions and disclose it (s. 5(א)(2); reg. 2(א)(13)).

## Leading cases

### ע״א 10085/08 תנובה נ׳ עזבון ראבי (4.12.2011)

Briefed in [lesson 29.6](../module-29/lesson-06.md). For this module the point is the Court's account of the tool: Hayut J. described the accumulation of small harms as a central feature of the class action and held that, where individual members cannot be identified, the court may award an estimated sum for the benefit of the class or the public under s. 20(ג). The award was spent on research grants and free milk for people in need. Why it matters: it is the Supreme Court's clearest statement that the class action exists for harms too small to sue for alone. [Versa (English)](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/tnuva-central-cooperative-v-raabi-estate)

### עע״ם 7752/12 אסל נ׳ מינהל מקרקעי ישראל (2.11.2014)

The Supreme Court asked whether the Israel Land Authority, when it charged permit fees contrary to its own Council's decisions, acted as a "business" under item 1 or as an "authority" under item 11. It held that the distinction between commercial and governmental activity is examined case by case, and that the dominant character of those decisions was commercial, so item 1 applied. Why it matters: the item you file under decides which protections the defendant gets (authorities get the 90-day cessation rule and the 24-month cap, ss. 9 and 21). The holding is summarised in the explanatory notes to the 2023 bill; we did not open the ruling itself. [Bill, explanatory notes](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/25/law/25_ls1_2558820.pdf)

### בג״ץ 3715/18 אברהם נ׳ שרת המשפטים (25.10.2022)

In reaction to Assel, the Minister of Justice amended item 11 by order in 2018 so that every restitution claim against the Israel Land Authority would go through item 11 with an authority's protections. The High Court of Justice voided the order as beyond the Minister's power under s. 30(2) (which allows the Minister to add to the Schedule, not to narrow it in this way), suspending the voidance for 12 months so that the Knesset could legislate. The Knesset then wrote the arrangement into item 11 by statute (amendment of 2024). Why it matters: it shows that the Second Schedule is a political compromise that the Knesset, not the Minister, controls. [Bill, explanatory notes](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/25/law/25_ls1_2558820.pdf) (ruling text not opened).

## How it works in practice

### Who actually files

The applicant must have a personal claim (s. 4(א)(1)), or be a listed public authority or a qualifying organisation (s. 4(א)(2)–(3); [lesson 33.5](lesson-05.md)). In practice the case is run by class counsel, who funds it and is paid only if the court awards fees ([lesson 33.4](lesson-04.md)). The court fee for an application is paid in two parts, the first on filing and the rest at the end (Court Fees Regulations reg. 7א); environmental, discrimination and disability claims are exempt (reg. 20(31)). Amounts: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### Where cases are filed

In the court with subject-matter and local jurisdiction, counting the **aggregate** claim of the whole class (s. 5(ב)(1)), so most consumer cases go to the district court and smaller ones to the magistrates' court; restitution claims against an authority go to the administrative affairs court (s. 5(ב)(2); [lesson 14.9](../module-14/lesson-09.md)); labour claims to the regional labour court (s. 2: "court" includes a labour court).

### The registry: how to search it (checked 18 October 2026)

1. Open Net HaMishpat: `https://www.court.gov.il/NGCS.Web.Site/HomePage.aspx`.
2. Click **פנקס תובענות ייצוגיות** in the home-page menu. (The direct address `.../SearchCase/RepresentativeRegistry.aspx` redirects to the home page if opened cold; go through the button.)
3. The page opens with the latest filings. A notice says that only the 300 documents filed in the most recent cases are shown and suggests refining the search.
4. Click **חיפוש מתקדם**. Useful fields: **שם משפחה/ גוף** (party name, for example the company), **קבוצה/ שאלה משפטית/ סעד מבוקש** (free text in the class definition, legal question or relief), **בית משפט**, **תאריך פתיחת תיק** (from–to) or year and month, **סטטוס התיק** (open/closed), **טווח סכום התביעה**.
5. Each result shows case number, name, opening date, court, the class definition, the legal question, the relief, the claimed sum, and a list of documents (for example בקשה לאישור תובענה ייצוגית, כתב תביעה, settlement applications, decisions) that you can open.
6. Cases filed before September 2014 are in an older archive on the Judiciary's old site (`https://elyon1.court.gov.il/heb/tovanot_yezugiyot/main.htm`, address from search results, not opened when checked), which cannot be filtered.

The Courts Administration's work directive 233/14 (in force from 15.12.2020) tells court registries to enter the amount, class, legal question, relief and summary from Form 3 when they open a class-action file, so that the registry updates automatically. [Directive 233/14](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/court90/he/פתיחת%20תובענה%20ייצוגית%20גביית%20אגרה%20ופרסומה%20בפנקס%20התובענות%20הייצוגיות.pdf) (⚠️ manual check: gov.il).

### The debate: consumer protection or "nuisance suits"?

State each side's strongest case before you decide.

- **For the tool as it is.** The Supreme Court in Tnuva treated aggregation of small harms as the heart of the class action and the reason it deters powerful defendants. Disability-rights organisations told the Knesset committee in October 2026 that class actions are a key enforcement tool for accessibility, not only a source of money, and that accessibility cases had already declined (reported by Davar, 15.10.2026). The Law's own purposes (s. 1) put access to court and enforcement first.
- **For reform.** The government's explanatory notes to Amendment 16 (July 2024), based on an inter-ministerial team report (April 2023), describe a "problem of meritless claims" (בעיית תביעות הסרק), many duplicate claims about technical regulatory breaches with little public harm, and many withdrawals; they report that only about 22% of judicial decisions ended in an outcome that benefited the class (settlement, cessation or a judgment for the class). Small-business organisations (for example Lahav's president, quoted by Davar) call many claims a means of economic pressure on owners who could easily fix the breach.
- **Where it stands.** On 15 October 2026 the joint Constitution and Economy committee approved for second and third reading two parts of Amendment 16: excluding businesses with annual turnover up to ₪9 million, and a duty to send a prior demand and allow 60 days to cure before filing for certain claims (spam, packaging weight, labelling, Hebrew consumer information; some accessibility claims against businesses up to ₪50 million). At the date of checking, the plenary had not voted (⚠️ check the Knesset site before relying on it). The rest of the bill (fee tables, organisations' standing, mediation) was not advanced.

## Worked example

Fatma Haddad, a teacher in Nazareth, notices that her mobile operator charges ₪4.90 a month for "content services" she never ordered. Her neighbour has the same charge.

1. **Is there a listed claim?** The operator is a business and Fatma is its customer: item 1 fits ("whether or not they made a deal" also covers people the operator wrongly treated as customers).
2. **Already filed?** In the registry she searches **שם משפחה/ גוף** for the operator's name, years 2024–2026. She finds two applications about content-service charges: one pending in the Tel Aviv district court since 2025, one closed by withdrawal.
3. **What it means for her.** She is already a potential member of the pending class (the class definition reads "all customers charged for content services without consent in the seven years before filing"). She does nothing yet: if the case is certified or settled, a notice will be published and she can decide then ([lessons 33.3](lesson-03.md) and 33.7).
4. **Her own money now.** She can still demand a refund directly and complain to the regulator ([lesson 12.4](../module-12/lesson-04.md)); a personal refund does not stop the class action.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A business, bank, insurer or authority you pay regularly, and one charge or practice you think is wrong (or the supplied case: Yonatan Biton of Ofakim, charged a "paper statement fee" by his credit-card company although he receives statements by email).
- Net HaMishpat in a browser; the Second Schedule open in another tab.
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. Write the grievance in one sentence: who did what to whom, and how often.
2. Pick the Second Schedule item that fits and copy its exact words. If none fits, write "no class action possible" and stop at step 6.
3. Check the s. 3(א) exception: is your complaint really that an authority failed to supervise someone else? If so, no class action lies against the authority.
4. Search the registry by the defendant's name and by a key word in the class definition. Record case number, court, opening date, status and class definition for each hit.
5. Open one application and read only its first pages: the class definition, the legal questions and the claimed amount. Are you inside the class?
6. Write three lines: the item, whether a case already exists, and whether you are in its class.

### Template

None. Use a plain note; [lesson 33.6](lesson-06.md) adds the decision worksheet.

### How you know it worked

- You named an item by number and quoted its words, or explained why none fits.
- You found at least one registry entry (or recorded a search that returned none, with the fields you used).
- You can say whether you are a member of the class as defined.

### Self-check

- [ ] I know the Second Schedule is a closed list.
- [ ] I know item 11 is the only route for restitution claims against an authority.
- [ ] I can open the registry through the Net HaMishpat home page and use the advanced search.
- [ ] I can state one argument for and one against the current class-action system, each with its source.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Partly. Reading the registry, checking whether you are a class member, opting out and objecting are things any member does in their own name (lessons 33.3, 33.7, 33.8). Filing an application for certification is, in practice, a lawyer's case: the Law builds the procedure around class counsel, defined as a lawyer (s. 2), whose identity the court must state when it certifies (s. 14(א)(2)), and whose adequacy is a condition of certification (s. 8(א)(3)). Acting for the class is representing others, which s. 20 of the Bar Association Law reserves to lawyers.

### Where and how

- Registry: Net HaMishpat home page → פנקס תובענות ייצוגיות (free, no login).
- If you want a case brought: write to a consumer organisation, the Israel Consumer Council (which may file even without showing that an individual would find it hard to, s. 4(א)(3)), or a lawyer who practises class actions; give them your documents.
- Free help: court information centres; Kol-Zchut on class actions (⚠️ manual check).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You want to be the representative plaintiff: you will sign an affidavit, may be cross-examined, and can be ordered to pay costs if the application fails.
- The defendant offers you a personal payment to drop a complaint that you have already filed as a class action (s. 16 requires the court's approval).
- The grievance is large for you personally (a personal-injury or significant financial loss): your own claim may be worth more than your share of a class award.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Assuming any shared grievance can be a class action | Wasted time; application struck out | Check the Second Schedule item | Start from the list, not from the anger |
| Filing a restitution claim against a municipality under item 1 | Wrong court and wrong rules | Refile under item 11 in the administrative affairs court | Item 11 is exclusive for such claims |
| Ignoring the registry | A duplicate case; your application may be transferred or struck out (s. 7) | Join the existing case as a member or ask to take part (s. 15) | Search the registry first (s. 5(א)(2)) |
| Treating a published notice as advertising | You are bound by a settlement you never read | Check whether the objection or opt-out period is still open | Read every notice that names a business you use |
| Presenting the debate as settled | One-sided analysis | Quote both the reform report and the opposing organisations | Attribute every claim to its source |

## Review

- A class action is brought for a group that did not authorise the plaintiff, on common questions; it needs the court's certification.
- Only claims in the Second Schedule or expressly allowed by statute qualify; item 1 (business and customer) is by far the most used; item 11 is the exclusive route against authorities for unlawful charges.
- The Class Actions Registry on Net HaMishpat is public; search it through the home-page button and the advanced search.
- The policy debate pits access and deterrence against meritless and duplicate claims; Amendment 16 was partly approved in committee in October 2026 and awaits the plenary.
