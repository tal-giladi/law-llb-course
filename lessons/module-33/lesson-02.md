# 33.2 · Certification: the requirements, the representative plaintiff and the lawyer

By the end of this lesson you will be able to read an application for certification the way a judge does: check who is applying and whether they may, test the four conditions of s. 8, spot the special defences that public bodies, banks and insurers can raise, and understand why the court, not the parties, controls withdrawal and payments to the applicant. Certification is the battlefield of Israeli class actions: most cases end before or at this stage, by withdrawal, settlement or dismissal.

## Why it matters

A class action exists only once the court certifies it. Until then there is an application (בקשה לאישור) by an applicant (מבקש) whose lawyer seeks the right to represent thousands of people who have never met either of them. The certification decision decides who is in the class, which questions will be tried and who controls the case. For a class member, the stage matters because withdrawals, settlements reached before certification and the definition of the class all affect your rights, and because you may ask to take part in the hearing (s. 15).

## The law

### Who may apply

> אלה רשאים להגיש לבית המשפט בקשה לאישור תובענה ייצוגית כמפורט להלן: (1) אדם שיש לו עילה בתביעה או בענין כאמור בסעיף 3(א), המעוררת שאלות מהותיות של עובדה או משפט המשותפות לכלל החברים הנמנים עם קבוצת בני אדם – בשם אותה קבוצה; (2) רשות ציבורית [...]; (3) ארגון [...] ובלבד שבית המשפט שוכנע כי, בנסיבות הענין, קיים קושי להגיש את הבקשה בידי אדם כאמור בפסקה (1) [...]

*Gist:* An individual with a personal claim of a permitted kind may apply on behalf of the class; so may a listed public authority within its public purposes, and an organisation within its purposes if the court is persuaded that it would be hard for an individual to apply (the Israel Consumer Council need not show this). [s. 4(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_4) Where damage is an element of the claim, an individual need only show that he was prima facie harmed; an authority or organisation need only show a reasonable possibility that the class was harmed (s. 4(ב)).

An "organisation" (ארגון) must have operated regularly for at least a year for a public purpose, use its assets only for that purpose, and not act for or in connection with a political party (s. 2).

### The application

The application is in writing with the draft claim attached (s. 5(א)(1)), filed in the court with jurisdiction over the aggregate claim (s. 5(ב)(1)). Regulation 2(א) lists what it must set out, including the Schedule item, the applicant's personal cause, the class definition and estimated size, the estimated aggregate sum and how it was calculated, why the applicant and counsel will represent the class adequately and in good faith, the common questions, why a class action is the efficient and fair way, the proposed fee and award, and any similar case found in the registry. An affidavit supports the facts (reg. 2(ב)); the respondent answers with an affidavit within ninety days (reg. 2(ג)); the applicant may reply (reg. 2(ד)); page limits apply (reg. 2(ז)). The court may decide on the papers or after cross-examination of the affiants (reg. 2(ה)), may order disclosure of documents relevant to certification once the applicant has laid a preliminary evidential basis (reg. 4(ב)), and will not require security for the respondent's costs except for special reasons (reg. 2(ו)). The applicant notifies the Director of Courts for the registry (s. 6) and, by Schedule item, the relevant regulator (reg. 3).

### The four conditions

> בית המשפט רשאי לאשר תובענה ייצוגית, אם מצא שהתקיימו כל אלה: (1) התובענה מעוררת שאלות מהותיות של עובדה או משפט המשותפות לכלל חברי הקבוצה, ויש אפשרות סבירה שהן יוכרעו בתובענה לטובת הקבוצה; (2) תובענה ייצוגית היא הדרך היעילה וההוגנת להכרעה במחלוקת בנסיבות הענין; (3) קיים יסוד סביר להניח כי ענינם של כלל חברי הקבוצה ייוצג וינוהל בדרך הולמת; הנתבע לא רשאי לערער או לבקש לערער על החלטה בענין זה; (4) קיים יסוד סביר להניח כי ענינם של כלל חברי הקבוצה ייוצג וינוהל בתום לב.

*Gist:* The court may certify only if (1) there are substantial common questions with a reasonable possibility of being decided for the class, (2) a class action is the efficient and fair way to decide the dispute, (3) there is reasonable ground to believe the class will be represented adequately (the defendant cannot appeal on this point), and (4) in good faith. [s. 8(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_8)

How courts apply them:

- **(1) Commonality and merit.** Not every question must be common; the core ones must be. "Reasonable possibility" is a preliminary merits test, higher than "not frivolous" and lower than proof.
- **(2) Efficiency and fairness.** Here the court weighs whether individual issues (reliance, damage) will swamp the common ones, the size of individual claims, the benefit to the class against the cost, and whether the harm is trivial (Zeligman, below).
- **(3)–(4) Representation.** Is the applicant genuine, informed and free of conflicts? Is counsel competent and loyal? A defect here does not end the case: the court may certify and add or replace the representative or counsel (s. 8(ג)(1)); if the case is good but the applicant lacks standing under s. 4, the court certifies and replaces him (s. 8(ג)(2)).

### Special considerations for public bodies and the financial system

> הוגשה בקשה לאישור נגד המדינה, רשות מרשויותיה, רשות מקומית או תאגיד שהוקם על פי דין ושוכנע בית המשפט כי עצם ניהול ההליך כתובענה ייצוגית צפוי לגרום נזק חמור לציבור הנזקק לשירותיו של הנתבע או לציבור בכללותו לעומת התועלת הצפויה [...] רשאי בית המשפט להתחשב בכך בבואו להחליט אם לאשר תובענה ייצוגית;

*Gist:* Against the State, an authority, a local authority or a statutory corporation, the court may take into account that the very conduct of a class action is expected to cause serious harm to the public relative to its expected benefit, if certification with changes cannot prevent it. [s. 8(ב)(1)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_8) A similar consideration applies to essential-service providers, banks, the stock exchange, clearing houses and insurers where the harm would come from damage to the defendant's financial stability (s. 8(ב)(2)).

For restitution claims against an authority (item 11), the court waits ninety days from filing, and will not certify if the authority announced that it would stop the charge and proved it stopped by that date, though it may still award a fee and an award (s. 9).

### Competing applications, the class, and the decision

- A later application raising the same or similar questions may be transferred to the court hearing the earlier one; if the class is the same or similar, it must be; the court may join them, strike one, or join or replace applicants and counsel (s. 7).
- The decision defines the class (people whose cause arose after certification are excluded), may create sub-classes, and states the representative, counsel, causes, common questions and relief (ss. 10, 14). The court may certify with any changes (s. 13). The draft claim then becomes the statement of claim and the defendant files a defence within forty-five days (reg. 5(א)).
- A decision to certify can be appealed only with leave (s. 8(ד)). A refusal ends the class case; the applicant may sue personally in a separate claim (reg. 9).
- Certification stops limitation for all class members from the filing date (s. 26(א)); after a refusal or a narrowed class, members have at least a year from the final decision (s. 26(ב)–(ג)).

### Withdrawal and side payments

> מבקש, תובע מייצג או בא כוח מייצג, לא יסתלק מבקשה לאישור או מתובענה ייצוגית, אלא באישור בית המשפט, וכן לא יקבל, במישרין או בעקיפין, טובת הנאה מהנתבע או מאדם אחר בקשר להסתלקותו כאמור, אלא באישור בית המשפט [...]

*Gist:* No applicant, representative or counsel may withdraw, or take any benefit from the defendant in connection with withdrawing, without the court's approval; the court weighs whether the application showed a prima facie cause and what benefit it brought to the class. [s. 16(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_16) The request is supported by an affidavit disclosing every material detail and every benefit received (s. 16(ב); reg. 11(א)). After certification the court may publish the withdrawal so that another member can take over within a set period (s. 16(ד)); members, organisations and the Attorney General may file a position on the request (reg. 11(ג)). A withdrawal binds only the applicant, not the class.

> במילוי תפקידיו, יפעל בא כוח מייצג בנאמנות ובמסירות לטובת הקבוצה [...] כאילו היתה שולחתו [...]

*Gist:* Class counsel must act loyally and devotedly for the class as if it were his client. [s. 17](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_17)

## Leading cases

### דנ״מ 5519/15 יונס נ׳ מי הגליל תאגיד המים והביוב האזורי (17.12.2019)

The panel in the original appeal (עע״ם 2978/13, 2015) had required a prior demand to the authority before an item-11 application. In the further hearing the majority held that current law imposes no general duty to send a prior demand, and no basis to award a fee for a demand that was answered; but failing to send one may, in particular circumstances, show bad faith (condition (4)). Why it matters: it is why Amendment 16 proposes a statutory prior-demand duty for some claims, approved in committee in October 2026 (lesson 33.1). Holding as summarised in the explanatory notes to the 2024 bill; ruling text not opened. [Bill PDF](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/25/law/25_ls1_4708827.pdf)

### דנ״א 4960/18 זליגמן נ׳ הפניקס חברה לביטוח (4.7.2021)

The further hearing held that the de minimis defence (Civil Wrongs Ordinance and similar rules) does not apply as such in class actions, because the Law already lets the court refuse certification when a class action is not the efficient and fair way (s. 8(א)(2)). Why it matters: a tiny harm per member is not a defence in itself, but triviality is weighed inside condition (2). The government's notes cite trivial-claim examples (a glue stick without instructions, "100% chicken" schnitzel with breadcrumbs) to argue for an earlier filter; others answer that such cases are already dismissed under s. 8(א)(2). Summarised in the 2024 bill notes; ruling text not opened. [Bill PDF](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/25/law/25_ls1_4708827.pdf)

### Key text: the four conditions read together (s. 8)

No single free Supreme Court text sets out the certification test better than the section itself: read s. 8(א) with s. 4(ב) (prima facie damage), reg. 2(א)(5)–(10) (what the applicant must show) and reg. 4(ב) (disclosure only after a preliminary evidential basis). [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_8)

## How it works in practice

- **Time.** Applications typically take one to several years to a certification decision; many end earlier by withdrawal or settlement. Check a case's progress in the registry.
- **Costs.** The applicant pays the first part of the court fee ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)); if the application is dismissed, he pays the rest and may be ordered to pay the respondent's costs. Class counsel usually carries these risks.
- **Taking part.** A class member, a public authority or an organisation may ask to take part in the hearing of the application if needed for an efficient and fair hearing or to protect members' interests (s. 15(א), (ג)). This is the member's voice before a settlement exists.
- **Withdrawal notices.** A request to withdraw after certification is published, and members may comment within the period in reg. 11(ג). Watch for a withdrawal that gives the applicant and counsel money while the class gets "a fix for the future" only.

## Worked example

Rivka Adler of Bnei Brak finds that a supermarket chain sold "family packs" of rice at a higher price per kilo than single packs while labelling them "money saving". Her lawyer files an application under item 1 for "all buyers of family packs in the last seven years".

1. **Standing (s. 4).** Rivka bought family packs; her receipts show prima facie damage (s. 4(ב)(1)).
2. **Condition (1).** The common questions: was the label misleading (Consumer Protection Law s. 2), and did buyers pay more per kilo? Reasonable possibility: the shelf prices are documented.
3. **Condition (2).** The chain argues each buyer's reliance differs. The court notes that Tnuva allows group-level causation in consumer misleading and that the per-buyer loss is small but real: a class action is efficient.
4. **Conditions (3)–(4).** Rivka sent the chain a prior demand that was ignored; no sign of bad faith. Counsel has run similar cases.
5. **Result.** The court certifies with changes (s. 13): the class is limited to branches where the label appeared, and the period ends on the certification date (s. 10(א)). Rivka checks the registry for the published notice and the opt-out period (s. 11).

## Do it yourself

### You need

- One real application for certification from the registry (lesson 33.1), preferably against a business you use, or one the registry shows on its first page.
- Sections 4, 8 and 16 and reg. 2 open.
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. Note the case number, court, Schedule item claimed and the class definition.
2. **Standing:** who is the applicant (individual, authority, organisation)? What personal cause and prima facie damage does he show?
3. **Condition (1):** list the common questions in the application. Mark each as "law" or "fact". Write one sentence on whether there is a reasonable possibility of success.
4. **Condition (2):** list the individual issues that might defeat efficiency (reliance, varying damage, identification of members). Is any harm trivial?
5. **Conditions (3)–(4):** anything suggesting inadequate or bad-faith representation (no prior demand where one was natural, a copy of another firm's application, conflicts)?
6. **Special defences:** is the respondent a public body, bank, insurer or essential-service provider (s. 8(ב))?
7. Write a five-line prediction: certify, certify with changes (what changes), or dismiss.

### Template

None. Write your analysis as a short memo using the headings of s. 8(א); the [research memo](../../templates/research-memo.md) format works.

### How you know it worked

- Every conclusion cites a paragraph of s. 4 or s. 8 and a passage of the application.
- You identified at least one individual issue and explained why it does or does not defeat efficiency.
- Your prediction names a concrete change to the class definition if you chose "with changes".

### Self-check

- [ ] I can state the four conditions from memory.
- [ ] I know that a defendant cannot appeal on adequacy of representation, and that certification is appealable only with leave.
- [ ] I know that a withdrawal and any payment to the applicant need the court's approval.
- [ ] I know when limitation stops for class members.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Partly. As a class member you may ask to take part in the certification hearing (s. 15) and comment on a withdrawal request (reg. 11(ג)) in your own name. Bringing the application and acting as representative is, in practice, done with class counsel (s. 2 defines class counsel as a lawyer; s. 8(א)(3)). If you file alone, expect the court to examine adequacy of representation closely; this is an Unverified area with no clear free ruling.

### Where and how

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- A request to take part: a written motion in the case file ([written motion template](../../templates/written-motion.md)), stating why your participation is needed (s. 15(א)); file through Net HaMishpat or the court registry.
- A position on a withdrawal: within the period in reg. 11(ג) from service or publication ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- Free help: court information centres; consumer organisations; the Israel Consumer Council.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You are considering becoming the applicant or replacing a withdrawing one (s. 16(ד)(4)).
- The defendant contacts you personally with an offer connected to a pending application.
- You are asked to sign an affidavit for an application.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Applicant has no personal cause | Replaced, or application dismissed (s. 8(ג)(2)) | Ask the court to substitute a member with a cause | Check s. 4(א)(1) before filing |
| Class defined to include people with no claim | Certification with narrowing, or refusal | Propose a narrower definition (s. 13) | Define the class by the conduct and the harm |
| Withdrawal with a quiet payment | The payment is unlawful without approval (s. 16(א)) | Disclose and seek approval | Every benefit goes in the affidavit |
| Ignoring the s. 8(ב) defence for a bank or authority | Surprise refusal | Propose changes that reduce the public harm | Address s. 8(ב) in the application |
| Members assume a refusal ends their own claim | They miss their personal claim | Sue individually within the extended period (s. 26(ב)) | Diary one year from the final decision |

## Review

- Individuals with a personal permitted claim, three listed public authorities and qualifying organisations may apply (s. 4).
- Certification requires common substantial questions with a reasonable possibility of success, efficiency and fairness, and adequate good-faith representation (s. 8(א)); public bodies and the financial system get extra weighing (s. 8(ב)).
- Defects in representation can be cured by substitution (s. 8(ג)); certification is appealable only with leave (s. 8(ד)).
- No withdrawal or side benefit without court approval (s. 16); class counsel owes the class loyalty (s. 17); limitation stops on filing (s. 26).
