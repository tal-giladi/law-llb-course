# 33.6 · Choosing the tool: class action, small claim or regulator complaint

By the end of this lesson you will be able to look at a grievance shared with many others and choose the route that best serves you and, where you care about it, the others: your own small claim for your own money (with exemplary damages where a statute allows), a complaint that sets a regulator in motion, or a class action that you start through a lawyer or an organisation, join, or simply stay inside. The routes are not exclusive, and the order in which you use them matters.

## Why it matters

People choose badly in both directions. Some give up a strong ₪3,000 small claim because "there's a class action", and then receive a ₪5 voucher. Others leave a class action to sue alone for ₪40 and spend a day in court. Some file a regulator complaint and wait for money that regulators rarely order. The right choice depends on the size of your own loss, your evidence, the remedies each forum can give, how long you can wait, and whether a group remedy already exists.

## The law

### Your own claim while a class action is pending

> אישר בית המשפט תובענה ייצוגית לפי הוראות סעיף 8, יראו, לענין התיישנות, את כל מי שנמנה עם הקבוצה שהגדיר בית המשפט לפי הוראות סעיף 10 כאילו הגיש תובענה במועד שבו הוגשה הבקשה לאישור.

*Gist:* Once a class action is certified, every member of the class is treated, for limitation, as if he had sued on the date the application was filed. [s. 26(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_26) If certification is refused or struck out, a member's own limitation period does not end before one year from the decision becoming final, provided it had not expired when the application was filed (s. 26(ב)); the same applies to people left out of a narrowed class (s. 26(ג)) and to people who opt out or leave a settlement, counting a year from their notice (s. 26(ד)).

So: a pending application protects you if it is certified, and gives you at least a year after a refusal. It does not stop you from suing yourself now.

### What binds you

A class judgment or approved settlement binds every member who did not leave (s. 24). The exits are s. 11 after certification, s. 18(ו) from a settlement, and an exit condition under s. 19(ד) ([lesson 33.3](lesson-03.md)). If you sued and won personally, you hold a judgment on your own claim; tell class counsel and the court, so that you are not paid twice.

### What each forum can award

> בית המשפט לא יפסוק בתובענה ייצוגית פיצויים לדוגמה, וכן לא יפסוק פיצויים בלא הוכחת נזק, למעט בתביעה כמפורט בפרט 9 בתוספת השניה [...]

*Gist:* A class action cannot award exemplary damages or damages without proof of damage, except in accessibility claims; non-pecuniary damage is allowed. [s. 20(ה)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות#סעיף_20)

In your own claim, the Consumer Protection Law allows exemplary damages without proof of damage for listed breaches, after a written request ([lesson 29.9](../module-29/lesson-09.md)), and the Communications Law does the same for spam. Small claims are open to an individual up to the ceiling in the Courts Law s. 60, with lawyers only by leave (s. 63) ([lesson 08.3](../module-08/lesson-03.md)); ceiling: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

Regulators (the Consumer Protection and Fair Trade Authority, the Banking Supervisor, the Capital Market Authority, the Ministry of Communications) can investigate, impose financial sanctions and order changes; most do not order compensation to you ([lesson 12.4](../module-12/lesson-04.md), [lesson 29.6](../module-29/lesson-06.md)). A regulator's finding does not extend limitation.

## Leading cases

### דנ״א 5712/01 ברזני נ׳ בזק, פ״ד נז(6) 385 (2003)

Briefed in [lesson 20.7](../module-20/lesson-07.md): a consumer suing personally for misleading advertising must show that he himself was misled and harmed. Why it matters here: in your own claim, an ad you never saw gives no damages, but statutory exemplary damages may, where the breach is on the s. 31א list. [Versa](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/barazani-v-bezeq)

### ע״א 10085/08 תנובה נ׳ עזבון ראבי (4.12.2011)

Briefed in lesson 29.6: in a class action, causation can be established at the group level, and harm to autonomy is compensable. Why it matters here: when your individual causation is weak (you cannot show you relied on the label), the class action may succeed where your small claim would fail. Read Barazani and Tnuva together when choosing. [Versa](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/tnuva-central-cooperative-v-raabi-estate)

## How it works in practice

### The comparison

| | Your own small claim | Regulator complaint | Class action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who acts | You, without a lawyer | You | A representative with class counsel (or an organisation) |
| Cost to you | Court fee ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)) | Free | Nothing if you are a member; risk and effort if you are the applicant |
| Time | Months | Months to years; you may never hear the result | Years |
| What you get | Your loss; exemplary damages where a statute allows | Usually no money; a change in practice | Your share, a credit, or nothing personally (public relief) |
| Exemplary damages | Yes, under the Consumer Protection Law s. 31א or Communications Law s. 30א | No | No (except accessibility, item 9) |
| Effect on others | None, except deterrence | Can change the practice for all | Binds and compensates the class |
| Best when | Your loss is meaningful and your evidence is personal | You want enforcement, or the business ignores you | Many small identical losses, or group causation |

### Order of moves

1. **Demand first.** Write to the business ([lesson 12.3](../module-12/lesson-03.md)). Keep proof. Amendment 16, if enacted, will make a prior demand a condition for some class actions (approved in committee 15.10.2026, ⚠️ plenary pending).
2. **Check the registry** for a pending class action (lesson 33.1).
3. **Decide your own claim.** Use the [should-you-sue worksheet](../../templates/should-you-sue-worksheet.md) from [lesson 11.5](../module-11/lesson-05.md) with the extra questions below.
4. **Complain to the regulator** in parallel when the practice harms many; attach your demand.
5. **Watch for notices** if a class action exists; decide at the settlement stage (lessons 33.7, 33.8).

### Extra questions for the worksheet

- Is there a pending application or certified class action covering me? Am I inside the class definition and period?
- If I sue myself, what can I get that the class cannot (exemplary damages, full personal loss)?
- If I stay in the class, what is the realistic value of my share, and when?
- Is my individual causation strong (I saw the ad, I have the receipt) or weak (group causation only)?
- Would a small claim against a repeat player help others (a reasoned judgment) or only me?

## Worked example

Moshe Dahan of Kiryat Shmona received 27 marketing SMS messages from a furniture chain after he had refused consent. A registry search shows a pending application against the chain under item 12 (spam), filed in 2025, class: "all recipients of SMS without consent in the seven years before filing".

1. **Own claim.** The Communications Law s. 30א allows statutory damages per message without proof of damage in a personal claim; 27 messages make a meaningful small claim (amount per message: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
2. **Class value.** If the class action settles, members typically receive a small credit or nothing personally; exemplary damages are not available in the class action (s. 20(ה)).
3. **Interaction.** Suing now does not need an exit, because the application is not yet certified; if it is certified while his claim is pending, he can opt out under s. 11 to avoid any overlap.
4. **Regulator.** He also reports the chain to the Ministry of Communications' spam enforcement, which may impose sanctions.
5. **Decision.** Small claim now, with the messages as exhibits; opt out if the class action is certified before his judgment; complaint in parallel.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A consumer, bank, insurance, employment or municipal dispute of your own, or the supplied one: Sarit Ben-Ami of Haifa, whose bank charged a ₪15 monthly "account management" fee for 26 months although she qualified for a fee-exempt basic track.
- The [should-you-sue worksheet](../../templates/should-you-sue-worksheet.md); the registry; current numbers.
- 60 minutes.

### Steps

1. State your loss in shekels with documents, and the period.
2. Search the registry for a class action covering your issue; record case number, class definition, period and stage.
3. List what each route can give you (own claim, regulator, class) using the comparison table.
4. Fill in the worksheet's expected-value section for your own claim, adding exemplary damages only if a statute allows them for this breach.
5. Estimate your realistic share from the class action: zero, a credit, or a cash share; note when.
6. Note limitation: when does your own claim expire, and does a pending application protect you (s. 26)?
7. Write the decision in five lines: which routes, in which order, and what would change your mind (for example, certification of the class action).

### Template

[Should-you-sue worksheet](../../templates/should-you-sue-worksheet.md) (add a section "class action status" with the questions above); [regulator complaint](../../templates/regulator-complaint.md).

### How you know it worked

- Your decision compares at least two routes with numbers.
- You checked the registry and wrote what you found.
- You know your limitation date and how s. 26 affects it.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes for the small claim, the demand, the regulator complaint, opting out and objecting. A class action as applicant needs class counsel in practice (lesson 33.1). A company or an organisation suing in small claims has its own rules ([can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md)).

### Where and how

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- Small claim: the magistrates' court sitting as small-claims court, online or at the registry ([lesson 08.3](../module-08/lesson-03.md)); fee and ceiling in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Regulator complaint: the regulator's online form ([lesson 12.4](../module-12/lesson-04.md)).
- Opt-out: Form 1 model, within the period in the published notice.
- Free help: court information centres, Legal Aid's consumer service where eligible, the Israel Consumer Council.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- Your own loss exceeds the small-claims ceiling.
- The class action's settlement would release a claim of yours that is large (an injury, a significant financial loss).
- You are offered payment to withdraw a small claim that you described publicly as affecting many customers.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dropping a strong personal claim because "there's a class action" | You get a voucher instead of your loss | Sue within limitation; opt out if needed | Compare values first |
| Suing personally and also taking the class benefit | Double recovery; credibility damage | Tell the court and class counsel | Track both cases |
| Expecting the regulator to refund you | Long wait, no money | Sue yourself within limitation | Treat the complaint as enforcement, not compensation |
| Opting out with a tiny claim | Lose the class benefit, never sue | Ask to withdraw the opt-out before approval | Opt out only for a claim you will pursue |
| Forgetting limitation after a refused certification | Claim time-barred | Act within the year in s. 26(ב) | Diary the date the refusal becomes final |

## Review

- Your own small claim gives you your loss and, where a statute allows, exemplary damages; a class action cannot give exemplary damages except for accessibility.
- A pending application does not stop you suing; certification stops limitation for members; refusals and exits give at least a year (s. 26).
- Regulators enforce; they rarely compensate.
- Order of moves: demand, registry, decide your own claim, complain to the regulator, decide at the settlement stage.
