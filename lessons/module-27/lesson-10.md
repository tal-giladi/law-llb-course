# 27.10 · Practice lab: an arnona objection and appeal

In this lab you audit a real arnona bill (yours, or the supplied case), write an objection to the arnona director on the grounds the law allows, and then prepare the appeal to the appeals committee as if the director had refused. If your bill is wrong and you are within the 90 days, you may file the objection for real. It is one of the safest and most useful legal steps a resident can take alone.

## Why it matters

The objection is a short statutory procedure with a built-in advantage for the resident: if the director does not answer within 60 days, the objection is deemed accepted (s. 4(ב)). The appeals committee is made up of residents eligible for the council, not judges, and decides mostly on documents. Most arnona errors (area, use, holder) are factual, and a resident with a tape measure, photographs and the arnona order can prove them. The cost of missing the deadline, on the other hand, is that the charge becomes final for that year.

## The law

The objection and its grounds (s. 3), the director's answer and deemed acceptance (s. 4), the appeals committee (s. 5), the appeal and administrative appeal (s. 6) and the duty to state rights on the bill (s. 7א) were introduced in lessons [01.7](../module-01/lesson-07.md), [14.10](../module-14/lesson-10.md) and [27.8](lesson-08.md). Two more texts govern this lab:

> הרואה עצמו מקופח בתשובת מנהל הארנונה על השגתו רשאי, תוך שלושים יום מיום שנמסרה לו התשובה, לערור עליה לפני ועדת ערר.

*Gist:* Appeal to the appeals committee within 30 days of receiving the director's answer. [s. 6(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הרשויות_המקומיות_%28ערר_על_קביעת_ארנונה_כללית%29#סעיף_6)

> בכתב הערר יצויינו – שם העורר, משלח ידו, מענו והמען להמצאת מסמכים; פרטי הנכס שבשלו נדרש התשלום; התאריך שבו נמסרה לעורר תשובת המנהל על השגתו; הנימוקים עליהם מבסס העורר את עררו; האם העורר מבקש להתייצב לפני הועדה כדי להשמיע טענותיו.

*Gist:* The notice of appeal must contain these five items; it is filed in four copies, signed, by hand or registered mail; the director answers within 30 days; hearings are public as the council's procedure says. [Procedure Regulations regs. 2–6, 11א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הרשויות_המקומיות_%28ערר_על_קביעת_ארנונה_כללית%29_%28סדרי_דין_בוועדת_ערר%29)

From the committee, either side may bring an administrative appeal to the Administrative Affairs Court (s. 6(ב)); since no special period is set, the general period of 60 days applies (Administrative Courts Procedure Regulations reg. 23(ב)). (see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

## Leading cases

None new. Use s. 3(ב) of the Arnona Appeals Law (no illegality claims in this route; lesson 27.8) and רע״א 187/05 (limitation of old arnona debts; lesson 27.8) where they fit. In the appeal, cite the clause of your own city's arnona order; it is the main legal text the committee applies.

## How it works in practice

### Timeline

| Day | Step |
|---|---|
| 0 | You receive the payment notice (keep the envelope or the email) |
| By 90 | Objection to the director (s. 3(א)) |
| Objection + 60 | Director's answer, or deemed acceptance (s. 4) unless extended by up to 30 days |
| Answer + 30 | Appeal to the appeals committee (s. 6(א)) |
| Decision + 60 | Administrative appeal (reg. 23(ב)) |

### Evidence that wins

- **Area:** a licensed surveyor's measurement under the method of your city's arnona order; the permit plans; the purchase contract's appendix.
- **Use:** photos, a short affidavit, the business licence or its absence, the employer's letter for remote work.
- **Holder:** the s. 325 notice, the lease with the new tenant's details, the sale contract and handover protocol.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- Your arnona payment notice for 2026 (or the supplied case), your city's 2026 arnona order (⚠️ manual check on the city's website), a tape measure or a surveyor's report, photos, and any lease or sale documents.
- The draft template [arnona objection and appeal](../../templates/arnona-objection-and-appeal.md).
- About two and a half hours.

### The supplied case

Samer Halabi lives in Daliyat al-Karmel with his wife and two children. On 5 August 2026 he received the 2026 arnona payment notice for his house:

- residential, 148 m², with the 2025 measurement unchanged;
- a separate ground-floor unit of 30 m² classified as "commerce and offices", at a rate four times the residential rate.

Facts: a licensed surveyor measured the house at 131 m² by the method in the local council's order; the difference is an open, uncovered balcony that the council counted as built area. The ground-floor unit was a grocery until December 2025; since January 2026 it has been empty, then used only for household storage. Samer gave no notice of the change of use. His father, who lives with him, receives an old-age pension and income support.

### Steps

1. **Audit (30 min).** Copy the six fields of the bill (lesson 27.8). Compare area, use, type, zone and holder with your evidence.
2. **Deadline (5 min).** Compute 90 days from receipt of the notice. Diarise 14 days earlier as your own target.
3. **Grounds (20 min).** For each problem decide: s. 3(א)(1) zone, (2) type, size or use, (3) not the holder; or outside the route (discount, illegality).
4. **Evidence (30 min).** Collect the measurement, photos, documents; write a short factual statement.
5. **Objection (30 min).** Fill in Part A of the template; one ground per section; ask for the measurement sheet and a joint measurement.
6. **Discount (10 min).** If a discount fits, prepare the separate application (Form 1, reg. 4).
7. **File (5 min).** File online or by email and registered mail; keep proof of the date.
8. **Answer (scenario, 10 min).** Assume the director accepts the area claim but rejects the use claim, saying "no notice of change of use was given". Decide: appeal or accept.
9. **Appeal (20 min).** Fill in Part B: the five items of reg. 2, your answer to the director's reasons, the remedy and whether you want to appear.
10. **Next step (5 min).** Write what you would do after an adverse committee decision, and whether the amount justifies a court appeal.

### How you know it worked

- Your objection was (or would be) filed within 90 days, on the right grounds, with evidence.
- The discount request, if any, is separate.
- Your appeal contains the five items of reg. 2 and answers each reason of the director.
- Your diary has the 60-day, 30-day and 60-day dates.

<details><summary>Model answer for the supplied case</summary>

**Deadline.** Notice received 5 August 2026: objection by 3 November 2026 (90 days; s. 3(א)). Target: 20 October.

**Ground 1: size (s. 3(א)(2)).** The house is 131 m² by the method in the council's order; the open, uncovered balcony is not built area under the order (cite the clause). Evidence: the surveyor's report and photos. Remedy: 131 m² from 1 January 2026, and a refund of the difference.

**Ground 2: use (s. 3(א)(2)).** Since January 2026 the ground-floor unit is not a business; it is empty and then household storage. Evidence: photos, the closure notice to the VAT authority or the end of the business licence, a short affidavit. Remedy: classify it as residential (or as the order's storage classification for homes) from January 2026. The council's answer that no notice was given is not decisive for classification: arnona follows actual use. Samer should, however, now notify the council in writing to prevent future disputes.

**Outside the route.** The father's income-support status may give a discount, but it is given to the holder of the property (reg. 17) and up to 100 m² (reg. 2(א)(1)(ב)); Samer, not his father, is the holder, so the father's status helps only if the father is registered as the holder, or through the income test for the household (reg. 2(א)(8)). This is a discount application, not an objection.

**After the director's answer.** If the director does not answer within 60 days of the objection and the appeals committee did not extend the period, the objection is deemed accepted (s. 4(ב)): Samer writes to the director asking for a corrected bill. If the director rejects the use claim, Samer appeals within 30 days of receiving the answer, in four copies, stating name, occupation, address, the property, the date he received the answer, his reasons, and that he wishes to appear (regs. 2–3).

**Court.** The yearly difference is a few thousand shekels; an administrative appeal within 60 days is a court proceeding with costs risk. Samer would go to court only if the committee's decision rests on a legal error that will repeat every year (for example a misreading of the order's measurement clause), and after advice.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. The objection and the appeal are routinely filed by holders themselves; the appeals committee does not require a lawyer ([can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md)). An administrative appeal to the court can also be filed in person, but it is a court proceeding with costs risk. Paid non-lawyer "arnona consultants" may not represent you (Bar Association Law s. 20).

### Where and how

- Objection: the arnona director, at the address and in the manner stated on the bill (s. 7א).
- Appeal: the authority's arnona appeals committee (secretariat details on the authority's website; ⚠️ manual check).
- Free help: Kol-Zchut on arnona (⚠️ manual check), the authority's public-complaints officer, Legal Aid for people eligible, [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The property is a business with large sums over several years.
- The authority claims a large debt, attaches accounts or sues.
- You want to bring an administrative appeal or challenge the arnona order itself.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Objection on day 95 | Out of time; charge final for the year | Ask for an extension with reasons; object next year | Diarise from receipt of the notice |
| Appeal without the date the answer was received | Defective notice | Correct quickly | Use the five items of reg. 2 |
| Waiting for an answer after 60 days | Deemed acceptance not used | Write to the director to implement it | Diarise day 60 |
| Stopping all payment during the objection | Arrears, interest, collection | Pay the undisputed part | Pay what you do not dispute |
| Mixing a discount request into the appeal | Committee lacks power | Apply for the discount separately | Separate the routes |

## Review

- Audit the bill, then object to the director within 90 days on the s. 3(א) grounds, with evidence.
- No answer in 60 days (unless extended) means the objection is accepted.
- Appeal to the appeals committee within 30 days with the five items of reg. 2, in four copies.
- Administrative appeal to the court within 60 days, only where the stakes or a repeating legal error justify it.
- Discounts and illegality claims run on separate routes.
