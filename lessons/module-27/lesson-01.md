# 27.1 · The planning institutions under the Planning and Building Law

By the end of this lesson you will be able to name, for your own home, every body that decides what may be built around it: the National Planning Council, the district committee, the local (or regional) committee and its licensing authority, and the appeals committees that review them. You will also understand why the law spreads planning power across these bodies and why that matters when you object, appeal or apply for a permit.

## Why it matters

Every planning dispute starts with the question "who decides?". A neighbour's extension, a new tower on the corner, a road through the orchard, a fine for an enclosed balcony: each belongs to a different body, with a different procedure, deadline and appeal. Filing an objection with the wrong committee, or appealing to a court when the law sends you to an appeals committee, loses time that the short planning deadlines do not allow.

The Planning and Building Law 1965 (חוק התכנון והבניה) built a hierarchy of institutions (מוסדות תכנון) that mirrors the hierarchy of plans (lesson 27.2). The upper levels are dominated by government ministries; the local level is, in most cities, the elected city council itself. That design reflects a tension that runs through the whole subject: local democracy and local knowledge on one side, national interests and protection against local pressure on the other. The Supreme Court has long described the risk that a local council sitting as planning committee may give in to pressure; Parliament has answered in both directions, sometimes widening local powers (Amendment 101, 2014, and the "independent local committee"), sometimes adding professional and ministry representatives and control units.

## The law

### The National Council: s. 2

> תקום מועצה ארצית לתכנון ולבניה (להלן – המועצה הארצית) לייעץ לממשלה בכל הנוגע לקו הכללי בביצוע חוק זה [...] ולמלא את יתר התפקידים המוטלים עליה בחוק זה ובכל דין אחר.

*Gist:* The National Council advises the Government on planning policy and prepares national outline plans; it is chaired by the Minister of the Interior and includes ministers' representatives, mayors, professionals and public bodies. [s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#סעיף_2)

### District committees: s. 7

> לכל מחוז תהיה ועדה מחוזית לתכנון ולבניה (להלן – ועדה מחוזית), וזה הרכבה: (1) נציג שר הפנים, והוא יהיה היושב ראש; [...]

*Gist:* Each of the six planning districts has a district committee chaired by the Interior Minister's representative, mostly made up of ministry representatives plus local-authority members and professionals. [s. 7](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#סעיף_7)

### Local committees: ss. 17–19

> במרחב תכנון מקומי הכולל תחום רשות מקומית אחת בלבד, תהיה מועצת הרשות המקומית – הועדה המקומית.

*Gist:* Where a planning area contains one local authority, its elected council is the local committee; ministries' representatives and planning professionals attend with an advisory voice only. [s. 18(א)–(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#סעיף_18)

Where an area covers several authorities (a "regional" or מרחבית committee, typical for regional councils and small towns), the committee's chair is appointed by the Interior Minister and its members are recommended by the authorities (s. 19).

### The licensing authority: s. 30

> יושב ראש הועדה המקומית או יושב ראש ועדת המשנה של הועדה המקומית ומהנדס הועדה המקומית, יחדיו, יהיו רשות רישוי מקומית לענין מתן היתר לפי סעיף 145.

*Gist:* Ordinary building permits are granted by the chair and the committee engineer together; the full committee decides deviations such as relief (הקלה) and non-conforming use. [s. 30](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#סעיף_30)

### Appeals committees: ss. 12א–12ב, 12ו

> לכל מחוז תהיה ועדת ערר [...] וזה הרכבה: (1) עורך דין, בעל ותק של חמש שנים לפחות, ובלבד שהוא מצוי בעניני תכנון ובניה, שימנו שר הפנים ושר המשפטים כאחד והוא יהיה היושב ראש; [...]

*Gist:* Each district has an appeals committee chaired by an experienced lawyer appointed jointly by the Interior and Justice ministers, with a planner, an architect or engineer and two public representatives who may not be local councillors or employees. [s. 12א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#סעיף_12א)

It hears appeals against local committees and licensing authorities "in every matter in which this Law gives a right of appeal": permits, relief, non-conforming use, land division, and plans within the local committee's power (s. 12ב(א)). Its decisions on permits are final (s. 12ב(ד)(1)); a decision on a local plan may go, with leave, to the district committee (s. 111). A separate **appeals committee for compensation and betterment levy** (s. 12ו) hears the money disputes of lesson 27.6.

### The independent local committee: s. 31א

The Minister may certify a local committee as "independent" (עצמאית) if it runs a professional and efficient planning system, has an engineer, a prosecutor and an enforcement manager, and has an advisory professional representative; independence widens its power to approve plans under s. 62א (lesson 27.2), for three to five years at a time.

### Courts

Planning decisions are reviewed by the Administrative Affairs Court, except enforcement under Chapter 10 (Administrative Courts Law, First Schedule, item on planning and building; lesson [14.9](../module-14/lesson-09.md)). Enforcement orders go to the local-affairs or magistrates' court (lesson 27.5).

## Leading cases

### עע״ם 1446/06 הוועדה המחוזית לתכנון ולבניה מרכז נ' השתתפויות הנכסים בישראל (21.9.2010)

The Supreme Court held that the power given to local committees in 1995 to approve some plans (s. 62א) was narrow and allowed only minor changes with a "limited local effect"; broader changes belonged to the district committee. It quoted the classic warning from בג״ץ 235/76 *בניני קדמת לוד* that councillors sitting as a local committee may be swept by development pressure and "at worst" open the door to favours and corruption. Why it matters: the case explains the structure you see today, and the 2014 reform that widened local powers was Parliament's answer. Source: [Hebrew Wikipedia, ועדה מקומית לתכנון ולבנייה](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/ועדה_מקומית_לתכנון_ולבנייה) (secondary source; the ruling's text on the Judiciary's site could not be reached).

### Key text: s. 12א(א)(4) and the independence of appeals committees

The law bars councillors and local-committee members or employees from serving as public representatives on the appeals committee. Why it matters: an appeal is heard by a body outside the authority whose decision you challenge, chaired by a lawyer. Use that in your appeal: write for a legally trained chair, cite sections and the plan's provisions, not local politics.

## How it works in practice

### Find your institutions in four steps

1. **Local committee.** Search the municipality's website for "הוועדה המקומית לתכנון ובנייה" or "הנדסה". In regional councils and many small towns it is a regional committee (for example a committee serving several councils).
2. **District.** Six planning districts: North, Haifa, Center, Tel Aviv, Jerusalem, South. The district committee's decisions and plans appear on the Planning Administration's sites.
3. **Appeals committee.** One per district for permits and local plans; one for compensation and betterment levy. Their decisions are published; the Planning Administration runs an online system for filing appeals (⚠️ manual check of the current URL on gov.il).
4. **Planning Administration (מינהל התכנון).** The national body whose systems publish plans (the "Mavat" plan system at mavat.iplan.gov.il) and permits ("רישוי זמין"). The sites block automated checking, so open them yourself (⚠️ manual check).

### Who decides what

| Question | Decides | Appeal |
|---|---|---|
| National outline plan (TAMA) | National Council, Government approval | Petition |
| District plan; most local plans that add rights | District committee | National Council (s. 110, limited) or petition |
| Local plan within s. 62א | Local committee | Appeals committee (s. 112) |
| Ordinary permit | Licensing authority (chair + engineer) | Appeals committee (s. 152) |
| Relief, non-conforming use | Local committee | Appeals committee |
| National infrastructure | National Infrastructure Committee | Petition |
| Betterment levy, s. 197 compensation | Local committee | Compensation and levy appeals committee |

## Worked example

Yael Ben-Shimol lives in a moshav in the Lakhish regional council. A developer has asked to build a logistics centre on farmland next to the moshav, and a neighbour received a permit to enclose a porch.

1. **Local level.** The moshav has no municipality of its own; it is in the area of a regional planning committee (s. 19) whose chair the Interior Minister appointed. That committee, through its licensing authority (s. 30), granted the porch permit.
2. **District level.** Changing farmland to logistics needs a plan that adds uses and rights: the Southern District committee decides (lesson 27.2), and objections go there (s. 103).
3. **Appeals.** The porch permit could be appealed to the Southern appeals committee only by someone the law allows (lesson 27.4); Yael is not a party unless relief was requested and she objected to it.
4. **Court.** If the district committee approves the plan unlawfully, the remedy is an administrative petition (lesson [14.11](../module-14/lesson-11.md)), within the short petition period.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your home address (or any address you care about).
- The municipality's website and the Planning Administration's plan site (Mavat) and GovMap (⚠️ manual check: they block automated tools; open them in a normal browser).
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. Find the name of the local or regional committee for your address and whether it is an "independent" committee (the committee's site or the Planning Administration list).
2. Find the committee's engineer, its legal adviser and how it publishes agendas and decisions (s. 48ד requires minutes to be published).
3. Find your planning district and the name of the district committee.
4. Find the appeals committee for your district and how appeals are filed.
5. Write a one-page "planning map" for your address: the bodies, their websites, and for each, one decision it could make that would affect you.

### Template

None. Use the table in "Who decides what" above as the layout of your planning map.

### How you know it worked

- Your map names four bodies (local, district, appeals, Planning Administration) with working contact details.
- For each body you can name the section of the law that gives it the power you listed.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Finding information, attending public hearings of objections (s. 107) and reading minutes are open to anyone. Filing an objection or appeal in your own name is allowed (lessons 27.3 and 27.9); representing neighbours for a fee, or as a regular service, is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20). Whether a non-lawyer may represent another person before an appeals committee is not regulated clearly (unverified); see [Can I act without a lawyer?](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md).

### Where and how

- The local committee's offices and website; the Planning Administration's sites (⚠️ manual check).
- Freedom of information requests to the committee for documents not online (lesson [14.7](../module-14/lesson-07.md)).
- Free help: environmental and planning NGOs, community planners in some cities, and [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- A large project or an expropriation affects your property's value or use.
- You need an urgent interim order against a permit or works.
- You are a developer or own land in a plan with complex rights.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Objecting to the local committee on a plan within district power | Objection sent to the wrong body; deadline may pass | Refile with the body named in the deposit notice | Read s. 103 and the notice |
| Petitioning the High Court on a planning decision | Transferred or dismissed; time lost | File in the Administrative Affairs Court | Check the First Schedule first |
| Assuming the city council's political promise binds the committee | Disappointment; no legal claim | Rely on plan provisions and objections | Separate political lobbying from planning procedure |
| Ignoring the regional committee because "we are not a city" | Missing deposits that affect you | Follow the regional committee's notices | Map your institutions now |

## Review

- Planning power is spread across the National Council, district committees, local committees and their licensing authorities, with separate appeals committees.
- In a single-authority area the elected council is the local committee; ministries and professionals sit with an advisory voice.
- Ordinary permits come from the chair and engineer; deviations need the full committee.
- Appeals committees are chaired by lawyers and exclude local councillors; courts review planning through administrative petitions.
