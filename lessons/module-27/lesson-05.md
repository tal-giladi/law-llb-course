# 27.5 · Planning enforcement: offences and demolition orders

By the end of this lesson you will be able to recognise each planning enforcement tool, know who may use it and against whom, and choose the right response, with its deadline, when an order or a fine reaches you or a property you are about to buy.

> [!WARNING]
> Building offences are criminal offences. If you receive an order, a fine, a summons to an interrogation or an indictment, the deadlines are short (some are 15 days) and the consequences include demolition at your expense, heavy fines and a criminal record. Read this lesson as orientation; for anything already served on you, get legal advice quickly.

## Why it matters

Unpermitted building is widespread in Israel, from enclosed balconies in Tel Aviv to farm buildings in moshavim and whole neighbourhoods in Arab towns and in the Negev, where, residents and their representatives argue, plans lag behind need. Amendment 116 (2017), known as the "Kaminitz Law" after the deputy attorney general whose committee proposed it, rewrote Chapter 10 of the Law: it widened administrative orders that need no court, introduced administrative fines that can reach hundreds of thousands of shekels, and limited the courts' power to delay demolition.

The debate is sharp and both sides have strong legal arguments. Supporters, including the Kaminitz committee and the government that sponsored the amendment, point to the rule of law, safety, protection of public land and open space, and fairness to those who wait for permits; data cited after the amendment show a large fall in new building offences. Critics, especially from Arab communities, argue that enforcement was strengthened before plans were approved that would let people build lawfully, so that it falls on families with no lawful alternative; in 2020 the Attorney General's office announced an easing of enforcement against residential buildings on agricultural land in such communities pending plans (Hebrew Wikipedia, secondary). The law itself, as applied today, is the subject of this lesson.

## The law

### Who enforces: s. 203

The National Land Enforcement Authority (היחידה הארצית לאכיפה), the local committee chair and the committee engineer (or, in an authority certified for enforcement, its head and engineer) may give administrative orders; prosecutors of the local committee or the State Attorney's land unit prosecute.

### Administrative stop-work order: s. 216

Where there is a reasonable basis to believe that prohibited work is being done, the authority may order the owner, holder, permit holder, occupier or contractor to stop immediately; in urgent cases a temporary order of up to five days may be given on the spot (s. 216(ג)–(ד)).

### Administrative demolition order: ss. 221–222

> [...] רשאי הוא לצוות בכתב על הריסת העבודה האסורה [...], ובלבד שבמועד הגשת התצהיר לא הסתיימה העבודה האסורה או שלא חלפו יותר משישה חודשים מיום שהסתיימה, ולעניין עבודה אסורה לגבי בית מגורים – בית המגורים לא אוכלס או שלא חלפו יותר מ־30 ימים מהיום שאוכלס [...]

*Gist:* An administrative demolition order may be given, on an inspector's affidavit, only if the work is unfinished or was finished no more than six months before the affidavit; for a dwelling, only if it is not yet occupied or was occupied no more than 30 days. [s. 221(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#סעיף_221)

The order is executed within 60 days of the affidavit, but not earlier than 4 days after service for unfinished work and 7 days for finished work (s. 222(ב)–(ג)). Before giving any administrative order the official must consult a prosecutor or legal adviser, in writing (s. 225). The order may be registered as a note in the land registry (s. 254יא(ג)).

### Cancelling an administrative order: ss. 228–229

> הרואה את עצמו נפגע מצו מינהלי רשאי להגיש בקשה לביטול הצו לבית המשפט המוסמך [...] בתוך 15 ימים מיום המצאת הצו [...]

*Gist:* A person harmed by an administrative order may apply to cancel it within 15 days of service: to the local-affairs court (or the magistrates' court where there is none) for a local committee's order, to the magistrates' court for the national authority's order; filing does not stay execution. [s. 228](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#סעיף_228) (see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

The court cancels only if the work or use was lawful, the conditions for the order were not met, or there is a serious defect (s. 229). You may also bring your arguments to the official who gave the order (s. 228(ד)).

### Judicial orders: ss. 234–239

On a prosecutor's request the court may order the prevention of preparatory acts (s. 234), stop work or use or close a building (s. 236), and order demolition even without a criminal case where there is a special public interest and the offender cannot be found, served or identified, has died, or the prosecutor decided not to prosecute (s. 239; executed at least 21 days after service).

### Offences and fines: ss. 243–245; the 2018 fine regulations

> המבצע עבודה אסורה במקרקעין שאינם מפורטים בסעיף קטן (א), דינו – מאסר שנתיים או קנס [...] וכן קנס לפי סעיף 245.

*Gist:* Prohibited work is punishable by two years' imprisonment or a fine, doubled for sensitive land (national infrastructure, the coastal zone, declared agricultural land, parks and reserves, public open space); the court may add a fine equal to the fees and levies avoided and up to double the value of the unlawful construction. [s. 243](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#סעיף_243); [s. 245](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#סעיף_245)

The persons who may be charged include the permit holder or the person who had to obtain one, the owner at the time (with limits for co-owners), the person in control, the occupier and the person who actually did the work (s. 243(ג)). On conviction the court orders demolition or cessation unless special reasons are recorded (s. 254ב), to be carried out within 30 days unless postponed on conditions (s. 254ד).

Many offences are also **administrative offences** with fixed fines by area: for prohibited work on ordinary land, from ₪25,000 for 25–50 m² upwards; on sensitive land from ₪10,000 for up to 10 m² and up to ₪300,000 above 100 m² (2018 Regulations, Schedule; double for a company); fines for prohibited use are halved where the use is residential (reg. 3), and as a rule a fine is imposed only after a written warning that gives 30 days to correct the violation or restore the previous state, unless earlier proceedings were taken (reg. 5). (see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)) Under the Administrative Offences Law the person fined may ask a prosecutor to cancel the fine within 30 days (s. 8א) or notify that they wish to be tried within 30 days (s. 8(ג)); asking for cancellation does not postpone the payment date (s. 8א(ד)).

### Stays of execution: ss. 254ח–254ט

A court will not stay a demolition order where the work continued after service of the order (s. 254ח(ד)). A final order may be stayed once, only for special reasons and if the work does not endanger the public, and not if the work does not conform to the applicable plan; a stay is up to six months, extendable in limited cases (s. 254ט).

## Leading cases

### Key text: s. 221(א) — the time window for administrative demolition

The administrative route is designed for fresh offences: unfinished work, work finished within six months, or a dwelling occupied for no more than 30 days. Why it matters: the date of completion and of occupation is often the whole defence in a request to cancel. Keep evidence of dates (photos with metadata, invoices, arnona registration of occupancy, utility bills).

### Key text: s. 254ט(ד)(2) — no stay for work contrary to the plan

Before 2017 courts often granted long stays while owners sought to legalise their buildings. Now a stay of a final order is barred if the work does not conform to the applicable plan (a deposited plan counts in some cases), and in any case is limited in time. Why it matters: the realistic route for an owner is legalisation through a permit or a plan, pursued early, not delay in court.

## How it works in practice

### When something arrives

| You receive | First step | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Temporary stop-work order on site | Stop works; photograph; ask for the written order | Up to 5 days validity |
| Administrative stop-work or demolition order | Request to cancel in the local-affairs or magistrates' court; and/or arguments to the official | 15 days from service (s. 228) |
| Administrative fine notice | Request to cancel to the prosecutor, or notice of wish to be tried | 30 days (ss. 8(ג), 8א) |
| Summons to interrogation | Legal advice; rights in interrogation (lesson [24.3](../module-24/lesson-03.md)) | As summoned |
| Indictment | Defence; consider legalisation and plea | Per court |

### Buying a property

Check the land registry extract for s. 254יא notes, the committee's building and enforcement files, and the seller's statements; in a purchase contract, require disclosure of any unpermitted work and a price adjustment or indemnity (lesson [13.9](../module-13/lesson-09.md)).

## Worked example

Ibrahim Abu Rabia, from Tel Sheva, completed a 40 m² extension to his family home on land designated for housing under the town's plan, without a permit, on 10 May 2026. The family moved into it on 1 June. On 20 July an inspector photographed it, and on 1 August an administrative demolition order was served.

1. **Window.** The work was finished less than six months before the affidavit, but this is a dwelling occupied since 1 June: more than 30 days before the affidavit date. The conditions of s. 221(א) for a dwelling appear not to be met.
2. **Request.** Ibrahim files a request to cancel in the magistrates' court for the area (Tel Sheva has no local-affairs court; ⚠️ check) within 15 days of 1 August, with evidence of occupation (school registration at the address, utility and arnona bills) (s. 228).
3. **Ground.** The requirements for the order were not met (s. 229).
4. **Risk.** Even if the order is cancelled, the work is still prohibited: the committee may prosecute (s. 243(ב)) or impose an administrative fine after a 30-day warning (40 m² of work on ordinary land falls in the ₪25,000 band). Since the land is designated for housing, the lasting solution is a permit application, possibly with relief.
5. **Lawyer.** Because a demolition and a criminal exposure are at stake, Ibrahim contacts Legal Aid or a lawyer immediately; he prepares the evidence file himself.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Ibrahim's case, or a real situation you know (a neighbour's, a property you consider buying). Do not file anything for another person.
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. Identify the work, the dates of completion and occupation, and the land's designation.
2. Decide which enforcement tools are available to the authority (ss. 216, 221, 236, 239, 243, the fine regulations).
3. For an administrative demolition order, test the s. 221(א) window.
4. Write the remedy, the forum and the deadline for each tool (table above).
5. List the evidence you would gather for dates and lawful status.
6. Write the legalisation route: permit, relief, or plan change (lessons 27.2–27.4).

### Template

For a fine where you want a trial: [request to be tried](../../templates/request-to-be-tried.md) (adapt to the Administrative Offences Law). Otherwise none.

### How you know it worked

- You can name, for each tool, the remedy, the forum and the deadline.
- Your dates analysis is backed by evidence.
- You have a legalisation plan, not only a defence.

### Self-check

- [ ] I know that a request to cancel does not by itself stop a demolition.
- [ ] I know the 15-day and 30-day deadlines.
- [ ] I know who may be charged for prohibited work.
- [ ] I know a stay is not available if works continue after service.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Partly. You may file a request to cancel an administrative order, a request to cancel a fine or a notice of wish to be tried in your own name, and defend yourself in court (the general rule in criminal proceedings). Given the stakes (demolition, large fines, a criminal record), self-representation should be a last resort. A company charged with an offence needs a lawyer in practice.

### Where and how

- Request to cancel an order: the local-affairs court or the magistrates' court nearest the land (s. 228(ב)).
- Fines: the prosecutor named in the fine notice (s. 8א), or notice of wish to be tried (s. 8(ג)).
- Free help: Legal Aid (civil aspects), the Public Defender if eligible in criminal proceedings (lesson [01.6](../module-01/lesson-06.md)), planning NGOs and community planners for legalisation.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- Any order, fine or indictment has been served on you.
- You are summoned to an interrogation.
- You are buying a property with an enforcement note or open file.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Continuing to build after an order | Separate offence (s. 246); no stay possible (s. 254ח(ד)) | Stop at once; document | Stop on the first order |
| Filing a request to cancel on day 20 | Late; order executed | Ask for an extension with reasons | Diarise 15 days from service |
| Assuming a request stops demolition | Demolition goes ahead | Ask the court for a stay expressly | Request a stay in the same filing |
| Paying the fine and then asking to be tried | Payment may be treated as admission | Get advice before paying | Decide within 30 days, then pay or request trial |
| Buying without checking enforcement files | Inherit orders and exposure | Seek remedies from the seller | Check registry notes and the committee files |

## Review

- Amendment 116 (2017) created administrative stop and demolition orders, administrative fines and strict limits on stays.
- An administrative demolition order is limited to fresh work: six months from completion, 30 days of occupation for dwellings.
- Request to cancel an administrative order within 15 days; fines: cancel request or wish to be tried within 30 days.
- Prohibited work carries imprisonment or fines (doubled on sensitive land) and demolition orders on conviction.
- The real solution is legalisation through a permit or a plan, pursued early.
