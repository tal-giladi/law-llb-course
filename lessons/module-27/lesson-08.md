# 27.8 · Arnona: classification, exemptions and discounts

By the end of this lesson you will be able to read an arnona bill line by line, check the area, type and zone it uses, end your liability correctly when you move, find the discounts your household may claim, and choose the right route when the bill is wrong. The objection procedure itself was introduced in lessons [01.7](../module-01/lesson-07.md) and [14.10](../module-14/lesson-10.md) and is practised in lesson 27.10.

## Why it matters

Arnona is the main local tax and the largest source of a municipality's own income. Every holder of a property pays it, every year, and errors are common: an area measured with the wrong method, a balcony counted twice, a business rate on a home office, a bill to a tenant who left. The amounts add up over years, and the statutory objection periods are short. At the same time the Discount Regulations give large discounts (up to 100% for some pensioners on a limited area) that many eligible people never claim.

The courts have long criticised the complexity of the system: one city has thousands of classifications, and Justice Rubinstein wrote that "the laws of arnona cry out for logical simplification" (quoted in Hebrew Wikipedia, ⚠️ secondary). Since 1993 the Arrangements Law caps annual increases and requires ministerial approval for exceptional ones, so the fight is rarely about the rate itself and usually about the facts: area, use, type, zone and who the holder is.

## The law

### Imposition: Arrangements Law ss. 8–9

> מועצה תטיל בכל שנת כספים ארנונה כללית על הנכסים שבתחומה שאינם אדמת בנין: הארנונה תחושב לפי יחידת שטח בהתאם לסוג הנכס, לשימושו ולמקומו, ותשולם בידי המחזיק בנכס.

*Gist:* Each year the council imposes arnona on the properties in its area, calculated per square metre by type, use and location, and paid by the holder. [s. 8(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הסדרים_במשק_המדינה_%28תיקוני_חקיקה_להשגת_יעדי_התקציב%29#סעיף_8)

Area is measured in square metres, rounded to the nearest whole metre with a half rounded down (s. 8(ב1)). The rate per m² is last year's rate updated by a statutory index, within ministerial minimum and maximum amounts; higher increases need ministerial approval after notice to the holders (s. 9).

### The holder: Municipalities Ordinance ss. 1, 269

> ”מחזיק“ – אדם המחזיק למעשה בנכס כבעל או כשוכר או בכל אופן אחר [...]

*Gist:* The holder is the person in actual possession, whether owner, tenant or otherwise; a subtenant renting a room from the holder is not the holder. [s. 1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_העיריות#סעיף_1); [s. 269](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_העיריות#סעיף_269)

### When liability ends and begins: ss. 325–326

> חדל אדם ביום מן הימים להיות בעלם או מחזיקם של קרקע או של בנין שהוא חב עליהם בארנונה [...] ימסור הוא או נציגו הודעה על כך בכתב לעיריה ולאחר מכן לא יהיה חייב בשיעורי ארנונה נוספים [...]

*Gist:* A person who stops being owner or holder must notify the municipality in writing and is not liable after the notice, though still liable for what accrued before. [s. 325](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_העיריות#סעיף_325)

On a sale, transfer or a lease of a year or more, the seller or landlord must notify the municipality of the new holder (s. 326). Without notice, liability continues: the most common and avoidable arnona debt.

### Destroyed or damaged buildings: s. 330

A building destroyed or damaged so that it cannot be lived in, and that no one lives in, is exempt for three years after written notice; then a minimum rate for five years; then exempt again on further notice (s. 330).

### Discounts: Arrangements Law s. 12; Discount Regulations 1993

The council may give only the discounts the regulations allow (s. 12(ג)); it decides each year which to adopt. The main discounts (reg. 2(א)), each a maximum:

| Who | Maximum discount |
|---|---|
| Senior receiving old-age, survivors' or work-injury pension | 25% on up to 100 m² |
| Senior also receiving income support | 100% on up to 100 m² |
| Person with 75%+ incapacity for work (full NII disability pension) | 80% |
| 90%+ medical disability | 40% |
| Holocaust-persecution and Prisoner of Zion benefits | 66% on up to 70 m² (90 m² for large families) |
| Blind person (certificate) | 90% |
| New immigrant | 90% on up to 100 m² for 12 of the first 24 months |
| Long-term care (nursing) benefit recipient | 70% |
| Income test (by household size, table in the First Schedule) | As in the table |
| Single parent (also while a child up to 21 serves in the army or national service) | 20% |
| Parent of a child with an NII disabled-child benefit | 33% on up to 100 m² |
| Active reservist (certificate) | Up to 5% (reg. 3ו) |

Other discounts: for a person in need because of exceptional medical expenses or a sudden severe change in finances, up to 70%, decided by a discounts committee with a welfare opinion (reg. 7); a new empty building for its first owner, up to 100% for 12 months (reg. 12); an empty building, up to 100% for 6 months, then 66.66%, then 50% to month 36, cumulatively per ownership, with notice before reuse (reg. 13).

Rules that catch people out: one discount only, the highest (reg. 17(א)); one property only (reg. 17(ב)); a discount lapses if the year's arnona is not fully paid by 31 December (reg. 16); applications on Form 1 (reg. 4) by the date the council sets (reg. 21).

### The statutory route: Arnona Appeals Law ss. 3–7א

The objection to the arnona director covers only: wrong zone, an error in type, size or use, not being the holder, and certain business-control claims (s. 3(א)); other claims of illegality, and discounts, are outside it (s. 3(ב)). The payment notice must state the objection and appeal rights and deadlines (s. 7א). Periods and the deemed-acceptance rule are in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); the procedure is the subject of lesson 27.10.

## Leading cases

### רע״א 187/05 נסייר נ' עיריית נצרת עילית (20.6.2010)

The Supreme Court held that arnona debts are subject to limitation, and that a person may ask the court to stop a local authority from collecting time-barred arnona even when the authority uses "passive collection", such as refusing to issue the certificate of no debts needed for a sale. Why it matters: an old bill that the municipality never pursued may be unenforceable; check dates before paying an old debt to get a certificate. Source: [Hebrew Wikipedia, ארנונה](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/ארנונה) (secondary; the ruling's text on the Judiciary's site could not be reached).

### Key text: s. 3(ב) of the Arnona Appeals Law — the limits of the objection

The director and the appeals committee may not decide a claim that the council's imposition or rates were illegal, beyond the listed grounds. Why it matters: "the rate is discriminatory" or "the order was not approved in time" goes to an administrative petition (lesson [14.11](../module-14/lesson-11.md)); "my flat is 82 m², not 95 m²" goes to the director.

## How it works in practice

### Reading the bill

Check six fields: holder's name; property number; zone; classification (type and use); area (m²) and how it was measured (ask for the measurement sheet); and the discounts applied. Compare the area with your purchase contract or a licensed surveyor's measurement, and the use with reality (a home office used only by you is usually still a dwelling; ask what the order says).

### Choosing the route

| Problem | Route |
|---|---|
| Wrong area, type, use or zone; not the holder | Objection to the arnona director (s. 3(א)); then appeal; then administrative appeal |
| You moved out and are still billed | Written notice under s. 325 now; objection "not the holder" for the period after notice |
| You are eligible for a discount | Application on Form 1 to the arnona department (reg. 4) |
| Sudden hardship | Discounts committee request (reg. 7) |
| The rate or order itself is unlawful | Prior demand, then administrative petition |
| Old debts | Check limitation; ask for the debt statement (רע״א 187/05) |

## Worked example

Dvora Klein, 71, a widow in Kiryat Motzkin, receives an old-age pension and income support. Her flat is 92 m², but the 2026 bill charges 104 m² and gives her a 25% discount. Her son, who lived with her until March, rented his own flat and still appears as a co-holder.

1. **Area.** The difference may be a measurement method issue (balconies, storage). She asks for the measurement sheet and gets a surveyor's report showing 93 m². She files an objection to the director on "size" (s. 3(א)(2)) within the period from the payment notice (lesson 27.10).
2. **Discount.** With income support she may be eligible for up to 100% on 100 m² (reg. 2(א)(1)(ב)), if the council adopted it. She applies on Form 1 with the NII certificates; the application is separate from the objection.
3. **Holder.** Her son's name is irrelevant to her liability, but if the city adds him as holder at his new flat, he should check his own bill; the discount is given to the holder in the property (reg. 17).
4. **Payment.** She keeps paying the undisputed part and makes sure the year is paid by 31 December, or the discount lapses (reg. 16).

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your latest arnona bill (or Dvora's case), your purchase or rental contract, and any measurement you have.
- Your municipality's arnona order for 2026 and its discounts page (⚠️ manual check).
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. Copy the six fields from your bill.
2. Compare the area with your documents; note any difference above 1 m².
3. Find your property's classification in the arnona order and check it matches the use.
4. List the discounts your household may fit (table above) and the evidence each needs.
5. Check that your liability for any previous home ended with a written s. 325 notice.
6. Decide the route for each problem (table above) and the deadline.

### Template

For an objection: the draft [arnona objection and appeal](../../templates/arnona-objection-and-appeal.md) (used in lesson 27.10). For a discount request: the city's Form 1, or a [letter to a public authority](../../templates/letter-to-authority.md).

### How you know it worked

- Every field on your bill is checked against a source.
- You know which discounts you will claim and which you do not qualify for.
- You have a route and a deadline for each problem.

### Self-check

- [ ] I know arnona is paid by the actual holder.
- [ ] I know liability ends only after written notice (s. 325).
- [ ] I know the objection does not cover discounts or illegality claims.
- [ ] I know a discount lapses if the year is not paid by 31 December.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Objections, discount applications and appeals to the arnona appeals committee are routinely filed by holders themselves ([can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md)). Paid arnona "consultants" who are not lawyers may not represent you in legal proceedings (Bar Association Law s. 20); a licensed surveyor may measure the property for you.

### Where and how

- The arnona department (often online forms) for discounts and notices of change of holder.
- The arnona director for objections; the appeals committee for appeals (lesson 27.10).
- Free help: Kol-Zchut pages on arnona discounts (⚠️ manual check), the city's welfare department for hardship requests, [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The property is a business with large sums or classification disputes over several years.
- The municipality sues you or attaches your bank account for old debts.
- You want to challenge the arnona order itself.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| No s. 325 notice when moving | Billed for the old flat for years | Give notice now; object for later periods | Notify in writing on the day you move |
| Asking for a discount in an objection | The director cannot decide it there | Apply on Form 1 | Separate objection and discount |
| Not paying the year by 31 December | Discount cancelled (reg. 16) | Pay; ask for reconsideration | Use a standing order |
| Claiming two discounts | Only the highest is given (reg. 17) | Choose the best one | Compare before applying |
| Arguing "the rate is unfair" before the director | Outside his power (s. 3(ב)) | Petition | Match the ground to the route |

## Review

- Arnona is imposed yearly by the council per m², by type, use and zone, and paid by the holder in actual possession.
- Liability ends only with written notice to the municipality (s. 325); sellers and landlords must notify the new holder (s. 326).
- The 1993 Regulations allow listed discounts (seniors, disability, immigrants, income test, hardship, empty buildings); one discount, one property, paid by 31 December.
- Objections cover area, type, use, zone and holder; discounts and illegality claims go by application or petition.
