# 27.6 · Betterment levy and compensation for planning harm

By the end of this lesson you will be able to tell when a plan creates a debt to the local committee (a betterment levy on the increase in value) or a claim against it (compensation for the decrease), calculate the order of magnitude, find the exemptions that matter to homeowners, and challenge an assessment or file a claim on time.

## Why it matters

Plans move money. When a plan, relief or non-conforming use raises the value of land, the owner pays the local committee half of the increase, usually when selling or building. When a plan lowers the value of land, the owner may claim compensation. Both are large sums in Israeli real estate (often tens or hundreds of thousands of shekels), both are decided by appraisers, and both have short or strict deadlines. Many homeowners learn about the levy only when the committee refuses to sign the certificate the Land Registry needs to register a sale.

The policy idea is symmetry: the public, which created the extra value by planning, shares in it; and an owner who is singled out to bear a planning burden is compensated. The symmetry is imperfect. The levy is broad; compensation is narrowed by s. 200, which denies it for "reasonable" harm from ordinary planning provisions. The courts have spent decades on where "reasonable" ends, without a fixed percentage.

## The law

### The levy: Third Schedule ss. 1–3

> חלה השבחה במקרקעין [...] ישלם בעלם היטל השבחה לפי האמור בתוספת זו [...]; היטל בעקבות אישור תכנית יחול על מקרקעין הנמצאים בתחום התכנית או הגובלים עמו בלבד.

*Gist:* When land increases in value because of a plan, relief or non-conforming use, the owner (or long-term lessee) pays a betterment levy; for a plan, only land within the plan or adjoining it is liable. [Third Schedule s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#תוספת_3_פרט_2)

> שיעור ההיטל הוא מחצית ההשבחה.

*Gist:* The levy is half of the increase in value. [Third Schedule s. 3](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#תוספת_3_פרט_3)

"Realisation" means a permit that could not be given but for the plan, first actual use under the plan, or a transfer of the land or a long lease, for value or not, except transfer by law, gifts to relatives and transfers between spouses on separation (s. 1(א)). The levy is paid no later than realisation (s. 7).

### How the levy is assessed: s. 4

A land appraiser fixes the increase as at the plan's commencement, or the date relief or non-conforming use was approved, as if sold on the free market (s. 4(1), (7)). The committee may postpone the assessment to the time of realisation; an owner may demand an assessment at any time, which must be made within 90 days, against a fee (s. 4(4)). For successive plans with no levy paid, the increase runs from before the first plan to after the last (s. 4(5)).

### Enforcement of payment: s. 10

The Land Registry does not register a realisation without the committee chair's certificate that the levy due has been paid (s. 10(א)); a mortgage needs no certificate (s. 10(א1)). No permit, relief or non-conforming use is granted until the levy due is paid or secured (s. 10(ג)).

### Challenges: s. 14

> על שומה לפי סעיף 4 ניתן לערור לפני ועדת ערר לפיצויים ולהיטל השבחה, בתוך 45 ימים מיום שבו הובאה השומה לידיעת החייב [...]

*Gist:* Within 45 days of being told of the assessment, the owner may appeal to the compensation and levy appeals committee; if he disputes only the amount, he may instead ask the chair of the Land Appraisers Council to appoint a decisive appraiser, whose decision may be appealed to the committee within 45 days; for assessments up to ₪90,000 he may first ask the local committee's appraiser to review it (s. 14(ב1)); further appeal lies to the Administrative Affairs Court. [Third Schedule s. 14](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#תוספת_3_פרט_14) (see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

Filing does not delay a sale if the levy is paid; a person who needs a permit for his own or a relative's home gets it on giving security for the disputed amount (s. 14(ה)).

### Exemptions: s. 19

The exemptions most relevant to individuals:

- **Own home up to 140 m²**: building or extending a home for the holder or a relative is not "realisation" if the home after the work is no more than 140 m²; beyond that, the levy is paid on the extra area (s. 19(ג)(1)).
- **Shelters**: a protected room (ממ״ד) within the area set under the Civil Defence Law (s. 19(ב)(9)).
- **Earthquake strengthening**: TAMA 38 permits and plans under it, with reduced rates in some cases; earthquake-resilience plans under ss. 70א–70ט pay a quarter of the increase (s. 19(ב)(10)–(10א)).
- **Public institutions** (education, religion, health, charity) and renewal neighbourhoods (s. 19(ב)(1)–(4)).
- **Accessibility**: service areas for accessibility, including a lift, up to the statutory limit (s. 19(ב)(7)).
- **Hardship**: for relief or non-conforming use (not plans), the committee may exempt for financial hardship (s. 19(א)).

### Compensation: ss. 197–200

> נפגעו על ידי תכנית, שלא בדרך הפקעה, מקרקעין הנמצאים בתחום התכנית או גובלים עמו, מי שביום תחילתה של התכנית היה בעל המקרקעין או בעל זכות בהם זכאי לפיצויים מהועדה המקומית, בכפוף לאמור בסעיף 200.

*Gist:* An owner or rights holder on the day a plan commenced, whose land within or adjoining the plan was harmed by it (other than by expropriation), is entitled to compensation from the local committee, subject to s. 200. [s. 197(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#סעיף_197)

The claim is filed at the local committee's offices within three years of the plan's commencement; the minister may extend for special reasons, even after the period (s. 197(ב)). The date of publication in Reshumot is the relevant date (s. 119(ב)). The committee decides within 90 days; the claimant may appeal to the compensation and levy appeals committee within 45 days, or by agreement go to a decisive appraiser on amount (s. 198). (see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

Section 200 denies compensation where the harm comes from listed ordinary provisions (changes of zones and uses, spacing, number, size, height and appearance of buildings, restrictions for safety or public cost, parking and shelters, and more) if the harm "does not exceed the bounds of what is reasonable in the circumstances" and justice does not require compensation. Plans whose only purpose is shelters are excluded from compensation (s. 200(ב)).

## Leading cases

### ע״א 7368/06 דירות יוקרה בע״מ נ' ראש עיריית יבנה (27.6.2011)

The Supreme Court reviewed the practice of local authorities reaching agreements with owners to pay a betterment levy in circumstances where the law did not require it at all, for example before a plan was approved. Parliament's Amendment 84 (2008) had already restricted negotiated levy deals and introduced the decisive-appraiser procedure, which Hebrew Wikipedia reports has worked efficiently and improved transparency. Why it matters: the levy is a statutory tax-like charge; an authority may not create it by contract, and an owner should insist on an assessment under s. 4 and the s. 14 procedure. Source: [Hebrew Wikipedia, היטל השבחה](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/היטל_השבחה) (secondary; the ruling's text on the Judiciary's site could not be reached).

### דנ״א 1333/02 הוועדה המקומית לתכנון ולבניה רעננה נ' הורוביץ (12.5.2004)

A seven-justice panel of the Supreme Court (דנ״א 1333/02 *הוועדה המקומית לתכנון ולבניה רעננה נ' הורוביץ*, 12.5.2004) dealt with a plan that reduced the value of an owner's land by about 11.8%. The court declined to set a percentage that is "reasonable" for all cases, held that reasonableness depends on the circumstances, and ordered the local committee to pay compensation ([Globes, 5.12.2004](https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=860638); secondary source, ⚠️ the ruling's text could not be reached). Why it matters: in a s. 197 claim the committee will often answer "s. 200"; your claim should show why the harm is unusual for you (its size, its concentration on your property, its permanence) and why justice requires compensation.

## How it works in practice

### When the levy hits

| Event | What happens |
|---|---|
| Plan approved | Committee may publish an assessment table (לוח שומה) within six months (s. 6) or postpone to realisation |
| You apply for a permit using new rights | Assessment; levy or security before the permit (s. 10(ג)) |
| You sell | You need the committee's certificate for the Land Registry (s. 10(א)); the levy is usually the seller's cost unless the contract says otherwise |
| You disagree | 45 days: appeal or decisive appraiser (s. 14) |

### A compensation claim

Gather the plan's commencement date (Reshumot), an appraiser's opinion comparing value before and after, and evidence of the harm (views, noise, shadow, loss of rights). File at the committee's offices within three years, on the form in the 1971 Regulations (⚠️ manual check of the current form). Expect the committee to notify the developer or other payer (s. 198(א)) and to raise s. 200.

## Worked example

Sarit and Avi Cohen own a semi-detached house in Rehovot. In 2024 a local plan allowed an extra floor on their street. In 2026 they apply for a permit to build a second floor that will bring their home from 110 m² to 180 m². At the same time, a district plan for a new interchange 80 metres from their house was published in Reshumot on 15 March 2026.

1. **Levy on the extension.** The permit uses the 2024 rights: realisation (s. 1). The extension is for their own home; the 140 m² exemption covers the first 30 m² of the 70 m² extension; the levy is paid on the 40 m² above 140 m² (s. 19(ג)(1)).
2. **Assessment.** The committee's appraiser values the increase attributable to those 40 m²; the levy is half (s. 3).
3. **Dispute.** They think the value is too high but accept liability: they ask for a decisive appraiser within 45 days (s. 14(ב)). To start building they give security for the disputed part (s. 14(ה)).
4. **Compensation.** The interchange plan adjoins their street; noise and loss of value may be harm under s. 197. The deadline is three years from 15 March 2026. They commission an appraisal and expect a s. 200 defence ("reasonable" harm from a road), so they document the unusual noise level and the drop in comparable sales.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A property of yours or of family, or the Cohens' case.
- The plan profile from lesson 27.2.
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. List the plans approved since the property was last bought, and any relief or non-conforming use granted on it.
2. For each, decide: increase in value (levy) or decrease (possible compensation).
3. For the levy: identify the realisation event that will trigger payment and any exemption (140 m², shelter, strengthening, hardship for relief).
4. Estimate the order of magnitude: (value after − value before) × 50%. Use rough market data; note that only an appraiser's assessment counts.
5. For compensation: compute the three-year deadline from the Reshumot date and list the s. 200 arguments you will face.
6. Write the challenge path and deadlines (s. 14; s. 198).

### Template

None. A short letter to the committee asking for an assessment under s. 4(4) can follow the [letter to a public authority](../../templates/letter-to-authority.md).

### How you know it worked

- You can name the realisation event and the exemption, if any.
- You have both deadlines (45 days; three years) written with their trigger dates.
- You know which forum hears a dispute on liability and which on amount.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Partly. You may ask for an assessment, request a decisive appraiser and file an appeal or a compensation claim in your own name. In practice the dispute is between appraisers, and you will need your own land appraiser's opinion (a licensed appraiser may give it; representing you in the legal proceeding is a lawyer's task under s. 20, except where the law allows otherwise). Appeals to the compensation and levy appeals committee carry a fee (s. 198(ה1) for compensation; see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).

### Where and how

- Assessment and certificate: the local committee's betterment levy department.
- Decisive appraiser: the chair of the Land Appraisers Council in the Ministry of Justice (⚠️ manual check of the online request service).
- Appeals: the district appeals committee for compensation and betterment levy (s. 12ו).
- Compensation claim: at the local committee's offices (s. 197(ב)).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The amounts are large (tens of thousands of shekels or more) or the dispute is on liability rather than value.
- You are selling and the buyer's lawyer demands the certificate before a date you cannot meet.
- You consider a s. 197 claim against a major plan with several payers.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Missing the 45 days | Assessment becomes final | Ask the chair for an extension for special reasons | Diarise when you receive the assessment |
| Choosing a decisive appraiser while disputing liability | Liability not decided there | Appeal to the committee on liability | Decide first: liability or amount |
| Not using the 140 m² exemption | Paying levy on exempt area | Ask for a corrected assessment | State the exemption in your permit file |
| Filing a s. 197 claim after three years | Time-barred | Ask the minister for an extension (s. 197(ב)) | Diarise from the Reshumot date |
| Signing a "levy agreement" before a plan is approved | Paying without statutory basis | Seek advice; challenge the agreement | Insist on the statutory process |

## Review

- A betterment levy of half the increase in value is due on realisation: a permit using new rights, first use, or a sale.
- Challenge an assessment within 45 days: appeals committee on liability, decisive appraiser on amount.
- Owners extending their own home up to 140 m², shelters and strengthening enjoy exemptions.
- Harm from a plan may give compensation (s. 197), claimed within three years, but s. 200 excludes reasonable harm from ordinary provisions.
