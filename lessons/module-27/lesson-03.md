# 27.3 · Objections to plans and planning appeals

By the end of this lesson you will be able to object to a deposited plan in the form the law demands, take part in the public hearing, and choose the right appeal if your objection is rejected. Objection is the most important tool an individual has in Israeli planning: it is open to anyone harmed, it costs nothing to file, and after the plan is approved most of the chances to change it are gone.

## Why it matters

A plan, once approved, is very hard to attack. Courts defer to planning judgment and ask whether the challenger used the objection stage; a person who stayed silent during deposit and then petitions will often be told that the time for objections has passed. Conversely, a reasoned objection with an affidavit forces the committee to address your arguments in a public hearing and in written reasons (s. 108(ב)), creates the record for any appeal, and frequently produces changes: a moved building line, a lower tower, a preserved tree, a widened path.

Public participation in planning is a principle the Israeli system takes seriously in form: deposit notices in Reshumot, newspapers, local offices, a sign on site and, for small plans, to neighbours. Its critics say that the formal stage comes too late, after a developer and the committee have shaped the plan; its defenders say that deposit is exactly when a concrete plan exists to object to. Both views lead to the same practical lesson: watch for deposits and object in time, and, where you can, take part earlier through meetings and comments.

## The law

### Notice of deposit: ss. 89, 89א, 92

A deposit notice is published in Reshumot and in newspapers, at the offices of the local authorities and on neighbourhood notice boards (s. 89). For local and detailed plans the developer must also post a sign on the site for the whole objection period, in Arabic too where Arabic speakers are at least 10% of the local planning area's population, listing the main differences from the existing planning situation (s. 89א(א)–(ב)). For a plan on up to 3,000 m², notice is also delivered to owners and occupiers of adjoining plots (s. 89א(ד)). The notice must include the block and parcel numbers, the streets, the main provisions, and where and when to object (s. 92).

### Who may object: s. 100

> כל מעונין בקרקע, בבנין או בכל פרט תכנוני אחר הרואה את עצמו נפגע על ידי תכנית מיתאר מחוזית או מקומית או תכנית מפורטת שהופקדו, רשאי להגיש התנגדות להן [...]

*Gist:* Anyone with an interest in land, a building "or any other planning detail" who sees themselves harmed by a deposited plan may object; so may local committees and authorities, ministries, the district planner, and public bodies approved by order (such as nature, heritage and environmental organisations). [s. 100](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#סעיף_100)

"Any other planning detail" is read broadly: a resident who uses a park or a street can object even without owning land in the plan.

### When: s. 102

> התנגדות לתכנית תוגש תוך חודשיים מהיום שפורסמה הודעה על הפקדתה [...] מועד פרסום ההודעה יהיה המועד המאוחר שבין הפרסומים בעתון.

*Gist:* Objections are filed within two months from the deposit notice; the institution may extend the period to up to three months; the period runs from the later of the newspaper publications. [s. 102](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#סעיף_102) (see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

### Where: s. 103

To a district plan: the National Council, with a copy to the district committee. To a plan in district power: the district committee, with a copy to the local committee. To a plan in local power: the local committee, with a copy to the district committee. The regulations require proof that the copy was delivered (Objections Regulations reg. 4).

### Form: s. 103א; regs. 3, 7

> התנגדות לתכנית לא תתקבל ולא תידון אלא אם כן הוגשה בכתב בפירוט הנמקות ובליווי תצהיר המאמת את העובדות שעליהן היא מסתמכת.

*Gist:* An objection is heard only if it is in writing, gives detailed reasons and is supported by an affidavit verifying its facts; in public matters of heritage, nature, landscape and environment one objector may file the affidavit for a group with similar objections. [s. 103א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#סעיף_103א)

The objection states the objector's interest in the land, building or planning detail and how the plan harms it, and an address for service; it is filed in four copies (reg. 3). A defective objection may be struck out or the objector allowed to correct it (reg. 7).

### The hearing: ss. 104, 106–108; regs. 6, 12, 14–15

The objector and the plan's submitter are invited at least 14 days in advance (reg. 6) and the hearing is public (s. 107). The objector speaks first and has a right of reply (reg. 12). Arguments are limited to the grounds written in the objection unless the institution decides otherwise (reg. 14): put every argument in writing. The institution may reject, accept or change the plan; if accepting an objection would harm someone else, it must hear them first (s. 106(ב)). Vexatious objections may cost the objector the developer's costs, and a justified objection may earn costs from the developer (s. 106(ד)). Where objections are many, an investigator (חוקר) may hear them and recommend (s. 107א). Reasons must be sent to every objector in writing (s. 108(ב)). With no objections, the plan is deemed approved 30 days after the period ends unless the institution decides otherwise (s. 108(ג)).

### Appeals: ss. 110–112

| Decision | Appeal | Who | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Local committee plan (s. 62א) | Appeals committee (s. 112) | Objector whose objection was rejected; submitter; two committee members; district planner and others | 15 days from service of the decision (s. 112(ב)); if the committee is independent, objectors need leave of the chair (s. 112(א1)) |
| Appeals committee decision on such a plan | District committee (s. 111) | Parties, with leave of the appeals committee chair | Leave: 7 days; appeal: 7 days after leave |
| District committee plan | National Council (s. 110) | Objector whose objection was rejected: only with leave of the district committee chair | Leave: 15 days; appeal: 30 days |

(see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

Otherwise the route is an administrative petition to the Administrative Affairs Court, without delay and within 45 days of the decision being published or known (Administrative Courts Procedure Regulations reg. 3(ב)); see lesson [14.11](../module-14/lesson-11.md).

### Objecting to relief or non-conforming use: s. 149

A request for relief (הקלה) or non-conforming use (שימוש חורג) is published, posted on the building and sent to owners and occupiers of the plot and adjoining plots; objections are filed within 15 days of publication (s. 149(א)). Lesson 27.4 deals with the appeal that follows.

## Leading cases

### Key text: s. 103א and reg. 14 — the written objection is the whole case

No ruling is needed to see the trap: an objection without an affidavit is not heard, and an argument not written in the objection may not be raised at the hearing without special leave. Why it matters: most self-represented objections fail on form or on scope, not on merits. Write every ground, attach evidence, and sign the affidavit before a lawyer as the Evidence Ordinance requires (lesson [23.9](../module-23/lesson-09.md)).

### Key text: s. 106(ד) — costs both ways

A committee may order a bad-faith, vexatious objector to pay the developer's costs of the procedure, and may order the developer to pay a justified objector's costs. Why it matters: object on planning grounds, honestly and with evidence; a well-founded objection is not only safe, it may cover its own expenses.

## How it works in practice

### Finding deposits in time

- Watch the newspapers' notice pages, the signs on sites near you and the local authority's notice board.
- On Mavat, search your area for plans with status "deposited" and read the objection deadline in the plan's timeline (⚠️ manual check).
- Ask the local committee to send you deposit notices by email, if it offers a subscription.

### Filing

The objection is filed with the institution named in the deposit notice, in four copies or through its online channel. The Planning Administration offers online filing of objections for many plans through its site (⚠️ manual check of the current service on gov.il). Send the copy required by s. 103 by registered mail and keep proof (reg. 4). Filing an objection carries no fee (Kol-Zchut, ⚠️ manual check).

### What makes a good objection

- **Planning grounds**, not only personal ones: density without public services, missing open space (s. 61(5)), traffic and parking, shadow and privacy, heritage, trees, flood risk.
- **Legal grounds**: no power (s. 62א), conflict with a higher plan (ss. 129–131), defective notice (ss. 89–92), improper considerations, lack of an environmental survey where required.
- **Concrete remedies**: "move the building line back 3 m", "reduce to 6 floors", "designate the corner lot as open space". A committee can adopt a remedy; it cannot adopt an emotion.

## Worked example

Rania Haddad lives in a three-storey building in Haifa's Wadi Nisnas neighbourhood. A plan for a 14-storey hotel on the adjoining lot was deposited by the Haifa district committee; the newspaper notice appeared on 2 and 4 September 2026.

1. **Standing.** Rania owns a flat next to the plot; the tower would block her light and the plan cancels a small public garden she uses: an interest in land and in a "planning detail" (s. 100).
2. **Deadline.** Two months from the later newspaper notice, 4 September: 4 November 2026 (s. 102). She diarises 25 October as her own filing date.
3. **Where.** A plan in district power: to the Haifa district committee, copy to the Haifa local committee (s. 103(2)).
4. **Grounds.** Planning: loss of the only public garden in a dense neighbourhood (s. 61(5)); shadow study missing; traffic in a narrow street. Legal: the deposit sign was posted only in Hebrew; if Arabic speakers are at least 10% of the population of the local planning area (she checks the CBS figures for Haifa), the sign must also be in Arabic (s. 89א(ב)).
5. **Remedies.** Keep the garden or replace it nearby; reduce to 8 floors; require a shadow study; repost the notice in Arabic and extend the period.
6. **Form.** Written reasons, an affidavit signed before a lawyer, her address, four copies, proof of the copy to the local committee.
7. **After.** At the hearing she speaks first and replies last. If her objection is rejected, she may ask the district chair for leave to appeal to the National Council within 15 days (s. 110), or petition the Administrative Affairs Court.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A deposited plan (from lesson 27.2) or Rania's case.
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. Write the deposit date (later newspaper notice) and compute the objection deadline.
2. Identify the deciding institution and where the copy goes (s. 103).
3. Write your standing in two sentences: your interest and the harm.
4. List three grounds (at least one planning and one legal), each with the evidence you would attach.
5. Write one concrete remedy for each ground.
6. Decide who will sign the affidavit and where (lesson [23.9](../module-23/lesson-09.md)).
7. Write the appeal route and deadline if the objection is rejected.

### Template

Draft: [Objection to a deposited plan](../../templates/plan-objection.md) (used in full in lesson 27.9).

### How you know it worked

- The deadline is computed from the right date.
- Every ground has evidence and a remedy.
- You know the next step and its deadline if you lose.

- [ ] I know which appeals need leave.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Objections are designed for the public and are filed by individuals in their own name; the affidavit must be signed before a lawyer (or another person authorised to take affidavits). Neighbours may file a joint objection, and in public environmental matters one objector may file the affidavit for a group (s. 103א(ב)). Drafting objections for others for pay, or representing others as an occupation, is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20). Representation of others before appeals committees by non-lawyers: unverified.

### Where and how

- The institution named in the notice, in four copies or online (⚠️ manual check), with the copy and proof required by s. 103 and reg. 4.
- Hearings are public; bring a short written summary and photos.
- Free help: environmental and planning NGOs that are approved public bodies under s. 100(3), community planners, and [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The plan expropriates or redesignates your own land.
- You want to petition the court after the objection, especially with an interim order.
- The developer threatens costs or a claim, or the plan is a national infrastructure plan with its own rules.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| No affidavit | Objection not heard (s. 103א) | Ask to correct under reg. 7 before the hearing | Sign the affidavit when you sign the objection |
| Counting from the first newspaper notice | You think you are late, or you calculate wrongly | Recount from the later publication (s. 102) | Write both dates |
| New arguments only at the hearing | Not heard (reg. 14) | Ask for special leave, with reasons | Put every ground in writing |
| Personal attacks on the developer | Credibility lost; costs risk (s. 106(ד)) | Re-focus on planning grounds | Use the grounds list above |
| Skipping the objection and petitioning later | Petition weakened or dismissed | Explain why you could not object | Object at deposit |

## Review

- Anyone with an interest in land, a building or a planning detail who is harmed may object within two months of the later newspaper notice.
- The objection goes to the deciding institution with a copy to the other committee, in writing, with reasons and an affidavit.
- Hearings are public; only written grounds may be argued; reasons must be given in writing.
- Appeals: local plans to the appeals committee in 15 days; district plans to the National Council with leave; otherwise an administrative petition within 45 days.
- Relief and non-conforming use have their own 15-day objection under s. 149.
