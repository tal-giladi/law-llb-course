# 27.4 · Building permits and exemptions

By the end of this lesson you will be able to tell whether a job (a pergola, a storeroom, an enclosed balcony, an extra room, a change of use) needs a building permit, is exempt, or needs a special decision such as relief; follow the online permit process; and know who can appeal a permit decision. You will also be able to check a property's permits before you buy or rent it.

## Why it matters

Building without a permit is a criminal offence and exposes the owner, the occupier and the contractor to demolition orders and heavy fines (lesson 27.5). Buying a flat with an unpermitted extension means buying that exposure, and sometimes a refused mortgage or a blocked electricity upgrade (s. 157א). On the other side, neighbours often assume they can stop any permit; in fact the law gives them a voice mainly when the applicant asks to deviate from the plan.

The licensing reform of 2014–2016 moved the whole process online ("רישוי זמין"), introduced "information for a permit" with deadlines, private control institutes (מכוני בקרה) that check structural and safety design, and an exemption list for simple works. A 2022 amendment added licensed "permit authorisers" (מורשה להיתר) who may issue some residential permits instead of the committee. The policy aim is speed and certainty; the critics' concern is weaker local control. The tools you need depend on which side of the fence you are on.

## The law

### What needs a permit: s. 145(א)

> לא יעשה אדם אחד מאלה ולא יתחיל לעשותו אלא לאחר שנתנה לו רשות הרישוי המקומית היתר לכך ולא יעשה אותו אלא בהתאם לתנאי ההיתר: [...] (2) הקמתו של בנין, הריסתו והקמתו שנית, כולו או מקצתו, הוספה לבנין קיים וכל תיקון בו, למעט שינוי פנימי בדירה;

*Gist:* Building, demolishing, adding to or repairing a building, laying a road, and any use or work the regulations list need a permit and must follow its conditions; an internal change in a flat that does not touch the exterior, the structure, common property or shared pipes, harm others, or change the area or number of units does not. [s. 145(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#סעיף_145)

### Permits only by the plan: s. 145(ב)

A permit is granted only if the work or use conforms to the plans, the spatial guidelines and the Law (s. 145(ב)(1)); minor deviations with negligible effect listed by the Minister may be allowed without relief proceedings (s. 145(ב)(2)). The licensing authority decides within 45 working days from intake of the application (s. 145(ב1)); if it does not, the applicant may go to the appeals committee as if refused (s. 157(א)). (see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

### Exemptions: s. 145ג and the 2014 Regulations

The Minister exempts works that are simple, create no real risk or nuisance, have little effect on the building's look and the area, and do not harm the structure (s. 145ג(א)). An exemption does not permit anything contrary to a plan (s. 145ג(ד)). The Exemption Regulations add general conditions:

> הם יבוצעו בידי בעל זכות במקרקעין [...]; הם יבוצעו באופן שתובטח יציבות המבנה [...]; הם מתאימים לתכניות ולהנחיות המרחביות החלות במקום [...]; הם אינם בתחום מגרש, אתר, מתחם, או בניין המיועדים לשימור [...]

*Gist:* Exempt work must be done by a rights holder, be stable and safe, conform to the plans and spatial guidelines, and not be in a preservation site or on the seashore. [Exemption Regs reg. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_התכנון_והבנייה_%28עבודות_ומבנים_הפטורים_מהיתר%29)

Examples: a pergola of up to 50 m² or a quarter of the free area (the larger), on the ground or roof, with notice to the licensing authority within 45 days and an engineer's stability approval (reg. 12); one light-material storeroom per unit, up to 6 m² and 2.05 m high, not connected to utilities, with notice within 45 days (reg. 29); fences, sunshades, air conditioners, solar heaters and photovoltaic panels under conditions. Notices may be filed online (reg. 4א). An exemption is no defence to a neighbour's civil claim or to the co-owners' rights (reg. 4).

### Relief, non-conforming use and substantial deviation: ss. 146–151

The local committee (not the licensing authority) may grant relief (הקלה) and allow non-conforming use (ss. 146–147), only after publication, a notice on the building and notices to owners and occupiers of the plot and adjoining plots, with 15 days to object (s. 149). Relief may not add floor area, units or floors and may not be a "substantial deviation" (s. 151(ב)); non-conforming use from a plan is granted for a limited time (s. 148).

### Consent of co-owners: Licensing Regulations regs. 36–37

An application needs the consent of all rights holders in the property; a rights holder who does not consent receives notice and may object within 15 days (regs. 36–37). In a condominium this meets the consent rules of the Land Law (lesson [13.6](../module-13/lesson-06.md)): planning permission and property consent are separate requirements.

### Appeal: s. 152

> הרואה עצמו נפגע מהחלטה של ועדה מקומית או של רשות רישוי מקומית לסרב לתת היתר לפי פרק זה או לדחות התנגדות שהוגשה לפי הוראות סעיף 149(א) רשאי לערור בפני ועדת הערר תוך שלושים ימים מיום שהומצאה לו ההחלטה [...]

*Gist:* The applicant refused a permit, and a person whose objection to relief or non-conforming use was rejected, may appeal to the appeals committee within 30 days; two committee members or two advisory representatives may appeal within 15 days; while the appeal is pending no permit is issued unless the committee decides otherwise. [s. 152](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#סעיף_152)

A neighbour who objects to an ordinary permit that conforms to the plan has no statutory appeal; the route is an administrative petition (lesson [14.11](../module-14/lesson-11.md)). For permits issued by a permit authoriser, only the licensing authority may appeal (s. 158נז).

### Permit validity and completion

A permit is valid for six years from issue, up to nine for complex works; a permit for a use lapses if the use does not begin within a year (Licensing Regulations reg. 59). Utilities: the electricity company connects a new building only with the committee's approval (s. 157א).

## Leading cases

### ע״א 4079/05 הוועדה המקומית לתכנון ובניה שומרון נ' מעונה חברה לבנין בע״מ (11.11.2010)

The Supreme Court held a local committee liable in tort to a developer for damage caused by an unlawful permit it had issued, although the developer knew the permit was unlawful and had applied for it; the damages were reduced for the developer's contributory fault. Justice Procaccia described a "troubling dynamic" in which local committees bend the law to promote local development together with developers. Why it matters: an unlawful permit is not a safe harbour for anyone, and a committee that grants one may pay for it. Source: [Hebrew Wikipedia](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/ועדה_מקומית_לתכנון_ולבנייה) (secondary; the ruling's text on the Judiciary's site could not be reached).

### Key text: s. 145ג(ד) — "exempt" is not "permitted"

An exemption removes the need for a permit, not the need to conform to the plan. A pergola that is exempt in size but sits beyond the building line, or a storeroom placed on a parking space designated in the permit, is unlawful. Why it matters: before relying on an exemption, check the plan's building lines and the spatial guidelines.

## How it works in practice

### The online route (רישוי זמין)

1. **Information for a permit.** Request through the online system; the engineer answers within the regulatory period (reg. 20); valid two years (reg. 23).
2. **Licensed designer.** An architect or engineer (עורך הבקשה) prepares and files the application; owners' consents or notices (regs. 36–37).
3. **Intake and check.** The engineer checks preconditions and conformity; relief or non-conforming use goes to the committee after s. 149 publication.
4. **Decision.** The licensing authority decides within 45 working days of intake (s. 145(ב1)).
5. **Design control.** A control institute checks structural and safety design where required (s. 145(ב3)).
6. **Fees and levies.** Permit fees, development levies and betterment levy (lesson 27.6) are paid before the permit issues.
7. **Execution and completion.** Start-of-works approval, supervision, completion certificate (תעודת גמר).

### Checking a property

Ask the committee for the building file (תיק בניין) and the permit plans; compare them with the flat (extra rooms, enclosed balconies, roof structures); check for enforcement files, orders or administrative fines; and check the arnona measurement against the permitted area (lesson 27.8). See lesson [13.9](../module-13/lesson-09.md) for the purchase checklist.

## Worked example

Moshe and Esti Friedman live on the ground floor of a condominium in Beit Shemesh with a private garden attached to their flat. They want (a) a 20 m² aluminium pergola, (b) a 6 m² storeroom, (c) to enclose a 9 m² covered porch as a room, and (d) to open a home kindergarten.

1. **Pergola.** Exempt under reg. 12 if within the plan's building lines and spatial guidelines, by the rights holder, with an engineer's approval and notice to the licensing authority within 45 days.
2. **Storeroom.** Exempt under reg. 29: light material, up to 6 m², 2.05 m high, no utilities, not on a designated parking area; notice within 45 days.
3. **Enclosure.** Changes the façade and adds area: a permit is required (s. 145(א)(2)); if the plan has no unused rights it needs relief, which cannot add floor area (s. 151(ב)), so a plan change may be needed. The garden attachment and common property also need the co-owners' consent (lesson 13.6).
4. **Kindergarten.** A non-residential use in a residential zone: non-conforming use (s. 146), with publication and 15 days for neighbours to object (s. 149), granted for a limited time (s. 148), plus a business licence where required.
5. **Neighbours.** If the committee approves the kindergarten over a neighbour's objection, that neighbour may appeal within 30 days (s. 152(א)(1)); if it refuses, the Friedmans may appeal.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your own home or a property you are interested in, or the Friedmans' list.
- The plan profile from lesson 27.2.
- The Exemption Regulations (linked above).
- 60 minutes.

### Steps

1. List every change you want to make, or every change you see in the property compared with what you expect it was built as.
2. For each item decide: internal change, exempt (which regulation and which conditions), permit, relief, non-conforming use, or plan change.
3. For exempt items, list the conditions and the notice you must file.
4. For permit items, list the documents: information for a permit, designer, consents, control institute.
5. If you are checking a property: request the building file and compare permitted plans with reality.
6. Write a one-page "permit status" note with your conclusions and open questions for the committee engineer.

### Template

None. Use the decision categories in step 2 as columns.

### How you know it worked

- Each item has a category and a legal source.
- Exempt items list their conditions and notices.
- You know who could object or appeal for each item.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Partly. Owners may file exemption notices and objections to relief themselves, and may request their building file. A permit application must be prepared and filed by a licensed designer (architect or engineer), and structural approvals need an engineer. An appeal against a refusal or an objection decision may be filed in your own name.

### Where and how

- The online licensing system of the Planning Administration ("רישוי זמין") and the local committee's engineering department (⚠️ manual check of the current portal).
- Exemption notices on the form in the Exemption Regulations' schedule, online (reg. 4א).
- Appeals: the district appeals committee, within 30 days (s. 152).
- Free help: [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- An enforcement file, order or fine already exists on the property.
- You are buying or selling a property with unpermitted parts.
- A neighbour's works affect your property rights or common property and the co-owners disagree.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Building an "exempt" pergola beyond the building line | Unpermitted work; fines | Move or reduce it; apply for relief | Check the plan, not only the size limit |
| No notice for exempt work | The exemption may be lost; enforcement | File the notice late with the engineer's approval | Diarise 45 days from completion |
| Enclosing a balcony as an "internal change" | Unpermitted addition to area and façade | Apply for a permit or restore | Read the definition in s. 145(א)(2) |
| Neighbour appeals a conforming permit to the appeals committee | Dismissed for no right of appeal | Consider a petition, quickly | Check s. 152 standing first |
| Ignoring co-owners' consent | Civil claim and possible permit challenge | Obtain consent or the superintendent's order | Apply regs. 36–37 and the Land Law together |

## Review

- Building, adding and most works need a permit; internal changes in a flat do not.
- Simple works may be exempt under the 2014 Regulations, but must still conform to the plans and be notified.
- Relief and non-conforming use are decided by the committee after publication and a 15-day objection; relief cannot add area, units or floors.
- Applicants refused a permit and objectors to relief may appeal within 30 days; neighbours of a conforming permit generally must petition.
- Permits are processed online, decided within 45 working days, and valid for six years.
