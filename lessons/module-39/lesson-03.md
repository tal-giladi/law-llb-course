# 39.3 · Building the evidence and research file

Your case theory is only as strong as the proof behind each element and the law behind each step. In this lesson you build the two files a careful advocate keeps: the evidence file, organised as a proof matrix with an exhibit index, and the research file, a short memo for each contested legal question. You collect only what you may lawfully collect, ask for the records that others hold about you, and check that every rule you rely on is the current text, read in full.

## Why it matters

Most self-represented cases are lost on proof, not on law. The judge accepts the rule you cite and then asks for the document that shows the fact, and it is at home, or it is a screenshot without a date, or it is a recording of a conversation you were not part of. The clinics taught the rules: lawful collection in lesson [12.2](../module-12/lesson-02.md), documents and digital evidence in lesson [23.4](../module-23/lesson-04.md), the affidavit and exhibit bundle in lesson [23.9](../module-23/lesson-09.md). The capstone asks you to apply them to a matter you chose, before you write the letter, so that every sentence of the letter can point to an exhibit.

The research file matters for a different reason. Your strategy map (lesson [39.2](lesson-02.md)) named the other side's likely defences. Each defence that turns on a point of law is a research question, and the answer changes your remedy, your route or your decision to get a lawyer. A memo written now costs two hours; a wrong assumption discovered in the defence costs the case.

## The law

### Recording a conversation you take part in

> ”האזנת סתר“ – האזנה ללא הסכמה של אף אחד מבעלי השיחה;

*Gist:* Secret eavesdropping is listening to or recording a conversation with the consent of none of its parties; a party who records his own conversation is not eavesdropping. ([Eavesdropping Law s. 1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_האזנת_סתר)) Recording a conversation between other people is an offence and the recording is inadmissible (s. 13). A recording you made of your own call is generally usable; the court still weighs it, and you need a transcript (lesson 12.2).

### Material obtained by infringing privacy

> חומר שהושג תוך פגיעה בפרטיות יהיה פסול לשמש ראיה בבית משפט, ללא הסכמת הנפגע, זולת אם בית המשפט התיר מטעמים שיירשמו להשתמש בחומר, או אם היו לפוגע, שהיה צד להליך, הגנה או פטור לפי חוק זה.

*Gist:* Material obtained by infringing privacy is inadmissible without the victim's consent, unless the court allows it for recorded reasons or the party has a defence under the Law. ([Privacy Law s. 32](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_הפרטיות)) Logging into another person's account, copying his messages from his phone or photographing inside his home are the classic traps; they can also expose you to a privacy suit.

### Records others hold about you

> (א) מטופל זכאי לקבל מהמטפל או מהמוסד הרפואי מידע רפואי מהרשומה הרפואית, לרבות העתקה, המתייחסת אליו.

*Gist:* A patient is entitled to his medical information from the record, including a copy. ([Patient's Rights Law s. 18](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_זכויות_החולה))

> לכל אזרח ישראלי או תושב הזכות לקבל מידע מרשות ציבורית בהתאם להוראות חוק זה.

*Gist:* Every citizen or resident may obtain information from a public authority under the FOI Law. ([FOI Law s. 1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חופש_המידע)) Information about yourself is free of the application fee (lesson [14.7](../module-14/lesson-07.md); [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).

Other records you can ask for in your own right appear in the lessons for each field: payslips and the notice of employment terms (lesson [17.3](../module-17/lesson-03.md)), your pension fund's statements, your own criminal information (lesson [24.10](../module-24/lesson-10.md)), your credit data (lesson [29.7](../module-29/lesson-07.md)), a land-registry extract that anyone may obtain (lesson [13.2](../module-13/lesson-02.md)).

### Proving records and signing affidavits

An institution's records (a bank statement, a phone company's log, a hospital file) can prove their content as institutional records under Evidence Ordinance s. 36 (lesson 23.4). Your affidavit must be signed before a lawyer or another person listed in s. 15, who gives the warning; that person does not represent you ([Evidence Ordinance](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_הראיות)). In small claims, a statement of claim filed from 11 April 2025 must list the documents and witnesses you rely on ([Small Claims Regulations reg. 3](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיפוט_בתביעות_קטנות_(סדרי_דין)); lesson [12.5](../module-12/lesson-05.md)).

## Leading cases

### Key text: the definitions in s. 1 of the Eavesdropping Law

No ruling is needed for the everyday question whether you may use a recording of your own call: the definition answers it. Where a ruling would matter (a recording you were not part of, a hidden camera, a tracking app) you are outside what this capstone should rely on. The rules are taught in lesson 12.2 from the text.

### בג״ץ 6650/04 פלונית נ׳ בית הדין הרבני האזורי בנתניה (14.5.2006)

A rabbinical court relied on photographs a husband took while infringing his wife's privacy. The High Court of Justice held that the Privacy Law, including its exclusion of privacy-infringing material, applies in all courts, rabbinical courts included, and that marriage does not waive one spouse's privacy against the other. Why it matters for your evidence file: the rule about how evidence was obtained follows you into every forum, and "I only did it to prove the truth" is not a defence. Source: [Hebrew Wikipedia, חוק הגנת הפרטיות](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_הפרטיות) (⚠️ secondary source; taught in lesson [23.5](../module-23/lesson-05.md)).

## How it works in practice

### The proof matrix

Copy the elements from part ג of your strategy map and expand each into a row:

| Column | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| Element | From the rule (lesson 39.2) |
| Fact | One fact per row, with a date |
| Evidence | The exhibit number or the witness |
| How obtained | Your own document; sent to you; requested; public; your own recording |
| Admissibility check | Lawful? Original or certified copy? Hearsay? Institutional record? |
| Weight | 1–3: does it prove the fact by itself, or only with other evidence? |
| Gap and plan | What is missing and the lawful way to get it, with a date |

A fact without evidence is a gap; a gap on an element is a risk on your map. Fill the gaps you can; for the rest, change the theory or the remedy.

### Where evidence comes from

| Source | Examples | How to keep it |
|---|---|---|
| Documents you hold | Contract, quote, receipts, bank transfers, letters | Originals in a folder; a PDF of each, named by exhibit number |
| Messages | WhatsApp, email, SMS | Export the whole chat with dates (not only screenshots); keep the device |
| Photos and video | Before, during, after | Keep the original files with their metadata; note who took each and when |
| Your own recordings | A call you took part in | Original file, date, participants, and a typed transcript of the relevant part |
| Records others hold about you | Payslips, pension statements, medical file, authority file, credit report | A written request, logged with its date and the answer |
| Witnesses | A neighbour, a colleague, a second contractor | Ask whether they will come; each writes his own affidavit if one is needed |
| Professional reports | A plumber's or engineer's report, a quote | Signed, dated, with photographs and the method used |

### The records-request log

| Request | To whom | Legal basis | Sent | Proof | Answer due | Answer | Next step |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pension fund statements, all years | The fund | Your right as a member | | | | | |
| FOI or own-information request | The authority | FOI Law | | | | | |

### The research file

For each contested legal question on your strategy map, write a two-page [research memo](../../templates/research-memo.md) (lesson 02.6):

1. **The question,** specific to your facts.
2. **The current text** of each section you rely on, read in full on a free source (lesson [02.1](../module-02/lesson-01.md)), with the date you checked it and any amendment.
3. **Rulings you actually read** on a free host (lesson [02.2](../module-02/lesson-02.md)): full citation, the holding in one sentence, whether it binds your forum. If you could not read the full text, say so and do not rely on it.
4. **The answer and its confidence:** clear, probable, or uncertain. An uncertain answer on a decisive point is a red flag on the get-a-lawyer checklist.

Add a procedure note for your forum: the form, the page limit, what must be attached, the fee and its exemptions, how service works ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).

### The exhibit index

Number the exhibits in the order they appear in your story, not in the order you found them. For a forum that takes affidavits, prepare the [affidavit and exhibit bundle](../../templates/affidavit-and-exhibit-bundle.md) of lesson 23.9; for small claims, the document list and witness list that reg. 3 requires; for an office or tribunal, the annexes its form asks for.

## Worked example

**Samira's evidence file.** Elements and exhibits: employment period (contract of 2020, payslips for the last year, her Form 161); the transfer (the company's message of 12 June closing the branch and naming Karmiel); material deterioration (a printout of the public-transport journey planner showing the travel time from her home at 07:00, the children's school registration, a note of the hours she would lose each week); prior warning (her email of 10 July, with the delivery receipt, asking to stay in Haifa or be dismissed); resignation because of the transfer (her letter of 1 August, which says so); the amount (her pension fund's annual statements showing the severance component and the percentage deposited). Gaps: the fund statements for the first two years (she requests them from the fund on 20 October and logs the request); whether a branch existed in Haifa she could have moved to (she asks a former colleague, who agrees to give evidence).

Her research file has two memos. *Question 1:* when do the labour courts treat a transfer to another branch as a material deterioration, and does a transfer clause in the contract change the answer? She reads the statute, the Severance Pay Regulations on resignation treated as dismissal, and the rulings she finds on the Judiciary's free decisions search, records the citation and holding of each one she read in full, and grades her answer "probable". *Question 2:* how is the top-up computed under a s. 14 arrangement at the rate her fund shows? Lesson 17.4 warns that the general approval's terms must be checked; she marks the answer "uncertain until the fund statements arrive" and keeps the claim sum provisional.

**Tsega's evidence file.** Contract and scope (the contractor's WhatsApp quote: "ריצוף ואיטום מקלחת כולל שיפועים", ₪28,000); payment (three bank transfers totalling ₪22,000); the defect (the plumber's signed report with a pressure test of the building pipes and photographs of the missing waterproofing in the corner; her own video of water pooling); notice and chance to repair (the WhatsApp chat exported in full, showing her message of 14 September and his visit on 17 September); his answer (a recording of her call on 20 September in which he says "it's the building's pipes, not my problem"; she was a party, so it is not eavesdropping; she types a transcript of the two relevant minutes); loss (the second contractor's itemised quote; the downstairs neighbour's receipt for the ceiling repair she reimbursed). The neighbour agrees to come to the hearing. Gap: the contractor's full legal name and ID number for the claim; she finds his business details on his invoice to her and checks them against his public business page.

Her research file: *Question 1:* is her WhatsApp message a sufficient notice under s. 3(א)? Probably, but she will send a formal written notice with a fixed date anyway (lesson 39.4), which removes the question. *Question 2:* can she recover the neighbour's ceiling? She reads the foreseeability rule for damages in the Remedies Law (lesson [03.8](../module-03/lesson-08.md)) and grades it "probable": a leak into the flat below is the natural result of a floor without waterproofing. *Procedure note:* small claims, a body of no more than five pages, documents and witnesses listed, the fee in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md), the venue rule in lesson 12.5.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- Your strategy map from lesson 39.2, part ג (elements and proof).
- Your documents, devices and email.
- The [clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md) part ד (evidence index), the [research memo](../../templates/research-memo.md), the [FOI request](../../templates/foi-request.md) if a public authority holds records, and the [affidavit and exhibit bundle](../../templates/affidavit-and-exhibit-bundle.md) if your forum uses affidavits.
- About 180 minutes, plus waiting for answers.

### Steps

1. **Gather (40 min).** Collect every document, message, photo and recording you hold. Export chats whole; keep originals; name each file by a provisional exhibit number.
2. **Proof matrix (40 min).** One row per fact, under its element. Fill the admissibility check honestly: anything obtained from someone else's device, account or home goes in a separate column marked "do not use without advice".
3. **Requests (20 min).** Send the requests for records others hold about you: employer, fund, clinic, authority, bank. Log each with its legal basis and proof of sending.
4. **Witnesses (15 min).** Ask each witness whether they will give evidence. Do not write their affidavit for them; tell them which facts the case is about.
5. **Research (50 min).** One memo per contested legal question; read the current text and the rulings in full on free sources; grade the answer.
6. **Procedure note (15 min).** For your forum: form, attachments, page limit, fee and exemption, service, the next deadline.
7. **Index (10 min).** Renumber the exhibits in story order; update the evidence index and the strategy map's gaps column.

### Template

- [Clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md), part ד (evidence index) and part ו (log the requests).
- [Research memo](../../templates/research-memo.md) (lesson 02.6).
- [FOI request](../../templates/foi-request.md) (lesson 14.10), for records held by a public authority.
- [Affidavit and exhibit bundle](../../templates/affidavit-and-exhibit-bundle.md) (lesson 23.9), where the forum uses affidavits.

### How you know it worked

- Every element on your map has at least one exhibit of weight 2 or more, or a written decision about the gap.
- Every exhibit has a lawful "how obtained" entry.
- Every memo cites only texts and rulings you read, with the date you checked them.
- Every records request has a logged date and an answer-due date.

### Self-check

- [ ] I exported whole conversations, not only the helpful screenshots.
- [ ] My recordings are only of conversations I took part in, and each has a transcript.
- [ ] I did not write anyone else's affidavit.
- [ ] I know what my forum requires me to attach, and by when.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Collecting your own documents, requesting records about yourself, and researching the law of your own matter are open to everyone. Your affidavit is signed before a lawyer or another person listed in Evidence Ordinance s. 15, who confirms the warning without representing you. Advising others on their evidence for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).

### Where and how

- Records requests: to the body that holds the record, in writing, with proof of sending; FOI requests through the authority's FOI officer or the government online service (lesson 14.7).
- Free legal texts and rulings: Wikisource and the Knesset database for legislation, the Judiciary's free decisions search for rulings (lessons 02.1–02.2).
- Fees and periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); free help: [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- Your strongest evidence came from someone else's phone, account or home.
- A decisive memo ends "uncertain" and the other side is represented.
- The case needs an expert opinion beyond a tradesman's report (medical, engineering, valuation) and the other side will contest it.
- A records holder refuses a request you are entitled to and the matter cannot wait for a petition.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Screenshots without dates or context | The other side says they are selective | Export the whole chat; keep the device | Export on the day you collect |
| Recording a conversation you were not part of | Inadmissible; possible offence | Do not use it; get advice | Record only your own calls |
| Relying on a ruling you only saw summarised | It turns out to say something else, or was overturned | Read the full text or drop the citation | Cite only what you read |
| Writing a witness's affidavit for him | Weight lost; s. 20 problem | The witness writes his own | Tell him the facts in issue, nothing more |
| Waiting for records without a log | Weeks pass unnoticed | Chase in writing with the original date | Log every request with an answer-due date |

## Review

- The proof matrix ties every element to a fact, every fact to an exhibit, and every exhibit to a lawful source; gaps become tasks or changes to the theory.
- Record only conversations you take part in; material obtained by infringing privacy is presumptively inadmissible in every court.
- Ask in writing for the records others hold about you, and log every request.
- Research only the contested points, from current texts and rulings you actually read, and grade each answer.
- Number exhibits in story order and prepare what your forum requires you to attach.
