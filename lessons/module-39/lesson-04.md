# 39.4 · The documents: letters, filings and submissions

Every move on your strategy map is made by a document: a notice, a demand, a request, a claim, a reply, a submission, a settlement. In this lesson you match each stage of your matter to the document that does its job and to the course template it starts from, draft the first letter and the formal filing to the standard your forum expects, check them against a self-review list, and send the first one for real, in your own name, with proof of delivery.

## Why it matters

The course has given you more than seventy templates. The capstone tests whether you can pick the right one, adapt it to facts it was not written for, and leave out what does not belong. A demand letter that reads like a statement of claim frightens no one and commits you to positions too early; a statement of claim that reads like a letter is struck out or ignored. Each forum also has its own form: small claims caps the body at five pages and requires lists of documents and witnesses (lesson [12.5](../module-12/lesson-05.md)); the labour court wants the particulars of reg. 9 "and nothing else" (lesson [22.2](../module-22/lesson-02.md)); an authority or tribunal has its own form, online system or annex list.

Documents are also evidence. The letter you send today will be exhibit 9 in the claim; the other side will read every sentence for an admission. Write only what you can prove, in a tone you would be comfortable having read aloud by a judge.

## The law

### Your own documents, not others'

Drafting legal documents for another person as an occupation or for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20(3); [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לשכת_עורכי_הדין)). Everything you draft in the capstone is signed by you and concerns your own matter. If a relative asks you to "use your template" for their dispute, give them the course's template page, not a filled document.

### What the forum requires

> בגוף כתב התביעה יפרט התובע את כל העניינים האלה: (1) מקום המעשה או המחדל שבשלו תובעים; (2) הסעד המבוקש; (3) נימוקי התביעה; (4) רשימת המסמכים…; (5) רשימת העדים…; (6) אם הוא מעדיף לקיים את הדיון באמצעי טכנולוגי… וכן אם הוא מעדיף הכרעה על יסוד הכתב…; (7) מספר התביעות הקטנות שהגיש במהלך השנה האחרונה.

*Gist:* A small-claims statement sets out the place of the act, the remedy, the grounds, the documents, the witnesses, your hearing preference and your number of small claims in the last year, in a body of no more than five pages (reg. 3(ה)). ([Small Claims Regulations reg. 3](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיפוט_בתביעות_קטנות_(סדרי_דין)))

> אלה הפרטים שיכיל כתב תביעה, וחוץ מן האמור בתקנות להלן לא יכיל אלא פרטים אלה [...]

*Gist:* A labour-court claim contains the listed particulars (parties, facts of the cause of action, jurisdiction, the remedy, its value) and nothing else; claim the whole remedy on the same cause, since a remedy left out without leave cannot be claimed later (regs 9, 26). ([Labour Court Procedure Regulations](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_בית_הדין_לעבודה_(סדרי_דין)))

### Notice before self-help

For a contractor's defect, the customer must notify within a reasonable time and give the contractor a proper chance to repair, or he may not rely on the defect (Contract for Services Law s. 3; [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חוזה_קבלנות)). Many fields have a notice of this kind: the landlord's notice before using the deposit, the consumer's written request before exemplary damages, the employee's warning before a resignation that counts as dismissal. Your research file (lesson [39.3](lesson-03.md)) should say whether your matter has one; if it does, the notice is your first document.

### A settlement that can be enforced

> (ב) אין באמור בסעיף קטן (א) כדי לגרוע מסמכותו של בית המשפט להציע לבעלי הדין הסדר פשרה או לתת, לבקשת בעלי הדין, תוקף של פסק דין להסדר פשרה שעשו ביניהם.

*Gist:* Once a case is open, the parties may ask the court to give their settlement the force of a judgment, so that it can go straight to the Execution Office. ([Courts Law s. 79א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט#סעיף_79א)) Before a case is open, a settlement is a contract, which is why the default clause matters (lesson [12.3](../module-12/lesson-03.md)).

## Leading cases

### רע״א 9615/05 שמש נ׳ פוקצ׳טה בע״מ (5.7.2006)

The Supreme Court treated a self-represented small claim based on a protective statute as a serious vehicle for enforcing it. Why it matters for drafting: name the statute and the section, connect each element to an exhibit, and ask for a remedy the statute gives. A short document that pleads the law precisely is taken more seriously than a long one that pleads indignation. [English translation (Cardozo)](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/346)

### בג״ץ 3/58 ברמן נ׳ שר הפנים (30.10.1958)

An authority must give a person a fair opportunity to be heard before a decision that harms him, and a person who knows a process concerning him is under way should ask to be heard. Why it matters for drafting: when your first document goes to a public body, ask expressly to be heard and for a reasoned decision; the request itself becomes part of your case if it is ignored (lesson [37.2](../module-37/lesson-02.md)). [English translation (Cardozo)](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/93)

### The drafting standard

Every document in your capstone, whatever its template, has these parts:

1. **Heading:** your full name, ID number, address for service, phone and email; the other side's legal name and ID or company number, checked against its source (lesson [12.1](../module-12/lesson-01.md)).
2. **Subject line** that says what the document is ("הודעה על פגם ודרישה לתיקון"; "כתב תביעה").
3. **Numbered facts,** one per paragraph, each with its date and its exhibit number from your index.
4. **The legal basis,** by section, in one or two sentences per rule.
5. **The remedy or request,** precise: a sum broken down, an act with a date, a decision.
6. **The deadline** you give, or the deadline the law gives the other side.
7. **What happens next** if it is not met, without threats beyond the lawful route.
8. **Signature and date,** yours.

Leave out: adjectives, history that proves no element, insults, a criminal threat to get paid, and anything you concede only in negotiation. Mark settlement offers "ללא פגיעה בזכויות" where the offer should not be read as an admission.

### Sending and filing

- Letters: by email and by registered mail with delivery confirmation to the address in the contract and, for a company, to its registered office. Keep the receipt and a PDF of what you sent.
- Filings: online (Net HaMishpat, the small-claims service, the labour court's online claim service, an authority's portal) or at the registry; the fee or an exemption request with the first filing; numbers in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md). Write the filing date, the case number and the next deadline in your log the same day (lesson 39.5).

## Worked example

**Tsega's notice of defect** (from the [notice of breach](../../templates/notice-of-breach-and-cancellation.md), adapted to the Contract for Services Law). Its operative paragraphs:

```text
1. ביום 2.7.2026 התקשרנו בהסכם לשיפוץ חדר הרחצה בדירתי, לרבות "ריצוף ואיטום מקלחת כולל שיפועים", תמורת 28,000 ש"ח (נספח 1). שילמתי 22,000 ש"ח (נספח 2).
2. רצפת המקלחת נבנתה ללא שיפוע לניקוז ושכבת האיטום חסרה בפינה הצפון-מזרחית; מים חודרים לדירה שמתחת (חוות דעת אינסטלטור מיום 25.9.2026, נספח 3).
3. בהתאם לסעיף 3 לחוק חוזה קבלנות, התשל"ד-1974, אני מודיעה לך על הפגם ודורשת שתתקן אותו. אני מציעה את התאריכים 8.10.2026 או 9.10.2026 לתחילת העבודה.
4. אם לא תתקן את הפגם עד 18.10.2026, אתקן אותו באמצעות קבלן אחר ואדרוש ממך את הוצאותיי הסבירות, בניכוי יתרת התמורה שטרם שולמה (סעיף 4 לחוק).
```

She sends it by WhatsApp, email and registered mail on 1 October and keeps the delivery confirmation. He does not come. On 20 October she sends the [pre-action letter](../../templates/pre-action-letter.md) for ₪13,650 with seven days to pay. Her draft small claim follows the [small claim template](../../templates/small-claim-contract-consumer.md): four pages, the facts numbered with exhibits 1–9, the legal basis in three steps (contract and scope; defect, notice and failure to repair under ss. 3–4; the reasonable cost and the foreseeable damage below), the remedy broken down with the ₪6,000 deduction shown openly, the document and witness lists (the plumber, the neighbour), her preference for an in-person hearing, and the reg. 3 declaration.

**Samira's letter and claim.** Her letter to the company's management (from the [letter to employer](../../templates/letter-to-employer-wage-claim.md)) states her employment period, the transfer, her email of 10 July and the lack of an answer, her resignation "מחמת הרעה מוחשית בתנאי העבודה" under s. 11(א), and the top-up she claims, provisional until the fund statements arrive, with 14 days to pay. It does not argue the case law; that belongs in the hearing. The company's lawyer answers that she resigned and that the transfer clause governs. Her [labour-court claim](../../templates/labour-court-statement-of-claim.md) follows reg. 9 and Form 3 (severance): parties, employment details, the facts of the transfer and resignation, jurisdiction, the remedy with linkage and interest, the value, and the document list (reg. 11). She claims the whole remedy on the cause (reg. 26) and states that the sum is within the expedited ceiling ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)). She files it through the labour court's online claim service for unrepresented employees (⚠️ check the service's current scope and login on the day, lesson 22.2) and checks, in her procedure note, the fee rules for a severance claim (lesson 22.2).

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- Your strategy map (lesson 39.2), proof matrix and research file (lesson 39.3).
- The templates the ladder names for your stages.
- A way to prove delivery: registered mail with delivery confirmation, an email account that keeps sent items, the forum's online system.
- About 180 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Ladder (15 min).** For each stage on your map, write the document, the template and the delivery method.
2. **First document (45 min).** Draft the notice, demand or request. Use the drafting standard; cite exhibits by number.
3. **Self-review (15 min).** Run the checklist; fix every "no".
4. **Send (15 min).** Send it for real if your gates, deadlines and exposure check still pass; otherwise record why it stays a dry run. Log the date and proof.
5. **Formal filing draft (60 min).** Draft the claim, appeal, objection or application to the forum's form, so that it is ready the day a trigger fires.
6. **Settlement clauses (15 min).** Write the clauses you would want in a settlement: the sum and date, the method of payment, a default clause, the scope of the release, approval by the court if a case is open.
7. **Archive (15 min).** PDF every version you sent, named by date, in the case file; update the strategy map.

### Template

- The templates in the ladder above for your matter's stages.
- [Pre-action letter](../../templates/pre-action-letter.md) (lesson 08.8) for the last letter.
- [Clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md), part ו, to log each document sent.

### How you know it worked

- The first document is sent (or a written reason why not) and you hold proof of its delivery.
- The formal filing is complete in draft and fits your forum's form and length.
- Every fact in both documents has an exhibit number.
- Your settlement clauses are written before any negotiation.

### Self-check

- [ ] The document is signed by me and concerns only my matter.
- [ ] The remedy is the revised one from my case theory, with concessions shown.
- [ ] I gave a date, not "a reasonable time".
- [ ] I logged the sending date and the deadline it creates.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. You may write, sign, send and file your own letters, claims and requests in every forum the capstone allows (reg. 170 and the per-forum rows of [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md)). Drafting them for others as an occupation or for pay is reserved to lawyers (s. 20(3)).

### Where and how

- Letters: to the address for notices and, for a company, its registered office (Companies Registrar search).
- Small claims: the Judiciary's online service or the registry; labour court: the online claim service for unrepresented employees on the expedited track, Net HaMishpat or the secretariat (lesson 22.2); offices and tribunals: their portals or forms.
- Fees and exemptions: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); free help with forms: court information centres and the bodies in [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The other side's answer raises a defence your research file did not foresee and it goes to the heart of the claim.
- You are asked to sign a settlement that releases claims you have not identified, or includes a confidentiality or non-disparagement clause you do not understand.
- The forum's form asks for things you cannot supply (a lawyer's verification, a professional's signature).
- A document you sent is answered with a threat of a defamation suit or a criminal complaint.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| The other side's name or number wrong | Service fails; the judgment is against the wrong person | Amend and re-serve | Check the registry or the invoice before drafting |
| A demand letter that argues the whole case | You commit to positions early and give the defence a preview | Keep later letters short | One document, one job |
| A claim that leaves out part of the remedy | That part cannot be claimed later (labour reg. 26(ג); the general rule against splitting) | Ask leave to amend at once | Claim the whole remedy, or waive part expressly |
| No proof of delivery | The other side says it never received the notice | Re-send with registered mail and email | Registered mail with confirmation for every legal notice |
| An oral settlement | Nothing to enforce | Put it in writing; ask the court to approve it | Settle only in writing with a default clause |

## Review

- Each stage of the map has a document with one job and a template to start from.
- Every document follows the same standard: heading, numbered facts with exhibits, legal basis by section, precise remedy, a date, your signature.
- The forum's form governs: five pages and lists in small claims, reg. 9 and nothing else in the labour court, the office's form elsewhere.
- Send the first document for real, with proof of delivery, once the gates still pass.
- Write settlement clauses before negotiating; once a case is open, ask the court to give the settlement the force of a judgment.
