# 39.1 · Choosing your capstone matter

The capstone is one real matter of your own, from any field of the course, taken through the whole cycle: choice, case theory and strategy, evidence and research, documents, running the matter with a log, and a retrospective. In this lesson you list your candidate matters, look each one up in a cross-index of every matter type the course teaches, run them through a decision matrix that asks whether the matter is yours, whether you may and should act alone in its forum, and whether it is worth the effort, and you choose one. You end with an open file and the first deadline in your calendar.

## Why it matters

The four clinics (modules [12](../module-12/lesson-01.md), [22](../module-22/lesson-01.md), [31](../module-31/lesson-01.md) and [37](../module-37/lesson-01.md)) each handed you a family of matters and a track. The capstone hands you nothing. You decide which dispute, application or complaint in your life deserves a few months of disciplined work, and that decision is itself the first legal skill a practitioner uses: most lost cases were lost at intake, by taking the wrong matter, against the wrong party, in the wrong forum, or too late.

A good capstone matter has four properties. It is **yours**, so that you may act in it at all (Bar Association Law s. 20). It is **safe to run alone**, so that you are not exposing yourself to a criminal charge, a ruinous costs order or a counterclaim you cannot answer. It sits in a **forum where an individual may act and usually does** (small claims, the labour court's expedited track, a registrar, a tax or arnona objection, a tribunal, a public authority). And it has **enough at stake and enough stages** to exercise the cycle: a first letter, a filing or formal request, an answer, a decision point.

If you have no live dispute, you are not excused: every adult has a matter that passes all four tests, such as an employee's tax-refund review across the open years (lesson [31.5](../module-31/lesson-05.md)), an arnona audit (lesson [31.7](../module-31/lesson-07.md)), your own will (lesson [31.2](../module-31/lesson-02.md)), or a request for information an authority holds about you (lesson [14.10](../module-14/lesson-10.md)).

## The law

### Only your own matter: s. 20

> הפעולות המנויות להלן, לא יעשה אותן דרך עיסוק, או בתמורה אף שלא דרך עיסוק, אלא עורך דין; [...] (3) עריכת מסמכים בעלי אופי משפטי בשביל אדם אחר, לרבות ייצוג אדם אחר במשא ומתן משפטי לקראת עריכת מסמך כזה; (4) ייעוץ וחיווי דעת משפטיים.

*Gist:* Representing others, drafting legal documents for others and giving legal advice, as an occupation or for payment, are reserved to lawyers. ([Bar Association Law s. 20](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לשכת_עורכי_הדין)) Your capstone is your own matter: not your mother's benefit, not your brother's share in the flat, not your friend's ticket. You may help a relative understand their file; they sign and they appear (lesson [01.1](../module-01/lesson-01.md)).

### You may act for yourself: reg. 170

> כל פעולה בבית משפט הנדרשת מבעל דין או המותרת לו לפי דין, רשאי הוא לעשותה בעצמו או בידי עורך דין שמינה לכך כדין, והוא כשאין הוראה מפורשת אחרת בכל דין.

*Gist:* A party may take any step in court himself or through a lawyer, unless a law provides otherwise. ([Civil Procedure Regulations reg. 170](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי)) The exceptions matter for the choice: a company generally needs a lawyer; only an individual may sue in small claims (Courts Law s. 60), where a lawyer appears only with leave (s. 63); some criminal steps are closed to an unrepresented defendant (lesson [24.6](../module-24/lesson-06.md)); an enduring power of attorney must be signed before a trained lawyer (lesson [26.7](../module-26/lesson-07.md)). The forum-by-forum answer is in [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md).

### The clock decides some choices for you

> תביעה לקיים זכות כל שהיא נתונה להתיישנות, ואם הוגשה תובענה על תביעה שהתיישנה וטען הנתבע טענת התיישנות, לא יזדקק בית המשפט לתובענה [...]

*Gist:* Every claim is subject to limitation; if the defendant pleads it, the court will not hear a time-barred claim, though the right itself survives. ([Prescription Law s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההתיישנות)) The general civil period is in s. 5; special laws set shorter ones (insurance, late wages, administrative petitions, objections and appeals). All periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md). A matter whose door closes in five days is not a capstone; it is an emergency, and you act or get help at once.

## Leading cases

### רע״א 8120/08 (no "close friend" in court)

The Supreme Court held that, absent an express statutory provision, only a licensed lawyer may represent a party in court; there is no general right to be represented by a relative or a "close friend". Why it matters for the choice: the first gate of the matrix is legal, not practical. A matter that is "really" your parent's, sibling's or partner's cannot be your capstone, because you could not stand in for them at the hearing. [Full text (supremedecisions)](https://supremedecisions.court.gov.il/Home/Download?path=HebrewVerdicts%2F08%2F200%2F081%2Fv04&fileName=08081200_v04.txt&type=2) (⚠️ host unreachable on 18 October 2026; citation from the course research file).

### רע״א 9615/05 שמש נ׳ פוקצ׳טה בע״מ (5.7.2006)

A self-represented mother sued a restaurant in small claims for letting customers smoke; the case reached the Supreme Court, which increased her compensation and stressed that private claims are a legitimate way to enforce protective statutes. Why it matters for the choice: a matter with modest money but a clear statutory duty and good evidence can be the best capstone of all, because its value is not only the sum. Score "learning value" and "principle" honestly, but do not let them override the gates. [English translation (Cardozo)](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/346)

### The decision matrix

Run every candidate through three gates first. A matter that fails a gate is out, however attractive.

| Gate | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|
| G1 Yours | You are the party: the claimant, the applicant, the person the decision or record is about | A relative's, friend's or employer's matter; a company's claim |
| G2 Low exposure | Nothing you will write admits an offence; no investigation of you is open; a loss would not leave you with costs or a counterclaim you cannot bear | You are also a suspect; the other side has threatened a large counterclaim; a defamation risk in what you would publish |
| G3 Allowed alone | The cross-index and the reference page say an individual may act in that forum | The forum requires a lawyer or a trained professional for your step |

Then score each surviving candidate from 1 (poor) to 3 (good) and multiply by the weight.

| Criterion | Weight | 3 means | 1 means |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stakes | 3 | Meaningful to you, and a loss is bearable | Trivial, or so large that losing would hurt badly |
| Merits and proof | 3 | You hold most of the documents; the law is clear | It turns on your word against theirs, or on a contested point of law |
| Forum fit | 2 | Small claims, a labour expedited track, an office, a tribunal | A district court, the High Court, a multi-party case |
| Sensible alone | 3 | No red answer and at most one amber on the [get-a-lawyer checklist](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md) part א | One red, or two or more ambers |
| Time | 2 | Every fatal deadline is at least two weeks away, and a decision point can come within about six months | A deadline within days; or nothing can happen for a year |
| Personal cost | 1 | No relationship you need to keep; manageable stress | A neighbour, a family member, a current employer |
| Learning value | 1 | At least three stages: letter, formal step, answer or hearing | One form and done |

Maximum score: 45. A matter scoring 33 or more is a strong capstone; 25 to 32 is workable if the weak criteria are named in your strategy map; below 25, choose another or one of the always-available matters above.

## Worked example

Two learners carry their capstone through all six lessons of this module. Their names are invented.

**Samira Khoury**, 41, a pharmacist from Haifa, worked six years and two months for a private pharmacy company with four branches in the north. In June 2026 the company closed her Haifa branch and told her to report to its Karmiel branch, about an hour each way by bus; she has two children in school in Haifa. She wrote to the manager asking to stay in Haifa or to be dismissed with severance, received no answer, and resigned on 1 August with a letter saying she was resigning because of the transfer. The company paid her last wage and released her pension fund, but says she resigned and owes her nothing more. Her other candidates: her late father's flat, which she and her brother inherited and disagree about; and an arnona bill she thinks is for too many square metres.

| Candidate | G1 | G2 | G3 | Stakes ×3 | Merits ×3 | Forum ×2 | Alone ×3 | Time ×2 | Personal ×1 | Learning ×1 | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Severance top-up after a forced transfer | Yes | Yes | Yes (labour court) | 3 → 9 | 2 → 6 | 3 → 6 | 2 → 6 | 3 → 6 | 3 → 3 | 3 → 3 | **39** |
| Father's flat with her brother | Yes (as heir) | Yes | Partly | 3 → 9 | 2 → 6 | 1 → 2 | 1 → 3 | 2 → 4 | 1 → 1 | 2 → 2 | 27 |
| Arnona area | Yes | Yes | Yes | 1 → 3 | 3 → 9 | 3 → 6 | 3 → 9 | 3 → 6 | 3 → 3 | 1 → 1 | 37 |

Samira chooses the severance matter. Its merits are not certain (whether a transfer is a "material deterioration" under Severance Pay Law s. 11(א) is the whole case, lesson [17.4](../module-17/lesson-04.md)), and she scores "sensible alone" at 2 because the company will probably be represented. The arnona matter scores almost as high but has one stage; she keeps it as a side filing. The flat is a co-ownership and family matter with a represented sibling; she writes it down for a consultation, not for the capstone. Her first deadline: the general limitation period is years away, but the late-payment compensation for wages expires fast (lesson [22.1](../module-22/lesson-01.md)); she diarises a date to check which of her claims carry such a clock.

**Tsega Wondimu**, 36, a bus driver from Kiryat Malachi, paid a self-employed contractor ₪22,000 of a ₪28,000 price to renovate her bathroom. In September 2026 water began dripping into the downstairs flat; a plumber found that the shower floor had no slope to the drain and that the waterproofing layer was missing in one corner. The contractor came once, said "it's the building's pipes", and stopped answering. A second contractor quoted ₪16,800 to break and redo the floor. Her other candidates: a municipal parking fine; her mother's National Insurance old-age benefit, which was reduced; and pay docked by her employer for late arrival.

Her first deadline: she has already told the contractor about the defect by WhatsApp, but the Contract for Services Law expects notice and a chance to repair before she repairs at his expense (lesson [20.4](../module-20/lesson-04.md)); she diarises a written notice for this week, and asks the downstairs neighbour not to wait for her before claiming from the building's insurer.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- A list of every dispute, refusal, unanswered request, debt, decision or document in your life from the last two years that might be a legal matter; include the boring ones.
- The documents you hold for each.
- The cross-index above, the [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md) page, the [get-a-lawyer checklist](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md) and the draft [capstone strategy map](../../templates/capstone-strategy-map.md) (part א is the matrix).
- About 120 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Inventory (20 min).** Write at least five candidates. If you have fewer, add the always-available matters: tax-refund review, arnona audit, your own will, an information request about yourself.
2. **Cross-index (20 min).** For each candidate, find its row: the lessons, templates, forum and first step. A matter with no row is either outside the course (write "lawyer" next to it) or a combination of rows (name them).
3. **Gates (15 min).** G1, G2, G3 for each, with one sentence of reason. Check G3 in the reference page, not from memory.
4. **Score (25 min).** Score the survivors on the seven criteria. For "sensible alone", actually run part א of the get-a-lawyer checklist. For "merits", list the two or three facts you must prove and whether you hold a document for each.
5. **Choose (10 min).** Pick the highest-scoring matter that passes the gates. Write three sentences: why this one, its weakest criterion, and what you will do about it.
6. **Open the file (20 min).** Open the [clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md) parts א–ב for the chosen matter and the deadline table (part ז). Diarise every date you already know, with a reminder a week earlier.
7. **Park the rest (10 min).** For each matter you did not choose, one line: drop, later, a consultation, or a side filing.

- [Clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md) (module 12), parts א, ב and ז.
- [Get-a-lawyer checklist](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md) (lesson 12.8), part א.

### How you know it worked

- Your chosen matter passes all three gates, with a written reason for each.
- Every score has a reason a stranger could check (a document, a checklist answer, a date).
- The first deadline is in your calendar, with the event it runs from and the document proving that date.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Choosing which of your own matters to pursue is your decision alone; the gates make sure the matter you choose is one in which the law lets an individual act (reg. 170; Bar Association Law s. 20; the per-forum exceptions in [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md)).

### Where and how

- No filing in this lesson; the first real-world step is in lesson 39.4.
- If the forum or the deadline is unclear, the court information centres, Kol-Zchut and the clinics listed in [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md) can tell you where a matter belongs, without taking it over.
- Periods for the deadline table: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- Every candidate fails G2: you are a suspect, or a counterclaim or defamation risk sits over all of them.
- The matter you care most about fails G3 or scores 1 on "sensible alone": book a consultation now and choose a different capstone.
- A fatal deadline is within days and you are not sure of the forum.
- The other side is a represented company and the sum is large relative to your income.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Choosing a relative's matter "because I know it best" | You cannot sign, file or appear; unlawful if paid | Help the relative run it themselves; choose your own | Apply G1 first |
| Choosing the biggest matter for the experience | A represented opponent, an expert dispute, a costs risk | Downgrade it to a consultation; pick a matter scoring 33+ | Score stakes as "meaningful but bearable" |
| Scoring merits on how wronged you feel | A weak case that collapses on the first answer | Re-score on documents only | List the facts to prove and the document for each |
| Ignoring a short special period | The claim or appeal is lost before you start | Act at once or get help | Diarise every period on the day you choose |
| A matter with one stage and nothing to decide | A thin capstone | Add a side filing or choose again | Score learning value |

## Review

- A capstone matter must pass three gates: it is yours, it carries low exposure, and the law lets an individual act in its forum.
- The cross-index places any matter: the lessons, templates, forum and whether acting alone is allowed and sensible.
- The weighted matrix (stakes, merits, forum fit, sensible alone, time, personal cost, learning value) turns intuition into a reasoned choice; write the reason and the weakest criterion.
- The always-available matters (tax refund, arnona, will, information request) mean every learner can run a real capstone.
- Diarise the first deadline on the day you choose.
