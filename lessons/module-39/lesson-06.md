# 39.6 · Retrospective: what you learned and your next steps

The last lesson of the course turns your capstone into something you keep. You close the matter properly, so that the money, the decision or the correction actually arrives and every date that outlives it is in your calendar. Then you write a retrospective: what you predicted and what happened, what worked, what went wrong and why, and which lessons you would reread. Finally you plan what comes next: the matters you parked in lesson 39.1, a yearly check-up of your own legal affairs, and the lawful ways to go further in law or to help the people around you.

## Why it matters

Lawyers get better by running many matters and looking back at each. You will run far fewer, so each one has to teach you more. The retrospective is where the course's tools are tested against reality: was your chance of winning honest, did the other side behave as your map predicted, did the clock move as you expected, were the templates right for your facts? Writing it down turns a single experience into judgment you can use the next time a landlord, an employer, an authority or an insurer says no.

Closing also protects the result. Every clinic ended with the same warning (lessons [12.7](../module-12/lesson-07.md), [22.8](../module-22/lesson-08.md), [31.8](../module-31/lesson-08.md), [37.8](../module-37/lesson-08.md)): a judgment is paper until it is paid, a settlement until it is performed, a decision until it is carried out. The capstone is not finished when the judge speaks; it is finished when the closing check is complete and the memo is on top of the archived file.

## The law

### When you may enforce

> (ב) לא תוגש בקשה לביצוע פסק דין… אלא לאחר שחלף המועד שנקבע בפסק הדין לביצועו, ואם לא נקבע מועד כאמור – לאחר שחלפו 30 ימים מיום מתן פסק הדין…

*Gist:* You may open an execution file once the date for payment in the judgment has passed, or, if none was set, after the statutory period from the judgment. ([Execution Law s. 6(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_6)) A settlement given the force of a judgment is enforced the same way (lesson [08.9](../module-08/lesson-09.md)). Fees and periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### When you may go further

Each forum has its own route and period: leave to appeal a small-claims judgment within the period in reg. 16 ([Small Claims Regulations](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיפוט_בתביעות_קטנות_(סדרי_דין)); lesson [12.7](../module-12/lesson-07.md)); leave from the National Labour Court on the expedited track (Labour Court Law s. 31(ד); lesson [22.2](../module-22/lesson-02.md)); an appeal from a registrar, a committee or a tribunal to the court the statute names (the lessons for each field). Decide inside the period, with the retrospective in front of you, not after it.

### Helping others: what stays reserved

> הפעולות המנויות להלן, לא יעשה אותן דרך עיסוק, או בתמורה אף שלא דרך עיסוק, אלא עורך דין; [...] (3) עריכת מסמכים בעלי אופי משפטי בשביל אדם אחר, לרבות ייצוג אדם אחר במשא ומתן משפטי לקראת עריכת מסמך כזה; (4) ייעוץ וחיווי דעת משפטיים.

*Gist:* Representing others, drafting their legal documents and advising them, as an occupation or for payment, are reserved to lawyers. ([Bar Association Law s. 20](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לשכת_עורכי_הדין)) In court, only a lawyer may represent another at all, unless a statute makes an exception (רע״א 8120/08; the exceptions in s. 21 and the forum rules, such as union appointees in the labour court). After this course you will be the person friends ask. The lawful answer is to explain where things are, show them the course's free templates and the help pages, and let them run their own matter and sign their own documents.

## Leading cases

### ר״ע 103/82 חניון חיפה בע״מ נ׳ מצת אור (הדר חיפה) בע״מ, פ״ד לו(3) 123

The leading case on the third instance: the basic conception of Israeli procedure is two instances, and leave for a further appeal is given only where a case raises a question of legal or public importance beyond the parties' own interest. Why it matters for the retrospective: the decision whether to go further is a calculation, not a reflex. After a small claim or an expedited labour case, leave is the exception; the realistic question is usually whether to enforce, not whether to appeal. Free summary: [Hebrew Wikipedia](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/פסק_דין_חניון_חיפה_בע%22מ_נגד_מצת_אור_(הדר_חיפה)_בע%22מ) (⚠️ secondary source; taught in lesson [15.8](../module-15/lesson-08.md)).

### ע״א 1530/02 מנורה חברה לביטוח בע״מ נ׳ יובלים אגודה שיתופית

The Supreme Court restated that an appeal court interferes with findings of fact and credibility only in a narrow range of cases. Why it matters for the retrospective: if you lost on the facts, the lesson is usually in your proof matrix, not in an appeal. Ask what evidence you lacked and when you could have obtained it. Free summary: [Hebrew Wikipedia, ערעור](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/ערעור) (⚠️ secondary source; taught in lesson 15.8).

## How it works in practice

### Closing the matter

Use the closing checklist of the clinic that matches your matter: lesson 12.7 and 22.8 for money judgments, settlements and labour or NII decisions; lesson 31.8 for registrations, refunds and payment orders; lesson 37.8 for public and criminal matters. In every case:

1. **Check the result itself:** the money in your account, the corrected bill, the new decision carried out, the register showing the change.
2. **Enforce or chase** what has not arrived: an execution file after the payment date (s. 6(ב)), a letter quoting the decision, a request in the case file.
3. **Decide on any further step** within its period, and write the reason.
4. **Write the closing memo** (one page): outcome and reference numbers; money and costs; where the originals are; surviving dates (limitation of related claims, a recheck date, an automatic deletion date); three sentences on what you would do differently.
5. **Archive:** one folder, the memo on top; keep it at least until every surviving date has passed.

### The retrospective

Fill the draft [capstone retrospective](../../templates/capstone-retrospective.md). Its core is calibration: put your predictions from the strategy map beside what happened.

| Prediction | You wrote | What happened | Why the difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chance of winning ($p$) | | | |
| Chance of collecting ($c$) | | | |
| Months to a result | | | |
| Your days and costs | | | |
| The other side's main defence | | | |
| The deciding fact or document | | | |

Then three short lists: **what worked** (keep doing it), **what went wrong** (each with its cause: knowledge, evidence, timing, communication, emotion), and **lessons to revisit** (the lesson numbers, so that next time you start there). Finish with a one-paragraph answer to the question every advocate asks after a case: "If I had this matter again tomorrow, what would I do in the first week?"

**Helping others lawfully.** You may explain, point to sources, and share the course's templates and the [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md) page. You may volunteer with a legal-aid organisation or a university clinic, where lawyers supervise the work. You may not run another person's matter for them, sign for them, or charge for help with their legal documents (s. 20). When a friend's matter fails the gates of lesson 39.1 for you, it may pass them for them: help them choose it, and let them run it.

## Worked example

**Samira closes.** The company pays the ₪16,000 on day 28 of the 30 the settlement allowed; she checks her bank statement, records the date and archives the court's decision. Closing memo: outcome (settlement with the force of a judgment, ₪16,000, case number); costs (the fee, registered mail, two half-days off); originals in the folder; surviving dates: none for the severance, but a note that a claim for any other employment right from the same period would be subject to limitation from the end of employment. *Calibration:* she predicted $p = 0.55$ and got 65% of her claim in a certain settlement; she predicted five months and it took three and a half; the defence she did not foresee was the Kiryat Ata "offer", and the deciding evidence was her own log and her colleague. *What went wrong:* she sent the letter before the fund statements arrived and had to change her figure (cause: timing; next time, records before letters, lesson 39.3). *Lessons to revisit:* 17.4 on s. 14 arrangements. *Next steps:* her arnona objection, the side filing, was accepted and the area corrected; the flat with her brother goes to a mediator, with a one-hour consultation first (lesson [35.5](../module-35/lesson-05.md)); she volunteers one evening a month at a workers' rights hotline run by a non-profit whose lawyers supervise the advice.

**Tsega closes.** The contractor does not pay within the 30 days. On day 31 she opens an execution file with the judgment (lesson [08.9](../module-08/lesson-09.md)); after the warning he calls, and agrees to pay in three instalments, which she accepts in writing through the file. The last instalment arrives in June 2027; she asks the execution office to close the file and archives the confirmation. *Calibration:* $p = 0.8$, and she won ₪12,400 of ₪13,650; $c = 0.8$, but collection took an execution file, which she had not costed; time: eight months to the last shekel, against four predicted. *What went wrong:* her first notice was a WhatsApp message without a date for repair (cause: knowledge; fixed by the formal notice in lesson 39.4); she did not budget for enforcement. *What worked:* the open set-off of the unpaid ₪6,000, which left the counterclaim with nothing to stand on. *Next steps:* the docked-pay question at work, now with lesson 17.3 and her union's representative; her mother's NII review, which her mother files and signs, with Tsega beside her; and an hour each January for the annual check-up.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- Your complete capstone file: strategy map, proof matrix and research memos, documents, log, costs ledger.
- The closing checklist of the clinic lesson that fits your matter (12.7, 22.8, 31.8 or 37.8).
- The draft [capstone retrospective](../../templates/capstone-retrospective.md) and the [get-a-lawyer checklist](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md).
- About 120 minutes, plus any enforcement or chasing the closing check shows is needed.

### Steps

1. **Closing check (20 min).** Confirm the result itself; list what is missing.
2. **Chase or enforce (20 min).** One written step for each gap; an execution file if the payment date has passed.
3. **Further step (10 min).** If an appeal, leave or review is open, decide within its period and write the reason.
4. **Closing memo (15 min).** One page, with every surviving date in your calendar.
5. **Retrospective (35 min).** Calibration table, what worked, what went wrong with causes, lessons to revisit, the first-week paragraph.
6. **Next steps (20 min).** The parked matters; your annual check-up date; one lawful way you will go further or help others.

### Template

- [Capstone retrospective (draft)](../../templates/capstone-retrospective.md). New in this module.
- [Clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md), closing sections; [execution request checklist](../../templates/execution-request-checklist.md) if you must enforce.
- [Get-a-lawyer checklist](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md), if the matter turned or a parked matter needs a consultation.

### How you know it worked

- The result itself is confirmed, or a dated step is under way to get it.
- Every surviving date is in your calendar.
- Every difference in the calibration table has a cause, and every cause has a lesson number or a habit to change.
- Your annual check-up is in the calendar.

### Self-check

- [ ] I checked the money, the register or the decision itself, not only the letter.
- [ ] I decided on any further step inside its period.
- [ ] My retrospective is honest about my own mistakes, not only the other side's.
- [ ] I know how to help a friend without running their matter for them.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Closing, enforcing and reviewing your own matter are yours: an execution file for a judgment in your favour, a request in the case file, a letter quoting a decision ([can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md), Execution Office rows). Advising or acting for others in their matters, as an occupation or for payment, remains reserved to lawyers (s. 20).

### Where and how

- Execution Office: online personal area or a branch (lesson 08.9); fees in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Leave to appeal or an appeal: the court the statute names, within its period (the lesson for your field).
- Volunteering: legal-aid non-profits and university clinics listed in [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md), where lawyers supervise.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You are considering leave to appeal or an appeal on a point of law: the argument and the security for costs are technical (lesson 15.8).
- The other side does not comply and is a public body or a company that has moved its assets.
- The matter reopens against you (a new claim, a complaint, a refusal based on the old file).
- A parked matter is larger, family-based or involves land: start with a consultation.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Closing on the judgment instead of the payment | Money never arrives; limitation of enforcement runs | Open an execution file now | Check the result itself |
| Deciding on an appeal after the period | No appeal, or an extension request with poor prospects | Ask for an extension at once with reasons | Diarise the period on judgment day |
| A retrospective that blames only the other side | Nothing learned | Rewrite the "what went wrong" list with your own causes | Fill the calibration table first |
| Becoming the family's unpaid lawyer | Unlawful representation risk; their documents in your name | Hand back; help them run it themselves | Explain, point, share templates; they sign |
| Archiving without surviving dates | A related claim expires; a recheck is forgotten | Add the dates now | The closing memo's surviving-dates line |

## Review

- A matter is closed when the result itself has happened and every surviving date is in your calendar, not when the decision is given.
- Enforce after the date set or the statutory period (Execution Law s. 6(ב)); decide on any further step inside its period, remembering that appeals on facts rarely succeed.
- The retrospective compares predictions with outcomes, names your own mistakes and their causes, and lists the lessons to revisit.
- Next steps: the parked matters, an annual check-up of your own affairs, and lawful ways to go further or help others without running their matters.
- The course does not make you a lawyer; it makes you a careful, informed party in your own affairs.
