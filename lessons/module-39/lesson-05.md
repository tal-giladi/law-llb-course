# 39.5 · Running the matter and keeping the log

Once the first document is sent, the matter has a life of its own: answers arrive, deadlines start, the court sets dates, the other side changes its story, and weeks pass with nothing at all. In this lesson you run your capstone matter through that stretch with three tools: a case log that records every event and its proof, a deadline diary you review every week, and a decision-point routine for every incoming document. You also prepare the hearing or decision stage and keep a costs ledger, so that if you win you can recover what you spent.

## Why it matters

The clinics ran their matters in a few lessons; real matters take months. Samira's labour claim and Tsega's small claim each take four to five months from the first letter to a result, and most of that time is waiting. Matters are lost in the waiting: a defence arrives while you are on holiday and the reply period passes; a court decision asking for a document goes unread in the Net HaMishpat inbox; the other side's lawyer phones, you agree to "something" in a hurry, and the call becomes his version of a settlement. Discipline in the log is what makes a self-represented party look, and be, reliable.

The log also protects you later. If the judgment is delayed, you have the dates for a complaint to the Judicial Ombudsman (lesson [37.6](../module-37/lesson-06.md)); if the other side says it never received a letter, you have the proof; if costs are argued, you have the receipts; and in the retrospective (lesson [39.6](lesson-06.md)) you have the record from which to learn.

## The law

### If you do not come

> (1) לא התייצב התובע, ימחק בית המשפט את כתב התביעה או ידחה את התביעה אם מצא טעמים מיוחדים לדחייה;
> (2) לא התייצב הנתבע והתובע התייצב, רשאי התובע להוכיח את תביעתו, ויהיה זכאי לסעד שביקש לפי הראיות שהתקבלו.

*Gist:* In the civil courts, a plaintiff who does not appear has the claim struck out; a defendant who does not appear lets the plaintiff prove the claim and get the relief the evidence supports. ([Civil Procedure Regulations reg. 75](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי)) Small claims has its own rule to the same effect, with a 30-day period to ask to set aside a decision given in your absence (Small Claims Regulations regs 11–12; lesson [12.6](../module-12/lesson-06.md)). The hearing date is the one date in your diary that cannot move without a written request granted by the court.

### Your expenses are recovered only if you list them

> בית המשפט יפסוק הוצאות משפט, ובלבד שבעל הדין פירט בסיכומי טענותיו באופן מפורש ומפורט את הוצאות המשפט כפי שנדרשו לצורך ההליך וצירף מסמכים התומכים בכך.

*Gist:* Legal expenses are awarded only if the party listed them explicitly and in detail, with supporting documents. ([reg. 155(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי)) For a self-represented party, who is rarely awarded a lawyer's fee, this rule decides whether costs are worth anything (lesson [11.5](../module-11/lesson-05.md)). In small claims and the labour court, bring the same list and receipts to the hearing.

### Dates the forum sets

- Small claims: the defendant's defence period runs from service; the plaintiff checks service in time before the hearing (reg. 7(ב1)); judgment at the end of the hearing or within the period in reg. 15 ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- Labour court: a defence is filed when the court orders it, within the period in regs 27–28; the court gives notice of a hearing in advance (reg. 47; lesson [22.2](../module-22/lesson-02.md)).
- Every forum: a court decision during the case may set its own date; it binds you from the day it is delivered to you, including through Net HaMishpat.

### If the judge does not decide

A person harmed by a judge's conduct in office, including undue delay in giving judgment, may complain to the Judicial Ombudsman, in writing and signed; the one-year rule applies ([Judicial Ombudsman Law ss. 14–18](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_נציב_תלונות_הציבור_על_שופטים); lesson 37.6). The Ombudsman deals with conduct, not the merits.

## Leading cases

### בג״ץ 9029/16 אבירם נ׳ שרת המשפטים (1.2.2017)

Self-represented petitioners raised an important question and lost on delay: the Court weighed subjective delay, objective delay and the public interest. Why it matters while running a matter: time is part of the merits in every forum. The deadline diary is not administration; it is the part of your case that you control completely. [English translation](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/aviram-v-minister-justice)

### רע״א 5234/17 (representation in small claims)

The Supreme Court held that a small-claims judge does not have to tell an unrepresented party that he may ask for permission to be represented. Why it matters while running a matter: the court will help you present your case, but it will not run it for you. Requests for an interpreter, a witness summons, a postponement or a video hearing are yours to make, in writing, in time. (⚠️ manual check: decision text on the Judiciary's site; taught in lesson 12.6.)

## How it works in practice

### The case log

Use part ו of the [clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md). One row per event, written the same day:

| Column | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| Date | When it happened (and, for a document, when it reached you) |
| Event | Sent, received, call, meeting, filing, decision, hearing, payment |
| With whom | Name and role |
| Content | What was sent or said, in one or two lines; the exact words if they matter |
| Proof | Delivery confirmation, screenshot, upload receipt, minutes |
| Deadline created | The date and its source (law, regulation, decision, letter) |
| Next step | What you will do and by when |

After every phone call, send an email the same day: "Further to our call today, I understood that…". The email, not the call, is the record.

### The deadline diary and the weekly review

Every deadline in the log goes into your calendar with two reminders: a week before and two days before. Once a week, at a fixed time, spend fifteen minutes:

1. Check every inbox the matter uses: email, the court's system, the post.
2. Read the diary for the next three weeks.
3. Compare the log with the strategy map: has a trigger fired that you did not act on?
4. Write one line in the log: "weekly review: nothing new" is a valid entry.

### The decision-point routine

When anything arrives (an answer, a defence, a court decision, an offer, a silence that passes its trigger date):

1. **Record** the date it reached you and how; save the document.
2. **Read it twice,** the second time with a pen: what does it say, what does it ask of you, by when?
3. **Compare** it with the strategy map: which arrow is this? Does it raise anything the map did not foresee?
4. **Re-test** the case theory against any new fact (lesson [39.2](lesson-02.md)): does it still pass the five tests?
5. **Re-run** part א of the [get-a-lawyer checklist](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md).
6. **Decide** within 48 hours, unless the deadline is shorter, and write the decision and its reason in part י of the strategy map.

### Offers and negotiation while the case runs

Compare every offer with the walk-away figure you set before contact. Answer in writing. A settlement proposed by the judge at the hearing is the most common ending in small claims and the labour court's expedited track; you decide on it against your walk-away figure and the risks in your register, not on the mood of the room (lessons [12.6](../module-12/lesson-06.md) and [22.7](../module-22/lesson-07.md)).

| | Court fee | | |
| | Registered mail, copies, printing | | |
| | Professional report | | |
| | Travel, parking | | |
| | Days off work (keep the employer's confirmation) | | |

Bring it, with the receipts, to the hearing or attach it to your summations (reg. 155(א)).

### The hearing or decision stage

Prepare with the [hearing-day kit](../../templates/hearing-day-kit.md) of lesson 12.6, adapted by forum: lesson 22.7 for the labour court and tribunals, lesson 37.7 for administrative courts and traffic tribunals, and the [witness examination plan](../../templates/witness-examination-plan.md) of lesson 23.10 if witnesses will be questioned. The kit's settlement sheet is your walk-away figure and your conditions, written in advance. After the hearing, write the log entry the same evening: who attended, what the judge asked, what was agreed or ordered, the date of the next step; and ask for the minutes if you need them.

### When the matter stalls

| Stall | What to do |
|---|---|
| The other side does not answer your letter | The trigger on your map fires: send the next document on its date |
| A records request goes unanswered | Chase in writing, quoting the original date and the legal basis |
| Service on the defendant cannot be confirmed | Check the court's system; ask the court in writing how to proceed before the hearing |
| The court sets no date for months | A short written request in the case file asking for a date |
| Judgment is not given long after the hearing | A polite request in the file; then, if the delay is undue, the Judicial Ombudsman |

## Worked example

**Samira's log (extract).**

| Date | Event | Proof | Deadline created | Next step |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 22.10.2026 | Letter to management sent | Email receipt; registered mail slip | 14 days to answer: 5.11 | Wait; chase fund |
| 29.10.2026 | Company's lawyer answers: resignation, transfer clause | Email | — | Decision point |
| 5.11.2026 | Fund statements arrive | PDF | — | Recompute top-up |
| 10.11.2026 | Claim filed online, expedited track | Filing receipt, case number | — | Check service |
| 14.12.2026 | Defence: transfer clause; "offered a place at the Kiryat Ata branch" | Net HaMishpat | — | Decision point |
| 2.2.2027 | Hearing | Minutes | — | Decide on proposal |

*Decision point 29 October:* nothing new; the trigger (refusal) fires; she files. *Recompute 5 November:* the statements show the severance component deposited at the full rate for only part of the period; the top-up is ₪24,800, and she amends the provisional figure before filing. *Decision point 14 December:* the Kiryat Ata "offer" is a new fact that could destroy the theory, since Kiryat Ata is close to Haifa. Her log shows no such offer, and her former colleague confirms that none was made; she writes to the company's lawyer asking for any document recording the offer, and adds the colleague to her witness list. The get-a-lawyer check: one amber (represented opponent); she continues. *Hearing:* the judge asks each side about the alleged offer; the company has no document. The judge proposes ₪16,000 in full settlement within 30 days. Her walk-away figure is ₪12,000; the proposal is above it and certain. She accepts; the court gives the agreement the force of a judgment.

**Tsega's log (extract).** 27 October: no payment by the pre-action deadline; trigger fires. 2 November: small claim filed online; fee paid; the court serves the contractor. 1 December: his defence arrives: she "refused to let him in" and he "counterclaims the ₪6,000 she owes". *Decision point:* her WhatsApp export shows her two proposed dates and his silence; the counterclaim is already netted off in her claim, and she files a two-paragraph reply saying so, with the calculation. 18 February 2027: the hearing. Her kit has a two-minute opening, three photographs, the plumber's report, the neighbour as witness, and the costs ledger (fee, registered mail, the report, half a day off). The judge asks the contractor why he did not come on the proposed dates; he has no answer. Judgment a week later: ₪12,400 plus ₪600 in expenses, payable within 30 days.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- Your case file, strategy map and the documents sent in lesson 39.4.
- A calendar with reminders; access to every inbox the matter uses (email, the court's system, post).
- The [hearing-day kit](../../templates/hearing-day-kit.md) and, if witnesses will be questioned, the [witness examination plan](../../templates/witness-examination-plan.md).
- About 150 minutes now, then fifteen minutes a week and the time each event needs.

### Steps

1. **Back-fill the log (25 min).** Every event since you chose the matter, with proof and deadlines.
2. **Diary (15 min).** Every deadline into the calendar with two reminders; a recurring weekly review slot.
3. **Costs ledger (10 min).** Every expense so far, with receipts in one folder.
4. **First decision point (30 min).** Apply the routine to the most recent incoming document or silence: record, read, compare, re-test, lawyer check, decide in writing.
5. **Hearing or decision preparation (50 min).** Fill the hearing-day kit (or the forum's equivalent) as far as you can now, including the settlement sheet with your walk-away figure and conditions.
6. **Run it (ongoing).** Weekly review; the routine for every event; the hearing; the same-evening log entry after it.
7. **Stalls (20 min, when needed).** Use the stall table; one written step for each stall.

### Template

- [Clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md), parts ו (log) and ז (deadlines).
- [Capstone strategy map (draft)](../../templates/capstone-strategy-map.md), part י, for the decisions.
- [Hearing-day kit](../../templates/hearing-day-kit.md) (lesson 12.6) and [witness examination plan](../../templates/witness-examination-plan.md) (lesson 23.10).

### How you know it worked

- Every event has a log row with proof, and every deadline is in the calendar with two reminders.
- Every incoming document has a written decision within 48 hours (or before its deadline).
- Your settlement sheet was written before the hearing, and your decision at the hearing matched it or the log says why not.
- The costs ledger matches the receipts folder.

### Self-check

- [ ] Every phone call has a same-day email confirming it.
- [ ] I check the court's system as well as my email.
- [ ] I know what happens if I miss the hearing, and how to ask for a postponement in writing.
- [ ] I can recover my expenses because I listed them with receipts.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Running your own matter (receiving and answering documents, attending, questioning witnesses, negotiating and signing a settlement) is what reg. 170 and the forum rules let you do in person. No relative or friend may do it for you (רע״א 8120/08); in small claims a lawyer appears only with leave, and a non-lawyer helper only with the court's approval (Courts Law s. 63; [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md)).

### Where and how

- Court documents and decisions: Net HaMishpat with the national digital login, or the forum's own system; check it weekly.
- Requests to the court (postponement, interpreter, witness summons, video hearing): in writing in the case file, in time.
- Judicial delay: a request in the file, then the Judicial Ombudsman (lesson 37.6).
- Free help on the day: the court information centres; other help in [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md); periods in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- A defence or motion raises a threshold objection (jurisdiction, limitation, standing) your research file did not cover.
- A counterclaim exceeds your claim, or the other side asks for security for costs.
- The court transfers the case to another track or court, or orders an expert opinion you must pay for.
- You cannot meet a deadline and the court refuses an extension.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Not checking the court's system | A decision with a deadline passes unread | Ask at once to extend, with reasons | Weekly review of every inbox |
| Agreeing to something on the phone | The other side's version becomes "the deal" | Email your understanding the same day; correct theirs in writing | Never settle orally |
| Missing the hearing | The claim is struck out, or judgment given against you | Apply at once to set it aside (small claims: within 30 days) | Two reminders; arrive early |
| Answering a defence emotionally | Admissions and a weaker record | Withdraw or correct the reply; stick to the facts | The 48-hour routine |
| No receipts for expenses | Nothing recovered | Collect what you can before the hearing | The costs ledger from day one |

## Review

- The case log records every event, its proof and the deadline it creates; every call is confirmed by email the same day.
- The deadline diary has two reminders per date and a fifteen-minute weekly review.
- Every incoming document goes through the routine: record, read twice, compare with the map, re-test the theory, re-run the lawyer check, decide in writing.
- Decide on settlement proposals against the walk-away figure written before the hearing.
- List your expenses with receipts, or you will not recover them (reg. 155(א)).
