# 25.8 · Collection, penalties and voluntary disclosure

By the end of this lesson you will know what happens when tax is paid late or under-reported: which additions are automatic, which fines depend on negligence, when the conduct becomes a criminal offence, and what lawful routes exist to put things right. The lesson explains offences so that you can stay on the right side of them and correct mistakes properly; it is not a guide to avoiding detection.

> [!WARNING]
> Tax offences carry prison sentences. If you think you may have committed one, or the Tax Authority's investigations unit has contacted you, speak to a criminal-tax lawyer **before** you say or file anything. What you tell an accountant is not privileged in the way that what you tell a lawyer is. Nothing in this lesson is a substitute for that advice.

## Why it matters

Most people who owe the Tax Authority money did not set out to cheat: they forgot to report rent, did not know a side job required a return, or paid advances on an old base year. The law treats them very differently from a person who hid income deliberately, but only if the facts show it. The additions for late payment are automatic and grow every month; fines for negligent under-reporting can be reduced or waived on request; deliberate evasion is a crime, with a ten-year limitation period. Knowing the line lets an honest taxpayer fix the past quickly and cheaply, and tells someone in real trouble that he needs a lawyer now.

## The law

### Civil additions and fines

- **Late filing.** A monthly fine for every full month of delay in filing the return (s. 188(א)), linked every 1 January (s. 188(ח)); see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- **Late advances and late tax.** Linkage and interest (index plus 4% a year, s. 159א(א)) on late advances and late tax (s. 190).
- **Deficiency fine.**

> נישום שלגביו נקבע גרעון העולה על 50% מהמס שהוא חייב בו ולא הוכיח להנחת דעתו של פקיד השומה שלא התרשל בעריכת הדו״ח שמסר או באי מסירת דו״ח, יהא חייב בקנס בשיעור של 15% מסכום הגרעון;

*Gist:* If the assessed tax exceeds the reported tax by more than 50% of the tax due and the taxpayer does not show he was not negligent, a fine of 15% of the deficiency is added; double if the deficiency was wilful with intent to evade; 30% for large deficiencies arising from unreported reportable tax planning. [s. 191](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_191) The fine is part of the assessment for objection and appeal (s. 191(ד)).

- **Reduction.** The Director may reduce or waive interest and fines under ss. 186–190 if the delay was due to reasons beyond the taxpayer's control (s. 192; ⚠️ the full wording of the condition was not read).
- **Collection.** Unpaid tax may be collected administratively under the Taxes (Collection) Ordinance, including attachments (s. 193), and by restrictions through the Execution Office's centre for restrictions (s. 194א).

### Offences (Income Tax Ordinance)

| Section | Conduct | Maximum |
|---|---|---|
| s. 216 | Failing without sufficient reason to comply with a demand, to appear, or other listed duties | 1 year |
| s. 215א | Not notifying the start of a business | listed in the section |
| s. 217 | Without reasonable justification, filing an incorrect return (omitting or understating income) or giving incorrect information | 2 years |
| s. 220 | **Wilfully, with intent to evade tax**: omitting income, false statements, false books, "any fraud, artifice or device" | 7 years, or a fine plus twice the income concealed |

> אדם אשר במזיד, בכוונה להתחמק ממס או לעזור לאדם אחר להתחמק ממס, עבר אחת העבירות המנויות להלן, דינו – מאסר שבע שנים [...]

*Gist:* Wilful evasion is punishable by up to seven years' imprisonment or a heavy fine, or both. [s. 220](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_220) The limitation period for prosecution is six years from the tax year of the offence, and ten years for s. 220 (s. 225). The VAT Law has parallel offences: up to one year for listed breaches, five years with intent to evade, seven in aggravated circumstances (VAT Law s. 117(א), (ב), (ב2)); the Real Estate Taxation Law likewise (s. 98).

### Composition fine

> אדם שעבר עבירה לפי הסעיפים 215–220, רשאי המנהל, בהסכמתו של האדם, לקחת מידו כופר כסף [...], ואם עשה כן – ייפסק כל הליך נגדו על אותה עבירה [...]

*Gist:* For offences under ss. 215–220 the Director may, with the person's consent, take a composition fine of up to twice the maximum fine, and the proceedings stop. [s. 221](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_221) A composition is not a conviction, but it extends the Director's power to reopen assessments (s. 147(א)(2)).

### Voluntary disclosure

Voluntary disclosure is not in the statute. It is a Tax Authority **procedure** approved by the Attorney General: a person who comes forward before any investigation or inquiry has begun, discloses unreported income or assets in full and pays the tax, interest and fines is not prosecuted for the disclosed offences. According to law-firm and press summaries, the procedure published on 25 August 2025 was a temporary order valid until **31 August 2026**, had no anonymous track, and allowed the information to be used in some circumstances. ⚠️ Whether it was extended or replaced after 31 August 2026 must be checked on the Tax Authority's site (it blocks automated access).

## Leading cases

### ע״א 4157/13 דמארי נ׳ פקיד שומה רחובות; דנ״א 2308/15 (12.9.2017)

Two people convicted of taking large sums (an accounts manager who stole about ₪2.4 million from her employer, and a second appellant, Hirschson, in a separate case) were assessed to income tax on the money. The Supreme Court held that money obtained by theft or embezzlement is income of the thief in the year it was taken, and that repaying it is an expense in the year of repayment, which may be set off against income of that year or later years. On a further hearing the court held that the repayment cannot be carried back against the year of the theft. Why it matters: illegal income is taxable like any other (s. 2(10)); criminal proceedings do not replace the tax liability (s. 226); and the timing of deductions follows the statute. Source: [Hebrew Wikipedia article on the rulings](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/פסק_דין_הירשזון-דמארי) (secondary; ⚠️ official texts not opened).

## How it works in practice

### If you find a mistake in your own past returns

1. **Stop and classify.** Was it a mistake (forgotten rent, a missed Form 106) or something you knew about? If there is any question of intent, or a large amount, or foreign assets: lawyer first.
2. **Mistakes.** File an amended return (or the missing return), pay the tax with linkage and interest, and ask in writing to waive fines with the reasons (s. 192). For a person who was exempt from filing and had small rental income, check the special rent tracks before filing.
3. **Deliberate omissions.** Only through a lawyer, and only after checking whether a voluntary-disclosure procedure is in force and whether an investigation has already begun (after that, the procedure does not help).
4. **Never** destroy documents, back-date papers or "fix" books: these are separate offences (s. 220(4); Real Estate Taxation Law s. 98(ב1)).

### If the Tax Authority contacts you

- A letter from the assessing officer asking for documents is a civil inquiry: answer on time, with copies, or through an accountant.
- A summons from the investigations unit (חקירות) or a search is criminal: the rights of a suspect apply ([lesson 24.3](../module-24/lesson-03.md)). Ask for a lawyer before answering.

## Worked example

Galit Shapira, a nurse from Ra'anana, inherited a small apartment in 2020 and has let it since for ₪3,500 a month. She never filed a return because "I'm salaried". In 2026 her accountant friend tells her rent above the exemption ceiling must be reported.

1. **Classify.** Galit did not know; she reported nothing and paid no tax on the rent, but there is no false document. The risk is civil; the s. 217 offence requires an incorrect return "without reasonable justification", and she filed none. She should still take advice, because not filing when required can be an offence under s. 216 and related sections.
2. **Check the law.** Residential rent below the exemption ceiling of the Rental Income Exemption Law is exempt (⚠️ check the 2026 ceiling); otherwise she can choose a track: the exemption (with its reduction above the ceiling), 10% of the gross rent paid within 30 days after the year (Income Tax Ordinance s. 122), or regular tax. With ₪42,000 a year she may have been exempt for some years; she checks the ceiling for each year.
3. **Act.** If tax was due, she files returns for the open years (or the special 10% payment), pays with linkage and interest, and asks to waive fines (s. 192), explaining she relied on the belief that salaried employees need not file.
4. **Not voluntary disclosure.** Her case is an honest correction; the formal voluntary-disclosure procedure is designed for deliberate concealment and is handled by lawyers.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your own last six years of income (or the Shapira case).
- The list of sources from lesson 25.1 and the filing rules from lesson 25.2.
- 40 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Gap check (15 min).** For each of the last six years, list every source of income and whether it was reported (payroll, return, special track). Mark gaps.
2. **Classify (10 min).** For each gap: mistake or knowing omission? Small or large? Any foreign element?
3. **Route (10 min).** For each gap write the lawful route: amended return and payment; waiver request; or "lawyer first".
4. **Red lines (5 min).** Write three things you will not do (destroy, back-date, speak to investigators without a lawyer).

### Template

None. A waiver request can follow the structure of the [tax assessment objection (draft)](../../templates/tax-assessment-objection.md), adapted.

### How you know it worked

- Every gap has a route and a reason.
- Anything with intent, large sums or foreign assets is marked "lawyer first".
- You can explain the difference between s. 217 and s. 220.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Partly. Filing a missing or amended return, paying and asking for a waiver of fines are things you may do yourself in your own name. Anything with a criminal aspect (deliberate omission, an investigation, voluntary disclosure) is for a lawyer: the stakes include imprisonment, and privilege attaches to legal advice.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Where and how

- The assessing office and the Tax Authority's online services for amended returns and payments (⚠️ manual check).
- Waiver of fines: a written request to the assessing officer under s. 192.
- Voluntary disclosure: the Tax Authority's dedicated unit, through a lawyer (⚠️ check whether a procedure is in force after 31.8.2026).
- Free help: Legal Aid does not usually cover tax; Public Defender only if charged and eligible ([lesson 24.3](../module-24/lesson-03.md)).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You knowingly left out income, used false invoices or hold undeclared foreign assets.
- You receive a summons from the investigations unit, a search, or a request to "come and give your version".
- A deficiency fine for intent (double fine, s. 191(ג)) is proposed.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ignoring a late-filing problem | Monthly fines and interest grow | File now; ask for a waiver | File on time or ask for an extension |
| Talking to investigators without a lawyer | Statements used in a criminal case | Stop; ask for a lawyer | Know the suspect's rights (24.3) |
| "Fixing" old books or back-dating | A separate, serious offence | Lawyer at once | Never alter records |
| Assuming voluntary disclosure is always open | The procedure may have lapsed | Check its status; lawyer | Check the Tax Authority's current notice |
| Treating repayment of illegal gains as erasing the tax | Tax still due for the year of the gain | Claim the deduction in the year of repayment | Follow Damari |

## Review

- Late payment carries linkage and 4% interest; late filing a monthly fine; a deficiency above half the tax a 15% fine unless no negligence is shown (ss. 188, 190, 191).
- Incorrect returns without reasonable justification are an offence (s. 217); wilful evasion carries up to seven years, with a ten-year limitation (ss. 220, 225).
- The Director may settle offences by a composition fine with the person's consent (s. 221) and may reduce fines for reasons beyond the taxpayer's control (s. 192).
- Voluntary disclosure is an administrative procedure; the 2025 version ran until 31.8.2026 (⚠️ check its successor).
- Illegal income is taxable; repayment is deductible in the year of repayment (Damari).
