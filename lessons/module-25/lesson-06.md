# 25.6 · Real-estate taxation: purchase tax

By the end of this lesson you will be able to work out the purchase tax on an apartment, decide whether the buyer is buying a "single apartment" and so pays at the reduced brackets, and handle the buyer's side of the filing: the declaration within 30 days, the self-assessment, payment within 60 days, and the route to object and appeal. This is a Bar-exam subject and one of the largest one-time taxes most families ever pay.

## Why it matters

Purchase tax (מס רכישה) is paid by the buyer on almost every acquisition of a right in Israeli land. For a family buying its only home the first slice of the price is tax-free; for an investor buying an additional apartment the rate starts at 8%. On a ₪2.8 million apartment the difference between the two is about ₪190,000, so whether the buyer has "a single apartment" is often the most valuable legal question in the deal. Purchase tax is also the gate to registration: the Land Registry will not register the transfer without proof that the tax was paid or secured ([lesson 13.2](../module-13/lesson-02.md)). And the buyer files his own declaration and self-assessment; if he does nothing, a fine accrues every two weeks.

## The law

### Who pays, and on what

> במכירת זכות במקרקעין יהא הרוכש חייב במס רכישת מקרקעין [...]; מס הרכישה יהיה בשיעור משווי המכירה או בסכום קבוע, לפי סוג המכירה או המקרקעין, הכל כפי שיקבע שר האוצר [...]

*Gist:* In every sale of a right in land the buyer pays purchase tax at rates set by the Minister of Finance with the Finance Committee's approval. [Real Estate Taxation Law s. 9(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מיסוי_מקרקעין_(שבח_ורכישה)#סעיף_9) For land that is not a residential apartment (an office, a shop, a plot) the rate is 6% (Purchase Tax Regulations reg. 2(1)); for a residential apartment the brackets in s. 9 apply (reg. 2(2)).

### Residential apartments: the two scales

**Single apartment (s. 9(ג1ג)(2)–(3)).** A resident individual who buys a residential apartment that is his only apartment, or who sells his previous only apartment within 24 months (12 months after delivery when buying from a contractor), pays:

> על חלק השווי שעד 1,978,745 שקלים חדשים – לא ישולם מס.

*Gist:* Nothing on the first slice, then 3.5%, 5%, 8% and 10% on the higher slices; the bracket amounts are frozen for 2025–2027 (s. 9(ג2)), so they apply unchanged in 2026. [s. 9(ג1ג)(3)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מיסוי_מקרקעין_(שבח_ורכישה)#סעיף_9)

| Slice of the value (2026) | Rate |
|---|---|
| up to ₪1,978,745 | 0% |
| ₪1,978,745 – ₪2,347,040 | 3.5% |
| ₪2,347,040 – ₪6,055,070 | 5% |
| ₪6,055,070 – ₪20,183,565 | 8% |
| above ₪20,183,565 | 10% |

**Any other residential purchase** (an additional apartment, a buyer who is not a resident individual, a company): until 31.12.2026 the temporary scale applies, 8% up to ₪6,055,070 and 10% above (s. 9(ג1ו), extended to 31.12.2026 by order). After that, unless extended again, the general scale in s. 9(ג1ג)(1) (5%–10%) would apply. See [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### "Single apartment" and the buyer unit

> [...] יראו רוכש ובן זוגו, למעט בן הזוג הגר בדרך קבע בנפרד, וילדיהם שטרם מלאו להם 18 שנים, למעט ילד נשוי או ילד יתום מאחד או משני הוריו – כרוכש אחד;

*Gist:* The buyer, his spouse (unless permanently living apart) and their unmarried minor children count as one buyer, so an apartment owned by either spouse counts against both. [s. 9(ג1ג)(4)(ג)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מיסוי_מקרקעין_(שבח_ורכישה)#סעיף_9)

A "single apartment" is the buyer's only residential apartment in Israel and the Area (s. 16א); an apartment under old protected tenancy (let before 1.1.1997) and a small share of another apartment (as in s. 49ג(3): up to a third, or up to a half if inherited) are disregarded (s. 9(ג1ג)(4)(א)). A person who becomes a first-time resident or a returning resident within two years of the purchase counts as a resident (para. (4)(ב)).

### Reliefs in the regulations

- **Disabled persons, the blind, victims and bereaved families** buying for their own housing: reduced tax (reg. 11).
- **New immigrants**: a reduced scale once, within the period set in reg. 12 (reg. 12א).
- **Gift to a relative**: one third of the regular tax (reg. 20); **gift of a share in the home to a spouse who lives there**: exempt (reg. 21).

### Filing, assessment and payment

> הרוכש זכות במקרקעין או זכות באיגוד מקרקעין ימסור למנהל, בתוך 30 ימים מיום הרכישה, הצהרה [...]

*Gist:* The buyer files a declaration within 30 days of the purchase. [s. 73(ג)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מיסוי_מקרקעין_(שבח_ורכישה)#סעיף_73) The declaration is a self-assessment; the director sends a notice of the tax within 20 days and may review it within eight months and assess by best judgment (s. 78). The tax is paid within 60 days of the sale (s. 90א). A late declaration costs a fine for every two weeks (₪310 in 2026 per the Wikisource note; s. 94א), which the director may waive for justified reasons.

### Objection, appeals committee, Supreme Court

> [...] ותוגש בתוך 30 ימים מיום שנמסרה לו הודעת השומה [...]

*Gist:* An objection states the grounds and the undisputed tax and is filed within 30 days of the assessment notice; the director decides with reasons within eight months (extendable to a year), after a hearing, or the objection is accepted. [s. 87](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מיסוי_מקרקעין_(שבח_ורכישה)#סעיף_87)

> הרואה עצמו מקופח בהחלטת המנהל רשאי, תוך שלושים יום מיום שנמסרה לו ההחלטה, לערור עליה לפני ועדת ערר [...]

*Gist:* An appeal (ערר) from the director's decision goes to an appeals committee of three, chaired by a judge, within 30 days; from it, an appeal on a point of law to the Supreme Court within 45 days. [ss. 88–90](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מיסוי_מקרקעין_(שבח_ורכישה)#סעיף_88) The committee may review even discretionary decisions of the director (s. 89(ב)).

## Leading cases

### Key text: s. 9(ג1ג) and the "single apartment"

The decisive text is s. 9(ג1ג)(2)–(4). Three features produce most disputes and appeals-committee decisions: (1) the **buyer unit** (spouses and minor children are one buyer, unless a spouse lives permanently apart, which must be proved by facts, not by a declaration); (2) the **replacement rule** (the old apartment must be sold within 24 months, or 12 months after delivery from a contractor, otherwise the reduced scale is lost and the difference is collected); and (3) what counts as a **residential apartment** (s. 9(ג): one that "serves or is intended to serve" for residence, including one under construction with the seller's obligation to complete it). Free decisions of the appeals committees are published by the Judiciary and the Tax Authority (⚠️ manual check; not opened). For the property-law side of the purchase, see [lesson 13.3](../module-13/lesson-03.md) and, for a new apartment from a contractor, [lesson 20.2](../module-20/lesson-02.md).

## How it works in practice

### Who files

In most purchases a lawyer handles the transaction and files the declarations online for both parties. A private buyer may file his own declaration (in person at the regional Real Estate Taxation office, or online ⚠️ manual check of the current service for unrepresented parties). The buyer's declaration includes a sworn statement that the apartment will be used as a single residential unit (reg. 2(2)(ד)) and, for the single-apartment scale, a declaration of the apartments the buyer unit owns.

### The sequence

1. Contract signed: the sale date (generally the date of the binding contract).
2. Within 30 days: the buyer's declaration (s. 73(ג)), with the contract and the buyer's statements.
3. Within about 20 days: the notice of tax under the self-assessment (s. 78(א)).
4. Within 60 days of the sale: payment (s. 90א); late payment carries linkage and interest.
5. Within eight months: the director may review and issue a best-judgment assessment (s. 78(ב)).
6. If assessed: objection within 30 days (s. 87), then appeals committee within 30 days (s. 88).
7. After payment: the tax certificate for registration at the Land Registry (s. 16).

## Worked example

Tamar and Ido Mizrahi, a married couple from Rishon LeZion, sign a contract on 1 September 2026 to buy an apartment for ₪2,800,000. Ido owns a third of an apartment in Haifa that he inherited with his two siblings; the couple own nothing else.

1. **Buyer unit.** Tamar, Ido and their son (age 6) are one buyer (s. 9(ג1ג)(4)(ג)).
2. **Single apartment?** Ido's share in Haifa is a third of an inherited apartment, within the s. 49ג(3) shares that are disregarded (up to a half for an inherited apartment). The new apartment is therefore their single apartment.
3. **Computation (2026 brackets).** 0% on ₪1,978,745 = ₪0. 3.5% on ₪368,295 (₪2,347,040 − ₪1,978,745) = ₪12,890. 5% on ₪452,960 (₪2,800,000 − ₪2,347,040) = ₪22,648. Total about **₪35,538**.
4. **Comparison.** As an additional apartment it would be 8% × ₪2,800,000 = ₪224,000 (s. 9(ג1ו), until 31.12.2026).
5. **Filing.** Declaration by 1 October 2026 (30 days); payment by 31 October 2026 (60 days). They keep the inheritance order and the Haifa extract to prove the share.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A real or planned purchase (or the Mizrahi case): price, date, the buyer unit's apartments.
- The [purchase-tax worksheet (draft)](../../templates/purchase-tax-worksheet.md).
- The [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md) page.
- 60 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Buyer unit (10 min).** List everyone in the buyer unit and every apartment or share any of them owns in Israel and the Area.
2. **Classification (10 min).** Residential or not? Single apartment, replacement (with a sale deadline), or additional?
3. **Computation (15 min).** Apply the right scale slice by slice; then compute the other scale for comparison.
4. **Reliefs (5 min).** Check regs 11, 12א, 20 and 21.
5. **Timeline (10 min).** Write the declaration, payment and review dates; if replacement, the date by which the old apartment must be sold.
6. **Risk note (10 min).** What could make the director reassess (a spouse's apartment, a share above the threshold, a late sale), and what evidence answers it?

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Partly. A buyer may file his own declaration and self-assessment and object and appeal in his own name. But a land purchase also involves the contract, the caveat and registration, where a lawyer is usual and protects you ([lesson 13.9](../module-13/lesson-09.md)); and the seller's and buyer's declarations are normally filed by the lawyers. Drafting for or representing the other party is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Where and how

- The regional Real Estate Taxation office (מיסוי מקרקעין); online declarations (⚠️ manual check of the service available to unrepresented buyers on gov.il).
- Deadlines: declaration 30 days; payment 60 days; objection 30 days; appeals committee 30 days; Supreme Court 45 days ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- Free help: the Tax Authority's real-estate taxation simulator and call centre (⚠️ manual check); [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You buy from a contractor, in a purchase group, through a company, or with a combination deal.
- The single-apartment status depends on a spouse living apart, a share in another apartment, or a replacement sale you may not complete in time.
- The director has assessed you by best judgment or questions the price.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Forgetting the spouse's apartment | Reassessment at the additional-apartment rate | Object if a disregard applies (share, separation) | List the whole buyer unit's property |
| Replacement sale not done in time | Difference in tax collected with linkage and interest | Sell quickly; ask the director in special circumstances | Plan the sale before signing |
| Late declaration | Fine every two weeks (s. 94א) | File now; ask for a waiver with reasons | Diary 30 days from the contract |
| Paying late | Linkage and interest | Pay the undisputed amount at once | Diary 60 days |
| Treating a share above a third as disregarded | Single-apartment status refused | Check s. 49ג(3) | Check the share before relying on it |

## Review

- The buyer pays purchase tax; 6% on non-residential land, the s. 9 brackets on apartments (s. 9; reg. 2).
- In 2026 a single apartment pays 0% up to ₪1,978,745, then 3.5%, 5%, 8%, 10%; other residential purchases 8% and 10% until 31.12.2026.
- Spouses and minor children are one buyer; small shares and protected-tenancy apartments are disregarded; a replacement must be sold within 24 months (12 after contractor delivery).
- Declaration within 30 days, payment within 60, objection 30 days, appeals committee 30 days, Supreme Court on law 45 days (ss. 73, 87, 88, 90, 90א).
