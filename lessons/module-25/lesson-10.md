# 25.10 · Practice lab: an employee's tax-refund request

In this lab you prepare a real or supplied refund claim from start to finish: you decide which years are open, rebuild the year's tax with the correct credit points and credits, gather the proof, and write the request and cover letter. At the end you have a file you could submit in your own name.

## Why it matters

Lessons 25.2 and 25.4 gave you the rules; this lab is where they turn into money. A good refund file is short, organised by year, and proves every claim with a document, so that the assessing officer can approve it without a meeting.

## The law

The rules are in lessons 25.2 and 25.4. The essentials: credit points and their value (ss. 33א–40ג, 66); credits for pension, life insurance and donations (ss. 45א, 46); the exemption from filing and its exclusions (Exemption Regulations regs 2–3); the six-year window and linkage and interest (s. 160(א)); the refund after a return (s. 159א). [Income Tax Ordinance on Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_160)

## Leading cases

### Key text: s. 160(א)

The lab applies s. 160(א): the report for each year must be filed within six years after it, and the refund carries linkage and interest. No ruling is needed for a routine claim.

## How it works in practice

You file Form 135 (or use the online refund service) for each year if you are exempt from filing, or an annual return if you must file (⚠️ manual check of the current form and online service on gov.il). Attach the employer certificates and the proof of each credit claimed. Keep a copy of everything and the filing receipt.

## Worked example

The supplied case below has a model answer at the end of "Do it yourself". Read the case, do the work, then compare.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- Your own Forms 106, NII certificates and receipts for open years, **or** the supplied case below.
- The [tax refund request (draft)](../../templates/tax-refund-request.md).
- The [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md) page (credit-point value, brackets).
- 150 minutes.

**Supplied case.** Inbal Sasson, 27, from Jerusalem. Single, no children. Discharged from the army in October 2019 after 24 months of service. B.A. (three years) completed in July 2023. In 2024 she worked for Employer A from January to June (₪9,000 a month gross) and for Employer B from August to December (₪11,000 a month gross), Employer A gave her resident and woman points. At Employer B she ticked "I have another income" on Form 101 out of caution and never obtained a tax coordination, so B gave no points and withheld at a high rate. She received NII unemployment benefit in July 2024 (₪5,800, tax withheld ₪580). She donated ₪1,000 to a recognised charity in 2024. She has never filed. Her Forms 106 show tax withheld of ₪1,900 (A) and ₪8,500 (B) for 2024.

### Steps

1. **Open years (10 min).** It is 2026. List the years you can still claim (s. 160(א)). For the supplied case, focus on 2024 and note whether 2023 and 2025 are worth checking.
2. **Filing status (10 min).** Is the person exempt from filing for each year (regs 2–3)? Write the reason.
3. **Credit points (25 min).** Table the points for the year with sections: resident, travel, woman, degree, discharged soldier, children, others. Note which points the employers gave.
4. **Annual tax (40 min).** Add all taxable income (both salaries and the benefit). Apply the year's brackets for income from personal exertion (s. 121; the 2024 brackets are in the Wikisource table) to get the tax before credits. Subtract the credit points × the point value (₪2,904 for 2024) and the donation credit (35%). Compare with total tax withheld.
5. **Documents (20 min).** List each document as a numbered exhibit: Forms 106, NII certificate, degree certificate, discharge certificate, donation receipt, bank confirmation.
6. **Draft (35 min).** Fill the template: cover letter, request, the list of exhibits, the bank details for the refund.
7. **Review (10 min).** Check against the "How you know it worked" list below.

### How you know it worked

- Every point and credit in your computation has a section and a document.
- Your computation uses the right year's brackets and point value and adds the NII benefit to income.
- Your request names the tax year, the route (Form 135 / online / return), the exhibits and the bank account.
- The year is within the six-year window.

<details><summary>Model answer for the supplied case (outline)</summary>

**Open years.** In 2026, 2020–2025 are open (2020 until 31.12.2026). Check 2023 (degree finished July 2023, points start in 2024, so 2023 is probably fine) and 2025 (degree points continue; if Employer B still gives only resident and woman points, 2025 is also a refund year).

**Filing status.** 2024 income: salaries and an NII benefit, below the reg. 2 salary ceiling, no reg. 3 exclusion: exempt from filing; refund via Form 135 or the online service.

**Points 2024.** Resident 2 (s. 34) + travel ¼ (s. 36) + woman ½ (s. 36א) = 2¾. Degree: studies ended in 2023, so 1 point a year for 2024–2026 (s. 40ג). Discharged soldier: 36 months from November 2019 ended in October 2022, so nothing in 2024 (s. 39א). Total 3¾ points × ₪2,904 = ₪10,890.

**Income 2024.** A: 6 × ₪9,000 = ₪54,000. B: 5 × ₪11,000 = ₪55,000. NII: ₪5,800. Total ₪114,800.

**Tax before credits (2024 brackets for personal exertion).** 10% on ₪84,120 = ₪8,412; 14% on ₪30,680 (₪114,800 − ₪84,120) = ₪4,295. Total ₪12,707.

**Credits.** Points ₪10,890 + donation 35% × ₪1,000 = ₪350, together ₪11,240. Tax due ₪12,707 − ₪11,240 = ₪1,467.

**Comparison.** Withheld: ₪1,900 (A) + ₪8,500 (B) + ₪580 (NII) = ₪10,980. Due: ₪1,467. Refund about **₪9,500**, plus linkage and interest (s. 160(א)). (Figures are illustrative: check the pension credits on Form 106, which would increase the refund, and the exact brackets.)

**Exhibits.** 1: Form 106, Employer A. 2: Form 106, Employer B. 3: NII certificate for unemployment benefit and tax withheld. 4: Degree eligibility certificate. 5: Donation receipt (s. 46). 6: Bank account confirmation.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. A refund request is filed by the taxpayer in his own name. Do not prepare requests for other people for pay (Income Tax Ordinance s. 236; Bar Association Law s. 20).

### Where and how

- Form 135 or the online refund service for employees; the assessing office for your area (⚠️ manual check).
- No fee (⚠️ manual check). Six-year window ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- Free help: Tax Authority call centre; [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer (or accountant) when

- You find a year in which you were required to file and did not (lesson 25.8).
- The claim involves severance, pension withdrawals, securities or foreign income.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leaving out the NII benefit from income | Wrong computation; request returned | Add it with the NII certificate | List every payer |
| Claiming discharged-soldier points after 36 months | Points refused | Remove them | Count 36 months from the end of service |
| No bank details | Refund delayed | Send the bank confirmation | Attach it with the request |
| One request for several years without a breakdown | Delays and questions | Separate request or section per year | Organise by year |

## Review

- A refund file is organised by year, with a computation and a document for every claim.
- Rebuild the year's tax: all income, the year's brackets, points × the point value, credits; compare with all tax withheld.
- Exempt filers use Form 135 or the online service; the six-year window applies to each year.
