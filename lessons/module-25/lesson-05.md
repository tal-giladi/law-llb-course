# 25.5 · Assessment, objection and appeal

By the end of this lesson you will be able to read an income-tax assessment notice, know which stage of the process you are in and what your next deadline is, write a reasoned objection, and tell whether the assessing officer has missed his own deadline so that your objection is treated as accepted. You will also know what an appeal to the District Court involves and when it is time for professional help.

## Why it matters

Tax disputes are won and lost on procedure. The Ordinance gives the taxpayer a strong position: his own return is the assessment unless the assessing officer acts within four years; he must be heard before a best-judgment assessment; an objection must be decided within a time limit or it is accepted; the appeal is to a District Court judge, in private. But the taxpayer must also act: an objection is due within 30 days and must give reasons; a person who never filed can object only by filing the return; an appeal is due within 30 days of the order. Someone who misses these steps can be bound by an assessment he could have reversed. The same structure appears in VAT (ss. 82–83) and, with differences, in real-estate taxation (lesson 25.6), so learning it once serves three taxes.

## The law

### Stage 1: the return is the assessment

> מסר אדם דו״ח לפי סעיף 131, יראו את הדו״ח כקביעת הכנסה בידי אותו אדם (להלן – שומה עצמית) [...]

*Gist:* A filed return is a self-assessment; the assessing officer may, within four years from the end of the tax year in which the return was filed, confirm it or, if he has reasonable grounds to think it is wrong, assess by his best judgment. [s. 145(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_145) If no return was filed, he may assess by best judgment at any time (s. 145(ב)), and that does not remove liability for not filing.

> לא תיעשה שומה לפי מיטב השפיטה על פי סעיף 145 ולא יינתן צו לפי סעיף 152, בלי שניתנה לנישום הזדמנות סבירה להשמיע טענותיו.

*Gist:* No best-judgment assessment and no order may be made without giving the taxpayer a reasonable opportunity to be heard, and the notice must give reasons and the method of the assessment; this does not apply to a person who filed no return. [s. 158א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_158א) This is the tax version of the right to be heard of [lesson 14.4](../module-14/lesson-04.md).

### Stage 2: the objection

> היה אדם חולק על השומה, רשאי הוא לבקש מאת פקיד השומה, בהודעת השגה, בכתב, לחזור ולעיין ולשנות את השומה; בקשה כאמור תפרש בדיוק את הנימוקים להשגה על השומה ותוגש תוך שלושים יום מיום המצאת הודעת השומה [...]

*Gist:* An objection is in writing, sets out the grounds exactly, and is filed within 30 days of service of the assessment notice; the assessing officer may extend the time if the taxpayer was abroad, ill or had another reasonable cause. [s. 150(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_150)

- A person who did not file and received a s. 145(ב) assessment can object only by filing the return for that year (s. 150(ב)).
- The officer who made the assessment does not decide the objection (s. 150א).
- In the objection the officer may demand documents and summon witnesses (s. 151).

### Stage 3: agreement or order, and the deemed acceptance

> לא הושג הסכם, יקבע פקיד השומה את המס בצו שבכתב, ורשאי הוא לקיים בו את השומה, להגדילה או להפחיתה.

*Gist:* If there is agreement, the assessment is amended accordingly; if not, the officer issues a written order that may confirm, increase or reduce the assessment. [s. 152(א)–(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_152)

> אם בתוך ארבע שנים מתום שנת המס שבה נמסר הדו״ח לפי סעיף 131 או בתום שנה מיום שהוגשה השגה, לפי המאוחר מביניהם, לא הושג הסכם [...] ולא השתמש פקיד השומה בסמכויותיו לפי סעיף קטן (ב), יראו את ההשגה כאילו התקבלה;

*Gist:* If neither an agreement nor an order is made by the later of four years from the end of the tax year in which the return was filed or one year from the objection, the objection is treated as accepted; for an objection to a no-return assessment the limit is five years from the end of the year in which it was filed. [s. 152(ג)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_152)

### Stage 4: appeal to the District Court

> מי שרואה עצמו מקופח על ידי החלטת פקיד השומה על פי סעיף 152(ב), רשאי לערער לפני בית המשפט המחוזי, שבאזור שיפוטו פעל פקיד השומה.

*Gist:* A taxpayer aggrieved by the order may appeal to the District Court of the assessing officer's district. [s. 153](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_153)

> ערעור על שומה יוגש בהודעה בכתב תוך שלושים יום מהיום שבו נמסרה השומה למערער.

*Gist:* The appeal is filed within 30 days of service of the order. [Income Tax Appeals Regulations reg. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_בית_משפט_(ערעורים_בעניני_מס_הכנסה))

- The notice of appeal states the court, the appellant's details and tax years, the decision and its date of service, the amounts determined and the amounts the appellant says are right, and the remedies; the order is attached (reg. 4). A court fee applies (reg. 3; ⚠️ amount not checked).
- The assessing officer files his grounds (נימוקי שומה) within 30 days of receiving the appeal; the appellant then files his grounds (נימוקי ערעור) within 30 days; a party who does not file may lose the appeal (regs 5, 7).
- Evidence-in-chief is by affidavit; summations are in writing (reg. 9(ב1)).
- The hearing is closed to the public unless the appellant asks otherwise (s. 154(ג)).
- The burden of proving the assessment excessive is on the appellant, except where he kept admissible books (s. 155).
- The court may confirm, reduce, increase or cancel the assessment (s. 156); appeal to the Supreme Court as in civil appeals (s. 157).

### Who may represent

> אלה רשאים לייצג נישומים [...] (1) רואה חשבון [...] (3) יועץ מס מייצג [...]

*Gist:* Accountants and representing tax advisers may represent taxpayers before the Tax Authority, without affecting a lawyer's rights. [s. 236](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_236) In the District Court appeal, the taxpayer may appear himself or through a lawyer; whether an accountant may appear in court is not settled in the sources checked (⚠️ unverified, see [can I act without a lawyer?](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md)).

## Leading cases

### Key text: the statutory time limits as taxpayer protection

The assessment chapter is applied in hundreds of District Court appeals a year, but its structure is clear from the text, which is the key source here. Three features carry most of the weight in practice. The four-year limit in s. 145(א)(2) makes the self-assessment final if the officer does nothing. The deemed acceptance in s. 152(ג) makes delay the officer's risk, not the taxpayer's. And s. 158א requires a hearing and reasons before a best-judgment assessment or order, which courts read together with the general administrative duties of fairness ([lesson 14.4](../module-14/lesson-04.md)). The Director may still reopen an assessment within limits (s. 147), longer where there was a conviction or a composition fine (lesson 25.8). The VAT Law follows the same pattern (ss. 82–83; [lesson 25.3](lesson-03.md)).

## How it works in practice

### Reading the notice

An assessment notice states the taxable income, the tax and your rights under s. 150 or s. 153 (s. 149). Note the **date you received it**: the 30 days for an objection, and later for an appeal, run from service.

### The objection, step by step

1. **Read the reasons** (s. 158א(ב)): what did the officer not accept, and how did he compute the assessment?
2. **Collect proof**: documents, bank statements, contracts, witnesses.
3. **Write** a short, numbered objection: the assessment and its date; each point disputed, with the facts, the evidence and the law; the amount you say is right and how you computed it; a request to be heard; your contact details. Attach copies, not originals.
4. **File** at the assessing office or through the online service, within 30 days; keep proof of filing (⚠️ manual check of the current online objection service).
5. **Meetings.** The officer will usually invite you to a discussion. Bring a written summary; ask for the protocol.
6. **Outcome.** Agreement → amended assessment. No agreement → an order with reasons. No decision in time → deemed acceptance (s. 152(ג)): write to the office and say so.

### The appeal

An appeal is a full court case, with pleadings, affidavits and cross-examination ([lesson 15.7](../module-15/lesson-07.md)). Many self-represented taxpayers manage the objection themselves and take professional help for the appeal.

## Worked example

Shmuel Cohen, a self-employed electrician from Bnei Brak, filed his 2023 return on time. On 10 June 2026 he received a best-judgment assessment: the assessing officer added ₪60,000 to his income, saying deposits in his bank account were unreported fees. Before the assessment Shmuel was invited to a meeting where he explained some of the deposits.

1. **Timing.** The return for 2023 was filed in 2024; the officer may assess until the end of 2028 (four years from the end of 2024). The assessment is in time.
2. **Hearing and reasons.** He was heard and the notice explains the method (deposits analysis): s. 158א was complied with.
3. **Objection deadline.** 30 days from 10 June: by 10 July 2026 (s. 150(א)).
4. **Grounds.** Of the ₪60,000, ₪25,000 was a loan from his brother (signed loan agreement, bank transfer from the brother's account); ₪20,000 was a transfer between his own accounts (statements); ₪15,000 he cannot explain. He objects to ₪45,000 with documents, accepts ₪15,000, and asks to be heard.
5. **Decision.** The objection was filed on 5 July 2026. The officer must reach agreement or issue an order by the later of four years from the end of 2024 (end of 2028) and one year from the objection (5 July 2027), so by the end of 2028; otherwise the objection is accepted (s. 152(ג)).
6. **If an order confirms the assessment**, Shmuel has 30 days from service to appeal to the District Court of the district in which his assessing officer acted (s. 153; reg. 2), where the burden of showing the assessment excessive is his (s. 155).

## Do it yourself

### You need

- An assessment notice of your own, or the Cohen case.
- The documents supporting your return.
- The [tax assessment objection (draft)](../../templates/tax-assessment-objection.md).
- 75 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Stage and deadlines (10 min).** Which stage are you in? Write the date of service, the objection deadline, the four-year and one-year limits under s. 152(ג), and the appeal deadline if an order is made.
2. **Reasons check (10 min).** Did the notice give reasons and the method? Were you heard (s. 158א)?
3. **Grounds table (20 min).** For each item in dispute: the officer's position, your position, your evidence, the law.
4. **Draft the objection (30 min).** Use the template: numbered grounds, the amount you accept, the amount you dispute, the computation, documents attached, request to be heard.
5. **Appeal outline (5 min).** In five lines: what would you appeal, to which court, by when, and what the regs require in the notice.

### Template

[Tax assessment objection (draft)](../../templates/tax-assessment-objection.md).

### How you know it worked

- Your objection is dated within 30 days of service and gives exact grounds, not just "I disagree" (s. 150(א)).
- You accept what you cannot dispute and give a figure for what you think is right.
- You can state the date on which the objection will be treated as accepted if no order is made.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Partly. The objection to the assessing officer: yes, in your own name. Accountants and representing tax advisers may also represent you there (s. 236). The appeal to the District Court: you may appear yourself, but it is a full civil-style proceeding with affidavits and cross-examination, closed to the public; consider a lawyer or tax specialist.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Where and how

- Objection: the assessing office that issued the assessment, in writing or online (⚠️ manual check of the online service). No fee (⚠️ manual check). Deadline: 30 days ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- Appeal: the District Court of the assessing officer's district, through the court registry or Net HaMishpat (unrepresented filing ⚠️ check current options); 30 days from the order; court fee (⚠️ check).
- Free help: Tax Authority call centre; the Tax Authority's internal ombudsman for service complaints (⚠️ manual check); [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The assessment includes a fine for a deficiency of more than half the tax (s. 191) or alleges intent to evade.
- There is a criminal investigation or the officer mentions one (lesson 25.8).
- You reach the appeal stage and the amounts or the issues are substantial.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Objection filed after 30 days | Assessment becomes final | Ask for an extension with the reason (abroad, ill) | Diary the date of service |
| "I object" without grounds | Objection rejected as not reasoned | File a supplementary reasoned objection quickly | Number every ground with evidence |
| Never filed, objects by letter | Not a valid objection (s. 150(ב)) | File the return for the year | File the return itself |
| Not noticing the officer's deadline passed | Losing a deemed acceptance | Write to the office citing s. 152(ג) | Diary the s. 152(ג) date |
| Appeal without the order attached or amounts stated | Defective notice | Amend quickly | Follow reg. 4 point by point |

## Review

- A filed return is the assessment unless the officer acts within four years; a best-judgment assessment needs a hearing and reasons (ss. 145, 158א).
- Object in writing with exact grounds within 30 days; a non-filer objects by filing the return (s. 150).
- No agreement → an order; no decision within the s. 152(ג) period → the objection is accepted.
- Appeal from the order to the District Court within 30 days; closed doors; burden on the appellant (ss. 153–156; reg. 2).
