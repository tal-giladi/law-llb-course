# 25.3 · The self-employed: VAT, bookkeeping and advance payments

By the end of this lesson you will be able to run the tax side of a one-person business for a year: work out the VAT for a period, know when an exempt dealer has to switch to licensed status, choose between keeping full expense records and the 30% small-business track, pay income-tax advances, and object if the VAT office assesses you. Registration itself (the three files, exempt or licensed, the professions that are always licensed) was taught in [lesson 16.7](../module-16/lesson-07.md); read it first.

## Why it matters

A salaried employee's tax is collected by the employer; a self-employed person collects and pays his own taxes, and the law treats him accordingly. He charges VAT to customers and holds it for the State until the next report; he pays income tax in advances during the year and settles in his annual return; he keeps books that may be rejected if they are not kept as required. Most tax trouble of small businesses is not fraud but drift: an exempt dealer who grew past the ceiling and kept issuing receipts, a licensed dealer who deducted VAT on a large invoice without an allocation number, a freelancer whose advances were set too low and who faces a large bill with interest. This lesson is the yearly operating manual.

## The law

### The VAT charge and the rate

> על עסקה בישראל ועל יבוא טובין יוטל מס ערך מוסף בשיעור אחד ממחיר העסקה או הטובין, כפי שקבע שר האוצר בצו [...]

*Gist:* VAT is charged at a single rate on every transaction in Israel and on imports; the Wikisource note records the current order setting it at 18%. [VAT Law s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מס_ערך_מוסף#סעיף_2) See [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

An exempt dealer's transactions are exempt (s. 31(3)): he neither charges VAT nor deducts it. The exempt-dealer ceiling is linked to the index every 1 January (s. 126(א)); for 2026 the Wikisource note and a business press guide both give **₪122,833** (see current numbers; ⚠️ still to be confirmed on the Tax Authority's site, which blocks automated access). This reconciles the figure flagged in module 16.

### Deducting input tax

> עוסק זכאי לנכות מהמס שהוא חייב בו את מס התשומות הכלול בחשבונית מס שהוצאה לו כדין [...] ובלבד [...] שהניכוי ייעשה בתוך ששה חדשים מיום הוצאת המסמך [...]

*Gist:* A licensed dealer deducts the VAT on business purchases if he holds a lawful tax invoice in his name, within six months of its issue. [s. 38(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מס_ערך_מוסף#סעיף_38)

> לא יותר ניכוי מס התשומות הכלול בחשבונית מס שסכומה, בלא המס, עולה על 5,000 שקלים חדשים [...] ושאינה כוללת מספר שהקצה לה המנהל [...]

*Gist:* Input tax on an invoice above the threshold (before VAT) may not be deducted unless the invoice carries an allocation number issued by the Tax Authority. [s. 38(א1)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מס_ערך_מוסף#סעיף_38) The Wikisource note gives ₪10,000 for January–May 2026 and ₪5,000 under the section from then on (see current numbers). The rule is aimed at fictitious invoices; the buyer protects himself by checking the number before paying.

### Periodic reports

> דו״ח תקופתי יוגש תוך חמישה עשר יום לאחר תקופת הדו״ח שבו, אף אם לא היו באותה תקופה עסקים או פעילות המחייבים בתשלום מס [...]

*Gist:* The periodic report and payment are due within 15 days after each period, even with no activity. [s. 67(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מס_ערך_מוסף#סעיף_67) The period is two months for a dealer whose turnover is up to the threshold in s. 67(א2) (₪1,775,000 since September 2025 per the Wikisource note; module 16 used the older ₪1,500,000) and one month above it. The deadline moves when three rest days fall in the five days before it (s. 67(ב1)).

### Objection and appeal in VAT

> החולק על שומה רשאי להשיג עליה בכתב מנומק לפני המנהל, תוך שלושים יום לאחר שהומצאה לו הודעת השומה [...]

*Gist:* A dealer who disputes a VAT assessment objects in writing, with reasons, within 30 days; if the director does not decide within a year the objection is treated as accepted; the person who made the assessment may not decide the objection. [s. 82](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מס_ערך_מוסף#סעיף_82) An appeal from the decision lies to the District Court, heard by one judge, closed to the public unless the appellant asks otherwise; the burden is on the appellant if the report is not supported by lawful books (s. 83). The same pattern, objection then appeal, governs income tax (lesson 25.5).

### Income tax for the self-employed

- **Books.** The Director may require books for business income (s. 130); the Bookkeeping Instructions 1973 set what each type of business keeps. Books found inadmissible lose the reduced personal-exertion rates (s. 121(ב)(2)) and shift the burden of proof.
- **Small-business track.** A resident individual whose turnover does not exceed the exempt-dealer ceiling and who is registered by the assessing officer as a small-business owner (בעל עסק זעיר) deducts **30% of turnover** as deemed expenses instead of proving actual expenses (ss. 87ב–87ד); an exempt dealer is registered automatically unless he asks not to be (s. 87ג(א)). The track suits a business with low real expenses and can be cancelled on request.
- **Advances.**

> כל נישום חייב לשלם ביום החמישה עשר של כל אחד מעשרת החדשים פברואר עד נובמבר של כל שנת מס, על חשבון המס לאותה שנה, מקדמה של 10% מסכום המס שנתחייב בו לשנה הקובעת [...]

*Gist:* By default a taxpayer pays ten monthly advances, February to November, each 10% of the tax for the base year; regulations allow advances as a percentage of turnover instead, which is what most self-employed people pay. [s. 175](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_175)

- **Annual return.** Every self-employed person must file, online, by the s. 132 date, with a profit and loss statement (s. 131(ב), (ב2)).

## Leading cases

### Key text: VAT Law s. 38(א1) and the allocation-number reform

No single ruling governs this lesson's practice; the key text is the allocation-number rule. The Wikisource note shows ₪10,000 for January–May 2026 and the statutory ₪5,000 afterwards. Read it with s. 38(א) (the six-month window) and s. 117 (offences): issuing or using a fictitious invoice to evade tax is an offence of up to five years, seven in aggravated circumstances (s. 117(ב), (ב2)). For the leading case on what a partner owns when a business moves to a company, see *Sadot* in [lesson 16.7](../module-16/lesson-07.md); for the business-or-capital indications, *Magid* in [lesson 25.1](lesson-01.md).

## How it works in practice

### The self-employed calendar (licensed dealer, bimonthly)

| When | What |
|---|---|
| 15th of the month after each two-month period | VAT report and payment (s. 67) |
| 15th of each month, February–November (or monthly with the VAT report, by turnover) | Income-tax advance (s. 175; the rate notice from the assessing officer) |
| Monthly (by the 15th, per NII notice) | National Insurance advances ([lesson 17.7](../module-17/lesson-07.md)) |
| 30 April / 31 May after the year (often extended) | Annual income-tax return with the P&L (ss. 131, 132) |
| 31 January | Exempt dealer only: declaration of last year's turnover (VAT Registration Regs reg. 15, lesson 16.7) |

### Crossing the exempt ceiling

Track turnover monthly. When it is clear the year's turnover will pass the ceiling, ask the VAT office to change your status; transactions from the moment of crossing are taxable, and VAT you did not charge comes out of your own pocket. Your income-tax small-business registration ends at the same time (s. 87ג(ג)(1)).

### Online services

VAT reports, advances and the annual return are filed through the Tax Authority's online services and personal area (⚠️ manual check of the current services on gov.il; the site blocks automated access). Invoices above the threshold need an allocation number obtained by the seller through the Tax Authority's system or certified invoicing software.

## Worked example

Rawan Khatib, from Nazareth, became a private maths tutor in 2025 and registered as an exempt dealer (assume her occupation is not in reg. 13). Her 2025 turnover was ₪90,000. In 2026 she starts teaching groups and by 31 August her turnover is ₪95,000; she expects ₪150,000 for the year. On 1 September she buys a laptop for ₪6,500 plus VAT from a computer shop.

1. **Status.** ₪150,000 is above the 2026 ceiling of ₪122,833: she must become a licensed dealer once she passes it. She asks the VAT office to change her status in September, before she crosses it, and from then charges 18% VAT on her fees.
2. **Laptop.** As a licensed dealer, she may deduct the input tax on the laptop (s. 38(א)) if the invoice is in her name and she deducts it within six months. The invoice is ₪6,500 before VAT, above ₪5,000 (the threshold from June 2026): without an allocation number she cannot deduct it (s. 38(א1)). She asks the shop for an allocation number before paying.
3. **Reports.** Her first bimonthly report for September–October is due by 15 November (s. 67(ב)).
4. **Income tax.** She was registered as a small-business owner while exempt. Once her turnover passes the ceiling she no longer qualifies (s. 87ב), so for 2026 she computes income on actual expenses with proper books; the assessing officer updates her advances as a percentage of turnover.
5. **Return.** She files an online annual return for 2026 with her profit and loss statement by the 2027 deadline.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your own planned or existing business (or the Khatib case).
- A list of last year's sales and purchases, or a realistic estimate.
- The [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md) page.
- 60 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Status check (10 min).** Compare your expected 2026 turnover with the ceiling and your occupation with reg. 13 ([lesson 16.7](../module-16/lesson-07.md)). Exempt or licensed? From when?
2. **One VAT period (15 min).** For a licensed dealer, take two months: list sales (output tax at 18%) and purchases (input tax). Mark which invoices you may not deduct (not in your name, older than six months, above the threshold without an allocation number). Compute the payment.
3. **Income-tax track (10 min).** If you qualify for the small-business track, compare 30% of turnover with your real expenses. Which is better?
4. **Calendar (15 min).** Fill the table above for your business for twelve months, with the dates.
5. **Objection outline (10 min).** Assume the VAT office assessed you for a period, disallowing an invoice. Write three numbered reasons for an objection under s. 82, and the date it must be filed.

### Template

None specific; for the objection letter use the structure in the [tax assessment objection (draft)](../../templates/tax-assessment-objection.md) from lesson 25.5.

### How you know it worked

- Your VAT computation separates output and input tax and excludes at least one invoice for a stated reason (or explains why none is excluded).
- Your calendar has the 15th-of-the-month VAT dates and the advance dates.
- Your objection outline is dated within 30 days of the assessment and gives reasons, not just "I disagree".

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. A sole trader files his own VAT reports, pays advances, files his own return and may object to and appeal a VAT assessment himself. Others may represent him before the Tax Authority only if they are lawyers, accountants or representing tax advisers (Income Tax Ordinance s. 236; Bar Association Law s. 21).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Where and how

- The regional VAT office and the assessing office; the Tax Authority's online services (⚠️ manual check).
- National Insurance for self-employed advances (⚠️ manual check on btl.gov.il).
- Free help: Ma'of small-business centres; the Tax Authority call centre; [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer (or accountant) when

- The VAT office or assessing officer rejects your books or opens an investigation.
- You received or issued an invoice you suspect is fictitious.
- You are moving from sole trader to a company (Sadot, lesson 16.7) or taking a partner.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Staying exempt after passing the ceiling | VAT on past sales from your own pocket | Ask for licensed status; report | Track turnover monthly |
| Deducting input VAT on a large invoice without an allocation number | Deduction disallowed in an assessment | Ask the seller to obtain a number; object if the law allows | Check the number before paying |
| No VAT report for a period with no sales | Fines and a best-judgment assessment | File a nil report now | File every period (s. 67(ב)) |
| Advances set on an old, low base year | A large bill with interest after the year | Ask the assessing officer to update advances | Update advances when income rises |
| Mixing private and business purchases | Input tax and expenses disallowed | Separate them in the books | Separate account and card for the business |

## Review

- VAT is 18% in 2026; an exempt dealer below ₪122,833 charges and deducts none (ss. 2, 31(3); ⚠️ confirm the ceiling on the Tax Authority site).
- Input tax is deductible only with a lawful invoice in your name within six months, and invoices above the threshold need an allocation number (s. 38).
- Periodic reports are due by the 15th after each period, even with no activity; a VAT objection is due within 30 days, then appeal to the District Court (ss. 67, 82, 83).
- Small businesses under the ceiling may deduct 30% of turnover instead of actual expenses (ss. 87ב–87ד); advances are paid monthly (s. 175).
