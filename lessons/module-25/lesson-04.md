# 25.4 · Tax refunds for employees

By the end of this lesson you will be able to spot an overpayment of income tax in your own pay history, choose the right way to claim it, check that the year is still open, and know what interest the State adds. The practice lab in lesson 25.10 turns this into a complete refund file.

## Why it matters

The monthly withholding system assumes that a person works the whole year for one employer at the same salary with all his credit points recorded. Real life is different: people start and leave jobs mid-year, have two jobs, are on unemployment or maternity pay, serve in the reserves, give donations, finish degrees and have children. Each of these can make the tax withheld higher than the tax due. The difference is not returned automatically to an employee who is exempt from filing; he has to ask. The law gives him six years to do it, and the State pays linkage and interest on the overpayment, so a refund claimed late is not worth less, but a year that slips past the six-year limit is lost.

## The law

### The six-year window and the refund after an assessment

> הוכח להנחת דעתו של פקיד השומה, כי אדם שילם מס לשנת מס פלונית – בין דרך ניכוי ובין בדרך אחרת – יתר על הסכום שהוא חייב בו, והדו״ח לאותה שנה הוגש לא יאוחר מתום שש שנים אחריה, יהא אותו אדם זכאי [...] שיוחזר לו תשלום היתר בתוספת הפרשי הצמדה וריבית [...]

*Gist:* A person who overpaid tax for a year, by withholding or otherwise, is entitled to the refund with linkage and interest if the report for that year was filed no later than six years after the end of the year. [Income Tax Ordinance s. 160(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_160)

So in 2026 you can still claim for 2020 if you file by the end of 2026 (2020 + six years). The refund is paid within a year of the assessment that fixed the overpayment or two years after the end of the year in which the tax was paid, whichever is later, with linkage and interest from the end of the tax year or the payment date. An employee who decides on the assessing officer's refund decision has the same rights of objection and appeal as against an assessment (s. 160(ג); lesson 25.5).

> לא יוחזר לאדם תשלום לשנת מס שעליה לא מסר דו״ח, או הזניח למסרו, [...] אלא אם הוכיח [...] שאי־המסירה או ההזנחה למסור דו״ח אמיתי ונכון אין מקורן מרמה או מעשה או מחדל שבמזיד [...]

*Gist:* Someone who was required to file and did not, or filed a false return, gets a refund only if he shows the failure was not fraud or wilful. [s. 160(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_160)

### The refund after a return

> [...] תוחזר לו היתרה תוך 90 יום מיום שהגיש את הדו״ח, או ביום 31 ביולי בשנת המס שלאחר שנת המס שלגביה הוגש הדו״ח, לפי המאוחר [...]

*Gist:* When a return based on proper books or documents shows an overpayment, the balance is refunded within 90 days of filing or on 31 July of the following year, whichever is later, with linkage and 4% interest. [s. 159א(ב)–(ג)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_159א) The refund is paid into the taxpayer's bank account (Refund Regulations reg. 1); a refund paid by mistake becomes a tax debt (s. 159א(ד)).

### Who uses which route

- An individual who **must** file (s. 131; reg. 3 of the Exemption Regulations, lesson 25.2) claims the refund in the annual return (Form 1301).
- An individual who is **exempt** from filing (reg. 2) may ask for a refund with a request for a refund for a person not required to file (Form 135), on paper at the assessing office or through the Tax Authority's online refund service for employees (⚠️ manual check of the current form version and online service on gov.il).

### Combat reservists

> בחישוב המס לשנת המס של יחיד ששירת שירות מילואים כלוחם בשנת המס שקדמה לה [...] יובאו בחשבון נקודות זיכוי [...]

*Gist:* A combat reservist receives credit points in the year after his service according to the number of days served, up to four points; a temporary scale applies for 2026 and 2027. [s. 39ב](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_39ב) Points missed through payroll are a frequent refund item (⚠️ check the scale in the official text before you rely on it).

## Leading cases

### Key text: s. 160 and the six-year rule

The refund provisions are applied every day by assessing officers rather than litigated, so the key text is the statute. Three points matter in practice. First, the six years run from the end of the tax year, not from the date you discovered the overpayment. Second, the refund carries linkage and interest (s. 159א(א): index plus 4% a year), so the State bears the cost of delay. Third, a person who should have filed and did not must show the failure was not fraudulent or wilful (s. 160(ב)); this is why someone with, for example, unreported rent should first regularise his filing before asking for money back (lesson 25.8). For the doctrine of what can be deducted, see *Vered Peri* in [lesson 25.2](lesson-02.md).

## How it works in practice

### Typical reasons an employee overpaid

| Situation | Why tax was overpaid |
|---|---|
| Started or left a job mid-year | Monthly withholding assumed a full year of salary at that level |
| Two employers without tax coordination | Second job withheld at the maximum rate |
| Unemployment, maternity or reserve pay | NII withheld tax on the benefit without the full annual picture |
| Credit points not claimed | Degree, discharged soldier, children, single parent, new immigrant, combat reservist |
| Donations, life insurance, private pension deposits | Credits not reported through payroll |
| Losses on securities or tax withheld on interest | Losses can be set off against gains in a return |
| A severance or pension withdrawal taxed at a flat rate | Spreading or exemptions not applied |

### Steps and timing

1. **Gather documents** for each year: Form 106 from every employer; NII annual certificates for benefits; receipts for donations; insurance and pension certificates; degree, discharge, reserve-service and children's documents; bank statements of securities with tax certificates.
2. **Choose the route**: exempt from filing → Form 135 or the online refund service; required to file → annual return.
3. **File** online in your personal area or at the assessing office. Keep a copy and the receipt.
4. **Wait** for the assessment. The assessing officer may ask for documents. A refund decision you disagree with can be objected to (s. 160(ג)).
5. **Payment** goes to your bank account with linkage and interest.

### Avoid paid "refund companies" if you can do it yourself

Some private firms advertise refund checks for a percentage of the refund. You may use them, but they are preparing a tax filing for you; check whom you are dealing with (representation before the Tax Authority is limited to lawyers, accountants and representing tax advisers, s. 236) and what they charge. The procedure is designed to be done by the taxpayer.

## Worked example

Yonatan Levi, 31, from Be'er Sheva, worked for Company A from January to April 2024 (₪12,000 a month), was unemployed and received unemployment benefit from May to July, and joined Company B in August. In 2024 he also served 25 days of combat reserve duty and gave ₪600 to a recognised charity. He never filed anything.

1. **Overpayment signs.** Two employers in one year, each withholding as if the salary were for a full year; NII withholding on the unemployment benefit; a reserve service that gives points in 2025, not 2024 (s. 39ב: the year *after* the service); a donation.
2. **Route.** His only income was salary and NII benefits below the ceiling, with no reg. 3 exclusion: he is exempt from filing. He uses Form 135 (or the online service) for 2024.
3. **Documents.** Form 106 from A and from B, the NII certificate for unemployment benefit and tax withheld, the donation receipt.
4. **Window.** For 2024 he may file until the end of 2030 (s. 160(א)). He also checks 2025, where the reserve points belong.
5. **Result.** The assessing officer computes the tax for 2024 on his actual annual income with his credit points, credits 35% of the donation, and refunds the difference with linkage and interest to his bank account.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your Forms 106 and NII certificates for the last six years (or the Levi case). Employers must give Form 106; ask the payroll department for copies, and the NII certificates are available in its personal area (⚠️ manual check).
- Receipts and certificates for credits and points (lesson 25.2).
- 60 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Year list (10 min).** Write the last six tax years. Cross out any year you already filed for.
2. **Signs of overpayment (20 min).** For each open year, check the table above and mark every sign.
3. **Documents (15 min).** For each marked year list the documents you have and those you must get.
4. **Route (5 min).** For each year: Form 135 / online service, or an annual return? Why?
5. **Deadline (5 min).** Write the last date for each year under s. 160(א). Put the oldest one in your calendar.
6. **Decision (5 min).** Which year will you claim first, and when?

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. A refund request is filed by the taxpayer for himself, on paper or online. If someone else prepares it, check that he is entitled to represent you (Income Tax Ordinance s. 236) and what he charges.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Where and how

- Form 135 or the online refund service for employees in the Tax Authority's personal area (⚠️ manual check; gov.il blocks automated access, so confirm the current form and service yourself).
- The assessing office for your area (in person or by mail).
- Fee: none (⚠️ manual check).
- Deadline: the report for each year must be filed within six years after the end of that year ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- Free help: the Tax Authority call centre; Kol-Zchut's refund guide (⚠️ manual check); [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer (or accountant) when

- You find you were required to file for some years and did not (rent, business or foreign income): regularise first (lesson 25.8).
- The refund involves a severance or pension withdrawal, securities losses across years, or foreign tax.
- The assessing officer refuses the refund and you want to object (lesson 25.5).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Waiting more than six years | The oldest year is lost | Claim every year still open now | Check every January which year is about to close |
| Claiming with Form 135 when you must file a return | Request treated as inadequate | File the full annual return | Check reg. 3 exclusions first |
| Missing Form 106 from a short job | Assessment ignores tax withheld | Ask the employer; the Tax Authority may have the data | Keep every Form 106 |
| Putting reserve points in the year of service | Points claimed in the wrong year | Claim in the following year (s. 39ב) | Read the section's timing |
| Paying a firm a large share for a simple refund | Loss of part of the refund | Ask whether you can file yourself | Read this lesson first |

## Review

- An overpayment can be reclaimed if the report for the year is filed within six years after its end; the refund carries linkage and interest (s. 160).
- After a return based on proper documents, the refund is paid within 90 days or by 31 July, whichever is later (s. 159א).
- An employee exempt from filing uses Form 135 or the online service; one who must file uses the annual return.
- A person who failed to file when required must show no fraud or wilful default (s. 160(ב)).
