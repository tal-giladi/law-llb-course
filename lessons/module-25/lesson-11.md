# 25.11 · Practice lab: a purchase-tax self-assessment and an objection

In this lab you prepare the buyer's side of a 2026 apartment purchase: the single-apartment analysis, the computation and the timeline. Then the director disagrees and assesses at the additional-apartment rate, and you write the objection. The result is a complete purchase-tax file you can reuse in the year-3 clinic (module 31).

## Why it matters

Purchase-tax disputes are common, the amounts are large, and the procedure is short: 30 days to object, 30 days to go to the appeals committee. A buyer who has his analysis and documents ready on the day of the contract can answer the director in days, not months.

## The law

The rules are in lesson 25.6: the single-apartment scale and the buyer unit (s. 9(ג1ג)(2)–(4)); the disregarded shares (s. 49ג(3): up to a third, or up to a half if inherited); the declaration within 30 days (s. 73(ג)); review within eight months (s. 78(ב)); payment within 60 days (s. 90א); the objection with the grounds and the undisputed tax within 30 days (s. 87(א)); the appeals committee within 30 days (s. 88). [Real Estate Taxation Law on Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מיסוי_מקרקעין_(שבח_ורכישה)#סעיף_87)

## Leading cases

### Key text: s. 87(א) and the undisputed amount

Section 87(א) requires the objection to state the grounds, the amount of tax that follows from them and how it is computed (the "undisputed amount"). An objection that only says "the assessment is wrong" is defective. The director must decide with reasons within eight months (extendable to one year) and must hear you before rejecting it; if he does not decide in time, the objection is accepted (s. 87(ד)–(ז)).

## How it works in practice

The buyer's declaration and the objection are filed with the regional Real Estate Taxation office, in person or online (⚠️ manual check of the online service for unrepresented parties). Pay the undisputed amount on time to stop interest running on it, and keep the assessment notice, which starts the 30 days.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- A real or planned purchase of your own, **or** the supplied case below.
- The [purchase-tax worksheet (draft)](../../templates/purchase-tax-worksheet.md) and the [tax assessment objection (draft)](../../templates/tax-assessment-objection.md).
- The [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md) page.
- 150 minutes.

**Supplied case.** Noam Ohana, 38, from Netanya, married to Liat; they have two children aged 7 and 12. On 5 February 2026 they sign a contract to buy an apartment in Netanya for ₪2,450,000 from a private seller. They rent their current home. Noam owns 40% of an apartment in Ashkelon that he and his sister inherited from their father in 2022 (succession order and land-registry extract available); his sister owns 60%. Liat owns nothing. Their lawyer is on holiday, so Noam files the buyer's declaration himself on 3 March 2026, claiming the single-apartment scale. On 20 August 2026 the director issues a best-judgment assessment at the additional-apartment rate: "the buyer holds a 40% share in another residential apartment, which exceeds one third". The notice is received on 25 August 2026.

### Steps

1. **Buyer unit and property (10 min).** List the buyer unit and every apartment or share.
2. **Single apartment? (20 min).** Apply s. 9(ג1ג)(2) and (4)(א) with s. 49ג(3). Is the Ashkelon share disregarded? Why?
3. **Computation (25 min).** Compute the tax on the single-apartment scale slice by slice and on the additional-apartment scale (s. 9(ג1ו)). Write the difference.
4. **Timeline (15 min).** Declaration deadline (was it met?), payment deadline, the director's review period (did he act within eight months?), objection deadline, director's decision deadline, appeals-committee deadline.
5. **Objection (50 min).** Draft the objection with the template: the assessment and its date of service; the facts with exhibits; the legal grounds; the undisputed amount and its computation; the remedy; a request to be heard.
6. **Appeals-committee plan (15 min).** If the objection is rejected: forum, deadline, what you would attach.
7. **Review (15 min).** Check against "How you know it worked".

### How you know it worked

- Your analysis cites s. 9(ג1ג)(4)(א) and s. 49ג(3) and the fact that the share was inherited.
- Your computation shows each slice with the 2026 amounts.
- Your objection states the undisputed amount and how it is computed (s. 87(א)) and is filed within 30 days of service.
- Every deadline in your timeline is counted from the right event.

<details><summary>Model answer for the supplied case (outline)</summary>

**Buyer unit.** Noam, Liat and the two children (under 18) are one buyer (s. 9(ג1ג)(4)(ג)). Property: 40% of the Ashkelon apartment (Noam).

**Single apartment.** The Ashkelon apartment counts unless disregarded. Section 9(ג1ג)(4)(א) disregards an apartment in which the buyer's share is "as stated in s. 49ג(3)": up to a third, and **for an inherited apartment up to a half**. Noam's 40% share was inherited (succession order), so it is disregarded and the Netanya apartment is their single apartment. The director applied the one-third limit for purchased shares; that is the error.

**Computation (2026).** 0% on ₪1,978,745 = ₪0. 3.5% on ₪368,295 = ₪12,890. 5% on ₪102,960 (₪2,450,000 − ₪2,347,040) = ₪5,148. Total about **₪18,038**. Additional-apartment scale: 8% × ₪2,450,000 = ₪196,000. Difference about ₪177,962.

**Timeline.** Contract 5.2.2026; declaration due 7.3.2026 (30 days), filed 3.3.2026 (on time); payment due 6.4.2026 (60 days). The director's assessment on 20.8.2026 is within eight months of the declaration (s. 78(ב)). Objection due 30 days from service on 25.8.2026, i.e. by 24.9.2026 (s. 87(א)). The director decides within eight months of the objection (extendable to one year), after a hearing, or the objection is accepted (s. 87(ד), (ו), (ז)). If rejected: appeals committee within 30 days of the decision (s. 88); then the Supreme Court on a point of law within 45 days (s. 90).

**Objection (outline in Hebrew).**
1. ביום 25.8.2026 נמסרה לי שומה לפי מיטב השפיטה מיום 20.8.2026 בעסקה ___, שבה נקבע מס רכישה לפי שיעורי דירה נוספת.
2. העובדות: רכשתי עם רעייתי דירה בנתניה בהסכם מיום 5.2.2026 בתמורה ל־2,450,000 ש״ח. אין בבעלותנו דירה אחרת, למעט 40% מדירה באשקלון שירשתי מאבי המנוח (צו ירושה – נספח 1; נסח – נספח 2).
3. הטענה המשפטית: לפי סעיף 9(ג1ג)(4)(א) לחוק, יראו דירה כדירה יחידה גם אם לרוכש חלק בדירה אחרת כאמור בסעיף 49ג(3), דהיינו עד מחצית בדירה שהתקבלה בירושה. חלקי, 40%, התקבל בירושה ואינו עולה על מחצית.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Partly. A buyer may file his own declaration and objection and go to the appeals committee in his own name. The purchase itself (contract, caveat, registration) usually needs a lawyer ([lesson 13.9](../module-13/lesson-09.md)); representing or drafting for the other party is reserved (Bar Association Law s. 20).

### Where and how

- The regional Real Estate Taxation office; online declarations and objections (⚠️ manual check).
- Deadlines: declaration 30 days; payment 60 days; objection 30 days; appeals committee 30 days ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- Free help: Tax Authority call centre and simulator (⚠️ manual check); [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The director disputes the price or the value, or alleges a false declaration (s. 98).
- The status depends on a spouse living apart or on a replacement sale near its deadline.
- The objection is rejected and the amount justifies the appeals committee.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Objection without the undisputed amount | Defective objection | Add the computation at once | Follow s. 87(א) |
| Counting 30 days from the assessment date, not service | Risk of filing late or too early by mistake | Count from the day the notice was received | Note the date of receipt |
| Not paying the undisputed amount | Interest runs on it | Pay it now | Pay what you accept on time |
| Forgetting the succession order | The share cannot be shown as inherited | Attach it | Gather documents before filing |

## Review

- Prepare the single-apartment analysis, computation and timeline before the contract is signed.
- An inherited share up to a half is disregarded; a purchased share only up to a third (s. 49ג(3)).
- An objection states the grounds, the undisputed amount and its computation, within 30 days; then the appeals committee within 30 days (ss. 87–88).
