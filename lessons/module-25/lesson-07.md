# 25.7 · Capital gains on real estate and the single-apartment exemption

By the end of this lesson you will be able to tell a seller of an apartment whether he will pay land appreciation tax (מס שבח) or sell exempt, and, if he pays, how the gain and the tax are worked out in outline. You will apply the single-apartment exemption that most Israeli families rely on when they move, the presumptions that keep it available while they hold two apartments for a short time, the ceiling that limits it, and the special exemption for an inherited apartment.

## Why it matters

When land is sold, the seller pays tax on the increase in value, the buyer pays purchase tax (lesson 25.6), and both file declarations within 30 days. For a family selling its only home the tax is usually zero, but only if the conditions are met and the exemption is *requested* in the declaration. Families moving home often hold two apartments for a period, inherit a share of a parent's apartment, or sell a valuable apartment above the ceiling; each of these can turn an exempt sale into a taxable one. Because the tax rate is 25% of the real gain, mistakes are large. This is also a Bar-exam subject.

## The law

### The charge

> מס שבח מקרקעין (להלן – המס) יוטל על השבח במכירת זכות במקרקעין. השבח הוא הסכום שבו עולה שווי המכירה על יתרת שווי הרכישה כמשמעותה בסעיף 47.

*Gist:* Land appreciation tax is charged on the gain on the sale of a right in land: sale value minus the balance of acquisition value (cost plus allowed expenses, less depreciation). [Real Estate Taxation Law s. 6](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מיסוי_מקרקעין_(שבח_ורכישה)#סעיף_6)

The gain is split into an inflationary part (exempt) and a real part (taxed) by the consumer price index (s. 47). Allowed expenses (s. 39) include improvement costs, broker's fees on purchase and on sale (generally up to 2% of the price), lawyer's, appraiser's and surveyor's fees, and the other items the section lists; keep the receipts from the day you buy.

### The rate

> יחיד יהיה חייב במס על שבח ריאלי כאמור בסעיף 121 לפקודה, בשיעור של עד 25%.

*Gist:* An individual pays tax on the real gain at his marginal rate up to 25%; a company at the corporate rate. [s. 48א(ב)(1)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מיסוי_מקרקעין_(שבח_ורכישה)#סעיף_48א) For land bought before 7.11.2001 ("the start date") or before 1.1.2012 ("the change date"), the real gain is split by time and each part is taxed at the rate of its period (s. 48א(ב1)): the highest marginal rate before 7.11.2001, up to 20% between 2001 and 2012, and up to 25% after. Special linear rules for residential apartments (s. 48א(ב2)–(ב3)) are beyond this lesson (⚠️ not studied here; see an accountant).

### The single-apartment exemption

> המוכר דירת מגורים מזכה יהא זכאי לפטור ממס במכירתה אם נתקיים אחד מאלה: [...] (2) הדירה הנמכרת היא דירתו היחידה של המוכר בישראל ובאזור [...], והמוכר הוא בעל הזכות בה במשך 18 חודשים לפחות מיום שהיתה לדירת מגורים, ולא מכר, במשך שמונה עשר החודשים שקדמו למכירה האמורה, דירת מגורים אחרת כאמור, בפטור ממס לפי פסקה זו;

*Gist:* A seller of a qualifying apartment is exempt if it is his only apartment in Israel and the Area, he has owned it for at least 18 months since it became a residence, and he did not sell another apartment with this exemption in the previous 18 months. [s. 49ב(2)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מיסוי_מקרקעין_(שבח_ורכישה)#סעיף_49ב)

- **Qualifying apartment.** One used mainly as a residence for at least four-fifths of the period of the gain, or for the four years before the sale (s. 49(א)). An empty period counts as residential use.
- **One seller.** The seller, his spouse (unless permanently apart) and their minor children count as one seller (s. 49(ב)).
- **Request and all rights.** The exemption must be requested in the seller's declaration, and he must sell all his rights in the apartment (s. 49א(א)).
- **Ceiling.** If the sale value exceeds the ceiling (₪5,008,000, frozen for 2025–2027 per s. 49א(א1); see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)), only the part up to the ceiling is exempt and the excess is taxed proportionally.

### Presumptions: when two apartments still count as one

> [...] יראו את הדירה הנמכרת כדירת המגורים היחידה שבבעלות המוכר גם אם יש לו, בנוסף על הדירה הנמכרת, דירת מגורים שנתקיים לגביה אחד מאלה: (1) היא נרכשה כתחליף לדירה הנמכרת ב־24 החודשים שקדמו למכירה; [...]

*Gist:* The sold apartment is still the "single apartment" if the other apartment was bought as its replacement in the 24 months before the sale, was let under protected tenancy before 1997, is held in a share of up to a third (up to a half if inherited), or was inherited under the conditions of s. 49ב(5). [s. 49ג](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מיסוי_מקרקעין_(שבח_ורכישה)#סעיף_49ג)

So a family that buys a new home and sells the old one within 24 months keeps the exemption on the old one; if they wait longer, they lose it.

### Inherited apartment

> המכירה היא של דירת מגורים מזכה שקיבל המוכר בירושה, ובלבד שהתקיימו כל אלה: (א) המוכר הוא בן זוגו של המוריש, או צאצא של המוריש, או בן זוגו של צאצא של המוריש; (ב) לפני פטירתו היה המוריש בעלה של דירת מגורים אחת בלבד; (ג) אילו היה המוריש עדיין בחיים ומוכר את דירת המגורים, היה פטור ממס בשל המכירה;

*Gist:* An heir who is the deceased's spouse, descendant or a descendant's spouse may sell an inherited apartment exempt if the deceased owned only that apartment and would have been exempt himself. [s. 49ב(5)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מיסוי_מקרקעין_(שבח_ורכישה)#סעיף_49ב) This exemption does not use up the heir's own single-apartment exemption.

### Filing

The seller files a declaration within 30 days of the sale with the details of the sale and the acquisition, the deductions, the tax computed and any exemption claimed (s. 73(א)); it is a self-assessment that the director may review within eight months (s. 78); the tax is paid within 60 days (s. 90א). Objection, appeals committee and Supreme Court as for purchase tax ([lesson 25.6](lesson-06.md)). An individual who sold land without paying tax at the full rate must also report the sale in his income-tax return (Income Tax Ordinance s. 131(א)(5א)).

## Leading cases

### ע״א 6672/19 יקותיאלי נ׳ מנהל מיסוי מקרקעין חיפה (28.12.2020)

In a combination deal (land swapped for apartments to be built by a developer), the Haifa director valued the construction services received by the landowners and added 15% "developer's profit" on top of the contractor's profit already included in the construction costs. The Supreme Court accepted the landowners' appeal and held that adding developer's profit undermined established case law. Why it matters: the sale value in a combination deal is the value of the construction services received, and the director cannot inflate it by a further margin; landowners in urban-renewal and combination deals should check how the sale value was computed. Source: summary in the [Hebrew Wikipedia article "מס שבח מקרקעין"](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/מס_שבח_מקרקעין) linking the ruling's text on gov.il (⚠️ secondary; the official text was not opened).

### Key text: s. 49ב(2) with s. 49ג

Most single-apartment disputes turn on the text: was it the seller's *only* apartment (including the spouse's), was it held 18 months since it became a residence, and does a s. 49ג presumption cover the second apartment? Read them together with the one-seller rule in s. 49(ב).

## How it works in practice

### The seller's checklist

1. **Seller unit.** List every apartment or share owned by the seller, spouse and minor children in Israel and the Area.
2. **Qualifying use.** Was the apartment mainly a residence for four-fifths of the period, or for the last four years?
3. **18 months.** Has he owned it 18 months since it became a residence? Has he sold another apartment exempt under s. 49ב(2) in the last 18 months?
4. **Second apartment?** If yes, is it covered by s. 49ג (replacement bought in the last 24 months; protected tenancy; small share; inheritance)?
5. **Ceiling.** Is the price above the ceiling?
6. **Declare.** Request the exemption in the seller's declaration within 30 days; attach proof.

### Who files

The seller's lawyer usually files online. A seller may file his own declaration (⚠️ manual check of the online service for unrepresented parties). The Tax Authority's online simulator helps estimate the tax (⚠️ manual check).

## Worked example

Adel and Lina Haddad, from Haifa, own one apartment bought in 2015 and lived in since. In January 2026 they buy a larger apartment for ₪2,600,000 and in October 2026 sign to sell the old one for ₪2,100,000. Lina also inherited half of her late mother's apartment in Akko in 2024 (her mother owned only that apartment); her brother holds the other half.

1. **Seller unit.** Adel, Lina and their minor children are one seller (s. 49(ב)). They own the old Haifa apartment, the new one and half of the Akko apartment.
2. **The new apartment.** Bought in January 2026 as a replacement, within 24 months before the October sale: presumption s. 49ג(1) applies.
3. **The Akko half.** An inherited share not above a half: presumption s. 49ג(3) applies.
4. **Conditions.** Lived in for 11 years (qualifying), owned more than 18 months, no exempt sale in the last 18 months, price below the ceiling: the sale of the old Haifa apartment is exempt under s. 49ב(2). They request the exemption in the declaration within 30 days.
5. **Purchase tax on the new apartment** (lesson 25.6): when they bought it in January they still owned the old apartment, so they paid the single-apartment scale under the replacement rule only if they sell the old one within 24 months; the October sale satisfies it.
6. **Later, Akko.** If Lina and her brother sell the Akko apartment, each may claim the inheritance exemption (s. 49ב(5)) if the mother would have been exempt.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- An apartment you own or may sell or inherit (or the Haddad case).
- The land-registry extract and purchase documents.
- The [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md) page.
- 50 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Seller unit and property (10 min).** List every apartment and share in the unit.
2. **Exemption test (20 min).** Go through the checklist above; write the section for each answer.
3. **Timeline (10 min).** If you are moving home, write the date by which the old apartment must be sold for the presumption (24 months) and for the purchase-tax replacement rule (lesson 25.6).
4. **If taxable (10 min).** In outline: sale value, acquisition value, allowed expenses, and the rate period (before 2001 / 2001–2012 / after 2012). Note what an accountant must compute.

### Template

None specific; the [purchase-tax worksheet (draft)](../../templates/purchase-tax-worksheet.md) has a short appreciation-tax section.

### How you know it worked

- Every answer in the exemption test cites a section.
- Your timeline shows both 24-month deadlines (s. 49ג(1) and s. 9(ג1ג)(2)(ב)).
- You identified any reason the exemption could fail, and the evidence to keep.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Partly. A seller may file his own declaration and claim the exemption, and object and appeal himself. Selling land also involves the contract, releasing mortgages and registration, where a lawyer is usual; a seller who is not sure of the exemption should get advice before signing, because the contract date fixes the tax.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Where and how

- The regional Real Estate Taxation office; online declarations (⚠️ manual check).
- Deadlines: declaration 30 days; payment 60 days ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- Free help: the Tax Authority call centre and simulator (⚠️ manual check); [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer (or accountant) when

- The price is near or above the ceiling, you own more than one apartment, or the gain must be split by period.
- You are in a combination or urban-renewal deal.
- The exemption depends on a spouse living apart or on an inheritance with several heirs.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Not requesting the exemption in the declaration | Assessed as taxable | Object within 30 days and request it | Request it expressly with proof |
| Waiting more than 24 months to sell the old home | Old home is no longer the "single apartment" | Consider other exemptions; get advice | Plan the sale before buying |
| Forgetting the spouse's apartment | Exemption refused | Object if a presumption applies | List the whole seller unit |
| Selling only part of one's rights | Exemption refused (s. 49א(א)) | Sell all rights | Check the share sold |
| Ignoring the ceiling | Unexpected tax on the excess | Pay the proportional tax | Check the ceiling before pricing |

## Review

- Land appreciation tax is charged on the real gain; individuals pay up to 25%, with linear splits for older assets (ss. 6, 47, 48א).
- A single qualifying apartment held 18 months is exempt if no other exempt sale took place in 18 months (s. 49ב(2)), up to the ₪5,008,000 ceiling in 2026 (s. 49א(א1)).
- A replacement bought in the last 24 months, a protected-tenancy apartment and small shares do not break the exemption (s. 49ג).
- An heir may sell the deceased's only apartment exempt (s. 49ב(5)); the exemption must be requested in the declaration within 30 days.
