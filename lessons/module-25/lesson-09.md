# 25.9 · Tax and benefits: the work grant and the National Insurance interplay

By the end of this lesson you will be able to tell a low-income worker whether to apply for the work grant, the "negative income tax" paid by the Tax Authority, and to see a household's year as one picture: wages and tax, National Insurance contributions and benefits, and the places where they meet. This lesson closes the gap left in lesson 25.2: credit points help only those who pay tax; the work grant reaches those who earn too little to use them.

## Why it matters

Israel's tax system gives most of its personal relief through credit points, which are worth nothing to a worker whose tax is already zero. Since 2007 the work grant (מענק עבודה, also called מס הכנסה שלילי) has paid low-income working parents, older workers and some others an annual sum, through the Tax Authority, on application. Entitled people must apply, and not all do; the State Comptroller has reported on the programme's implementation at the Tax Authority. At the same time, National Insurance benefits (unemployment, maternity, reserve pay) are taxable and have tax withheld, and the self-employed deduct part of their contributions. A person who understands these links can claim a grant, a refund and the right deduction in the same year.

## The law

### The work grant

The Work Grant Law (חוק להגדלת שיעור ההשתתפות בכוח העבודה ולצמצום פערים חברתיים (מענק עבודה), התשס״ח–2007) entitles a worker with earned income within a band, who meets conditions of age, family status, residency and assets, to an annual grant paid by the Tax Authority. ⚠️ A free consolidated text of the law was not located for this lesson (it is not on Wikisource, and the Knesset and gov.il sites block automated access), so the rules below come from the Knesset's 2023 amendment document and secondary guides; check the current text before relying on any detail.

- **Who.** Employees and the self-employed with low earned income; the main group is working parents, and workers without children from an older age (55 in the secondary sources). The 2023 amendment lowered the minimum age for parents from 23 to 21 and changed "working mother" to "working parent" (Knesset document; ⚠️ secondary).
- **Amount.** It rises with earned income up to a level and then phases out; parents receive more, and parents of very young children an additional amount; the bands and amounts change every year (see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md), ⚠️ manual check).
- **When and how.** The grant for a tax year is claimed after the year ends, online through the Tax Authority's service (one-time registration and identification), and is paid in instalments. The gov.il service page, per a search summary, gives 31.12.2027 as the last date for claiming the 2025 grant (⚠️ manual check).
- **Disputes.** The decision states how to ask for a review; ⚠️ the law's objection and appeal sections were not checked.

### Benefits that are taxable

NII benefits that replace wages (unemployment, maternity, reserve-duty pay, work-injury allowance) are taxable income, and the NII withholds tax from them when it pays (⚠️ the withholding provision was not checked for this lesson). Because the NII does not know the worker's full year, the withholding is often too high or too low; the annual picture is settled by a return or a refund request ([lesson 25.4](lesson-04.md)).

### The child allowance and credit points

> יחיד תושב ישראל זכאי לנקודות קיצבה בשל כל אחד מילדיו [...]; תשלום נקודות הקיצבה ייעשה בידי המוסד לביטוח לאומי לפי חוק הביטוח הלאומי.

*Gist:* The child allowance is expressed in "allowance points" paid by the NII, separate from the parents' credit points against tax. [Income Tax Ordinance s. 40(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_40) The value of an allowance point is set in s. 33א (₪242 a month for 2024–2027 in the Wikisource note).

### Contributions of the self-employed

> יחיד שבשנת מס שילם דמי ביטוח [...] ומס מקביל [...] בשל הכנסה שאינה הכנסת עבודה, יותר לו ניכוי של 52% מהסכום ששילם [...]

*Gist:* A self-employed person (and an employee whose employer does not pay for him) deducts 52% of the National Insurance contributions and health tax he paid on non-employment income. [s. 47א(א)–(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_מס_הכנסה#סעיף_47א)

### Information sharing

The Tax Authority may receive from the NII information on payments and benefits that are income (s. 140א). In practice, the work grant system and refund computations use NII data, and a person who reported nothing to the Tax Authority may still find his benefits on record.

## Leading cases

### Key text: the Work Grant Law and the State Comptroller's review

There is no leading appellate ruling taught here. The key texts are the Work Grant Law itself (⚠️ consolidated text to be confirmed) and the State Comptroller's reports on the implementation of the work-grant programme at the Tax Authority ([State Comptroller library](https://library.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Pages/Reports/147-11.aspx)), which discuss how the programme is run. The policy debate is real: supporters see an in-work benefit as the best tool against working poverty; critics point out that it must be claimed, is complex for the people it targets, and depends on accurate employer reporting. Weigh both views when you discuss it. For the welfare benefits themselves, see [lesson 17.8](../module-17/lesson-08.md).

## How it works in practice

### Applying for the work grant

1. **Check eligibility** with the Tax Authority's online eligibility check for the year (⚠️ manual check).
2. **Register** once for the online service and identify yourself.
3. **Apply** for the previous tax year, after the employers' annual reports reach the Tax Authority (secondary guides suggest from May).
4. **Bank details** for payment; the grant is paid in instalments.
5. **Keep** the decision; if you disagree, follow the review route stated in it.

### A household's year in one table

| Item | Who pays / collects | Tax effect |
|---|---|---|
| Wages | Employer, withholding tax and NII | Taxed; credit points |
| Unemployment / maternity / reserve pay | NII, withholding tax | Taxable; often a refund item |
| Child allowance | NII | Not part of the work grant computation ⚠️ check |
| Work grant | Tax Authority | Paid on application |
| Self-employed NII contributions | Self-employed person | 52% deductible (s. 47א) |
| Income support | NII on the State's behalf ([lesson 17.8](../module-17/lesson-08.md)) | Means-tested; check how other income counts |

## Worked example

Sigal and Dmitri Petrov, from Kiryat Shmona, have three children (ages 1, 4 and 9). In 2025 Sigal worked part time as a kindergarten assistant (₪5,500 a month) and received maternity pay for three months; Dmitri worked as a self-employed handyman with a small income and paid NII contributions.

1. **Work grant.** Both are working parents with low earned income; both may be entitled for 2025. They run the online eligibility check and apply for 2025 in 2026, well before the deadline (⚠️ manual check: 31.12.2027 per the service page).
2. **Maternity pay.** The NII withheld tax on Sigal's maternity pay; with her low annual income she probably overpaid. She asks for a refund for 2025 (lesson 25.4).
3. **Dmitri's return.** As self-employed he must file an annual return; he deducts 52% of his NII contributions (s. 47א) and claims his credit points.
4. **Credit points.** Sigal's points for three children (s. 66(ג)(4)) exceed her tax; the unused part is lost, which is exactly what the grant compensates.
5. **Result.** The family claims the grant for both parents, a tax refund for Sigal and the right deduction for Dmitri, and checks the child allowance in the NII personal area.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your household's income for last year (or the Petrov case): Forms 106, NII certificates, business turnover.
- Access to the Tax Authority's and NII's personal areas (⚠️ manual check of the services).
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Household table (15 min).** Fill the table above for your household for last year.
2. **Work grant check (10 min).** For each working adult: age, children, earned income per month. Note whether the eligibility check is worth running.
3. **Refund signals (10 min).** Mark taxable NII benefits and other refund signs (lesson 25.4).
4. **Deductions (5 min).** For a self-employed adult: the 52% contributions deduction.
5. **Plan (5 min).** List every claim (grant, refund, return) with its deadline.

### Template

None; use the [tax refund request (draft)](../../templates/tax-refund-request.md) for the refund part.

### How you know it worked

- Every adult in the household has a line in the table and a decision on the grant.
- Taxable NII benefits are identified as possible refund items.
- Each claim has a deadline.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. The work grant is claimed by the worker himself online; a refund request and an annual return likewise (lessons 25.2, 25.4). NII claims and appeals were covered in [lesson 17.9](../module-17/lesson-09.md).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Where and how

- Tax Authority: the work-grant online service and eligibility check (⚠️ manual check on gov.il).
- NII personal area for benefit certificates and child allowance (⚠️ manual check on btl.gov.il).
- Free help: Kol-Zchut's work-grant guide (⚠️ manual check); NII's "יד מכוונת" advice centres; [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer (or accountant) when

- The grant is refused because of residency, assets or a dispute about whether you worked.
- You received a demand to repay a grant you were paid.
- NII and Tax Authority records conflict and the amounts are large.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Never applying for the grant | Money lost after the deadline | Apply for every open year | Check eligibility every spring |
| Assuming maternity or unemployment pay is tax-free | Missed refund or a surprise bill | Refund request or return | Treat wage-replacing benefits as taxable |
| Self-employed not deducting NII contributions | Too much income tax | Amend the return | Use s. 47א every year |
| Wrong bank details | Payment delayed | Update in the service | Check details when applying |

## Review

- The work grant pays low-income working parents and older workers an annual sum through the Tax Authority, on application after the year (⚠️ check the current law and figures).
- Wage-replacing NII benefits are taxable and have tax withheld, often a refund item.
- The self-employed deduct 52% of their NII contributions and health tax (s. 47א).
- The Tax Authority receives NII data on payments (s. 140א); the child allowance is paid by the NII in allowance points (s. 40(א)).
