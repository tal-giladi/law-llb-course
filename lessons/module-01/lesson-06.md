# 01.6 · The legal system's people

By the end of this lesson you will be able to name every person you are likely to meet in an Israeli legal process, say what each of them may and may not do for you, and decide whether you can get a free lawyer from the Public Defender or Legal Aid before you start acting on your own.

## Why it matters

> [!WARNING]
> Parts of this lesson concern police questioning and criminal proceedings. If you are a suspect or have been summoned for questioning, ask to consult a lawyer before you answer; do not use this lesson as a substitute for a defender.

Self-represented people waste time asking the wrong person. The court secretary cannot tell you whether your claim is good; the judge will not explain how to fill in a form; the prosecutor does not represent the victim; the Judicial Ombudsman cannot change a judgment. Each role has a legal basis that sets its limits. Knowing them tells you who to ask, what to ask for and when a free lawyer may be available.

## The law

### Judges: independence and appointment, Basic Law: The Judiciary ss. 2 and 4

> בענייני שפיטה אין מרות על מי שבידו סמכות שפיטה, זולת מרותו של הדין.

*Gist:* In judicial matters, a person with judicial power is subject to no authority but the law. [s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק-יסוד:_השפיטה#סעיף_2)

> (א) שופט יתמנה בידי נשיא המדינה לפי בחירה של ועדה לבחירת שופטים.

*Gist:* Judges are appointed by the President on the selection of the Judicial Selection Committee. [s. 4](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק-יסוד:_השפיטה#סעיף_4)

The committee has nine members: three Supreme Court justices, two ministers, two Knesset members and two Bar representatives. A 2025 amendment to s. 4 changes the composition (replacing the Bar representatives with two public representatives chosen by the Knesset, one by the coalition and one by the opposition) and the voting rules, **from the start of the 26th Knesset's term**. Supporters argue that elected representatives should have a greater say in choosing judges; opponents argue that the change weakens judicial independence. Check the current text and any court ruling on the amendment before relying on either version.

### Registrars: s. 21

> לבית משפט יכול שיהיה רשם, בין שופט ובין שאינו שופט.

*Gist:* A court may have a registrar, who may or may not be a judge. [s. 21](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק-יסוד:_השפיטה#סעיף_21)

Registrars decide many procedural requests (extensions, service, fee exemptions) and, in some courts, hear certain claims. A registrar's decision can usually be appealed to a judge of the same court; check the deadline in each case.

### Prosecution: Criminal Procedure Law ss. 11–12 and 68

> המאשים במשפט פלילי הוא המדינה והיא תיוצג בידי תובע שינהל את התביעה.

*Gist:* In a criminal trial the State is the accuser, represented by a prosecutor. [s. 11](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_סדר_הדין_הפלילי#סעיף_11)

Section 12 lists the prosecutors: the Attorney General and his or her representatives (the State Attorney and district attorneys' offices), and police officers appointed as prosecutors. In practice the police prosecution handles many offences in the magistrates' courts, and the State Attorney's Office handles the more serious ones. Section 68 is the exception: "רשאי כל אדם להאשים בעבירה מן המנויות בתוספת השניה על ידי הגשת קובלנה" (any person may prosecute certain listed offences by a private complaint, module 5). [s. 68](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_סדר_הדין_הפלילי#סעיף_68)

The prosecutor represents the public interest, not the victim. Victims have their own rights to information and to be heard (module 5).

### The Public Defender: Criminal Procedure Law s. 15, Public Defender Law s. 18

> (א) נאשם שאין לו סניגור [...] ימנה לו בית המשפט סניגור אם נתקיים בו אחד מאלה: (1) הוא הואשם ברצח או בעבירה שדינה מיתה או מאסר עולם או שהואשם בבית משפט מחוזי בעבירה שדינה מאסר עשר שנים או יותר [...] (2) לא מלאו לו שש עשרה שנים [...] (3) הוא אילם, עיוור או חרש או שיש חשש שהוא חולה נפש [...]

*Gist:* The court must appoint a defender for an accused who has none in listed cases: serious charges, minors under 16 outside the youth court, people with certain disabilities, and others listed in the section. [s. 15](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_סדר_הדין_הפלילי#סעיף_15)

Section 18 of the Public Defender Law makes these people, and accused persons who lack means under the minister's criteria, eligible for representation by the Public Defender. [s. 18](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הסניגוריה_הציבורית#סעיף_18)

### Legal Aid: Legal Aid Law ss. 2–3

> לשכת סיוע משפטי תתן שירות משפטי [...] בענינים ובהיקף שנקבעו בתקנות, למי שידו אינה משגת לשאת בהם, או למי שנקבע בתוספת בענינים ובהיקף שנקבעו בה;

*Gist:* Legal Aid gives free legal services in the matters set by regulations to people who cannot afford them, and to groups listed in the schedule in the matters listed there. [s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הסיוע_המשפטי#סעיף_2)

An applicant applies in writing (s. 3) and must pass a subject test (is this matter covered?), a means test (unless the schedule exempts it) and a merit test. Some matters, for example National Insurance claims, are listed without a means test (check the current schedule).

### The Judicial Ombudsman: what it cannot do

> הנציב לא יברר תלונה שהיא כמפורט להלן: [...] (3) ענינה של התלונה בשאלה משפטית או ראייתית מהותית; (4) התלונה היא בענין הניתן לערעור על פי דין, למעט בענין הנוגע להתנהגותו של שופט במסגרת מילוי תפקידו [...]

*Gist:* The Ombudsman does not examine complaints about substantive legal or evidentiary questions, or matters that can be appealed, except a judge's conduct, including the way a trial is managed. [Ombudsman Law s. 17](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_נציב_תלונות_הציבור_על_שופטים#סעיף_17)

If you think the judgment is wrong, appeal. If the judge was rude, delayed a decision unreasonably or would not let you speak, the Ombudsman is the channel.

## Leading cases

### ע״פ 5121/98 יששכרוב נ׳ התובע הצבאי הראשי, פ״ד סא(1) 461 (2006)

A soldier confessed to drug offences after military police deliberately failed to tell him of his right to consult a lawyer. A nine-justice panel adopted a case-law rule allowing courts to exclude illegally obtained evidence where admitting it would substantially harm the right to a fair trial, and excluded his confessions. The rule is discretionary, not automatic; Justice Grunis dissented on its application. Since 2022 the rule is codified in Evidence Ordinance s. 56א (lesson [23.5](../module-23/lesson-05.md)).

Why it matters: it shows the lawyer's role is not a formality. The right to consult a lawyer before questioning is protected strongly enough that ignoring it can cost the prosecution its evidence. If you are ever summoned for questioning (module 5), ask to speak with a lawyer first; the Public Defender runs a service for detainees. [English translation](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/411)

### רע״א 8120/08 (recap from lesson 01.1)

Only a lawyer may represent another person in a civil court absent an express provision. It defines the boundary between the people in this lesson who may speak for you (lawyers, defenders, Legal Aid lawyers, rabbinical pleaders in their courts) and those who may only help (relatives, volunteers, information centres). [Text on the Judiciary's site](https://supremedecisions.court.gov.il/Home/Download?path=HebrewVerdicts%2F08%2F200%2F081%2Fv04&fileName=08081200_v04.txt&type=2)

## How it works in practice

| Person | What they can do for you | What they cannot do |
|---|---|---|
| Judge | Decide your case; manage the hearing; propose a settlement | Advise you, or help one side prepare |
| Registrar | Decide procedural requests (extensions, fee exemptions, service) and some claims | Change a judge's decision |
| Court registry / information centre | Explain procedure, accept filings, give dates | Tell you whether your claim is good |
| Your lawyer | Represent you, draft, advise; bound by Bar ethics rules | Act against your instructions or with a conflict of interest |
| Prosecutor (police or State Attorney) | Decide whether to charge; present the case | Represent the victim personally |
| Public Defender | Represent eligible suspects and accused | Represent victims or civil parties |
| Legal Aid | Represent eligible people in civil matters within its list | Take matters outside its subject list, or people who fail its tests |
| Mediator | Help the parties reach a settlement | Decide the case |
| Welfare assistance unit (family and religious courts) | Intake, information, mediation in family matters | Represent you |
| Judicial Ombudsman | Examine complaints about a judge's conduct | Overturn or review a decision on the merits |

**Getting a free lawyer.**
- **Criminal:** at the first hearing, tell the judge you have no lawyer and ask that one be appointed. If you are detained, ask the police to call the Public Defender's duty lawyer.
- **Civil:** apply to Legal Aid online or at a district office: [Legal Aid application](https://www.gov.il/he/service/legal_aid_application). Bring income documents and the papers of your case. Answers take time; if a deadline is close, file in your own name to protect it and tell Legal Aid.
- **Other free help:** university clinics, bar association pro bono programmes and NGOs in specific fields. See [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

## Worked example

Olga Petrenko, 68, from Nahariya, receives two letters in one week: a summons to the magistrates' court as a defendant in a ₪60,000 debt claim from a renovation contractor, and a notice that her National Insurance old-age supplement has been cut.

1. **Debt claim.** It is a civil claim in the magistrates' court. She may defend herself (reg. 170). She applies to Legal Aid: the subject (a civil claim against her) may be covered, and her income, a pension and the supplement, is likely below the means threshold. While she waits, she files her defence in her own name before the deadline, so she is not in default.
2. **National Insurance.** She has a right to appeal the decision (module 17 in year 2 covers the route). Legal Aid represents National Insurance claims without a means test (check the current schedule), so she includes that matter in the application.
3. **At the hearing.** If she is still unrepresented, she speaks for herself. The judge may propose a settlement or refer the parties to mediation. Her son may sit beside her and help with documents, but not argue (lesson 01.1).
4. **Afterwards.** If the judge was dismissive and refused to let her speak, she may complain to the Judicial Ombudsman. If she thinks the judgment is wrong, the route is an appeal, not a complaint.

### You need

- Your inventory from lesson 01.1.

### Steps

1. For each matter in your inventory, write which people from the table above you would meet (court, prosecution, mediator, registry).
2. For each civil matter, check Legal Aid: is the subject on its list? Would you likely pass the means test? Write "likely", "unlikely" or "check".
3. For any criminal exposure (as suspect or accused), write whether s. 15 or s. 18 could apply.
4. Write the exact sentence you would say at a first criminal hearing to ask for a defender, or the first line of a Legal Aid application, in Hebrew.
5. Draft two one-line notes: what you would complain to the Judicial Ombudsman about, and what you would appeal instead. Use an invented example if you have none.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes: you may apply to Legal Aid, ask the court to appoint a defender, and complain to the Judicial Ombudsman yourself. These are the routes that can bring you a lawyer for free, so use them before deciding to act alone in anything serious.

### Where and how

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

- Legal Aid: [online application](https://www.gov.il/he/service/legal_aid_application) or a district office. Bring ID, income documents and your case papers.
- Public Defender: ask the judge at the first hearing, or the police on arrest. See [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).
- Judicial Ombudsman: a written complaint, describing the conduct, the case number and the date. It does not stop any deadline for appeal.
- Deadlines keep running while you wait for Legal Aid. Protect them by filing in your own name.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You are a suspect or accused: ask for a defender immediately; do not wait to see how questioning goes.
- Legal Aid refuses you and the matter is serious (your home, custody, a large debt): consult a private lawyer, at least for an hour, to plan.
- You want to challenge a judge's impartiality in your case: that is a disqualification request (בקשת פסלות) with its own procedure, not an Ombudsman complaint.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Asking the court registry whether your claim will win | No answer, frustration | Ask procedural questions only; use Legal Aid or a clinic for advice | Know each role's limits |
| Waiting for Legal Aid while a deadline passes | Default judgment or lost appeal | Apply to set aside or extend, with reasons | File in your own name to protect the deadline |
| Complaining to the Ombudsman instead of appealing | The appeal deadline passes; the Ombudsman cannot change the judgment | Ask for an extension to appeal, with reasons | Appeal the merits; complain only about conduct |
| Answering police questions before asking for a lawyer | Statements used against you | Tell your defender everything at once | Ask to consult a lawyer before questioning |
| Expecting the prosecutor to act for you as a victim | Surprise at plea deals or closures | Use your victims' rights (module 5) | Understand the prosecutor represents the public interest |

## Review

- Judges are independent (s. 2) and appointed through the Judicial Selection Committee (s. 4); a 2025 amendment changes the committee from the 26th Knesset.
- Registrars decide procedural matters; their decisions can usually be challenged before a judge.
- The State prosecutes through the AG's representatives and police prosecutors; private complaints are possible for listed offences.
- The Public Defender represents eligible accused and suspects (s. 15 and s. 18); Legal Aid represents eligible people in listed civil matters.
- The Judicial Ombudsman handles conduct, not the merits; Yissacharov shows how seriously courts treat the right to a lawyer.
