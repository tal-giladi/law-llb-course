# 01.7 · Practice lab: map your dispute to the right forum and decide if you can act alone

In this lab you take one real dispute from your own life (or the supplied case below), and work it through every question of this module: which law governs it, which forum has jurisdiction, in which city, what must happen before any filing, whether you may act alone there, what free help exists, which deadlines run, and when to stop and call a lawyer. The result is a one-page forum decision memo you will reuse in later modules.

## Why it matters

Most self-represented cases that fail do so before the first hearing: the claim is filed in the wrong court, after the deadline, without the required prior step, or by someone who may not act there. Every one of those failures is avoidable with an hour of structured checking. This lab is that hour, done properly once, so that it becomes a habit.

## The law

This lab uses the sections from lessons 01.1–01.6. Two more apply to the supplied case.

> התקופה שבה מתיישנת תביעה שלא הוגשה עליה תובענה [...] היא – (1) בשאינו מקרקעין – שבע שנים; [...]

*Gist:* A civil claim that does not concern land is time-barred if not filed within the general limitation period; land claims have longer periods. [Prescription Law s. 5](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההתיישנות#סעיף_5) Special laws set shorter periods for many claims; the periods the course uses are in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

> מי שחוייב בתשלום ארנונה כללית רשאי תוך תשעים ימים מיום קבלת הודעת התשלום להשיג עליה לפני מנהל הארנונה על יסוד טענה מטענות אלה: [...]

*Gist:* A person charged municipal property tax (arnona) may object to the arnona director within a fixed period of receiving the payment notice, on listed grounds (wrong zone, wrong type, size or use of the property, or not being the occupier); appeals go to the appeals committee and then to the administrative affairs court. [Arnona Appeals Law ss. 3–6](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הרשויות_המקומיות_%28ערר_על_קביעת_ארנונה_כללית%29#סעיף_3) The periods are in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

The arnona route shows a pattern you will meet again: for many disputes with authorities, a statute creates its own objection and appeal path, and you must use it instead of going to a general court.

## Leading cases

The lab applies the cases of this module rather than new ones:

- **Amir (בג״ץ 8638/03):** consent cannot give a forum jurisdiction. Check jurisdiction claim by claim. [English translation](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/49)
- **רע״א 8120/08:** in civil courts you act yourself or through a lawyer, with no lay friend. [Text on the Judiciary's site](https://supremedecisions.court.gov.il/Home/Download?path=HebrewVerdicts%2F08%2F200%2F081%2Fv04&fileName=08081200_v04.txt&type=2)
- **Basic Law: The Judiciary s. 20:** use binding precedent from the Supreme Court first (lesson 01.5).

## How it works in practice

Work in this order. Each step has a one-line output that goes into your memo.

1. **Facts:** who, what, when, where, how much (lesson 01.4, part A of the worksheet).
2. **Governing law:** the main statute and its layer (lessons 01.2–01.3).
3. **Exclusive forum?** Labour, religious, family, administrative, special tribunal (lesson 01.4).
4. **General forum and venue:** small claims, magistrates', district; reg. 7.
5. **Required first step:** demand letter, objection to the authority, internal appeal, mediation intake.
6. **Who may act:** you, a permitted representative, a lawyer only (lesson 01.1).
7. **Free help:** Legal Aid, Public Defender, clinics, information centres (lesson 01.6).
8. **Deadlines:** limitation, statutory objection periods, appeal periods (via current numbers).
9. **Stop signals:** the red flags from this module.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. This lab is a dry run: nothing is sent or filed.

### You need

- One real dispute of yours from your inventory (lesson 01.1), or the supplied case below.
- The [forum check worksheet](../../templates/forum-check-worksheet.md).
- The statutes linked in this module, the [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md) page and [Can I act without a lawyer?](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md).
- About 2.5 hours: 30 minutes for facts, 60 for analysis, 30 for the memo, 30 for checking.

### The supplied case

Noor Khatib, 34, lives in Umm al-Fahm and works as a pharmacist's assistant for a pharmacy chain in Hadera. In the past year:

- **(a)** She bought a washing machine for ₪3,800 from an online shop based in Rishon LeZion. It broke after three weeks; the shop ignores her emails.
- **(b)** Her employer has not paid her for 22 overtime hours over four months. Her manager says "that is how it works here".
- **(c)** The Umm al-Fahm municipality sent her a new arnona notice six weeks ago, charging her for 120 m² although her apartment is 95 m².
- **(d)** Her uncle in Nazareth suggests his friend, a retired bank clerk who "handles claims for people for a small fee", should take over all three matters.

### Steps

1. **Facts.** Fill in part A of the worksheet for your dispute (or for each of (a)–(c)). Keep it factual: dates, amounts, places, documents you have.
2. **Governing law.** Name the main statute for each matter and its layer. Copy one key sentence from it.
3. **Exclusive forum.** For each matter, test the exclusive forums in order: labour court (s. 24), religious court, Family Court, administrative court, a special statutory route (like arnona). Write "none" or the forum with its section.
4. **General forum.** For civil money claims, compare the amount with the small-claims and magistrates' ceilings in current numbers, and note whether you are an individual (s. 60).
5. **Venue.** Apply reg. 7: defendant's residence or business, place of the act or omission, internet trade, land.
6. **First step.** Write the required or sensible step before filing: a demand letter (civil), a letter to the employer (labour), a statutory objection (arnona). Note who it goes to.
7. **Who may act.** For each forum, write: "I may act myself because…", "a representative may act because…", "only a lawyer because…". For (d), decide what the retired clerk may and may not do.
8. **Free help.** Note Legal Aid eligibility, unions or workers' rights organisations, consumer bodies, court information centres.
9. **Deadlines.** Write every running deadline with its source and the date you checked it. Mark the most urgent.
10. **Stop signals.** List any red flags that would send you to a lawyer.
11. **Memo.** Write the one-page forum decision memo (format below).
12. **Check.** Hand the memo to someone else, or reread it the next day, and check that every claim in it can be verified from a linked statute.

### Memo format

```text
מזכר החלטה על ערכאה – [שם העניין]          תאריך: ______

1. העובדות (3–5 שורות)
2. הדין החל (חוק, סעיף, ציטוט קצר)
3. הערכאה המוסמכת והבסיס לסמכות
4. מקום השיפוט (תקנה 7)
5. צעד ראשון לפני הגשה, למי ועד מתי
6. האם אני רשאי/ת לפעול בעצמי? מי עוד רשאי לייצג?
7. עזרה חינם זמינה
8. מועדים (מקור ותאריך בדיקה)
9. סימני עצירה – מתי לפנות לעורך דין
```

### How you know it worked

- Every line of the memo can be checked against a named section, regulation or ruling.
- No matter is sent to two forums for the same claim, and no exclusive forum was missed.
- The most urgent deadline is identified with a date.
- Your answer on who may act matches the reasoning of lesson 01.1.

<details><summary>Model answer for the supplied case</summary>

**(a) Washing machine.**
Law: consumer and sale law (Israeli statutes; module 3 and year 3 go deeper). Exclusive forum: none. General forum: Noor is an individual and ₪3,800 is far below the small-claims ceiling (s. 60), so the small claims court is available; the magistrates' court is also possible (s. 61) but more formal. Venue: the purchase was online trade, so reg. 7(ב) lets her file where she lives (Umm al-Fahm's area court) or where the shop does business (Rishon LeZion); she would choose the court for her area. First step: a written demand to the shop with a deadline to repair, replace or refund. Who may act: Noor herself; with the court's leave a non-professional, unpaid person under s. 63(ב). Deadlines: well within the general limitation period (see current numbers); act soon anyway because evidence fades. Stop signals: none at this value.

**(b) Overtime.**
Law: employment law (hours of work and wage protection statutes; year 2). Exclusive forum: the regional labour court, s. 24(א)(1); the small claims court has no jurisdiction even though the amount is small. Venue: the labour court's regional rules (the Hadera workplace area). First step: a written request to the employer with a calculation; check whether a union or workers' rights organisation can help. Who may act: Noor herself; an authorised union representative under the Labour Court Law. Free help: workers' rights organisations, Legal Aid if eligible. Stop signals: if she is dismissed or threatened after raising the claim, get advice quickly.

**(c) Arnona.**
Law: the Arnona Appeals Law. Exclusive route: an objection (השגה) to the arnona director within the statutory period from the notice (s. 3(א)(2): wrong size), then appeal to the appeals committee, then to the administrative affairs court. A small claim or an HCJ petition is the wrong route. Deadline: six weeks have passed since the notice; the objection period (see current numbers) is running and is the most urgent item. Who may act: Noor herself. First step: a written objection with the apartment's measurements (a plan or a measurement report). Stop signals: none unless the municipality claims she changed the property's use.

**(d) The uncle's friend.**
He offers to handle claims "for a small fee" and does it for other people: that is "דרך עיסוק" and "בתמורה", exactly what s. 20 of the Bar Association Law reserves to lawyers. He may not represent Noor in the labour court (only a lawyer or an authorised union representative), not before the arnona director as a paid representative, and the small claims court could not approve him under s. 63(ב) because he represents regularly and for pay. Noor should decline and act herself.

**Most urgent:** (c), the arnona objection deadline.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Mapping your own dispute is preparation in your own name. Acting on the map (sending the letter, filing the objection or claim) is also yours to do in most forums; see [Can I act without a lawyer?](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md) for each forum.

### Where and how

- Small claims and magistrates' courts: module 8 (procedure) and module 12 (clinic) take you through filing.
- Labour courts: year 2 (module 17 and clinic II).
- Arnona and other authority routes: year 3 (local government) and the authority's own website for the objection form.
- Free help: [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The dispute is in a family forum where a "race" between the Family Court and a religious court is possible.
- Land ownership, a large sum near a jurisdiction ceiling, or criminal exposure is involved.
- A deadline will pass before you can prepare properly.
- The other side has a lawyer and the case depends on complex law or expert evidence.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Filing an overtime claim in small claims because it is small | Struck out for lack of jurisdiction | Ask for transfer under Courts Law s. 79, or refile in the labour court | Test exclusive forums first |
| Suing the municipality over arnona in small claims | Wrong route; statutory objection deadline missed | Object late with a request to extend, giving reasons; get advice | Look for a statutory objection route in any dispute with an authority |
| Letting a paid non-lawyer "handle" claims | Invalid steps, lost deadlines, s. 96 exposure for them | Take the file back in your own name | Apply s. 20 to every offer of help |
| Leaving deadlines to the end of the memo | The urgent item is noticed too late | Act on the most urgent deadline first | Mark deadlines as soon as you identify the forum |

## Review

- A dispute is mapped in nine steps: facts, law, exclusive forum, general forum, venue, first step, who may act, free help, deadlines and stop signals.
- Exclusive forums and statutory objection routes come before the general courts.
- The small claims court is available only to individuals and only within its ceiling and subject matter.
- Paid, regular help from a non-lawyer is barred by s. 20; s. 63(ב) does not cure it.
- The forum decision memo is your reusable output; keep it with your case file.
