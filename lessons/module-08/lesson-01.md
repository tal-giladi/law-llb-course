# 08.1 · Why procedure matters: the Civil Procedure Regulations 2018 and their principles

By the end of this lesson you will know why procedure decides more cases than substantive law does, what the five opening principles of the Civil Procedure Regulations 2018 say and how judges use them, which procedural code governs each kind of case, and what duties you take on the moment you act for yourself in a civil court.

## Why it matters

A strong claim can be lost on procedure: filed in the wrong court, served on the wrong address, answered too late, or buried in a statement of claim three times longer than the rules allow. A weak defence can win on procedure: a plaintiff who did not appear, did not list a witness, or did not ask for the right remedy. Procedure is the set of rules that turns a dispute into a decision. For a self-represented party it is the part of law you meet first and most often.

Israel replaced its 1984 civil procedure code with the Civil Procedure Regulations 2018 (תקנות סדר הדין האזרחי, התשע״ט–2018), in force from 1 January 2021. The reform did three things a learner must understand:

1. **It put principles first.** Part A (regs 1–5א) states the purposes of procedure and the duties of court and parties. Every other rule is read in their light.
2. **It made the judge a case manager.** The court is "responsible" for running the proceeding (reg. 3(א)), may act on its own initiative, and must balance the parties' interests against the public interest in an efficient court system (reg. 5).
3. **It front-loaded the work.** Short pleadings with page limits, early disclosure, a compulsory preliminary discussion between represented parties, and a free mediation-intake meeting for most claims above a threshold all happen before trial (lesson 08.2).

The academic debate is real. Supporters say the reform cuts delay and cost and brings Israeli practice closer to the English Civil Procedure Rules and their "overriding objective". Critics say broad principles give judges too much discretion, that page limits and costs sanctions fall hardest on parties without lawyers, and that "efficiency" can crowd out the search for truth. Reg. 1 tries to hold both: it names "certainty", "preventing arbitrariness" and a fair process alongside reaching the truth and a just result.

## The law

### The purposes: reg. 1

> מטרות תקנות סדר הדין האזרחי הן לקבוע סדר דין לניהול ההליכים האזרחיים בבית המשפט, ליצור ודאות דיונית, למנוע שרירותיות ולהגשים את העקרונות החוקתיים העומדים ביסודו של הליך שיפוטי ראוי והוגן, כדי להגיע לחקר האמת, ולהשיג תוצאה נכונה ופתרון צודק של הסכסוך.

*Gist:* Procedure exists to give certainty, prevent arbitrariness and realise the constitutional principles of a fair process, so as to reach the truth and a correct, just resolution. ([reg. 1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

### A proper and fair process: reg. 2

> הליך שיפוטי ראוי והוגן מתקיים במערכת שיפוטית עצמאית ובלתי תלויה שהוקמה לפי דין, נגישה לציבור, מקיימת דיון לפי כללי הצדק הטבעי, מכריעה בתוך זמן סביר…

*Gist:* A fair process needs an independent court, access for the public, natural justice, a decision within a reasonable time, equal and proportionate case management, and reasons. ([reg. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

### The court manages; the parties cooperate: reg. 3

> (ב) חובת בעלי הדין ובאי כוחם היא לסייע לבית המשפט בקיום המוטל עליו לפי תקנות אלה, וכן לנהוג בתום לב ובהגינות דיונית…

*Gist:* The court is responsible for running the case and may act on its own initiative (reg. 3(א)); parties and lawyers must help it, act in good faith and with procedural fairness, and work to identify and narrow the real issues (reg. 3(ב)). ([reg. 3](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

### Abuse of process: reg. 4

> לא יעשה בעל דין או בא כוחו שימוש לרעה בהליכי משפט ובכלל זאת פעולה בהליך שמטרתה או תוצאתה לשבשו, להשהותו או להטריד בעל דין…

*Gist:* A step whose purpose or effect is to disrupt, delay or harass, including a step disproportionate to the nature, value or complexity of the case, is an abuse. ([reg. 4](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי)) Abuse can lead to costs in favour of the other side or the State Treasury (reg. 151(ג)) and, in serious cases, to striking out (reg. 42).

### Balancing interests: regs 5 and 5א

Reg. 5 tells the court to balance the parties' interest against the "public interest", defined as access to the courts, a fair, fast and efficient hearing, saving time and cost, avoiding conflicting decisions and preventing abuse. Reg. 5א (2021) adds that the principles guide the court whenever it exercises discretion under the Regulations or under any other enactment that refers to them. *Gist:* the principles are not decoration; they are the lens for every discretionary decision. ([regs 5–5א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

### Scope and start date: reg. 180

Part A applies to every civil matter before a court; Part B (the detailed rules) applies unless a law sets special procedure (reg. 180(א)–(ב)). The Regulations apply to proceedings opened from 1 January 2021 (reg. 180(ג)). *Gist:* small claims, family, labour and administrative courts have their own rules, but the basic principles still frame them. ([reg. 180](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

### Acting for yourself: reg. 170 and the Bar Association Law

> כל פעולה בבית משפט הנדרשת מבעל דין או המותרת לו לפי דין, רשאי הוא לעשותה בעצמו או בידי עורך דין שמינה לכך כדין…

*Gist:* Any step a party must or may take in court, it may take itself or through a lawyer it appointed, unless a law says otherwise. ([reg. 170](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי)) Representing others in court is reserved to lawyers ([Bar Association Law s. 20](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לשכת_עורכי_הדין)); lesson [01.1](../module-01/lesson-01.md) explains the limit.

## Leading cases

### בג״ץ 5870/14 חשבים ה.פ.ס. מידע עסקי בע״מ נ׳ הנהלת בתי המשפט (12.11.2015)

The Courts Administration made access to its database of judgments conditional on an undertaking not to let search engines index the decisions. The High Court of Justice granted the petition: the condition infringed freedom of occupation, freedom of expression and the principle of open justice, and such an infringement needs authority in primary legislation, which the Administration did not have. Why it matters: "accessible to the public" in reg. 2 is not a slogan; open justice is a constitutional principle that limits how even the courts' own administration may run its systems. [English translation](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/185)

### רע״א 8120/08 (no "lay friend")

Recap from lesson [01.6](../module-01/lesson-06.md): absent an express provision, only a licensed lawyer may represent another party; there is no "close friend" (ידיד קרוב) exception. Why it matters: reg. 170 lets you act for yourself, but you cannot send a relative or a knowledgeable friend to argue for you in a civil court. (Decision text on the Judiciary's site, ⚠️ manual check; the link is in [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md).)

### Key text: the courts' own information channels

The judiciary publishes decisions in public cases on Net HaMishpat 48 hours after signing (home-page notice; see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)), and its information centre answers procedural questions by phone. Lesson [02.2](../module-02/lesson-02.md) shows how to search them. For procedure, the point is practical: when a rule is unclear, ask the registry, write down the answer, the date and the clerk's name, and act on the written rule if they conflict.

## How it works in practice

### Which code governs your case?

| Proceeding | Main procedural code | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Civil claim in a magistrates' or district court | Civil Procedure Regulations 2018 | 08.2 |
| Small claim | Courts Law ss. 59–64 and the Small Claims (Procedure) Regulations 1976 | 08.3, 04.9 |
| Criminal case | Criminal Procedure Law [Consolidated] 1982 and the Arrests Law 1996 | 08.4 |
| Petition against an authority in the Administrative Affairs Court | Administrative Affairs Courts Law 2000 and its procedure regulations | 08.5 |
| HCJ petition | HCJ Procedure Regulations | 06.8 |
| Arbitration; court-referred mediation | Arbitration Law 1968; Courts Law ss. 79א–79ג | 08.6 |
| Enforcing a judgment | Execution Law 1967 and the Execution Regulations 1979 | 08.7 |
| Labour, family, religious courts | their own rules | year 2; module 10 |

Even outside the 2018 Regulations, courts borrow their principles: the small claims court, for instance, is "not bound" by ordinary procedure and acts in the way it finds most useful for a just and fast decision ([Courts Law s. 62(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט#סעיף_62)).

### What the principles change for you

- **Good faith is a procedural duty**, not only a contract doctrine (compare lesson [03.4](../module-03/lesson-04.md)). Hiding a document you know the other side needs, or filing a motion only to delay, breaches reg. 3(ב) and reg. 4.
- **Proportionality cuts both ways.** A twenty-page motion over a ₪3,000 dispute is "disproportionate to the nature of the case" (reg. 4). So is a demand for every email the other side ever sent.
- **The judge will manage you.** Expect orders on your own initiative: narrow the issues, file a short summary, attend in person, try mediation. Comply, or ask in writing for a change with reasons.
- **Costs follow conduct.** Costs compensate the winner "in light of the result, the resources needed and the parties' conduct" (reg. 151(א)). A self-represented winner usually recovers expenses, not a lawyer's fee; a self-represented loser can be ordered to pay the other side's legal costs.

### Net HaMishpat at a glance

Net HaMishpat (נט המשפט) is the courts' case system. On 17 October 2026 its [home page](https://www.court.gov.il/NGCS.Web.Site/HomePage.aspx) offered: case search by number, court diaries, decisions search, payment of court vouchers, a "פתיחת תיק או ביצוע פעולות בתיק" (open a case or act in a case) entry after identification by smart card or the national digital ID (הזדהות לאומית), a separate "הגשת תביעה קטנה" (file a small claim) entry, and a page listing case types that cannot be filed remotely. Which case types an unrepresented party may open online changes over time (⚠️ manual check on the day; a 2022 State Comptroller report found that unrepresented parties could file in existing cases but not open new ones). Lesson 08.8 walks through it.

## Worked example

Yusuf Haddad, a pharmacist from Shefa-'Amr, is sued in the Haifa magistrates' court by a supplier for ₪28,000 for medicines he says he returned. He decides to defend himself.

1. **Which code?** A civil claim in a magistrates' court: the 2018 Regulations (reg. 180). Part A frames everything.
2. **May he act alone?** Yes (reg. 170). His brother, an accountant, may help him prepare, but may not sign pleadings for him or argue in court (Bar Association Law s. 20; רע״א 8120/08).
3. **Conduct duties.** He has the supplier's own delivery note confirming the return. Under reg. 3(ב) he should not hold it back for a "surprise" at trial; the Regulations expect early disclosure (lesson 08.2), and surprise documents may be excluded.
4. **Proportionality.** He is tempted to file a counterclaim for "damage to reputation" to pressure the supplier. Without a real cause of action, that risks costs for abuse of process (regs 4, 151(ג)).
5. **Result.** He files a short, honest defence with the delivery note, asks in writing for the free mediation-intake meeting if it applies (lesson 08.2), and keeps a log of every deadline.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- The text of regs 1–5א and 170 of the 2018 Regulations ([Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי)).
- A dispute of your own (or the supplied case below), and your forum analysis from lesson [01.7](../module-01/lesson-07.md) if you did it.
- 45 minutes.

Supplied case: Noa Ben-David, a student in Be'er Sheva, paid a moving company ₪4,800; it broke her sofa and refuses to pay. The company's lawyer has written threatening a counterclaim for "defamation" because Noa posted a negative review.

### Steps

1. **Identify the code (5 min).** Write which procedural code governs the forum you chose and cite the provision (table above).
2. **Read Part A (10 min).** Copy one phrase from each of regs 1–5 that matters to your case and write, in one sentence each, what it would let the judge do.
3. **Map your duties (10 min).** List three things reg. 3(ב) and reg. 4 require of you in this case (e.g. disclose the photos, do not file a motion only to delay, keep pleadings short).
4. **Check representation (5 min).** May you act alone? Who may not act for you? Cite reg. 170 and s. 20.
5. **Spot abuse (10 min).** In the supplied case, is the threatened counterclaim an abuse of process? Write the strongest argument each way.
6. **Find the system (5 min).** Open the [Net HaMishpat home page](https://www.court.gov.il/NGCS.Web.Site/HomePage.aspx) and note which entries you would use; do not log in yet.

### Template

None. Use the [forum check worksheet](../../templates/forum-check-worksheet.md) from module 1 if you have not mapped your forum.

### How you know it worked

- You can name the code that governs your forum and the provision that says so.
- For each of regs 1–5 you have one concrete consequence for your case.
- Your list of duties includes at least one thing you must give the other side, not only things you must avoid.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Every individual may take any step in a civil court personally (reg. 170). A company generally acts through a lawyer in the civil courts (case-law rule, Unverified in a free source; see [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md)). Nobody may represent you for pay or as an occupation unless they are a lawyer (Bar Association Law s. 20).

### Where and how

- The court's registry (מזכירות) and the Judiciary's information centre (numbers in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)) answer procedural questions, not legal ones.
- Volunteer help desks in some courts help unrepresented parties fill forms (ask the information centre for times).
- Legal Aid (סיוע משפטי) represents eligible people in many civil matters; see [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The other side is a company with a lawyer and has filed a motion to strike out or for summary dismissal.
- A procedural deadline has already passed and you need an extension or to set aside a decision.
- The amount or the issue matters to you more than the cost of a consultation (a single consultation is often enough to check a pleading).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Treating the principles as decoration and ignoring a judge's case-management order | Costs, or the claim suspended or struck out (regs 38, 151) | Comply quickly; explain the delay in writing | Diary every order the day it is given |
| Holding back a key document for trial | The court may refuse to admit it; costs | Disclose now with an explanation | Disclose early; assume surprise documents are excluded |
| Asking a relative to argue for you | The court will not hear them | Appear yourself or appoint a lawyer | Know reg. 170 and s. 20 before the hearing |
| Long, emotional filings | Ordered to amend; looks like abuse | Rewrite to the page limits (lesson 08.2) | Write to the structure the Regulations require |
| Assuming small claims or labour rules are the same as civil rules | Wrong deadlines | Check the specific code | Use the table in this lesson |

## Review

- The 2018 Regulations (in force from 1.1.2021) open with principles: fairness, truth, efficiency and the public interest (regs 1–5א).
- The court manages the case; parties must cooperate in good faith and not abuse the process (regs 3–4).
- Different forums have different codes; check which one applies before counting any deadline.
- You may act for yourself in any civil court (reg. 170), but no non-lawyer may act for you.
- Open justice and access to the courts are constitutional principles (Hashavim).
