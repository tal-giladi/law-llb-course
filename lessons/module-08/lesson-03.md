# 08.3 · Small claims and the magistrates' court

Lesson [04.9](../module-04/lesson-09.md) showed you how to write a small claim. This lesson steps back: it compares the small claims court with the ordinary magistrates' court, so that you choose the right one for each dispute, and it sets out the current filing routes and what the plaintiff must do between filing and the hearing.

## Why it matters

Most Israelis who go to court without a lawyer go to small claims. It is cheap, fast and built for them. But it is not always the right choice. A company cannot sue there; a debt bought from someone else cannot be sued there; an injunction cannot be obtained there; and a claim above the ceiling must either be cut down (and the rest lost) or go to the magistrates' court. Conversely, people sometimes go to the magistrates' court with a small claim and find themselves facing a lawyer, fuller procedure and higher costs. The choice is strategic, and it is yours: Courts Law s. 61 lets you take a small claim to another court instead.

The design question is old and still debated. Small claims trades procedural safeguards (rules of evidence, lawyers, full appeal) for access and speed. Critics point to repeat players: businesses that defend hundreds of small claims, and serial plaintiffs who file many (s. 60(ב) lets the court refuse a plaintiff who filed more than five that year in the same court). Supporters point to cases like Shemesh, where a self-represented consumer used a small claim to enforce a public-health statute.

## The law

### Who may sue, and for what: s. 60(א)

> (א) בית משפט לתביעות קטנות ידון בתביעה אזרחית שהגיש יחיד – (1) לתשלום סכום שאינו עולה על 30,000 שקלים חדשים…; (2) למתן צו להחלפת מצרך או לתיקונו או לביטול עסקה…

*Gist:* The court hears civil claims filed by an individual for money up to a linked ceiling, or for an order to replace or repair goods or cancel a transaction within the same value; also counterclaims of that kind; but not claims based on an assigned right, and not matters in another court's exclusive jurisdiction. ([Courts Law s. 60(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט#סעיף_60); the current linked ceiling is in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

### Transfer: s. 60(ב)

The court may decline to hear a claim or counterclaim and transfer it to the magistrates' court or the competent court if small claims "is not suitable" for it, or if the plaintiff already filed more than five claims that year in the same court. *Gist:* complex cases and serial filers can be moved out. ([s. 60(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט#סעיף_60))

### Your choice of court: s. 61

> האמור בסעיף 60 אין בו כדי למנוע – (1) בעל דין מהגיש תביעה כאמור בו לבית משפט אחר במקום להגישה לבית משפט לתביעות קטנות…

*Gist:* Small claims is an option, never an obligation: you may file the same claim in the magistrates' court; and a defendant whose counterclaim exceeds what small claims can award may sue separately for the excess. ([s. 61](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט#סעיף_61))

### Evidence and procedure: s. 62

> (ב) בית משפט לתביעות קטנות אינו קשור בסדרי הדין הנוהגים בבית משפט אחר, ובכפוף לסדרי דין שהתקין שר המשפטים…, יפעל בדרך הנראית לו מועילה ביותר להכרעה צודקת ומהירה.

*Gist:* The small claims court may admit evidence that would be inadmissible elsewhere (except privilege) and is not bound by ordinary procedure; within its own regulations it acts in the way most useful for a just and fast decision. ([s. 62](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט#סעיף_62))

### Representation and appeal: ss. 63–64

A lawyer may represent a party only with the court's leave, for special reasons recorded; a non-lawyer may represent with the court's approval and a filed power of attorney, unless he represents regularly, in the course of business, or for payment (s. 63). A small-claims judgment may be appealed to the district court only with leave of a district judge (s. 64). ([ss. 63–64](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט#סעיף_63)) The application period is in reg. 16(א) of the Small Claims Regulations (longer for proceedings opened from 11.4.2025; see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).

### The magistrates' court: s. 51(א)(2)

The magistrates' court hears civil claims, other than land claims, up to its own (much higher) ceiling on the day of filing, and claims about possession or use of land whatever their value. ([s. 51](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט#סעיף_51); ceiling in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)) Procedure there is the 2018 Regulations, usually on the fast track for smaller money claims (lesson 08.2).

## Leading cases

### רע״א 9615/05 שמש נ׳ פוקצ׳טה בע״מ (5.7.2006)

Recap from lessons 04.4 and 04.9. A self-represented mother sued a restaurant in small claims for exposing her to smoking in breach of the Prevention of Smoking law; the case reached the Supreme Court, which increased her compensation and stressed that private suits are a legitimate way to enforce protective statutes. Why it matters here: a small claim can raise serious legal questions; the path upward is leave to appeal (s. 64), then a further application for leave to the Supreme Court. [English translation](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/346)

### רע״א 5234/17 (representation in small claims)

Recap from lesson 04.9: a small-claims judge does not have to tell an unrepresented party that they may ask to be represented. Why it matters: if you want help in the hearing (an interpreter, a non-lawyer under s. 63(ב)), ask for it in writing before the hearing. (⚠️ manual check: decision text on the Judiciary's site.)

## How it works in practice

### Small claims or magistrates' court?

| Question | Small claims | Magistrates' court |
|---|---|---|
| Who may sue? | An individual only (including a sole trader) | Anyone; a company usually through a lawyer |
| What remedy? | Money up to the ceiling; replace, repair or cancel a consumer purchase | Money up to the much higher ceiling; injunctions; declarations; land possession |
| Assigned debt? | No | Yes |
| Lawyers? | Only with leave | Yes, normal |
| Evidence rules | Relaxed (s. 62(א)) | Full rules of evidence |
| Procedure | Small Claims Regulations; short forms; judge runs the hearing | 2018 Regulations; fast track for smaller sums |
| Fee | Low percentage with a small minimum | Higher percentage with a higher minimum |
| Costs if you lose | Usually modest | Can include the other side's legal fees |
| Appeal | Only with leave (s. 64) | As of right to the district court (reg. 137) |

**Rules of thumb.** Choose small claims for a straightforward money claim by an individual within the ceiling, especially against a business. Choose the magistrates' court when you need an order other than money (stop a nuisance, return property), when you are a company, when the claim is above the ceiling and you do not want to give up the excess, or when the dispute turns on complex documents or expert evidence a 15-minute hearing cannot handle. Do not split one claim into two small claims: you can claim only the full remedy for one cause of action once (compare reg. 25 of the 2018 Regulations), and the court may transfer or dismiss.

### Filing a small claim today

Checked on 17 October 2026 against [Kol-Zchut's small-claims page](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/הגשת_תביעה_קטנה), which links to the Judiciary's official service page:

- **Online.** The Judiciary's service "[הגשת תביעה קטנה](https://www.gov.il/he/service/filing_a_small_claim)" (⚠️ manual check: gov.il blocks automated access) leads to a guided form. You use a computer, not a phone; log in with the national digital ID (הזדהות לאומית); fill in your details, the grounds and the evidence; attach PDF files only, up to a total size limit; and pay the fee online. The Net HaMishpat home page also has a "הגשת תביעה קטנה" entry with the same login options.
- **Audio or video evidence** goes on USB drives (one for the court, one per defendant), announced in the claim and delivered to the registry, with a transcript; bring a laptop to play it.
- **On paper.** Download the claim form from the Judiciary's site, file three complete copies plus one per extra defendant at the registry of the right court (reg. 2: the defendant's area or the place of the act; for internet trade also the plaintiff's area), and pay the fee there. Claims may be filed in Hebrew or Arabic.
- **Fee.** A percentage of the claim with a minimum (see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)); an exemption may be requested for inability to pay, on the Judiciary's form.
- **Free help.** Volunteer desks in some courts help draft claims; the information centre gives their hours.

### After filing: the plaintiff's jobs

1. **Check service.** At least 60 days before the hearing, check on Net HaMishpat or with the information centre that the defendant received the claim and summons. If not, serve them yourself as the registry instructs (reg. 7(ב1)). Without proof of service the judge cannot proceed and the claim may be struck out.
2. **Summon your own witnesses.** The court does not; ask them and, if needed, apply for a witness summons.
3. **Update your details** with the registry if your address or phone changes.
4. **Watch the case.** Net HaMishpat shows the hearing date, decisions and registry messages; you also get SMS and email notices.
5. **Read the defence.** The defendant has a set period from service to file it (reg. 4(ב)) and must attach its documents (reg. 5(א1)); it may add a counterclaim or a third-party notice.

### Kol-Zchut and the regulation disagree on one date

On 17 October 2026 Kol-Zchut's page still gave the older period for leave to appeal; the regulation as amended gives a longer period for proceedings opened from 11.4.2025 (reg. 16(א)). Always count from the regulation, and check the date your case was opened.

## Worked example

Grigori Volkov, a retired engineer in Ashdod, and his neighbour's company are in two disputes.

**Dispute 1.** A furniture shop delivered a broken wardrobe and refuses to replace it. Price ₪7,200. Grigori wants the wardrobe replaced, not money. Small claims can order replacement of goods within the ceiling (s. 60(א)(2)). He files online, attaches the receipt, photos and his WhatsApp messages as PDFs, and asks for replacement or, alternatively, a refund.

**Dispute 2.** A renovation company working next door, "א.ש. שיפוצים בע״מ", leaves building waste on Grigori's private parking space every week and has done so for months. Grigori wants it stopped and ₪3,000 for the cleaning he paid for. The money alone would fit small claims, but an order to stop is an injunction, which small claims cannot give. He files in the Ashdod magistrates' court (nuisance and trespass, lesson [04.4](../module-04/lesson-04.md)), on the fast track, attaching an affidavit and his documents (reg. 79). He accepts the higher fee and the risk of costs because the order is what he really needs.

**A trap avoided.** Grigori's son, who owns a small company, asks Grigori to "buy" a ₪9,000 debt a customer owes the company and sue in small claims, since the company cannot. That would be a claim "by an assigned right", which s. 60(א) excludes. The company must sue in the magistrates' court.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- One or two money disputes of your own, or both disputes in the worked example.
- Courts Law ss. 51 and 59–64 and the Small Claims Regulations open in another window.
- About 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Eligibility (10 min).** For each dispute, answer: Am I an individual suing in my own right (not an assigned debt)? Is the remedy money, or replacement, repair or cancellation of a purchase? Is the value within the ceiling in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)? Is the matter in another court's exclusive jurisdiction (labour, family, land)? Cite the section for each answer.
2. **Choose (10 min).** Fill in the comparison table for your dispute and write two sentences: which court, and why.
3. **Venue (5 min).** Under reg. 2 of the Small Claims Regulations (or reg. 7 of the 2018 Regulations), list the courts you may file in and pick one.
4. **Filing plan (10 min).** Write the filing route you will use (online or paper), the documents you will convert to PDF, any audio or video evidence and how you will deliver it, and the fee.
5. **After-filing diary (10 min).** Write the dates: 60 days before the hearing (check service), the defence period, the hearing, and the leave-to-appeal period for a case opened today.

### Template

- [Small claim for property damage](../../templates/small-claim-property-damage.md), adaptable to any money claim (lesson 04.9).

### How you know it worked

- You can say in one sentence why small claims can or cannot hear each dispute, citing s. 60 or s. 61.
- Your filing plan names the route, the court and the evidence format.
- Your diary counts the leave-to-appeal period from the regulation, not from a secondary website.

- [ ] I know a company cannot sue in small claims and an assigned debt cannot be sued there.
- [ ] I know small claims cannot order a neighbour to stop a nuisance.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes; small claims is designed for it, and lawyers appear only with leave (s. 63). In the magistrates' court you may also act for yourself (reg. 170), but the other side will often have a lawyer. Only an individual may sue in small claims; a company may be sued there and defends through an authorised person.

### Where and how

- Small claims: the Judiciary's online service (national digital ID; ⚠️ manual check of the current page) or the registry of the right court; Net HaMishpat to follow the case.
- Magistrates' court: the registry or Net HaMishpat where allowed (lesson 08.8).
- Ceilings, fees and periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md). Free help: volunteer desks, the information centre, [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md); forum rules: [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The small-claims judge transfers your case to the magistrates' court (s. 60(ב)) and the defendant has a lawyer.
- You need an injunction urgently (lesson 08.2; a temporary injunction needs an affidavit and an undertaking).
- You are considering leave to appeal a small-claims judgment on a point of law.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Company owner sues in small claims in his own name for the company's debt | Claim dismissed or transferred: not his right, or an assigned right | Company sues in the magistrates' court | Check who the creditor is on the invoice |
| Asking small claims to stop a nuisance | Court cannot give the injunction | Withdraw and file in the magistrates' court | Check the remedy against s. 60(א) first |
| Cutting a ₪45,000 claim to fit the ceiling | The rest is lost | Before judgment, ask to transfer and amend | Decide the court before filing |
| Filing by phone with photos as JPGs | Upload fails or is rejected | Use a computer; convert to PDF | Prepare PDFs before you start |
| Not checking service | Hearing cannot proceed; claim may be struck out | Serve yourself and file proof | Diary "60 days before the hearing" |
| Counting leave to appeal from a website | Application late | Ask for an extension at once with reasons | Count from reg. 16(א) and the case-opening date |

## Review

- Small claims hears an individual's money claims within a linked ceiling and certain consumer orders; not companies as plaintiffs, assigned debts, or other courts' exclusive matters (s. 60).
- It is optional: you may take any claim to the magistrates' court instead (s. 61).
- Relaxed evidence and procedure (s. 62), lawyers only with leave (s. 63), appeal only with leave (s. 64).
- Filing is online (computer, national digital ID, PDFs) or on paper at the registry; the plaintiff must check service and summon witnesses.
- Choose by remedy, party, value and complexity, not by habit.
