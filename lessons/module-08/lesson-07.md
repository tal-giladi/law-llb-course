# 08.7 · Enforcing a judgment: the Execution Office (הוצאה לפועל)

A judgment in your favour is a piece of paper until the debtor pays. This lesson explains how the Execution Office turns a judgment, a confirmed arbitral award, an approved settlement or a cheque into money, what the debtor can do at each stage, and where the law protects debtors. By the end you will be able to plan an execution file and anticipate the debtor's response; lesson 08.9 is the lab where you prepare one.

## Why it matters

Winning and collecting are different skills. Many self-represented creditors stop at the judgment and never see the money; others open a file and then do nothing, so it closes for inaction. The Execution Office (לשכת ההוצאה לפועל), run by the Enforcement and Collection Authority (רשות האכיפה והגבייה), has strong tools: liens on bank accounts, wages and cars, restrictions on passports and credit cards, sale of property. It is also the place where Israeli law has struggled most visibly with poverty and debt.

That struggle is the academic story. Until the 1990s the Execution Office could order a debtor's imprisonment for non-payment almost routinely. In בג״ץ 5304/92 (below), a challenge to imprisonment orders issued without examining the debtor's means, Deputy President Elon described imprisonment for debt as an affront to liberty and dignity. Later reforms, especially in 2008, made imprisonment a last resort and replaced it with targeted restrictions on a debtor who can pay but evades. The Execution Law now tries to separate two kinds of debtor: the one who cannot pay (who gets a payment order, a consolidation of files, or a route to insolvency proceedings) and the one who can but will not (who faces restrictions and investigation). As a creditor you must know both sides.

## The law

### What can be executed: ss. 1, 81, 81א, 81א1

"Judgment" means a judgment or other decision of a court in a civil matter, including a costs order in a non-civil matter (s. 1). Registered mortgages, and decisions and documents that any law says are to be executed like a court judgment, are executed too (s. 81): for example an arbitral award once confirmed (Arbitration Law s. 23) and a settlement given the force of a judgment (Courts Law ss. 79א, 79ג; lesson 08.6). Cheques and notes can be executed directly (s. 81א), and a creditor with written evidence of a fixed-sum debt up to a ceiling can file a "claim for a fixed sum" (תביעה על סכום קצוב) in the Execution Office after a registered-mail warning (s. 81א1). ([Execution Law](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל))

### Opening a file: s. 6

> (א) הזוכה רשאי להגיש לכל לשכת הוצאה לפועל בקשה לביצוע פסק הדין; הוא יציין בה פרטים מזהים של החייב…, וכן יפרש בה את ההליכים שהוא רוצה שיינקטו…

*Gist:* The creditor may file a request at any Execution Office branch, identifying the debtor and specifying the steps it wants; it may ask for more steps later. ([s. 6(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_6))

> (ב) לא תוגש בקשה לביצוע פסק דין… אלא לאחר שחלף המועד שנקבע בפסק הדין לביצועו, ואם לא נקבע מועד כאמור – לאחר שחלפו 30 ימים מיום מתן פסק הדין…

*Gist:* You wait until the date for payment in the judgment, or, if none, the set period from the judgment (from service if it was given in the debtor's absence and you had to serve it); the registrar may allow earlier filing on an affidavit showing a risk that delay will defeat execution (s. 6(ב1)). ([s. 6(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_6); periods in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)) If you wait more than a year after you could have filed, interest is limited (s. 6(ב2)).

### The warning: s. 7

> (א) משהוגשה בקשת ביצוע, ימציא מנהל לשכת ההוצאה לפועל לחייב אזהרה שעליו למלא אחר פסק־הדין… או להגיש בקשה לצו תשלומים לפי סעיף 7א, והכל בתוך תקופה של 20 ימים מיום המצאת האזהרה…

*Gist:* The office serves the debtor a warning: comply with the judgment or apply for a payment order within the warning period; the warning must explain, in plain language, that a debtor who cannot pay must attend a means inquiry within a slightly longer period, with documents (s. 7(א1)). Enforcement starts only after the warning period, unless the registrar orders otherwise (s. 7(ה)). ([s. 7](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_7); periods in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

### The debtor's options: ss. 7א, 7א2, 19, 67

- **Payment order (s. 7א).** A debtor who cannot pay in full applies, within the warning period, with a reasoned, documented statement of means and a waiver of confidentiality; the creditor is told and may respond; the registrar may grant instalments or call a means inquiry.
- **Cannot pay at all (s. 7א2).** The debtor notifies the registrar; depending on the total debt, the notice is treated as an application to open insolvency proceedings or the office gives information on applying to the insolvency official (year 3, module 28).
- **"I have paid" (s. 19).** A debtor claiming he already paid, or no longer owes, bears the burden of proof; the registrar decides and may stay execution only for special reasons, usually against security.
- **Means inquiry (s. 67).** A debtor who does not attend is treated as one "with means who evades payment" (s. 67(ד)), which opens the way to restrictions.

The debtor cannot reopen the merits of the judgment in the Execution Office; that is for an appeal or an application to set aside in the court that gave it.

### Liens and their limits: ss. 22, 38

The office may attach movables, vehicles, bank accounts, wages and money held by third parties, and land. But some property cannot be attached:

> (א) ואלה מיטלטלין שאין מעקלים אותם: (1) צרכי אוכל כדי מחיית החייב ובני משפחתו… (2) ריהוט, מערכות בגדים, מיטות… (4) כלים, מכשירים, מכונות ומיטלטלין אחרים לרבות רכב…, שבלעדיהם אין החייב יכול לקיים מקצועו…

*Gist:* Food for the family for a month, basic furniture, clothing and household items, religious articles, tools of the trade (including a vehicle) needed for the debtor's livelihood, a disabled person's aids, pets, and personal phones and computers are exempt, among others. ([s. 22](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_22)) A debtor's home may be sold only after the registrar is satisfied that the debtor and family will have reasonable alternative housing, and only after a hearing (s. 38).

### Restrictions on an evading debtor: ss. 66א–66ב

The registrar may, on the creditor's request or on his own initiative, restrict a debtor from holding a passport, leaving the country, using cheques (as a "special restricted customer"), using credit cards, or founding or being an interested party in a company (s. 66א). Restrictions require a condition in s. 66ב: for example, the debtor was found to have means and to be evading, or a set time has passed since the warning and the debt exceeds a threshold, or the debt is maintenance. ([ss. 66א–66ב](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_66א); thresholds in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

### The short track: s. 20ב

Money judgments and other money obligations up to a ceiling (not maintenance, not mortgages) may be executed in a short track, unless the debtor's total execution debts exceed a higher figure; the office runs the collection steps itself, and the file aims to close within months. ([s. 20ב](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל#סעיף_20ב); figures in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

### Fees: s. 9 and the fee regulations

The creditor pays the fees and estimated expenses before each step, and they are added to the debt and charged to the debtor, unless the registrar finds a step was requested in bad faith (s. 9). Opening a file for a fixed money judgment costs a percentage of the debt with a minimum, and the short track has its own fee (Fee Regulations, Schedule item (א)(1), (6א); see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)). A creditor exempt from court fees is exempt here too (reg. 5).

### Review: s. 80

A person harmed by an act of the office's director or staff files an objection (ערר) to the registrar; the registrar's decisions are appealable to the magistrates' court, mostly with leave, and as of right for listed decisions (arrest to prevent obstruction, exit ban, "I have paid", sale of the home and others) (s. 80). An objection or appeal does not stay the file unless ordered (s. 80(ג)).

## Leading cases

### בג״ץ 5304/92 פר״ח 1992 – סיוע משפטי לנפגעי חוק ההוצאה לפועל נ׳ שר המשפטים, פ״ד מז(4) 715 (1993)

An association acting for debtors challenged the practice of issuing imprisonment orders against debtors without first examining their ability to pay or letting them argue before the registrar. The opinion of Deputy President Menachem Elon described imprisonment for debt as a denial of liberty and an affront to human dignity. The course could not open the full text, so the exact operative order is not stated here. Why it matters: it is the constitutional root of today's distinction between the debtor who cannot pay and the one who evades, and of the means inquiry in s. 67. ## How it works in practice

### Opening a file for a money judgment

Checked on 17 October 2026 against [Kol-Zchut](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/בקשה_לביצוע_פסק_דין_כספי_בהוצאה_לפועל), which links to the Authority's service page ([gov.il](https://www.gov.il/he/service/opening_monetary_verdict_file); ⚠️ manual check, gov.il blocks automated access):

- **Form.** Request to execute a money judgment, form 206 ("טופס בקשה לביצוע פסק דין (כספי בלבד)"); form 600 to add each additional debtor or creditor; a declaration if there are several creditors; a special accompanying form if the judgment arose from a loan.
- **Attach.** The judgment, original or certified true copy, showing the parties' ID numbers (or a certified court document showing the defendants' IDs); a copy of a cheque or a bank confirmation of the creditor's account; proof that the judgment was served on the debtor, where relevant.
- **File.** Online through the Authority's personal area, or by hand at any Execution Office branch by prior appointment; the call centre is *35592 (⚠️ manual check whether the online personal area accepts unrepresented creditors; an earlier Kol-Zchut version listed mail, fax and email as well).
- **Pay** the opening fee.
- **Choose the track.** For debts within the short-track ceiling you may choose the short track (s. 20ב) or the regular track.

### After opening: the creditor's plan

1. The office serves the warning (or you serve it yourself if you said so in the form).
2. Wait for the warning period. Watch for a payment-order request, an "I have paid" claim, or a means inquiry date.
3. If nothing happens, request steps: information on the debtor's bank accounts, employer and vehicles; liens on accounts and wages; a lien on a car; restrictions once the conditions in s. 66ב are met.
4. Keep the file active and update the office when payments arrive directly (s. 19א); files with no activity can be closed.
5. Check whether the debtor has a maintenance file: maintenance debts are collected first.

### The debtor's side, briefly

If you are the debtor: act within the warning period. If you can pay in instalments, apply for a payment order with documents; if you cannot pay at all, see the insolvency route; if you already paid, file an "I have paid" claim with proof. Free help: Legal Aid has an execution-office service (Kol-Zchut: "סיוע משפטי מטעם משרד המשפטים בתחום ההוצאה לפועל", ⚠️ manual check), and NGOs support debtors.

## Worked example

Rachel Abergel, a kindergarten teacher in Kiryat Shmona, won a small-claims judgment for ₪6,400 plus ₪500 costs against "קלין־פרו שירותי ניקיון בע״מ", payable within 30 days. Sixty days later nothing has been paid.

1. **Can she file?** The payment date has passed (s. 6(ב)). The judgment is a court judgment in a civil matter (s. 1).
2. **Track.** The debt is within the short-track ceiling, and she has no information about the company's other debts. She chooses the short track (s. 20ב), where the office takes the collection steps.
3. **Documents.** Form 206 with the company's registration number from the judgment; a certified copy of the judgment from Net HaMishpat; a cheque copy for her bank details. The judgment was given at a hearing the company attended, so no separate proof of service is needed.
4. **Fee.** The short-track fee (see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)), added to the debt.
5. **Filing.** She books an appointment at the Kiryat Shmona branch through the Authority's appointment system and files in person.
6. **Result.** The warning reaches the company; three weeks later its bank account is attached; it pays the full debt with linkage, interest and fees. Rachel asks the office to close the file.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A judgment, settlement with the force of a judgment, or confirmed award in your favour; or the supplied case: Rachel's judgment above.
- Execution Law ss. 6, 7, 20ב, 22, 66א–66ב and 67 open in another window; Kol-Zchut's page on executing a money judgment.
- 40 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Executable? (5 min).** Which category (s. 1, 81, 81א, 81א1)? Cite it.
2. **When? (5 min).** Compute the first day you may file under s. 6(ב) using [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md). Is there a reason to ask for earlier filing (s. 6(ב1))?
3. **Track (5 min).** Short or regular? Write why.
4. **Debtor profile (10 min).** What do you know: ID or company number, address, bank, employer, car, property? Which liens fit? Which assets are exempt (s. 22)?
5. **Debtor's likely response (10 min).** Write the debtor's three most likely moves (payment order, "I have paid", no response) and your answer to each.
6. **Fees and diary (5 min).** List the fees and the dates: filing, end of the warning period, when restrictions become possible.

## Without a lawyer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. A creditor or debtor may act for himself in the Execution Office; representing another person there is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20(2) names the Execution Office expressly). Maintenance files are partly collected on the office's initiative (maintenance track).

### Where and how

- Any Execution Office branch (by appointment) or the Authority's online personal area (⚠️ manual check for unrepresented creditors); call centre *35592.
- Forms 206 and 600 from the Authority's site (linked from Kol-Zchut).
- Fees, waiting periods, warning period, short-track ceiling and restriction thresholds: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md). Free help: [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md); forum rules: [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The debtor files an "I have paid" claim or an objection you cannot answer with documents.
- You want to sell land or a home, or the debtor is in insolvency proceedings.
- A third party claims the attached property is theirs (s. 40, s. 65).
- You are the debtor and face restrictions or a large debt: Legal Aid or an NGO first.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Filing the day after judgment | Request rejected (s. 6(ב)) | Refile after the date | Diary the payment date or the default period |
| Judgment copy without ID numbers | Request not accepted | Get a certified copy or court document with IDs | Ask the registry for a certified copy when judgment is given |
| Opening the file and doing nothing | File inactive, may close; debtor moves money | Request liens and information now | Plan the steps before filing |
| Asking to attach the debtor's work van and basic furniture | Lien cancelled (s. 22); possible costs | Target bank accounts and wages | Check exemptions first |
| Not reporting direct payments | Over-collection; costs against you (s. 20) | Update the office at once | Report every payment (s. 19א) |
| Waiting years to open | Interest limited (s. 6(ב2)) | File now | Open within a year of being able to |

## Review

- The Execution Office enforces judgments, confirmed awards, settlements with the force of a judgment, cheques and notes, and fixed-sum claims.
- File after the payment date or the default period; the debtor gets a warning with a set period to pay, ask for instalments or attend a means inquiry.
- Debtors who cannot pay get payment orders or an insolvency route; debtors who can but evade face restrictions (ss. 66א–66ב).
- Necessities, tools of trade and the family home are protected (ss. 22, 38).
- The creditor pays fees up front and recovers them from the debtor; the short track suits small debts.
