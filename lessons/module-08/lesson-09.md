# 08.9 · Practice lab: opening an execution file for a judgment

In this lab you prepare an execution file for a money judgment from start to finish: the certified judgment, form 206 and its attachments, the date, the track and the fee, and a plan for the months after the file opens. Then you switch sides and write the debtor's answer to the warning. It is a dry run unless you hold a judgment of your own and decide to file in your own name.

## Why it matters

Lesson 08.7 gave you the law. In practice, files fail on documents (a judgment copy without ID numbers, no bank confirmation), on timing (filed before the payment date), or on passivity (opened, then left alone). Understanding the debtor's side makes you a better creditor and, if you are ever a debtor, protects you: most of the debtor's rights depend on acting within the warning period.

## The law

The rules are in lesson 08.7: filing after the payment date or the default period (s. 6(ב)); the warning and its period (s. 7); the debtor's payment-order request (s. 7א) and "I have paid" claim (s. 19); fees paid by the creditor and charged to the debtor (s. 9); the short track (s. 20ב); exempt property (s. 22); restrictions and their conditions (ss. 66א–66ב); the means inquiry (s. 67). Full text: [Execution Law](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ההוצאה_לפועל). Periods, ceilings and fees: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

## How it works in practice

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

The route on 17 October 2026, per Kol-Zchut:

1. Form 206; form 600 for each extra party; a declaration if several creditors; a loan form if the judgment arose from a loan.
2. Attachments: the judgment (original or certified true copy showing the parties' ID numbers, or a certified court document with the defendant's ID); a cheque copy or bank confirmation; proof of service of the judgment if it was given in the debtor's absence.
3. Filing: the Authority's online personal area, or in person at any branch by appointment (appointment system or call centre *35592).
4. Fee on opening.
5. The office serves the warning, unless you choose to serve it yourself.

## Worked example

Moshe Cohen-Amar, a retired bus driver in Dimona, won ₪11,000 plus ₪800 costs in a small claim against Yaniv Dahan, a private individual who borrowed money and did not repay. The judgment, given at a hearing both attended on 1 July 2026, sets no payment date.

1. **Earliest date.** No payment date and the debtor was present: the default period from 1 July (s. 6(ב)). Moshe diaries the first permitted day.
2. **Documents.** He downloads the judgment from Net HaMishpat; the copy shows both ID numbers. He asks the registry for a certified copy. He photocopies a cheque.
3. **Loan.** The judgment arose from a loan, so he adds the loan form linked from Kol-Zchut.
4. **Track.** The debt is within the short-track ceiling. He does not know Yaniv's other debts; he chooses the short track, accepting that it moves to the regular track if the debtor's total debts are too high (s. 20ב(ב)).
5. **Filing.** By appointment at the Be'er Sheva branch; he pays the fee.
6. **Yaniv's response.** Within the warning period Yaniv files a payment-order request offering ₪200 a month, with payslips. Moshe answers in writing: Yaniv's bank statements show a second income; ₪200 would take almost five years. The registrar sets ₪450 a month.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A money judgment, settlement with the force of a judgment, or confirmed award in your favour; or Moshe's case above.
- Form 206 (download the current version), the [execution request checklist](../../templates/execution-request-checklist.md), and Execution Law ss. 6–7א, 19, 20ב, 22, 66א–67.
- About two hours.

### Steps

**Part A — the creditor's file (75 min)**

1. **Check executability and date (10 min).** Fill in part A of the [checklist](../../templates/execution-request-checklist.md): type of document, judgment date, payment date or default period, first filing day, any reason for early filing.
2. **Get the right judgment copy (10 min).** Check that your copy shows the parties' ID or registration numbers. If not, plan how to get a certified court document that does.
3. **Parties and bank (10 min).** Part B: exact names and numbers as in the judgment; your bank details; extra parties and form 600.
4. **Debt table (10 min).** Part D: principal, costs, payments already received.
5. **Track and fee (10 min).** Part E: short or regular track and why; the fee from [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); any exemption.
6. **Fill form 206 (15 min).** Complete every field you can. Leave nothing blank without writing why. Do not file (dry run) unless you have decided to.
7. **Enforcement plan (10 min).** Part G: the date the warning period ends; the information requests and liens you will ask for; when restrictions may become possible (s. 66ב); exempt assets you will not target (s. 22).

**Part B — the debtor's answer (45 min)**

Use this case: you are Yaniv in Moshe's file. You earn ₪7,800 a month net, pay ₪3,900 rent, support two children and have a ₪12,000 bank loan.

8. **Options (10 min).** List the options open to you within the warning period (pay, payment order, means inquiry, "I have paid") and which fits.
9. **Payment-order request (25 min).** Draft, in Hebrew, a one-page reasoned request under s. 7א: income, fixed expenses, dependants, other debts, the monthly amount you offer and why, the documents you attach (payslips, rent contract, loan statement), and the waiver of confidentiality the law requires.
10. **Creditor's view (10 min).** Switch back to Moshe: what would you check in Yaniv's request, and what would you answer?

- [Execution request checklist (רשימת בדיקה לפתיחת תיק בהוצאה לפועל)](../../templates/execution-request-checklist.md) (Hebrew), with the Authority's form 206.

### How you know it worked

- Your checklist has a first filing date with its source, the right judgment copy, the track with a reason and the fee.
- Form 206 is complete or every gap is explained.
- Your enforcement plan has dates and avoids exempt assets.
- Your payment-order request offers a specific monthly amount supported by documents.

### Self-check

- [ ] I am acting in my own name about my own judgment.
- [ ] I will not file before the payment date or the default period.
- [ ] I know the debtor's options and their deadlines.
- [ ] I will update the office about any payment received directly (s. 19א).

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes, as creditor or debtor. Representing someone else before the Execution Office is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20(2)).

### Where and how

- Any Execution Office branch by appointment, or the Authority's online personal area (⚠️ manual check for unrepresented creditors); call centre *35592; forms 206 and 600 from the Authority's site.
- Fees, periods, ceilings and thresholds: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md). Debtors: Legal Aid's execution-office service and NGOs ([where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md)).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The debtor files an "I have paid" claim or an objection and a hearing is set.
- You want to attach or sell land, or the debtor enters insolvency proceedings.
- As a debtor, your debts exceed your ability to pay and restrictions have been imposed: Legal Aid or an NGO first.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Judgment copy without ID numbers | Request not accepted | Get a certified court document with the IDs | Check before the appointment |
| Leaving fields blank on form 206 | Request returned | Complete and refile | Use the checklist; explain any gap |
| Filing the day after judgment | Rejected (s. 6(ב)) | Refile on the right date | Diary the first filing day |
| Debtor ignores the warning period | Treated as having means and evading (s. 67(ד)); restrictions | Apply now for a payment order with documents | Act within the warning period |
| Debtor offers an amount with no documents | Request not accepted (s. 7א(א1)) | Refile with documents and the waiver | Attach payslips, statements, waiver |

## Review

- An execution request needs form 206, the right judgment copy with IDs, bank details and the fee, filed after the payment date or default period.
- Choose the track deliberately; plan liens and requests before filing; do not target exempt assets.
- The debtor's rights depend on acting within the warning period: payment order, means inquiry, or "I have paid".
- Keep the file active and report direct payments.
