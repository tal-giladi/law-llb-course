# 08.6 · Settling out of court: mediation, arbitration, settlement

Most civil disputes in Israel end without a judgment on the merits: they settle, often after a mediation-intake meeting, a judge's proposal or a mediation. This lesson teaches the legal framework of the three main alternatives to trial, so that you can choose between them, read an arbitration clause before you sign it, and turn a settlement into something you can enforce.

## Why it matters

A judgment after a full trial costs time, money and nerves, and its result is uncertain. Mediation can end a dispute in one or two meetings; arbitration gives a private, expert and sometimes faster decision; and a settlement approved by the court is as enforceable as a judgment. But each has traps. An arbitration clause hidden in a contract can take your dispute out of court entirely, with very limited review. A mediated agreement that is only a signed paper is a contract, not a judgment, until a court gives it that force. And an unrepresented party can feel pressed to settle for too little.

The academic debate runs between two views. The "access to justice" view sees mediation and arbitration as cheaper, more flexible and more participatory forms of justice that relieve congested courts; the 2018 Regulations' compulsory mediation-intake meeting reflects it (lesson 08.2). The critical view, associated with Owen Fiss's "Against Settlement", warns that private settlement hides power imbalances, deprives the public of precedent, and lets the stronger party buy peace on its own terms. Israeli law takes a middle path: it pushes parties to *consider* alternatives but keeps mediation itself voluntary (Courts Law s. 79ג(ב)) and lets courts set aside arbitral awards on a closed list of grounds (Arbitration Law s. 24).

## The law

### Mediation: Courts Law s. 79ג

> ”גישור“ – הליך שבו נועד מגשר עם בעלי הדין, כדי להביאם לידי הסכמה ליישוב הסכסוך, מבלי שיש בידו סמכות להכריע בו;

*Gist:* Mediation is a process in which a mediator meets the parties to bring them to agreement, without power to decide. ([s. 79ג(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט#סעיף_79ג))

The court may refer a claim to mediation with the parties' consent (s. 79ג(ב)) and stays the proceedings for a set period (s. 79ג(ה)). The mediator may meet the parties together or separately, and a party without his lawyer with consent (s. 79ג(ג)).

> (ד) דברים שנמסרו במסגרת הליך גישור, לא ישמשו ראיה בהליך משפטי אזרחי.

*Gist:* What is said in mediation is not evidence in civil proceedings. ([s. 79ג(ד)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט#סעיף_79ג))

If the parties settle, the court may give the agreement the force of a judgment (s. 79ג(ז)); and even if no claim was ever filed, the court competent to hear the dispute may give force of judgment to a settlement reached in mediation under the law (s. 79ג(ח)). The Courts (Mediation) Regulations 1993 govern referral, the mediator's duties, fees and the settlement. The free mediation-intake meeting under reg. 37 of the 2018 Regulations uses the same confidentiality rules (reg. 37(יב)).

### Compromise and settlement: Courts Law s. 79א

> (א) בית משפט הדן בענין אזרחי רשאי, בהסכמת בעלי הדין, לפסוק בענין שלפניו, כולו או מקצתו, בדרך של פשרה.

*Gist:* With the parties' consent the court may decide all or part of a case "by way of compromise", without full reasons; it may also propose a settlement or, on request, give the parties' own settlement the force of a judgment (s. 79א(ב)). ([s. 79א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט#סעיף_79א))

### Court referral to arbitration: s. 79ב

With the parties' consent the court may refer a case, or part of it, to arbitration, and approve or appoint the arbitrator; the Arbitration Law then applies (s. 79ב). ([s. 79ב](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט#סעיף_79ב))

### Arbitration: the agreement and the implied terms

> ”הסכם בוררות“ – הסכם בכתב למסור לבוררות סכסוך שנתגלע בין צדדים להסכם או שעשוי להתגלע ביניהם בעתיד…

*Gist:* An arbitration agreement must be in writing; it may cover an existing or a future dispute. ([Arbitration Law s. 1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הבוררות)) A matter that cannot be the subject of an agreement between the parties cannot be arbitrated (s. 3); this excludes, for example, criminal liability and some questions of personal status.

Unless the agreement shows a different intention, it is read as containing the First Schedule (s. 2), including: a single arbitrator; the arbitrator's power to order disclosure as a court could; an award within three months of starting, extendable; reasons for the award; and

> הבורר… לא יהיה קשור בדין המהותי, בדיני הראיות או בסדרי הדין הנוהגים בבתי המשפט…

*Gist:* by default the arbitrator is not bound by substantive law, the rules of evidence or court procedure, and decides by his best judgment on the material before him. ([First Schedule, item יד](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הבוררות)) Parties who want the law applied must say so.

### Stay of court proceedings: s. 5

> (א) הוגשה תובענה לבית משפט בסכסוך שהוסכם למסרו לבוררות וביקש בעל־דין שהוא צד להסכם הבוררות לעכב את ההליכים בתובענה, יעכב בית המשפט את ההליכים…

*Gist:* If a party to an arbitration agreement is sued in court and asks for a stay, the court stays the proceedings, provided the applicant was and remains ready to arbitrate; the request must come in the defence or before the applicant first argues the merits; the court may refuse for a special reason. ([s. 5](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הבוררות))

### The award: binding, confirmed, set aside

An award binds the parties "as res judicata" (s. 21). The court may confirm it, and a confirmed award is like a court judgment except for appeal (s. 23), which means it can be enforced through the Execution Office (lesson 08.7). The court may set aside, complete, correct or return an award only on the grounds in s. 24:

> (1) לא היה הסכם בוררות בר־תוקף; (2) הפסק ניתן על ידי בורר שלא נתמנה כדין; (3) הבורר פעל ללא סמכות או שחרג מהסמכויות…; (4) לא ניתנה לבעל־דין הזדמנות נאותה לטעון טענותיו או להביא ראיותיו; …(9) תכנו של הפסק מנוגד לתקנת הציבור…

*Gist:* The grounds are about validity, appointment, authority, a fair hearing, a matter left undecided, missing reasons or failure to apply the law where the agreement required them, lateness, public policy, and grounds that would set aside a final judgment. ([s. 24](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הבוררות)) Even then the court may refuse if no miscarriage of justice occurred (s. 26(א)). The application must be filed within the set period from the award or its delivery (s. 27; see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)), and not after the award was confirmed (s. 27(ג)).

Parties may agree on an appeal to another arbitrator (s. 21א) or, where the arbitrator must decide by law, on an appeal by leave to the court for a fundamental error in applying the law (s. 29ב).

### Who may represent you: Bar Association Law s. 20

Representing another person "before courts, tribunals, arbitrators" is reserved to lawyers (s. 20(1)); s. 21 allows workers' and employers' organisations to represent in labour arbitration. You may always appear for yourself. ([s. 20](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לשכת_עורכי_הדין))

## Leading cases

### Key text: the closed list in s. 24

The free English-language collections the course can open contain no leading Supreme Court decision on setting aside arbitral awards, so the statute is the authority here. The doctrinal point that matters is that s. 24 is a closed list and does not include "the arbitrator was wrong on the facts or the law". Unless the parties agreed on an appeal (ss. 21א, 29ב), a mistaken award stands. Read ss. 21–29ב in full on [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הבוררות) before signing any arbitration clause.

### Key text: the default rule in item יד of the First Schedule

Because an arbitrator is not bound by substantive law unless the agreement says otherwise, an arbitration clause without the words "בהתאם לדין" (according to law) gives the arbitrator a very wide discretion and removes ground (7) of s. 24. This is the single most important drafting point for a non-lawyer reading a contract.

## How it works in practice

### Choosing a process

| | Negotiation | Mediation | Arbitration | Court settlement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Who decides | The parties | The parties, helped by a mediator | The arbitrator | The parties (or the judge "by compromise") |
| Binding? | Only if signed as a contract | Only if signed; force of judgment if approved (s. 79ג(ז)–(ח)) | Yes, as res judicata (s. 21); enforceable once confirmed (s. 23) | Yes; force of judgment (s. 79א) |
| Review | None | None | Narrow: s. 24 | Very narrow (consent judgment) |
| Confidential | Only if agreed | Statements not evidence (s. 79ג(ד)) | Private hearing | Court file may be public |
| Cost | Free | Intake meeting free; mediator's fee for further meetings | Arbitrator's fee, often high | Court fee already paid |
| Speed | Fast | Weeks | Months | At any hearing |

### Before mediation

- Know your BATNA (best alternative to a negotiated agreement): your realistic result in court, minus costs and time (module 11 develops this).
- Decide your lowest acceptable figure and why, in writing, before you go in.
- Bring documents, not speeches. Ask the mediator for a private meeting if you want to say something you would not say in front of the other side.

### Writing a settlement that works

A settlement should state: the parties; the dispute it ends; what each side does and by when (amounts, instalments, bank details); what happens on default (the whole balance falls due, with interest; consent to judgment for the unpaid balance); whether it includes costs; mutual waiver of claims "in connection with" the dispute, and nothing broader than you intend; and a request that the court give it the force of a judgment. Only then can you open an execution file if it is not paid (lesson 08.7).

### Arbitration in consumer and employment contracts

An arbitration clause in a standard contract may be challenged as a depriving term (lesson [03.6](../module-03/lesson-06.md)); in an employment dispute many rights are protected by law and cannot be waived. Get advice before relying on, or fighting, such a clause.

## Worked example

Shira and Avi Mizrahi, a couple in Tiberias, hired a contractor to renovate their kitchen for ₪60,000 and withheld the last ₪15,000 because of leaks. The contractor sued them in the magistrates' court. Their contract has a clause: "כל מחלוקת תוכרע בבוררות בפני מהנדס שימונה על ידי הקבלן."

1. **Stay?** If they want arbitration, they must ask for a stay in their defence or before they argue the merits (s. 5(ב)); if they file a full defence on the merits without asking, they lose the right.
2. **Do they want it?** The arbitrator would be chosen by the contractor and, under item יד, would not be bound by law. The clause appears in the contractor's standard form. They decide not to ask for a stay and to argue, if the contractor asks for one, that the appointment mechanism is unfair. They file a defence and a counterclaim for the repair cost.
3. **Mediation-intake meeting.** The claim is above the threshold, so a meeting is appointed (reg. 37). They attend; both sides agree to two paid mediation sessions.
4. **Settlement.** The contractor repairs the leaks within 30 days; the couple pay ₪11,000 within 14 days of an engineer's confirmation; if the contractor does not repair, the couple owe nothing more and may claim the repair cost; each side bears its costs. They ask the court to give it the force of a judgment (s. 79ג(ז)). It does.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A contract you signed that contains a dispute-resolution clause (rental, building, employment, an online service's terms), or the clause in the worked example.
- A dispute you might settle, or the supplied case from lesson 08.1.
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Read the clause (10 min).** Is it an arbitration agreement in writing (s. 1)? Who appoints the arbitrator? Is the arbitrator bound by law? Any appeal (ss. 21א, 29ב)? What does the First Schedule add?
2. **Map the choice (10 min).** Fill in the comparison table for your dispute with your own estimates of cost and time.
3. **BATNA (10 min).** Write your realistic court result, your costs, the time, and your lowest acceptable settlement.
4. **Draft a settlement (15 min).** In Hebrew, ten numbered clauses covering all the items in "Writing a settlement that works", ending with a request to give it the force of a judgment.

### Template

None in the course yet; your draft settlement from step 4 becomes your own template. Lesson 08.9 shows how a settlement with the force of a judgment is enforced.

### How you know it worked

- You can say in one sentence whether your clause would stay a court claim and what an award under it could be set aside for.
- Your settlement has a default clause and a request for the force of a judgment.
- Your lowest figure is written down with a reason.

### Self-check

- [ ] I know mediation is voluntary but the intake meeting is compulsory.
- [ ] I know an arbitrator is not bound by law unless the agreement says so.
- [ ] I know a stay must be requested before arguing the merits.
- [ ] I know the short period to apply to set aside an award.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes for negotiation, mediation and signing a settlement in your own name. You may appear for yourself in arbitration; only a lawyer may represent someone else before an arbitrator (Bar Association Law s. 20(1)). Drafting a settlement for someone else is reserved to lawyers (s. 20(3)).

### Where and how

- Mediation-intake: appointed by the court's mediation unit (reg. 37); further mediation with a mediator from the courts' list (Net HaMishpat home page, "רשימת מגשרים חיצונים") or privately.
- Settlement approval: a joint request to the court in your case, or under s. 79ג(ח) to the competent court if no claim was filed.
- Set-aside period for an award, mediation thresholds: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md). Free help: community mediation centres in some municipalities (ask the municipality), [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You are asked to sign an arbitration agreement for a large or long-term contract (building, business partnership).
- An award has been given against you and you think there are grounds under s. 24: the period is short.
- The settlement includes waivers of statutory rights (employment, consumer) or land.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Filing a full defence, then asking for a stay to arbitration | Stay refused (s. 5(ב)) | Argue the case in court | Decide on arbitration before your first answer on the merits |
| Signing a clause where one side appoints the arbitrator | Partial arbitrator; narrow review | Argue invalidity or unfairness early | Insist on agreed or institutional appointment |
| Assuming an award can be appealed on the merits | Mistaken award stands | Check s. 24 grounds; act within the period | Agree on an appeal (ss. 21א, 29ב) when drafting |
| Settlement signed but not approved by the court | Must sue on it as a contract if broken | Ask the court to give it force of judgment | Include the request in the settlement |
| Repeating a mediation admission in court | Inadmissible (s. 79ג(ד)) and damages trust | Rely on independent evidence | Treat mediation statements as off the record |

## Review

- Mediation is voluntary; the court may refer with consent; what is said is not evidence; a mediated settlement can get the force of a judgment (s. 79ג).
- The court may decide by compromise with consent or approve a settlement as a judgment (s. 79א).
- An arbitration agreement must be in writing; the First Schedule fills gaps, including that the arbitrator is not bound by law unless agreed (ss. 1–2).
- A court claim is stayed for arbitration if requested in time (s. 5); an award binds and, once confirmed, is enforced like a judgment (ss. 21, 23).
- Setting aside is limited to the closed list in s. 24 and the short period in s. 27.
