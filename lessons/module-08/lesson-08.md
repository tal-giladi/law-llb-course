# 08.8 · Practice lab: a pre-action letter and opening a case on Net HaMishpat (dry run)

In this lab you take one money dispute of your own from the last letter to the moment before you press "submit": you write the pre-action letter, assemble the filing file, and walk through Net HaMishpat and the Judiciary's small-claims service as far as you can without filing. It is a dry run; you send the letter and file only if you decide to, in your own name.

## Why it matters

Self-represented claims fail most often in the administrative middle: a letter with no deadline, a claim filed against the wrong company name, photos that will not upload, a fee exemption request forgotten, a national digital ID never set up. None of these is legal doctrine; all of them cost weeks. This lab turns lessons 08.2 and 08.3 into a checked, ready file.

## The law

### The letter is optional; conduct counts

The 2018 Regulations open a case with a statement of claim (reg. 9(א)) and do not require a letter; costs reflect the parties' conduct (reg. 151(א)). See lesson 08.2 for when other laws do require prior notice. ([Regulations](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

### Filing and service for an unrepresented party

A party not represented by a lawyer files with the registry, which serves the document (reg. 160); electronic filing to the court is through Net HaMishpat (reg. 161(1)(ב)). A small claim follows regs 2–3 of the Small Claims Regulations (venue and contents, lesson 04.9) and pays the fee in reg. 6 ([Small Claims Regulations](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיפוט_בתביעות_קטנות_(סדרי_דין)); amounts in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).

## How it works in practice

What the course found on 17 October 2026:

- **Net HaMishpat home page.** Entries for case search, court diaries, decisions, voucher payment and advance payment of a request fee; "פתיחת תיק או ביצוע פעולות בתיק" with login by smart card or national digital ID; a separate "הגשת תביעה קטנה" entry; a page "תיקים שלא ניתן להגיש בהם תביעה מרחוק" (cases in which a claim cannot be filed remotely); a user guide; technical support by phone and WhatsApp. Notices on the page reported intermittent problems with smart-card login (use the national ID) and with document downloads.
- **Small claims online.** Computer, not phone; national digital ID; personal details, grounds, evidence as PDF files up to a total size; fee online; audio and video on USB drives delivered to the registry.
- **Regular civil claim online, unrepresented.** Not confirmed. The Judiciary's service pages for ordinary-procedure and fast-track claims are written for lawyers; a 2022 State Comptroller report found that unrepresented parties could act in existing cases but not open new ones. Ask the information centre (numbers in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)); if online opening is not available to you, file at the registry.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

## Worked example

Hadas Peretz, a physiotherapist in Haifa, treated a sports club's players for three months under a written agreement. The club, "מועדון ספורט הכרמל (ע״ר)", an association, paid ₪6,000 of ₪13,500 and stopped answering.

1. **Who is the debtor?** She searches the association's registration number in the Registrar of Associations' public search and uses the exact registered name.
2. **Letter.** On the [pre-action letter template](../../templates/pre-action-letter.md): agreement (exhibit A), treatment logs (B), invoices (C), her earlier email (D); amount ₪7,500; deadline 14 days; the court she will sue in (small claims in Haifa, the defendant's area); an offer to talk. Sent by email and registered mail; she photographs the receipt.
3. **No payment by the deadline.** She prepares the small claim: five PDFs, total size checked; a one-page summary of the facts; documents and witnesses lists; the declaration; the number of small claims she filed this year (none).
4. **Dry run online.** She logs in with the national digital ID on her laptop, opens the small-claims service, fills in every screen up to the payment page, and saves screenshots of what each screen asked. She stops there to decide.
5. **Decision.** The club calls her and offers ₪6,500 in two instalments. She asks for ₪7,000 with a default clause (lesson 08.6) and a written settlement; the club agrees.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A money dispute of your own up to a few thousand shekels (unpaid work, a refund, a deposit), or Hadas's case above.
- The [pre-action letter template](../../templates/pre-action-letter.md); the [small claim template](../../templates/small-claim-property-damage.md) from lesson 04.9.
- A computer with a PDF scanner app or printer-scanner; your national digital ID set up (allow time for registration; help via the information centre).
- About two and a half hours, in three sittings.

### Steps

**Part A — the letter (60 min)**

1. **Facts file (15 min).** Write the facts as dated, numbered sentences. Give each a document and an exhibit letter.
2. **Debtor's identity (10 min).** Find the exact name and ID or registration number (contract, invoice, Companies Registrar, Registrar of Associations). Write the registered address.
3. **Amount (10 min).** Itemise the principal. Do not add penalties you cannot justify; note linkage and interest as "according to law".
4. **Draft (20 min).** Fill in the [template](../../templates/pre-action-letter.md). Name the court you would actually sue in, using lesson 08.3. Set a dated deadline.
5. **Check (5 min).** Run the checklist at the end of the template. Read the letter aloud: no insults, no threats of a police complaint, nothing you cannot prove.

**Part B — the filing file (45 min)**

6. **Choose the court (5 min).** Small claims or magistrates' court; venue under reg. 2 (small claims) or reg. 7 (civil). Write one sentence why.
7. **Documents (15 min).** Scan each exhibit to PDF, name the files "נספח א – הסכם.pdf" and so on, check the total size, and put audio or video on two USB drives with a transcript.
8. **Fee (10 min).** Compute the fee from [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md). If you cannot pay, download the fee-exemption form linked from the small-claims guidance and list the income and asset documents it needs.
9. **Draft the claim (15 min).** For small claims, complete the template within five pages; for the magistrates' court, a heading (reg. 10) and a one-page summary (reg. 11).

**Part C — the dry run online (45 min)**

10. **Home page tour (10 min).** Open [Net HaMishpat](https://www.court.gov.il/NGCS.Web.Site/HomePage.aspx). Write down: the login options, the small-claims entry, the list of case types that cannot be filed remotely, the support numbers and today's system notices.
11. **Log in (10 min).** Log in with the national digital ID. Note what the personal area shows (your cases, if any) and what "open a case" offers you as an unrepresented user today.
12. **Small-claims form (20 min).** If your claim is a small claim, open the online service and fill in each screen up to, but not including, payment and submission. Take a screenshot of every screen and note every field you could not answer.
13. **Phone check (5 min).** If you want a regular civil claim, call the information centre and ask whether an unrepresented plaintiff can open that case type online today. Write down the answer, the date and the time.

**Decision**

14. Write one paragraph: send the letter or not; if no payment, file or not; which court; and the dates you diarised.

### How you know it worked

- The letter names the right debtor with its number, itemises the amount, has a dated deadline and says which court you would sue in.
- Every exhibit is a correctly named PDF, and you know the total size.
- You have screenshots of every online screen up to payment and a list of the fields you could not fill.
- You know, from the system or the information centre, whether you can open your case type online.

### Self-check

- [ ] I am acting in my own name about my own claim.
- [ ] My letter contains no threat of a criminal complaint.
- [ ] I kept proof of delivery of the letter.
- [ ] I stopped before payment and submission (dry run), or filed deliberately in my own name.
- [ ] I diarised the letter deadline and, if I file, the service check 60 days before the hearing.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Writing your own letter and filing your own claim are exactly what reg. 170 and the small-claims system allow. Writing such a letter or claim for someone else as an occupation or for pay is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20(3)).

### Where and how

- Net HaMishpat and the Judiciary's small-claims service (national digital ID); the court registry for paper filing.
- Information centre and technical support numbers: Net HaMishpat home page and [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md). Volunteer help desks at some courts; [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The debtor answers the letter through a lawyer with a legal defence you cannot assess (limitation, set-off, an arbitration clause).
- The amount is above the small-claims ceiling and the dispute is document-heavy.
- You are not sure who the right defendant is (a group of companies, a franchise, a dissolved company).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Letter to a trade name, not the legal entity | Claim against a non-existent party | Find the registered name and send again | Check the Registrar before writing |
| "Pay immediately or else" with no date | No clear point at which to sue | Send a short follow-up with a date | Always give a dated deadline |
| Threatening a police complaint to get paid | Possible offence of threats or extortion; credibility lost | Withdraw the threat in writing | Keep civil demands civil |
| Photos as phone images, files too large | Upload fails at the last screen | Convert and compress to PDF | Prepare PDFs in Part B |
| No national digital ID | Cannot log in on filing day | Register; meanwhile file on paper | Set it up a week ahead |
| Assuming online opening is available for every case type | Wasted time; deadline pressure | File at the registry | Check the "cannot be filed remotely" list and call |

## Review

- A pre-action letter is optional but powerful: exact debtor, itemised amount, dated deadline, named court, proof of delivery.
- Prepare the filing file before you go online: court, PDFs, fee or exemption, login.
- Small claims can be filed online with the national digital ID; whether an unrepresented party can open other case types online must be checked on the day.
- A dry run means stopping before payment and submission and recording what the system asks.
