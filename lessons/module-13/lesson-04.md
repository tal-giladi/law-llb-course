# 13.4 · The caveat (הערת אזהרה): what it protects and how it is registered

By the end of this lesson you will know exactly what a caveat does to the seller, to a later buyer, to a bank and to a creditor; who may ask for one; which documents and signatures the registry requires; and how to remove it. You will prepare a full caveat request file for a real or supplied deal.

## Why it matters

Lesson 13.3 showed that a buyer who has signed but not registered is exposed, and that a buyer who fails to register a caveat may even lose priority (Ganz). The caveat is the cheap, fast fix: a short note in the register that blocks any conflicting registration and survives the seller's creditors and bankruptcy. It costs a small registry fee and can be done without a lawyer by signing before the registrar. It is also used beyond purchases: for a lender who has a promise of a mortgage, for co-owners who agree not to sell, for a tenant under a long lease.

## The law

### When the registrar registers a caveat

> הוכח להנחת דעתו של הרשם כי בעל מקרקעין, או בעל זכות שכירות, שאילה, זיקת הנאה, זכות קדימה או משכנתה, התחייב בכתב לעשות בהם עסקה, או להימנע מעשות בהם עסקה, ירשום הרשם, על פי בקשת מי שהתחייב או מי שזכאי לפי ההתחייבות (להלן – הזכאי), הערה על כך;

*Gist:* If the registrar is satisfied that the holder of a registered right undertook in writing to make, or to refrain from, a transaction, he registers a caveat at the request of either the obligor or the beneficiary; the undertaking may be in any document, express or implied, absolute or conditional. [Land Law s. 126(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המקרקעין#סעיף_126)

If only the beneficiary applies, the registrar must notify the obligor at once (s. 126(ב)).

### What it does

> (א) נרשמה הערת אזהרה וכל עוד לא נמחקה, לא תירשם עסקה הסותרת את תוכן ההערה, אלא בהסכמת הזכאי או לפי צו בית המשפט.

*Gist:* While the caveat stands, no transaction that contradicts it is registered without the beneficiary's consent or a court order. [Land Law s. 127(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המקרקעין#סעיף_127)

> (ב) נרשמה הערת אזהרה ולאחר מכן הוטל עיקול על המקרקעין [...] או שניתן צו לקבלת נכסים בפשיטת רגל או צו פירוק נגד בעל המקרקעין [...] הרי כל עוד לא נמחקה ההערה, לא יהיה בכל אלה כדי לפגוע בזכויות הזכאי הנובעות מההתחייבות נשוא ההערה [...]

*Gist:* A later attachment, bankruptcy, liquidation or receiver does not harm the beneficiary's rights under the noted undertaking, though defences that would have applied had the deal been performed on time still apply. [Land Law s. 127(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המקרקעין#סעיף_127)

A caveat registered at the Land Registry needs no registration in any other register (s. 127(ב1)).

### What a caveat does not do

It does not make the beneficiary an owner, does not cure a void contract, and does not rank ahead of rights already registered before it (an existing mortgage stays first). It protects only what the undertaking says: a caveat for "sale of sub-parcel 12" does not protect a promised parking space that is a separate sub-parcel.

### Deletion

> (א) הערה שנרשמה לפי סעיפים 126, 128 או 129, תימחק על פי צו של בית משפט או אם הוכח, להנחת דעתו של הרשם, אחד מאלה:
> (1) הצדדים המעוניינים הסכימו למחיקה;
> (2) עילת ההערה בטלה, ובלבד שהרשם הודיע מראש לצדדים המעוניינים על כוונתו למחוק את ההערה ונתן להם זמן סביר לפנות לבית המשפט;

*Gist:* A caveat is deleted by court order, by consent of the interested parties, or when its basis has lapsed after notice to them. [Land Law s. 132(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המקרקעין#סעיף_132)

### The procedure: who signs and before whom

> רישום הערת אזהרה לפי סעיף 126 לחוק יהיה על פי בקשה חתומה על ידי המתחייב או הזכאי, המאומתת בידי עורך דין או רשם, בדרך שמאמתים שטר עסקה;

*Gist:* The request is signed by the obligor or the beneficiary, and the signature is verified by **a lawyer or the registrar**, as for a deed; it is filed at the office where the land is registered. [Management and Registration Regulations reg. 21(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_המקרקעין_(ניהול_ורישום))

Attachments (reg. 21(ב)): the written undertaking (for a joint request the commissioner may waive it); if the obligor is not the registered owner, the owner's verified **consent**; and identity documents of everyone who signed (company documents for a company). Deletion by consent needs a request verified the same way (reg. 22(א)). The fees for registering and deleting a caveat are in the fee regulations, item 13 (see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).

Related notes: a note on the need for a third party's consent (s. 128), on a restriction of legal capacity (s. 129) and a note ordered by a court in a land case (s. 130), all of which block contradicting transactions (s. 131).

## Leading cases

### Key text: Ganz and Aharonov, revisited

The two rulings in [lesson 13.3](lesson-03.md) define the caveat's place in Israeli law. Ganz (ע״א 2643/97) treats a long, unexplained failure to register one as possible bad faith that can cost the first buyer priority; Aharonov (ע״א 189/95) protects a paying buyer against a later attaching creditor even without one, but says the buyer's conduct counts. Together they mean: the caveat is legally optional, practically essential. ⚠️ Both full texts: search the case numbers on [the Judiciary's decision search](https://supremedecisions.court.gov.il) (unreachable on 17 October 2026).

### Key text: s. 127(ב) and insolvency

Section 127(ב) makes the caveat the buyer's shield in the seller's insolvency, with one reservation: a claim to set aside the deal as a preference or a fraudulent transfer "that would have stood had the transaction been performed on time" still stands. The Insolvency and Economic Rehabilitation Law 2018 is covered in year 3 (module 28).

## How it works in practice

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Routes to registration on 17 October 2026

| Route | Who signs | Verification | Filing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Through a lawyer (the usual way) | Buyer or seller | The lawyer verifies (reg. 21(א)) | Online by the lawyer (Ministry of Justice system for lawyers) |
| Before the registrar | You, in person, with original ID | The registrar verifies | At the Land Registry office where the land is registered, by appointment (⚠️ manual check of booking rules) |
| Ministry of Justice online service | See the service page | Lawyer's verification is generally needed for consent documents | [gov.il caveat service](https://www.gov.il/he/service/caveat_filling) (⚠️ manual check whether a private person can complete it end to end) |

### The usual cases

- **Buyer of a registered flat:** caveat for "התחייבות למכירת זכויות" on the sub-parcel. Most sale contracts oblige the seller to sign the request or an irrevocable power of attorney for it.
- **Buyer from a developer:** the Sale (Apartments) Law (module 20) and the contract require the developer to register a caveat in the buyer's favour, often on the whole parcel until the condominium is registered.
- **Lender promised a mortgage:** caveat for "התחייבות לרישום משכנתה" until the mortgage itself is registered.
- **Co-owners or spouses agreeing not to sell:** caveat for an undertaking "to refrain from a transaction".
- **Israel Land Authority land not registered at the registry:** a caveat cannot be registered there; the equivalent note is recorded at the ILA (⚠️ manual check of the ILA procedure).

## Worked example

Michal Azoulay, a teacher from Sderot, signs a contract on 3 September 2026 to buy a two-room flat in Ashkelon from Yigal Moran for ₪1.1 million. The flat is sub-parcel 9 of a registered condominium. Her lawyer handles the deal, but she wants to understand and check the caveat herself. The extract shows Moran as sole owner and a mortgage to Bank Leumi.

1. **Basis.** The signed contract is a written undertaking to transfer ownership (s. 126(א)).
2. **Applicant.** Either Moran or Michal may apply; the contract says Moran signs on signing day.
3. **Verification.** Her lawyer verifies Moran's signature (reg. 21(א)). Had she no lawyer, both could sign before the registrar at the Ashkelon office.
4. **Attachments.** The contract (or the commissioner's waiver for a joint request), ID copies, the fee receipt.
5. **Rank.** The bank's mortgage is registered first and stays first. The contract provides that part of the price goes directly to the bank to discharge it; Michal checks the bank's confirmation of the payoff amount (מכתב כוונות).
6. **Follow-up.** Three working days later Michal orders a fresh extract: the caveat appears with her name and the date. On the transfer, the caveat is deleted at the same time as her ownership is registered.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A deal of your own (a purchase, a promise of a mortgage to you, a co-owners' undertaking), or the Azoulay case.
- A current extract of the property (lesson 13.2).
- The [caveat request checklist (draft)](../../templates/caveat-request-checklist.md).
- About an hour.

### Steps

1. **Check the property (10 min).** From the extract: registered owner(s), sub-parcel, existing mortgages, caveats, attachments, notes. Is the obligor the registered owner? If not, whose consent is needed (reg. 21(ב)(2))?
2. **Find the undertaking (10 min).** Quote the clause that obliges the owner to make, or refrain from, a transaction. Is it signed? Does it identify the property?
3. **Choose the caveat type (5 min).** Sale, mortgage, refraining from a transaction, or s. 128 consent.
4. **Fill in the checklist (20 min).** Parties with ID numbers; block, parcel, sub-parcel; the type and wording of the undertaking; attachments; how the signature will be verified (lawyer or registrar); the fee.
5. **Plan the route (5 min).** Lawyer, registrar appointment, or the online service; note what you must bring (original ID).
6. **Plan the end (10 min).** When and how the caveat will be deleted: on registration of the transfer, on cancellation by consent (reg. 22), or by court order. Write who signs the deletion request.
7. **Real or dry run.** If you are the buyer in a real deal, and the contract allows, file it in your own name. Otherwise stop before filing.

### How you know it worked

- The checklist names the exact undertaking, the right parcel and sub-parcel, and every attachment.
- You know who will verify the signatures.
- After a real filing, a new extract shows the caveat with the right beneficiary and description.

- [ ] I know who may apply (s. 126(א)) and that the obligor is notified if only I apply (s. 126(ב)).
- [ ] I know a caveat does not outrank rights registered before it.
- [ ] I know the three ways a caveat is deleted (s. 132).

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Partly. The law lets the obligor or the beneficiary apply (s. 126(א)), and the regulations let the **registrar** verify the signature instead of a lawyer (reg. 21(א)). In practice a private person signs before the registrar at the office in person; online filing is designed for lawyers, and the online service for private persons may still need a lawyer-verified consent (⚠️ manual check). Representing another person before the registry is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20(2)).

### Where and how

- The Land Registry office where the property is registered; bring original ID and the original undertaking.
- Fees for registering and deleting a caveat: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- After filing: order a new extract to confirm; the Ministry of Justice also offers an alert service for activity on a property (⚠️ manual check: "שירות התראות על פעולות בנכס").
- Free help: Kol-Zchut guides; [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- You are buying, selling or mortgaging: the whole transaction, not only the caveat, needs a lawyer.
- The registrar refuses the caveat, or the owner objects or applies to delete it.
- The extract shows an earlier caveat, attachment or receiver.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Caveat on the wrong sub-parcel | The flat is unprotected | File a correct request; ask to delete the wrong one | Copy the details from a fresh extract |
| No caveat for the parking space or storage room that is a separate sub-parcel | That unit can be sold or attached | Add a caveat for it | List every unit in the contract and in the request |
| Leaving a caveat after the deal is cancelled | The owner's claim for deletion and costs | Sign a deletion request (reg. 22) | Agree in the contract how and when it is deleted |
| Assuming the caveat beats an earlier mortgage | Paying a seller whose bank comes first | Arrange direct payoff to the bank | Read the extract's mortgage section |
| Unverified signature or missing owner's consent | Request returned | Re-sign before the registrar or a lawyer | Check reg. 21(ב) before going |

## Review

- A caveat records a written undertaking to make or refrain from a transaction (s. 126) and blocks contradicting registrations without the beneficiary's consent or a court order (s. 127(א)).
- It protects against later attachments, bankruptcy, liquidation and receivers (s. 127(ב)), but not against rights registered before it.
- The obligor or the beneficiary may apply; signatures are verified by a lawyer or the registrar (reg. 21).
- It is deleted by consent, by lapse of its basis after notice, or by court order (s. 132).
- Register it the day the undertaking is signed; plan its deletion at the same time.
