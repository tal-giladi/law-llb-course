# 13.9 · Practice lab: read a land-registry extract and check an apartment before buying

In this lab you read a land-registry extract line by line, run the checks a careful buyer makes before signing, and turn what you find into questions and contract conditions. You work on a supplied extract and, if you can, on a real flat: your own, a family member's, or one you are considering. It is a dry run; you will not sign anything.

## Why it matters

Lessons 13.2–13.6 gave you the law: what the register proves, how priority works, what a caveat does, what is attached to a flat. In practice the buyer's protection depends on an hour of careful reading before the first payment. Most disasters (a flat with a bank mortgage the seller cannot pay off, a parking space that is not attached, a seller who is one of three heirs, an attachment registered last week) are visible on the extract or in the condominium file. Your lawyer will do this too, but a buyer who understands the extract asks the right questions and is not rushed.

## The law

Anyone may get an extract (Land Law s. 124; reg. 92); the online signed extract is the evidence, not its printout (reg. 92(ג)). For settled land the register is conclusive and a good-faith buyer may rely on it (ss. 10, 125). A caveat blocks contradicting registrations and survives attachments and insolvency (s. 127). Attachments to a flat are recorded in the condominium register (s. 55(ג)). Full text: [Land Law](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המקרקעין); fees: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

## Leading cases

### Key text: lessons 13.3 and 13.4

The rulings that matter here are in lessons [13.3](lesson-03.md) and [13.4](lesson-04.md): Ganz (a buyer who does not register a caveat may lose priority) and Aharonov (a paying buyer against an attaching creditor). Both point to the same practical rule: check the extract the day you sign and the day you pay, and register a caveat at once.

## How it works in practice

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### The sources a careful buyer checks

| Source | What it shows | How to get it |
|---|---|---|
| Current extract of the sub-parcel | Owners and shares, leases, mortgages, caveats, attachments, other notes | Ministry of Justice online service or a registry office |
| Concentrated extract and condominium file (registration order, bylaws, plan) | Each flat's attachments and share; the bylaws | Online (concentrated extract) and the registry (file documents) |
| Israel Land Authority confirmation | Rights not registered at the registry; lease terms | ILA online services or office (⚠️ manual check) |
| Municipality: building file and arnona | Permits, unauthorised building, debts, classification | Municipal engineering department; arnona office |
| Planning information | Approved and pending plans, TAMA 38, expropriations | Local planning committee; national planning portal (⚠️ manual check) |
| The house committee | Arrears, planned works, disputes before the superintendent | Ask in writing |

### The red flags on an extract

- The seller is not the only registered owner, or his share is less than the whole.
- A mortgage in favour of a bank or a private lender.
- A caveat in favour of someone other than your buyer-to-be (a previous buyer, a lender).
- Attachment notes (עיקול) from the Execution Office or a court; a receiver (כונס נכסים); a note on restricted capacity (s. 129) or the need for consent (s. 128).
- Inheritance not registered: the registered owner died; the heirs have not registered.
- The parking or storage you are buying is not listed as an attachment, or is a separate sub-parcel.
- The deed history (historical extract) shows rapid resales at odd prices or a recent gift between strangers.

## Worked example

Below is a learning sample (not a real extract) for a flat in Haifa. Read it before the steps.

```text
נסח רישום מקרקעין – דוגמה לימודית בלבד
לשכת רישום: חיפה          גוש: 10XXX   חלקה: 45   תת חלקה: 12
תיאור: בית משותף, 4 קומות, 16 דירות
החלק ברכוש המשותף: 6/100
הצמדות: מחסן (ס), שטח 6 מ"ר, סימון במפה: 12א
        חניה (ח), סימון במפה: 12ב

בעלויות:
  שטר 5512/2009/1  מכר  בעלים: כהן אברהם ת"ז 0XXXXXXX  חלק בנכס: 1/2
  שטר 5512/2009/1  מכר  בעלים: כהן מרים ת"ז 0XXXXXXX   חלק בנכס: 1/2

משכנתאות:
  שטר 5513/2009/1  משכנתא  לטובת: בנק דוגמה בע"מ  דרגה: ראשונה  סכום: ללא הגבלת סכום

הערות:
  שטר 2201/2026/1  הערת אזהרה סעיף 126  לטובת: לוי דנה ת"ז 0XXXXXXX  על: הבעלות
  שטר 3310/2026/1  עיקול לפי צו הוצאה לפועל  תיק 5-XX-XXXXX-XX  על: חלקו של כהן אברהם
```

1. **Owners.** Avraham and Miriam Cohen, half each. Both must sign the sale; if only Avraham offered the flat, ask why.
2. **Mortgage.** A first-rank "all-sums" mortgage to a bank from 2009. The seller must produce a payoff letter (מכתב כוונות) and part of the price must go directly to the bank.
3. **Caveat for Dana Levi, 2026.** Someone else already has a registered undertaking for the ownership. Is that an earlier buyer? Your later caveat will rank after hers (s. 127(א)); do not proceed until it is explained and deleted with her consent or by court order (s. 132).
4. **Execution attachment on Avraham's half, 2026.** A creditor of Avraham attached his share. Under s. 127(ב) Dana's earlier caveat may protect her against it; you have no such protection. The attachment must be lifted before you pay.
5. **Attachments.** A storage room and a parking space are attached to sub-parcel 12: good. Check on the plan that "12ב" is the space the seller showed you.
6. **Conclusion.** This flat cannot be bought safely until the earlier caveat and the attachment are cleared and the bank's payoff is arranged.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- The sample above, and if possible a real current extract (and a concentrated extract) of a flat you know; the fee is in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- The [apartment purchase check (draft)](../../templates/apartment-purchase-check.md) and the [caveat request checklist (draft)](../../templates/caveat-request-checklist.md).
- About two and a half hours.

### Steps

**Part A — the extract (60 min)**

1. **Header (5 min).** Office, block, parcel, sub-parcel, description, share in the common property. Check that they match the seller's documents and the address.
2. **Ownership (10 min).** List owners, shares, deed types and dates. Is the seller the sole owner? Was the last deed a sale, a gift or an inheritance?
3. **Leases (5 min).** Any lease? For Israel Land Authority land, who is the lessor and to when?
4. **Mortgages (10 min).** Each mortgage: lender, rank, amount. Write how each will be paid off at closing.
5. **Notes (15 min).** Each caveat, attachment, receiver or other note: in whose favour, on what, and what must happen before you pay.
6. **Attachments (10 min).** Roof, yard, parking, storage: listed? On the plan? Matching what you were shown?
7. **History (5 min, optional).** A historical extract: any suspicious sequence of deeds?

**Part B — beyond the extract (45 min)**

8. **Condominium file (10 min).** Registered bylaws or model? Anything unusual on expenses or use?
9. **Municipality and planning (15 min).** List the questions for the building file (permits for any extension, enclosed balconies), arnona debt, and planning (TAMA 38, expropriation, a planned road).
10. **House committee (10 min).** Write the questions: arrears of the flat, planned works and special levies, pending superintendent cases.
11. **ILA (10 min).** If relevant: what rights confirmation you need, and whether the lease is capitalised.

**Part C — decisions (45 min)**

12. **Red-flag table (15 min).** For each finding: risk, what the seller must provide, what the contract must say.
13. **Contract conditions (15 min).** Draft three to five clauses in Hebrew: direct payoff to the bank; deletion of a foreign caveat or attachment before the second payment; the buyer's caveat on signing and its deletion if the deal ends; inclusion of named attachments.
14. **Caveat plan (10 min).** Fill in the caveat checklist for your hypothetical purchase.
15. **Go or no-go (5 min).** One paragraph: would you sign now, sign with conditions, or walk away?

### How you know it worked

- Every line of the extract has a meaning and an action next to it.
- Your red-flag table names who must cure each problem and by when.
- Your contract clauses connect payments to cures (no payment before the attachment is lifted).
- You reached a reasoned go or no-go decision.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Partly. Ordering and reading extracts, asking questions and planning are yours to do (s. 124). The purchase itself, the deed and the online filing need a lawyer in practice; you may sign a caveat request before the registrar (reg. 21). Checking a flat for someone else as a paid service, or advising them, is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).

### Where and how

- Extracts: the Ministry of Justice online service or any registry office; fees in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Municipality and planning files: the local authority's engineering department; arnona office.
- Free help: Kol-Zchut's guides on buying a flat (⚠️ manual check); [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- Always, before you sign a memorandum or contract to buy a flat.
- The extract shows any caveat, attachment, receiver or unregistered inheritance.
- The seller's rights are not registered at the registry.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reading only the ownership section | Missing a mortgage or attachment | Read the whole extract | Use the checklist line by line |
| Old extract (from the seller or agent) | Missing new notes | Order a fresh one | Order your own, the same day |
| Parking "comes with the flat" by word of mouth | No right in the space | Have it attached or bought | Check attachments and plan |
| Paying before a foreign caveat is deleted | Your money is at risk (s. 127(א)) | Stop payments; get advice | Link payments to cures in the contract |
| Ignoring an attachment on one co-owner's share | The creditor can block or sell that share | Require lifting before payment | Read the notes section carefully |

## Review

- An extract has a header, ownership, leases, mortgages, notes and attachments: read all of them.
- Every mortgage, foreign caveat and attachment must be cured, and the contract must tie payments to the cures.
- Check beyond the extract: the condominium file, the municipality, planning, the house committee, the ILA.
- Order your own fresh extract on the day you sign and on the day you pay; register your caveat at once.
