# 13.10 · Practice lab: a condominium dispute before the superintendent

In this lab you take a building dispute from the first letter to a filed-ready claim before the superintendent of land registration: you collect the bylaws and the decisions, write the demand, draft the claim with the particulars the regulations require, and plan the hearing, enforcement and appeal. Work on a real dispute in your own building if you have one, or on the supplied case. Filing is your own decision; the default is a dry run.

## Why it matters

The superintendent is the forum most flat owners will actually meet: unpaid house-committee fees, a neighbour who took the roof or closed the stairwell, a dispute over how a renovation is split, a committee that does not function. Proceedings are simpler than in court (the superintendent may depart from the rules of evidence and procedure, s. 75(א)), and the decision is enforced like a magistrates' court judgment (s. 76). A well-prepared owner with the bylaws, the decisions book and photographs often wins without a lawyer.

## The law

The superintendent decides disputes about the bylaws and expenses, and may decide trespass disputes between owners if the claimant chooses (s. 72); owners, other parties and the committee may sue (s. 73); he has a magistrates' judge's powers (s. 74), may relax evidence and procedure with reasons and gives reasoned written decisions (s. 75); his decisions and interim orders are enforced like a magistrates' court's (s. 76); appeal lies to the district court, which does not stay execution unless ordered (s. 77). The regulations apply civil procedure with adaptations (reg. 78), require filing with the superintendent for the land office where the building is or will be registered (reg. 79), and add to the usual particulars the building's address, block and parcel, number of buildings, flats in each and entrances (reg. 80); for an unregistered building with agreed bylaws, attach them (reg. 81); the appeal period is in reg. 83. Fees follow the magistrates' court fees for civil claims (fee regulations). Details: lesson [13.6](lesson-06.md); periods and fees: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

## Leading cases

### Key text: Roker v. Solomon and the model bylaws

For trespass on common property, [Roker v. Solomon](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/roker-v-solomon) (lesson 13.1, 13.6) is the authority: consent first, permit irrelevant. For expenses, s. 58(א) and the model bylaws in the Schedule to the Land Law. Superintendents' decisions are published on the Ministry of Justice site and in free databases inconsistently; we did not cite one.

## How it works in practice

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### The route

1. **Gather:** the concentrated extract, the registered bylaws (or the model bylaws), the plan, the decisions book entries, invoices and the budget, photographs with dates.
2. **Demand letter** to the neighbour or committee, with a deadline ([pre-action letter template](../../templates/pre-action-letter.md)).
3. **Claim** on the superintendent's form, or as a statement of claim with the reg. 80 particulars; attach exhibits; pay the fee.
4. **Filing** at the superintendent's regional office, by the method the office accepts (⚠️ manual check of current online filing for unrepresented parties on the [service page](https://www.gov.il/he/service/condominiums-supervisor-claims)).
5. **Defence and hearing:** the defendant files a defence; the superintendent may hold a preliminary hearing, visit the building, and push for a settlement.
6. **Decision:** written and reasoned (s. 75(ג)).
7. **Enforcement:** an Execution Office file like a judgment (s. 76; [lesson 08.9](../module-08/lesson-09.md)).
8. **Appeal:** to the district court within the reg. 83 period from service of the decision.

## Worked example

**Supplied case.** A six-storey building of 18 flats in Bat Yam, registered as a condominium with the model bylaws. In 2025 the general meeting (11 flats present, minutes in the decisions book) decided to waterproof the roof for ₪54,000, split by floor area. Flat 18, on the top floor (90 m², of a total 1,620 m²), is owned by Igor Rabinovich, who refused to pay, saying "the roof is attached to my flat, so it's my business". The extract shows no attachment of the roof. Igor has also put a locked gate on the roof stairwell, so the technician could not reach the water tanks. The committee (Ayelet Mor, Hassan Jaber, Shira Biton) wants payment and the gate removed.

1. **Money.** Waterproofing is proper maintenance (s. 58(א)); the roof is common property (no attachment, s. 55(ג)); Igor's share is 90/1,620 × ₪54,000 = ₪3,000.
2. **Gate.** Closing access to common property is trespass by an owner (s. 72(ב)): the committee may choose the superintendent or a court; the superintendent can hear both parts together.
3. **Plaintiff.** The committee (s. 73(2)), acting as the owners' agent (s. 69).
4. **Remedies.** Payment of ₪3,000 with linkage and interest; an order to remove the gate and give the committee keys; an interim order for access if the tanks need urgent work.
5. **Forum and filing.** The superintendent for the Tel Aviv land office region (reg. 79); particulars under reg. 80; fee as a magistrates' civil claim.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A real dispute in your building, or the Bat Yam case.
- The concentrated extract, the bylaws (or model bylaws), the decisions book or minutes, invoices, photographs.
- The [superintendent claim template (draft)](../../templates/superintendent-claim.md) and the [pre-action letter](../../templates/pre-action-letter.md).
- About two and a half hours.

### Steps

**Part A — the file (45 min)**

1. **Status (5 min).** Registered condominium or not (s. 77א)? Which land office?
2. **Rules (15 min).** Copy the bylaw or section that governs the dispute (expenses: s. 58 and any bylaw ratio; use of common property: s. 55, bylaws; meetings: the model bylaws).
3. **Decision (10 min).** Find the decision in the decisions book: date, attendance, majority, wording. Was it adopted under the bylaws (s. 71(א))?
4. **Evidence (15 min).** Number the exhibits: extract, bylaws, minutes, invoices, letters, photographs.

**Part B — the demand (20 min)**

5. Write a one-page demand in Hebrew: facts, the rule, the amount or act demanded, a 14-day deadline, and that otherwise the committee or you will sue the superintendent.

**Part C — the claim (60 min)**

6. **Heading (10 min).** Superintendent's office; the parties with ID numbers and addresses; the building's address, block, parcel, number of buildings, flats per building and entrances (reg. 80).
7. **Facts (20 min).** Short numbered paragraphs, each with an exhibit.
8. **Law (10 min).** The sections and bylaws; why the superintendent has jurisdiction (s. 72(א) or (ב)).
9. **Remedies (10 min).** The exact sum and its calculation; the exact order (remove the gate within 14 days; give keys to the committee).
10. **Interim relief (5 min).** If urgent, a short request for an interim order with the reason for urgency.
11. **Fee and filing (5 min).** Compute the fee from [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); plan the filing method.

**Part D — after filing (25 min)**

12. **Hearing kit (10 min).** Three points you must prove, the documents for each, and a settlement figure ([hearing-day kit](../../templates/hearing-day-kit.md)).
13. **Enforcement (10 min).** If you win: opening an execution file (lesson 08.9).
14. **Appeal (5 min).** The appeal period and the court; whether you would ask for a stay.

### How you know it worked

- Your claim has every reg. 80 particular and a numbered exhibit for each fact.
- The remedy is a specific sum with its calculation, or a specific act by a date.
- You stated why the superintendent, not a court, decides (or why you chose him under s. 72(ב)).
- You have a date for the appeal period and an enforcement plan.

### Self-check

- [ ] I sent, or drafted, a demand before the claim.
- [ ] I computed the share by floor area or the bylaws, not equally.
- [ ] I know the committee may sue in its own name (s. 73(2)).
- [ ] I know that filing an appeal does not stay the decision unless ordered (s. 77(ב)).

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes, as an owner or the committee's party in your own name (s. 73; reg. 78 applying civil procedure, including the right to act for yourself). Representing another owner as an occupation or for pay is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20(1)). Whether a non-lawyer committee member may conduct the case for the committee: Unverified (⚠️ check with the superintendent's office).

### Where and how

- The superintendent's office for the land office region of the building (reg. 79); forms and filing on the [Ministry of Justice service page](https://www.gov.il/he/service/condominiums-supervisor-claims) (⚠️ manual check).
- Fee: as a civil claim in the magistrates' court; appeal period: reg. 83 ([current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- Free help: court information centres; Kol-Zchut's page on claims to the superintendent (⚠️ manual check); community mediation in many municipalities.

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The defendant counterclaims for building rights or attachments worth a lot.
- The dispute involves TAMA 38, urban renewal or an extension decision under s. 71ב.
- You need urgent demolition or an interim order against construction in progress.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Missing reg. 80 particulars | The claim is returned or delayed | Add them and refile | Use the template heading |
| Claiming an equal split | The claim is reduced | Recalculate by floor area | Read s. 58 and the bylaws |
| No decision in the decisions book | The expense is disputed | Convene a meeting and record the decision | Record every decision (s. 71(א)) |
| Suing in small claims over fees | Likely dismissal or transfer | File with the superintendent | Check s. 72 first |
| Missing the appeal period | The decision is final | Ask for an extension only with strong reasons | Diary the date of service |

## Review

- The superintendent decides bylaw and expense disputes and, if chosen, trespass between owners (s. 72).
- A claim needs the usual particulars plus the building's address, block, parcel, buildings, flats and entrances (reg. 80).
- Prepare with the extract, the bylaws, the decisions book and evidence; send a demand first.
- Decisions are enforced like a magistrates' judgment (s. 76); appeal lies to the district court within the reg. 83 period (s. 77).
