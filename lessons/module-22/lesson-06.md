# 22.6 · Welfare and housing benefits: application and review

Lesson 17.8 taught the substance of income support, rent assistance and the welfare services. In this clinic lesson you take one refused or reduced benefit of your own household and push it through the review the law offers: a reconsideration request to the National Insurance Institute and, if needed, a fee-free claim in the labour court; an appeal against a Ministry of Construction and Housing rent-assistance decision; or an appeal to the welfare appeals committee against the municipal social services department.

## Why it matters

Means-tested benefits are decided by officials applying detailed rules to messy lives, and mistakes are common: an asset counted that the law excludes, an income figure out of date, a household member listed who left. The people affected are, almost by definition, those least able to pay for help. Three facts make a self-represented review realistic. The NII must decide in writing and every appealable decision must tell you about free Legal Aid (NII Law s. 397). A public body must answer a written request within 45 days and give reasons for a refusal (Decisions and Reasons Law ss. 2, 2א). And the welfare appeals committee is a tribunal built for people acting alone: not bound by the rules of evidence, deciding within 10 days of the last hearing.

The other reason it matters is constitutional. The Supreme Court has recognised a right to a dignified minimum existence and struck down a blanket income-support rule that ignored individual circumstances (lesson [17.8](../module-17/lesson-08.md)). An individual review is how that principle reaches a real household.

## The law

### Income support: the car rule and the court

> תובע שהוא בעל שני כלי רכב או יותר או שהוא בעל רכב ששוויו עולה על סכום שקבע השר [...] לא יהיה זכאי לגמלה לפי חוק זה [...]

*Gist:* A claimant with two or more vehicles, or one worth more than a set amount, is not entitled; for one cheaper car the minister may set a deduction from the benefit (s. 9א(ב1)); and the rule does not apply in listed medical and disability cases (s. 9א(ג)). ([Income Support Law s. 9א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הבטחת_הכנסה); amounts in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)) A refusal "because you own a car" is therefore not a complete reason: the decision must say which limb applies.

> לבית דין אזורי [...] תהיה סמכות יחודית לדון ולפסוק בכל תובענה לפי חוק זה ובכל הנובע ממנו [...]

*Gist:* The regional labour court has exclusive jurisdiction over income-support claims. ([s. 26](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הבטחת_הכנסה)) Section 23 applies to income support the NII Law's provisions on representation (s. 386) and the regulations under s. 396, including the Time for Claims Regulations: sue no earlier than 30 days after claiming (unless decided) and within 12 months of notice of the decision. The proceeding is fee-exempt (Labour Court Fees Regulations reg. 4(22)), and Legal Aid represents individuals without a means test in labour-court proceedings to which the NII is a party on matters under any law under which it pays (NII Law s. 397).

### Any public body: answer within 45 days, give reasons

> נתבקש עובד הציבור, בכתב, להשתמש בסמכות שניתנה לו על פי דין, יחליט בבקשה וישיב למבקש בכתב בהקדם, אך לא יאוחר מארבעים וחמישה ימים מיום קבלת הבקשה.

*Gist:* A public servant asked in writing to exercise a power must decide and answer in writing within 45 days, or explain within that time why not (s. 2(א), (ג)); a refusal must give reasons (s. 2א); in an appeal or court proceeding, the burden of showing that an unreasoned decision was lawful is on the official (s. 6(א)); and silence for three months is treated as a refusal without reasons (s. 6(ב)). ([Decisions and Reasons Law](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לתיקון_סדרי_המינהל_(החלטות_והנמקות)); lesson [14.4](../module-14/lesson-04.md)) This is the tool for the Ministry and the municipality alike.

### The welfare department and its appeals committee

> הרואה את עצמו נפגע על ידי אי מתן תשובה תוך זמן סביר או על ידי החלטה רשאי לערור לפני ועדת ערר בכתב.

*Gist:* A request to the municipal social services department must be answered in writing within a reasonable time (s. 2(ב)); a person harmed by a decision or by no answer may appeal in writing to a three-member welfare appeals committee (s. 2(ג), (ה)), which may confirm, change or cancel the decision, set the assistance, or order the department to reconsider (s. 2(ז)–(ח)); its final decision is appealable to the Administrative Affairs Court (s. 2(ט)). ([Welfare Services Law s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_שירותי_הסעד)) The department must also help people realise their rights in welfare, health, education, employment and housing (s. 2ב). It may not give support meant to secure an adequate subsistence income (s. 2א): that is income support's job.

The committee's procedure ([Appeals Committees Procedure Regulations](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שירותי_הסעד_(סדרי_הנוהל_בועדות_ערר))): the appeal is filed in writing with the committee for the local authority's area (reg. 3(א)); the chair notifies the hearing date within 15 days (reg. 3(ב)); you may appear yourself or by a representative (reg. 4(א)); the department sends your file to the committee (reg. 5); the committee is not bound by the rules of evidence (reg. 7); it decides within 10 days of the last hearing, with written reasons (regs 10–11); hearings are closed (reg. 13).

### Rent assistance: rules, not statute

Rent assistance is given by the Ministry of Construction and Housing under its eligibility procedures, through companies that handle applications for it, for groups such as income-support recipients, single-parent families, people with disabilities, new immigrants and others (lesson 17.8). Per Kol-Zchut (⚠️ manual check), an applicant may appeal the amount or a refusal through the handling company, with a reasoned letter and documents; an exceptions committee may grant assistance outside the rules; and a public appeals committee hears appeals from it. Because these are administrative criteria, the general tools apply: ask for the criteria (an FOI request, lesson [14.10](../module-14/lesson-10.md)), demand written reasons, and, after the internal route, judicial review (lesson [14.9](../module-14/lesson-09.md); ⚠️ check which court on the day).

## Leading cases

### בג״ץ 10662/04 חסן נ׳ המוסד לביטוח לאומי (28.2.2012)

A seven-justice panel struck down the rule that treated any car owner as having income equal to the benefit: an absolute presumption infringed the right to a dignified minimum existence disproportionately, because an individual assessment was available. The Knesset replaced it with the value-based rule in s. 9א. Why it matters in a review request: it is the reason the NII must apply the current limbs of s. 9א to your car and your household, not a blanket "car = no benefit". [English translation](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/hassan-v-national-insurance-institute)

### בג״ץ 366/03 עמותת מחויבות לשלום וצדק חברתי נ׳ שר האוצר (12.12.2005)

The Court recognised a constitutional right to a dignified minimum existence but held that the petitioners had not shown that the overall system of support fell below it. Why it matters: it sets the constitutional floor and explains why individual reviews, with real household figures, succeed where abstract challenges fail. [English translation](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/commitment-peace-and-social-justice-society-v-minister-finance)

## How it works in practice

### The review map

| Benefit | Decided by | Review | Above it | Clock |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Income support | NII claims officer | Request for reconsideration (practice, not statute); does not stop the clock | Regional labour court, fee-free; Legal Aid without a means test | 12 months from notice |
| Rent assistance | Ministry of Construction and Housing, through the handling company | Appeal through the company; exceptions committee; public appeals committee (⚠️ manual check) | Judicial review after the internal route (⚠️) | Period in the decision letter (⚠️) |
| Welfare department help (care, a social worker's services, one-off aid) | Head of the municipal department | Ask for a written decision; remind | Welfare appeals committee; then the Administrative Affairs Court | "Reasonable time"; 45 days is the general yardstick |

### Turning "no" into an appealable decision

1. Make every request in writing, with documents, and keep proof.
2. Ask expressly for a written, reasoned decision.
3. If there is no answer in 45 days, write that the period has passed (s. 2) and ask for the decision or the reason for the delay.
4. If the answer is a phone call or a counter clerk's "you're not eligible", write it down and ask for it in writing.
5. Request your file (Privacy Protection Law s. 13).

### Order of moves when benefits depend on each other

Rent assistance for income-support recipients depends on the income-support decision. Restore the "gateway" benefit first, then reapply for, or appeal, the dependent one, attaching the new decision. Record the dependency in the track sheet of your case file.

## Worked example

**Supplied case E.** Natalia Petrenko, 41, came to Israel from Ukraine in 2023 and lives in Ashdod with her two children, 8 and 12. She works 20 hours a week as a care assistant for ₪3,900 a month. She owns one car, a 2014 hatchback worth about ₪22,000 by the price list, which she uses to take her younger son, who has asthma, to the hospital's paediatric clinic twice a week under a treatment plan. In September 2026 the NII refused income support: "בעלות על רכב". Because she does not receive income support, the rent-assistance company refused her application under the income-support route.

1. **Track sheet.** Gateway: income support (NII → labour court, 12 months from 20 September, the delivery date). Dependent: rent assistance (Ministry → its appeal route; she also checks the single-parent route). Possible welfare help: after-school care (municipal department → appeals committee).
2. **Income support: reconsideration (template part A).** On 1 October she writes to the NII branch: one car only; value well below the threshold for her category in s. 9א(ב) (she checks the current figure; ⚠️); at most a deduction under s. 9א(ב1); and the exception in s. 9א(ג)(2) for regular medical treatment outside the home (clinic letter and the treatment plan attached). She asks for a written, reasoned decision and her file, and notes that the request does not extend the court deadline.
3. **No answer in 45 days.** On 16 November she sends a reminder citing the Decisions and Reasons Law s. 2 and applies to Legal Aid, which is free of a means test against the NII (s. 397). She diarises 15 January as her own date to file in the Be'er Sheva or Tel Aviv region court for her residence (⚠️ check which regional court serves Ashdod) if nothing changes.
4. **Outcome.** In December the NII reverses itself, applies the exception and pays from the claim date. Natalia enters the decision date and the arrears in her log.
5. **Rent assistance (template part C).** She files an appeal through the handling company within the period in the refusal letter, attaching the new income-support decision and her lease, and asks in the alternative to be considered under the single-parent route. The appeal is accepted.
6. **Welfare department (template part D, not needed).** Her written request for help with after-school care was answered in writing within a month; she would otherwise have appealed to the welfare appeals committee for Ashdod's area.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- A refused, reduced or unanswered benefit request of your own household, with the decision and its delivery date; or supplied case E.
- The draft [welfare and housing benefit review request](../../templates/welfare-benefit-review-request.md) template (parts A–D) and your [clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md).
- The Income Support Law (s. 9א if a car is involved), the Welfare Services Law s. 2, and [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- About two hours.

### Steps

1. **Track sheet (15 min).** For each benefit: who decided, the review, the body above it, the clock. Mark which benefit is the gateway.
2. **Reasons check (15 min).** Write down the reason the decision gives. Is it a complete reason under the rule (which limb, which figure)? If not, that is your first ground.
3. **Evidence (20 min).** For each ground, the document that proves it (price list, clinic letter, payslips, lease, ID of the household members).
4. **Draft (40 min).** Fill in the right part of the template. One ground per paragraph; ask for a written, reasoned decision and your file.
5. **Clocks (10 min).** Enter the 45-day answer date, the 12-month NII claim date (or the Ministry's period), and your own earlier target for the next step.
6. **Help (10 min).** Decide whether to apply to Legal Aid (NII matters) or ask the social services department for help realising your rights (s. 2ב). Record the date.
7. **Next step plan (10 min).** If the answer is no, or there is no answer: which document you file next, where, by when.

### Template

[Welfare and housing benefit review request](../../templates/welfare-benefit-review-request.md) (new in this module, draft for the main session): A (NII reconsideration), B (labour-court claim outline), C (rent-assistance appeal), D (welfare appeals committee). For other authorities: the [objection to an administrative decision](../../templates/objection-to-administrative-decision.md) (lesson 14.10).

### How you know it worked

- Your request names the rule, the limb you rely on and the evidence for each ground.
- It asks for a written, reasoned decision and your file.
- Your log shows the 45-day date and the final date for the next forum, and the dependent benefit is planned after the gateway one.

### Self-check

- [ ] I know which court hears income-support disputes, that it is fee-free and that Legal Aid is free of a means test there.
- [ ] I know that a reconsideration request does not stop the 12-month clock.
- [ ] I know what the welfare appeals committee can order and how it works.
- [ ] I know the 45-day answer duty and what three months' silence means.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Benefits are claimed and challenged by the claimant in person; income-support claims in the labour court are fee-free and common without a lawyer; the welfare appeals committee hears people who appear themselves (reg. 4). Before the NII, a close friend or others listed in s. 386 may represent you (applied by Income Support Law s. 23).

### Where and how

- NII: the branch or the NII personal area; the regional labour court for your residence.
- Rent assistance: the handling company for the Ministry and the Ministry's online services (⚠️ manual check of the current route).
- Welfare: your municipal social services department; the welfare appeals committee for its area (ask the department for the address).
- Free help: Legal Aid (NII matters without a means test); the department's duty to help realise rights (s. 2ב); rights-realisation centres and NGOs (see [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md)).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The NII alleges false statements or demands repayment of past benefits.
- You face eviction from public housing or loss of eligibility for it.
- The appeals committee's final decision went against you and you consider an appeal to the Administrative Affairs Court.
- A private company offers to "realise your rights" for a percentage: compare with Legal Aid and the statutory fee cap first.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Waiting for an answer to a reconsideration request past 12 months | Claim in the labour court out of time | Ask the court to extend, with reasons | Diary the 12-month date from the first decision |
| Accepting an oral "not eligible" at the counter | Nothing to appeal | Ask in writing for a reasoned decision | Every request in writing |
| Arguing that the rule is unfair instead of that it was misapplied | The official cannot change the rule | Show the limb and the evidence the decision missed | Start from the rule's exact words |
| Appealing the dependent benefit before the gateway one | Refused again for the same reason | Restore the gateway benefit, then reapply | Map dependencies on the track sheet |
| Asking the welfare department for subsistence money | Refused: s. 2א excludes it | Claim income support from the NII | Know which body pays what |

## Review

- Income support: the NII decides; reconsideration is optional and does not stop the clock; the regional labour court has exclusive jurisdiction, fee-free, within 12 months, with Legal Aid free of a means test.
- Rent assistance follows Ministry criteria; appeal through the handling company and its committees (⚠️ check the current route); restore the gateway benefit first.
- The welfare department must answer in writing; its decisions and its silence go to the welfare appeals committee, which is informal, closed and decides within 10 days of the last hearing.
- Any public body must answer a written request within 45 days and give reasons; three months' silence is a refusal without reasons.
