# 22.2 · An employment claim: from the letter to the labour court

In this lesson you take an unpaid final settlement, or another wage claim of your own, from the demand letter of lesson 17.10 to a filed statement of claim in the regional labour court. You will decide whether the claim fits the expedited track, write the claim with the particulars the regulations require, choose the court and the fee, and enter the next deadlines in your case log.

## Why it matters

Most employees who are owed money never sue, and most who do are represented. Yet the labour court is built for employees acting alone more than any other Israeli court: it is not bound by the rules of evidence (Labour Court Law s. 32), small claims go on an expedited track that is "not bound by procedure" (s. 31(ב)), the Judiciary offers an online drafting and filing service for unrepresented employees on that track, and many wage proceedings are fee-exempt. What sinks self-represented claims is not the law of wages (module 17) but the claim itself: the wrong defendant, a total that crosses the ceiling, sums with no calculation, missing annexes, or a claim that argues instead of stating facts.

The ceiling question is also where module 17 left an open conflict: Kol-Zchut gave ₪40,700 for 2026 while a courts' fee table showed ₪39,500. The regulations settle it, as you will see below.

## The law

### The expedited track: s. 31

> בתובענה כאמור בסעיף 24(א)(1) או (3) לתשלום סכום שלא יעלה על הסכום שקבעו [השרים] ידון בית דין אזורי בדרך של דיון מהיר [...]

*Gist:* An employee–employer money claim (or a claim against a provident fund) up to a ceiling set by regulation is heard on the expedited track. ([Labour Court Law s. 31(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בית_הדין_לעבודה)) On that track the court "is not bound by procedure" and acts in the way it finds most useful for a just and quick decision (s. 31(ב)); it may decide by law or, with consent, by compromise (s. 31(ג)); its judgment can be appealed only with leave of the National Labour Court (s. 31(ד)); and it may stop the expedited hearing and move the case to the ordinary track if the issues or the evidence are too complex (s. 31(ה)).

### The ceiling and how it moves

> ביום 1 בינואר של כל שנה יעודכן הסכום [...] כך שיהיה שווה לפי שלושה מהשכר הממוצע במועד העדכון, ויעוגל למאה השקלים החדשים הקרובים.

*Gist:* Each 1 January the ceiling becomes three times the average wage (as defined in NII Law s. 1) on that date, rounded to the nearest ₪100, and the Courts Director publishes it. ([Expedited Hearing Regulations reg. 1א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_בית_הדין_לעבודה_(קביעת_סכום_התביעה_בדיון_מהיר)), as summarised on Wikisource) The amount is measured on the filing date and **excludes** late-wage compensation, linkage differences and interest (reg. 1).

**The conflict settled.** The NII's average wage for 2026 under s. 1 is ₪13,566 (see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)). Three times that is ₪40,698, which rounds to ₪40,700, the figure Wikisource's regulation page gives for 2026 (Kol-Zchut and the Judiciary's online claim service give the same figure, as reported by a search engine; ⚠️ not opened). The ₪39,500 figure is the 2025 amount (Wikisource's note to s. 31 says so). Use the 2026 figure in current numbers, and recheck it every January.

### What the claim must contain: reg. 9

> אלה הפרטים שיכיל כתב תביעה, וחוץ מן האמור בתקנות להלן לא יכיל אלא פרטים אלה [...]

*Gist:* The claim names the court; the claimant's name, ID, residence and address for service; the defendant's name, ID and residence as far as known, and if it is a corporation, its form; the facts of the cause of action and when it arose; the facts showing the court's jurisdiction; the remedy, including late-wage compensation, interest, linkage and costs; and the value claimed. ([reg. 9](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_בית_הדין_לעבודה_(סדרי_דין))) Nothing else: no argument, no history of the workplace. A self-represented claimant's address for service is their home (reg. 10(1)); a claim without the filer's ID number at the top is not registered (reg. 8א).

### Documents, forms, the whole remedy

- **Documents (reg. 11):** list every document you rely on, attach those you hold, and say who holds the rest.
- **Forms (reg. 13):** a claim for wages is made on Form 2 and a claim for severance on Form 3 of the Schedule. The course template follows their headings and reg. 9; the online service produces the official layout (⚠️ check the current form on the day).
- **The whole remedy (reg. 26):** claim everything due on the same cause of action; you may waive part (reg. 26(א)), which is how a slightly larger claim can be brought within the ceiling. A remedy left out without the court's leave cannot be claimed later (reg. 26(ג)).

### Venue and the defence

The court is the regional labour court where the workplace is, or where the work was done (reg. 3(א)(1)). A defendant files a defence when the court orders it, within 30 days of the order (regs 27–28); a defendant who does not may face judgment on the claim alone (reg. 43).

### Wages: two rules you will use in the claim

> לא ינוכו משכר עבודה אלא סכומים אלה: (1) סכום שחובה לנכותו, או שמותר לנכותו על פי חיקוק; (2) תרומות שהעובד הסכים בכתב כי ינוכו [...]

*Gist:* An employer may deduct from wages only the sums the law lists (tax, NII, pension, written consents and a few others). A "till shortage" deduction is not on the list. ([Wage Protection Law s. 25](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_השכר)) Late-wage compensation (ss. 17, 20) is claimed in the same claim but expires fast (s. 17א; lesson [22.1](lesson-01.md)).

### Fees

On the expedited track the fee is half the regular fee, with a minimum (Fees Regulations reg. 6); the regular fee for a liquidated claim is a percentage of the amount with a minimum (First Schedule item 1). Proceedings under the Wage Protection Law, the Minimum Wage Law, Annual Leave Law s. 14, Hours of Work Law s. 30, the Equal Pay Law and others are exempt (reg. 4). Amounts: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

## How it works in practice

### Decision: which track

| Question | If yes | If no |
|---|---|---|
| Are you an employee (or former employee) claiming money from the employer or a provident fund? | Labour court (s. 24(א)(1), (3)) | A contractor's fee claim: civil courts, small claims (module 12) |
| Is the total, on the filing date, without late-wage compensation, linkage and interest, within the ceiling? | Expedited track | Ordinary track, or waive the excess in writing (reg. 26(א)) |
| Are the facts simple enough for one hearing? | Stay on the expedited track | Expect the court to move you to the ordinary track (s. 31(ה)) |

### Filing routes

1. **Online (unrepresented employees, expedited track).** The Judiciary's labour-court online claim service lets an employee draft a statement of claim or defence on the expedited track and file it directly; per its home page, the ceiling for the service is the 2026 expedited amount (⚠️ manual check of the service, its login and current scope).
2. **Net HaMishpat.** Unrepresented parties log in with the national identification to file documents in an existing case; the Judiciary's guidance for defendants describes this route (⚠️ manual check whether an unrepresented claimant can open a case there).
3. **By hand.** At the regional court's secretariat, with the copies the secretariat asks for (reg. 8; the court's service page currently lists two copies plus one per defendant, ⚠️ check on the day). Pleadings may not be faxed.

### After filing

- The secretariat serves the summons on the employer (reg. 12, Form 1). Note the service date in the log.
- The court sets a date with at least 30 days' notice (reg. 47) and may order a defence within 30 days (regs 27–28).
- Many expedited cases are decided at a single hearing; prepare for it as lesson 22.7 explains.
- If the employer pays after filing, any late payment starts the 60-day clock for late-wage compensation (s. 17א). Do not withdraw the claim without dealing with it.

## Worked example

**Supplied case A.** Rawan Haddad, 29, worked as a monthly-paid waitress for a restaurant company in Haifa (invented: "ש.ב. גליל מזון בע״מ") from 1 May 2024 to 31 August 2026, at ₪6,800 a month for a five-day week. She was dismissed by WhatsApp on 31 August "as of today", with no notice. Her pension fund shows severance contributions of 6% from November 2024; her contract has no s. 14 arrangement. She has 8 unused leave days. Recuperation was never paid. Her August payslip shows a ₪824 deduction labelled "חוסר בקופה". Her demand letter of 10 September (template from lesson 17.10) went unanswered.

**Her calculation** (method of lesson [17.10](../module-17/lesson-10.md); ⚠️ recuperation days and rates as in that lesson):

**Track.** ₪20,436, without late-wage compensation, is well within the 2026 ceiling: expedited track. **Court:** Haifa Regional Labour Court (workplace in Haifa, reg. 3(א)(1)). **Fee:** half of 1% of the amount, not below the expedited minimum (about ₪102 on these figures), unless the secretariat treats the wage items as exempt under reg. 4 (see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- Your clinic case file from lesson 22.1, your final-settlement worksheet and the demand letter with proof of delivery (lesson 17.10), or supplied case A.
- The draft [labour-court statement of claim (expedited track)](../../templates/labour-court-statement-of-claim.md) template.
- The procedure regulations open at regs 3, 9, 11 and 26; the current expedited ceiling and fee in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- About two and a half hours.

### Steps

1. **Confirm the defendant (10 min).** Copy the employer's legal name and number from the payslip; check it in the Companies Registrar's free search; note the registered address and the date you checked.
2. **Track (10 min).** Total your heads of claim without late-wage compensation, linkage or interest. Compare with the ceiling for the year of filing. If you are slightly above it, decide in writing whether to waive the excess (reg. 26(א)) and record why.
3. **Facts (40 min).** Turn your chronology into numbered paragraphs: employment, wage, ending, demand, response. One fact per paragraph, each with its annex. Delete every adjective and every argument.
4. **Jurisdiction and venue (10 min).** One paragraph each: s. 24(א)(1) and reg. 3(א)(1).
5. **Remedies table (30 min).** Paste the worksheet's summary: item, basis, short calculation, amount. Add late-wage compensation as an alternative, and any documents you want ordered (form 161, the release letter to the fund, missing payslips).
6. **Documents and witnesses (15 min).** List every annex in order; name documents the employer holds (attendance records, CCTV policy, tip records).
7. **Read-aloud test (10 min).** Read the claim aloud in under five minutes. If you cannot, cut.
8. **Filing decision (25 min).** Tick the checklist at the end of the template. Choose the route (online service, by hand). Decide: file now (real, own name), send one last settlement offer first, or keep as a dry run. Record the decision and its reason in your case log, and enter the expected service date, the 30-day defence period once ordered and the hearing date once set.

### How you know it worked

- The claim contains every particular in reg. 9 and nothing that reg. 9 does not ask for.
- Every shekel in the total has a basis and a calculation in an annex.
- The amount is within the ceiling of the filing year, or you have recorded a decision to waive or to go on the ordinary track.
- The court is the one for your workplace, and your ID and home address for service are at the top.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Employees file in their own name in the labour court every day; the expedited track and the online service are designed for it. A company employer may appear through a manager or authorised officer (reg. 23), so you may face a lawyer or a manager. Your union may appoint a representative if you are a member (s. 34).

### Where and how

- The regional labour court for your workplace: Jerusalem, Tel Aviv, Haifa, Be'er Sheva or Nazareth (and their branches); online through the Judiciary's labour-court claim service for unrepresented employees (⚠️ manual check), or by hand at the secretariat.
- Fee and ceiling: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); forum rules: [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md).
- Free help: your union; workers' rights organisations and the Ministry of Labour's enforcement unit for wage complaints; Legal Aid if you meet the means test (see [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md)). Some regional courts have volunteer help desks (⚠️ check locally).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The total is well above the ceiling, or the claim includes discrimination, harassment, unlawful dismissal of a protected employee (pregnancy, fertility treatment, reserve duty) or reinstatement.
- The employer sues you back (breach of confidence, competition, damage) or alleges theft.
- The employer is insolvent or has disappeared (claims go to the NII's insolvency benefits route and need care).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Suing the restaurant's trade name instead of the company | Judgment against a name with no assets | Amend the defendant before the hearing | Copy the name and number from the payslip; check the Registrar |
| Counting interest and late-wage compensation in the ceiling | Wrongly filing on the ordinary track, or a refused expedited claim | Recalculate without them (reg. 1) | Total the heads of claim only |
| Leaving out recuperation "to keep it simple" | It cannot be claimed later without leave (reg. 26(ג)) | Ask leave to amend promptly | Claim the whole remedy now |
| A claim that argues and accuses | The court cannot see the facts; tone hurts | Rewrite as facts with annexes | Reg. 9: facts and nothing else |
| Accepting a late payment and forgetting compensation | Late-wage compensation expires after 60 days | Amend or file within the period; ask to extend to 90 days | Log every payment date |

## Review

- The expedited track covers employee money claims up to a ceiling set each 1 January at three times the average wage, rounded to ₪100: ₪40,700 for 2026; late-wage compensation, linkage and interest do not count.
- The claim contains the reg. 9 particulars and nothing else, a document list (reg. 11), and the whole remedy (reg. 26); wage and severance claims follow Forms 2 and 3.
- File in the court for your workplace (reg. 3), online or by hand; the fee on the expedited track is half, with a minimum, and many wage proceedings are exempt.
- After filing, log service, the defence period and the hearing date; a late payment starts the 60-day clock for late-wage compensation.
