# 22.3 · A National Insurance appeal from start to finish

Lessons 17.9 and 17.11 taught the NII's two tracks and the appeal to the medical appeals committee. In this clinic lesson you run one medical dispute of your own the whole way: get the file, appeal within 30 days, prepare for and attend the committee, read its minutes, and, if it errs in law, appeal to the regional labour court within 60 days, with or without Legal Aid. The focus is the second half, where most self-represented claimants stop.

## Why it matters

A medical appeals committee's decision is final on the facts. The only way past it is an appeal "on a point of law only" to the regional labour court (NII Law s. 213), and most claimants either do not know this or write an appeal that argues the percentages again, which the court must dismiss. Yet the labour courts regularly return decisions to the committee when it failed to examine an impairment, ignored a medical document, did not give reasons, or applied the wrong item of the impairment list. Those defects are visible only to someone who reads the minutes against the file. That reading is the clinic skill.

The stakes are long-term: a general disability pension, a work-injury pension or a special-services allowance is paid monthly for years. A 60-day appeal that returns the case to the committee can change that.

## The law

### Stage 1: the appeal to the committee

The medical appeal (ערר) is due within 30 days of notice, with 30 more days only with the NII's consent, filed in writing with reasons (Disability Regulations regs 20–21); in general disability you may bring your treating doctor or a specialist (reg. 22). The thresholds and the doctor's duty to give reasons are in NII Law s. 208. All of this is lesson [17.11](../module-17/lesson-11.md); use its worksheet.

### Stage 2: the appeal on law

> החלטות של ועדה רפואית לעררים, ועדה לעררים וועדה לעררים לשירותים מיוחדים, ניתנות לערעור, בשאלה משפטית בלבד, לפני בית דין אזורי לעבודה; פסק דינו של בית הדין ניתן לערעור לפני בית הדין הארצי לעבודה אם נתקבלה רשות לכך [...]

*Gist:* Decisions of the medical appeals committee, the appeals committee and the special-services appeals committee may be appealed to the regional labour court on a point of law only; a further appeal to the National Labour Court needs leave. ([NII Law s. 213](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הביטוח_הלאומי); s. 123 is the work-injury twin)

> ערעור על החלטה יוגש לבית הדין האזורי לעבודה תוך שישים ימים מהיום שבו נמסרה ההחלטה למערער [...]

*Gist:* The appeal is due within 60 days of the day the decision was delivered to you. ([Appeal Time Regulations reg. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הביטוח_הלאומי_(מועד_להגשת_ערעור_על_החלטות_מסוימות)))

### Who hears it

The regional labour court normally sits as a judge with two public representatives (Labour Court Law s. 18(א)), but appeals on law from NII medical appeals committees and appeals committees are listed in the Third Schedule and are heard by a **single judge** (s. 18(ב)(1); Third Schedule item 1). Expect a short, legal hearing, often decided on written submissions.

### Legal Aid without a means test

> [...] יתקין תקנות בדבר מתן סיוע משפטי לכל מבקש, שאינו חבר־בני־אדם, בהליכים בבית הדין לעבודה שהמוסד הוא צד בהם [...]

*Gist:* Legal Aid represents any individual in labour-court proceedings to which the NII is a party, on matters under the NII Law or any other law under which the NII pays, without the usual means test; every appealable NII decision must say so in plain language. ([NII Law s. 397](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הביטוח_הלאומי)) The clinic's choice is real: you may file yourself, or file yourself and ask Legal Aid to take over, or ask Legal Aid first. Ask early: Legal Aid needs time inside your 60 days.

### Getting the minutes and the file

The committee's decision and minutes are sent to you; the full file (earlier minutes, the NII doctors' opinions, documents you submitted) can be requested from the NII, including under Privacy Protection Law s. 13 (lesson [22.1](lesson-01.md)). Medical information that the NII considers harmful may be given to your doctor instead (s. 13(ג)).

## Leading cases

### Key text: what counts as a "point of law"

No free full-text National Labour Court ruling was confirmed for this course (lesson [17.9](../module-17/lesson-09.md) explains). The doctrine the regional courts apply, as described in the NII's and Kol-Zchut's guides and in lesson 17.9, treats as points of law: a failure to examine or decide an impairment the claimant raised; ignoring a medical document that was before the committee; failing to give reasons, or giving reasons that do not engage with the evidence; applying an item of the impairment list that does not fit the findings; departing from an earlier finding without explanation; and procedural defects such as a wrong composition. A bare disagreement with a percentage is a medical question and is not a ground. Read current regional-court judgments on the Judiciary's site for examples (⚠️ manual check).

### בג״ץ 3/58 ברמן נ׳ שר הפנים (1958)

Taught in lesson [17.4](../module-17/lesson-04.md): a public authority must give a person a fair opportunity to be heard before a decision that harms him. Why it matters here: a committee that did not hear your complaints, or did not record them, has a procedural defect, and procedure is law. Use it as the principle behind a "complaints not recorded" ground, not as a substitute for the specific defect in your minutes.

## How it works in practice

### The whole timeline

| Step | Who | Deadline | What you keep |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decision of the authorised doctor or first committee | NII | — | Letter and envelope (delivery date) |
| Request the file and minutes | You | At once | Copy of the request and its date |
| Appeal to the medical appeals committee | You | 30 days from delivery (+30 with consent) | Appeal, proof of filing |
| Committee examination | Committee, you, your doctor | Date in the invitation | Your one-page summary; documents handed in |
| Committee decision delivered | NII | — | Decision, minutes, delivery date |
| Decide: accept, re-assess later, or appeal on law | You (and Legal Aid) | Within the 60 days | Your "minutes check" (below) |
| Appeal on law to the regional labour court | You or Legal Aid | 60 days from delivery | Appeal, proof of filing |
| NII's response; hearing or written submissions | Court | As ordered | Case log |
| Judgment | Single judge | — | Judgment; if remitted, the new committee date |

### The minutes check

Put the minutes beside your file and answer, impairment by impairment:

1. Did the committee record each complaint you made and each impairment you claimed?
2. Did it examine each one (a finding, not just a percentage)?
3. Did it mention each medical document you submitted, and say why it accepted or rejected it?
4. Does the item of the impairment list it chose fit the findings it wrote?
5. Did it explain any departure from an earlier finding (a previous committee, a temporary degree)?
6. Was the composition correct, and were you allowed your doctor (reg. 22) or a companion (reg. 16)?

Every "no" is a candidate point of law. Every "I think the percentage should be higher" is not.

### Filing the appeal on law

File in the regional labour court for your place of residence, as a short appeal (כתב ערעור) with the committee's decision attached: the parties (you against the NII), the decision and its delivery date, the grounds as numbered points of law, each pointing to the page and line of the minutes, and the remedy (usually: set aside the decision and remit to the committee, in another composition, with instructions). The proceeding is fee-exempt (Fees Regulations reg. 4(26)). The [NII appeal template](../../templates/nii-medical-committee-appeal.md), part B, gives the outline (⚠️ check the court's current form on the day).

## Worked example

**Supplied case B.** Moshe Azoulay, 58, from Dimona, worked for 30 years in a factory until 2025. He claimed a general disability pension. The authorised doctor gave: lumbar spine 20%, diabetes with complications 10%, below the threshold. The decision reached him on 5 October 2026.

1. **Stage 1 (lesson 17.11 method).** He requests his file the same day and files his medical appeal on 20 October, within 30 days, with grounds per impairment: spine (an MRI and an orthopaedic surgeon's letter describing nerve-root compression and limited movement), diabetes (endocrinologist's letter on neuropathy in both feet), and a new impairment not considered at all, moderate depression (psychiatrist's letter).
2. **The committee.** It examines his back and records "תנועות מוגבלות", sets 20% under the same item, sets diabetes at 10%, and writes nothing about the psychiatric letter or the neuropathy. Its decision is delivered on 10 December 2026.
3. **Minutes check.** Q1: the psychiatric complaint is recorded in his summary but not in the committee's findings. Q3: the psychiatrist's letter and the endocrinologist's neuropathy findings are not mentioned. Q4: the spine item chosen covers limitation without nerve-root involvement, while the surgeon's letter describes nerve-root compression. These are three points of law. His view that "20% is too low" is not.
4. **Legal Aid decision.** On 14 December he applies to Legal Aid (no means test, s. 397) and, to be safe, prepares his own appeal.
5. **Deadline.** 60 days from 10 December 2026: 8 February 2027. He diarises 25 January as his own filing date if Legal Aid has not taken the case.
6. **Appeal.** He files on 25 January in the Be'er Sheva Regional Labour Court, fee-free. A single judge hears it. The NII agrees, in its response, to remit the matter to the committee in another composition to consider the psychiatric letter and the neuropathy and to reconsider the spine item with reasons. The court gives the agreement the force of a judgment.
7. **Back at the committee.** He returns with the same letters and a one-page summary. The new committee decision is his next "delivery date" in the log.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- An NII medical decision of your own, with its delivery date, and (if you have reached stage 2) the committee's decision and minutes; or supplied case B.
- The [NII medical committee appeal template](../../templates/nii-medical-committee-appeal.md), parts A and B, and your [clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md).
- About two hours.

### Steps

1. **Deadline table (10 min).** Enter the delivery date, the 30-day (stage 1) or 60-day (stage 2) deadline, and your own earlier target date.
2. **File request (10 min).** Send a written request to the NII for the full file and minutes; record the date.
3. **Stage 1, if you are there (40 min).** Follow lesson 17.11: one paragraph per impairment, its evidence and the list item you rely on; ask for your doctor to attend if you can.
4. **Minutes check (30 min).** For stage 2, answer the six questions for every impairment; mark each "no" with the page and line of the minutes.
5. **Grounds (20 min).** Write each "no" as a numbered point of law in the part B outline. Delete every sentence that only disagrees with a percentage.
6. **Legal Aid (5 min).** Decide whether to apply now; if you do, record the application date and keep your own filing date inside the 60 days.
7. **Filing plan (5 min).** Court (your residence), how you will file, what you attach (the decision, the minutes, the documents the committee ignored), and how you prove the filing date.

### Template

[NII medical committee appeal](../../templates/nii-medical-committee-appeal.md) (module 17): part A for the committee, part B for the appeal on law.

### How you know it worked

- Every ground in your appeal on law points to something the committee did or failed to do, at a page and line of the minutes.
- None of your grounds says only that a percentage is too low.
- Your filing date is inside the 60 days, with a margin, whether or not Legal Aid takes the case.

### Self-check

- [ ] I know the difference between a medical disagreement and a point of law.
- [ ] I know that a single judge hears the appeal and that it is fee-exempt.
- [ ] I know that Legal Aid is available without a means test and how to ask for it.
- [ ] My case log has the delivery date of every NII decision and committee decision.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes, at both stages. The insured person files the medical appeal and the appeal on law in their own name. Before the NII, a close friend, a union representative or others may represent you (NII Law s. 386); before the medical appeals committee in general disability, your treating doctor or a specialist (reg. 22). In the labour court, a lawyer, Legal Aid, or your union's appointee (Labour Court Law s. 34).

### Where and how

- Stage 1: in writing through the NII branch or the NII personal area online.
- Stage 2: the regional labour court for your place of residence; fee-exempt (Fees Regulations reg. 4(26)); deadlines in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Free help: Legal Aid (NII matters, no means test, s. 397); disability-rights organisations; the NII's guidance services for claimants (⚠️ manual check); [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The labour court dismissed your appeal on law and you are considering leave to appeal to the National Labour Court.
- The case combines a work injury with a tort claim against the employer or a third party, or with a private insurance claim.
- The NII demands repayment of benefits already paid, or alleges false statements.
- A private "rights company" asks you to sign a fee agreement: compare with Legal Aid first and check the statutory fee cap (lesson 17.9).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Appealing to the labour court that "40% is too low" | Dismissed: law only | Recast as specific defects in the minutes | Do the minutes check first |
| Waiting for Legal Aid past day 60 | Appeal out of time | Ask the court to extend with reasons (reg. 125, special reasons) | Keep your own filing date inside the period |
| Not reading the minutes before leaving the committee | Complaints missing from the record | Raise the omission as a ground | Ask that complaints be recorded; read the summary |
| New medical evidence only at the labour court | The court reviews the committee's record, not new facts | Ask for re-assessment, or remittal on a recorded defect | Bring every document to the committee |
| Ignoring that a committee can lower a percentage | Losing an existing degree | Consider withdrawing if the NII signals a lower degree | Read lesson 17.9's warning |

## Review

- Stage 1: appeal to the medical appeals committee within 30 days (+30 with consent), with grounds per impairment and your doctor if you can (lesson 17.11).
- Stage 2: appeal to the regional labour court on a point of law only, within 60 days of delivery (s. 213; reg. 2), fee-exempt, before a single judge.
- Points of law come from the minutes: impairments not examined, documents ignored, no reasons, the wrong list item, unexplained departures, procedural defects.
- Legal Aid represents individuals against the NII without a means test (s. 397); apply early and keep your own filing date.
