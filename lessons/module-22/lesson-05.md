# 22.5 · A tenancy dispute: deposit and repairs from letter to small claims

Module 12's clinic case was a deposit kept after the tenant had left. This lesson takes the harder version: a defect while you still live in the flat, a landlord you will meet in the stairwell, and a deposit that becomes the battleground when the lease ends. You will run the repair demand and its clock, decide what self-help the law allows, and, if it comes to it, bring repairs and deposit together in one small claim.

## Why it matters

Since the 2017 amendment the residential-lease chapter gives tenants clear rights: the landlord repairs at his own cost anything not caused by the tenant's unreasonable use and not trivial, within 30 days of a demand, or three days for a defect that makes the flat unreasonable to live in (s. 25ח(ב)); if he does not, the tenant may repair and claim the cost, or reduce the rent while the defect lasts (s. 9). The security may be used only for four things, after notice (s. 25י(ג)–(ד)). These rules protect a tenant only if the tenant uses them in the right order and keeps the evidence. In practice the disputes turn on three questions: Was the demand made, and when? Was the defect urgent? And did the tenant's self-help (a rent deduction, a technician) give the landlord an excuse to keep the deposit?

## The law

### The landlord's repair duty and its clock

> [...] המשכיר יתקן על חשבונו כל ליקוי או פגם אחר בדירה המושכרת שאינו כאמור בסעיף קטן (א) ואינו קל ערך, בתוך זמן סביר ולא יאוחר מ־30 ימים מיום שקיבל דרישה לכך מאת השוכר, ואם היה פגם שתיקונו דחוף – יתקן המשכיר את הפגם בתוך זמן סביר ולא יאוחר משלושה ימים [...]; לעניין זה, ”פגם שתיקונו דחוף“ – פגם שאינו מאפשר מגורים בדירה באופן סביר [...]

*Gist:* The landlord must repair any non-trivial defect not caused by the tenant's unreasonable use within a reasonable time and at most 30 days from the tenant's demand, or three days for a defect that does not allow reasonable living in the flat. ([s. 25ח(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_השכירות_והשאילה)) The tenant is responsible only for defects caused by unreasonable use (s. 25ח(א)). The clock starts when the landlord **receives** the demand.

### The tenant's remedies

> דרש השוכר מהמשכיר לתקן את הפגם [...] ולא תיקן המשכיר [...] בתוך זמן סביר [...], רשאי השוכר – (1) לתקן את הפגם [...] ולדרוש מהמשכיר החזרת הוצאותיו הסבירות; (2) להפחית את דמי השכירות, כל עוד לא תוקן הפגם [...], לפי היחס שבו פחת שווי השכירות עקב הפגם [...]

*Gist:* If the landlord does not repair in time, the tenant may repair and claim reasonable costs, or reduce the rent in proportion to the loss of rental value while the defect lasts. ([s. 9(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_השכירות_והשאילה)) A tenant who intends to repair must tell the landlord a reasonable time in advance (s. 9(ב)); a defect so urgent that the tenant cannot be expected to wait may be repaired without a demand or notice (s. 9(ג)). For residential leases, the periods in s. 25ח(ב) define "in time" (s. 25ח(ג)).

### The security

The landlord may use the security only for unpaid rent, repairs the tenant was responsible for under s. 25ח(א), unpaid running charges, or not vacating, and only up to those amounts (s. 25י(ג)); he must give the tenant reasonable notice and a chance to fix (s. 25י(ד)); and he must return it, or the balance, with its fruits, within 60 days of getting the flat back, or when the tenant's debts are paid, whichever is later (s. 25י(ה)). The cap on the security is in s. 25י(ב). ([s. 25י](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_השכירות_והשאילה); figures in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

**The trap.** A rent reduction the court later finds excessive is unpaid rent, which is one of the four permitted uses of the security (s. 25י(ג)(1)). Self-help is lawful; overdoing it hands the landlord a lawful reason to keep money.

### Forum

An individual's claim for money up to the ceiling goes to small claims (Courts Law s. 60; lesson [12.5](../module-12/lesson-05.md)). The chapter applies only to leases inside s. 25טו (not holiday lets, protected tenancies, leases of up to three months without an option, over ten years, or above the rent ceiling): check first (lesson [20.3](../module-20/lesson-03.md)).

## Leading cases

### רע״א 9615/05 שמש נ׳ פוקצ׳טה בע״מ (5.7.2006)

Taught in lesson [12.1](../module-12/lesson-01.md): a self-represented claimant's small claim to enforce a protective statute reached the Supreme Court, which stressed that private claims are a legitimate way to enforce such statutes. Why it matters here: the residential-lease chapter is protective legislation; a well-documented small claim is the way tenants enforce it. [English translation (Cardozo)](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/346)

### Key text: the 2017 residential chapter

No free official text of a Supreme Court ruling interpreting the 2017 chapter was found (lesson 20.3 records the search). The key texts are ss. 25ח and 25י and the general remedies in s. 9. Small-claims judgments vary on what counts as "urgent" and on how much rent reduction is proportionate; your evidence (dates, photos, the technician's report, the rental value) decides more than any precedent.

## How it works in practice

### The repair decision tree

| Situation | What you send | Clock | If not repaired |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary defect (mould, a broken shutter, a dripping tap that is not trivial) | Written demand with photos (template part A) | 30 days from receipt | Notice of intent to repair, then repair and claim costs; or proportionate rent reduction, in writing, with a calculation |
| Urgent defect (no hot water in winter, no electricity, a burst pipe, a broken front-door lock) | Demand at once, by message and call; say why it is urgent | 3 days from receipt | Repair and claim costs; keep the invoice and the technician's note on the cause |
| Emergency you cannot wait for (flooding now) | Act first; tell the landlord the same day | — | Repair and claim reasonable costs (s. 9(ג)) |
| Defect caused by you | Repair it yourself | — | The landlord may claim it from the security after notice (s. 25י(ג)(2), (ד)) |

### Documenting

- Photos and video with dates; the technician's written note on the cause (age, wear, roof leak, misuse).
- Proof of delivery of every demand (WhatsApp ticks plus email; registered mail for the important ones).
- For rent reduction: a short calculation (which room or function is lost, what share of the flat's value it represents, for how many days).
- A diary of the defect's effect on daily life.

### At the end of the lease

1. Agree a handover date; photograph every room and meter reading; send them to the landlord the same day.
2. Ask in writing for the security back within the statutory period, offering to settle any running charges you owe (template part B).
3. If the landlord announces a deduction, ask for the notice and the chance to fix that s. 25י(ד) requires, and for the quote or invoice.
4. Diary day 60 after handover; then send the demand letter (lesson [12.3](../module-12/lesson-03.md)) and, if needed, file.

## Worked example

**Supplied case D.** Yusuf Abu-Ras, 45, and his wife Lina rent a three-room flat in Jaffa from a private landlord, Shlomo Ben-Ami (invented), for ₪5,000 a month from 1 June 2026 to 31 May 2027, with a cash security of ₪15,000. Both signed the lease.

1. **The water heater (urgent).** On Thursday 10 December 2026 the electric water heater stops working; the family has no hot water. Yusuf writes on WhatsApp and by email the same evening: the heater is dead, there is no hot water, the children cannot shower, this is urgent under s. 25ח(ב), please repair within three days. Read ticks at 20:14. No answer. On 14 December he sends notice that he will call a technician the next day, and does. The technician's invoice: ₪2,350 for a new element and thermostat, with the note "בלאי – גיל הדוד כ־15 שנה". Yusuf sends the invoice and asks for reimbursement within 14 days (s. 9(א)(1)).
2. **The mould (ordinary).** The same week, mould spreads on the children's bedroom ceiling below the roof after rain. Demand on 11 December with photos (template part A). Thirty days pass. The landlord answers on 20 January: "it's your ventilation". Yusuf asks a roofer for a written opinion (₪400): water penetration from the roof sealing. He decides **not** to deduct rent unilaterally, because the security is ₪15,000 and the landlord already disputes the cause; he records the loss of the room's use instead (the children sleep in the living room from 15 January to 30 April) and gives notice that he will claim it.
3. **The handover.** On 31 May 2027 the family leaves, photographs every room, and sends the photos. On 10 July the landlord transfers ₪9,000 and keeps ₪6,000 "for mould treatment and painting". He gave no prior notice and no chance to fix (s. 25י(ד)).
4. **Remedy table.**

| # | Item | Basis | ₪ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Water heater repair | s. 9(א)(1); urgent defect, 3-day period (s. 25ח(ב)) | 2,350 |
| 2 | Roofer's opinion | Reasonable cost of proving the cause | 400 |
| 3 | Loss of use of one of three rooms, 15.1–30.4 (3.5 months) | s. 9(א)(2) by analogy, as damages: one room ≈ 20% of the rental value × ₪5,000 × 3.5 | 3,500 |
| 4 | Security withheld | s. 25י(ג)–(ה): no permitted use, no notice; the mould was the landlord's defect | 6,000 |
| | **Total** | | **12,250** |

5. **Forum and filing.** Small claims in Tel Aviv–Jaffa, both tenants as plaintiffs (each signs), on the [small claim template](../../templates/small-claim-contract-consumer.md), within the ceiling (see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)). Exhibits: lease, receipt for the security, the 10 December messages with read ticks, the technician's invoice and note, the 11 December demand and photos, the landlord's 20 January reply, the roofer's opinion, the handover photos, the bank transfer of ₪9,000.

**Why line 3 is claimed as damages, not deducted.** Had Yusuf paid only 80% of the rent for three and a half months, the landlord could argue that ₪3,500 of rent was unpaid and lawfully keep that much of the security (s. 25י(ג)(1)), turning the hearing into a debate about Yusuf's arithmetic. Claiming it in court, with the roofer's opinion, leaves the burden where it belongs.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- Your own lease dispute (or supplied case D): the lease, the security receipt, messages, photos, invoices.
- The [rental repair and deposit letter](../../templates/rental-repair-and-deposit-letter.md) (parts A and B), the [small claim template](../../templates/small-claim-contract-consumer.md) and your [clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md).
- About two hours.

### Steps

1. **Scope (10 min).** Check that your lease is inside the residential chapter (s. 25טו) and note the security cap (s. 25י(ב)).
2. **Classify each defect (15 min).** Ordinary, urgent, emergency or caused by you; write one sentence of reasons for each, with the evidence.
3. **Demand (20 min).** Fill in part A: the defect, when it appeared, photos, why it is (or is not) urgent, the statutory period, and what you will do if it is not repaired. Send it with proof of delivery; log the receipt date and the deadline.
4. **Self-help decision (15 min).** For each defect not repaired in time, decide: repair and claim costs, reduce rent, or claim damages later. Write your reasons, including the security trap.
5. **End of lease (15 min).** Plan the handover: photos, meters, a written request for the security, the day-60 diary entry.
6. **Remedy table (20 min).** List every head of claim with its basis, calculation and exhibit.
7. **Small claim (25 min).** Draft the claim on the template, using the model in lesson 12.5 as a guide, with the remedy table and exhibit list. Decide: file (real, own name), send a final demand first, or keep as a dry run, and log the decision.

### Template

[Rental repair and deposit letter](../../templates/rental-repair-and-deposit-letter.md) (module 20); [small claim for a contract or consumer dispute](../../templates/small-claim-contract-consumer.md) (module 12).

### How you know it worked

- Every demand has a proof of receipt and a computed deadline.
- Each defect is classified with a reason a judge could accept.
- Your remedy table has a basis and an exhibit for every line, and your self-help did not create "unpaid rent".

### Self-check

- [ ] I know the 30-day and 3-day periods and when they start.
- [ ] I know the two remedies in s. 9(א) and the notice rule in s. 9(ב).
- [ ] I know the four permitted uses of the security and the prior-notice duty.
- [ ] I know why a disputed rent deduction can cost me part of my deposit.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Tenants send these demands and bring these claims in small claims in their own name every day; each co-tenant may sign. A company landlord can be sued in small claims but cannot sue there. In small claims a lawyer appears only with the court's leave (lesson [12.6](../module-12/lesson-06.md)).

### Where and how

- Letters: to the landlord's address in the lease and by message; the small claims court for the area of the flat or the landlord's residence (online or by hand; lesson [08.8](../module-08/lesson-08.md)).
- Ceiling, fee and periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); forum rules: [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md).
- Free help: tenants' rights organisations and municipal tenant-support services where they exist; Legal Aid's consumer service in some cases (see [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md)).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The landlord files an eviction claim, or threatens to change the locks.
- The defect is a safety hazard (gas, electricity, structural cracks) or affects health seriously.
- The claim, with counterclaims, is above the small-claims ceiling, or the lease is a protected tenancy.
- Rent-assistance or public-housing rules depend on the outcome (lesson 22.6).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phoning about the defect and never writing | No proof the clock started | Send a written demand now; confirm the call in writing | Always demand in writing with proof of delivery |
| Calling every defect "urgent" | Judge doubts the urgent ones too | Classify honestly; explain the urgent ones | Use the decision tree |
| Deducting 30% of the rent without a calculation | Landlord keeps that much of the security as "unpaid rent" | Pay the balance under protest and claim in court | Claim reduction as damages unless the calculation is solid |
| Repairing without the notice in s. 9(ב) | Landlord disputes the cost or the need | Show the demand, the delay and the invoice | Send notice of intent before the technician comes |
| No photos at handover | Word against word about "damage" | Ask neighbours or the next tenant for evidence | Photograph every room and meter on the day |

## Review

- The landlord repairs non-trivial defects not caused by the tenant within 30 days of receiving a demand, or 3 days if the flat is not reasonably habitable (s. 25ח(ב)).
- If he does not, the tenant may repair and claim reasonable costs (with prior notice unless it is urgent) or reduce the rent proportionately (s. 9).
- The security may be used only for the four purposes in s. 25י(ג), after notice and a chance to fix, and is returned within 60 days of the flat's return.
- Self-help that creates "unpaid rent" can cost you the deposit; claiming in small claims with a remedy table keeps the burden on the landlord.
