# 22.7 · The hearing in the labour court and tribunals

Lesson 12.6 prepared you for a small-claims hearing. This lesson adapts that preparation to the labour court, where a judge often sits with two public representatives, the expedited track compresses everything into one sitting, and the employer may answer your wage claim with an accusation. It also covers the informal tribunals of this module: the NII's medical appeals committee and the welfare appeals committee.

## Why it matters

Labour-court hearings are less formal than civil trials but more structured than small claims. The court "is not bound by the rules of evidence" (Labour Court Law s. 32), and on the expedited track it is not bound by procedure either (s. 31(ב)), so the judge decides what matters and in what order. A self-represented employee who arrives with an organised bundle, a two-minute account and a few precise questions is at no disadvantage against a manager or a lawyer. One who arrives with a story and no documents usually leaves with a compromise below what the documents would have won.

Tribunals are even less formal and even more dependent on the record: a committee decides on what it writes down. Your job there is to make sure it writes the right things.

## The law

### Who sits

> בית דין אזורי ידון בשלושה, שהם השופט – והוא יהיה אב בית הדין – ושני נציגי ציבור, האחד מנציגי העובדים והאחד מנציגי המעסיקים.

*Gist:* A regional labour court normally sits as a judge with two public representatives, one from employees and one from employers. ([Labour Court Law s. 18(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בית_הדין_לעבודה)) Proceedings in the Third Schedule (including NII medical appeals) are heard by a judge alone (s. 18(ב)(1)), and in a three-member case the judge alone handles the preliminary stage: checking pleadings, listing the issues, setting the order of the hearing, default judgments, judgments by consent and giving a settlement the force of a judgment (s. 18(ד); Fourth Schedule). Address the bench as "כבוד בית הדין".

### How the hearing runs

> [...] ייפתח הדיון בבית הדין האזורי בדברי פתיחה של התובע ולאחר מכן יישמעו עדיו [...]; לאחר מכן ישמע בית הדין את דברי הפתיחה של הנתבע ואת עדיו [...]

*Gist:* Subject to the judge's directions, the claimant opens and calls witnesses and other evidence, then the defendant; then the claimant sums up and the defendant replies, with a possible right of reply. ([reg. 51](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_בית_הדין_לעבודה_(סדרי_דין))) The court may order written summations and limit their length; missing the date for them is treated like not appearing (reg. 52).

### Evidence and witnesses

- **Not bound by the rules of evidence** (s. 32), and free to choose the procedure best suited to justice where none is set (s. 33). Documents speak more than in a civil court, but the court still weighs their source.
- **Affidavits:** the court may order that the facts relied on be verified by affidavit or a declaration before the registrar (reg. 117); the other side may require the declarant to attend for cross-examination, with notice in good time (reg. 118).
- **Witness summons:** the court may summon a witness on Form 9 (reg. 53); a party tells its own witness first that it intends to summon them (reg. 53(ג), applying the civil rules).

### Dates, absence and setting aside

Notice of the hearing date is sent at least 30 days ahead unless the matter is urgent (reg. 47). If the claimant does not appear, the court may dismiss or strike the claim; if the defendant does not appear, it may hear the claim in its absence or give judgment on the claim (reg. 49). A party who did not receive notice or did not appear may ask to set aside the decision within 30 days of receiving it (reg. 50).

### Ending by agreement

The court may give a judgment in agreed terms, even if the parties agreed only on the merits and left costs to the court (reg. 58). On the expedited track it may decide by compromise with the parties' consent (s. 31(ג)). If a case on the ordinary track ends in a settlement early (before the end of the third preliminary hearing), most of the fee is refunded (Fees Regulations reg. 5; not on the expedited track).

### The welfare appeals committee

You may appear and argue yourself or through a representative (reg. 4(א)); the committee may summon anyone whose evidence helps (reg. 4(ב)); it is not bound by the rules of evidence or court procedure (reg. 7); hearings are closed (reg. 13); it decides within 10 days of the last hearing, in writing with reasons (regs 10–11). ([Regulations](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שירותי_הסעד_(סדרי_הנוהל_בועדות_ערר)))

## Leading cases

### Key text: Labour Court Law ss. 31–33

There is no leading appellate ruling on how a self-represented employee should conduct a labour-court hearing; the court's own wide discretion is the rule. The controlling texts are s. 31 (the expedited track, compromise with consent, leave to appeal, stopping the expedited hearing), s. 32 (not bound by the rules of evidence) and s. 33 (procedure best suited to justice), read with regs 47–58 and 117–118. Read them before the hearing: [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בית_הדין_לעבודה).

### בג״ץ 3/58 ברמן נ׳ שר הפנים (1958)

Taught in lessons [14.4](../module-14/lesson-04.md) and 17.4: a fair opportunity to be heard is a rule of natural justice. Why it matters before tribunals: if a committee did not let you state your complaints, or did not record them, that is a defect you can raise on appeal (lesson [22.3](lesson-03.md)). Ask, politely and on the spot, that your complaints be recorded.

## How it works in practice

### Before the hearing

1. **Check the bench and track** in the summons: expedited or ordinary; single judge or a panel.
2. **Read the defence** (if one was ordered) and list every fact it disputes and every new allegation.
3. **Bundle:** three copies of every document (court, other side, you), tabbed in the order of your claim's annexes, plus anything that answers the defence.
4. **Witnesses:** decide who proves a disputed fact you cannot prove by documents; tell them, and ask the court for a summons if they may not come willingly.
5. **Prepare the [hearing-day kit](../../templates/hearing-day-kit.md)**, adapted: a two-minute opening, the disputed facts and their proof, short closed questions for the other side, the court's likely questions, and your settlement range.

### Settlement range

Work out three numbers before you go and do not show them: the sum you would accept today (with costs and the time saved in mind), the sum you will not go below, and what you expect from a judgment. On the expedited track a judge commonly proposes a compromise; the public representatives may help you see how an employer would see it. Agreeing is your choice; you may ask for a few minutes outside to think.

### At the hearing

- Stand when you speak to the bench; speak to the bench, not to the other side.
- Open in two minutes: who you are, the job, the ending, what you claim and the one document that proves it.
- When the other side accuses, do not argue back. Ask your prepared questions; point to the documents.
- If something important is said and not recorded, ask the court politely to record it in the protocol.
- Before leaving, make sure you know the next date or that judgment will be given, and how you will receive it.

### Before a tribunal

- **Medical appeals committee:** bring the one-page summary and copies of every document; describe your daily limitations concretely; ask that your complaints be recorded; ask to see the summary of complaints if allowed (lesson [17.9](../module-17/lesson-09.md)).
- **Welfare appeals committee:** a short written outline, the department's decision, your documents; ask that the department's file be produced (reg. 5); state the help you ask for.

## Worked example

**Supplied case A, the hearing.** Rawan's expedited claim (lesson 22.2) is set for one hearing in the Haifa Regional Labour Court, before a judge and two public representatives. The employer files a short defence: Rawan "took tips and cash from the till"; the deduction of ₪824 was "agreed"; she was dismissed "for cause", so no notice and no severance are due. The restaurant's operations manager appears for the company.

**Her preparation.**

1. **Disputed facts:** (a) cause for dismissal; (b) consent to the deduction; (c) the leave balance (the employer says 3 days, she says 8).
2. **Documents:** the WhatsApp dismissal message ("מהיום אין צורך שתגיעי"), which mentions nothing about theft; her August payslip; her leave record from the payslips; the restaurant's shared-till schedule she photographed in July; no written consent to any deduction exists.
3. **Questions for the manager (short, closed):** "Was any complaint about Rawan made to the police?" "Was she invited to a hearing before the dismissal?" "Is there a document in which she agreed to the deduction?" "How many waiters used the same till on 28 August?"
4. **Settlement range:** today ₪16,000; floor ₪13,000; expected judgment about ₪18,000–20,436 (the leave balance and the notice are the weakest items if the court partly accepts the "cause" story).

**The hearing.** Rawan opens in under two minutes. The manager repeats the theft allegation; Rawan asks her four questions: no police complaint, no hearing, no written consent, five waiters on the shared till. The judge observes that dismissal "for cause" without a hearing and without evidence is unlikely to deprive her of severance, and proposes a compromise of ₪17,500 within 30 days, each side bearing its own costs. After a few minutes outside, Rawan accepts, on condition that the employer also delivers form 161 and the release letter to the pension fund. The court gives the agreement the force of a judgment (s. 31(ג); reg. 58).

**Her log entry the same day.** Date, bench, who attended; the agreed sum, the 30-day payment date, the documents to be delivered; "judgment by consent; no appeal". Next action: diary day 31 for enforcement if unpaid (lesson 22.8).

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- Your filed claim (lesson 22.2) and any defence, or your NII appeal (22.3) or welfare appeal (22.6); or supplied case A with the defence above.
- The [hearing-day kit](../../templates/hearing-day-kit.md) (module 12) and your [clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md).
- About two hours, plus one hour watching a public labour-court hearing if you can (hearings in employee–employer cases are generally public; family and some other hearings are closed).

### Steps

1. **Bench and track (5 min).** From the summons, write who will sit and on which track.
2. **Disputes (20 min).** List every fact the other side disputes, and the document or witness that proves your version.
3. **Bundle (20 min).** Three copies, tabbed, in annex order, plus the answers to the defence.
4. **Opening (15 min).** Write and time a two-minute opening; read it aloud twice.
5. **Questions (20 min).** Write no more than six short, closed questions for the other side's witness, one fact each.
6. **Likely questions (15 min).** Write the five questions the judge or the public representatives are most likely to ask you, with one-sentence answers.
7. **Settlement range (10 min).** Today, floor, expected judgment; the conditions you will add (documents, payment date).
8. **Tribunal version (15 min).** If your matter is before a committee: a one-page summary, the documents, the complaints you will ask to be recorded.

### Template

[Hearing-day kit (ערכת יום דיון)](../../templates/hearing-day-kit.md) (module 12), adapted: in part א add the bench and the track; in part ז add the public representatives' likely questions; in part ח add non-money conditions (form 161, release letter, a reference).

### How you know it worked

- Every disputed fact has a document or a witness against it in your kit.
- Your opening takes under two minutes and names the decisive document.
- Your questions are short, closed and do not argue.
- You know your settlement range and the conditions you will ask for.

### Self-check

- [ ] I know who sits in my case and what the judge alone may do.
- [ ] I know the order of argument and that the court may order written summations.
- [ ] I know what happens if I do not appear and how to ask to set aside a decision within 30 days.
- [ ] I know how to make sure a tribunal records my complaints.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Self-represented employees appear in the labour court every day, and the court is used to them. A company may appear by a manager or authorised officer (reg. 23); you may have the help of your union's appointee (s. 34). Before the welfare appeals committee you may appear yourself (reg. 4); before the medical appeals committee in general disability, your treating doctor or a specialist may accompany you (lesson 17.9).

### Where and how

- The regional labour court in the summons (in person or by video if the court directs); bring ID and your bundle.
- Watch a hearing first if you can; ask the secretariat which halls hear individual disputes that day.
- Free help: your union; Legal Aid in NII matters; [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The court moves your case from the expedited track to the ordinary track and the other side is represented (s. 31(ה)).
- The employer files a counterclaim, or the theft allegation becomes a police complaint.
- You are asked to sign a settlement that waives rights you have not calculated (pension, future claims).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arguing with the manager across the room | The bench loses patience; your documents are not heard | Stop; address the bench; point to the tab | Prepare closed questions; speak to the bench |
| No copies for the court and the other side | Delay; documents not considered | Ask for a short recess to copy | Three tabbed copies |
| Accepting a compromise that omits form 161 and the fund release | Money paid, severance in the fund still blocked | Ask the court to add the documents to the agreement | Put non-money conditions in your range |
| Missing a hearing because the notice went to an old address | Claim struck (reg. 49) | Apply to set aside within 30 days (reg. 50) | Keep your address for service updated |
| Leaving the medical committee without checking the record | Complaints missing; no ground for appeal | Raise the omission in writing at once | Ask that complaints be recorded |

## Review

- The labour court usually sits as a judge and two public representatives; NII medical appeals are heard by a judge alone; the judge alone runs the preliminary stage.
- The claimant opens, then the defendant; the court is not bound by the rules of evidence and may order affidavits, cross-examination and written summations.
- On the expedited track, expect one hearing and a compromise proposal; know your range and your non-money conditions; a settlement can be given the force of a judgment.
- Absence can end your case; a decision given in your absence can be set aside on application within 30 days.
- Before tribunals, the record is everything: bring a summary and documents, and ask that your complaints be recorded.
