# 22.8 · Closing the file and when to get a lawyer

A matter is not finished when the hearing ends. In this last lesson you close your Clinic II file: check that what was ordered or agreed actually arrived, enforce it if it did not, decide within the appeal period whether to go further, and write the closing memo that keeps the deadlines that outlive the case. You also run the get-a-lawyer checklist one last time, across all five tracks of the module, and record where free help fits your situation.

## Why it matters

Self-represented parties who win often lose at the end: the employer pays the agreed sum but not the release letter to the pension fund; the NII reverses its decision but pays arrears from the wrong date; the landlord's transfer is short by the "bank fees"; a family agreement approved as a judgment is breached and no one opens an execution file. Others miss the short appeal periods of the labour court, or sign a "final settlement" on the last day that the employer later waves at them.

Closing also teaches the module's last skill: knowing when you have reached the limit of what you should do alone. A clinic that ends with a well-chosen referral, with a one-page summary the lawyer can read in five minutes, is a success.

## The law

### Enforcing a labour-court judgment

> פסק דין של בית הדין דינו, בכל הנוגע להוצאה לפועל, כדין פסק דין של בית משפט, והוראות חוק ההוצאה לפועל, תשכ״ז–1967, והתקנות לפיו, יחולו בשינויים המחוייבים.

*Gist:* A labour-court judgment, including one giving an agreement the force of a judgment, is enforced through the Execution Office like any court judgment. ([Labour Court Law s. 37](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בית_הדין_לעבודה)) The steps, documents and timing are in lessons [08.7](../module-08/lesson-07.md) and [12.7](../module-12/lesson-07.md); the same route enforces a small-claims judgment and an approved family agreement.

### Appeal periods in the labour courts

> המועד להגשת ערעור על פסק דין של בית דין אזורי הוא שלושים ימים מיום השימוע, או מהיום שבו הומצא למערער פסק הדין אם ניתן בהעדר הצדדים [...]

*Gist:* A regional labour court's judgment on the ordinary track is appealable as of right to the National Labour Court within 30 days of its delivery in your presence, or of service if given in your absence ([reg. 73](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_בית_הדין_לעבודה_(סדרי_דין)); s. 26). Leave to appeal another decision is sought within 15 days (reg. 74). An **expedited-track** judgment is appealable only with leave of the National Labour Court (s. 31(ד)); a regional court's judgment on an appeal from an NII appeals committee needs leave (NII Law s. 213), sought within 30 days (reg. 75). A judgment by consent is not appealed; it is performed or enforced. Periods: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### Waivers on the way out

> פשרה לענין פיצויי פיטורים והודאת סילוק לא יהא להם תוקף, אלא אם נערכו בכתב ונאמר בהם במפורש שהם לגבי פיצויי הפיטורים.

*Gist:* A compromise or final-settlement receipt about severance has no effect unless it is in writing and says expressly that it concerns severance. ([Severance Pay Law s. 29](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_פיצויי_פיטורים)) More generally, protective labour rights are cogent, and the labour courts read a general "I have no further claims" narrowly (lessons [17.2](../module-17/lesson-02.md), [17.4](../module-17/lesson-04.md)). On the last day sign only a receipt for the sums you actually received; let a settlement that waives rights go through the court (reg. 58) or a lawyer.

### Late payment after the case

If an employer pays wages or severance late, even after a judgment or agreement, the separate right to late-wage compensation must be claimed within 60 days of receiving the late payment, or one year from the delay, whichever is earlier ([Wage Protection Law s. 17א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_השכר)); a judgment debt in the Execution Office carries its own linkage and interest instead. Record the payment date.

### Free representation that stays available

Legal Aid represents individuals against the NII in the labour court, including on leave to appeal, without a means test (NII Law s. 397). For other matters Legal Aid applies its means and merit tests (lesson [12.8](../module-12/lesson-08.md)).

## Leading cases

### רע״א 8120/08 (no "close friend" in court)

Lesson [22.1](lesson-01.md): only a lawyer may represent another person in court unless a statute says otherwise. Why it matters at the end of the clinic: when you hand the matter over, hand it to a lawyer, Legal Aid or (in the labour court) your union's appointee, not to a helpful relative. [Full text (supremedecisions)](https://supremedecisions.court.gov.il/Home/Download?path=HebrewVerdicts%2F08%2F200%2F081%2Fv04&fileName=08081200_v04.txt&type=2) (⚠️ host unreachable on 17 October 2026).

### Key text: the closing rules in each track

There is no single leading case on closing a file; the decisive texts are the enforcement and appeal provisions above, and for each track: the medical re-assessment rules (lesson 17.9), the Dispute Resolution Law's s. 3(ט) on approved agreements (lesson 22.4), and the Rental and Borrowing Law's s. 25י(ה) on the deposit (lesson 22.5).

## How it works in practice

### The closing checklist

| Check | Employment | NII | Family | Tenancy | Welfare |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Money arrived, right amount, right date | Agreed or adjudged sum | Arrears from the right date | Maintenance from the first due date | Deposit, costs | Arrears; the dependent benefit |
| Documents arrived | Form 161, release letter to the fund, corrected payslips | Decision letter with the new degree | Copy of the judgment | Receipt | Written decisions |
| If not | Execution file after the payment date (s. 37) | Write; then the labour court | Execution file; NII maintenance guarantee if eligible | Execution file | Written reminder; appeal |
| Appeal? | 30 days as of right; leave on expedited (s. 31(ד)) | Leave to the National Labour Court, 30 days (reg. 75) | Family-court appeal periods (lesson 18.1) | Leave to appeal small claims (lesson 12.7) | Administrative Affairs Court after the committee |
| Surviving deadlines | Late-wage compensation 60 days | Re-assessment; temporary degree end date | Review clause; child turns 6, 15, 18 | Next lease: security cap | Renewal or re-check dates |

### The closing memo

One page at the front of the file:

1. **Outcome:** what was decided or agreed, by whom, on what date; the file number.
2. **Money:** what was due, what arrived, when; any balance and what you did about it.
3. **Documents:** what arrived; where the originals are.
4. **Surviving deadlines:** each with its date and a reminder in your calendar.
5. **What I would do differently:** three sentences for next time.
6. **Archive:** where the file is kept and for how long (keep employment and benefit files for at least the limitation period; keep family judgments indefinitely).

### The last get-a-lawyer run

Run the [get-a-lawyer checklist](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md) again with year-2 red flags added:

| Red flag | Track |
|---|---|
| The employer counterclaims, alleges theft, or a police complaint is filed | Employment |
| Dismissal during pregnancy, fertility treatment or reserve duty; discrimination or harassment | Employment |
| You lost on law in the regional court and want leave to appeal | NII, employment |
| The NII demands repayment or alleges false statements | NII, welfare |
| Violence, a child at risk, a party abroad, a religious-court filing race | Family |
| Property, pensions, a business or a home you own are being divided | Family |
| Eviction claim, protected tenancy, safety hazard | Tenancy |
| Eviction from public housing | Welfare |

One red flag: get a lawyer (or Legal Aid). Two amber signs (the other side is represented and the stakes exceed a month's income; a deadline you cannot meet; facts you cannot prove without disclosure): get at least one consultation, and bring the one-page summary.

## Worked example

**Closing supplied case A.** The judgment by consent (lesson 22.7) ordered the employer to pay Rawan ₪17,500 within 30 days and deliver form 161 and the release letter to the pension fund.

1. **Day 30.** ₪17,500 arrives on day 28. Form 161 arrives; the release letter does not.
2. **The missing letter.** Rawan writes to the employer the same day, quoting the judgment, with a 7-day deadline. No answer. Because the judgment orders the letter's delivery, she opens an execution file for that part of the judgment (s. 37; lessons 08.7 and 12.7). The letter arrives a week after the warning.
3. **No appeal.** A judgment by consent is not appealed; she notes that in the memo.
4. **Surviving deadlines.** None for late-wage compensation (the payment was on time). She diarises the pension fund's release of the severance balance and a check of her annual tax position (form 161 matters for the tax on severance; ⚠️ outside this course's scope).
5. **Closing memo.** Outcome; ₪17,500 on [date]; form 161 and the release letter on [dates]; execution file closed; "next time: put the release letter in the settlement as a condition with a date and a daily penalty".

**Closing the other supplied cases (one line each).** B: Moshe's matter is back before a new committee; his file stays open with the new hearing date and the 30-day and 60-day periods ready to restart. C: Almaz's approved agreement is a judgment; she diarises the review clause and notes that if maintenance stops she can open an execution file and check eligibility for the NII's maintenance-payment guarantee (lesson 18.5). D: Yusuf's small-claims judgment for ₪12,250 is paid in full; he closes the file and keeps the lease and photos for seven years. E: Natalia's income support and rent assistance are restored; she diarises the renewal of the rent-assistance approval and the NII's periodic income check.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- Your clinic case file with the outcome (or the supplied cases and their outcomes above).
- The [get-a-lawyer checklist](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md) and your [clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md).
- About 90 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Compare (15 min).** Put the judgment, decision or agreement beside your bank statement and the documents received. List every gap.
2. **Chase (15 min).** For each gap, write a short demand quoting the judgment or decision, with a date. Diary the date.
3. **Enforce or appeal (20 min).** If a gap remains after the date, plan the execution file (lesson 12.7). If you are considering an appeal, write the period, the court and whether you need leave; decide by day 10 of the period whether to get advice.
4. **Surviving deadlines (10 min).** List each deadline that outlives the case and put it in your calendar.
5. **Get-a-lawyer run (15 min).** Run the checklist with the year-2 red flags; write the result and, if needed, the one-page summary.
6. **Closing memo (15 min).** Write the six parts and file it at the front of the case file.

### Template

- [Get-a-lawyer checklist and one-page case summary](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md) (module 12), with the year-2 red flags above.
- [Clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md) (module 12): add the closing memo at the front.

### How you know it worked

- Every item in the judgment, decision or agreement is marked "received" with a date, or has a chasing step with a date.
- Every surviving deadline is in your calendar.
- Someone else could pick up your file from the closing memo alone.

### Self-check

- [ ] I know that labour-court judgments are enforced through the Execution Office (s. 37).
- [ ] I know the 30-day appeal as of right, the leave rule for expedited judgments and the 30-day leave period in NII appeals.
- [ ] I signed nothing on the way out except receipts for sums actually received.
- [ ] I know my red flags and where my free help is.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Closing, chasing and opening an execution file are within what an individual does alone (lesson 12.7). An appeal to the National Labour Court can be filed in person, but leave to appeal and appeals on law are where a lawyer, Legal Aid or your union's representative add most value.

### Where and how

- Execution Office: online or at a branch (lessons 08.7, 12.7).
- National Labour Court: Jerusalem; periods in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- Free help by track: Legal Aid (NII matters without a means test; family, tenancy and employment on means and merit); your union; the court's assistance unit (family); the social services department (welfare, s. 2ב); university legal clinics; see [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- Any red flag in the table above appears.
- The other side appeals, or applies to set aside the judgment.
- Enforcement fails because the debtor is a company that stopped trading, or claims insolvency.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Closing the file when the money arrives, before the documents | Severance balance stuck in the fund | Chase, then enforce the documents part | Put documents in the closing checklist |
| Counting the appeal period from the day you read the judgment online | Appeal out of time | Ask to extend with reasons | Count from delivery in your presence or service (regs 73–75) |
| Signing "no further claims" on the last day | The employer relies on it | Argue it is not specific (s. 29) and not informed | Sign receipts only |
| Not diarising a temporary NII degree's end date | Benefit stops without your noticing | Claim again at once | List surviving deadlines in the memo |
| Handing the file to a relative to "deal with it" | No one may represent you but a lawyer or a statutory representative | Take it back; use Legal Aid or a lawyer | Remember s. 20 and רע״א 8120/08 |

## Review

- Close against the judgment or agreement: money, documents and dates; chase gaps, then enforce through the Execution Office (s. 37).
- Appeals: 30 days as of right from an ordinary labour-court judgment; expedited judgments only with leave; NII appeal judgments with leave within 30 days; consent judgments are performed, not appealed.
- Sign only receipts on the way out; waivers of protective rights are read narrowly (s. 29).
- Write a closing memo with the surviving deadlines; run the get-a-lawyer checklist one last time and record your free help.
