# 22.1 · Choosing the matter and building the file

Clinic II takes one real matter of your own, at work, with National Insurance, in your family or in your home, through the forum the law gives it: the labour court, the medical appeals committee, the family court's assistance unit, the small claims court or a welfare review body. In this lesson you choose the matter, identify its track and every deadline that can end it, and open the file and case log you will carry through lessons 22.2 to 22.8.

## Why it matters

Clinic I (module 12) was a civil money dispute against a shop or a landlord: one forum, one long limitation period, an opponent you could walk away from. Year-2 matters are different in three ways that decide how you choose.

First, **the opponent is often someone you depend on**: your employer, the State's benefits agency, the other parent of your children, the landlord of the flat you still live in. The legal remedy and the relationship pull in different directions, and the file must record both.

Second, **the deadlines are short and many are fatal**. A medical appeal is due 30 days after notice; an appeal on law 60 days after the appeals committee's decision; the first filer's window after a family stay is 15 days; late-wage compensation must be claimed within 60 days of receiving the late wage. Module 12's seven-year limitation period teaches the wrong instinct here.

Third, **each matter has its own forum and its own procedure**: the labour court is not bound by the rules of evidence and has an expedited track for small employee claims (lesson [17.9](../module-17/lesson-09.md)); NII medical questions go first to an internal committee; family claims are frozen until a bare request to settle has run its course (lesson [18.9](../module-18/lesson-09.md)). Choosing the matter is therefore choosing the track, and the track sets the calendar.

The theory of each track was taught in modules 17, 18 and 20. This module does not repeat it; it links back and concentrates on running the file.

## The law

### Your own matter only: Bar Association Law s. 20

Only a lawyer may represent another person in court, draft legal documents for another, or give legal advice as an occupation ([s. 20](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לשכת_עורכי_הדין)). The clinic is for your own matter. A spouse's dismissal or a parent's disability claim is not yours, even if you live with the consequences. The labour court and the NII have their own, narrow exceptions (below); none of them makes you your relative's representative in court.

### Who else may stand with you

> בבית הדין רשאי בעל דין להיות מיוצג על ידי מי שקבע לכך ארגון עובדים או מעבידים שהמיוצג חבר בו; הייצוג יהיה על פי הרשאה מיוחדת בלבד [...]

*Gist:* In the labour court a party may be represented, besides a lawyer, by a person appointed by the workers' or employers' organisation of which the party is a member, under a special authorisation. ([Labour Court Law s. 34](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בית_הדין_לעבודה)) Before the NII itself, a close friend, a union representative and others listed in NII Law s. 386 may represent you (lesson [17.9](../module-17/lesson-09.md)). In small claims, a non-lawyer helper needs the court's leave (lesson [12.6](../module-12/lesson-06.md)).

### Your right to your own file

> כל אדם זכאי לעיין בעצמו, או על ידי בא־כוחו שהרשהו בכתב או על ידי אפוטרופוסו, במידע האישי שעליו המוחזק במאגר מידע.

*Gist:* Everyone may inspect the personal information about them held in a database, in Hebrew, Arabic or English (s. 13(ב)); medical information that could seriously harm the person may be given to their doctor instead (s. 13(ג)). ([Privacy Protection Law s. 13](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_הפרטיות)) This is how you get the NII's medical file, the authority's eligibility file or your employer's attendance records before you write a word.

### Deadlines that end a matter

> החליט המוסד בתביעה ונמסרה לתובע הודעה על כך, תוגש תובענה לבית הדין לעבודה תוך שניים עשר חודשים מיום מסירת ההודעה לתובע [...]

*Gist:* A claim against the NII must be filed in the labour court within 12 months of notice of its decision, and not before 30 days after claiming unless it has decided. ([Time for Claims Regulations reg. 1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הביטוח_הלאומי_(מועדים_להגשת_תובענות)))

> הזכות לפיצויי הלנת שכר, להבדיל משכר עבודה, תתיישן אם לא הוגשה תובענה [...] תוך שנה מהיום שבו רואים את השכר כמולן, או תוך 60 ימים מהיום שקיבל העובד את השכר שבו קשור הפיצוי, הכל לפי המוקדם [...]

*Gist:* Wages themselves follow the ordinary limitation period, but the separate right to late-wage compensation expires within one year of the wage becoming late, or 60 days after you received the late wage, whichever is earlier (the court may extend the 60 days to 90). ([Wage Protection Law s. 17א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_השכר)) Severance is "late" 15 days after the date it was due (s. 20(ב)).

The other short periods (medical appeal, appeal on law, the family-court 15-day window, the landlord's 30-day and 3-day repair periods, the 60-day deposit return) are collected, with sources, in [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md) and in the deadline table below.

## Leading cases

### רע״א 8120/08 (no "close friend" in court)

The Supreme Court held that, absent an express statutory provision, only a licensed lawyer may represent a party in court; there is no general right to be represented by a relative or a "close friend" (ידיד קרוב). Why it matters for choosing: the matter you run must be yours, because no one but a lawyer can stand in for you in court, and you cannot stand in for anyone else. The narrow exceptions are those a statute creates (Labour Court Law s. 34; NII Law s. 386 before the NII itself; Courts Law s. 63 with leave in small claims). [Full text (supremedecisions)](https://supremedecisions.court.gov.il/Home/Download?path=HebrewVerdicts%2F08%2F200%2F081%2Fv04&fileName=08081200_v04.txt&type=2) (⚠️ host unreachable on 17 October 2026; citation from the course research file).

### Key text: the track statutes

For most clinic matters the decisive text is the statute that sends the matter to its forum: Labour Court Law s. 24 (exclusive jurisdiction over employee–employer claims), NII Law s. 391 and the medical-appeal sections (lesson 17.9), the Dispute Resolution in Family Disputes Law s. 3 (lesson 18.9), the Rental and Borrowing Law ss. 25ח and 25י (lesson 20.3) and the Income Support Law s. 26 (lesson 17.8). Put the section that gives the forum, the one that sets the deadline and the one that gives the remedy on page 1 of your file.

## How it works in practice

### Five filters for a Clinic II matter

| Filter | Pass | Fail: choose another matter or another route |
|---|---|---|
| Yours | Your own job, your own benefit, your own children and spouse, your own tenancy | Your partner's dismissal, a parent's disability claim, a friend's lease |
| A known track | You can name the forum and the first step (table below) | You cannot tell whether it is labour, NII, family or civil: do lesson [01.7](../module-01/lesson-07.md) first |
| In time | Every fatal deadline is at least two weeks away | A deadline passed or is days away and you have no documents |
| Provable | You hold, or can request, the payslips, decisions, medical file, lease or messages | Only your word, and the other side holds everything |
| Manageable risk | The worst realistic outcome is losing the claim or a modest costs order | Fraud allegations, a large NII repayment demand, violence, a child at risk, a counterclaim far above your claim |

If none of your matters passes, use one of the supplied cases below.

### The track map

| Matter | Forum and first step | First fatal deadline | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unpaid wages or final settlement | Letter to the employer; then the regional labour court (expedited track if within the ceiling) | Late-wage compensation: 60 days after receiving a late wage; severance late after 15 days | 22.2 (theory 17.3, 17.4, 17.10) |
| NII medical degree too low | Appeal (ערר) to the medical appeals committee | 30 days from notice (+30 with NII consent) | 22.3 (17.9, 17.11) |
| NII non-medical refusal (entitlement, income, residence) | Claim in the regional labour court (or ask the NII to reconsider first) | 12 months from notice | 22.3, 22.6 (17.9) |
| Child maintenance, parenting, property between spouses | Request to settle a family dispute, then meetings at the assistance unit | 15-day window for the first filer after the stay | 22.4 (18.9) |
| Repairs or deposit in a residential lease | Repair demand or deposit demand to the landlord; then small claims | Landlord: 30 days (urgent 3 days) to repair; 60 days to return the deposit | 22.5 (20.3, 20.10, 12.5) |
| Income support, rent assistance, welfare department | NII reconsideration or labour court; the Ministry's appeal route; the welfare appeals committee | 12 months (NII); the Ministry's period (⚠️ manual check); "reasonable time" (welfare) | 22.6 (17.8) |

### The file

Use the [clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md) from module 12, with three additions for year-2 matters:

1. **Track sheet.** On the cover page, write the forum, the internal step (if any) before it, and the appeal route after it.
2. **Deadline table.** Every fatal deadline, the event it runs from (notice delivered, decision given, last meeting, demand received), the date of that event and how you prove it (envelope, email header, signature on delivery).
3. **Relationship note.** One paragraph: what you need from this person or body after the dispute (references, continued benefits, co-parenting, staying in the flat), and what you will not do to protect it.

### Get your file first

Before writing to anyone, ask for the documents the other side holds: payslips, attendance and pension reports from the employer (the [payslip worksheet](../../templates/payslip-and-final-settlement-worksheet.md) lists them); the NII medical file and the committee minutes; the Ministry's eligibility decision with reasons. A written request under Privacy Protection Law s. 13 costs nothing to send and puts a date on the record.

**Rawan through the five filters.** Hers: yes, her own employment. Track: labour court; the amount is within the expedited ceiling (see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)), so the expedited track. In time: the final settlement was due on 31 August; severance became "late" 15 days later (Wage Protection Law s. 20(ב)); nothing is near expiry, but late-wage compensation on any late payment she accepts must be claimed within 60 days of receiving it. ## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### You need

- A list of the work, benefit, family and housing problems in your life in the last year, or the supplied docket.
- Every document you hold for the matter you pick: contract, payslips, decisions and the envelopes they came in, lease, messages.
- The [clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md), the [forum check worksheet](../../templates/forum-check-worksheet.md) and the [get-a-lawyer checklist](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md).
- About 90 minutes.

### Steps

1. **Shortlist (15 min).** Write down up to three matters. Run each through the five filters; record pass or fail with one reason.
2. **Choose and name the track (10 min).** Pick one that passes all five. Write the forum, the internal step before it and the appeal after it, from the track map.
3. **Deadline table (20 min).** List every fatal deadline in the matter, the event it runs from, the date and your proof of that date. Put each deadline, and a reminder one week earlier, in your calendar now.
4. **Parties (10 min).** The employer's legal name and company number (from the payslip and the Companies Registrar search), the NII branch, the landlord's ID from the lease, or the other parent's details.
5. **Request your file (15 min).** Draft one short request for the documents the other side holds, citing Privacy Protection Law s. 13 where it is personal information, or the payslip and Notice to Employee duties for an employer.
6. **Relationship note (5 min).** Write the paragraph on what you need to keep.
7. **Open the case log (15 min).** Enter every past event you can document; then the next action with its date.

### Template

- [Clinic case file](../../templates/clinic-case-file.md) (module 12), with the three year-2 additions above.
- [Forum check worksheet](../../templates/forum-check-worksheet.md) (lesson 01.7).
- [Get-a-lawyer checklist](../../templates/get-a-lawyer-checklist.md) (lesson 12.8): run it once now.

### How you know it worked

- A stranger reading your cover page can say who owes what to whom, in which forum, and by when you must act.
- Every deadline has a start event and a proof of its date.
- Your request for your file is ready to send, dated and addressed to the right body.

### Self-check

- [ ] The matter is mine, not my partner's, parent's or friend's.
- [ ] I can name the forum, the internal step before it and the appeal route after it.
- [ ] Every fatal deadline is in my calendar with a reminder.
- [ ] I have asked, or am about to ask, for my own file.

## Without a lawyer

### Can you do this yourself?

Yes. Choosing the matter and building the file need no permission. Individuals may act for themselves in the labour court, before the NII and its committees, in the family court and in small claims (see [Can I act without a lawyer?](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md)). Helping a relative understand their papers is fine; running their file is not (s. 20).

### Where and how

- Track and forum rules: [can I act without a lawyer](../../references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md); deadlines and ceilings: [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); free help by subject: [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).
- Free help that fits year-2 matters: Legal Aid (NII matters without a means test, lesson 17.9), your union if you are a member (Labour Court Law s. 34), the assistance unit beside the family court (lesson 18.9), the municipal social services department, which must help you realise your rights (Welfare Services Law s. 2ב).

### Stop and get a lawyer when

- The matter mixes tracks you cannot separate: a work accident that is also a tort claim, a dismissal during pregnancy or fertility treatment, divorce with property abroad.
- The other side alleges fraud, theft or violence, or the NII demands a large repayment.
- A fatal deadline is days away and you do not yet have the decision or the documents.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Counting a deadline from the date on the letter instead of the date it reached you | You file "late" or panic early | Find the proof of delivery (envelope, email, signature) and recount | Record the delivery date and its proof the day the letter arrives |
| Choosing your spouse's dismissal because the household lost the income | You are not the party; s. 20 problem if you run it | Your spouse files in their own name; you may help them understand | Apply filter 1 first |
| Treating a medical NII decision as a labour-court matter | Appeal on law only; the medical point is lost | File the medical appeal within its period first | Use the track map |
| Starting a family claim before the request to settle | The secretariat refuses it | File the bare request first (lesson 18.9) | Read the track map before drafting |
| No record of what the other side holds | You cannot prove your case | Send a written request for the file | Request your file before your demand letter |

## Review

- A Clinic II matter must be yours, on a known track, in time, provable and of manageable risk.
- The track sets the calendar: medical appeal 30 days, appeal on law 60 days, NII claim 12 months, family window 15 days, late-wage compensation 60 days after receipt.
- The file is module 12's clinic case file plus a track sheet, a deadline table with proof of each start date, and a relationship note.
- Ask for your own file first; Privacy Protection Law s. 13 gives you the right to inspect personal information about you.
