# Neutrality QA: modules 1–12

Date: 2026-10-17. Standard: plan §9 (each side's strongest legal argument, attributed, plus the current law; no verdict in the course voice; worked examples rotate communities without stereotypes; no quiz answer that is a political opinion).

## Scope and method

- Read in full: 06.1, 06.2, 06.4, 06.6, 10.2, 10.3; the contested sections of 06.3, 06.5, 06.7, 06.8, 09.4, 09.5, 09.6, 10.4, 01.3, 02.1, 02.4, 04.7, 05.1, 05.5, 05.8, 07.1, 07.2, 07.4, 08.1, 11.1, 11.3.
- Keyword sweep of all lessons and projects of modules 1–12 for contested topics and loaded language (reform, protest, police, Haredi, Arab, territories, Nation-State, draft, religion, marriage, LGBT, "unelected", "attack on" and similar).
- Every quiz, module quiz and question-bank item touching a contested topic was extracted and read (106 items), along with all module 6 lesson quizzes.
- Worked-example opening lines for all 99 lessons, plus every project's supplied case, were checked for rotation and stereotyping.

Overall: module 6 (judicial review, constituent authority, 2023–2026 dispute), module 9 (theory) and module 10 (religious law) already present both sides with attribution and state the current law. No quiz or question-bank item has a political opinion as its correct answer: the "neutrality" items reward the fairly stated argument and mark slogans and appeals to authority as wrong. Worked examples rotate well across Jewish (secular, Haredi, Mizrahi, Ethiopian, FSU), Arab (Muslim, Christian), Druze and Bedouin characters, and offenders are mostly unnamed.
