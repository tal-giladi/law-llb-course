## One-line summary

The Israeli law degree, course by course, with a practice track that teaches you to handle your own legal affairs without a lawyer.

## Who it is for

Adults in Israel with no legal background who want university-level knowledge of Israeli law and to run their own legal matters; pre-law students.

## What you will learn

- Read and research any Israeli statute or ruling for free, and write a legal memo and letter.
- The core of private law: contracts, torts, property, special contracts, family and inheritance.
- Criminal law and procedure, including your rights as a victim, witness or suspect.
- Constitutional and administrative law, and how to challenge a public authority.
- Civil procedure: from the demand letter to small claims, the magistrates' court, appeals and the Execution Office.
- Labour, National Insurance, tax and consumer law in practice.
- When you can act for yourself, where and how, and when to get a lawyer.

## Risk notice (shown to learners before they start)

This course is not legal advice and does not make you a lawyer. Acting for yourself can cost you a case, costs or a missed deadline, so check current rules, act only in your own name, and get help from Legal Aid, a clinic or a lawyer when the stakes are high.
