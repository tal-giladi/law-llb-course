# Who may act without a lawyer, forum by forum (research, 2026-10-16)

Research by a sub-agent; Wikisource law texts are unofficial consolidations; check against the official text before quoting. "Unverified" = no official source found.

## General rules

- Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20: only a lawyer may, for another person or for pay, represent before courts, tribunals, arbitrators and listed registrars/offices; draft legal documents; give legal advice or opinions. https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לשכת_עורכי_הדין
- s. 21 exceptions: religious courts and military courts; representation regulated by another statute (Income Tax Ordinance named); patent agents; accountants within their law; unions/employer organisations in labour arbitration and labour-agreement matters; opinions requested by a lawyer or a state authority; foreign lawyers in some arbitrations. No family-member exception, no company-employee exception.
- s. 22: right to a lawyer before state and public bodies (not an exception to s. 20).
- Civil Procedure Regulations 2018, reg. 170: a party may take any step "בעצמו או בידי עורך דין" unless a law says otherwise; reg. 172א guardian for a party without capacity. https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי
- No lay friend: רע"א 8120/08 — absent an express provision, only a licensed lawyer may represent; no "ידיד קרוב". https://supremedecisions.court.gov.il/Home/Download?path=HebrewVerdicts%2F08%2F200%2F081%2Fv04&fileName=08081200_v04.txt&type=2
- Corporations need a lawyer in the civil courts: the known case-law rule; Unverified (no free source found). Regs 37(ט), 61(ב) only cover a corporation's representative at mediation intake and pre-trial.

## Help for unrepresented parties

- Net HaMishpat: unrepresented parties may file documents in an existing case with the national ID login; opening a case online was not possible per the State Comptroller 2022 (current status Unverified). https://library.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Documents/2022/2022.3/2022.3-304-NET.pdf
- Judiciary information centre: 077-2703333 / *3852 (Unverified on the courts' site).
- Labour-court volunteer desks in some regions.
- Welfare Ministry assistance units (37) at family and religious courts: intake and mediation, not representation.
- Legal Aid: subject, means and merit tests; NI matters exempt from the means test. https://www.gov.il/he/service/legal_aid_application
