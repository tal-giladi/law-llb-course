## Bar exam

| Item | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Legal basis | Bar Association Law ss. 38–40 (exam after internship; programme by regulations; 9-member written-exam committee) | https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לשכת_עורכי_הדין |
| Format (reg. 18) | Written, 3 parts: MCQ on procedure and practical subjects; MCQ on substantive law with statute excerpts; drafting task. Each MCQ ≥ 40%, drafting ≥ 15%. Committee publishes order, times, weights and statute list ≥ 3 months ahead | REG |
| Subjects (reg. 18א) | Procedure: court powers, civil procedure, criminal procedure, enforcement and insolvency, procedural aspects of Basic Laws, disciplinary procedure. Substantive: obligations (contracts and torts), criminal law, property, commercial law, professional ethics, Basic Laws, property taxation, limitation | REG |
| Questions per part | Set per sitting; prep sites report 40 + 40 (Unverified officially) | https://be-lawyers.co.il/about-exams/ |
| Pass mark | 60 (reg. 18ג); June 2026 sitting only lowered to 55; December 2024 to 56 | REG; https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1001554748 |
| Oral | No regular oral; temporary order to 31.12.2026 allows a supplementary oral for some reservists scoring 55–59 | REG; Globes |
| Sittings | Twice a year (in practice June and December), announced ≥ 90 days ahead (reg. 15); no limit on retakes found | REG |
| June 2026 results | 1,243 of 2,919 passed (~42%) | Globes |

## Internship

- 12 months, now permanent: Bar Association Law s. 35(א) "תקופת ההתמחות תהיה 12 חודשים" (amendment 47, 2025). https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_לשכת_עורכי_הדין ; bill: https://fs.knesset.gov.il/25/law/25_ls1_5980752.pdf . Exact commencement and transition: Unverified.
- Previously 18 months (2017–2023), cut by a wartime temporary order.
- Mentors: s. 29 (judges; lawyers with 5+ years approved by the Bar; public-service lawyers with 5+ years). Intern may appear in the magistrates' court for the mentor's clients in the last 6 months (s. 37).
