# Module 12: rows for references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md

New and expanded rows (checked 2026-10-17):

| Forum | Act for yourself? | Who else may represent you | Source | Covered in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Small claims court (expand: hearing) | Yes. Non-appearance: neither party → claim struck out; plaintiff only → judgment on the claim after declaring it true; defendant only → claim dismissed (reg. 11); set aside within 30 days of service (reg. 12). Requests may be made orally at the hearing (reg. 8) | As in the existing row (Courts Law s. 63) | https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיפוט_בתביעות_קטנות_(סדרי_דין) | Module 12 (12.6) |
| Leave to appeal a small-claims decision (רת״ק), district court (new) | Yes in law (reg. 170 of the 2018 Regulations applies through reg. 16(ג)); in practice a written legal argument, advice recommended | A lawyer | Courts Law s. 64; Small Claims Regulations regs 16, 16א; CPR regs 134–135, 145, 149 | Module 12 (12.7). Security for costs per CPR Third Schedule item 71, exemption possible (reg. 135(ב)) |
| Israel Consumer Council (new) | Yes; free advice and intervention | The Council itself may represent consumers before courts (Council Law s. 2(א)(4)(ג)) | https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המועצה_הישראלית_לצרכנות | Module 12 (12.4, 12.8) |
| Banking Supervision public-enquiries unit, Bank of Israel (expand the regulator row) | Yes; free; complain to the bank first (⚠️ manual check); findings give no new right and do not extend deadlines (Banking (Service to Customer) Law s. 16(ד)) | Anyone may help; drafting for others for pay is reserved (Bar Law s. 20) | https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הבנקאות_(שירות_ללקוח) | Module 12 (12.4) |
| Capital Market Authority public-enquiries unit (expand) | Yes; free; not normally investigated once a court or arbitration hearing has begun (Insurance Supervision Law s. 60(ב)) | As above | https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הפיקוח_על_שירותים_פיננסיים_(ביטוח) | Module 12 (12.4) |
| Ministry of Communications complaints unit (new) | Yes; free | As above | gov.il (⚠️ manual check) | Module 12 (12.4) |
| Legal Aid (expand) | Apply in writing yourself (Legal Aid Law s. 3(א)); the office may represent you, or decide you appear in person with written instructions (Legal Aid Regs reg. 4(א)(5)); people aged 65+ in exploitation-based contract or consumer cases need no means test (Schedule item 16, 5786) | Legal Aid lawyers | https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הסיוע_המשפטי ; https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הסיוע_המשפטי | Module 12 (12.8) |
