## Corrections to the task brief

- The res gestae provision is Evidence Ordinance s. 9 (and the victim-of-violence statement s. 10); s. 11 is proof of the accused's statement. Privileges are ss. 44–52, not only 47–52. Section 57א is the manufacturer's presumption. The lessons use the correct numbers.
