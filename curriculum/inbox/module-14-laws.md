## Verification results requested for this module

- **Self-representation in the Administrative Affairs Court: settled (from the regulations).** Reg. 44א of the Administrative Affairs Courts (Procedure) Regulations applies chapter 20 of the Civil Procedure Regulations 2018 (except regs 176(ב), 177(5)–(6), 178, 179) in the Administrative Affairs Court; chapter 20, sign A, opens with reg. 170: "כל פעולה בבית משפט הנדרשת מבעל דין או המותרת לו לפי דין, רשאי הוא לעשותה בעצמו או בידי עורך דין". Regs 5(ב)(1), 10(ב)(1) and 24(ב)(1) ask for the lawyer's details only "אם הוא מיוצג". Conclusion: an individual may petition, appeal and appear in person. Corporations: still unverified (no free source).
- **Reasonableness, October 2026.** s. 15(ד1) void since 1.1.2024 (official summary: 12/15 reviewability; 8/15 voidness). Web searches on 2026-10-17 found no new legislation or ruling restoring or re-enacting a reasonableness limit. ⚠️ manual check of the Knesset database before relying on this; the Knesset dissolved in July 2026 and elections are on 27.10.2026 (per lesson 06.5).
- **Conflict of interest (14.4):** no free text of a leading ruling found; lesson uses a "key text" and refers to the May 2025 Shin Bet dismissal ruling as news-based (as in 06.6) ⚠️.
- **Administrative evidence (14.5):** doctrine stated without a citation (the classic ruling's text was not available free); Ometz used for indictments as administrative evidence.
- **gov.il pages not opened:** Government FOI unit online system; Reshumot (Yalkut HaPirsumim) search; Judiciary forms 1–3 (⚠️ manual check).
