| Developer defects and delay claims (new) | Yes, in your own name; small claims for an individual within the ceiling, magistrates' court above; an engineer's expert opinion is usually needed | A lawyer; the house committee may hire a lawyer for common-property defects | Sale (Apartments) Law ss. 4–5א, https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המכר_(דירות) ; Courts Law s. 60 | Module 20 (20.2, 20.9, project) |

| Capital Market, Insurance and Savings Authority public enquiries (new) | Yes; free; does not stop limitation (Insurance Contract Law s. 31א) | Same as above | ⚠️ manual check of the gov.il page | Module 20 (20.6, 20.10) |
