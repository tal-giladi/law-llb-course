# Module 7: rows for references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md

New rows (checked 2026-10-17):

| Forum | Act for yourself? | Who else may represent you | Source | Covered in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Public comment on a government bill memorandum or draft regulation (government legislation site, regulators' drafts pages) | Yes; open to anyone, no fee | Anyone may comment for an organisation that authorises them; drafting comments for others for pay or as an occupation is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20) | AG guideline 2.3005 paras. 26–27, https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/guide/legislation-documents/he/2-3005-190921.pdf ; https://www.tazkirim.gov.il/s/ (⚠️ manual check of the site's current steps) | Module 7 (07.5, project) |
| Knesset committee sessions (attending, written submissions, speaking if invited) | Yes; sessions are public by default (Rules s. 118); the chair decides whom to invite and hear (s. 125) | Unpaid representatives of organisations; paid advocacy for a client is lobbying and needs a Knesset permit (Knesset Law ss. 65–66) | https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנון_הכנסת#סעיף_118 ; https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הכנסת#סעיף_65 | Module 7 (07.6, project) |
| Complaint to a regulator (e.g. Consumer Protection and Fair Trade Authority, Supervisor of Banks, Capital Market Authority) | Yes; free | Anyone may help; handling claims for others for pay is reserved (s. 20) | Consumer Protection Law s. 20(א)(2), https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_הצרכן#סעיף_20 ; other regulators ⚠️ manual check on gov.il | Module 7 (07.3) |
| Indirect challenge to a regulation's validity in your own case | Yes, as part of your own claim or defence | A lawyer | Wael, CA 4926/08, https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/iscp-opinions/393 | Module 7 (07.4) |
| Petition to annul a regulation or Government decision (usually HCJ) | Legally open to an individual; in practice a lawyer's task | A lawyer | see module 6 rows (HCJ) | Module 7 (07.4, pointer) |
