# Module 8: rows for references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md

New and expanded rows (checked 2026-10-17):

| Forum | Act for yourself? | Who else may represent you | Source | Covered in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Magistrates' and District courts, civil (expand the existing row) | Yes (reg. 170). The Regulations adapt for you: no compulsory preliminary discussion (reg. 35(א)); the registry serves your documents (reg. 160); video pre-trial only with your consent (reg. 61(ג)); the mediation-intake meeting applies to you (reg. 37(ב)) | Only a lawyer; no lay friend (רע"א 8120/08) | https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי | Module 8 (08.1, 08.2, 08.8). Online opening of a regular civil case by an unrepresented party: Unverified on 2026-10-17 (service pages written for lawyers; Comptroller 2022 found it unavailable); registry filing always available |
| Small claims court (add filing note) | Yes; online through the Judiciary's service with the national digital ID (computer, PDFs) or on paper at the registry | As in the existing row (Courts Law s. 63) | https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/הגשת_תביעה_קטנה (opened 2026-10-17); https://www.gov.il/he/service/filing_a_small_claim (⚠️ manual check) | Module 8 (08.3, 08.8) |
| Criminal: suspect at the pre-indictment stage (new) | Partly: a felony suspect may write to the prosecution within the s. 60א period himself, but a lawyer is strongly advised | A lawyer; the Public Defender if eligible | CPL s. 60א, https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_סדר_הדין_הפלילי#סעיף_60א | Module 8 (08.4) |
| Criminal: detainee (new) | Has the right to meet and consult a lawyer without delay, in private (Arrests Law s. 34); brought before a judge within 24 hours (s. 29) | A lawyer; Public Defender | https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_סדר_הדין_הפלילי_(סמכויות_אכיפה_–_מעצרים) | Module 8 (08.4) |
| Criminal defence: mandatory appointment (expand the existing row) | The court must appoint a defender in the cases in CPL s. 15(א) (serious charges, minors under 16 outside juvenile court, disabilities, mandatory or sought actual imprisonment) | A lawyer; Public Defender | https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_סדר_הדין_הפלילי#סעיף_15 | Module 8 (08.4) |
| Administrative Affairs Court (expand the existing "Presumably yes" row) | Still Unverified in an official free source; reg. 170 of the civil regulations is the general rule and the admin. procedure regulations contemplate petitioners' own details "and, if represented, the lawyer's" (reg. 5(ב)(1)), implying unrepresented petitioners are expected | A lawyer | https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_בתי_משפט_לענינים_מינהליים_(סדרי_דין) | Module 8 (08.5) |
