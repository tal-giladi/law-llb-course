# Laws and rulings inbox: module 10

Statute texts were read in full on Wikisource through its API (raw wikitext, 2026-10-17). Wikisource is an unofficial consolidation; each page's header links the official Reshumot publication. Knesset PDFs (fs.knesset.gov.il) were downloaded and read for the 2018 and 2025 amendments and the 2025 bill. Cardozo case pages were read for the abstracts (the full-text PDFs sit behind a Cloudflare check and were not read). supremedecisions.court.gov.il was not used.
