# Module 15: numbers used (for references/current-numbers.md)

Checked 2026-10-17 against Wikisource consolidated texts (unofficial; downloaded with action=raw and read section by section; confirm against Reshumot or the Knesset database when merging). The Civil Procedure Regulations text on Wikisource includes amendments up to ק"ת תשפ"ז 178 (a temporary order effective 7.10.2026). Rows already on the page (magistrates' ceiling, small-claims figures, defence period, page limits, reply, mediation-intake threshold, fast-track ceiling and periods, appeal period, magistrates' fixed-sum fee, fee exemption, general limitation periods, limitation in minority) are used by module 15 but not repeated, except where module 15 adds a detail.
