# Module 29: laws and rulings cited (for references/laws-index.md)

Checked 2026-10-18. Wikisource texts are unofficial consolidations, downloaded as raw wikitext and read section by section. supremedecisions.court.gov.il was not used (unreachable). Rulings were read on Versa (Cardozo) in English translation. gov.il, kolzchut.org.il and boi.org.il block bots; their pages are marked ⚠️ manual check.

**Notes for the brief:**
- **"Execution Law s. 81 harassment prohibitions?"**: s. 81 of the Execution Law is "an obligation enforceable as a judgment"; it contains no rules on collection conduct. We found no general Israeli statute on debt collectors' conduct (⚠️ not exhaustively searched). Lesson 29.7 teaches the scattered rules instead: Consumer Protection Law s. 13ב2 (debt notice), Fair Credit Law ss. 7–8 and 7א (acceleration, collection costs), Execution Law s. 81א4 (reduction of interest), Penal Law offences of threats and extortion and the Prevention of Stalking Law (named, not quoted), and a Bank of Israel press release imposing sanctions on banks for improper debt collection (title seen on boi.org.il; text blocked ⚠️).
- **"2024+ movable-collateral reform"**: none enacted. A Ministry of Justice memorandum (2010) and a government bill (first reading 2012 and again 25.7.2015, per Calcalist; Knesset PDF https://fs.knesset.gov.il/20/law/20_ls1_313219.pdf could not be text-extracted) were not enacted; the Wikisource consolidated Pledges Law shows no replacement. The 1994 Pledges Regulations were amended in 2023 (online filing for non-authorised filers, regs 13טו1–13טו2). ⚠️ Tal may confirm in the Knesset bills database that no 2024–2026 bill is pending.
- **Credit Data Law**: a 2026 (תשפ״ו) amendment takes effect on 26.1.2027 (companies, opt-out, correction periods); lessons flag it. Amendment number and Reshumot reference not checked ⚠️.
- **Fair Credit Law**: "as amended 2017–2018" = Amendment No. 5 (ס״ח תשע״ז, 1208) and its 2019 amendment (תשע״ט, 48); exact commencement date of Amendment 5 not checked ⚠️.
- **Pledges Registrar online search**: the service and its 2026 fee (₪12 per criterion online) come from the regulations' Second Schedule as updated on Wikisource; the gov.il service page was not opened (⚠️ manual check).
