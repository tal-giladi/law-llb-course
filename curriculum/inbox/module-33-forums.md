# Module 33: rows for references/can-i-act-without-a-lawyer.md

New rows (checked 2026-10-18).

| Forum | Act for yourself? | Who else may represent you | Source | Covered in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Class action: applying for certification (district / magistrates' / administrative affairs court / regional labour court) | In practice no. The Law builds the procedure around class counsel, defined as a lawyer (s. 2); the court states counsel's identity in the certification (s. 14(א)(2)) and tests adequacy of representation (s. 8(א)(3)). Whether a self-represented individual can ever be certified: Unverified (no free ruling found) | A lawyer as class counsel; a qualifying organisation or listed public authority may be the applicant (s. 4(א)(2)–(3)) | https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_תובענות_ייצוגיות | 33.1, 33.2 |
| Class action: objecting to a settlement | Yes, a class member in his own name (s. 18(ד)); reasoned, written, within 45 days of publication; affidavit advisable | A lawyer; organisations, relevant public authorities and the AG object in their own right | same, s. 18(ד), (ד1) | 33.3, 33.8 |
| Class action: opting out / leaving a settlement | Yes, the member himself: Form 1 after certification (s. 11, reg. 7); request under s. 18(ו) for a settlement | A lawyer | same; https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_תובענות_ייצוגיות | 33.3, 33.7 |
| Class action: taking part in the hearing (s. 15) or commenting on a withdrawal (reg. 11(ג)) | Yes, a member, by written request in the case file | A lawyer; organisations and public authorities | same | 33.2, 33.5 |
| Class action: challenge to an administrator's decision (s. 20(ב)(1), reg. 15) | Yes, the member affected, with affidavit, within 30 days | A lawyer | same | 33.4 |
| Amicus curiae application | No for individuals in practice; an organisation applies, usually through its lawyer | Lawyer for the organisation | Kozli (1999) via Doron et al. (2015) ⚠️ | 33.5 |
