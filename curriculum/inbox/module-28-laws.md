**Notes for the brief:**
- The brief's "limited means debtor s. 69א–" is the s. 69ג–69י1 chapter (s. 69א is the payment-instruction rules); since 15.9.2019 no new declarations can be made (s. 69י1), and the 2015–2019 discharge track (ss. 69י2–69י23) has expired except for pending files.
- The brief's "small debts under ₪150,000 at the Commissioner vs court above" is not the current structure: debts not exceeding the threshold (₪150,000 in 2019 terms, ₪176,923.12 in 2026) go to a **dedicated execution registrar** (Insolvency Law s. 103(ב), ch. 12); above it, to the **Insolvency Commissioner** (s. 103(א)); the court (magistrates' court for individuals) hears creditors' petitions and the rehabilitation order (s. 103(ג), s. 161).
- "2025+ amendments": Amendment No. 9 to the Insolvency Law (passed 19.1.2026, ס״ח 3488, 21.1.2026, p. 236) added s. 121(ב) and made permanent a temporary stay of proceedings to form a debt arrangement for companies (ss. 319ג–319טז) and individuals (ss. 319יז ff.); it also amended the Execution Law (Amendment 79). Its commencement and transitional provisions could not be read reliably from the PDF text layer (⚠️). The Execution Law s. 7 shows a תשפ״ה (2025) amendment (digital service under the Digital Communication with Public Bodies Law, ss. 3ו–3ז), not studied in detail.
- Enforcement and Collection Authority personal area: a search of gov.il service pages (2026-10-18) shows "הגשת בקשה כללית להוצאה לפועל" listing creditor and debtor as filers through the personal area, code 988, and an online form for those without access; the page itself returned 403 (⚠️ manual check).
