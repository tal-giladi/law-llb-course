# Module 32: laws and rulings cited (for references/laws-index.md)

Checked 2026-10-18. Wikisource texts are unofficial consolidations, downloaded through the Wikisource API (wikitext) and read section by section. supremedecisions.court.gov.il was not used (unreachable). Supreme Court judgments were read in English translation on Cardozo Versa (versa.cardozo.yu.edu), or on another free host where stated. gov.il and kolzchut.org.il block bots; pages there are marked ⚠️ manual check.

**Findings and corrections to the brief:**
- **Statutory notice-and-takedown: none.** The Copyright Law 2007 (as consolidated on 2026-10-18, including Amendment 5 of 2019 and the 2026 amendments) has no safe harbour and no takedown procedure. The only notice-based provisions are s. 27א (orphan works) and s. 56א (no statutory damages against certain non-commercial online posters who stop after notice). Courts apply a case-law notice-and-takedown standard to hosts (Rotter, 2011, as reported). Platform takedowns are taught as private practice.
- **Statutory damages s. 56:** the ceiling is still ₪100,000 per infringement; no ministerial order under s. 56(ד) was found (⚠️ search not exhaustive).
- **2019 Amendment 5** (ס"ח 2777, 9.1.2019, p. 187) added, among others: s. 27א (orphan works), s. 48א (indirect infringement by profit-making facilitation of access), s. 53א (blocking orders), s. 56א (online exception to statutory damages) and chapter 8.1, ss. 60א–60ח (disclosure of an online infringer's identity). Procedure regulations: תקנות זכות יוצרים (סדרי דין בבקשה לחשיפת זהותו...), התש"ף–2019.
- **2026 amendments** (תשפ"ו; Amendment 10 to the Performers and Broadcasters Rights Law and others) change ss. 13, 28א, 29, 31, 41, 56א, 67 and add a Schedule, mostly from 20.5.2027 (micro-business exemption from public performance; educational frameworks). Wikisource also lists a 2026 HCJ interim order (בג"ץ 49765-07-26) in the law's header; its subject was not checked (⚠️). Not taught in module 32 beyond a mention.
- **Trade Marks Ordinance title on Wikisource** is "פקודת סימני מסחר" (no ה-), URL https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_סימני_מסחר.
- **The Ordinance has no statutory damages**; statutory damages for brand cases come from the Commercial Torts Law s. 13 (passing off).
- **Online filing of trade marks:** the Regulations allow individuals to file on paper or online (reg. 6), require online filing for corporations and professionals (reg. 6(ב)) and electronic identification online (reg. 6ב(ג)). The Patent Office's 2024 WIPO report says all new applications must be filed through the online platform: conflict noted in 32.9 as "check the live service" (⚠️ manual check).
- **Employee inventions:** the right to compensation is waivable (בג"ץ 4353/14 ברזני נ' ישקר, as reported by Szatmary 2020); the judgment text was not verified on a free host (⚠️).
