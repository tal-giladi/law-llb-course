# Module 24: laws and rulings cited (for references/laws-index.md)

Checked 2026-10-17. Wikisource texts are unofficial consolidations, downloaded through the Wikisource API (wikitext) and read section by section. supremedecisions.court.gov.il was not used (unreachable); rulings are linked to the Cardozo Israeli Supreme Court Project (versa.cardozo.yu.edu / larc.cardozo.yu.edu) where an English translation exists, or to Hebrew Wikipedia as a marked secondary source. gov.il, kolzchut.org.il and police.gov.il block bots: items from them are marked ⚠️ manual check.

**Important correction for the brief and for older sources:** the Criminal Register and Rehabilitation of Offenders Law 1981 (including its ss. 14 and 16 on limitation and deletion, and s. 11א on closed files) was replaced on 12.7.2022 by the Criminal Information and Rehabilitation of Offenders Law 2019. The module teaches the 2019 law (police-record cancellation is s. 33; limitation and deletion ss. 18–23) and mentions the 1981 sections only to translate older documents. CPL s. 67א(ד)(2) and s. 67ה(5) still refer to the 1981 law in the Wikisource text; ⚠️ check whether a consequential amendment updated them.
