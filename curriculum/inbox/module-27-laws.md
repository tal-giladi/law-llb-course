# Module 27: laws and rulings cited (for references/laws-index.md)

Checked 2026-10-18. Wikisource texts are unofficial consolidations, downloaded through the Wikisource API (wikitext) and read section by section. supremedecisions.court.gov.il was not used (unreachable); no Supreme Court ruling's full text was reached, so every ruling rests on a marked secondary source (Hebrew Wikipedia, Cardozo Versa translation, or press). gov.il, kolzchut.org.il and the Planning Administration's sites (mavat.iplan.gov.il, iplan.gov.il) returned error or 403 pages to automated requests; they are marked ⚠️ manual check.

**Note for the brief:** the brief's "permits ss. 145–158" and "s. 12ב appeals committees" are correct; note that Chapter 10 (ss. 203–254יח) was wholly replaced by Amendment 116 (2017). The brief's "objections ss. 100–107" is right, but the formal requirement (affidavit) is in s. 103א and the appeals are in ss. 110–112. The administrative-appeal period from an arnona appeals committee is now 60 days under reg. 23(ב) of the Administrative Courts Procedure Regulations (amended 2022; the Arnona Appeals Law sets none).
