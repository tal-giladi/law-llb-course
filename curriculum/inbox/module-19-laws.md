# Module 19: laws, treaties and rulings cited (for references/laws-index.md)

Checked 2026-10-17. Israeli statute texts were fetched as raw wikitext from he.wikisource.org and read section by section (unofficial consolidations). Treaty texts: the UN Office of Legal Affairs PDF (VCLT), the ICJ site (Statute), the UN Charter page, Avalon (Montevideo), HCCH (Hague Abduction), UNICEF (CRC), the UN CRPD PDF, the ICC's Rome Statute PDF.
