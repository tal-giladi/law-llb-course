# Module 26: laws and rulings cited (for references/laws-index.md)

Checked 2026-10-17. Wikisource texts are unofficial consolidations, downloaded through the Wikisource API (wikitext) and read section by section. supremedecisions.court.gov.il was not used (unreachable); no ruling's full text was reached, so every ruling below rests on Hebrew Wikipedia as a marked secondary source (⚠️). gov.il and kolzchut.org.il block bots; Kol-Zchut pages were read as raw wikitext and are marked ⚠️ manual check.

**Note for the brief:** the brief's "s. 104 maintenance from the estate" is wrong: s. 104 is the order of priority of estate debts; maintenance from the estate is ss. 56–65. Section 25 is indeed the curing rule ("basic elements", Amendment 11, 2004; Registrar added 2023).
